# Steelcast

## Verdict for the portfolio
Not now. A clean, debt-free, high-return foundry that should roughly double profit to about ₹170cr by FY30 as its plant fills and a new foundry starts. But at ₹3,536cr (39 times trailing profit, against a five-year median of about 22) the price already assumes all of that: at a fair 22 times FY30 profit a buyer earns about 2% a year, and even management's full plan at 26 times gives only about 12%. Q1 volume also ran well behind the raised guide.

## Clean profit base
- **Reported:** FY26 profit ₹87cr; TTM to June 2026 ₹91cr (operating profit ₹119cr, other income ₹16cr, depreciation ₹13cr, no interest).
- **Removed:** FY26 other income included an ₹8cr currency gain (the note); Steelcast keeps its foreign-exchange exposure open and shares rupee moves with customers, so this is not a steady earner. Removing it (₹6cr after tax) gives **clean TTM ≈ ₹85cr**. The remaining ~₹8cr of other income is interest on ~₹100–120cr of cash, which I keep but let fall as the cash goes into the new foundry.
- **P/E check:** site 39.0 (₹3,536cr ÷ ₹91cr) and Screener 39.7 agree; on the clean base it is about 42.

Drivers:
- **Latest call (28 Sep 2026 conference):** use of the 29,000-tonne plant 48% in FY26, about 60% in FY27 (63% was said in July), 79% in FY28 (75–76% earlier in the same call) and 90% in FY29, a 24% a year volume growth over three years; new 8,500t foundry (₹120cr) by March 2028, ~₹300cr of sales at peak over about three years; ₹225cr of capex to FY29 all from own cash; sustainable margin 25–26% ("lately 28–29% because of rupee depreciation" and other benefits that may not last); order book ₹140cr (3–4 months).
- **Track record:** sales were flat from FY23 to FY26 (₹477cr → ₹423cr) and profit grew only ~7% a year; the FY27 guide was raised to 25–30% in July, yet Q1 tonnes rose 11.6% and the core margin stayed at 25.6%; the hybrid power plant slipped from June to December. So I run volumes about a year behind the plan: 57% use in FY27, 70% in FY28, 80% in FY29 and 86% in FY30, plus ~2,500t from the new foundry in its second year.
- **Price:** ~₹3.04 lakh a tonne in FY26, rising ~3% a year (cost pass-through each quarter; more ground-engaging tools, 1% of sales in FY26 heading to ~6% by FY29).
- **Margin:** 26.5–27%, inside management's own 25–26% "sustainable" plus a little operating leverage; 26.5% in FY30 as the new plant starts up.
- **Below the line:** depreciation ₹14cr → ₹27cr as ₹225cr of capex lands; no interest; other income ₹7cr → ₹5cr; tax 25%.
- **Growth rate:** about 22% a year from the clean TTM base (18% from FY26 over four years), close to the note's ~20%.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
In March 2030 the old plant will be near 86–90% full, but the new foundry will be only about a third used, giving visible growth toward its ~₹300cr peak in FY31–FY32, on top of end markets management sees growing 5–6% a year, more parts (350+ now) and more countries (16 → 18). The market will expect about 12–16% growth → the 18–26 band. Upper-half features: net cash, return on capital of 32–42% for four years. Lower-half features: three customer groups take 75–88% of sales, mining and earthmoving are cyclical (so FY30, with plants nearly full, is closer to a good year than a bad one), and the US and Germany are ~70% of exports. The stock's own five-year median P/E is about 22 (MoneyWorks4Me; Wisesheets shows a 21 five-year average). I use **22**. Today's 39 reflects a re-rating since 2025 (price up 57% in a year) that already pays for the volume ramp.

## Returns
Market value ₹3,536.43cr (site, 15 Oct; Screener ₹3,603cr on 16 Oct). No dilution (management ruled out a rights issue; a bonus issue would not change value). Dividend yield 0.47%.
- **Normal case:** ₹170cr × 22 = ₹3,740cr. (3,740 ÷ 3,536)^(1/3.45) − 1 + 0.47% = **2.1% a year**.
- At 26× it is 7.1%; management's full plan (profit ~₹200cr) at 26× gives 12.3%; holding today's 39× gives 20.5%. To earn 25% on my FY30 profit the exit P/E would need to be about 44.
- **Bad case:** mining and earthmoving orders cool and use stalls near 70%; margin 25%; FY30 profit ~₹120cr; exit P/E one band down at 17. ₹120cr × 17 = ₹2,040cr → **−14.3% a year**.
- **Tail risk (separate):** one of the three main customer groups (75–88% of sales) cutting orders sharply or moving a part family to another foundry; new US duties on Indian castings.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet 5/5:** no borrowings since FY23, ₹96cr of net cash, and ₹225cr of capex to FY29 funded from cash flow; the only legal item found is a 2010 land purchase before the Gujarat High Court.
- **Earnings quality 4/5:** ₹350cr of operating cash on ₹512cr of operating profit over FY22–FY26 (68%), and ₹86cr in FY26 (about all of profit after tax); but the cash cycle lengthened from 111 to 158 days in two years and FY26 other income included an ₹8cr currency gain.
- **Management 3/5:** high and steady margins and no debt, but sales were flat for three years, the raised FY27 guide started with an 11.6% Q1 and was quietly trimmed in September, a power project slipped six months, and the chairman's 1.48% pledge was disclosed inconsistently.
- **Moat and runway 3/5:** each of 350+ parts is approved by the customer and prices move by formula, with 54 customers in nine sectors; but customers keep two or three suppliers per part (Steelcast's share 20–50%), three groups dominate sales, and demand follows the mining and construction-machine cycle.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
- Q1 FY27 tonnes +11.6% against a 25–30% guide; September trimmed FY27 use to 60% → my FY27 is 57% use, +18.5% volume.
- Sales flat FY23–FY26; profit ~7% a year → plant-fill haircut of about a year throughout.
- Management calls 25–26% the sustainable margin; 28–29% was helped by the rupee → margin held at 26.5–27%.
- Three customer groups take 75–88% of sales → lower-half factor; bad case and tail risk.
- Cash cycle 111 → 158 days in two years → earnings quality 4, not 5.
- Chairman's 1.48% pledge in the annual report vs none in his April filing → management 3.
- 2010 land purchase before the Gujarat High Court → noted; no size disclosed.
- Family pay about 8% of profit → management 3.
- Hybrid power plant slipped from June to December 2026; gas 50–55% dearer than February → margin not lifted above 27%.
- US and Germany ~70% of exports; US Section 301 duty of 10% on most Indian goods since July 2026 → bad case.
- P/E 39–40 against a five-year median of ~22 → exit P/E 22 is the core reason the return is low.
