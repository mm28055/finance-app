# Deep Industries

## Verdict for the portfolio
A good, fast-growing oilfield-services business, but not a 25% idea at ₹747. On clean profit the stock trades at about 17 times, not the 11.5 the site shows. Management's ₹500cr FY28 target needs things that have not happened yet: the stalled ONGC field must double its output and offshore boats must be bought. The promoter-company loan, which the note made a condition of owning, has slipped from "by end of Q2" to "by March". The base case is about 16% a year. **Not now**; revisit if Q2 and Q3 show the loan cleared and the field ramping.

## Clean profit base
- **Reported profit is distorted both ways.** FY26 reported profit was ₹197cr. The March quarter carried about ₹208cr of Kandla write-offs (other income of −₹183cr against about +₹25cr normally) and a ₹112.5cr tax credit; reported Q4 profit was −₹7cr.
- **What I removed.** I took Q4 FY26 at its pre-write-off pre-tax profit of ₹88.5cr (from the note), taxed at 21%: about ₹70cr. I also deducted the 25% of Dolphin Offshore owned by outsiders (about ₹3.5cr a quarter, from the gap between total profit and EPS × 6.40cr shares).
- **Clean FY26 profit for shareholders: about ₹260cr.** By quarter: Q1 ₹59cr, Q2 ₹67cr, Q3 ₹68cr, Q4 ₹66cr.
- **Clean profit over the last four quarters (to June 2026): about ₹287cr.** Q1 FY27 was ₹85.4cr for shareholders (₹89.1cr total).
- **P/E check.** The site shows 11.5 and Screener 11.6. Neither fits:
  - market value ₹4,784cr ÷ reported trailing profit of ₹224cr = 21×;
  - ÷ clean shareholders' profit of ₹287cr = **16.7×**.
  - The dashboard stores the "adjusted" Q4 profit of ₹148.6cr, which includes the tax credit. Use 16.7×.
- **Other income needs watching.** It runs at ₹10–24cr a quarter, about ₹90cr a year outside the write-off quarters, against only about ₹250cr of cash and investments. The CFO says it is recurring treasury income. Some of it is 12% interest on the Prabha Energy loan (about ₹16cr a year), which ends when the loan is repaid. I carry ₹55–60cr a year of other income forward, not ₹90cr.

## Profit path FY27–FY30

| Year | Revenue | Operating margin (excl. other income) | Profit to shareholders |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY26 (clean) | ₹891cr | 40% | ₹260cr |
| FY27 | ₹1,130cr | 40% | ₹314cr |
| FY28 | ₹1,420cr | 41.5% | ₹381cr |
| FY29 | ₹1,630cr | 41.5% | ₹435cr |
| FY30 | ₹1,870cr | 41.5% | ₹500cr |

**Drivers.** Management says the standalone Indian business, flat at about ₹175cr a quarter (₹700cr a year) for five quarters, grows 18–20% in FY27 as four or five new compression and processing contracts start. I use 18% in FY27 and 15% after.
- **ONGC production contract (Rajahmundry):** the CFO gave about ₹70cr of revenue in FY27 (30 Sep call) and ₹150cr+ in FY28 if output rises from 1.44 to 2.5–3 lakh cubic metres a day.
- **Dolphin Offshore:** ₹160–170cr a year from the one big barge at a 60–65% margin. I add one more vessel from FY28 (about ₹80cr of revenue), since management is "in active discussions" but has signed nothing.
- **Dubai:** ₹60–100cr a year. Q1's "more than ₹50cr" in a single quarter included equipment sales, so it flattered Q1 growth.

**Costs below operating profit.** Depreciation rises from ₹59cr to about ₹130cr on ₹250–300cr a year of capex. Interest rises to about ₹30cr as rigs and vessels are bought partly on debt. Tax is 22%, and the outside owners of Dolphin take ₹15–25cr a year.

**Growth rate.** FY30 profit is about **₹500cr, about 18% a year from FY26's clean ₹260cr**.

**Are management's FY27 ₹350cr and FY28 ₹500cr profit targets credible?**
- **FY27 ₹350cr: roughly, with a 5–10% haircut.** Q1's ₹89cr total profit × 4 = ₹356cr, so the target is the current run rate, not a stretch. But:
  - Q1 was helped by a lumpy Dubai quarter.
  - Q1's operating margin was 39% on Screener's basis against the 43–45% guided. The company's 43.6% counts ₹23.7cr of other income, and the CFO said on 30 Sep that margin improvement must "wait for 1 quarter".
  - FY27 needs no new write-off. My figure is ₹329cr in total, or ₹314cr for shareholders.
- **FY28 ₹500cr: not credible on what is signed today.** It needs a 43% jump in one year. The three things that would deliver it are all unproven:
  1. **The ONGC field.** Mori-5, the key well, has been shut since the January gas leak and fire. The "incremental production" date has slipped at every call:
     - "a month or two" earlier in the year;
     - "one or two quarters" in May;
     - "by October 2026" in July's opening remarks, then "Q4 or Q1 of next year" in July's Q&A;
     - "will begin increasing during October" on 30 Sep.
     Doubling output needs about ₹150cr of new wells to work. That is geological risk the company does not carry anywhere else.
  2. **Offshore.** One or two vessel purchases are targeted for H2 FY27. Nothing is finalised, and capex is committed only against a contract.
  3. **New production-contract wins.** ONGC's Round 2 bids are due in October; nothing is won yet.

  On signed work and normal growth I get about ₹400cr total (₹381cr for shareholders) in FY28, about 20% below target.
- **Kandla write-offs.** Management said in July "completely done with the legacy issues at Kandla, so there will be no more write-offs", and on 30 Sep "no exceptional item this year". But two March quarters running carried write-offs: about ₹257cr in FY25 and ₹208cr in FY26, ₹458cr in total. The auditor wrote emphasis paragraphs on the Kandla restatement and on disputed receivables, and about ₹160cr of old Dolphin receivables backed by arbitration awards is still unprovided. I do not assume a third write-off in the base case, but I do in the bad case.
- **Promoter-company loan.** The balance was ₹134.8cr plus ₹19cr of interest at March, and ₹86cr has been recovered. In July the target for the rest was "by the end of Q2". On 30 Sep, the last day of Q2, the CFO said "we believe the balance should be nil by March". The loan earns 12%, so it adds to other income while outstanding. But it shows management lends company money to promoter entities and lets the timetable slide. Deep also bought 70% of another promoter-linked company in December.

**Track record.** Deep beat growth guidance in Q1: revenue +40% against 25–30%. But it missed on the production contract's timing and on margin, and its profit has twice been wiped out by year-end write-offs. Hence the haircut.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
- **What supports growth after FY30:**
  - The ONGC production contract runs 15 years, and management hopes to finish it in 10.
  - ONGC and Oil India plan more production-contract rounds.
  - ONGC spends more than ₹30,000cr a year of capex.
  - Samudra Manthan (₹84,084cr to FY31) should lift demand for offshore support vessels.
  - Compressed biogas needs compression.
  - India has 215–220 onshore rigs and about half are outsourced, so the outsourced share is rising slowly.
- **What limits it:**
  - Almost all work is fixed-price tenders won by the lowest bidder from state producers.
  - Each new rig, compressor or vessel needs capex, at 30% of sales.
  - The production contract carries geological risk.
  - Two promoter families run the company, with related-party dealings.
- **Expected growth after FY30: about 12% a year.**
- **Anchors.** Deep's year-end P/E was 5.1 (FY22), 6.6 (FY23) and 15.9 (FY24) per Value Research. The stock was ₹366 on 19 March 2026 and has doubled since. Investing.com shows an industry average of about 11. The market pays 16.7× clean profit today.
- **Exit P/E: 16**, inside the 14–20 band for 8–12% growth. Not higher than today, because the governance discount is not clearing; the loan slipped.

## Returns
- **Normal case:** ₹500cr × 16 = ₹8,000cr in March 2030. Market value today is ₹4,784cr (site, 15 Oct). Dilution is 2.3%: the DIL ESOP 2026 allows up to 15 lakh options on 6.40cr shares; the QIP was dropped. Dividend yield 0.33%.
  ((500 × 16) ÷ (4,784 × 1.023))^(1/3.45) − 1 + 0.33% = **15.6% a year**.
- **Bad case:** the field disappoints, with Mori-5's replacement wells under-delivering and production-contract revenue stuck near ₹70–100cr. Standalone growth also stays at single digits, and another year-end receivable write-off hits. FY30 profit ₹380cr, exit P/E 12 (one band down). ((380 × 12) ÷ (4,784 × 1.023))^(1/3.45) − 1 + 0.33% = **−1.7% a year**.
- **Tail risk:** a fresh related-party loan or a large write-off on Dolphin's ₹160cr of arbitration-backed receivables, the kind of event that historically cut the P/E to 5–7×.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet: 3.** Net debt is only about ₹150cr against ₹382cr of operating profit. But ₹154cr (loan plus interest) sat with a promoter company at March, ₹160cr of old Dolphin receivables is unprovided, and capex of ₹250–300cr a year will add debt.
- **Earnings quality: 2.** Five-year operating cash was 87% of operating profit (₹878cr against ₹1,013cr). But ₹458cr of write-offs hit two years running, debtor days fell to 197 only because ₹208cr of receivables were written off, and about a quarter of pre-tax profit is other income.
- **Management: 2.** Growth guidance was beaten in Q1. But the production contract slipped four times, the loan repayment slipped from Q2 to March, it bought a promoter-linked company, there were two auditor emphasis paragraphs, the company secretary resigned in July, and a promoter relative was made a paid President.
- **Moat and runway: 3.** About 85% of outsourced gas compression and 30 years of ONGC approvals. But it is the lowest-price bidder on fixed-price state tenders, and owned assets must keep being bought to grow.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
1. **₹458cr of Kandla write-offs in the March quarters of FY25 and FY26.** Base excludes them as one-offs; the bad case assumes another. Score 2 on earnings quality.
2. **Promoter-company loan (Prabha Energy):** ₹134.8cr + ₹19cr interest at March. The rest was promised by Q2 and is now promised "by March". Kept as a hard check; it is the main reason the call changes from own to not now.
3. **Mori-5 shut since January; the production contract slipped at every call.** Base takes only ₹70cr of FY27 revenue from the field and haircuts FY28.
4. **Standalone revenue flat at about ₹175cr a quarter for five quarters.** Base uses 18% growth in FY27, then 15%, not the 20%+ implied by the FY28 target.
5. **Q1 operating margin 39% against 43–45% guided.** The company's figure includes other income. Base holds 40–41.5%.
6. **Site P/E of 11.5 is wrong; true clean P/E is 16.7.** I used 16.7 and capped the exit at 16.
7. **Other income of about ₹90cr a year looks large for ₹250cr of treasury.** Base carries ₹55–60cr.
8. **Two auditor emphasis paragraphs** (Kandla restatement, disputed receivables). Reflected in the management and earnings scores.
9. **Fixed-price, lowest-bidder state tenders; ONGC is the largest client** (no client above 40% of FY26). This caps the exit P/E.
10. **Bought 70% of another promoter-linked company in December 2025.** Governance discount kept.
11. **Capex about 30% of sales; five-year free cash only about ₹99cr.** Depreciation and interest are built into the path.
12. **Company secretary resigned July 2026; promoter relative Shail Savla made a paid President from October 2026.** Management score 2.
13. **Q1's growth flattered by a lumpy Dubai quarter** (₹50cr+ in Q1 against ₹60–100cr guided for the year). FY27 revenue held to ₹1,130cr.
