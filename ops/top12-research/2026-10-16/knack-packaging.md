# Knack Packaging

## Verdict for the portfolio
Not now. A genuinely good small business (returns above 30%, 21% volume growth in Q1, a funded plant that lifts capacity by about 60%), but the new plant only arrives in October 2027, so profit is flat-ish in FY28 and reaches about ₹170cr in FY30; at a fair 22 times that, a buyer at ₹2,537cr earns about 12% a year. Governance is unproven: a fifth of FY25 revenue was with promoter-related parties, the new plant sits on promoter land, and Cargill alone is 16–19% of sales.

## Clean profit base
- **Reported:** consolidated FY26 profit ₹90cr (company: ₹823cr revenue, 10.99% margin); Screener standalone ₹89cr. TTM to June 2026: ₹90cr − ₹20.6cr (Q1 FY26) + ₹30.5cr (Q1 FY27) = **₹100cr** consolidated, after a ₹3.4cr loss from the new Mexican joint venture.
- **Removed:** about ₹8cr of other income above the normal run-rate (FY26 other income jumped to ₹20cr from ₹11cr, ₹10cr of it in Q4 FY26, most likely currency gains on exports) → about ₹6cr after tax. **Clean TTM ≈ ₹94cr.** Treasury income on the ₹380cr IPO money only starts in Q2 FY27 and is temporary; I keep it out of the base and show it separately in FY27–FY28.
- **P/E check:** site 24.5 (₹2,537cr ÷ ~₹104cr) and Screener 25.1 agree; on my clean base it is about 27. Note the company's own "EBITDA margin" (20.2% in FY26, 22.35% in Q1) includes other income; Screener's operating margin was 18% and 21%.

Drivers:
- **Volume.** Latest call (30 Sep 2026 conference): FY27 plan 40,000–42,000 tonnes on 45,000–48,000 tonnes of capacity (one owned plant plus four rented); Q2 "on the same projection", no loss of revenue or profit. FY26 sold about 36,000–38,000 tonnes; Q1 FY27 10,940 tonnes (+21%). I use 42,500t (FY27), 47,000t (FY28), 54,000t (FY29), 61,500t (FY30). The Kadi plant (₹320cr of IPO money, due October 2027) takes owned capacity to about 70,000t; management will close most rented plants, cut the old plant from 26,000t to 15,000t, and ramp at 60→90% over one to two years with a 100–120-day customer onboarding cycle. I assume about 82% use of ~75,000t (owned plus one rented) in FY30, a little slower than the plan, because the company has no listed track record and FY28 is a messy move between plants.
- **Price and margin.** Revenue per kilo ₹235 in FY27 (polymer is high) easing to ~₹230. Operating profit per kilo (Screener basis): ₹38 in FY26, ₹48 in Q1 FY27 (pinch-bottom bags 22–23% of sales against 10% in FY24, an 11 MW solar farm, more exports). I use ₹47 in FY27, ₹45 in FY28 (start-up costs), rising to ₹48 by FY30. About half of customers pay polymer plus a fixed conversion fee; the rest are repriced per order.
- **Below the line.** Depreciation ₹40cr in FY27 (Q1 ₹10cr) rising to ₹64cr once the ₹320cr plant runs; interest ₹16cr falling to ₹10cr; other income ₹22cr in FY27 (IPO cash) falling to ₹6cr as it is spent; tax 25.2%; the Mexican venture loses ~₹10cr in FY27, breaking even by FY30.
- **Growth rate:** about 19% a year from the clean ₹94cr base to FY30 (17% from FY26's ₹90cr over four years), close to the note's ~18%, but with a flat FY28.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
In March 2030 the market will see a plant about 80–85% full, so further growth needs another plant; the end market (printed laminated woven bags in India) is projected by Technopak to grow about 15% a year, exports reach 74 countries, and Knack has about 10% of India. A reasonable expectation is 12–16% profit growth for FY31–FY33 → the 18–26 band. Upper-half features: high returns on capital (ROCE 30–34%) and modest debt. Lower-half features: concentration on Cargill, polymer-linked pricing, promoter-related dealings, and machinery anyone can buy. Listed peer Commercial Syn Bags traded at about 25–32 times trailing profit in June–August 2026; Knack has no listed price history before July 2026 (IPO at ₹170, now ₹207). I use **22**.

## Returns
Market value ₹2,537.12cr (site, 15 Oct; Screener ₹2,556cr on 16 Oct). The IPO shares are already in today's count (12.24cr shares), so dilution is 0; no dividend yet.
- **Normal case:** ₹170cr × 22 = ₹3,740cr. (3,740 ÷ 2,537)^(1/3.45) − 1 = **11.9% a year**.
- At 26× (top of band) it is 17.5%; on a faster ramp (64,000t at ₹52 a kilo, profit ~₹195cr) at 24× it is about 19%. To earn 25% a year on my FY30 profit the exit P/E would need to be about 32.
- **Bad case:** Cargill trims volumes or polymer swings squeeze profit per kilo to ~₹44, and the plant fills slowly (54,000t in FY30); profit ~₹123cr; exit P/E one band down at 16. ₹123cr × 16 = ₹1,968cr → **−7.1% a year**.
- **Tail risk (separate):** money leaking to the promoters through leases and related-party dealings (20.4% of FY25 revenue), or Cargill leaving.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet 4/5:** ₹380cr of fresh IPO money arrived in July against borrowings of about ₹180–190cr (0.59 times equity in June), so net cash until the ₹320cr plant is paid; no guarantees disclosed, but plants and land are leased from promoters.
- **Earnings quality 3/5:** ₹192cr of operating cash on ₹382cr of operating profit over FY21–FY25 (50%), improving to ₹92cr on ₹130cr in FY25; FY26 cash flow not yet on Screener; cash cycle ~108 days because it pays cash for polymer.
- **Management 2/5:** delivered its first quarter (volume +21%, plant on track), but has no listed record, does a fifth of business with related parties, built the new plant on promoter land, and its statements do not reconcile (it told one audience no customer is above 2–2.5% after saying Cargill is 16–17%).
- **Moat and runway 3/5:** colour recipes, 73,000 customer cylinders in its custody, 93.75% customer continuity and a patented pinch-bottom sealing system give stickiness; the runway is a 15%-a-year market; but the machinery can be bought by anyone.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
- Related-party dealings 20.4% of FY25 revenue; offices, plants and the new plant's land leased from promoters → management 2, tail risk, exit P/E not above mid-band.
- Cargill 19% of FY25 sales (16–17% per the August call); top ten customers 41% → bad case built on a Cargill cut.
- Management said in September no customer exceeds 2–2.5%, contradicting its own August figure; some investor figures do not reconcile → management 2.
- Listed only on 8 July 2026; one results call; no record against guidance → plant ramp haircut versus plan.
- Mexican joint venture lost ₹3.4cr in Q1; Oman subsidiary in liquidation → JV losses included in the path.
- Operating cash ~50% of operating profit over five years; FY26 cash flow not yet published → earnings quality 3.
- Returns will fall as fixed assets roughly double with the ₹320cr plant → depreciation rises to ₹64cr; FY28 profit flat.
- FY28 transition: selling machinery from the old plant, closing rented plants, onboarding takes 100–120 days → FY28 volume only +10%.
- Q4 FY26 operating margin dropped to 15% (Screener) → margin path held near 20%, not the 22–23% management calls sustainable.
- Promoter lock-ins will expire (promoter group 70.6%) → noted; supply overhang risk.
- US customers 27% of FY25 sales; US tariffs (50% for eight months, Section 301 duty since July 2026) → bad case.
