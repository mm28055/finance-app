# V2 Retail

## Verdict for the portfolio
The only one of my five that clears 25% in the normal case (about 28% a year), because store openings can roughly triple revenue by FY30 and the market still pays 30x+ for a chain with a long runway. But it is a starter, not a full position: operating cash was −₹103cr in FY26, and the Q2 FY27 update (2 Oct) showed same-store growth of only 0.5% after adjusting for the festival shift. Management cut its same-store guide to 5–7% from 8–10%. Start small and add only when Q3 festive same-store growth and FY27 operating cash come in positive.

## Clean profit base
- Screener (consolidated): FY26 profit ₹162cr; last four quarters (Sep-25 to Jun-26) ₹179cr.
- One-offs: Q3 FY26 carried ₹32cr of other income, against ₹1–4cr in every other quarter. It is unexplained, so I treat it as one-off (about ₹24cr after tax). Q4 FY26 carried two one-off costs: ₹6–7cr of small items written off through depreciation (CEO, May call) and a ₹5.77cr asset write-off after physical verification. Together that is about ₹9cr after tax.
- Clean trailing profit: about **₹165cr** (179 − 24 + 9, rounded). Clean FY26 is about ₹147cr.
- P/E check: the site's 37.0 (₹6,062cr ÷ ~₹164cr) matches my clean base (36.7). Screener's 39.6 at ₹172 is in the same range. No mismatch.
- Market value used: ₹6,062cr (site, 15 Oct close ₹166.25). There are no other share-based claims, and the company says no new share sale is needed.

## Profit path FY27–FY30
| Year | Revenue (₹cr) | Operating margin (filed, before rent) | Profit (₹cr) | Drivers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY26 (actual) | 3,067 | 15% | 162 (clean ~147) | 136 net new stores; same-store +8.6% |
| FY27 | 4,450 (+45%) | 14.5% | ~210 | 170–200 stores guided; H1 revenue ₹1,902cr; Q2 same-store 0.5% festival-adjusted; festivals fall in Q3 |
| FY28 | 6,000 (+35%) | 14.5% | ~280 | about 180 net stores (management says 250 "if momentum holds"); new stores sell at 65–70% of mature-store levels |
| FY29 | 7,500 (+25%) | 14.5% | ~360 | about 150 stores; older cohorts mature |
| FY30 | 9,150 (+22%) | 14.5–15% | **~440** | about 150 stores; roughly 950–1,000 stores by March 2030 |

- **Volume × price:** revenue per store is about ₹10cr a year (10–12k sq ft at ₹700–890 per sq ft a month). Growth is almost all new space. I assume same-store growth of 4–6%, below the new 5–7% guide, plus price rises of 4–5% from Q3 FY27 to pass on yarn costs.
- **Margin:** management guides "similar" margins while it adds 50%+ new space a year. The margin after rent was 8.0% in Q1 FY27 against 8.3% a year earlier. I hold profit at about 4.8% of revenue, the same as today.
- **Interest and depreciation:** each store needs about ₹1.2cr of capex plus ₹1.4–1.6cr of stock. At 150–180 stores a year that is ₹400–450cr a year, against operating profit before rent of roughly ₹400–700cr. Bank debt will rise, and the extra interest is why the margin does not expand.
- **Track record:** it promised 130 net stores in FY26 and delivered 136. It promised at least 50% revenue growth and delivered 63% in FY26 and 58% in Q1 FY27. But same-store growth missed (7.5% in Q1, then 0.5% in Q2), and FY26 operating cash went negative although management had said "positive" was possible. My path is about 10–20% below management's openings plan.
- **Result:** FY30 profit of about ₹440cr, about **30% a year** from the clean ₹165cr.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
- **Runway in March 2030:** management sizes India at 2,500 V2 stores. At about 1,000 stores in 2030 the chain is less than halfway there. Value fashion is growing more than twice as fast as other segments (CRISIL, per the note), and Zudio had 982 stores in June 2026. If openings and new-store economics hold, the market will expect 20–25% profit growth for FY31–FY33.
- **Against that:** there is no brand moat. 80% of its stores already have three or four value rivals nearby, Google ratings are 3.6 against 4.1–4.2 for peers, cash conversion is weak, and same-store growth has just stalled.
- **Anchors:** V2's own year-end P/E was 52 (FY24), 83 (FY25) and 43 (FY26) (stockanalysis), and 37 today. V-Mart traded at about 45–47 in August 2026 and Trent at about 83–95.
- **Exit P/E: 32**, in the lower half of the 30–42 band for 20–25% growth. It sits below today's 37, because cash conversion is poor and the chain will still be burning cash to open stores in 2030.

## Returns
- **Normal:** ₹440cr × 32 = ₹14,080cr in March 2030 against ₹6,062cr today. No dilution and no dividend. ((14,080 ÷ 6,062)^(1/3.45) − 1) = **27.7% a year**.
- **Bad case:** same-store growth stays at 0–5%, a value-fashion price war (Zudio, Reliance's Yousta, V-Mart) slows openings to about 100 a year, and the profit margin slips to 4%. Then FY30 revenue is about ₹6,950cr and profit about ₹278cr. Exit P/E 26 (one band down). ₹278cr × 26 = ₹7,228cr. ((7,228 ÷ 6,062)^(1/3.45) − 1) = **5.2% a year**.
- **Tail risk (not in the bad case):** the founder's earlier chain, Vishal Retail, collapsed after the 2008 downturn on heavy short-term borrowing during rapid expansion. Fast store growth paid for with working capital and debt is the same pattern.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet: 3.** Net debt is ₹988cr, mostly store leases, about 1.9 times operating profit before rent. Bank debt is modest and India Ratings upgraded it to A/Positive in September. But operating cash was negative in FY26 and the expansion needs more debt.
- **Earnings quality: 2.** Over FY22–FY26 only 33% of operating profit (₹336cr of ₹1,033cr) arrived as operating cash. Inventory is 195 days, and the auditor drew attention to a ₹12.88cr advance to the Times group outstanding since 2019.
- **Management: 3.** It has met its store and revenue promises (136 net stores against 130; 58% growth against 50%+). But the same-store guide was cut from 8–10% to 5–7%, there is a past audit qualification (fixed assets, resolved by a ₹5.77cr write-off), and a small promoter pledge. Promoters are down from 54.3% to 51.4%.
- **Moat and runway: 3.** The edge is cost: about ₹190 of cost per sq ft, 0.3% of sales on marketing and a deliberately low gross margin. There is no brand edge. The runway is long (400 to 2,500 stores possible).

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
1. **Operating cash −₹103cr in FY26; 33% five-year conversion.** That is why earnings quality scores 2, why the call is Starter, and why the first add is tied to FY27 operating cash turning positive.
2. **Same-store growth collapsed to 0.5% in Q2 FY27 (festival-adjusted) and −14.9% on the calendar; the guide was cut to 5–7%.** My path uses 4–6% and does not let the margin expand. The bad case uses 0–5%.
3. **Sales per sq ft fell to ₹700 a month in Q2 from ₹886 in Q1.** Part of that is seasonal and from new stores. The case needs new stores to stay well above the ₹500 break-even. A breaks-it trigger watches this.
4. **Margin after rent only 8.0% (Q1), gross margin 28.6%, below the 29–30% guide.** The margin is held flat, with no operating leverage in the base case.
5. **₹12.88cr Times group advance outstanding since 2019, now under an auditor emphasis; the contract end date passed on 7 July 2026.** It is small (0.2% of market value) but a governance flag, and part of the management score of 3.
6. **No brand moat; 80% of stores face three or four value rivals; Google ratings 3.6.** This is why the exit P/E sits in the lower half of its band and why the bad case is a price war.
7. **Founder's earlier chain (Vishal Retail) failed on debt-funded expansion.** Named as the tail risk, and why I would not let net debt run above about 2 times operating profit after rent.
8. **Promoter stake down 2.9 points to 51.4% after the share sale; small pledge for working capital.** It is minor. I watch whether the pledge grows.

## Since the note was written
- **2 Oct, Q2 FY27 business update:** standalone revenue rose 28.4% to ₹905cr. It opened 49 stores and closed 3, reaching 427 stores. Same-store growth was 0.5% festival-adjusted and −14.9% on the calendar, because Navratri and Durga Puja moved into Q3. Sales per sq ft were ₹700 a month against ₹886 in Q1. The full-year same-store guide was cut to 5–7% from 8–10%, while the "over 50%" revenue guide and the 170–200 store plan were kept. It also soft-launched an online store, V2Kart.
- **Share price:** it fell up to 19% on 5 Oct (low ₹162.55), rose 12.7% on 14 Oct with no news, and closed at ₹166.25 on 15 Oct.
- India Ratings' upgrade to A/Positive (2 Sep) came before the note and is already in it.
