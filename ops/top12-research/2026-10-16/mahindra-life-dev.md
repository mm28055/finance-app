# Mahindra Life. Dev

## Verdict for the portfolio
The safest developer in this group: net cash, an AA rating, the Mahindra parent and a ₹50,000cr pipeline. But it is not cheap enough for 25%. Pre-sales should roughly double to about ₹8,000cr by FY30, and profit about doubles to roughly ₹610cr. At a fair 21 times that, the return is about 19% a year, and the CEO himself says "we have seen the strongest part of the current cycle". **Not now** for a 25% target; it is the developer to buy in a real-estate sell-off rather than at 24 times trailing profit.

## Clean profit base
- Screener (consolidated): FY26 net profit **₹298cr**; last four quarters (Jul-25 to Jun-26) **₹332cr** reported, about **₹310cr** to shareholders after partners' minority shares (Screener's P/E of 23.8 on ₹7,389cr implies ₹310cr).
- What the profit is made of: FY26 had a filed operating **loss** of ₹121cr. Profit came from ₹462cr of other income, of which **₹349cr was its share of joint-venture profit** (already after tax) and the rest mostly treasury interest on the cash from the ₹1,500cr rights issue. Tax was only 3% because the consolidated entity's own result was a loss. A few small deferred-tax credits (Q1 FY26 −23%, Q4 FY26 −7% tax rates) add perhaps ₹10–15cr. I do not strip the joint-venture share: it is the core business, since most projects sit in joint ventures.
- Industrial parks (IC&IC) are lumpy: about ₹360cr of new lease revenue came in Q4 FY26 alone. Management guides ₹400–500cr of IC revenue and ₹100–150cr of profit a year, so FY26 had a strong IC year in it.
- P/E check: site ₹7,323cr ÷ ₹310cr = 23.6 (site 23.62; Screener 23.8). They agree.

## Profit path FY27–FY30
Built by segment, because filed revenue and margin understate a business run through joint ventures:
1. **Residential.** Pre-sales were ₹2,330cr (FY24), ₹2,800cr (FY25) and ₹3,410cr (FY26). FY27 is guided at ₹4,500–5,000cr (Emkay expects Rainforest alone to give more than ₹1,000cr), and the FY30 target is ₹9,500–10,000cr. I assume ₹4,500cr in FY27, then ₹5,800cr, ₹7,000cr and **₹8,000cr in FY30**, a 20% haircut to the target, because Mumbai redevelopment approvals are slow and the CEO expects only 4–6% price growth. Profit is booked 3–4 years after sale. FY26 residential profit (about ₹150cr) was about 11% of sales made 3–4 years earlier, held down by old affordable projects at single-digit or negative margins. New premium projects earn about 26% before tax (Eden Phase 2, Luminare), and affordable projects end after FY27. I take residential profit to about 12–13% of lagged sales: about ₹450cr in FY30.
2. **Industrial parks.** About ₹130cr a year of profit (guide ₹100–150cr; ₹1,500cr over ten years). Origins Ahmedabad still has no anchor client, and Pune land aggregation runs into FY27–FY28.
3. **Rent and treasury.** About ₹30cr net in FY30. Rent of ₹150–200cr is guided "4 to 5 years out", and treasury income falls as cash goes into land.

FY30 profit **₹610cr**, about **20% a year** from FY26 (about 21% a year from the trailing ₹310cr). Cross-checks: Emkay (4 Oct 2026, BUY, target ₹500) forecasts ₹284cr for FY28 and ₹467cr for FY29 on its "adjusted" basis, which excludes the joint-venture share, so it is not directly comparable but points the same way. Track record: the 8 planned completion certificates arrived (the last 7 days after year end); Rainforest launched, slipping from FY26 into Q1 FY27; Mahalakshmi (Beacon Hill) was targeted for Q1 and opened sales in August; Origins Ahmedabad's anchor client was promised "this year" and has not arrived. Small, frequent slips, no big misses.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
- **Runway:** the pipeline is about ₹50,000cr of GDV (Emkay: ₹37,700cr at the start of Q2, excluding some), about 15 years of FY26 pre-sales. ₹10,000–20,000cr more is being added in FY27 (₹7,100cr so far: K2 Kandivali plus three Mumbai societies). Thane (6 mn sq ft) launches from Q1 FY28. Its 7.5% borrowing cost against 12–15% for small developers is a real edge in buying land. IC&IC land at Chennai and Jaipur is already paid for.
- **Against that:** about 70% of the pipeline is Mumbai, where unsold stock is 16.5 months of sales, against 8–10 in Pune and Bengaluru. Redevelopment is slow. The CEO expects price growth of 4–6% a year, against wage inflation of 8–10%.
- **What the market will expect in March 2030:** about 13–16% profit growth for FY31–FY33, from pre-sales growth slowing from the 20%+ needed to hit ₹8,000–10,000cr, plus rent starting.
- **Anchors:** Brigade at 22.6 times FY26 profit, Oberoi 21.8, Prestige 52.8 (PL Capital, 8 Oct 2026); Mahindra Lifespace about 22 (Value Research, 9 Oct, in search results). Its own P/E was 60–150 in FY22–FY25 on tiny profits, and 20–31 over the past year on FY26 EPS (₹287–428 range). Emkay says the stock trades at a 12% discount to its net asset value.
- **Exit P/E: 21** on FY30 profit. That is the upper part of the lower half of the 12–16% band (18–26): net cash and a strong parent lift it, while filed cash conversion and a cycle the CEO calls past its best hold it down. It is below today's 23.6, which sits on a trailing profit inflated by a strong industrial year. Cross-check: ₹610cr × 21 = ₹12,810cr, which is 1.6 times FY30 pre-sales, the same as today (₹7,323cr is 1.6 times FY27's ₹4,500cr).

## Returns
Today's market value is ₹7,323cr (site, 15 Oct). Dilution: 0 (the ₹1,500cr rights issue was completed in FY26; partners such as Mitsui invest at project level). The CEO says "shareholders are comfortable putting more money behind us", so a further raise is possible but not in the base. Dividend yield 1.0%.

- **Normal case:** (₹610cr × 21 = ₹12,810cr) ÷ ₹7,323cr = 1.75×. Over 3.45 years that is 17.6% a year, plus 1.0% = **18.6% a year**.
- **Bad case:** the cycle turns as the CEO warns, Mumbai redevelopments slip, pre-sales stall at about ₹5,500cr from FY28, and cost inflation (8–10%) outruns price growth (4–6%). FY30 profit is about ₹420cr and the exit P/E drops one band to 16. (₹420cr × 16 = ₹6,720cr) ÷ ₹7,323cr = 0.92×, which is **−1.5% a year** with the dividend.
- For reference, the case that clears 25%: pre-sales hit the ₹10,000cr target, profit reaches about ₹750cr and the P/E stays at 24, giving about 31% a year.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet: 4.** The company reports net cash: ₹1,127cr of cash against ₹383cr of gross debt at March, and net debt to equity of −0.20 at June. Screener, on its definition, shows about ₹127cr of net debt. It is AA-rated (CRISIL, India Ratings) with group deposits at 7.5%. Cash will be spent on ₹10,000–20,000cr of new GDV, and there are likely guarantees to joint ventures, so 4, not 5.
- **Earnings quality: 2.** Filed operating cash was −₹1,948cr against ₹722cr of profit over FY22–FY26 (−₹545cr in FY26). Filed operating profit has been negative for six years. Profit arrives as joint-venture share (₹349cr in FY26) and lumpy industrial deals. The company's own operating cash was +₹840cr in FY26, after ₹200–250cr of Rainforest approval costs but before ₹903cr of land, so cash roughly matched land buying.
- **Management: 4.** It delivered the planned completion certificates, Rainforest and Q1 pre-sales up 106%. It is conservative: it cut its price assumption to 4–6% and says it "underprices and over-costs" land. Governance is clean (M&M 52.4%, no pledges, independent chair). Minus: repeated small launch slips and an Origins Ahmedabad anchor still missing; the CFO rotated to M&M in October 2025.
- **Moat and runway: 4.** The Mahindra brand (launches sell 60% in a week) and AA money at 7.5% against 12–15% for smaller rivals are real advantages. The pipeline is about 15 years long, and the industrial land is already paid for. Mumbai redevelopment is slow and housing is cyclical.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
1. **Filed operating cash −₹1,948cr against ₹722cr profit over FY22–FY26; operating profit negative for six years.** Earnings quality scores 2, and the exit P/E is held below today's.
2. **Most profit comes as joint-venture share (₹349cr of FY26's ₹298cr net), which cannot be checked from consolidated cash.** The path is built by segment, with the residential profit rate only modestly above FY26's.
3. **CEO: "we have seen the strongest part of the current cycle"; prices to rise 4–6% against 8–10% wage inflation.** Pre-sales are haircut 20% below the FY30 target, and the bad case is a turning cycle.
4. **About 70% of the pipeline is slow Mumbai redevelopment; Mumbai unsold stock is 16.5 months.** Reflected in the haircut and in the 3–4 year recognition lag.
5. **Industrial parks are lumpy (₹41cr of revenue in Q1 FY27 against ₹360cr of leases in Q4 FY26), and Origins Ahmedabad still has no anchor client.** IC is held at ₹130cr a year, the middle of the guide, with nothing from Ahmedabad.
6. **Old affordable projects (Tathawade, Palghar, Kalyan) at single-digit or negative margins still in FY27.** FY27 profit is held to ₹350cr.
7. **Possible further equity raises ("shareholders comfortable putting more money").** Not in the base. A raise would lower per-share returns unless matched by profitable land.
8. **The trailing P/E of 23.6 includes a strong industrial year.** The exit P/E of 21 is set below it.
