# M & B Engineer.

## Verdict for the portfolio
The note's main objection is mostly an accounting mistake that the company has already corrected. FY26's "−₹31cr" of operating cash counted ₹120.81cr of IPO money placed in fixed deposits as an operating outflow. In June the company moved it to investing, which makes FY26 operating cash **+₹90cr**, about 97% of profit. With net cash, a 23% return on capital and capacity rising about 48% by Q3 FY28, profit can grow about 16% a year to ₹170cr in FY30. At 20 times that, it returns **about 22% a year** from the 16 Oct price of ₹307, and about 26% from the 15 Oct price of ₹273, before the unexplained 12% jump. **Medium**: start small. Add if H1 FY27 operating cash is positive and the margin holds near 11%.

## Clean profit base
- Screener (consolidated): FY26 net profit **₹93cr**; last four quarters **₹97cr** (EPS ₹16.89 × 5.72cr shares). There are no exceptional items and tax is a normal 25%.
- Other income is ₹15cr a year, mostly interest on unspent IPO money (₹121cr in deposits at March). I keep it. It shrinks as the money goes into plants, and the path cuts it to about ₹8cr.
- P/E check: Screener ₹1,745cr (16 Oct, ₹305–307) ÷ ₹97cr = **18.0**. On the site's 15 Oct value (₹1,559cr) it is 16.0. The 12% gap is a price move on 16 Oct (+12.1% by 1:28 pm) with no filing behind it. I use today's ₹1,745cr, the price a buyer actually pays.

## Profit path FY27–FY30
**Drivers:**
- **Capacity**: Sanand is "chock-a-block full" at 72,000 tonnes. 20,000 tonnes more was "more or less operational" on 29 September (slipped from Q2 FY27). A ₹30cr heavy-steel line adds 10,000 tonnes by Q1 FY28, and Cheyyar adds 20,000 tonnes by Q3 FY28. Total goes from 104,000 to about 154,000 tonnes (+48%). A third plant in North India is being scouted but is not in my path.
- **Volume and price**: 72,000 tonnes in FY26, with 85,000–90,000 targeted for FY27. Phenix realisation is about ₹1.25–1.38 lakh a tonne. Proflex roofing (26% of revenue) has 21 lakh m² of capacity after two new mobile units, about 75% of its niche, and takes 100% advances.
- **Orders**: ₹1,053cr at June (Phenix ₹837cr, with ₹278cr of exports; Proflex ₹216cr). The enquiry pipeline is about ₹4,000cr. Q1 orders were about ₹260cr against an aim of ₹100cr a month, with large orders delayed to Q2.
- **Exports**: ₹65cr in FY25, ₹166cr in FY26, and about ₹275cr "on track" for FY27, at a 15–16% margin (17–18% if freight normalises) against about 11% at home. US volume growth is guided at 10–15% a year, and West Coast shipments from Cheyyar start in FY28.
- **Guidance (Aug and 29 Sep)**: FY27 revenue "over 25%" (₹1,600cr+), then at least 20% a year for 3–5 years. Margin "approximately 11–11.5%" this year. No margin figure beyond that: "Margins will grow, but I am not providing any guidance."
- **Track record**: FY26 revenue grew 27% as promised, but the margin fell from 13.6% (FY25, company figure) to 11.4%. Q1 FY27 grew 22.5%, below the 25% guide. I take about 4–8 points a year off the growth guide, and capacity caps FY30 without a third plant.
- **Below operating profit**: capex about ₹100cr in FY27, then about ₹60cr a year, so depreciation goes from ₹15cr to about ₹30cr. Interest stays at about ₹15–18cr (borrowings ₹84cr). Tax 25%.

FY30 profit **₹170cr**, about **16% a year** from ₹97cr (the note's base was 15%). Management's path (20%+ growth and a rising margin) would give about ₹210–230cr.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
- **Runway**: steel buildings use about 2 million of India's 150 million tonnes of steel, against 15–18% in advanced countries (management). Management sees the industry growing 13–15% a year for at least five years. Data centres (US$60–70bn planned by 2032, 8–10% of it steel structure) and steel high-rises are new demand. The US market is about US$13bn and M&B ships only about 10,000 tonnes a year. Europe certification is being pursued. Growth after FY30 needs a third plant, which management is actively scouting. In March 2030 the market should expect about 14% growth.
- **Against that**: thin margins (11%) with fixed-price contracts and steel exposure, a 12–15% hit rate, all four listed makers adding capacity at once, and freight and US tariff risk on exports.
- **Anchors**: Interarch trades at about 22–23 times (Motilal Oswal, 6 and 18 Aug 2026) and EPack at about 24 (29 Aug 2026). M&B listed at ₹385 (about 25 times FY25 profit) and has traded between about 13 and 33 times since (₹223–₹536). Today it is 18 times.
- **Exit P/E: 20.** That is the lower half of the 18–26 band for 12–16% growth, and below peers at 22–24, because of the thinner margin record, smaller scale and a five-person family board. It sits 2 points above today. The reason for the discount, "profit not arriving as cash", was largely a misclassification the company has already corrected, and Q1 operating cash was "positive and very good". I expect it to clear once the H1 FY27 cash-flow statement (due with Q2 results) shows it. If it does not, 18 is the right number.

## Returns
Market value today ₹1,745cr (Screener 16 Oct; site 15 Oct ₹1,559cr). Dilution is 0: capex comes from ₹121cr of IPO money and internal cash, and promoters bought shares in June. Dividend yield is 0.35%.
- **Normal case**: (₹170cr × 20 = ₹3,400cr) ÷ ₹1,745cr = 1.95 times. Over 3.45 years that is 21.3% a year, plus 0.35% = **21.7% a year**. At a P/E of 18 it is 18.0%. From the site's 15 Oct value (₹1,559cr) it would be 25.7%. The return depends heavily on the entry price.
- **Bad case**: steel rises again on fixed-price orders, private factory spending slows while Interarch, EPack and Pennar add capacity, and freight stays high. Revenue grows about 11% a year to ₹1,910cr at a 10% margin, so FY30 profit is about ₹112cr. The P/E steps down to 14. (₹112cr × 14 = ₹1,568cr) ÷ ₹1,745cr = 0.90 times, which is **−2.7% a year**.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet: 4.** Net cash ₹195cr at March 2026 (borrowings ₹84cr), CRISIL A/Stable, and capex funded. It is not a 5 because steel-building contracts carry performance guarantees and ₹54cr of receivables are over six months old (mostly 10% retention money).
- **Earnings quality: 3.** On the corrected figures, five-year operating cash was ₹198cr: 43% of operating profit and 75% of net profit. FY26 alone was ₹90cr (63% of operating profit), against the −₹31cr shown in the audited accounts. FY24 was only ₹5cr. Inventory is 175 days on Screener's basis (101 on the company's), and the audited cash-flow statement itself had to be reclassified.
- **Management: 3.** It delivered the post-IPO growth promise (+27% in FY26) and refuses low-margin orders. But the margin slid about 2 points, margin guidance has been withheld for two quarters, Q1 growth and orders came in under target, and the Sanand expansion slipped a quarter. The IPO was mostly promoters selling (₹375cr of ₹650cr). Governance is otherwise clean: the FY26 audit was clean, promoters hold 70.61% and bought more in June, and the board chair is independent.
- **Moat and runway: 3.** Real edges: both plants are US-certified (AISC, among only two Indian steel-building plants), about 75% share of self-supported roofing, and 60–70% repeat customers including Reliance, Adani, Tata and Japanese contractors. But it cannot set prices at home, and each plant serves only about 1,000–1,200 km.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
1. **FY26 operating cash shown as −₹31cr in the audited accounts.** The company revised it to +₹90cr by moving ₹120.81cr of IPO deposits to investing (June 2026). Earnings quality scores 3 (five-year 43%), not higher, until the H1 FY27 statement confirms it.
2. **Margin down from 13.6% (FY25) to 11.4% in Q1 FY27; margin guidance withheld.** The path holds 11–11.5%, which is management's own "this year" range, and the bad case uses 10%.
3. **Order book dipped from ₹1,083cr to ₹1,053cr; Q1 orders about ₹260cr against a ₹100cr-a-month aim.** Growth is haircut to 21% in FY27, against the "over 25%" guide.
4. **Steel and freight on fixed-price contracts (domestic steel +20% in Q4 FY26; US freight US$10,000–15,000 a container).** It is in the bad case. Management fixes 80–85% of the steel at booking.
5. **Inventory 175 days (Screener) and a 137-day cash cycle.** This is why the exit P/E is held below peers.
6. **IPO was mostly a promoter sale; the shares are about 20% below the ₹385 issue price.** Management scores 3.
7. **Unexplained price jumps (+13.8% on 7 Oct, +12% on 16 Oct).** The case uses the higher 16 Oct price, which costs about 4 points of annual return.
8. **All four steel-building makers are adding capacity at once.** It is in the bad case (P/E 14).

## Since the note was written
- **The key fact the note missed**: on about 1 June 2026, before the note was written, M&B revised its FY26 cash flow. ₹120.81cr of IPO money placed in fixed deposits had been counted as an operating outflow. Moving it to investing changes FY26 operating cash from −₹30.88cr to **+₹89.93cr**, and investing from −₹39.25cr to −₹160.06cr (ScanX report of the revised investor presentation; Stockanalysis shows the unrevised −₹30.9cr). The Q1 call confirms the reclassification and says Q1 FY27 operating cash was "positive and very good".
- **29 Sep investor meet** (Arihant conference): the Sanand +20,000 tonnes was "more or less operational", with orders for it to be taken from the following week. FY27 growth is guided at about 25%, the margin at about 11–11.5% this year, and at least 20% a year for 3–5 years. Exports are on track for about ₹275cr. US freight is quoted at up to US$15,000 a container. Receivables over a year old are about 9–9.5%, which management says is retention money.
- **17 Sep AGM**: two whole-time directors were re-appointed for 2027–2030.
- **Price**: ₹273 on 15 Oct, then ₹305–307 on 16 Oct (+12.1%), with no filing or order announcement found. There was a 13.8% unexplained rise on 7 Oct too.
