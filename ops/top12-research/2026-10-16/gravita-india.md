# Gravita India

## Verdict for the portfolio
Not now. Gravita is a proven recycler with a long runway (lead, copper, aluminium, plastic; capacity heading to 8 lakh tonnes), and I expect profit to grow about 17% a year to roughly ₹680cr by FY30. But at ₹10,928cr the stock already trades at 16 times that FY30 profit; at a fair 22 times, a buyer earns about 10% a year, and even if the market keeps paying today's 28 times it is about 18%. Weak cash conversion (48% over five years), rising working-capital debt and a promoter who sold 10 points of stake keep the exit P/E from going higher.

## Clean profit base
- **Reported:** FY26 profit ₹378cr; TTM to June 2026 ₹392cr (₹378cr − ₹93cr Q1 FY26 + ₹106cr Q1 FY27). No exceptional items.
- **Treatment:** Other income (₹94cr TTM) has two parts: hedging gains the company calls operational (₹35cr in Q1, part of the business: they offset losses booked in operating costs) and treasury income on leftover QIP cash (₹13cr in Q1). The treasury part is roughly matched by interest cost (₹11cr in Q1) and is shrinking as the cash is spent, so I leave it in the base and let it fade in the forecast. Q1 also carried a lead scarcity premium (₹24,181 a tonne against a "sustainable" ₹19,000–20,000), worth perhaps ₹15cr; it is operating profit, so I keep it but do not extrapolate it. **Clean TTM base ₹392cr.**
- **P/E check:** site 27.85 (₹10,928cr ÷ ₹392cr) and Screener 28.0 agree.

Drivers:
- **Latest call (28 Jul 2026):** volume +4% (lead fell; 15–20% of scrap comes from or through the Gulf and almost none arrived), revenue +42% (copper ₹376cr at 50% use), adjusted EBITDA ₹145cr (+29%), profit +14%; group plants at ~52% against ~70% normal; copper ~₹55,000 a tonne now, ~₹60,000 by March 2027, ₹70,000–75,000 in 2–3 years; copper capacity to double to ~60,000t in three years (29,400t Mandvi recycling plant, ₹160cr, within 12 months); ₹1,680cr capex to FY29; "25–30% profit CAGR for the next 4–5 years" kept; no volume guidance by metal any more; net debt ~₹150cr and working capital ~95 days.
- **Track record:** the company says profit grew 31% a year over FY21–26 and 24% over the last three years; Screener shows 19% for three years. It missed its 20–25% volume promise in FY26 and Q1 FY27, dropped rubber, and has waited a year for an aluminium hedge contract. I therefore haircut the 25–30% target to about 17%.
- **Build (adjusted operating profit ₹610cr → ₹1,015cr):** lead, aluminium and plastic grow ~12% a year from the Q1 organic run-rate (~₹500cr a year) as the idle Mundra (+80,300t) and Phagi (+40,500t) capacity fills when scrap returns; copper rises from ~₹86cr a year now to ~₹280–300cr (45,000–50,000t at ₹60,000–65,000). Revenue rises faster than profit because copper carries a high metal price per tonne.
- **Below the line:** depreciation ₹60cr → ₹125cr as ₹1,680cr of capex lands (₹600cr/₹700cr/₹400cr in FY27–FY29); interest ₹45cr → ₹70cr as working-capital debt rises to the ₹800–900cr management has flagged; treasury income ₹40cr → ₹15cr; tax 18% (company guide 17–18%).
- **Growth rate:** about 17% a year from the TTM base (16% from FY26 over four years), matching the note's ~17%.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
In March 2030 the market will see 8 lakh tonnes of capacity only 55–60% used, so volume can still grow without much capex; India's battery-recycling rules (EPR credits now traded on an exchange, audits of recyclers, a possible reverse-charge GST) push scrap from informal to licensed recyclers; lithium-ion refining and further copper are optional extras. That supports about 15% growth for FY31–FY33 → the 18–26 band. Lower half, because only 48% of five years' operating profit has turned into cash, scrap supply can be cut off (as in 2026), and margins depend on metal spreads. Anchors: the stock's own P/E ranged from 7 (2020) to 72 (Sept 2024), median ~50 over five years, during a period of 30%+ profit growth that has now slowed; peer Pondy Oxides traded at ~29 in July 2026. I use **22**; a lower P/E than today's 28 is the honest expectation once growth settles in the mid-teens.

## Returns
Market value ₹10,928.08cr (site, 15 Oct; Screener ₹10,975cr on 16 Oct). No dilution planned (capex from own cash; working capital by debt; the ₹95cr RMIL rights issue is inside the group). Dividend yield 0.43%.
- **Normal case:** ₹680cr × 22 = ₹14,960cr. (14,960 ÷ 10,928)^(1/3.45) − 1 + 0.43% = **10.0% a year**.
- At today's 28 it is 17.9%; on management's plan (~₹1,000cr profit in FY30) at 26 it is 29%. To earn 25% on my FY30 profit the exit P/E would need to be about 34.
- **Bad case:** Gulf and shipping disruption keeps lead plants near 55%, copper stays at ~₹55,000 a tonne and working-capital interest rises; FY30 profit ~₹540cr; exit P/E one band down at 17. ₹540cr × 17 = ₹9,180cr → **−4.5% a year**.
- **Tail risk (separate):** the ₹70.1cr customs demand and other disputed claims (₹158cr in all) going against it; a hedging failure on ₹1,000cr+ of metal stock.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet 3/5:** borrowings rose from ₹286cr to ₹736cr in FY26; net debt is ₹534cr on cash alone or ~₹120–150cr counting ₹413cr of investments, against ₹444cr of operating profit; management expects working-capital debt to reach ₹800–900cr; ₹158cr of disputed claims; ICRA AA.
- **Earnings quality 2/5:** ₹703cr of operating cash on ₹1,478cr of operating profit over FY22–FY26 (48%), only ₹169cr on ₹439cr in FY26; cash cycle stretched from 92 to 139 days; the company's "adjusted" margin adds hedging income.
- **Management 3/5:** a long record of profit growth and a copper beat (₹55,151 against ₹45,000 a tonne), but volume promises missed, rubber dropped, aluminium hedge still pending, and the promoter sold from 66.5% to 55.9% partly "for personal purposes"; the founder died in 2026.
- **Moat and runway 4/5:** own scrap yards and buyers in many countries, licences, strict hedging and an LME-listed lead brand give it cheaper, steadier scrap than informal recyclers, and recycling rules are tightening in its favour; the weakness is scrap supply.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
- Q1 FY27 volume +4% against a 20–25% promise; lead volume fell → profit growth haircut to ~17%.
- Filed margin 7.5% in Q1 against the company's 9.8% "adjusted" → table shows both; returns use profit after all items.
- FY26 operating cash ₹169cr against ₹439cr of operating profit; five-year 48% → earnings quality 2, lower-half exit P/E.
- Cash cycle 92 → 139 days; ~₹1,040cr of stock in June → interest rises to ₹70cr in the path.
- Borrowings ₹286cr → ₹736cr; working-capital debt planned to ₹800–900cr → balance sheet 3.
- Return on capital fell from 31–32% (FY22–23) to 16% (FY26) → no premium P/E.
- Promoter stake down from 66.5% to 55.9%, partly for personal liquidity → management 3; watch for more.
- Founder died in 2026 (had left management in 2024) → noted; professional CEO and CFO run the business.
- ₹158cr of disputed claims, including a ₹70.1cr customs demand → tail risk.
- FY26 standalone audit report headed "Qualified Opinion" though the text is clean → noted; should be clarified.
- Rashtriya Metal bought at ~1.7 times book; goodwill at risk if copper stalls → bad case has copper stuck at ₹55,000.
- Rubber plan dropped; aluminium MCX hedge pending for over a year → no aluminium growth in India assumed.
- Q1 lead profit per tonne includes a scarcity premium → not extrapolated.
