# Neetu Yoshi

## Verdict for the portfolio
Not now. Profit can roughly triple from ₹22.5cr (clean FY26) to about ₹69cr by FY30 as the new bogie plant fills, but the plant started five months late, the FY27 target has been trimmed three times, and only about a third of five years' operating profit has come in as cash. At ₹949cr plus 6.8% warrant dilution, a fair 20 times FY30 profit gives about 9% a year; only full delivery of management's targets gets to 25%.

## Clean profit base
- **Reported:** FY26 (the latest 12 months; the company reports half-yearly and H1 FY27 is not out) profit ₹25cr on sales of ₹98cr; operating profit ₹31cr (31%).
- **Removed:** ₹3cr of other income, which management said was interest on IPO money held in deposits; that money has now been spent on the plant. After 17.5% tax that is about ₹2.5cr, so **clean FY26 profit ≈ ₹22.5cr**.
- **P/E check:** site 37.9 (₹949cr ÷ ₹25cr) and Screener 36.3 (₹908cr on 16 Oct) agree; on the clean base it is about 42.

## Profit path FY27–FY30
| Year | Revenue | Operating margin | Profit |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY26 (actual) | ₹98cr | 31% | ₹25cr (₹22.5cr clean) |
| FY27 | ₹165cr | 30% | ₹37.5cr |
| FY28 | ₹240cr | 29% | ₹52.5cr |
| FY29 | ₹300cr | 28% | ₹62cr |
| FY30 | ₹350cr | 27.5% | ₹69cr |

Drivers:
- **Latest call (25 Sep 2026 conference):** FY27 target now "₹200cr-plus" (it was ₹220–230cr in late 2025, ₹210–220cr in June); new plant (parts up to 1,000kg, started 13 Sep) to add about ₹60cr in H2, though later in the same call "₹40–50cr"; old plant phase 1 (₹100cr capacity) at 90–95%, phase 2 (₹50–60cr) at about 50%; FY28 target ₹350cr; order book ₹160–170cr (₹140–150cr in June); PAT margin target 25% and EBITDA 31–33%; 1–3 approvals a month expected, 20–25 products in the pipeline.
- **My FY27:** old plant ~₹125cr (phase 1 full, phase 2 filling, track parts) plus ~₹40cr from the new plant = ₹165cr, in line with the note's ₹150–175cr. Track record forces this: the plant came five months late, FY26 sales of ₹98cr missed the ₹110cr "bare minimum", and the FY27 target has been cut three times.
- **FY28–FY30:** I give management's ₹350cr target two extra years. Capacity at full use is about ₹410–430cr (old plant ₹150–160cr, new plant ₹200cr, track turnouts ₹60–70cr), so ₹350cr in FY30 is about 85% use. The ₹27.48cr warrant money funds the working capital for track turnouts (rails bought at ₹65–70 a kilo and held two months), so its earnings are inside this path.
- **Margin:** 31% in FY26 (36% in FY24); management itself says margins fall as more suppliers get approved and bogie prices fell from ₹3.25 lakh to ₹2.70–2.80 lakh. Complete bogies include bought-in springs and rubber (~₹35,000–40,000 a bogie). I let the margin drift to 27.5%.
- **Below the line:** depreciation ₹4cr rising to ₹10cr (₹50cr plant, then spring and rubber lines from own cash); small interest as working capital grows; lifetime tax rate 17.5% under the new-company scheme.
- **Growth rate:** about 32% a year from the clean FY26 base over four years (38% over the 3.45-year hold), against the note's ~22%.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
In March 2030 the market will be looking at a ₹350cr company near full use of its plants, planning wagon assembly (about 200 wagons a year), spring and rubber lines, exports and power-plant parts, inside an Indian Railways capex cycle (a ~1 lakh wagon tender is being prepared). That supports an expectation of about 15% growth → the 18–26 band. Lower half, because only ~32% of operating profit has turned into cash, sales go to one buyer group (Indian Railways and its wagon, coach and track makers), approvals hold only 4–7 suppliers per part and margins fall as more are approved, and there are governance flags (late statutory dues, no internal auditor, the MD is also CFO). It is also SME-listed with about 2,150 shareholders. I use **20**.

## Returns
Market value ₹949cr (site, 15 Oct; Screener ₹908cr on 16 Oct). Dilution 6.8%: 26.42 lakh warrants at ₹104 allotted on 31 Aug 2026 against ~3.89cr shares. No dividend.
- **Normal case:** ₹69cr × 20 = ₹1,380cr. (1,380 ÷ (949 × 1.068))^(1/3.45) − 1 = **9.4% a year**.
- Management case (about ₹100cr profit in FY30) at 22× gives 25.2%. To earn 25% on my FY30 profit the exit P/E would need to be about 32.
- **Bad case:** the wagon tender is slow, the new plant runs near half capacity and the margin slips to 25%: FY30 sales ~₹250cr, profit ~₹43cr; exit P/E one band down at 15. ₹43cr × 15 = ₹645cr → **−12.3% a year**.
- **Tail risk (separate):** a change in railway procurement or loss of approvals for a key part; a weak-controls problem (late statutory dues, switch-off audit trail, no internal auditor) turning into a tax or accounting finding.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet 4/5:** debt-free (borrowings ₹4cr, ₹9cr net cash at March 2026) and the warrant money is equity, not debt; small size and receivables are the weak spots.
- **Earnings quality 2/5:** ₹23cr of operating cash on ₹72cr of operating profit over FY22–FY26 (32%), only ₹4cr on ₹31cr in FY26; debtor days rose from 54 to 118.
- **Management 2/5:** repeated misses (plant April → 13 Sep; FY26 ₹98cr vs ₹110cr; FY27 target cut three times) and governance flags (late TDS, advance tax, PF and ESI; no internal auditor; IPO costs 10.4% of money raised; MD doubles as CFO).
- **Moat and runway 3/5:** RDSO "Class A" approvals take 3–7 months each and limit each part to 4–7 suppliers, with Indian Railways spending as the runway; but one buyer sets prices and margins erode as new suppliers are approved.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
- New plant started 13 Sep 2026 against April promised → FY27 at ₹165cr, FY28 target pushed back two years.
- FY26 sales ₹98cr against a ₹110cr "bare minimum" → haircut throughout.
- FY27 target cut from ₹220–230cr to ₹210–220cr to "₹200cr-plus" → my ₹165cr.
- Operating cash ₹23cr against ₹72cr of operating profit over five years; ₹4cr against ₹31cr in FY26 → earnings quality 2, exit P/E in the lower half.
- Debtor days 54 → 118 → same.
- Auditor noted late TDS, advance tax, PF and ESI; no internal auditor; audit trail can be switched off → management 2, tail risk.
- IPO costs 10.4% of the money raised; ₹11.32cr unspent at March → management 2.
- ₹27.48cr of warrants (6.8% dilution) for working capital instead of debt → dilution in the return maths.
- Margin down from 36% (FY24) to 31%; bogie prices fell ~15% → margin path drifts to 27.5%.
- Sells only to Indian Railways and its suppliers → bad case and lower-half P/E.
- SME listing, half-yearly results, thin trading; price doubled from ₹121 (June) to ₹244 → noted; supports caution on the exit P/E.
