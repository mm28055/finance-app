# Samhi Hotels

## Verdict for the portfolio
The headline P/E of 7.7 is an illusion. Without the ₹311cr deferred-tax income and the write-backs, Samhi earned only about ₹100cr for shareholders over the last year, so the stock is at about 32 times clean profit, or about 12 times operating profit with debt included. Profit can still roughly triple to about ₹300cr by FY30: interest has already fallen, the GST hit laps, and about 1,670 mostly upscale rooms open. At an unchanged operating-profit multiple that is about 23% a year. **Medium**: ownable, but not a 25% name, and with 3.2 times debt and a ₹750cr share-sale authority it should be a small position. Any share sale below about ₹180 ends the case.

## Clean profit base
- Screener (consolidated): FY26 net profit ₹567cr; last four quarters ₹572cr (₹504cr to shareholders: EPS ₹22.71 × 22.2cr shares). Both are badly distorted. The CFO listed what was in FY26:
  - **Deferred-tax income of about ₹311cr** in Q4 FY26, from recognising past losses and unabsorbed depreciation (Screener shows Q4 tax at −477%). It is "non-cash, accounting-driven".
  - **Impairment reversals of close to ₹83cr** (Navi Mumbai and others; the note says ₹97cr) and a **gain on selling Kaspia Delhi**. Other income was ₹132cr in FY26 against a normal ₹10–20cr.
  - **Partners' share:** GIC owns 35% of the upscale platform, and its share runs about ₹7–8cr a quarter.
- Management's own clean figure: FY26 profit before tax and exceptional items was **about ₹165cr** (up 89%).
- **Clean profit, last four quarters:** operating profit ₹439cr, plus normal other income about ₹15cr, minus interest at the current run rate ₹150cr, minus depreciation ₹128cr, gives about ₹175cr before tax. Tax at about 22% (Q1 FY27 was 24%) leaves about ₹135cr, and after about ₹30cr to GIC that is **about ₹100cr to shareholders**. PL Capital's "adjusted PAT" for FY26 is ₹87.5cr, which agrees.
- **P/E check:** the site shows 7.65 and Screener 7.71, both on the flattered ₹504–572cr. On clean profit it is **about 32** (₹3,224cr ÷ ₹100cr). The site's P/E is wrong for this stock. On operating profit with debt (EV/EBITDA), after GIC's share, it is about 12.4 times trailing and about 10.5 times FY27.

## Profit path FY27–FY30
**What management promises (May and August calls):** revenue from ₹1,200cr to ₹3,000cr in "four to five years" (2.5 times), with no acquisitions assumed. Same-store revenue growth of 9–11% a year, which it has kept through repeated shocks (9.5% in FY26; RevPAR +9.6% in Q1 FY27). A 38% margin in FY27 and "40%-plus" structurally. New hotels adding ₹400–500cr of operating profit from FY28 onwards. Over ₹3,000cr of free cash after interest over FY27–FY31. No new debt and "without diluting equity" (May), though a ₹750cr share authority followed in August.

**Track record against that:** the same-store promise is met. The margin (32.2% in Q1 against 38%), the 2.5 times debt target ("12–18 months" in May, FY28 in August) and W Hyderabad ("end of FY27" in May, H2 FY28 in August) have slipped. Room rates rose only 2.6–4% in Q1, with occupancy already 79%.

**My path:**
- Same-store growth of 9% in FY27, then 8–9%.
- New rooms: W Hyderabad (260 rooms, leased, about +6–7% to revenue from H2 FY28); Tribute and Westin Bengaluru Whitefield (end FY28 to FY29); Ingka Noida (162 rooms, leased); Sriperumbudur Marriott (FY29–FY30). Navi Mumbai (700 rooms) and the Thane Westin/Fairfield open only after FY30.
- RARE India: ₹100–120cr of revenue and ₹35–40cr of operating profit within two years.
- Margin from 35% to 37.5%, below the 38–40% guide.
- Interest about ₹148cr (guide ₹135–140cr; Q1 run rate ₹151cr), flat after that as lease interest on new hotels offsets debt reduction.
- Depreciation from ₹141cr to ₹190cr. Tax 22%. GIC's share about 21% of profit.

FY30 profit **about ₹300cr**, about 35% a year from FY26's clean ₹88cr (about 30% a year from the trailing ₹100cr). Much of that is a one-time lift from lower interest and a recovering margin; operating profit grows about 16% a year. My FY30 revenue of ₹2,150cr is about 15–20% below the path to management's ₹3,000cr. Cross-check: PL forecasts ₹151cr (FY27) and ₹217cr (FY28), a little above my path.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
- **Runway:** Navi Mumbai and Thane (700 rooms, about ₹325cr of revenue at today's RevPARs) open in FY31–FY32. Same-store growth of 8–10% is supported by office districts in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and NCR, which absorbed 58 mn sq ft in FY26. Free cash should keep cutting debt, and RARE scales without capital. FY31–FY33 profit growth of about 13–16% is a reasonable market expectation, but without FY27–FY30's interest-cut lift.
- **Against that:** India's branded hotel pipeline is 114,151 rooms, up 58%, mostly mid-market, where Samhi has 60% of revenue and carries the GST cost. Bengaluru supply is growing about 5–5.5% a year (management). Samhi does not own its brands, and room-rate power is weak.
- **Anchors:** PL values it at 10.5 times FY28 operating profit after GIC's share (target ₹201), and another broker at 11 times (₹240), "a significant discount to peers". Branded hotel chains trade at far higher P/Es, but they own brands or hold net cash. Samhi has no P/E history: it made losses until FY25.
- **Exit P/E: 22** on FY30 profit, the top of the lower half of the 12–16% band (18–26). Lower half because of leverage, no brand ownership, mid-market supply and the share overhang. Top of it because the stock already trades below peers on EV/EBITDA. Cross-check: ₹300cr × 22 = ₹6,600cr of equity. Adding about ₹1,300cr of attributable net debt gives about 12 times FY30 attributable operating profit (about ₹660cr), the same as today's 12.4 times trailing. No re-rating is assumed. It is well below today's 32 on clean profit, because the interest-cut lift will be over by 2030.

## Returns
Today's market value is ₹3,224cr (site, 15 Oct). Dilution: 0 in the base, since the ₹750cr is an "enabling" authority that management says it will renew "every year". No dividend.

- **Normal case:** (₹300cr × 22 = ₹6,600cr) ÷ ₹3,224cr = 2.05×. Over 3.45 years that is **23.1% a year**. At an exit P/E of 20 it is 19.7%.
- **If the full ₹750cr were sold at ₹145** (5.2cr shares, d = 0.23): interest saved adds about ₹45cr (FY30 profit ₹345cr), and the return becomes **20.7% a year**.
- **Bad case:** room rates stay weak as mid-market supply arrives and foreign travel stays low, same-store growth slows to about 6%, W Hyderabad and Whitefield open late, and the margin stays at about 34%. FY30 revenue is about ₹1,850cr, profit about ₹185cr, and the exit P/E drops one band to 16 (about 9 times operating profit). (₹185cr × 16 = ₹2,960cr) ÷ ₹3,224cr = 0.92×, which is **−2.4% a year**.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet: 2.** Company net debt is ₹1,490cr at June, 3.2 times trailing operating profit (2.4 times on hotels already open). Screener shows ₹1,660cr including leases (3.8 times). CARE's downgrade trigger is 3.3 times. On the positive side: A+ rating, 7.8% cost, debt of about 12 years with little repayment for 3–5 years, and GIC's ₹150cr still to come. The 2.5 times target is now FY28.
- **Earnings quality: 4.** Five-year operating cash was 95% of operating profit (₹407cr against ₹432cr in FY26), free cash was about ₹300cr after interest in FY26, receivables are 20 days and working capital is negative. Not a 5, because FY26 reported profit carries ₹311cr of tax income, about ₹83cr of write-backs and a sale gain.
- **Management: 3.** It kept its one operating promise (same-store 9–11%), cut net debt by ₹516cr in FY26 via GIC, and recycles non-core hotels (four sold at about 20 times EV/EBITDA). The team has been stable since 2011. But the margin, the leverage target and W Hyderabad slipped within three months. "Without diluting equity" (May) became a ₹750cr share authority (August) that passed with only 79.6% (75% needed). There is no promoter.
- **Moat and runway: 3.** It has Marriott, Hyatt and IHG clusters beside India's densest office districts, a proven buy-and-turn-around skill (asset ROCE about 18–19%), GIC as a capital partner and a growing upscale mix. But it does not own the brands, room-rate power is weak (+2.6–4%), and a 58% bigger industry pipeline is coming.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
1. **FY26 profit of ₹567cr included ₹311cr of deferred-tax income, about ₹83cr of write-backs and a sale gain; clean profit is about ₹100cr.** The whole case uses the clean figure: P/E 32, not 7.7.
2. **Net debt at 3.2 times operating profit, near CARE's 3.3 times trigger; the 2.5 times target moved from "12–18 months" to FY28.** The balance sheet scores 2, the exit P/E sits in the lower half, and a breach is the tail risk.
3. **₹750cr share authority (about 23% of the company at ₹145), to be renewed yearly, passed with only 79.6%.** Shown separately: full use at today's price cuts the return from 23.1% to 20.7%. Use below about ₹180 is a stop.
4. **Room rates rose only 2.6–4% with occupancy already at 79%.** Same-store growth is held at 8–9%, below the 9–11% promise, and the bad case is weak rates.
5. **The GST change is a permanent cost (₹7.5–10cr a quarter); Q1 margin was 32.2% against a 38% guide.** The path starts at 35% and never reaches the guided 38–40%.
6. **Industry pipeline up 58% to 114,151 rooms, mostly mid-market.** This drives the bad case and the lower-half exit P/E.
7. **Delivery slips within one quarter (W Hyderabad, leverage, margin).** Revenue is about 15–20% below management's ₹3,000cr path, and new hotels open late.
8. **The interest-cut lift happens once.** Growth after FY30 is set at 13–16%, not FY27–FY30's ~30%.
