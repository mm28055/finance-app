# Genus Power

## Verdict for the portfolio
Execution is excellent, but at 14 times profit the market is already pricing the problem: the smart-meter boom it is living off has a date on it (RDSS ends 31 March 2028). Its order book has shrunk for two quarters, it lost the Kerala tender by bidding 54% above the winner, and it has not turned profit into cash for three years. On my numbers FY30 profit is ~₹830cr and the return is about **6% a year**, with a deep bad case. **Not now.**

## Clean profit base
- **FY26:** consolidated ₹592cr (standalone continuing operations ₹605cr).
- **Last four quarters to June 2026:** ₹652cr reported (₹143cr + ₹140cr + ₹172cr + ₹197cr).
  - Q1 FY27's ₹197cr is ₹34cr above standalone (₹163cr), partly because of notional gains on shares held through a trust (Genus Paper & Boards). Management said the associate line swung from ₹11cr to ₹4cr a quarter for this reason.
  - I remove ~₹20cr of those gains, which gives **~₹630cr clean**. Interest on ₹719cr of deposits is kept, as it is ordinary treasury income for a contractor holding cash.
- **P/E check:** ₹8,829cr (site, 15 Oct, ₹290.2) ÷ ₹652cr = 13.5, matching the site's 13.58 and Screener's 13.6. On clean profit it is **14.0**.

## Profit path FY27–FY30
| Year | Revenue | Operating margin | Profit after tax |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY26 (actual) | ₹4,751cr | 20.3% | ₹592cr |
| FY27 | ~₹6,000cr | 18.0% | ~₹665cr |
| FY28 | ~₹6,400cr | 17.5% | ~₹700cr |
| FY29 | ~₹5,800cr | 17.5% | ~₹710cr |
| FY30 | ~₹6,300cr | 17.5% | **~₹830cr** |

**Drivers.**
- **Installs on the existing book.** Genus has 3.61 crore meters awarded. 1.5 crore were installed by 30 June 2026, leaving about 2.1 crore. Utilities can add up to 30% more meters at the same price, which management expects to take the total to 4–4.25 crore.
  - FY27 target: at least 1 crore installs (2.3 million in Q1, slower in the monsoon).
  - FY27 revenue guide ₹6,000–6,500cr, repeated on 6 October. I take the bottom of the range: Q1 was ₹1,365cr, so Q2–Q4 must average ~₹1,550cr against a Q4 FY26 peak of ₹1,537cr.
  - The rest of the book is installed in FY28 under the March 2028 RDSS deadline.
- **Order book.** It fell from ₹27,217cr (December) to ₹25,173cr (March) to ₹24,020cr (June), with no large win in a year. About ₹16,000cr of the March book was meter supply and installation; the rest is 8–9 years of operation and maintenance.
- **After the installs.** FY29–FY30 revenue then rests on:
  - Operation-and-maintenance income: ₹150cr in FY26, with management now saying "at least 800–900 crores" a year once installs are complete.
  - Gas meters: ₹12–13cr last year, ₹35cr won by Q1, and a ₹35,000–36,000cr industry over five years. I give it ~₹900cr by FY30.
  - Exports: target ₹500cr in two to three years.
  - Meters sold to other operators.
  - New metering-service wins: about 15 crore meters are still to be tendered, with West Bengal 1 crore, Haryana 50–60 lakh and Tamil Nadu ~50 lakh expected over the next year. I assume Genus wins roughly 1 crore meters by FY29, about half its 22–23% historical share, because it is refusing to match low bids. That gives ~₹3,000cr of install revenue in FY30.
- **Margin.** FY27 guide is 18%, cut from 20–21% because contracts are fixed-price ("there is no pass-through in our contracts") and 40–45% of components are imported. Q1 delivered 19.1%. I hold 17.5% from FY28: new tenders are being bid much lower, and Kerala's L1 was ₹3,241cr against Genus's ₹4,985cr.
- **Interest.** Net debt rises from ₹1,573cr to a peak of ~₹2,000cr (guided), so interest is ~₹200cr in FY27. It falls to ~₹100cr by FY30 as inventory (₹1,350cr), receivables (~₹1,500cr) and contract assets (~₹870cr) turn into cash once installs finish.
- **GIC platform.** Genus is putting another ₹600–700cr into the platform it owns 26% of, on top of ₹487cr already in. I let its share of platform profit rise to ~₹100cr by FY30, roughly 9% on ~₹1,150cr invested. This is my assumption; the platform's profits are not disclosed.
- **Result:** FY30 profit ~₹830cr, **8.8% a year** from FY26, almost all of it before FY28 and again in FY30, if the order book is refilled.

**Track record:**
- Met: installs (87 lakh against 80–90 lakh in FY26), revenue, and beat the margin guide in Q1.
- Missed or slipped: turning cash-positive moved from end-FY27 to Q1–Q2 FY28 ("at par or maybe a little negative" for FY27); the margin guide was cut by 2–2.5 points; and the operation-and-maintenance figure has moved around (₹800–900cr within two to three years, then ₹600–700cr from FY28 rising to ₹1,100–1,200cr, now "800–900 crores" once installs are done).

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
**What March 2030 will look like:**
- The national programme (31–32 crore meters) should be largely installed around 2031.
- What is left is the operation-and-maintenance annuity (8–9 years, with new connections added), gas meters (the government mandate is for piped-gas homes to get smart gas meters), water meters ("3–4 years from now", still nascent) and exports.
- Electricity metering after 2031 is a replacement market.
- Tender prices are falling: younger players are bidding ~50% below Genus.

The market will likely expect ~7–9% growth for FY31–33, with a real risk that revenue falls once installs stop.

**Exit P/E:** this sits at the border of the 10–15 and 14–20 bands. I go to the lower part because:
- five years of operating cash were negative (−41% of operating profit);
- the customers are state utilities;
- prices are fixed with no pass-through;
- a money-laundering complaint (PMLA) is before a Patna court.

I use **13**, a little below today's clean 14. The discount reason (the RDSS cliff) will not clear by 2030; it will have arrived. Cash flow should improve, but that only stops the P/E falling further.

## Returns
Formula: ((FY30 profit × exit P/E) ÷ (market value × (1 + d)))^(1/3.45) − 1 + dividend yield. Market value ₹8,829cr (site, 15 Oct). No share sale planned (d = 0). Dividend yield 0.17%.

- **Normal case:** ₹830cr × 13 = ₹10,790cr. (10,790 ÷ 8,829)^(1/3.45) − 1 = 6.0%, plus 0.17% = **6.2% a year**.
- **Bad case:** few new metering awards (Genus keeps losing on price), Uttar Pradesh stays slow (installs cut to a quarter of the pace until the elections), and the margin falls to 15% as fixed-price contracts meet costlier chips. FY30 revenue ~₹4,000cr, mostly maintenance, gas and meter sales; profit ~₹420cr; exit P/E 10. (4,200 ÷ 8,829)^(1/3.45) − 1 = −19.4%, plus 0.17% = **−19.2% a year**.
- **Tail risk:** the Enforcement Directorate's PMLA complaint, now before a Patna court and flagged by the auditor, escalates (attachment of assets, or a bar on government tenders). A second tail risk is state utilities delaying payment on ~₹2,400cr of receivables and unbilled contract assets.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet: 2.** Net debt is ₹1,573cr (1.6 times operating profit), up from ₹605cr a year earlier and heading to ~₹2,000cr. Bank limits are ~93% used, and ₹600–700cr more is due into the GIC platform by Q1 FY29. Its CRISIL AA− rating helps. Guarantees for platform debt were not verified (the annual report would not load).
- **Earnings quality: 1.** Operating cash was −₹152cr, −₹443cr and −₹219cr in FY24–FY26; over five years it was −41% of operating profit. Working capital is ~₹3,700cr (inventory ₹1,350cr, receivables ~₹1,500cr, contract assets ~₹870cr). Consolidated profit includes notional share gains.
- **Management: 2.** It met install, revenue and Q1 margin promises. Against that: cash-positive slipped a year, the margin guide was cut, and the auditor flags an Enforcement Directorate complaint. Promoters went from 50.3% to 39.3%, and the promoter group sold 0.79% (₹78.9cr at ₹318) on 30 September. Three family executives took ~₹21cr each.
- **Moat and runway: 2.** It makes over 30% of India's meters, has 22–23% of metering-service awards, and owns its radio network and software. But prices are fixed, it lost Kerala bidding 54% above the winner, and the RDSS ends in March 2028; gas and water meters are still small.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
- **Negative operating cash for three years (five-year conversion −41%).** Earnings quality scores 1; the case relies on working capital unwinding from FY28. If it does not, interest stays near ₹200cr and the bad case applies.
- **Net debt ₹1,573cr rising to ~₹2,000cr; bank limits ~93% used.** Interest is in the path, and balance sheet scores 2.
- **Order book fell from ₹27,217cr to ₹24,020cr in six months with no large win.** FY29 revenue falls in the base case, and new wins are assumed at about half the historical share.
- **Lost Kerala: Genus bid ₹4,985cr against GridCrest's ₹3,241cr (L1).** Margin is held at 17.5% and the market share of new tenders halved.
- **RDSS ends 31 March 2028.** This is the core of the case; the exit P/E is held at 13.
- **Enforcement Directorate PMLA complaint before a Patna court, carried by the auditor as an emphasis of matter.** Named as the tail risk; it keeps the exit P/E low.
- **Margin guide cut from 20–21% to 18%; no cost pass-through; 40–45% of components imported.** I use 17.5% from FY28 and 15% in the bad case.
- **Cash-positive date pushed from end-FY27 to Q1–Q2 FY28.** This is the first thing to check.
- **Promoters down from 50.3% to 39.3%; promoter group sold 0.79% at ₹318 on 30 September 2026.** Management scores 2.
- **Loans and advances, mostly to the GIC platform, up from ~₹75cr to ₹528cr; another ₹600–700cr to come.** Counted in debt; the platform return is my assumption (~₹100cr by FY30).
- **Three family executives paid ~₹21cr each (~₹62cr, ~10% of profit).** Noted under management.
- **Consolidated profit includes notional share gains.** ~₹20cr removed from the base.
- **Uttar Pradesh installs slowed to about a quarter of the earlier pace until the elections.** In the bad case.
