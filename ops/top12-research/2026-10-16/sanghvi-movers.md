# Sanghvi Movers

## Verdict for the portfolio
India's largest crane-hire company is growing operating profit 25–30% on guidance it has so far met, and trades at about 15 times clean profit. But every rupee of growth needs new cranes bought with debt. Depreciation and interest therefore eat much of the gain, and free cash has been negative two years running. The base case is about 19% a year: close, but short of 25%, with a capital-hungry, cyclical business. **Not now**; the base case reaches 25% only below about ₹300 a share, or if the Saudi cranes start earning on time with collections fixed.

## Clean profit base
- **What I removed.** FY26 reported profit was ₹184cr. The CFO said Q3 FY26 profit was ₹37cr "before exceptional items" against ₹29cr reported, so about ₹8cr after tax was one-off (other income was −₹3cr that quarter). Clean FY26: **about ₹192cr**.
- **Trailing four quarters to June 2026:** reported ₹199cr, clean **about ₹207cr**.
- **What I left in.** I did not remove Q1 FY27's ₹6.2cr bad-debt provision or ₹1.4cr currency loss; provisions recur in this business. Treasury income (about ₹300cr of surplus cash) is modest at ₹21–27cr a year and was left in.
- **P/E check:** ₹3,034cr (site) ÷ ₹207cr = **14.7**; the site shows 14.8 and Screener 14.5. Consistent.

## Profit path FY27–FY30

**Guidance and track record.**
- **FY27:** total income ₹1,400–1,500cr and EBITDA ₹525–575cr (FY26: ₹429cr). Q1 delivered income +40% and EBITDA +30%, so it is on track.
- **FY28:** revenue +30–40% and EBITDA ₹650–700cr.
- **FY26:** profit grew 17.7% against a hoped-for repeat of ~30% growth.
- **Saudi:** the 24-month pipeline was cut from about $50m (May) to about $38m (August).
- I take the low half of guidance: ₹540cr EBITDA in FY27 and ₹660cr in FY28.

**Fleet and E&C drivers.**
- **Cranes:** 492 cranes, gross block about ₹3,300cr, 86% used. India yield is 2.29% a month and Gulf yield 4.10%.
- **FY27 capex of ₹652cr:** ₹92cr is done; ₹560cr arrives in H2, including ₹324cr for Saudi Arabia and Qatar. Management says it adds about 15% to revenue within the year.
- **Wind E&C:** the erection business has doubled each year (₹250cr → ₹500cr → about ₹1,000cr hoped). It runs on a ₹686cr order book at a 12–15% margin; management warns that about 15% of it may slip into FY28.
- **Margin trend:** as E&C grows, the group margin keeps drifting down, from 63% in FY24 to 37% in FY26 and 33% in Q1 on Screener's basis.

**Costs below operating profit.** This is where the growth leaks.
- **Depreciation:** about ₹167cr in FY27 rising to about ₹225cr by FY30 (Q1 was ₹39cr a quarter), as the fleet grows ₹500–650cr a year.
- **Interest:** rises from ₹37cr (FY26) to about ₹60cr (FY27) and ₹80–85cr later. Gross debt climbs toward the 0.72 debt-to-equity ceiling, which management says means about ₹1,000–1,100cr.
- **Tax:** 27%.

**Growth rate.** After FY28 I slow EBITDA growth to 10–12% a year: capex is limited by the debt ceiling, and Saudi growth depends on a region at war. FY30 profit is **₹365cr, about 17% a year from clean FY26**.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
- **What supports growth after FY30:**
  - India's wind build-out (GWEC's 107 GW by 2030 implies about 12 GW a year, against 6 GW last year).
  - Thermal (62 GW bid or awarded), steel (300 million tonnes by 2030), cement and refinery capex.
  - Saudi Vision 2030.
- **What limits it:**
  - Growth requires buying more cranes at a 2% monthly yield hurdle, so it is capital-bound.
  - Wind and industrial capex are cyclical.
  - Saudi Vision 2030 projects peak around 2030.
  - The low-margin E&C share keeps rising.
- **Expected growth after FY30: about 10%.**
- **Anchors:** Value Research year-end P/E was 27.2 (FY22), 13.5 (FY23), 15.7 (FY24) and 13.5 (FY25), and 18.3 on 12 June 2026. It is 14.7 today on clean profit.
- **Exit P/E: 15**, the lower half of the 14–20 band for 8–12% growth. Free cash flow is negative and the business is cyclical. I set it at about today's level, not above.

## Returns
- **Normal case:** ₹365cr × 15 = ₹5,475cr against ₹3,034cr today (site, 15 Oct). No dilution. Dividend yield 0.57%.
  ((365 × 15) ÷ 3,034)^(1/3.45) − 1 + 0.57% = **19.2% a year**.
- **Bad case:** wind additions stall below 6 GW a year and Saudi cranes arrive late or sit idle. Gulf collections stay above 200 days, forcing more bad-debt provisions, while interest and depreciation on the new fleet still arrive. FY30 profit ₹260cr, exit P/E 11. ((260 × 11) ÷ 3,034)^(1/3.45) − 1 + 0.57% = **−1.1% a year**.
- **Tail risk:** the old sales-tax and VAT demands on crane hire for FY2011–FY2018 (several of ₹70–160cr each) are not counted as contingent liabilities because earlier years were won. A loss would be large against ₹1,311cr of net worth.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet: 3.** Net debt is about ₹545cr (Screener), 1.3 times operating profit, and gross debt-to-equity 0.54. But it is heading to the 0.72 ceiling (about ₹1,000–1,100cr of gross debt) with FY27 capex of ₹652cr, and old tax demands are left out of contingent liabilities.
- **Earnings quality: 3.** Operating cash covered 85–101% of operating profit in most years (65% in FY25, 85% in FY26). But free cash was −₹75cr in FY25 and −₹130cr in FY26, group debtor days are 116 (201 in the Gulf), and Q1 needed a ₹6.2cr bad-debt provision.
- **Management: 3.** It is on track for FY27 guidance, improved disclosure, and Saudi broke even early. But the Saudi pipeline was cut within a quarter, the MD's ₹6.46cr pay exceeded the listing-rule limit and needed shareholder ratification, the CFO changed in 2025, and an independent director resigned in September.
- **Moat and runway: 3.** It is the world's third-largest crane-hire firm by lifting capacity and India's largest, with few rivals for 600–800-tonne cranes. But staying ahead means spending heavily every year, and demand follows project cycles.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
1. **Margin fell from 63% (FY24) to 37% (FY26) and 33% (Q1 FY27) as low-margin wind E&C grew.** Path keeps the margin about 37% (company basis); no recovery assumed.
2. **Free cash flow negative: −₹75cr (FY25), −₹130cr (FY26); ₹652cr capex in FY27.** Interest and depreciation built into the path; exit P/E lower half.
3. **Gulf customers pay in 201 days; group 116; ₹6.2cr bad-debt provision in Q1.** Bad case assumes Gulf collections stay slow; next check.
4. **Saudi pipeline cut from $50m to $38m between May and August; the Saudi arm lost about ₹10cr in FY26; West Asia war risk.** EBITDA growth slowed to 10–12% after FY28.
5. **Old sales-tax demands of ₹70–160cr each not counted as contingent liabilities.** Named as tail risk.
6. **Debt rising toward ₹1,000–1,100cr (0.72 debt-to-equity ceiling).** Interest rises to about ₹85cr in the path.
7. **MD's pay of ₹6.46cr above the listing-rule limit; independent director resigned 7 Sep; CFO changed in 2025.** Management score 3.
8. **About 15% of the wind E&C order book may slip a year (percentage-of-completion accounting).** FY28 revenue held at +27%, below the 30–40% guide.
9. **On the May call management could not reconcile an analyst's fleet × yield × utilisation arithmetic with reported crane revenue.** Noted; no adjustment.
