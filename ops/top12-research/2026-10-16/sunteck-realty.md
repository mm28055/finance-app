# Sunteck Realty

## Verdict for the portfolio
A cheap-looking Mumbai luxury developer: the stock fell 37% in a year to 19 times trailing profit and 1.1 times book. Profit can roughly double by FY30 from luxury handovers and a new office-and-retail rent stream. The arithmetic gives about 22% a year, but it rests on two things Sunteck has repeatedly been late on, Nepean Sea Road approvals and building the Oshiwara rent asset, and only 45% of its pre-sales arrive as cash. **Medium, at the low edge.** It is not a 25% name, so buy only after Nepean Sea Road gets RERA and collections pick up.

## Clean profit base
- Screener (consolidated): FY26 net profit ₹202cr (EPS ₹13.92 × 14.67cr shares = ₹204cr to shareholders); last four quarters (Jul-25 to Jun-26) **₹211cr**. There are no exceptional items, tax credits or land gains. Tax was a normal 25% in FY26.
- Other income of ₹43cr (about 15% of pre-tax profit) is interest on deposits and loans to joint ventures. It is part of how the business is funded, so I leave it in, but I flag it.
- P/E check: site ₹4,025cr ÷ ₹211cr = 19.1 (site 18.8; Screener 18.9). They agree.
- Revenue is booked at possession, so FY26's ₹1,124cr of revenue reflects homes sold three or more years ago. FY26 pre-sales were ₹3,157cr (+25%), Q1 FY27 ₹787cr (+20%), but collections were only ₹1,433cr in FY26 (45%) and ₹409cr in Q1.

## Profit path FY27–FY30
Drivers:
1. **Residential handovers.** Sunteck World (Naigaon) received its occupation certificate in September 2026, and ready flats at BKC and Oshiwara are being booked as sold. Pre-sales are guided up 25–30% in FY27 (I assume 22–25% a year, since Q1 was +20%). Recognised revenue runs at about 55% of pre-sales from three years earlier, because joint ventures are not consolidated and Nepean Sea Road has not started construction.
2. **Margins.** Management says new sales carry 35–40% embedded operating margins (Altea Tower 1 about 45%), but it is booking older township sales now. Reported margin: 27% in FY26, 35% in Q1. I assume 29% rising to 32%.
3. **Rent.** Two BKC office buildings earn about ₹76–80cr. Management guided ₹86cr in FY27, ₹96cr in FY28, then +₹150cr in each of FY29 and FY30 from the 1.5–1.6 mn sq ft Oshiwara commercial cluster, reaching about ₹450cr "by the end of this decade". Construction had not started at July 2026 and takes 24–30 months. I take **₹180cr in FY29 and ₹230cr in FY30**, about 40% below the guide.
4. **Interest** rises from ₹73cr to about ₹130cr: gross debt heads to India Ratings' ₹1,100–1,200cr peak and commercial construction borrowing stops being capitalised once the cluster opens. Depreciation rises from ₹15cr to ₹45cr when the cluster is complete. Tax 25%.
5. **Warrants.** About 1.14cr warrants at ₹425 (mostly promoter group) bring about ₹360cr by June 2027. That money cuts debt, worth about ₹25cr a year after tax from FY29 (₹20cr in FY28).

| FY26 (actual) | 1,124 | 27% | 202 | Luxury and Naigaon handovers |
| FY27 | 1,300 | 29% | 245 | Sunteck World OC; BKC/ODC ready stock; rent ₹86cr |

FY30 profit **₹475cr**, about **24% a year** from FY26's ₹202cr. Of this, residential is about ₹365cr (about 19% a year) and rent net of its interest and depreciation is about ₹85cr. Cross-check: PL Capital (8 Oct 2026) forecasts ₹280cr for FY27 and ₹315cr for FY28, so my early years sit slightly below theirs. Track record: pre-sales growth has been met (25% in FY26 as guided), but flagship timelines have slipped repeatedly. Nepean Sea Road's RERA was expected "by end of Q4 FY26 or Q1 FY27" and is now "clarity by end of Q3 FY27"; Dubai is "recalibrated". Over ten years profit went nowhere (₹216cr in FY17, ₹202cr in FY26), and return on equity averaged 4%.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
- **Runway:** ₹42,700cr of GDV is about 13 years of FY26 pre-sales. ₹8,370cr is launched, ₹7,100cr is to be launched in FY27 excluding Dubai, plus Nepean Sea Road (Emaance, priced from about ₹2.5 lakh per sq ft) and a 50% share of a ₹9,000cr Downtown Dubai plot. New land keeps coming: about ₹5,000cr of GDV in the past 12 months, plus 4.5 acres at Borivali on 5 Oct 2026. Rent should reach about ₹400–450cr a year in FY31, so FY31–FY33 profit growth should still be 15%+ if the cluster lets.
- **Against that:** Mumbai-only, and an ROE that rises only to about 9% by FY30. Luxury softness is visible: PL's channel checks note "some softness is seen in luxury projects".
- **Anchors:** PL Capital (8 Oct 2026) puts Brigade at 22.6 times FY26 profit and 18.6 times FY27E, and Oberoi at 21.8 and 18.4. Both earn 11–15% returns on equity. Sunteck trades at 20.0 times FY26 and 14.6 times FY27E. Sunteck's own trailing P/E over the past year ran from about 19 (₹265 low) to 34 (₹472 high) on FY26 EPS. Book value is ₹246 a share (1.1 times today).
- **Exit P/E: 18** on FY30 profit. That is the lower end of the 12–16% growth band (18–26), because cash conversion is weak and returns on equity are single digit. It is just below today's 19. Cross-check: ₹475cr × 18 = ₹8,550cr, about 1.6 times FY30 book of roughly ₹5,300cr at a 9% ROE. The rent stream alone (₹400cr+ by FY31) would be worth ₹4,000cr+ at an 8.5% yield before its debt.

## Returns
Today's market value is ₹4,025cr (site, 15 Oct). Dilution d = 7.8% (1.14cr warrants at ₹425 against 14.67cr shares; I assume they convert, since the promoter group is most of the issue; the ₹360cr is in the profit path). Dividend yield 0.55%.

- **Normal case:** (₹475cr × 18 = ₹8,550cr) ÷ (₹4,025cr × 1.078 = ₹4,339cr) = 1.97×. Over 3.45 years that is 21.7% a year, plus 0.55% = **22.3% a year**. If the warrants lapse (they are worth less than the ₹319 still payable while the share is ₹274): ₹450cr × 18 ÷ ₹4,025cr gives 23.0%. At an exit P/E of 17 the base falls to 20.3%.
- **Bad case:** Nepean Sea Road approvals slip into FY28, Dubai stays shelved and the Oshiwara cluster opens a year late, so FY30 rent is about ₹120cr. Revenue reaches only about ₹1,900cr at a 28% margin, and FY30 profit is about ₹360cr. The exit P/E drops one band to 14. (₹360cr × 14 = ₹5,040cr) ÷ ₹4,339cr = 1.16×, which is **5.0% a year** with the dividend.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet: 3.** Screener net debt was ₹679cr at March 2026: 2.1 times filed operating profit, but 1.2 times the company's own ₹552cr operating surplus and only 0.19 times equity. Gross debt doubled to ₹774cr to buy land, with India Ratings' peak at ₹1,100–1,200cr. Rated AA. There are no pledges, and the promoter is putting in ₹500cr through warrants.
- **Earnings quality: 2.** Five-year filed operating cash was ₹122cr against ₹768cr of operating profit (16%), and −₹432cr in FY26 after ₹810cr of land buying. On its own measure (collections less construction and overheads, before land) the surplus was ₹552cr in FY26 and ₹193cr in Q1, but collections are only 45% of pre-sales, because Nepean Sea Road bookings bring no cash until construction starts.
- **Management: 3.** It met the FY26 pre-sales guide (+25%), and the promoter is subscribing warrants at ₹425, 55% above today's price. But the flagships keep slipping (Nepean Sea Road RERA, Dubai), and ten years of flat profit (₹216cr in FY17, ₹202cr in FY26) at a 4% average ROE is a poor record of turning land into profit. A GST notice arrived in September 2026.
- **Moat and runway: 3.** It owns trophy addresses (BKC, Nepean Sea Road, ODC Goregaon), unsold stock is under 12 months of sales, and the pipeline is 13 years long. But it is confined to Mumbai against Lodha, Oberoi and Godrej, and rent is still mostly a plan.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
1. **Filed operating cash ₹122cr against ₹768cr of operating profit over five years; −₹432cr in FY26.** Earnings quality scores 2 and the exit P/E sits at the bottom of its band.
2. **Collections only 45% of FY26 pre-sales (₹1,433cr against ₹3,157cr).** The path books revenue at about 55% of lagged pre-sales; collections above ₹1,800cr in FY27 is a must-go-right.
3. **Nepean Sea Road RERA has slipped at least two quarters; Dubai is deferred by the West Asia war.** Neither contributes to FY30 profit in the base, and the bad case adds a further slip.
4. **Gross debt doubled to ₹774cr, heading to ₹1,100–1,200cr.** Interest charged to profit nearly doubles to ₹130cr by FY30.
5. **Ten years of flat profit and a 3–6% ROE.** This is why the exit P/E is held below today's 19 and below Oberoi and Brigade.
6. **The rent guide (₹450cr by FY29–FY30) relies on an asset whose construction had not started in July 2026.** The base takes about 60% of the guide (₹230cr in FY30), and the bad case ₹120cr.
7. **Warrants at ₹425 are now worth less than the remaining payment.** If the promoter group still pays, that is a positive signal; if they lapse, there is no dilution but ₹360cr less money (base 23.0%).
8. **Luxury softness and a 37% share price fall in a year.** The bad case lowers margin to 28%.
