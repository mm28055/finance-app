# J Kumar Infra

## Verdict for the portfolio
The cheapest clean business in this batch: under 9 times profit, net cash, a 14–15% margin every quarter and a record ₹24,700cr order book including the latest win and a lowest-bid position. The whole case is conversion. Revenue was flat for two years against a 15% promise, so I assume about 11% a year, not management's 15%. That still gives about 25% a year if the P/E only returns to its own middle of about 11. **Medium**: the best risk-reward among my five. Start it, and add once H2 FY27 shows quarters of ₹1,700–1,800cr.

## Clean profit base
- Screener (consolidated): FY26 net profit **₹387cr**; last four quarters **₹381cr** (EPS ₹50.32 × 7.57cr shares). There are no exceptional items, tax was a normal 25–30%, and there is no minority interest.
- Other income rose from ₹34cr (FY25) to ₹69cr (FY26) and ₹77cr in the last four quarters. Management says it is interest on fixed-deposit margin money kept against bank guarantees and on surplus cash, and "will be a recurring number". It is about 14% of pre-tax profit. I leave it in, because it scales with the guarantees the business needs.
- Coming one-off to exclude later: a ₹180cr sale of 30 acres of Vizag land, expected in Q2–Q3 FY27, should bring a gain management describes only as "good profitability". It is not in the base or the path.
- P/E check: site ₹3,363cr ÷ ₹381cr = 8.8 (site 8.64; Screener 8.59). They agree.

## Profit path FY27–FY30
**Drivers:**
- **Order book.** ₹22,246cr at June 2026, plus about ₹1,000cr won in early August and a ₹1,500cr lowest bid at Delhi Metro (World Bank-funded, LoA expected in Q2): about ₹24,700cr, roughly 4 years of revenue. Wins so far in FY27 are about ₹5,500cr, against a target of ₹8,000–10,000cr (FY26: ₹1,039cr).
- **Big jobs now moving.** GMLR twin tunnel: the first TBM was ready to bore in August, the second within about two months; about ₹3,000–4,000cr of revenue over the next two years; target completion June–December 2029. Chennai elevated corridor: 20% done, completion December 2028, with NHAI paying delay costs. Anand Nagar–Saket: 15% done, due Oct–Nov 2028. VDCR (Versova–Dahisar) speeding up from Q3. Vadhvan port road earthwork from October.
- **Guidance.** FY27 revenue +15% (about ₹6,500cr); ₹7,500cr in FY28; margin 14–15%, aiming for 15–16% in 6–8 quarters. Q1 FY27 was only +2% (BMC water-use ban, war, monsoon), so the rest of FY27 needs about +20%.
- **Track record.** The FY26 15% guide delivered about 1%. The ₹7,500cr target moved from FY27 to FY28. In May management said "zero impact" from the war; in August it blamed the war for a slow quarter. The margin promise has been kept every quarter.
- **Haircut.** I take +9% in FY27 and 10–12% a year after that, so revenue reaches ₹8,600cr in FY30 (11% a year from FY26), with the margin held at 14.3–14.5% (no 15–16%).
- **Below operating profit.** Depreciation rises from ₹196cr to about ₹235–250cr as the ₹300cr TBM is capitalised (₹150cr a year of capex after). Interest rises from ₹168cr to about ₹205cr: interest-bearing client advances of ₹470cr at 8.5–10% and more guarantees. Other income about ₹70–78cr. Tax 26.5%.

FY30 profit **₹640cr**, about **13.4% a year** from FY26. That matches its own history (3-year profit growth 13%, 10-year 15%), and sits about 8% below what management's guide implies (about ₹700cr at a 7% profit margin on 15% growth). The note's base was 10%. My figure is higher mainly because interest and depreciation grow more slowly than operating profit once the TBM is in. The note's low case of 4% is close to my bad case.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
- **Runway:** management sees ₹50,000cr–₹1 lakh crore of Mumbai-region bids in 9–12 months: the Uttan–Virar elevated corridor (₹50,000–60,000cr), Metro Lines 5, 10, 13 and 14, and Shaktipeeth and MSRDC corridors. The central capital budget is up 11.5% to about ₹12.2 lakh crore. Its skills (twin TBMs under a city, an elevated road built on top of an existing road) put it among few bidders. In March 2030, the market will look at the book and FY30–FY31 wins. With ₹8,000–10,000cr a year of wins, 10–12% growth after FY30 is reasonable.
- **Against that:** it is a contractor, so growth depends on the award cycle (highway awards fell from 12,731 km in FY22 to about 7,000 km in FY26). Mumbai agencies are concentrated, large jobs need bigger bank-guarantee lines (65% used), and site handovers set the pace.
- **Anchors:** peers trade at about 9–11 times trailing profit (Motilal Oswal pages, 6 Aug 2026: KNR 8.9, H.G. Infra 10.8–10.9, Patel 10.5; J Kumar 9.5 then). J Kumar's own range over five years runs from about 7 times (FY23) to 20 times (2024 peak), and about 8–13 times over the past year on FY26 EPS (₹425–672).
- **Exit P/E: 11.** This is below the table's 14–20 for 8–12% growth, because Indian contractors have traded below it through this down-cycle and J Kumar has rarely held 14 or more. 11 is roughly the middle of its own range and the top of today's peers. It is above today's 8.8, and the reason must clear: the two-year revenue stall caused by approvals ends as GMLR, Chennai and VDCR execute. If H2 FY27 revenue does not reach about ₹1,700cr a quarter, that reason has not cleared and 9 is the right number.

## Returns
Today's market value is ₹3,363cr (site, 15 Oct). Dilution: 0. Management says a fund-raise is "not for the moment", and a buyback is "on our cards". Dividend yield 0.9%.

- **Normal case:** (₹640cr × 11 = ₹7,040cr) ÷ ₹3,363cr = 2.09×. Over 3.45 years that is 23.9% a year, plus 0.9% = **24.8% a year**. At an exit P/E of 10 it is 21.4%; at 12 it is 27.9%. On management's own path (about ₹700cr) at 12, it is 31.3%.
- **Bad case:** site handovers keep slipping (Vadhvan land, VDCR, metro awards late), revenue grows only about 6% a year to ₹7,200cr, the margin is 14%, and FY30 profit is about ₹475cr. The P/E stays at a trough 8, one band down. (₹475cr × 8 = ₹3,800cr) ÷ ₹3,363cr = 1.13×, which is **4.5% a year** with the dividend. That is still positive because of net cash and a stable margin.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet: 4.** Net cash: ₹45cr at June (company) and ₹72cr at March (Screener). Gross debt is ₹840cr after the last TBM instalment, guided below ₹800cr by March 2027 (0.24 times equity). It is rated A+ with a Positive outlook (ICRA). But bank guarantees and surety bonds rose 48% to ₹4,364cr and those lines are 65% used, and all ₹470cr of client advances carry interest. So it is not a 5.
- **Earnings quality: 3.** Four-year operating cash was ₹2,063cr against ₹2,950cr of operating profit (70%). That ratio leans on FY26's ₹1,128cr, which included ₹706cr of client advances at March; FY23–FY25 alone was 44%. Net cash then fell from ₹264cr to ₹45cr in Q1 as advances were used. Working capital is 99–103 days, retention money ₹464cr and unbilled revenue ₹578cr. There are no write-offs, and the audit opinion is clean.
- **Management: 3.** Margin discipline has been real for years: it turned down work below its margin, winning only ₹1,039cr in FY26. Promoters hold 46.64% with no pledge, unchanged for three years. But revenue guidance has failed (15% promised, 1% delivered), the ₹7,500cr target slipped a year, and statements on the war changed between May and August. It is a family-run business (founder plus two sons as MDs).
- **Moat and runway: 3.** Specialist urban work (twin TBMs, road-on-road elevated corridors) with prompt-paying central and Mumbai clients keeps margins at 14–15%, above most contractors. But order-cycle exposure, concentration in Maharashtra, rivals L&T and Afcons for the largest jobs, and limited guarantee lines cap it.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
1. **FY26 revenue growth about 1% against a 15% guide; Q1 FY27 +2%.** The path takes 9% for FY27 and 10–12% after, about 3–4 points below the guide.
2. **The ₹7,500cr revenue target moved from FY27 to FY28.** My FY28 is ₹7,000cr.
3. **The order book roughly doubled while revenue stayed flat for two years; one project (Orange Gate) was cancelled; Vadhvan still waits on land.** The bad case assumes handovers keep slipping. The exit P/E falls to 9–10 if H2 FY27 misses.
4. **Bank-guarantee lines 65% used; guarantees up 48% to ₹4,364cr.** Order wins in the path stay at ₹8,000–10,000cr a year, not the ₹15,000–20,000cr management says it could take. Balance sheet scores 4, not 5.
5. **FY26's ₹1,128cr operating cash was flattered by ₹706cr of client advances; net cash fell ₹220cr in Q1.** Earnings quality scores 3.
6. **Statements shifted ("zero impact" from the war in May, blamed in August).** Management scores 3, with no credit for guidance.
7. **TBM depreciation (₹300cr capitalised in Q2) and interest on advances.** Depreciation reaches about ₹250cr in FY28 and interest about ₹205cr in FY30.
8. **Concentration on Mumbai agencies (BMC, MMRDA, MSRDC).** Mitigated by Chennai (NHAI), Delhi Metro, NBCC and work in seven states. The tail risk is a Maharashtra spending freeze.
