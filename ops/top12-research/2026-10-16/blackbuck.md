# BlackBuck

## Verdict for the portfolio
A real habit business: 9 lakh truckers pay tolls and track trucks on it every month, with a 93% contribution margin. But it is valued at about 89 times a normally taxed profit, and that profit is flattered by interest on cash. Pre-tax profit fell in Q1 as device depreciation jumped, and the new growth lines (loads, loans) earn far thinner margins. Even with profit 2.5 times higher by FY30, the return at today's price is slightly negative. **Not now.**

## Clean profit base
- **Filed figures (Screener, consolidated):**
  - FY26: revenue ₹652cr, operating profit ₹167cr (26%), other income ₹59cr, depreciation ₹53cr, pre-tax profit ₹167cr, net profit ₹160cr (4% tax).
  - Last four quarters (Sep-25 to Jun-26): revenue ₹712cr, operating profit ₹176cr, other income ₹59cr, depreciation ₹66cr, pre-tax ₹163cr, net ₹169cr.
- **What I removed.**
  - **Tax credits.** Net profit exceeds pre-tax profit because of deferred-tax credits from about ₹255cr of past losses: Q4 FY26 was taxed at −63% and Q1 FY27 at 0%. The CFO says current and deferred tax will offset each other for two more quarters. I tax at 25%, which gives a clean TTM profit of **about ₹122cr** (FY26: about ₹125cr).
  - **Treasury income.** Other income of ₹59cr is interest on about ₹1,000cr of cash (ICRA counted ₹1,025cr in Sep 2025; Screener's net cash is ₹629cr). That is 36% of pre-tax profit. Without it, the business earned only about ₹78cr after a normal tax.
- **Pre-tax profit trend.** Excluding treasury income, it was ₹26cr in Q1 FY27 against ₹30cr a year earlier (−13%), because depreciation on tracking devices rose from ₹9cr to ₹23cr.
- **The P/E.** The site's 63.3 matches Screener's: ₹10,866cr ÷ ₹169cr of reported profit. On clean, taxed profit it is **about 89**. Cash is about **6–9% of today's value**.

## Profit path FY27–FY30

| Year | Revenue (₹cr) | Operating margin | Profit at 25% tax (₹cr) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY26 (actual) | 652 | 26% | ~125 (reported 160) |
| FY27 | 880 | 24% | ~130 |
| FY28 | 1,110 | 25% | ~175 |
| FY29 | 1,370 | 26.5% | ~235 |
| FY30 | 1,660 | 28% | **~310** |

**Drivers.**
- **Revenue.** Gross revenue grows +35%, +26%, +23%, +21% (Q1 FY27: +42% gross, +25% net). The parts:
  - **Core (tolling and tracking):** grows about 20%. Toll value is up 16% against low single digits for the national toll network, there were record device sales, and renewals run at 70–85%.
  - **Superloads and vehicle finance:** grow faster. These "growth" lines were ₹59cr in Q1, +153% on the year, but are booked gross at thin margins.
- **Margin.** Management says 60–85% of extra core revenue reaches operating profit, but spending on Superloads pulls the other way, so the filed margin has slipped from 33% to 24%. I let it recover to 28% by FY30 as vehicle finance turns profitable (promised by end-FY27) and Superloads hubs mature.
- **Below the margin.**
  - Depreciation rises to about ₹130cr as devices are written off over two years.
  - Other income rises to about ₹85cr. Interest is ₹6cr.
- **Growth rate:** about 28% a year from the clean taxed base (₹122cr to ₹310cr over 3.75 years). Against reported profit it is about 18% (₹160cr to ₹310cr over four years).
- **Guidance and track record.** Management refuses to give numbers. Its May warning, that the West Asia war and suspended fuel-loyalty schemes would hurt, came true: core revenue rose only 1% on the quarter. It will not split profit between core and growth businesses for "three to four quarters".

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
**The runway.**
- **For long growth:** trucking is about a $200bn market. BlackBuck reaches roughly 25–30% of truckers, and Superloads is live in 14 of 300+ freight hubs. If Superloads and lending work, the market will expect 20%+ growth in FY31–FY33 (band 30–42).
- **Against:**
  - The regulator sets the toll-payment fee: NHAI cut it from 1.5% to 1.0% in 2022.
  - Delhivery has said it may enter tolling, fuel and truck finance.
  - The new revenue earns far less than tolling.
  - Governance flags are below.
- **I use 32**, the lower half of the band. The stock has traded between ₹495 and ₹748 in the past year, which is 55–80 times reported profit and roughly 75–110 times taxed profit. I do not assume that holds once the tax credits run out.

## Returns
Market value ₹10,866cr (site, 15 Oct). Dilution 2% (employee options). No dividend.
- **Normal case:** ₹310cr × 32 = ₹9,920cr. (9,920 ÷ (10,866 × 1.02))^(1/3.45) − 1 = **−3.2% a year**.
- **Bad case:** the truck cycle turns (ICRA expects commercial-vehicle sales to fall in H2 FY27), Superloads keeps burning cash, and Delhivery or banks price toll cards aggressively. FY30 profit is about ₹200cr and the exit P/E is 24. ₹4,800cr gives **−21.5% a year**.
- **What it would take:** 25% a year needs about ₹750cr of FY30 profit at 32 times. That is six times today's clean profit.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet 5/5:** about ₹1,000cr of cash and liquid investments against ₹59cr of borrowings. The ₹22.4cr GST demand is small next to ₹1,422cr of net worth.
- **Earnings quality 2/5:**
  - FY26 operating cash (₹169cr) matched operating profit. But free cash was only ₹58cr against ₹160cr of reported profit, because device purchases are real cash.
  - Reported profit is inflated by tax credits, and 36% of pre-tax profit is interest.
  - The five-year cash ratio is meaningless because of the early losses.
- **Management 2/5:**
  - Delivery is mixed: the warned slowdown bit, while the growth arms beat.
  - It gives no guidance and no segment profit.
  - In FY25 it booked a ₹390cr charge when share-conversion terms were changed in favour of promoter directors.
  - The people, product and company-secretary heads left within six months.
  - Promoters hold only 24.99%, and Accel sold 1.48% in September.
- **Moat and runway 3/5:** a near-daily habit in tolling and tracking, with share gains of 16% against low single digits, and no scaled digital rival (ICRA). But the regulator sets the price, and loads are an infrequent need where its edge is unproven.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
- **Pre-tax profit fell 8% in Q1 FY27 (₹42cr against ₹46cr); excluding interest, −13%.** The base case has FY27 profit roughly flat at about ₹130cr on a taxed basis.
- **Profit after tax rose only because of tax credits.** Everything is re-taxed at 25%: the clean P/E is about 89, not 63.
- **36% of pre-tax profit is treasury income.** It is kept in profit, but the exit P/E is not lifted for it.
- **Device depreciation jumped from ₹9cr to ₹23cr a quarter; FY26 free cash was ₹58cr against ₹160cr of profit.** Depreciation in the model rises to ₹130cr.
- **Growth is shifting to low-margin loads and lending; net revenue grew 25% against 42% gross.** The margin path recovers only to 28%.
- **FY25 ₹390cr charge on share-conversion terms that favoured promoter directors.** Management is scored 2.
- **Three senior exits in six months (product, people, company secretary).** Management is scored 2.
- **₹22.4cr GST demand upheld (Aug 2026).** It is small against the cash.
- **Accel selling; promoters at 24.99%.** This caps the price. No uplift is assumed.
- **NHAI sets FASTag fees and cut them before.** Named as the tail risk.
- **Delhivery may enter tolling and truck finance.** In the bad case.
- **ICRA expects a weaker truck cycle in H2 FY27.** In the bad case.
