# Cartrade Tech

## Verdict for the portfolio
A very good business at a price that already pays for most of the next four years. Even if profit more than doubles to about ₹500cr by FY30, a buyer at ₹14,409cr earns about 3% a year if the market pays a still-generous 32 times profit in 2030. To earn 25% a year you need about ₹890cr of FY30 profit at 35 times, which is management's ₹1,000cr goal delivered almost in full and on time. **Not now** for a 25% target. Follow it and buy on a large price fall or on proof that the per-car fee model is working.

## Clean profit base
- **Filed figures (Screener, consolidated):** FY26 revenue ₹779cr, operating profit ₹258cr (33%), other income ₹84cr, pre-tax profit ₹290cr, net profit ₹244cr, EPS ₹46.58. Last four quarters (Sep-25 to Jun-26): revenue ₹807cr, operating profit ₹276cr, other income ₹84cr, pre-tax ₹307cr, net ₹253cr, EPS ₹48.37.
- **What I removed:**
  - **Tax credit.** Q4 FY26 was taxed at only 6% (₹71cr profit on ₹75cr pre-tax). The CFO now guides 23–24% because OLX has used up its tax shelter. I re-taxed everything at 24%.
  - **Minority share.** 44.57% of Shriram Automall's profit belongs to other holders: about ₹18cr over the last year (₹253cr net minus ₹235cr attributable, using EPS × about 4.86cr shares).
- **Clean attributable profit:** TTM Jun-26 = ₹307cr × 0.76 = ₹233cr, less about ₹18cr minority = **about ₹215cr**. FY26 on the same basis = **about ₹200cr** (reported attributable about ₹223cr).
- **Treasury income.** Treasury income is ₹84cr a year, 27% of pre-tax profit, and about ₹64cr after tax. It is real cash income, but it is not the business. Without it, the core platform earns about ₹151cr.
- **The P/E.** The site's 60.6 and Screener's 60.9 agree: market value ₹14,409cr ÷ ₹235cr reported attributable profit. On clean profit the P/E is **about 67**. Set aside the ₹1,321cr of cash (company figure; **about 9% of today's value**) and the core business costs about 87 times its core profit.

## Profit path FY27–FY30

| Year | Revenue (₹cr) | Operating margin | Attributable profit at 24% tax (₹cr) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY26 (actual) | 779 | 33% | ~200 (reported ~223) |
| FY27 | 915 | 34% | ~250 |
| FY28 | 1,085 | 36% | ~320 |
| FY29 | 1,295 | 37.5% | ~400 |
| FY30 | 1,540 | 39% | **~500** |

**Drivers.**
- **Revenue.** I assume about 17–19% growth a year: CarWale/BikeWale +18% (Q1 FY27), OLX +29% now and fading to the high teens, and remarketing +13%. Q1 FY27 group revenue grew only 16% and slipped 1% on the quarter. The used-car market itself grows about 10% a year: 5–6 million cars now, 8–9 million in 4–5 years, according to management.
- **Margin.** It rises about 1.5 points a year towards management's 40%, because wage rises land once a year in April and costs grew only 7% in Q1.
- **Below the operating line.** Other income rises from ₹84cr to about ₹170cr as cash builds to about ₹2,700cr with no payout. Depreciation is about ₹50cr and lease interest about ₹15cr. Tax is 24%, and the minority takes about 7%.
- **Growth rate.** The result is about 25% a year from the clean TTM base (₹215cr to ₹500cr over 3.75 years).

**Against guidance and track record.**
- **Track record.** Management has a good record: 16 straight quarters of 45%+ EBITDA growth, and on the Q1 FY27 call it delivered the OLX step-up it promised in May.
- **Why I haircut the goal.** Management's ₹1,000cr profit goal "over the next 4 to 5 years" (restated 29 July 2026) needs about 35–40% a year. I take half of it, for three reasons:
  - Revenue growth has slowed from 31% (FY25) to 22% (FY26) to 16% (Q1 FY27).
  - The new levers (Elite Buyer paid plans, Spinny and Landmark per-car fees, IDFC loans) have no disclosed revenue yet.
  - Dealer lead prices are, in management's words, "pretty static".

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
**The runway.**
- In March 2030 the market will ask whether CarTrade has turned listing fees into a cut of each transaction.
- If it has, profit growth of about 20% can continue: OLX buyer plans, loans at 2–3% of loan value, and the fee per car.
- If not, growth falls towards the used-car market's 10% plus margin creep.
- I assume the market expects about 16–20% for FY31–FY33. That band is 24–34.

**Where in the band.**
- It earns the upper half: net cash, 95% free traffic, 63% of car sellers on OLX, almost no capex. But reported return on capital is only 12%, because ₹1,324cr of acquisition goodwill and the idle cash sit on the balance sheet.
- **I use 32.** That is about 34 times core profit with the cash counted at face value.

**Anchors.**
- Info Edge trades at 55 times and IndiaMART, a classifieds leader whose growth stalled, at 19.8 times (Screener, 16 Oct 2026).
- CarTrade itself traded between about 38 and 68 times trailing profit over the last year (₹1,523–₹3,291).
- An exit at 32 is half of today's 61–67. I do not assume today's multiple holds, because growth is slowing, not speeding up.

## Returns
Market value ₹14,409cr (site, 15 Oct). Dilution 1% (options, including 20 lakh for the chairman). No dividend.
- **Normal case:** ₹500cr × 32 = ₹16,000cr. (16,000 ÷ (14,409 × 1.01))^(1/3.45) − 1 = **+2.8% a year**.
- **Bad case:** OLX paid plans meet resistance and the Spinny/Landmark model stays small. Revenue grows about 14% a year, the margin stalls at 35%, FY30 profit is about ₹380cr, and the exit P/E drops one band to 24. ₹9,120cr gives **−12.7% a year**.
- **What it would take:** at 36 times, management's goal nearly met (₹850cr) gives about 24% a year. Only that outcome clears the bar.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet 5/5:** ₹1,321cr of cash and investments (company; Screener counts ₹811cr net cash). The only borrowings are ₹140cr of leases, and no guarantees are flagged.
- **Earnings quality 4/5:** five-year operating cash of ₹555cr against operating profit of ₹391cr (142%) and debtor days of 44. But 27% of pre-tax profit is treasury income, and Q4 FY26 was helped by a 6% tax rate.
- **Management 4/5:** it delivered what it promised for Q1 (OLX +29%, all segment margins up), with no promoter pledges and no dilution. Against that:
  - The chairman is paid 175 times the median employee.
  - His son is chief strategy officer and OLX chairman, and his daughter joined the board in 2025.
  - The consumer-business CEO resigned in August 2026.
  - There is still no payout policy.
- **Moat and runway 4/5:** three brands with 150 million users a year, 95% of them arriving free. The threats are AI search taking traffic and most deals still closing off the platform.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
- **Revenue slowed to +16% (Q1 FY27) and fell 1% on the quarter.** The base case uses 17–19%, not the 29% three-year rate.
- **Treasury income is 27% of pre-tax profit.** I counted the cash separately and checked that the exit value works on core profit plus cash.
- **No dividend or buyback, and cash keeps piling up (return on equity 10%).** The base case assumes no payout. A buyback would be upside.
- **Q4 FY26 was taxed at 6%; the rate is now 23–24%.** Everything is re-taxed at 24%.
- **44.57% of Shriram Automall's profit goes to minorities.** About 7% of group profit is removed.
- **The ₹1,000cr goal cannot be checked: Elite Buyer, Spinny and Landmark revenue are undisclosed.** The base case takes about half of it.
- **Governance: the chairman's pay, his son and daughter in senior roles, the consumer CEO's exit.** Management is scored 4, not 5. This is not a reason to avoid the stock, but it argues against paying top-of-band.
- **₹1,324cr of goodwill.** No impairment so far. It is a reason the reported return on capital is low.
- **AI search could divert free traffic.** Traffic is still up 7%. This is in the bad case as slower growth.
- **ICRA expects commercial-vehicle sales to fall in H2 FY27.** This hits auction volumes. Remarketing is only one of three businesses, so I make no separate cut.
- **Foreign holders cut from 68.5% (Sep 2025) to 51.2% (Sep 2026).** Domestic funds bought (9.95% to 19.4%), so the selling has been absorbed so far.
