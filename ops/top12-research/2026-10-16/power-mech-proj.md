# Power Mech Proj.

## Verdict for the portfolio
A solid, contract-backed builder and maintainer of coal power plants with long mining and upkeep contracts. But it has missed its own targets repeatedly: FY26 revenue, FY26 orders and the Q1 margin, and three cuts to its mining guidance. Its profit also turns into cash slowly. At about 20 times clean profit, a believable 15% a year profit growth gives about 13% a year. That is a reasonable holding, not a 25% one. **Not now** for this portfolio.

## Clean profit base
- **Shareholders' profit, not total profit.** Screener's "net profit" includes the share owed to minority partners in subsidiaries (the Karnataka royalty-collection contract and the mine companies). Shareholders' profit comes from EPS × 3.16cr shares:
  - FY26: ₹115.13 × 3.162 = **₹364cr** (total ₹412cr);
  - last four quarters to June 2026: ₹123.75 × 3.162 = ₹391cr (total ₹420cr).
- **Tax adjustment.** The last four quarters were taxed at only 23%: Q3 FY26 at 20% and Q4 at 18%, against 26–35% in other years. At a normal 27% the trailing figure is about **₹373cr**.
- **One-off revenue in the base.** Q1 FY26 included ₹51cr of one-off "seized quantity" penalty revenue on the Karnataka contract, most of which went to minority partners. It is in the FY26 base, which therefore slightly flatters it.
- **P/E check:** ₹7,658cr ÷ ₹391cr = 19.6 (site 19.6, Screener 19.5). On normal tax it is **20.5**.

## Profit path FY27–FY30

| Year | Revenue | Operating margin (excl. other income) | Profit to shareholders |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY26 | ₹6,062cr | 11.6% | ₹364cr |
| FY27 | ₹7,000cr | 11.3% | ₹401cr |
| FY28 | ₹8,000cr | 11.8% | ₹472cr |
| FY29 | ₹9,000cr | 12.2% | ₹557cr |
| FY30 | ₹10,000cr | 12.5% | ₹645cr |

**What management guides:**
- FY27 revenue about ₹7,300cr (+21%) and a 12.5% EBITDA margin including other income.
- Orders of ₹12,000cr in FY27.
- Margin rising about 0.5 points a year to about 14% by FY30.
- Coal-mining revenue of ₹500cr in FY27 and ₹1,100–1,200cr in FY28, at 15–17% margins, reaching 21–23% from FY29 at full output (about ₹2,100–2,200cr of mining revenue by FY29–FY30).
- O&M (plant upkeep, 18–20% margin) growing about 24–28% in FY27.

**Why I haircut it:**
1. **FY26 misses.** Revenue was ₹6,062cr against the ₹6,500cr guided (the water division's bills were held up), and orders were ₹7,210cr against ₹10,000cr (a ₹1,563cr battery order was cancelled).
2. **Q1 FY27 margin.** It was 10.8% (company) and 10% on Screener's basis, against 12.5% guided. Causes: war-driven steel, gas and diesel costs, a royalty change that cut the Karnataka contract's margin from 14% to 10–11%, and heavy waste-rock removal at the KBP mine.
3. **Mining guidance cut three times for FY28.** It went from ₹1,800–1,900cr (February) to ₹1,250cr (May) to ₹1,100–1,200cr (August). The Tasra washing plant, due November–December, must work before Tasra ramps up.
4. **Erection capacity is full** at 14–15 large boilers at once and can grow only 5–7% a year.

So I use:
- **Revenue:** ₹7,000cr in FY27 (+15%), then 11–14% a year. FY28 mining is taken at about ₹900cr.
- **Margin:** rising 0.3–0.4 points a year to 12.5% by FY30 (about 13% with other income), not 14%.

**Costs below operating profit.** Interest rises from ₹116cr to about ₹155cr as revenue grows on working-capital lines; management says current lines support up to about ₹10,000cr of revenue. Depreciation rises from ₹75cr to about ₹165cr (₹400cr Tasra washery and ₹100–120cr a year of equipment). Tax is 27%, and minority partners take about 10%.

**Growth rate.** FY30 shareholders' profit is **₹645cr, about 15% a year from ₹364cr**, close to the note's 16%.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
- **What supports growth after FY30:**
  - India plans about 63 GW (management says up to 100 GW) of new coal capacity by 2032, so erection and civil work stays busy until the early 2030s.
  - Every new plant then becomes an O&M opportunity worth ₹800–1,000cr a year of new contracts.
  - The two mining contracts run for decades (₹55,398cr of orders including mining).
  - Metro upkeep, steel and NMDC mining projects add options.
- **What limits it:**
  - Thermal-plant construction is a cycle that should crest around FY30–32.
  - BHEL and Adani dominate the order book.
  - About half of revenue is now civil work, with competition from general contractors.
- **Expected growth after FY30: about 12% a year.**
- **Anchors:**
  - Year-end P/E was 8.9 (FY22), 17.6 (FY23), 31.9 (FY24), 26.3 (FY25) and 15.1 (FY26), per StockAnalysis; Moneyworks4me gives a 23.9 five-year median.
  - Kalpataru Projects is at about 17.4 and NCC at about 13.4.
  - Power Mech is at 19.6–20.5 today.
- **Exit P/E: 18**, at the boundary of the 14–20 and 18–26 bands. The higher-margin mining and upkeep mix argues up; weak cash conversion and the thermal cycle argue down. I keep it below today's level because the cash weakness is not clearing.

## Returns
- **Normal case:** ₹645cr × 18 = ₹11,610cr against ₹7,658cr today (site, 15 Oct). No dilution. Dividend yield 0.06%.
  ((645 × 18) ÷ 7,658)^(1/3.45) − 1 + 0.06% = **12.9% a year**.
- **Bad case:** the margin stays near 11% as mining ramps slowly and Karnataka royalty income shrinks, and thermal orders shift to rivals as BHEL repackages tenders. FY30 revenue is about ₹9,000cr and profit ₹500cr, at exit P/E 14. ((500 × 14) ÷ 7,658)^(1/3.45) − 1 + 0.06% = **−2.5% a year**.
- **Tail risk:** a bank-guarantee call or a large claim on a big fixed-price plant package. It has ₹2,814cr of guarantees outstanding and is newly taking whole plant sections, such as the ₹2,550cr BHEL Singareni package, where it carries design and procurement risk for the first time.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet: 4.** Net debt is only ₹171cr (Screener; company ₹163cr) against ₹699cr of operating profit, with no promoter pledge. But ₹2,814cr of bank guarantees are outstanding, and about ₹218cr is stuck in the water scheme: ₹128cr of work not yet certified and ₹90cr of receivables.
- **Earnings quality: 2.** Only 38% of five-year operating profit came in as cash (₹950cr against ₹2,479cr). FY25's operating cash was ₹1cr. FY26 recovered to ₹387cr, helped by suppliers waiting 493 days.
- **Management: 2.** FY26 revenue and orders were missed, the Q1 margin came in 1.7 points under guide, and mining guidance for FY28 was cut three times in six months. Governance is clean (no pledge, unmodified audit), and the promoters' stake fell from 64% to 58.4% between September 2023 and June 2024 (part of it a QIP) and has been flat since.
- **Moat and runway: 4.** It is the largest independent erector and maintainer of thermal plants, wins 60–65% of the power bids it makes, and has O&M contracts that renew and decades-long mining contracts. But the business is concentrated on thermal power, BHEL and Adani.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
1. **FY26 revenue ₹6,062cr against ₹6,500cr guided; orders ₹7,210cr against ₹10,000cr.** Revenue path set below guidance (₹7,000cr against ₹7,300cr for FY27).
2. **Q1 FY27 margin 10.8% against 12.5% guided; Screener basis 10%.** Margin path rises slowly to 12.5%, not 14%.
3. **Mining revenue guidance for FY28 cut from ₹1,800–1,900cr to ₹1,250cr to ₹1,100–1,200cr in six months.** FY28 mining taken at about ₹900cr.
4. **Only 38% of five-year operating profit turned into cash; FY25 operating cash ₹1cr.** Exit P/E capped at 18; earnings-quality score 2.
5. **Karnataka royalty contract margin cut from 14% to 10–11%; seized-quantity penalties are shrinking.** Bad case assumes further decline.
6. **₹900cr water order on hold; ₹218cr of work and bills stuck awaiting state and central funds.** No recovery assumed in the base.
7. **₹1,563cr battery order cancelled by West Bengal.** Shows order-book fragility; orders haircut.
8. **₹2,814cr of contingent bank guarantees; first whole-plant package (BHEL Singareni ₹2,550cr) adds design and procurement risk.** Named as tail risk.
9. **Concentration: power 54% of revenue; BHEL about ₹5,300cr and Adani about ₹2,400cr of the book.** Growth after FY30 set at 12%.
10. **H2 FY26 profit flattered by 18–20% tax; minority partners take about 10%.** Base uses shareholders' profit at normal tax.
11. **Accident halted a Vedanta site for 3–4 months; erection capacity capped at 14–15 boilers.** Revenue growth held to 11–15%.
12. **Promoter holding fell from 64.05% (Sep 2023) to 58.41% by June 2024 (part of it a QIP) and has been flat since.** No new dilution expected; noted.

## Since the note was written
- **New order (29 Sep):** ₹549cr, five-year O&M contract from Moxie Power (Adani group, Tuticorin), on top of the ₹970cr Vedanta Sakti O&M order on 9 Sep. Orders so far in FY27 total about ₹3,380cr (₹1,864cr in Q1 plus these two), about 28% of the ₹12,000cr target at the half-year mark.
- **Shareholding, September 2026:** promoters unchanged at 58.41%.
- **Q2 FY27 results:** not yet out; Screener has no September quarter. No news found on the Tasra washery commissioning.
