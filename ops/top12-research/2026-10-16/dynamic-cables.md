# Dynamic Cables

## Verdict for the portfolio
A disciplined, high-return cable maker at a fair price, and the best expected return of the five. It earns 26% on capital, holds a 10.5–11% margin and carries almost no debt. But growth is steady, not spectacular:
- volume rose only 5–6% in Q1
- the order book is flat at about ₹811cr
- the new plant is over a year late

On my numbers profit reaches ₹156cr in FY30 (about 16–17% a year). At an exit P/E of 22 (about today's), a buyer earns about **17% a year: good, but short of 25%**. **Not now** at ₹409. It becomes a buy below about ₹380 (20% a year), or if the new plant ramps faster than I assume.

**Where it sits among the five:** best base return (17.4%, ahead of Transrail 14.5%, Pitti 10.5%, Atlanta 2.4% and TD Power −8.8%) and the smallest bad-case loss (−2.4%). Second-cheapest at 22 times profit. Fourth on growth (about 16–17% a year, against 26–28% at Atlanta and TD Power). It is also the smallest company (₹1,983cr) with the thinnest institutional ownership (under 2%).

## Clean profit base
- **Reported (Screener, standalone):** FY26 profit ₹84cr; last four quarters to June 2026 ₹91cr.
- **One-offs:** none of note. Other income is ₹5–7cr a year (interest on the 2024 share-issue money), the tax rate a normal 25%, and there were no exceptional items.
- **Clean base: ₹91cr, last four quarters.**
- **P/E check:** site 21.9, Screener 21.8 (₹1,983cr ÷ ₹91cr). Consistent.

## Profit path FY27–FY30
| Year | Revenue | Operating margin | Profit after tax |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY26 (actual) | ₹1,198cr | 10.9% | ₹84cr |
| FY27 | ₹1,420cr | 10.8% | ₹99cr |
| FY28 | ₹1,650cr | 10.8% | ₹113cr |
| FY29 | ₹1,930cr | 10.8% | ₹134cr |
| FY30 | ₹2,240cr | 10.8% | ₹156cr |

**Drivers.**
- **FY27: +18%, mostly aluminium price.**
  - Q1 revenue rose 33%, but volume only 5–6%. Management says Q2 growth is also "price-led" (29 Sep investor call).
  - The existing plants run at about 85%.
  - Customers book only 2–4 months ahead while metal is dear ("price is king"), so the order book sits at about ₹811cr.
- **FY28: +16%, the Reengus plant.**
  - The ₹40–45cr plant should add ₹280–300cr of sales at full use (6–7 times the money spent) and reach 80–85% use within 12–18 months.
  - Commissioning was promised for Q2 FY26, then March 2026, then September 2026. On 29 September it was "sometime in October", after Navratri.
  - I give it about ₹150cr in FY28 and full use only in FY29.
- **FY29–FY30: about 16–17% a year.**
  - Management's long-run aim is 18–20% a year (its ten-year revenue record is 16% a year on Screener).
  - Industry volume growth is low double digits.
  - High-voltage cables are now 68% of sales (35% in FY19), solar cables are about 20% and growing 25–30%, and exports are 13% with a "vision" of 20%.
  - Reaching ₹2,240cr needs a modest further expansion: management says Reengus has room to expand inside the site, at its usual 6–7 times turnover.
- **Margin 10.8%.** Its "80-10-10" model: 80% materials, 10% other costs, 10–11% operating margin. It has held 10.3–11.4% for nine quarters. 80–90% of orders pass metal prices through. I do not assume it rises; the IR adviser on 29 September spoke of an "8–10% to 11%" range.
- **Below the operating line.** Interest ₹12–16cr (metal-heavy working capital); depreciation ₹14–23cr (the new plant and its electron-beam line); tax 25%.
- **Track record.** Margin promises are kept; plant timelines are not. So the revenue path is below management's 18–20%.
- **Result:** FY30 profit is ₹156cr, about 16.7% a year from FY26 (15.5% from the last four quarters).

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
- **Runway.**
  - Grid and renewable cable demand continues to 2032: a ₹9.15 lakh crore transmission plan and about 45 GW of renewables a year.
  - Utility approvals take years and keep casual rivals out.
  - US licences for low, medium and high-voltage cables give a long replacement market.
  - HTLS conductor and data-centre power cables are options, not yet revenue.
- **Against.**
  - Every cable maker is adding 20–22% capacity.
  - UltraTech (in wires), Adani and Birla (both customers) are entering cables.
  - Demand slows whenever metal is dear.
  - The market in 2030 will likely price **about 14% a year**.
- **Exit P/E: 22, in the 18–26 band for 12–16%.**
  - Middle of the band: 26% return on capital and low debt pull it up; 48% cash conversion, small size and family-heavy management pull it down.
  - **Anchors:** its own five-year P/E was 7–54, median 23.4. Today it is 21.8. Peers on Screener (16 Oct): KEI 43, Polycab 40, Paramount 33. It is far smaller and business-to-business only, so I do not borrow their multiples.

## Returns
Market value used: **₹1,983cr (Screener, 16 Oct)**. No new shares expected (the ₹96.6cr 2024 share issue funds the plant). Dividend yield 0.12%.

- **Normal case:** ₹156cr × 22 = ₹3,432cr. ₹3,432 ÷ ₹1,983 = 1.731. 1.731^(1/3.45) − 1 = 17.2%, plus 0.12% = **17.4% a year**.
  - At an exit P/E of 24 it is 20.3%.
- **Bad case** (Reengus ramps slowly, volume grows about 8%, aluminium falls back as J.P. Morgan forecasts for 2027, and margin slips to 10%):
  - FY30 revenue ₹1,750cr. Profit before tax ₹143cr, profit ₹107cr.
  - Exit P/E one band down: 17 (14–20 band).
  - ₹107cr × 17 = ₹1,819cr. ₹1,819 ÷ ₹1,983 = 0.917. 0.917^(1/3.45) − 1 = −2.5%, plus 0.12% = **−2.4% a year**.
- **Tail risk:** a large private customer (Adani or Birla) moves its cable buying to its own new cable business.
- **Price that works:** ₹1,836cr market value (about ₹379 a share) for 20% a year; ₹1,595cr (about ₹329) for 25%.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet 4/5.** Net debt about ₹15cr at March 2026 (borrowings ₹43cr). Borrowings rose in Q1 only because the same stock cost 20–30% more. It uses about 24% of its bank limits; CRISIL A. Not a 5: ₹208cr of contingent liabilities (mostly bank guarantees) and promoters' personal guarantees on loans.
- **Earnings quality 3/5.** Five-year operating cash ₹210cr against ₹435cr of operating profit (48%), and FY26 free cash only ₹4–5cr. Debtor days improved from 121 (FY24) to 88, but working-capital days rose from 56 to 82. No one-offs.
- **Management 3/5.**
  - Margin delivered for nine quarters, and a clean audit.
  - But the plant is about 15–18 months late.
  - India Ratings withdrew its rating at the company's request (June 2026).
  - The MD's wife, son and daughter hold senior posts; the son's pay was raised in July.
  - The daughter (head of HR) and the GM Quality resigned in September ("regular attrition").
- **Moat and runway 3/5.** Utility, Power Grid, US UL and nuclear-regulator approvals and 80–90% price-pass-through contracts protect the margin. But cables are made by many firms, capacity is rising everywhere, and growth follows project spending.
