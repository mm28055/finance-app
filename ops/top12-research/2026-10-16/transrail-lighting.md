# Transrail Lighting

## Verdict for the portfolio
The cheapest company here: 15 times profit, with the best margins among Indian transmission contractors. But cheap for reasons that have not cleared:
- **Orders:** ₹2,429cr won by 14 October, against a ₹10,000cr-plus target for FY27.
- **A tax search:** the income-tax department searched it in March 2026.
- **A related-company loan:** ₹79cr lent to a related company, not shown as repaid.
- **A share sale:** a ₹600cr share sale has been approved.

On my numbers a buyer earns about 15% a year: profit grows about 14% a year, and the P/E stays at 15. That is **not enough for 25%**. If the loan and tax questions clear and the market pays 18, it reaches about 21%. **Not now; re-check after the Q2 accounts in November.**

**Where it sits among the five:**
- Lowest P/E of the five (15, against Dynamic 22, Pitti 31, Atlanta 61, TD Power 87).
- Slowest growth I expect (about 14–15% a year).
- Weakest governance record and the weakest cash conversion after TD Power.
- Second-best base return (14.5%), behind Dynamic (17%). The only one whose return rises sharply if a specific doubt clears.

## Clean profit base
- **Reported:** FY26 profit ₹404cr (company "operational" ₹421cr). Last four quarters to June 2026: ₹405cr (Screener, consolidated).
- **Removed:** about ₹6cr after tax of interest booked on the Burberry Infra loan but not received. Interest owed rose from ₹16.5cr to ₹25.1cr in FY26 (annual report). **Clean base: ₹399cr, last four quarters.**
- **Screener's annual figures:** they show a 14% FY26 operating margin and ₹339cr of interest. Its own quarters add up to ₹817cr of operating profit and ₹219cr of interest (company: 11.9%). Screener's annual view shifts about ₹120cr of charges between the two lines; profit is the same. I use the quarterly (company) basis.
- **P/E check:** site 15.0, Screener 15.0 (₹6,289cr ÷ ₹405cr), clean 15.8. Consistent.

**Drivers.**
- **Revenue.**
  - **FY27: +15%, not the guided 20%.** Q1 grew 5% (₹1,736cr). Overseas revenue fell to about ₹600cr from about ₹1,000cr, and 20% would need about ₹2,200cr a quarter from here.
  - **Order book:** ₹16,035cr in June, about 2.3 years of sales, covers FY27.
  - **FY28–FY30: about 14% a year.** That needs about ₹9,000–11,000cr of wins a year. FY26 orders were ₹8,520cr against ₹10,000cr promised, and FY27 has ₹2,429cr won plus ₹409cr as lowest bidder by 14 October. About ₹20,000cr of bids were due for decision in Q2–Q3, at a 10–15% expected win rate.
  - **Market:** Indian line tendering is expected at ₹90,000cr–1 lakh crore in FY27 (Skipper, in the sector note), so demand exists. Converting it is the question.
- **Capacity.** Not the limit. Tower capacity rose to 184,400 tonnes (30 September) and conductor capacity to 40,800 km a year (+70%, 21 September); ₹203cr of site equipment is mostly bought this year.
- **Margin 11.5%.** Management guides "about 11%-plus", and Q1 was 11.7%. Only 30–35% of contracts pass on metal costs, so I do not assume it rises.
- **Below the operating line.**
  - Interest stays at about 3.1% of sales (₹255cr to ₹355cr), because the business runs on letters of credit and about ₹1,200cr of supplier credit.
  - Depreciation ₹80–110cr; other income ₹40cr; tax 26% (Q1 25%).
- **QIP.** If the approved ₹600cr is raised, about 10% more shares at today's price. Repaying 9% working-capital debt saves about ₹54cr before tax, ₹40cr after, giving ₹721cr in FY30. I include both the new shares and the saving in the base, because shareholders approved it on 11 September and management described it as aimed at institutions.
- **Track record.** FY26 revenue +30% against a 25% guide; FY26 orders missed by 15%; Q1 FY27 revenue far behind. I haircut the 20% revenue guide to 14–15%.
- **Result:** FY30 profit is ₹681cr before the QIP (15.6% a year from FY26), and ₹721cr with it.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
- **Runway.** The National Electricity Plan adds about 1.9 lakh circuit-km of lines and ₹9.15 lakh crore of spending to 2032. Overseas, there are lines in Africa and the Middle East and Australian monopoles; new areas include cooling towers (Capital Turnkey), and data-centre and battery-storage contracting. In 2030 the market will likely expect **about 12% a year** from a tendered contractor of this size.
- **Exit P/E: 15, the same as today**, low in the 14–20 band for 8–12% growth.
  - Lower half because cash conversion was 40% over five years, it is a tender business with limited price clauses, and governance questions are open.
  - **Peers (Screener, 16 Oct):** KEC 15.6, Kalpataru 21.1, Techno Electric 25.6, Skipper 28.2.
  - **Own history:** listed December 2024 at about 32 times; median 21; low 13.
- **Re-rating not assumed.** It needs the Burberry loan repaid, the tax search to end without a demand, and orders to catch up. Each can be checked in the Q2 accounts (November) and Q3 orders. If all three clear, 18 is reasonable (Kalpataru trades at 21 with lower margins).

## Returns
Market value used: **₹6,289cr (Screener, 16 Oct)**. New shares d = 0.10 (the approved ₹600cr QIP at about today's price). Dividend yield 0.65%.

- **Normal case:** ₹721cr × 15 = ₹10,815cr. ₹10,815 ÷ (₹6,289 × 1.10) = 1.563. 1.563^(1/3.45) − 1 = 13.8%, plus 0.65% = **14.5% a year**.
  - Without the QIP (d = 0, profit ₹681cr): 15.7%.
  - With the re-rating to 18: 20.7%.
- **Bad case** (orders stay thin, so revenue grows only 8% a year to ₹9,350cr; margin 10.5%; interest ₹340cr):
  - FY30 profit about ₹470cr including the QIP saving.
  - Exit P/E one band down: 12 (10–15 band).
  - ₹470cr × 12 = ₹5,640cr. ₹5,640 ÷ ₹6,918 = 0.815. 0.815^(1/3.45) − 1 = −5.7%, plus 0.65% = **−5.1% a year**.
- **Tail risk:** the March 2026 income-tax search leads to a large demand or prosecution, or the ₹104cr owed by Burberry Infra (loan plus interest) is written off.
- **Price that works:** ₹5,341cr market value (about ₹397 a share) for 20% a year without a re-rating; ₹4,636cr (about ₹345) for 25%.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet 3/5.** Company net debt ₹548cr in June, about 0.7× a year's operating profit. It was ₹267cr in March, and Screener showed ₹85cr of net cash. It also carries about ₹1,200cr of trade acceptances, bank limits of about ₹7,500cr, and disputed indirect tax of ₹133.8cr. Rated AA− by CRISIL and India Ratings (upgraded in August).
- **Earnings quality 2/5.** Five-year operating cash ₹1,154cr against ₹2,887cr of Screener operating profit (40%). FY26 was much better (₹643cr, 68–79% depending on basis). Inventory days rose from 62 to 91 in two years. Some interest income is accrued but not received (Burberry).
- **Management 2/5.**
  - Beat on FY26 revenue and margin, but missed the order target and Q1 revenue.
  - The tax search was not mentioned on either call.
  - On the August call it said ₹30cr of the related loan was received last year, while the annual report shows the loan unchanged.
  - The deputy MD left on 31 July 2026 and an independent director on 9 August 2026.
- **Moat and runway 3/5.** Its own tower and conductor plants and its 765 kV/HVDC record give it some of the best margins among Indian line contractors (11–12% against Kalpataru's 9% and KEC's 8%). But it wins work by open tender, is much smaller than KEC and Kalpataru, and road builders are entering.
