# Sudarshan Chemicals

## Verdict for the portfolio
Not now. The Heubach turnaround is real and ahead of plan, but at ₹9,113cr the market already pays about 13 times the profit I expect in FY30 (₹690cr), so a normal exit P/E for a 3–4%-a-year pigment market gives only about 10% a year. Even full delivery of management's FY29 plan earns about 20% a year, and the governance and liability list (73% of revenue audited by others, 8.2% promoter stake, a ₹264cr clean-up provision, an unquantified European restructuring charge) argues against paying up.

## Clean profit base
- **Reported:** Screener TTM to June 2026 shows profit of ₹88cr (₹89cr in the headline), but profit attributable to Sudarshan's own shareholders is only about ₹72cr (TTM EPS ₹9.05 × 7.96cr shares). FY26 attributable profit was about ₹23cr (EPS ₹2.84). Tax took 62% of TTM pre-tax profit because loss-making European entities get no tax credit.
- **What I removed or added back (TTM, pre-tax ₹232cr):** removed the Q4 FY26 non-cash acquisition-accounting credit (−₹37cr) and the one-time depreciation cut in Q4 FY26 (₹50cr booked against a ~₹95cr run-rate, −₹45cr); added back the ₹82cr of stock-related overhead released in Q4 FY26 and the ₹36cr of integration costs inside Q1 FY27 operating costs; removed the ₹18cr Q1 FY27 stock gain. That gives about ₹250cr pre-tax; at a normal 30% tax and after about ₹22cr to minority holders (30% of the listed Indian subsidiary), **clean TTM profit is roughly ₹155cr**. This is rough: Q3 FY26 also carried stock-related charges the company did not size.
- **P/E check:** site 89.7 (₹9,113cr ÷ ~₹102cr) and Screener 89.0 agree, but both use total profit before minorities. On attributable profit the trailing P/E is about 125; on my clean ₹155cr it is about 59. None of these is useful for a business whose profit is being rebuilt, so the case is built from operating profit (EBITDA) forward, as instructed.

Drivers:
- **Operating profit.** Latest call (14 Aug 2026): business EBITDA ₹247cr in Q1 (9.4%), acquired group ₹128cr in one quarter against a €35m (~₹370cr) full-year guide; FY27 guide (revenue ₹9,800–10,200cr, EBITDA ~₹800cr) kept, to be revisited after Q2; FY29 target ₹12,000–14,000cr revenue and ₹1,400–1,500cr EBITDA, "a substantial portion" from cost cuts. I take FY27 at ₹900cr (above the guide because of Q1, below a straight ×4 because Q3 is seasonally weak and integration costs continue), then build to ₹1,330cr in FY30: legacy India business ~₹600cr (15% on ~₹4,000cr), acquired group ~€71m (~₹745cr, a 20–25% haircut to the €90–100m acquisition target and net of VP4's €4m), RIECO ~₹20cr, less ~₹50cr group costs. That is management's FY29 number one year late and ~10% short. Haircut reason: the turnaround is 18 months old, the legacy business missed in FY26 (flat revenue against growth), US/European paint demand is weak and the acquired group's "high single digit / low double digit" margin target has not been shown yet.
- **Depreciation:** ~₹370cr a year (Q1 FY27 ₹93cr), flat because capex (₹150–250cr a year, no volume capex needed to FY29) roughly equals it.
- **Interest:** net interest cost (interest less other income) was about ₹83cr TTM. The €76.5m (~₹790cr) VP4 sale prepays acquisition debt; with ~₹400cr a year of free cash the group should be net cash by FY28–FY29, so net interest moves from −₹40cr (FY27) to about +₹25cr (FY30).
- **Tax:** 30% in FY27 falling to 27% by FY30 as European entities turn profitable. **Minorities:** ₹24–33cr a year.
- **Excluded from clean profit but real cash:** the Works Council restructuring charge (size due with Q2), the ₹264cr decommissioning provision (₹388cr undiscounted over ~8 years).
- **Growth rate:** about 54% a year from the clean ₹155cr base, but that is recovery, not compounding; from FY27 to FY30 it is about 29% a year.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
By March 2030 the repair is done: debt gone, margins near 11%, value capture largely booked. What is left is a pigment market growing 3–4% a year (management's own figure), share regained from Heubach's lost customers, high-performance and specialty grades, and the low-cost Asian footprint. The market will expect roughly 8–12% profit growth for FY31–FY33, which the guide table prices at 14–20. Upper half is earned only partly: the group should be net cash, but return on capital will be mid-teens, not high, and the runway is a slow market. Anchors: the stock's own five-year median P/E is about 29 (MoneyWorks4Me), but that was the smaller, 13–15%-margin Indian business; its five-year median EV/EBITDA is about 16 against about 10 times my FY27 EBITDA today. I use **18**. Today's 90 is a trailing-profit artefact, so a lower exit P/E is not a de-rating of the business, just the end of the recovery premium.

## Returns
Market value ₹9,112.89cr (site, 15 Oct; Screener ₹9,036cr on 16 Oct). No dilution expected (the promoter's 9.8 lakh warrants converted in June 2026). Dividend yield 0.45% (Screener).
- **Normal case:** ₹690cr × 18 = ₹12,420cr. (12,420 ÷ 9,113)^(1/3.45) − 1 + 0.45% = **9.8% a year**.
- At 20× it is 13.2%; on management's full plan (FY30 EBITDA ~₹1,550cr, profit ~₹840cr) at 20× it is 19.8%. To earn 25% a year on my FY30 profit the exit P/E would have to be about 28.
- **Bad case:** the acquired group's margin stalls near 7% and revenue grows ~3% a year as Western paint and plastics demand stays weak; FY30 EBITDA ~₹1,050cr, profit ~₹465cr; exit P/E one band down at 14. ₹465cr × 14 = ₹6,510cr → **−8.8% a year**.
- **Tail risk (separate):** a large European restructuring or site clean-up bill, or a problem found in the 57 subsidiaries audited by other firms.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet 3/5:** net debt ₹531cr in June (company) against ~₹750cr of trailing EBITDA, heading to net cash after VP4; but ₹264cr of decommissioning provisions, ₹189cr of tax disputes and an unquantified restructuring charge sit outside that figure.
- **Earnings quality 3/5:** ₹1,073cr of operating cash on ₹1,795cr of operating profit over FY22–FY26 (60%); profit is full of acquisition-accounting credits, stock-overhead swings and integration costs, and stock was 179 days of sales in FY26.
- **Management 3/5:** beat on the acquired group (₹128cr in Q1 vs a ~₹370cr full-year guide; net debt ₹922cr → ₹531cr), but the legacy business missed in FY26; the promoter group owns 8.2% (35.8% in Sep 2023), the CMD was paid ₹16.1cr (39% of FY26 profit), the company secretary resigned in September and GST officials searched offices in October.
- **Moat and runway 3/5:** world no. 2 in pigments, 1,600 products, approvals that take 1–5 years and 50–60% of assets in low-cost Asia; but approvals protect volume not price (it had to drop its insolvency surcharge), and the market grows only 3–4% a year.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
- Other auditors cover 57 subsidiaries and ₹7,127cr of revenue (~73%) → tail risk, and a reason to stay in the lower half of the P/E band.
- ₹264cr decommissioning provision (₹388cr undiscounted), ₹189cr tax disputes → treated as cash claims outside clean profit; part of why I give the balance sheet 3, not 4.
- Works Council restructuring charge not yet sized → excluded from clean profit, flagged in tail risk and next checks.
- Promoters at 8.2%, down from 35.8% → management score 3; no premium P/E.
- CMD pay ₹16.1cr, 39% of FY26 profit → management score.
- FY26 profit ₹41cr on ₹9,787cr revenue; ROCE 5% → the case is built on recovery to ~11% margin, haircut versus the guide.
- Cash conversion 60% over five years; 179 inventory days → earnings quality 3.
- Q1 FY27 profit includes ₹18cr stock gain and excludes nothing for ₹36cr integration costs; Q4 FY26 had a ₹37cr accounting credit → all adjusted in the clean base.
- Guidance kept despite Q1; an analyst read underlying Q1 volume as falling → my FY27 is only modestly above the guide.
- Legacy business revenue flat in FY26 → legacy grows 8% a year in my path, not the 12–13% management mentioned.
- Market grows 3–4% a year; inks declining; US/European paint weak → exit P/E 18.
- RIECO lost money in Q1 → given only ~₹20cr EBITDA by FY30.
- GST search 1–6 Oct 2026 (company expects no material impact) → noted; no adjustment.
- Dividend payout 176% of FY26 profit → no issue, but the 0.45% yield is all the cash return there is.
