# Sharda Cropchem

## Verdict for the portfolio
Not now, but close. A debt-free owner of 3,016 crop-protection registrations, with ₹767cr of cash and a 2% dividend, priced at about 10 times clean profit. On a mid-cycle margin, profit grows only about 8% a year to roughly ₹870cr by FY30 (rising amortisation of registration spending eats part of the sales growth), and at an exit P/E of 13 that gives about 19% a year: a fair, fairly safe return, but short of 25%. The low P/E is partly a reason that will not clear (profit swings with the euro and the farm-chemical cycle, everything is made in China, and profit runs well ahead of free cash).

## Clean profit base
- **Reported:** FY26 profit ₹681cr; trailing four quarters to June 2026 ₹626cr (₹74cr + ₹145cr + ₹319cr + ₹88cr).
- **Currency swings removed:** Sharda books unrealised gains and losses on foreign-currency receivables and payables each quarter. Q1 FY26 carried a ₹73cr gain (euro up ~10%); Q4 FY26 a ₹26cr loss; Q1 FY27 a ₹7.5cr gain. Screener's operating profit includes these; the company's own EBITDA does not. Trailing operating profit is ₹1,047cr on Screener against ₹1,076cr on the company's basis (₹1,040cr FY26 − ₹142cr Q1 FY26 + ₹178cr Q1 FY27), so the trailing year carries about ₹29cr of net currency loss. Adding it back after 20% tax gives **clean TTM ≈ ₹650cr**. FY26 on the same basis is about ₹665cr (reported ₹681cr less ~₹15cr of net gain after tax).
- **Kept:** other income of ~₹107cr in the trailing year, of which roughly ₹45–50cr is interest on ₹700–767cr of cash. It is about 6% of pre-tax profit and the cash is part of how the business runs (it pays Chinese suppliers and funds registrations from it), so I keep it.
- **P/E check:** site 10.39 (₹6,532cr ÷ ₹626cr = 10.4) and Screener 10.5 agree. On the clean ₹650cr it is 10.0.

Drivers:
- **Latest call (30 Jul 2026, Q1 FY27):** FY27 revenue +10–15%, volume "almost 5–10%", gross margin 35–37%, operating margin 18–20%, tax 18–20%, amortisation ₹370–375cr (₹325cr in FY26), registration spending "500 crores, 550 crores, or 480 crores" (₹273cr already in Q1 on one-off data payments). Q1 revenue +9% = volume −1.6%, currency +12.7%, mix −2.1%; Europe fell (heatwave destocking) but its gross margin rose to 44.2%; LatAm grew but its margin fell to 16.9% from 28%. "The last month of Q2 has been better than the entirety of Q1."
- **Track record:** FY26 delivered 22% revenue growth, but 10.3 points was currency and price/mix was −1.8%. The FY27 revenue guide was 15–20% on the Q3 FY26 call, 10–15% in May; the volume guide was ~15% in May and 5–10% in July. Prices are "stabilised and slowly improving" after the FY24 slump. So I take the low end of the revenue guide (+10%) and the low end of the margin guide.
- **Volume × price:** +10% a year in rupees = ~5% volume (management says it has under 5% of the global market; 1,027 registrations pending), ~2% price and mix, and ~2–3% from a slowly weakening rupee (it sells in euros, dollars and LatAm currencies).
- **Margin:** 18.5% throughout. That is the mid-cycle level: Screener's 12-year average is ~18% (17–23% in FY15–FY22, 10% in the FY24 slump, 20% in FY26), and FY26's 19.7% was helped by a 10% stronger euro against the dollar it buys in. I use mid-cycle because this is a cyclical business (FY24 profit fell to ₹32cr).
- **Below the line:** amortisation ₹372cr → ₹400cr → ₹425cr → ₹450cr as ~₹500cr a year of registration spending works through; other income ₹100–115cr; interest ~₹5cr; tax 20%.
- **Growth rate:** about 8% a year from the clean TTM ₹650cr (7% a year from clean FY26). The note's own estimate is 10% (range 5–15%); I am lower because amortisation grows faster than sales.

## Growth after FY30 and the exit P/E
In March 2030 the market will expect roughly 8–10% a year for FY31–FY33: 5% global share in a market growing with food demand, a 1,000-plus pending registration pipeline, and rupee depreciation. That is the 14–20 band. Lower-half features dominate: earnings swing with the euro and the farm-chemical cycle (profit ₹342cr in FY23, ₹32cr in FY24, ₹681cr in FY26); every product is made under contract, mainly in China; and profit runs ahead of free cash (five years of free cash ₹555cr against ₹1,708cr of profit, because registration spending runs above amortisation and receivables sit at 166 days). Upper-half features: no debt, ₹767cr of cash, 30% return on capital in FY26.

The stock's own year-end P/E was 12–19 in normal years (12.0 FY15, 23.1 FY16, 18.1 FY17, 19.1 FY18, 11.3 FY21, 15.9 FY22, 12.9 FY23, 16.9 FY25; Pocketful), and the price was ₹1,298 within the last year, about 18 times clean profit. Today's ~10 has two parts. One should clear: the headline Q1 profit fall of 38% (all currency base effect; clean pre-tax profit rose 16%), the European heatwave destocking and the cut volume guide. One will not: the cycle, the China dependence, the currency swings and the gap between profit and free cash. I therefore allow only a partial re-rating, to **13**: the bottom of the band less a trim for the cash gap, and the low end of the stock's own range. At 14 the return is 21.8%; if the P/E stays at today's ~10 it is 11.9%.

## Returns
Market value ₹6,532.41cr (site, 15 Oct; Screener ₹6,550cr on 16 Oct). No dilution: no equity raised in over ten years, promoters steady at 74.82%. Dividend yield 2.0%: ₹15 a share for FY26 (₹6 interim + ₹9 final) on ₹724; the payout has been 16–27% of profit in normal years (₹6, ₹6, ₹3, ₹9, ₹15 for FY22–FY26).
- **Normal case:** ₹870cr × 13 = ₹11,310cr. (11,310 ÷ 6,532)^(1/3.45) − 1 + 2.0% = **19.2% a year**.
- **Bad case:** the euro weakens and the farm-chemical cycle turns down again in FY29–FY30 (LatAm prices keep falling, Europe destocks again); revenue grows 5–8% a year and the margin slides to 15.5% by FY30, close to FY23's 16%; FY30 profit ~₹552cr; exit P/E one band down at 10 (its FY21 trough was 11). ₹552cr × 10 = ₹5,520cr → **−2.8% a year** (dividend included).
- **Tail risk (separate):** a break in Chinese supply (export controls or sanctions) or European regulators withdrawing approval for a large older molecule, forcing a write-off of registrations.

## Quality
- **Balance sheet 5/5:** no borrowings at March 2026, ₹767cr of cash and liquid investments at June, and registration spending paid from cash flow.
- **Earnings quality 3/5:** 66% of five years' operating profit came in as operating cash (₹2,196cr of ₹3,332cr); receivables at 166 days and inventory at 121 days; quarterly profit swings by up to ₹73cr on unrealised currency moves; FY24 profit collapsed to ₹32cr on an inventory glut.
- **Management 3/5:** clean governance (family 74.8% steady, no pledges, no dilution, rising dividends, 30% return on capital), but the FY27 revenue guide went from 15–20% to 10–15% and the volume guide from ~15% to 5–10% within two quarters, and the chairman declines to give detail ("I am not doing all these detailed calculations").
- **Moat and runway 3/5:** registrations in its own name keep competitors out (the chairman says 80–90% of cost increases are passed on), but the library grew only from ~2,700 in FY22 to 3,016, with fewer than 100 net additions in three years, and it owns no plants or patents.

## Worst facts and how the case deals with them
- Q1 FY27 volume −1.6%; volume guide cut from ~15% to 5–10% → revenue +10% a year, low end of guide.
- FY27 revenue guide cut from 15–20% (Q3 FY26 call) to 10–15% → low end used.
- FY26's 22% growth included 10.3 points of currency; price/mix −1.8% → margin held at mid-cycle 18.5%, not FY26's 19.7%.
- Reported profit swings with currency (₹73cr gain Q1 FY26, ₹26cr loss Q4 FY26) → removed from the base; lower-half P/E.
- Fewer than 100 net new registrations in three years; only 5 added in Q1 FY27 against ₹273cr spent → growth held to ~8%; moat score 3.
- Registration spending rising to ₹480–550cr; amortisation ₹325cr → ₹450cr by FY30 → built into the path.
- Free cash only ₹555cr over five years against ₹1,708cr of profit → exit P/E trimmed to 13.
- All products made under contract, mainly in China → tail risk.
- LatAm gross margin fell to 16.9% from 28% → bad case.
- FY24 profit fell to ₹32cr in the post-COVID inventory glut → cyclical, mid-cycle margin, bad case.
- Receivables at 166 days → earnings quality 3.
- Foreign funds cut their stake from 5.48% (Jun 2025) to 3.27% (Sep 2026); price down 44% from its ₹1,298 high → noted; partly why the P/E is low.
- El Niño expected to last to March 2027 → part of the bad case.
