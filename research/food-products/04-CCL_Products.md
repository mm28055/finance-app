# CCL Products (India) (CCL, ISIN INE421D01022)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Consolidated figures (Screener) unless marked.

## Business and segments
- One of the world's largest private-label instant-coffee makers (B2B): spray-dried, agglomerated and freeze-dried coffee plus roasted blends, sold to global retailers, coffee brands and foodservice in 110+ countries. Exports are ~87% of revenue; the top five customers are only ~15% of revenue (FY26). [ICRA Jul-26]
- Capacity ~77,000 t a year (up from 55,000 t in FY23) across India (Duggirala, Kuppam) and Vietnam (36,000 t after expansion, operational in FY26); a Swiss trading arm. The parent company's capacity has been flat for 10+ years, and all new capacity sits in subsidiaries. [ICRA Jul-26] [AR FY26] [Q1 call]
- Utilisation ~65% in FY26 (ICRA says ~60%) and 65–70% in Q1 FY27; freeze-dried runs above average. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [ICRA Jul-26]
- B2C (~10% of FY26 sales): the "Continental" coffee brand in India is #3 nationally (#2 in some regions and platforms), with >6% urban share in South India, double-digit shares in Reliance and DMart, and high single digits on quick commerce. Also Percol in the UK (~₹25–30 cr revenue), and "Malgudi" South-Indian snacks in pilot. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [ICRA Jul-26]
- Domestic gross turnover FY26 ~₹650 cr (branded ~₹440 cr); Q1 FY27 ~₹180 cr (branded ~₹125 cr, +26%). Online is ~20–25% of branded sales. Retail reach 1.7 lakh+ outlets. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [Screener]
- Business model: cost-plus. Green coffee is bought back-to-back against customer contracts, so profit is managed per kilo (EBITDA/kg), not as a percentage of sales. [Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 1,056 → Sep-25 1,127 → Dec-25 1,051 → Mar-26 1,224 → Jun-26 1,200. Operating profit 159 → 197 → 185 → 192 → 194. OPM 15% → 18% → 18% → 16% → 16%. Net profit 72 → 101 → 100 → 115 → 117. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹1,203.6 cr (+13.8%) on ~20% volume growth (lower green-coffee prices reduced value growth); EBITDA ₹196.7 cr (+21.8%); PBT ₹129 cr (+37%); PAT ₹116.9 cr (+61%); EBITDA/kg ~₹140. [Q1 call]
- Annual revenue (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 1,462 → 2,071 → 2,654 → 3,106 → 4,457. OPM 23% → 19% → 17% → 18% → 16% (the fall is "optical" — coffee-price inflation raises revenue). Net profit 204 → 284 → 250 → 310 → 388. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- FY26: revenue +43% (18–20% volume plus 20–25% coffee-price pass-through); EBITDA ₹741 cr (+32%); PAT ₹388 cr (+25%). EBITDA/kg rose from ~₹125–130 to ~₹140, driven by more freeze-dried coffee (earning 30–40% more EBITDA/kg than spray-dried) and small packs (~20% of volume, from ~15%). [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [ICRA Jul-26]
- ROCE 15.8%, ROE 18%. Valuation: market cap ₹13,970 cr, P/E 32.3. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **FY27 volume and EBITDA:** ~15% volume and ~15% EBITDA growth (Q4; earlier guidance was 10–20% volume). Q1 delivered 20% volume and 22% EBITDA, but management "not upgrading" because of coffee volatility. **Kept; Q1 ahead.** ICRA independently expects only 10–12% volume growth. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [ICRA Jul-26]
- **EBITDA/kg:** sustain ~₹135–140 through FY27 ("sacrosanct"). More freeze-dried could lower it slightly, offset by small packs and direct clients. **Kept.** [Q1 call]
- **B2C:** 25% volume growth (Q4); 25–30% and ₹550–600 cr domestic branded revenue in FY27 (Q1); EBITDA held at ~4–6% for 3–4 years (profits reinvested); Percol UK to ~₹100 cr in 2–3 years. **Raised slightly.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Debt:** Q4 guided ~₹1,100–1,200 cr gross debt at FY27-end; Q1 targets ~₹1,000 cr gross and ~₹800 cr net, with ~₹200 cr of term-loan repayment in FY27, ~₹200 cr in FY28 and the rest after. **Improved.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Capex:** Q4 said "no planned big capex" for two years (maintenance ₹25–35 cr); Q1 gave ₹25–50 cr for FY27. Utilisation path ~65% → 72–73% (FY27) → 80–85% (FY28). New capacity is considered above 75% utilisation and needed at 85–90%, as a brownfield project or tie-up taking 9–12 months. **Kept.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Interest ~₹100 cr and tax ~17% for FY27. [Q4 call]
- Long-term: 15% volume growth for "next 3, 4 years"; ~10–11% share of the global contract instant-coffee market at 77,000 t, with room to 1–1.2 lakh t. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- The big spending phase ended in FY26: Vietnam freeze-dried and spray-dried expansion to 36,000 t and Indian freeze-dried additions. CWIP fell from ₹450 cr (FY25) to ₹3 cr (FY26). [AR FY26] [Screener]
- FY27 capex only ₹25–50 cr (upgrades). [Q1 call]
- In the past it bought outside capacity (2021, during COVID) rather than lose growth, and may do a tie-up again if freeze-dried demand runs ahead. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 655 → 920 → 1,622 → 1,815 → 1,324. Gross debt peaked at ~₹1,950 cr (Dec-24) and was ₹1,268 cr at Mar-26 (term loans ₹517 cr, working capital ₹751 cr). Net debt ₹1,073 cr (Mar-26) → ₹963 cr (Jun-26). Net debt/EBITDA 1.45x (from 3.1x). [Screener] [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 116 → 173 → 55 → 290 → 858; FCF -74 → -159 → -458 → -128 → 788. The CFO warns the FY26 figure was a "multi-year correction" and will not repeat. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Cash conversion cycle 166 days (FY26) vs 246 (FY25); inventory days 131 vs 208. Green-coffee stock is typically 2.5–3 months. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Dividend payout ~20%; ESOP allotments continue. [Screener] [BSE filings, Sep 2026]
- Rating: ICRA assigned AA (Stable)/A1+ on ₹1,200 cr of bank lines (30 Jul 2026), expecting TD/OPBDITA of ~1.7x and interest cover of 6.6x in FY27. [ICRA Jul-26]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and cost: roughly 10–11% of the global contract instant-coffee market; plants in both India and Vietnam allow flexible sourcing (Robusta from Vietnam, Arabica and Robusta from India/Brazil). [Q1 call] [ICRA Jul-26]
- Pass-through: the cost-plus model protected EBITDA/kg through the 2024–25 coffee-price spike. EBITDA grew faster than volume in FY26 (32% vs ~19%). [Q4 call]
- Customer stickiness: long-term freeze-dried contracts are increasing as prices stabilise. [Q4 call]
- Brand building in India: distributor and retailer margins are now at FMCG-major levels (retailer ~10%, distributor 5–6%); 70% of branded sales are cash-and-carry; prices on some platforms are now above the market leaders. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- The main input is green coffee (Robusta mainly, plus Arabica); plus packaging (glass, laminates) and energy. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Q4 FY26: prices "stable", and a good Brazil crop could soften them. Q1 FY27: the range is ~3,300–3,800 (Robusta, per tonne as quoted), with volatility from El Niño risk to Vietnam's Nov–Dec harvest. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Cheaper coffee lowers revenue (value growth < volume growth) but not EBITDA/kg, and it reduces working capital and interest. [Q1 call]
- Freight and energy: Middle East conflict costs are partly absorbed on CIF contracts (~30% of exports; 70% are FOB). Packaging and logistics inflation hit standalone (India) small-pack margins in Q1 FY27. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Currency: naturally hedged (imports and exports), with a forex policy covering the net exposure. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Coffee volatility and working capital:** a renewed price spike would tie up cash again. FY24 free cash flow was -₹458 cr. [Screener]
- **Capacity headroom:** at 15% growth, utilisation reaches 80–85% by FY28 with no capex booked; a late decision could cap growth, while an early one risks idle freeze-dried plants (24/7 high-capex plants). [Q1 call]
- **Margin optics:** subsidiaries versus standalone profits swing quarter to quarter because production is centrally allocated. The standalone company showed weaker Q1 FY27 margins on packaging and logistics costs. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **B2C investment** keeps branded EBITDA at ~4–6% for 3–4 years; adjacent categories (snacks) are unproven. [Q1 call]
- **Peer comparison:** an analyst noted a listed peer earns ₹160–170/kg against CCL's ~₹137–140; management declined to comment. [Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities (standalone):** income-tax disputes ₹38.8 cr (₹28.8 cr paid under protest), bank guarantees ₹19.3 cr. The auditor's report is clean (only key audit matter: revenue recognition), with no qualification or emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]
- **Ownership:** promoter holding 46.14% (below 50%) and steady; no pledge noted; FIIs rising (10.2% → 12.07% over 15 months). [Screener]
- **Tariffs:** the US is a smaller market for CCL than Europe/CIS; no specific tariff hit was cited, but logistics disruption from the Gulf conflict continues. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Net debt fell below ₹1,000 cr, and the FY27 gross-debt target was tightened from ~₹1,100–1,200 cr to ~₹1,000 cr.
- Management opened the door to acquisitions (B2C, "leverage our omnichannel distribution") once deleveraging is done; in Q4 it had been "evaluate if it comes".
- B2C guidance was lifted to 25–30% volume with a ₹550–600 cr domestic branded target; Malgudi snacks moved from pilot to a wider rollout.
- Coffee outlook moved from "could further soften" (Q4) to "volatile" (El Niño risk to Vietnam).
- Standalone margin weakness from packaging and logistics costs was newly flagged.

## My read
The business is improving. Volumes grow 15–20%, EBITDA/kg is up about ₹10–15 in two years on a freeze-dried and small-pack mix, and the balance sheet has been repaired (net debt/EBITDA ~1.3x, AA rating). The Indian brand is scaling but deliberately kept at low margin. At ~32x earnings, the market is paying for steady mid-teens profit growth, which the cost-plus model makes fairly predictable. The view would improve if volumes stay near 20% and B2C reaches ₹550 cr+. It would worsen if EBITDA/kg slips below ₹130 as more freeze-dried and lower-margin traders come in, or if a new coffee-price spike pushes debt back up.
