# Godrej Agrovet (GODREJAGRO, ISIN INE850D01014)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Consolidated figures (Screener) unless marked.

## Business and segments
- Diversified agri-business of the Godrej group. Segments (Q1 FY27 revenue): [IP Q1FY27]
  - **Animal Nutrition (India)** — ₹1,302 cr, 402,257 t, +12.6%. Cattle feed ~55–56% of volume; fish feed ~7%, growing 20%+; broiler feed being cut back by choice.
  - **Oil Palm** — ₹591 cr, +28.9%. India's largest domestic producer of crude palm oil (CPO) and palm kernel oil (PKO) from ~80,000 ha under contract farming.
  - **Crop Care** — ₹271 cr, -17.2%; heavily dependent on its in-house cotton herbicide (Hitweed).
  - **Astec LifeSciences** (67% owned, listed) — agrochemical active ingredients and contract manufacturing (CDMO); ₹83 cr.
  - **Creamline Dairy** (CDPL, "Godrej Jersey", 99.78% owned) — ₹465 cr.
  - **Godrej Foods** — Yummiez frozen foods and Real Good Chicken; ₹188 cr.
  - **ACI Godrej** — Bangladesh feed JV (volume 109,191 t, +14.5%).
- Animal feed was 46% of FY26 revenue (48% in FY25), down from 80% in FY12. [Screener] [CRISIL Jul-26]
- Strategy reset (Q1 FY27 deck), capital allocated by bucket: [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call]
  - **Invest:** cattle feed, oil palm, crop care, branded foods.
  - **Transform:** Creamline, ACI, Astec.
  - **Restrict:** broiler feed, Real Good Chicken B2B.
  - **Exit:** live-bird trading.
  - **Strategic review:** shrimp feed, seeds and cattle genetics.
- Oil palm scale: ~637,000 t of fresh fruit bunches (FFB) processed a year; 17,000 ha added in FY26 (a record); plan to reach ~150,000 ha (new areas in Telangana and the Northeast); ~50% of trees are still juvenile (0–4 years), so yields rise as they mature. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **Consolidated FY27:** "early double-digit" revenue growth and "mid-teens" PBT growth (Q4). Q1: revenue +10% but PBT -8%. Guidance softened to "double-digit" PBT growth, with the range to be clarified by the end of Q2. **Cut / under review.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Crop Care:** Q4 called FY27 a "year of recovery", "very high double-digit" from Q2 onward. Q1 revenue -17% and result -38% after the driest June in about a century (40% rain deficit). A view will come by the end of September; segment EBIT margin to hold at ~26–27%. **At risk.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **New crop products:** Ashitaka (maize herbicide) and Takai (paddy insecticide) were expected to reach 16–18% of crop-care sales (Q4); they reached ~18–20% of Q1 sales. **Ahead.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Animal Nutrition:** double-digit revenue growth (Q4). Q1 +12.6%, underlying result +36%. **Delivered.** [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Oil palm:** early double-digit FFB volume growth (Q4). Q1 FFB was flat against a strong base; full-year growth is still guided at high single digit to double digit. Downstream processing (specialty-fats refinery from Sep 2026; seed garden and refinery in 18–24 months) is expected to add ~200 bps to group EBITDA when scaled, with ~50–55% of the segment value-added by FY31. **Kept.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Dairy:** Q4 expected milk prices to "cool down" from Q2. Q1 said procurement prices stay elevated for 2–3 more quarters; about 70% of cost inflation is being passed on. **Worse.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Astec:** ~15% (Astec COO) to ~20% (CEO) revenue growth (Q4). Q1: "more than 20%" full year despite -9% in Q1. **Raised.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Capex and cash:** ₹300–350 cr a year (FY27 ~₹400 cr incl. all), ~50% to oil palm, all funded internally; FCF surplus after capex ~₹100–125 cr. **Kept.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- #1 organised compound-feed player in India; Animal Nutrition volume +15% in Q4 FY26 and +7% in Q1 FY27 (cattle +15–24%). High milk prices push farmers toward branded premium feed (Dhanalaxmi G, Bypro Plus). [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [CRISIL Jul-26]
- Oil palm: exclusive government-allotted command areas for palm planting and processing, a best-in-class extraction ratio (~18.8% in Q1; 20.77% in Q4), and roughly 65–70% of FY26 profit growth from internal efficiency rather than price. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Crop Care: industry-leading EBIT margin (~26–27%) on patented in-house products, but a "single point of failure" in cotton herbicide. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Consumer brands are small: Godrej Jersey (value-added share 49%), Yummiez (+22% volume; nugget share on quick commerce 9%, up from 5%). [IP Q1FY27]

## Input costs
- **Feed:** maize, soybean meal, de-oiled rice bran and other grains. Sharp maize inflation in 2026 (war-related); the company passed on ~60–70% and benefited from well-timed commodity buying, which it says "may not be exactly structural". [Q1 call]
- **Dairy:** raw milk procurement prices have been elevated for four quarters; packaging costs rose with crude and LPG during the Gulf conflict, expected to ease by August. [Q1 call]
- **Oil palm:** revenue tracks the international CPO price (linked to crude oil via biodiesel). Farmer FFB prices follow a government formula that changes monthly, which squeezed Q1 margin. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Crop Care and Astec:** Chinese intermediates (~47% of Astec's imports); Astec is a net exporter, which gives a natural currency hedge. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Weather:** crop care and dairy both hit by the June 2026 drought; El Niño risk for H2 and for FY28 palm yields. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Guidance slippage:** the mid-teens PBT target from May was already diluted by August. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Management churn:** Crop Care CEO Rajavelu N.K. resigned effective 24 Nov 2026 (interim CEO named); the Head of HR left Aug 2026 (new CHRO from 7 Sep); chairmanship is moving from Nadir Godrej to Burjis Godrej. [BSE filings, 21 Sep and 2 Sep 2026] [Q1 call]
- **Structure and minorities:** Astec's future (possible group chemicals restructuring) is "being evaluated"; management promises to protect minority interests. [Q4 call]
- **Related parties:** pet-food contract manufacturing for Godrej Consumer Products shows up as other income (~₹9.5 cr profit in Q4 FY26); a surety bond was given for a related party. [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- **Contingent liabilities (consolidated):** ~₹131 cr of claims not acknowledged as debt (excise, customs, GST, income tax). The auditor raised no qualification or emphasis of matter; key audit matters were revenue recognition (crop protection) and goodwill impairment. [AR FY26]
- **Supplier financing** flatters working capital, and part of the payable-days jump depends on it. [IP Q1FY27] [Screener]
- Promoter holding steady at 67.75%; no pledge noted. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Nadir Godrej chaired the Q4 call; Burjis Godrej (Chairman Designate) led Q1.
- Guidance shifted from "mid-teens PBT" to "double-digit PBT, range by Q2".
- The milk-price outlook worsened (now elevated for 2–3 more quarters); packaging inflation was added.
- A new strategy deck: a portfolio triage, live-bird trading to close, plants evaluated for closure, and a strategic review of shrimp feed, seeds and cattle genetics.
- The downstream palm plan got specific (specialty fats in Sep 2026; ~200 bps EBITDA upside).
- New disclosure on ACI Bangladesh's turnaround (+14.5% volume).

## My read
The quality of the portfolio is improving: working capital is down sharply, ROCE is near 20%, Astec is at breakeven, feed volumes are strong, and oil palm has a built-in maturity tailwind. But FY27 has started badly because crop care (the highest-margin unit) and dairy were hit by weather and milk costs, and the mid-teens PBT growth promise has already been softened. At 27x earnings with a ~1.7% dividend yield, the stock needs H2 crop-care recovery and the palm downstream ramp to deliver. Watch the end-of-September crop-care update and whether the planned exits (shrimp feed, seeds, live birds) actually happen. A worsening El Niño or continued milk inflation would push FY27 PBT toward flat.
