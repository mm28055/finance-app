# JD Cables (BSE SME: 544524 / JDCABLES) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹190 (52-week ₹130–254), market cap ₹428 cr, P/E 13.5, book value ₹64.8, ROCE 33.6%, ROE 36.0%, no dividend [Screener standalone, 27-Sep-26]. **SME-listed (IPO 25-Sep-25 at ₹152), reports half-yearly — there is no Q1 FY27 result**; H1 FY27 (Apr–Sep 2026) is due around Nov-26. Promoter holding 70.00%, unchanged since listing; no pledge shown [Screener shareholding]. Rating: Infomerics IVR BBB-/Stable withdrawn 23-Jun-26 (lender repaid), new IVR BBB/Stable assigned 3-Jul-26 on ₹83 cr of bank lines [Infomerics Jun-26, Jul-26].

## Business and segments
- Kolkata company (founded 2015) making LT aerial bunched cables, LT XLPE/PVC power and control cables (up to 1.1 kV), service wires and aluminium conductors (AAC, AAAC, ACSR) for state electricity boards and EPC contractors in ~16 states, mainly East and North-East [IP H2FY26 slides 5–7; Official H2 transcript].
- FY26 revenue ₹364.6 cr: cables & wires ₹252.6 cr (FY25 ₹202.1 cr, +25%), aluminium conductors ₹81.5 cr (₹48.4 cr, +68%), and ~₹30 cr from EPC (derived; MD said "around INR30 crores" booked in FY26) [IP H2FY26 slide 15; Official H2 transcript].
- **EPC:** a ₹407 cr NH-2 six-laning job (Chordaha–Gohar, Bihar–Jharkhand border, NHDP Phase V) with bridges and electrical works — very large against ₹146 cr net worth; ~10% complete at Jun-26 [IP H2FY26 slide 9; Official H2 transcript].
- Private vs government: ~80–85% of cable revenue from large private EPC contractors [Sep-26 investor meet].
- Capacity: Unit I (Belgachia) 6,000 km, Unit II (Dankuni) 22,000 km; FY26 production 4,946 km + 18,603 km = **23,549 km (+4% vs 22,610 km in FY25)**, utilisation 82.4% and 84.6% [IP H2FY26 slide 19]. Unit III (Chakundi, Dankuni; ~1.18 lakh sq ft) adds 28,000 km — conductor division started 7-Jul-26, cable division 14-Sep-26 [BSE filing 14-Sep-26].
- Order book ₹515 cr at Mar-26 (MD: ~₹300 cr EPC, ~₹200 cr cables & conductors) → ~₹600 cr at Sep-26 (~₹380 cr cables, ~₹250 cr EPC) [Official H2 transcript; Sep-26 investor meet]. A ₹12 cr cable & conductor order was announced 15-Sep-26 [Screener announcement list; filing not opened].

## Key numbers
- Half-yearly sales (₹ cr): H1 FY25 107 → H2 FY25 143 → H1 FY26 121 → **H2 FY26 243**. Operating profit 15 → 19 → 19 → 28; margin 14% → 13% → 16% → **12%**. Net profit 10 → 12 → 12 → **20** [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹101 cr, op. profit ₹7 cr (7%), PAT ₹5 cr; FY25 ₹251 cr, ₹34 cr (14%), ₹22 cr; **FY26 ₹365 cr (+46%), ₹48 cr (13%), ₹32 cr (+44%)** [Screener]. Company: FY26 EBITDA ₹48.11 cr, PAT ₹31.72 cr [IP H2FY26 slide 25].
- Unit economics: volume grew only ~4% in FY26 while revenue grew 46% — growth came from price (aluminium), product mix (more conductors) and ~₹30 cr of new EPC revenue. Implied revenue per km of cable/conductor ~₹1.42 lakh in FY26 vs ~₹1.11 lakh in FY25 (derived from product revenue ÷ km produced) [IP H2FY26 slides 15, 19].
- H2 FY26 margin fell to ~12% from 16% in H1; MD blamed "lots of expenses" and bulk supplies; other expenses rose from ~₹4 cr to ~₹24 cr, explained as EPC costs with bills pending [Official H2 transcript].

## Guidance history
- **FY27 revenue:** June H2 call — "50% to 60% revenue growth in this financial year and even in the next"; EPC "minimum INR200 crores" in FY27 (from ~₹30 cr); cables & conductors +30–40%; EBITDA "double-digit" [Official H2 transcript]. Sep-26 meet — **"approximately 40-50%"** for FY27 and FY28; H1 FY27 revenue growth ~30–40%; EBITDA margin "approximately the current level"; the MD claimed he had "earlier" guided 40–50% [Sep-26 investor meet, unofficial copy]. **Cut.**
- **EPC:** June — "forward integration … huge advantage", "recurring in nature", bidding ~₹1,000 cr of EPC + cable tenders. Sep — "not looking at any new EPC projects"; only the NH-2 job continues [Official H2 transcript; Sep-26 meet]. **Dropped.**
- **Order book:** June — ₹700–800 cr by Mar-27 "on the conservative side"; Sep — ~₹600 cr [same]. **On track only if inflows pick up.**
- **New plant:** June — conductor division power "within this month", cable division "in the next two months", 70–80% utilisation next year, "good pace" by Sep-end. Actual — conductors 7-Jul-26, cables 14-Sep-26; Sep meet: ~30–40% utilisation and ~₹100 cr revenue from the new plant in FY27; MVCC in 1–1.5 months (monsoon-delayed shed and late parts); HT via Sioplas from ~Jan-27, CCV line later; normal running "after 3-4 months" (Puja-season labour) [Official H2 transcript; BSE filings; Sep-26 meet]. **Slipped ~6 weeks; ramp slower.**
- **New products/margins:** MVCC, AL-59, HTLS and HT cables expected 2–3 pts higher margin, "on the safer side" from FY28; house wires and solar cables possibly FY28 [Sep-26 meet].
- **Cash flow:** June — no problem, banks ready to lend; Sep — "should be in a better position than last year", no commitment to positive cash flow [Official H2 transcript; Sep-26 meet].

## Capex and capacity
- ~₹40 cr spent in the last year (Unit III land, shed, machines); fixed assets ₹10 cr → ₹21 cr, CWIP ₹6 cr at Mar-26 [Sep-26 meet; Screener].
- Next: ~₹20 cr now plus ₹15–20 cr more in FY27 (land adjacent to Unit III, more machines); a CCV line for HT cables would cost ~₹70–100 cr including land; VCV (66 kV+) later [Sep-26 meet].
- Payback maths (derived): 56,000 km total capacity × ~80% × ~₹1.4 lakh/km ≈ ₹630 cr — consistent with management's "₹600 crores, and even 700 crores" potential [Sep-26 meet]. The new capacity cost well under ₹50 cr, so asset turns are very high (10x+) on conductors and LT cables.
- Funding: reserves or bank debt; equity "later" if debt is not enough [Sep-26 meet].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Operating cash flow: −₹13 cr (FY24), −₹18 cr (FY25), **−₹74 cr (FY26)**; free cash flow −₹92 cr — funded by the IPO (₹84.4 cr fresh issue), a pre-IPO placement (₹5.2 cr) and debt [Screener; AR FY26 note 3].
- Borrowings ₹46 cr → ₹57 cr (Infomerics total debt ₹56.6 cr), including an unsecured loan from the MD of **₹10.8 cr** at Mar-26 (₹1.3 cr a year earlier; ₹9.2 cr taken in FY26); gearing 0.39x; interest cover 10.8x [Screener; AR FY26 note 34; Infomerics Jul-26].
- Cash and bank only **₹0.54 cr** at Mar-26 (₹3.86 cr a year earlier) — at odds with the MD's June remark about a "comfortable cash position" [AR FY26 note 17; Official H2 transcript].
- FY26 debtor days 96, inventory days 101, payables 29 (56 in FY25) — cash cycle **168 days** (96) [Screener]. Infomerics: gross current assets 204 days, operating cycle 113 days (64); limits used only ~43% on average [Infomerics Jul-26]. MD's explanation for faster supplier payments: IPO cash used to pay upfront for discounts [Sep-26 meet].
- Government payment cycle "approximately 60 days", up to 90 in some states [Sep-26 meet].

## Moat evidence
- Approvals: BIS licences, vendor approval with multiple state electricity boards; IEEMA price-variation formula on most orders (monthly metal pass-through) [IP H2FY26; Sep-26 meet].
- Cost position: small overheads (~70 professionals; MD pay ₹21.6 lakh) and cheap capacity additions [IP H2FY26 slide 31; AR FY26 note 34].
- Limits: commodity LT cables and conductors; Infomerics flags a "highly fragmented & competitive sector with significant price war"; MD says it cannot raise prices much without losing contractors; competitors named as Laser Power & Infra and Lumino in Kolkata [Infomerics Jul-26; Sep-26 meet].

## Risks and red flags
- **Cash burn and thin cash:** three straight years of negative operating cash flow; ₹0.5 cr cash at Mar-26; growth relies on bank lines and promoter loans.
- **EPC exposure:** a ₹407 cr highway project (2.8x net worth) outside the core business, only ~10% done, now "not a focus" — execution, cost-overrun and retention-money risk; bank-guarantee margin deposits exist but contingent liabilities are shown as **nil** [IP H2FY26; AR FY26 notes 17, 26].
- **Disclosure quality:** trade-payable ageing puts ₹22.4 cr under "Disputed dues–MSME" while the MSME note says only ₹8.25 lakh is owed to MSMEs — apparently a mislabelled row; the official H2 transcript locates the new plant in "Jamshedpur" while filings say Dankuni; the Sep-26 meet (unofficial copy) quotes FY26 EBITDA margin as "20.17%" (actual 13.2%) [AR FY26 note 9; Official H2 transcript].
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion (Vinod Singhal & Co. LLP); no key audit matters (not required for SME); CARO clean on dues, loans and deposits [AR FY26 auditor's report, CARO].
- **Promoter dealings:** MD sold 7.6 lakh shares (~₹11.6 cr) in the IPO offer-for-sale while lending ₹10.8 cr back to the company at interest (₹0.37 cr in FY26); pre-IPO placement at ~₹116.5/share (bonus-adjusted) in Apr-25 [AR FY26 notes 3.8–3.10, 34].
- **Guidance reliability:** growth cut from 50–60% to 40–50% within 16 weeks; EPC strategy reversed; plant timelines slipped.
- **Liquidity of stock:** SME board, ~1,900 shareholders; DIIs cut from 9.9% to 4.1% and FIIs from 4.6% to 0.6% since listing [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls (H2 FY26 call Jun-26 → investor meet Sep-26)
- Note: the second event was an Arihant Capital investor conference, not a results call; no results were published between them.
- Tone: from expansive (EPC as "huge advantage", 50–60% growth, ₹700–800 cr order book) to defensive (no new EPC, 40–50%, monsoon and Puja delays).
- Numbers: order book ₹515 cr → ~₹600 cr, but mix flipped from EPC-heavy (~₹300 cr) to cable-heavy (~₹380 cr); new-plant FY27 contribution now pegged at ~₹100 cr.
- New: HT cables via Sioplas (Jan-27) and CCV; house wires/solar in FY28; talk of UltraTech's entry (MD: JD has no house-wire share today).

## My read
JD Cables is a small, fast-growing, low-cost maker of commodity LT cables and conductors that now has doubled capacity for little money, which could lift sales towards ₹600 cr in FY28 if the new plant fills. But FY26 growth was mostly price and a one-off EPC entry (volume +4%), cash flow has been negative for three years, year-end cash was almost nil, and management's guidance and strategy changed sharply within one quarter. The low P/E reflects SME status and these risks; I would want to see the H1 FY27 numbers (due Nov-26) show positive operating cash flow and the new plant running above 50% before giving credit to the growth story.
