# Finolex Cables — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code FINCABLES (consolidated; includes 32.39% associate Finolex Industries and 50:50-type JV Finolex J-Power Systems by equity method). Price ₹1,461, market cap ₹22,343 cr, 52-week range ₹701–1,498 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Four reported segments (FY26, standalone, before inter-segment eliminations): Electrical cables ₹5,490 cr revenue, EBIT ₹562.9 cr (10.3%); Communication cables ₹500.3 cr, EBIT ₹13.9 cr (2.8%); Copper rods ₹2,142.6 cr (mostly captive), EBIT ₹3.2 cr; Others (FMEG/trading: fans, lights, switches, conduits, water heaters) ₹261.5 cr, EBIT ₹4.7 cr [AR FY26 Note 36 p.207–208].
- Q1 FY27: Electrical ₹1,767 cr (+47% y/y), EBIT ₹182 cr (10.3%); Communication ₹176 cr (+62%), EBIT ₹52.5 cr (~30%); Copper rod external sales ₹8 cr (vs ₹403 cr segment revenue a year ago — plant shut for lack of commercial LPG); Others ₹61 cr, EBIT ₹1.2 cr. Mix: electrical 88%, communication 9%, others 3% [IP Q1FY27 slides 4–7; Q1 call].
- Electrical mix (wires): ~60% construction/building wire, and roughly 10–14% each for auto, agriculture and industrial, with solar small but growing; LV+HV power cables only ~7–8% on top [Q1 call]. Retail:project split moved from 80:20 to ~75:25 [Q4 call].
- Market share: 23.9% of the organised wire industry (company claim) [IP Q1FY27 slide 14].
- Communication: ~¾ optical fibre cable (OFC), rest copper telecom/coax (declining). Fibre draw capacity 4 mn fibre-km, running ~3.2 mn (Q4) and fully used in Q1 as far as preforms allowed; cabling capacity 8 mn fkm, one plant full and one ~¾ full. Preform plant (100 tonnes, ≈4 mn fkm of fibre) commissioned mid-March 2026, still stabilising — Finolex is one of only two Indian preform makers [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 slide 11].
- Electrical capacity utilisation ~66% at Mar-26; management treats 80–85% as practical full; conduits ~85% [Q4 call]. Greenfield only after utilisation crosses 70% [IP Q1FY27 slide 11].
- Distribution: ~800 distributors, ~5,000 channel partners, ~2.15 lakh retailers (target 2.5 lakh) [IP Q1FY27 slides 11, 14].
- Exports tiny but rising: ₹30 cr FY25 → ₹52 cr FY26 → ~₹50 cr in Q1 FY27 alone (₹35–40 cr of it fibre/OFC to the US and Europe) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- JV Finolex J-Power Systems (EHV cables with Sumitomo/J-Power): FY26 revenue ~₹450–458 cr, profit ~₹21 cr (Q4 call) — the Q1 call called it ₹24 cr; Q1 FY27 revenue ~₹87 cr, profit ~₹7 cr; order book ₹380 cr at start of FY27 [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener consolidated; revenue / operating profit / OPM / PAT, ₹ cr): Q1 FY26 1,396 / 136 / 10% / 163; Q2 1,376 / 145 / 11% / 163; Q3 1,599 / 158 / 10% / 164; Q4 1,951 / 180 / 9% / 224; Q1 FY27 2,013 / 244 / 12% / 249 [Screener].
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹5,014 cr → FY25 ₹5,319 cr → FY26 ₹6,321 cr (+19%); operating profit ₹588 → ₹543 → ₹620 cr; OPM 12% → 10% → 10%; PAT ₹652 → ₹701 → ₹714 cr. TTM revenue ₹6,939 cr, TTM PAT ₹800 cr [Screener].
- Other income is large: ₹370 cr in FY26 (60% of operating profit) — treasury income plus the associate share; standalone "unallocable" income net ₹224 cr [Screener; AR FY26 Note 36].
- Q1 FY27 standalone: gross margin 19.4% (17.5% in Q4), EBITDA ₹248 cr (12.3%), PAT ₹221 cr; consolidated PAT ₹249 cr including ₹41 cr share of associate/JV [IP Q1FY27 slides 9–10].
- Price and volume: 14 price changes in FY26, net price increase ~24–25%; one ~3% increase in May 2026. Q1 FY27 volume growth: auto, agri, flexible and solar "high double digits"; building wire low single digits (channel de-stocking). OFC volume +~30% in Q1; FY26 communication volume +7% (AR says +5% OFC) [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Fibre prices: standard G.652.D went from $5–6/km (Dec 2025) to $17–18 and was ~$12–13 in August 2026; premium grades sold at $25–50/km [Q1 call].
- Returns: ROCE 16% FY26 (18% FY25), ROE 12% — diluted by ~₹4,000 cr of investments and cash [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Management gives no formal revenue/margin guidance ("I do not give out specific guidance…in a volatile situation") [Q4 call].
- Q4 FY26 call (29 May 2026) promises: FY27 capex ~₹300 cr; fibre draw to 8 mn fkm by end-Q2 FY27 (first 4→6, then decide on 8); preform stabilises in 2–3 months with benefit from end-Q2/Q3; communication margins to improve on FY26's ~6% EBIT and 8–9% EBIT possible "subject to no supply-chain constraints"; long-term fibre contract re-priced from June; H2 FY27 "much better"; exports to reach 2–3% of sales in two years; FMEG ₹500 cr revenue by FY28 [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 delivery (13 Aug 2026): communication EBIT ~30% (far above the 8–9% talked of in May) but explicitly flagged as inventory gains on fibre bought in Nov–Dec 2025 — to fall to "low double digits" by year-end; fibre draw expansion raised to 4→8 mn fkm in one step, ready by September 2026; capex kept at ~₹300 cr; cabling to 10 mn fkm later; electrical EBIT ~10.5% called sustainable; FMEG target of ₹500 cr by FY28 kept despite a lost fan season; preform "next couple of months" to stabilise (slipped slightly vs May) [Q1 call].
- Rating agency view: Crisil expects 8–10% revenue growth and 10–11% margins over the medium term [Crisil Nov-25].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 spend ~₹240 cr, including an ₹88.2 cr equity infusion into the EHV JV [Q4 call; AR FY26 Note 35A].
- FY27 plan ~₹300 cr: ~₹100 cr to finish the fibre draw expansion (to 8 mn fkm, September 2026) and preform phase 1; ~₹200 cr "completely new" capacity, including doubling solar cable lines, cabling 8→10 mn fkm, and e-beam cured cables; preform phase 2 (doubling to 200 tonnes) under study [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 slide 11].
- Funding: fully internal; nil long-term debt [Crisil Nov-25; Screener borrowings ₹19 cr, mainly leases].
- Unexecuted capital commitments were only ₹22.5 cr at Mar-26 (₹73.6 cr at Mar-25), so most FY27 capex was yet to be ordered at year-end [AR FY26 Note 32].
- Payback (management arithmetic): 8 mn fkm × $11/km ≈ $88 mn a year of fibre alone (~₹750 cr), plus 20–30% value added if cabled; so ~₹100 cr of draw capex adds roughly ₹350–400 cr of potential revenue at today's prices (my arithmetic) — very fast payback if prices hold, but fibre is a boom-bust market [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash: cash and liquid investments ~₹2,713 cr at Sep-25, nil long-term debt, net worth ₹5,495 cr [Crisil Nov-25]; Screener investments ₹4,195 cr at Mar-26 (includes the Finolex Industries stake).
- Cash conversion deteriorated: CFO ₹49 cr in FY26 (₹207 cr FY25, ₹577 cr FY24); free cash flow −₹105 cr. Cause: inventory up ~₹300 cr as a war hedge in Q4 FY26 [Screener; Q4 call].
- Days: inventory 73 (61 FY25), debtors 21, payables 16, cash conversion cycle 78 days (57) [Screener].
- Dividend ~19% payout; dividends paid to Orbit Electricals ₹37.6 cr and Finolex Industries ₹17.8 cr in FY26; dividend received from Finolex Industries ₹72.4 cr [Screener; AR FY26 Note 35A].
- Rating: Crisil AA+/Stable/A1+ reaffirmed Nov 2025; ₹150 cr proposed NCD rating withdrawn at company request [Crisil Nov-25].

## Moat evidence
- Brand and share: 23.9% organised wire share; 2.15–2.25 lakh retailers; backward integration into copper rods, compounds and glass preform [IP Q1FY27 slides 12, 14; Crisil Nov-25].
- Pricing: passed through ~25% price rises in FY26 but lost retail volume — management: dealers resist holding stock through 14 price changes; project sales held up [Q4 call]. Weak pricing power in retail wires relative to leaders.
- Technology in OFC: one of two Indian preform makers; preform saves the 5% import duty on non-Japanese preform and should cost 5–10% below market once stable [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 slide 11].
- EHV JV uses vertical-insulation (VCV) technology that competitors are only now copying [Q4 call].
- Weakness: long-term fibre contracts (>half of OFC revenue) fixed price for a year — caps upside in rising markets; FMEG sub-scale (₹262 cr, ~2% EBIT) [Q1 call; AR FY26 Note 36].

## Risks and red flags
- Promoter control dispute: Deepak Chhabria vs Prakash Chhabria (Orbit Electricals, 30.70% holder). The company has appealed to the Bombay High Court against a Pune civil court order on votes cast by Orbit (30.70%) and Finolex Industries (14.51%); Crisil flags it as sub judice. Board churn: seven non-executive directors served terms of weeks to months between Mar and Nov 2024 [AR FY26 Secretarial Audit Report; AR FY26 Note 35; Crisil Nov-25].
- Cross-holding: FCL owns 32.39% of Finolex Industries, which owns 14.51% of FCL — complicates control and inflates reported "other income" [AR FY26 Note 6, Note 35].
- JV stress: investment in Finolex J-Power carried at ₹110.9 cr after ₹208.6 cr of past impairment; auditor's key audit matter; JV net worth "substantially eroded"; FCL gives unconditional financial support and a ₹185.7 cr bank guarantee (₹146.5 cr last year); FCL sold ₹53.5 cr of material to the JV [AR FY26 KAM p.144; Notes 32, 35A].
- Contingent liabilities ₹178.7 cr (sales tax ₹66.2 cr, income tax ₹51.1 cr, GST ₹36.5 cr — GST up from ₹8.2 cr, excise ₹23.9 cr) [AR FY26 Note 32].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; only key audit matter is the JV impairment; audit trail operated throughout the year [AR FY26 pp.144, Note 39].
- OFC boom risk: 30% margins are inventory gains; management expects "low double digits"; fibre prices already fell from $17–18 to $12–13; germanium tetrachloride and helium supply is tight (helium ₹1,600→5,000→₹3,400–3,500 per m³) [Q1 call].
- Operational: LPG shortage shut the copper-rod plant for a quarter and hurt fan output; PVC shortages hurt conduits [Q1 call].
- Management turnover: Mahesh Viswanathan moved from Deputy CEO & CFO (May) to CEO; new CFO Sachin Naik from 1 June 2026; compliance-officer changes in FY26 [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 Note 35].
- Competition: large new entrants in wires (management did not quantify on these calls).

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: May was cautious (war, retail wire resistance, preform teething, fixed-price fibre contracts); August was upbeat on OFC and exports, cautious on how long it lasts.
- Numbers: communication EBIT margin ~6% (Q4) → ~30% (Q1); electrical EBIT ~10.5% held; exports ₹52 cr for FY26 → ~₹50 cr in one quarter.
- Plans: fibre expansion accelerated (4→8 mn fkm in one step by September); preform phase 2 and data-centre high-fibre-count cables (up to 14,000 fibres) now "under study"/"R&D and capex: yes to both".
- New problems: LPG shortage (copper rods shut; fans lost season); germanium supply "hand-to-mouth".

## My read
The core wire business is steady but not gaining much — building-wire volume barely grew in FY26 and retail pricing power is weaker than peers, while the faster growth is in auto, solar and agri cables. The real change is optical fibre: backward integration into preform plus doubling draw capacity gives Finolex a cheap, fast-payback bet on the global fibre shortage, but Q1's 30% margin was largely an inventory windfall and will fade. Cash flow was poor in FY26 (CFO ₹49 cr), and the governance overhang — the Chhabria control fight, cross-holding with Finolex Industries and a weak EHV JV needing guarantees — deserves a discount. I would turn more positive if the 8 mn fkm line runs near full by Q4 FY27 with communication margins holding in double digits and FY27 cash flow recovers; a fibre price slump or an adverse court outcome on control would change the view.
