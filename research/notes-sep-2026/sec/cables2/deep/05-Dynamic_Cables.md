# Dynamic Cables (NSE: DYCL / BSE: 540795) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹457 (52-week ₹237–560), market cap ₹2,214 cr, P/E 24.3, book value ₹94.4, ROCE 26.2%, ROE 19.9%, dividend yield 0.11% [Screener, 27-Sep-26]. Standalone only (no subsidiaries). Promoter holding 68.18%, unchanged since Jun-24 (it fell from ~74.5% when the ₹96.58 cr preferential issue was made in Jun-24); no pledge disclosed [Screener shareholding; AR FY26 note 14].

## Business and segments
- Makes power cables and conductors for the grid: HV/MV power cables up to 66 kV, LV cables, solar (renewable) cables, aerial bunched cables, niche conductors (AL-59, ACSR), and since Feb-26 fire-safe building wires for B2B buyers only. Four plants in Jaipur and Reengus (Rajasthan); 1,073 permanent staff [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q4FY26].
- FY26 product sales (₹ cr): HT/HV cables 755.9 (63%), LT/LV cables 346.6 (29%), conductors 72.4 (6%), others 23.0; total 1,197.8 [AR FY26 MD&A p.72]. Q1 FY27: HV 68%, LV 30%, conductors 2% [Q1 call].
- FY26 customer sales (₹ cr): government 160.0 (13%), private 956.3 (80%), exports 81.6 (7%) [AR FY26 p.72]. Q1 FY27: government 16%, private 71%, exports 13% [Q1 call].
- Renewable (solar) cables grew over 100% in FY26 to ~18–19% of revenue (FY25 ₹105 cr, ~10%); Q1 FY27 ~20% [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Deliberate exits: railway signalling cables went from 4% of FY25 sales to zero in FY26; low-value LV conductors fell from 8–9% to under 5% (volume −50%) [Official Q4 transcript]. Conductors were 20.6% of sales in FY19, 6.0% in FY26; HV share rose from 34.9% to 63.1% [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Capacity: management's stated peak turnover capacity is ~₹135 cr a month (set before the metal price rise), with 85–90% the practical maximum; utilisation ~85% in Q1 FY27 [Official Q4 transcript; Q1 call].
- Order book ₹808 cr at Mar-26 (+11% YoY) and ₹811 cr at Jun-26 (~+10% YoY) — flat quarter on quarter [IP Q4FY26 slide 14; Q1 call].
- US entry: UL-listed HV/MV/LV cables; first shipments in Q1 FY27 through US distributors; US was ~15% of Q1 exports; Gulf exports "negligible" in Q1 because of the Middle East war [Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Q1 FY26 262 → Q2 282 → Q3 299 → Q4 355 → **Q1 FY27 349**. Operating profit 27 → 31 → 34 → 38 → **38**; margin 10% → 11% → 11% → 11% → **10.9%**. Net profit 18 → 20 → 22 → 24 → **25** [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 vs Q1 FY26: sales +33%, operating profit +41%, profit +37% — but volume grew only 5–6%; the rest was aluminium-led price inflation [Q1 call]. Q4 FY26 sales grew just 7% YoY; core products +20% after stripping out exited lines, of which 12% was price [Official Q4 transcript].
- Annual (₹ cr): FY24 sales 768, op. profit 77 (10%), PAT 38; FY25 1,025, 105 (10%), 65; FY26 1,198 (+17%), 130 (10.8%), 84 (+30%). TTM sales 1,285, PAT 91 [Screener].
- 3-year sales growth 21% a year, profit 39% a year; 10-year sales 16% a year [Screener].
- Unit economics: management runs an "80-10-10" model — ~80% raw material, ~10% conversion cost, ~10% EBITDA; operating margin guided at 10.5–11% and delivered for six straight quarters [Official Q4 transcript; Screener].
- Interest cost fell from ₹21 cr (FY24) to ₹11 cr (FY26) after the Jun-24 equity raise and a CRISIL upgrade to A/Stable [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A].

## Guidance history
- **Long-term growth:** "18%–20%" revenue growth a year in value terms, multi-year; no annual guidance. Repeated in Q4 (May-26) and Q1 (Jul-26) calls; Q4 call also said it can keep growing at ~1.5x industry [Official Q4 transcript; Q1 call]. Delivered: FY26 +17%, Q1 FY27 +33% (price-led). **Kept.**
- **Margin:** 10.5–11% operating margin; delivered 10.8% FY26, 10.9% Q1 FY27 [Official Q4 transcript; Screener]. **Met.**
- **New plant timing:** promised for Q2 FY26 at the Q4 FY25 call; slipped ~15 months because of regulatory approvals (AERB, needed for the electron-beam unit), imported machines held up by Iran-war freight and compliance. Q4 FY26 call: "go live by September" 2026; Q1 call: "delay is now behind us", commissioning "in September" [Official Q4 transcript; Q1 call]. The Q4 FY26 deck printed "Commencement of Production: September 2027" — apparently a typo, but it contradicts the call [IP Q4FY26 slide 16]. No commissioning announcement on BSE as of 27-Sep-26 [BSE filings]. **Kept (so far), after a long slip.**
- **H1 FY27 from existing plants:** Q4 call said current capacity is sufficient for H1; Q1 delivered record Q1 sales at ~85% utilisation. **Met.**
- **Solar share:** Q4 call — share rising from 18% to "20% to 23%" in FY27; Q1 call — ~20%, similar for the year, bigger rise only in FY28; solar to grow 25–30% a year for 3–4 years [Official Q4 transcript; Q1 call]. **Slightly softened.**
- **Exports/US:** Q4 call — "some contribution" from US in FY27, too early to size; export share to reach 10% then 15% medium term. Q1 delivered first US shipments and exports at 13% of sales [Official Q4 transcript; Q1 call]. **Ahead of plan.**
- **New plant ramp:** meaningful revenue only from Q4 FY27; 80–85% utilisation within ~18 months, i.e. by end-FY28 [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Greenfield plant at Reengus: ~₹40–45 cr (deck ~₹45 cr), with E-beam (electron-beam cross-linking — needed for DC solar cables) and fungible HV/LV capacity; brownfield room inside the new site afterwards [IP Q4FY26; Q1 call].
- Payback: management's historic fixed-asset turn is ~6x (range 6–7x; 5x "reasonable"), so ₹40–45 cr implies about ₹240–300 cr of annual sales at full use — roughly +20–25% on FY26 sales [Official Q4 transcript; Q1 call — derived].
- FY26 spend: fixed assets ₹84 cr → ₹97 cr; CWIP ₹0 → ₹33 cr (₹32.5 cr at Mar-26); cash used in investing ₹32 cr [Screener; AR FY26 CWIP schedule].
- Funding: the Jun-24 preferential issue of ₹96.58 cr; ₹85.40 cr used by Mar-26, ₹11.19 cr unused and parked in mutual funds; capital commitment of ₹11.19 cr left [AR FY26 SOCE note; note 38(b)].
- HTLS (high-temperature, low-sag) carbon-core conductors: technical tie-up with TS Conductor Corp (USA), one-year renewable deal; type tests and utility approvals take about a year; little extra capex needed. PGCIL approval received for ACSR and AL-59 conductors [Official Q4 transcript; IP Q4FY26].
- Data-centre power cables: product development and approvals under way, "very nascent", no timeline [Q1 call].
- Further capex: "not crystallised"; ~17,000–20,000 m² spare land mentioned by an analyst; CRISIL says no debt-funded capex planned near term [Official Q4 transcript; CRISIL Mar-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹119 cr (Mar-24) → ₹59 cr (Mar-25) → ₹43 cr (Mar-26); cash credit ₹17.4 cr; unsecured promoter loans ₹2.2 cr at 9% [Screener; AR FY26 notes 15, 19]. Mutual funds ₹29.3 cr plus fixed deposits — roughly **net cash neutral** at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 10].
- Debt rose in Q1 FY27: same stock quantity but ~20–30% higher inventory value from metal prices, and bigger receivables after two record quarters; debtor days on six-month sales unchanged [Q1 call]. No Jun-26 balance sheet is published (quarterly results only).
- Operating cash flow ₹1 cr (FY24), ₹56 cr (FY25), ₹62 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹4 cr in FY26 after capex [Screener].
- FY26 debtor days 88, inventory 65, payables 60, cash cycle 93 days (FY24: 121/74/89/106) [Screener]. Company's own measure: receivables ~68 days of trailing six-month gross sales at Q4 FY26 [IP Q4FY26].
- Working-capital limits used only ~24% on average in the 12 months to Dec-25; bank lines raised to ₹350 cr (mostly non-fund: ₹155 cr guarantees, ₹125 cr LCs) [CRISIL Mar-26].
- Dividend ₹1.21 cr paid in FY26 (payout ~3%); 1:1 bonus issue Jul-25 [AR FY26 notes 14, 14.2].

## Moat evidence
- Approvals: UL (USA), PGCIL for ACSR/AL-59, NABL labs, BIS; management says few Indian players hold its approval set and new entrants face "a long approval process" [IP Q4FY26; Q1 call].
- Pricing: ~80% of orders carry full metal price-variation clauses; for the ~20% fixed-price orders it books metal at order time. Margin held at 10–11% through the 2025–26 copper and aluminium spike [Q1 call; Screener].
- Mix shift: government share cut from 50–55% five years ago to 10–20%, private EPC and renewables up — better payment terms [CRISIL Mar-26].
- Limits: no retail brand or dealer network; mid-size player (FY26 sales ₹1,198 cr vs leaders at ₹10,000 cr+); CRISIL lists "intense competition from larger players" as a weakness [CRISIL Mar-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Order intake stalled:** order book flat at ₹808–811 cr for two quarters; customers refused to lock prices at elevated aluminium levels and give only 2–4-month orders [Q1 call]. If metal prices stay high, volume growth (5–6% in Q1) stays weak even as revenue looks strong.
- **Capacity bottleneck:** ~85% utilisation; H2 FY27 growth depends on the new plant, which already slipped 15 months [Q1 call; Official Q4 transcript].
- **Contingent liabilities ₹207.5 cr** (₹172.1 cr a year earlier), mainly bank guarantees ₹174.4 cr and LC bills discounted ₹25.1 cr; income-tax demands ₹2.65 cr; indirect-tax demands ₹1.94 cr; collateral for a related party's loan now nil (₹5 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 note 38]. CARO lists a GST input-credit dispute of ₹1.57 cr where the Rajasthan High Court rejected the company's writ (Jan-25) [AR FY26 CARO (vii)(b)].
- **Related parties:** MD Ashish Mangal paid ₹5.35 cr (FY25 ₹5.00 cr), ~6% of PAT; promoter loans rotated (₹13.3 cr taken, ₹12.9 cr repaid); ₹11 cr property bought from promoter entity Shiv Kripa Pipes in FY25; sales of ₹1.96 cr to chairman's Mangal Electrical Industries; a ₹344.75 cr corporate guarantee from promoter firm Indokrates shown a year earlier is now withdrawn [AR FY26 note 41; Official Q4 transcript].
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matters are revenue timing and trade-receivable credit-loss judgement; CARO clean on statutory dues, defaults and stock statements [AR FY26 auditor's report, CARO].
- **Rating withdrawal:** India Ratings affirmed IND A/Stable/IND A1 on ₹356 cr of bank lines and then withdrew it at the company's request on 12-Jun-26; CRISIL A/Stable remains — likely just avoiding two rating fees, not a credit issue [NSE filing 12-Jun-26; CRISIL Mar-26].
- **Management/family exits:** Aditi Mangal (MD's daughter, Head HR & Corporate Communication) resigned 23-Sep-26; DGM-Quality resigned Sep-26 [BSE filings, Sep-26].
- **Disclosure:** press release uploaded late for Q1 (company blamed a technical glitch); deck's "September 2027" date conflicts with the call [Q1 call; IP Q4FY26]. Exchanges asked for a price-movement clarification on 2-Sep-26; company said nothing undisclosed [BSE filing 3-Sep-26].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (Q4: 7% growth, plant delay, gross margin dip to 17.9%) to confident (Q1: record Q1, US shipments, plant "on track").
- Numbers: Q4 volume growth ~8% (core) vs Q1 5–6%; price share of growth rose from 12% to ~27%. Exports jumped from 7% of sales to 13%.
- New issues in Q1: order book stall blamed on "price is king" — clients waiting for metal to fall; rising debt from inventory value; Adani and Birla (UltraTech) entry into cables raised by analysts — management says Birla targets house wires and approvals protect its segment.
- Dropped: detailed explanation of discontinued railway and conductor lines (base effect gone).

## Best management quotes
- "Price is king. So, I mean nobody is willing to book material at such high prices for project executions in Q3." [Q1 call]
- "5% to 6% is our volume growth and balance is large part of it is kind of derived by the price growth of aluminium" [Q1 call]
- "We have been growing at 18%-20% since last 10-15 years and we should be able to maintain our growth rates" [Q1 call]

## My read
Dynamic is a steady, well-run mid-size power-cable maker: margins pinned at 10–11%, ROCE ~26%, debt near zero and a clean audit. The weak spot is volume — only 5–6% in Q1 with plants at ~85% — so FY27 growth really hinges on the late Reengus plant starting on time and ramping by FY28, plus solar and the new US channel. At ~24x trailing profit the price already assumes the 18–20% long-term growth continues; a flat order book for a third quarter, another plant slip, or margins under 10% would weaken that case, while visible US re-orders and a Q4 FY27 revenue step-up from the new plant would strengthen it.
