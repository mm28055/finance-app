# Indian wires & cables — sector research, Sep 2026

## Market size and growth
- Organised W&C revenue ~₹1 lakh crore in FY26; Crisil Ratings' sample of 17 makers covers ~70% of it [Crisil Ratings, 2-Jun-26]. A year earlier Crisil put organised revenue at ₹80,000–82,000 cr, about two-thirds of the whole sector [Crisil Ratings, 26-Mar-25].
- Market size by third parties: USD 21.22 bn (2025) → USD 23.13 bn (2026) → USD 35.58 bn (2031), a 9.01% CAGR in dollar terms; organised share ~74% in 2025; housing wires the largest product (32.3%); communication cables fastest (12.4% CAGR to 2031) [Mordor Intelligence, quoted in Paramount AR FY26 MD&A]. Polycab expects the domestic market to reach ~₹1.7 lakh cr by 2030 [News: Whalesbook, 18-Sep-26]. Industry size "Rs 1.2 trillion by end of FY27" [News: The Core, 2-Jul-26].
- Track record: FY25 revenue growth ~16%; Crisil forecast +15–16% for FY26 (Mar-25) and the year delivered **20%+ volume growth** — so FY26 beat its forecaster [Crisil Ratings, 26-Mar-25; 2-Jun-26].
- Volumes grew at >15% CAGR over the five years to FY26 [Crisil Ratings via KNN India, 3-Jun-26].

## Demand drivers
- **Power transmission:** the CEA's National Electricity Plan (Transmission) plans ~₹9.15 lakh cr of investment and ~190,000 circuit-km of new lines to 2032; inter-regional capacity 119 GW → 143 GW (2027) → 168 GW (2032) [News: Business Today, 15-Oct-24, on CEA NEP]. Polycab says line additions averaged ~15,000 ckm a year historically and should run "north of 20,000", with 2,000 ckm done in Apr–May 2026 [Polycab Q1 FY27 call via Investing.com].
- **Distribution/RDSS and renewables:** RE build-out (500 GW non-fossil target by 2030; ~283 GW non-fossil capacity by Mar-26) drives solar DC cables, HT cables and conductors; solar cables were ~19–20% of Dynamic Cables' sales after >100% growth in FY26 [Dynamic Cables AR FY26 MD&A; Dynamic Q1 FY27 call]. RE additions of 44.6 GW in FY26 [Paramount AR FY26 MD&A].
- **Housing:** housing wires and power cables are ~50% of industry revenue [Crisil Ratings, 2-Jun-26]. Housing demand softened: top-7-city sales fell 6% YoY (−11% QoQ) to 90,715 units in Apr–Jun 2026 on Middle East war uncertainty, while launches rose 7% [ANAROCK via Outlook Money, Jun-26]. Building-wire volumes grew only "low single digits" in Q1 FY27 as dealers de-stocked when metal prices fell in June [Finolex Q1 FY27 call, via cables2 deep file 02; Polycab Q1 FY27 call].
- **Data centres:** Polycab estimates a ₹20,000–25,000 cr cable opportunity over 6–8 years; India DC capacity ~1.7 GW → 8 GW+ by 2030 (company claim) [Polycab Q1 FY27 call; Paramount Q4 FY26 call]. Mid-caps (Dynamic, JD) describe DC cables as "nascent".
- **Industrial capex:** Crisil expects some deferment of discretionary industrial capex because of 18–20% higher cable prices [Crisil Ratings, 2-Jun-26]; Dynamic saw EPC clients book only 2–4-month orders in Apr–Jun 2026 ("price is king") [Dynamic Q1 FY27 call].

## Exports and trade
- US tariffs: reciprocal plus penalty duties on Indian goods reached ~50% in Aug-25; the US Supreme Court struck down IEEPA tariffs 6-3 on 20-Feb-26, replaced by a temporary 10% global Section 122 tariff (24-Feb to 24-Jul-26); refunds process unclear at the time [Miller & Chevalier, Feb-26; ICRA on Paramount, 14-Jul-26].
- Effect on exporters: Paramount ran US exports at a ~4% EBITDA loss in FY26 and cut US shipments from ₹381 cr (H1) to ₹169 cr (H2); Q1 FY27 exports recovered to ₹155 cr; it has received ~₹8 cr of tariff refunds and expects ₹20–22 cr more [Paramount Q1 FY27 call; ICRA Jul-26].
- Middle East war hit Gulf exports in H1 2026: Polycab international revenue −13% YoY in Q1 FY27 (North America now 45–50% of international sales; target exports >10% of sales by FY30); KEI exports ₹341 cr vs ₹375 cr; Dynamic's Gulf sales "negligible" [Polycab Q1 FY27 call; KEI Q1 FY27 call via InvestyWise; Dynamic Q1 FY27 call].
- New US entrants from India: Dynamic Cables' first US shipments in Q1 FY27 (15% of its exports); Paramount has 8 US distributors [Dynamic Q1 FY27 call; Paramount Q4 FY26 call].

## Cost drivers — copper, aluminium, PVC
- FY26: copper and aluminium prices rose 22–27%, PVC ~12% [Crisil Ratings via KNN India, 3-Jun-26]. Copper went from ~$9,200–9,700/t (Apr-25) to peaks above $13,000–14,000/t in early 2026, ~$12,500–13,000 by Apr–May 2026; aluminium from ~$2,400–2,600/t to $3,300–3,600/t [Dynamic Cables AR FY26 MD&A].
- Forecast: J.P. Morgan (21-Aug-26) sees copper averaging $13,885/t in 2026 (Q3 $14,500, Q4 $14,800) and $13,800/t in 2027; aluminium $3,587/t in 2026 (Q3 $3,800) falling to **$3,075/t in 2027**; aluminium tight because Middle East smelters (~6% of world supply) and Hormuz shipping were disrupted [J.P. Morgan metals outlook, Aug-26].
- Volatility: aluminium fell 18–20% in June 2026, triggering channel de-stocking; Polycab cut prices ~3–4% in early July [Polycab Q1 FY27 call].
- Pass-through: metals are ~75–80% of raw material cost (>80% of input cost per ICRA); most B2B orders carry IEEMA price-variation clauses; B2C prices revised frequently (Finolex: 14 changes in FY26, net +24–25%) [Crisil on Dynamic, Mar-26; ICRA on Paramount, Jul-26; Finolex Q4 FY26 call via deep file 02]. Effect: FY27 revenue growth is inflated by price — Crisil expects realisations +18–20% vs volume ~10% [Crisil Ratings, 2-Jun-26].

## Policy and quality rules
- BIS certification (ISI mark) has been compulsory for PVC-insulated cables (IS 694) and heavy-duty cables (IS 1554) for two decades; no new cable-specific Quality Control Order is pending on BIS's list as of 22-Sep-26 [BIS "Upcoming QCOs" page, 22-Sep-26; industry certification guides]. Utility vendor approvals (PGCIL, NTPC, state boards), type tests and UL for the US remain the real entry barriers for power cables [Dynamic Q1 FY27 call; Paramount Q4 FY26 call].
- Domestic-content rules in solar (ALMM for cells from 1-Jun-26) favour Indian supply chains for government RE tenders [Advait Q4 FY26 call].
- Budget FY27 central capex ₹12.2 lakh cr (+~9%) supports infrastructure demand [Dynamic AR FY26 MD&A; Paramount AR FY26 MD&A].

## Competitive structure and capacity additions
- Leader: Polycab's share of the organised domestic C&W market rose to **30–31% in FY26 from 26–27%** [News: Multibagg on Polycab Q4 FY26, 7-May-26].
- Q1 FY27 growth and size (₹ cr, quarter): Polycab W&C +39% YoY (domestic +43%, international −13%; W&C revenue ~7,202 per press reports); KEI 3,185 (+23%, company total); Havells cables 2,456 (+27%); RR Kabel 3,168 (company total); Finolex 2,013 (company total) [FM Live on Polycab release; InvestyWise on KEI; Business Upturn on Havells; cables2 deep files 01–02]. On a ~₹1.2 lakh cr FY27 market, Polycab's run-rate is roughly a quarter of the whole market (derived).
- Industry utilisation ~75% in FY26; capacity to rise **20–22% by end-FY27, nearly half from new players** — versus ~10% volume growth [Crisil Ratings, 2-Jun-26].
- **UltraTech (Aditya Birla) — "Ultravolt":** ₹1,800 cr plant at Jhagadia, Bharuch (Gujarat), 10.98 lakh km a year, commercial production from 1-Sep-26 (ahead of the Dec-26 target); focus on house wires and light-duty cables [ICICI Direct, 1-Sep-26; The Core, 2-Jul-26].
- **Adani:** entry via Kutch Copper/Praneetha Ventures; plant near Ahmedabad, foundation laid Sep-25; no commissioning date disclosed [News: The Core, 2-Jul-26].
- Incumbent capex: Polycab ₹1,200–1,600 cr a year, ₹6,000–8,000 cr over five years ("Project Spring"); KEI Sanand greenfield ~₹1,700 cr, now at 50% use, ₹1,500–2,000 cr revenue in FY27, to be stretched to ~₹7,000 cr revenue capacity, then ₹600–700 cr capex a year for 3–4 years; Havells ₹1,400 cr FY27 capex mainly cables; RR Kabel ₹1,200 cr modular capex FY26–FY28, up to 220 kV [Multibagg May-26; KEI Q1 FY27 call via InvestyWise/Sahi; Business Upturn Jul-26; RR Kabel Q4 FY26 call via deep file 01]. Crisil counted ₹8,000–8,500 cr of sector capex for FY25–FY26 alone [Crisil Ratings, 26-Mar-25].
- Small caps adding too: Dynamic ₹40–45 cr greenfield (Sep-26); Paramount ₹300 cr Narmadapuram (FY28); JD Cables +28,000 km (Sep-26); Advait's Gangad plant (Q4 FY27) [company files 05–08].
- Wire capacity already under-used: RR Kabel runs wires at 65–70% vs cables at 90%+ [RR Kabel Q1 FY27 call via deep file 01] — so UltraTech's volume lands in the segment with the most spare capacity.

## Where profit sits
- Leaders earn low-teens: Polycab W&C EBIT ~13.3% in Q1 FY27 per press reports, up sequentially (C&W margin ~13% in Q4 FY26, −2 pts YoY); management's medium-term W&C band 11–13% [FM Live; Investing.com Q1 FY27 call; Multibagg May-26]. KEI targets 11–12% operating margin [KEI Q1 FY27 call].
- Pressure visible: Havells cables EBIT margin fell from ~12.6% to 10.4% in Q1 FY27 as input costs rose faster than prices [Business Upturn, 17-Jul-26].
- Mid/small caps: Dynamic ~10.5–11% (steady); JD Cables ~13% FY26 (12% in H2); Paramount 6.6% (Q1 FY27, recovering from 3.5% in FY26); Advait's transmission-products segment ~17% but its solar EPC ~5% [company files 05–08].
- Crisil expects absolute operating profit up only 12–13% in FY27 against revenue +28–30% — i.e., margins compress as price inflates the top line; debt/EBITDA 0.5–0.6x and interest cover 16–17x stay strong [Crisil Ratings via KNN India, 3-Jun-26].
- Profit pool: highest in EHV/HV power cables with approvals (KEI EHV sales +47% to ₹186 cr in Q1; EHV order book ₹793 cr), niche transmission products and B2C brands with pricing power; lowest in LT cables/conductors sold by tender and in EPC [KEI Q1 FY27 call; company files].

## Cycle position
- Mid-to-late upcycle: demand still strong (T&D, RE, DC), but FY27 growth is mostly price; capacity is growing twice as fast as volume; new deep-pocketed entrants arrive in wires just as housing sales cool. Expect margin pressure first in house wires and commodity LT cables, least in EHV/HV and export-approved products.
- Working capital is the stress point for smaller players: Paramount, Advait and JD Cables all had negative operating cash flow in FY26 [Screener, via company files].

## Outlook FY27–FY29 (forecaster named)
- **FY27 — Crisil Ratings (2-Jun-26):** revenue +28–30%; volume ~10%; realisations +18–20%; absolute operating profit +12–13%; capacity +20–22% by year-end, half from new players.
- **FY27–FY31 — Mordor Intelligence (in Paramount AR FY26):** ~9% a year in USD terms 2026–2031 (roughly 11–13% in rupees if the rupee keeps depreciating 2–4% a year — my conversion).
- **To FY30 — Polycab:** ₹1.7 lakh cr domestic market by 2030 (~13–14% a year from ~₹1 lakh cr in FY26, my arithmetic); Polycab aims to grow 1.5x the market [Whalesbook Sep-26; Polycab Q1 FY27 call].
- **Metals — J.P. Morgan (Aug-26):** aluminium ~14% lower in 2027 than 2026 and copper flat — so the FY27 price tailwind should fade or reverse in FY28, making FY28 value growth closer to volume growth.
- **Company guidance for FY27:** KEI 20%+ CAGR; RR Kabel W&C volume 16–18%; Dynamic 18–20% long-term; Paramount 15–20%; Advait 40%+ (deck FY27P +75%); JD Cables 40–50% [company calls].
- **My synthesis:** FY27 revenue +25–30% (mostly price), FY28–FY29 value growth ~11–14% a year with volume ~10–12%, provided T&D ordering (CEA plan, RDSS) holds; industry EBIT margins likely drift down 0.5–1.5 pts from FY26 levels by FY28 as ~20% new capacity and UltraTech/Adani compete in wires; companies with HV/EHV approvals, exports and B2C brands should hold margins best.
