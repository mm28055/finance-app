# Advait Energy Transitions (NSE/BSE: ADVAIT, 543230) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹1,906 (52-week ₹1,321–2,486), market cap ₹2,087 cr, P/E 36.1, book value ₹254, ROCE 27.9%, ROE 21.5%, dividend yield 0.10% [Screener consolidated, 27-Sep-26]. Formerly Advait Infratech; BSE main board Jul-23, NSE main board 20-Jan-26 [IP Q1FY27 slide 3]. Promoter holding 73.5% (Jun-24) → 66.76% (Jun-26), diluted by equity raises in FY25; no pledge shown [Screener shareholding]. **No Q1 FY27 earnings call was held** — only a Q1 presentation (10-Aug-26); the latest two calls are Q3 FY26 (12-Feb-26) and Q4 FY26 (1-Jun-26).

## Business and segments
- Not a cable maker in the usual sense: two divisions. **PTS (Power Transmission Solutions)** — makes OPGW (optical-fibre earth wire for transmission towers), ACS wire, emergency restoration systems (ERS, temporary towers), stringing tools, and does live-line OPGW installation, HTLS re-conductoring and discom (RDSS) EPC. **NRE (New & Renewable Energy)** via 69%-owned Advait Greenergy (AGPL) — solar EPC (balance of plant), BESS (battery storage) EPC, green-hydrogen electrolysers, fuel cells (JV/tech deal with AVL and TECO), carbon credits [IP Q1FY27 slides 2, 5; Official Q4 transcript].
- FY26 segment revenue (₹ cr): PTS 459.2 (FY25 301.6), segment result 79.0 (17.2% margin); NRE 268.1 (FY25 103.0), result 14.0 (5.2%) [AR FY26 consolidated note 45]. So PTS earns ~85% of segment profit on ~63% of revenue.
- Standalone (mostly PTS) FY26 revenue ₹447.7 cr, EBITDA margin 15.8%; FY26 PTS product split: live-line/discom projects ₹223.2 cr (50%), stringing tools ₹73.5 cr, OPGW supply ₹59.1 cr, HTLS re-conductoring ₹41.3 cr, ACS ₹26.4 cr, ERS ₹6.5 cr [IP Q1FY27 slides 12, 14].
- AGPL FY26 revenue ₹267.4 cr (solar EPC ₹211.9 cr = 79%; BESS EPC ₹49.1 cr; GH2 EPC ₹6.2 cr), PAT ₹10.4 cr [IP Q1FY27 slides 18–20].
- Geography: 98% India; outside India ₹3.9 cr in FY26 [AR FY26 note 45(b)].
- Order book ₹503.5 cr (Mar-25) → ₹1,048 cr (Dec-25) → ₹1,304 cr (Mar-26; PTS 64%, NRE 36%) → **₹1,330 cr (Jun-26; PTS 71%, NRE 29%)**; the deck says the order book **includes L1 positions** (bids where Advait is lowest but not yet awarded). Q1 inflow ~₹255 cr [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27 slides 7, 26].
- Capacity (Kadi, Gujarat): OPGW 7,000 km/yr (to 10,000), 300+ ERS towers; Unit 2 (2026) 30 MW electrolyser semi-assembly [IP Q1FY27 slide 23].

## Guidance history
- **FY26 revenue:** Q3 call guided "approximately 40% to 45%" for FY26 even though nine-month growth was already 138% (the stored analysis says the Q2 call had targeted 50–60%; not re-verified); delivered +80% [Official Q3 transcript; Official Q4 transcript]. **Beaten (after sandbagging).**
- **FY27 revenue:** Q4 call "40% plus", called "a little conservative"; Q1 deck shows **FY27P revenue ₹1,251 cr (+75%)** [Official Q4 transcript; IP Q1FY27 slide 6]. Q1 delivered +51%. **Effectively raised.**
- **FY27 margin:** Q4 call "at least 1%" EBITDA margin improvement over FY26's 11.7%; Q1 13.8% [Official Q4 transcript; IP Q1FY27]. **On track.**
- **Order book:** Q4 call — ₹1,600–1,650 cr order book by end-FY27, pipeline ~₹2,000 cr; the Q1 deck restates this as "expected order inflow for FY27 ₹1,600–1,650 cr" — a different (larger) claim [Official Q4 transcript; IP Q1FY27 slide 6]. Q1 book ₹1,330 cr. **Wording shifted.**
- **NRE mix:** Q3 call — NRE mix to rise "5% to 7% … maybe 10%" a year; Q4 call — FY27 order mix ~65:35 PTS:NRE; Jun-26 book is 71:29 [Official Q3 transcript; Official Q4 transcript; IP Q1FY27]. **Behind.**
- **Electrolysers:** Q2 call 10 MW by Jan-26 → Q3 call 30 MW by 15-Mar-26 → installed as 30 MW semi-assembly unit in 2026; 100 MW phase by Q4 FY27; ~₹200 cr capex through FY27; first full year revenue ₹250–300 cr (FY28) at 8–10% net margin; Q4 call says 5–10% margin at start, ~20% later [Official Q3 transcript; Official Q4 transcript; IP Q1FY27 slide 23]. **Slipped once, now "on plan".**
- **BESS factory (2.5 GWh):** Q3 call "commission by October"; Q4 call "operational September–October", ₹100–200 cr FY27 revenue, ₹1,000 cr+ from FY28; Q1 deck — plant & machinery ordered from HG Tech (China), "arrival and installation scheduled for Q3 FY27" [Official Q3 transcript; Official Q4 transcript; IP Q1FY27 slide 20]. **Slipping by ~1 quarter.**
- **Solar EPC:** Q4 call — full EPC (with modules) orders "in this year itself"; Q1 solar revenue collapsed to ₹3.1 cr [Official Q4 transcript; IP Q1FY27]. **Not yet delivered.**
- **Khavda (Adani) solar:** 75 MW commissioned by Q4 FY26; remaining 67.5 MW promised for Q1 FY27 — completed in Q1 but full commissioning moved to Q2 FY27 [Official Q4 transcript; IP Q1FY27 slide 20]. **Slight slip.**

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹100 cr in PTS (internal funds) plus electrolyser/BESS spend; fixed assets ₹50 cr → ₹85 cr, CWIP ₹2 cr → ₹59 cr; free cash flow −₹119 cr [Official Q4 transcript; Screener].
- FY27 plan: **₹300–350 cr** — ₹137 cr ex-IPP, ~₹75 cr into subsidiaries (BESS, electrolysers), rest for owned BESS projects [Official Q4 transcript]. That is ~1.1x FY26 net worth (₹291 cr).
- Unit 3, Gangad/Dholera: 4 lakh sq ft integrated plant for OPGW, ERS, tools, specialised conductors (12,000 km), BESS (2.5 GWh), electrolysers (300 MW), PEM fuel cells; operational Q4 FY27 [IP Q1FY27 slide 23].
- Owned BESS assets (AURA/ABBPL): GUVNL 50 MW/100 MWh Radhanpur (COD Nov-26; VGF ₹27 lakh/MWh) and 150 MW/300 MWh Bhesan-Junagadh (COD Nov-27; VGF ₹18 lakh/MWh); "1 GW" asset platform ambition, 5 GW roadmap [IP Q1FY27 slides 4, 24]. CRISIL cites ₹140 cr BESS capex, debt plus equity funded [CRISIL Mar-26].
- Cells: MSA with Hithium (18-Sep-26) for up to 1 GWh of LFP cells over a year at US$49.5/kWh for the first 350 MWh [BSE filing 19-Sep-26].
- Returns claimed: manufacturing ROCE 25–30%, EPC 15–25%, development (owned assets) 12–15% [Official Q3 transcript] — the mix is shifting toward the lowest-return bucket.
- Funding: "equity route, but more predominantly … debt route" [Official Q4 transcript]. AGM (21-Sep-26) raised borrowing limit to ₹1,500 cr and loan/guarantee limit to ₹800 cr; approved loans up to ₹100 cr to AURA, ₹65 cr to ABBPL, ₹100 cr to AGPL, and related-party dealings with AGPL up to ₹700 cr [AR FY26 AGM notice items 7–12].
- AGPL fundraise: Q3 call said AGPL would raise ₹90–100 cr from outside investors "in the next 2 to 3 months"; instead the parent's stake rose from 66.91% to 69.00% (Mar-26) and 74.63% per the AGM notice — no outside raise disclosed [Official Q3 transcript; AR FY26 consolidation note; AGM notice].

- Approvals: OPGW approval from 10+ utilities in FY26, PGCIL OPGW approval in Q1 FY27, NABL-accredited lab [Official Q4 transcript; IP Q1FY27 slide 15].

## Risks and red flags
- **Capital intensity jump:** ₹300–350 cr capex in FY27 against negative operating cash flow and ₹55 cr profit; shift from asset-light EPC to factories and owned BESS assets (12–15% ROCE by its own numbers).
- **Possible intra-group revenue:** Q4 call said Q4 BESS revenue (~₹43 cr) was EPC done by AGPL for the group's own build-own-operate project; AETL bought ₹58.4 cr from AGPL in FY26, yet consolidated revenue (₹714.5 cr) is almost exactly standalone (₹447.7 cr) + AGPL (₹267.4 cr) and segment revenue shows no inter-segment elimination. Could not reconcile; needs checking whether group-own asset construction is booked as revenue [Official Q4 transcript; AR FY26 standalone RPT note; note 45; IP Q1FY27].
- **Contingent liabilities ₹362.8 cr** (₹211.8 cr a year earlier): bank guarantees ₹211.4 cr, LCs/bills ₹104.9 cr, ₹20 cr corporate guarantee to ICICI for a subsidiary, surety bonds ₹22.5 cr, customs demand ₹3.98 cr (2020-21, under penalty sections 114A/114AA, pending appeal) [AR FY26 consolidated note 42; standalone CARO (vii)].
- **Audit coverage:** subsidiaries/JV with revenue of ₹268.6 cr (~38% of group) audited by other auditors; foreign subsidiary Advait Energy Holding AS (being voluntarily wound up) unaudited, management-certified — ₹2.88 cr revenue and ₹1.32 cr profit on ₹0.73 lakh of assets; a ₹19.9 cr loan to it at Mar-25 is nil at Mar-26. Opinion unmodified; KAM only revenue recognition; the consolidated report reuses "standalone" wording [AR FY26 auditor's reports].
- **Disclosure quality:** MD&A ratio table says "FY26 ratios to be updated upon finalisation of audited financial statements" in the final annual report; Q4 call deflected who owns the 20% of the new carbon subsidiary ("ask on email") [AR FY26 MD&A; Official Q4 transcript].
- **Family roles:** MD's daughter Rutvi Sheth (Head HR & Strategy; pay ceiling raised to ₹55 lakh) and son-in-law Vatsal Kundalia (MD of AGPL; ceiling ₹75 lakh); Rejal Sheth (MD's wife) stepped down as CFO Nov-25, Narayan Singh appointed [AR FY26 AGM notice items 5–6; note 44].
- **Customer concentration / government tenders:** Gujarat utilities (GETCO, DGVCL, GUVNL) dominate orders; Q1 inflow included ~₹256 cr of Gujarat discom EPC [IP Q1FY27 slide 15].
- **Execution slips:** electrolyser (10 MW Jan-26 → 30 MW Mar-26), BESS factory (Oct → Q3 FY27 machinery), Khavda (Q1 → Q2 FY27), solar EPC orders not yet visible.

## What changed between the two calls (Q3 FY26 Feb-26 → Q4 FY26 Jun-26), plus Q1 deck
- Tone went from cautious ("capacity and capability building zone"; NRE margin catch-up "2 to 3 years") to expansive (40%+ growth, +1 pt margin, ₹300–350 cr capex, four new subsidiaries).
- Numbers: NRE share of order book 16% → 36% (Mar-26) → back to 29% (Jun-26). Capex frame grew from ₹100 cr PTS + ₹180–200 cr AGPL to ₹300–350 cr for FY27.
- New: owned BESS projects (asset ownership), fuel-cell JV (AVL/TECO) with 1.5–3 years to a plant, ALMM (domestic cell) rules as a tailwind.
- Dropped: the AGPL outside fundraise of ₹90–100 cr (not mentioned in Q4 call); insulator assembly plan.
- Q1 deck: margins up (13.8%), solar EPC near zero, FY27P revenue ₹1,251 cr.

## My read
Advait's core transmission-products business is genuinely good — niche, ~16–17% margins, growing 50% a year — and Q1 FY27 margins recovered to ~14%. But the group is now spending more than its net worth on BESS, electrolysers, fuel cells and owned storage assets, funded by debt, while operating cash flow was negative in FY26 and the revenue booked on group-own BESS construction needs checking. At ~36x trailing profit the price already assumes the deck's +75% FY27 and a successful factory start; I would want to see positive operating cash flow, the BESS plant running by Q4 FY27, and clean elimination of intra-group EPC before believing the NRE story.
