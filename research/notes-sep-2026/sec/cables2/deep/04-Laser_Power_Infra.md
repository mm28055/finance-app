# Laser Power & Infra — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code LASERPOWER (consolidated; one 51% subsidiary UIC Udyog and an SPV — standalone ≈ consolidated). Listed 16 July 2026 after a ₹742 cr IPO. Price ₹272, market cap ₹3,819 cr, range since listing ₹250–344 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026; IP Q1FY27 slide 6].

## Business and segments
- Two segments: Manufacturing (power cables, conductors, aluminium rods, specialty products) and EPC (power-distribution projects: lines, 33/11 kV substations, underground cabling for DISCOMs under RDSS/DDUGJY-type schemes). FY26 split: manufacturing ₹1,691 cr (72.7%), EPC ₹635 cr (27.3%) [IP Q1FY27 slide 5].
- Q1 FY27: manufacturing revenue ₹382 cr (−2.7% y/y), EBITDA ₹38.8 cr (~10%); EPC ₹139 cr (+129%), EBITDA ₹27.6 cr (~20%). Management says EPC margins "roughly 15–20%" but lumpy by project stage [Q1 call].
- Within manufacturing ~90% cables, ~10% conductors; conventional conductors are the lowest-margin line; HT (high-tension) cables rose from 9% to ~29% of revenue in nine quarters (Acuité: 12% FY25 → 24–25% FY26); HT cables earn ~2–3 points more EBITDA margin than LT cables [Q1 call; Acuité May-26].
- Capacity: three West Bengal plants (two at Dhulagarh, one at Kharagpur acquired via NCLT scheme from Bhuvee Stenovate in 2023), 85,448 t. Production was flat while capacity rose 38%: FY24 53,191 t (85.8% use), FY25 55,724 t (76.2%), FY26 52,629 t (61.6%) [IP Q1FY27 slides 6, 12; Q1 call].
- Customers: 65% of revenue from government (DISCOMs in J&K, Kerala, Bihar, WB, UP, Odisha, Jharkhand, Assam, Rajasthan; Indian Railways); EPC is 100% government; manufacturing ~50:50 government/private (Tata Power, CESC); exports to African utilities (Ethiopia, Togo, Rwanda, Uganda) [IP Q1FY27 slides 11, 14; Q1 call].
- Technology: licensed stranding partner (since March 2025) of US-based TS Conductor for AECC-type HTLS (high-temperature, low-sag) conductors used to re-conductor existing lines; the carbon core is imported from TS Conductor; Laser strands and installs [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 slide 15].
- Order book: ₹2,788 cr at June 2026 (manufacturing ₹1,433 cr, EPC ₹1,356 cr) [IP Q1FY27 slide 25].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener consolidated; revenue / operating profit / OPM / PAT, ₹ cr): Q1 FY26 454 / 52 / 12% / 49 (includes a one-off gain; standalone PAT ₹16 cr); Q4 FY26 674 / 86 / 13% / 36; Q1 FY27 522 / 66 / 13% / 21. Only these quarters are on Screener [Screener].
- Annual (consolidated): revenue FY24 ₹1,748 cr → FY25 ₹2,570 cr → FY26 ₹2,326 cr (−9.5%); operating profit ₹156 → ₹250 → ₹301 cr; OPM 9% → 10% → 13%; PAT ₹40 → ₹107 → ₹152 cr. Standalone FY26 revenue ₹2,326 cr vs ₹2,438 cr (−4.6%), PAT ₹119 cr [Screener; IP Q1FY27 slide 17].
- FY26 consolidated PAT includes a one-time ₹32 cr gain; management says the comparable base is ~₹120 cr [Q1 call].
- Finance cost is the swing factor: interest ₹133 cr in FY26 (44% of operating profit); Q1 FY27 ₹36 cr, ~55% of EBITDA [Screener; Q1 call].
- Unit economics (my arithmetic): FY26 manufacturing revenue ₹1,691 cr on 52,629 t produced ≈ ₹3.2 lakh per ton; operating profit per ton not disclosed.
- ROCE 20.5% (consolidated), ROE 16.8% [Screener].

## Guidance history
- There was no Q4 FY26 call (company was unlisted); the Q1 FY27 call (11 Aug 2026) is its first. Guidance there: revenue to keep growing ~15–16% a year like the last five years; EBITDA margins "stable"; annual interest saving ~₹40 cr from the ₹490 cr IPO-funded debt repayment (full effect from Q3 FY27); operating cash flow to turn positive; working-capital days typically 100–120; EPC share of revenue to stay similar; HTLS orders expected "in the next few quarters" [Q1 call].
- Check against record: the "15–16%" claim does not fit FY26, when revenue fell ~5% (standalone) / ~10% (consolidated); the 3-year CAGR is 21% (FY23–26) [Screener]. Management attributes flat volumes to the mix shift toward HT cables [Q1 call; Acuité May-26].
- HTLS pipeline: bids in ~₹1,250 cr of ₹3,500 cr HTLS/re-conductoring tenders floated over a year; Acuité (May 2026) quoted ~₹1,000 cr bid and an expected 10–15% win rate; first commercial HTLS re-conductoring order announced in Q1 (value undisclosed) [Q1 call; Acuité May-26; IP Q1FY27 slide 24].

## Capex and capacity
- Recent capex ₹43–49 cr a year (FY24–FY26); installed capacity up from 62,000 t to 85,448 t in three years; current plan is to use existing capacity — "future capex will be undertaken in a phased manner"; policy is to add capacity when utilisation reaches 75–80% [IP Q1FY27 slide 18; Q1 call].
- New: provisional LOI (24 Sep 2026) from WBIDC for ~40 acres at Vidyasagar Industrial Park, Kharagpur (99-year lease) for an integrated power cables and conductors plant — no cost, capacity or timeline disclosed [BSE filing 24-Sep-26].
- IPO use: ~₹490 cr of fresh issue used to repay loans; gross debt now ~₹360 cr against ~₹240 cr of fixed deposits held mainly as bank margin money [Q1 call].
- Payback: not disclosed. Asset turns are high (net fixed-asset turnover ~11× on FY26 revenue vs ₹214 cr fixed assets — my arithmetic), so the constraint is working capital, not plant.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹871 cr at Mar-26 (consolidated), up from ₹504 cr; post-IPO gross debt ~₹360 cr; management calls net debt "negligible" after counting ₹240 cr FDs, though those are largely pledged as margin money [Screener; Q1 call].
- Cash flow: CFO −₹119 cr in FY26 (₹60 cr FY25, ₹171 cr FY24); free cash flow −₹162 cr [Screener].
- Days (Screener FY26): debtors 216 (159 FY25), inventory 124, payables 173, cash conversion cycle 168 days. Company's working-capital days: 77 (FY24) → 86 (FY25) → 129 (FY26); working capital ₹1,021 cr [Screener; IP Q1FY27 slide 18]. ~₹800 cr of EPC projects started in Q4 FY26–Q1 FY27 tie up cash; FG/WIP inventory rose ₹90 cr in Q1 [Q1 call].
- Taxes: an FY23 acquisition brought ~₹500 cr of carried-forward losses; ~₹125–130 cr tax saving booked as deferred tax — cash tax starts from FY28 [Q1 call].
- Dividend: none [Screener].
- Rating: Acuité A+/Positive/A1+ reaffirmed (5 May 2026) on ₹2,500 cr bank lines; interest cover 2.6× and debt/EBITDA 1.87× in FY25; gross current assets 260 days [Acuité May-26].

## Moat evidence
- Integration: in-house aluminium rod mill and XLPE/PVC compounding; manufacturing feeds own EPC [IP Q1FY27 slides 11–12].
- Approvals: RDSO-approved railway supplier; pre-qualified with many DISCOMs; 43 EPC projects completed, 34 ongoing [IP Q1FY27 slides 5, 13].
- Technology licence: TS Conductor AECC; tenders are open to rival HTLS technologies (Apar, Sterlite use CTC Global) — "all partners, everybody are eligible to bid" [Q1 call].
- Weak points: conductors are commoditised; volumes flat for three years; heavy dependence on state DISCOM spending; no pricing-power evidence disclosed.

## Risks and red flags
- Order-book inconsistency: presentation shows Mar-26 order book ₹3,243 cr (manufacturing ₹1,669 cr + EPC ₹1,575 cr), but Acuité's May 2026 rationale gives ₹2,443 cr at the same date (manufacturing ₹724 cr + EPC ₹1,719 cr) [IP Q1FY27 slide 16; Acuité May-26].
- Receivables: 216 debtor days; government DISCOM exposure; operating cash flow negative in FY26 [Screener].
- Related parties (FY25 AR — the FY26 annual report on the company website is a scanned image with no text layer, so FY25 was read): purchases from promoter-linked P.S. Enterprise ₹36.6 cr, Ceebuild ₹27.5 cr, 51% subsidiary UIC Udyog ₹27.0 cr, Lumino Power ₹3.7 cr; rent to promoter company Devesh Buildcon ₹1.4 cr; factory electricity from two promoter family trusts ₹1.1 cr; loans from promoter entities Laser Solar LLP and AJ Finance (repaid) [AR FY25 Note 44.9].
- Contingent liabilities (Mar-25): income tax ₹38.1 cr, GST ₹4.5 cr (High Court stay), factoring recourse ₹29.4 cr, product guarantees ₹3.6 cr; entry-tax ₹10 cr provided after losing in Calcutta High Court, SLP pending in Supreme Court [AR FY25 Note 44.1–44.2].
- Auditor (FY25): unmodified opinion; audit trail not enabled at database level; CARO — Mar-25 stock statement to banks showed current assets ₹93.4 cr lower than books (₹1,366 cr vs ₹1,459 cr) [AR FY25 auditor's report and CARO ii(b)].
- Governance: company secretary resigned 23 Sep 2026 (effective 5 Oct), two months after listing, citing personal reasons; two zero-holding promoter-group entities reclassified to public; former whole-time director Navin Saffar left the board in June 2025 but runs operations as ED & COO [BSE filing 23-Sep-26; AR FY25 Note 44.9; IP Q1FY27].
- Customer concentration in government schemes; EPC timing risk; HTLS depends on an imported core and a single licensor.

## My read
The business improved in quality in FY26 — margins rose from ~10% to 13% on a shift to HT cables and EPC — but not in size: revenue fell and plant utilisation slid to ~62%. The post-IPO debt paydown should add ~₹40 cr a year to pre-tax profit, which is the most reliable earnings driver for FY27; HTLS re-conductoring is a real option but still at the tender stage with competing technologies. Cash is the weak point — 216 debtor days, negative operating cash flow and heavy reliance on state DISCOMs — and early governance signals (CS exit, promoter-linked purchases, order-book numbers that differ between the company and its rating agency) warrant caution. The price needs revenue growth back in the mid-teens with 13% margins held; clear HTLS wins and a positive FY27 operating cash flow would improve the view, while further working-capital build-up would weaken it.
