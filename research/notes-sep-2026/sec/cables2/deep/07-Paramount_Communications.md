# Paramount Communications (NSE: PARACABLES / BSE: 530555) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹59.8 (52-week ₹28.3–75.7), market cap ₹1,957 cr, P/E 32.1, book value ₹25.5, ROCE 12.4%, ROE 8.0%, no dividend [Screener consolidated, 27-Sep-26]. Promoter holding 49.19% (Jun-26) → **45.88% (Jul-26)** after a preferential issue to outside investors; no pledge disclosed [Screener shareholding]. Credit rating only **ICRA BBB (Stable)/A3+**, reaffirmed 14-Jul-26 and taken off "watch negative" [ICRA Jul-26].

## Business and segments
- Delhi-based maker of power cables (LT/HT up to 33 kV; 66 kV approved but little made), telecom cables (optical fibre, FTTH, jelly-filled), railway signalling/axle-counter cables, house wires, solar, fire-survival, EV and HTLS-type conductors; also small EPC (OPGW, submarine cable repair). 25+ product lines, 2,500+ SKUs; plants at Dharuhera (Haryana) and Khushkhera (Rajasthan), both at "near-optimal" / ~100% use [IP Q1FY27; Official Q4 transcript; Official Q1 transcript].
- Single reported segment (wires and cables); no product-wise profit disclosed [AR FY26 note 43].
- FY26 revenue ₹1,912 cr: India institutional (B2B) ₹1,001 cr (+37%), B2G ₹182 cr (flat, ~10%), B2C retail ₹179 cr (+11%), exports (mostly US) ₹550 cr (+14%, 29%) [IP Q1FY27 revenue-mix slide; Official Q4 transcript].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹529 cr: power cables 57.2%; domestic ₹374 cr (71%) — B2B ₹296 cr (+92%), B2C ₹44 cr (+15%), B2G ₹35 cr; exports ₹155 cr (29.3%) [Official Q1 transcript; IP Q1FY27].
- Exports swing hard: 22% of sales (FY22) → 50% (FY23) → 26% (FY24) → 31% (FY25) → 43% (H1 FY26) → 16% (H2 FY26) → 29% (Q1 FY27). US is ~all of it; 8 US distributors (2 in FY22); largest Indian exporter of ≤600 V cables to the US in CY2025 [IP Q1FY27; Official Q4 transcript].
- Metal throughput 9,691 t (FY23) → 16,987 → 26,601 → **29,664 t (FY26, +12%)**; Q1 FY27 volume only **+4% YoY** [IP Q1FY27; Official Q1 transcript].
- Order book ₹583 cr (Mar-26; exports ₹76 cr) → **₹615 cr (Jun-26; domestic ₹518 cr, exports ₹97 cr, power cables ₹455 cr)**. Deliberately short: no firm-price orders beyond 3–4 months; metal booked the next working day [Official Q1 transcript; IP Q1FY27].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Q1 FY26 451 → Q2 428 → Q3 461 → Q4 573 → **Q1 FY27 529**. Operating profit 15 → 6 → 15 → 30 → **35**; margin 3.4% → 1.4% → 3.3% → 5% → **7% (6.6% company)**. Other income 18 → 20 → 5 → 9 → **3**. Net profit 18 → 13 → 7 → 21 → **20** [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹529.4 cr (+17.4% YoY, −7.7% QoQ); operating profit ₹34.7 cr (+129%, margin 6.6% vs 3.4%); EBITDA incl. other income ₹37.8 cr (7.1% — same as Q1 FY26 because other income fell from ₹18.0 cr to ₹3.1 cr); PAT ₹19.7 cr (+3.7%) [IP Q1FY27 income statement].
- Annual (₹ cr): FY24 sales 1,068, op. profit 89 (8%), PAT 86 (near-zero tax); FY25 1,574, 123 (8%), 87; FY26 1,913 (+22%), 66 (3.5%), 60 [Screener].
- FY26 profit quality: other income ₹51 cr included **₹27.8 cr from maturity of key-man insurance policies** and ~₹11 cr foreign-exchange gains; a ₹2.4–2.5 cr one-time labour-code charge. Without key-man money, FY26 PAT would be roughly ₹39 cr (derived, at ~25% tax) [Official Q4 transcript; ICRA Jul-26; AR FY26 other-income note].
- Unit economics: US exports ran at a **~4% EBITDA loss** in FY26 under 50% tariffs (vs ~1.5 pts above domestic margins before) [Official Q1 transcript].
- 4-year revenue CAGR 34.7% (FY22–26); 3-year profit CAGR 8% [IP Q1FY27; Screener].

## Guidance history
- **FY27 revenue:** Q4 call — "15-20% growth in top line", "minimum 10% of volume growth", assuming current metal prices; Q1 call — 15–20% kept [Official Q4 transcript; Official Q1 transcript]. Q1 delivered +17.4% value but only +4% volume. **Kept; volume behind.**
- **Margin:** Q4 call — back to FY25 (pre-tariff) margins "by end of FY27" (FY25 operating margin 7.7–8%); Q1 call — 6.6% is "a meaningful step", FY25 level "at least by the last quarter of FY27" [same]. **On track.**
- **Exports:** Q4 call — US demand "tepid", good pull "Q2 onwards"; Q1 call — ₹155 cr in Q1 (+77% QoQ), FY27 exports ₹700–800 cr [same]. **Ahead of plan in Q1.**
- **Receivables/working capital:** Q4 call — Mar-26 receivables spike (65% of Q4 dispatches in the last half of March, mostly against LCs) "will be normalized within this quarter"; cycle 90–100 days. Q1: receivable days 79 → 64, cycle 101 → 96 days [same]. **Met.**
- **Narmadapuram (MP) plant:** ₹300 cr phase 1; partial start Q1 FY28; ₹500 cr revenue FY28, ₹1,200 cr FY29 (~4x asset turn at ~75% use; 5.5–6x after phase 2); 9–10% EBITDA margin; payback 3–4 years; 132 kV approvals ~6–12 months after production, all by FY29; E-beam cables certified by end-FY29 [Official Q4 transcript; Official Q1 transcript]. **Kept; ~₹30 cr+ spent by Aug-26.**
- **Long term:** ₹5,000 cr revenue by FY31 (phase 1 + existing plants ~₹3,600–4,000 cr; phase 2 needed beyond) [Official Q4 transcript].
- **Funding:** Q4 call — ₹122 cr equity (₹30 cr promoters, ₹92 cr investors), internal accruals, "modest" debt; Q1 call — "we have not taken any debt as yet", D/E to stay below 0.3x [same]. ICRA: term debt "yet to be tied up", expected by Q3 FY27 [ICRA Jul-26]. **In progress.**

## Capex and capacity
- Maintenance/debottlenecking: ~₹50–55 cr a year for three years (₹155–160 cr cumulative) in existing plants; now space-constrained [Official Q4 transcript; Official Q1 transcript]. Fixed assets ₹169 cr (FY24) → ₹221 cr → ₹260 cr (FY26) [Screener].
- Narmadapuram: 31 acres from MPIDC; ₹300 cr outlay over FY27–FY28 (ICRA: ₹175–200 cr in FY27); products — EHV cables up to 132 kV (220/400 kV being studied), specialised transmission conductors, E-beam and EPR/elastomer cables; fungible with 11/33 kV so it can sell from day one; long-lead machinery already ordered; civil works 6–7 months [Official Q4 transcript; Official Q1 transcript; ICRA Jul-26].
- Payback check (derived): ₹1,200 cr sales × 9–10% margin ≈ ₹110–120 cr EBITDA a year on ₹300 cr capex plus working capital — attractive if approvals and volumes arrive on time.
- HTLS-type conductors: ~₹100 cr of business in hand [Official Q1 transcript].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹103 cr (FY24) → ₹33 cr (FY25) → **₹123 cr (FY26)**; ICRA total debt/net worth 0.27x (0.09x), TOL/TNW 0.56x; company quotes D/E 0.15x [Screener; ICRA Jul-26; IP Q1FY27].
- Operating cash flow ₹104 cr (FY25) → **−₹38 cr (FY26)**; free cash flow −₹91 cr [Screener].
- FY26 debtor days 79 (47), inventory 72, payables 50, cash cycle 101 days; receivables ₹413 cr vs ₹278 cr; NWC/sales 34% [Screener; IP Q1FY27 balance sheet; ICRA Jul-26].
- Liquidity thin: free cash ~₹5 cr, ~₹72 cr of cash encumbered as margin money; uses ECLGS 5.0 support; more working-capital lines being sought (H2 CY2026). US customs has refunded ~₹8 cr of FY26 tariffs; ₹20–22 cr more expected — a one-off gain ahead [ICRA Jul-26].
- CARO: advance-tax instalments of ₹1.75 cr, ₹5.50 cr and ₹0.50 cr paid late (Jun/Sep/Dec-25 quarters) and "few delays" in other statutory dues — a sign of tight cash during the tariff shock [AR FY26 CARO (vii)].
- Equity: 2.20 cr shares to non-promoters (₹92.39 cr) and 72 lakh warrants to promoters (₹30.24 cr; 25% = ₹7.56 cr paid) at ₹42, allotted 1-Jul-26; ₹99.95 cr received; ₹22.7 cr of promoter money due within 12–18 months [AR FY26 Directors' report; ICRA Jul-26]. No dividend.

## Moat evidence
- Approvals: BIS, NTPC, PGCIL, RDSO, UL (USA), LPCB (UK), BASEC; 950+ institutional clients incl. NTPC, PGCIL, L&T, Adani, Tata Steel, Siemens, Reliance [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Jul-26].
- US distribution: 8 distributors, "zero rejections" over six years; customers now bear tariff changes (pass-through) [Official Q4 transcript; Official Q1 transcript].
- Metal risk: short firm-price window and next-day metal booking; ICRA notes raw materials are >80% of cost [ICRA Jul-26].
- Limits: ICRA — "limited pricing power in an intensely competitive market", "relatively modest presence in the retail segment"; margins (3.5–8%) are well below large peers' 10–13% [ICRA Jul-26; Screener].

## Risks and red flags
- **Governance on the equity raise:** in Aug-25 shareholders **rejected** a promoter warrant issue at ₹60/warrant (₹30 cr); in May–Jun-26 a new issue gave promoters warrants at **₹42** — a lower price — alongside ₹92 cr to outsiders, and this time it passed [AR FY26 Corporate Governance report; Directors' report]. Promoters pay only 25% upfront.
- **Earnings quality:** FY26 profit leaned on ₹27.8 cr key-man insurance maturities (the policies had been pledged for a 9.5% loan, repaid on maturity) and forex gains; FY27 will get US tariff refunds (~₹28–30 cr total). Reported P/E overstates core earnings [AR FY26 borrowings note; ICRA Jul-26].
- **Cash and funding:** negative FY26 operating cash flow; term debt for a ₹300 cr project not yet tied up; working-capital lines still being negotiated; ICRA BBB rating [Screener; ICRA Jul-26].
- **Contingent liabilities ~₹151 cr** (₹81 cr): bank guarantees ₹66.7 cr, bills discounted ₹78.1 cr, tax/duty demands ₹6.5 cr (incl. income tax ₹5.29 cr) [AR FY26 note 46].
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; KAMs are generic (doubtful debts, revenue, impairment); CARO notes title deeds of some property still in the name of the erstwhile partnership firm Paramount Cable Corporation [AR FY26 auditor's report, CARO (i)(c)].
- **Legacy:** past one-time settlement with lenders (FY15–16 losses; negative reserves until FY17); UK subsidiary "06196375 Cables Ltd" (ex-AEI Cables) in administration/liquidation; Indian subsidiary Valens Technologies exited Nov-25 after repaying ₹8.9 cr loan [Screener; AR FY26 note on related parties].
- **Family pay:** three third-generation relatives (Dhruv, Tushar, Parth Aggarwal) paid ₹0.70 cr each in FY26, double FY25; MD and CEO ₹1.87–1.89 cr each [AR FY26 RPT note].
- **US policy risk:** exports are ~30% of sales and one market; FY26 showed a tariff shock can erase margins within a quarter.

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 (May-26) was about surviving the tariff year and a receivables spike; Q1 (Aug-26) about "dual-engine growth" and margin recovery.
- Numbers: exports 15% of Q4 sales → 29% of Q1; operating margin 5.2% → 6.6%; receivable days 79 → 64; order book ₹583 cr → ₹615 cr; export order book only ₹76 cr → ₹97 cr (management keeps it short on purpose).
- New in Q1: FY27 export target ₹700–800 cr; solar cables above 600 V and data-centre products for the US; 220/400 kV studied for Narmadapuram; ₹30 cr+ spent on the new plant, no debt yet.
- Dropped: receivables explanation; Q1 volume (+4%) quietly below the "minimum 10%" volume growth mentioned in Q4.

## My read
Paramount showed real flexibility in FY26 — it grew domestic sales by ~₹370 cr half-on-half while US exports fell ~₹210 cr — and Q1 FY27 shows core margins recovering (6.6%) with the tariff gone. But underlying profit is smaller than reported (key-man insurance, forex, tariff refunds), cash flow was negative, the credit rating is only BBB, and the company is space-bound until Narmadapuram starts in FY28. At a reported P/E in the low 30s — nearer 40x core profit — the price already assumes the ₹500 cr → ₹1,200 cr ramp and a return to ~8% margins; watch Q2–Q3 export run-rate, the term-loan tie-up, and 132 kV approvals.
