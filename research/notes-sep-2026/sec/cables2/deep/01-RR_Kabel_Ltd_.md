# RR Kabel Ltd — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code RRKABEL (consolidated). Price ₹2,528, market cap ₹28,596 cr, 52-week range ₹1,180–2,984 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Two segments: Wires & Cables (W&C) ₹2,880 cr revenue in Q1 FY27 (91% of sales, segment margin 9.9%) and FMEG (fans, lights, switches, appliances) ₹288 cr (9%, 0.0% margin, first break-even quarter) [IP Q1FY27 slides 5–7].
- FY26: W&C revenue ₹8,764 cr (+31%), segment profit ₹775.6 cr (+56%, margin 8.9% vs 7.4% FY25); FMEG ₹958.6 cr (+3%) with a segment loss of about ₹33 cr (FY25 loss ₹45.9 cr, FY24 ₹60 cr) [Q4 call; Ind-Ra Sep-26].
- Product mix in W&C FY26: 73% wires (house wires, where RR is a top retail brand) and 27% cables; management expects cables to reach ~31% by end-FY27 [Q4 call].
- Exports: 29% of revenue in Q1 FY27 (25% in Q4 FY26), about 26% of FY26 turnover; 66+ countries, 42+ international certifications (BASEC, UL, VDE, CSA, LPCB). Europe and Middle East are the largest markets; Middle East ≈ 40% of exports ≈ 12% of group revenue. Company claims to be India's largest W&C exporter [IP Q1FY27 slide 7; AR FY26 MD&A p.60–62; Q4 call].
- Margins differ sharply by channel: domestic wires 11–12% vs export wires ~5%; export cables 11–12% vs domestic cables 6–7%, which management wants to lift to 10–11% with scale [Q1 call].
- Domestic geography: North + West ≈ 65% of domestic sales; South improving; East still weak [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A p.62].
- Capacity & utilisation: five plants (Waghodia, Silvassa, Roorkee, Bengaluru, Gagret). Cables ran at ~90%+ utilisation; wires at 65–70% [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A p.60]. Current cable capability up to 66 kV; new capex takes it to 220 kV [Q4 call]. FMEG: fans capacity 3.3 mn units, switches/switchgear 15.3 mn units; about one-third of FMEG made in-house, two-thirds outsourced, and management intends to keep it so [AR FY26 p.63; Q1 call].
- Distribution: 7,200+ distributors/dealers, 2,15,000+ retail touchpoints at FY26 end (the call said "more than 1.5 lakh"), 6.65 lakh electricians on loyalty platforms; dealer count being consolidated, 20–25k retailers added last year [AR FY26 MD&A p.60; Q1 call].
- New: on 24–25 Sep 2026 the board approved buying U M Cables' (Usha Martin subsidiary) optical-fibre-cable business by slump sale for ₹77 cr cash — entry into OFC/communication cables with ready plant and approvals [BSE filing 25-Sep-26].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener, consolidated; revenue / operating profit / OPM / PAT, ₹ cr): Q1 FY26 2,059 / 142 / 7% / 90; Q2 2,164 / 176 / 8% / 116; Q3 2,536 / 204 / 8% / 118; Q4 2,964 / 262 / 9% / 168; Q1 FY27 3,168 / 283 / 9% / 205 [Screener].
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹6,595 cr → FY25 ₹7,618 cr → FY26 ₹9,722 cr (+28%); operating profit ₹462 → ₹487 → ₹786 cr (+61%); OPM 7% → 6% → 8%; PAT ₹298 → ₹312 → ₹492 cr (+58%). TTM revenue ₹10,832 cr, TTM PAT ₹608 cr [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 PAT of ₹205 cr (+129%) is flattered: other income jumped to ₹32.9 cr from ₹12.4 cr (mostly forex gains on a weaker rupee — management said this is "not a normal run rate"), and a ₹13.8 cr exceptional gain reversed part of the Q3 FY26 labour-code charge (₹19 cr). Operating EBITDA still roughly doubled (₹285 cr vs ₹143 cr) [IP Q1FY27 slide 8; Q1 call].
- Volumes: W&C volume +17% in Q1 FY27 (cables >25%, wires ~12%), after +10% in Q4 FY26 and ~16% for FY26. Ind-Ra puts FY26 volume at 69,864 tonnes vs 57,779 tonnes in FY25 [Q1 call; Q4 call (official); Ind-Ra Sep-26].
- Price: metal prices ~40% higher y/y lifted product prices ~25%; management expected Q2 LME inflation of ~30% y/y if prices held [Q1 call].
- Unit economics (my arithmetic from the above): FY26 W&C revenue ≈ ₹12.5 lakh per tonne and segment profit ≈ ₹1.1 lakh per tonne [derived from Q4 call + Ind-Ra].
- Returns: ROCE 28% FY26 (20% FY25), ROE 21% [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Project RRise (set in FY25/26, three-year roadmap to FY28): 18% W&C volume CAGR, 25% FMEG CAGR, cumulative 2.5× EBITDA growth, +300 bp W&C margin to 10.5% by FY28, ~100 bp a year [Q4 call (official); AR FY26 MD&A p.61].
- Q4 FY26 call (30 Apr 2026): FY27 W&C volume 16–18%; W&C margin 9.5% for FY27; FMEG value growth 20–25%; FMEG break-even pushed from FY26 to FY27 (missed because of weak summer and input costs); Q1 exports expected to be hurt more than Q4 by the Middle East war; majority of ₹1,200 cr capex in FY27 [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 delivery: volume +17% (in range); W&C margin 9.9% (already above the 9.5% FY27 guide); FMEG broke even in Q1 (ahead of the full-year target), but management warned Q2 may slip back given FMEG seasonality; exports matched domestic growth despite the Middle East [Q1 call].
- Q1 call: guidance kept, not raised — "we continue to maintain … 10.5% by FY28", while "endeavouring" to touch double digits in FY27; FY27 volume now "around 18%"; FMEG ~20% growth and sustained break-even for FY27, profitable in 2–3 years; H2 expected stronger than H1 [Q1 call].
- Track record: FY26 volume target met (~16%) and +150 bp margin delivered (AR says "stated goal of a 150 basis-point margin improvement"); FMEG break-even target missed once (FY26) then hit a quarter early in FY27 [AR FY26 MD&A p.61; Q4 call].

## Capex and capacity
- ₹1,200 cr modular capex (FY26–FY28), ~80% for cables (copper and aluminium cable capacity, move up to 220 kV HV cables, efficiency). Capacity is added "every six months" rather than in one block [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26 p.60].
- Spend: management says ~₹300 cr in FY26 and ₹600–650 cr in FY27; Ind-Ra's rationale says only "around 5%" of the total was incurred in FY26 — a discrepancy. Screener shows investing outflow ₹263 cr and net block + CWIP up from ₹1,004 cr to ₹1,245 cr in FY26, which fits management more than Ind-Ra. Unexecuted capital commitments jumped to ₹256.7 cr (FY25 ₹75.5 cr) [Q1 call; Ind-Ra Sep-26; Screener; AR FY26 Note 28 p.226].
- Timing: Silvassa wire lines in Q2 FY27; Waghodia cable lines during FY27; programme completes FY28 [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Funding: mix of term debt and internal accruals; no long-term repayments in FY27, ₹34.4 cr in FY28 [Ind-Ra Sep-26].
- Payback/asset turns: not disclosed. FY26 revenue was ~7.8× net block + CWIP (my arithmetic); cable plants are more capital-heavy than wire lines, so incremental turns will be lower. Ind-Ra calls the cable capacity "margin accretive".
- Add-on: ₹77 cr OFC acquisition (Sep 2026), outside the ₹1,200 cr plan [BSE 25-Sep-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Balance sheet Mar-26: borrowings ₹232 cr (mostly short-term) + lease liabilities ₹105 cr; cash and liquid investments ₹140.8 cr; quoted equity investments ₹163 cr [AR FY26 Note 37; Ind-Ra Sep-26].
- Hidden leverage: trade payables of ₹1,364 cr include ₹1,107 cr of interest-bearing LC acceptances (FY25 ₹374 cr), settled within ~60 days. Ind-Ra's adjusted net leverage including acceptances rose to 1.78× EBITDA in FY26 (0.82× FY25), falling to 1.02× annualised in Q1 FY27 as acceptances came down. Excluding acceptances, net leverage < 1× [AR FY26 Note 19.1; Ind-Ra Sep-26].
- Cash conversion weakened: CFO ₹295 cr in FY26 vs operating profit ₹786 cr (57%); free cash flow just ₹7 cr (FY25 ₹129 cr) [Screener].
- Days: inventory days 81 (59 in FY25) due to exports stuck in transit and higher metal prices; debtor days 37; payable days 63 (44); cash conversion cycle 56 days. Company's own net working capital days: 49 at Mar-26, 50 at Jun-26; receivable days fell 3 days in Q1 [Screener; IP Q1FY27 slide 7; Q1 call].
- Dividends: ₹9.50/share for FY26 (payout ~22%) [Q4 call; Screener].
- Rating: India Ratings upgraded long-term bank lines to IND AA/Stable from IND AA- on 16 Sep 2026 and rated ₹3,000 cr of additional limits; interest cover 10.4× FY26 [Ind-Ra Sep-26].

## Moat evidence
- Brand/retail: house-wire leader in North and West; wires sell at a brand premium in India (domestic wire margin 11–12%) [Q1 call; AR FY26 p.60].
- Export approvals: 42+ certifications and 66 countries; export cable margin 11–12%; India–EU FTA may remove EU tariffs on Indian W&C [AR FY26 MD&A p.60–62; Q1 call].
- Pricing: price changes whenever raw materials move 2–3%; 100% back-to-back metal buying for B2B/export orders; gross margin held ~18.4–18.6% through the copper spike [Q4 call; AR FY26 p.61; IP Q1FY27 slide 8].
- Share gains: volume growth of 16–17% vs industry 10–12% (management's estimate) [Q1 call]. Unorganised-to-organised shift ~2–3 points a year [Q1 call].
- Weak spots: domestic cables still small and low-margin (6–7%); FMEG two-thirds outsourced with no manufacturing edge; data-centre sales "very less" [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- Income-tax disputes: contingent income-tax demands jumped to ₹105.3 cr (FY25 ₹11.5 cr), of which ₹103.4 cr is unpaid, covering 2008-09 to 2023-24, before CIT(A); Ind-Ra notes FY26 assessment/reassessment orders of ~₹88 cr. Total contingent liabilities (tax, GST, customs export obligations) ≈ ₹130 cr [AR FY26 Note 28 p.226; CARO para vii(b) p.180; Ind-Ra Sep-26].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matters = revenue cut-off/fictitious sales risk and impairment testing of the loss-making FMEG cash-generating unit. Audit-trail exception: one accounting software lacked database-level and some application-level edit logs, and change logs were missing until 24 Nov 2025 [AR FY26 auditor's report pp.257–258, standalone report para B(f)].
- Supplier-credit dependence: LC acceptances ₹1,107 cr at Mar-26 flatter reported working capital and net debt [AR Note 19.1].
- Guarantee: letter of comfort to Standard Chartered for up to BDT 75 cr of loans to the Bangladesh JV RR-Imperial Electricals; sales to that JV ₹69.7 cr in FY26 [AR FY26 Notes 28, 36].
- Related parties: promoters personally guarantee ₹369 cr of secured and ₹146 cr of unsecured bank lines; rent paid to promoter family ≈ ₹0.66 cr; executive directors' commission ₹6.6 cr — modest, arm's length per auditor [AR FY26 Note 36].
- Commodity and export concentration: copper is 80–90% of raw material; one-way metal moves can swing margins 100–150 bp; Middle East ≈ 12% of sales [Ind-Ra Sep-26; Q4 call].
- Competition: UltraTech's ₹1,800 cr W&C entry flagged by Ind-Ra; Q1 management saw "nothing special" in pricing pressure [Ind-Ra Sep-26; Q1 call].
- Promoter holding drifting down: 62.76% (Sep-23) → 61.38% (Jun-26); no pledges disclosed [Screener].
- Management change: founder Tribhuvanprasad Kabra stepped down as Executive Chairman on 31 May 2025; CFO changed Nov 2025 (Rajesh Jain became COO, Jigar Mehta CFO) [AR FY26 Note 36].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 was cautious (war, stuck exports, inventory up, FMEG miss); Q1 was confident (record quarter, Middle East shipments normal by May–June, FMEG break-even).
- Numbers: volume growth 10% → 17%; W&C margin 9.6% → 9.9%; FMEG loss ₹9 cr → break-even; export share 25% → 29% [IP Q1FY27].
- New issues in Q1: some de-stocking at quarter-end as metal prices corrected; one-off forex gains in other income; confirmation that data-centre revenue is tiny; US seen as future opportunity once tariffs are clear.
- Dropped: talk of freight-in-transit inventory; Q4's "inventory gain — positive, marginally" was replaced by a flat claim that Q1 margins had no inventory gain [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## My read
The core business is clearly improving: volumes are growing well ahead of the industry, W&C margins have risen from 7.4% to ~9.9% in five quarters, and FMEG has stopped losing money. The next leg depends on the ₹1,200 cr cable capex landing on time in FY27 and on domestic cable margins moving from 6–7% toward 10%. The weak spots are cash conversion (FY26 free cash flow near zero), heavy reliance on ₹1,100 cr of LC acceptances, and a new ₹105 cr income-tax dispute. At a trailing P/E near 47 the price already assumes RRise targets are met; I would turn more positive if FY27 cash flow recovers with margins ≥9.5%, and more cautious if FMEG slips back into losses or acceptances climb again.
