# Anondita Medicare (NSE SME: ANONDITA) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

SME-listed (NSE Emerge, 1 Sep 2025); reports half-yearly. Only one earnings call has ever been held (27 May 2026, on H2/FY26); there is no Q1 FY27 call and H1 FY27 results are due after 1 Oct 2026. Figures are consolidated (Screener, company filings) unless stated.

## Business and segments
- Makes male latex condoms (flavoured and textured variants) at one site in Sector 80, Noida, on 15 lines; sells under its own brand **Cobra** (owned by the 89.03%-held subsidiary Anondita Healthcare and Rubber Products India Ltd, AHRPIL), to the Government of India's Nirodh / AIDS-control programmes, and until recently as a contract maker for Mankind (Manforce), Zydus and others [Q4 call; IP May-26; AR FY26 AOC-1].
- FY26 revenue ₹137.4cr: condoms ₹118cr, gloves and other products ~₹19cr [Q4 call]. By channel: ~42% government, ~58% own brand [Q4 call, CFO]. Exports were under 10% of revenue, so no geographic segment is reported; the AR says one business segment [AR FY26 note 2.31].
- Also sells, mostly bought-in under the Cobra name: surgical and household gloves, pregnancy test kits, emergency contraceptive pills, sildenafil tablets [IP May-26; Q4 call].
- Capacity: 562 million condoms a year until Feb 2026; 307 million added on 23 Feb 2026 to 869 million; a further ~250 million (4 lines) under trial [IP May-26; AR FY26 MD letter]. Production rose from about 259 million pieces in FY25 to about 432 million in FY26 [IP May-26 capacity chart].
- Female condoms: trial production done on its own patented machines; current capacity 5 million pieces; world-first registration in Brazil (ANVISA) for latex and non-latex female condoms claimed [Q4 call; AR FY26].
- Distribution of Cobra: 26 super stockists, 195 distributors, 4,569 retailers across 8–9 states (Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Karnataka, UP, Assam, Bihar, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Odisha) [IP May-26; Q4 call].
- Certifications: ISO 13485, WHO-GMP, BIS, MDSAP (Nov 2025; covers US, Canada, Brazil, Japan, Australia), South African SABS (male condoms) [IP May-26; AR FY26].

## Key numbers
- Revenue: FY23 ₹35.9cr and FY24 ₹46.4cr (the promoter's proprietorship, before the April 2024 business transfer), FY25 ₹77.0cr, FY26 ₹137.4cr (+78%) [IP Mar-26/May-26; Screener].
- Half-years (Screener): H1 FY25 ₹31cr, H2 FY25 ₹46cr, H1 FY26 ₹54cr, H2 FY26 ₹83cr. Company figures: H2 FY26 ₹83.32cr vs ₹45.59cr (+83%); H1 FY26 ₹54.1cr vs ₹31.4cr (+72%) [IP May-26].
- Operating margin (EBITDA): FY24 19.9%, FY25 33.5%, FY26 37.5%; half-years 32%, 35%, 35%, 37% (Screener). H2 FY26 EBITDA ₹31.9cr, margin 38.3% [IP May-26].
- Profit after tax: FY24 ₹3.8cr, FY25 ₹16.5cr, FY26 ₹34.3cr (+108%); H2 FY26 ₹21.3cr (+103%) [IP May-26; Screener ₹16cr / ₹34cr].
- Gross margin rose from about 50% to 56%; materials fell from 50% to 44% of sales (Screener cost split). Management credits own-brand mix, fixed-cost absorption and a changed raw-material formula [Q4 call].
- Other expenses tripled, ₹6.2cr to ₹18.4cr (rent for two new buildings, licensing and certification fees, listing fees, advertising, travel) [IP May-26; Q4 call].
- Return on capital employed 44% in FY26 (Screener); FY25 not meaningful (first year).
- Unit economics: roughly ₹2.7 of condom revenue per piece (₹118cr / ~432 million pieces) — my arithmetic from company figures; management says a condom costing it ₹2 sells for ₹10 abroad [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Only one call exists (27 May 2026). Its numbers do not agree with each other or with later documents:
  - Government (Nirodh) business +5–10% in FY27; Cobra +5–10% (only a few states) [Q4 call].
  - South Africa: 250 million pieces "this year" (25% of a 1 billion-piece, five-year tender) [Q4 call].
  - "Take this growth up to ₹250–500 crores in the coming 2027" [Q4 call, official transcript].
  - Female condoms: if it wins 25% of orders at ₹50 a piece on 240 million pieces of capacity, ₹330cr revenue and ₹150cr profit in FY27 [Q4 call].
  - CFO: the ₹62cr capex programme (₹24cr spent) gives 307 million + 250 million pieces; maximum turnover on that capacity ~₹325cr [Q4 call].
  - MD, later in the call: existing ₹137cr intact, extra capacity adds 25% this year, ₹200–300cr "by next year" once all machines are installed "by the second quarter" [Q4 call].
  - ₹1,000cr revenue "within two years" [Q4 call].
  - Margins will "improve significantly — 110%" [Q4 call].
- Presentation of the same day: 60–70% revenue growth in FY27 at an EBITDA margin of about 30%; exports 25% of revenue by FY27 [IP May-26].
- Rights-issue Letter of Offer (27 Aug 2026), the most recent and most formal statement: FY27 turnover ~₹205cr (+49%), FY28 ~₹450cr, FY29 ~₹950cr; female condom plant sized at **120 million pieces**, of which **60 million** to run in FY27 (versus 240 million on the call) [LoF Aug-26].
- Brickwork (26 May 2026) rating sensitivities: upgrade if FY27 income exceeds 180% of FY26, downgrade if below 162% or if the EBITDA margin falls below 25% — implying its base case sits between those levels on the standalone ₹111.7cr [BWR May-26].
- Delivery against the call: no H1 FY27 results yet. The South Africa "PO in a few days" arrived (₹43.14cr, 1 June; ₹5.11cr, 23 June; ₹48.5cr in all, to be delivered by September 2026) [LoF Aug-26; News: Trade Brains, Jun 2026]. The UN hearing in Geneva (16–17 June) and a July inspection were promised; no outcome has been disclosed. The 250 million-piece lines, due "within this financial year"/"second quarter" on the call, were "under trial" in the Letter of Offer (production "within the next 2–3 months") and in the AR four lines are said to be ready "by January 2027" [AR FY26 MD letter; LoF Aug-26].
- Verdict: guidance is promotional and inconsistent; the Letter of Offer is the number to hold them to (~₹205cr in FY27).

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹63cr (Screener cash flow), about 46% of sales; capital work in progress ₹42cr at March 2026 (₹6cr a year earlier) (Screener).
- Male-condom expansion: project cost "~₹75cr + taxes", funded by internal accruals, debt and equity [IP May-26]; ₹62cr spent/committed per the CFO [Q4 call]. Machines are fabricated in-house under the MD's patent, which management says keeps cost and lead time low [Q4 call].
- Female-condom plant: ₹54.6cr of machinery for two lines (latex and non-latex/nitrile), ₹50cr from the rights issue, balance from accruals; 120 million pieces a year, 60 million in FY27 [LoF Aug-26]. The company has never made female condoms commercially and needs WHO/UNFPA pre-qualification for most institutional buyers [LoF risk factors 22, 23].
- Roadmap in the deck: non-latex male and female condoms 2028; a latex plantation 2029; medicines and surgical products 2030 [IP May-26].
- Asset turns: sales per ₹1 of net fixed assets fell from 5.9 to 3.5 as the plant grew (Screener). Management's ₹325cr "maximum" on the new capacity would be over 3 times the ₹100cr-odd programme cost.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Operating cash: −₹6.5cr in FY25, +₹10.6cr in FY26 against ₹51.5cr of EBITDA; free cash −₹27cr and −₹52cr (Screener; IP May-26). Standalone H1 FY26 operating cash was about −₹12.5cr [IP Mar-26].
- Receivables rose ₹25cr and inventory ₹7cr in FY26; ~₹37cr of the ₹65cr Q4 sales were to the government, which pays in at least 120 days [Q4 call]. Debtor days 127 → 138; cash conversion cycle 218 → 214 days (Screener).
- Borrowings ₹34cr (Deutsche Bank working capital, IDFC, Karur Vysya, Tata Capital, Oxyzo and others); cash ₹10cr; net debt about ₹21cr at March 2026 (Screener) [AR FY26 notes 2.3, 2.6].
- Equity: IPO fresh issue ₹69.5cr at ₹145 (Aug 2025, 277 times subscribed) [AR FY26]; rights issue ₹91.0cr at ₹950, allotted 24 Sep 2026 (9,58,166 shares, ~5% dilution) — net ₹85.5cr: ₹50cr female-condom plant, ₹25.7cr working capital, ₹9.8cr general purposes (which may include investment in the subsidiary) [LoF Aug-26; NSE filing 24 Sep 2026]. After the rights money the company is in net cash.
- Brickwork: BWR BBB/Stable (long term) and A3 (short term) on ₹56.21cr of bank lines; gearing 0.31x, interest cover 12.1x (FY26 provisional); working-capital lines ~60% used [BWR May-26].
- No dividend.

## Moat evidence
- Cost position: in-house machine building (patented), in-house foil lamination and packaging, CNG/biofuel heating — claims only; no peer cost data [Q4 call; IP May-26].
- Approvals: MDSAP, SABS, WHO-GMP, ISO 13485, Brazil registration; qualified as a vendor under South Africa's national tender RT75-2025 [AR FY26; LoF Aug-26].
- Government contract: sole rate-contract supplier to India's AIDS-control Nirodh programme for two years running, per the MD; the Letter of Offer adds that as an MSME it may take up to 25% of a tender by matching the lowest bid [Q4 call; LoF Aug-26].
- Brand: Cobra is regional (North and East India); Manforce (Mankind) holds ~27%, Durex ~16%, Skore ~11% of an estimated ₹2,600–2,800cr Indian market; Anondita sits in "others" [IP May-26].
- Pricing: gross margin rose six points in FY26 while latex was steady; no evidence of a price increase [Q4 call].
- Verdict: a low-cost, well-certified maker of a commodity medical device, with a small brand; the moat is cost and approvals, not brand.

## Risks and red flags
- Auditor's emphasis of matter (FY26, unmodified opinion): two years after the business transfer, title deeds of all its immovable properties, a ₹2.92cr IDFC loan and a Deutsche Bank property loan, fixed deposits, a ₹66 lakh bank guarantee and 12 of 15 vehicles are still in the name of the promoter's old proprietorship; it holds a 50% share in a partnership firm owning a Noida property whose paperwork is incomplete [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 2.32].
- CARO: ₹14.6cr lent to the subsidiary in the year with no repayment schedule; loans outstanding ₹5.7cr (subsidiary) and ₹2.7cr (an associate firm); not regular in depositing advance income tax [AR FY26 CARO].
- Related parties (consolidated note 2.29): a ₹4.9cr short-term advance outstanding to Anondita Healthcare (Partnership), a promoter-family firm; a ₹2.2cr corporate guarantee for a car loan of that "associate concern"; factory D-001 rented from promoter company Anondita Healthcare Products Pvt Ltd (₹1.33cr a year); the MD lent and was repaid about ₹4.5cr during the year. The IDFC loan is jointly owed with the partnership firm and the subsidiary [AR FY26; BWR May-26].
- AGM (29 Sep 2026) resolutions raise related-party limits: up to ₹55.5cr with the MD (loans to the company, guarantees, rent), up to ₹75cr with the subsidiary (loans, guarantees, sales), and permit the subsidiary to buy up to ₹20cr of goods from the promoter partnership firm [AR FY26 AGM notice].
- The Cobra brand sits in a subsidiary that is 89.03% owned, and the MD talked of listing it separately "once we reach 10 states" [AR FY26 AOC-1; Q4 call].
- Promoters (62.4%) did not subscribe to the rights issue and renounced their entitlements; they fall to about 59% [LoF Aug-26; my arithmetic]. The issue size was changed by a corrigendum, "nullified", then restored [NSE filings 8 and 24 Sep 2026].
- NSE fined it ₹64,900 for filing its related-party disclosure 11 days late [LoF Aug-26].
- Litigation: 4 criminal and 3 civil cases filed by the company, 1 civil case against it (₹77 lakh); a long dispute with Swear Healthcare over glove-machine designs is in the Supreme Court [LoF Aug-26; AR FY26 note 2.26].
- Board churn: a whole-time director (Oct 2025–Feb 2026), the company secretary (Nov 2025) and promoter Sonia Ghosh (Oct 2025) left in FY26 [AR FY26].
- Concentration: ~42% of sales to one government buyer; South Africa orders come through local distributors under one national tender [Q4 call; LoF].
- Latex prices (Kerala monsoon) and a rating that assumes timely commissioning [BWR May-26].

## What changed between the two calls
- Only one call has been held. Against the March 2026 deck (H1 FY26): exports were "targeted to begin by end-FY26 or early Q1"; they began in June 2026 with the South Africa orders. The deck's "~₹75cr" project cost became a ₹62cr figure on the call and a separate ₹54.6cr female plant in August. Female-condom ambitions shrank from 240 million pieces (May call) to 120 million, 60 million in FY27 (Aug Letter of Offer); FY27 revenue from "₹250–500cr" (call) to ~₹205cr (Letter of Offer).

## Stored-data errors
- Dashboard labels the half-years as "Q4 FY26" and "Q2 FY26"; they are H2 and H1 figures.
- "ttm=214" is the sum of four half-years (two years of revenue); trailing revenue is ₹137cr.
- P/E 65.6 in the dashboard vs 63.7 on Screener (price move); ROCE 43.8% matches.
- Stored profile: exports "< 5%" (AR says under 10%); female capacity "5 to 240 million" (Letter of Offer plans 120 million); "Aurum Connect" / "Sunita Nethani" should be Orim Connect / Sunita Naithani.
- Stored transcript vs official (company website): tidied copy (figures match; names and a few phrases garbled, e.g. "Love It capsules" for "energy capsules"). The official copy itself misstates H2 FY25 EBITDA as ₹51.96cr (it was ₹15.96cr).

## My read
A fast-growing, genuinely profitable small maker (37% margin, 44% return on capital) that has just added a lot of capacity and won its first export orders. But almost all the profit has gone into receivables and plant, management's targets are promotional and contradict each other, and the annual report shows a business still entangled with the promoter family's firms two years after it was carved out. The price needs about 27% a year; I expect about 20% from here, and the formal FY27 plan (₹205cr) already implies slower growth than FY26. I would change my view on two clean half-years with positive operating cash, the property and loan transfers completed, and female-condom sales actually booked.
