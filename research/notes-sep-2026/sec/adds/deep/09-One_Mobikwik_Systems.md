# One MobiKwik Systems — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 28 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. Screener consolidated page (code MOBIKWIK) for all filed figures. The company's "EBITDA" includes other income (treasury interest); Screener's operating profit does not. Stored transcripts: both carry the BSE covering letter and **match the official transcripts** (Q1 figures ₹437cr net cash, ₹40cr PAT target and ₹320cr working-capital lines checked against the BSE PDF).

## Business and segments
- A Gurugram consumer fintech: a mobile wallet (the largest in India by wallet spend, about 19% share of PPI wallet value), a UPI app, bill payments and recharges, and small loans made by partner lenders through its app. Two reported businesses: **Payments** and **Financial Services** (lending). Newer lines: offline merchant acquiring (QR codes, soundboxes, card machines) and online payment gateway **Zaakpay**; wealth (AUA ₹17,968cr), broking and insurance distribution are small. [IP Q1FY27 slides 4, 6, 11, KPI table]
- Single operating segment in the filed accounts ("payment services and financial services"; the board reviews only revenue by unit). Entirely India. [AR FY26 standalone note 35]
- Scale Q1 FY27: 192.8 mn registered users, 5.02 mn merchants, 1.64 mn active loan (Zip EMI) users; platform GMV ₹58,700cr (+50% YoY), of which UPI ₹26,900cr (+99%), wallet ₹12,500cr (+24%), recharge and bills ₹8,900cr (+37%), merchant payments ₹12,600cr (+17% QoQ); Zaakpay GMV ₹2,563cr. [IP Q1FY27 slides 4, 11–14; Q1 call]
- Revenue split Q1 FY27 (company): Payments ₹208cr (−2% YoY), Financial Services ₹73.3cr (+26%). FY26: Payments ₹857cr (+12%), Financial Services ₹262cr. [IP Q1FY27 slide 29; AR FY26 financial waterfall]
- Unit economics: payments revenue fell from 54 bps of GMV (Q1 FY26) to 35 bps (Q1 FY27), but gateway costs fell faster (36 → 20 bps), so **net payment margin held at 13 bps** (guided 12–15). Payments gross profit ₹77.7cr (+31%), gross margin 37.3% (27.9% a year ago). [IP Q1FY27 slides 15–16]
- Lending: disbursals ₹737cr in Q1 (+6% YoY, −12% QoQ), 68% under first-loss guarantee (FLDG, where MobiKwik bears up to 5% of losses) and 32% pure distribution; 9 major lending partners; top-3 lender share cut from 91% to 71%. Net financial-services margin 5.87% of disbursals, of which **1.87 points were recoveries on old loans** (write-backs of guarantee money). [IP Q1FY27 slides 19, 23, 26; Q1 call]

- **Profit quality:** past-book recoveries were about ₹14–16cr in Q1 (management: "roughly INR 16 odd crores"), about twice the quarter's PAT of ₹7.6cr. Management says write-backs "will keep coming… maybe not to the same tune". [Q1 call; IP slide 26]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (12 May 2026):** "baseline profitable" every quarter while reinvesting; FY27 EBITDA margin "similar to the 5% range"; fixed costs to rise 15–20% in FY27; merchant-business investment "in similar range" to FY26's ₹55cr; offline merchants targeting 5x devices and 10x revenue by FY28, Zaakpay 10x GMV by FY28, both at EBITDA breakeven by FY28; digital-credit GMV +30–35% in FY27; lending margin "4.5% still sounds more sustainable"; net payment margin 12–15 bps; NBFC set up in 3–6 months and co-lending disbursals in 6–9 months. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (3 Aug 2026):** PAT ₹7.6cr and EBITDA margin 5.5% — **met**; net payment margin 13 bps — **met**; lending margin 5.9% — **beat**, but on write-backs; disbursals ₹737cr, down 12% QoQ — **missed** (blamed on moving lending into a subsidiary and cutting lender concentration); NBFC — **delayed** ("we are still some time away"). Fixed costs fell 4% QoQ. [Q1 call; IP slides 26, 31]
- **New targets on the Q1 call:** FY27 **PAT ₹40cr and EBITDA ₹75cr** ("fairly confident"); disbursals back to ₹1,000cr a quarter ("already on the INR1,000 crores run rate in Q2"); long-run lending margin raised to 4.5–5.5%; consumer payments revenue +5–6% a quarter, merchant revenue +25% a quarter; merchant burn ₹50–60cr a year, breakeven FY28. The ₹75cr/₹40cr pair was quoted by an analyst as Q4 guidance; the Q4 transcript does not contain it — management confirmed it on the Q1 call. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: capex 0.2–1.1% of revenue over five years (Screener). Devices (soundboxes, card machines) are capitalised and depreciated; about ₹25cr of IPO money remains for devices this year. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- IPO (Dec 2024) net proceeds ₹530.5cr; ₹348.4cr used by March 2026, ₹182.2cr unused (device capex ₹58.7cr of ₹70.3cr unused). Shareholders approved changing the objects and extending the timeline on 2 July 2026, partly to capitalise the lending subsidiary. [AR FY26 note 44; AR FY26 directors' report]
- NBFC: RBI in-principle approval 27 Apr 2026, conditional on first moving the lending-service business into wholly-owned MobiKwik Distribution Services (slump sale approved 2 Jul, completion targeted Aug 2026); then apply for the final registration. [IP Q1FY27 slide 7; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Management: net cash ₹437cr at 30 Jun 2026 (₹434cr "unencumbered" in March); all long-term debt repaid by March (₹50cr BlackSoil NCDs redeemed 28 Feb 2026); ₹320cr of short-term bank lines used for weekend and holiday settlements (₹261cr at March). Screener: borrowings ₹276cr (March), net cash ₹626cr. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26; Screener]
- Finance cost ₹3.6cr in Q1 (₹5.1cr in Q4, ₹7.2cr in Q3). [IP slide 32; Q4 call]
- Working capital: debtor days 17–24 (FY25–FY26); ₹57cr receivable from payment gateways at March. FLDG deposits with lenders grow as the book grows. [Screener; AR FY26 RPT note; Q4 call]
- Tax losses of about ₹900–1,000cr, so no tax "anytime soon". No dividend. No credit rating found (its NCDs were unrated). [Q1 call; web search]

## Moat evidence
- Largest wallet by spend (~19–20% share), 40 mn full-KYC wallets; UPI transactions +130% against 24% for the industry, but it ranks only 13th among UPI apps. [IP slides 10–11; Q1 call]
- Cost position improving: direct costs −21% YoY; lending costs −40% as credit quality improved (static-pool losses ~25% lower; 60% of loans to repeat borrowers; all borrowers bureau 700+). [IP slides 20, 25, 31]
- Weak pricing power: gross take rate keeps falling with UPI and regulatory pauses; the PPI-on-UPI fee RBI mandated has not been switched on by NPCI "despite 2.5 years" — "I am as demoralized about it as I can tell you." [Q1 call]
- Lending depends on partners' balance sheets; no own NBFC book yet. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Merchant fraud (Sep 2025):** merchants and users in the Nuh–Mewat area exploited an app bug to claim ₹40.4cr of settlements; ₹27.6cr recovered and ₹0.9cr secured by March 2026; ₹11.8cr written off as an exceptional item. The auditor's CARO report records it. (An earlier ₹6.9cr gift-card fraud in FY23.) [AR FY26 note 24.2, CARO xi]
- **Audit trail:** seven accounting software packages lack a database-level edit log, so the auditor could not comment on them; one keeps its log only one year. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Regulation:** card-linked categories (rent, education) paused in Q4 and Q1; PPI-on-UPI fee stalled; RBI digital-lending and FLDG rules; the NBFC timeline. [Q1 call]
- **Profit leans on write-backs and on no tax:** Q1 recoveries exceeded PAT. [Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities:** service-tax demand ₹7.9cr plus ₹7.9cr penalty (FY15–FY18 recharge commissions) under appeal; income-tax adjustments absorbed by losses. [AR FY26 note 33]
- **Ownership:** founders Bipin Preet Singh 14.5% and Upasana Taku 10.1%; promoters 25.1% (Screener). Unused IPO money re-purposed. [AR FY26; Screener]
- Top-3 lender concentration still 71%. [IP slide 23]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "four growth engines" and "AI-first by FY28" (Q4) to a "tightrope walk" between profit and investment (Q1). The AI-coding claims (80% of code) were dropped; AI is now pitched at recovering loan-funnel drop-offs (+₹100cr disbursals a quarter targeted).
- Numbers: disbursals ₹838cr → ₹737cr; lending margin 5.4% → 5.9%; EBITDA margin 5.9% → 5.5%; PAT ₹4.4cr → ₹7.6cr.
- New: explicit FY27 PAT ₹40cr target; merchant GMV disclosed (₹12,600cr); NBFC pushed out; new long-run lending range 4.5–5.5% (was "4%" to "4.5%").

## My read
MobiKwik has stopped burning money: three profitable quarters, costs down, payments gross profit up 31% on flat revenue. But revenue has not grown for two years (FY26 below FY25), the Q1 profit of ₹7.6cr sits on about ₹14–16cr of one-off lending write-backs, and the merchant push costs ₹50–60cr a year. The price (about ₹1,570cr) is roughly 39 times management's own ₹40cr FY27 profit target, with ₹437cr of net cash. I would wait for four quarters of profit without write-backs, disbursals back at ₹1,000cr a quarter and the NBFC licence; a UPI or PPI-on-UPI fee would be the upside surprise. More fraud, another regulatory pause or a missed ₹40cr would end the case.
