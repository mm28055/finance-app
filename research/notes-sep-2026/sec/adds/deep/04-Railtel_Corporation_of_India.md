# Railtel Corporation of India — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

Screener code: RAILTEL, **standalone** page (the consolidated page stops at Sep 2023 and is stale; the company reports standalone only, ICRA also rates on standalone). Price ₹258, market value ₹8,296cr, P/E 22.4 (Screener, Sep 2026).

## Business and segments
- Navratna CPSE under the Ministry of Railways (government holds 72.84%, unchanged for three years). Owns 63,000+ route-km of optic fibre laid along railway tracks, 21,000+ km of city access network, 11,000+ points of presence including 6,117 railway stations. [IP Mar-26; ICRA Feb-26]
- Two segments. **Telecom services** (licensed NLD, ISP, IP-1, plus data centres, Aadhaar authentication and other digital services, which are booked inside telecom): FY26 revenue ₹1,500.7cr (+10%), segment result ₹400.3cr (26.7% of revenue; FY25 22.2%). **Projects** (railway signalling incl. Kavach, video surveillance, e-office/HMIS, state-government ICT, school labs, smart city): FY26 revenue ₹2,776.8cr (+31%), segment result ₹109.5cr (3.9%; FY25 4.4%). Projects were 65% of FY26 revenue. [AR FY26 note 52]
- Q1 FY27: telecom ₹361cr, projects ₹532cr. Inside telecom: NLD ₹134cr (₹151cr a year earlier), ISP ₹112cr, IP-1 ₹27cr; the rest (about ₹88cr) is data centre and digital services. [Q1 call]
- Q4 FY26: telecom ₹449cr, of which "pure telecom" ₹332cr (NLD ₹182cr, ISP ₹120cr, IP-1 ₹30cr) and ₹117cr data centre and digital services. [Q4 call]
- Data centre revenue ₹127cr in FY25 → ₹202cr in FY26; Tier-III centres at Gurgaon and Secunderabad, MeitY-empanelled cloud, edge centres at Gurgaon and Mumbai; roughly 3 MW own capacity. [Q4 call; IP Mar-26]
- RailWire home broadband: 6.15 lakh subscribers in FY26, about ₹350cr revenue, ARPU about ₹474, 11,500 partners; 6.23 lakh subscribers in Q1 FY27. [AR FY26 chairman's speech; Q1 call]
- Indian Railways itself was ₹693cr of FY26 revenue (about 16%), against ₹708cr in FY25. [AR FY26 note 48]
- Geography: India; small MEA-funded overseas work (Ethiopia data centre ₹18cr, laptops for Trinidad, BHISHM medical cubes). 928 employees. [Q1 call; AR FY26]

## Key numbers (Screener standalone)
- Revenue FY22–FY26: ₹1,522cr → ₹1,957cr → ₹2,568cr → ₹3,478cr → ₹4,277cr (3-yr CAGR 30%). Operating profit ₹368cr → ₹379cr → ₹464cr → ₹535cr → ₹638cr; margin 24% → 19% → 18% → 15% → 15%. Net profit ₹208cr → ₹188cr → ₹246cr → ₹300cr → ₹346cr (FY26 +16%). [Screener]
- Quarters (revenue / operating profit / margin / profit): Q1 FY26 ₹744cr / ₹116cr / 15.6% / ₹66cr; Q2 ₹951cr / ₹154cr / 16.2% / ₹76cr; Q3 ₹913cr / ₹133cr / 14.6% / ₹62cr; Q4 ₹1,669cr / ₹233cr / 14.0% / ₹142cr; Q1 FY27 ₹893cr / ₹132cr / 14.8% / ₹66cr. Revenue growth year on year +33%, +13%, +19%, +28%, +20%. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 profit before tax and exceptional items ₹96cr (+12%); after an ECL provision booked as an exceptional item, PBT ₹89cr and PAT ₹66cr, flat on a year. New orders ₹1,688cr (₹721cr a year earlier). [Q1 call]
- Strong Q4 seasonality: Q4 was 39% of FY26 revenue and 41% of profit. [Screener]
- Return on capital 16% (FY22) → 23% (FY26); ROE 17% last year. Depreciation ₹160cr → ₹189cr. [Screener]
- Unit economics: projects earn a 4–5% margin by management's own target; telecom ~35–40% EBITDA margin (39% in FY26, management). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (1 May 2026):** FY27 revenue growth "around 20%" ("as the absolute revenue increases, the growth percentage ... will now be around 20%"); project revenue from the order book ₹3,000–3,500cr; project margin 4–5%; capex ₹300cr+ mostly data centres; 5 MW own data-centre capacity by May 2027; at least three more edge data centres in FY27 (Indore, Ujjain, Chandigarh, Visakhapatnam named). [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (31 Jul 2026):** asked whether "the outlook that we gave last quarter, that revenue will grow at 25%" still holds, the CMD said "Yes, that remains the outlook" — so the latest word is 20–25% (the May figure was 20%; the analyst's 25% went unchallenged). Project revenue ₹3,000–3,500cr "anticipated and projected"; Kavach revenue to start this year; data-centre revenue "around ₹300cr" in FY27 and "approximately ₹500cr" by FY28; a 10 MW Noida facility "by May 2027" (the March presentation calls it Phase 1 of 5 MW by May 2027); project margins kept at 4–5%, Kavach "slightly better". [Q1 call; IP Mar-26]
- **Delivery vs Q4 promise:** revenue +20% in Q1 (in line); profit flat on ECL (not flagged in May); NLD fell 11% on delayed government renewals after management had described telecom as strong in May; edge-data-centre roll-out quietly scaled back to 500 kW additions; RDSO approval of its Kavach partner still "any time now". Overall: **Mixed**.
- Track record: FY26 revenue +23% against a ~20–25% aim; the FY25 guide was met with 22% (management). [Q4 call; AR FY26]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex (Screener cash flow): ₹117cr (FY22), ₹176cr, ₹210cr, ₹325cr, ₹272cr (FY26), about 6–9% of revenue. [Screener]
- FY27 plan: ₹300cr+ (Q4 call), "approximately ₹241cr" (FY26 annual report, chairman's speech) — towards network, data centres and technology upgrades. Capital commitments ₹345cr at March 2026 (₹312cr a year earlier). [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 51]
- 10 MW Noida data centre being built with Techno Electric & Engineering (bhoomi pujan in FY26); first 5 MW by May 2027. Capacity also leased from Adani and Anant Raj, where RailTel installs its own servers and sells managed/cloud services ("capex-light"). Edge centres: 2 built, target of 100 over time. [IP Mar-26; Q1 call; AR FY26]
- Dark fibre: private demand exists; management wants a revenue-share partner rather than spending its own capital. [Q1 call]
- AI: working on air-gapped small language models for Railways and GPU capacity ("you will see something in the news"). [Q1 call]
- Funding: internal accruals; no borrowings beyond leases. [AR FY26 MD&A]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹64cr (leases); cash and bank balances ₹654cr at March 2026 (cash equivalents ₹397cr, other bank balances ₹257cr, part of which is margin money held against bank guarantees); Screener net cash ₹590cr. ICRA: "debt-free", cash ₹319cr at Sep 2025. [Screener; AR FY26 notes 12–13; ICRA Feb-26]
- Operating cash ₹258cr → ₹284cr → ₹556cr → ₹255cr → ₹316cr; operating cash ÷ operating profit 70%, 75%, 120%, 48%, 50% (five-year 70%). Free cash ₹141cr, ₹108cr, ₹346cr, −₹70cr, ₹44cr. [Screener]
- Debtor days 160 (FY22), 195, 180, 166, 175 (FY26). Gross trade receivables ₹2,196cr (+29% vs revenue +23%), of which ₹238cr is over three years old; ECL allowance ₹143cr (₹120cr). Unbilled revenue ₹898cr. Receivables from Indian Railways ₹615cr (₹420cr a year earlier). [AR FY26 notes 11, 53, 56.12, 48]
- ECL charge booked as an exceptional item: ₹24.4cr in FY26 (₹21.7cr FY25) and again in Q1 FY27. Management: "non-cash", "none of the accounts are doubtful", reversible when paid. [AR FY26 note 40; Q1 call]
- ICRA: working-capital intensity "negative/very low" because many receivables are back-to-back with subcontractors and customers pay advances. [ICRA Feb-26]
- Dividend FY26 ₹3.25 a share (₹2 interim + ₹1.25 final), about 30% payout; ICRA flags dividend obligations to the government as a liquidity call. [AR FY26; ICRA Feb-26]
- Bank guarantees given ₹1,482cr (₹724cr a year earlier), doubling with project wins. [AR FY26 note 51]

## Moat evidence
- Exclusive right of way along railway tracks: a separate fibre route from the road-based networks of telcos; private players are asking for dark fibre. Fibre network 62,000–63,000 km. [ICRA Feb-26; Q1 call; IP Mar-26]
- Status as a Railways PSU: executes connectivity work for Indian Railways and wins nomination-style work from other PSUs; designated critical information infrastructure (NCIIPC, Apr 2024); MeitY-empanelled cloud; CMMI Level 5. ICRA notes the Railways' shift toward open tenders but says order procurement "remains healthy". [ICRA Feb-26; IP Mar-26; AR FY26]
- Order book ₹11,747cr (Q1 FY27), 2.7 times FY26 revenue; ₹10,166cr in March split state governments 43.2%, Indian Railways 24.6%, government departments 16.6%, CPSEs 11.4%, banks 2.3%, private 1.7%, telcos 0.3%. Largest FY26 orders: Maharashtra registration offices ₹1,136cr, Bihar school labs ₹822cr. Kavach order book about ₹1,000cr. FY26 order inflow ₹7,363cr. [Q1 call; IP Mar-26; AR FY26]
- No pricing power in telecom: CMD — "there is continuous pressure on prices in telecom"; RailWire ARPU "continuously under pressure". Project work is won on bids at 4–5% margins. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Receivables and ECL**: receivables growing faster than revenue; ₹238cr over three years old; ECL is a key audit matter; Railways' own dues up 46%. [AR FY26]
- **Margin mix**: every rupee of growth comes mostly at 4% (projects); ICRA expects margins to keep moderating. [ICRA Feb-26]
- **Board governance**: auditor's "Other Matter" — only one independent director, not compliant with the Companies Act; NSE and BSE fined it (₹5.31 lakh each in May 2026 for Q4 and about ₹9.0 lakh each in August 2026) for board composition; institutional holders opposed a director reappointment at the August 2026 AGM. The government appoints directors, so the fix is out of the company's hands. [AR FY26 auditor's report; News: ScanX, May–Aug 2026]
- **Contingent liabilities** ₹447cr (arbitration and court cases ₹406cr), a key audit matter; bank guarantees ₹1,482cr. [AR FY26 note 51]
- **Order cancellations** by states: Navodaya Vidyalaya ₹17.1cr (Apr 2026), Himachal Pradesh ₹20.4cr (Sep 2026), a smart-class order in Q3 FY26. [News: ScanX; stored profile]
- **Kavach dependence on partner OEM approval** (RDSO) — still pending at end-July; no new Kavach tenders expected this year. [Q1 call]
- No auditor qualification; CAG "nil comments" on FY26 accounts; CARO shows only small disputed GST dues. [AR FY26; Q1 call]
- Related parties: government-related-entity exemption; transactions with Railways and railway PSUs at market terms. Nothing unusual found. [AR FY26 note 48]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: May was celebratory (record Q4, telecom +25%); July was defensive — flat profit, ECL, NLD decline explained as delayed renewals.
- Growth: 20% (May) → "yes" to 25% (July).
- Data centres: "5 MW by May next year" → "10 MW facility by May 2027"; edge-centre plan downplayed; new leasing tie-ups with Adani and Anant Raj; FY27 DC revenue ~₹300cr and ~₹500cr by FY28 made explicit.
- New: AI (air-gapped SLMs for Railways, GPUs), Starlink and Air Force as NLD customers.
- Railways' share of order book 21% (May) → 23.5% (July).

## My read
RailTel is two businesses: a cash-generating telecom and data-centre arm on a unique railway fibre route (about 35% of revenue, most of the profit) and a fast-growing but thin-margin government project business. Revenue should grow near 20% on an ₹11,747cr order book, but group margins keep drifting down, receivables are rising faster than sales and the ECL charge is eating the growth, so I expect earnings growth of about 13% a year (low 5%, high 20%). The price needs about 14–15%, so it is roughly fair, with ₹590cr of net cash and a 1.25% yield. What would change my view: data-centre revenue reaching ₹300cr with the Noida site on time and Kavach revenue starting (better), or another year of receivables growing faster than revenue and flat profit (worse).
