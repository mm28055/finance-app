## 4. Caveats

- **Leases (Ind AS 116) flatter every operating figure.** Rent on leased hospitals is not in "operating profit": it sits in depreciation (₹84.5cr of right-of-use depreciation in FY26) and interest (₹85.9cr of lease interest). In cash terms HCG paid ₹131cr of lease principal plus interest in FY26 (₹46.6cr + ₹84.3cr), and those payments sit in financing, not in operating cash flow (AR FY26 MD&A; note 6). After those payments, FY26 free cash flow was about **−₹72cr**, not +₹59cr. Pre-Ind AS EBITDA for FY26 was about ₹330–345cr against Screener's ₹459cr (company: net debt ₹493cr ÷ 1.43x ≈ ₹345cr).
- **Acquisitions are not in capex or free cash flow.** Vizag (MG Cancer Hospital): ₹206cr paid for 51% in Oct 2024 (in FY25 "other investing items", −₹290cr) and ₹154.5cr for another 34% on 13 Apr 2026 (FY27); a put option on the last 15% remains. FY22 includes ₹119cr of acquisitions. Q1–Q2 FY26 revenue growth includes about 5 points from Vizag, which was consolidated from 2 Oct 2024.
- **Milann (fertility) is inside every figure up to 29 Jun 2026** (₹60.5cr revenue in FY26, ₹16.9cr in Q1 FY27). From Q2 FY27 reported growth loses about 2.5 points.
- **FY26 cash conversion** is lowered by a one-off ₹58cr cash payment to settle employee options after the change of control. It went through operating cash flow but not through the profit and loss account (it was charged to equity).
- **One-offs in profit:** FY26 has ₹44.6cr of exceptional charges (₹31.9cr goodwill write-down on Milann, ₹12.7cr labour-code gratuity) and a ₹18.2cr tax write-back. That is why Screener's P/E is so high. Owners' profit after tax was ₹13.8cr in FY26 (₹55.7cr adjusted by the company). Screener's 182x does not tie to its own TTM EPS of ₹1.53, which would give about 385x. On owners' adjusted TTM profit of about ₹65cr the P/E is about 136x.
- **Screener's "Fixed Assets"** include ₹388cr of goodwill, ₹33cr of intangibles and ₹648cr of right-of-use (leased) assets. Asset turnover on owned hospital assets is higher than shown.
- **Net debt after March:** the ₹154.5cr Vizag payment (April), the ₹28.2cr Milann receipt (June) and Q1 cash flow (about ₹70cr from operations against about ₹75cr of capex) put net debt including leases at roughly ₹1,320cr by June (my estimate; no June balance sheet is published).
- **Company ROCE (14%)** is pre-Ind AS, excludes Milann, uses average net capital employed and is before goodwill adjustments. It is not comparable with Screener's 8%. The annual report's own ROCE ratio is 6.63%.
