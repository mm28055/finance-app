# Sterlite Technologies Ltd (STL) — deep research (NSE: STLTECH, ISIN INE089C01029), as of 20 Oct 2026

## Business and segments
- **What STL sells now:** preform (used in-house), optical fibre (incl. G.654.E, multi-core, hollow-core), cable (Celesta, up to 6,912 fibres) and connectivity (Neuralis pre-terminated trunks for AI data centres; CONCAT kits for US fibre-to-home). It also keeps copper cable in Italy and STL Digital, an IT-services arm (₹284 cr FY26 revenue, ₹3 cr EBITDA). [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 p.38-39]
- **Segments (Q1 FY27):** optical ₹1,842 cr revenue, ₹401 cr EBITDA (21.8%); digital ₹72 cr, ₹2 cr. FY26 optical: ₹4,486 cr / ₹606 cr (13.5%). No split by fibre, cable or connectivity. [Q1 results; AR FY26 p.38; Q1 call]
- **Customer mix, checked against slide 24:** data centre & cloud was **1% of FY26 revenue and 21% in Q1 FY27**; large enterprise 17% → 18%; telecom & citizen networks 82% → 61%. The FY27 target is for **data centre plus enterprise combined to reach 50%**, raised from 30% on the Q4 call. [IP Q1FY27 slide 24; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Geography:** Americas 39% (FY26) → 54% (Q1); Europe 39% → 25%; rest of world incl. India 22%. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Customers and plants:** supplies "almost all the major hyperscalers" but names none; ex-China cable share 8% (FY26) → 9% (Q1). Plants: fibre at Aurangabad and Jiangsu (China); cable/connectivity at Silvassa, Dadra, Dello (Italy) and Lugoff (South Carolina). [Sep-3 meet; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 plant list]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (29 Apr):** 20% EBITDA margin by end-FY27; data centre plus enterprise 30% of FY27 revenue; net debt below 1.2x EBITDA; capex ~₹500 cr; ₹1,468 cr due in Q1. No revenue guidance; capacity no longer disclosed. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 delivery (24 Jul):** revenue ₹1,910 cr (30% above that book); margin 20.8%, three quarters early. **FY27 margin target raised to 23%**; data-centre target to 50%; capex ₹500 cr a year for three years. Attach rate (connectivity ÷ cable revenue) 16%, guided above 20% from Q2 and 25% by Q4. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **3 Sep investor day ("Lakshya"):** **₹20,000 cr revenue and 27%+ EBITDA margin by FY29** (from ₹4,750 cr and 13.2% in FY26). Capex doubled to **~₹1,000 cr a year for three years** for +50% preform, fibre and cable capacity plus a greenfield connectivity plant in India; R&D ~2% of revenue. [Sep-3 meet; NSE filing 3-Sep-26]
- **Verdict:** **raised twice in four months**: margin 20% → 23% → 27%+; data-centre share 30% → 50%; capex doubled. The 3 Sep figures lead. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Sep-3 meet]

## Capex and capacity
- **Capacity:** not disclosed. The 3 Sep filing gives **utilisation at about 70%** and **+50% capacity by end FY29 for about ₹3,000 cr**, funded by "internal accruals and/or debt". The last public figure (2023) was about 50 million fibre-km of fibre. [NSE filing 3-Sep-26; News: Outlook Business 31-Oct-23]
- **US:** up to **$100 m over five years** for a connectivity plant (400–500 jobs), announced May 2026; details are due at the Q2 call. [Q1 call; web-search snippet, ISE Magazine May-26]
- **Funding gap check:** CRISIL models ₹1,500–2,000 cr capex over three years, below the company's ₹3,000 cr. A 4x revenue target on +50% capacity needs ~95% utilisation plus much more value per unit. [CRISIL Sep-26; my calc]
- **Equity raised:** ₹1,000 cr QIP in Apr 2024 at ₹113.05; ₹1,500 cr QIP closed 30 Jun 2026 at **₹583.01** (2.57 cr shares allotted 1 Jul; 75% to repay debt). [Web-search snippet, Business Standard Apr-24; NSE filings 30-Jun/1-Jul-26; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Operating cash vs profit (FY22–26):** ₹584 / 228 / 791 / 348 / 520 cr (₹2,471 cr total) against cumulative net profit of ₹48 cr and operating profit of ₹3,016 cr (82% conversion); free cash flow ₹985 cr. Depreciation and interest absorbed the profit. [Screener cons]
- **Working capital (FY25 → FY26):** debtor days 75 → 82; inventory days 134 → 139; payable days 188 → 204. Net working capital is 8.5% of revenue after the demerger. [Screener cons; ICRA Jul-26]
- **Debt (Mar 2026):** gross ₹1,827 cr; **net ₹1,128 cr** (1.80x EBITDA per the annual report; the Q4 call said 1.3x). All-in cost ~11–12%; listed bonds pay 8.5%. [AR FY26 p.40; Q4 call; Q1 results note 4]
- **"Net debt-free" (Jun 2026):** the ₹483 cr net cash **includes the ₹1,500 cr QIP money and ~₹391 cr held against the Prysmian appeal**. Excluding the QIP, net debt was ~₹1,017 cr (~₹1,400 cr also excluding restricted cash). CRISIL sees about ₹2,400 cr cash and ₹266 cr term repayments in FY27. [IP Q1FY27 slide 26; CRISIL Sep-26]
- **Ratings and guarantees:** upgraded to AA (Stable) by ICRA (6 Jul) and CRISIL (1 Sep). CRISIL would downgrade if margin stays below 15% or capex is more debt-funded than expected. CARO: no default; guarantees of ₹45 cr plus ₹347 cr (the latter for STL Networks). No dividend for FY24–26. [NSE filings 7-Jul/2-Sep-26; CRISIL Sep-26; AR FY26 CARO]

## Moat evidence
- **Full chain from glass to connectors:** few makers outside China do preform, fibre, cable and connectivity in-house, and preform is the bottleneck when demand is tight. [Q1 call; ICRA Jul-26]
- **Contracts:** hyperscaler deals carry capped penalties on both sides for demand or supply shortfalls, which gives real lock-in, but they are not take-or-pay. [NSE filings 22-May/29-Aug/1-Oct-26]
- **IP:** 785+ patents. Fujikura's patent EP3796060 was revoked in full (July 2026), ending the UK Celesta case. STL also holds US Conec MMC certification. [NSE filing 3-Jul-26; IP Q1FY27]
- **Lowest trade-remedy rates among Indian makers:** EU countervailing duty 3.7% plus anti-dumping 8.8% (HFCL's countervailing duty is 8.1%). China's anti-dumping duty on Indian fibre, renewed 14 Aug 2026, is 7.4% for STL versus 24.5–30.6% for most others. [EU Reg 2025/1135; Peacock Tariff Consulting Aug-26]
- **Limits:** ICRA: "stiff competition limits its pricing flexibility"; Q1 gross margin was flat despite the better mix (input costs). [ICRA Jul-26; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Cyclicality:** operating margin fell from 21–23% (FY17–20) to 10% (FY25) when US fibre inventories built up. Today's 20% sits in a data-centre-driven peak. [Screener cons; ICRA Jul-26]
- **Quality of the order book:** the **$1.1 bn deal is real** but soft: a Product Award Letter (USD 1.11 bn, 22 May 2026), through a subsidiary, FY27–29, at "potential value… based on prevailing selling prices", with purchase orders released over time. The Q1 book counts it in full (₹10,000+ cr of the ₹13,100 cr intake). Later: **USD ~1.2 bn hyperscaler supply agreement (calendar 2026–30, 1 Oct)**, USD 288 m hyperscaler cable, USD 210 m from a telecom infrastructure firm, ₹960 cr from an Indian operator. [NSE filings 22-May, 1-Aug, 5-Aug, 29-Aug, 1-Oct-26; IP Q1FY27]
- **Prysmian judgment, not provided for:** **$101.25 m** (about ₹900 cr) against the US subsidiary (trade secrets), confirmed Aug 2025; on appeal at the Fourth Circuit with a $41.53 m bond posted. PwC flags it as an emphasis of matter in FY26 and Q1. [AR FY26 p.353-354; Q1 results note 6]
- **US trade:** I found **no US anti-dumping or countervailing case on Indian optical fibre cable**. The exposure is reciprocal tariffs, which management says fell from 50% to 15%. STL paid "north of ₹100 crores" and seeks refunds with no timeline; the Lugoff plant and the $100 m plan reduce the exposure. [Q4 call; web search]
- **Promoter holding fell by dilution, not selling:** 53.98% → 44.18% from the Apr 2024 QIP, and 44.52% → 42.29% from the Jul 2026 QIP (the company's own note). Twin Star (Mauritius) holds 20.94 cr shares; control runs through Vedanta Inc (ex-Volcan) to the Anil Agarwal Discretionary Trust. No pledge was declared in FY26. [Screener; Q1 results note 5; AR FY26 p.228, 372; NSE SAST filings Apr-26]
- **Promoter warrants at ₹110:** 4.53 cr warrants allotted 30 Mar 2026 (approved 7 Feb), 25% paid; **₹373.7 cr is due on conversion by about Sep 2027**. They are worth about ₹4,000 cr at ₹887 and dilute others by about 8.8%. The price was set weeks before the $1.11 bn award. [NSE filing 30-Mar-26; my calc]
- **Related parties (FY26):** purchases from the group ₹295 cr (about ₹265 cr apparently Vedanta Ltd); sales ₹350 cr (Vedanta Ltd ₹176 cr, Hindustan Zinc ₹57 cr, STL Networks ₹56 cr); the non-STL-Networks part (~₹294 cr) roughly equals STL Digital's revenue (my inference). With **STL Networks**: a ₹347 cr guarantee and **₹3,434 cr of pass-through banking and collections** pending separation. [AR FY26 p.372-377]
- **Governance:** ₹11 cr of managerial pay exceeded legal limits and needed AGM approval; CARO notes slight delays in tax and provident-fund deposits. [Q1 results note 8; AR FY26 CARO]
- **Surveillance:** NSE lists the stock in series **BE** (trade-for-trade: delivery only, no intraday trading) and its 20 Oct ASM list puts it at **Long-Term ASM Stage IV**, the top stage, after the run-up. BSE stage IV since **11 May 2026** (Trendlyne); a 25 Jun report already describes BE trading. Exact NSE start date not found. [NSE EQUITY_L & ASM report 20-Oct-26; Trendlyne; askfuzz 25-Jun-26]
- **Inputs:** germanium and helium are tight and limit order-taking. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone went from defensive (April: was STL failing hyperscaler approvals?) to triumphant: the $1.11 bn award, a record ₹18,618 cr order book (2.4x), choosing orders for lack of capacity. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New issues: capacity is now the constraint, input costs are pressuring gross margin, and the US plant details are pending. Prysmian went unmentioned on the Q1 call although the auditor still flags it. [Q1 call; Q1 results]

## My read
- The business has turned: two hyperscaler agreements of about $1.1–1.2 bn each, margin above 20%, and a balance sheet repaired with equity. But it remains a historically cyclical fibre maker whose FY26 profit was ₹56 cr. [Screener; NSE filings]
- **What the price needs:** at ₹45,610 cr and a trailing P/E of about 193, it needs about 42% a year for ten years on trailing profit, or about 26% on Q1 annualised profit (~₹790 cr). Full Lakshya (₹20,000 cr at 27%) implies FY29 profit of about ₹3,500 cr, about 14x on 56 cr diluted shares. My base case (₹13,000–15,000 cr at 22–23%, profit ₹1,700–2,000 cr) implies about 25–30x FY29. The market is pricing in most of Lakshya. [Screener; my calc]
- **What would change the view:** Q2–Q3 delivery against the ₹2,228 cr book and 23% margin; hyperscaler purchase orders flowing; the Prysmian appeal; capex funding. Base case: ~25% a year earnings growth for 3–5 years from the current run-rate. [my view]
