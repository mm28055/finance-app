# Tips Music — deep research (Phase A1), 28 Sep 2026

Screener code TIPSMUSIC (standalone; the consolidated page is empty). Price ₹664, market value ₹8,493cr, P/E 39.6 (Screener, 28 Sep 2026).

## Business and segments
- A music label (founded 1988, Taurani family) that owns 38,000+ songs across 25+ languages, strongest in 1990s Hindi film music; licenses them to YouTube, Spotify, JioSaavn, Meta, Apple, Amazon, broadcasters and for public performance. 96 employees. [IP Q1FY27]
- Single reported segment (music). [AR FY26 note 8]
- Revenue mix: digital ~75% in Q1 FY27 (70% for FY26), non-digital (publishing, public performance, brand deals, TV) the rest. Paid subscription is 10–15% of digital revenue. ~75% of receipts are billed in foreign currency because YouTube and Warner bill from abroad. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- 85% of revenue comes from songs older than three years. [Q1 call]
- Distribution: Warner Music (digital distribution) and Sony Music Publishing — "the only major label" with both. [Q1 call]
- New content: roughly one-third from outside producers, one-third from Tips Films (a promoter-group company), one-third in-house non-film. Q1 FY27 released 73 songs (55 film). [Q1 call]
- Gujarati catalogue of ~4,000 songs bought in FY26 and digitised. [Q1 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, standalone; company figures where stated)
- Quarterly revenue (₹cr) Q1 FY26–Q1 FY27: 88, 89, 94, 104, 107; operating margin 64%, 76%, 79%, 74%, 50%; net profit 46, 53, 59, 59, 44. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹106.5cr (+21%), content cost ₹44.6cr (+90%), operating EBITDA ₹53.5cr (50.3%, from 64.2%), PAT ₹43.9cr (−4%). [IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: revenue FY24 242 → FY25 311 → FY26 376; operating profit 158 → 207 → 276 (margin 66% → 67% → 73%); net profit 127 → 167 → 217. FY26 content cost ₹59.2cr (FY25 ₹71.0cr) — only 16% of revenue because a film slipped. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- Return on capital 104% FY24, 109% FY25, 118% FY26; ROE 92% FY26 (company). [Screener; IP]
- Policy: the entire cost of a new album is expensed in the quarter of release; nothing is capitalised ("only listed player" to do so). [IP; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (23 Apr 2026): FY27 target 20% revenue and 20% profit growth ("for the comfort of investors … don't want to over-promise"); content spend ~₹80–90cr, 20–25% of revenue; YouTube Shorts renewal due June 2026; FY26 dividends ₹166cr. [Q4 call]
- Q1 call (22 Jul 2026): kept 20%/20% and 65–70% annual EBITDA margin; raised FY27 content budget to ₹90–100cr (a later answer on the same call accepted "₹85–90cr"); Shorts deal "still under negotiation", update by end-Q2; promised to return all of FY26 profit (₹217cr) via dividends and a buyback. [Q1 call]
- Delivery: Q1 revenue +21% (on target), PAT −4% (content cost front-loaded; revenue from the albums started mid-May). Q4 FY26 had delivered +32% revenue and +93% PAT; FY26 +21% revenue, +30% PAT against a 20%/25% target. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Buyback: board approved on 5 Aug 2026 an open-market buyback of up to ₹44.5cr at up to ₹750 (≈5.93 lakh shares, 0.46% of equity), promoters excluded; opens 9 Sep 2026. [AR FY26 notice; News: multibagg/ipowatch, Aug-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Almost none: fixed-asset purchases ₹0–6cr a year (capex 1% of sales over five years). Content is the real investment and it runs through the P&L. [Screener]
- Content budget FY27 ₹90–100cr (25%+ of revenue at the target) vs ₹59cr in FY26 and ₹71cr in FY25; management says it could reach 30% in some years. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and investments ₹345cr at June 2026; debt-free. Screener's cash-equivalent schedule shows only ₹8cr cash plus ₹150cr investments at Mar-26 (bank deposits sit elsewhere), so Screener-based net cash (₹153cr) understates the company figure. [IP; Screener]
- Operating cash FY26 ₹197cr (71% of operating profit), free cash ₹191cr; five-year cash conversion 80%. Debtor days 33. [Screener]
- Pays out heavily: dividend payout 61% FY24, 54% FY25, 77% FY26; plan to return 100% of FY26 profit in FY27. Buybacks in 2009–2025 cut the share count 26%. [Screener; IP]

## Moat evidence
- Catalogue power: 90s songs repeatedly trend on Instagram (e.g. "Deewana Mujhko Deewana Kar Gaya" 3bn views); YouTube subscribers 158.3m (153m in April). Annual YouTube views FY26 210bn vs 228bn FY25 (Shorts decline). [Q4, Q1 calls; IP]
- Pricing discipline: refuses bidding wars ("If I pay 40 crores … and it only does business of 2–3 crores … goes down the drain"). [Q4 call]
- Returns: operating margin 73% and ROCE 118% in FY26 show a catalogue earning far above its cost. [Screener]

## Risks and red flags
- Related-party content from Tips Films (Kumar and Ramesh Taurani each own ~20%): FY26 purchases of audio-video rights ₹17.3cr plus ₹15.5cr advance; shareholders asked to approve up to ₹60cr for FY27 (16% of turnover). Tips Films lost ₹15.8cr on ₹158cr turnover in FY26. Pricing to be checked by a valuer "after completion of the project". [AR FY26 AGM notice]
- Promoter selling: stake 75% (Sep 2023) → 68.92% after a 6.07% block sale (Dec 2023) → 64.15% (Jun 2026); press reports talks to sell a minority stake (UMG talks stalled). [Screener; News: Outlook Business, Dec-23; web search summary]
- CEO Hari Nair (who negotiated the Warner and Shorts deals) left on 30 Apr 2026; no replacement yet. [AR FY26; Q1 call]
- Platform dependence (YouTube, Warner-distributed DSPs); platform-wise split not disclosed; Shorts deal pending. [Q1 call]
- Contingent liabilities tiny (₹1.9cr). Auditor's opinion unmodified; key audit matter revenue recognition. [AR FY26]
- Content-cost inflation: Q1 cost per song ~₹61 lakh vs ~₹15 lakh last year (analyst); management says film songs cost more and per-song comparison is wrong. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- April: celebration of record margins and "90s repertoire". July: defending a 50% margin quarter and higher content budget (₹80–90cr → ₹90–100cr). Buyback deferred from 22 Jul to 5 Aug to use new open-market rules. [Q4, Q1 calls]
- Subscription: "10–15% of digital revenue", growing 30–40% (April) / 40–50% (July). [calls]

## Best management quotes
- "We are very cautious … It is better that we all take a dividend." — Kumar Taurani, Q4 call.
- "On an annual basis, the EBITDA margins will be in the range of 65–70%." — Q1 call.
- "The recovery of content costs … takes about 4 to 5 years." — Q1 call.

## My read
An exceptional, capital-light catalogue business that hands almost all its profit back. The next three years are harder than the last three: content spend is rising from 16% to 25%+ of revenue, the easy YouTube and subscription gains are shared with the whole industry (music grows ~9% a year per FICCI-EY), a promoter-group film company is becoming a larger supplier, and the CEO who ran platform deals has gone. I expect ~17% a year against the ~21% the price needs. A signed Shorts deal with ad-share, visible subscription growth, or content cost staying near 20% of revenue would lift my estimate.
