# AvenuesAI (formerly Infibeam Avenues) — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 28 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. Screener consolidated page /company/CCAVENUE/consolidated/ (the ticker changed from INFIBEAM to CCAVENUE in Feb 2026). The company reports **gross revenue** (the full merchant discount it collects, most of which it pays on to banks and card networks) and **net revenue** (what it keeps); Screener's revenue is the gross figure. A 1-for-10 share consolidation (face value ₹1 → ₹10) is under way, so company EPS figures are on the new ₹10 basis.
Stored transcripts are AI-generated copies from a third party. **Q1 FY27: tidied copy (figures match the official BSE transcript; names garbled — "Neuromix/Fronetic" for Neuromind/Phronetic).** **Q4 FY26: materially wrong in places** — it inserts "BillDesk" into the strategy (the official text names CCAvenue, RediffPay and Rediff) and calls the US associate "Exclus" (officially **XDuce**); financial figures match.

## Business and segments
- India's oldest listed payment gateway (**CCAvenue**, listed 2016): online and offline payment acceptance for 10 mn+ merchants, bill payments (**BillAvenue**, a Bharat Bill Pay operating unit), government and B2B payments, hotel booking software (RezAvenue), plus the **Rediff** consumer and enterprise platform (Rediffmail/RediffOne, RediffPay UPI app, Rediff TV), 82.66% owned. International business in the UAE, Oman and Saudi Arabia. [IP Q1FY27 slides 12–24; AR FY26]
- Two filed segments, FY26: **Payments** revenue ₹7,888cr (FY25 ₹3,787cr), segment result ₹203cr; **E-commerce platform** (enterprise software incl. Rediff) revenue ₹227cr (FY25 ₹206cr), segment result ₹124cr. So the small platform arm earned roughly 38% of segment profit. [AR FY26 consolidated segment note]
- Market share: "~8%" of India's digital payments (slide 18), "8% to 10%" of online acquiring (Q1 call); top 3 among B2B online gateways; "top 2" non-bank private player in the UAE. About 22% of transactions are UPI (earn no fee today). [IP Q1FY27 slide 18; Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27: payments volume (TPV) ₹1.48 lakh cr (+74% YoY, −11% QoQ); **payments net take rate 5.3 bps against 10.4 bps a year ago (−49%)** and 6.0 bps in Q4. [IP Q1FY27 slide 14–15]
- Marquee clients: IndiGo, Taj, Oberoi, ITC, MakeMyTrip, HUL, Jio, Airtel, Government of India, Emaar; management says it is not on Amazon or quick-commerce apps. [IP slide 18; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (29 May 2026):** no numbers; "we do not see a hockey stick in FY27"; FY27 profitability "similar to what we have seen"; guidance promised on the Q1 call; costs front-loaded for AI; will "work very hard" on take rates. International: 30% of revenue was a February 2024 target (shareholder's reminder), not met. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (11 Aug 2026):** first guidance — FY27 gross revenue ₹11,000–13,000cr (+36–60%) and **EPS ₹8.75–9.50 against FY26's ₹9.42, i.e. −7% to +1%**; a 15% "guardrail" (not a target) under which margins will not fall; AI revenue "9–18 months away"; Rediff IPO "slightly early to speak about"; platform business to show movement in Q3–Q4. International target now 12–15% of payments net revenue by FY28. [Q1 call; IP slides 11, 21]
- Delivery against Q4 tone: Q1 PAT +45% was better than "no hockey stick", but net revenue fell and take rates halved; guidance, when it came, was for flat EPS. **Kept (flat).**

## Capex and capacity
- Capex 3–9% of gross revenue (five-year 6%); FY26 segment capex ₹15.5cr payments/platform plus software and data centre spend. No large AI data-centre spending planned ("we may selectively invest in smaller capacities"). [Screener; Q1 call; AR FY26 segment note]
- Investments: up to 2.5% of NBFC **Ratnaafin Capital** (book above ₹2,000cr) and 7% of **Online PSB Loans**; 20% of US-based **XDuce** for $10m (2024). [IP slides 12–13; Q4 call]
- **₹700cr rights issue (FY26)**: ₹69.8cr repaid a subsidiary's loans, ₹87.7cr was earmarked for Rediff (₹76.7cr used), ₹294cr for the AI subsidiary (only ₹9.4cr used by March) and ₹243.9cr for "acquisitions of unidentified businesses" and general purposes (₹169.4cr used). [AR FY26 directors' report]
- Corporate actions: name change to AvenuesAI, merger of AI subsidiary Neuromind (Phronetic AI) into the parent, 1-for-10 consolidation, slump sale of infrastructure business to Rediff. [Q1 call; AR FY26]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and licences: payment aggregator (online), bill-payment unit, cross-border, PPI (new, Q1 FY27), UAE Category III in-principle licence. [IP slides 12, 20]
- Workflow lock-in: hotel reservation software for 3,000+ hotels certified with Opera; form builder onboarded 5,000+ institutions in a month; Shiprocket checkout integration. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Against that: the take rate halved in a year, net revenue fell while volume rose 74%, and management admits it wins volume "at a cost". India is "highly competitive". [IP slide 15; Q4 call; Q1 call]

- **UPI fee as the swing factor:** from 15 October 2026 merchants pay 0.4% on UPI payments above ₹2,000 (capped at ₹300 for payments of ₹75,000 and more); small payments and person-to-person transfers stay free. Bernstein puts the whole pool at up to ~₹22,000cr by FY28 and Citi at ₹16,000–17,000cr a year; how it is split among banks, apps and acquirers is not yet fixed. With 22% of its transactions on UPI and an 8–10% share of online acquiring, AvenuesAI is a natural gainer, but management says large incumbents "can subsidize payments" and it does not know the extent of the benefit. [Q1 call; News: Tribune, 17 Sep 2026]
- **Consumer leg:** RediffPay, a UPI app, left closed testing in August 2026; management assumes no meaningful contribution in FY27 guidance. Rediff's revenue is not disclosed separately; shareholders asked for it on the Q4 call. [Q1 call; Q4 call; News: Medianama, Aug 2026]

## Risks and red flags
- **Shrinking take rate** and net revenue; EPS guided flat.
- **Complexity and related entities:** AI subsidiary merged back; Rediff to list separately (SEBI approval reported Aug 2026; ₹600–800cr issue); XDuce associate made unrelated investments (shareholder on Q4 call) — management: "We do not participate on the board." [Q4 call; News: Tribune, 17 Sep 2026]
- **Dilution:** ~25% more shares in FY26 from the ₹700cr rights issue, a third of it for unnamed acquisitions; promoter holding 27.3% (29.1% in Sep 2023). [Screener]
- **Goodwill** ₹1,612cr; **corporate guarantee** ₹400cr. [AR FY26]
- Other income is a quarter of pre-tax profit. [Screener]
- Chairman takes no pay; the MD's remuneration was revised upward from April 2026 (up to ₹1.07cr fixed). [AR FY26 notice]
- The auditor's opinion is unmodified; audit-trail feature operated. [AR FY26 auditor's report]

## My read
AvenuesAI is a profitable, cash-generating payment gateway at a modest price (the live P/E is about 18), but the business under the headline is standing still: net revenue fell 3% in Q1 while volume rose 74%, the take rate halved, profit growth came from cost cuts and interest income, and management itself guides FY27 EPS at −7% to +1%. Add a 25% rise in share count, ₹1,612cr of goodwill, a stream of restructurings and rebrands, and a US associate whose spending shareholders question. The price needs about 12% a year; I expect about 8%. The UPI merchant fee and a Rediff listing are the upside; I would pass until net revenue grows again.
