# Basilic Fly Studio — deep research (Phase A1), 28 Sep 2026

Screener code BASILIC (consolidated; NSE SME Emerge). Price ₹167, market value ₹421cr, P/E 8.8 (Screener, 28 Sep 2026).

## Business and segments
- Visual-effects (VFX) services studio founded 2013 in Chennai; listed on NSE SME in Sep 2023 (287x oversubscribed). Studios in Chennai, Pune, Bengaluru (remote, physical site from Oct 2026), London, Paris and Vancouver; ~790–800 staff. Single segment (VFX services). [Q1 call Aug-26; IP Q1FY27]
- Bought 70% of UK studio One of Us (BAFTA/Emmy-winning; ~300 people) in July/August 2024; One of Us was a client before. Work on Mission: Impossible, The Crown, Napoleon, Stranger Things 5, F1, Peaky Blinders. [Q1 call; IP]
- Two models: India does sub-contract and "service" work (roto, prep, compositing) on time-and-material; overseas subsidiaries hold direct studio contracts, often fixed-milestone for whole series. [Conference 25 Sep; Q1 call]
- FY26 consolidated revenue ₹408cr; India standalone ₹120cr (+64%), which includes intercompany work for One of Us. Geography Q1 FY27: Europe 79%, North America 10%, others 11% (FY26: Europe 60%, North America 21%). Netflix and Amazon >50% of revenue; top five clients ~60%. [Q4 call Jun-26; IP Q1FY27; investor meet Jul-26]
- Q1 FY27: 89 projects for 60 clients (10 new); domestic OTT (Netflix, Amazon India originals) already >2x FY26's domestic revenue. [Q1 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly revenue (₹cr) Q1 FY26–Q1 FY27: 94.2, 95.2, 105.0, 113.4, 103.5; operating margin 19.7%, 23.7%, 16.9%, 21.1%, 13.9%; net profit 12.0, 14.4, 8.9, 15.4, 6.7. Q1 FY27 revenue +9.9%, EBITDA −23%, PAT −44%. [Screener; IP]
- Annual: revenue FY24 103 → FY25 304 → FY26 408 (One of Us consolidated from Aug 2024); operating profit 49 → 72 → 85 (margin 48% → 24% → 21%); net profit 37 → 45 → 51; TTM profit 45. [Screener]
- India standalone FY26: EBITDA 43.1%, PAT ₹31.6cr (24.2% margin); Q1 FY27 revenue ₹26cr (+28%), EBITDA 42.3%. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- ROCE 63% FY24 → 30% FY25 → 22% FY26; company ROE 18%. [Screener; IP]
- Q1 FY27 one-offs named by the CFO: unrealised FX loss (Canadian dollar), management travel for M&A, overlapping migration and IT costs; severance ₹2.9cr (exceptional) vs ₹3.2cr for all of FY26. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (Jun 2026): FY27 revenue growth ~30% "conservative" (organic), margin +1.5–2 points (later "2–2.5%"), PAT "₹65–70cr … should surpass ₹70cr"; order book ₹232cr, bid pipeline ₹456cr, historical win rate 60–70%; aged receivables to be cleared "latest maybe by H1". [Q4 call]
- Investor meet (2 Jul): revenue growth "in line with or slightly better than last year" (34%); margins +2–2.5 points. [Investor meet]
- Q1 call (17 Aug): Q1 PAT −44%; kept "~30%, or surpass the historical trend" organic growth; order book ₹250–260cr (end-July, to be delivered in FY27, including ₹105cr won this year); bid pipeline ₹700cr (40% advanced); Q3–Q4 to be the best quarters; no margin number repeated. [Q1 call]
- Track record: the Q4 promise of margin expansion was followed by a 13.9% margin quarter; the order book and pipeline grew. Main-board migration "due from September 26", still under discussion with a Big Four firm. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 investing outflow ₹86cr; capex ₹73cr on AI tools, USD pipeline, owned NetApp storage (replacing AWS cloud; claimed ~40% cost cut, earlier "62%"). [Q4 call; Screener; investor meet]
- ₹59cr of "intangible assets under development" at Mar-26, largely internal staff time capitalised as software/R&D; to be capitalised into use in Q2–Q3 FY27, so depreciation rises. [Q4 call]
- Bengaluru: 39 remote artists, physical 150-seat site from October, target 100–150 by Mar-27. [Q1 call]
- M&A: a North American studio (commercials, profitable) at an advanced stage, ticket size ₹200–300cr (majority stake), to be funded from cash; ₹37cr of QIP and ₹21cr of IPO money unused. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Operating cash FY24 −₹28cr, FY25 +₹20cr, FY26 +₹23cr; free cash flow negative every year FY24–FY26 (−₹29cr, −₹80cr, −₹50cr). Five-year operating cash ÷ operating profit 11%. [Screener]
- Borrowings ₹106cr at Mar-26 (incl. HDFC term loan ~₹19cr secured by FDs and a pledge of shares; UK government-backed Coutts loan ~₹11cr; leases), cash ₹74cr → net debt ₹32cr (Screener). Company calls itself "virtually net debt-free" (net debt/equity 0.03). [Screener; AR FY26 note on borrowings; investor meet]
- Debtor days 124 (FY26); India receivables ₹100cr at March, of which ₹35cr intercompany; aged (>6 months) ~₹44cr being collected in tranches. Raised ₹85cr by QIP in Sep 2025. No dividend. [Screener; Q4 call; conference 25 Sep]
- CRISIL A-/Stable (Jan 2026): strengths — track record, clientele, global delivery model, low gearing; weaknesses — technological obsolescence, FX risk (99% export), large working capital (249 days of gross current assets); bank-limit use ~85%. [CRISIL Jan-26]

## Moat evidence
- Cost: India delivery 30–40% cheaper; an India artist costs 1/7–1/8 of London or Paris. UK and France tax credits allow part of the work (management: 10–15% allowance) to be done in India. [Q1 call; conference]
- Security certifications (TPN, Apple, Disney; "TPN Stella" award) needed to handle pre-release studio content. [Q1 call]
- Creative credibility through One of Us; bidding ticket size up from GBP 1–2m to GBP 4–5m after senior hires; one GBP 4–4.5m win. [Q1 call]
- But: FICCI-EY says VFX was repriced in 2025 ("fewer premium builds") and management admits it gave discounts in 2024–25. [FICCI-EY via AnimationXpress; conference]

## Risks and red flags
- **Unaudited subsidiary**: the auditor (L U Krishnan & Co.) says Basilic Fly Studio Canada's accounts (revenue ₹67cr, profit ₹10.1cr — about a fifth of group profit) are unaudited and were furnished by management; UK subsidiaries (revenue ₹287cr, profit ₹7.2cr) were audited by other auditors. [AR FY26 consolidated audit report, Other Matter]
- Capitalised internal costs (₹59cr) are larger than a year's profit; if the AI/pipeline spend does not pay back, it will be written down. [Q4 call]
- Cash: profits have not become cash (11% conversion over five years; free cash negative three years running). [Screener]
- Concentration: Netflix + Amazon >50%; Europe 79% of Q1 revenue. [Q4 call; IP]
- Execution: severance and migration from London/Paris; M&A may restart the cycle (CFO: "Yes, it may start"). [Q1 call]
- Promoter holding 59.9% (Sep 2023) → 55.3% (Jun 2026), mostly QIP dilution; related-party loans with a whole-time director appear in the standalone accounts. Contingent liabilities: nil. [Screener; AR FY26 note 34]
- Investors on the Q1 call: share price roughly halved since IPO, margins halved; "history of negative surprises". [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- June: "30% growth is conservative", PAT ₹65–70cr, margin up 1.5–2.5 points. August: a −44% PAT quarter blamed on one-offs; guidance restated as organic ~30%, no margin number; pipeline up from ₹456cr to ₹700cr; order book ₹232cr → ₹250–260cr. [Q4, Q1 calls]

## My read
A real cost advantage and a growing order book, bought at a price that asks for almost no growth (the P/E of ~9 needs ~4% a year). But the numbers have gone the wrong way for two years — margin from 24% to 14%, profit from ₹51cr to a TTM ₹45cr, cash conversion near zero — and a fifth of profit sits in unaudited Canadian accounts while ₹59cr of staff time has been capitalised. I expect ~10% earnings growth with a wide range (0–25%). I would need to see H2 FY27 deliver the promised margin, receivables fall below ~90 days, free cash flow turn positive and the Canada subsidiary audited before owning it.
