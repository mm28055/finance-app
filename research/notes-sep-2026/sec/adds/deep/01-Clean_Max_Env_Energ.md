# Clean Max Env. Energ — deep research (Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions, NSE: CLEANMAX)

Screener code used: CLEANMAX (consolidated page has figures). Listed 2 March 2026. Written 28 Sep 2026.

## Business and segments
- India's largest renewable-power seller to companies (commercial and industrial, "C&I"): it builds, owns and runs solar, wind and hybrid plants and sells the power under long contracts (PPAs) averaging ~23 years (23.25 years at June 2026), with an average lock-in of ~18 years. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.26]
- Two segments. **RE Power Sales** (own plants, "opex" model): Q1 FY27 revenue ₹528cr, adjusted EBITDA ₹460cr, margin 83.7% (76.4% a year earlier). **RE Services** (building plants that clients own on their books, plus O&M, common-infrastructure fees and carbon credits): Q1 revenue ₹300cr (₹41cr a year earlier), EBITDA margin 11.2%; FY26 revenue ₹497cr at 19.6%. [IP Q1FY27 p.8, p.27]
- Capacity at 30 June 2026: 3,493 MW operational in power sales (80% solar, 20% wind; 2,791 MWp solar DC), 2,510 MW contracted and under execution, 6,003 MW contracted in total; RE Services 682 MW operational plus 147 MW under construction. Q1 additions: 403 MW power sales + 127 MW services. [IP Q1FY27 p.6–7]
- Run-rate EBITDA by location (FY26 end, ~₹1,870cr): Karnataka STU ~33%, Gujarat STU ~30%, CTU (Rajasthan) ~13%, onsite rooftop ~11%, others ~13%. 593 customers, 1,328 PPAs/contracts, 7 states operating and 3 more under construction; rooftops also in UAE, Bahrain and Thailand. [IP Q1FY27 p.26, p.35]
- Customers: 42% of contracted capacity is data centres and AI (Amazon, Google, Meta, Apple JV, NTT, STT, Equinix, Iron Mountain, Princeton Digital, L&T Data); 58% conventional industry (UltraTech, Grasim, GACL, Cargill, Sansera, Saint-Gobain). About 1.6–1.8 GW of the 5.7 GW is contract-for-difference (VPPA/EAPA) with global tech firms: power sold on the exchange, the buyer tops up to a fixed price. [IP Q1FY27 p.12; Q4 call]

- Company EBITDA includes other income: FY26 reported EBITDA ₹1,295cr (+28%) against Screener's ₹1,132cr; Q1 reported EBITDA ₹463cr, adjusted ₹494cr (+74%). PAT attributable to owners FY26 ₹94cr, Q1 FY27 ₹48.5cr. [IP Q1FY27 p.27, p.30–31]
- Unit economics: realised tariff ₹4.06/kWh (TTM); 2.5 GW under execution contracted at ₹4.00 (Q4: ₹3.85 for 2.6 GW); capex ~₹3.5cr/MWp solar, ~₹7.8cr/MW wind (excl. 5–7% soft costs); EBITDA ₹50–55 lakh/MWp solar, ₹100–110 lakh/MW wind. PLFs TTM: wind 35.4%, offsite solar 25.4% AC, hybrid 47.6%. Generation Q1 1,302 million kWh (+53%). [IP Q1FY27 p.10, p.25–26]
- ROCE 6% (FY26), ROE 1.8% (Screener). Management cites cash ROIC ~13% and cash ROE ~17% (shareholder letter, not read). [Screener; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (13 May 2026): at least 1.5 GW of new power-sales capacity in FY27, "100%" already contracted; no financial guidance, but reported EBITDA has historically been ~1.1x the prior year-end run-rate (₹1,870cr at March 2026). Bikaner curtailment ~30%; grid said relief by September but management told investors to be more conservative. A storage announcement promised "in three or four months". RE power-sales margin to reach ~86% in 3–4 years. Would not predict interest costs. [Q4 call]
- Q1 call (3 Aug 2026): 500 MW commissioned in Q1 (403 MW sales); "meet or exceed" 1.5 GW; **new guidance: reported EBITDA of at least ₹3,000cr in FY28** (2.3x FY26's ₹1,295cr) on at least 4.6 GW operating by 1 April 2027, with steady-state net debt of ₹16,000cr. First battery (BESS) project approved in Rajasthan and MOUs with three clients. Curtailment at Bikaner now ~70% and assumed for all of FY27 (~₹170cr of EBITDA, "8% or 9%"). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.10]
- Verdict: guidance kept and extended; the storage step arrived on time; the curtailment hit worsened. Latest call leads: the Q4 view that curtailment might ease by September is superseded.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹5,754cr (Screener cash-flow purchase of fixed assets); CWIP ₹5,343cr at March 2026. Gross block ₹14,138cr at June. [Screener; Q1 call]
- 1.5 GW a year at a 70:30 solar-wind mix implies roughly ₹7,000cr+ of capex a year (my arithmetic from the unit costs). Funded 70–80% with project debt (CRISIL). CRISIL (7 Sep 2026) rated a planned ₹2,500cr NCD: ~₹1,100cr to refinance SPV debt, ~₹1,400cr for equity in plants over 15–18 months. [CRISIL Sep-26]
- CTU pipeline: one ~500 MW CTU site a year; FY27 includes the 529–543 MW Koppal (Karnataka) hybrid, two bays due October 2026 and March 2027. Evacuation available/applied for ~6 GW beyond contracted capacity; 12.9 GW total portfolio. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.34]
- Built within budget four years running (actual 95–98% of budget). TTM commissioning 1,743 MW vs 449 MW a year earlier. [IP Q1FY27 p.14]
- Apple JV: Apple invests ₹104cr for 49% of 150 MW (second JV). [Q4 call]
- Payback: management targets constant IRRs; module-cost changes pass into tariffs. ALMM List-II deferral saves ~₹60 lakh/MW on plants finished by 31 Dec 2026, to CleanMax's account on contracted volumes. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt (company) ₹11,809cr at June vs ₹9,684cr at March; 44% (₹5,155cr) against plants running >1 year (Q1 EBITDA ₹335cr, so ~3.8x annualised, my arithmetic), ₹2,045cr against plants <1 year, 38% (₹4,484cr) against assets under construction. Screener: borrowings ₹12,684cr, cash ₹2,288cr at March. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.17; Screener]
- Cost of project debt 8.45% (9.05% a year earlier); ~40% fixed-rate; average loan tenor ~19 years vs PPA 23 years; DSCR 1.3x on stabilised projects. Ratings: CARE AA-/Stable (upgraded from A+ in May 2026); CRISIL AA/Stable assigned 7 Sep 2026. [Q4 call; CARE May-26; CRISIL Sep-26]
- IPO Feb 2026 at ₹1,053 a share: ₹3,080cr issue (₹1,196cr fresh, ₹1,884cr offer for sale); listed 2 March at ₹867.50 on NSE. With the pre-IPO round, ₹1,500cr of fresh money, ~₹1,120cr used to repay debt. [News: Chittorgarh; CRISIL Sep-26; CARE May-26]
- Receivables: 22 days in power sales (Q1), 25 days FY26; Screener's 53 debtor days include the services business. Operating cash FY26 ₹1,731cr (Screener), 153% of operating profit; free cash −₹4,039cr (growth capex). No dividend. [IP Q1FY27 p.13; Screener]
- CRISIL expects net debt/EBITDA 5.5–5.7x in FY27; CARE 5.0–5.3x (opening). Company target 5.0–5.5x. [CRISIL; CARE; IP p.10]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and focus: 100% C&I for 15+ years; ~14% share of the fragmented C&I renewables market (up from ~12%, per management citing recent statistics) and an estimated ~35% of hyperscaler clean-power deals in India (company estimate). [Q1 call]
- Switching costs: group-captive clients must put 26% equity into the plant company (~100 SPVs with client equity); 74–79% of new volumes from existing clients; 1,300+ contracts and "not a single ongoing client dispute". [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Credit quality: 81% of capacity with AA/AAA/MNC clients, 95% A- and above. [IP Q1FY27 p.26]
- Pricing: tariff on new contracts steady to rising (₹3.85 → ₹4.00); customers still save ~22–25% on power bills. Management claims 30–35% higher EBITDA per MW than utility players on a like-for-like (AC vs DC) basis. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Cost of capital falling (9.5% → 8.45% in two years) as ratings rise. Weakness: the model rests on state open-access and group-captive rules. [IP Q1FY27 p.19; CRISIL]

## Risks and red flags
- **Curtailment:** 525 MW Bikaner CTU plant ~70% curtailed, ~₹170cr FY27 EBITDA hit; PPAs are not take-or-pay. New deviation-settlement (DSM) rules sub judice. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Regulation:** open-access charges, banking rules (Maharashtra cut its banking window from ~17 to ~8 hours), group-captive eligibility; Karnataka and Gujarat are ~63% of run-rate EBITDA. [CARE May-26; IP p.35]
- **Leverage and refinancing:** ₹400cr non-project debt bullet in June 2027; ~₹1,200cr FCNR(B) project debt due for renewal Sept 2028; most loans floating. Interest coverage 1.7x FY26 (CRISIL). [CARE May-26; CRISIL Sep-26]
- **Founder pledge:** at 31 March 2026 Kuldeep Jain and KEMPINC LLP had pledged 1,15,97,866 shares (~9.9% of the company, roughly half of the founder group's holding, my arithmetic) to 360 ONE Prime for KEMPINC borrowings. [AR FY26 note 19(g)]
- **Overhang:** Brookfield holds ~21.3% (down from ~50%); promoter lock-in ends February 2027 except 20% locked to Feb 2029. Screener shows FIIs 29.8% → 11.2% and public 6.0% → 24.7% from March to June, probably a reclassification (not verified). [CARE May-26; Screener]
- **Auditor remarks (B S R & Co., new auditor for FY26):** unmodified opinion; key audit matters on percentage-of-completion revenue (₹386cr of project revenue) and impairment of ₹16,729cr of plant and CWIP. Audit trail not enabled at database level for the holding company and 32 subsidiaries until 27 March 2026. CARO flags for the holding company: title deeds (i)(c) and late statutory dues (vii)(a) — ₹24cr and ₹73cr of FY26 advance tax paid in March–April 2026, 6–9 months late; 12 subsidiaries with cash losses. [AR FY26 pp.144–152, standalone CARO]
- **Contingent items:** tax claims ~₹98cr; bank guarantees ₹1,037cr; capital commitments ₹831cr; put options to buy back the 26% client stakes at fair value. [AR FY26 note 38]
- **Related parties:** ~35 material related-party resolutions with subsidiaries at the July AGM (intra-group EPC, loans, guarantees); a September corrigendum changed the tenure of RPT approvals in a postal ballot. Transactions with Brookfield entities small (rent, rooftop power). [AR FY26 AGM notice, note 48; Screener announcements]
- **Accounting noise:** FY26 ₹414cr pre-tax cash-flow-hedge gain in other comprehensive income on VPPA contracts (no P&L effect per management); minority interest negative as young SPVs make accounting losses; Screener flags possible interest capitalisation. [Q4 call; Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone more confident: FY28 EBITDA floor added; storage moved from "decision in 3–4 months" to a first project and three MOUs; bond issuance after the rating upgrade.
- Worse: Bikaner curtailment from ~30% (with a September grid fix) to ~70% for the whole year.
- Tariff on the under-construction book up from ₹3.85 to ₹4.00; repeat share 79% vs 74%.
- Refused to give Q1 capex; stopped publishing quarterly run-rate EBITDA.

## My read
The business is improving: capacity is compounding, the power-sales margin has risen from 75% to 84%, debt is getting cheaper and ratings are rising, and management has kept every capacity promise so far. The contracted book and 23-year PPAs make its earnings far more visible than anything else in this note, and cheaper modules help it. But the price (P/E about 103) needs about 33% a year of earnings growth for a decade; I expect about 30% over three to five years from a tiny profit base as young plants mature, which makes it full rather than cheap. Heavy debt (net ₹11,809cr), curtailment, open-access rule changes, a founder pledge and the Brookfield lock-in ending in February 2027 are the things that would change my mind; a fall toward the IPO price (₹1,053) with the FY28 floor on track would make it an own.
