# Saregama India — deep research (Phase A1), 28 Sep 2026

Screener code SAREGAMA (consolidated). Price ₹490, market value ₹9,444cr, P/E 41.4 (Screener, 28 Sep 2026).

## Business and segments
- India's oldest music label (RPSG group, 1902 legacy): owns 1.8 lakh+ songs "in perpetuity" (masters and underlying works), adds ~5,000–6,000 releases a year; 680m+ followers/subscribers across owned channels. [IP Q1FY27]
- FY26 segments (consolidated, ₹cr): Music (licensing + Carvaan/vinyl retail) revenue 683.5, result 364.1; Artist management 130.9, result 12.7; Video (films, TV, digital, short form) 108.4, result 1.5; Events 61.8, result −7.1. Total revenue 984.6 (FY25: 1,171.4, which included the one-off Diljit Dosanjh tour, events ₹285cr). [AR FY26 note 42]
- From FY26 management reports "music vertical" = licensing + artist management + retail: FY26 ₹814cr (+17%), EBITDA ₹517cr (+22%), music net margin (EBITDA less content charge) ₹377cr (+28%). [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹263.6cr: licensing + artist management ₹213.1cr (+43%), retail ₹17.5cr (+3%), video ₹17.0cr (−52%), live events ₹16.0cr (+214%). Music EBITDA ₹139.8cr (+36%), music net margin ₹99.6cr (+31%). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Revenue sources FY26: 67% platforms (Spotify, YouTube, Netflix, JioHotstar, Sun TV), 19% brands, 14% direct-to-customer. [IP Q1FY27]
- Geography FY26: India ₹601.6cr, abroad ₹383.0cr (39%). One customer was >10% of revenue (₹129cr; FY25 two customers ₹351cr). [AR FY26 note 42]
- 60% of FY26 music revenue came from songs released after 2000, 45% from after 2020. [Q1 call]
- Artist management: 309 artists (33 added in Q1) with 440m+ followers; ~₹40cr a quarter at ~10% margin. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Pocket Aces (90.93% owned; FilterCopy, short-form video) reached break-even in FY26; Bhansali Productions: ₹325cr for 28% (CCPS) completed 30 Jan 2026, right to go to 51%, exclusive music of all future Bhansali films at a pre-agreed formula. [AR FY26; Q4 call; MBW Dec-25]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly revenue (₹cr) Q1 FY26–Q1 FY27: 207, 230, 260, 287, 264; operating margin 27%, 30%, 35%, 42%, 35%; net profit 37, 44, 51, 74, 52. Q1 FY27 revenue +27.5% y/y, profit +41% (company PAT ₹51.9cr vs ₹36.5cr). [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: revenue FY24 803 → FY25 1,171 → FY26 985; operating profit 249 → 277 → 337 (margin 31% → 24% → 34%); net profit 198 → 204 → 206. TTM revenue 1,041, operating profit 374, profit 221. [Screener]
- Depreciation (mostly music-rights amortisation) FY22 13 → FY26 82 → TTM 88; other income FY24 61 → FY26 34 → TTM 24 as QIP cash is spent. [Screener]
- Return on equity 13.3% FY26 (company); ROCE 17% FY25–FY26 (Screener) vs 23% FY22. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Company "adjusted EBITDA" (before content charge) FY26 ₹404.7cr (41% margin), content charge ₹139.9cr; Q1 FY27 adjusted EBITDA ₹112.4cr (+69%). [IP Q1FY27]
- IRR on new music claimed at 26%; new music is charged through the P&L over ~10 years, pays back in ~5, then earns 60–80 years. [IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (14 May 2026): music vertical revenue 20–23% CAGR over 3–5 years, music EBITDA margin 60–65%; new-content spend FY27 ₹300–350cr; ₹1,000cr over FY25–FY27 then growth "only in a linear fashion" (high single to low double digit); music EBIT margin +300–500bps over 3–5 years; UN40 festival to break even by FY28. [Q4 call]
- Q1 call (4 Aug 2026): all kept — 20–23% music growth "only", 60–65% music EBITDA margin, ₹300–350cr content spend "mostly already committed". Refused quarterly guidance. [Q1 call]
- Delivery: Q1 music revenue +39% (low base), music EBITDA margin 61% (within range); FY26 content spend ₹235cr organic + ₹105cr catalogues vs plan (big films Love & War, Paradise slipped). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Video wind-down on plan (−52%); Pocket Aces break-even delivered in FY26 as promised. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- "Capex" here is buying music: Screener shows fixed-asset purchases of ₹162cr (FY25) and ₹214cr (FY26); company cash-flow line "spent on new content" ₹265cr in FY26 and ₹44cr in Q1 FY27. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- CARE counts ~₹316cr invested in FY25 and ~₹339cr in FY26 of the ₹1,000cr FY25–FY27 plan. [CARE Jul-26]
- Bhansali ₹325cr (FY26), Pocket Aces second tranche (Apr 2025), Finnet Media (Nov 2025) bought by Pocket Aces. [AR FY26]
- An analyst on the Q1 call could not reconcile the content-spend line (₹186cr in the Q4 deck vs ₹265cr in Q1's); management took it offline. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Free cash flow (Screener): FY25 +₹170cr, FY26 −₹114cr (operating cash ₹100cr, content purchases ₹214cr). Five-year operating cash ÷ operating profit 55%. [Screener]
- Net cash: borrowings ₹73cr at Mar-26 (working-capital lines, ₹10.5cr ESOP trust credit); cash and liquid investments ~₹203cr incl. ₹101cr unspent QIP money (CARE). Screener's ₹379cr of investments includes the ₹325cr Bhansali stake, so it is not spare cash. [CARE Jul-26; Screener]
- Cash cycle 164 days FY26 (debtor days 66; film/TV inventory). [Screener]
- Dividend ₹4.5 a share, ~42% payout. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Second-largest label by music revenue after T-Series; No. 1 in new music (last 12 months) across multiple languages; "on non-digital I'm number 1". [Q4 call]
- 25–30% share of new film music bought in the market, which management now wants to hold rather than grow. [Q4 call]
- Refused to buy Dhurandhar 2's music on price (five-year payback rule). [Q4 call]
- CARE AA-/Stable (reaffirmed 3 Jul 2026): strong brand, largest archive, 56% of licensing revenue from 21st-century songs; weaknesses: format obsolescence, piracy, high cost of content. [CARE Jul-26]

## Risks and red flags
- Content inflation: ₹1,000cr over three years; charges and amortisation have swallowed revenue growth (net profit flat FY24–FY26). [Screener; Q4 call]
- Earnings quality/disclosure: content-spend line inconsistency; "unallocable expenditure" rose from ₹7cr (FY23) to ₹74cr (FY26) — explained as falling interest income netted in; the analyst said the numbers did not add up. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Bhansali: ₹325cr in a private film producer whose value is linked to its next three years; auditors made it a key audit matter; the company is committed to buy more if its stake falls below 28%. [AR FY26 KAM]
- Contingent liabilities: income tax ₹28.8cr, indirect taxes ₹99.2cr (~7.5% of net worth ₹1,692cr). [AR FY26 note 39]
- Related party: ₹15cr brand royalty to RPSG Resources (promoter-controlled) FY26 (₹14cr FY25); CSR routed to RPSG CSR Trust. [AR FY26 note 36; CARE]
- CFO Pankaj Chaturvedi left 31 Mar 2026; new CFO Abhishek Kapoor (ex-Sula) from Q1 FY27. [AR FY26; Q1 call]
- Customer concentration: one customer ₹129cr (13%). Short-form (Instagram/YouTube Shorts) deals are flat-fee annual renewals — "one of the areas which bothers us". [AR FY26; Q4 call]
- Auditor's opinion unmodified; key audit matters: revenue recognition from licence fees, Bhansali acquisition; audit trail operated. [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "free-platform shutdowns are behind us" (May) to AI and "new-age IP company" (Aug); guidance unchanged. [Q4, Q1 calls]
- New: two GenAI teams (AI music videos for old songs at ~₹70,000 each, podcasts), a multi-language pop content deal, Carvaan Live (23 shows), US tours (Ilaiyaraaja, Arjun Dhillon). [Q1 call]
- Music EBITDA fell 1% y/y in Q1? — an analyst's reading; management blamed mix (artist management at ~10% margin). Company's own Q1 music EBITDA was +36% on music revenue +39%. [Q1 call; IP]

## My read
The music engine is healthy (music revenue +17% in FY26 and +39% in Q1, net margin up), and the heavy content-buying phase ends with FY27, so profits should finally grow faster than the flat ₹198–206cr of FY24–FY26. But this is a capital-hungry model (₹1,000cr of content plus ₹325cr for Bhansali) that earns 13% on equity, turns a little over half its operating profit into cash and relies on a film producer for its best new albums. At about 41 times earnings the price needs ~22% a year; I expect ~15%. It would change with two to three years of music net margin rising and free cash flow turning positive after FY27.
