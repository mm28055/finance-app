## Capex and capacity
- **Today:** fibre 28 mn fkm and cable 39 mn fkm, both fully used. The ribbon-cable (IBR) line is ~14 mn fkm. No own preform (the glass rod fibre is drawn from); HFCL buys ~1,000 t a year [NSE 14-Sep-26; Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Fibre and cable:** fibre to 33.9 mn fkm by Dec 2026 and 43.1 by July 2028; cable to 42.36 by Dec 2026 and 62.0 by July 2028 [NSE 4-Aug-26, 14-Sep-26].
- **Preform:** first planned as 300 t for ₹580 cr by July 2029. It is now 600 t for ~₹1,250 cr by Oct 2028, in Madhya Pradesh. The fibre, cable and preform plans total ~₹1,800 cr [NSE 25-Mar-26, 14-Sep-26; IP Q2FY27].
- **Other projects:**
  - Data-centre assemblies: ₹215 cr for 2.7 lakh a year by Sep 2027; HTL capacity to rise 5x.
  - Grenade plant: ₹230 cr for 40 lakh units a year by Dec 2027.

  [NSE 22-Jul-26, 14-May-26; Q1 call]
- **Spend:** capex guidance was FY27 ₹640 cr and FY28 ₹615 cr (Q1), up from ₹600 cr and ₹350 cr (Q4). Both predate ₹1,220 cr of later approvals (₹400 cr in Aug, ₹820 cr in Sep). H1 FY27 actual was ₹436 cr [Q1 call; Q4 call; Q2 results].
- **Funding and payback:** funded by internal cash, debt and warrant money. Making preform is "at least 30%" cheaper than buying (Q4 said 15–20%), which would cut cable material cost by ~10–12%. FY26 sales were ~4.1x net block [NSE 14-Sep-26; Q1 call; Screener].

## Risks and red flags
- **Cycle:** in April Nahata said prices had "reached... almost the final level" [Q4 call]. CARE's downgrade trigger is a margin below 12%; CARE cut HFCL to A− in Sep 2025 before raising it to A (Positive) in Jul 2026 [CARE Jul-26].
- **Promoter holding fell from 37.84% (Dec 2023) to 28.29%**, mostly through selling:
  - MN Ventures sold 1.70 cr shares in Mar 2025, 4.03 cr in Jun 2025 and 2.05 cr in Jul–Nov 2025, falling from 18.98% to 14.77%. Anant Nahata also sold 20 lakh [NSE SAST Reg 29(2)].
  - In all, about 11 cr fewer promoter shares (~7 points) between Jun 2024 and Sep 2025; dilution, mainly the Dec 2025 QIP, explains ~2 points [Screener; AR FY26].
  - The same group now holds warrants for 7.5 cr shares at ₹74 with the price at ₹269. Conversion would lift the promoter stake to ~31.6% [NSE 25-Mar-26].
- **Pledges:** 90 lakh promoter shares (2.08% of promoter holding). Most bank loans also carry the MD's personal guarantee and an MN Ventures guarantee, plus a pledge of 23.9% of HTL [NSE pledge data 30-Sep-26; AR FY26 p.331].
- **Defence subsidiary (HASPL):** HFCL will own only 51%.
  - Other holders: the MD's relative Anant Nahata 6.5%; the Gupta sellers 9.5%, with veto rights; outside investors 18%; an employee pool 15%.
  - HFCL sells its thermal-sight business (₹50 cr) and 80% of Raddef (₹75 cr) into it.
  - ~₹292 cr of the Defsys business's net liabilities becomes goodwill.

  [NSE 25-Mar-26, 3-Jun-26; AR FY26 p.348]
- **Related parties:** FY26 purchases from promoter-group Exicom were ₹64 cr (₹11 cr in FY25). MD pay was ₹12.07 cr (₹7.50 cr). There is no regular chairman [AR FY26 pp.348–350; AR CG report].
- **History:** a 2007 SEBI order found that HFCL and its promoters gave ₹736 cr (net ₹448 cr) to Ketan Parekh entities in 2000–01; this was settled in 2010 for ₹10 cr. In 2025 SEBI sent an administrative warning on insider-trading code lapses, and HFCL paid ₹2.9 cr to compound late tax-at-source deposits [SEBI order Mar-07; Moneylife Jan-10; NSE 26-Jul-25, 10-Jun-25].
- **Surveillance:** HFCL is in NSE long-term ASM Stage IV, the strictest stage of an exchange watch list for stocks with big price moves. That brings trade-for-trade ("BE") settlement, 100% margin and a 5% daily band. It has been in Stage IV since 31 Aug 2026 (BSE), with at least 90 days before review [NSE ASM list 20-Oct-26; NSE sec_list; Trendlyne ASM; NSE ASM FAQ].
- **Disclosure gaps:**
  - Q4 FY26 telecom revenue was ₹1,206 cr on the call but ₹1,508 cr in the Q1 filing (regrouped).
  - The acquired business's export order book was quoted as ₹1,570, 1,930, 1,890 and ~1,600 cr in different documents.

  [Q4 call; Q1 results; NSE 25-Mar-26, 3-Jun-26; IP Q2FY27]
- **Other:** the E&Y-led restructuring may split the businesses [Q1 call]. The auditor's opinion and the CARO report (auditor's statutory checklist) are clean [AR FY26 pp.207–214].

## My read
The business has changed very fast. The telecom margin went from 6% to 31% in eighteen months, and H1 FY27 profit (₹640 cr) is already about double FY26, with each guidance raise beaten. The weak points are elsewhere:
- five years of profit produced little cash;
- ₹2.5k cr is tied up in a loss-making turnkey arm;
- promoters sold about 11 cr shares, then took warrants at ₹74;
- a promoter relative sits in the defence subsidiary;
- the stock is up 4.5x and under the strictest surveillance stage.

At ₹269 the price needs the FY27 jump to hold as fibre prices normalise. It is a peak-margin cyclical story, not a compounder. What would change the view: two more quarters of positive operating cash with receivables under 100 days, the turnkey loss halved, and preform on time.
