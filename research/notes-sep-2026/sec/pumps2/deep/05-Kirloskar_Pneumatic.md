# Kirloskar Pneumatic (KPCL) — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code KIRLPNU. Price ₹692 (face value now ₹1 after the 1:2 split), market cap ₹8,990 cr, standalone P/E 32.9 (consolidated 34.2), ROCE 30.3%, ROE 22.7%, dividend yield 0.87% [Screener, 28-Sep-26]. Standalone figures are used below: the consolidated page only starts from FY25 (one small subsidiary).

## Business and segments
- Compressors and compression systems, ~93–94% of income ("compression" is the only reportable segment): **air & refrigeration (ACR) ~40–45%** (ammonia compressors, Khione semi-hermetic, packages for cold storage, dairy, food, pharma, fisheries), **process gas systems (PGS) ~35–40%** (CNG station compressors and boosters, upstream/midstream gas packages, biogas, hydrogen), **air compressors ~18–20%** (Tezcatlipoca centrifugal, screw, reciprocating). A new precision engineering division (forgings, castings, gearboxes, loco transmissions) makes up the rest. [Q4 call official; IP Q1FY27 p.3]
- Compression segment margin 24.9% in FY26 (21.7% in FY25); 22.1% in Q1 FY27 (18.3%). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Recurring service: operation & maintenance of 1,000+ CNG stations (from ~700 two years ago), uptime ≥98.5%; service income grew from ₹204 cr to ~₹300 cr in FY26 (O&M part +25–30%). [Q1 call]
- Exports ~6% of sales (Q4 call) / ~8% (CRISIL); Middle East was the largest export market for packages. [Q4 call; CRISIL May-26]
- Plants: Hadapsar (Pune), Saswad, Nashik; subsidiary Systems & Components (India) — industrial refrigeration, Murbad — 55.26% from Dec-2024, 100% from 4 May 2026. [IP Q1FY27 p.4, p.15; AR FY26]
- Market position: Tezcatlipoca centrifugal has a "mid-teens" share of a ₹500 cr+ addressable market; 130 machines in the field, 85 commissioned. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- Standalone sales FY24 ₹1,323 cr → FY25 ₹1,629 cr → FY26 ₹1,759 cr (+8%); operating profit ₹205 cr → ₹294 cr → ₹363 cr; OPM 15% → 18% → 21%; net profit ₹133 cr → ₹211 cr → ₹258 cr. TTM sales ₹1,788 cr, TTM profit ₹264 cr. [Screener standalone]
- Company totals FY26: total income ₹1,786 cr, EBITDA ₹388 cr (21.7% of total income), PBT ₹356 cr, PAT ₹258 cr, EPS ₹39.80. [Q4 call]
- Last five quarters, sales / operating profit / net profit (₹ cr): Jun-25 272/36/28; Sep-25 378/58/43; Dec-25 404/81/43; Mar-26 706/186/144; Jun-26 300/46/34. Revenue is heavily back-ended into Q4 (40% of FY26 sales). [Screener standalone]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹300 cr (+10%), EBITDA ₹54 cr (+23%; 17.6% of total income vs 15.7%), PBT ₹45.6 cr, PAT ₹34.1 cr (+21%). Material cost 43.9% of sales vs 47.6% (mix and in-house parts); other expenses 12.0% vs 10.4%. Packages were under 25% of Q1 sales. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.5–9]
- Orders: book ₹1,624 cr (1-Apr-25) → ₹1,853–1,863 cr (1-Apr-26; the company quotes both) → ₹1,853 cr (1-Jul-26); Q1 intake ~₹300 cr (book-to-bill ~1). About ₹500 cr of the April book executes beyond FY27; the "touch and go" equipment business adds ₹600–700 cr a year not in the book. Precision engineering is ~15% of the order book. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.6]

## Guidance history
- Long-term aspiration: 20%+ growth in revenue and profit "over a long period of time"; EBITDA 18–20% ("strive for 21%"). [Q4 call]
- Q4 call (27 Apr 2026) promises vs Q1 delivery:
  - FY27 growth "20% plus" (an analyst quoted ₹2,100 cr; management kept it on the Q1 call). Q1 grew 10%; "some revenue… deferred to the next quarter" (mix of packages and products). To hit ~₹2,100 cr the remaining three quarters must grow ~22%. **Kept, behind pace.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
  - EBITDA 18–20% after FY26's 21.7%. Q1: 17.6% (seasonally weak quarter). **On track.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
  - Zephyros (ammonia-based air-conditioning package under PLI) "commercialised… in Q1 FY27". Q1: quotes began in June, first external order in July (Q2); "substantial jump" only in FY28. **Slipped slightly.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
  - A800 small centrifugal compressor "maybe one month" to launch. Q1: launched. **Delivered.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
  - Precision engineering "in execution phase". Q1: "slow quarter", dispatches from Q2 into Q3. **Delayed.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
  - Tezcatlipoca: expects >₹100 cr of orders in FY27 (new in Q1). [Q1 call]
- CRISIL (May 2026) is more cautious: FY27 revenue +17–18%, 12–13% a year over the medium term, operating margin 17–18%. [CRISIL May-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Routine capex ₹100 cr planned for FY27; ₹165 cr spent over FY25–FY26 (Nashik and Saswad backward integration: forging, casting, fabrication). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- PLI commitment ₹320 cr for Zephyros and related parts (motors, heat exchangers, sheet metal) at Saswad and Nashik; ~₹60 cr spent by Mar-26, rest over FY27–FY28. An analyst recalled management saying it could generate 4–5× its cost in turnover; management only said the "substantial jump will happen in FY28". [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 p.57]
- Incremental Tezcatlipoca centrifugal capacity commissioned (Q4 FY26). [Q4 call]
- Funded internally: CRISIL expects capex to be met from accruals with nil gearing. [CRISIL May-26]
- My view: total planned spend (~₹360 cr over two years) is roughly 1.4× FY26 profit — big for KPCL — and Zephyros is a new market (commercial air-conditioning) where management admits it "will not be targeting those kinds of margins".

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free; cash and liquid investments ~₹460 cr at 1-Apr-26. Bank limits raised to ₹840 cr (₹800 cr non-fund, for guarantees on packages). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.11; CRISIL May-26]
- Operating cash flow FY24 ₹183 cr, FY25 ₹215 cr, FY26 ₹228 cr (85% of operating profit); free cash flow ₹163 cr in FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital (FY26): debtor days 108, inventory 88, payables 106, cash conversion cycle 90 days (103 a year earlier). Capital employed in compression fell to ₹373 cr in Q1 from ₹417 cr. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Dividend FY26 ₹12 per ₹2 share (600%; interim ₹3.50 + final ₹8.50), payout ~30%; final paid within 24 hours of the AGM. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- In-house engineering and IP: 57 IP filings in FY26 (128+ total), 35 in Q1 FY27; CII "top 30 IP-driven organisation"; PLI officials were told there is no technology partner. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Backward integration lifting gross margin: material cost down 3.3–3.7 points in Q1; Q4 FY26 material cost 50.1% vs 56.4%. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Import substitution and share gains: centrifugal compressors (mid-teens share), Khione record bookings; refused price wars in boosters and screw compressors in FY26. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Annuity: CNG O&M across 1,000+ stations; India's CNG stations expected to grow from ~7,000 to ~15,000 by 2031. [Q1 call]
- Limits: in Middle East gas packages "many approved vendors from lower-cost countries" make it hard to compete; basic CNG boosters are commoditised. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Lumpiness and timing:** Q4 carries ~40% of annual sales; Q1 already missed plan; Middle East package orders and dispatches are stalled "for some time". [Screener; Q1 call]
- **New-product risk:** Zephyros needs "concept selling" of an ammonia-based system for buildings; revenue meaningful only from FY28; ₹320 cr committed under PLI with sales conditions. Precision engineering sustainability unproven ("we don't really know whether it is sustainable yet"). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Subsidiary:** Systems & Components lost ₹4.3 cr in FY26 on ₹28.3 cr turnover (reserves fell to ₹5.7 cr); KPCL bought the remaining 44.74% in May 2026; the CFO said restructuring "will take some time". [AR FY26 Form AOC-1; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.15]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter is year-end revenue cut-off; CARO: no whistle-blower complaints, no disputed statutory dues of note (₹0.7 cr). Contingent liabilities small: claims ₹11.8 cr, customs ₹1.4 cr. Exceptional labour-code charge ₹14.0 cr in FY26; road-roller business fully written off (₹3.8 cr impairment in FY25). [AR FY26 auditor's report, CARO, notes 41, 52]
- Promoter holding only 38.81% (drifting down slightly via ESOP dilution); no pledges disclosed. Management transition to Aman Kirloskar as MD after K. Srinivasan. [Screener; Q4 call; AR FY26]
- Commodity costs: CRISIL flags West Asia-driven raw-material and freight inflation; package orders are fixed-price but vendors were locked in. [CRISIL May-26; Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: celebratory (record FY26, 26% Q4 margin) but cautioned FY26 margins were "a bit of an exception". Q1: "headwinds" — Middle East froze package bookings; equipment business had record orders. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New in Q1: Tonalli food-waste biogas plant, A800 launch, 100% ownership of Systems & Components, rating upgrade to AA/Stable (CRISIL, 5 May 2026), heavier emphasis on O&M service income. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Guidance language unchanged (20%+ growth, 18–20% margin) despite a 10% Q1. [Q1 call]

## My read
KPCL has genuinely improved: margins rose from 15% to 21% in two years on in-house manufacturing and IP-led products, cash is ₹460 cr, and new lines (centrifugal compressors, Khione, CNG O&M) are real. But FY26 revenue grew only ~8–11%, Q1 FY27 only 10%, and the 20%+ growth promise now relies on a heavy H2, stalled Middle East packages restarting, and new ventures (Zephyros, precision engineering) that management itself says will scale slowly. The price needs mid-to-high-teens earnings growth; CRISIL's 12–13% medium-term growth with 17–18% margins would not deliver that. The view improves if H1 FY27 revenue reaches ~₹750–800 cr with margins near 19% and Zephyros books repeat orders; it worsens if Q2 misses again or margins slip toward CRISIL's 17%.
