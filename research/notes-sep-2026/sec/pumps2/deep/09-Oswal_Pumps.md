# Oswal Pumps — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code OSWALPUMPS. Listed June 2025. Price ₹275, market cap ≈ ₹3,135 cr, 52-week range ₹266–790 (down 62% in a year); promoters 75.7% [Screener, 27 Sep 2026; IP Q1FY27 p.14].

## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** turnkey solar pumping systems (pump, motor, solar modules, structures, controller, installation) under PM-KUSUM and state schemes, plus grid-connected farm and domestic pumps, and solar modules (subsidiary Oswal Solar, Karnal) [IP Q1FY27 p.5, p.9].
- **Who pays:** state nodal agencies. Cumulative systems supplied directly: 1,19,402 by 31 Jul 2026 — Haryana 53,968, Maharashtra Magel Tyala 27,223, Rajasthan 24,851, others small [IP Q1FY27 p.8]. FY26 revenue mix (company basis, ₹1,965 cr): PM-KUSUM direct ₹535 cr, other government schemes (mainly Magel Tyala) ₹964 cr, supplies to other KUSUM players ₹75 cr, non-scheme business ₹391 cr [IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- **Q1 FY27 mix (₹444 cr):** PM-KUSUM direct just ₹39 cr, other government schemes ₹240 cr, supplies to KUSUM players ₹21 cr, non-scheme ₹144 cr — the core KUSUM business has almost stopped while the central scheme waits for renewal [IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- **Volumes:** solar pumps FY24 48,263 → FY25 71,167 → FY26 87,323; Q1 FY27 19,724. Total pumps (solar + grid) Q1 FY27 43,077 vs ~56,000 a year earlier [IP Q1FY27 p.11; Q1 call].
- **Capacity:** pumps 4,704 MT, 85% used in FY26 (51% in FY25); motors 1,986 MT; solar modules 570 MW today, going to 2.1 GW [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27 p.5; Q4 call].
- New lines: rooftop solar (PM Surya Ghar), utility and C&I solar EPC — order book ~72 MW, pipeline 359 MW, tenders participated 953 MW; a 60% SPV for rooftop plants on Rajasthan government buildings (hybrid annuity); wires and cables through dealers; Jal Jeevan Mission pumps (pipeline ~42,000 pumps); in-house inverter in ~6 months [IP Q1FY27 p.4, p.10; Q1 call].

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly revenue / operating profit (margin) / net profit: Jun-25 514 / 141 (27%) / 95; Sep-25 540 / 128 (24%) / 98; Dec-25 501 / 127 (25%) / 92; Mar-26 510 / 118 (23%) / 93; Jun-26 474 / 74 (16%) / 54 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: revenue −7.9% YoY; operating EBITDA ₹74 cr (15.7%) vs ₹141 cr (27.4%); gross margin 32.9% vs 39.3% for FY26; PAT ₹54 cr (−43%) [IP Q1FY27 p.7, p.13, p.16].
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹759 cr → FY25 ₹1,430 cr → FY26 ₹2,064 cr; operating profit ₹153 → ₹420 → ₹514 cr; margin 20% → 29% → 25%; net profit ₹98 → ₹281 → ₹376 cr [Screener]. TTM revenue ₹2,024 cr, TTM profit ₹336 cr [Screener].
- ROCE 74% (FY24), 78% (FY25), 38% (FY26 — diluted by IPO cash) [Screener].
- **Unit economics:** FY26 solar-scheme revenue ₹1,574 cr on 87,323 solar pumps ≈ ₹1.8 lakh per pump; Q1 FY27 ₹300 cr on 19,724 ≈ ₹1.5 lakh (includes pump-only supplies) [IP Q1FY27 p.11–12, my calculation]. Margin bridge Q1: ~8–9 points from Magel Tyala price cuts (realisation −9%), ~3–3.5 points from raw materials, ~1–1.5 points from lower operating leverage, partly offset by value engineering → −7.9 points net [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (18 May 2026):** FY27 revenue +20–25%, back-ended, H1 "moderate but temporary" decline; operating EBITDA margin 22–23%; PAT margin 15–16%; 30–40% medium-term growth; 1 GW module phase by Q1 FY27, 0.5 GW more by Q3 FY27; FY27 capex ~₹350 cr; peak revenue from expanded capacity ~₹6,000–6,500 cr; KUSUM 2.0 "imminent"; receivables expected to normalise [Q4 call].
- **Q1 delivery:** revenue −7.9% (within the flagged H1 dip); margin 15.7% — far below 22–23%; module phase slipped to "end of Q2" (commercial production expected first/second week of September); KUSUM 2.0 still not launched; receivable days jumped from 155 to 229 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.17].
- **Q1 call (10 Aug 2026):** revenue guidance **kept** at +20–25% (Q2 +10–15% YoY); EBITDA margin **cut** to 15–17% and PAT margin to 11–13%; new ₹800–1,000 cr revenue target from ~2 lakh PM Surya Ghar rooftop installations in Q2–Q4; 30–40% medium-term growth kept [Q1 call].
- Arithmetic check: +20–25% on ₹2,064 cr needs ₹2,480–2,580 cr; after Q1's ₹474 cr, Q2–Q4 must average ₹670–700 cr a quarter, ~35–40% above the FY26 quarterly run-rate, largely from a business (rooftop) with a 72 MW order book [my calculation from Screener and IP Q1FY27 p.10].

## Capex and capacity
- IPO (June 2025) net proceeds ₹841.5 cr: ₹280 cr to repay Oswal Solar debt and ₹31 cr of company debt (done); ₹168 cr general purposes (₹165 cr used, for working capital); ₹272.8 cr for the Karnal module/backward-integration plant (₹93.8 cr used, **₹179 cr unused**); ₹89.9 cr for pump/motor automation and capacity (₹36.2 cr used, ₹53.7 cr unused) — ₹235.5 cr unused at 30 Jun 2026 [IP Q1FY27 p.18].
- Module capacity 0.6 GW → 2.1 GW including aluminium extrusion and EVA (backward integration); pump capacity up ~2.25x and module capacity ~2.5–3x, supporting "₹6,000 crores plus" revenue without more capex [Q4 call].
- About ₹200 cr of capex was spent in Q1 FY27; gross block rose to ₹287 cr [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.18].
- Payback: not disclosed. At FY26's 12x fixed-asset turnover, the new capacity is only valuable if volumes return; with KUSUM stalled, utilisation of the new module lines depends on rooftop and third-party module sales [IP Q1FY27 p.7; my read].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Cash flow is the problem:** operating cash flow −₹151 cr (FY25) and −₹77 cr (FY26); free cash flow −₹198 cr and −₹213 cr [Screener]. The company says ₹116 cr received from Maharashtra on 31 Mar landed on 2 Apr and would have made FY26 operating cash flow +₹39 cr [Q4 call].
- Net debt ₹266 cr at 30 Jun 2026 (₹135 cr at Mar-26): borrowings ₹308 cr (incl. supplier credit reclassified as debt), cash ₹41 cr [IP Q1FY27 p.7].
- Receivable days 126 (Jun-25) → 155 (Mar-26) → **229 (Jun-26)**; cash conversion cycle 137 → 172 → **244 days**; ₹305 cr of receivables "not due yet" at 30 Jun [IP Q1FY27 p.17; Q1 call]. Screener FY26 debtor days 199 [Screener]. Expected-credit-loss provision rose from ₹3.8 cr to ₹8.8 cr in FY26 [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Payment terms: 90% within ~120 days, 10% in the next 120 days [Q4 call].
- Management expects collections "much better" in Q2 and a "significant change" in Q3 [Q1 call].
- **ICRA A+: outlook cut to Negative on 22 Sep 2026** (also for Oswal Solar), citing a "substantial build-up of receivables" and weaker profitability from competition and input prices [BSE filing 23 Sep 2026].
- No dividend [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Backward integration (pumps, motors, modules, structures, BoS; inverters coming) and 23+ years in pumps; on the approved module list (ALMM) [IP Q1FY27 p.5].
- Scale in Haryana and Maharashtra; ranked third in MSEDCL's Magel Tyala record installation drive [AR FY26].
- **Evidence against pricing power:** bidders rose to ~40–45 per KUSUM tender as profits attracted entrants; installed pumps nationally rose from ~1.5 lakh (FY24) to ~5 lakh (FY26); with KUSUM stalled everyone crowded into Maharashtra and bid "not sustainable" prices; Oswal matched them (−9% realisation) [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **CFO exit:** CFO Subodh Kumar resigned on 27 May 2026 (nine days after the Q4 call), relieved 20 Jun; Vijay Kumar Yadav (ex-CFO, Duroply) became CFO on 8 Aug 2026 [News: Business Standard, 22 Jun 2026; News: Energetica India, Aug 2026].
- **Related-party purchases:** ₹132 cr of goods bought in FY26 from **Walso Solar Solution** (an associate formed by merging two promoter-linked partnership firms, Solar Structure India and Solar Solution India) vs ₹64.5 cr in FY25; the CMD also lent to the company and receives rent [AR FY26 consolidated note 39.8].
- **Auditor/CARO:** unmodified opinion; key audit matters are inventory valuation and revenue recognition; audit trail not enabled at database level and not capturing some changes (ERP upgrade under way); bank drawing-power statements had to be **revised** to match the books [AR FY26 auditor's report; standalone note 22.2].
- Contingent liabilities small: GST ₹3.8 cr, labour ₹1.0 cr, civil ₹0.2 cr [AR FY26 consolidated note 39.2].
- **Scheme and state concentration:** revenue rests on Maharashtra and Haryana agencies; KUSUM 2.0 timing is outside its control ("Beyond a point, we can only hope") [Q1 call].
- **Guidance credibility:** margin guidance cut by ~6 points within 12 weeks; module timeline slipped one quarter; FY27 capex quoted as ₹350 cr in May and "360–400" discussed in August [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New businesses (rooftop EPC, some IPP/annuity-type projects where "we will receive the revenue this year, but profitability… over the next 5 years", wires and cables) carry lower margins and more capital [Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Margin guidance: 22–23% → 15–17%; PAT 15–16% → 11–13% [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Working capital: from "early signs of normalisation" to receivables at 229 days and net debt doubling [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Growth source: from KUSUM 2.0 "imminent" to a ₹800–1,000 cr rooftop target plus wires/cables and Jal Jeevan Mission [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.4].
- Tone: management admitted it cannot forecast KUSUM timing and called FY27 "the most turbulent year" [Q1 call].
- CFO changed between the calls [News: Business Standard, Jun 2026].

## My read
Oswal's FY24–FY26 surge was a KUSUM-plus-Maharashtra boom at 25–29% margins; with KUSUM 2.0 stalled, margins have fallen to ~16%, receivables have ballooned to 229 days and cash burn continues, which is why ICRA moved to a negative outlook and the stock is down ~62%. The trailing P/E looks low, but profits are falling, free cash flow has been negative for two years, and FY27 guidance relies on an untested rooftop business delivering ₹800–1,000 cr in nine months. The view would improve on KUSUM 2.0 tenders, a quarter of falling receivables and the 1 GW module line running; it would worsen if Q2 misses the +10–15% guide, receivables exceed 250 days, or related-party purchases keep rising.
