# Powerica — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Listed on NSE/BSE on 2 Apr 2026 (IPO ₹1,100 cr at ₹395: ₹700 cr fresh issue + ₹400 cr offer for sale). Screener code POWERICA. Price ₹583, market cap ≈ ₹7,377 cr, 52-week range ₹365–677 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- **Generator sets (83.1% of FY26 revenue):** one of three genset makers (OEMs) that package **Cummins** engines and alternators into diesel generator (DG) sets, 7.5 kVA to 3,750 kVA; it makes everything else in-house (enclosures, fuel/exhaust systems, control panels) at Bengaluru, Silvassa and Khopoli [Q4 call; Crisil Nov-25]. Installed capacity FY26: 8,956 DG sets at Bengaluru, 1,320 at Silvassa, plus 3,000 Schneider PRISMA panels, 1,800 canopies, and defence EMI/EMC shelters [IP Q1FY27 p.20]. Utilisation "about 75–80%" on one shift [Q1 call].
- Within gensets: Cummins-powered DG sets ≈ 66% of FY26 revenue, medium-speed large generators (MSLG, Hyundai, 3–10 MVA) ≈ 5%, and an "allied" business (defence shelters, PRISMA panels, project work) ≈ 12.5% [Q4 call]. The supply deal with Cummins is **non-exclusive** (general supply agreement of 11 Jun 2025) [IP Q1FY27 p.15].
- MSLG's biggest job is a 63 MW prime-power order for NPCIL worth ₹283.6 cr plus US$52.4 mn of imported items, to be executed by Q1 FY28 [IP Q1FY27 p.16; Q1 call].
- **Wind power (16.9% of FY26 revenue, ₹512 cr):** 12 owned wind farms in Gujarat, 330.85 MW, selling on 25-year fixed tariffs (₹2.4–4.19/kWh) to GUVNL (Gujarat's state power buyer, 82% of capacity) and SECI (18%) [IP Q1FY27 p.22; Crisil Nov-25]. It also builds the "balance of plant" (foundations, lines, substations) for other wind developers — IPP was ~40% and EPC/O&M ~60% of FY26 wind revenue [Q4 call].
- **Platino Automotive (50% associate):** retrofit emission-control devices (RECD) for old DG sets; FY26 revenue ₹95.1 cr, EBITDA ₹30.1 cr (31.6%) vs ₹75.9 cr / ₹26.1 cr in FY25 [IP Q1FY27 p.19]. Q4 FY26 sales ₹22 cr, PBT ₹5.8 cr [Q4 call]; Q1 FY27 sales fell to about ₹16–17 cr because of the NCR pollution-control plan (GRAP) and the West Asia crisis [Q1 call].
- End markets: manufacturing is the largest, then real estate; data centres are the fastest-growing, then rentals [Q1 call]. Data centres were 12% of FY26 genset revenue and "above 20%" in Q1 FY27 [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.6]. High-horsepower sets are over 50% of genset value [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 segment split: gensets ₹635.2 cr revenue at 5.6% EBITDA margin (about ₹35 cr, down from about ₹52 cr a year earlier); wind ₹145.0 cr at 48.6% margin (about ₹71 cr vs ₹37 cr) [IP Q1FY27 p.5, p.9]. **Wind already earns about two-thirds of segment profit on under one-fifth of revenue.**

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly revenue / operating profit / net profit: Jun-25 616 / 85 / 51; Sep-25 832 / 129 / 84; Dec-25 763 / 78 / 98; Mar-26 801 / 84 / 45; Jun-26 780 / 106 / 64 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: revenue +26.7% year on year; operating margin 13.6% (vs 13.8%); gross margin 33.5% vs 36.8% because of costlier commodities; profit +27% to ₹64 cr helped by interest falling from ₹6 cr to ₹2 cr after the IPO debt repayment [IP Q1FY27 p.8; Screener].
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹2,210 cr → FY25 ₹2,653 cr → FY26 ₹3,012 cr (+13.5%); operating profit ₹362 → ₹336 → ₹376 cr; margin 16% → 13% → 12% [Screener]. FY26 net profit ₹267 cr (company: ₹277 cr) was flattered by a ₹51 cr deferred-tax credit on moving to the lower tax regime; the underlying tax rate is now ≈25% [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 49]. FY24 profit included a one-time ₹85 cr gain from selling two Tamil Nadu wind farms [IP Q1FY27 p.25; Crisil Nov-25].
- TTM revenue ₹3,176 cr, TTM profit ₹291 cr; ROCE fell from 27% (FY24) to 16% (FY26) as margins slipped and IPO cash landed [Screener].
- Unit economics: genset margin swings with commodity costs and emission-rule changes — ~8% in FY25 (CPCB-IV cost shock), 9.1% in FY26, 5.6% in Q1 FY27 [Crisil Nov-25; Q4 call; Q1 call]. Old wind farms: ~26% average plant load factor (PLF — share of the year's maximum output actually produced) and 60–63% EBITDA margin; new turbines ~36–36.5% PLF and 82–83% margin after year one [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Wind EPC earns 10–11% margin on ≈₹1.6–1.7 cr of revenue per MW [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (29 May 2026):** "double-digit top-line growth in FY27"; DG organic growth 11–12% vs an industry rate of ~10.5%; Q1 FY27 demand would be hit by West Asia; the margin dip was "temporary"; 50 MW of wind to be capitalised in FY27; Platino to grow faster than DG [Q4 call]. Wind to rise to 20–25% of revenue over 4–5 years [Q4 call].
- **Q1 delivery:** revenue +26.7% (well ahead of "double digit"); genset margin fell to 5.6% (worse than FY26's 9.1%); Platino sales fell (₹22 cr → ₹16–17 cr) [Q1 call].
- **Q1 call (10 Aug 2026):** double-digit revenue guidance **kept**; margin guidance for gensets **dropped** for now ("This is an impacted year… wait for Q2"); genset margin to stay weak through Q1 and part of Q2, recovering from Q3; management expects a return to FY23-type genset margins after Q3/Q4 [Q1 call]. The MD's commentary says H1 FY27 will stay "relatively subdued" [IP Q1FY27 p.4].
- Wind roadmap: 50 MW in FY27 (Q4), 150 MW in FY28, 100 MW in FY29, taking owned capacity to **638.35 MW** (the company corrected the 633.55 MW said on the call) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.7]. Wind EPC steady at 250–300 MW a year, about ₹400 cr revenue [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- **Wind build-out is the capex story.** The annual report budgets ₹380 cr for the 52.7 MW project and ₹750 cr for the ~100 MW GUVNL project, funded **70–75% by debt** and the rest from internal cash [AR FY26 MD&A p.79]. Capital commitments jumped to ₹934 cr at Mar-26 from ₹157 cr [AR FY26 consolidated note 39].
- The 100 MW GUVNL project has a signed PPA at ₹3.435/kWh for 25 years and site work has started; the 50 MW GUVNL award is waiting for its letter of award (PPA expected by Nov–Dec 2026); the 100 MW SECI award has its LOA but no PPA yet [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.7].
- Longer term: a joint development agreement with GE Vernova for a Khavda (Gujarat) project is stuck waiting for land from the state; ROW (right of way for power lines) "is not improving" [Q4 call]. New subsidiaries Windfusion and Whisperwind were set up for transmission connectivity, and a 49% stake was bought in Fuji-Kailash Energy (solar/bio-energy) [Q1 call].
- My payback arithmetic (not company guidance): at ~₹7.2–7.5 cr per MW, 36.5% PLF and ₹3.435/kWh, a new MW earns ≈₹1.1 cr revenue and ≈₹0.9 cr EBITDA a year — about a 12% pre-tax cash yield, i.e. roughly 8 years' payback. Adding ~300 MW would add ≈₹270 cr of EBITDA by FY29–FY30, versus ₹397 cr TTM group operating profit [my estimate from AR FY26 and Q1 call figures].
- Genset capacity: no expansion needed — a second shift is available; defence shelter capacity expansion is "ongoing"; Khopoli has spare land [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.18, p.20]. MSLG needs no capex [Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings were ₹572 cr at Mar-26 [Screener]; ₹525 cr was repaid from IPO money in Q1 FY27 and the company reported **net cash of ₹193 cr at 30 Jun 2026** [IP Q1FY27 p.8]. (The ₹450 cr said on the Q4 call was gross cash and investments on 26 May, not net cash [Q4 call].)
- Net cash will turn back into net debt as the ₹1,130 cr of wind projects draw 70–75% bank funding [AR FY26 p.79]. Crisil expected debt to rise but stay comfortable given >₹300 cr a year of cash accruals and a ~₹100 cr annual repayment load [Crisil Nov-25].
- Operating cash flow ₹453 cr in FY26 (133% of operating profit) but free cash flow −₹121 cr because of wind capex; FY25 FCF −₹59 cr [Screener].
- FY26 working capital: debtor days 41, inventory days 60, payable days 72, cash cycle 29 days (44 in FY25) [Screener].
- Dividend payout 13% of FY26 profit per Screener; no dividend decided after listing as of the Q1 call [Screener; Q1 call].
- Ratings: Crisil AA/Stable and A1+ on ₹1,263 cr of bank lines (Nov 2025); ICRA also rated the company in Feb 2026 (rationale page did not load) [Crisil Nov-25; Screener document list].

## Moat evidence
- One of only three Cummins genset OEMs, a 40-year relationship; Cummins is India's leader in medium and high horsepower engines (Frost & Sullivan, cited by the company) [Crisil Nov-25; IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- Data-centre approvals and execution record: in the segment since 2021; order book for data centres doubled from ₹400–500 cr to ₹900 cr (31 Jul) and ₹1,100 cr (7 Aug) after a single order of over ₹200 cr [Q1 call]. Installation work adds ~30% of order value on data-centre jobs [Q1 call].
- Pricing: passed on about a 33% price rise over nine months when CPCB-IV+ emission rules came in (Jul 2024); took two price rises in mid-Q1 and early Q2 FY27 [Q1 call].
- Limits: the relationship is non-exclusive; Cummins' parent took two data-centre projects directly last year (raised by an analyst; management would not comment) [IP Q1FY27 p.15; Q1 call]. "Competition continues to increase" in data centres [Q1 call]. Market share is not disclosed [Q1 call].
- Wind: highly rated buyers (GUVNL "AA" by CARE, SECI "AAA" by ICRA), 98–99% plant availability [IP Q1FY27 p.22].

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin risk on fixed-price orders:** orders carry no price-escalation clause; the data-centre book is a mix of pre- and post-price-rise orders [Q1 call].
- **Supplier dependence:** everything hinges on Cummins engines and alternators; a change in Cummins' channel policy would hit the core business [Crisil Nov-25; IP Q1FY27 p.15].
- **Customer concentration is rising:** the top-10 customer share chart shows figures of 13.3%, 18.1%, 18.9% and 27.6% for FY23–FY26 (the chart layout does not show clearly which year is which; the rise tracks large data-centre orders) [IP Q1FY27 p.15].
- **Wind execution:** land and ROW bottlenecks; the Khavda project is waiting for land; wind output below P-90 levels has been a Crisil monitorable [Q4 call; Crisil Nov-25]. About ₹10 cr of old Tamil Nadu dues are being recovered under the late-payment scheme [Crisil Nov-25].
- **Auditor and annual report:** unqualified opinion; the only key audit matter is revenue recognition; no fraud or whistle-blower complaints reported; audit trail working [AR FY26 auditor's report & CARO]. Contingent liabilities are small — disputed sales-tax, service-tax, GST, customs and income-tax demands of roughly ₹45–50 cr in total (largest single item ≈₹33 cr; the extracted table does not show clearly which tax), plus ₹68 cr of letters of credit — against net worth of ≈₹1,990 cr [AR FY26 consolidated note 39; Screener]. Related-party dealings are small (Platino sales, rent, director pay; none above 1% of revenue) [AR FY26 consolidated note 48].
- **Management changes:** founder Naresh Oberoi stepped down as CMD on 10 Dec 2025 (son Bharat Oberoi became CMD); the CFO changed in Jul 2025 [AR FY26 note 48]. A related party (the whole-time director's son) holds an office of profit, approved by shareholders [AR FY26 AGM notice].
- Promoters hold 77.18%; free float is small (FIIs 2.05%, DIIs 16.58%) [Screener, Jun-26]. No pledge is disclosed.
- Long-term: gas/battery/fuel-cell backup could erode diesel's role in data centres; management says the genset "will be there" for 20–25 years [Q4 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "listing milestone, broad growth" to "**data centres plus wind**": the data-centre book more than doubled, and 250 MW of new wind awards were added [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Margin view worsened: in May, Q4's dip was "temporary" and FY27 would be "in the target"; in August, genset margin fell to 5.6% and guidance was pulled until Q2 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- MSLG slowed: Q4 highlighted the nearly finished Australia job; by Q1, overseas enquiries had paused because of West Asia, and MSLG revenue was "marginal"; one ₹41 cr PSU tender was won [Q1 call].
- Platino slipped from ₹22 cr to about ₹16–17 cr of quarterly sales [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## My read
The business is improving in mix, not yet in margin. Revenue is growing faster than guided (+27% in Q1) and the data-centre order book has more than doubled, but the core genset margin fell to 5.6% because orders are fixed-price and commodity costs rose. Profit growth over FY27–FY29 depends mostly on wind: about 300 MW of new high-margin turbines could add roughly ₹270 cr of EBITDA by FY29–FY30, but this needs ~₹1,100 cr of mostly debt-funded capex, land and grid access, and the payback is about 8 years. The price needs genset margins to recover to ~9–10% from Q3 FY27 and the 150 MW FY28 wind tranche to land on time; a third weak genset quarter, a Cummins channel change, or wind delays would change the view.
