# Pumps, compressors, engines & gensets — India sector research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Scope: diesel engines and gensets (Cummins India, Kirloskar Oil, Powerica, Greaves), pumps incl. solar pumps (KSB, Kirloskar Brothers, Shakti, Oswal), air compressors (Elgi, Kirloskar Pneumatic). Source tags at the end of each bullet; company-call facts already verified in the company deep files are tagged with that file.

## 1. Market size and growth
- **Gensets:** India's DG-set market grew ~18% in FY26 to ~1.79 lakh units (incl. telecom); Kirloskar Oil sold 50,000+ (~28% of units) [deep/01: KOEL Q4 FY26 call, AR FY26]. By value the Indian diesel-generator market was ~US$1.83 bn in FY26 (US$1.66 bn FY25), with 7.18% CAGR expected over five years [Greaves AR FY26 MD&A, citing Mordor Intelligence]. Industrial users are ~49% of demand [Greaves AR FY26 MD&A].
- **Market leader's run-rate:** Cummins India Q1 FY27 sales ₹3,375 cr (+18%); domestic powergen ₹1,424 cr (+35%, a record); data centres 40% of powergen revenue [Cummins Q1 FY27 call summary, Cofacto].
- **Pumps (overall):** Indian industrial pump market ~US$2.35 bn (2024) → ~US$4.04 bn by 2032, ~7% CAGR (Verified Market Research, Dec 2025); submersible water pumps ~US$441 mn (2024) → ~US$653 mn by 2033, 4.45% CAGR (IMARC) [Shakti AR FY26 MD&A]. These consultancy numbers look low against listed-company sales (KSB pumps alone ₹2,215 cr in CY25; Shakti ₹2,698 cr) and should be treated as indicative only [deep/03; Screener].
- **Solar pumps:** CRISIL MI&A (Dec 2024) sized the domestic solar-pump system market at ~₹39 bn in FY24 (15% CAGR from ₹19.3 bn in FY19) and forecast **₹280–300 bn by FY29 (~49% CAGR)**, with the PM-KUSUM installed base rising from 0.3 mn to ~3.6 mn [CRISIL report for GK Energy, Dec 2024]. Pace so far: industry installations ~1.5 lakh (FY24) → ~3.5 lakh (FY25) → ~5 lakh (FY26) per Oswal's management [deep/09: Q1 call].
- **Compressors:** global market ~US$23 bn (2025), ~3.5% a year growth; Elgi is India's second-largest compressor maker with ~25% share [deep/02: Elgi AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL May-26].
- **Exports (FY26, EEPC):** engineering exports record US$122.4 bn (+4.9%); IC engines & parts ~US$4.1 bn (+6%); pumps of all types ~US$1.6 bn (+5%); but March 2026 fell (IC engines −13%, pumps −7%) as the Strait of Hormuz disruption hit; exports to WANA −8%, UAE −10%, Saudi −13% [EEPC India, Apr 2026].

## 2. Demand drivers
- **Data centres (the strongest driver for large gensets):** India colocation capacity doubled to 1.2 GW over FY22–FY25 and is forecast by **CareEdge Ratings to reach ~4 GW by FY30** (~₹1.5 lakh cr investment; industry revenue CAGR ~24% FY26–FY30) [CareEdge, 25 Mar 2026]. ICRA notes services-sector project announcements hit a 12-quarter high of ₹10 trn in Q4 FY26, "led by transport services and data centre projects" [ICRA Investment Tracker, Apr 2026]. On the ground: Powerica's data-centre genset book doubled to ₹1,100 cr (Aug 2026); KOEL won a 192 MW hyperscale order; Cummins gets 40% of powergen from data centres [deep/06; deep/01; Cofacto].
- **Government capex:** GoI capex budgeted at ₹12.2 trn in FY27 (+11.5%); 19 states budget +14.3% — ICRA calls the state figure "optimistic" and flags fiscal-slippage risk from subsidies and weaker receipts [ICRA Investment Tracker, Apr 2026].
- **Private industrial capex is pausing:** new project announcements nearly halved to ₹13.5 trn in Q4 FY26 (₹25.5 trn a year earlier); private proposals fell to ₹7.0 trn from ₹17.3 trn; manufacturing announcements hit a 7-quarter low of ₹1.5 trn [ICRA Investment Tracker, Apr 2026]. This hits compressors, industrial pumps and process-industry gensets with a 2–4 quarter lag.
- **Agriculture:** the 2026 monsoon was forecast by IMD at 90% of LPA with a 60% chance of a deficient season, El Niño >90% likely [IMD via Down To Earth, 29 May 2026]; by end-August ~47% of the country was rain-deficient [Business Standard, 31 Aug 2026 — search summary only]. A dry year raises irrigation need (pump replacement, diesel pumps) but cuts farm incomes and state finances, which fund solar-pump subsidies.
- **Rooftop solar (new outlet for pump makers):** PM Surya Ghar — ~7.8 mn applications, ~4.23 mn installations, ~15 GW installed, ₹28,516 cr subsidy released by 7 Aug 2026; ₹22,000 cr allocated in the FY27 budget [Oswal IP Q1FY27 p.10, citing MNRE/PIB].

## 3. Policy
- **PM-KUSUM (farm solar pumps):** Component B sanctioned 13.07 lakh standalone pumps, 11.24 lakh installed by 30 Jun 2026 (86%) [Shakti IP Q1FY27 p.9; Shakti AR FY26 MD&A]. PIB (10 Mar 2026): >10 lakh standalone pumps installed and >13 lakh grid-connected pumps solarised; **PM-KUSUM 2.0 being prepared with a dedicated 10 GW agri-PV component** [PIB, 10 Mar 2026]. As of late September 2026 the Minister still describes 2.0 as coming "soon" — **no cabinet approval yet**; industry had expected it by end-Q1 FY27, then August [DD India, Sep 2026; deep/07 and deep/09 calls]. A ₹50,000 cr outlay is quoted by trade blogs but is **not confirmed** by any official source read.
- **State schemes fill the gap:** Maharashtra's Magel Tyala targets 10 lakh off-grid pumps over five years (1.52 lakh+ installed per Shakti's AR) and is now the main market — bidders from across India crowd into it, cutting realisations ~9% [Shakti AR FY26 MD&A; deep/09 Q1 call].
- **Domestic content (DCR/ALMM List-II):** from 1 Jun 2026, modules for PM-KUSUM, PM Surya Ghar and other government-linked projects must use cells from ALMM List-II (approved Indian cells); DCR cells are 40–50% of a solar pump-set's cost — the reason Shakti (2.2 GW) and Oswal (2.1 GW modules) are building cell/module plants [Shakti AR FY26 MD&A].
- **CPCB IV+ genset emission norms (from July 2024):** needed after-treatment (SCR/DPF), raised genset prices sharply — Powerica passed on ~33% over nine months — and created a retrofit market (RECD kits) for pre-CPCB-IV sets, pushed by state mandates and NCR's GRAP rules [deep/06 Q1 call; deep/06 Q4 call]. The transition caused pre-buying in FY24–FY25 and a Q1 FY26 dip (Crisil on Powerica), then an 18% volume rebound in FY26 [Crisil, Nov 2025; deep/01].
- **EV subsidies (for Greaves):** PM E-DRIVE for e-two-wheelers extended only to 31 Jul 2026; for L5 e-three-wheelers funding ran out in Dec 2025; for e-rickshaws extended to Mar 2028 [Greaves AR FY26].

## 4. Cost drivers
- **Copper at records:** US$6.85/lb in September 2026 (all-time high); +42% over the past year [Trading Economics, 27 Sep 2026]. Copper is the main input for motors and alternators.
- **Steel up again:** 2 mm HR coil rose 8% to ₹76,085/t in September 2026; finished-steel demand +7.4% to 70.5 mt in Apr–Aug 2026 [Indian Masterminds, 25 Sep 2026, citing Ministry of Steel]. Cummins cites pig iron +14% YoY plus steel, aluminium, copper and freight [Cofacto].
- **Pass-through lags everywhere:** Cummins took one price increase at the start of Q2 FY27 but says realisation lags a quarter and gross margin will not quickly return to 36–37% [Cofacto]. Powerica's orders have no escalation clause (genset margin 5.6% in Q1) [deep/06]. Greaves' OEM contracts index raw materials with a lag (core margin 12.6% vs 14.9%) [deep/08]. Solar-pump tender prices are fixed at bid (Shakti ~6-point and Oswal ~3–3.5-point margin hits from raw materials) [deep/07; deep/09].
- CRISIL notes steel, copper and the solar module are the three big cost items in a solar pump; modules are ~40% of the bill of materials [CRISIL, Dec 2024].

## 5. Competitive structure and share
- **Gensets:** Cummins India is the engine leader in medium/high horsepower; it sells through three genset OEMs (packagers) incl. Powerica; Kirloskar Oil (~28% of units, gaining), Mahindra, Greaves (medium-HP niches, own 650 kVA engine) and others [Crisil Nov-25; deep/01; deep/08]. Data-centre work is attracting more competitors ("competition continues to increase") [deep/06].
- **Solar pumps:** top-10 players had ~73% of installations Jan 2022–Dec 2024; Shakti was the leading pump maker, GK Energy the largest pure EPC [CRISIL, Dec 2024]. Shakti claims ~25% of PM-KUSUM [Shakti IP Q1FY27]. Bidders per tender rose to ~40–45 as profits drew entrants [deep/09].
- **Industrial pumps:** KSB (nuclear, thermal, aftermarket) and Kirloskar Brothers (large projects, UK water, US data-centre packages) lead; agri pumps face unorganised competition [deep/03; deep/04].
- **Compressors:** Atlas Copco, Elgi (~25%), Ingersoll Rand, Kirloskar Pneumatic; the bottom ~20% of the market is price-driven and flooded by Chinese machines [deep/02].

## 6. Where the profit sits
- Highest margins: aftermarket/service (KSB aftermarket gross margin 2–3x new pumps; Elgi parts 28–30% of India revenue), large engineered/qualified niches (nuclear, defence, data-centre HHP), exports (Shakti exports ~10 points above domestic) [deep/03; deep/02; deep/07].
- Squeezed: government solar-pump tenders (margins fell from 24–29% to 10–16% within a year), fixed-price genset order books, EV (Greaves' EV arm lost ₹273 cr in FY26) [deep/07; deep/09; deep/06; deep/08].
- Annuity-like: Powerica's wind IPP (60–83% EBITDA margins) [deep/06].

## 7. Cycle position (Sep 2026)
- **Gensets: demand up-cycle, margin down-cycle.** Volumes strong (data centres, rentals, manufacturing); margins hit by record copper and lagged pricing; exports to the Middle East weak [Cofacto; deep/01; deep/06].
- **Solar pumps: policy air-pocket.** KUSUM 1.0 finished, 2.0 not approved; order books shrinking (Shakti ₹1,500 cr → ₹1,000 cr; Oswal 22,025 pumps); receivables rising (Shakti ₹1,799 cr; Oswal 229 days); ICRA cut Oswal's outlook to Negative on 22 Sep 2026 [deep/07; deep/09].
- **Industrial pumps & compressors: slowing** with private capex; KSB H1 CY26 sales +2–3% [deep/03; ICRA].
- **Exports: soft** after Hormuz/West Asia; US steady despite tariffs [EEPC; Cofacto].

## 8. Outlook FY27–FY29 (forecaster named)
- **Macro:** ICRA expects GDP growth to slow to 6.5% in FY27 (from 7.5%) assuming US$85 crude; investment outlook "clouded by external shocks" [ICRA, Apr 2026].
- **Gensets:** 7.18% value CAGR over five years (Mordor Intelligence, via Greaves AR); the data-centre leg much faster — CareEdge sees capacity 1.2 GW → ~4 GW by FY30 [Greaves AR FY26; CareEdge Mar-26]. My view: FY27 genset volumes +12–15% with margins recovering from H2 FY27 as price rises catch up; FY28–FY29 driven by HHP/data centres, with rising competition.
- **Solar pumps:** CRISIL's ₹280–300 bn FY29 market (Dec 2024) assumed KUSUM momentum continuing; with 2.0 not yet approved and state budgets under pressure (ICRA), FY27 is likely flat-to-down in volume and FY28–FY29 depends entirely on 2.0's size and payment terms. Company targets: Shakti ₹5,000 cr revenue by 2029; Oswal 30–40% medium-term growth [deep/07; deep/09].
- **Compressors/industrial pumps:** tied to private capex, which ICRA expects to stay subdued near term; recovery in FY28 if energy prices normalise [ICRA Apr-26].
- **Rooftop:** PM Surya Ghar budget ₹22,000 cr in FY27 gives pump makers an outlet, at lower margins [Oswal IP Q1FY27].
