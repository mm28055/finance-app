# Elgi Equipments — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code ELGIEQUIP. Price ₹603, market cap ₹19,110 cr, consolidated P/E 41.2, ROCE 22.1%, ROE 19.0%, dividend yield 0.45% [Screener, 28-Sep-26].

## Business and segments
- Air compressors (screw, reciprocating, portable diesel, oil-free, centrifugal; now vacuum pumps) plus a small automotive garage-equipment arm (ATS Elgi). FY26 segment revenue: compressors ₹3,613 cr, automotive ₹339 cr; segment profit before tax ₹548 cr and ₹37 cr. Compressors are ~91–92% of sales every quarter. [AR FY26 note 41; IP Q1FY27 p.3]
- Customers: all of industry — food processing, engineering, foundry/forging, textiles, cement, steel, pharma, railways (intercity locomotives), water-well drilling, plus new EV, solar and semiconductor factories. No customer is 10% of revenue. Sold mainly through distributors; Elgi keeps parts, dealers keep the service income. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26 note 41]
- Geography (compressors): India ~49–51%, rest of world ~49–51%. Q1 FY27 growth: India +28%, North America +37%, Europe +21%, Australia +17% (7 points of the 23% group growth came from a weaker rupee). [IP Q1FY27 p.3–4; Q1 call]
- Market position: second-largest compressor maker in India with ~25% share, sixth-largest globally (eighth in FY21). [CRISIL May-26]
- Global market ~US$23 bn (2025), growing ~3.5% a year to ~US$27 bn by 2030; Elgi talks of a US$25 bn addressable pool of which vacuum is 12–15%. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- Capacity: main plants in Coimbatore (a new "MK2" campus is replacing the old city factory), Italy (Rotair portables), US assembly; motors now made in-house for 88% of needs, lead time 3 days. No utilisation figure is disclosed. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- Aftermarket (parts only): ~28–30% of revenue in India, ~15–16% elsewhere; global peers ~35–40%. [Q1 call]

## Key numbers
- Consolidated sales FY24 ₹3,218 cr → FY25 ₹3,510 cr → FY26 ₹3,951 cr (+13%); operating profit ₹491 cr → ₹529 cr → ₹583 cr; OPM steady at 15%; net profit ₹312 cr → ₹350 cr → ₹430 cr. TTM sales ₹4,146 cr, TTM profit ₹448 cr. [Screener]
- FY26 profit was helped by other income of ₹110 cr (vs ₹63 cr; includes a US property sale) — core operating margin did not rise. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Last five quarters, sales / operating profit / net profit (₹ cr): Jun-25 867/121/86; Sep-25 968/140/121; Dec-25 1,003/144/95; Mar-26 1,113/173/128; Jun-26 1,062/155/103. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 company view: sales ₹1,062 cr (+23%), EBITDA ₹155 cr (+28%, margin 14.6% vs 14.0%), exceptional restructuring cost ₹7.3 cr, PAT ₹103 cr = 9.7% of sales (10.4% before the exceptional). EBITDA bridge: volume +₹74 cr, contribution −₹25 cr (materials, tariffs, mix), exchange +₹11 cr, employee cost −₹9 cr (+5%), other expenses −₹17 cr (+12%). [IP Q1FY27 p.5–6]
- Unit economics: FY26 volume growth was only ~3–4%; the rest was exchange and a little price. Q1 FY27 India growth was "primarily volume-driven". Material costs rose 5–6% vs a 3% price correction; a further ~9% cost buffer was priced in, effective late Q2/Q3. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Five-year plan (from the FY26 AR): revenue ₹6,615 cr by FY31 (from ₹3,951 cr, ~10.9% a year), EBITDA margin 18%, ROCE 35%. On the Q1 call the MD added "our target is to grow to 20" but the formal guidance stays 18% by 2031. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call official transcript]
- Q4 call (29 May 2026) promises vs Q1 delivery:
  - Q1 top line "very similar… growth… as last year, maybe a little better" (i.e. ~12%+); margin "roughly the same". Delivered +23% revenue (+16% ex-FX) and 14.6% margin vs 14.0%. **Beat.** [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
  - Tier-4 low-cost range to counter Chinese machines: launch in India in September 2026, rest of world FY28. Q1: validated, first orders taken, September launch confirmed; "only a start year". **On track.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
  - Europe: "at the worst case… break even, more realistic… marginal profitability" in FY27. Q1: Europe "is staying" at breakeven; plans to enter Germany. **On track, but only breakeven.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
  - Price correction in June for commodity inflation. Q1: done (9% cost buffer), impact from end-Q2. **Kept.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
  - Demand=Match (energy-saving control technology launched in India Sept 2025): global rollout "this year". **Kept.** [Q1 call]
- CRISIL is more conservative: revenue growth 7–9% a year and margins "~14–15%" over the medium term. [CRISIL May-26]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹150 cr (CRISIL estimate ₹180–200 cr). FY27 budget ~₹200 cr: ₹120–130 cr for moving the Coimbatore city factory to the MK2 campus, ~₹70 cr balancing capex; the MD warns spending usually lags plans. Q1 FY27 capex ₹34.8 cr, including land in Italy contracted six years ago. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.7; CRISIL May-26]
- CRISIL: ₹100–150 cr a year in FY27–FY28 for diesel-portable (DPSAC) capacity and the new Global Support Centre at Kinathukadavu (due Q1 FY27), funded from ₹300–400 cr annual cash accrual. [CRISIL May-26; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Capital commitments fell to ₹62 cr at Mar-26 from ₹151 cr. CWIP ₹136 cr. [AR FY26 note 44; Screener]
- Other investments are opex-heavy: IT/process programme for "three to four years", shared services moved to India, a new vacuum-pump line (Jan 2026, licensed from DVP). [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- My view: capex is modest (~4–5% of sales) and self-funded; growth depends on distribution and product, not on factories. The FY31 plan needs ~11% a year, which matches its 10-year sales CAGR of 11%.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash ₹680 cr at Jun-26 (cash ₹1,065 cr, debt ₹385 cr) vs ₹621 cr at Mar-26 and ₹475 cr at Jun-25. Q1 movement: cash profit +₹149 cr, working capital −₹24 cr, tax −₹31 cr, capex −₹35 cr. [IP Q1FY27 p.7]
- Parent is debt-free; group borrowings ₹533 cr (Mar-26) sit in subsidiaries, backed by ₹272 cr of parent financial guarantees (₹317 cr a year earlier). [Screener; CRISIL May-26; AR FY26 standalone liquidity note]
- Operating cash flow FY24 ₹285 cr, FY25 ₹391 cr, FY26 ₹454 cr (103% of operating profit); free cash flow ₹348 cr in FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital is heavy: inventory 134 days, receivables 67 days, payables 87 days, cash conversion cycle 114 days (FY26). Management says inventory cuts have begun and forecasting accuracy is ~60–70% (target ~90%). [Screener; Q4 call]
- Dividend payout ~20%. US tariff refund approved ~US$4 mn; US$1.55 mn received per a 29 Aug 2026 exchange filing title (filing not opened). [Screener; Q1 call; Screener document list]

## Moat evidence
- Share: ~25% in India (#2), #6 globally; grew share across Indian segments in FY26 and "dominant" in railway compressors. [CRISIL May-26; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Product: best-in-world energy efficiency on ~70% of industrial models (independent data, per MD); Demand=Match gives up to 17% energy savings and "higher price and margin realisation"; won "customers who were never our customers". [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Cost/quality: in-house motors (88% of needs) cut lead times from 3–6 months to 3 days and softened US tariffs; warranty cost below 1% of revenue, allowing 10-year/lifetime warranties. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- Pricing power: absorbed a 25–50% US tariff while growing North America; passed on a 9% cost buffer in June. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Limits: the bottom ~20% of the market buys on price and is flooded by Chinese machines (hence Tier-4); overseas growth is gated by winning distributors from Atlas Copco and Ingersoll Rand — "there is no switch we can quickly turn on". [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Europe drag:** Elgi Compressors Europe S.R.L. lost ₹22.2 cr in FY26 on ₹267 cr turnover and has negative reserves of ₹386 cr; Rotair (Italy) lost ₹0.5 cr as US tariffs hit its exports. The auditor's key audit matter is the carrying value of ₹170 cr of investments in subsidiaries, with cash-flow projections needed for one subsidiary (investment and guarantees). [AR FY26 Form AOC-1; standalone auditor's report]
- **US distribution businesses weak:** Michigan Air Solutions pre-tax loss ₹6.8 cr, negative reserves ₹29 cr; Patton's has negative reserves ₹72 cr; F.R. Pulford (Australia) lost ₹2.6 cr; distribution "revenue targets not fully achieved". [AR FY26 AOC-1, MD&A]
- **Regulatory enquiry:** summons under FEMA (foreign-exchange law) seeking data on imports, exports, sales to subsidiaries and overseas investments of earlier years; management expects no significant impact. [AR FY26 standalone note 43(iv)]
- **Whistle-blower complaints** received in FY26, some still open at Mar-26; "based on preliminary findings" no significant impact. Auditor (Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP) considered them in CARO. [AR FY26 note 43A; CARO (xi)(c)]
- Contingent liabilities are small (₹1.3 cr tax disputes; a railway-bridge arbitration where Elgi is the claimant). [AR FY26 note 43]
- Commodity and tariff exposure (US tariff ~25% on India/Italy-sourced machines); order finalisation slowed by the West Asia war. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Restructuring costs will continue "during the year" (Australia, Europe, US, SE Asia). [Q1 call]
- FII holding fell from 29.1% (Mar-25) to 21.9% (Jun-26); promoters steady at 31.19%, no pledges disclosed. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (commodity "blindsided" risk, Europe losses, cautious order conversion) to confident (23% growth, margin up, Demand=Match "outstanding"). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New: explicit volume-led India growth (+28%), new EV/solar/semiconductor customers, vacuum sales "phenomenal percentage growth" from a tiny base, a new process/"process layer" programme. [Q1 call]
- New problems named: Australia service/distribution and North American distribution service "not performing". [Q1 call]
- Management said Q1 India growth will not repeat because the Q1 FY26 base was weak. [Q1 call]

## My read
Elgi is a steady compounder that is improving at the edges: India is taking share with a genuinely new product (Demand=Match), North America is growing fast despite tariffs, and the balance sheet is net cash with ~100% cash conversion. The weak parts are not hidden — Europe still has a large accumulated deficit, US and Australian distribution units lose money, and there is an open FEMA enquiry and whistle-blower complaints to watch. The FY31 plan (₹6,615 cr revenue, 18% margin) implies ~11% sales growth and margin up ~3 points; CRISIL expects only 7–9% growth and 14–15% margins, so the price needs something between the two. The view improves if Q2–Q3 FY27 show the price hikes lifting margin above 16% and Tier-4 gaining traction; it worsens if Europe slips back to losses or the FEMA matter produces a penalty.
