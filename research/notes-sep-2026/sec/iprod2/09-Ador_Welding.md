## PROFILE (Q4 FY26)
# Ador Welding

## Executive Summary
Ador Welding operates as a critical industrial enabler, providing the "molecular glue" for India’s infrastructure and manufacturing sectors through a dual portfolio of welding consumables and equipment. Unlike its MNC peers, Ador stands as a indigenous Challenger that has historically traded at a valuation discount due to capital misallocation in its Projects business and inconsistent margin profiles. **The market is currently missing the efficacy of Ador's strategic pivot:** The company has presented a "clean slate" turnaround, successfully exiting high-risk, large-exposure EPC contracts to become a pure-play welding technology firm. By effectively capping the volatility from its Projects division—which has now been dissolved as a standalone unit after ring-fencing the legacy Uran project—and enforcing strict pricing discipline over volume chasing, Ador has structurally reset its operational EBITDA margins to a 12% steady state, targeting an additional 100 bps expansion in FY27. With a renewed focus on high-tech automation (cobots) and specialized applications (EV battery welding, defense shipbuilding), Ador is transitioning from a cyclical commodity player into a specialized engineering compounder targeting ₹2,000+ Cr in revenues by FY29.

## The Business Model

### Revenue Drivers
*   **Core Offerings:** Ador does not simply sell "welding supplies"; it sells continuity and efficiency for metal fabrication. The portfolio is bifurcated into **Consumables** (electrodes, wires, fluxes—the recurring "razor blade" model operating at ~70% utilization, with specific lines maxing at 95%+) and **Equipment/Automation** (inverters, cutters, and increasingly complex robotic/cobot integration—~30,000 unit capacity). Recent R&D and strategic collaborations, such as a new global partnership with Miller for Submerged Arc Welding, have pushed high-value SKUs including "Rhino E" battery welders, nuclear/wind-approved consumables, and data-mapped welding interfaces for smart factories.
*   **Customer Base:** The company relies on a B2B model, servicing end-users through a massive tiered distribution network (general engineering, construction) and direct technical sales for specialized sectors like Defense (shipbuilding/submarines), Automotive, and Process Industries.
*   **Monetization Mechanics:**
    *   **Consumables:** High-velocity spot sales driven by industrial utilization rates and steel consumption cycles. To meet demand, Ador is initiating a ₹30-35 Cr annual growth capex cycle to modernize and expand consumable lines.
    *   **Equipment:** CAPEX-driven sales; however, the shift toward drum packaging for MIG wire in the automotive sector indicates a move to lock in higher recurring consumable volumes alongside equipment installation. The automation segment is currently reporting its best-ever order book.
    *   **Projects (Flares & Process Equipment):** Management has decisively shut down this loss-making standalone division. Moving forward, large-exposure EPC contracts are eliminated, and legacy operations are subsumed purely as a strictly controlled ₹20-30 Cr internal product line to eliminate balance sheet drag.

**Segment Commentary:**
The **Consumables** segment is exhibiting a successful shift in philosophy; management has ceased aggressive pricing wars to chase volume, accepting modest 2% overall revenue growth (closing FY26 at ₹1,135 Cr) in exchange for an impressive gross margin expansion of 250 bps for the full year to 38%. The **Equipment** segment is benefiting from the "China Plus One" and modernization trends, with Ador finally gaining real traction in automation and robotics after historically trailing the curve. The **Projects** division is no longer a drag; with the protracted Uran project effectively concluded and fully provided for (absorbing a ₹25 Cr net hit in FY26), management has dissolved the FPE division entirely. As this segment’s negative impact on blended margins evaporates, Ador is now reporting as a single pure-play Welding entity, offering high earnings visibility and revealing the robust underlying profitability of the core business.
