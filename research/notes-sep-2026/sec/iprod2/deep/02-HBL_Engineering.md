## Business and segments
- Engineering company run by founder Dr A J Prasad on a "fill technology gaps in niche markets" model; nine business units in three segments [AR FY26 MD&A]:
  - **Industrial Electronics:** rail signalling — **Kavach** (train collision avoidance, TCAS), train management system (TMS/CTC); electric drive trains for 55-tonne trucks and marine vessels.
  - **Industrial Batteries:** lead (telecom, railways, UPS, PLT for data centres and Cummins gensets), nickel-cadmium (HBL says it is the world's No. 2 industrial Ni-Cd supplier), lithium-ion packs from imported cells.
  - **Defence:** aircraft/missile/torpedo/submarine batteries, electronic fuzes, tank communication systems.
- **Kavach was "almost 50% of sales" in FY26**, and HBL expects about the same share in FY27 [AR FY26 MD&A].
- FY26 reported segment revenue (consolidated): Electronics ₹1,626 cr (FY25 ₹298 cr), Batteries ₹1,416 cr (₹1,397 cr), Defence & Aviation ₹212 cr (₹227 cr); total ₹3,311 cr (₹1,972 cr) [AR FY26 Note 44.9]. So essentially **all FY26 growth came from Kavach-led Electronics**; batteries were flat and defence fell.
- Exports FY26 ≈ ₹528 cr (batteries ₹402 cr, defence ₹110 cr, electronics ₹16 cr) [AR FY26 Note 44.9]. Ni-Cd exports grew ~15% in FY26 [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Capacity use (FY25): lead-acid 54%, Ni-Cd 90% [CARE Feb-26]. PLT capacity being doubled after 100% growth in data-centre sales; Ni-Cd expansion under way [AR FY26 MD&A].

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM: Jun-25 ₹602 cr / ₹192 cr / 32%; Sep-25 ₹1,223 cr / ₹544 cr / 44%; Dec-25 ₹874 cr / ₹302 cr / 35%; Mar-26 ₹604 cr / ₹75 cr / 12%; Jun-26 ₹638 cr / ₹147 cr / 23% [Screener].
- Quarterly net profit: ₹143 cr, ₹387 cr, ₹220 cr, ₹64 cr, ₹109 cr [Screener]. Q1 FY27 sales +6% YoY but profit –24% YoY; the Sep-25 quarter was the peak of the CLW locomotive Kavach order.
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹2,233 cr, OP ₹423 cr (19%), PAT ₹280 cr; FY25 ₹1,967 cr, ₹393 cr (20%), ₹276 cr; FY26 ₹3,303 cr, ₹1,113 cr (34%), ₹814 cr; TTM ₹3,339 cr, ₹1,068 cr, ₹780 cr [Screener].
- ROCE 59% and ROE 45% in FY26 (13–14% in FY22–FY23) [Screener].
- Standalone FY26: revenue ₹3,251.8 cr (+67.1%), EBITDA ₹1,160.5 cr (35.7% margin vs 21.4%), PAT ₹796.8 cr [AR FY26 Directors' Report].
- Unit economics: Kavach margins "vary significantly from quarter to quarter" because contracts differ (loco vs trackside) and are competitively bid [AR FY26 MD&A]. Raw materials are 50–60% of revenue; lead is ~60% of battery cost [CARE Feb-26].

## Guidance history
- HBL gives no formal guidance; the chairman gives "budgets" and multi-year scenarios.
- **FY26:** AGM Sep-25 "budgeting for 3000 crores in sales" → delivered ₹3,303 cr (**beat**) [AGM 2025; Screener].
- **Kavach durability:** AGM Sep-25 said "there could be a decline in FY28"; AR FY26 now says sales "good in FY 27 and FY 28" and decline "from FY 29 onwards" — **pushed out a year (raised)** [AGM 2025; AR FY26 MD&A].
- **FY30 scenario:** FY25 MD&A "₹4,500 cr by FY30" (restated at the AGM) → FY26 MD&A "more than 5,000 Crs in total sales in FY 2030" — **raised** [AGM 2025; AR FY26 MD&A]. From TTM ₹3,339 cr, ₹5,000 cr implies ~11% a year.
- **E-trucks — missed/delayed:** FY25 report said sales could begin Oct-2026; now homologation by Mar-27 and pilot sales from Jul-27 — a nine-month delay caused by redesigning to "magnet-less" motors after rare-earth magnet supply problems [AR FY26 MD&A].
- **Electronic fuzes:** at the AGM Sep-25 meaningful sales were put at FY28; now grenade fuzes approved by Home Ministry and selling, Army approval and 155 mm artillery fuze approval both expected in 2027 [AGM 2025; AR FY26 MD&A]. Kept.
- **CLW locomotive Kavach order:** HBL supplied 76% of the order by mid-Dec-25 and the balance was cancelled per order terms [CARE Feb-26] — the AGM had already warned "we will not be able to finish our entire order this year" [AGM 2025].
- **Dividend:** AGM promised a second (interim) dividend in March — delivered: ₹2 interim (₹55.4 cr) plus ₹1 final proposed [AGM 2025; AR FY26 Directors' Report].

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is small by design ("avoid capital-intensive products"). Fixed assets ₹441 cr and CWIP ₹127 cr at Mar-26 (₹397 cr / ₹68 cr a year earlier) [Screener]. Capital commitments ₹46.9 cr [AR FY26 Note 43.2].
- About ₹200 cr invested in R&D and a plant for defence lithium cells (technology licensed from DRDO's NSTL) [AR FY26 MD&A; AGM 2025].
- Ongoing: PLT battery capacity doubling, Ni-Cd expansion, electronic-fuze licensed plant in Telangana, lithium-ion division, charging hubs — all funded from internal accruals [AR FY26 MD&A; CARE Feb-26].
- New JV: **Green Maritime Propulsion** with Cochin Shipyard (HBL 41%), incorporated 11-Jun-26, for electric drive trains for ferries and tugs; first order for batteries for two e-tugs; sales limited until Sep-27 [AR FY26 MD&A; AGM notice].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings only ₹67 cr; investments ₹402 cr; reserves ₹2,187 cr at Mar-26 [Screener]. Debt/equity 0.03 [AR FY26].
- FY26 CFO ₹738 cr and FCF ₹594 cr (FY25: ₹247 cr / ₹96 cr) [Screener].
- Debtor days 71, inventory days 148, cash conversion cycle 171 days (202 in FY25, when Kavach inventory was built ahead of approval) [Screener; CARE Feb-26].
- Dividend payout ~10% of profit [Screener]. Surplus-cash policy: more R&D, higher dividends, PE funds as limited partner, direct start-up stakes, then listed shares; the earlier plan for a single-investor AIF ("Mittelstand Technology Partners") was dropped [AR FY26 MD&A].

## Moat evidence
- **Kavach pioneer:** developed with RDSO since 2005; among the first two of five vendors cleared for version 4.0, with an 8–9-week lead [AGM 2025]; SIL-4 certified [AR FY26].
- Order flow: order book ₹2,999 cr at 31-Dec-25 plus ₹1,375 cr of fresh onboard-Kavach orders in Q4 FY26 [CARE Feb-26]; then a ₹1,714 cr CLW onboard Kavach (v4.0) order on 28-May-26, 12-month execution, plus ₹180 cr (Banaras Loco Works) and ₹84 cr (Patiala) in Apr-26; the same summary cites a cumulative Kavach order book of ₹3,763.8 cr to be executed over 24 months (ICF order sizes reported inconsistently across news sites — not used) [News: multibagg summary of exchange filing, 28-May-26].
- Niche positions: world No. 2 in industrial Ni-Cd; only Indian maker of PLT lead batteries; only Indian supplier of tank voice/data systems; claims to be the only Indian company making every fuze component including the battery [AR FY26 MD&A; AGM 2025].
- Customer quality: Indian Railways, BSNL, Indus Towers, Cummins (two-decade PLT relationship), data-centre operators (STT, NTT, CtrlS, Reliance), Siemens Germany picked HBL as one of two LIB development suppliers [CARE Feb-26; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Weak spot: telecom VRLA faces excess industry capacity and price pressure as towers move to lithium [AR FY26 MD&A].

## Risks and red flags
- **Kavach concentration and cliff:** ~50% of sales from one product and one customer (Indian Railways); management itself expects competition to cut Kavach sales and profit from FY29 [AR FY26 MD&A]. Mar-26 quarter OPM dropped to 12% when the CLW order ended [Screener].
- **Revenue-recognition key audit matter:** Kavach revenue is booked on percentage-of-completion using estimated costs to complete; auditors flag the estimates as complex (opinion unmodified) [AR FY26 auditor's report, KAM].
- Kavach warranty provision ₹92.75 cr for the defect-liability period [AR FY26 standalone Note 39.2].
- Bank guarantees outstanding ₹303.4 cr (₹207.1 cr a year earlier); tax and other claims are small (excise ₹24.7 cr, sales tax ₹6.3 cr, SCIL Infracon arbitration ₹5.4 cr) [AR FY26 Note 43.2].
- Related parties: shareholders asked to approve up to ₹700 cr a year of transactions with subsidiaries, the Cochin Shipyard JV and associates (Tonbo Imaging, Naval Systems & Technologies, Xalten Systems), where promoter-family director Kavita Prasad Aluru also sits [AR FY26 AGM notice Item 8]. Associate investments were ₹86.7 cr at Mar-25 [CARE Feb-26].
- **Key-person risk:** strategy and capital allocation rest heavily on the founder-chairman; succession plan is to run nine semi-independent units, not to name a single CEO [AR FY26 MD&A; AGM 2025].
- Disclosure gap: no earnings calls, no investor presentation, no reported order book after Dec-25, and segment profit is hard to read in the report.
- Lead handling / environmental and EPR (battery recycling) obligations [CARE Feb-26; AR FY26 Note 39.3].
- Credit view: CARE A+ / Positive outlook, A1+; strengths — diversified niches, negligible debt, strong liquidity; weaknesses — working-capital intensity, raw-material volatility, competition [CARE Feb-26].

## What changed between the two "management" touchpoints (AGM Sep-25 → AR FY26, Sep-26)
- Kavach decline pushed from FY28 to FY29; FY30 sales scenario raised from ₹4,500 cr to over ₹5,000 cr.
- E-truck launch slipped nine months; marine drive trains added via the Cochin Shipyard JV.
- CLW loco order closed at 76% completion with the balance cancelled; new ₹1,714 cr CLW v4.0 order replaced it.
- The single-investor AIF plan was dropped in favour of direct PE/start-up investing.
- Numbers: quarterly profit fell from ₹387 cr (Sep-25) to ₹64 cr (Mar-26) and ₹109 cr (Jun-26) as the big order ended.

## My read
HBL is a high-quality, cash-rich niche engineer whose FY26 profit tripled on one lumpy Kavach contract; the Mar-26 and Jun-26 quarters show how quickly margins fall back (12% and 23% vs 44% at the peak). The new ₹1,714 cr CLW order and trackside work should keep FY27 near FY26, and management itself sees FY27–FY28 as good years before competition bites from FY29. The price (about 28x trailing profit) therefore depends on whether fuzes, e-trucks, TMS and batteries can grow into the gap after Kavach — all are real but mostly FY28+ and have already slipped once (e-trucks). What would change the view: quarterly profit back above ₹200 cr as the CLW order executes, Army fuze approval in 2027, and any disclosed order book after Dec-25.
