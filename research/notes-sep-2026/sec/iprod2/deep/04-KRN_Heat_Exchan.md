# KRN Heat Exchanger and Refrigeration — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code KRN (consolidated). Price ₹1,382, market cap ₹9,045 cr, 52-week range ₹590–1,687 [Screener, 27-Sep-26].

**Transcript verdicts.** Q4 FY26 stored copy (tijoristack re-typing of the 15-May-26 Dolat Capital call) — **tidied copy (figures match)**: FY26 total income ₹609.81 cr, EBITDA ₹112.48 cr, PAT ₹76.47 cr, Q4 ₹181.40 / ₹33.55 / ₹23.36 cr, ₹120 cr export order book, ₹500 cr QIP enabling resolution, 50%/80% utilisation, PLI 5% and RIPS 1.56% all agree with the official BSE transcript. Q1 FY27 stored copy — **not the earnings call**: it is a Kirin Advisors investor webinar dated 21-Aug-26; the official Q1 FY27 earnings call was held 14-Aug-26 (Dolat Capital) and was read in full from the company's website. The webinar copy also contains internally inconsistent claims (bus AC share "10–15%" in one answer and "approximately 50%" in another); the official call says 15%.

## Business and segments
- Maker of fin-and-tube heat exchangers (coils) for commercial HVAC&R OEMs — not room ACs; customers include Daikin, Schneider Electric, Carrier, Voltas, Blue Star, Knorr-Bremse [AR FY26 MD&A]. Founder-CMD Santosh Kumar Yadav (ex-Lloyd Electric) [IP Q1FY27].
- Two entities: **KRN (Plant I, Neemrana)** — legacy coils, ran at 84–86% utilisation in FY26 [AR FY26]; **KRN HVAC Products (Plant II, wholly owned)** — new plant "six times" the old one, commissioned May-25, formally inaugurated Mar-26, with 8–9 new products: bar-and-plate (railway/transformer oil coolers), refrigerator components (wire-on-tube, roll-bond, frost-free), dry coolers, ammonia evaporators, complete bus AC (team and assets bought from Sphere Refrigeration in Q2 FY26) [Q4 call; AR FY26].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹252.3 cr: KRN ~₹140 cr, HVAC subsidiary ~₹115 cr [Q1 call official]. Standalone revenue ₹182.0 cr [IP Q1FY27].
- Mix: fin-and-tube ~95% of Q4 FY26 revenue [Q4 call]; data centres 16% of FY26 revenue, 18.7% in Q4 [Q4 call]; exports 16.6% of FY26, 20.8% of Q1 FY27 (~₹50 cr+ in Q1, +177% YoY) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Target products FY27: bus AC run-rate ₹5–6 cr/month, aiming for ~15% of a ₹1,000 cr market; oil coolers ~₹40 cr; export ₹200 cr (from ~₹100 cr) [Q1 call official].
- Indian commercial heat-exchanger market ~₹2,000 cr (total incl. room AC ₹7,000–8,000 cr) — management estimate [webinar 21-Aug-26].

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM: Jun-25 ₹115 cr / ₹18 cr / 15%; Sep-25 ₹152 / ₹30 / 20%; Dec-25 ₹153 / ₹31 / 20%; Mar-26 ₹179 / ₹34 / 19%; Jun-26 ₹252 / ₹49 / 19% [Screener].
- Quarterly net profit: ₹12 cr, ₹18 cr, ₹23 cr, ₹23 cr, ₹33 cr [Screener]. Tax rate was unusually low (12–13%) in Dec-25 and Mar-26, flattering those profits; back to 22% in Jun-26 [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹307 cr, OP ₹59 cr (19%), PAT ₹39 cr; FY25 ₹430 cr, ₹70 cr (16%), ₹53 cr; FY26 ₹600 cr, ₹112 cr (19%), ₹76 cr; TTM ₹737 cr, ₹144 cr, ₹97 cr [Screener].
- ROCE 21% (FY25) → 16% (FY26); ROE 14% [Screener].
- **Standalone vs consolidated margin gap:** Q1 standalone EBITDA margin fell to 13.6% from 17.3%, while consolidated rose to 19.4% from 15.3% — the new subsidiary is now carrying the margin [IP Q1FY27]. Employee costs rose sharply (headcount 1,500+) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Pricing: contracts pass through LME copper/aluminium and USD/INR quarterly, using prior-quarter averages (so a lag) [Q4 call]. CRISIL notes raw materials are 65–75% of income, imports are the majority of purchases, and margins have ranged 12.5–20.5% over five years [CRISIL Jun-26].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 promises:** Plant II at 50% utilisation in FY27, 80% in FY28; exports to double to ~₹200 cr; bus AC ≥15% market share; Vertiv supplies to begin "in the coming month"; capex only ₹30–40 cr for the existing facility; margins to rise slightly with PLI (5% of heat-exchanger production) and RIPS (1.56% of new-plant sales for 10 years); inventory "under control within the next 2–3 quarters"; "In each of the next 8 quarters, you will see our highest figures ever" [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** revenue +119% to a record ₹252 cr — **delivered**; exports on track for ₹200 cr — **on track**; bus AC run-rate ₹5–6 cr/month with a large anchor customer starting Q3 — **on track but back-ended**; Vertiv: only a sample order received, audit passed [webinar 21-Aug-26] — **slipped** vs "coming month"; PLI not yet claimed (needs ₹400 cr fin-and-tube sales in the year; application expected Q3/Q4) and RIPS entitlement received ~June but accounting treatment undecided — **pending** [Q1 call official].
- **Revenue targets:** FY27 "more than ₹1,000 cr" (webinar); ₹2,000 cr at 80% utilisation in FY28 — "Still same" [Q1 call official]. An analyst's ₹2,250–2,850 cr figure was not confirmed [Q4 call].
- **Capex guidance raised:** Q4 said ₹30–40 cr; Q1 added ~₹100 cr within a year for a Bangalore plant (10–15% capacity addition, mainly shifting ~₹100 cr of existing southern business) and a Rajasthan site for bus AC and railway HVAC [Q1 call official].
- Margin: "20-plus" EBITDA at scale [Q1 call official].

## Capex and capacity
- Fixed assets jumped from ₹85 cr to ₹314 cr in FY26 as Plant II was capitalised; CWIP ₹33 cr [Screener]. Capital commitments ₹42.4 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 Note 34].
- Plant II funded by IPO money (~₹350 cr, Oct-24) plus internal accruals; the company says it invested more in the plant than the IPO raised [Q4 call].
- 8 MW solar plant operational [Q4 call]. Micro-channel line to be ordered, production in ~1 year; plate heat exchangers for liquid-cooling CDUs at an early stage [webinar 21-Aug-26].
- Payback depends on filling Plant II: 15% utilisation in FY26 → 50% FY27 → 80% FY28 [Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Cash burn in FY26:** CFO –₹114 cr, FCF –₹312 cr [Screener]. Borrowings rose from ₹33 cr to ₹187 cr; debt/equity 0.07 → 0.33 [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]. Cash ₹46 cr at Mar-26, of which only ₹17–18 cr unencumbered [CRISIL Jun-26].
- Working capital blew out: inventory days 110 → 242, debtor days 79 → 106, payable days 63 → 140, cash conversion cycle 208 days [Screener]. Reasons given: UAE order stuck by war (₹15–20 cr), ₹8 cr shipped without bill of lading, minimum stocks for new products, pre-BIS stock of inner-groove tubes [Q4 call]. CRISIL puts gross current assets at 340–350 days [CRISIL Jun-26].
- **Equity raised again:** ₹350 cr QIP in May-26 (received end-June) "to cater to increased working capital" [CRISIL Jun-26; Q1 call]; promoter holding fell from 70.79% to 65.69% [Screener]. Management says the money covers two years of working capital at the current pace [Q1 call official].
- No dividend [Screener]. Standalone has ₹48.3 cr of on-demand loans to subsidiaries and ₹350.7 cr invested in them (70% of standalone net worth) [AR FY26 KAM].

## Moat evidence
- Scale: "the largest manufacturer in India" of heat exchangers other than OEM captive units [Q1 call official]; small vendors struggle with metal-price working capital [Q1 call].
- Customisation: products are made to order (coils up to ~3 m × 1.5 m), so Chinese catalogue imports are not a direct threat [Q4 call; webinar].
- Qualification wins: railway bar-and-plate approved after a 6-month field trial; L1 in CLW tender (~₹8 cr share of ₹70 cr) [Q1 call official]; Vertiv vendor audit passed first time [Q4 call].
- Export entry: US data-centre customers approached because local makers (Modine, Direct Coil, Can Coil) are capacity-constrained; KRN pays or shares a 25% US duty [webinar].
- Weakness: largest legacy customer Daikin works on a fixed rupee margin — no percentage upside [webinar].

## Risks and red flags
- **Working capital and dilution:** negative FCF of ₹312 cr in FY26 and a second equity raise within 19 months of the IPO [Screener; CRISIL Jun-26].
- **Auditor key audit matters (standalone):** revenue recognition — flagged because of fast growth, "the commencement of the new trading activity" and "the volume of transactions with related parties" (sales to the subsidiary); and carrying value of ₹399 cr of investments and loans in subsidiaries (70% of standalone net worth). Opinions unmodified [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Trading purchases rose to ₹34.2 cr (consolidated) from ₹3.4 cr [AR FY26 Note 25].
- Contingent liabilities: letters of credit ₹44.7 cr, bank guarantees ₹20.7 cr, indirect tax ₹4.9 cr, **EPCG/advance-authorisation export obligations ₹29.4 cr** (₹8.1 cr a year earlier) — duty would become payable if export obligations are not met [AR FY26 Note 34].
- Margin quality: new plant margin supported by incentives not yet booked (PLI, RIPS); standalone margin fell to 13.6% [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Geopolitics: UAE freight up from ~$1,500 to ~$6,000 per container; US warehouse plan paused over tariff uncertainty [Q1 call official; webinar].
- Technology: AI data centres shift indoor cooling to liquid (plate heat exchangers/CDUs), where KRN has no product yet [Q4 call].
- Disclosure: no order book (rolling one-month orders), segment split "not with me" on calls [Q1 call; webinar].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defending an inventory pile-up and a QIP (Q4) to growth and new plants (Q1). Revenue more than doubled YoY, but capex guidance rose (₹30–40 cr → plus ~₹100 cr for Bangalore/Rajasthan).
- QIP completed at ₹350 cr; interest cost to fall.
- New: Bangalore plant, Rajasthan land for bus AC/railway HVAC, railway cab-AC development orders from BLW [webinar].
- UAE still weak; North America the main export driver.

## My read
KRN's revenue is growing very fast (Q1 +119%) and the new plant, export data-centre customers and bus AC give real visibility; consolidated margins held at ~19%. But the business consumed ₹312 cr of free cash in FY26, inventory doubled in days, standalone margins fell, and shareholders funded the gap with a second equity raise. At about 93x trailing earnings the price needs the ₹2,000 cr FY28 revenue (roughly 2.7x TTM) to arrive on time with margins above 20% and working capital normalising. What would change the view: positive operating cash flow in H1/H2 FY27, inventory days back toward 150, PLI/RIPS actually booked, and Vertiv volume orders; another fund-raise or a margin dip below 17% would argue for a lower estimate.
