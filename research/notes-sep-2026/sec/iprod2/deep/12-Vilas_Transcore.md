Inputs read: stored file (metrics, analysis, profile, and two AI-made transcripts: "Q1 FY27" = Arihant Capital "Bharat Confs" investor conference, 25 Sep 2026; "General 18 Jun 2026" = Hem Securities "Samridhi" webinar — **neither is an earnings call**); official H2 FY26 earnings-call transcript (NSE, call 12 May 2026, filed 19 May 2026) read in full; H2 FY26 investor presentation (NSE); Annual Report FY26 (NSE) — board report extracts, AGM notice (Atlas RPT approval), auditor's report, notes 43 (contingencies), 46 (related parties), 47 (CSR), 48 (segments); ICRA rationale (18 Sep 2026); NSE filing on copper-conductor launch (16 Sep 2026); Screener standalone page (raw HTML; no consolidated accounts). SME company, half-yearly results.

## Business and segments
- Vadodara maker of CRGO (cold-rolled grain-oriented steel) transformer laminations and cores — slit coils, stacked/wound/toroidal cores — for power and distribution transformer makers; single reportable segment [AR FY26 note 48; ICRA Sep-26].
- Capacity: CRGO laminations 12,000 → 36,000 t/yr after Unit 3 (commissioned Jul 2025); nanocrystalline cores 240 t/yr (from Nov 2025); radiators 7,200 t/yr (sales from Apr 2026); copper/aluminium paper-covered conductors 1,500–1,800 t/yr phase 1 (launched 15 Sep 2026) [H2 call; IP H2FY26; NSE filing 16 Sep 2026].
- CRGO volumes: FY25 ~12,069 t → FY26 ~19,856 t (+65%) [IP H2FY26; ICRA Sep-26]. FY26 volume up ~64–65% but revenue only ~30% because CRGO prices fell to ~₹180–185/kg [H2 call; IP H2FY26].
- Customers: Voltamp, Atlanta Electricals (new, ~9% of FY26 revenue), Electrotherm, ECE, Kirloskar, Shilchar, CG Power; **largest customer 40–50% of revenue for four years** [H2 call; ICRA Sep-26]. Exports <2% [ICRA Sep-26].
- Pricing: two customers on quarterly fixed-price contracts (50–60% of sales); the rest spot [Sep conference; H2 call].
- Unit margins stated by management: nanocrystalline 20–25% EBITDA (PAT ~25%), radiators ~15–18%, copper conductors ~5–7%, CRGO ~11–13% [H2 call; Sep conference; Jun webinar].
- Market: Indian CRGO demand ~4.5–5 lakh t/yr, Vilas ~5–7%; organised 60–65% (management estimate) [Sep conference].
- Nanocrystalline revenue ₹3.48 cr in H2 FY26 (~30 t) [IP H2FY26].

## Guidance history
- **IPO (Jun 2024):** ₹1,000 cr revenue in FY27, assuming 30,000 t at ₹270–280/kg [H2 call].
- **H2 FY26 call (12 May 2026):** CRGO volume +45–50% to ~30,000 t; revenue ₹750–800 cr at ₹210/kg (copper ₹100–120 cr, radiators ~₹25 cr, nanocrystalline ₹18–20 cr); EBITDA 11–12%, PAT 7–8% (PAT floor 7% "in last 25 to 30 years"); FY27 capex ₹30–40 cr; ₹1,000 cr in FY28 once copper is full; H1 weaker than H2 (~40:60); working-capital cycle ~95–100 days [H2 call].
- **Hem webinar (Jun 2026):** "revenue target of 750 crores" [Jun webinar].
- **Arihant conference (25 Sep 2026):** CRGO 25,000–30,000 t "without compromising the margin"; revenue ~₹680 cr (~40% growth); copper ~₹80 cr this year; FY28 growth 15–20%; "at present, no" new capex; PAT not below 7% [Sep conference].
- Track record: FY26 volume target (~64%) met; margins fell; FY27 revenue guide **cut** from ₹750–800 cr to ~₹680 cr and copper from ₹100–120 cr to ~₹80 cr within four months; the IPO-era ₹1,000 cr target has slipped to FY28 at best.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹60 cr (Unit 3, radiators, copper), "fully paid"; ~₹30 cr above the IPO plan, funded partly by a ₹15 cr bank loan [H2 call; Sep conference].
- Copper plant cost ₹25–30 cr (₹20–25 cr per Sep conference); peak revenue ~₹360–400 cr at full 300 t/month, depending on copper price [H2 call; Sep conference].
- Capital commitments ₹30.0 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 43]. Adjacent land being acquired; ₹4.2 cr advance for land paid to related party SMT Packaging [AR FY26 note 46; Sep conference].
- High-voltage bushings: new company, **Vilas 25%, promoter 75%**; ₹10 cr first-phase R&D capex for OIP bushings up to 145 kV; CEO is the founder of Yash Highvoltage; the promoter kept it outside the listed company because "I didn't wanted to risk any money of the Vilas Transcore" [H2 call]. Commercialisation not expected in FY27 [Jun webinar; Sep conference].
- Next CRGO expansion (other regions or abroad) to be decided around Mar-27 [H2 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- CFO: FY24 ₹49 cr, FY25 −₹42 cr, FY26 −₹2 cr; free cash flow −₹80 cr and −₹41 cr [Screener].
- Debt ₹38.9 cr at Mar-26 (no long-term debt), incl. buyers' credit ₹17.4 cr; interest cover 24.2x; TOL/TNW 0.2x; bank lines ~39% used [ICRA Sep-26; AR FY26 note 45]. Company calls itself "net debt-free" [H2 call].
- Working capital: inventory days 70, debtor days 61, cash cycle 104 days (FY25: 103) [Screener]; ICRA expects NWC/OI of 30–32% [ICRA Sep-26]. Inventory policy: two months of sales, higher when imports are uncertain [Jun webinar].
- Advances to suppliers for imports ₹33.9 cr (unhedged) at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 45].
- No dividend; none planned while growing [Jun webinar; Screener].
- Migration to NSE main board planned after June 2027 [H2 call].

## Moat evidence
- Scale buying: orders of 5,000–6,000 t with Chinese mills earn better prices than small rivals buying 100–200 t [Jun webinar].
- Flexible sourcing: can swing 50% ± 20% between Indian and imported coil (China, Russia, Poland, Germany), helped by keeping Indian mills supplied in gluts [Sep conference; H2 call].
- Approvals: PGCIL registration at 220 kV; 400 kV non-PGCIL jobs executed; upgrade to 400/765 kV pending [Sep conference].
- Weaknesses: industry "highly fragmented… intense competition"; no pricing power on spot sales; CRGO cannot be hedged [ICRA Sep-26; H2 call]. Export orders lost to Chinese pricing (Malaysia LOI "at the Chinese price") [H2 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party dealings with the MD's transformer company**: Atlas Transformers India Ltd (MD Nilesh Patel owns 98.70%) sold ₹34.8 cr of goods to Vilas in FY26 (₹25.1 cr in FY25) and bought ₹5.5 cr (₹19.2 cr); ₹17.0 cr was payable to Atlas at Mar-26; the 28 Sep 2026 AGM sought approval for Atlas transactions of up to ₹100 cr a year [AR FY26 note 46; AGM notice Sep-26]. Sales to Tushar Transequipment (KMP-influenced) ₹11.8 cr, ₹6.6 cr receivable [AR FY26 note 46].
- **Promoter economics outside the listed company**: 75% of the bushing venture sits with the promoter [H2 call].
- **Pay**: MD remuneration ₹4.2 cr (~10.6% of FY26 PAT) [AR FY26 note 46].
- **Customer concentration** 40–50% from one buyer [ICRA Sep-26].
- **Cash**: two years of negative CFO despite IPO money [Screener].
- **Commodity/regulation**: CRGO price swings (fell ~50–60% from peak); Chinese mills' BIS licences (renewed for two years per Sep conference); possible anti-dumping duty [H2 call; Sep conference].
- CSR: ₹71.4 lakh unspent in FY26, moved to an unspent-CSR account in Apr-26 [AR FY26 note 47].
- Contingent liabilities small: ₹0.64 cr (income tax, GST, CESTAT refund, legal claims) [AR FY26 note 43]. Auditor: no key audit matters reported [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Management: CFO changed twice in FY26 (Vipul Patel → Jagat Mazumdar May–Nov 2025 → Vipul Patel); company secretary resigned Jul 2026 [AR FY26 note 46; Sep conference].

## My read
Vilas has tripled CRGO capacity and grown volume 65%, but it remains a price-taker in a fragmented, commodity-linked business: margins fell in H2 FY26 as CRGO prices dropped, and FY27 guidance has already been cut from ₹750–800 cr to ~₹680 cr. Cash generation has been weak for two years, and governance needs watching — large purchases from the MD's own transformer company and a promoter-majority bushing venture outside the listed entity. The price needs 15–20% annual earnings growth, which depends on filling the new CRGO capacity at 11–12% EBITDA and copper conductors ramping up. Positive operating cash flow in H1 FY27 and a smaller Atlas related-party flow would improve the view; another guidance cut or PAT margin below 7% would weaken it.
