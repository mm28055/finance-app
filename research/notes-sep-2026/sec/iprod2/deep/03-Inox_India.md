# INOX India (INOXCVA) — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code INOXINDIA (consolidated). Price ₹2,157, market cap ₹19,576 cr, 52-week range ₹1,031–2,340 [Screener, 27-Sep-26].

**Transcript verdicts.** Q1 FY27 stored copy is the company's own BSE filing (letter dated 10-Aug-26) — **matches official**. Q4 FY26 stored copy (tijoristack re-typing) — **materially wrong (in parts)** when checked against the official BSE transcript: (1) FY26 adjusted EBITDA given as ₹380 cr — official ₹388 cr; (2) answer on margins invents "this year, our total year budget is around 21.6%" — official says "this year, it is 23.8%. Other figures (order book ₹1,514 cr, 18–20% growth, ₹450–500 cr quarterly inflow, Cochin ₹85 cr, Bahamas ₹240 cr, keg numbers) match.

## Business and segments
- India's largest cryogenic-tank maker (70–75% domestic share of cryogenic tanks in FY25; ~50% in cryo containers) [CRISIL Sep-25]. Promoter: Pavan Jain / Siddharth Jain faction of the INOX group (74.86%) [CRISIL Sep-25; Screener].
- Four divisions, share of revenue FY25 → FY26 → Q1 FY27: **Industrial Gas (IG)** 61% → 54% → 53%; **LNG** 17% → 28% → 22%; **Cryo-Scientific (CSD)** 17% → 14% → 20%; **Others incl. beverage kegs** 5% → 4% → 5% [IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 segment revenue (incl. other income, ₹382 cr): IG ₹202 cr, LNG ₹86 cr, CSD ₹75 cr (record), others ₹19 cr; exports 58% of revenue [IP Q1FY27].
- LNG FY26 revenue ₹459 cr, ~60% export; Indian LNG product share 60–65%, global 6–8% [Q4 call official]. Disposable cylinders ~₹150 cr in FY26 (>2 million units; >1.5 million to the US) [Q4 call]. Kegs only ₹26 cr in FY26 (61,000 units) from a 300,000-keg plant running at about one-third [Q4 call].
- North/Central America rose from 14% to ~26% of FY26 revenue [Q4 call].
- The company reports **one segment** (cryogenic equipment) in its accounts and says "we don't calculate segment-wise EBITDA" [AR FY26 Note 40; Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 promises:** FY27 revenue growth 18–20% on FY26 total income ₹1,632 cr; quarterly order inflow ₹450–500 cr; ≥₹1,200 cr of the ₹1,514 cr backlog to be executed in FY27; EBITDA margin band 21–24%; Kandla plant in 9–10 months; ISRO third-launch-pad tender "by end of this quarter"; next Highview Power order "within the next 6–8 months" [Q4 call official].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** revenue +8.3% (management's own Q1 target was ₹410–415 cr; ~₹32–35 cr of finished goods held back due to freight rates doubling to $8–9k per container to Europe) — **missed**; order inflow ₹532 cr — **beat**; margin 23.5% (company basis) — within band; Kandla on track for Dec-26/mid-Jan-27 — **kept**; ISRO RFQ "by month-end" — **slipped**; Highview delayed by client's regulatory issue — **slipped**. 18–20% FY27 growth reaffirmed: "Absolutely" [Q1 call].
- Order-inflow guidance: management hinted it could be revised "slightly" upward on lumpy aerospace and mini-LNG orders [Q1 call].
- Non-aerospace IG growth guided at 15–18% [Q1 call]. ITER to give "at least 50–60 crores" of orders a year for five years [Q4 call].
- Earlier track record: FY26 total income growth 21.2% vs 18–20% type guidance; margin 23.8% vs 21–24% band [Q4 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Capex ₹127.4 cr (FY25, mainly the Savli cryo/non-cryo and keg plant) and ₹47.4 cr (FY26) [AR FY26 cash-flow/segment data]. CRISIL expects ~₹80 cr a year, all internally funded [CRISIL Sep-25]. Capital commitments ₹15.8 cr at Mar-26 (₹8.0 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 Note 46].
- **Kandla** (7 acres, 30-year lease, 2.5 km from the port): for ultra-large tanks up to 8–9 m diameter, 60 m length, 500 tonnes — LNG mega-storage and aerospace tanks; civil work and machines ordered; start Dec-26/Jan-27 [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Investment amount not disclosed.
- Savli: cryo shop and keg shop "under full production"; keg utilisation ~30% targeted to rise to 50–60% by year-end; keg building can take 1 million kegs with more machines [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Aerospace (US private space customer) orders now exceed ₹1,000 cr across Q4 FY26–Q2 FY27, to be delivered by end-FY28, booked on percentage-of-completion this year [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Free funds ₹257 cr (Mar-26) → ₹331 cr (Jun-26); net-debt-free [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]. Borrowings ₹81 cr against investments ₹297 cr at Mar-26 [Screener]; the company deliberately uses bank lines rather than selling long-term mutual funds, for tax reasons [IP Q1FY27].
- **Weak cash conversion:** CFO ₹177 cr (FY23), ₹122 cr (FY24), ₹122 cr (FY25), ₹117 cr (FY26) while sales grew 64%; FCF ₹32 cr, –₹3 cr, ₹11 cr; CFO/operating profit 58% in FY26 [Screener]. Cause: project orders (now >60% of backlog vs ~30% earlier) booked on percentage-of-completion with milestone billing, so contract assets build up; working capital rose from ₹731 cr to ₹990 cr in FY26; customer advances ~₹500 cr [Q4 call official].
- Inventory days 247, debtor days 72, cash conversion cycle 238 days (FY26) [Screener].
- Dividend payout only 7% of FY26 profit [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Share: 70–75% of Indian cryogenic tanks, ~50% of cryo containers [CRISIL Sep-25]; 60–65% of Indian LNG products; >250 LNG semi-trailers and ~1,500 LNG truck fuel tanks on Indian roads [Q4 call].
- Certifications: "more than 40 types"; management says a rival would need "10–15 years" to replicate [Q4 call]. AS9100D aerospace certification won in Q1 FY27 — needed to supply on-board flight hardware (propellant tanks), not just ground equipment [Q1 call].
- Competition for very large space tanks: "only one competitor… Chart Industries" [Q1 call].
- PESO approval acts as a barrier to Chinese LNG fuel tanks; no Chinese shop is PESO-approved [Q4 call].
- Blue-chip customers: Air Liquide, Linde/Praxair, ITER, CERN (22 cryogenic modules for HL-LHC), Heineken/AB InBev/Molson Coors (kegs), Cochin Shipyard (six 800 m³ LNG fuel tanks, ₹85 cr) [CRISIL Sep-25; IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Pricing: large contracts carry raw-material price formulas; kegs are a price-competitive commodity ("a little dicey business") [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Revenue-recognition key audit matter:** long-term design-build contracts on percentage-of-completion; auditors flag the cost-to-complete estimates as judgemental. Opinion unmodified [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report, KAM].
- **US non-compete / arbitration:** a 10-year non-compete from the 2018 sale of its US subsidiary CVA runs to Nov-2028; an arbitral tribunal found that an agency storage arrangement with Allcryo breached it and ordered **INOX India** to pay US$0.94 mn (₹8.5 cr) of Taylor-Wharton's costs, booked as an exceptional expense; other claims dismissed [AR FY26 consolidated Note 35]. Management confirms it cannot set up in the US before 2028 [Q1 call].
- Tax: reassessment notice on a ₹52 cr deduction claimed for money paid on behalf of the old US subsidiary CVA; Gujarat High Court interim relief, hearing adjourned to 14-Jul-26 [AR FY26 standalone Note 46].
- Contingent liabilities otherwise small: bank/corporate guarantees ₹245 cr (normal for project bids), disputed service tax ₹3.1 cr, income tax ₹1.1 cr [AR FY26 Note 46].
- **Related party:** sales to promoter-group INOX Air Products ₹145.0 cr in FY26 (₹151.6 cr FY25) — about 9% of revenue; smaller purchases from INOX Air Products and Bombay Fluid System Components, rent to INOX Chemicals LLP [AR FY26 consolidated Note 45].
- Concentration: one US private space customer >₹1,000 cr of orders, ~₹400 cr in the current book [Q1 call].
- Logistics/geopolitics: West Asia conflict delayed Q1 dispatches and raised freight; 68% of the backlog is export [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Keg plant under-used; transformer tanks tried and being dropped [Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "execute LNG projects and explain working capital" (Q4) to "aerospace, semiconductor and new ventures" (Q1): AS9100D, a semiconductor pipeline skill centre with ITM SLS Baroda University, a Wayout (Sweden) water micro-factory tie-up, data-centre liquid-nitrogen cooling prototype (commercial in 1–1.5 years) [Q1 call].
- Numbers: backlog up ₹172 cr to ₹1,686 cr; but Q1 revenue growth slowed to 8% and margin dipped to 23.5% (20% on Screener's basis) [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- New issue: freight shock; two expected orders (ISRO launch pad, Highview Power) slipped.
- Working-capital questions, which dominated Q4, were not raised in Q1; free funds rose ₹74 cr thanks to advances.

## My read
The business is improving in quality of orders — record backlog, a world-class space customer, CERN and ITER work, and a Kandla plant that opens up very large tanks — but reported growth and cash flow lag the story: Q1 revenue rose only 8%, margins are drifting down (22% → 20% on Screener), and operating cash flow has not grown in three years as project work ties up cash. At about 76x trailing earnings the price assumes the 18–20% guidance is met this year and the aerospace/LNG pipeline keeps compounding into FY28. What would change the view: H1 FY27 revenue growth back near 15–20%, CFO above ₹200 cr in FY27, and an ISRO/Highview/mini-LNG award; a second quarter of freight-related misses would argue for a lower growth estimate.
