# Borosil Renewables — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code BORORENEW (consolidated). Price ₹455, market cap ₹6,699 cr, 52-week range ₹374–721 [Screener, 27-Sep-26].

**Transcript verdicts.** Both stored transcripts (Q1 FY27, call 17-Jul-26; Q4 FY26, call 13-May-26) are the company's own BSE filings on letterhead — **matches official**.

## Business and segments
- India's first and largest maker of low-iron textured solar glass for PV modules; one plant at Bharuch, Gujarat, 1,000 tonnes/day (TPD), ≈6.5 GW of modules a year, running at full capacity [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Single segment. Exports only 7.4% of FY26 sales (₹113 cr) and 2.8% in Q4 FY26 — "major export markets continue to face low demand" [Q4 call]. The German plant (GMB, 350 TPD) went insolvent and left the group on 4-Jul-25 [Ind-Ra Dec-25].
- New rooftop-solar trading division (Borosil-branded modules, inverters, batteries, all outsourced) in Gujarat, Rajasthan, UP; Q1 FY27 revenue ₹1.3 cr [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Customers: domestic module makers; top 10 customers are 65–68% of volume [Q1 call].
- Market (management figures): FY26 installations 44.6 GW AC ≈ 62 GW DC of modules needing ~11,000 TPD of glass vs domestic glass capacity 2,600 TPD (18 GW); imports ~70% of use; domestic capacity to reach ~7,700 TPD (51 GW) by Mar-27, mostly captive to large module makers [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Protection: anti-dumping duty on Chinese and Vietnamese solar glass since Dec-24 (five years); 9.71% countervailing duty on Malaysian glass extended five years by notification of 2-Jun-26 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].

- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM: Jun-25 ₹347 cr / ₹63 cr / 18% (includes German losses until July); Sep-25 ₹379 / ₹118 / 31%; Dec-25 ₹390 / ₹123 / 31%; Mar-26 ₹440 / ₹136 / 31%; Jun-26 ₹406 / ₹127 / 31% [Screener].
- Quarterly net profit: –₹203 cr (₹326 cr write-off of the German exposure), ₹62 cr, ₹100 cr, ₹169 cr (includes ~₹75 cr tax write-back on that write-off), ₹87 cr [Screener; Q1 call].
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹1,371 cr, OP ₹58 cr (4%), PAT –₹50 cr; FY25 ₹1,476 cr, ₹58 cr (4%), –₹87 cr; FY26 ₹1,556 cr, ₹440 cr (28%), ₹127 cr; TTM ₹1,615 cr, ₹504 cr (31%), ₹418 cr [Screener]. TTM profit is inflated by the Q4 tax credit.
- ROCE –4% (FY24, FY25) → 25% (FY26) [Screener].
- Unit economics: average ex-factory price ₹113.4/mm·m² (FY25) → ₹146.7 (FY26) → ₹160.3 in Q1 FY27 (includes a ₹9.50 fuel surcharge from 10-Mar-26, now being reduced); volumes +8% in FY26 and +8% YoY in Q1; Q1 production 125 lakh m² [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Standalone EBITDA margin 35.0% in Q1 FY27 vs 27.9% a year earlier — the company's EBITDA includes other income; Screener's OPM is 31% [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- Q4 FY26 sales included ₹32 cr of goods dispatched earlier but delivered in Q4 (Ind AS timing); management said the normal run-rate is ₹400–410 cr a quarter [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 promises:** EBITDA margin 30–33% "barring any unforeseen circumstances"; quarterly sales run-rate ₹400–410 cr; rooftop ~₹75 cr in year one; 600 TPD expansion (SG-4, SG-5, ₹950 cr) with first furnace ~Jan-27, revenue "sensibly" from FY28; tax ~21%; no need to use the ₹750 cr equity enabling resolution [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** sales ₹406 cr — **in line**; margin 35% — **beat** (helped by the surcharge and a ₹6 cr+ quarter of wind-solar power savings); rooftop target **cut** to ₹36 cr for FY27; tax rate guidance **raised** to ~25.2%; expansion "construction by December '26, commission… January to March '27", model revenue from 1-Apr-27 — **kept then** [Q1 call].
- **After Q1 (22-Sep-26 board):** expansion cost **raised by ₹150 cr to ₹1,100 cr** (scope added) and commissioning moved to "end of March 2027", citing the Middle East conflict; funded from internal funds with no new borrowing — **delayed and costlier** [BSE board outcome 22-Sep-26].
- Longer term: grow revenue from ~₹2,500 cr (post-expansion run-rate) to "at least INR4,000 crores" over 3–4 years; decision on a further glass furnace or an adjacent glass product within ~6 months [Q1 call].
- Ind-Ra (Dec-25) expected only nominal FY27 revenue growth because the new capacity arrives at the end of FY27 [Ind-Ra Dec-25].

## Capex and capacity
- SG-4 + SG-5 (2 × 300 TPD) at the existing site: +60% capacity to 1,600 TPD; now ₹1,100 cr; management sizes the gain at ~₹80–85 cr of extra EBITDA a quarter at current prices [22-Sep-26 filing; Q1 call]. At ₹1,100 cr for ~₹330 cr a year of EBITDA, simple payback is roughly 3–4 years at today's protected prices.
- **SG-1/SG-2 rebuild:** about ₹100 cr, a ~90-day shutdown (75 days down, 15 to restart) of the rebuilt furnace(s), timing Q4 FY27 to Q2 FY28 depending on furnace condition; a 4-day patch on SG-1 already cost ~₹3 cr of output [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Solar-wind hybrid captive power (commissioned Mar-26): renewables 93% of power; ~₹18 cr a year saving [Q1 call].
- Funding: fresh equity of ₹889 cr — Feb-25 preferential issue (promoters ₹100 cr equity; non-promoter warrants ₹417.7 cr at ₹530 each, balance due by 13-Aug-26) and an Oct-25 promoter preferential issue of ₹371.5 cr; ₹286 cr unspent in money-market funds at Jun-26 [IP Q1FY27]. 53.4 lakh warrant shares were allotted on 14-Aug-26 and 3.1 lakh on 15-Jul-26 [News: SolarQuarter/Investywise, Jul–Aug 2026].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings fell from ₹576 cr (FY24) to ₹252 cr (FY25) and ₹162 cr (FY26); investments rose to ₹606 cr (mostly unspent issue money) [Screener].
- CFO ₹423 cr and FCF ₹226 cr in FY26 (FY25: ₹100 cr / –₹8 cr) [Screener].
- Inventory days 115, debtor days 28, cash conversion cycle 96 days in FY26 (174 in FY25) [Screener]; management calls working capital "about 60 days" [Q1 call].
- Ind-Ra: net debt/EBITDA 0.4x in 1H FY26 (3.1x FY25), interest cover 23x [Ind-Ra Dec-25].
- No dividend [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- First mover (since FY10) and largest single-location capacity in India; other large players started in FY24 [Ind-Ra Dec-25].
- Customer stickiness: high-speed module lines "cannot take risks with new suppliers"; the company sells its entire output and cannot serve some past customers [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Cost: 93% renewable power; old furnaces fully depreciated [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- But pricing is set by the landed cost of Chinese imports plus duties; Ind-Ra flags "limited bargaining power; dependence on government incentives" [Ind-Ra Dec-25]. Management: "We are already at almost peak of the pricing" [Q4 call].
- Threats named by management: a Chinese-owned 1,500 TPD plant in Indonesia (not yet a price threat), a UAE plant held up by the Hormuz situation, Sisecam (Turkey) blocked by freight [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Policy dependence:** margins jumped from 4% to 31% purely after anti-dumping duties; any dilution of ADD/CVD, or a new low-cost source (Indonesia), would hit realisations [Ind-Ra Dec-25; Q4 call].
- **Captive capacity wave:** 33 GW of new domestic glass capacity by Mar-27, mostly captive — shrinks the open market just as BRL adds 600 TPD [Q4 call].
- **Execution:** expansion now ₹150 cr over budget and later; plus a ~₹100 cr, ~90-day rebuild of old furnaces likely in the first half of FY28, cutting FY28 volumes [22-Sep-26 filing; Q1 call].
- Customer concentration: top 10 = 65–68% of volume; module-maker consolidation expected [Q1 call].
- Dilution: promoter holding 61.93% (Jun-25) → 56.01% (Aug-26) after warrant conversions to non-promoters [Screener; News].
- German exit: ₹325.9 cr written off; the insolvency administrator reported creditors unlikely to receive anything [Q4 call].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter only on revenue cut-off (timing of transfer of control) — relevant given the ₹32 cr Q4 timing effect [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report].
- Contingent liabilities small: disputed taxes ~₹14 cr, bank guarantees ₹54.3 cr, supplier LCs ₹12.2 cr; an old ₹19.5 cr AY2016-17 tax demand was quashed by the Bombay HC [AR FY26 Note 40].
- Related party: new royalty of ₹1.4 cr and expense reimbursements of ₹2.3 cr paid to group company Borosil Limited in FY26 — small [AR FY26 Note 44].
- Rooftop diversification is a low-margin (single-digit) trading business and a distraction risk [Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: expansion and a branded rooftop push ("₹75 crores… first year"), margin guide 30–33%. Q1: rooftop cut to ₹36 cr, focus back on glass; margin 35% on surcharge; ALMM-III (wafers/ingots from June 2028) and the Malaysian CVD extension added as tailwinds.
- New issue in Q1: furnace rebuild of ~90 days; an analyst asked about "articles regarding strategic investment" — management declined to comment [Q1 call].
- After the calls: ₹150 cr cost overrun and slip to end-March 2027 (22-Sep-26); warrant money (~₹212 cr for the Aug-26 tranche at ₹397.50 each) received.

## My read
The business has been transformed by duties: margins went from 4% to 31%, debt is nearly gone, and the plant sells everything it makes. Growth now depends on SG-4/SG-5 arriving by end-March 2027 (already ₹150 cr over budget) and on prices holding while captive glass capacity triples and old furnaces go down for a rebuild in FY28 — so FY27 is flat-ish and FY28 has both a +60% capacity step and a ~90-day hole. The stock at about 18x trailing earnings (TTM flattered by a one-off tax credit) prices in some normalisation; the key test is whether realisations hold near ₹150/m² once the fuel surcharge goes and captive capacity lands. What would change the view: first-furnace firing on time, clarity on the next project (another furnace vs a new product) within six months, and any change to ADD/CVD.
