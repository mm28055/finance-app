## Business and segments
- Pune (Chakan) builder of turnkey factory automation lines — body-in-white (BIW) welding lines, assembly lines, special-purpose machines, conveyors, test stations — for auto/EV OEMs and, increasingly, defence (ammunition lines), battery-energy-storage (BESS) "data centre" container lines, heavy fabrication, railways [H1 call; IP H2FY26].
- Mix: H1 FY26 non-automotive 51.4% vs 10.95% in FY25 (per MD); order book at May-26 ~60–62% automotive, ~38–40% non-automotive; within auto, EV ~33% of business [H1 call; H2 call]. BESS lines were ">15%" of H1 revenue [H1 call].
- Pricing of a BESS line ₹15–30 cr; delivery 4.5–6 months; claims to be 20–25% cheaper than Chinese lines [H2 call].
- Capacity: ~2 lakh sq ft at Chakan (two old units + 59,000 sq ft new facility inaugurated Nov/Dec 2025), 15,000 sq ft rented unit at Faridabad (Apr 2026), 160-seat design centre in Pune; total revenue capacity ~₹250–300 cr; FY26 utilisation ~85% [IP H2FY26; H2 call].
- Subsidiaries (acquired 19 Sep 2025, 60% each): Pentaco Automation (powertrain lines) for ₹3.00 cr and MII Robotics (defence/government automation) for ₹0.60 cr; goodwill ₹3.29 cr; both now operate inside Patil's plant [AR FY26 consolidation notes; H1 call].
- FY26 revenue by entity: Patil standalone ₹157 cr, Pentaco ~₹18 cr, MII Robotics ~₹2.3 cr [H2 call].
- >60% repeat business in automotive; exports only via Indian OEMs' overseas plants [H2 call].
- Competitors: Wipro PARI (~₹2,000 cr), Comau, Jendamark, Faith Automation and many small firms [H2 call].

## Guidance history
- **H1 FY26 call (20 Nov 2025):** FY26 revenue ₹150–170 cr (**delivered** — ₹167 cr consolidated per Screener, ₹172.8 cr total income per company); FY27 ₹250–260 cr from the two Chakan facilities, with subsidiaries possibly on top (~₹50 cr); EBITDA margin "a little bit more" than ~13–17%; no plan for another expansion [H1 call].
- **Jan 2026 investor meet:** FY27 "250 plus crores"; margin to 14%; no dilution planned [Jan meet].
- **H2 FY26 call (12 May 2026):** FY27 revenue ₹260–270 cr (group); PAT "10 plus up to 11"%; FY28 ₹380–385 cr (needs ~₹85 cr of new capacity, decision in 4–5 months); >₹700 cr group revenue by 2030; each subsidiary to reach ₹100 cr+ "in future"; working-capital cycle 90–110 days; ₹18.5 cr of IPO money to be spent in 3–4 months [H2 call].
- Track record: FY26 revenue promise met; the FY27 target was restated as a group number (H1 implied ~₹300 cr incl. subsidiaries, May said ₹260–270 cr group) — effectively **kept-to-slightly-cut**; order book fell from ₹170 cr (Jan) to ₹118 cr (May) even as the target rose. **No H1 FY27 results yet.**

## Capex and capacity
- IPO (Sep 2025) raised ~₹69.6 cr: ₹62.0 cr for "working capital expenditure towards setup of new manufacturing facility", ₹4.0 cr debt repayment, ₹0.56 cr general purposes, ₹3.05 cr issue costs; ₹18.47 cr unspent at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 38]. Management said the balance was for mezzanine floors and equipment, to be spent by ~Sep 2026 [H2 call].
- In Sep 2026 the company instead published a notice of a special resolution to **vary the objects/terms of use of the IPO proceeds** (postal ballot/EGM) [NSE filing 12 Sep 2026]. Details of the new use were not in the text-readable part of the filing.
- Building a similar 59,000 sq ft facility would cost ~₹55 cr (land + building) and add ~₹150 cr of revenue capacity [H1 call]. New capacity for FY28 (~₹85 cr of revenue) not yet decided — rented, greenfield, south or north [H2 call].
- Design centre hiring: +38 people so far, 110 more being recruited [H2 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- CFO (standalone): FY24 ₹7 cr, FY25 ₹1 cr, FY26 −₹4 cr; consolidated FY26 CFO −₹1 cr, free cash flow −₹46 cr [Screener].
- Working capital (consolidated FY26): debtor days 115, inventory days 217 (mostly project WIP ~₹57 cr), payable days 143, cash cycle 189 days [Screener; H2 call]. Management says the business runs on 20–40% advances and a 90–110 day cycle [H1 call; H2 call].
- Borrowings ₹21 cr consolidated (₹19 cr standalone, an overdraft against FDs) [Screener; AR FY26 note 39]. Guarantees ₹1.47 cr are the only contingent liability [AR FY26 note 35].
- A ₹6 cr loan from the MD was repaid in FY26 [AR FY26 note 36]. No dividend [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Scale and breadth: claims to be the only mid-size Indian firm doing BIW, assembly, powertrain, defence, BESS and heavy-fabrication lines under one roof, with in-house design, trials and 4–4.5 month delivery vs ~6 months for rivals [H2 call].
- Choice of orders: management says demand exceeds capacity and it picks jobs on margin, delivery time and payment terms [H2 call; Jan meet]. Automotive work faces "pricing pressure due to high volumes" [Jan meet].
- Raw-material risk is modest (mostly Indian parts); price moves above 1–2% are renegotiated [H2 call].
- Limits: no disclosed market share; lines last 6–7 years, so revenue is project-based and must be re-won [H2 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party payments to the promoter family are large**: factory rent ₹2.32 cr each to MD Manoj Patil and director Aarti Patil (₹4.64 cr in total, ~29% of standalone FY26 PAT); salaries MD ₹1.80 cr, Aarti Patil ₹1.68 cr, other relatives ~₹0.9 cr; services from three relative-linked firms (SAP Enterprises ₹1.78 cr, AV Enterprises ₹1.36 cr, Shree Samarth Engineering ₹1.40 cr); a daughter bought a flat from the company for ₹1.02 cr in FY25; Evtric Motors (KMP-interested) repaid a ₹13.7 cr loan in FY25 [AR FY26 note 36].
- **IPO money**: first spent more slowly than promised, now subject to a change of objects [AR FY26 note 38; NSE filing Sep-26].
- **Cash**: negative CFO and 189-day cash cycle; heavy WIP inventory [Screener].
- **Order-book dip**: ₹170 cr (Jan) → ₹118 cr (May) against a ₹260–270 cr FY27 target [Jan meet; H2 call].
- Concentration in auto OEM capex cycles; lumpy dispatches [H1 call].
- Disclosure quality: management often lacked data on calls (geographic split, depreciation, debt) [H2 call; H1 call; Jan meet].
- Auditor: no qualifications; no key audit matters; subsidiaries audited by another firm (S B Ohara & Co) [AR FY26 auditor's report; AGM proceedings Sep-26]. Company secretary changed (Niharika Singhal → Vishakha Pathak) [NSE filings].

## My read
Patil is a small, fast-growing automation integrator that has diversified from auto into defence and battery-storage lines and delivered its FY26 revenue promise. But the numbers need care: operating cash flow has been negative, inventory and receivables are high, the order book shrank between January and May while targets rose, and IPO money has moved slowly and is now being redirected. Promoter-family payments — ₹4.6 cr of factory rent plus ₹3.5 cr of salaries — are large relative to ~₹16 cr of standalone profit. The price needs roughly 30%+ earnings growth for two years (₹260–270 cr in FY27, ₹380 cr in FY28 at 10–11% PAT). Positive cash flow and a rebuilt order book (>₹200 cr) in H1 FY27 would support the story; a miss on H1 revenue or further related-party growth would argue for caution.
