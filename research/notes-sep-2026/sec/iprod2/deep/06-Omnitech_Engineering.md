# Omnitech Engineering — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code OMNI (consolidated). Price ₹534, market cap ₹6,603 cr, 52-week range ₹176–769 (listed 5-Mar-26 at an IPO band of ₹216–227) [Screener, 27-Sep-26; AR FY26].

**Transcript verdicts.** Both stored transcripts (Q1 FY27 call 7-Aug-26; Q4 FY26 call 27-May-26) are the company's own BSE filings on letterhead — **matches official**. (The official Q1 text itself has the CMD misspeaking revenue as "INR116 crores"; the CFO and presentation give ₹166.6 cr.)

## Business and segments
- Rajkot-based build-to-print precision machining (tolerances to 5 microns), sub-assemblies and contract manufacturing for global OEMs; 19 years old, 256+ customers in 24 countries; three plants (Metoda, Chhapara, Padavala) with ~32 lakh machine-hours a year at Jun-26, +20.7% vs FY26 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Revenue by vertical, Q1 FY27: Energy 49% (mostly oilfield services — Weatherford, SLB etc.; pure oil & gas "below 40"), Motion control & automation 24%, Industrial equipment 19%, Others incl. first defence/aerospace articles 7% [Q1 call].
- Geography Q1 FY27: North America 52%, Asia 27%, India 17%, Europe/UK 3%; ~78% exports [Q1 call]. FY26 by customer location: USA ₹263.6 cr, India ₹131.6 cr, UAE ₹78.6 cr, others ₹37.5 cr [AR FY26 Note 30].
- One customer = ₹78.3 cr (15%) of FY26 revenue [AR FY26 Note 30].
- Materials: stainless steel, Inconel (<15%), carbon/low-alloy steel, aluminium, titanium [Q1 call].

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM: Jun-25 ₹103 cr / ₹27 cr / 26%; Sep-25 ₹125 / ₹44 / 35%; Dec-25 ₹134 / ₹51 / 38%; Mar-26 ₹149 / ₹50 / 33%; Jun-26 ₹167 / ₹51 / 30% [Screener].
- Quarterly net profit: ₹5 cr, ₹23 cr, ₹22 cr, ₹29 cr, ₹30 cr [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹178 cr, OP ₹65 cr (36%), PAT ₹19 cr; FY25 ₹343 cr, ₹118 cr (34%), ₹44 cr; FY26 ₹511 cr, ₹171 cr (33%), ₹79 cr; TTM ₹575 cr, ₹195 cr, ₹104 cr [Screener]. 3-yr sales CAGR 42% [Screener].
- ROCE 18–19% (FY24–FY26) [Screener]; company-defined ROCE 13.7% FY26, 17.8% annualised Q1 [Q1 call].
- **Margin trend down:** OPM 38% (Dec-25) → 33% → 30%; raw-material cost rose from ~20% to 24% to 28% of sales over three quarters; gross margin guided at 68–71% [Q1 call; Screener].
- **Accounting change flatters Q1 profit:** depreciation switched from written-down-value to straight-line from FY27 — depreciation fell from ₹13.5 cr (Q4 FY26) to ₹6.3 cr (Q1 FY27) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]. Q1 PBT rose only 2.8% QoQ even with this ~₹7 cr benefit, so underlying profit fell quarter-on-quarter.
- Q1 other income ₹5.8 cr (forex and interest on unspent IPO money) [IP Q1FY27].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 promises:** FY27 revenue growth 30–35% ("as per previous years"); EBITDA 30–35%; working-capital days (294) to come down via inventory, receivable and payable levers; Weatherford MPA (>₹900 cr, 5 years, ~₹184 cr/yr at maturity) and a second ~₹1,000 cr oilfield order to start in FY27; new Chhapara capacity to start ramping "from next year, after the Q1"; AS9100 done, Nadcap started, four defence/aerospace first-article orders [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** revenue +61.5% YoY (+12% QoQ) — **ahead**; EBITDA margin 30.4% — **at the bottom of the band**; NWC days 294 → 233 (inventory 225 → 182, receivables 153 → 119) — **delivered**; growth guidance restated as 35–40% YoY — **nudged up**; margin "30% and above" [Q1 call].
- Big orders: only "INR50 crores and more" of the two mega-orders expected this year — a slow ramp [Q1 call].
- Capex: ₹250 cr at Chhapara (₹100 cr buildings, ₹150 cr machines, of which ₹25 cr in the existing Chhapara plant); 1–1.5 months behind due to monsoon, some spill to FY28; new plants operational in FY28 ("over the 14 months") — **slipped slightly** [Q1 call].
- Capacity: existing plants can do ~₹800–900 cr of revenue; after capex roughly double (analyst's ₹1,600 cr peak — "roughly… similar") [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- IPO (Feb-26): ₹583 cr total, ₹418 cr fresh issue, ₹165 cr offer for sale by promoters [AR FY26]. ₹50 cr of long-term debt repaid; rest mainly for Chhapara plants [Q1 call].
- Machine-hours 26.5 lakh (FY26) → 32 lakh (Jun-26) → 42–43 lakh after the new plants [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]. Land also bought in Ahmedabad (future) and a small leased unit in Hyderabad for defence customers [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Capital commitments ₹73.0 cr at Mar-26 (₹23.6 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 Note 29].
- Target asset turn 2–2.5x and ROCE "more than 20" at maturity [Q1 call]. 1.2 MW rooftop solar running [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **No free cash flow yet:** CFO –₹69 cr (FY25) and –₹2 cr (FY26); FCF –₹144 cr and –₹129 cr [Screener].
- Borrowings ₹435 cr at Mar-26 [Screener]; total debt ~₹390 cr after IPO repayment; cash ₹133.8 cr at Jun-26 (₹163 cr at Mar-26); net debt/equity 0.34 → 0.41 [Q1 call]. Borrowings carry personal guarantees of directors and a relative [AR FY26 borrowings note].
- Working capital: NWC 233 days (Jun-26); CRISIL put gross current assets at 362 days (Mar-25) and bank-line use ~88% [Q1 call; CRISIL Apr-26]. Screener's inventory-days metric (946) is inflated by its raw-material-only denominator; the company's own figure is 182 days [Screener; Q1 call].
- GST refund of ₹24.3 cr due in FY27 [Q4 call]. No dividend [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Order book grew from ₹57 cr (FY23) → ₹83 cr (FY24) → ₹283 cr (FY25) → ~₹3,000 cr (May-26) → ₹3,055 cr (31-Jul-26); Energy 77% of the book [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Multi-year supply agreements with oilfield majors (Weatherford >US$100 mn) — these book supply chains behind their own Aramco/ADNOC/Petrobras contracts [Q4 call].
- Qualification/stickiness: >90% of motion-control and industrial revenue from repeat customers; AS9100 certified; Nadcap in process; approved at ABB, Siemens, Oshkosh [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Pricing: material and forex pass-through with a 2–3-month lag via quarterly reviews; no energy pass-through clause [Q1 call]. CRISIL: "limited bargaining power against original equipment manufacturers" [CRISIL Apr-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Concentration:** Energy 77% of order book, North America ~64% of it; one customer 15% of revenue [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; AR FY26].
- **Order-book quality:** the two big contracts are master purchase agreements with schedules, not firm call-offs; management itself cites a slow ₹50 cr first-year ramp [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Depreciation-method change** boosts reported profit from FY27; compare like-for-like [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Auditor: unmodified; key audit matter on revenue cut-off ("incentives or external pressures to meet expectations"); **audit-trail exception** — auditors could not confirm the database-level edit log for the third-party stock-records software (important for an inventory-heavy business) [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Related parties: promoter-family members lend to the company and are paid interest; amounts are a few crore, small against ~₹390 cr of debt (the note's columns are hard to read) [AR FY26 Note 34].
- Contingent liabilities negligible (₹0.2 cr GST) [AR FY26 Note 29].
- Execution: two plants plus Hyderabad at once; monsoon delay already [Q1 call].
- Short listing history (since Mar-26); exchange sought clarification on unusual volume on 21-Sep-26 — company said no undisclosed information [BSE announcement via Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: order-book story and margin dip explanations (450 bp gross-margin drop). Q1: execution, working-capital release, capacity numbers; growth guide nudged to 35–40%.
- New in Q1: monsoon delay to Chhapara; straight-line depreciation; Ahmedabad land; energy share of revenue down to 49%.
- Net debt/equity rose (0.34 → 0.41) as cash was used for capex.

## Best management quotes
- "we are projecting around 35% to 40%" — on year-on-year growth [Q1 call]
- "roughly around you can say like INR800 crores to INR900 crores roughly we can do from the existing" — capacity [Q1 call]
- "there will be a some slightly spillover in FY28 due to some recent rainy seasons" [Q1 call]

## My read
Omnitech has real momentum — revenue up 61% YoY, an order book over 5x trailing sales, and qualification with demanding oilfield and industrial OEMs — and working capital improved in Q1 as promised. But margins have slid three quarters in a row, Q1 profit growth depended on a depreciation-method change, the business has not yet produced free cash, and the book is 77% energy with the mega-orders ramping slowly. At about 64x trailing earnings the price needs the 35–40% growth to hold for several years with ~30% margins and the Chhapara plants arriving on time. What would change the view: positive operating cash flow in FY27, margins stabilising at 30%+, and firm call-offs under the two master agreements; further margin slippage or capex delays would argue for a lower estimate.
