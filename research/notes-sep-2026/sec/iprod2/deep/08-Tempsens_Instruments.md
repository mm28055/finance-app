# Tempsens Instruments (India) Ltd — deep research (Phase A1)

Inputs read: stored file (metrics, profile, Q1 FY27 transcript — the **only** call so far; the company listed on 28 Aug 2026, so there is no second transcript); official Q1 FY27 transcript (BSE, 22 Sep 2026); Q1 FY27 investor presentation (BSE, 16 Sep 2026); audited FY26 consolidated financial statements (company website — scanned; read auditor's report, note 35 contingencies, related-party note); ICRA rationale (27 Nov 2025); IPO press/notes; Screener consolidated page (raw HTML tables).

## Business and segments
- Udaipur-based maker of engineered-to-order "thermal loop" products: temperature sensors (thermocouples, RTDs, infrared pyrometers, thermal imagers), specialised cables (high-temperature instrumentation/control, mineral-insulated metal-sheath, nickel-alloy conductors) and electrical heating (process/component heaters, lab and process furnaces, now medium-voltage heaters) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- FY26 revenue mix by vertical: temperature sensing 44.6%, specialised cables 34.7%, electrical heating 20.7% (heating was 3.9% in FY24, before the Marathon Heater amalgamation) [IP Q1FY27].
- FY26 end-markets: petrochemical 21.5%, metal 19.7%, power 12.3%, trader 9.6%, manufacturer 9.4%, defence & nuclear 7.0%, glass 6.7%, plastic 3.9%, others 10.0%; no end-market above 22% [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- FY26 geography: India 71.5%, Europe 9.7%, Asia-Pacific 6.7%, Africa & MENA 10.0%, Americas ~2.1%; exports 28.5% of product revenue [IP Q1FY27]. Q1 FY27 exports 34% (₹39.8 cr, +78%) vs ~25% a year ago, helped by newly consolidated Tempsens Germany and Tempsens Polska [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Business type: projects/OEM 67.6%, replacement (MRO) 32.5% of FY26 revenue [IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 vertical revenue: sensing ₹60.3 cr (+42%), cables ₹32.5 cr (−8.8%, export timing), heating ₹25.0 cr (+149% on a low base; heater plant started end-Q1 FY26) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Market position (Frost & Sullivan, commissioned for the IPO): largest Indian maker of contact and non-contact temperature sensors, ~10.5% share in contact and ~21.3% in non-contact; the only Indian maker of non-contact sensors [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- 15 manufacturing units (5 abroad), 83 R&D staff, 12 patents; certifications UL, ATEX, IECEx, PESO, ASME U/R stamps [IP Q1FY27].
- Utilisation of existing capacity ~80%, which management treats as practical full [Q1 call].
- Group structure: Marathon Heater (India) amalgamated w.e.f. 1 Apr 2024, which brought a 50.01% stake in Pyrosens Technologies (non-contact sensors); JVs in Indonesia (PT Tempsens Asia Jaya), Germany (Tempsens Instruments GmbH), Korea; new subsidiaries Tempsens Polska, Tempsens Measurement & Control; Victura Tempsens JV for automotive heating cables [ICRA Nov-25; IP Q1FY27].

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Annual — FY23: sales 240, operating profit 44 (18%), PAT 32 | FY24: 275, 55 (20%), 41 | FY25: 379, 91 (24%), 63 | FY26: 445, 101 (23%), 71 [Screener].
- Quarterly — Q1 FY26: sales 89, OP 21 (23%), PAT 14 | Q4 FY26: 133, 29 (22%), 21 | Q1 FY27: 119, 24 (20%), 16 [Screener].
- The company's "EBITDA" **includes other income**: FY26 company EBITDA ₹113.2 cr (24.8% of total income) vs Screener operating profit ₹101 cr + other income ₹13 cr; Q1 FY27 company EBITDA ₹26.6 cr (22.0%) vs Screener OP ₹24 cr (20%) [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹118.7 cr (+33.4%); gross margin 47.4% vs 49.3% in Q1 FY26 (FY26 full year 46%); employee cost ₹20.4 cr (+45%), including a ₹0.57 cr ESOP charge; PAT ₹16.3 cr (+15.6%); adjusted PAT (ex-acquisition amortisation) ₹17.3 cr [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- FY25's jump (revenue +38%) was largely the Marathon amalgamation, which added ₹70–75 cr of FY25 revenue; organic growth was lower [ICRA Nov-25].
- Growth was 27% CAGR FY24–26 (company) and TTM revenue +18% (Screener, based on the few quarters available); profit TTM +10% [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- Returns: Screener ROE 14% last year, 17% 3-year average; ROCE fell 36% (FY22) → 23% (FY25) → 18% (FY26), partly because the amalgamation added ~₹106 cr of goodwill/intangibles and equity [Screener; IP Q1FY27]. Company's "adjusted ROE" (excluding intangibles) 23.1% FY26 [IP Q1FY27].
- Unit economics: products are <1% of a plant's cost but safety-critical; inputs are booked when an order is received, so metal price moves are passed through [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- There is only one call. On it (17 Sep 2026) management guided FY27 revenue growth "in line with our blended growth over the last 3 years" (~25–27%; the CFO accepted an analyst's "about 25%") and EBITDA margin "broadly similar to last year" (company-defined ~24.8%) [Q1 call].
- Margin recovery promised in H2 FY27 ("in '27 also, you can see the recovery of the margin in next half year") and profit growth to catch up with revenue growth by year-end [Q1 call].
- OEM plus new products to exceed 25% of revenue from FY28 (OEMs are "insignificant" in FY26 revenue) [Q1 call].
- Four new facilities with ₹500 cr aggregate peak revenue potential between Q4 FY27 and Q3 FY28; full use "by FY29 end, FY starting '30", broadly [Q1 call]. The company stresses this is "neither a forecast nor guidance" [Q1 call].
- Q1 delivered revenue ahead of the guided pace (+33%) but margins below (company EBITDA 22.0% vs 25.3%), so the full-year margin promise depends on H2 [IP Q1FY27]. **Track record: too early to judge.**

## Capex and capacity
- Unit 1 brownfield, 50,000 sq ft, OEM contact sensors: commissioning Q3 FY28, peak revenue ~₹120 cr [Q1 call].
- Unit 6, 50,000 sq ft, medium-voltage heaters up to 15 MW (for green hydrogen, carbon capture, battery storage): Q1 FY28, peak ~₹200 cr; explosion-proof certification targeted Q4 FY27 [Q1 call].
- Unit 8 greenfield, specialised cables: Q2 FY28, peak ~₹60 cr; land bought in Q1 FY27 for ~₹25 cr from internal accruals [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Victura Tempsens JV: agreement to buy an 80,000 sq ft site with a shed; production from Q4 FY27, peak ~₹120 cr; electrical heating cables for commercial-vehicle emission systems [Q1 call].
- Also ~25,000 sq ft for custom furnaces this year; 5-year private-label furnace supply contract with a life-sciences OEM; EDGE pyrometer range co-developed with Micro-Epsilon, dispatches from Q3 FY27, addressing a ~₹3,000 cr market where Tempsens has <1% [Q1 call].
- Rupee capex for the four plants is **not disclosed**. IPO fresh issue earmarked only ₹18.1 cr for capex in heating and cables [News: Sushil Finance IPO note, Aug-26]. Contracted capital commitments were just ₹1.3 cr at Mar-26 (₹2.6 cr at Mar-25) [AR FY26 note 35].
- Implied asset turn: ₹500 cr peak revenue on undisclosed capex; FY26 fixed assets were ₹299 cr (incl. intangibles) for ₹445 cr revenue [Screener].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹30 cr (FY24) → ₹72 cr (FY25, after taking on Marathon's debt and promoter loans) → ₹78 cr (FY26) [Screener]. ₹55 cr of the ₹95 cr IPO fresh issue has repaid debt; finance cost to fall from Q2 [Q1 call].
- Cash & bank ₹84.4 cr at Mar-25; working-capital limits ~56% used [ICRA Nov-25]. ICRA FY25 metrics: TOL/TNW 0.2x, debt/OPBDITA 0.8x, interest cover 32.9x [ICRA Nov-25].
- CFO: FY23 22, FY24 37, FY25 54, FY26 42 (63% of operating profit) [Screener].
- Working capital is heavy and worsening: inventory days 125 (FY24) → 157 → 170 (FY26); cash-conversion cycle 151 → 193 → 210 days; debtor days 70 [Screener]. Inventory ₹110–120 cr, 65–70% raw material [Q1 call]. ICRA also flags "high working capital intensity" (35.4% of revenue in FY25) [ICRA Nov-25].
- Dividend: small interim dividend in FY26 (payout 4%) [Screener; AR FY26 auditor's report].

## Moat evidence
- Qualification barriers: first approval with a critical-industry customer takes 3–5 years; later products are easier. A PSU that began buying sensors in 2004 now buys cables, pyrometers, furnace cameras and heaters; revenue from it rose to ₹15.8 cr in FY26 from ₹12.4 cr in FY24 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Cross-selling: ₹267 cr (60% of FY26 revenue) came from customers buying more than one vertical, up from ₹158 cr (57.6%) in FY24 [IP Q1FY27].
- Low concentration: top-10 customers 18.6% of FY26 revenue (24.7% in FY24); top-20 customers average 10 years with the company; 3,800+ customers [IP Q1FY27].
- Backward integration into alloys and mineral-insulated cable used across all three verticals — "amongst the few companies worldwide" [Q1 call].
- Gross margin steady at 46–47% over FY25–FY26 [Q1 call]. Management estimates 75–90% of revenue comes from "critical" applications with limited competition; ordinary plastics-type uses face many rivals [Q1 call].
- Counterpoint: ICRA says the steady consumable demand means "low entry barriers" and stiff export competition gave "modest export growth" [ICRA Nov-25].

## Risks and red flags
- **Valuation reset risk**: IPO priced at ₹300 (₹650 cr issue, of which ₹555 cr was an offer for sale), listed on 28 Aug 2026 at ~₹587 on NSE (some reports say ₹634), now ₹531 (52-week range ₹505–635); subscription reported at 128x (Chittorgarh) to 184x (other press) [News: Chittorgarh, Aug-26; Screener].
- **Sellers**: the OFS was by five individuals — Amit, Puneet, Chandra Prakash and Ankit Talesara and Nirmal Kumar Pande — not the Rathi managing family [News: Sushil Finance IPO note]. Promoter-group holding fell from 80.5% to 65.7% through the issue [News: Chittorgarh]. Ankit Talesara remains a non-executive director [IP Q1FY27].
- **Working capital**: 210-day cash cycle and falling CFO/OP (90% FY24 → 63% FY26) [Screener].
- **Margin dip**: employee cost +45% YoY in Q1 as the company hires ahead of plants; newly acquired European units have "lower revenue base while adding employee cost" [Q1 call].
- **Execution**: four plants in 12 months, medium-voltage heater depends on certification by Q4 FY27; OEM orders not quantified ("work in progress") [Q1 call].
- **Litigation**: a subsidiary faces a civil proceeding of ₹11.04 cr (≈15.5% of FY26 PAT) per the RHP [News: Sushil Finance IPO note]. Group contingent liabilities are small: ₹1.09 cr indirect tax and ₹0.65 cr land claim from the Udaipur Collector on ex-Marathon land [AR FY26 note 35].
- **Auditor**: Walker Chandiok (unmodified opinion; FY25 audited jointly with predecessor Bansilal Shah & Co.). Only exception: accounting-software audit trail was not enabled at database level for the parent and 2 subsidiaries [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Related parties**: FY26 sales to JVs PT Tempsens Asia Jaya ₹6.6 cr and Tempsens GmbH ₹4.8 cr; purchases from Pyrotech Electronics ₹4.1 cr (₹0.5 cr in FY25); promoter pay ₹2.6 cr (Virendra and Vinay Rathi), plus ₹1.1 cr to Aryan Rathi and ₹0.6 cr to Sonal Rathi; promoter-family loans of ₹4.9 cr repaid in FY26 [AR FY26 related-party note].
- **ESOPs**: 3.47 lakh options granted Sep–Oct 2025 mostly at ₹123 (vs ₹300 IPO price) [AR FY26 note 36].
- Geopolitics: ~10% exposure to Africa/MENA and petrochemicals; management sees no near-term hit [Q1 call].

## My read
Tempsens is a genuinely niche, well-qualified business growing ~25% a year with 46–47% gross margins and low customer concentration, but it has just listed at a rich price and is spending ahead of growth. The two weak spots are cash — a 210-day cash cycle and CFO of only 63% of operating profit in FY26 — and returns, which have fallen from 26% ROCE (FY24) to 18% as goodwill from the Marathon amalgamation and acquisitions came in. The price needs roughly 25% growth to hold for several years, which depends on four new plants (₹500 cr peak revenue) filling from FY28 and OEM business becoming >25% of sales. H2 FY27 margins returning to ~24–25% (company basis) and a visible cut in inventory days would support the case; a second quarter of 20% operating margin or slipping plant dates would argue for caution.
