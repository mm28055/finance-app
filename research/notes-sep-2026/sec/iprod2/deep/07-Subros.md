## Business and segments
- India's largest auto air-conditioning maker (compressors, HVAC modules, condensers, hoses/tubes) — a JV of the Suri family (36.79%), Denso (20%) and Suzuki (11.96%); technology from Denso [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- Share of business Q1 FY27: 41% in passenger cars, 41% in truck AC/blower, 16% in bus AC [Q1 call].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹1,032 cr: Maruti AC products ₹695 cr, ECM products ~₹135 cr, other customers (Mahindra, Renault etc.) ₹200 cr+; by segment: passenger vehicles ~₹930 cr, trucks ₹75 cr (+77% YoY), buses ₹12 cr (+6%) [Q1 call].
- **Related-party concentration:** sales to related parties (chiefly Maruti Suzuki, a related party through Suzuki's stake and common directors) were **82% of FY26 sales** (85% in FY25) [AR FY26 BRSR]. Shareholders approved Maruti transactions of up to ₹3,048 cr for FY26 [AR FY26].
- Hybrid/EV/CNG thermal systems ~25% of revenue [Q1 call].
- Capacity: HVAC ~2.6 million units across nine plants, hoses/tubes 2.3 mn, compressors 2.2 mn (Noida fully used), heat exchangers 2.4–2.5 mn [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 promises:** FY27 "more pressurised"; aim to hold current EBITDA with "moderate improvement" in absolute terms; double-digit margin aspiration "remains intact" long-term; truck AC ₹325–350 cr (from ₹263 cr); Kharkhoda SOP by end-Q2 FY27; e-compressor operational Q3 FY28; wage increases to be "substantially" compensated by customers; OCF/EBITDA to recover toward 70–75% [Q4 call official].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** absolute EBITDA fell 0.8% YoY — **missed** "moderate improvement"; truck AC target **cut** to ~₹300 cr for FY27 (₹400–450 cr in 2–3 years); Kharkhoda SOP **slipped** to Q3 FY27; double-digit (12%) margin aspiration **pushed out** ("we are not seeing in a short term moving to the double digit"); company-level revenue guidance **withdrawn** ("difficult to provide any number"), will track industry — now "moderate single digits"; wage compensation talks ongoing with "positive signals"; e-compressor SOPs **kept** (Nov-2027, Feb-2028, then a third) [Q1 call official].
- Railways: ₹31 cr firm order + ₹50 cr four-year AMC; target >₹100 cr railway revenue in 3 years [Q1 call]. E-compressor to reach company-level margins around FY28–29 [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- **Kharkhoda (Haryana)** greenfield to follow Maruti's Sonipat plant: ₹150 cr; phase 1 ~4.75 lakh units of HVAC and hose/tube, phase 2 another ~4.75 lakh; ₹200–250 cr of revenue at full use within ~2 years of SOP; SOP now Q3 FY27 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Karsanpura (Gujarat)** e-compressor plant plus ICE compressor capacity: ~₹175 cr; equipment ready by Mar-27; three-phase localisation — import content 80–85% at start, 55% in phase 2, 70% localisation at the end; backed by a ₹1,200 cr Maruti e-compressor order over ~7 years (~₹250 cr a year at peak); technology assistance agreement signed with Denso and Toyota Industries in Aug-26 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- A further plant near Maruti's planned 1-million-unit Sanand-area plant is being studied [Q1 call].
- Funding: "long-term funding" approvals for Kharkhoda and Karsanpura; internal accruals for the rest [Q1 call]. Borrowings still small (₹75 cr at Mar-26) [Screener].
- The auditor flags ₹31.1 cr of internal development cost capitalised in FY26 as intangible assets under development (project-linked know-how and payroll) [AR FY26 KAM].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- CFO ₹175 cr (FY25) → ₹105 cr (FY26); FCF ₹58 cr → –₹42 cr [Screener]. Causes: stopped using customer early-payment (cash discount) facility, receivables +₹124 cr; inventory up as import lead times stretched from 25–28 to 45–60 days [Q4 call official].
- Debtor days 49 → 56, cash conversion cycle 21 → 34 days [Screener].
- Near debt-free (borrowings ₹75 cr, investments ₹143 cr); dividend payout ~12% [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- ~41% share in car AC and truck AC; Denso technology and Suzuki/Toyota links; the only Indian e-compressor localisation programme with Denso/Toyota Industries [Q1 call].
- Truck AC mandate for N2/N3 trucks (from Jun-25) grew truck revenue 111% in FY26 and 77% in Q1 FY27; market ₹600–700 cr now, ~₹800 cr later; competitors include Mahle, Sanden, Air International [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Weak pricing power: cost increases pass through only with a lag and at cost (no markup), so margins fall when inputs rise; sudden wage jumps are not in the formula [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration:** 82% of sales to related parties (Maruti) [AR FY26].
- **Margin erosion:** fourth straight quarter below expectations; wage hikes of ~30–32% (Haryana) and ~26–28% (UP) in Q1; aspiration of 12% pushed out after "2 to 2.5 years" of slippage [Q1 call official].
- Growth lagging the customer: Q1 production-based industry growth ~16.8–20% vs Subros +17.5%; model mix may favour competitors on some Maruti models [Q1 call].
- **Contingent liabilities rose to ₹79.5 cr** (₹47.8 cr a year earlier): customs ₹37.6 cr (₹7.8 cr), GST ₹20.2 cr (₹1.3 cr), income tax ₹11.1 cr, workmen claims ₹6.9 cr [AR FY26 Note 26].
- Royalty ₹54.6 cr in FY26 (₹50.8 cr) — technology payments to the collaborator [AR FY26 other expenses note].
- Auditor: unmodified; key audit matter on capitalising development costs (judgement on future viability) [AR FY26].
- Import dependence and freight disruption (West Asia) until localisation is achieved [Q4 call].
- Credit rating: an ICRA rationale dated 27-Mar-26 is listed on Screener but its text could not be retrieved (page renders by script) — not used.

## My read
Revenue is growing well (Q1 +17.5%) on Maruti volumes and the truck-AC mandate, and the e-compressor programme with Denso/Toyota Industries is a genuine long-term content gain. But profit is not following: absolute EBITDA fell in Q1, margins have drifted from 10% to 8.5%, cash flow halved in FY26, and management has withdrawn guidance and pushed out its 12% margin goal. At about 27x trailing earnings the price assumes margins recover toward 10% as wage and commodity costs are recovered and Kharkhoda/e-compressor start on time. What would change the view: evidence of customer compensation for the wage hikes in Q2/Q3 (margin back above 9.5%), Kharkhoda SOP in Q3 FY27, and CFO back above ₹200 cr; another margin step-down would argue for a lower estimate.
