# Airfloa Rail Technology Ltd — deep research (Phase A1)

Inputs read: stored file (metrics, profile, H2 FY26 and H1 FY26 transcripts — SME company, half-yearly calls); official H2 FY26 transcript (BSE, filed 8 Jun 2026; the stored copy is this filing) and official H1 FY26 transcript (BSE, filed 24 Nov 2025), both read in full; H2 FY26 investor presentation (BSE); Annual Report FY26 (BSE) — board's reply to auditor, auditor's report and CARO annexure, IPO-utilisation statement, AS-18 related-party note, contingent liabilities; BSE filings of 5, 12 and 14 Sep 2026 (₹31.08 cr LOA; MoU and press release on KIN Railway acquisition); Screener standalone page (raw HTML; consolidated is identical). No credit-rating rationale is listed on Screener; none found.

## Business and segments
- Chennai-based (27 years; SME IPO on BSE in Sep 2025 at ₹140) maker of rolling-stock interiors and components — turnkey interior furnishing, seats, FRP/aluminium composite structures, windows, lighting — for ICF, MCF, RCF, metros (Kolkata, Chennai, Agra-Kanpur, Bangalore), RRTS/Namo Bharat and exports (Sri Lanka DEMU, Mozambique); small aerospace/defence work (simulator parts, T-90 hull assemblies for HVF Avadi, artillery structures) [IP H2FY26; H1 call].
- FY26 revenue split: railways ~72%, aeronautical/defence/others ~28% (per presentation chart); order book 96% railways, 89% government [IP H2FY26].
- Order book ₹469 cr at March-end (₹472 cr a year earlier); ₹486.9 cr by May 2026, of which government ₹415–420 cr and non-government ₹50–55 cr; defence ~₹29 cr [IP H2FY26; H2 call].
- Order inflow FY26 ₹380 cr (FY25 ₹329 cr); bid pipeline ~₹1,200 cr (₹900 cr railways, ₹120 cr metro, ₹60–70 cr HAL simulators); claimed win ratio 20–25% [IP H2FY26; H2 call].
- Capacity: 5 plants (7 units incl. small rented ones), utilisation 90% (FY25 85%, FY24 79%), two shifts since late 2025 [IP H2FY26; H2 call].
- 81% repeat customers; 359 employees [IP H2FY26].
- New: MoU (27 Aug 2026 board) to buy the business of KIN Railway Equipment (Coimbatore) for ₹48 cr via slump sale/asset purchase, funded by debt and accruals, bringing a ₹180 cr order book; subject to due diligence [BSE filing 12 Sep 2026; press release 14 Sep 2026]. ₹31.08 cr LOA from RCF Kapurthala (25 Aug 2026) for Namo Bharat interior furnishing [BSE filing 5 Sep 2026].

## Guidance history
- **H1 FY26 call (18 Nov 2025):** FY26 revenue >₹300 cr (**delivered** ₹319.6 cr); order book of ₹1,000 cr "by March end" (**missed** — ₹469 cr); FY27 revenue ₹550 cr as "bare minimum"; defence revenue ₹100–120 cr in FY27; defence to exceed the rail order book within two years; new 14-acre plant (50,000 sq ft first) by Dec 2026 for ₹20–30 cr [H1 call].
- **H2 FY26 call (3 Jun 2026):** FY27 revenue **cut** to ~₹500 cr with 12–13% PAT margin; the ₹1,000 cr revenue milestone "may extend modestly" because the company asked customers to re-tender loss-making work; yet, asked about FY28, the MD said ₹1,000 cr revenue and ₹150 cr PAT could be achieved "even this year… if everything goes good" — inconsistent with the ₹500 cr guide [H2 call].
- H2 call also: defence FY27 = ₹29 cr in book + ₹60–70 cr hoped from HAL (down from ₹100–120 cr); refurbishment orders of ≥₹100 cr hoped for in FY27; receivable and working-capital cycles to fall to 60–70 days within 3–4 months; ~₹100–110 cr collections by end-June [H2 call].
- Track record: revenue promise kept; order-book, defence and FY27 revenue promises cut or missed. **Guidance: cut.** No H1 FY27 results yet (due after Sep 2026).

## Capex and capacity
- IPO (Sep 2025): ₹91.1 cr fresh issue; ₹59.3 cr went to working capital, ₹6 cr to debt repayment, ₹9.9 cr general purposes; only ₹2.9 cr of the ₹13.7 cr machinery allocation spent by Mar-26 (₹10.8 cr unspent) because Chinese suppliers need Indian end-user certificates for high-precision machines; the company may switch machines [AR FY26 IPO utilisation; H2 call].
- New 14-acre site: 50,000–1 lakh sq ft shed in FY27 for ~₹30–35 cr, term-loan funded; aim to double capacity and cut ~₹20–25 lakh/month of rent by consolidating units [H2 call].
- Defence JV with Big Bang Boom Solutions (anti-drone, autonomous drones, electronic warfare): Airfloa to invest ~₹25 cr; the JV holds the technology and gives Airfloa manufacturing rights; incorporation promised "before June 15, 2026" [H2 call]. H1 call spoke of ₹100–150 cr needed for defence over 12–18 months [H1 call].
- R&D to rise from ~4% to 8–9% of sales in FY27 and to be **capitalised on the balance sheet**, not expensed [H2 call]. Intangible assets of ₹8.4 cr appeared for the first time at Mar-26 [AR FY26 balance sheet].
- Channel-partner model: zonal-railway refurbishment work to be won and executed through regional partners, with Airfloa manufacturing in Chennai [H2 call].

- Trade receivables ₹214.0 cr at Mar-26 (₹127.6 cr at Mar-25) against FY26 revenue ₹319.6 cr [AR FY26; H2 call]. Screener debtor days 244, cash-conversion cycle 264 days, working-capital days 186 [Screener]. Company-reported debtor days 195 (FY25 219) [IP H2FY26].
- Borrowings ₹68 cr at Mar-26 [Screener]; new term/working-capital debt of ~₹120 cr at 8.25% being drawn (₹60 cr sanctioned by June) [H2 call]. Company net debt/equity 0.2x [IP H2FY26].

## Moat evidence

- Named competitors: Kineco, Hindustan Fiberglass Works, DTL Ancillaries, Vibgyor, Universal Engineers; in seating, Tata-Fainsa and a Korean firm [H2 call].

- Advisory bench includes Sudhanshu Mani (led the Train 18/Vande Bharat project) and Tilak Seth (ex-Siemens Mobility) [H2 call; IP H2FY26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Cash**: negative CFO for two years, receivables two-thirds of annual revenue, funding need met by debt; IPO money mostly went into working capital [Screener; AR FY26].
- **Guidance credibility**: ₹1,000 cr order book promised, ₹469 cr delivered; FY27 revenue cut from ₹550 cr to ₹500 cr, while the MD simultaneously floated ₹1,000 cr/₹150 cr PAT [H1 call; H2 call].
- **Accounting choice**: plan to capitalise R&D at 8–9% of sales flatters reported profit; this is unusual for a company with ₹29 cr of defence orders [H2 call].
- **Compliance lapses**: RoC Chennai penalties totalling ~₹0.9 cr (₹17.3 lakh, ₹26.0 lakh, ₹27.1 lakh, ₹19.6 lakh) for CSR non-compliance in FY20–FY23, under appeal; GST demand of ₹20.0 lakh disputed; late RPT disclosure to BSE (fined) and delayed statutory filings noted by the secretarial auditor [AR FY26 CARO; AR FY26 board's reply].
- **Related parties**: promoters D. Venkatesan and D. Manikandan each paid ₹75 lakh plus rent; family rents ~₹0.4 cr; a web of promoter-linked concerns (Airflow Dafeng Rail Equipments, Bharani Engineering, Apex Material Sciences, Nautone, Raghavendra Industries, Starkeon) with advances — e.g., ₹1.21 cr outstanding from Apex Material Sciences; promoter advances of ₹2.6 cr and ₹6.0 cr in FY25 were repaid in FY26 [AR FY26 related-party note].
- Auditor (Varadarajan & Co, a small Chennai firm): no qualification or emphasis of matter; CARO lists the disputes above [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Accounts use Indian GAAP (AS), not Ind AS [AR FY26 notes].
- **Acquisition/JV risk**: ₹48 cr KIN deal and ₹25 cr defence JV on top of ₹30–35 cr plant capex, all debt-funded, while CFO is negative [BSE filings Sep-26; H2 call].
- Concentration: ~89–96% government/railways; tender-driven, lumpy, Q4-heavy [IP H2FY26].

## My read
Airfloa is growing quickly on the back of Indian Railways' coach and metro programmes — revenue up 66% in FY26 — but the quality of that growth is poor so far: operating cash flow was −₹57 cr, receivables equal two-thirds of a year's sales, margins fell 500 bps, and most IPO money went into working capital. Management's forecasts have been unreliable (₹1,000 cr order book promised, ₹469 cr delivered) and it plans to capitalise R&D and add debt for a plant, a defence JV and a ₹48 cr acquisition at the same time. The price needs the ₹500 cr FY27 target and 12–13% PAT margins to be met and sustained. What would change the view: H1 FY27 showing positive operating cash flow and receivables falling toward the promised 60–70 days; another half-year of negative CFO or more guidance cuts would argue strongly for caution.
