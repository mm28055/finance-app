# Industrial products (niche engineering) — India sector research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Scope: the 13 companies in this note — abrasives & ceramics (Carborundum Universal), rail/defence electronics & batteries (HBL Engineering), cryogenic tanks (Inox India), HVAC heat exchangers (KRN), solar glass (Borosil Renewables), precision machining for global OEMs (Omnitech), auto/truck AC (Subros), temperature instrumentation (Tempsens), welding (Ador Welding; Diffusion Engineers in hard-facing/wear parts), rolling-stock interiors (Airfloa Rail), CRGO transformer cores (Vilas Transcore) and factory automation lines (Patil Automation). This is not one industry: the common threads are (a) the Indian capex cycle, (b) government spending on railways, defence and the power grid, (c) metal and energy input costs, and (d) working capital. Company facts already verified in the deep files are tagged `deep/NN`.

## 1. Market size and growth — the capex backdrop
- **Capital goods overall:** Crisil Ratings expects capital-goods revenue to grow **12–14% in FY27** (same as FY26), with operating margins "range-bound at 12–13%". Order books of large companies rose 1.5x in two years to **₹5.2 lakh cr (Dec-25)**, and book-to-bill (order book ÷ annual sales) improved from 3.1x (FY24) to 3.7x (FY26). Drivers named: government spending, power/mining/oil & gas/metal/auto capacity, data centres, EV infrastructure; power capacity additions of 58–62 GW in FY27 [Crisil Ratings via Upstox, 25-May-26; Whalesbook, 22-May-26].
- **Investment in the economy:** real GDP grew 7.8% in Q1 FY27; gross fixed capital formation (GFCF — spending on machinery, buildings, infrastructure) grew **11.9%**, the fastest in 13 quarters, and rose to **34.3% of GDP** from 31.4% [MoSPI Q1 FY27 GDP release, 29-Aug-26, via Business Standard/BusinessToday search summaries; Free Press Journal, 22-Sep-26]. Manufacturing capacity utilisation ~75% [Free Press Journal, 22-Sep-26; RBI OBICUS 75.6% for Dec-25 quarter via CEIC search summary]. Bank credit to industry +19% YoY in June 2026 [Free Press Journal, 22-Sep-26].
- **Factory output:** IIP capital goods **+16.1% YoY in July 2026** (overall IIP +6.7%, manufacturing +7.3%) [MoSPI IIP Jul-26 via search summaries of MoSPI/Business Standard, 28-Aug-26].
- **Project pipeline is volatile:** new project announcements nearly halved to ₹13.5 trn in Q4 FY26 (₹25.5 trn a year earlier); private proposals fell to ₹7.0 trn from ₹17.3 trn; manufacturing announcements hit a 7-quarter low of ₹1.5 trn, while services (transport, data centres) hit a 12-quarter high of ₹10.0 trn. Project **completions** hit a record ₹4.1 trn in Q4 and ₹10.4 trn in FY26 (₹6.3 trn FY25) [ICRA Investment Tracker, Apr-26]. Then private announcements rebounded ~70% to ~₹13.1 trn in Q1 FY27, led by power [Business Standard, 1-Jul-26, CMIE data — headline/search summary only; page blocked].
- **Five-year capex:** Crisil estimates India Inc capex of **₹145–155 lakh cr over FY27–FY31**, ~50% above the previous five years; industrial capex rising to ~₹9.1 lakh cr a year (1.5x) [Crisil via BW Businessworld — search summary only; page blocked].

## 2. Niche market sizes (the companies' own addressable markets)
- **Abrasives:** consultancy sizes vary (US$0.56–1.2 bn) and are unreliable; CUMI and Grindwell Norton together hold roughly half the market; CUMI has >30% of bonded abrasives [search summaries of Ken/TechSci; Crisil Mar-26 via deep/01]. Best live read: Grindwell Norton's abrasives segment grew **10.0%** YoY in Q1 FY27 to ₹386.9 cr with a 12.7% segment margin, while its ceramics & plastics grew 19% with profit +45% [ScanX, 24-Jul-26]. CUMI standalone sales +21% in Q1 but "very little or no price increase" [deep/01]. Abrasives = volume business tracking manufacturing; ceramics = where growth and margin are.
- **Cryogenic tanks / industrial gases:** Inox has 70–75% of Indian cryogenic tanks and 60–65% of Indian LNG products [Crisil Sep-25 via deep/03]. Indian industrial-gas market ~US$1.4 bn (2025), ~7.5% CAGR; Linde India and INOX Air Products each ~25% share [Ken Research — search summary only]. Inox's growth is mostly export (58% of Q1 revenue; 68% of backlog) — so global LNG, space and science projects matter more than India [deep/03].
- **LNG trucking (India):** PNGRB estimates LNG is ~22% cheaper than diesel on an energy basis (₹1,863 vs ₹2,390 per mmBtu) and sees a possible ~6 MMTPA LNG-for-transport market by 2030; regas terminal utilisation is below 50% [PNGRB case study, 30-Dec-25]. Filling stations are still few (~20) with a 50,000-truck-by-2030 goal [ETV Bharat/PNGRB search summaries].
- **Heat exchangers:** Indian commercial HVAC heat-exchanger market ~₹2,000 cr (₹7,000–8,000 cr incl. room AC); bus AC ~₹1,000 cr — management estimates only [deep/04]. Data-centre cooling is the fastest part (16% of KRN FY26 revenue) [deep/04].
- **Data centres:** CareEdge expects Indian colocation capacity to rise from **1.2 GW (FY25) to ~4 GW by FY30**, ~₹1.5 lakh cr investment, industry revenue CAGR ~24% FY26–FY30 [CareEdge Ratings, Mar-26, via search summaries of the CareEdge PDF/ANI].
- **Solar glass:** FY26 solar installations a record **44.6 GW** (34.8 GW ground-mounted +106%; 8.7 GW rooftop +69%) [JMK Research, 10-Apr-26]. Cumulative solar 164.6 GW at July 2026; 28.8 GW added in Jan–Jul 2026 [MNRE/CEA via SolarQuarter, 12-Aug-26]. Glass need ~11,000 tonnes/day (TPD) vs domestic capacity 2,600 TPD in FY26 (imports ~70%), rising to ~7,700 TPD by Mar-27, mostly captive [Borosil mgmt via deep/05].
- **Welding:** Indian welding industry ₹10,000–12,000 cr — consumables 70%, equipment 15%, cutting 10%, automation 5%; organised players 50–55% [CARE Jan-26 via deep/09]. Ador ~17–18% share (management) [deep/09].
- **Steel use (proxy for welding, abrasives, wear parts):** finished-steel consumption +8.3% to 41.6 mt in Q1 FY27 [JPC via IBEF/PIB, 27-Jul-26]; FY26 demand +7.6% [ICRA Steel Outlook, May-26].
- **Temperature instrumentation:** Indian temperature-sensor market **₹1,880 cr (FY26) → ₹2,880 cr (FY31), ~8.9% CAGR** [Frost & Sullivan in Tempsens RHP, via IPO review search summaries]. Tempsens ~10.5% of contact and ~21.3% of non-contact sensors [deep/08].
- **CRGO steel (transformer cores):** Indian use ~4–4.5 lakh t/yr, ~90% imported (China, Japan, Korea, Russia); domestic output only 40,000–50,000 t [GTRI via The Hitavada, 27-Jun-26]. Vilas ~5–7% share (management) [deep/12].
- **Grid:** 113,013 MVA of transformation capacity added in FY26 (90% of CEA's 126,007 MVA target); FY27 target **158,339 MVA** (+40%), 35,670 MVA done by July; National Electricity Plan ₹9.15 lakh cr transmission spend FY23–FY32 [CEA/MoP via Outlook Business, 26-Sep-26].
- **Factory automation:** estimates vary widely (industrial automation ~US$17 bn 2026, ~8.4% CAGR — Ken Research, search summary only). India installed 9,120 industrial robots in 2024 (+7%); robot density 7 per 10,000 workers vs global 141 [IFR data via search summary]. Patil competes with Wipro PARI (~₹2,000 cr), Comau, Jendamark [deep/13].
- **Auto AC:** SIAM put FY26 PV wholesales at 4.64 mn (+7.9%) and FY27 PV growth at **5–7%** [SIAM via Autocar Pro/Upstox search summaries]. Truck-AC mandate (N2/N3 trucks from Jun-25) created a ₹600–700 cr market [deep/07].

## 3. Demand drivers
- **Railways (Airfloa, HBL, KRN rail coolers, Diffusion):** FY27 capex budgeted at **₹2,93,030 cr, +10.5%** over FY26 RE (₹2,65,200 cr); 95% funded by the Centre; rolling-stock capex ~₹65,500 cr (+3%). Physical targets FY27: coaches 10,392 (+9%), wagons 32,000 (+23% on a weak FY26 RE of 26,000), electric locos 1,800. Operating ratio 98.4% — the Railways cannot self-fund [PRS Railways DfG 2026-27, 4-Feb-26]. ₹1,14,973 cr (39%) spent by July [Railway Ministry via NewKerala/ANI, 5-Aug-26].
- **Kavach (HBL's ~50% of sales):** v4.0 commissioned on 2,633 route km at 31-Jul-26; trackside work taken up on **21,794 route km**; 6,290 locomotives fitted, another 7,190 locos and 1,200 EMU/MEMU in the pipeline; ₹3,875 cr spent to Jun-26; **only ₹2,066 cr allocated in FY27** [Railway Ministry via NewKerala, 5-Aug-26]. The budget line is small relative to order books, so execution pace (not demand) is the constraint; HBL itself sees FY27–FY28 as strong and competition from FY29 [deep/02].
- **New trains:** 50 Vande Bharat sleeper rakes at ICF, 10 at BEML, 120 at Kinet (first prototype by Mar-27); 100 Amrit Bharat trains (ICF/RCF) [search summaries of Metro Rail News/Wikipedia]. These are the interiors/HVAC pool for Airfloa and KRN.
- **Defence (HBL fuzes/batteries, Omnitech's first defence parts, Airfloa T-90 assemblies, Patil ammunition lines, Tempsens nuclear/defence 7%):** defence production a record **₹1.78 lakh cr in FY26 (+15.6%)**, private sector 24% (~₹42,000 cr); exports ₹38,424 cr [MoD via DD India, 17-Jun-26]. FY27 defence capital outlay ₹2,19,306 cr, +17.6% on FY26 RE [PRS Budget analysis, search summary]. DAC approvals (AoN) of **₹6.73 lakh cr in FY26** (55 proposals); 75% of the capital budget reserved for domestic buying, 25% of that for private firms [MoD Annual Report via Bharat Shakti, 14-Sep-26]; a further ~₹1.10 lakh cr AoN on 7-Sep-26, ~98% Indian-sourced [MoD via SSBCrack, Sep-26]. AoN is an early stage — contracts follow over years.
- **Power/renewables (Borosil, Vilas, Tempsens power 12%, CUMI metallised cylinders):** JMK expects **53–55 GW** of solar + wind in FY27 [JMK Research, Apr-26]; but Crisil warns **35–37 GW** of RE capacity faces curtailment risk in FY27 for lack of long-term grid access [Crisil Ratings via pv magazine/Business Standard, 17-Mar-26]. Grid transformation targets (+40% in FY27) drive CRGO and radiators.
- **Data centres (KRN, HBL PLT batteries, Tempsens):** 1.2 GW → 4 GW by FY30 [CareEdge, Mar-26]; ICRA notes data-centre projects drove the Q4 FY26 services spike [ICRA Apr-26].
- **Steel/cement/process industries (CUMI, Ador, Diffusion, Tempsens):** ICRA expects steel demand growth to **improve to 9–10% in FY27** on railways/roads/infrastructure (budget allocations to steel-intensive sectors up ~25%) [ICRA Steel Outlook, May-26].
- **Global oil & gas (Omnitech ~49% energy):** IEA's World Energy Investment 2026 puts total energy investment at ~US$3.4 trn in 2026 but notes the Middle East conflict damaged LNG facilities and pushed back some Qatari LNG timelines [IEA WEI 2026 launch deck, 28-May-26]. Search summaries (IEA/Wood Mackenzie) put 2026 upstream spend flat to down 2–3% — a headwind for oilfield-services machining [search summary only].
- **Shipbuilding (Ador, Inox LNG fuel tanks):** ₹69,725 cr shipbuilding and maritime package (Sep-25): financial-assistance scheme extended to 2036 (₹24,736 cr), ₹25,000 cr Maritime Development Fund, target 4.5 mn gross tonnes a year [Cabinet via Tribune/HDFC Sky search summaries].

## 4. Policy
- **Solar glass protection:** anti-dumping duty on Chinese and Vietnamese solar glass (reference price ~US$570–664/t) from Dec-24 for five years [Mercom/TaiyangNews search summaries]; 9.71% CVD on Malaysian glass extended five years (2-Jun-26) [deep/05]. ALMM List-III (wafers/ingots) from June 2028 supports domestic chains [deep/05]. Borosil's margin went from 4% to 31% on these duties alone [deep/05] — policy is the moat.
- **CRGO:** DGTR began an anti-dumping probe on 22-Jun-26 into CRGO and amorphous steel from China, Japan, Korea and Russia (applicant JSW JFE Electrical Steel); GTRI warns duties would raise transformer costs without cutting import dependence [The Hitavada, 27-Jun-26]. Duties would raise Vilas's input prices; pass-through depends on its spot vs fixed-price mix (50–60% on quarterly fixed prices) [deep/12].
- **US tariffs (exporters: Omnitech ~52% North America, Inox ~26%, KRN, CUMI EMD):** after the Feb-26 India–US deal cut the reciprocal rate from 25% to 18% and removed the extra 25%, the US Supreme Court struck down IEEPA tariffs on 20-Feb-26; a temporary 10% global tariff under Section 122 replaced them [All India Radio, 21-Feb-26; India Briefing search summary]. Later reports say a 10% Section 301 baseline replaced Section 122 on 24-Jul-26 [search summary only — unverified]. Metal-content (Section 232) duties still apply to copper/steel-heavy goods — KRN says it pays or shares a 25% US duty [deep/04].
- **Railways safety:** Kavach mandated across the Golden Quadrilateral/Diagonal and high-density network (21,794 route km taken up) [Railway Ministry, Aug-26].
- **Defence indigenisation:** 75% of capital acquisition earmarked for domestic industry [MoD AR 2025-26].
- **Incentives:** KRN's margins assume white-goods PLI (5% of heat-exchanger production) and Rajasthan RIPS (1.56% of new-plant sales for 10 years), neither yet booked [deep/04].
- **Truck AC mandate** (from Jun-25) drove Subros's truck revenue +111% in FY26 [deep/07].
- **Steel safeguard duty:** finished-steel imports fell 32% after the safeguard duty in late FY26; ICRA expects imports to stay muted in FY27 [ICRA Steel Outlook, May-26] — this raises domestic steel costs for fabricators (Airfloa, Diffusion, Patil).

## 5. Cost drivers
- **Energy and freight:** Brent **~US$104/bbl** on 25-Sep-26, +51% YoY and +20% in a month, on the Strait of Hormuz disruption [Trading Economics, 25-Sep-26]. ICRA's FY27 GDP call of 6.5% assumed US$85 crude [ICRA Apr-26]. Hits Borosil (furnace gas — it added a fuel surcharge from Mar-26), CUMI (fusion power), Inox (freight misses) [deep/05; deep/01; deep/03].
- **Copper and aluminium:** copper ~US$6.70/lb, +42% YoY, near an all-time high set in Sep-26 [Trading Economics, 25-Sep-26]; aluminium ~US$3,282/t, +24% YoY [Trading Economics via search summary]. KRN and Subros pass these through with a one-quarter lag; Airfloa recovers only 50–60% via price-variation clauses (aluminium +80%, stainless +60–65% hurt FY26 margins by 500 bps) [deep/04; deep/07; deep/11].
- **Steel:** domestic HRC rebounded ~14% QoQ to ₹57,700/t by March 2026; ICRA sees prices range-bound, with coking coal and iron ore costs rising [ICRA Steel Outlook, May-26]. Diffusion flagged steel +~20% and tungsten up "a few hundred percent" on fixed-price orders [deep/10].
- **CRGO price:** long-run ₹180–190/kg; peak ₹270–290; ~₹195–210 now — Vilas's FY26 volume +65% gave only ~30% revenue growth [deep/12].
- **Labour:** wage inflation and hiring ahead of new plants hit Q1 margins at Inox (+23% staff cost), Tempsens (+45%), KRN and Subros [deep/03; deep/08; deep/04; deep/07].

## 7. Where profit sits (latest quarter operating margin, Screener)
- **Policy-protected or niche-technology:** Borosil 31% (duty-driven), Omnitech 30% (precision machining, down from 38%), HBL 23% (Kavach quarters swing 12–44%), Tempsens 20%, Inox 20%, KRN 19% [deep files; Screener].
- **Mid-margin engineered products:** Airfloa ~18–20%, Patil ~15%, Diffusion 13%, Ador 12% (welding segment 15% in FY26), Grindwell abrasives segment ~13% [deep files; ScanX].
- **Commodity-linked / OEM-dependent:** Vilas 9–11% (price-taker in CRGO), CUMI 9% consolidated (Ceramics ~25% vs Abrasives ~9% at segment level), Subros 8% (82% of sales to related party Maruti) [deep/12; deep/01; deep/07].
- **Cash is the real divider:** several fast growers turn little profit into cash — KRN FY26 FCF –₹312 cr and a second equity raise; Airfloa CFO –₹57 cr; Omnitech, Patil and Diffusion negative FCF; Tempsens 210-day cash cycle. Against that, Ador (CFO 122% of operating profit), HBL, Borosil and CUMI self-fund [deep/04, 11, 06, 13, 10, 08, 09, 02, 05, 01]. Rating agencies flag the same point company by company (Crisil on KRN and Omnitech gross current assets of 340–360 days; ICRA on Tempsens "high working capital intensity") [deep/04; deep/06; deep/08].

## 8. Cycle position (Sep 2026)
- **Mid-cycle, public-led, private hesitant but improving.** GFCF at 34% of GDP and IIP capital goods +16% say the cycle is alive; ICRA (Apr-26) expected private capex to be "subdued" in FY27 because of West Asia energy and input shocks; Q1 FY27 announcements rebounded (CMIE) [sources above]. Order books (3.7x book-to-bill) give 2–3 years of visibility to large capital-goods firms [Crisil May-26].
- **Government capex growth is slowing to ~10–12%** (Centre +11.5%, Railways +10.5%, defence capital +17.6%) from the 25–35% years; ICRA flags fiscal-slippage risk to the Centre's capex and calls states' +14.3% "optimistic" [ICRA Apr-26; PRS].
- **Sub-cycles differ:** Kavach at peak spend (FY26–FY28); solar glass at "almost peak" pricing with a captive capacity wave arriving by Mar-27 [deep/05]; CRGO prices off the peak; auto steady (5–7%); global oilfield capex flat; grid and data centres early-to-mid upcycle.
- **Margins are under input-cost pressure now** (oil ~US$104, copper at records, steel up) — pass-through lags mean Q2–Q3 FY27 margins may lag revenue for KRN, Subros, Airfloa, Diffusion, Vilas.

## 9. Outlook FY27–FY29 (forecaster named)
- **Capital goods revenue +12–14% in FY27, margins 12–13%** — Crisil Ratings (May-26).
- **India Inc capex ₹145–155 lakh cr over FY27–FY31 (~1.5x prior five years)** — Crisil (search summary).
- **GDP 6.5% in FY27, private capex subdued, risks to the downside** — ICRA (Apr-26, pre-Q1 data; Q1 came in at 7.8%).
- **Steel demand +9–10% in FY27**; steel margins roughly stable — ICRA (May-26). Supports abrasives, welding, wear parts.
- **Solar + wind additions 53–55 GW in FY27** — JMK Research; **35–37 GW curtailment risk** — Crisil Ratings. Borosil revenue: only "nominal" FY27 growth until new furnaces start at end-FY27 — India Ratings (Dec-25) [deep/05].
- **Data-centre capacity ~4 GW by FY30, industry revenue CAGR ~24%** — CareEdge (Mar-26).
- **Grid transformation +40% in FY27 (158 GVA target)** — CEA.
- **Temperature sensors ~8.9% CAGR FY26–FY31** — Frost & Sullivan (Tempsens RHP).
- **Passenger vehicles +5–7% in FY27** — SIAM.
- **CUMI revenue growth only 4–5%, margins ~14%** — Crisil (Mar-26) [deep/01].
- **Defence exports target ₹50,000 cr by 2029** — Government (Rajnath Singh; search summary only).
- **My synthesis:** industrial-products demand should grow roughly **10–14% a year FY27–FY29** in nominal terms, with the best niches (data-centre cooling, defence electronics, grid materials, cryogenics for space/LNG/science, precision machining) at 20%+ and the steel-linked consumables (abrasives, welding) at 7–10%. The two biggest swing factors are (1) whether private manufacturing capex follows the Q1 FY27 announcements into orders, and (2) whether oil/copper stay high long enough to squeeze fixed-price and lag-pass-through contracts. Company-specific risks (Kavach peak at HBL, duty dependence at Borosil, cash burn at KRN/Airfloa/Omnitech/Patil) matter more than the sector average.

## 10. What to watch
- ICRA/CMIE Q2 FY27 project announcements and completions (Oct–Nov 2026); RBI OBICUS capacity utilisation.
- Railway capex utilisation (39% by July) and Kavach allocation vs order books; Vande Bharat sleeper/Amrit Bharat rake output.
- DGTR CRGO anti-dumping finding; any change to solar-glass ADD/CVD; US tariff regime after Section 122/301 changes.
- Brent, copper and HRC prices; Q2 FY27 margin commentary on pass-through.
- Solar curtailment and module-maker utilisation (which set solar-glass demand for merchant sellers).
