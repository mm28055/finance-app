# Freshara Agro Exports (FRESHARA, NSE Emerge SME)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. SME company that reports half-yearly. Latest period: H2 FY26 (Oct 2025–Mar 2026). Consolidated figures unless marked.

**Coverage gap:** as an SME it holds no Q1 FY27 or Q4 FY26 call. The two latest calls, H2 FY26 (4 Jun 2026) and H1 FY26 (5 Dec 2025), were used instead. The June 2026 investor presentation filed on NSE could not be downloaded (the request returned HTML), so deck figures come from what management said on the call.

## Business and segments
- Tamil Nadu-based processor and exporter of gherkins (pickled cucumbers). It covers the chain from contract farming (4,000+ farmers) to brining, processing and bottling. [H2 call] [CRISIL Jul-26]
  - Two plants with ~550 t of processing capacity.
  - Sells to 100+ customers in 40+ countries.
- FY26 gherkin exports were 43,600 t worth ₹268 cr, about 85% of sales. The rest is vinegar, spice preparations and other pickled vegetables. [H2 call]
- Packaging mix: 71% industrial (drums and bulk) and 16% retail jars. [H2 call]
- Geography: [H2 call] [H1 call]
  - Russia is ~33–35% of sales, up from 18% of exports a year earlier. Freshara claims a 35–40% share of Russia's gherkin imports.
  - Spain is #2, with 60+ customers.
  - The US is ~10%.
- **Spanish acquisition (Feb–Mar 2026):** Freshara bought Conservas Selectas Españolas S.L. and Gandin Invest S.L., owners of the Sarasa brand (founded 1968), out of bankruptcy as an asset purchase. [H2 call] [AR FY26]
  - Price: ~₹34 cr for raw-material stock, ~₹7.5 cr for brands and assets, and an assumed mortgage liability.
  - The standalone company invested ₹7.53 cr of equity for 100% of both.
- Competitors named on the call are Global Green and Reitzel. [H2 call]

## Guidance history
- **H1 FY26 call (Dec 2025):** [H1 call]
  - H1 revenue ₹140 cr (+30–31%) and an order book of ₹91 cr.
  - FY26 volume target of 35,000 t, beaten at 43,600 t.
  - Revenue growth of 30–40% a year.
  - FY27 combined target of "600 crores": ~₹400 cr India plus ~₹200 cr from the Spanish company.
  - ₹1,000 cr within three years.
- **H2 FY26 call (Jun 2026):** [H2 call]
  - FY27 guidance of ~₹575 cr: India ~₹400 cr (+25%) and Spain ₹200 cr+.
  - Spain at 12–14% EBITDA and 8–10% PAT; blended PAT of 10–11%.
  - ₹1,000 cr by FY30.
  - **Slightly trimmed** (₹600 cr to ₹575 cr), and the ₹1,000 cr target moved from "three years" to FY30.
- **Capex:** none planned. A "₹150 cr" figure on the deck was the notional cost of building a Spanish platform from scratch, not a spend plan. Spain has ~₹500 cr of revenue capacity, less than half used. [H2 call]
- **Cash flow:** management expects to be cash-positive in FY27, blaming FY26's negative cash on seasonal, pre-war inventory purchases. [H2 call]
- **Track record:** only one half-year has been tested against guidance. FY26 volumes beat the target, but both years' growth came with sharply negative operating cash flow (below).

## Capex and capacity
- Capex (operating cash flow minus FCF) was ~₹22 cr in FY25 and ~₹51 cr in FY26 on a consolidated basis; FY26 includes the Spanish assets. FY25 included ₹9.5 cr of capex bought from Concrete Builders, a related party. The standalone company put ₹7.53 cr of equity and ₹15.9 cr of inter-corporate loans into the Spanish subsidiaries. [Screener] [AR FY26]
- There is headroom in India, and Spain's plant is under 50% used, so the growth plan needs working capital rather than plant. [H2 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Cash burn:** consolidated operating cash flow was -₹2 cr in FY25 and -₹57 cr in FY26; FCF -₹24 cr and -₹108 cr. Profits of ₹29 cr and ₹38 cr have not turned into cash. [Screener]
- Borrowings ₹180 cr (FY26) against net worth of ₹176 cr (equity ₹24 cr plus reserves ₹152 cr). [Screener]
- The FY26 working-capital cycle is severe: debtor days 160, inventory days 282, cash conversion cycle 328 days. [Screener]
  - Management says the inventory was a pre-war build that saved 15–20% on packing materials. It says cash is positive in April–May and negative around July–September and March. [H2 call]
- **CRISIL BBB/Stable, A3+ (reaffirmed 9 Jul 2026):** bank lines enhanced from ₹100 cr to ₹175 cr. [CRISIL Jul-26]
  - Strengths: FY25 interest cover 7.44x; gearing 0.74x.
  - Weaknesses: gross current assets of 283 days (Mar 2025); climate risk.
  - Liquidity "adequate": ~64% bank-limit use and cash accruals of ₹40 cr+ against ₹4.5–7 cr of term-debt repayments.
  - Downgrade trigger: operating margin below 12% or a large working-capital build-up.
- **Equity and borrowing:** 23.16 lakh warrants allotted on 13 Mar 2026 at ₹168 (₹38.9 cr); ₹9.5 cr received so far went into the Spanish deal. [AR FY26] [H2 call]
  - On 18 Sep 2026 the board noted warrant-holders' intent to convert.
  - The 30 Sep 2026 AGM will vote on raising the borrowing limit to ₹400 cr.

## Moat evidence
- **Scale and sourcing:** India is the largest gherkin exporter and Freshara is one of its biggest players. Its contract-farming base secures supply, and CRISIL calls its position "established". [CRISIL Jul-26] [H2 call]
- **Cost:** management says Reitzel makes ~70% for itself, leaving Freshara "head to head" with Global Green and with better margins "because of our cost control". This is a company claim, not verified. [H2 call]
- **Brands:** Sarasa gives it an EU retail brand and a Spanish plant inside the EU tariff wall. The company says this is worth 7–14% duty savings until an India–EU trade deal (expected early 2027) removes the gap. [H2 call]
- **Market:** management estimates India's gherkin market grew 40% in two years to ~₹3,000 cr. [H2 call]
- **Durability:** 15–18% OPMs on a commodity product with no evident pricing power. The main evidence of durability is two years of steady margins. [Screener]

## Input costs
- Fresh gherkins come from contract farmers at pre-agreed prices. Supply depends on the monsoon and irrigation; CRISIL flags climate as a key weakness. [CRISIL Jul-26]
- The auditor lists the main raw and packing materials as acetic acid, vinegar, chemicals, salt, fresh greens, gherkins, garlic and spices. [AR FY26]
- Packing materials (drums, glass) and freight were hit by the Gulf-war disruption. The pre-war inventory buy saved 15–20% on packing materials. [H2 call]
- The business is exposed to the rupee, with 100% export sales, and to the rouble through its Russian customers. The hedging policy was not disclosed on the calls.

## Risks and red flags
- **Cash conversion:** OCF was negative in both FY25 and FY26 while reported profit rose. The 328-day cycle and ₹180 cr of debt, heading for a ₹400 cr borrowing limit, are the key issues. [Screener] [AR FY26]
- **Russia concentration:** about a third of sales go to one market, exposed to sanctions, payment and currency risks. [H2 call]
- **Spain integration:** a bankrupt business earning 4–5% PAT is guided to reach 8–10% within a year, and its bank loans carry a ₹30 cr corporate guarantee from Freshara. [AR FY26] [H2 call]
- **Contingent liabilities** of ₹32.5 cr: [AR FY26]
  - Corporate guarantee to Conservas Selectas ₹30 cr.
  - GST demand ₹2.5 cr, raised in the name of the predecessor partnership, Freshara Picklz Exports.
  - TDS demand ~₹0.19 lakh.
- **Auditor:** no qualification or emphasis of matter. The key audit matters were the foreign-subsidiary investments and inventory valuation and existence. The inventory KAM matters given 282 inventory days. [AR FY26]
- **Related parties:** [AR FY26]
  - Rent to promoter family members (Iqbal Ahmed ₹57 lakh, Kouser Afshan ₹11.4 lakh).
  - Inter-corporate loans to the Spanish subsidiaries ₹15.9 cr.
  - FY25 borrowing from the CMD ₹11.2 cr.
  - FY25 capex purchases from Concrete Builders ₹9.5 cr.
  - None are large relative to sales, but the family ties are dense.
- **Disclosure:** half-yearly reporting only, a small team, and a patchy deck and transcript record on NSE.
- Promoter holding is 62.58%; no pledge disclosed. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- The Spanish acquisition went from an idea (Dec 2025) to a completed deal (Feb–Mar 2026) funded by warrants and a ₹29–30 cr bank limit.
- FY27 guidance was trimmed from ₹600 cr to ₹575 cr, and the ₹1,000 cr goal slipped to FY30.
- Inventory and debt ballooned in H2 (pre-war stocking), turning FCF deeply negative. Management now promises to be cash-positive in FY27.
- Russia remained the #1 market. The EU trade deal and the Spanish plant have become the new pitch.
- CRISIL reaffirmed BBB/Stable (Jul 2026) and increased the rated bank limit to ₹175 cr.

## My read
Freshara is a fast-growing gherkin exporter: revenue has quadrupled since FY24 with steady 15–18% margins, and it has bought an EU brand and plant cheaply out of bankruptcy. The problem is cash. Operating cash flow was negative in both FY25 and FY26 despite ₹67 cr of combined profit, working capital runs to 328 days, and debt is heading toward a ₹400 cr limit. The P/E of 26 assumes FY27 delivers ₹575 cr of revenue and a turnaround in Spain. I would want positive operating cash flow in H1 FY27, lower inventory days and Spanish PAT near 8% before trusting the growth. Russia concentration and a CRISIL margin floor of 12% are the downside triggers.
