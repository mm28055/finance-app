# Venky's (India) (VENKEYS, ISIN INE398A01010)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Standalone figures (Screener; the consolidated page is empty).

**Coverage gap:** no Q1 FY27 earnings call was found. Screener's concall list ends with the Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026), and no investor presentation has been filed since Feb 2025. The two most recent calls, Q4 FY26 and Q2 FY26 (10 Nov 2025), were used instead.

## Business and segments
- Listed flagship of the Pune-based VH Group (Venkateshwara Hatcheries), India's largest integrated poultry group. The group's breeds (Venco/VRB) are India's best-selling. Venky's covers north and west India; the unlisted parent VHPL covers south and east. [CARE Jan-26]
- Three segments (FY26 revenue; segment result): [AR FY26]
  - **Poultry and poultry products** ₹1,897 cr; result ₹65.5 cr (₹99.5 cr in FY25). Day-old broiler and layer chicks, grown broilers, processed chicken (~14 "Venky's Xpress" outlets) and SPF (specific-pathogen-free) eggs. Venky's is one of Asia's largest SPF egg producers, running at 48% utilisation of 1.88 crore eggs a year.
  - **Oilseed** (soybean crushing, oil and de-oiled cake) ₹1,554 cr (₹1,141 cr in FY25); result ₹54.4 cr (₹19.1 cr). Capacity utilisation is 65–70%, targeted at ~80% in FY27.
  - **Animal health products (AHP)** ₹392 cr; result ₹99.0 cr (₹72.3 cr). This is now the largest profit contributor.
- FY26 volumes: [Q4 call]
  - Commercial broilers 8.69 crore kg (9.17 crore).
  - Broiler chicks 11.46 crore (10.77 crore).
  - Layer chicks 4.57 crore (4.38 crore).
  - About 80% of broiler chicks are grown in-house through integration; 20% are sold on the open market.
- FY26 realisations: broiler ₹89.59/kg (cost ~₹89/kg); broiler chick ₹34.94 (cost ~₹32); layer chick ₹45.15 (cost ₹35–37). [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q2 FY26 call (Nov 2025):** H2 FY26 poultry would "cover up… the losses" and "make money"; realisations were improving from late October. H2 delivered strongly: Q3 PAT ₹49 cr and Q4 ₹101 cr. **Delivered.** [Q2 FY26 call] [Screener]
- **Q4 FY26 call (May 2026):** [Q4 call]
  - Poultry: volumes to grow 4–5% in broilers; layer chicks from 4.57 crore to 5 crore+.
  - Q1 is traditionally strong for broiler prices, but prices were already falling from 10–11 May.
  - AHP: double-digit growth with a full-year segment margin of 22–23% or more (Q4 was ~29%).
  - Oilseed: Q4's ~5.3% EBIT margin to be sustained at least to September; utilisation ~80%.
- **Q1 FY27 outcome:** PAT halved sequentially. Segment detail is not available because there was no call or deck. **Mixed; not reviewable.** [Screener]
- **Capex:** no major poultry capex; oilseed capex to be considered after a year of consolidation. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is modest: ~₹37 cr of depreciation a year and FY26 investing outflow of ₹108 cr (including ₹91 cr more in investments). The newer AHP plant (therapeutics) runs at 35–40% utilisation; the older AHP plant at 75–80%. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- Oilseed: three units with four plants; no capex planned for the next year. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹167 cr against reserves of ₹1,582 cr (gearing ~0.1x). Investments ₹213 cr. [Screener]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 5 → 86 → 82 → 66 → 191. FCF: -25 → 44 → 32 → 21 → 151. [Screener]
- Receivables are high at ₹544–556 cr (~53 debtor days), including dues from group companies. CARE lists "delayed realisation of debtors from group companies" as a downgrade trigger. [Q4 call] [CARE Jan-26]
- Dividend payout ~10%. Investors asked for a buyback and an AHP demerger; management took these "as a suggestion". [Screener] [Q4 call]
- Rating: CARE A+ (Stable)/A1+, reaffirmed on 13 Jan 2026, on ₹311.5 cr of facilities. [CARE Jan-26]
  - Downgrade triggers: cash accrual below ₹50 cr, delays in collecting group receivables, or gearing of 0.7x or more.
  - Upgrade trigger: revenue above ₹5,000 cr.

## Moat evidence
- Group integration from pure-line genetics to feed, vaccines and processing. The group's breeds have a favourable feed conversion ratio, and chicks go to the in-house broiler division at cost (₹28 a chick in FY26). [CARE Jan-26] [Q4 call]
- AHP: ~24–25% average market share in its product lines (40–45% in feed and food safety), a national distribution network, and a shift to natural growth promoters and therapeutics. Segment margin rose from ~17% a decade ago to ~25% in FY26. [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- Poultry pricing power is nil: realisations swing ±30% within a quarter. [Q4 call]
- The parent's large share of parent-bird placements, cited on the Q2 FY26 call, gives the group supply visibility but no control over prices. [Q2 FY26 call]

## Input costs
- Feed is maize (~60%) and soybean meal. The FY26 average feed cost was ₹31,658/t (broiler feed ~₹39,000/t in Q3). [Q4 call]
- Maize is linked to ethanol demand, and soya to crude oil and biodiesel. Management expects prices to stay volatile but notes Indian prices are above world prices, so imports cap them. [Q4 call]
- Oilseed earns a conversion margin plus inventory gains. The 40% oil / 60% meal split means crude oil and edible-oil prices drive realisations. [Q4 call]
- There is no pass-through in broilers: profit is simply the market price minus a production cost of ~₹85–89/kg. [Q2 FY26 call] [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party dependence:** [AR FY26] [Q2 FY26 call]
  - FY26 sales to related parties were ₹1,243 cr (₹753 cr to the holding company VHPL), about a third of revenue. Purchases from related parties were ₹460 cr.
  - The auditor lists related-party transactions as the key audit matter.
  - Investors complained on calls about money "due from the promoters".
- **Governance:** the Managing Director draws remuneration from the holding company VHPL rather than from Venky's. [AR FY26]
- **Competition probe:** the Competition Commission of India (CCI) has opened an antitrust inquiry into group parent-breeding practices. Management says Venky's is "not affected as of now". [Q4 call]
- **Earnings volatility:** poultry EBIT was about -₹58 cr in Q2 FY26. Summer productivity is 10–12% lower, and religious calendars (Adhik Maas, Shravan) cut demand. [Q2 FY26 call] [Q4 call]
- **Contingent liabilities** ~₹32.5 cr: excise ₹15.5 cr, income tax ₹5.8 cr, electricity ₹2.9 cr, GST ₹2.2 cr. No auditor qualification. [AR FY26]
- **Disclosure:** no Q1 FY27 call, no investor deck since Feb 2025, and no quarterly volume data in filings. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- Promoter holding is 56.11% with no pledge; institutional ownership is ~1.4%. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q2 FY26 (Nov 2025): record poultry losses, an H2 recovery promised, raw materials trading below MSP.
- Q4 FY26 (May 2026): the recovery was delivered (Q4 PAT ₹101 cr) and AHP and oilseed were at record margins. But broiler prices had already started falling in May, and maize and soya were firming.
- New items in May 2026: the CCI probe, investor pressure for an AHP demerger and a buyback, and the 5 crore layer-chick target.
- Q1 FY27 filings show profit halving from Q4, with no management commentary.

## My read
Venky's earnings have become more resilient because the higher-margin animal-health business and a recovering oilseed unit now carry the group. Poultry swings from losses (Q2 FY26) to windfalls (Q4 FY26) with bird prices. FY26 profit of ₹139 cr and Q1 FY27 of ₹50 cr show a cyclical upturn. The stock trades at ~1.5x book because a third of sales and a large share of receivables sit with unlisted promoter companies, disclosure is thin, and the auditor flags related-party dealings. A buyback, a demerger of animal health, or lower group receivables would change the view. So would regular calls and decks. A weak broiler cycle in H1 FY27 combined with firm maize would push earnings back toward FY24 levels.
