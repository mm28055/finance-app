# Kaveri Seed Company (KSCL, ISIN INE455I01029)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Consolidated figures (Screener) unless marked. The business is highly seasonal: kharif seed sales make Q1 (Apr–Jun) the dominant quarter, with 55–65% of annual revenue and all of the profit.

## Business and segments
- One of India's largest listed hybrid-seed companies, based in Secunderabad. It sells cotton (Bt), maize, hybrid and "selection" (open-pollinated) rice, bajra (pearl millet), sunflower, jowar and vegetable seeds. Subsidiaries (Aditya Agritech, Genomix Agri Genetics and others) sell "me-too" hybrids under other brands and compete with the parent. [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- Q1 FY27 revenue by company measure, ₹815 cr (-13.8% YoY): [IP Q1FY27]
  - Non-cotton ₹601.6 cr (-17.2%): hybrid rice ₹247.1 cr (vs ₹280.2 cr), maize ₹137.7 cr (vs ₹241.0 cr, -42.9%), selection rice ₹159.1 cr (+0.95%), vegetables ₹15.1 cr, others ₹42.7 cr.
  - Cotton ₹213.4 cr (-2.5%).
- Mix shift: cotton was ~90% of sales in FY15 and is now ~20–25%. Cotton EBITDA margin has fallen from ~35% to ~15% under government price caps (MRP control). [Q1 call]
- Newer hybrids (launched in the last 3 years): [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
  - 37% of cotton sales in Q1 FY27 (22% a year earlier).
  - Single-cross hybrids are 20%+ of maize.
  - New bajra hybrids are 65% of bajra volume.
- Geography: kharif maize is concentrated in Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana (Karnataka was ~25–30% of last year's kharif sales); rabi in Bihar, UP and Maharashtra. [Q1 call]
- Exports: ₹5.79 cr in Q1 (vs ₹1.15 cr). Targeted at ₹100 cr within 3 years (Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia and Bangladesh). [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- **FY27 revenue +15–20%**, volume-led, with cotton above 20% (Q4). Q1 was -14% after a 40% June rainfall deficit. On Q1 management said the gap "will narrow" and gave no number. **Dropped.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 margin above FY26's ~24%** (Q4: lower production cost, prices held). Q1 margin was higher at ~35–40%, but management warned the full-year margin will be lower than Q1's. **Partly intact.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Cotton would grow** on a good sentiment (Q4). Q1 cotton was -2.5%, hit by illegal Bt seed in Gujarat and Maharashtra and delayed rain. **Missed.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Medium-term growth of 15–18%** (earlier "18–20%") over 2–3 years. **Reaffirmed, softened.** [Q1 call]
- **Exports:** ₹100 cr "in 5 years" (last year) was pulled forward to "3 years". On Q1, FY27 exports were guided up at least 25%. **Raised.** [Q1 call]
- **Buyback:** in Q4 the company said it would "come back" on a buyback once inventory is converted to cash in 3–5 months. In Q1 it was left to the board. **Pending.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capital work in progress was ₹130 cr (consolidated, Mar-26): office building (~₹30 cr), land for R&D, seed-processing units and machinery. Annual capex is typically ₹50–150 cr, all from internal cash. [Q4 call] [Screener]
- Production is contracted with seed growers. The company will "reduce our productions next year" to work off inventory. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free. Cash on books was ₹265–270 cr at 30 Jun 2026, and treasury investments were ₹401 cr at Mar-26 (₹613 cr a year earlier). [Q1 call] [Screener]
- Working capital deteriorated sharply: [Screener] [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
  - FY26 operating cash flow was -₹6 cr (₹197 cr in FY25; ₹389 cr in FY24) and FCF -₹155 cr.
  - Inventory days were 608, and working-capital days rose from 99 to 275.
  - Inventory was up ~₹200 cr YoY at Q1 (analyst estimate: ~₹1,200 cr total), because the company over-produced in expectation of a good El Niño-year season.
  - Dealer advances fell ₹75–80 cr in FY26 because the company chose not to pay incentives for advances.
- Shareholder returns: dividend payout ~9% and regular buybacks (FY24: ~₹425 cr of financing outflow). No buyback in FY26. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- Credit rating: none listed on Screener, and none found.

## Moat evidence
- R&D-driven hybrid pipeline. Management calls it "one of the best in the last 10 years": new hybrids now make up 37% of cotton sales and 62% of new-product sales in hybrid rice come from the two launches KRH7344 and KRH7227. [Q1 call]
- Cotton realisations are in the "top 3" of the industry despite MRP caps. Kaveri is "one of the largest players in rice". [Q1 call]
- Weaknesses: [Q1 call]
  - Cotton market share has been lost for 7–8 years.
  - No product for Madhya Pradesh maize, where acreage grew. Maize fell 40% while peers such as Bayer and Advanta grew 20%+.
  - Illegal herbicide-tolerant Bt cotton takes share when the season is delayed.

## Input costs
- Cost of production is grower payments (per kg of seed) plus processing. FY26 yields were 5–7% higher, so production cost fell 4–5% while realisations fell 2–3%, lifting gross margin by 2–3 points in Q1. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Pricing: cotton seed MRP is fixed by the government. Other crops are priced freely, but high industry inventory limits price increases in FY27. [Q4 call]
- Excess inventory is carried for up to 1.3–1.5 years; cotton seed stays viable for about 3 years. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Tax dispute over agricultural income:** [BSE filing 18-Sep-26] [AR FY26]
  - The effective tax rate is ~1–5% because nearly all income is claimed as exempt agricultural income. The Income Tax Department disputes this.
  - Demands of ₹56.2 cr (AY 2022–23) and ₹69.6 cr (AY 2023–24) were set aside by the CIT(A) in April 2026.
  - The department has appealed the AY 2022–23 order to the ITAT, with a hearing on 15 Oct 2026.
  - Block assessments for FY12–FY18 after a 2017–18 search are pending.
  - An adverse outcome would affect both the historical tax and the future tax rate.
- **Contingent liabilities:** ₹225.7 cr at Mar-26 (tax ₹129.0 cr; legal claims ₹96.6 cr, up from ₹19.7 cr). The jump in legal claims is not explained in the notes read. [AR FY26]
- **Auditor:** no qualification or emphasis of matter. The key audit matter was the estimate of sales returns, discounts and schemes, which is material in a business where unsold seed comes back from dealers. [AR FY26]
- **Weather and concentration:** a deficient monsoon (El Niño) hit Q1, with Karnataka maize down ~1,100–1,200 t (₹40–60 cr of sales). [Q1 call]
- **Governance and disclosure:**
  - Short calls with a single executive director answering.
  - Company revenue figures don't match the exchange-filed/Screener figures.
  - Sister-brand subsidiaries compete with the parent.
  - An employee trust is consolidated.
  - [Q1 call] [AR FY26]
- **Regulation:** a new Seeds Bill is expected "very soon". BG-III (next-generation GM cotton) approval is "moving at a very slow pace". [Q4 call]
- Promoter holding is stable at 60.50%; no pledge noted. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- The outlook went from +15–20% FY27 growth, with cotton to grow and El Niño impact "not much", to a -14% Q1 and "one-off tough year" language.
- The inventory build was reframed from "strategic buffer" to "anticipated a good season", with production to be cut next year.
- The tax picture improved (favourable CIT(A) orders in April), but the ITAT appeal is now scheduled.
- The export target was pulled forward to 3 years.

## My read
Kaveri is a cash-rich, low-valuation seed company whose earnings have not grown in a decade. Non-cotton growth has only replaced lost cotton profit. FY27 has started badly: a monsoon-hit Q1 and a ~₹1,200 cr inventory pile. FY26's negative cash flow shows how quickly working capital can absorb the balance sheet. The key swing factor for value is the agricultural-income tax dispute, since a normal tax rate would cut earnings by roughly a quarter. The view improves with a strong rabi maize season, cash conversion of inventory that funds a buyback, and a favourable ITAT ruling on 15 Oct. It worsens if inventory has to be written down or tax exemptions are denied.
