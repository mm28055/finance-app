# Food Products (India): sector view

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Written after the 18 company files in this folder. Company-level facts are tagged `[Co: name, source]` and trace back to those files. External sources from the last 12 months are tagged by publisher and date and listed at the end.

**Scope:** this "food products" basket is not one industry. It holds at least nine businesses with different economics:
- branded snacks and staples (Bikaji, Gopal, Ganesh);
- biscuits and bakery (Mrs Bectors);
- nutrition and wellness (Zydus Wellness);
- export processors (CCL and Vintage in coffee; Apex and Avanti in shrimp; Freshara in gherkins; ADF in ethnic foods);
- basmati (KRBL, LT Foods);
- animal feed, poultry and dairy (Godrej Agrovet, Venky's, Avanti);
- seeds (Kaveri);
- contract manufacturing (Hindustan Foods);
- specialty fats (Manorama).

The sections below apply sector-wide points first, then go through the task file's focus areas one by one.

## Market size and growth
- **Organised FMCG:** revenue was an estimated ~₹6.6 lakh crore in FY26, and food and beverages is ~50% of it. [CRISIL May-26]
  - CRISIL expects organised FMCG revenue to grow **8–10% in FY27** (FY26 ~8%).
  - Growth will be driven by price: realisations +6–7% against volume +2–3%, down from 5–6% volume growth in FY26.
  - Urban is ~60% of revenue and rural ~40%.
- **Packaged snacks:** one market-research estimate puts the Indian snacks market at ~₹50,590 cr in 2025, growing ~8.3% a year to 2034. [IMARC, 2026]
  - Treat this as indicative only: it is a commercial report and was not checked against primary data.
  - Company data shows faster growth for listed leaders: Bikaji guides "mid-to-high teens" for FY27, and Gopal grew 31% in Q1 on a fire-hit base. [Co: Bikaji Q1 call] [Co: Gopal Q1 call]
- **Seafood exports (MPEDA):** FY26 was a record, ₹72,326 cr (US$8.28 bn) on 1.93 million t. [MPEDA via Upstox, Apr-26]
  - Frozen shrimp was ₹47,973 cr (US$5.51 bn, over two-thirds of the total), up 4.6% in volume and 6.35% in value.
- **Basmati exports (APEDA data):** FY26 volume was 6.52 million t, up 7.4% from 6.07 million t, while value fell 4.5% to US$5.67 bn (FY25 US$5.94 bn). [Policy Circle, 28 Aug 2026]
  - More volume at lower prices is the signature of FY26 in basmati.
- **Coffee:** India's coffee export earnings rose from ~US$1.29 bn (FY24) to ~US$2.08 bn (FY26). [Spherical Insights, 2026; secondary, citing Coffee Board data]
  - Coffee Board figures for Jan–Apr 2026: exports 1.74 lakh t (+26.6%).
  - Instant coffee exports were 20,332 t (17,504 t a year earlier), and instant re-exports 38,169 t (30,274 t). [Coffee Board via KNN, 5 May 2026]
  - The 2025–26 crop is estimated at a record 4.03 lakh t: arabica ~1.18 lakh t, robusta ~2.84 lakh t. [Coffee Board via KNN, 5 May 2026]
- **Staples:** the company-commissioned Technopak report cited by Ganesh sizes the markets at ~₹1.85 lakh cr for wheat flour and derivatives, ~₹0.5 lakh cr for gram flour, and ~₹1.4 lakh cr for spices (2025). [Co: Ganesh IP Q1FY27, citing Technopak]
  - Organised shares are only ~13–16%, projected to grow ~18–20% a year.
  - This is a company-commissioned source and should be read as the bull case.
- **Poultry:** ICRA's rated sample expects revenue growth of **9–11% in FY27**. [ICRA Jul-26 poultry]

## Demand drivers
- **GST cut (22 Sep 2025):** most packaged foods moved to 5% (see Policy). Government said the cut would "reduce prices for almost all food products" and "boost demand". [IndiFoodBev, 9 Sep 2025]
  - The company evidence is mixed. Biscuit makers held ₹5 price points and changed grammage, and share shifted at the ₹4.50 vs ₹5 price points (Parle/ITC vs Bectors). [Co: Mrs Bectors Q4 call]
  - Staple flours were already at 5% and got nothing. [Co: Ganesh Q3 call]
- **Real volume is weak; price is doing the work.** CRISIL sees FMCG volume slowing to 2–3% in FY27 as crude-linked inflation hits household budgets. [CRISIL May-26]
  - Company results agree. Ganesh revenue fell 7% in Q1 FY27 on a heatwave, LPG shortages and elections. Zydus's seasonal brands fell 12%. Bikaji took two price rises before Diwali. [Co: Ganesh Q1 call] [Co: Zydus Q1 call] [Co: Bikaji Q1 call]
- **Rural risk from monsoon and El Niño:** [CRISIL May-26] [IMD/DEA via Mongabay, 2 Sep 2026]
  - CRISIL flagged that rural demand, which had outperformed for two years, could weaken with a below-normal monsoon (forecast 92% of the long-period average).
  - By 19 Aug 2026 the cumulative all-India rainfall deficit was 13%, and kharif sowing was 105.67 million ha, 1.5% below last year. Paddy was 34.48 million ha against 36.07 million ha; maize and soybean were lower.
  - INCOIS gives an 80–98% probability that El Niño persists from August 2026 through March 2027.
- **Channel shift:** quick commerce and e-commerce are growing fast but remain small for mass brands.
  - Zydus: organised channels were 38% of domestic sales in Q1 FY27 (23% a year earlier).
  - LT Foods: 40%+ online share in its basmati brand.
  - Bikaji: e-commerce plus quick commerce ~3% of sales.
  - Gopal: alternate channels are tiny.
  - [Co: Zydus IP Q1FY27] [Co: LT Foods IP Q1FY27] [Co: Bikaji Q1 call] [Co: Gopal Q1 call]
- **Export demand and diversification:** [MPEDA via Upstox, Apr-26] [Co: Freshara H2 call] [Co: CCL Q1 call]
  - Seafood: the EU (+37.9% in value), China (+22.7%) and Southeast Asia (+36%) offset a 14.5% drop in value to the US in FY26.
  - Gherkins go to Russia and Spain.
  - Instant coffee goes to 110+ countries.

## Policy: GST, duties, price controls and subsidies
- **GST 2.0 (56th GST Council, effective 22 Sep 2025):** [IndiFoodBev, 9 Sep 2025]
  - Rates moved to a two-slab structure (5% and 18%, plus 40% on sin goods).
  - Food items cut to 5% (previous rate in brackets): biscuits and baked goods (18%), chocolate and cocoa products (18%), malt and malt-based foods (18%), sugar confectionery (18%), pasta and noodles (12%), juices (12%), ice cream (18%), condensed milk (12%), soya milk (12%). UHT milk went to 0% (5%).
  - Namkeen and bhujia moved from 12% to 5%. [Upstox, Sep-25]
  - CRISIL estimates 50–60% of the FMCG portfolio is now at 5%, against 8–10% before. [CRISIL May-26]
  - Side effects: an inverted duty structure, where inputs are taxed at a higher rate than outputs, now affects some businesses. For example, Hindustan Foods says GST inversion is a working-capital cost for it. [Co: Hindustan Foods Q4 call]
  - GST disputes are live at Gopal (~₹69 cr plus a new ₹4.11 cr show-cause notice) and at Freshara (₹2.5 cr). [Co: Gopal AR FY26] [Co: Freshara AR FY26]
- **US tariffs on Indian goods: timeline.**
  - A 25% reciprocal tariff was raised to 50% from Aug 2025 (Russia-oil penalty). With anti-dumping duty (ADD) of 2.49% and countervailing duty (CVD) of 5.77%, the effective duty on shrimp reached ~58.26%. [MPEDA via Upstox, Apr-26]
  - The US–India deal (Feb 2026) cut the tariff to 18%. From Apr–Nov 2025, US importers had paid ~US$256 mn of extra tariffs on 90,591 t of Indian shrimp, and Ecuador (at 15%) overtook India as the top US supplier in Oct 2025. [Ocean Treasure, 13 Feb 2026]
  - On 20 Feb 2026 the US Supreme Court held that the emergency-powers law (IEEPA) does not authorise tariffs. The administration then imposed a 10% global tariff under Section 122 from 24 Feb 2026 for 150 days, later raised to 15%. Refunds of IEEPA duties go through customs claims or litigation. [WilmerHale, 20 Feb 2026]
  - Company read-through: Apex cites a 10% tariff plus ADD/CVD on the Q1 FY27 call. ADF has booked a US$2.08 mn tariff refund. [Co: Apex Q1 call] [Co: ADF Q1 call]
- **US shrimp anti-dumping review:** the final 2024–25 administrative review (published 4 Sep 2026) set 5.53% for non-selected Indian exporters. The CVD review is due in December 2026. [Federal Register, 4 Sep 2026] [Co: Apex Q1 call]
- **Iran and the Gulf:** the US "Operation Economic Outcast" (24 Aug 2026) widened secondary-sanction risk on Iran trade. The UAE suspended Iran dealings. In March 2026, ₹346 cr of rice consignments were stuck at Kandla and Mundra. [Policy Circle, 28 Aug 2026]
  - Iran was 13.9% of FY26 basmati exports.
  - The minimum export price on basmati has been gone since Sep 2024.
- **EU:** stricter checks on pesticide residue in basmati, and 50% port testing for Indian shrimp. [Policy Circle, 28 Aug 2026] [Co: Apex Q1 call]
  - India–UK trade agreement in force from 15 Jul 2026; India–EU FTA expected around end-2026 or early 2027. Freshara says the EU deal would remove its 7–14% gherkin duty disadvantage. [Co: Apex Q1 call] [Co: Freshara H2 call]
- **Seed price control:** the Cotton Seeds Price (Control) Order, 2015 caps Bt cotton seed prices. [SmartInfoIndia, 23 Mar 2026] [Co: Kaveri Q4 call]
  - Bollgard II MRP was ₹900 per 450 g packet in 2025–26 (₹864 in 2024–25); Bollgard I has been at ₹635 since 2016.
  - In March 2026 the government was "unlikely to raise seed prices" for 2026–27.
  - Bt varieties cover ~95% of cotton acreage. Illegal herbicide-tolerant Bt seed takes share in late seasons.
- **PLI (production-linked incentive) for food:** the scheme ends after FY27.
  - Bikaji: ~₹50 cr, about 150 bps of EBITDA.
  - ADF and others lose support from FY28.
  - [Co: Bikaji Q1 call] [Co: ADF Q1 call]
- **Ethanol policy:** the Economic Survey flags "an emerging tension between Aatmanirbharta in energy and Aatmanirbharta in food". [Economic Survey 2025–26 via Down To Earth]
  - Maize-based ethanol prices grew at an 11.7% CAGR over FY22–FY25.
  - Maize output grew at 8.77% a year, displacing pulses, oilseeds and cotton in Maharashtra and Karnataka.
  - Feed uses 60–70% of India's maize.

## Cost drivers
- **Crude and packaging:** CRISIL assumes crude at US$90–95/bbl in FY27, 30–35% higher year on year. [CRISIL May-26]
  - Crude-linked inputs, including packaging, are 25–30% of FMCG raw materials but only ~15% for food and beverages.
  - CRISIL expects gross margin down 300–350 bps and operating margin down 150–200 bps to 17–18%.
  - Companies saw the Gulf-war shock in packaging first. Bikaji's laminate cost went from ₹190–200 to ₹260–270 at the peak. Hindustan Foods' footwear polymers rose 50–60% and its freight rose 4x. [Co: Bikaji Q4 call] [Co: Hindustan Foods Q1 call]
- **Freight:** a Hormuz and Gulf disruption hit exporters broadly. [Co: KRBL Q1 call] [Co: LT Foods Q4 call] [Co: Apex Q1 call] [Co: Manorama Q1 call]
  - West Coast–Middle East container rates rose from ~US$500 to as much as US$5,000 (KRBL).
  - LT Foods saw rates of US$200 go to US$2,200–4,000.
  - Apex's shrimp freight "more than doubled".
  - Manorama's shea-seed freight lifted other expenses by 16%.
- **Edible oil:** palm oil is 26–27% of Gopal's purchase basket. Bikaji's edible-oil index is at 1.63x its base, and oil inflation is hurting Zydus's Nutralite. [Co: Gopal Q4 call] [Co: Bikaji IP Q1FY27] [Co: Zydus IP Q1FY27]
  - Oil-palm growers such as Godrej Agrovet gain when crude palm oil prices are high.
- **Grains and pulses:** [Co: Ganesh Q3 call] [Co: Ganesh Q1 call] [Co: Bikaji Q1 call] [Co: Gopal Q1 call]
  - Wheat and chana were 8–10% cheaper year on year through FY26. That helped staples margins but let unbranded mills undercut on price.
  - By Q1 FY27, moth and chana dal prices were rising on weak rain. Gopal had covered chana through Q3 FY27.
- **Feed:** maize is ~65–70% of poultry feed cost and soymeal ~25–30%. Feed is ~70% of a poultry company's variable cost. [ICRA Jul-26 poultry] [Co: Avanti Q1 call] [Co: Godrej Agrovet Q1 call]
  - In FY26, maize fell ~10% and soybean rose ~6%, spiking in May 2026 after a 17.5% fall in soybean output.
  - Fish meal spiked in Q1 FY27, cutting Avanti's feed EBITDA margin from 16.8% (FY26) to 7.5%.
  - Godrej Agrovet saw "sharp" maize inflation in 2026 and passed on 60–70% of it.
- **Milk:** farmer procurement prices rose ~6% over the year to May 2026. Amul and Mother Dairy raised consumer prices by ₹2/litre from 14 May 2026, citing feed, packaging film and fuel. Mother Dairy called it "only a partial pass through". [BusinessToday, 13 May 2026]
  - Godrej Agrovet's Creamline passes on ~70% and expects elevated milk costs for 2–3 more quarters. [Co: Godrej Agrovet Q1 call]
- **Paddy:** basmati paddy rose 20–25% during the 2025 season. [Co: LT Foods Q4 call] [Co: KRBL Q1 call]
  - Pusa 1121 reached ₹4,300–4,700/quintal by Feb 2026.
  - Both KRBL and LT Foods expect "firm" prices for the 2026 crop in an El Niño year, though 80–85% of the basmati area is irrigated.
- **Coffee:** robusta is at ~US$3,300–3,800/t per CCL, with El Niño risk to Vietnam's Nov–Dec harvest. [Co: CCL Q1 call]
  - The cost-plus model (EBITDA per kg) insulates CCL's margin. Lower prices cut working capital and revenue rather than profit.
- **Cocoa:** ICCO's August 2026 bulletin shows a 2024/25 surplus of 37,000 t. [ICCO, 31 Aug 2026]
  - Production was 4.733 million t (+8.5%) and grindings 4.649 million t (-3.3%).
  - Stocks were 1.309 million t, a stocks-to-grindings ratio of 28.2%.
  - ICCO flags El Niño weather risk for the coming season.
  - Cheaper cocoa weakens the price case for cocoa-butter substitutes, although Manorama says its CBE prices bear "no relation" to cocoa. [Co: Manorama Q4 call]
- **Wages and energy:** minimum-wage increases in Haryana (~30%), UP and Karnataka; LPG shortages affected sattu roasting and hotel/restaurant (HoReCa) demand; coal added 30–40 bps to Bikaji's manufacturing cost. [Co: Hindustan Foods Q1 call] [Co: Mrs Bectors Q1 call] [Co: Ganesh Q1 call] [Co: Bikaji Q1 call]

## Competitive structure and market share
- **Snacks and namkeen:** large national players compete with strong regional brands and a big unorganised tail.
  - Bikaji is the #3 ethnic-snacks company, largest in Bikaneri bhujia, and ~70% of its revenue comes from its top three states.
  - Gopal leads gathiya in Gujarat. It estimates ~65% of the Gujarat gathiya market is unorganised and names strong regional rivals: Balaji, Gokul and Shri Hari.
  - Grammage cuts in ₹5/₹10 packs are the main tool for passing on costs.
  - [Co: Bikaji ICRA Aug-25] [Co: Gopal Q1 call]
- **Biscuits and bakery:** large national players, including Parle and ITC, set mass price points. Bectors' Cremica has only ~35% weighted availability in North India and calls competition "very intense". [Co: Mrs Bectors Q1 call]
  - English Oven (premium bread) and quick-service-restaurant buns are Bectors' niche strengths.
- **Staples (atta/besan/sattu):** regional leaders such as Ganesh in West Bengal face national entrants (Emami in FY26, and Patanjali and Fortune earlier) and unbranded mills. [Co: Ganesh Q3 call] [Co: Ganesh IP Q1FY27]
  - Ganesh held share (+1 point in value, Nielsen) while the West Bengal packaged-atta category shrank 10.6% quarter on quarter.
- **Health drinks and wellness:** Zydus holds dominant shares in small niches: Glucon-D ~59%, Sugar Free ~96%. Complan is #4 in nutrition drinks behind larger peers. [Co: Zydus IP Q1FY27]
- **Basmati:** a consolidated branded top end.
  - KRBL (India Gate) has 37–41% of branded basmati; LT Foods (Daawat) has 23.1% (Nielsen).
  - LT Foods' Royal brand holds 60%+ of US basmati imports.
  - Exports are fragmented and Pakistan is a price competitor: ~US$990/t against ~US$1,150/t for Indian Pusa (Jan 2026).
  - [Co: KRBL IP Q1FY27] [Co: LT Foods IP Q1FY27] [Policy Circle, 28 Aug 2026]
- **Instant coffee (private label):** CCL claims ~10–11% of the global contract instant-coffee market at 77,000 t. Vintage Coffee is a fast-growing challenger (capacity doubled; a freeze-dried plant is planned). [Co: CCL Q1 call] [Co: Vintage IP Jan-26]
- **Shrimp:** India competes with Ecuador (lower US tariff), Vietnam and Indonesia. [Ocean Treasure, 13 Feb 2026] [Co: Avanti Q1 call] [Co: Apex Q4 call]
  - Avanti dominates shrimp feed and has a Thai Union link (Thai Union owns a stake and provides a technical tie-up).
  - Processors are fragmented. Apex runs at ~30–38% utilisation.
- **Feed and poultry:** Venky's and its parent group (VH Group), and Godrej Agrovet (cattle feed #1 focus).
  - Poultry prices are set by the market; integrators differ in genetics and feed conversion.
  - A Competition Commission (CCI) probe into parent-bird breeding practices is under way.
  - [Co: Venky's Q4 call] [Co: Godrej Agrovet IP Q1FY27]
- **Seeds:** multinationals (Bayer, Advanta) are gaining in maize, while Kaveri has lost cotton share for 7–8 years. [Co: Kaveri Q1 call]
- **Contract manufacturing:** Hindustan Foods is the scaled independent, with 42 plants and 8+ year contracts. The model is take-or-pay with negotiated pre-tax ROE of 16–22%. [Co: Hindustan Foods IP Q1FY27] [Co: Hindustan Foods Q4 call]
  - Ganesh's Hyderabad unit does job work for one large FMCG client. [Co: Ganesh Q1 call]
- **Specialty fats:** Manorama is a niche sal/shea/mango-kernel fat maker with 3–4 year customer approval cycles. Market share is not disclosed. [Co: Manorama CARE Mar-26]

## Where the profit sits
- **Highest and most durable margins go to niche ingredient or brand monopolies with sourcing moats:**
  - Manorama: ~26–27% operating margin.
  - Zydus: Glucon-D and Sugar Free, with a 17–18% EBITDA target.
  - Freshara: ~15–18% (company claim).
  - CCL: ~₹135–140/kg EBITDA.
  - [Co: Manorama Screener] [Co: Zydus Q4 call] [Co: Freshara Screener] [Co: CCL Q1 call]
- **Branded basmati is mid-cycle-rich:** KRBL guides 17–18% EBITDA for FY27 and LT Foods ~12% (global). Both hold a year of inventory, which amplifies price moves. [Co: KRBL Q1 call] [Co: LT Foods Q4 call]
- **Snacks and bakery sit at 7–14% EBITDA and are under pressure:** Bikaji 13–13.5% (including PLI), Bectors guiding a 14% exit rate, Gopal ~7% against 12–14% before its fire. [Co: Bikaji Q1 call] [Co: Mrs Bectors Q1 call] [Co: Gopal Q1 call]
- **Staples earn ~10–11%:** Ganesh's record 11.2% came from cheap wheat. [Co: Ganesh Q1 call]
- **The thinnest and most volatile margins are in commodity spread businesses:** [ICRA Jul-26 poultry] [Co: Apex Screener] [Co: Avanti Q1 call]
  - Poultry: ICRA's sample runs at ~5.6–6.0% operating margin.
  - Shrimp processing: Apex ranged from 3.1% (FY25) to 12.7% (Q1 FY27).
  - Shrimp feed: Avanti fell from 16.8% to 7.5%.
- **Contract manufacturing:** returns are capped by design (~16–22% pre-tax ROE), but the business has been cash-hungry: Hindustan Foods has had negative FCF every year. [Co: Hindustan Foods Q4 call] [Co: Hindustan Foods Screener]
- **Cash conversion separates the winners:** [Co: company Screener pages]
  - Converting well: Bikaji, CCL (improving), KRBL and Ganesh.
  - Fast-growing but consuming cash: Freshara (FY26 operating cash flow -₹57 cr on ₹38 cr PAT), Vintage, Hindustan Foods, Kaveri (FY26 OCF -₹6 cr).

## Where the cycle is now
- **FMCG broadly:** early in a margin-squeeze phase. The input shock (crude, packaging, freight) began in Feb–Mar 2026. CRISIL expects operating margins to fall 150–200 bps in FY27, with volumes slowing. [CRISIL May-26]
  - Company evidence: Bikaji took two price rises, Bectors expects Q2 to be only partly covered, and Gopal absorbed 0.8% of cost inflation. [Co: Bikaji Q1 call] [Co: Mrs Bectors Q1 call] [Co: Gopal Q1 call]
- **Poultry:** an upswing. Broiler prices were +31% year on year in Q4 FY26 and realisations +8–10% in Q1 FY27. ICRA expects FY27 margins of 5.8–6.0% against 5.6%, but feed (soymeal, maize under El Niño) is the risk. [ICRA Jul-26 poultry]
  - Venky's Q1 profit halved from Q4 as broiler prices fell from mid-May, which shows how short these swings are. [Co: Venky's Screener]
  - Big integrators plan ₹2,750–3,500 cr of capex over FY27–28, a 25%+ rise in gross assets. That is a late-upcycle signal. [ICRA Jul-26 poultry]
- **Shrimp:** partial recovery. The worst of the US tariff (58% effective) is over. [Ocean Treasure, 13 Feb 2026] [WilmerHale, 20 Feb 2026] [Co: Apex Q1 call] [Co: Avanti Q1 call]
  - Processors have margin tailwinds from the rupee and prices.
  - But farm-gate costs, feed (fish meal), freight and duty reviews (ADD 5.53% final; CVD in December) all point to a cyclical high in processing margins rather than a new base.
- **Basmati:** after a price-led peak, value fell in FY26 while volume rose. [Policy Circle, 28 Aug 2026] [Co: KRBL Q1 call] [Co: LT Foods Q1 call] [IMD/DEA via Mongabay, 2 Sep 2026]
  - Branded players had record Q1 margins on cheaper inventory.
  - Iran sanctions, Gulf freight and a firm 2026 paddy crop are the headwinds.
  - A 2–4 quarter cost lag means FY28 margins face the tougher comparison.
- **Coffee:** after the 2024–25 price spike, green coffee has eased and the Coffee Board expects a record crop. That helps volume-led converters (CCL, Vintage) through lower working capital, while EBITDA/kg stays steady. [Coffee Board via KNN, 5 May 2026] [Co: CCL Q1 call]
- **Seeds:** in a down-cycle. Industry inventory is high, cotton MRP is frozen, maize is shifting to multinational hybrids, and El Niño hurt kharif demand. Kaveri's Q1 revenue fell 14%. [Co: Kaveri Q1 call] [SmartInfoIndia, 23 Mar 2026]
- **Dairy:** input costs are in an upswing (procurement +6%) with partial pass-through. Godrej Agrovet expects relief only after 2–3 quarters. [BusinessToday, 13 May 2026] [Co: Godrej Agrovet Q1 call]
- **Specialty fats and cocoa:** cocoa has normalised (a surplus season), so the "cocoa too expensive" tailwind for CBE is fading. Structural adoption and customer approvals now matter more than price. [ICCO, 31 Aug 2026] [Co: Manorama Q1 call]

## Focus-area notes

### Packaged snacks and namkeen
- GST fell from 12% to 5% on namkeen and bhujia from 22 Sep 2025. [Upstox, Sep-25]
- Growth depends on distribution and focus states: Bikaji +18.9% in focus states and UP +37%; Gopal's twice-weekly deliveries lift run-rates 14–25%. [Co: Bikaji IP Q1FY27] [Co: Gopal Q1 call]
- Profit swing factors: palm oil, chana and moth, laminates, and the PLI cliff after FY27. Margins in FY27 are guided flat to slightly up only because of price and grammage actions. [Co: Bikaji Q1 call] [Co: Gopal Q4 call]

### Biscuits and bakery
- GST on all biscuits fell to a uniform 5% (from 18%), which removed the premium/mass split. [IndiFoodBev, 9 Sep 2025]
- The price-point battle is fierce: leaders held ₹4.50 packs while Bectors moved to ₹5 and lost share. [Co: Mrs Bectors Q4 call]
- Inputs: wheat, palm oil, sugar, milk powder and packaging. Bectors expects to recover inflation fully only by Q3 FY27. [Co: Mrs Bectors Q1 call]

### Health and nutrition drinks, dairy-based foods
- Malt and malt-based foods moved from 18% to 5%, condensed milk from 12% to 5%, and UHT milk to 0%. [IndiFoodBev, 9 Sep 2025]
- Zydus's growth is coming from non-summer brands and UK supplements (Comfort Click). Summer-dependent brands (Glucon-D, Nycil) failed two years running on weather. [Co: Zydus Q1 call]
- Milk (+7.6% year on year for Zydus in Q1) and edible oil (+6.8%) are the inflation points. [Co: Zydus IP Q1FY27]

### Coffee (instant and export)
- Record crop and exports, and instant-coffee re-exports are rising: India imports green coffee, processes it and re-exports. [Coffee Board via KNN, 5 May 2026]
- CCL (77,000 t, ~65–70% utilisation, a Vietnam base) guides ~15% volume and EBITDA growth for FY27. [Co: CCL Q4 call]
- Vintage is adding freeze-dried capacity (a ₹550 cr plant) funded by debt and equity, with pledged promoter shares. [Co: Vintage IP Jan-26]
- Competition for private-label contracts is increasing, and freeze-dried coffee carries lower EBITDA/kg. [Co: CCL Q1 call]

### Basmati rice (domestic, Iran/Gulf, EU)
- FY26: 6.52 million t, US$5.67 bn. Top markets: Saudi Arabia 16.6%, Iran 13.9%, Iraq 11.2%, UAE 8.2%, Yemen 5.7%, US 5%. [Policy Circle, 28 Aug 2026]
- Iran payment and sanctions stress rose after 24 Aug 2026. The EU is tightening pesticide-residue checks. [Policy Circle, 28 Aug 2026]
- Domestic branded: KRBL took a ~9% price rise in Q1 FY27 but lost 2 points of share in traditional trade. LT Foods' India share dipped to 23.1%. [Co: KRBL Q1 call] [Co: LT Foods IP Q1FY27]
- Governance matters here. KRBL has a qualified audit opinion linked to the Enforcement Directorate (ED) probe, and LT Foods has an insurance-claim appeal pending. [Co: KRBL AR FY26] [Co: LT Foods Q1 call]

### Animal feed, poultry and dairy
- ICRA FY27 (poultry sample): [ICRA Jul-26 poultry]
  - Revenue +9–11%; operating margin 5.8–6.0%.
  - Interest cover 4.8–5.0x (4.7x); debt/EBITDA 2.6–2.8x (2.9x).
- Feed makers face maize tension from ethanol. [Economic Survey 2025–26 via Down To Earth] [Co: Godrej Agrovet Q1 call]
  - Cattle feed (Godrej Agrovet +12.6% volume) is the steadier sub-segment. Broiler feed is being cut back.
- Venky's earnings now lean on animal health and oilseed, not birds. [Co: Venky's Q4 call]

### Shrimp aquaculture and exports
- FY26 exports were a record despite tariffs: shrimp US$5.51 bn and the US at US$2.32 bn (-14.5%). [MPEDA via Upstox, Apr-26]
- US duties now: [Federal Register, 4 Sep 2026] [WilmerHale, 20 Feb 2026] [Co: Apex Q1 call]
  - Reciprocal tariff: 50% → 18% (Feb 2026). IEEPA tariffs were then struck down, and a Section 122 global tariff (10%, later 15%) followed.

## Summary judgement
- FY27 is a year of **price-led growth and margin defence**. Branded players with pricing power and cost-plus or pass-through models (CCL, Zydus's niches, KRBL, Hindustan Foods) should hold margins. Mass snacks, biscuits and value staples will feel the squeeze.
- Export processors (shrimp, gherkins, rice) now face **policy risk** more than demand risk: US duty reviews, Iran sanctions, EU testing and FTA timing. Freight is the swing cost.
- Feed, poultry, seeds and basmati are exposed to **El Niño through March 2027** via maize, soymeal, paddy and farmer demand.
- Many of the fastest growers in this set (Freshara, Vintage, Hindustan Foods) have not yet turned profit into cash. Cash conversion is the key diligence test for FY27.
