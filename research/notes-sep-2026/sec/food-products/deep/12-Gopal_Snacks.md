# Gopal Snacks (GOPAL, ISIN INE0L9R01028)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Standalone figures (Screener; the company has no material subsidiaries) unless marked.

## Business and segments
- Rajkot-based maker of Gujarati ethnic snacks. It claims to be the "number one gathiya company" (gathiya is a gram-flour fried snack). [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- Product lines: gathiya (~29% of Q1 FY27 revenue), namkeen (~23%), snack pellets/fryums (~16%), potato wafers (~11%), plus papad, besan, spices, extruded snacks, noodles, rusk and wafer biscuits. [IP Q1FY27]
- FY26 category bases: gathiya ~₹410 cr, namkeen ~₹350 cr, fryums ~₹250 cr, wafers ~₹155 cr. New products grew from ₹25 cr to ₹63 cr in FY26. [Q4 call]
- Geography: CRISIL says ~70% of revenue comes from Gujarat (the "core state"). [CRISIL Sep-26] [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
  - Focus states: Maharashtra, Rajasthan, MP, UP and Chhattisgarh, ~₹400 cr a year.
  - UP sales are ~₹6.5 cr a month, driven by two gathiya SKUs.
- Distribution: 1,007 distributors (plus 135 micro-distributors) and ~5.25–5.5 lakh outlets, targeting 6 lakh by end-FY27. 97% of sales go through direct distributors. The ₹5 price point is ~60% of sales (from ~80% in FY22). [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Capacity: 407,428 MTPA in total. [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call]
  - Rajkot 105,233 MTPA (rebuilt after the Dec 2024 fire, restarted Q1 FY27; the Gondal plant has closed).
  - Modasa 83,431 MTPA (43% utilised).
  - Nagpur 72,788 MTPA (27%).
  - Third-party units at Kashipur (UP) and Chhattisgarh.
  - Blended utilisation is ~25–30%, guided at 43–45% for FY27.

## Capex and capacity
- The rebuilt Rajkot plant (105,233 MTPA) is operational, funded by insurance proceeds, internal cash and debt. FY26 capex was ~₹126 cr (investing cash flow). [Q4 call] [Screener]
- FY27 capex is guided at only ₹40–45 cr (a corporate office plus maintenance). Growth will come from utilisation: capacity is 3–4x current volumes. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings rose from ₹67 cr to ₹154 cr in FY26 (gearing 0.32x per CRISIL). The rise came from larger chana (chickpea) stocking ahead of inflation and from rebuilding capex. [Screener] [CRISIL Sep-26] [Q4 call]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 59 → 122 → 69 → 68 → 46. FCF: -14 → 96 → 39 → -15 → -100. [Screener]
- Cash conversion cycle 66 days; debtor days rose to 14. [Screener]
- Dividend payout 17% (FY26). [Screener]
- **CRISIL (18 Sep 2026):** A/Negative, outlook revised from Stable; A1 reaffirmed on ₹150.31 cr of facilities. [CRISIL Sep-26]
  - Reasons: "subdued business risk profile over the medium term", FY26 operating margin of 6.74% (the lowest in four years) and Gujarat concentration.
  - Downgrade trigger: cash accrual falling 30%.
  - Upgrade: 25% revenue growth at 8–9% margins.

## Moat evidence
- A leading brand in gathiya and a strong value-for-money franchise in Gujarat. CRISIL cites leadership in gathiya. Management estimates ~65% of Gujarat's gathiya market is still unorganised. [CRISIL Sep-26] [Q1 call]
- Distribution depth: 38% of core outlets now receive two deliveries a week, with a 14–25% run-rate uplift for those distributors. Inflation is passed through by trimming grammage in ₹5 packs, which were cut twice in Q1. [Q1 call]
- Weaknesses: regional brands (Balaji, Gokul, Shri Hari) are strong; Mumbai and Pune are "tough markets"; alternate channels are tiny (e-commerce ₹2.8 cr, modern trade ₹1.9 cr a quarter). [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Purchase basket: palm oil 26–27%, chana 21–22%, laminates (packaging) 18–19%, potato ~6%. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 inflation was 5% of costs; 4.2% was passed on through price and grammage, leaving 0.8% absorbed. Another 0.2–0.3% gap remains at current prices. [Q1 call]
- Chana is stocked to the end of Q3 FY27 and potato is in cold storage, so pulse inflation is lagged. Palm oil and laminates are bought at spot. Five of seven new products in H2 are non-palm-oil. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter pledge:** MD Bipinbhai Hadvani pledged a further 47 lakh shares to Tata Capital, taking his encumbrance to 1.05 crore shares (8.42% of equity), per the secretarial audit. [AR FY26]
- **GST disputes:** [AR FY26] [BSE filing 21-Sep-26]
  - Undeposited disputed GST dues of ~₹69 cr (FY17–FY22, GST audit, Rajkot branch), about 14% of net worth.
  - A new show-cause notice on 21 Sep 2026 for FY23 at Modasa: ₹4.11 cr including interest and penalty, over input-tax-credit matters.
- **Auditor:** emphasis of matter on the Dec 2024 Rajkot fire and insurance accounting (₹37.46 cr received in FY26). The key audit matter was the estimate of discounts and rebates. [AR FY26]
- **Credit outlook negative;** geographic concentration (~70% Gujarat); low utilisation weighs on ROCE (11.7%) and ROE (4.7%). [CRISIL Sep-26] [Screener]
- **Earnings quality:** FY26 PAT of ₹74 cr includes ~₹44 cr of other income, mostly insurance. [Screener]
- **Supply shocks:** gas supply limits at plants in Q4 FY26; April 2026 lost 5–6 working days when production moved from Gondal to Rajkot (₹12–13 cr of sales). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Shareholding:** promoter holding 81.45%, so the free float is small. FII holding fell from 3.2% to 0.75%. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Rajkot moved from "commissioning" to "operational". Q1 revenue was the highest ever (+31%), with five quarters of sequential growth.
- Guidance was extended to FY28 (EBITDA 10–11%) and a sustainable 11–11.5%.
- Q1 margin came in below the 8–9% range because of palm-oil and packaging inflation; advertising was cut to 1% to protect margin.
- CRISIL revised the outlook to Negative (Sep 2026). A new GST notice arrived after the call.

## My read
Gopal is in recovery: sales are growing again (+31% in Q1 on a fire-hit base), the rebuilt Rajkot plant is running, and capacity is ample for years of growth with little new capex. But profitability has not recovered. EBITDA is ~7% against a pre-fire 12–14%, raw-material inflation is eating into pass-through, and CRISIL has turned the outlook negative. The promoter pledge (8.42%) and ~₹69 cr of disputed GST are uncomfortable for a company with ₹478 cr of net worth. At ~59x trailing earnings, the stock needs the promised 8–9% margin in H2 FY27 and 10–11% in FY28. Watch the Q2 margin, receipt of the ₹35–40 cr insurance balance, and any further pledging.
