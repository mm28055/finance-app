# Zydus Wellness (ZYDUSWELL, ISIN INE768C01028)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Figures are consolidated (Screener) unless marked. Rs crore = ₹ cr.

## Business and segments
- Branded consumer-wellness company of the Zydus group (Zydus Lifesciences is promoter). Brands: Glucon-D (glucose powder), Complan (malted/nutrition drink), Sugar Free (sweeteners, plus Sugar Free D'lite chocolates/cookies), Nutralite (fat spreads, butter, ghee, mayonnaise), Everyuth (facial cleansing), Nycil (prickly-heat powder), RiteBite Max Protein (protein bars/snacks), I'm Lite, Cuticolor (hair colour, launched ~6 months before May 2026). [AR FY26] [Q4 call]
- Reported segments are only two: Food & Nutrition and Personal Care. [Screener]
- Portfolio buckets used by management: "Seasonal" brands (Glucon-D, Nycil), "Skin and Hair Care", "Food & Nutrition". Q1 FY27 growth: seasonal -12.0%, skin & hair care +34.5%, food & nutrition +16.0%; domestic +4.6%; international (incl. Comfort Click, like-for-like) +24.8%. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- Comfort Click Ltd (UK) acquired Aug 2025 via Alidac UK Ltd at an equity value of GBP 242 million — an online-first vitamins, minerals and supplements (VMS) business (WeightWorld, Maxmedix, Animigo) with GBP 134 million revenue and GBP 21 million adjusted operating profit for the year to Jun-2025 and a 57% five-year revenue CAGR. Top five markets: UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain; sold mainly through Amazon marketplaces and its own D2C sites. [AR FY26] [Q4 call]
- International share of revenue rose from under 5% to over one-third after the deal. [AR FY26]
- Market positions (MAT Jun-2026, Nielsen/IQVIA): Glucon-D #1 in glucose powder (~59% share); Sugar Free #1 in sugar substitutes (~96%); Nycil #1 in prickly-heat powder (~33%); Everyuth #4 in total facial cleansing (8.2% share, +40 bps), #1 in scrubs (49.4%) and peel-off (75.5%); Complan #4 in nutrition drinks (~4% share). [IP Q1FY27]
- Channel: organised channels (modern trade 17% + e-commerce 21%) = 38% of domestic sales in Q1 FY27, up from 23% in Q1 FY26; quick commerce ~7–8% of total business at Q4. [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call]
- Manufacturing: own plants include Sikkim (two units), Ahmedabad and Aligarh, plus contract manufacturers; FY26 capex was ₹101.7 cr. [AR FY26] [IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 861 → Sep-25 650 → Dec-25 965 → Mar-26 1,485 → Jun-26 1,437. Q1 FY27 was up 67% YoY because of the Comfort Click acquisition. [Screener]
- Quarterly operating profit (₹ cr) / OPM: 156 / 18% → 23 / 3.5% → 61 / 6% → 270 / 18% → 242 / 17%. The business is highly seasonal: most profit comes in the Mar and Jun quarters (summer brands). [Screener]
- Quarterly net profit (₹ cr): 128 → -53 → -40 → 162 → 119. Q1 FY27 PAT fell 7% YoY. Interest was ₹26 cr and depreciation/amortisation ₹57 cr, against ₹2 cr and ₹11 cr a year earlier. [Screener]
- Company definitions for Q1 FY27: net sales ₹1,430 cr (+66.7%); gross margin 65.5% (vs 54.8%); EBITDA ₹241.7 cr (+55.3%), margin 16.8% (vs 18.1%); "adjusted" net profit before brand amortisation +26.5%; adjusted EPS ₹5.28 vs ₹4.17. [IP Q1FY27]
- Annual revenue (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 2,009 → 2,255 → 2,328 → 2,709 → 3,961. OPM: 17% → 15% → 13% → 14% → 13%. Net profit: 309 → 310 → 267 → 347 → 197. TTM revenue ₹4,537 cr, TTM profit ₹188 cr. [Screener]
- FY26 company view: net sales +46.4%; domestic only +2.4% (seasonal -18.8%, skin/hair +21.9%, food & nutrition +15.5%); EBITDA ₹509.7 cr (+34.2%); PAT -43.2%; PAT before exceptionals and brand amortisation ₹355.4 cr (+2.3%). [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- Returns are low because of the goodwill from the 2019 Heinz India and 2025 Comfort Click deals: ROCE ~5–6% every year FY19–FY26; ROE 3.98%. [Screener]
- Valuation (25 Sep 2026): market cap ₹16,579 cr, P/E 74.8, price/book 2.84x. [Screener]
- A&P spend 18.2% of sales in Q1 FY27 (Comfort Click spends a higher share); like-for-like core A&P flat YoY. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **EBITDA margin target:** long-standing aim of 17–18% within "the next couple of years", excluding Comfort Click, restated on the Q4 FY26 call. Q1 FY27 reported 16.8% (down from 18.1%); management says like-for-like margin rose ~0.4 percentage points. **Kept; not yet met.** [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Comfort Click accretion:** at the deal, management said it would add to EPS within two years. Q4 said it was already EPS-accretive in Q4 FY26, and Q1 repeated this. **Delivered early.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Comfort Click margins:** Q4 said it was "in line or slightly exceeded" the ~14% EBITDA expected at acquisition; about 3 percentage points of reported growth was a currency (GBP/₹) benefit. [Q4 call]
- **Seasonal brands:** on the Q4 call, management expected recovery "from May onwards", and the chairman agreed that Q1 FY27 seasonal brands could grow on a weak base. Actual Q1: seasonal brands -12% (Glucon-D flat, Nycil sharply down because of rain in the East and North and high stock with retailers). **Missed.** Management now expects a "more positive note" for the rest of FY27. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Tax:** Q4 guided ~25% effective tax in FY27. Q1 came in at ~27% because UK thin-capitalisation rules disallowed some interest; about 12–15% of it will be paid in cash in FY27, rising to the full 25% in cash from FY28. **Slightly worse.** [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Interest cost:** the GBP bridge loan was refinanced into a cheaper EUR loan at the end of Q4. Q1 interest fell to ₹26 cr from ₹39 cr in Q4, and the Q1 run-rate should hold. **Better.** [Q1 call] [Screener]
- **Growth:** management avoids numbers — "double-digit" for international and for the non-seasonal portfolio over 3–4 years. RiteBite is growing at more than twice its pre-acquisition 25% CAGR, reaching about ₹244 cr in FY26 (figure put by an analyst, not disputed). [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹101.7 cr (routine plant spend). The big capital outlay was the Comfort Click acquisition (GBP 242 million equity value, about ₹2,800–2,900 cr at the time), funded by a GBP bridge loan and then refinanced into a EUR facility; Screener shows cash from investing of -₹2,881 cr in FY26. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- Earlier, RiteBite (Naturell India) was bought in Q3 FY24 for a total acquisition cost of ₹390 cr (including ₹21 cr contingent consideration). Naturell India was merged into the parent through voluntary liquidation on 20 Sep 2025. [AR FY26] [Q1 call]
- No new greenfield plant was announced on either call. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings rose from ₹188 cr (Mar-25) to ₹3,203 cr (Mar-26) because of the acquisition. The annual report gives net debt/equity of 0.53. [Screener] [AR FY26]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 237 → 92 → 246 → 380 → 226; free cash flow 162 → 47 → 220 → 315 → 126. CFO/operating profit fell to 50% in FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 35, inventory days 178 and payable days 107 in FY26 (Screener basis). The cash conversion cycle worsened to 106 days from 75. The company's own measure (average inventory/net sales) improved to 59 days from 67, helped by Comfort Click's low inventory. [Screener] [AR FY26]
- Dividend payout 19% in FY26 (10–12% in FY22–FY25); no buyback. [Screener]
- The standalone company has given ₹3,290 cr of corporate guarantees (Mar-26), mainly for the acquisition debt of its subsidiaries. [AR FY26]

## Moat evidence
- Near-monopoly shares in niche categories: Sugar Free 96%, Glucon-D 59%, Everyuth scrubs 49% and peel-off 76%, Nycil 33%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Pricing power and mix: gross margin rose 772 bps in FY26 and a cumulative 940 bps over FY25–FY26, helped by lower dextrose, sucralose and stevia prices, hedging and a mix shift to premium products. Q1 FY27 gross margin was 65.5%. [AR FY26] [IP Q1FY27]
- Sugar Free Green (stevia) has posted 21 consecutive quarters of double-digit growth; sweetener market share rose 24 bps (MAT Mar-26). [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call]
- Weak spots: Complan has only ~4% share in a shrinking category (-7.7% YoY), and it is growing through adult and toddler extensions. Categories such as glucose powder (-9.2%) and nutrition drinks are shrinking. [IP Q1FY27]
- The digital VMS platform (Comfort Click) brings in-house online-marketing skills; online is about one-third of the group. [Q4 call]

## Input costs
- Key inputs: milk (Complan, Nutralite, ghee/butter), edible oils (Nutralite), dextrose monohydrate (Glucon-D), sucralose and stevia (Sugar Free). [IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 vs Q1 FY26: milk +7.6%, edible oils +6.8%, dextrose -9.1%, sucralose +7.3%, stevia -8.2%. Versus Q4 FY26: sucralose -13.4%, edible oils +6.8%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Pass-through: cheaper dextrose and stevia widened gross margin through FY26. Oil and milk inflation hurts Nutralite. The company runs long-term procurement contracts and hedges, and holds buffer inventory. [AR FY26]
- Currency: part of Comfort Click's costs and all its revenue are in GBP/EUR, so a weaker rupee raises reported growth (~3 percentage points in Q4 FY26). [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Goodwill risk:** consolidated goodwill ₹4,942 cr plus ₹539 cr of indefinite-life brands. The auditor flagged impairment testing as a key audit matter because it depends on long-term growth and discount-rate assumptions. Goodwill is close to the entire net worth (~₹5,826 cr). [AR FY26] [Screener]
- **Weather dependence:** two years in a row of poor summers (seasonal brands -18.8% in FY26, -12% in Q1 FY27). High retailer stock of Nycil and Glucon-D. The Sep and Dec quarters barely break even. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [Screener]
- **Leverage:** gross debt ₹3,203 cr, in euros (currency and interest-rate risk), and ₹3,290 cr of standalone guarantees. [Screener] [AR FY26]
- **Low reported profit:** amortisation of acquired brands (~₹55–57 cr a quarter) and interest mean reported EPS will lag the "adjusted" EPS that management highlights. FY26 also had exceptional items (new labour codes, deal costs, Naturell liquidation). [AR FY26] [Screener]
- **Management change:** CMO Arijit Sengupta resigned effective 18 Sep 2026. The exchange asked for a clarification on volume movement on 8 Sep 2026; the company said there was no undisclosed event. [BSE filing, 18 Sep 2026] [BSE filing, 8 Sep 2026]
- **Disclosure:** no geographic segment reporting despite one-third of revenue being overseas (an analyst asked for it on the Q4 call; management said "we'll consider it"). Brand-level numbers are not given. [Q4 call]
- **Rating:** no current public credit rating found; the latest on Screener is CRISIL/CARE from 2020. [Screener]
- **Governance positives:** promoter holding steady at 69.63%, no pledge reported; S&P Global ESG score 84 (99th percentile). [Screener] [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone on the seasonal portfolio moved from "recovery from May" (Q4) to "impacted, but second half of the quarter recovered" (Q1). Q1 showed a third straight poor summer quarter.
- Interest cost fell after the EUR refinancing. Tax came in slightly above guidance because of UK thin-cap rules.
- Comfort Click growth stayed high (like-for-like international +24.8% in Q1 vs +31.4% in Q4). New steps: a US D2C site, a Walmart listing, noon UAE and a physical UAE base.
- Organised-channel share jumped to 38% (Q1) from 30% (FY26 average).
- Chairman Sharvil Patel attended the Q4 call but not the Q1 call; the CEO and CFO ran Q1.

## My read
The group is improving in mix: gross margin is up ~1,000 bps, and non-summer brands, RiteBite and Comfort Click are growing in double digits. But reported profit is shrinking because of acquisition interest and amortisation, and the core domestic business grew only 2–5% as the summer brands failed two years running. At ~75x trailing earnings the stock prices in a recovery that has not yet shown up in reported EPS. The view would turn more positive if a normal summer delivers double-digit seasonal growth and EBITDA margin reaches 17–18%. It would turn negative if Comfort Click growth slows (goodwill impairment risk) or if euro debt is not paid down.
