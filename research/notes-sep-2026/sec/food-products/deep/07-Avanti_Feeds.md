# Avanti Feeds (AVANTIFEED, ISIN INE871C01038)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Consolidated figures (Screener) unless marked.

## Business and segments
- India's largest shrimp-feed maker. Avanti estimates its share of the Indian shrimp-feed market at ~51–53%. It has five feed plants (installed capacity 775,000 t a year) in Andhra Pradesh and Gujarat, and a hatchery (600 million post-larvae). [AR FY26]
- **Shrimp feed** (standalone): Q1 FY27 revenue ₹1,566 cr, 82% of group sales. FY26 feed revenue ₹4,378 cr on 562,060 t sold, ~73% of installed capacity. [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call]
- **Shrimp processing and export** (Avanti Frozen Foods Pvt Ltd, AFFPL): FY26 revenue ₹1,689 cr and PBT ₹178 cr. Exports were 16,976 t in FY26, up from 14,149 t. [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
  - Nameplate processing capacity is ~26,000–28,000 t. The effective figure is lower because shrimp supply is seasonal.
  - A new 7,000 TPA plant is being built at Krishnapuram, Kakinada, with a ₹14 cr Axis Bank loan.
- **Processed-shrimp destinations**: North America 62.6% of Q1 FY27 sales (83.4% in FY24). Europe was 16.3% and Asia 20.6%. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Thai Union link:** Thai Union Group (Thailand) owns 24.21% and provides technical, financial and marketing collaboration. Avanti pays royalties to Thai Union Feed Mill. [AR FY26]
- **New lines:** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
  - Pet food under the "Avant Furst" brand (Avanti PetCare): Q1 sales ₹1.8 cr, still imported from Thailand; own plant planned near Hyderabad.
  - Fish feed.
  - An Ecuador investment with a partner.

## Moat evidence
- ~51–53% share of Indian shrimp feed; the Thai Union technical tie-up; long-standing farmer relationships and dealer network. [AR FY26]
- Pricing power is limited:
  - Andhra Pradesh government statutes give the state "a say" in feed prices, and a committee with a Big Four consultant is designing a formula linking feed prices to input costs.
  - Management says it must balance farmer affordability.
  - Feed EBITDA/t fell by more than half in Q1 despite the market share. [Q1 call]
- Processing: a higher share of value-added products (cooked, breaded, ready-to-eat). Management says this gives margins "upward of a general market margin level". Processing EBITDA margin was 13–16% over the last four quarters. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- US anti-dumping: in the 2024–25 administrative review (final results 4 Sep 2026), Avanti Frozen Foods is a non-selected company at 5.53%. The examined companies got 4.04% (Devi group) and 7.01% (Sandhya). [News: Federal Register, 4 Sep 2026]

## Input costs
- Main feed inputs are fish meal, soybean meal and wheat flour. Average consumption prices: [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

| Input | Q1 FY26 | Q4 FY26 | Q1 FY27 | Current (Aug 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fish meal (₹/kg) | 93 | 123–130 | 153 | 225 |
| Soybean meal (₹/kg) | 40 | 49–55 | 58 | 71 |
| Wheat flour (₹/kg) | 31 | 31 | 28 | 33 |

- Because spot prices are still well above the Q1 consumption cost, Q2 margins look set to stay under pressure.
- Causes:
  - Fish meal doubled from ₹100 to ₹240 in May–June 2026. About 75% of Indian fish meal is exported, with export incentives, and local purchases attract 5% GST.
  - Soybean meal rose ~45% on a short crop, El Niño fears and a higher MSP (₹5,708/quintal).
  - The industry is lobbying for export curbs on fish meal and duty-free soymeal imports. [Q4 call]
- Pass-through happens with a lag through periodic price increases (Feb 2026, then ~10% in Jun 2026), and is politically constrained in Andhra Pradesh. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter pay:** CMD A. Indra Kumar was paid ₹52.1 cr in FY26 (₹46.8 cr in FY25; ₹30.0 cr in FY24). Whole-time directors together received ₹90.9 cr on a consolidated basis, about 10% of PBT. Pay above SEBI limits needs a special resolution each time. [AR FY26]
- **Related-party disclosure gap:** sales to Thai Union entities fell to ₹12.8 cr in FY26 from ₹334.8 cr. Sales to "entities over which KMP has significant influence" rose to ₹420.2 cr from almost nil. The note does not say which entity bought the goods. Worth asking. [AR FY26]
- Royalty of ₹7.9 cr a year is paid to Thai Union Feed Mill, and a GST demand of ₹2.63 cr tax plus ₹2.64 cr penalty on these royalties is under appeal. Other contingent liabilities are small: ₹3.65 cr (standalone) of disputed demands. [AR FY26]
- **Auditor:** no qualification or emphasis of matter. Key audit matters were revenue recognition, the valuation of financial instruments, and inventory. [AR FY26]
- **US exposure** (North America 62.6% of processed-shrimp sales): anti-dumping and countervailing-duty reviews are ongoing and a tariff refund is pending. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- **Weather and disease** (El Niño), and the associate Patikari Power (25.88%), which lost ₹4.16 cr. [Q1 call] [AR FY26]
- **Disclosure:** calls are short (35–49 minutes, 2–3 analysts). Management declined to give effective processing capacity. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Shareholding:** promoter holding stable at 43.23% with no pledge noted. FIIs cut from ~14.5% to 7.2% in Mar 2025. [Screener]

## My read
Avanti is a cash-rich, dominant shrimp-feed franchise whose profits swing with fish-meal and soymeal prices. FY26 was a near-peak year (feed EBITDA 16.8%), and Q1 FY27 shows how quickly that can reverse (7.5%). Volume is healthy (+17%), processing margins have improved, and the treasury book cushions earnings. But pass-through is slow and politically managed in Andhra Pradesh, and management expects Q2 to be weak too. The view improves if fish-meal prices ease after the monsoon or the state formula allows automatic price pass-through. It worsens with another US duty shock or if promoter pay keeps rising faster than profit.
