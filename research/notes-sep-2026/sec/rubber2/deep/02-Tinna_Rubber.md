# Tinna Rubber & Infrastructure — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Screener code TINNARUBR (consolidated). Price ₹978, market cap ₹1,762 cr, P/E 28.6, ROCE 22.5%, ROE 22.1%, dividend yield 0.33% [Screener, 27 Sep 2026]. Consolidates Global Recycle LLC (Oman), Tinna Rubber Arabia (Saudi), Tinna Rubber Chile SpA (from Jan 2026), associate TP Buildtech (construction chemicals) and JV Mbodla Investments (South Africa) [AR FY26 note 39; News: ScanX, Jan 2026].

## Business and segments
- India's largest end-of-life-tyre (ELT) recycler. Shreds truck, bus and now passenger tyres into crumb rubber, crumb-rubber modifier (CRM) and rubberised bitumen for roads, micronised rubber powder (MRP) and reclaimed rubber for tyre/rubber makers, rubber granules for sports surfaces, and high-carbon steel shot/abrasives from tyre wire; new lines are polymer composites and masterbatch (PCMB), tyre pyrolysis oil (TPO) and recovered carbon black (rCB) [CARE Oct-25; IP Q1FY27].
- Sells EPR (extended producer responsibility) certificates to tyre makers who must buy them under the waste-tyre EPR rules — about ₹29 cr in both FY25 and FY26 (CARE: ₹29.56 cr in FY25) [Q4 call; CARE Oct-25].
- Q1 FY27 segment revenue: Infrastructure ₹62 cr (+7% YoY; volume +33%), Industrial ₹46 cr (+58%; volume +27%) — **includes ₹6.75 cr of EPR**, Consumer ₹6 cr (volume −20%), Steel ₹26 cr (flat) [IP Q1FY27]. PCMB revenue ₹12 cr (₹4 cr a year ago), ~8% of sales [Q1 call].
- Plants: Panipat, Gummidipoondi, Haldia, Wada and Varle in India; Oman; South Africa JV [CARE Oct-25]. Tyre-crushing capacity 1,85,000 TPA in India, rising to 2,35,000 TPA in FY27; utilisation 88% India, 78% Oman in Q1 [Q1 call]. MRP capacity going to 20,000 TPA by Q3 FY27 ("largest in the world" — management's belief) [Q1 call]. PCMB capacity 18,000 TPA after Gannaur (Haryana) commissioning; the original 6,000 TPA line runs at 82% [IP Q1FY27]. Pyrolysis 100 TPD feed [Q4 call].
- Customers: MRF, Apollo, CEAT, JK Tyre, Indian Oil, Hindustan Colas, Yokohama-ATG and others; top ten ~36% of FY25 sales [CARE Oct-25]. **In FY26 one customer bought ₹54.4 cr (>10% of standalone revenue), up from none above 10% in FY25** [AR FY26 note 38].
- ~55% of raw material purchases were imported in FY25; exports ~6% of sales [CARE Oct-25]. Export volumes +30% in FY26 and +46% YoY in Q1 FY27 [Q4 call; Q1 call].

- FY26 standalone revenue ₹533 cr, EBITDA margin 17.2%; consolidated EBITDA margin 17.1% [Q4 call].
- **EPR is a large slice of profit**: ~₹29 cr against FY26 PBT of ₹72 cr — about 40% (my calculation) [Q4 call; Screener]. Q1 FY27 industrial revenue included ₹6.75 cr EPR; stripping it out, Q1 EBITDA is ~₹27 cr, a margin near 18% rather than 22% (my calculation) [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- EPR accounting: credits are booked as revenue on accrual once there is "reasonable certainty" of compliance and receipt [AR FY26 accounting policy 2.10(g)]; unsold credits sat on the balance sheet as "EPR credits realizable" of ₹19.1 cr at March 2026 (₹17.9 cr March 2025) [AR FY26 note 11]. In Q1 FY27 all accrued credits up to March 2026 (about 1 lakh units, ~₹21–25 cr at the ~₹2,500/unit floor price) were sold for cash — this is cash, not new profit [Q1 call].
- Unit economics: virgin rubber ~₹240–260/kg vs recycled rubber ~₹50/kg [Q4 call]. Blended realisation below ₹40,000/tonne (analyst's figure; management did not confirm) [Q4 call].
- Margin drivers per management: cheaper feedstock mix (more passenger-car tyres), more value-added MRP/reclaim, scale; inventory gain "very marginal" [Q1 call]. Material cost fell to ~46% of sales in Q1 from >51% historically (analyst's calculation, not disputed) [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (25 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth 20–25% (≈₹650–680 cr on FY26's ₹542 cr); EBITDA margin ~18%; PCMB ~₹75 cr (8–10% of revenue); TPO + rCB ~₹50–55 cr; steel abrasive volume +30%; capex ₹100 cr over FY27–FY28, mostly from accruals with up to ~₹20 cr debt; EPR stable [Q4 call].
- Q1 call (22 Jul 2026): revenue ₹670–700 cr (kept, slightly firmer); margin "18% to 20%" but in the same call "our guidance for the year… remains to be 18% plus" — **effectively kept, not formally raised**; TPO/rCB 7–10% of revenue (~₹50–60 cr); steel abrasive volume +50% (COO) vs ~30% (same-day presentation) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Delivery in Q1: revenue +20% YoY (below the 24–29% the ₹670–700 cr guide needs for the year); margin 22% (beat, but ~4 points came from EPR — see above) [Screener; IP Q1FY27].
- Vision 2029: ₹1,000 cr revenue, 25%+ revenue CAGR, 33%+ profit CAGR, 18%+ EBITDA margin, 30%+ ROCE, 10 locations; earlier this was "Vision 2028" — the date slipped a year [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Project slippage (tracked):
  - Pyrolysis/rCB (~₹40 cr): CARE (Oct 2025) expected commissioning by Q4 FY26 [CARE Oct-25]; Q4 call: TPO stable by end-Q1 FY27, rCB trials Q2, both full by Q3; Q1 call: TPO commercial sales Q2, stable Q3; rCB production Q3, commercial sales Q4 FY27 — **a slip of one to three quarters** [Q4 call; Q1 call].
  - South Africa phase-2 crumb production: "early Q2 FY27" (Q4) → "Q2 or Q3" (Q1) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
  - Saudi 24,000 TPA plant (~₹20–25 cr): works "end of Q2 or Q3" (Q4) → "towards the end of this calendar year", subject to the Middle East [Q4 call; Q1 call].
  - PCMB 18,000 TPA by end-Q1 FY27 — **delivered** [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex "over ₹100 crores" (pyrolysis/rCB ~₹40 cr, solar ~₹12 cr, Delhi office, Sapane warehouse, PCMB) [Q4 call; CARE Oct-25]; funded by QIP (₹78.7 cr raised in Q1 FY26 at the issue price, 8,86,257 shares), term loans and accruals [AR FY26 Directors' report].
- FY27–FY28 plan ~₹100 cr (₹27 cr spent in Q1; ~₹60 cr to be capitalised in FY27) for crushing capacity to 2,35,000 TPA, MRP +3,500 TPA, South Africa phase 2, Saudi plant; a possible new greenfield plant is being studied [Q1 call].
- Payback: management expects TPO/rCB at 15–19% EBITDA margin and 2–2.5x asset turns [Q4 call]; ₹40 cr × 2–2.5 = ₹80–100 cr revenue at full run-rate (my calculation) versus ~₹50–60 cr guided for FY27.
- Oman: ~₹30 cr revenue in FY26 at 85% utilisation; ~₹9 cr in Q1 FY27 at 8.5% EBITDA margin [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- ₹5 cr R&D budget for FY27 [Q1 call]. Solar capacity 4.48 MW, 51% of power in Q1, saving ₹1.19 cr [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹129 cr at March 2026 (₹135 cr March 2025) [Screener]; management quotes debt ₹121 cr, net debt/equity 0.39 (0.73), interest cover 7.5x [Q4 call].
- FY26 operating cash flow ₹57 cr, but investing outflow ₹103 cr → free cash flow −₹49 cr; FCF has been negative for three years (−₹13 cr, −₹31 cr, −₹49 cr) [Screener]. Equity raised to fund the gap (QIP) [AR FY26].
- FY26 debtor days 45 (30 in FY25), inventory days 99 (83), payable days 80, cash conversion cycle 64 days (51) [Screener]. Management blames PCMB's longer credit terms and says blended working capital is ~50 days and will not improve [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Dividend ₹3.25/share final for FY26; payout 11% [Q4 call; Screener].
- Rating: CARE BBB-/Stable, A3 (13 Oct 2025) — low investment grade, citing leveraged structure, modest coverage, forex and regulatory risk, and capex execution risk; downgrade triggers include debt-funded capex abroad and big advances to affiliates [CARE Oct-25].

Capital commitments ₹19.7 cr at March 2026 (₹7.1 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 note 30].

## Moat evidence
- Scale and feedstock diversity: 1,85,000 TPA crushing across five Indian sites plus Oman; ability to recycle passenger tyres as well as truck tyres; import and domestic ELT sourcing; new sourcing base in Chile [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Approvals: tyre-maker approvals take "2 to 5 years" after multiple audits [Q4 call]. Management concedes entry-level technology and capex are "within reach of many people" [Q4 call].
- Road business ties: modification work aligned with Indian Oil refineries — "assured business over next couple of years" [Q4 call]; 15,000 t rubberised-bitumen processing order for FY27 [Q1 call].
- Pricing: price revisions quarterly with a one-quarter lag [CARE Oct-25]. EPR certificates trade at the floor price (~₹2,500/unit) — i.e., no pricing power there; supply of certificates is ample [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Competition: over 500 tyre recyclers registered on the CPCB EPR portal; 12.23 lakh t processed and 11.69 lakh t of certificates generated by March 2026 [AR FY26 MD&A]. New entrants in CRMB and rCB acknowledged [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **EPR dependence and accounting**: ~40% of FY26 PBT; booked on accrual before sale; floor-price trading; a change in EPR rules or floor price would hit profit directly [Q4 call; AR FY26 policy 2.10(g), note 11]. Analysts on the Q1 call argued that without EPR, FY26 PBT fell; management answered "Your numbers are correct" and said EPR is integral [Q1 call].
- **Related-party dealings**: sales of goods to promoter-linked Fratelli Vineyards (formerly Tinna Trade) of about ₹18 cr in FY26 (~₹27 cr FY25); purchases from promoter-linked entities; shipping services from B.G.K. Shipping LLP ~₹5.4 cr; loans from the chairman (₹3 cr taken, ₹5.6 cr repaid in FY26; ₹32 cr taken in FY25); professional fees to a relative [AR FY26 note 39]. The table layout in the report is hard to read; figures are my best reading.
- **Customer concentration rose**: one customer ₹54.4 cr in FY26 [AR FY26 note 38].
- Land dispute: HSIIDC demand of ₹3.73 cr for Panipat plant land; Supreme Court SLP dismissed; curative petition pending [AR FY26 note 33(d)].
- Contingent liabilities are small: claims ₹0.43 cr, disputed taxes ~₹0.5 cr, EPCG export obligations, bank guarantees ~₹9.9 cr; advance-licence export obligation of ₹32.9 cr outstanding [AR FY26 note 30].
- **Investment in promoter-linked BGK Infratech**: Tinna held ₹6.43 cr of shares in BGK Infratech (an entity in which directors are interested); BGK bought back 1,40,167 shares at ₹399.59 in 2025, and the balance was revalued by an independent valuer to ₹19.4 cr through other comprehensive income [AR FY26 note 32]. Small, but a related-party valuation gain worth watching.
- Auditors (S.S. Kothari Mehta & Co. LLP): unqualified standalone and consolidated opinions, **no key audit matters**; two foreign subsidiaries and the JV audited by other auditors [AR FY26 auditor's reports].
- Disclosure quality: annual report reissued via corrigendum (9 Sep 2026) — footers said "2024-25" and share counts of two investee companies were wrong; no financial impact [BSE corrigendum, 9 Sep 2026]. On the Q1 call the COO said EBITDA "exceeding INR50 crores" (actual ~₹34 cr), and the other-expenses figure given did not match the analyst's [Q1 call].
- Management change: CFO Ravindra Chhabra left 15 Mar 2026; Abhay Kumar from 16 Mar 2026 [AR FY26 note 39].
- Promoter holding fell from 73.81% (Sep-23) to 67.21% (Jun-26) — mostly QIP dilution, plus a 0.37-point drop in the June 2026 quarter [Screener].
- Geopolitics: Saudi plant delayed; Oman volumes hit; consumer segment binder prices tripled [Q1 call].
- Cyclicality: road construction and tyre industries make up over half of revenue [CARE Oct-25].

## My read
Tinna is a real, growing recycler with rising value-added share (MRP, reclaim, PCMB), and Q1 FY27 was its best quarter. But about 40% of profit is EPR income booked on accrual, free cash flow has been negative for three years, and every new project (pyrolysis, rCB, South Africa, Saudi) is running a quarter or more late. At a P/E of about 29 the price needs roughly 17% a year earnings growth for a decade — close to Vision 2029's 33% profit CAGR only if the new lines deliver. I would turn more positive on rCB commercial sales by Q4 FY27 and positive free cash flow in FY27; more negative on any cut to EPR floor prices or further project slippage.
