# Apcotex Industries — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Screener code APCOTEXIND (standalone; the company has no operating subsidiaries — **Screener's "consolidated" page is stale and stops at FY16, showing a meaningless P/E of 82.8; use the standalone page**). Price ₹599, market cap ₹3,108 cr, P/E 19.3 on TTM EPS ₹31.10, ROCE 19.8% (FY26), dividend yield 1.33% [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Makes emulsion polymers in two families: **synthetic latex** (XSB/styrene-butadiene latex, vinyl-pyridine latex for tyre cord, styrene-acrylics, nitrile latex for gloves) and **synthetic rubber** (nitrile butadiene rubber, NBR; high-styrene rubber, HSR; NBR-PVC polyblends; powdered NBR) [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 p.24].
- Capacity: Taloja (Maharashtra) 1,03,000 MTPA synthetic latex + 7,000 MTPA HSR; Valia (Gujarat) 21,000 MTPA NBR and allied products + 50,000 MTPA nitrile latex [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Aug-26].
- Serves eight end-industries: paper/board, carpet, construction, textiles, rubber goods, footwear, tyre cord and gloves; no product line is above ~20% of revenue [Q4 call]. Named clients include ITC, Asian Paints, UltraTech, Pidilite, JK Paper, MRF, SRF, Century Enka, Kordsa, Parker Hannifin, Gates, Supreme [IP Q1FY27].
- **Only Indian maker of NBR, nitrile latex and HSR** [ICRA Aug-26; AR FY26 p.23]. NBR share of the Indian market is only about 30% because Apcotex is capacity-bound; the rest is imported (Korea, China, Russia) [Q1 call; ICRA Aug-26].
- Reported segments are geographic only. FY26 sales of products: India ₹974.2 cr, outside India ₹452.6 cr (31.7%); FY25: ₹928.6 cr and ₹449.0 cr (32.6%). No customer is ≥10% of revenue [AR FY26 note 45]. ICRA puts exports at ~33% of FY26 revenue [ICRA Aug-26].
- Middle East/North Africa (UAE, Kuwait, Saudi, Egypt, Turkey) is ~12% of revenue; Europe + US only 2–4% [Q4 call].
- Nitrile latex share of revenue: management said "15% to 20%" on the Q4 call but "8%, 10% or so" on the Q1 call — management has given two different figures within three months [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Utilisation: 90–100% across plants in Q4 FY26; NBR at 100% for several quarters and will stay there until new capacity; nitrile latex near 100% [Q4 call].

  - Q2 FY26: 337 / 41 / 12% / 25
  - Q3 FY26: 331 / 44 / 13% / 22
  - Q4 FY26: 398 / 55 / 14% / 35

- The company's own Q1 FY27 figures: revenue ₹525.6 cr (+39.9% YoY), EBITDA ₹117.1 cr (+202.6%), margin 22.28% (from 10.30%), PAT ₹78.9 cr, EPS ₹15.23 [IP Q1FY27].
- Annual: FY24 revenue 1,125 / EBITDA 114 (10%) / PAT 54; FY25 1,392 / 125 (9%) / 54; FY26 1,442 / 177 (12%) / 101; TTM 1,591 / 256 (16%) / 161 [Screener].
- Plain words: FY24–FY25 were a trough (nitrile-latex glut, margins 8–10%); FY26 recovered on volume (+14% volume but only +3.5% revenue because prices fell) [Q4 call; AR FY26 p.16]; Q1 FY27 was a price-and-spread spike — volumes fell 10–12% (exports down, domestic up 10%) yet revenue rose 40% [Q1 call].
- Q1 FY27 quality of profit: ~2 percentage points of the margin came from inventory gains (cheap raw material bought before prices rose) [Q1 call]. Rest came from competitors' supply failures during the gas shortage and the Strait of Hormuz closure.
- FY26 included one-offs: ₹8.0 cr provision for pending litigation, ₹4.78 cr for new labour codes and ₹3.23 cr for gratuity/leave policy changes, ₹2.61 cr long-term incentive plan (recurring ~₹2.6 cr a year), ₹3.73 cr impairment of the Valia co-gen turbine and ₹1.97 cr extra depreciation from shortening its life [AR FY26 notes 34, 37, PPE note]. Adding back the one-time items puts underlying FY26 EBITDA near ₹193 cr (my calculation).
- Unit economics: not disclosed per tonne. Management frames the business in "average" margins of 15–16% through the cycle [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (7 May 2026): no quarterly guidance; FY27 margins "better than the average of last year"; FY27 volume growth "low double digits" if demand holds; no big capacity until Q1 FY28; covered on raw materials to end-June [Q4 call].
- Q1 delivery: margin 22.3% (well above FY26's 12.3%) — beat. Volume fell 10–12% — missed the low-double-digit volume hope for the quarter because MENA exports stalled [Q1 call].
- Q1 call (30 Jul 2026): restated "15%, 16% average margins"; said this year volumes may not grow but margins could hold; 22% is not to be annualised [Q1 call]. This is a "kept" long-run guide with a softer FY27 volume view — not a raise.
- Longer term: announced projects (≈₹220 cr) to add "probably about ₹600 crores" of revenue, roughly +40% throughput; doubling possible with further investment [Q1 call].
- Timeline drift: Feb-2026 presentation said projects complete "by the end of FY27" [News: ScanX, 24 Feb 2026]; Q4 and Q1 calls said NBR in early Q1 FY28 (~April 2027) and SB latex by end-Q1 FY28 (~June 2027), with a final date to be confirmed on the Q3 call — a one-quarter slip, flagged early [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Nitrile latex phase-2: plan and costs ready; decision deferred again by "three, four months" because of China and new Malaysian capacity arriving July–August 2026 [Q1 call]. On the Q4 call the decision window was "next three to six months" — unchanged in substance.

## Capex and capacity
- Programme: ~₹220 cr (company) / ~₹210 cr (ICRA) for NBR debottlenecking-cum-expansion (~₹130–140 cr, almost doubling NBR capacity) and SB/synthetic latex (balance) [Q1 call; ICRA Aug-26].
- Why the NBR project now works: the earlier plan cost ₹200–250 cr and needed anti-dumping duty; the team found a debottlenecking route at ₹130–140 cr; no big NBR additions are coming globally [Q1 call]. The Finance Ministry had not notified DGTR's recommended anti-dumping duty on Korean NBR [Q4 call; ICRA Aug-26].
- Funding: ~₹150 cr term debt plus internal accruals [ICRA Aug-26]; management: "capex so far has been self-funded… we will [borrow] in the next couple of quarters" [Q1 call]. Only 15–20% spent by July; most equipment arrives Q3–Q4 FY27 [Q1 call]. Capital commitments were just ₹1.57 cr at 31 Mar 2026 [AR FY26 note 41B], so ordering is back-ended.
- Payback maths (mine): ₹600 cr extra revenue on ₹220 cr is ~2.7x asset turn, in line with the company's fixed-asset turnover of 3.75x in FY26 [IP Q1FY27]. At a 12–15% margin that is ₹70–90 cr extra EBITDA a year from FY29, i.e. payback in roughly 3 years if the NBR spread holds.
- Other capex: ₹20–25 cr new R&D centre [Q4 call]; two group-captive renewable power projects to meet 60–70% of power needs [AR FY26 Chairman's letter].
- Growth runway before FY28 is small: management said perhaps 5–10% more output from the current plants at best [Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt fell from ₹189 cr (Mar-25) to ₹96 cr (Mar-26); cash and investments ~₹149 cr; interest cover 16.2x; debt/EBITDA 0.5x [ICRA Aug-26; Screener]. Net cash ~₹70 cr at March [Q4 call].
- Net cash dropped to about ₹40 cr in June as working capital rose with raw-material prices (the official transcript also records "about 30 crores" in the same answer — the exact figure is unclear) [Q1 call].
- FY26 operating cash flow ₹203 cr, free cash flow ₹165 cr, CFO/EBITDA 134% — flattered by ~₹50 cr working-capital release when raw material prices were low; management expects part of it to reverse [Screener; Q4 call].
- FY26 working capital: debtor days 62, inventory days 49, payable days 70, cash conversion cycle 40 days — best in a decade [Screener].
- Dividends: ₹8/share for FY26 (₹2.5 interim + ₹5.5 final), payout 41% [Q4 call; Screener]. Fund-based limit utilisation ~35% average over 12 months [ICRA Aug-26].
- Term loans carry 5.70–7.45% interest, repayable to January 2028 [AR FY26 note 20].
- Rating: ICRA AA- (Stable)/A1+, reaffirmed 26 Aug 2026; rated term loans enhanced to ₹196.9 cr [ICRA Aug-26].

## Moat evidence
- Sole domestic NBR/nitrile-latex/HSR maker; import substitution with shorter lead times and lower customer inventories [ICRA Aug-26; AR FY26 p.23].
- Customer approvals and specialty grades: some grades made for 2–3 customers "that nobody else in the world makes" (technical textiles, oil-field, specialty paper, battery binders under development) [Q4 call]; ~25 new products and 6–7 nitrile latex grades added since COVID [Q1 call].
- Operational resilience: dual-fuel plants (gas and coal) ran without a day's shutdown in March–April 2026 while many Gujarat plants cut output 50%+ [Q4 call; Q1 call]; multiple raw-material origins (styrene switched from Kuwait/Saudi to China) [Q4 call].
- Pricing power is real but cyclical: FY26 volume +14%, revenue +3.5% (prices down); Q1 FY27 volume −10–12%, revenue +40% (prices up) [AR FY26 p.16; Q1 call]. Pass-through is formula-based with a lag [ICRA Aug-26].
- Weakness in the moat: only 30% NBR share; exporters in Turkey/Egypt saw Chinese latex for the first time as Apcotex's costs rose [Q4 call]; nitrile latex remains in a global oversupply cycle [AR FY26 risk table].

## Risks and red flags
- **Undisclosed litigation provision of ₹8 cr** (booked Q4 FY26 on legal advice); management would not describe the case because it is sub judice [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 34]. Worth watching for more.
- Contingent liabilities are small: income tax ₹9.32 cr (R&D/80-IA disallowances, transfer pricing), GST ₹2.30 cr, customs ₹1.42 cr (2005 notice), service tax ₹1.13 cr — about ₹14 cr, ~2% of net worth ₹621 cr [AR FY26 note 41A].
- Auditor (Manubhai & Shah LLP): unqualified opinion; one key audit matter (revenue recognition — routine); no emphasis of matter; CARO lists disputed tax dues pending at CIT(A), High Court and Supreme Court [AR FY26 auditor's report & CARO].
- Related-party dealings are tiny: sales to promoter-linked Choksey Chemicals ₹4.6 lakh, consultancy fees to Apco Enterprises LLP ₹15 lakh, rent ₹10 lakh; KMP pay ₹5.4 cr [AR FY26 note 46]. No pledges; promoter holding flat at 58.23% for 12 quarters [Screener].
- CFO change: Sachin Karwa left 27 May 2025; Vivek Thakur joined 30 Jul 2025 [AR FY26 note 46].
- Raw-material shock and inventory loss risk: management said a sharp fall in crude would hurt because they stocked high-cost material [Q4 call].
- Demand destruction: auto-component customers "being squeezed really hard"; some ran plants at a loss [Q4 call].
- Export exposure (~12% MENA) stays hostage to the West Asia war [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Leverage will rise temporarily with ~₹150 cr debt for capex [ICRA Aug-26].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "managing the war" (Q4: covered to June, week-to-week) to "we captured constrained supply" (Q1: record quarter).
- Q4 margin (13.8%) was described as partly a March blip; Q1 margin (22.3%) was bigger and also partly timing — management was explicit both times not to extrapolate.
- Exports: Q4 said "modest impact"; Q1 said volumes fell 10–12% entirely due to exports while domestic rose 10%.
- Nitrile latex: Q4 said it "structurally remains still a challenging business"; Q1 said margins were above 15% in Q1 and "things are improving".
- NBR expansion logic: Q4 still spoke of wanting five years of duty support; Q1 said the cheaper debottlenecking route makes it pay without duty.
- New on Q1: borrowing will start in the next couple of quarters; Malaysian capacity mentioned as a reason for caution.

## My read
The business is clearly better than in FY24–FY25: nitrile latex has stopped losing money, the balance sheet is net cash, and NBR capacity will nearly double in early FY28 at a low capital cost. But Q1 FY27's 22% margin was a war-time windfall, and management itself points to 15–16% as the through-cycle average. On TTM figures the price needs about 12.8% a year earnings growth for a decade (P/E 19.3), which is reachable only if FY28–FY29 volumes rise ~40% as promised and margins settle near 14–16% rather than 10%. What would change my view: a clean commissioning date on the Q3 call and a phase-2 nitrile latex go-ahead (positive), or margins dropping back below 12% as crude falls and inventory losses appear (negative).
