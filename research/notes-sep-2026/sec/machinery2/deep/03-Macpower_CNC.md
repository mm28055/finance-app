# Macpower CNC Machines (NSE: MACPOWER) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Screener snapshot (standalone; no subsidiaries), 27 Sep 2026: market cap ₹2,063 cr, price ₹2,062 (52-week 761–2,175), P/E 53, book value ₹175, ROCE 29.1%, ROE 21.3%, dividend yield 0.07%. [Screener]

## Business and segments
- Rajkot (Gujarat) maker of CNC turning centres, vertical machining centres, turn-mill centres, drill-tap centres and, recently, double-column and larger machines (DCM 2518, LX 1000, LX 3000, TOM 200 with Y-axis). ~364 variants; one machine has ~1,000 components; ~19,000 components kept in stock. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- No segment reporting. Price bands: low-end ₹10–20 lakh, mid ₹20–30 lakh, high-end ₹30 lakh–₹2 cr. Average realisation ~₹20 lakh per machine (new order bookings ~₹20.9 lakh in Q1 FY27). The premium "NEXA" range is ~40% of the order book and 39–40% of Q1 revenue, at ~₹29 lakh average (FY26), expected ₹29–32 lakh in FY27. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Customers: mostly Tier-3/Tier-4 job shops, general engineering, plastic dies & moulds; growing EMS (drill-tap centres, 2–3 a month) and references at BHEL, HAL, Ordnance Factory, Bharat Forge, Kirloskar, Polycab. Defence order book only ~₹20 cr; defence revenue "single digits" %. >50% of orders are repeat orders. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Market position: management puts its share at ~2% of Indian machine-tool production and ~1% of consumption (value); competition is "6 or 7 domestic players" holding ~90% of the domestic-made market. Aim: 10% share in five years. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Capacity: 2,500 machines a year (raised from 2,000 in FY26), using three rented premises; utilisation ~80% at Q4 FY26, expected >90% in FY27. Bottlenecks in components: spindle capacity ~1,700–1,800 and powder coating ~2,000 machines, so job work is outsourced. In-house share of components ~40–45%. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26]

- Seasonality: Q1 is typically 15–20% of the year, Q2 and Q3 similar (~25–30% each by management's arithmetic), Q4 30–35%. [Q1 call]

FY26 ~1,650–1,950 machines (management gave manufacturing of 1,800–1,950 units, declined exact sales). Management says the existing plant has ~₹500 cr revenue capacity at ₹20 lakh per machine. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (28 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth 28–30%; EBITDA margin kept at FY26 level or +1–2 points ("will not go below"); 13-acre leased site with ₹30–35 cr capex (₹32–35 cr in opening remarks); FY27 capex ₹30–40 cr; 60-acre government land expected in 3–4 months; cash from operations/PAT to stay ~40%; 25% EBITDA margin only after the 60-acre backward-integration plant. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (30 Jul 2026): revenue guidance **raised** to "30%+"; margins to rise each quarter from 16.2% (20% "confirmed" or not by Q3); 13-acre plant budget **raised** to ~₹50 cr (to use Gujarat subsidies), completion in 12 months, use from Q2 FY28; 60-acre land policy "within 15 days to a month". Later in the same call management also said "28–30%" growth — slight inconsistency. [Q1 call]
- AR FY26 MD&A says the 13-acre expansion "will enable us to sustain an annual growth rate of around 20%" — lower than the call guidance. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: Q1 revenue +56% and margin 16.2% (held); ~90% of the 80 finished machines held at March converted in Q1; receivables fell from ₹46 cr to ₹33 cr; 13-acre lease signed (30 years, not 25). Missed: the 60-acre government land — "signed by March end" (Feb call, per an analyst), then "3–4 months" (May), then "15 days to a month" (July) — still not signed. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹15.4 cr (FY25 ₹12.4 cr), all internal; Q1 FY27 ₹2.25 cr. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- 13-acre site (9 acres buildable + 4 green belt) on a 30-year registered lease at a token rent (<1% a year of ~₹40 cr land value): ~₹50 cr for a centrally air-conditioned 1.5–2 lakh sq ft assembly hall, component capacity (spindles, sheet metal) and debottlenecking; contractor appointed. Phase-1 provision for +1,000–1,500 machines once existing capacity is used. Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026: 25% capital subsidy (paid over 5 years) and 7% interest subsidy on bank loans at ~8.25%. Funding: ₹20–25 cr own cash plus subsidised debt; no equity raise planned. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- 60-acre government plot (defence/aerospace policy) is the long-term plan: Phase-1 2,000 machines for ~₹100 cr capex, 10,000 machines within five years, 50% for defence work, and a technology/co-branding tie-up with a European partner (talks ongoing; announcement "alongside" the capacity). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Payback (my arithmetic): ₹50 cr gross (≈₹37.5 cr after the 25% subsidy) for +1,000–1,500 machines ≈ ₹200–300 cr of extra revenue at ₹20 lakh, i.e. 5–8x asset turns if filled.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Effectively debt-free: borrowings ₹1 cr at FY26 (₹3 cr FY25); net cash surplus per company. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- Cash from operations FY24 17, FY25 7, FY26 14 cr; free cash flow FY26 ₹3 cr; CFO/operating profit 48%. Management expects CFO/PAT to stay ~40% during capex. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Working capital is heavy and rising: inventory days 259, debtor days 51, payable days 128, cash conversion cycle 182 days (FY26; 134 days in FY23). Management deliberately holds a year of imported parts (ball screws, LM guides from Taiwan) and says it builds machines only against advances. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Bank stock statements (inventory + receivables) ₹145.6 cr at Mar-26, small differences to books (₹0.6 cr). Dividend payout 4%. [AR FY26 Note 50; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Price increases stuck: 4–6% hike on new invoices from 1 June 2026, with existing orders given the choice to lift early. [Q1 call]
- Order inflow outpacing execution: Q1 FY27 inflow ~₹145 cr vs execution ₹95 cr; order book ₹456 cr (+32% YoY; ₹406 cr at March; ₹331 cr a year earlier), about 1.2x TTM sales. [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Fast delivery from stocked parts (competitors short of imported ball screws/guides) and a growing service network (10 branches, 8 technology centres, presence in 39 cities). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Limits: 99% of controllers are Fanuc/Siemens/Mitsubishi; 5-axis machines sold only 1–2 a year; competes mainly with domestic peers, not imports; R&D and technology depth modest. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Capacity wall:** FY27 near 90% use of 2,500 machines; growth in FY28 depends on the 13-acre plant starting in Q2 FY28 and on outsourced job work, which already pressured Q4 FY26 margin. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Serial land delays:** the 60-acre plot (first announced as 35 acres in March 2024) has slipped for over two years. [Q4 call]
- **Working capital:** inventory days rose from 184 (FY23) to 259 (FY26); cash conversion is weak (FY26 FCF ₹3 cr on ₹34 cr profit). [Screener]
- **Governance details:** the CMD (Rupesh Mehta) and a whole-time director (Nikesh Mehta) are paid from 1 Oct 2025 as "professional/management consultancy fees" (₹3 lakh and ₹2 lakh a month plus GST) rather than salary; auditor is a small Rajkot firm (S. C. Makhecha & Associates, audit fee ₹7 lakh). Related-party trade is small (sales of ₹1.48 cr to Modern Machine Tools). Promoters hold 73.22%; no pledge disclosed. [AR FY26 corporate governance report; Screener]
- Auditor's report FY26: unmodified; emphasis of matter on expired office leases (Jamnagar, Pune, Coimbatore, Hyderabad) pending renewal and two modified leases (right-of-use asset ₹2.59 cr and lease liability ₹2.40 cr derecognised) — housekeeping, not financial. Key audit matters: revenue recognition and contingent liabilities. Contingent liabilities: bank guarantees/LCs ₹11.6 cr only. [AR FY26 auditor's report, Notes 32, 48, 49]
- Disclosure: management declines unit volumes and sector split; no credit rating (no material debt). [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Guidance raised (28–30% → 30%+) and capex raised (₹30–35 cr → ₹50 cr) because the new Gujarat policy made subsidies available.
- The 13-acre site moved from "final stages" to a signed 30-year lease with a contractor appointed.
- Finished-goods overhang (80 machines at March) cleared; receivables down to ₹33 cr.
- Tone on the European JV softened: pilot/sampling only; focus on own backlog. Order inflow reached ~₹145 cr a quarter (record).

## My read
Macpower is executing well: record Q1 (sales +56%, profit +110%), a ₹456 cr order book growing faster than sales, price hikes accepted and a debt-free balance sheet with ~29% ROCE. The limits are physical and financial: the plant is nearly full until the 13-acre site opens around Q2 FY28, margins have plateaued at ~16% because of outsourced job work, and heavy inventory keeps cash conversion poor. At ~53x trailing earnings the price needs ~30% growth for several years plus margin gains towards 18–20%; the 30%+ call guidance is credible for FY27, but the annual report's "around 20%" and the repeated 60-acre slippage argue for caution on FY28–FY29. What would change the view: positive — the 60-acre land signed and a named technology partner, margins above 18%; negative — a construction delay at the 13-acre site or inventory days rising further.
