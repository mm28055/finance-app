# Jyoti CNC Automation (NSE: JYOTICNC) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Screener snapshot (27 Sep 2026): market cap ₹24,223 cr, price ₹1,065 (52-week 580–1,135), P/E 75.3, book value ₹88, ROCE 21.3%, ROE 18.2%, no dividend. [Screener]

## Business and segments
- Makes metal-cutting CNC machines in Rajkot (India) and, through step-down subsidiary Huron Graffenstaden SAS, in Strasbourg (France). 200+ variants: entry-level turning/milling centres (up to ₹50 lakh), mid-range (₹50 lakh–₹2 cr), high-end 5-axis and multi-tasking machines (over ₹2 cr). 1,40,000+ machines installed in 60+ countries; 15,000+ customers in India. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- One reported segment (CNC machines); split disclosed by end-industry. Q1 FY27 revenue: aerospace & defence (A&D) 37%, auto & auto components 35%, general engineering 17%, EMS 6%, others 5%. FY26 revenue: A&D 39%, auto 26%, general engineering 23%, rest EMS/dies & moulds/others. [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Mix by value is about 40% high-end, 60% entry/mid-level. Q1 FY27 units: 1,406 machines (entry 1,349; mid 33; high-end 24) vs 1,117 a year earlier. Average realisation ~₹34.6 lakh per machine (Q1 FY26 ₹34.4 lakh; FY26 ₹35.6 lakh), down from ₹40–45 lakh in FY25 as entry-level volume grows. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Capacity: 6,000 machines a year (India + Huron). Utilisation 86% in Q1 FY27; above 100% in Q4 FY26 (1,760 machines shipped in Q4; 5,550 in FY26). A further 10,000 machines a year (to 16,000) is due to start by end-September 2026. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Huron: standalone revenue ₹248 cr in FY26, of which ₹105 cr was sold to Jyoti India. Management says Huron capacity is about €75 million (≈₹750 cr) of large machines after the Nov–Dec 2025 expansion. About 70% of Huron's machine content is made in India. [Q4 call; official Q1 transcript]
- Exports were only 5.2% of standalone turnover in FY26 (BRSR). Standalone FY26 turnover ₹1,949 cr. [AR FY26 BRSR]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit: Jun-25 410 / 100 / 24% / 71; Sep-25 508 / 125 / 25% / 86; Dec-25 576 / 155 / 27% / 89; Mar-26 599 / 147 / 25% / 91; Jun-26 508 / 109 / 21% / 57. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: sales +24% YoY, but operating profit only +9% and net profit −20%, because Huron booked costs without revenue and there was a ₹10 cr unrealised forex loss (vs a forex gain a year earlier). Adjusted EBITDA ₹119 cr (23.4%). [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- India business alone (standalone) is strong: Q1 FY27 revenue ₹509 cr (+37%), adjusted EBITDA ₹145 cr (28.4%), reported EBITDA ₹139 cr (27.2%), PAT ₹87.5 cr (+21%), gross margin 52.8% (+230 bps). Standalone revenue includes ₹35 cr of sales to Huron. [IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: FY24 sales 1,338, OP 301 (22%), NP 151; FY25 1,818, 491 (27%), 316; FY26 2,093, 527 (25%), 336. TTM 2,191 / 535 / 322. Three-year sales CAGR 31%. [Screener]
- FY26 consolidated growth was only 15% (vs 36% in FY25) because of a ₹67 cr reversal of Huron revenue in Q4 FY26; ex-reversal growth would have been ~19%. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Per-unit economics: at ~₹34–35 lakh per machine and a 25% EBITDA margin, each machine earns ~₹8.5–9 lakh of EBITDA. An analyst on the Q4 call calculated standalone EBITDA per machine fell from ~₹13 lakh to ~₹10.85 lakh; management declined to give per-machine figures and pointed to the 25% margin. [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (29 May 2026): EBITDA margin "at least 25%" even at ₹30 lakh average price; 25–30% growth momentum; FY27 machines 8,000–9,000; Huron FY27 revenue back to ₹300–350 cr; ₹67 cr reversed revenue to be booked "once the licence comes", which management said could happen in Q1 FY27; new capacity in Q2 FY27; declined to quantify FY27 revenue. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (7 Aug 2026): 25–30% revenue growth and ~25% EBITDA margin **kept**; machines ">8,000" (low end of the earlier range); Huron guidance **cut** to ₹300–325 cr revenue at 8–10% EBITDA margin; FY27 order intake guided at ₹2,500–3,000 cr; capex ₹200–250 cr; operating cash flow ~50% of EBITDA; debt flat. [Q1 call]
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: Q1 consolidated growth 24% (below the 25–30% band) and reported margin 21.4% (below 25%). The ₹67 cr was **not** recognised; instead ~₹35 cr more Huron output went unbilled, so about ₹100 cr (7–8 machines) now waits on export licences. Capacity timing held (commissioning by end-September; foundry ~one month late, October). Standalone India beat (+37%). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Record: an unusual quarter-on-quarter order-intake dip (Q4 ₹700 cr+ → Q1 ₹601 cr); execution in Q1 ₹485 cr. Order book ₹4,848 cr at 30 Jun 2026 (₹4,732 cr at 31 Mar), about 2.2 years of TTM sales; management said the book is to be executed in 18–20 months. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- 10,000-machine Rajkot expansion budgeted at ~₹450 cr; FY26 consolidated capex ₹323 cr (cash spent on PP&E incl. CWIP); FY27 guided at ₹200–250 cr, including maintenance. Funded mainly by a ₹300 cr term loan (to June 2034) plus internal accruals. [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A; Infomerics Apr-26]
- Status on 7 Aug 2026: assembly building and machine shop ready (machine shop ~80% built with machines installed), sheet-metal and paint shops being fitted, foundry due in October. Infomerics notes a tentative commercial start of October 2026. [Q1 call; Infomerics Apr-26]
- Asset turns (my arithmetic): 10,000 machines at ₹30–35 lakh each = ₹3,000–3,500 cr of annual revenue on ~₹450 cr of capex, i.e. 7x+ asset turns if filled, but working capital (inventory ~450 days) is the real capital need, not the building.
- Further options: 20 acres bought in the Tumakuru Machine Tools Park, Karnataka (funded from internal accruals); an in-house CNC controller, drives and motors ("HUMA") with commercialisation targeted within ~2 years, pending approval under a government incentive scheme that management says gives ~25% central + ~25% state capital subsidy. [Infomerics Apr-26; Q1 call]
- Huron capacity was doubled in FY26 (first investment in Huron since the 2007 purchase); first dispatches from the new assembly were expected August–September 2026. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings (Screener): ₹304 cr (FY24) → ₹497 cr (FY25) → ₹853 cr (FY26); cash ₹53 cr at FY26, so net debt ≈ ₹800 cr (≈1.5x FY26 EBITDA). Management: debt in June 2026 "almost the same as March"; self-imposed limit of debt ≤ 2x EBITDA. [Screener; IP Q4FY26; Q1 call]
- Interest cost ₹70 cr in FY26 (₹42 cr FY25); Q1 FY27 interest ₹24 cr, the highest quarter since listing, and not capitalised because parts of the new plant are already in use. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Cash from operations: FY24 −48, FY25 −105, FY26 +54 cr; free cash flow FY26 −269 cr. Cumulative three-year CFO is negative despite ₹1,319 cr of EBITDA. [Screener]
- Working capital: inventory days 453, debtor days 104, payable days 198, cash conversion cycle 359 days (FY26). Inventory ₹1,166 cr (₹900 cr a year earlier); receivables ₹599 cr (₹487 cr). Management built stock "over the past 9 months" for the ramp-up and promises a "very positive surprise" in operating cash flow. [Screener; IP Q4FY26; Q1 call]
- No dividend; no buyback. IPO money (Jan 2024) repaid most debt, which has since been rebuilt. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- India's largest listed CNC maker by revenue; management put its share at ~10% of the Indian CNC market (question on the call). Imports met ~62% of Indian machine-tool consumption last year, so share gains are about import substitution. [official Q1 transcript]
- A&D credentials: FY26 A&D revenue above ₹800 cr, ~75% executed from India; preferred-supplier status with European primes setting up in India (MBDA orders received in Q1). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Pricing: raised prices on all new orders from 1 May 2026 after 2–3% raw-material inflation; existing orders are fixed-price, cushioned by long build cycles. [Q4 call]
- Scale and breadth: 200+ variants, 29 sales/service centres, 15,000+ customers; new NX double-column machine for railways targets a category where India imported 300+ machines last year at ₹3–5 cr each. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Limits: entry-level machines (the bulk of volume) compete with Taiwanese, Korean and Chinese imports; a rival was cited as adding a 10,000-machine plant in Hosur. Realisation per machine is falling. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor's emphasis of matter (FY26):** the standalone auditor (Choksi & Co.) flags a *material uncertainty over the going-concern status of the French subsidiary*: Jyoti SAS's net worth is eroded by Huron's recurring losses, and there is an ongoing French judicial investigation and machines under customs control. Jyoti India carries ₹341.6 cr of investment plus ₹160.1 cr of loans/advances in Jyoti SAS, which management has not impaired. Opinion not qualified. [AR FY26 auditor's report, Note 42]
- **French judicial investigation** (disclosed 12 Apr 2026) into suspected export of dual-use machines against EU law: Huron's director-general barred from duties, control over export licences of certain machines, interim seizure of funds (€4.0 m per Infomerics in April; €3.02 m "at present" per AR Note 42) and two residential properties of Jyoti SAS. On the Q4 call management said it could take "2, 3 or 5 years"; the stock fell ~16% on disclosure. [AR FY26 Note 42; Infomerics Apr-26; Q4 call; News: Business Standard, 13 Apr 2026]
- **Promoter pledge:** promoter Anilkumar Virani still has 1.524 cr shares (6.70% of the company, ~46% of his stake) pledged to HDFC Bank for a personal business loan after releasing 0.84% on 16 Sep 2026. Total promoter holding is 62.55% (unchanged). [BSE SAST filing, 17 Sep 2026; Screener]
- **Accounting judgement and disclosure:** Huron moved from percentage-of-completion to delivery-based recognition until export licences are in hand; ₹67 cr reversed in Q4 FY26, ~₹35 cr unbilled in Q1 FY27; Huron numbers are not separately disclosed each quarter (an analyst asked for this). The Q1 call also raised investor concerns about HR/finance processes and the auditor; management gave no specific change. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Cash burn:** three years of negative or weak operating cash flow; ₹1,166 cr of inventory; the whole de-leveraging case rests on the new plant releasing stock in H2 FY27. [Screener; IP Q4FY26]
- **EMS delay:** EMS order book ~13% (~₹630 cr) has been static ~1.5 years as customers wait for component-scheme approvals. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Other: Brickwork kept the company in "issuer not cooperating" (May 2025); Infomerics rates bank lines IVR A+/Stable, A1 (reaffirmed 21 Apr 2026) on a standalone basis. Contingent liabilities are small (disputed taxes ~₹25 cr; bank guarantees/LCs ~₹441 cr; capital commitments ₹56 cr). A fire in a coating facility at Rajkot on 26 Sep 2026 was contained within an hour, with no material loss per the company. [Infomerics Apr-26; AR FY26 Note 33; BSE, 27 Sep 2026]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone on Huron shifted from "deferment, to be booked soon" to "not in the very near future"; the licence delay was reframed as an EU-wide geopolitical issue (customers in China and Turkey named). The unbilled pile grew from ₹67 cr to ~₹100 cr.
- Huron FY27 guidance was trimmed (₹300–350 cr → ₹300–325 cr) and an 8–10% EBITDA margin was given for the first time.
- New disclosures: FY27 order-intake target (₹2,500–3,000 cr), capex (₹200–250 cr), cash conversion (~50% of EBITDA), NX railway machine, record July bookings (1,000+ machines in a month in India; 250+ in Rajkot alone).
- Unchanged: 25–30% growth, ~25% margin, September commissioning.

## My read
The India business is doing very well: standalone revenue +37% with 27–28% margins, a record order book and a plant that doubles capacity to 16,000 machines from October. The consolidated story is weaker than the headline because Huron is under a criminal-style judicial investigation, its revenue is now lumpy and delayed, and the auditor has flagged a going-concern doubt over the subsidiary that holds ~₹500 cr of the parent's money. At ~75x trailing earnings the price needs FY27–FY29 growth near the 25–30% guidance with margins back at 25% and real cash generation; three years of weak cash flow make the cash promise the key test. What would change my view: positive — H2 FY27 operating cash flow near 50% of EBITDA and Huron licences cleared; negative — an impairment of the Huron investment, further pledging, or the new plant filling slowly because entry-level demand is taken by imports.
