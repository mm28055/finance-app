# LMW Ltd (formerly Lakshmi Machine Works; NSE: LMW) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Screener snapshot (27 Sep 2026): market cap ₹18,303 cr, price ₹17,133 (52-week 11,729–20,151), P/E 100, book value ₹2,683/share, ROCE 5.6%, ROE 4.1%, dividend yield 0.2%, debt nil. [Screener]

## Business and segments
- Four divisions: Textile Machinery (TMD — full spinning lines from bale opener to ring/compact spinning, plus spares), Machine Tools (MTD — CNC turning centres, vertical/horizontal machining centres) with Foundry (~4,000 tonnes a month of castings, largely captive), and the Advanced Technology Centre (ATC — aerospace metal and composite parts, incl. the PSLV nose cone). [CRISIL Aug-26; Q4 call]
- FY26 standalone segment revenue / result (₹ cr): TMD 1,801 / 9.8 (0.5%); MTD & Foundry 1,205 / 97.2 (8.1%); ATC 207 / 28.7 (13.9%). FY25: TMD 1,840 / −15.6; MTD&F 1,003 / 59.3; ATC 169 / 17.0. Revenue share FY26 per CRISIL: TMD ~56%, MTD ~34%, Foundry ~4%, ATC ~6%. [AR FY26 segment note; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Q1 FY27 standalone: TMD ₹482 cr (+16% YoY), MTD & Foundry ₹343 cr (+37% YoY; foundry ~8%), ATC ₹60 cr (+30% YoY). TMD sales mix 64% domestic, 10% exports, 26% spares. MTD customers ~56% automotive. [Q1 call]
- Exports FY26 (standalone) ₹344 cr (FY25 ₹300 cr): textile machinery ₹144 cr, machine tools/castings ₹37 cr, aerospace ₹164 cr. ATC is ~90% export. Overseas subsidiaries: LMW Global FZE (Dubai; FY26 turnover ₹184 cr, loss ₹32 cr) and LMW Textile Machinery (Suzhou), China (FY26 turnover ₹130 cr, breakeven). [AR FY26; Q4 call]
- Capacity use: TMD ~60% in Q1 FY27 (50–55% at Q4); MTD 75–80% (70–75% at Q4); ATC composites ~50%. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit: Jun-25 694 / 14 / 2% / 11; Sep-25 822 / 44 / 5% / 41; Dec-25 758 / 42 / 5% / 15; Mar-26 933 / 70 / 7% / 64; Jun-26 861 / 64 / 7% / 56. Q1 FY27 sales +24% YoY; net profit 5x. [Screener]
- Annual: FY23 4,719 / 450 (10%) / 384 (peak of the last cycle); FY24 4,696 / 435 / 374; FY25 3,012 / 142 (4.7%) / 103; FY26 3,207 / 170 (5%) / 131; TTM 3,374 / 219 / 175. Three-year sales CAGR −12%; ten-year sales CAGR just 1%. [Screener]
- Other income (mostly treasury) is ₹132 cr in FY26, about 78% of operating profit; strip it out and the core business earned very little in FY25–FY26. [Screener]
- Company-quoted "revenue" includes other income: Q1 FY27 standalone ₹891 cr, consolidated ₹902 cr; PBT ₹85 cr standalone (vs ₹34 cr a year ago); FY26 consolidated ₹3,353 cr, "profit" ₹195 cr (before exceptional items). FY26 exceptional charge ~₹11.5 cr (new labour code + VRS). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- TMD economics: order book ₹3,200 cr (₹2,400 cr "active", i.e. customers ready to take delivery) vs ₹3,300 cr / ₹2,300 cr at Q4; only orders with a 10% deposit are counted. 70% of the book is full projects, 30% single machines. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (21 May 2026): textile demand improving (cotton spinners' spreads good; Bangladesh/Turkey reviving; FTAs signed) but "too early to say" whether a "hockey stick" like 2021–22 follows; MTD can grow ~20% on existing capacity; ATC order book ~₹360 cr over 18 months with new ATC capacity producing from H2 FY27; $30 m into a UAE holding company to rebuild exports towards the historic 23–25% share of sales within three years; no numeric revenue guidance. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (27 Jul 2026): gradual (not sharp) rise in spindle installations; state textile policies (Gujarat, Odisha, Madhya Pradesh) and FTAs as triggers; ATC order book ~₹1,000 cr over 3–3.5 years (~₹900 cr export); new ATC building ₹150 cr over 18–24 months; MTD can take 15–20% more turnover without capex; input costs up ~3–3.5%; auto-winder rollout in Q4 FY27. [Q1 call]
- Delivery: Q1 revenue +24% YoY (TMD +16%, MTD +37%, ATC +30%) — consistent with the Q4 tone. ATC order book jumped from ~₹360 cr to ~₹1,000 cr (a raise). MTD margin fell quarter on quarter (Q4 EBIT ~11.5% per an analyst) on lower volume; China subsidiary sales fell to ₹11 cr from ₹76 cr a year earlier. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- No FY27 revenue or margin guidance on either call; management repeatedly declines forward numbers.

## Capex and capacity
- Standalone capex FY26 ₹72 cr (FY25 ₹132 cr); depreciation ₹114 cr (standalone), so the company is now spending below depreciation. Over the prior three years ~₹300 cr+ went into MTD; ~50% of FY26 capex went into ATC machines. [AR FY26 segment note; Q4 call]
- Planned: ATC new facility ₹150 cr (land-and-building first, machines later) over 18–24 months on own land; management said the building can support more than ₹300 cr of ATC revenue (current ~₹230 cr run-rate). MTD capex will be triggered near 90% utilisation. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- $30 m investment into a UAE holding company for LMW Global (working capital plus growth); payback target not quantified. [Q4 call]
- Surplus land: 4.21 acres at Annur (Coimbatore) contributed to a joint development with Infinium Developers LLP on revenue share; income to be recognised as earned. [AR FY26 directors' report]
- Objects clause widened by special resolution (postal ballot passed 11 Sep 2026) as an "enabling" step to explore new areas; management named EV, pharma/specialty chemicals and advanced technology but gave no plan. Aerospace subsidiary renamed LMW Advanced Materials and Technologies Ltd (29 Aug 2026). [Q1 call; BSE filings Aug–Sep 2026]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free for years; cash and equivalents ~₹1,970 cr at 31 Mar 2026 (CRISIL), roughly 11% of market cap; net worth ~₹2,776 cr (CRISIL) / ₹2,867 cr (Screener equity + reserves). [CRISIL Aug-26; Screener]
- Operating cash flow FY24 141, FY25 29, FY26 177 cr; free cash flow FY26 ₹120 cr. [Screener]
- Working capital is negative (−11 days FY26) because spinning mills pay deposits; inventory days 106, debtor days 32, payable days 114. [Screener]
- Dividend payout 29% of FY26 profit; no buyback. [Screener]
- CRISIL assigned **AA/Stable** corporate credit rating on 7 Aug 2026. [CRISIL Aug-26]

## Moat evidence
- One of the few companies worldwide able to supply the full spinning line; CRISIL calls it a leader in Indian textile machinery with "strong brand recall among Indian spinning mills". Deposit-backed order book (10% advance) and large spares business (26% of TMD sales) reflect the installed base. [CRISIL Aug-26; Q1 call]
- ATC: 19 certifications incl. AS9100 and Nadcap; Tier-2 supplier to Boeing/Airbus Tier-1s, plus ISRO, HAL and defence; picks "technically challenging" parts; segment margin ~14% (FY26). [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- MTD: machining centres now >20% of mix; J-series VMCs (J2 aimed at electronics makers) well accepted; MTD segment result up 64% in FY26. [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- Limits: price hikes not taken in MTD at Q4; ~50% of MTD material imported (exposed to USD/EUR); TMD profit near zero through the downcycle despite leadership. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Deep cyclicality:** management cites an ~8-year textile cycle; TMD made ₹9.8 cr on ₹1,801 cr in FY26. Consolidated sales are still 32% below FY23. [Q4 call; AR FY26; Screener]
- **Large related-party purchases:** FY26 purchases of ₹162.8 cr from Lakshmi Electrical Control Systems (LECS, a group listed company), plus ₹9.3 cr sales and ₹2.1 cr services. Shareholder approval sought for up to ₹450 cr (FY27 ₹275 cr; FY28 ₹175 cr) — 14% of LMW's consolidated turnover and 212% of LECS's own FY25 turnover. A relative of the Chairman holds 7.6 lakh LECS shares. [AR FY26 AGM notice]
- **Loss-making subsidiaries:** LMW Global lost ₹32 cr in FY26 and ₹5.6 cr in Q1 FY27; China lost ₹7 cr in Q1 FY27 on ₹11 cr of sales. $30 m more is going into the UAE structure. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Diversification drift:** objects clause widened to unrelated areas (pharma/chemicals mentioned) with no stated plan — capital-allocation risk on a ₹1,970 cr cash pile. [Q1 call; BSE Sep-26]
- **Low returns:** ROCE 6% and ROE 4% (FY26) — the treasury and the weak TMD dilute returns. [Screener]
- Auditor's report (FY26): unmodified; key audit matter on investments (sale of equity/debt instruments); no emphasis of matter found. Contingent liabilities small (standalone): claims not acknowledged ₹0.98 cr, excise ₹0.93 cr, GST ₹6.2 cr, letters of credit ₹28.6 cr, guarantees ₹26.9 cr; capital commitments ₹14.9 cr. Promoter holding 30.80% (down from 31.13% in Jun-25), no pledge disclosed. [AR FY26 auditor's report, Note 30.1; Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "outlook positively good, much larger offtake" (Q4) to "gradual increase in spindle absorption" (Q1); active TMD order book still rose (₹2,300 cr → ₹2,400 cr) while total dipped (₹3,300 cr → ₹3,200 cr).
- ATC visibility jumped (₹360 cr over 18 months → ~₹1,000 cr over 3–3.5 years) and the ₹150 cr new facility was confirmed.
- New issues: Middle East-driven cost inflation (~3–3.5%) and gas/helium availability; USD/EUR strength hurting MTD/TMD costs; the enabling resolution for new businesses.
- Dropped: detail on the UAE investment; subsidiary losses reported without strategy.

## My read
LMW is recovering from a deep textile-machinery trough: Q1 FY27 sales rose 24% and core operating margin is back to ~7%, helped by machine tools (+37%) and aerospace (+30%, order book ~₹1,000 cr). But TMD still earns almost nothing, subsidiaries lose money, and treasury income props up profit, so ROCE is ~6% on a debt-free balance sheet with ~₹1,970 cr of cash. At ~100x trailing earnings the price already assumes a full textile upcycle (a return towards FY23's ₹450 cr operating profit) plus ATC scaling; FY27 so far points to a gradual, not sharp, recovery. What would change the view: positive — TMD active orders and utilisation rising above 75% with spares steady, and ATC revenue crossing ₹300 cr; negative — cash deployed into unrelated businesses, rising LECS purchases, or another year of subsidiary losses.
