## PROFILE (Q1 FY27)
# Refex Industries

## Executive Summary
Refex Industries is currently executing a high-stakes pivot from a diversified trading house into a specialized infrastructure and green energy player. Once a refrigerant gas distributor, the company has successfully repositioned itself as India’s dominant third-party service provider for **Coal & Ash Handling**, solving a critical environmental and logistical bottleneck for thermal power plants mandated to dispose of fly ash. 

The company is no longer a commodity trader; it is a **Specialized Service Provider** with an emerging manufacturing arm. By aggressively shedding low-margin legacy businesses (Power Trading and Refrigerant Gas are now fully exited) and spinning off its capital-intensive Green Mobility division (expected completion Q3 FY27), Refex is distilling its operations into two core verticals: a cash-generative Ash Handling business and a high-growth Wind Turbine manufacturing segment. With a combined order book of INR 2,935 Crores as of Q1 FY27, Refex is a **Category Leader** in niche logistics, currently transitioning into a Challenger in the renewable equipment space, punctuated by the recent erection of India's first 5.3 MW wind turbine.

## The Business Model

### Revenue Drivers
*   **Ash & Coal Handling (The Core Cash Cow):** Refex monetizes the complex logistics of thermal power generation. They do not buy/sell the ash; they are paid service fees by Power Generation Companies (GenCos) for the handling, transportation, and environmentally compliant disposal of fly ash and crushing/transport of coal. Revenue is driven by volume (metric tons handled) and distance transported. The company is actively introducing a Rail-cum-Road (RCR) logistics model utilizing discounted railway freight to open new regional markets.
*   **Wind Power (The New Engine):** Through its subsidiary Refex Whenwin, the company supplies 5.3 MW wind turbines. Revenue is project-based, derived from supply contracts with Independent Power Producers (IPPs). Refex handles manufacturing and supply, while IPPs handle land and installation. The company is actively shifting from importing components to domestic localization at its Silvassa facility.
*   **Green Mobility (Discontinued Operation):** Currently providing B2B employee transportation and EV fleet services. This asset base is being demerged into a separate listed entity to insulate Refex’s balance sheet from fleet-heavy debt, with NCLT shareholder meetings scheduled for August and completion expected by Q3 FY27.

### Competitive Moat (or Lack Thereof)
*   **The Moat: Logistical Density & Regulatory License.** In Ash Handling, the moat is widening. The network effect of handling 65,000-70,000 tons daily, coupled with a proprietary tech platform and a newly introduced Rail-cum-Road network, creates deep competitive trenches. Smaller players lack the balance sheet for bank guarantees and the fleet scale to absorb diesel supply shocks without breaking service.
*   **Durability:** **Moderate.** The ash business is tied to the lifecycle of thermal power. While management correctly identifies a 20-30 year runway for coal in India, it is ultimately a terminal industry.
*   **The Bear Case on Moat:** The Wind segment moat remains unproven. Refex is competing against established giants and relies on licensed German technology (Vensys). However, management is actively trying to build a moat here via an aggressive 85% component localization strategy (including in-house blade manufacturing) over the next 12 months.

## Growth Vectors

*   **Volume Expansion in Ash Handling:** The immediate growth lever is operational scaling. Management is targeting a throughput increase from the current 65,000-70,000 tons/day to 90,000-95,000 tons/day by Q4 FY27. The order book for this segment stands at a robust INR 1,635 Crores. Standalone revenue surged 76% YoY in Q1 FY27 purely on the back of this scaling.
*   **Wind Manufacturing Execution & Localization:** Execution is now a proven reality with ₹295 Cr delivered in Q1 and India's first 5.3 MW turbine successfully erected. With an remaining FY27 execution pipeline of INR 1,300 Crores, management is upgrading total FY27 execution targets to ~INR 1,700 Crores. The "blue sky" growth involves localizing 85% of turbine components to drive long-term EBITDA margins toward 18-20%.
*   **Corporate Restructuring:** The demerger of the Green Mobility business in Q3 FY27 will formally remove legacy losses from the P&L, leaving a clean, asset-light balance sheet with virtually net-zero utilized debt.

## Key Risks & Governance

*   **Forensic & Regulatory Red Flag (High Severity):** The Income Tax department conducted a search on company premises and senior executive residences in December 2025. While management asserts no incriminating evidence was found and operations are unaffected, the final assessment order is 1-2 years away. This overhang creates significant valuation uncertainty.
*   **Promoter Leverage:** Promoter pledging spiked to an alarming 41% in Q4 FY26 to secure holding company borrowing. However, management is actively mitigating this risk, with official exchange disclosures confirming milestone-based loan repayments and rolling share releases currently underway. 
*   **Execution & Supply Chain Risk in Wind:** The shift from importing blades to in-house manufacturing at the Silvassa facility introduces supply chain bottlenecks. If localization targets (85% within 12 months) are delayed, the wind business may remain stuck at current low margins, dragging down consolidated ROE.
*   **Geopolitical & Logistical Shocks:** The core Ash business is highly sensitive to domestic diesel supply constraints. While the company successfully absorbed acute diesel shortages in Q1 FY27 without sacrificing its 17% EBITDA margin, prolonged structural disruptions could severely squeeze future profitability.

## Investment Thesis

### The Bull Case
Refex is an under-appreciated infrastructure proxy compounding capital at an exceptional rate (Q1 Standalone PAT up 123% YoY). It has successfully purged legacy businesses to reveal a highly profitable monopoly in Ash Handling that consistently generates 16-18% EBITDA margins, even during severe supply chain shocks. The Q3 FY27 mobility demerger will finalize a clean, asset-light balance sheet with a INR 2,935 Cr order book. If the Wind vertical successfully executes its localization strategy, it will push segment margins from 5% to 18%, providing a massive, non-linear cash flow kicker on top of the steady, expanding Ash business.

### The Bear Case
The "Conglomerate Discount" may persist due to lingering governance optics surrounding the IT search and promoter pledges, even if the latter is unwinding. Furthermore, the wind business is currently operating at breakeven margins due to heavy upfront and transit costs. If the ambitious 85% localization timeline slips due to supply chain bottlenecks, the wind segment will remain a low-margin drag on the broader portfolio. Finally, the core profit engine (Ash) relies on "dirty energy" (Coal), potentially alienating institutional ESG capital regardless of the new wind energy narrative.
