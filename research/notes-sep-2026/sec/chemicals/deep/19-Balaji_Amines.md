## Business and segments
- India's largest aliphatic-amines maker (built on in-house technology): methylamines, ethylamines, isopropylamines and their derivatives (DMF, DMAc, dimethylamine HCl, choline chloride, morpholine, NMP, 2-pyrrolidone, PVP, GBL, DMC, PC, PG, acetonitrile, and now dimethyl ether, DME). Installed capacity 3,93,600+ MTPA incl. the new 1,00,000 TPA DME plant. Four units (Solapur ×3, Telangana ×1) plus a hotel in Solapur (~1.7% of revenue). [IP Q1FY27]
- Subsidiary Balaji Speciality Chemicals Ltd (BSCL, **55% owned**): ethylenediamine (EDA, ~22,000–37,000 TPA) and derivatives; expanding into DETA/TETA/piperazine/AEEA/AEP (brownfield) and hydrogen cyanide, sodium cyanide, EDTA (greenfield, Chincholi). [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 AOC-2; Q2 call]
- Chemistry & raw materials: methanol + ammonia → methylamines; ethanol + ammonia → ethylamines; EDA made via the ethylene oxide/ethanolamine route (not EDC). Methanol ~₹30/kg and ammonia ₹47–48/kg in Nov-2025; after the March 2026 West Asia shock "most raw material prices doubled or even tripled"; mono-ethylamine ~3x normal. Now buying every 3–4 days instead of monthly. [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- End markets: pharma (e.g. dimethylamine HCl for metformin), agrochemicals, solvents, water treatment, refineries, EV battery chemicals (DMC, NMP), aerosols/LPG substitute (DME). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Exports 14.3% of consolidated revenue in FY26; Europe 36.8% of exports; 50+ countries. [AR FY26 BRSR & exports section]
- Volumes (MT): Q2 FY26 26,165 (amines 7,685 / derivatives 8,374 / specialty 10,107); Q4 FY26 27,341 (7,746 / 8,935 / 10,660) vs 25,871 a year earlier; **Q1 FY27 21,587** (6,249 / 8,205 / 7,133) — a sharp drop. [Q2 call; Q4 call; News: Indian Chemical News, 28 Jul 2026]
- Utilisation: company-level ~35–40% (analyst, not disputed); methylamines 80–85%; butylamine 30–35%; DMC/PG 20–30%; battery-chemical unit 22–25%; BSCL low (running 10–20 days a month during modifications). [Q4 call; Q2 call]

- Unit economics: realisation ≈ ₹144/kg in Q4 FY26 → ≈ ₹211/kg in Q1 FY27 (revenue ÷ volume) — **Q1 was price-led, with volumes down ~21% QoQ**, i.e. a supply-shock windfall, not the promised volume growth. [derived from Screener and volume data]
- Product prices (Q4 call): EDA ~₹300/kg average in Q4 (spikes to ₹400), ₹270–280 in May; DME ₹100–120/kg (₹80–90 conservative); acetonitrile ~₹240/kg (was ₹140–150 in Nov-25); NMM ₹250–300/kg. [Q4 call; Q2 call]

- Core chemical ROCE (excluding hotel and BSCL CWIP) 13–14% vs 11–12% reported. [IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- Q2 call (Nov 2025): H2 FY26 volume growth 8–10%; FY27 "minimum 15%" growth in value and volume; sustainable EBITDA 20–22% (range 17.5–24%); DME by end-Q3 FY26, NMM by end-Q3 FY26; ACN (new process) Q1 FY27; BSCL brownfield Sept 2026, greenfield Dec 2026. [Q2 call official]
- Q4 delivery on those: H2 FY26 volume roughly flat to +6% YoY (not 8–10%); DME, NMM slipped into FY27; margin beat (25% in Q4). [Q4 call]
- Q4 call (May 2026): FY27 volume +10–15% consolidated; "by end of 2027" 25–30% volume growth; EBITDA 22–23% sustainable; ₹3,000 cr revenue in FY28 "if the situation continues" (earlier target ₹2,000 cr); DME commissioning "this month", 30–40% use in FY27, 60–70% next year; ACN Q2 FY27; NMM in FY27; BSCL brownfield H1 FY27, greenfield Q4 FY27. [Q4 call official]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: DME **commissioned** (India's first commercial plant). Margin 26% — beat. But volumes 21,587 MT — **missed** badly vs a 10–15% growth guide; ACN now "by end of FY27" (was Q2 FY27); BSCL brownfield "FY27" (was H1 FY27). No call, so no updated guidance. [IP Q1FY27; News: Indian Chemical News, Jul-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Standalone projects (DME, NMM, ACN): only ~₹20 cr left to spend (May 2026), all from internal accruals. [Q4 call]
- BSCL: ₹750 cr phased plan with Maharashtra "mega project" incentives (PSI 2019); phase 1 ₹350–400 cr; ₹100–110 cr spent, ₹200–250 cr in FY27. Consolidated FY27 capex ₹275–290 cr. [Q4 call]
- Screener CWIP ₹238 cr (Mar-25) → ₹512 cr (Mar-26); BSCL CWIP ₹343 cr. Net block ₹1,043 cr vs sales ₹1,419 cr — asset turn only ~1.4x with idle capacity; FY22 asset turns were much higher at peak prices. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- DME revenue potential: 80,000 t × ₹80–90/kg ≈ ₹640–720 cr at 80% use (management's own arithmetic), margin "~22%". [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Standalone zero debt; consolidated borrowings ₹133 cr (Mar-26; BSCL project loan from HDFC Bank) vs net worth ₹2,152 cr. Management expects nil finance cost in FY27. Bank facilities' ratings reportedly withdrawn in Sep 2026 after repaying all bank debt. [Q4 call; Screener; AR FY26 note; News: Whalesbook, Sep-26]
- Operating cash flow declining: ₹334 cr (FY24) → ₹255 cr (FY25) → ₹184 cr (FY26); investing outflow ₹344 cr in FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital (Screener FY26): debtor days 89 (72 in FY25), inventory 113, payables 39, cash conversion 163 days. Management calls 60–90 days receivables "normal". [Screener; Q2 call]
- Dividend payout 21% (FY26); yield 0.5%. Promoters 54.6%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Cost/technology: indigenous amines technology; ~80% of power from captive solar; new ACN process to give "very high purity at low cost". [Q2 call; IP Q1FY27]
- First movers: India's first commercial DME plant; "nobody else is making DETA in the country". [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Customer stickiness: large metformin maker that backward-integrated into methylamines is only 15–20% of Balaji's dimethylamine HCl output and "still buying from us". [Q4 call]
- China/anti-dumping: competes with China mainly in DMF (old anti-dumping case; not re-applied); EDA anti-dumping case at final stage but government made many products duty-free during the West Asia crisis (exemptions to end-June 2026); anti-dumping duty imposed on acetonitrile (per Q2 call). China "not competing in India" in DME. [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- Q4 FY26/Q1 FY27 pricing power came from rivals' plants shutting during the West Asia shock plus Balaji's cheap inventory — cyclical, not structural. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Volume collapse in Q1 FY27** (−21% QoQ) while margins peaked: earnings are riding a temporary price spike; guidance was for volume growth. [News: Indian Chemical News; Screener]
- Execution slippage: DME, NMM, ACN, BSCL timelines each slipped 1–3 quarters; battery chemicals waiting for Indian cell makers ("somebody says December, somebody says April"). [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- **Related-party trading**: sales to SVS Sourcings Pvt Ltd (enterprise where KMP has significant influence) ₹51 cr in FY26 (₹61 cr FY25); MVL Medisynth sales ₹11.8 cr, purchases ₹5.7 cr; office rents from promoter entities; ₹30 cr loan to BSCL. [AR FY26 note 39]
- BSCL's bank loan is backed by a pledge of the promoters' shares in BSCL (up to 1.5x the loan) and promoters' personal guarantee; BSCL is 45% owned by others (minority interest ₹227 cr). IPO of BSCL postponed. [AR FY26 consolidated notes; Q4 call]
- Contingent liabilities are small: ~₹12–14 cr (income tax ~₹5–7 cr incl. disallowed interest on loans to subsidiaries and hotel capex; customs ₹3.1 cr; GST ₹3.8 cr). Auditor's opinion unmodified; key audit matter is revenue recognition. [AR FY26 notes 38/40; auditor's report]
- DME demand is new and needs PESO cylinder/road-transport approvals and customer trials (100 × 500-kg cylinders sent); LPG blending needs government policy. [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- Disclosure: no Q1 FY27 call (plant visit instead, where no price-sensitive information may be shared); segment revenue/margin split is not disclosed, only volumes. [IP Q1FY27; News: Sahi, Aug-26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Nov-25: soft demand (pharma/agro), flat volumes, 19% margin, 15% FY27 growth promised. May-26: margin jumped to 25% on the West Asia supply shock; targets escalated to ₹3,000 cr by FY28 and 25–30% volume growth; raw-material buying became day-to-day. [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- New issue: the metformin customer's backward integration; EDA anti-dumping delayed by duty exemptions. [Q2 call; Q4 call]

## My read
Balaji is a well-financed (near debt-free) amines leader whose earnings halved from the FY22 peak and are now rebounding mainly on a price spike from the West Asia supply shock — Q1 FY27 margin was 26% but volumes fell ~21% quarter on quarter. The growth story (DME, ACN, NMM, BSCL derivatives, battery chemicals) is real but every project has slipped and several depend on new markets forming. At ~35x trailing earnings on peak-ish margins, the price needs both margins holding above ~20% and the new plants actually lifting volumes. I would turn more positive on DME volumes and a clean volume recovery in Q2–Q3 FY27; more negative if margins revert to 17–19% while volumes stay weak.
