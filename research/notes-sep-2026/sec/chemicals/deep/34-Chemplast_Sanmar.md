## Business and segments
- Three businesses (Q1 FY27 revenue split): **Suspension PVC** (S-PVC, pipe-grade resin) via 100% subsidiary Chemplast Cuddalore Vinyls (CCVL) — ₹569 cr, 51%; **Specialty chemicals** — paste PVC (for artificial leather, footwear, auto upholstery), custom manufactured chemicals (CMCD, for agro and pharma innovators) and refrigerant gas — ₹427 cr, 38%; **Value-added chemicals** (caustic soda, chloromethanes, hydrogen peroxide) — ₹139 cr, 11%. FY26 split 52/36/12. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Chemistry: chlor-alkali electrolysis of salt gives chlorine + caustic soda; chlorine + ethylene → EDC (ethylene dichloride) → VCM (vinyl chloride monomer) → PVC. The Mettur paste-PVC plant is integrated (captive salt, power, EDC, VCM); CCVL's S-PVC is **not** — it imports VCM, so its profit is the PVC–VCM spread in dollars. [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Mar-26]
- Capacity (tpa): S-PVC 331,000; paste PVC 107,000 (+7,000 debottleneck due Oct 2026); caustic 119,000; hydrogen peroxide 34,000; chloromethanes 35,000; CMCD 4,500 moving to ~5,400 t; R-22/R-32 swing plant 2,000 (R-32 new plants to 14,000 t by end-FY27). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Utilisation: Cuddalore paste PVC 41 ktpa ran at 100% in FY26; CMCD 60–70% on blocks commissioned before Phase 3; caustic runs 100–120 kt of 120 kt. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- Market position: paste PVC — ~80% of Indian capacity, ~45% of consumption including imports (CRISIL; the company claims ~66% share); Indian paste demand ~1.92 lakh t, growing 7–8%. S-PVC — second-largest Indian maker, ~20% of domestic capacity, ~10% including imports; India uses ~4.3 mt a year of which ~2.8 mt is imported. [CRISIL Mar-26; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Customers: mostly domestic; one customer above 10% of standalone revenue (₹291.6 cr in FY26, ~13%). CMCD revenue is "fairly concentrated, mostly in agchem"; 14 molecules commercial (Q1) — the Q4 call and AR said 17. [AR FY26 note 40; Q1 call; Q4 call]

- Q1 FY27 standalone (paste PVC + CMCD + VAC): revenue ₹592 cr (+20%), EBITDA ₹7 cr; so CCVL lost roughly ₹120 cr at EBITDA level. [IP Q1FY27]
- Spreads (unit economics): Q1 FY27 VCM landed at US$1,000+/t vs PVC realisation US$700–750/t — **negative spread**. Current replacement: VCM ~US$700/t delivered, PVC ~US$900/t → ~US$150–160/t. EBITDA break-even needs ~US$120–130/t; PBT break-even ~US$150–160/t. Normal PVC–VCM spread US$80–150/t. Paste PVC sells ~US$200/t above S-PVC. [Q1 call]
- PVC prices: ~₹60/kg a year ago vs ₹85–88/kg in May 2026 (analyst figures, Q4 call); Chinese PVC offered at US$820 CFR in May 2026. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- CMCD: >₹1,000 cr invested over 3–4 years with little profit so far; MPB-3 Phase 3 and pilot commissioned in Q1 FY27; target to triple revenue in three years. [CRISIL Mar-26; Q1 call]
- R-32 (air-conditioner refrigerant, GWP 675): 2,000 t swing plant producing since May 2026; new plants to 14,000 t by Mar-27. Risk: India's HFC production quotas (under the Kigali phase-down) are not yet allotted; baseline period runs to end-2026. HF (hydrofluoric acid) bought from outside. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Paste PVC +7 ktpa at Cuddalore (Oct 2026). Capital commitments ₹302 cr (standalone, Mar-26); CWIP ₹341 cr (consolidated). [AR FY26 note 42; Screener]
- Asset turns: ~1.0x (sales ₹4,224 cr on net fixed assets ₹4,386 cr, FY26; note CRISIL says ~₹1,500 cr of this is revaluation). [Screener; CRISIL Mar-26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹1,952 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹882 cr (Mar-22); consolidated net debt ₹1,419 cr. Unencumbered cash fell from >₹700 cr (four years) to ₹438 cr (Dec-25), expected ~₹400 cr. Debt repayments ₹186 cr (FY26) and ₹240–250 cr (FY27). Net debt/EBITDA >10x FY26; interest cover <1x. [Screener; Q4 call; CRISIL Mar-26]
- Cash flow: CFO ₹294 cr (FY26), ₹172 cr (FY25), −₹245 cr (FY24); FCF −₹22 cr in FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital is negative (payable days 224 vs inventory 82 — VCM bought on long supplier credit/LCs); this funds the business but reverses if volumes or credit shrink. [Screener]
- No dividend since listing. [Screener]
- Rating: **CRISIL A/Negative** (downgraded from A+/Negative, 25 Mar 2026); CRISIL left it unchanged after the July fire (28 Jul 2026). Downgrade triggers include margins below 3–4% and support to the group's Egypt company TCI Sanmar. [CRISIL Mar-26; CRISIL Jul-26]

## Moat evidence
- Paste PVC is the real franchise: one of only two Indian producers with the technology, integrated feedstock, high repeat business, ~US$200/t premium to S-PVC, and existing ADD on five other countries. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- CMCD: "one product to one customer" model with innovators; 15-year relationships; LOIs on 6 molecules. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- S-PVC has no moat: price is import-parity; Chinese carbide-route PVC is insulated from oil and gas shocks; CRISIL notes Reliance (1.25 mt, FY27) and Adani (1 mt, FY28) are adding S-PVC capacity. [Q4 call; CRISIL Mar-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Trade-policy dependence**: S-PVC ADD never imposed; QCO on PVC rescinded Nov 2025; customs duty waived Apr–Jul 2026; paste PVC ADD on EU/Japan lapsed (now relying on a Madras High Court interim order); MIP only for six months. [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Raw-material contracting**: VCM parcels booked in March at war prices caused ₹150 cr + ₹120 cr of provisions in two quarters; the auditor made onerous contracts and inventory NRV a key audit matter. [AR FY26 auditor's report; Q1 call]
- **Karaikal EDC fire (17 Jul 2026)**: Inspector of Factories and Pollution Control Committee issued prohibition orders; plant restarted only on 25 Sep 2026 under provisional revocation; plant damage ~₹5 cr; production impact not yet quantified; caustic output disrupted. Expect a weak Q2. [BSE filing 26-Sep-26; IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Jul-26]
- **Group exposure**: CRISIL flags possible support to TCI Sanmar (Egypt, rated BB+); Chemplast has pledged its (tiny) shareholding in TCI Sanmar to that company's lenders. Corporate guarantee of ₹332 cr to SIPCOT for a CCVL soft loan (₹77 cr drawn). [CRISIL Mar-26; AR FY26 notes 39, 41]
- Contingent liabilities (standalone): direct tax ₹55 cr, indirect tax ₹22 cr, other ₹16 cr. [AR FY26 note 41]
- **Management churn**: MD Ramkumar Shankar left 31 Mar 2026; S. Ganeshkumar MD from 1 Apr 2026; a board member (Sumit Maheshwari) left 9 Apr 2026; director V. S. Radhakrishnan resigned Sep 2026. A strategic-review committee of three independent directors (M&A/reorganisation) is working with advisers; the company denied a report of a definitive deal to buy Symed Labs (24 Aug 2026). [AR FY26 note 38; Screener announcements; Q1 call]
- Disclosure gaps: no segment EBITDA; CMCD molecule count changed from 17 to 14 without explanation; CCVL financials only via the presentation.

## My read
Chemplast is two companies: a decent specialty business (paste PVC, CMCD, soon R-32) and a structurally loss-making S-PVC subsidiary that depends on trade protection and on buying VCM well — and it failed at the second in Q4–Q1. The balance sheet is weakening (debt ~₹1,950 cr, cash down to ~₹400 cr, interest cover below 1x, CRISIL on Negative), so the specialty side's growth is being funded while CCVL drains cash. The stock needs spreads to hold near US$160/t from Q3, CMCD to reach ~₹1,000 cr in FY28, and R-32 quotas to come through; a clear separation or sale of CCVL by the strategic committee would be the biggest positive. Another quarter of provisions, MIP lapsing in early 2027 without an ADD, or a rating downgrade would turn the view negative.
