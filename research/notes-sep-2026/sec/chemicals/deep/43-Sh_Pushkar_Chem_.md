# Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers (SHREEPUSHK) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: the stored **Q1 FY27** transcript (13 Aug 2026) matches the official BSE transcript in substance, but several answers are attributed to the Company Secretary (Pankaj Manjani) when the official text shows the **CMD, Punit Makharia**, answered them, and it names the IR firm as "SGA Partners" (official: **Churchgate Partners**). The stored **"Q4 FY26 transcript" is not a transcript at all** — it is the 18 May 2026 board-meeting outcome and audit report. The official Q4 call (19 May 2026, filed 25 May) was read from BSE and is used below.

## Business and segments
- Chemistry — a "zero-waste" sulphur chain: imported sulphur is burnt to make **sulphuric acid, oleum (23%/65%) and chlorosulphonic acid**; the heat drives a waste-heat power plant. Acids feed (a) **dye intermediates** — K-acid, H-acid, vinyl sulphone, meta ureido aniline, R-salt — made by sulphonating aromatic bases, and then **reactive dyes** (Dyecol brand, for cotton/cellulosic textiles; GOTS/ZDHC/Bluesign certified); (b) spent acid is reacted with imported **rock phosphate** to make **single super phosphate (SSP)** fertiliser; gypsum residue becomes soil conditioner. Also NPK/complex fertilisers, sulphate of potash, cattle feed supplements. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- Revenue mix FY26 (consolidated ₹976.6 cr): **Chemicals ₹531.8 cr (54%), 72,423 t** (+27.9% volume); **Fertilisers ₹444.8 cr (46%), 2,52,777 t**; total volume ~3.25 lakh t (+2.5%). Q1 FY27: fertilisers ₹142 cr (51%), 66,527 t (−12.8%); chemicals ₹138 cr (49%), 9,113 t (−38.6%). [Q4 call official; IP Q1FY27]
- Unit economics: chemical realisation ≈ ₹1.51 lakh/t in Q1 FY27 vs ≈ ₹0.80 lakh/t a year earlier (less low-value acid sold, higher intermediate prices); fertiliser ≈ ₹21,300/t vs ≈ ₹18,000/t (+19%). K-acid went from ₹550 to ₹700+/kg; H-acid ₹525–530 → ₹750/kg; vinyl sulphone ₹240 → ₹350/kg (Feb–May 2026). [derived from IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Capacity: fertilisers **5,90,200 tpa** (to 10,40,200 after expansion), chemicals **1,27,460 tpa** (to 1,99,460); total 7,17,660 tpa across 9 plants (Ratnagiri Units 1–6, Deewanganj Unit 7 and Meghnagar Unit 8 in MP, Hisar Unit 9 in Haryana); solar 10.6 MW DC (+10 MW Nanded). [IP Q1FY27]
- Utilisation: management says **65–70%**; but FY26 fertiliser sales of 2.53 lakh t are only ~43% of the 5.9 lakh t stated capacity, and chemical sales ~57% of 1.27 lakh t (part of acid is captive) — the gap is unexplained. [Q4 call; derived from IP]
- Structure: standalone SPCFL (chemicals-heavy: ₹449 cr chemicals, ₹118 cr fertilisers of ₹567 cr) plus 100% subsidiaries Kisan Phosphates (Hisar) and Madhya Bharat Phosphate (MP) — SSP makers — and new Dyecol Color Technologies (Sep 2025) and Dyecol Bangladesh (Nov 2025). [AR FY26 MD&A; results filing May-26]
- Customers/geography: fertilisers sold to farmers via **800+ distributors**, 14 offices; dyes to textile mills. Exports ₹80.8 cr (FOB, FY26; ₹70.2 cr FY25) — **8.3%** of consolidated turnover; Bangladesh is a key dye market. Imports ₹91.4 cr (mainly rock phosphate). No customer concentration disclosed; single segment in accounts. [AR FY26 Directors' report, BRSR, notes 46, 51]

- Quarterly (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 255 / EBITDA 29 (11%) / PAT 21; Sep-25 255 / 26 (10%) / 18; Dec-25 249 / 22 (9%) / 18; Mar-26 218 / 22 (10%) / 13; **Jun-26 280 / 32 (11%) / 23**. [Screener]
- Annual (Screener consolidated): sales FY22 ₹584 cr, FY23 ₹684 cr, FY24 ₹726 cr, FY25 ₹806 cr, FY26 ₹977 cr; OPM 14% → 10% → 8% → 10% → 10%; PAT ₹56 → 37 → 37 → 59 → **70 cr**. TTM sales ₹1,002 cr, PAT ₹72 cr. 5-yr sales CAGR 22%, 3-yr profit CAGR 23%. [Screener]
- Company figures: FY26 EBITDA ₹99.5 cr (10.2%), PAT ₹70.1 cr (7.1%), ROE 12.2%, ROCE 15.3%. Q1 FY27 gross margin 31.9% (Q4: 38.4%; Q1 FY26: 33.0%), EBITDA ₹31.9 cr (11.4%), PAT ₹22.9 cr (8.2%). [IP Q1FY27]
- Raw materials: sulphur from **$250–300/t to ~$1,000–1,100/t** (₹30/kg → ~₹100/kg); ammonia ₹40–42/kg → ₹100+/kg (May). Management expects sulphur to settle at ₹70–75/kg, never back to ₹30. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Fertiliser subsidy: SSP depends on the Nutrient-Based Subsidy (NBS); the government raised NBS rates on 1 Feb and 1 Apr 2026 but the CMD said it "is not up to the required level"; ICRA noted an SSP subsidy cut in Oct 2023 hurt FY24. [Q4 call; ICRA May-24]

## Guidance history
- Before Q4 (Q3 call, per CMD): FY27 revenue **₹1,500 cr**. [Q4 call]
- Q4 call (19 May 2026): cut to **₹1,250–1,300 cr** because Units 5 and 6 would miss the Kharif season (raw-material prices tripled); Q1 FY27 revenue >₹250 cr and PAT 8–8.5%; 8–10% PAT margin "not a great fight"; tax 22–25%. [Q4 call official]
- Q1 delivery: revenue ₹280 cr (beat the >₹250 cr hint), PAT margin 8.2% (in range) — **delivered**, though volume fell sharply and FY26's March dispatch hold-back flattered Q1.
- Q1 call (13 Aug 2026): FY27 **raised** to ₹1,300–1,400 cr ("₹1,350–1,400 cr"); **new** FY28 target ₹1,700–1,750 cr (theoretical peak ₹2,000 cr); PAT 8.5–9% ("do not expect 10%"); Q2 FY27 "110% better" than Q2 FY26's ~₹255 cr (i.e. strongly higher — wording ambiguous); Unit 6 dry/load trials end-Aug/Sep, 4–5 months of Unit 6 in FY27. [Q1 call official]
- Longer record: ICRA (May 2024) described a ₹215 cr capex to be commissioned in phases **by March 2026**; the programme has since grown to ₹512 cr and Units 5/6 are ~6 months late (power-connection issue until March 2026, then the raw-material shock). [ICRA May-24; Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Total programme **₹512 cr**, ₹209 cr spent by 30 Jun 2026 (₹189 cr by Mar-26), ₹303 cr outstanding: Unit 8 Meghnagar (MP) ₹350 cr, 3 lakh tpa complex fertilisers + 800 tpd acid plant, target **March 2028** (₹60 cr spent); Unit 6 Ratnagiri ₹85 cr, 1.5 lakh tpa fertilisers + 66,000 tpa acid, Aug 2026 (₹72 cr spent); Unit 5 dyes ₹37 cr, 6,000 tpa, Aug 2026 (done, capitalisation not yet announced); 10 MW Nanded solar ₹35 cr, Sep 2026; 1.1 MW Hisar solar ₹5 cr, done. Plus ₹9.33 cr for 30,000 sq m land next to Unit 1 with no plan yet. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Adds 4.5 lakh tpa fertiliser and 72,000 tpa chemical capacity. [IP Q1FY27]
- Funding: ₹140 cr of investments/deposits + ₹30 cr promoter warrants for Meghnagar; balance ₹180 cr from accruals, or up to 25–30% term debt. [Q4 call]
- Asset turns: net fixed assets ₹305 cr + CWIP ₹149 cr (Mar-26) + ₹303 cr still to spend ≈ ₹757 cr after the programme; ₹1,700–1,750 cr FY28 revenue would be ~2.3x turns (FY26: ₹977 cr on ₹305 cr net block ≈ 3.2x). Management's ₹2,000 cr peak implies ~₹1,000 cr extra revenue from ₹512 cr capex (~2x). [derived from Screener, IP]

Non-lien deposits ₹140.7 cr (Mar-26), ₹124.9 cr (Jun-26); net debt/equity −0.01x. [Screener; AR FY26 note 49; Q4 call]

- Working capital: debtor days 80 → 79 → 67; inventory 48 → 115 → 77; payables 56 → 113 → 91; cash cycle 71 → 81 → 53 days. Sulphur needs advance payment while acid sells on credit — one reason acid plants were run at low load. Fertiliser stocking before Kharif (Q1) and subsidy timing drive working capital. [Screener; Q1 call; ICRA May-24]
- Dividend ₹2.10/share for FY26 (~₹7 cr). Promoter warrants: 7,36,196 at ₹407.50 (₹30 cr; 25% = ₹7.5 cr paid Dec 2025), to Joint MD Gautam Makharia; convert within 18 months. [results filing May-26; AR FY26]

## Moat evidence
- Integration: acid complex + waste-heat power + spent acid into SSP + gypsum into soil conditioner — ICRA calls it "advantageously placed" versus peers because of zero discharge and full integration. [ICRA May-24; IP Q1FY27]
- Scale in niche intermediates: management claims to be **India's largest (only) K-acid maker**; Chinese buyers are again placing K-acid/H-acid/vinyl sulphone orders. [Q1 call]
- Pricing power in Q1: raised prices sharply and let volume fall (fertiliser −13%, chemicals −39%) while revenue rose 10% — proves it can pass on cost when supply is tight. [IP Q1FY27]
- Weaknesses: SSP is a low-value, subsidy-dependent product with many regional makers; dyes face weak textile utilisation and Bangladesh demand. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party funding**: a cash-rich company took ₹26.6 cr of loans from promoters and their relatives in FY26 (repaid ₹5.5 cr) while the same promoter paid ₹7.5 cr warrant money; rent of ₹2.2 cr a year paid to Mrs Bhanu Makharia; ₹26.9 cr lent to subsidiary MBPPL; ₹109 cr of corporate guarantees for subsidiaries' debt (Kisan ₹48 cr, MBPPL ₹61 cr). Promoter pay is modest (₹0.59 cr each for Punit and Gautam). [AR FY26 notes 45, 49]
- **Disclosure quality**: two different auditors (statutory S K Patodia & Associates LLP; internal RMJ & Associates) — the stored profile confuses them; MD&A says Nanded solar was commissioned after year-end but the Aug call/presentation still show it pending; stated capacity vs volume gap unexplained; the CMD took the Q4 call from the plant without data. [results filing; AR FY26; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Auditor: unmodified; KAMs on revenue recognition (discounts, rebates, export incentives) and credit-loss allowance; audit trail operated all year. Contingent liabilities small (₹1.4 cr; FY25 ₹9.6 cr); bank guarantees ₹9.8 cr. [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 44]
- Raw materials: sulphur ~3.5x, ammonia ~2.5x; Hormuz still closed (Aug). Unit 6 depends on both; restart relies on long-term supply contracts "at advanced stage". [Q1 call]
- Subsidy/regulatory: NBS rates, 10% cap mentioned by CMD, subsidy timing; monsoon. [Q4 call; ICRA May-24]
- Execution: capex doubled to ₹512 cr and slipped; Meghnagar is 2.5x Unit 6 — large for a ₹1,000 cr company. [IP Q1FY27; ICRA May-24]
- Promoter holding 69.36%, rising slowly via warrants; no pledge shown. Credit rating: latest rationale read is ICRA A+ (Stable)/A1, May 2024; a newer one could not be opened. [Screener; ICRA May-24]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: defensive — "wait and watch", Kharif written off for Units 5/6, guidance cut from ₹1,500 cr to ₹1,250–1,300 cr, "old numbers will never be seen again".
- Q1: bullish — raw-material panic "gone", Unit 6 trials in 4–6 weeks, FY27 raised to ₹1,300–1,400 cr, FY28 ₹1,700–1,750 cr introduced.
- New: acid plants run at low load; China inquiries for intermediates; land purchase; ₹60 cr already spent at Meghnagar by June (call and presentation agree).
- Dropped: Bangladesh demand, labour-code and deferred-tax discussions.

## My read
The business is steadily improving — revenue up from ₹584 cr (FY22) to ₹977 cr (FY26), PAT back to ₹70 cr, operating cash flow ₹102 cr and almost no net debt — and the integrated sulphur chain gave it real pricing power in the 2026 shock. The next leg (₹1,700 cr+ in FY28) rests on starting Unit 6 in a raw-material market that has tripled and on a large Meghnagar project, so execution slippage is the main risk (the plan has already doubled in size and slipped ~6 months). At ~23x trailing earnings the price assumes most of the FY28 target; watch the Unit 6 start and Q2/Q3 volumes, whether fertiliser volume recovers without margin loss, and why promoters are lending money to a company holding ₹125 cr of deposits.
