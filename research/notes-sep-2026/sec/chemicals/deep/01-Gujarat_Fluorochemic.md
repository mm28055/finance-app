# Gujarat Fluorochemicals (GFL) — deep research (A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore, consolidated, from Screener unless tagged otherwise.

## Business and segments
- **What it makes.** GFL is an Inox-group fluorine chemistry company. Four product families: (1) **fluoropolymers** — PTFE, micro-powders, PVDF, FEP, PFA, FKM/FFKM, PPA — used in semiconductors, EV wiring, chemical process equipment, data centres and hydrogen electrolysers; (2) **fluorochemicals** — refrigerant gases (R-22, R-125, R-32, R-410 blends) and fluoro-specialities; (3) **bulk chemicals** — caustic soda and chloromethanes (chloroform, methylene dichloride, CTC); (4) **battery materials** through subsidiary GFCL EV Products — LiPF6 electrolyte salt, electrolyte, LFP cathode material, PVDF binder, and a planned natural-graphite anode. [AR FY26 notes 30, 39; Q4 call]
- **Chemistry chain.** Fluorspar (own mine and beneficiation in Morocco, GFL GM Fluorspar SA) → anhydrous hydrogen fluoride (AHF) → refrigerant gases and R-22/R-142b feedstock → TFE monomer → PTFE and other polymers. Chlorine and methanol feed the chloromethanes. This backward integration is the core cost advantage. [AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Mar-26]
- **Segment split, Q1 FY27 (per the presentation):** fluoropolymers ₹914 cr (+15% YoY, +8% QoQ), fluorochemicals ₹458 cr (+52% YoY, +44% QoQ), bulk chemicals ₹164 cr (+11% YoY), battery materials ₹14 cr. Chemicals segment total ₹1,574 cr with EBITDA ₹458 cr (29% margin) and PAT ₹261 cr. Battery segment EBITDA −₹30 cr and PAT −₹42 cr. [IP Q1FY27 as reported by Investing.com and Multibagg; Q1 call]
- **FY26 segment reporting:** Chemicals revenue ₹5,030 cr, EBITDA ₹1,371 cr (27%); EV products revenue ₹33 cr, EBITDA −₹80 cr (FY25: ₹9 cr revenue, −₹28 cr EBITDA). So the chemical business is earning a healthy margin and the EV unit is diluting group EBITDA by about 1.6 percentage points. [AR FY26 consolidated note on segments]
- **Geography and sales model.** Export-heavy. Polymers are sold through warehouses run by subsidiaries in Germany and the USA; plant stock 30–90 days, warehouse stock 30–90 days, sea transit 30–60 days. Marquee customers in Europe/US require 2–3 months of "insurance stock". [Q4 call]
- **Capacity and utilisation.** R-32: 10,000 t commissioned March 2026, fully used from April; expansion to 20,000 t due Q2 FY27. New fluoropolymer lines put up a few years ago are "almost fully utilised", so growth until new capacity (about a year away) comes from price, mix and debottlenecking. R-134a (brownfield, using imported trichloroethylene) due by end-FY27. AHF additions in phases from Q3 FY27. Management does not disclose polymer capacities or utilisation. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **End markets named by management:** semiconductors (PFA/FEP), batteries (PVDF), data centres, green hydrogen, automotive, cooling for AI data centres (refrigerants), grid batteries (LiPF6/LFP). [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (consolidated, Screener):** Q1 FY26 revenue 1,281 / EBITDA 344 (27%) / PAT 182; Q2 FY26 1,210 / 364 (30%) / 179; Q3 FY26 1,136 / 275 (24%) / 102; Q4 FY26 1,369 / 307 (22%) / 100; Q1 FY27 1,588 / 426 (27%) / 219. [Screener]
- **Plain words:** the second half of FY26 was weak — Q3 was hit by the R-22 production-quota limit and Q4 by Middle East shipping disruption and a jump in battery-unit losses after the LiPF6 plant was capitalised on 5 Jan 2026. Q1 FY27 was a sharp rebound: revenue +24% YoY and +16% QoQ, PAT more than doubled QoQ. [Screener; CRISIL Mar-26; Q4 call]
- **Annual:** revenue FY24 4,281 → FY25 4,737 → FY26 4,996; EBITDA 915 → 1,100 → 1,201; margin 21% → 23% → 24%; PAT 435 → 546 → 574. TTM revenue 5,303, PAT 600. FY23 (5,685 revenue, 35% margin) was the refrigerant/caustic price peak. [Screener]
- **Growth record:** 3-year sales CAGR −4% and profit CAGR −24% (from the FY23 peak); 5-year sales CAGR 14%. [Screener]
- **Returns:** Screener ROCE 9.6%, ROE 7.8%. The company quoted ROCE 16.6% and ROE 15.2% for Q1 FY27 annualised versus 14.0%/12.2% for FY26 — these are its own definitions (likely excluding capital work in progress) and are much higher than Screener's. [Screener; Q1 call]
- **Unit economics:** not disclosed per tonne. Management said R-32 domestic and export prices are "very close", and about 40–50% of R-32 is sold on long-term contracts. Fluoropolymer growth is "mostly mix plus some volume"; price hikes were small and only to cover input costs. [Q1 call]
- **Battery business scale:** about ₹20–30 cr a quarter of revenue at present against ~₹1,900–2,000 cr invested. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **Fluoropolymers:** Q4 call — "15–20% growth" in FY27, with price and volume both contributing. Q1 delivery — +15% YoY, the bottom of the range. In Q1 closing remarks management said "17–20% annually going forward"; in the Q&A it confirmed "about 20%" is "absolutely visible" for FY27 and FY28. **Kept / nudged up.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **R-32:** Q4 — 10,000 t running, 20,000 t with full capacity available for H2 FY27. Q1 — expansion "in Q2 FY27", full use expected in CY2027. **On track.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Battery materials revenue:** Q4 — "3-digit" (₹100 cr+) quarterly revenue by Q4 FY27; LFP final qualification by end-Q3 FY27, then supply against offtake for the whole plant; binders to start revenue "in next couple of quarters". Q1 — ₹14 cr revenue and −₹30 cr EBITDA; ₹100 cr in Q4 reaffirmed; cathode revenue pushed to "towards the end of this financial year"; "full potential from FY28". **Kept, but timetable slipping at the edges.** [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **EV return targets:** ₹6,000 cr cumulative capex by FY28, asset turns ~2x, EBITDA margin >25%, full earnings by FY29. **Kept.** [Q4 call]
- **Oman:** Q4 (26 May) — "We will go ahead with the capex… no impact on Oman". Q1 (12 Aug) — Oman project "on hold", moved to Dahej (Jolva site); the Oman sovereign (OIA) funding of ~₹1,200 cr was specific to Oman and will not be used. **Reversed within 11 weeks.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Working capital:** Q4 — would fall as turnover grows. Q1 — working-capital days 149 vs 192 in March. **Delivered.** [Q1 call]
- **Capex:** Q4 — FY27 ₹3,150 cr (EV ₹2,300 cr; GFL ₹850 cr: refrigerants ₹150 cr, electronic chemicals ₹222 cr, fluoropolymers ₹250 cr, backward integration/maintenance ₹230 cr). Q1 — "EV ₹2,300 cr and chemicals ~₹800 cr, holding on to that". **Kept.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Spend:** CFI −966 (FY24), −1,121 (FY25), −1,168 (FY26). CWIP rose from ₹1,128 cr (Mar-24) to ₹1,900 cr (Mar-26). [Screener]
- **FY27 plan ₹3,100–3,150 cr**, about 2.6x FY26 cash capex. EV unit: ~₹1,900–2,000 cr invested so far; ~₹2,300 cr in FY27 and a similar amount in FY28 to reach ₹6,000 cr. EV gross block capitalised is only ~₹836 cr; management expects EV CWIP of about ₹1,200 cr by end-FY27 because capitalisation waits for quality stabilisation. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Funding:** internal accruals (CRISIL expects ₹1,200–1,400 cr a year), ~₹430 cr equity raised in GFCL EV, IFC funding, plus fresh debt. Management: "we do not see funding as a constraint", fundraising "already underway". [CRISIL Mar-26; Q1 call]
- **Asset turns:** FY26 revenue ₹4,996 cr against net fixed assets ₹4,822 cr plus CWIP ₹1,900 cr — about 0.75x on total invested fixed capital. EV target is 2x after a 9–18 month qualification lag; if met, ₹6,000 cr could support ~₹12,000 cr revenue, but only from FY29. [Screener; Q4 call]
- **Anode (natural graphite):** capex not disclosed "for valid reasons… confidential". [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Borrowings:** ₹2,096 cr (Mar-24), ₹2,080 cr (Mar-25), ₹2,721 cr (Mar-26). Investments ₹277 cr. The standalone debt-equity ratio fell to 0.22 from 0.31, so the debt increase sits mainly in the EV subsidiary. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A ratios]
- **CRISIL (Mar-26):** debt ~₹1,532 cr at 31 Dec 2025 (rated entity), unencumbered liquidity ~₹176 cr, fund-based limits of ₹3,375 cr used 33% on average, adjusted net debt/EBITDA expected below 1.3x at March 2026 (3.1x at March 2024). Downgrade trigger: net debt/EBITDA above 1.75x. [CRISIL Mar-26]
- **Cash conversion:** CFO ₹626 cr (FY24), ₹545 cr (FY25), ₹961 cr (FY26); FY26 CFO/PAT 1.7x. Screener free cash flow −₹330 cr (FY24), −₹335 cr (FY25), −₹292 cr (FY26); it will be deeply negative in FY27 with ₹3,100 cr capex. [Screener]
- **Working capital:** Screener debtor days 94 and inventory days 453 (on cost of materials) for FY26; company-defined working-capital days 192 at Mar-26 → 149 at Jun-26. Structural reasons: warehouse stock abroad, insurance stock, longer sea routes, and few creditors because the chain is integrated. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Dividend:** payout ~6% of profit (yield 0.07%). No buyback. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- **Integration and cost:** captive fluorspar, AHF, R-22/TFE chain; CRISIL calls GFL "the largest PTFE manufacturer in India and among the top players globally". [CRISIL Mar-26]
- **Western exits:** 3M's PFAS exit has been "more or less absorbed" over two years; GFL is now filling the high-end space 3M vacated. AGC's planned UK plant closure has brought enquiries; GFL "has the grades" and qualification with AGC customers has started, benefit expected in 1–2 quarters. [Q1 call]
- **Qualification as switching cost:** semiconductor and fuel-cell grades take long approvals; "every quarter we continue to add approvals"; most FY27 approvals expected by year-end. LiPF6 approved by "most of the major global electrolyte players". [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Pricing:** formula-based pricing with prime polymer customers protected margins when TFE prices rose 3–4x and fluorspar rose; chemicals segment margin 29% in Q1 FY27 vs 26% in Q4 FY26. [Q1 call]
- **Refrigerant quota:** under the Kigali HFC phase-down, production entitlement is based on a baseline plus 2024–2026 production. GFL is adding R-32 and R-134a to use its full quota; management calls future R-134a supply "restricted", with HFO substitutes much dearer. This is a regulatory moat for incumbents. [Q1 call]
- **Contracts in EV:** Phase-1 capacities are "commissioned and contracted" with anchor customers, agreed specifications and pricing — but management would not say if they are take-or-pay. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Group guarantees:** GFL has guaranteed ₹498 cr of debt of promoter-group company Inox Renewable Solutions (up from ₹422 cr), plus balances of ₹94 cr due from IRSL and ₹43 cr from Inox Wind. Total consolidated contingent liabilities ₹698 cr (₹614 cr a year earlier), of which ₹200 cr are tax disputes. CRISIL flags group support as a weakness and a downgrade trigger if it rises. [AR FY26 consolidated note 42, RPT note 45; CRISIL Mar-26]
- **Tax disputes:** transfer-pricing additions on sales to foreign subsidiaries and corporate guarantees (AY 2017-18 to 2021-22) pending at ITAT; customs demand ₹80 cr on advance-licence conditions. No provision made. [AR FY26 note 42]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion, no key audit matters, audit trail maintained. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **EV execution:** ₹2,000 cr invested for ~₹20–30 cr quarterly revenue; LFP qualification has slipped from "end Q3" to "towards end of FY27"; Chinese LFP and LiPF6 prices are the benchmark. Losses rise as plants are capitalised before sales ramp. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Oman U-turn:** a stated plan reversed within one quarter; OIA funding lost; new debt needed. [Q1 call]
- **Disclosure gaps:** no capacity or utilisation numbers for polymers, no anode capex, no contract terms, no segment margins below "Chemicals". [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Accounting noise:** standalone vs consolidated gap moved because goods-in-transit on CIF terms were booked in the standalone rather than in the German/US units; EV dispatches on CIF terms were not in Q1 revenue. [Q1 call]
- **Refrigerants:** quota regime can cap volumes (Q3 FY26 R-22), and PFAS regulation in Europe/US is a long-term overhang for fluoropolymers. [CRISIL Mar-26; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Promoter holding** fell from 62.57% to 61.39% in the Sep-2025 quarter and has been flat since (reason not researched); no pledge shown. [Screener]

## My read
The chemicals business is improving: R-32 has turned fluorochemicals into a second engine, polymer margins held through a raw-material spike, and working capital came down. But the group is now a ₹6,000 cr battery-materials bet that has so far produced ~₹14 cr a quarter of revenue, and FY27 capex of ~₹3,100 cr will keep free cash flow negative and debt rising. Screener ROCE of ~10% is what the market is paying about 79x trailing earnings for, so the price needs both the 17–20% polymer growth and the EV ramp to ₹100 cr+ a quarter by Q4 FY27 and 2x asset turns by FY29. What would change my view: a missed Q4 FY27 EV revenue mark or further slippage in LFP qualification (negative); R-134a/R-32 full utilisation plus AGC share gains lifting chemicals EBITDA above ₹500 cr a quarter (positive); any rise in guarantees to Inox group firms (negative).
