# Navin Fluorine International (NFIL) — deep research (A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore, consolidated, from Screener unless tagged otherwise.
Transcript check: the stored Q1 FY27 transcript (5 Aug 2026) is the official company filing. The stored Q4 FY26
transcript (29 Apr 2026) is an AI re-write; it was checked against the official BSE filing and contains several

## Business and segments
- **What it makes.** A Mafatlal-group fluorine chemistry company with three verticals: **HPP (high-performance products)** — refrigerants (R-22, R-32, "Mafron" brand), hydrofluoro-olefin (HFO) made for Honeywell under a long-term contract, anhydrous hydrofluoric acid (AHF) and inorganic fluorides; **Specialty chemicals** — fluorinated intermediates made for global agrochemical and pharma innovators in multi-purpose plants (MPPs) and dedicated plants (e.g. "Project Nectar" for a marquee agrochemical customer); **CDMO (Navin Molecular)** — contract development and manufacturing for pharma innovators at Dewas (India) and Manchester Organics (UK). A fourth vertical, **Advanced Materials** (data-centre cooling fluids with Chemours, semiconductor/OLED chemicals, defence material with DRDO, electronic-grade HF), is being incubated. [AR FY26; CARE Aug-26; Q1 call]
- **FY26 segment revenue:** HPP ₹1,615 cr (+34%), Specialty ₹1,152 cr (+44%), CDMO ₹546 cr (+59%); shares ~49% / 35% / 16%. [AR FY26 business review; CARE Aug-26]
- **Q1 FY27 segment revenue:** HPP ₹540 cr (+33%), Specialty ₹325 cr (+48%), CDMO ₹180 cr (+82%). Q4 FY26: HPP ₹393 cr (+20%), Specialty ₹360 cr (+39%), CDMO ₹186 cr (+61%). [Q1 call; Q4 call official]
- **Exports ~67% of FY26 revenue** (₹2,225 cr export vs ₹1,089 cr domestic). Export share by vertical: HPP 55%, Specialty 71% (up from 51%), CDMO 96% (up from 71%). [AR FY26]
- **Customer concentration:** three customers each above 10% of revenue in FY26 — ₹545 cr, ₹391 cr and ₹342 cr, together ~39% of revenue (FY25: one customer, ₹499 cr). Seven of the top 10 customers have been with NFIL for over 10 years on average. Likely candidates (not named in the report) are the European CDMO partner, Honeywell (HFO) and the Nectar agro customer. [AR FY26 consolidated note 43; CARE Aug-26; Q1 call]
- **Capacity and utilisation.** R-32 ~9,000–10,000 t now; +15,000 t due Q3 FY27. AHF 60,000 t across Surat and Dahej (40,000 t commissioned Q4 FY26) — "good enough for the next four to five years". Honeywell HFO plant at ~80% of capacity. Specialty MPPs have visibility of ~80% utilisation, "as best as one would go in an MPP". Nectar plant: 75–80% utilisation by end-FY28, slower than hoped. [Q1 call; Q4 call official; AR FY26]
- **Raw materials:** fluorspar, sulphur (for sulphuric acid → HF), chloroform, boric acid. In FY26 sulphur prices rose while fluorspar, boric acid and chloroform fell. Raw materials were 41.5% of revenue. [AR FY26 MD&A]

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (Screener):** Q1 FY26 revenue 725 / EBITDA 207 (29%) / PAT 117; Q2 FY26 758 / 246 (32%) / 148; Q3 FY26 892 / 308 (34%) / 185; Q4 FY26 938 / 321 (34%) / 213; Q1 FY27 1,045 / 357 (34%) / 243. Seven straight quarters of growth. [Screener; CARE Aug-26]
- **Annual:** revenue FY24 2,065 → FY25 2,349 → FY26 3,314 (+41%); EBITDA 399 → 534 → 1,082; margin 19% → 23% → 33%; PAT 270 → 289 → 664. TTM revenue 3,634, PAT 790. [Screener]
- **Plain words:** FY26 was the payback year for the ₹3,000 cr invested over five years — R-32 phase 2, AHF, the Nectar plant, cGMP4 all came on line together as HFC prices were strong. Management said ~70% of the ~990 bps margin gain came from new capacity and volume, ~30% from pricing. [Q4 call official]
- **Returns:** ROCE 21%, ROE 19.6% (FY24–FY25 ROCE was 11%). [Screener]
- **Unit economics:** R-32 peak revenue guided at ₹600–825 cr on 15,000 t (≈$5–6/kg); management called that the board's downside case and noted Chinese R-32 prices near $9/kg; the first R-32 plant was justified at below $2/kg. Q1 gross margin dipped ~100 bps QoQ on raw-material inflation, offset by fixed-cost savings. [Q1 call; Q4 call official]

## Guidance history
- **EBITDA margin:** Q4 call — "we will endeavour to do 30%". Q1 call — "32%–33%, plus/minus 1%" for the next one to two years. Q1 delivered 34.2%. **Raised.** [Q4 call official; Q1 call]
- **CDMO US$100 m in FY27:** FY26 was ₹546 cr; Q1 FY27 ₹180 cr (annualising ~₹720 cr). "Very much on track." **Kept.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Chemours cooling-fluid plant:** Q4 — complete "end June, early July". Q1 — "end of Q2 FY27". Management says watch a 15–18 month adoption window. **Slipped one quarter.** [Q4 call official; Q1 call; AR FY26]
- **R-32 +15,000 t:** Q3 FY27 in both calls; 35–45% to be sold on five-year contracts, and "a couple of contracts" already signed (earlier aim was ~30%). **On track, contract share raised.** [Q1 call]
- **MPP debottlenecking (₹75 cr, ~2x asset turn):** Q3 FY27 in both calls. **On track.** [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- **Working capital:** Q4 — 74 days, guidance 75–80 days (was 90). Q1 — 81 days, "within the frame". **Kept, slightly above range.** [Q4 call official; Q1 call]
- **Specialty:** Q4 — visibility for ~80% MPP utilisation; 13 new molecules in FY26. Q1 — campaign orders for 4–5 agro molecules, 3 patented; LatAm pricing pressure persists; "not going back to 30% EBITDA taken for granted" in specialty. **Kept.** [Q4 call official; Q1 call]
- **Growth:** Q4 — "I would be surprised if it is not double-digit" for FY27. Q1 revenue +44%. **Beating.** [Q4 call official; Screener]
- **Employee cost:** Q4 — model 7.5–9% of sales (was 12–13% a year earlier). [Q4 call official]

## Capex and capacity
- **Ongoing:** R-32/HFC ₹236.5 cr (Q3 FY27); MPP debottleneck ₹75 cr (Q3 FY27); Chemours project US$14 m, of which US$5 m funded by Chemours (end Q2 FY27); cGMP4 total ₹288 cr — Phase 1 live Dec 2025, Phase 2 ₹125 cr approved Aug 2026 for Q4 FY27; Advanced-materials "adoption" capacity ₹90 cr at Surat in two phases (Phase 1 around Q4 FY27); group-captive hybrid renewable power ₹15.7 cr (>60% of energy once live). All funded from internal accruals. [Q1 call; AR FY26; CARE Aug-26]
- **Asset-turn targets:** cGMP4 3x by FY29 (≈₹900 cr on ₹288 cr); MPP debottleneck ~2x; R-32 ≈2.5–3.5x on ₹236.5 cr. Management: capex already done "bakes in" growth to FY28; the new capex is for FY29 onwards. [Q1 call]
- **Scale:** ~₹3,000 cr spent over five years and a similar amount planned over the next three to four. [Q1 call]
- **Regulatory deadline:** under India's Kigali rules, HFC output freezes at the 2024–26 baseline from 1 Jan 2028 (then −10% by 2032, −20% by 2037, −30% by 2042, −85% by 2047), and new/expanded HFC capacity must start production by 31 Dec 2027. The Q3 FY27 R-32 start matters for quota. [CARE Aug-26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Balance sheet (Mar-26):** borrowings ₹1,272 cr; cash and liquid investments ₹1,329 cr (CARE) — net cash. Net debt/EBITDA −0.05x (FY25: 1.79x). Gearing 0.33x. Current ratio 2.62x. [Screener; CARE Aug-26]
- **QIP:** ₹750 cr raised July 2025 at ₹4,680/share (16.0 lakh shares), used mainly to repay NFIL and subsidiary NFASL debt. Promoter holding fell from 28.4% to 27.1%. [AR FY26; Screener]
- **Cash flow:** CFO ₹750 cr (FY24), ₹571 cr (FY25), ₹894 cr (FY26); Q1 FY27 ₹173 cr. Screener free cash flow ₹72 cr (FY24), ₹4 cr (FY25), ₹404 cr (FY26); the large FY26 CFI (−₹1,235 cr) mostly reflects parking QIP money in investments (₹480 cr → ₹1,229 cr). [Screener; Q1 call]
- **Working capital:** debtor days 83, inventory days 121, payable days 144 (FY26); CARE operating cycle 69 days vs 94. Contract liabilities (customer money for Chemours and Nectar capex) ~₹211 cr. [Screener; CARE Aug-26; Q4 call official]
- **Dividend:** FY26 final ₹8.6/share; payout 7%. [Q4 call; Screener]
- **Rating:** CARE upgraded to AA+/Stable on 13 Aug 2026. Downgrade triggers: EBITDA margin below 25%, project delays, debt/EBITDA above 1.5x. [CARE Aug-26]

## Moat evidence
- **Hazardous chemistry and integration:** own AHF at two sites, fluorination know-how since 1967; Chinese players are the cost benchmark. [CARE Aug-26]
- **Sole supplier positions:** the only manufacturing site for Chemours' Opteon two-phase immersion-cooling fluid ("we are the only supplier to Chemours"); Honeywell HFO contract from July 2022 for seven years with a three-year extension at Honeywell's option, running ~₹460–470 cr a year. [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- **CDMO stickiness:** dedicated cGMP4 block for the European partner; new MSA takes NFIL to "API minus 1"; an early-phase molecule added with the same customer; 30–40 active molecules, ~10 late stage, 3–4 FDA readouts in 8–12 months (one readout did not go well). [Q1 call]
- **Customers fund capex:** Chemours pays US$5 m of US$14 m; contract liabilities show customers co-funding plants. [AR FY26; Q4 call official]
- **Quota moat:** R-32 entitlement is tied to the 2024–26 baseline plus historic R-22 production — "your right to win comes from the quota you already hold". [Q4 call official]
- **Lowest-cost R-32 claim:** integrated HF, productivity and renewable power; operating leverage from going 10,000 t → 25,000 t. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration:** ~39% of FY26 revenue from three customers; CARE flags customer and molecule concentration and blocks tied to single molecules (clinical failure risk). [AR FY26 note 43; CARE Aug-26]
- **R-32 oversupply in India:** analysts cite ~1.2 lakh t of Indian capacity by 2027; management concedes "India is going to be oversupplied for five years" and relies on exports and contracts. [Q1 call; Q4 call official]
- **Agrochemical pricing:** LatAm well supplied; pricing pressure persists; Nectar ramp slower (75–80% by FY28). [Q1 call; Q4 call official]
- **Auditor key audit matter:** carrying value of the Dewas CDMO plant (₹314 cr) and investments in UK subsidiaries incl. Manchester Organics (₹94 cr) — impairment testing relies on management forecasts. Opinion unmodified. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Subsidiary margin:** NFASL (Dahej) margin fell ~12 points QoQ in Q1 as AHF is transferred at arm's length; management says group margin unaffected. [Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities** small (~₹7 cr tax/excise plus employee matters). [AR FY26 note 51]
- **Disclosure gaps:** no capacity utilisation, no customer names, no R-32 volumes or prices, DRDO product undisclosed. [Q1 call]
- **Valuation:** 55x trailing earnings already prices a lot of the capex payoff. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Margin guidance moved up from "30%" to "32–33% ±1%". [Q4 call official; Q1 call]
- Advanced Materials became a formal vertical with its own ₹90 cr capex and a DRDO partnership; cGMP4 Phase 2 (₹125 cr) approved. [Q1 call]
- R-32 contract share raised to 35–45% on five-year terms; tone on Indian oversupply more explicit. [Q1 call]
- Chemours start slipped from early July to end-Q2. Working capital drifted up from 74 to 81 days. [Q1 call]
- Q4's worries (Middle East, raw-material availability, sulphur/methanol inflation) faded. [Q1 call]

## My read
The business has clearly improved: margins went from 19% to 34% in two years, ROCE doubled to 21%, the balance sheet is net cash after the QIP, and each vertical is growing. The next leg to FY28 is already paid for (R-32 expansion, MPP debottleneck, cGMP4, Chemours), so execution risk is lower than for peers. At about 55x trailing earnings the price needs CDMO to hit US$100 m this year and keep compounding, margins to hold near 32–33% as Indian R-32 capacity floods in before the 2028 freeze, and Chemours/advanced materials to become real revenue by FY28–29. What would change the view: an R-32 price slump or loss of a top-three customer (negative); a large Chemours follow-on capex or another CDMO block after FDA readouts (positive).

## Stored-data errors
- Quarterly figures in the metrics block match Screener. "yoy" is revenue growth, not profit growth (Q1 PAT grew 108%).
- **Stored Q4 FY26 transcript (AI re-write) errors vs the official BSE transcript:** Chairman named "Vishad Mohapatra" (is Vishad Mafatlal); CDMO Q4 growth "51%" (official 61%); "HFC capacities of 32,000 MT" (no such capacity; garbled); "Nexgen HFO project … 75–80% this year and full by end of next year" is **Project Nectar** (specialty), and official guidance is 75–80% utilisation **by end-FY28**; "Tiamulin"/"Firon" are the **Fermion** (European CDMO) contract; employee cost "6–7% of sales" a year ago (official 12–13%); margin guidance "30% to 32%" (official: "endeavour to do 30%"); R-32 range "600 to 875".
- Profile says hybrid renewable plant "now covers 60% of energy needs" — it is not yet operational; it will cover >60% once live (renewables were ~12% of electricity in FY26).
- Profile says CDMO pipeline 30–40 molecules; the Q4 call said 55–60. Both were said; the Q1 figure refers to "actively working" molecules.
- Profile says net-debt-free was achieved in Q1 FY27; CARE shows net cash already at 31 Mar 2026.
- Profile claims "no red flags" — it misses the auditor's key audit matter on Dewas/UK impairment and the three-customer concentration.
