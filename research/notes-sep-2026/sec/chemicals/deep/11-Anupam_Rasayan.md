# Anupam Rasayan India (ANURAS) — deep research, Q1 FY27

Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged otherwise. Price ₹1,192, market cap ₹13,575 cr (25 Sep 2026) [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Custom synthesis and manufacturing (CSM): multi-step, made-to-order molecules for global innovators. Core chemistries include fluorination, chlorination, Grignard, nitration, hydrogenation; now also continuous flow reactors (Corning G1/G4) and photochemistry [IP Q1FY27].
- Group is four platforms: Anupam standalone (custom synthesis, FY26 revenue ₹1,676 cr, 29,852 MT capacity), Tanfac (hydrofluoric acid, potassium fluoride and other fluorides, ₹711 cr, 1.35 lakh MT), Jayhawk Fine Chemicals, Kansas USA (₹722 cr pro forma at ₹95/$, 36,000 MT; acquired Feb 2026) and Bliss GVS Pharma (formulations, ₹927 cr; 43.3–48.2% stake plus open offer, closing expected first half of Sep 2026) [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Standalone FY26 mix by end market: agrochemicals 55%, pharma 20%, performance materials 18%, personal care 7%. Agro share was 76% in FY22. Pharma revenue went from ₹21 cr (FY22) to ₹339 cr (FY26); performance materials from ₹97 cr to ₹305 cr [Q4 call].
- Consolidated Q1 FY27 mix by platform: life-science specialty 41%, performance materials 31%, other fluorinated products (Tanfac) 28% [IP Q1FY27].
- Jayhawk: ~65% performance materials (polyimide monomers, electronic/semiconductor chemicals, defence, aerospace), ~35% life/crop science; revenue ~$76 mn, EBITDA ~$15 mn (~19%); 800-acre site, "<10% utilised" land; bought at enterprise value ~$134 mn [IP Q1FY27].
- Exports: 36% of Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue is "international business" [IP Q1FY27]; CRISIL puts exports at over 50% of revenue and imports at 15–20% of raw materials [CRISIL Aug-26]. Japan and the US grew as customer markets in FY26 [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Customers: 200+ clients including 55+ MNCs (presentation); CRISIL counts 31 MNCs among 75 clients. No top-customer share is disclosed — a disclosure gap for a contract manufacturer [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Aug-26].
- Capacity/utilisation: "combined installed capacity exceeding 200,000 MT" across eight plants; management says the standalone gross block of ~₹2,500–2,600 cr can support ~₹3,500 cr revenue vs ₹1,676 cr now, i.e. roughly half used [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]. Bliss is at ~30% utilisation, target 60–70% [Q4 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr) / operating margin: Jun-25 486 / 26%; Sep-25 731 / 19%; Dec-25 512 / 25%; Mar-26 636 / 22%; Jun-26 655 / 25% [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: revenue +35% y/y, operating profit ₹162 cr (+31%), but depreciation ₹56 cr (vs ₹31 cr) and interest ₹49 cr (vs ₹36 cr) left net profit at ₹51 cr, up only 6% [Screener].
- Strip out Jayhawk (~₹145 cr revenue, ~₹30 cr EBITDA, ~₹9 cr PAT in Q1) and consolidated growth was single-digit, which management confirmed; standalone revenue grew just 4% to ₹329 cr, but standalone EBITDA margin rose to 34% (from 31%) on a richer performance-materials mix [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹1,475 cr (OPM 26%, PAT ₹167 cr); FY25 ₹1,437 cr (28%, ₹160 cr); FY26 ₹2,365 cr (22%, ₹222 cr). FY26 growth came mostly from Tanfac's first full consolidation plus a 1-month Jayhawk stub; standalone FY26 revenue grew 86% as agro recovered [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Margin trend is down: consolidated OPM fell from 28% (FY25) to 22% (FY26) because Tanfac (commodity fluorides) and Jayhawk (~19–20%) carry lower margins than standalone custom synthesis; CRISIL also cites "limited ability to pass on higher cost" [Screener; CRISIL Aug-26].
- Returns are poor: ROCE 13% (FY23) → 9% (FY24) → 7% (FY25, FY26); ROE 5.6% [Screener].
- FY26 profit was flattered by tax: effective rate 13% (vs ~30% in FY22–FY24), with Dec-25 and Mar-26 quarters at about 0%; PBT rose only from ₹198 cr to ₹254 cr. Tax normalised to 27% in Q1 FY27; management guides ~25% standalone [Screener; Q4 call].
- Interest (₹149 cr) plus depreciation (₹140 cr) took 55% of FY26 operating profit of ₹526 cr [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (25 May 2026): standalone revenue growth of 20–25–30% a year over 3–5 years; blended EBITDA margin around FY26 levels (~24%); maintenance capex ₹50–75 cr; standalone tax rate ~25%; pro forma consolidated working capital 215–220 days (240–250 days ex-Jayhawk); pro forma four-company revenue >₹4,000 cr and EBITDA ~₹834 cr; Bliss to close in 2–3 months; Bliss "EPS accretive from day 1" [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (14 Aug 2026): organic growth "25 plus or minus a couple of percentage points" plus 10–15% from Jayhawk; EBITDA margin 24–26% standalone and 22–24% consolidated; capex ₹70–80 cr; interest cost at the Q1 run-rate; Bliss closing first half of September [Q1 call].
- Delivery vs promise: margin beat (25% on Screener, 26% incl. other income, vs 22–24% guide); organic growth missed badly in Q1 (single-digit, standalone +4%) — management blames Q1 seasonality; the 215–220-day working-capital number was not repeated, only "stable… we should be in the range we have been guiding" by year-end. Guidance kept, not raised [Q1 call; Screener].
- Order book: ₹14,000 cr at Q4, "roughly ₹18,000 cr" (slide total ₹17,496 cr) at Q1 after a US$300 mn, 10-year LOI with BASQUEVOLT, S.A. (a Spanish solid-state battery developer), commercialisation expected H2 FY27, meaningful revenue in 2–3 years [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Contribution from LOIs: ~₹400 cr in FY26, expected ~25% of revenue in FY27 and ~30% later [Q1 call]. By my reading of the slide, roughly ₹10,000 cr of the ₹17,496 cr is still marked "to be commercialised" in FY27–FY28 (Elementium, Korean MNC, US MNCs, BASQUEVOLT) [IP Q1FY27].

## Capex and capacity
- Multi-year capex cycle is finished: FY26 capex ₹315 cr "towards the last leg"; standalone capacity doubled in three years; CWIP fell from ₹609 cr (FY24) to ₹114 cr (FY26) [Q4 call; Screener; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Fixed assets jumped from ₹2,185 cr (FY25) to ₹3,713 cr (FY26), including goodwill of ₹540 cr on Jayhawk [Screener; AR FY26 note 2A].
- Asset turns are low: FY26 sales ₹2,365 cr on ~₹3,700 cr fixed assets is about 0.6x; management's ₹3,500 cr peak on the standalone block implies ~1.4x at full use [Screener; Q4 call].
- Acquisitions replace capex: Jayhawk EV ~$134 mn; Bliss consideration ~₹1,600 cr for 43.3–48.2% (₹299/share; ₹1,369.5 cr for 43.3%), funded by a ₹300 cr term loan/NCD and ~₹1,300 cr of "non-controlling, non-voting equity" from a global investment firm, which Anupam can buy out later [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; News: scanx.trade, May 2026].
- Bliss has a separate ~₹250 cr Halol CDMO capex for a large MNC pharma customer, with 4–5x asset turn guided [Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings: ₹1,069 cr (FY24) → ₹1,373 cr (FY25) → ₹1,867 cr (FY26); management cited ~₹1,500 cr gross / ~₹1,100 cr net, two-thirds working-capital loans [Screener; Q4 call]. CRISIL: cash ₹378 cr, adjusted debt/net worth 0.54x, interest cover 3.5x, bank lines 88% used on average [CRISIL Aug-26].
- **Off-balance-sheet guarantee:** Anupam guaranteed a US$120 mn facility (limit up to $200 mn; ₹1,136 cr disclosed) taken by Batam S.à r.l., a Luxembourg entity in which Anupam holds only 15%; Batam used it to buy non-voting Class B/C shares of Doriath S.à r.l., the holding company for Jayhawk. Total corporate guarantees rose to ₹432 cr standalone contingent liability plus this ₹1,136 cr, against ₹75 cr a year earlier. Anupam also pledged its shares in Doriath and Batam and gave a non-disposal undertaking over its Tanfac shares [AR FY26 CARO; notes 30, B6, 38(ii)].
- In plain words: the "quasi-equity" funding Jayhawk is bank debt raised by a third party and guaranteed by Anupam. The Bliss deal uses the same template for ~₹1,300 cr. Economic leverage is materially higher than the ₹1,867 cr on the balance sheet.
- Working capital: cash conversion cycle 524 days (FY24) → 715 (FY25) → 377 (FY26); inventory days 627 → 876 → 490 [Screener]. CRISIL gross current assets 646 → 504 days [CRISIL Aug-26].
- Cash flow: operating cash flow ₹59 cr (FY24), –₹30 cr (FY25), ₹334 cr (FY26); investing –₹392 / –₹330 / –₹820 cr; the gap was funded by ₹1,050 cr of equity (QIP FY23, preferential FY24) and debt [Screener; IP Q1FY27].
- Dividend yield 0.13% [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Long contracts: 10-year LOIs with Japanese and US MNCs, Elementium and BASQUEVOLT; order book ₹17,496 cr ≈ 7x FY26 revenue [IP Q1FY27].
- Backward integration into HF/KF via Tanfac is real and rare in India; management credits supply security for winning LOIs [Q4 call].
- Process technology: first globally to commercialise ethyl trifluoroacetate (ETFA, used in pharma, agro and electronics) by flow chemistry; market ~$0.5 bn; target 5–10% share initially, up to 15–30% [Q1 call].
- Standalone EBITDA margin 34% in Q1 FY27 suggests pricing power in niche molecules [IP Q1FY27].
- Against: consolidated returns (ROCE 7%) show the moat is not yet earning its cost of capital [Screener].

## Risks and red flags
- Guarantee of ~₹1,136 cr for a 15%-owned entity (Batam) funding Jayhawk, with pledged subsidiary shares; Bliss repeats the structure. The buy-out right means an eventual cash call or dilution [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27].
- Earnings quality: Jayhawk depreciation is under review ("different depreciation period" under old owner); EBITDA +35% turned into PAT +6% [Q1 call].
- Organic growth stalled in Q1 (standalone +4%) while M&A headlines grow; four listed/unlisted entities run "independently" [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Serial acquisitions: Tanfac (2022), Jayhawk (Feb 2026), Bliss (Sep 2026), and an API asset "being explored" [Q1 call].
- Agro cyclicality (55% of standalone), and Chinese price competition in agro intermediates; forex exposure with >50% exports [AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Aug-26].
- CRISIL has the rating on "Watch Developing"; downgrade triggers are margin below 18% or gearing above 1x with GCA over 450 days [CRISIL Aug-26].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; only key audit matter is revenue cut-off [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Promoter holding 59.07%; no pledge of promoter shares disclosed on Screener [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from deal-making (Bliss rationale, pro forma ₹4,000 cr revenue, debt math) to technology (ETFA flow chemistry, BASQUEVOLT LOI, semiconductors via Jayhawk).
- Numbers dropped: the ₹1,500 cr gross-debt figure and the 215–220-day working-capital target were not repeated.
- New admission: organic growth was single-digit in Q1. New issue: Jayhawk depreciation policy under review.
- Bliss commentary became guarded ("we are both listed entities"); Bain & Co. now runs the integration office.

## My read
The standalone custom-synthesis engine is improving in quality (34% EBITDA margin, faster pharma and polymer growth, a thick LOI book), but the group is being assembled faster than it is earning: ROCE is 7%, organic growth was 4% last quarter, and much of the acquisition funding sits outside the balance sheet as guaranteed "quasi-equity". At about 78 times trailing earnings the price needs roughly 30%+ annual profit growth for years, which requires both LOI conversion (₹10,000 cr still to commercialise) and Bliss utilisation rising from 30% to 60–70%. I would turn more positive on two quarters of 20%+ organic growth with consolidated working capital near 220 days; I would turn negative if the Batam/Bliss instruments are bought out with new debt or if Jayhawk depreciation is revised in a way that flatters profit.
