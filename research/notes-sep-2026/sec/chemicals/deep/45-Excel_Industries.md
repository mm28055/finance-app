# Excel Industries (EXCELINDUS) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: the stored Q4 FY26 transcript (call of 25 May 2026) matches the official copy on the company website word for word on every figure checked (₹281 cr revenue, ₹22 cr adjusted EBITDA, 8% margin, ₹1,094 cr FY26 revenue, 10.1% margin, ₹13.75 dividend, 65–85% utilisation). The stored Q4 FY25 transcript (16 May 2025, the company's first-ever call) is consistent with the company's own later references. **Gap:** the stored pair skips the company's H1 FY26 call (Nov 2025), which is the call immediately before Q4 FY26 and the one where the 13–15% margin target was last repeated; I read it in full from the company website. Excel holds only half-yearly calls, so there was **no Q1 FY27 call**; Q1 FY27 figures below come from Screener and the results release.

## Business and segments
- India's oldest phosphorus-chemistry company (since 1941). Four product groups: agrochemical intermediates (mainly DETC, used to make the insecticide chlorpyrifos, plus a "tactical" NATCP line), yellow-phosphorus (YP) derivatives, Performance Solutions (phosphonates, biocides, mining chemicals, polymer inputs such as THP for polycarbonate) and pharma intermediates/APIs (e.g. Teneligliptin, one veterinary API). Waste management ≈1% of revenue. Plants at Roha, Lote Parshuram (Maharashtra) and Visakhapatnam. [H1 FY26 call; Q4 FY26 call]
- Revenue mix (company ranges): agro intermediates 50–60%, YP derivatives 7–10%, Performance Solutions 25–30%, pharma 6–8%. CRISIL puts **DETC alone at ~41% of FY26 revenue** and non-agro at 43%. Top 10 products ≈90% of revenue; phosphorus chemistry ≈80%. [H1 FY26 call; CRISIL Sep-26; Q4 FY25 call]
- Market position: world No. 1 DETC producer (company claim); 50–55% domestic share in phosphonates; "significant" global share in THP. [Q4 FY26 call; results release via ScanX Aug-26]
- Geography: FY26 revenue outside India ₹223 cr vs ₹177 cr in FY25 (+27%), India ₹864 cr vs ₹796 cr (+9%) — exports ≈20%, mostly Performance Solutions; US and EU largest. [AR FY26 note 44; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Segment reporting was **reduced to a single "Chemicals" segment** in FY26 (earlier Chemicals + Environment); no product-group profit is disclosed anywhere. [AR FY26 note 44]
- Utilisation 70–75% company-wide (65–85% by product); management calls 85–90% the practical maximum. Installed capacities are not disclosed "for commercial reasons". [Q4 FY26 call; H1 FY26 call]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr — sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 310 / 42 / 14% / 34; Sep-25 270 / 30 / 11% / 21; Dec-25 234 / 16 / 7% / 8; Mar-26 281 / 22 / 8% / 12; **Jun-26 294 / 42 / 14% / 29**. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (standalone): revenue ₹293.7 cr (−5.1% YoY), EBITDA ₹42.4 cr (flat), margin 14.4% (13.7%), PAT ₹29.1 cr (−13%) as other income halved to ₹6.2 cr. [Results release via ScanX, 13 Aug 2026]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY22 ₹1,178 cr → FY23 1,090 → FY24 826 → FY25 978 → FY26 1,095; operating profit 232 → 129 → 25 → 121 → 114; OPM 20% → 12% → 3% → 12% → 10%; net profit 161 → 80 → 17 → 85 → 76. TTM sales ₹1,079 cr, PAT ₹71 cr. 3-year sales CAGR 0%, profit CAGR −2%. [Screener]
- FY26 story: revenue +12% on volume and market-share focus, but PBT −14% to ₹95 cr because yellow phosphorus and sulphur costs rose faster than prices; CRISIL margin 10.4% vs 12.5%. [AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Returns are poor on the reported equity: Screener ROCE 6.2%, ROE 4.6%; company RoE 6.1% (standalone). Book value ₹1,355/share vs price ₹929. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Unit economics: no volumes or prices disclosed. FY18 EBITDA was ₹125 cr vs ₹110–114 cr in FY26 (shareholder's figure, not contested) — eight years of no operating profit growth despite ₹422 cr capex since FY18. [Q4 FY26 call]

## Guidance history
- **May 2025 (Q4 FY25 call):** 13–15% EBITDA margin "over the medium term"; ₹200–300 cr capex over three years (incl. ₹40–50 cr/yr maintenance), growth capex at 1.25–1.5x asset turns; MNC contract supplies from Q2 FY26; R&D centre in H1 FY26. [Q4 FY25 call]
- **Nov 2025 (H1 FY26 call):** margin target converted into a **full-year FY26 guide of 13–15%**, repeated to at least four analysts even though H1 was ~12% and Q3 was flagged as lean. New ₹40 cr contract line to start "by June 2026". Three new product streams to add ~₹60 cr revenue at peak (biocide ₹15 cr, polymer contract ₹10–12 cr, new contract ₹35–40 cr excluding raw material), staggered over 12–18 months. [H1 FY26 call]
- **Delivery:** FY26 margin 10.1% (company) — a **clear miss** of 3–5 points. Contract line slipped from June to July 2026 and was completed on 23 July 2026. Biocide expansion (₹10.3 cr, doubles capacity) fully operational Nov 2025 — on time. R&D centre commissioned Oct 2025 (one quarter late). [Q4 FY26 call; AR FY26 MD&A; results release via ScanX Aug-26]
- **May 2026 (Q4 FY26 call):** guidance **dropped** — "not possible to make any forward statements". Only ROI 15–20% and asset turns 1–1.5x on new capex were restated. [Q4 FY26 call]
- Q1 FY27 margin of 14.4% sits inside the old band, but on falling revenue and one strong seasonal quarter (Q1 FY26 was also 14%). [Screener]

## Capex and capacity
- Plan: ₹200–300 cr over three years (CRISIL: ₹275 cr over FY27–29, ₹75–125 cr a year), all from internal accruals. Growth areas: Performance Solutions (biocides), contract manufacturing, YP derivatives. No split by project. [Q4 FY26 call; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Actual spending is running well below plan: investing cash outflow ₹51 cr FY24, ₹41 cr FY25, ₹25 cr FY26; CWIP rose from ₹13 cr to ₹38 cr in FY26 (the new contract line). [Screener]
- Contract line: ₹40 cr capex, five-year term with an Indian specialty chemicals company, ₹35–40 cr annual conversion revenue (customer supplies raw material), ₹25 cr trade advance from the customer — effectively funding most of the plant. [H1 FY26 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- History: ₹346 cr capex FY19–FY24 (incl. ~₹100 cr Vizag unit bought 2019); gross block roughly doubled while sales rose ~25% — asset turns fell from ~2.5x toward ~2.3x. [Q4 FY25 call; Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt negligible (₹8 cr borrowings, mainly leases); interest cover 34x. CRISIL A+/Stable, A1; bank lines enhanced to ₹200 cr; working-capital limits 76% used on average. [Screener; CRISIL Sep-26]
- **The "cash pile" is mostly shares, not cash.** Consolidated investments ₹1,126 cr, but CRISIL counts only **₹136 cr unencumbered cash** and classes ₹999 cr as strategic investments, "primarily group companies". The largest are: Tanfac Industries (20.4 lakh shares) ₹383 cr; **Silox India (unlisted, promoter-group) ₹338 cr**; Navin Fluorine ₹63 cr; Transpek Industry (promoter-group) ₹62 cr. [AR FY26 consolidated note 8; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Cash conversion weak: CFO ₹72 cr FY24, ₹62 cr FY25, ₹53 cr FY26 against PAT of ₹17 / 85 / 76 cr. Debtor days 81 → 87, inventory days 70 → 81, cash-conversion cycle 64 → 74 days in FY26. Inventory rose 15–20% in Q2 FY26 alone. [Screener; H1 FY26 call]
- Dividend ₹13.75/share for FY26 (≈₹17 cr, ~23% payout; yield 1.5%). No buyback; on the call the MD said only the Board would "evaluate" it. [Q4 FY26 call; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and integration in phosphorus chemistry: global-scale DETC, backward integration through multiple YP steps; India has no YP production and China restricts YP exports, so Excel's multi-source YP (Kazakhstan, Vietnam) is a real entry barrier for new Indian rivals. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 FY26 call]
- Domestic share: 50–55% in phosphonates; Excel and Aquapharm are the **only two Indian producers** of HEDP and ATMP phosphonic acids (100% of Indian output per DGTR initiation). [Q4 FY26 call; TPM summary of DGTR initiation, Sep-25]
- Pricing power is limited: management says it must be "agile" because it competes with large Chinese producers and prioritises market share; FY26 cost pass-through lagged. April–May 2026 increases were "largely" passed on. [Q4 FY26 call; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Contract wins show customers trust the chemistry: three contracts in 12 months, all in Performance Solutions, one with a customer-funded advance. But peak contract revenue is only ₹55–60 cr (~5% of sales). [H1 FY26 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Concentration:** DETC ~41% of revenue, tied to chlorpyrifos — an old organophosphate insecticide facing regulatory pressure; monsoon and El Niño sensitivity (management flagged El Niño risk for FY27). [CRISIL Sep-26; Q4 FY26 call]
- **Capital allocation / governance:** ~₹720 cr sits in two stakes (Tanfac, Silox) that earn a small dividend yield and inflate equity, halving reported ROE. Silox is an unlisted promoter-group company valued by a management-appointed valuer on comparable EV/EBITDA multiples — the auditor made this a **key audit matter**; a 10% change in the multiple moves value by ₹27 cr. [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 41]
- **Related parties:** stake in associate Mobitrash (39.98%) sold to promoter Ashwin C. Shroff in Nov 2025 at a ₹0.4 lakh gain (i.e. near book); Excel paid ₹1.65 cr CSR to a Shroff-linked institute; FY26 transactions of ₹6.4 cr with Silox. Individually small, but the promoter group sits on both sides repeatedly. [AR FY26 notes 45, AOC-1, consolidated note 45]
- **Contingent liabilities:** a new **₹13.5 cr GST demand** in FY26 (nil a year earlier), plus ₹1.4 cr customs, ₹0.4 cr excise, ₹0.3 cr claims. Small vs net worth. [AR FY26 note 46]
- Auditor's opinion unmodified; CARO clean (statutory dues deposited, no loans on pledge of securities). [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Disclosure: refuses capacity, R&D spend, raw-material split, product margins; reduced segment reporting; answers "let me get back to you" on basic questions. [Q4 FY26 call; AR FY26 note 44]
- Minor inconsistency: the first MNC contract was said to have "commenced" initial supplies in Nov 2025 but only "validation batches dispatched" in May 2026. [H1 FY26 call; Q4 FY26 call]

## What changed between the two calls (H1 FY26 → Q4 FY26)
- Tone went from confident (13–15% FY26 margin repeated) to defensive (no forward statements; El Niño and West Asia raw-material shocks).
- Excel shortened its own selling-price windows (from monthly/quarterly toward shorter periods) to cope with raw-material swings — this is Excel's pricing policy, not customers abandoning contracts.
- New issues: open shareholder anger over eight years of flat EBITDA and no buyback; DGTR anti-dumping case on Chinese HEDP/ATMP (filed by Excel and Aquapharm, initiated 29 Sep 2025; management expected findings by Sep 2026 and would not size the revenue affected); China's April 2026 VAT-refund withdrawal lifting Chinese landed prices. [Q4 FY26 call]
- Search results indicate DGTR issued final findings around 25 Sep 2026; I could not open the document, so the duty level is unverified. [DGTR site via search]

## My read
The operating business is a cyclical DETC/phosphonate franchise that has not grown operating profit since FY18, and management missed its own FY26 margin guide by 3–5 points before withdrawing guidance. The new contracts (~₹60 cr revenue at peak) and biocides are real but small against ₹1,100 cr of sales, so they cannot change the picture before FY28. The stock trades close to the value of its investment book, but most of that "cash" is illiquid promoter-group or single-company stakes, so the discount is partly deserved. The view would improve with an anti-dumping duty on Chinese HEDP/ATMP, two more quarters at 13–15% margin in a weak agro season, or a buyback/monetisation of the Silox or Tanfac stakes; it would worsen with chlorpyrifos regulatory action or margin back below 10%.
