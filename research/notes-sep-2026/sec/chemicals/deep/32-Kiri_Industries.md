# Kiri Industries (KIRIINDUS) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: the stored Q4 FY26 transcript (1 Jun 2026) is the official BSE copy. The stored Q1 FY27 transcript (12/13 Aug 2026) is an AI transcription and has material errors against the official BSE transcript: (1) it says "EBITDA margin was 21.9% compared to 23.5%" — the official text says **standalone material margin improved to 31.9% from 23.5%**; (2) it says the scrap-melting furnace comes "by December–January 2026-27" — official: **December 2027–January 2028**; (3) it calls the tube plant a "35 KT alloy plant" — official: **35 KT tube plant**; (4) it names the copper CEO "Rajesh Singh" and the JV "Longxin Kiri" — official: **Ranjit Singh Chugh** and **Lonsen Kiri**; (5) the stored analysis says rods are due "Q4 FY28" — the official presentation says **Q2 FY28** (Aug–Sep 2027).

## Business and segments
- Legacy business: reactive dyes (36,000 TPA) and disperse dyes (8,000 TPA) at Ahmedabad; dye intermediates at Vadodara — vinyl sulphone (VS) 18,000 TPA, H-acid 7,200 TPA, specialty intermediates 16,000 TPA, acetanilide 12,000 TPA; basic chemicals (sulphuric acid, oleum, chlorosulphonic acid, thionyl chloride) 500 TPD. It is a backward-integrated chain: sulphuric acid → naphthalene/aniline intermediates (H-acid, VS) → reactive dyes for cotton textiles. [AR FY26 p.12]
- Q1 FY27 standalone revenue mix: dye intermediates 57%, dyes 28%, basic chemicals 15% (FY26: 52/33/15). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Geography: FY26 standalone domestic revenue ₹654 cr (+18%), exports and other operating income ₹124 cr (+21%) — i.e. only ~16% exported directly; most exports go through DyStar-type buyers and the JV. [AR FY26 Directors' report]
- Customers: more than 95% are textile companies; sales to related parties were **35% of total sales in FY26** (mainly the Lonsen Kiri JV, ₹181 cr) versus 0.09% a year earlier. [Q4 call; AR FY26 BRSR]
- Utilisation: ~60% average in Q1 FY27; target 70–75% in FY27 "if the market supports". [Q1 call]
- Lonsen Kiri Chemical Industries (40% JV with China's Zhejiang Longsheng; Kiri runs it) makes finished dyes including indigo; FY26 revenue ~₹1,100 cr; 60–70% of its raw material comes from Kiri. Share of profit ₹58 cr in FY26 and ₹21 cr in Q1 FY27. [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26]
- New business (not yet producing): Indo Asia Copper Ltd (IACL) — 500,000 TPA copper smelter/refinery plus tubes, rods, foil, and IndoAsia Agrotech Fertilizers — NP/NPK 1,050,000 TPA and phosphoric acid 350,000 TPA — at Jafrabad/Lunsapur near Pipavav, Gujarat. Sulphuric acid from the smelter feeds the fertiliser plant. [IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (Jun 2026): dyes standalone revenue to "cross ₹1,000 cr" in FY27 (+20%); JV above ₹1,300 cr; standalone EBITDA margin target 10–15% ("12% we will be happy"); copper Phase 1 from April 2027 with "40,000 crores plus revenue" in FY28; capex ₹4,500–5,000 cr by Mar-27, ~₹5,000 cr in FY28, ₹2,000–3,000 cr by Mar-29 (total ~₹13,300 cr incl. interest); financial closure "in the next few months"; FY28 total debt ₹8,000–9,000 cr; no dividend in FY27. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: revenue on track (₹295 cr standalone ≈ ₹1,180 cr run-rate), but standalone EBITDA margin 5.9% — **below** the 10–15% target. Financial closure **not achieved** (>50% of debt commitments received). Capital deployed in the copper project only **₹1,400 cr** (all equity) versus ~₹2,500 cr "already" cited on the Q4 call (that figure included advances). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Q1 call (Aug 2026): FY28 copper revenue guidance **dropped** — "the 20,000–25,000 crores of revenue in FY28 does not hold"; full revenue now in **FY30**. Dyes revenue guidance **raised** to a possible ~₹2,000 cr if 75–80% utilisation and current prices hold. Earlier FY28 EBITDA figure of ₹1,000–1,200 cr called "a moving target". Debt repayment from 2029. [Q1 call]
- Timeline: tube plant Q1 FY28 (June 2027), rod plant Q2 FY28, part-refinery and scrap furnace ~Jan 2028, copper foil ~Mar 2028, smelter/acid/fertiliser Q1 CY2029 (refinery Q3 FY29 in the presentation). Construction 36 months from 1 Oct 2025. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Project cost (presentation): copper complex ~₹8,100 cr (incl. desalination, conveyor), fertiliser ~₹3,600 cr, renewable power and jetty ~₹1,600 cr — **~₹13,300 cr**; claimed IRR ~25% and ROE 22–30%. Equity ~₹4,000 cr, of which ₹1,036 cr went in Sep 2024. [IP Q1FY27]
- Funding: 8.5–9% project debt with a three-year moratorium; working-capital loans of ₹3,000–5,000 cr needed once running; Gujarat incentives of 30–35% of eligible capex (~₹3,000–3,500 cr over 10 years) applied for, not yet approved; tube plant qualified under PLI. [Q4 call]
- Feedstock: MOUs (non-binding) for ~1 m t of copper concentrate against a 1.5 m t need; rock phosphate "fully tied up". On 8 Sep 2026 Kiri's subsidiary Equinaire Holdings won a foreclosure auction for **40% of Makilala Mining Co. (MCB copper-gold project, Philippines) for US$5.01 m**, after the borrowers defaulted under a May 2025 loan agreement; Celsius Resources disputes the offtake and the matter is in court. [Q1 call; BSE filing 9-Sep-26]
- Asset turns: the legacy business turns ~0.8x on consolidated fixed assets of ₹1,067 cr (FY26). For copper, ₹13,300 cr for ~500,000 t implies sales of ₹60,000 cr+ at today's copper prices but margins of only ~₹15,000–20,000/t on rods and tubes (analyst figure not disputed by management) — the returns depend on smelter/refining charges and by-products, which are not disclosed. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Physical progress: civil work ~25–30% complete (Jun 2026); ~1,000 people on site; Tata Consulting Engineers is owner's engineer since Nov 2025. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- The group is "substantially free of external debt" after repaying the Claronex judgment-finance loan; Screener borrowings ₹42 cr at Mar-26 (₹1,124 cr a year earlier). That loan cost ~₹300 cr in interest and FX over five quarters. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; Screener]
- Cash and treasury: the award is parked in debt, equity, hybrid funds and inter-corporate loans (average return "more than 10%"); Screener investments ₹3,341 cr and other assets ₹2,589 cr at Mar-26. Treasury produced a ~₹114 cr loss in Q4 FY26 and ₹284 cr gain in Q1 FY27 — i.e. earnings now swing with markets. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 45, inventory 96, payable days 205 (Screener); management says it pays suppliers late because textile customers pay late. Trade payables ₹332 cr raised by an analyst. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Cash flow: CFO ₹4,555 cr in FY26 (award), −₹342 cr in FY25; CFI −₹3,050 cr. [Screener]
- Dividends: none for FY26 or FY27; board keeps cash for the project and a ₹1,500–2,000 cr working-capital margin. No buyback decided. [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- No current credit rating (last Fitch rating 2016).

## Moat evidence
- Scale: claims to be one of the largest global H-acid producers; integrated from sulphuric acid to dyes. [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- Pricing power is cyclical, not structural: FY26 operating margin was negative as "selling prices remained largely unchanged" while inputs rose; Q1 FY27 improved only because China cut intermediate output on environmental curbs and the Hormuz shock raised prices. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- JV linkage: the JV remains "the largest single supplier to DyStar" even after the exit, which gives Kiri a captive buyer for intermediates. [Q1 call]
- No anti-dumping duty on reactive dyes or H-acid is mentioned in any document read. Copper has no demonstrated moat: no track record, feedstock not contracted, larger rivals (Adani, Hindalco).

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor emphasis of matter (two)**: (1) the DyStar award treated largely as a non-taxable capital receipt on a legal opinion — a future tax demand on the bulk of ~₹5,800 cr is possible; (2) write-off of trade receivables, loans and deposits under exceptional items. Opinion unmodified. [AR FY26 auditor's reports]
- **Capital advances**: long-outstanding advances to equipment suppliers where goods were never received; "third party confirmations… are being requested… to determine… recoverability". [AR FY26 note 46]
- **FEMA/RBI**: foreign-currency receivables and payables outstanding beyond the RBI settlement period, to be "regularised". [AR FY26 note 50]
- **Loans without terms**: CARO notes loans of ₹198.7 cr to subsidiaries/associates in FY26 where the repayment schedule "has not been stipulated"; loans to Kiri Infrastructure (an associate) ₹54.8 cr. A corporate guarantee of ₹1,446 cr appears in the contingent-liability note (column layout unclear; most likely Mar-25, backing the Claronex loan since repaid — Kiri earned ₹12.4 cr guarantee commission from Claronex in FY26). Disputed tax ₹32–47 cr. [AR FY26 CARO, notes 46, 48]
- **Related parties**: sales to related parties 35% of total; purchases from promoter-linked Metallonia Metals, Colorstar; rent/interest to promoter-linked Saptak Buildcon. Promoter family converted warrants (₹79.5 cr in Aug 2025 and more since); promoter stake rose from 31.7% (Dec-24) to **41.7%** (Jun-26). [AR FY26 note 43; Screener]
- **Treasury opacity**: ₹3,000+ cr invested in unnamed instruments and ICDs; ₹114 cr MTM loss at year-end was booked inside operating expenses, not disclosed separately. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Project risk**: financial closure missing since at least Jun 2026; guidance withdrawn one quarter after being given; MD: "If I operate 30%, we cannot service the debt." Feedstock depends on court outcome in the Philippines. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Land for plants partly held in the name of the late chairman Pravin Kiri "in his fiduciary capacity". [AR FY26 PPE note]
- Minority shareholders repeatedly press for dividends on both calls; tone of answers defensive.

## My read
Kiri is now a cash box (Screener shows ₹3,341 cr of investments plus large loans and deposits at Mar-26) attached to a weak dyes business and a very large, unfunded copper-fertiliser project run by a team with no metals track record. The stock at about half of book value is pricing in poor capital allocation — and the evidence (withdrawn guidance, missing financial closure, treasury swings, auditor emphasis on tax and write-offs, related-party sales) supports that caution. The dyes cycle has turned up on China supply cuts, but even at doubled H-acid prices the standalone margin was only ~6%. The view would improve with financial closure, a signed concentrate contract, a clean tax outcome on the award, and a steady dyes EBITDA margin above 10%; it would worsen with further slippage past June 2027 for the tube plant or any tax demand on the capital-receipt treatment.
