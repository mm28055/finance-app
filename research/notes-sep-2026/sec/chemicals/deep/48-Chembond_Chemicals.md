# Chembond Chemicals (CHEMBONDCH) — deep research, Phase A1

Entity check: this is the **new Chembond Chemicals Ltd** (formerly Chembond Chemical Specialties; NSE CHEMBONDCH, BSE 544450), which received the water-treatment, construction-chemical, distribution and hygiene businesses in a demerger effective 1 Apr 2024 and listed in 2025. The old listed company was renamed **Chembond Material Technologies Ltd** (NSE CHEMBOND) — a different stock. Screener's CHEMBOND page is the old entity; the correct page is /company/CHEMBONDCH/consolidated/.

Transcript check: the company holds half-yearly investor meets only (Nov and May), so there is **no Q1 FY27 call**. The stored "Q2 FY26" transcript is the official BSE filing of the 21 Nov 2025 meet. The stored "Q4 FY26" transcript is an **AI-generated condensation** of the 16 May 2026 meet; I compared it with the official BSE transcript (filed 27 May 2026). Figures match (H2 water ₹162 cr, volume 9,905 → 14,813 t, FY26 ₹326.15 cr, EBITDA ₹51 cr, operating EBITDA ₹46.3 cr, PAT ₹34 cr, PSU 28–30%, ~3-point margin hit, ₹65 cr cash, no customer >5–6%). The official transcript itself has one slip ("162 lakhs", corrected to ₹162 cr in the next sentence).

## Business and segments
- Formulated specialty chemicals and services, not commodity making: cooling/boiler water treatment programmes, RO membrane chemicals, coagulants, microbial/enzyme wastewater remediation, dosing systems and chlorine-dioxide generators, online monitoring and O&M; plus concrete admixtures and sealants, imported specialty-chemical distribution, and a cleaning & hygiene JV with Germany's Calvatis. 3 plants (Gujarat, Himachal, Tamil Nadu), ~950 staff; subsidiaries in Malaysia (now 100%) and Thailand. [H2 meet May-26; AR FY26 note 42]
- FY26 revenue split: water technologies 87% (84% in FY25), construction chemicals 7%, distribution 6%, cleaning & hygiene 1%. Water is ~90% chemicals, ~10% equipment. [IP H2FY26; H1 meet Nov-25]
- H2 vs H1 FY26: water ₹162.2 cr vs ₹120.3 cr (volume 14,813 t vs 9,905 t); construction ₹13.3 cr vs ₹11.1 cr (2,590 t vs 1,988 t); distribution ₹12.5 cr vs ₹9.0 cr (1,049 t vs 594 t). FY26 growth: water +15.3%, construction −15%, distribution −8%, hygiene +22%. [IP H2FY26]
- Water customers: ~30% fertiliser, ~50% petrochemicals/refining/chemicals, rest metals and others; PSUs (NTPC, IOCL, HPCL) 28–30% of water revenue; 30–35% of water business is re-tendered each year. Claimed ~60–65% share of fertiliser-plant water treatment. [H1 meet Nov-25; H2 meet May-26]
- Geography: India ₹317 cr, overseas ₹8.8 cr (₹12.9 cr in FY25) of FY26 revenue. [AR FY26 note 38]
- Market: Indian water-treatment specialty chemicals ~₹9,500 cr growing ~5.2% a year; Chembond's niche (cooling water etc.) ~₹1,500–1,600 cr growing 7–8% (management citing an unnamed study). [H1 meet Nov-25]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr — sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 65.4 / 8.4 / 12.9% / 6.3; Sep-25 73.0 / 10.7 / 14.6% / 7.2; Dec-25 86.4 / 12.4 / 14.3% / 9.7; Mar-26 101.4 / 15.9 / 15.7% / 11.6; **Jun-26 86.5 / 11.3 / 13.1% / 9.6** (+32% sales, +52% PAT YoY). [Screener]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹283 cr → FY25 292 → FY26 326; OPM 15% → 14% → 15%; PAT 30 → 31 → 35. TTM sales ₹347 cr, PAT ₹38 cr. (FY22–FY23 figures on Screener are pre-demerger and not comparable.) [Screener]
- Company-reported FY26: revenue ₹326.15 cr (+11.6%), EBITDA ₹51.3 cr (+7%), operating EBITDA ₹46.3 cr (14%), PBT ₹45.3 cr, PAT ₹34 cr. [IP H2FY26; H2 meet]
- Cost structure: materials ~54% of sales, employees 18–23% (service-heavy: 40+ people at one site), other expenses ~23%. [H1 meet; H2 meet]
- **Quality of FY26 profit:** other expenses include a **₹4.4 cr write-back of the expected-credit-loss provision** (allowance fell from ₹11.3 cr to ₹7.0 cr) against a ₹0.27 cr charge in FY25 — about 10% of FY26 PBT. [AR FY26 consolidated notes on other expenses and receivable ageing]
- Returns: Screener ROCE 23.6%, ROE 17.7%. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Nov 2025 (H1 meet):** H2 to beat H1; ₹100 cr+ water order book (₹85–90 cr chemicals, ₹10–12 cr equipment), ₹40–45 cr of it to be invoiced in H2; water to grow 10–15% a year vs a 7–8% market; ₹1,000 cr revenue "in about four years" (would need inorganic moves). [H1 meet]
- **Delivery:** H2 revenue ₹187.7 cr, +35.6% over H1; water +15.3% for FY26 (top of range); EBITDA +31% H2 over H1. **Beat.** [IP H2FY26]
- **May 2026 (H2 meet):** FY27 volume growth ~10% water, 15–20% construction; revenue improving quarter by quarter (Q1 seasonally weaker than Q4); immediate ~3-point margin hit from metal-based raw materials (zinc, molybdenum) running 120%+ above normal, to be recovered over "a couple of quarters" via April contract renewals at higher prices; ₹1,000 cr softened to "an aspiratory number … not something that we are saying we will get to". [H2 meet]
- **Q1 FY27 (no call; Screener):** revenue +32% YoY but −15% QoQ; OPM 13.1% vs 15.7% in Q4 — a ~2.6-point hit, in line with the warning. Growth guidance **kept**; the ₹1,000 cr target effectively **dropped**. [Screener; H2 meet]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: fixed assets ₹23 cr, no CWIP; plants have "inherently flexible" capacity; no new plant planned. FY26 capex of ~₹21 cr was mainly demerger asset re-tagging and office renovation, per management. [Screener; H2 meet; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Spending priorities: network and geographic growth (Malaysia buy-out of JV partner, Thailand team of ~7), outsourced dry-mortar manufacturing, three new customer-trialled water applications moving to commercial use. [H2 meet]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free; ~₹65 cr cash and investments (Screener investments ₹30 cr plus cash). Dividend yield 0.5%, payout ~10%. [H2 meet; Screener]
- **Weak cash conversion:** CFO only ₹10 cr (FY25) and ₹8 cr (FY26) against PAT of ₹31 cr and ₹35 cr; trade and other receivables absorbed ₹25.7 cr in FY26. Screener debtor days 125 (FY25) and 128 (FY26); working-capital days 104 → 120. Management quotes segment DSOs of ~80–88 days, which cover only construction and distribution. [Screener; AR FY26 cash-flow statement; H2 meet]
- Receivable ageing (31 Mar 2026): of ₹114 cr net, only ₹7.6 cr not yet due; ₹99 cr up to 6 months overdue; ₹6.2 cr 6–12 months; ₹6.7 cr credit-impaired and fully provided. [AR FY26 note 47]

## Moat evidence
- Switching costs: customers spend little on water treatment but face costly downtime if it fails, so "attrition rates are very low"; first-fill chemicals follow equipment sales; on-site teams and online monitoring embed the supplier. [H1 meet]
- But ~30–35% of water revenue is re-bid every year, and small ex-MNC start-ups "break pricing"; tender business is where share is lost when margins fall. [H1 meet]
- Customer spread: no customer above 5–6% of revenue. [H2 meet]
- Pricing: private customers accepted 3-month surcharges (5–12%) during the raw-material spike; PSUs refused until the government declared force majeure. [H2 meet]
- Competitors: Ecolab/Nalco, Solenis, Veolia (Suez), Ion Exchange, Thermax, Vasu Chemicals (now owned by Dorf Ketal). Management says its margins are "at par" with peers. [H2 meet]

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party purchases:** ₹26.0 cr of goods bought from **Finor Piplaj Chemicals** (an entity the Shah family influences) in FY26 (₹26.6 cr in FY25) — roughly 15% of material costs; ₹3.4 cr payable to it at year-end. Also ₹2.9 cr purchases from and ₹1.3 cr sales to Chembond Material Technologies (the sister listed company); ₹40 lakh rent from it. Pricing basis not disclosed. [AR FY26 note 42]
- Profit quality: the ₹4.4 cr ECL write-back and receivables growth faster than sales mean FY26 profit converted poorly into cash. [AR FY26; Screener]
- Raw-material shock: metal-based inputs at 120%+ of normal; PSU fixed-price contracts limit pass-through. [H2 meet]
- Concentration in one segment (water 87%); construction chemicals is monsoon-dependent. [IP H2FY26]
- Contingent liabilities small (₹0.54 cr: GST ₹0.43 cr, income tax ₹0.11 cr). Auditor's opinion unmodified; key audit matter is revenue cut-off; audit trail maintained. [AR FY26 notes 46, 51; auditor's report]
- Pay: CMD ₹2.0 cr and water-subsidiary ED ₹1.5 cr (together ~10% of PAT). Promoters hold 68.0%, creeping up (SAST filings Aug 2026). [AR FY26 note 42; Screener]
- No current credit-rating rationale for the new entity found; the CRISIL ratings on Screener relate to the old company. [Screener; CRISIL search]

## What changed between the two meets (Nov 2025 → May 2026)
- From "flat revenue for two years, prolonged monsoon, IOCL plant shutdowns" to a record ₹101 cr quarter and ₹30–32 cr record month (March 2026).
- New issue: West Asia war cost spike; force majeure invoked; margins guided down ~3 points temporarily.
- ₹1,000 cr target moved from a four-year internal plan to an "aspirational" number.
- New disclosures: volumes by segment, three new water applications, Malaysia JV fully bought.

## My read
Post-demerger Chembond is a debt-free, asset-light water-treatment service business earning a steady 13–15% operating margin with a 24% ROCE, and H2 FY26 plus Q1 FY27 show real volume-led growth (+32% YoY in Q1). The weak points are cash conversion (two years of CFO at a quarter of profit, 128 debtor days), a ₹4.4 cr provision write-back flattering FY26, and ₹26 cr of purchases from a promoter-linked supplier. The stock at ~18x trailing earnings needs only ~10–12% growth, which looks achievable if margins recover toward 15%. The view improves with CFO catching up with profit and margin back at 15% by H2 FY27; it worsens if receivables keep climbing or raw-material costs stay high on PSU contracts.
