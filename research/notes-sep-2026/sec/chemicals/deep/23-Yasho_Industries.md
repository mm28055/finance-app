## Business and segments
- Makes speciality additives in batch plants: **rubber chemicals** (antioxidants and accelerators for tyres, belts, latex gloves, condoms), **lubricant additives** (antioxidants/anti-wear agents for hydraulic, turbine, engine and gear oils, metal-working fluids, greases), stabilisers for acrylics, inks and resins; plus a small **consumer** line (aroma/flavour, food, pharma and agro intermediates). About 140–150 products, 2,000+ customers in 50+ countries. Sells pure chemicals only — never formulations — so it does not compete with customers (Lubrizol, Infineum-type formulators). [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Jul-26; Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 mix: industrial chemicals 89% (87% a year ago), consumer 11%; exports 69%, domestic 31% (domestic grew only ~10% YoY). FY26: industrial 87%, exports ~62%. US and Europe are the main export markets; new push into Asia and Africa. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Plants: three units at Vapi (peak revenue potential ₹650–700 cr, "95–98%" utilised per an analyst — management declined to confirm) and the greenfield Pakhajan site (42 acres, only 25% of land used; commercial from April 2024; peak potential ₹800–850 cr at existing capacity). Company utilisation ~60% in FY26, 60–62% in March 2026, "over 65%" in Q1 FY27; target 75% in FY27. Capacity in tonnes is no longer disclosed. [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Jul-26; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Subsidiaries: Yasho Industries Europe B.V. and Yasho Inc. (US) — sales arms; receivables from them rose to ₹33.0 cr (₹18.5 cr a year earlier). [AR FY26 note 39; Q1 call]
- Customer concentration low: top customer ~7% of revenue. More than 50% of revenue now on long-term, formula-priced contracts (quarterly/half-yearly price resets) vs ~40% stated on the Q4 call. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly consolidated (Screener, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 199 / op. profit 33 (17%) / PAT 4; Sep-25 183 / 33 (18%) / 5; Dec-25 202 / 33 (17%) / 4; Mar-26 246 / 44 (18%) / 12; **Jun-26 308 / 73 (24%) / 36**. [Screener]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹594 cr → FY25 ₹676 cr → FY26 ₹830 cr; OPM 17% each year; PAT ₹58 cr → ₹6 cr → ₹25 cr (interest ₹15 cr → ₹60 cr → ₹56 cr and depreciation ₹16 cr → ₹50 cr → ₹55 cr once Pakhajan was capitalised). TTM sales ₹939 cr, op. profit ₹184 cr (20%), PAT ₹58 cr. 3-year sales CAGR 7%, profit CAGR −28%; ROCE 9%, ROE 5.9%. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹307.7 cr (+55% YoY, +25% QoQ), gross margin 42.4% (40.6% a year ago), EBITDA ₹74.4 cr (24.2% vs 16.5%), finance cost ₹11.3 cr (₹13.8 cr), PAT ₹36.1 cr (₹3.6 cr). [IP Q1FY27]
- Unit economics: volume +42% YoY, so price/mix added ~9%; a 10–15% price increase was taken from late March/April as raw materials rose. Margins are protected in **percentage** terms (formula pricing passes costs through), not per kg. New products launched in the last 12 months earn "about 10–12%" more margin; products from the last 2 years are ~30% of revenue. [Q1 call; Q4 call; derived]
- Raw materials: petrochemical derivatives, solvents and sulphur-based chemistry; imports are 50–55% of raw-material purchases, China 20–25% of imports. In Q4–Q1, raw materials were short and slow (imports taking 8–12 weeks vs 3–4), and exports to the US took 60–70 days vs 25; container bookings delayed 3–4 weeks. [ICRA Jul-26; Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (May 2026): FY28 revenue ₹1,500 cr; FY27 volume growth 35–45% (also stated as 40–50%) on ~15 points higher utilisation (to 75%); EBITDA margin up "2%, 3%" (~20%); FY27 capex ₹125 cr "completely funded through internal accruals"; working capital to 170–175 days; net debt/EBITDA to ~2.5x. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: revenue +55%, volume +42% — **on track/beat**; EBITDA margin 24.2% — **beat by ~4 points**; working capital 143 days — **beat**; capex funding — **changed** (now ₹100 cr of new borrowing). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Q1 call (Aug 2026): FY28 target **raised** to >₹1,600 cr (excluding the two new buildings); 30–40% annual revenue growth for 2–3 years; "maintain" 24% margin in FY27 (not expand); FY27 capex **doubled** to ₹250 cr for two new Pakhajan buildings (Phase 1 ₹100 cr, output from Q1 FY28; Phase 2 ₹150 cr, Q4 FY28); 60–65% of the new capacity has customer commitments; asset turn ~2.5x on the new capex (~₹600 cr of revenue potential). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Consistency flag: ICRA's rationale of 9 Jul 2026 records that the company "does not intend to take any new term loan"; three weeks later the company announced ~₹100 cr of borrowing. [ICRA Jul-26; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹75 cr (R&D centre ₹25 cr, Pakhajan ₹42 cr, Vapi ₹8 cr); Q1 FY27 capex ₹18.7 cr. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- MNC contract (15-year supply deal signed FY26, ~₹90 cr of dedicated capex): customer advance received ₹98.1 cr to date, ₹70.9 cr paid to equipment vendors; equipment delivery Q3 FY27, trials Q4 FY27, commercial production Q1 FY28. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- New: two Pakhajan buildings ₹250 cr (FY27–FY28), funded by ~₹100 cr debt plus accruals; management targets 2.5x asset turn. [Q1 call]
- Asset turns so far: FY26 sales ₹830 cr on ₹634 cr of net fixed assets ≈ 1.3x; peak potential of existing sites (₹1,450–1,550 cr) implies ~2.3x — the lift depends on filling Pakhajan. [Screener; IP Q1FY27; derived]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Total debt ₹548 cr (FY24), ₹559 cr (FY25), ₹541 cr (FY26), ₹552 cr (Q1 FY27) — flat in absolute terms. Screener borrowings ₹558 cr at FY26. Free cash only ₹3.5 cr at 31 Mar 2026; working-capital limits ~70% used. [IP Q1FY27; Screener; ICRA Jul-26]
- The "1.86x debt/EBITDA" headline uses Q1 EBITDA × 4 (₹74.4 cr × 4 ≈ ₹298 cr); on trailing Screener EBITDA of ₹184 cr the ratio is ~3.0x. ICRA: FY26 gearing 1.3x, debt/OPBDITA 3.9x, interest cover 2.6x, DSCR 1.7x (downgrade trigger: DSCR below 2x sustained). [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Jul-26; derived]
- Cash flow: CFO −₹41 cr (FY25) → +₹151 cr (FY26); FCF −₹51 cr → +₹77 cr. The FY26 CFO includes the ₹51 cr MNC advance. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Working capital: Screener inventory days 247 (FY25) → 186 (FY26), cash conversion cycle 259 → 201 days; company says 190 → 143 days in Q1 FY27, partly because raw material could not be obtained (management: inventory fell as "we did not have sufficient raw material coming in"). [Screener; IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Equity: ₹125 cr preferential issue in FY25 (issue price ~₹1,900/share); dividend payout ~2%. [AR FY26; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Approvals: MNC formulators need long qualification of each batch — the reason inventory is high — and several "key global customer" approvals came in Q1 FY27; one MNC paid ~₹98 cr upfront for a 15-year supply deal (the auditor made this a key audit matter). [Q1 call; ICRA Jul-26; AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Contract share: >50% of revenue on formula pricing (up from 30–40%); pricing held firm in Q1 despite raw-material swings. [Q1 call]
- R&D: 50+ scientists, new R&D centre and pilot plant at Pakhajan (Oct 2025); only chases molecules with ₹50–100 cr potential. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Limits: management calls Chinese competition in lube additives and industrial chemicals "very strong"; Yasho is a secondary source for many new customers. Margins were stuck at 15–18% for FY21–FY26 (ICRA) before Q1's 24%. No anti-dumping protection was cited for its products. [Q4 call; ICRA Jul-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **GST disputes ₹38.5 cr** (net of ₹4.0 cr paid under protest): ₹33.7 cr of export-refund denials (FY18–FY21) and ₹8.2 cr on the "pre-import condition" (FY18–FY20), pending before authorities; customs penalties ₹1.75 cr at CESTAT. Together ≈ 9% of net worth (~₹444 cr). [AR FY26 note 37; CARO]
- Leverage: debt ~₹550 cr against ~₹444 cr equity; rising again with ₹100 cr of new loans; profit is highly sensitive to interest (₹56 cr in FY26 vs PAT ₹25 cr). [Screener; ICRA Jul-26]
- Auditor (Gokhale & Sathe): unmodified opinion; key audit matters — accounting for the MNC advance (contract liability vs financing) and inventory valuation. No CARO qualifications found beyond the disputed dues list. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Governance/disclosure: management stopped disclosing plant capacities and plant-level utilisation; refused customer and product names (NDAs); promoter holding fell from 71.9% to 68.0% in Mar 2025 (preferential issue) and some promoter-group relatives were reclassified as public in Jan 2026. The MD's remark on the Q1 call "We have shut Vapi also" (R&D context) was not explained. [Q1 call; Screener; AR FY26 note 39]
- Supply chain: raw-material shortages and container delays could cap volume; margin gains partly reflect price increases in a disrupted market. [Q1 call]
- Execution: ₹250 cr of simultaneous capex plus the MNC project during a 30–40% growth year stretches a company with ₹3.5 cr of free cash. [ICRA Jul-26; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- From "hold ~20% margin, fund capex from cash" (May) to "sustain 24%, double capex, borrow ₹100 cr, FY28 >₹1,600 cr" (Aug). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Ratings: CRISIL upgraded to A-/Stable from BBB+/Positive (29 Jul 2026); ICRA assigned A-/Stable/A2+ on ₹500 cr (9 Jul 2026). [CRISIL letter 29-Jul-26; ICRA Jul-26]

## My read
The business is clearly improving: volumes up 42%, utilisation moving from 60% to 65–75%, a funded MNC contract starting in FY28 and a margin step-up to 24% as Pakhajan fills. But profits have only just recovered to FY23 levels, absolute debt has not fallen, the headline leverage ratio flatters by annualising one strong quarter, and ₹38.5 cr of GST disputes sit in the notes. The price needs the 30–40% growth path to hold through FY28–FY29 with margins near 24% and debt/EBITDA falling on a trailing basis; watch Q2–Q3 margins (whether Q1 was helped by scarcity pricing), MNC plant start-up in Q1 FY28, and the GST cases.
