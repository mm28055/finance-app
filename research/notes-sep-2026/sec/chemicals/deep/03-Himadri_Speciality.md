## Business and segments
- **What it makes.** A carbon-chemistry company built on coal tar distillation. Coal tar (a by-product of steel-plant coke ovens) is distilled into **coal tar pitch (CTP)** for aluminium smelter anodes and graphite electrodes, plus naphthalene, anthracene oil and carbon black feedstock oil. That oil feeds **carbon black** (rubber grades for tyres and "specialty" grades for plastics, inks, coatings, batteries). Downstream: SNF/PCE construction chemicals, naphthalene consumer products, and new lines — anthraquinone and carbazole, anode material, LFP cathode, carbon nanotubes (CNT) and "super speciality" carbon black. Since April 2025 it also owns **Birla Tyres**. [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- **Scale and share:** 600,000 t/yr coal tar distillation at one site ("world's largest single-location integrated carbon complex"); domestic CTP share over 65%; carbon black 250,000 t/yr, of which 130,000 t specialty (70,000 t added in FY26), 70+ specialty grades, "top five globally" in specialty black. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Utilisation:** 80% across coal tar and carbon black in Q1 FY27, expected 90%+ during the year; new specialty black line guided at 85–90% utilisation in FY27. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Segments (FY26, AR):** Carbon materials & chemicals external revenue ₹4,367 cr with segment result ₹828 cr (~19%); Power ₹19 cr; Others (tyres, mining) ₹274 cr. Birla Tyres revenue ₹187 cr in FY26 and ₹127 cr in Q1 FY27. Mining earned a profit in Q4 FY26 but nothing in Q1 FY27 (environmental clearance pending, "3–6 months"). [AR FY26 segment note; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Geography and customers:** exports ₹1,358 cr in FY26 (29% of revenue, up from ₹1,232 cr); 61 countries (56 a year earlier). No single customer above 10% of revenue in FY26 (two in FY25). New liquid-pitch export terminals at Haldia and Mangalore; ~50,000 t of CTP from new capacity to be exported by FY28. [AR FY26; Q4 call]
- **Raw materials:** coal tar (domestic and imported) and carbon black feedstock; management says it is "not dependent on China for anything" and not on West Asia; price increases are passed to customers, with a lag. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (Screener):** Q4 FY25 revenue 1,135 / EBITDA 233 (21%) / PAT 155; Q1 FY26 1,118 / 245 (22%) / 179; Q2 FY26 1,071 / 233 (22%) / 176; Q3 FY26 1,184 / 243 (20%) / 192; Q4 FY26 1,288 / 242 (19%) / 208; Q1 FY27 1,432 / 288 (20%) / 228. [Screener]
- **Company-reported EBITDA is higher** (Q1 FY27 ₹313 cr, 22%; Q4 FY26 ₹280 cr) because it excludes foreign-exchange losses that Screener counts in expenses; FY26 forex loss was ₹64 cr. [Q1 call; AR FY26 segment note]
- **Annual:** revenue FY24 4,185 → FY25 4,613 → FY26 4,661; EBITDA 645 → 854 → 962; margin 15% → 19% → 21%; PAT 411 → 555 → 755 (+36%). TTM revenue 4,974, PAT 804. [Screener]
- **Plain words:** for three years revenue barely moved (3-year sales CAGR 4%) while profit compounded (3-year PAT CAGR 47%) — the shift from commodity pitch/black to specialty grades. FY27 is the first year of real volume growth: Q1 revenue +28%, of which Birla Tyres is ~₹127 cr. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Other income matters:** ₹171 cr in FY26 and ₹56 cr in Q1 FY27 — interest on FDs (funded partly by bank loans and commercial paper), mutual-fund gains, and mark-to-market on IBC, Sicona and Birla Tyres-related NCDs. Management says it can swing 30–40% a quarter. Other income was ~17% of FY26 pre-tax profit. [Screener; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Unit economics:** average EBITDA ~₹17,000/t across the portfolio in FY26, specialty "significantly higher". [Q4 call]
- **Returns:** Screener ROCE 22%, ROE 17.8%; company quotes ROCE 32%. [Screener; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **PAT ₹1,100 cr+ in FY28** (double FY25's ₹555 cr): stated on Q4 call, reaffirmed on Q1 ("I'll remain at ₹1,100 crores"). TTM PAT is ₹804 cr; needs ~37% more in under two years. **Kept.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Growth:** Q4 — "in FY27 we will see growth in both top line and bottom line". Q1 — revenue +28%, PAT +27%. **Delivered.** No annual guidance is given ("never"). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Margins:** Q4 — confident of sustaining ~20% EBITDA margin. Q1 Screener margin 20%. **Kept.** [Q4 call; Screener]
- **LFP cathode:** 2,000 t first line in Q3 FY27 (both calls); full 40,000 t Phase 1 (₹1,125 cr) — Q4 call said full operations in FY29, Q1 call said "operational in FY28"; 200,000 t (₹4,800 cr announced) in ~5 years. **On track; Phase-1 wording drifted earlier.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Anode:** 200 t plant commissioned 23 Apr 2026 (₹120 cr spent); samples moved from "Sample A" (Q4) to "Sample B" to many customers (Q1); approval takes 1.5–2 years; no large-scale capex yet announced. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Anthraquinone/carbazole:** 2,600 t in Q2 FY27, 2,700 t more in Q2 FY28 (5,300 t total). **On track.** [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **New in Q1:** CNT 200 t (₹70 cr, Q4 FY27) and super speciality black 6,000 t (₹170 cr, FY28). [Q1 call]
- **Birla Tyres:** ₹3,000 cr revenue in 4–5 years; EBITDA breakeven and "cash positive" in FY27; passenger radial (PCR) plant by FY28. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Forex:** Q4 — losses from hedging during rupee swings, "after Q1 we expect to be in a strong position". Q1 — another loss; "this is the last quarter". **Missed once, re-promised.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Programme ~₹2,000 cr over FY27–FY28** (~₹1,000 cr each year): LFP Phase 1 ₹1,125 cr; CNT ₹70 cr; super speciality black ₹170 cr; anthraquinone etc. (part of ₹368 cr announced 15 July); Birla Tyres modernisation/OTR conversion ~₹500 cr. No capex planned for coal tar distillation. [Q1 call]
- **History:** CFI −₹405 cr (FY24), −₹235 cr (FY25), −₹963 cr (FY26, includes investments); FY26 fixed-asset additions ~₹450 cr. CWIP ₹372 cr. [Screener; AR FY26 segment note]
- **Asset-turn targets:** LFP 2x ("40,000 t at today's value ≈ ₹3,000 cr revenue" on ₹1,125 cr — i.e. ~2.7x); super speciality black 3x (~₹500 cr); anthraquinone/carbazole ₹250–300 cr; anode 200 t ≈ ₹120–130 cr; CNT 200 t < ₹100 cr. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Long-term lithium-ion ambition:** ~₹30,000 cr revenue from 100 GWh-equivalent of anode/cathode/silicon-carbon in 5–6 years (referred to on Q1 call). [Q1 call]
- **Funding:** "free cash flow will cover that. There is no question of any incremental debt." Yet FY26 FCF was −₹63 cr and FY27–28 capex is ~2x FY26 CFO. [Q1 call; Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Balance sheet:** borrowings ₹313 cr (Mar-25) → ₹770 cr (Mar-26); investments ₹582 cr → ₹1,060 cr; company-stated net cash ₹121 cr at March 2026 (it was larger a year earlier). Bank deposits of ~₹511 cr are pledged against credit facilities. Management says it borrows to "keep the limits intact" and earns a spread. [Screener; Q4 call; AR FY26 note on bank balances]
- **Cash flow:** CFO ₹405 cr (FY24), ₹447 cr (FY25), ₹382 cr (FY26) — only 64% of operating profit in FY26; free cash flow ₹352 cr, ₹276 cr, −₹63 cr. [Screener]
- **Working capital:** debtor days 55, inventory days 96 (up from 68), payable days 47; cash conversion cycle 103 days (73 in FY24). Working capital is stretching as revenue grows. [Screener]
- **Dividend:** payout ~5%. Promoter holding 52.5% (up from 51.6% after warrant conversion). [Screener]
- **Rating:** no current rating rationale could be opened (ICRA page required login); not used.

## Moat evidence
- **Feedstock integration and scale:** single-site 600,000 t distillation, own feedstock for carbon black and anode precursor, "we supply coal tar pitch at a price lower than China", 65%+ domestic CTP share. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Technology claims:** in-house anode precursor (first to supply pitch-based precursor to top Chinese anode makers), in-house LFP and CNT; R&D ~₹120–125 cr (2.6% of sales), 180+ scientists. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Qualification positions:** LFP Sample A approved; anode Sample B with Indian and global customers; IBC (≈19% stake) uses Himadri LFP/anode in its cells; IBC building a 7 GWh plant in India for Q4 FY27. [Q1 call]
- **Evidence of pricing power in the core:** margin rose from 6% (FY22) to 21% (FY26) with flat revenue; gross margin steady through the West Asia raw-material spike. [Screener; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Birla Tyres structure and funding:** Birla Tyres was bought out of insolvency in Oct 2023 by Dalmia Bharat Refractories (DBRL) with Himadri as strategic partner; the tyre undertaking was demerged into DBRL, Himadri holds 8% DBRL preference shares, OCDs, and near-zero-coupon NCDs of DBRL. Consolidated NCD investments rose to ~₹935 cr at Mar-26 from ~₹578 cr — a large, low-yield exposure to a non-group company that funds the tyre revival. On the Q1 call the CMD said Birla Tyres "is reflected on Dalmia Refractory balance sheet" while sales go through a Himadri subsidiary. Birla Tyres Ltd became a wholly owned subsidiary in April 2025; Himadri Birla Tyre Manufacturer (49%) is consolidated by contractual control. This is complex and hard to audit from outside. [AR FY26 note 7; Q1 call]
- **LFP economics:** an analyst's model showed a negative gross spread at Chinese LFP prices; management: "not possible for you to calculate my cost on Excel sheet… I cannot disclose my sources." China holds ~100% of LFP capacity. [Q1 call]
- **Earnings quality:** ~17% of FY26 PBT is other income, partly from borrowing to invest in FDs and from fair-value gains on unlisted investments (IBC, Sicona, DBRL NCDs). Repeated forex losses from a changed hedging stance. [Screener; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Cash conversion:** FY26 CFO/EBITDA 64% and negative FCF even before the ₹2,000 cr capex; "no incremental debt" promise sits awkwardly with borrowings more than doubling in FY26. [Screener]
- **Mining:** no revenue in Q1 pending environmental clearance. [Q1 call]
- **Auditor:** unmodified; key audit matter is revenue recognition (discounts and price adjustments). Contingent liabilities small (~₹32 cr tax/others); EPCG export obligation ₹10 cr. [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 35]
- **Disclosure gaps:** no volume split, no product margins, no offtake contracts for LFP (NDAs only), no anode capex plan. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 focused on anode commissioning and Birla Tyres revival; Q1 added two new product lines (CNT, super speciality black) and a firm FY27–28 capex split. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Revenue growth returned (+28%) after three flat years; volumes fell in Q1 but prices and mix rose. [Q1 call]
- Mining profit disappeared; forex loss recurred; other income stayed high. [Q1 call]
- LFP Phase 1 timeline language moved from "full operations FY29" to "operational in FY28". [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## My read
The core pitch and carbon black business is genuinely improved — margins tripled in four years on flat revenue, and specialty black capacity is now filling. The stock (about 43x trailing earnings) is pricing the FY28 ₹1,100 cr PAT target and some battery-materials optionality; that target needs ~37% growth from TTM in under two years, which depends on specialty black ramp, anthraquinone, and Birla Tyres reaching breakeven. The weak spots are cash conversion, a growing, opaque exposure to Birla Tyres through Dalmia Refractories NCDs, rising borrowings despite "no debt" talk, and unproven LFP economics against China. What would change my view: LFP customer contracts and a clean Birla Tyres breakeven (positive); further forex losses, falling CFO/EBITDA or more money lent to DBRL (negative).
