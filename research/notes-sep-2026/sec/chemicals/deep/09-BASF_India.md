# BASF India (BASF) — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged. BASF India holds no quarterly calls; it hosts a half-yearly analyst/fund-manager meet. The two latest official transcripts (19 Nov 2025 and 22 May 2026, both BSE filings) were read; the stored May-2026 transcript matches the official text. No meet has been held on the Q1 FY27 results (next expected after the 4 Nov 2026 board meeting).

## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** a 73.33%-owned listed arm of BASF SE. Six segments, FY26 revenue incl. coatings ₹15,539 cr: Materials ~₹4,620 cr (~30%: polyurethane systems, Celasto microcellular PU jounce bumpers for cars, engineering plastics, monomers/isocyanates); Nutrition & Care ₹3,501 cr (23%: personal-care and home-care ingredients, UV filters, pharma excipients, vitamins); Industrial Solutions ₹2,946 cr (19%: acrylic dispersions for paints, paper and construction chemicals, performance chemicals, process catalysts); Chemicals ₹1,847 cr (12%, purely traded intermediates); Agricultural Solutions ₹1,944 cr (12%: fungicides, herbicides, insecticides, mostly toll-manufactured); Surface Technologies (auto OEM/refinish coatings) ₹595 cr (4%) [IP May-26 p.8; CRISIL Jul-26].
- **Own-made vs traded:** own-manufactured products (OMP) ~42% of sales at 25–30% gross margin; merchandise (imported from BASF group, mainly via BASF Hong Kong) ~58% at 5–10% gross margin [May-26 meet].
- **Sites:** seven manufacturing sites incl. Dahej and Panoli (Gujarat), Mangalore, Thane/Turbhe (Navi Mumbai) [May-26 meet; Nov-25 meet].
- **Customers/end markets:** autos, two-wheelers, appliances, footwear, furniture, paints, construction, FMCG (personal/home care — incl. tolling for large FMCG companies), pharma, farmers via 1,700 distributors and ~2 lakh retailers [May-26 meet; Nov-25 meet].
- **Market share evidence:** ~7% of India's crop-protection market; "very strong market share" and market leader in Celasto; dispersions a strong OMP franchise (5th line coming) [May-26 meet; Nov-25 meet].
- **Portfolio changes:** coatings sold to Carlyle for ₹230.16 cr on 30 Jun 2026 (BASF group keeps 40% globally; India valued on DCF because the business "is just breaking even"); agro business to be demerged 1:1 into BASF Agricultural Solutions India Ltd — shareholders approved 24 Jun 2026, NCLT approval expected by end-2026, CRISIL says effective 1 Jan 2027 with listing likely March–April 2027. Agro was ~13% of FY26 revenue (₹1,944 cr) but ~39% of EBIT (₹254 cr) [May-26 meet; CRISIL Jul-26; News: ScanX, 12 Aug 2026].
- **Export share:** exports ₹348 cr = 2.3% of FY26 turnover; ~65% of input material by value is sourced from within India (68% in FY25), i.e. about a third is imported [AR FY26 Board's report and BRSR].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr, revenue / operating profit / margin / net profit):** Jun-25 3,875 / 214 / 6% / 137; Sep-25 3,896 / 155 / 4% / 107; Dec-25 3,863 / 170 / 4% / 107; Mar-26 3,444 / 111 / 3% / 69; Jun-26 4,824 / 504 / 10% / 360 [Screener].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹13,767 cr, op. profit ₹890 cr (6%), PAT ₹563 cr; FY25 ₹14,780 cr / ₹730 cr (5%) / ₹479 cr; FY26 ₹14,944 cr / ₹660 cr (4%) / ₹420 cr; TTM ₹16,027 cr / ₹941 cr (6%) / ₹643 cr [Screener]. EBITDA ~₹633 cr in FY21 and ~₹656 cr in FY26 — flat for five years (analyst question, not disputed) [May-26 meet].
- **FY26 drivers:** volume +6–7%, price −5–6%, so sales +1%; PBT before exceptional ₹564 cr vs ₹617 cr; Q4 PBT ₹90 cr vs ₹25 cr. Nutrition & Care PBT fell from ₹65 cr to ₹31 cr despite higher sales; Materials PBT +₹27 cr; coatings +₹3 cr [May-26 meet].
- **Q1 FY27 windfall:** revenue +29%, PBT before exceptional ₹499 cr vs ₹188 cr, "higher volumes and better price realisation" after the West Asia war lifted chemical prices. Segment results (₹ cr): Materials 213 on revenue 1,343; Industrial Solutions 125 on 1,000; Chemicals (traded) 96 on 928 (revenue more than doubled); Nutrition & Care revenue 984; Agricultural Solutions revenue lower at 557 (segment results for these two not confirmed); exceptional gain ₹18 cr from the coatings sale [News: ScanX, 12 Aug 2026, quoted text].
- **Returns:** FY26 ROCE 16.3% (18.5% in FY25); return on net operating assets 19.7%; EPS ₹97 (₹110) [May-26 meet]. Screener ROCE 15%, ROE 11% [Screener].

## Guidance history
- **Policy:** "we do not provide any forecast… and we do not comment on or give guidance on future results" — repeated at both meets [Nov-25 meet; May-26 meet].
- **Nov-2025:** no pricing relief expected in 2026 ("would not be fair to say that we see an immediate sort of hockey stick"); Celasto plant ready H2 2026; demerger during FY27; receivables to come down by December [Nov-25 meet].
- **May-2026:** expected "slight softening of demand" and input-cost pass-through with a lag; Celasto live by end-2026; dispersion line-3 by end-2027; FY27 capex "more than ₹200 cr" [May-26 meet].
- **Delivery:** Q1 FY27 beat everything implied — margin 10% vs 3–6% in FY26 — on war-driven price realisations, not on a structural change. Demerger and coatings sale on schedule; working capital unwound partly (Q1 CFO not published) [Screener; News: ScanX].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹200 cr, of which ~₹150 cr on Celasto (Dahej); another ₹150–170 cr needed to finish it; maintenance ~₹82–100 cr a year [May-26 meet].
- Dispersion line-3 at Mangalore (ground-broken Feb-2026) adds ~15% to India dispersion capacity, commissioning end-2027 [May-26 meet].
- Thane engineering-plastics compounding line expanded ~80% (2025) [Nov-25 meet].
- **Asset turns:** very high because most sales are traded: FY26 revenue ₹14,944 cr on fixed assets ₹725 cr ≈ 20x [Screener].
- **Group MDI project (not the listed company):** on 21 Sep 2026 BASF SE said it is at an advanced feasibility stage for an MDI (methylene diphenyl diisocyanate, a polyurethane building block) complex at Dahej, on land secured "through its Indian subsidiary, BASF India Polyurethanes Private Limited". The listed company's disclosure does not say BASF India Ltd will own or fund it [BSE filing 21-Sep-26]. Management in May: "in BIL right now, we don't see a large capex investment" [May-26 meet].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free; ~₹800–874 cr cash (₹874 cr at Mar-26); net worth ₹3,957 cr; ~₹1,510 cr bank lines, 13% average use [May-26 meet; CRISIL Jul-26].
- FY26 CFO −₹110 cr (₹373 cr in FY25): ~₹700 cr cash profit swallowed by ~₹800 cr working-capital build (payables cut from ₹4,200 cr to ₹3,800 cr, receivables up); FCF ≈ −₹310 cr [May-26 meet; Screener].
- Receivable days 65 with 8% overdue; inventory up ~10 days by choice during supply disruptions [May-26 meet].
- Dividend payout 26% of FY26 profit [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Parent technology: BASF spends ~€2 bn a year on R&D, ~half in agriculture; 12 agro launches in four years now ~25% of agro sales, 26 agro products above ₹10 cr each [May-26 meet; Nov-25 meet].
- Switching costs in Celasto (OEM homologation) and pharma/food-grade approvals [May-26 meet].
- But in traded chemicals and monomers, prices follow Chinese oversupply: FY26 prices fell 5–6% on higher volumes [May-26 meet].
- CRISIL AAA/Stable (reaffirmed 28 Jul 2026), resting on parent support; downgrade trigger is operating margin below 2.5–3% [CRISIL Jul-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Demerger dilution:** after 1 Jan 2027 the listed company loses agro (~39% of EBIT) — holders receive agro shares 1:1, but BASF India's own earnings base shrinks [CRISIL Jul-26].
- **Payments to parent:** royalty ~₹156 cr in FY26 (₹130 cr in FY25; rates 1.5–13% by product), IT charges ~₹150 cr, plus Kuala Lumpur shared-service fees; an analyst estimated royalty + service payments rose from ₹335 cr (FY23) to ₹436 cr (FY25) while sales stagnated [May-26 meet]. Material related-party purchases/sales: BASF Hong Kong ₹3,687 cr, BASF SE ₹1,029 cr (FY26, AOC-2), all declared arm's length [AR FY26 AOC-2].
- **Coatings sold at ~0.4x sales** (₹230 cr on ₹595 cr revenue), versus a much richer global deal; management says the Indian unit only breaks even [May-26 meet].
- **Group opportunities may sit outside the listed entity** (MDI via a separate subsidiary; new global digital and business-service hubs in Hyderabad are separate BASF SE companies) [BSE filing 21-Sep-26; May-26 meet].
- **Contingent liabilities** ₹236 cr, provisions ₹27 cr; auditor KAM on litigations; opinion unqualified [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Cash flow:** negative FY26 CFO; margin structurally thin (4–6%) outside windfall quarters [Screener].
- **Ownership:** promoter 73.33% (no pledge concern); free float ~27% [Screener].

## What changed between the two meets
- Nov-2025: H1 sales −4%, PBT −27%, prices −4–7%, CFO −₹900 cr, pessimistic on pricing. May-2026: Q4 PBT ₹90 cr vs ₹25 cr, volumes +12–15% in Q4, but war inflation and possible stagflation flagged [Nov-25 meet; May-26 meet].
- Demerger moved from "regulatory approvals ongoing" to SEBI/exchange clearance (2 Feb 2026) and shareholder meeting (24 Jun 2026) [meets].
- Coatings: from "evaluation under process" to ₹230 cr valuation; sale closed 30 Jun 2026 [meets; News: ScanX].
- New: royalty/service-fee scrutiny, ROCE fall to 16.3%, capex over ₹200 cr for FY27 [May-26 meet].

## My read
BASF India is a safe, cash-rich distributor-plus-manufacturer whose normal margin is 4–6%, with profit stuck around ₹650 cr of EBITDA for five years. Q1 FY27's ₹360 cr profit is a war-price windfall concentrated in traded chemicals and materials; it lifts TTM profit to ₹643 cr but is unlikely to repeat. From January 2027 agro (≈39% of EBIT) leaves, so the listed "remaining" company needs Celasto, dispersions and a better OMP mix to grow. I would turn positive if post-demerger margins hold above ~6% without the war tailwind and royalty/service payments stop rising faster than sales; negative if margins slip back toward 3–4% or group growth projects (MDI) stay outside the listed company.
