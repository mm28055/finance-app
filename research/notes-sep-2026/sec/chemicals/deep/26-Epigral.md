# Epigral (EPIGRAL) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: both stored transcripts were compared with the official BSE filings (Q1 FY27 call, 27 Jul 2026; Q4 FY26 call, 2 May 2026). The stored copies have material errors, listed under **Stored-data errors** at the end. The most important: the stored copies call the ₹30,000 → ₹35,000–36,000 figure an "ECH realisation" — the official text says it is the **ECU** (caustic-soda-plus-chlorine "electrochemical unit") realisation per tonne. ECH itself sells at about ₹180–190/kg.

## Business and segments
- **Chemistry.** Salt is electrolysed (chlor-alkali) into caustic soda, chlorine and hydrogen; one "ECU" is 1 tonne caustic + 0.89 tonne chlorine. Chlorine and hydrogen are then turned in-house into derivatives: chloromethanes (methanol + chlorine), hydrogen peroxide (from hydrogen), **epichlorohydrin (ECH)** made from bio-based glycerine + chlorine (India's first and only ECH plant), **CPVC resin** (ethylene-based PVC further chlorinated) and a new **chlorotoluene (CT) value chain** (toluene + chlorine → ring/photo-chlorinated agro and pharma intermediates). [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]
- **Capacity (TPA):** caustic soda 4,00,000; caustic potash 21,000; chloromethanes 50,000; H2O2 60,000; ECH 50,000 (→1,00,000); CPVC resin 75,000 (→1,50,000); CPVC compound 35,000; CT value chain 15,000; captive power 132 MW; wind-solar 18.34 MW (+19.8 MW). [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]
- **Market position:** 4th largest caustic soda, 3rd caustic potash, 6th chloromethanes, 5th H2O2 producer in India; largest Indian CPVC resin maker; first and largest ECH maker. [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Jun-26]
- **End markets:** caustic → alumina, textiles, chemicals; CPVC → hot/cold water pipes and fittings (Astral, Ashirvad-type pipe makers); ECH → epoxy resins (wind blades, coatings, electronics), water-treatment chemicals; CT → agrochem and pharma intermediates; CMS → pharma solvent, refrigerant, PTFE. Serves 16+ industries, 400+ customers. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- **Mix:** Derivatives & Specialty 45% of revenue FY24 → 54% FY25 → 52% FY26; target >70% by FY28. Chlor-alkali (caustic soda, potash) is the rest. Only one reportable segment in the accounts. [AR FY26 p.~8; AR FY26 note 39]
- **Geography / exports:** FY26 revenue within India ₹2,418 cr, outside India ₹77 cr (3%) vs ₹169 cr (7%) in FY25 — exports halved. Management: "we hardly have around 4% to 5% of exports", mostly ECH to Europe, with leased tank storage there. [AR FY26 note 39; AR FY26 BRSR; Q4 call]
- **Customer concentration:** no customer is disclosed as above 10%; the IP says revenue is "evened out" across the customer base. Group company Meghmani Organics bought ₹121.6 cr of goods in FY26 (≈5% of revenue); total sales to Meghmani group entities ≈ ₹185 cr (my sum of note 38 lines). [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 38]
- **Utilisation Q1 FY27:** caustic ~75%, ECH ~70–75%, CPVC ~50–55%, chloromethanes ~100%, H2O2 ~85–90%. Q4 FY26 overall plant utilisation above 80%; FY26 overall 78% (FY25 81%). Chlorine captive use 72% FY25 → 75% FY26 → target 90–95% in FY28. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]

Caustic prices rose 30–35% after the West Asia war began; power cost +~15%. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (May 2026):** FY27 volume growth 10–12% "barring the war"; EBITDA margin to stay "in that range" already achieved (22–25%); no value guidance; ECH and CPVC doubling to commission in **Q2 FY27**, ramping from 15–20% to optimum (CPVC ~75%, ECH ~80%) in FY28; CT plant ~40% utilisation in FY27, 70–75% in FY28; greenfield project to be announced "this year only". [Q4 call]
- **Q1 delivery:** volume +5% YoY (below the 10–12% annual pace; Q1 is seasonally weak for CPVC); margin 25% (met); new projects announced in July (met); interest cost normalised to ₹7 cr (met the implied fix). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Q1 call (Jul 2026):** 3–5 year CAGR 15–20% in both revenue and profit; ~₹5,000 cr revenue once all plants reach optimum; ROCE ~20% on new projects; capex ~₹400 cr in FY27 and ~₹400 cr in FY28. Volume guidance neither repeated nor withdrawn. CT + MPP derivatives ~₹500 cr revenue "FY29 or FY30"; CT + MPP combined ₹700–800 cr at 22–23% margin after 1.5–2 years ramp. [Q1 call]
- **Slippage signal:** on 27 Sep 2026 the CMD said the ECH/CPVC expansions will commission "within a couple of months" — i.e., Q3 FY27, not Q2 as promised — and set the ₹5,000 cr target for **FY31**; FY27 revenue expected ~₹2,900 cr (~15% growth). CRISIL also models 13–15% FY27 revenue growth. [News: Daily Excelsior/PTI, 27 Sep 2026; CRISIL Jun-26]

## Capex and capacity
- **ECH +50,000 TPA and CPVC +75,000 TPA** (plus 19.8 MW wind-solar): spend ₹195 cr FY25, ₹338 cr FY26, ₹62 cr Q1 FY27; the CMD puts this phase at ~₹650 cr, internally funded. After it, CPVC resin 1,50,000 TPA ("world's largest") and ECH 1,00,000 TPA. [IP Q1FY27; News: Daily Excelsior, 27 Sep 2026]
- **New ₹600 cr projects (approved Jul 2026):** epoxy resin & formulations 1,25,000 TPA plus a multipurpose plant (MPP) for ECH and CT derivatives, both at Dahej, commissioning **H2 FY28**; funding ~60% debt / 40% internal accruals; pilot plant for customer approvals by Sep 2026. Peak revenue ₹1,300–1,500 cr from ₹600 cr, i.e., asset turn ~2.2–2.5x vs ~1.2x for the existing portfolio (FY26 sales ₹2,527 cr on net fixed assets ₹2,117 cr). Management: epoxy's turnover-to-investment is "almost 3x higher" than the current basket, so margin % falls but ROCE stays ~20%. Half of 1 lakh t ECH will feed epoxy; >50% of epoxy raw-material value made in-house; bisphenol-A must be bought. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- **CT platform:** ~₹250 cr spent (commissioned Mar 2025) for ₹300–350 cr peak revenue (asset turn ~1.3x) — approvals took 7–8 months and it was "minuscule" in FY26. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26]
- Management also mentions a further "new chemistry" line of >₹1,000 cr with ~90% of Indian demand imported (unnamed). [News: Daily Excelsior, 27 Sep 2026]
- Capital commitments at Mar-26 ₹54.6 cr; EPCG export obligation ₹176 cr to be met by Feb 2032 (up from ₹25 cr). [AR FY26 note 40]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹489 cr (Mar-25) → ₹508 cr (Mar-26) → ₹474 cr (Jun-26); net debt/EBITDA 0.9x → 0.8x. Screener gross borrowings ₹572 cr at Mar-26 (₹964 cr Mar-24). CRISIL: gearing 0.26x, interest cover 8.1x (13.7x FY25), working-capital lines only ~9% used of ₹400 cr; debt repayments ₹115–120 cr over two years vs accruals ₹450–550 cr. [Q1 call; Q4 call; Screener; CRISIL Jun-26]
- Cash flow (Screener): CFO ₹441 cr FY25, ₹436 cr FY26 (90% of operating profit); free cash flow ₹254 cr FY25, ₹50 cr FY26 after capex. [Screener]
- Working capital worsened: debtor days 33 → 60 (FY25→FY26), inventory days 105 → 89, payable days 50 → 66; cash conversion cycle 88 → 83 days. The auditor's ratio note says receivables rose. [Screener; AR FY26 ratio note]
- Dividend ₹5/share (₹21.6 cr, 6.5% payout). No buyback. [AR FY26 Directors' report]
- FY26 interest cost ₹72 cr vs guided ₹45–50 cr: part was a mark-to-market loss on a foreign-currency loan with a derivative swap as the rupee fell ~10% in FY26; the MTM amount was not disclosed. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- With ~₹800 cr capex over FY27–28 and ₹600 cr of it 60% debt-funded, net debt should rise to roughly ₹700–900 cr (my estimate), still ~1.2–1.5x EBITDA — well below CRISIL's 2.0x downgrade trigger. [derived; CRISIL Jun-26]

## Moat evidence
- **Integration:** 75% of chlorine used in-house; chlorine sells at about −₹4,000/t, so every tonne converted into ECH/CPVC/CMS captures value a stand-alone caustic producer cannot. Captive 132 MW power cuts the largest cash cost (power is 50–55% of caustic cost per an analyst on the call). [AR FY26; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Sole domestic ECH producer**, protected by anti-dumping duty of up to US$557/t on ECH from China, Korea and Thailand (imposed Nov 2024, five years). Glycerine route is "greener" and ties cost to palm-oil/biodiesel rather than propylene. [News: ChemAnalyst, 15 Nov 2024; AR FY26]
- **CPVC:** largest Indian resin maker; one of three domestic makers (with DCW and Lubrizol) that filed an anti-circumvention complaint in Sep 2026 alleging Chinese/Korean resin is routed via Malaysia, Japan and Thailand to dodge existing CPVC anti-dumping duty. Epigral's special-grade PVC input was excluded from the PVC anti-dumping case. [News: Whalesbook, 23 Sep 2026; News: Polymerupdate]
- **Pricing power is partial:** ECU and ECH track global prices; CPVC price follows PVC with a lag ("not immediately, but gradually"). Q3 FY26 margin fell to 17% on an inventory loss in a falling PVC market. [Q1 call; AR FY26 Q&A]
- **Weakness:** CRISIL flags "high albeit reducing dependence on the intensely competitive chlor-alkali industry"; top 7 players hold only 40–50% of chlor-alkali. Grasim and others are adding CPVC; DCM Shriram and Grasim have epoxy capacity. [CRISIL Jun-26; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Overcapacity in CPVC and caustic:** CPVC utilisation 50–55% in Q1 before the 75,000 t addition; Grasim's new CPVC plant is ramping; management concedes "for some point of time, there is overlapping and the overcapacity". Caustic utilisation fell to ~75% as new Indian capacity lands. [Q1 call]
- **Execution slip:** ECH/CPVC commissioning has moved from "Q2 FY27" to "within a couple of months" (Sep 2026). CT took two years to win approvals; FY26 CT contribution was negligible. [Q4 call; News: Daily Excelsior, 27 Sep 2026; AR FY26]
- **Glycerine/ECH price risk:** Indonesia's B50 biodiesel push adds glycerine supply that could lower global ECH prices; management says palm-oil economics limit this. [Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities ₹84.7 cr** (≈4% of net worth): income tax ₹16.6 cr (80-IA on captive power), customs ₹6.2 cr, service tax ₹0.25 cr, new GST demand ₹17.3 cr (reverse charge on ECB interest), and ₹44.2 cr of commercial claims for alleged non-fulfilment of contract obligations. [AR FY26 note 40]
- **Auditor:** SRBC & Co (EY network) — unqualified; one key audit matter (revenue recognition/cut-off); audit-trail (edit log) not enabled for privileged/admin changes (a common CARO remark). CARO: bank stock statements agree with books; no defaults. [AR FY26 auditor's report & CARO]
- **Related parties:** sales to Meghmani group companies (~₹185 cr FY26) and purchase of renewable power from an associate; stated as arm's length. Promoter group runs the listed Meghmani Organics. Promoter holding 68.83%; no pledge reported. [AR FY26 note 38; Screener]
- **Management change:** CFO Sanjay Jain left 31 Aug 2025; Rakesh Agrawal joined 10 Nov 2025. [AR FY26 note 38]
- **FX:** foreign-currency swap loan produced an undisclosed MTM hit in FY26. [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (FY26 decline, interest overshoot, delayed greenfield) to expansionary (₹600 cr epoxy/MPP, ₹5,000 cr ambition, 15–20% CAGR).
- New numbers: FY27 and FY28 capex ~₹400 cr each; 60/40 debt/equity for the new projects; ROCE ~20% target replaced margin guidance; epoxy will lower margin % by design.
- The explicit 10–12% FY27 volume guide was not repeated; Q1 volume grew only 5%.
- New risks raised by analysts: CPVC overcapacity (Grasim), glycerine glut, caustic imports; PVC Minimum Import Price judged neutral-to-positive for ethylene-based CPVC.

## My read
The business is past its FY26 trough (Q3 FY26 margin 17% → Q1 FY27 25%), but Q1 was helped by war-driven ECU and ECH prices that are already easing (ECU ₹35k → ₹31–32k). The real test is H2 FY27–FY28: two big additions (CPVC and ECH doubling) arrive into a CPVC market running at 50–55% with a new competitor, so utilisation and price will lag for a year; the epoxy/MPP plan adds scale but at lower margins and with fresh debt. At about 17x trailing earnings, the price needs only ~10–12% profit growth — achievable if the expansions ramp as CRISIL expects (13–15% FY27 revenue growth). What would change my view: evidence of CPVC price cuts to fill the new plant, a further commissioning slip, or ECH falling below ~₹170/kg.
