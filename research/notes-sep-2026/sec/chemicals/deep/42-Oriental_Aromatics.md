# Oriental Aromatics (OAL) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: both stored transcripts (Q1 FY27, 31 Jul 2026; Q4 FY26, 21 May 2026) were compared with the official company transcripts (BSE filing of 4 Aug 2026; company site TranscriptQ42526.pdf). Content matches in substance; small errors listed under **Stored-data errors** (e.g. the Mahad product is **Evermoss**, not "Evermoff"; the zero-tax entity is the Mahad subsidiary "OAS", not an "overseas subsidiary").

## Business and segments
- Three businesses, each roughly one-third of sales (company refuses a formal split): (1) **Camphor & terpene chemicals** at Bareilly (UP) — synthetic camphor made from alpha-pinene (from gum turpentine, a pine-resin distillate); formulated, powder and pharma-grade (WHO-GMP, USFDA) camphor; brands Saraswati, 3 Pine, Bhimseni; ~7,900 tpa pine-based products; (2) **Specialty aroma ingredients** at Vadodara (~6,200 tpa; 75% of output exported) — pinene derivatives, sandalwood and musk molecules (Astromusk, Cambrettolide), esters and nitriles sold to IFF, Givaudan, Symrise; (3) **Fragrances & flavours** compounding at Ambernath (~6,000 tpa, runs one shift). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Mahad** (Oriental Aromatics & Sons Ltd, 100% subsidiary): single-product 250 tpa plant for **Evermoss** (an oakmoss-type specialty ingredient), commercial production since Nov 2024; 50–60% utilised; Q1 FY27 revenue only ~₹3 cr. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; ICRA Feb-26]
- Utilisation: Bareilly/Vadodara plants 85–90% (except the hydrogenation unit); fragrance plant has "substantial" spare capacity; Mahad 50–60%. [Q1 call]
- Geography FY26: India ₹685 cr (+29%), outside India ₹341 cr (−14%) → exports fell from 43% to 33% of revenue; Q1 FY27 exports 35%. Export markets include US, Europe, Indonesia, Japan. [AR FY26 note 47; Q1 call]
- Customer concentration: no customer above 10% disclosed; global F&F houses and FMCG firms; ICRA notes some **fixed-price contracts** that delay pass-through. [AR FY26; ICRA Feb-26]
- Raw materials ≈ 60% of revenue: alpha-pinene/gum turpentine/CST (imported, dollar-priced) and petrochemical intermediates. [ICRA Feb-26]
- Single reportable segment in the accounts. [AR FY26 note 47]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 225.5 / EBITDA 18.1 (8.0%) / PAT 0.5; Sep-25 271.3 / 17.2 (6.4%) / 0.7; Dec-25 251.6 / 13.2 (5.3%) / −1.9; Mar-26 282.4 / 19.5 (6.9%) / 4.0; **Jun-26 259.8 / 19.8 (7.6%) / 2.5**. [Screener]
- Annual (Screener consolidated): sales FY22 ₹873 cr, FY23 ₹854 cr, FY24 ₹842 cr, FY25 ₹928 cr, FY26 ₹1,031 cr; OPM 11% → 6% → 6% → 10% → 7%; PAT ₹53 → 20 → 9 → 34 → **3 cr**. TTM PAT ₹5 cr; 5-yr profit CAGR −50%. [Screener]
- Standalone vs consolidated: FY26 standalone PAT ₹25.3 cr vs consolidated ₹3.3 cr → Mahad cost ~₹22 cr after tax; Q1 FY27 standalone PAT ₹8.2 cr (EBITDA 8.77%) vs consolidated ₹2.5 cr (7.62%) → Mahad cost ~₹5.7 cr in the quarter. Management puts the Mahad drag at 1–1.5 points of EBITDA margin. [AR FY26 Directors' report; IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Volumes: FY26 sales volume +9%, production +5%; Q1 FY27 sales volume **+22%**, production +18%, revenue only +15% → realisation per unit down ~6% YoY. Some Q1 volume was customers pulling forward allocations because of the war. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Prices: alpha-pinene up **70–80%** in five months to July and "still firm"; gum turpentine/CST at all-time highs; OAL raised prices 25–27% vs a quarter earlier (May), in three-month steps for contracts and monthly price lists for spot; Indian camphor powder prices up "double-digit". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Returns: ROCE 4.85% (FY25 9.32%), ROE 0.48%; Screener ROCE 4.5%. Interest cover 2.65x (4.98x). [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A; Screener]
- R&D spend cut to 2–2.5% of sales from 3–4%. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (May 2026): no revenue guidance ("crystal ball"); "endeavour" to return to ~10% EBITDA margin; Mahad to reach **75–80% utilisation within one year** and turn EBITDA-neutral; Mahad FY27 revenue ~₹50 cr ("conservative"), ₹60–65 cr at full use; peak group revenue at current prices ₹1,200–1,250 cr; capex pause. [Q4 call official]
- Q1 delivery: revenue +15% YoY, EBITDA margin 7.62% (up from 6.89% QoQ but below 8.01% a year ago and far from 10%); Mahad sold only ~₹3 cr in the quarter (vs ~₹12.5 cr/quarter needed for ₹50 cr) because output was stockpiled after a petro raw-material shortage. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Q1 call (Jul 2026): **new** near-term goal of **10–15% sales growth over the next year**; kept the 75–80% Mahad target but gave no date; margin outlook "very fluid". The ₹50 cr Mahad FY27 figure was not repeated. [Q1 call]
- Verdict: revenue guidance introduced (positive); margin and Mahad targets are slipping.

## Capex and capacity
- The 5-year capex cycle (Vadodara multipurpose plant, Bareilly terpene expansion, hydrogenation, Mahad) is complete; fixed assets rose from ₹270 cr (Mar-24, with ₹173 cr CWIP) to ₹460 cr (Mar-26). FY26 capex only ₹11.8 cr vs ₹87 cr in FY25. [Screener; AR FY26 cash flow]
- Mahad: ~₹90 cr total, of which ₹60–65 cr for the plant and ₹25–27 cr for common utilities/land usable for phase 2; can be doubled for ₹12–15 cr. At ₹60–65 cr peak revenue, **asset turn ~0.7x** — poor for a specialty plant unless phase 2 is added. [Q4 call official]
- Parent has invested ₹130 cr equity in OASL, lent ₹3.9 cr and guaranteed ₹80 cr of its bank debt (₹78.6 cr drawn). [AR FY26 CARO & note 36]
- Management says no major new capex until utilisation and profits improve. [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹206 cr (Mar-24) → ₹353 cr (Mar-25) → **₹403 cr** (Mar-26); net debt/equity 0.58x (Mar-26), 0.56x (Jun-26); equity ~₹665 cr. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Operating cash flow (consolidated): FY25 −₹34.5 cr, FY26 **+₹2.4 cr** — FY26 receivables rose ₹55.8 cr while inventory fell ₹15.6 cr; the gap was funded by ₹65 cr more short-term debt. Interest paid ₹34.8 cr. [AR FY26 consolidated cash flow]
- Working capital (Screener): debtor days 78 → 74 → 89; inventory days 177 → 238 → 203; payable days 52 → 50 → 44; cash cycle 203 → 262 → **247 days**. ICRA: NWC ≈ 60% of sales, inventory 5–6 months, export credit 120–150 days, some fragrance/specialty customers 180 days; fund-based limits 75–80% used; cash ₹2.1 cr (Sep-25). Management said big customers asked for longer credit and it was "priced in"; it also deliberately overstocked petro inputs. [Screener; ICRA Feb-26; Q4 call]
- Dividend ₹0.50/share (≈₹1.7 cr). [Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- One of few globally integrated players from terpene chemistry to finished fragrance; captive ingredients give the fragrance division a cost and supply edge. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Long qualification: 500–1,000 days from sample to commercial allocation; Mahad has approvals from "a wide range of global customers" and is bidding in global RFQs for H2 2026. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- First synthetic camphor plant in India (DuPont technology, 1964); pharma-grade certifications; P&G supplier award (2023). [IP Q1FY27]
- Weak pricing power: aroma ingredients are a global buyer's market flooded by Chinese capacity; Indian camphor capacity far exceeds demand; management calls industry discipline "wishful thinking". Volume +22% with revenue +15% shows price is being given up for share. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- No anti-dumping protection on camphor; OAL has only "met government representatives" — no progress this quarter. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Rating pressure**: ICRA revised outlook to **Negative** (A-/A2+, ₹393 cr limits, 23 Feb 2026) citing 9M FY26 margin of ~6.5%, Mahad losses, inventory build-up (~400 t of camphor powder held back) and weak cash flow; debt/EBITDA 3.8x in FY25 and worse since. [ICRA Feb-26]
- **Promoter pay vs profit**: FY26 remuneration of Dharmil Bodani ₹4.74 cr and Shyamal Bodani ₹3.79 cr (₹8.5 cr combined) exceeds consolidated PAT of ₹3.3 cr; CEO Parag Satoskar and his relative Anita Satoskar (Chief R&D Officer) ₹1.93 cr each. [AR FY26 note 41]
- **Goodwill** ₹44.98 cr from the 2017 amalgamation is a key audit matter (no impairment booked). Audit trail not enabled at the SAP HANA database level (auditor remark). Unmodified opinion. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Contingent liabilities ₹87.9 cr, of which ₹78.6 cr is the guarantee for Mahad's debt; disputed tax items ~₹9 cr. Indonesian subsidiary PT Oriental Aromatics (being liquidated) was written off earlier (₹9.34 cr investment, ₹0.69 cr receivable). Rent paid to promoter entity Vaishnavi Chemicals ₹0.23 cr. [AR FY26 notes 36, 41]
- Raw-material shock: pinene +70–80%, petro-streams scarce; fixed-price contracts delay pass-through; rupee weakness. [Q1 call; ICRA Feb-26]
- Export slide: exports down 14% in FY26; US tariffs briefly at 50% in FY26. [AR FY26 note 47 & MD&A]
- Valuation: stock rose from ₹309 (30 Jun) to ₹534, P/E ~338x trailing on Screener — price is far ahead of reported earnings. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Promoter holding 74.17%, stable; no pledge shown. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: from a "triple shock" (pinene/CST, crude, rupee) and "the worst is not over" (May) to "positive start… direction encouraging" (July).
- Guidance: from no revenue number to 10–15% growth; the 10% margin "endeavour" was not repeated.
- Mahad: from "75–80% in the next one year, ₹50 cr FY27" to "move towards 75–80%", with output stockpiled after a raw-material disruption and Q1 revenue ~₹3 cr.
- New data: pinene +70–80%; Q1 volume +22% partly pull-forward; R&D cut to 2–2.5% of sales; net debt/equity 0.56x.
- Dropped: currency as a headline problem; camphor import-restriction talks (no progress).

## My read
The business is growing volume but not earning money: consolidated profit has fallen from ₹53 cr (FY22) to ₹3 cr (FY26) while debt nearly doubled and the cash cycle sits near 250 days. Standalone OAL earns a thin 7.5–9% EBITDA margin; the Mahad subsidiary is losing ~₹20 cr a year with poor asset turns, and ICRA has the rating on Negative outlook. The share price (up ~70% since June) needs margins back near 10% and Mahad at break-even within FY27 — neither is yet in sight given 70–80% pinene inflation and oversupplied camphor. Evidence that would change the view: two quarters of Mahad revenue above ₹12 cr, EBITDA margin above 9%, and a fall in inventory days/borrowings.
