# Gujarat Narmada Valley Fertilizers & Chemicals (GNFC) — deep research, Q1 FY27

Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged. Price ₹630 (near 52-week high ₹636), market cap ₹9,262 cr, P/E 8.9, P/B ~1.0, dividend yield 3.3% (25 Sep 2026) [Screener]. Gujarat government company; promoters (Gujarat State Investments and GSFC) hold 41.3% [Screener; AR FY26 note 37].

## Business and segments
- Two big complexes: Bharuch (ammonia, urea, nitric acids, ammonium nitrate, acetic acid, ethyl acetate, formic acid, aniline, TDI-I) and Dahej (TDI-II). TDI = toluene di-isocyanate, used to make flexible polyurethane foam for mattresses, furniture and car seats; GNFC is India's only maker [AR FY26; Acuité Aug-26].
- Chemicals ("industrial products") are the profit engine: Q1 FY27 chemicals revenue ~₹1,569 cr and segment result ~₹463 cr (my reading of the slide), while fertilisers (neem urea, ANP 20-20-0) lost ₹85 cr vs ₹24 cr in Q4 — of the ~₹60 cr deterioration, ~₹48 cr came from urea and ~₹12 cr from ANP [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- FY26 industrial turnover mix: technical-grade urea (TGU, used for diesel exhaust fluid) 22%, TDI 22%, ammonium nitrate melt 17% [AR FY26 Board's report].
- Pricing: chemicals sold on variable-price contracts tied to international prices (1–3 month contracts at most); urea is price-controlled with a government subsidy (FY26 urea subsidy income ₹1,690 cr) [Q4 call; AR FY26 KAM].
- Capacity use FY26: most Bharuch plants above rated capacity (ammonia 659,169 t = 148%, urea 833,679 t = 131%, acetic acid 154,840 t = 155%, TDI-I 18,167 t = 130%); TDI-II Dahej ran 276 days at 77% due to equipment, maintenance and gas constraints [AR FY26].
- FY26 production: ammonia ~3 lakh t (oil) + 3.6 lakh t (gas), WNA ~4.3 lakh t, CNA 1.47 lakh t (~45% sold), TDI ~57,000 t, AN melt ~1.7 lakh t, formic acid ~34,000 t, TGU >2.1 lakh t [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 production: ammonia ~173,000 t, TDI 12,800 t (66% Dahej), WNA 113,000 t, CNA 37,500 t, AN melt 55,600 t, TGU 74,800 t, formic acid 8,200 t [Q1 call].
- Largely domestic sales; small export incentives (₹12 cr in FY26) [AR FY26 notes].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr) / OPM / net profit: Jun-25 1,601 / 1.9% / 83 (Bharuch turnaround); Sep-25 1,968 / 9% / 179; Dec-25 1,996 / 9% / 150; Mar-26 2,208 / 22% / 396; Jun-26 2,238 / 18% / 312 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 was the second-highest quarterly profit in GNFC's history (after Q1 FY22), driven by war-led chemical prices, not volume — sales volumes fell well below production and inventory built up [Q1 call].
- Annual: FY22 ₹8,642 cr (OPM 28%, PAT ₹1,710 cr) peak; FY24 ₹7,930 cr (6%, ₹497 cr); FY25 ₹7,892 cr (8%, ₹598 cr); FY26 ₹7,773 cr (11%, ₹809 cr) [Screener]. FY26 included ~₹80 cr of one-offs (₹28 cr insurance, ₹15 cr urea-freight arrears, catalyst-dust sale, CCPP delay penalty) [Q4 call].
- Other income is large: ~₹500 cr a year (₹499 cr in FY26 = 47% of PBT of ₹1,065 cr), mostly treasury income [Screener].
- Spreads (company slides): Q1 FY27 fuel oil and imported coal costs up ~64–76% q/q, gas ~6%; TDI, acetic acid and aniline prices at the "highest" of the last five quarters [IP Q1FY27].
- ROCE 33% (FY22) → 8% (FY24) → 12% (FY26) [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Company policy: "we do not provide any forecast" [IP Q1FY27]. Management still gave project and cost markers.
- Q4 FY26 call (19 May 2026): FY27 capex "around ₹2,800 cr"; coal-based steam & power plant (CCPP, ₹613 cr) at Dahej to synchronise June, full operation by third week of August, saving ₹10–12 cr a month from H2 FY27; ammonia expansion, weak nitric acid-III and AN-II "next year"; WNA delayed ~2.5 months; urea fixed-cost/energy norms revision "overdue"; A.T. Kearney cost programme savings to "seep into" FY27 [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (6 Aug 2026): FY27 capex cut to ₹1,500–1,800 cr (₹300 cr in Q1); ₹2,800 cr is now described as the total of projects on hand, with ~₹1,500 cr more over two years; CCPP steam online, power in ~45 days (slipped again); saving now put at ₹30,000–40,000 per tonne of TDI; WNA delay ~3 months; Kearney savings (earlier estimated ₹250–300 cr) still unquantified, maybe visible "by the end of next quarter"; projects under execution to add ₹1,200–1,500 cr revenue and ₹500–600 cr contribution [Q1 call].
- Delivered: urea energy norm raised to 6.37 Gcal/t from 6.20 (1 Apr 2025–31 Mar 2028), worth ~₹61 cr arrears for Apr-25 to Jun-26, to be booked in Q2 FY27 [IP Q1FY27].
- Missed/slipped: CCPP (April → June → Aug → Sep), WNA, Kearney savings; Q1 volumes (acetic acid shut 6 May–1 Aug, ethyl acetate and TDI Bharuch down in July) [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Under execution: CCPP Dahej (150 t/hr steam + 18 MW), ammonia expansion Bharuch (50 KTPA), WNA-III (200 KTPA), AN-II (163 KTPA), new CFBC steam boiler (180–200 t/hr). Under consideration: bisphenol-A 150 KTPA and polyols 100 KTPA at Dahej, acetic acid 350 KTPA at Bharuch; an earlier INEOS JV for BPA is now a licensing discussion [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- CWIP rose from ₹382 cr to ₹900 cr in FY26; capital commitments ₹1,508 cr [Screener; AR FY26 note 36].
- Payback: ₹2,800 cr for ₹500–600 cr contribution implies roughly 5–6 years before overheads; revenue/capex ~0.5x — a low asset turn [Q1 call].
- GMDC MoU for underground coal gasification to secure cheaper feedstock [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free (borrowings ₹5 cr); cash and liquid assets ~₹4,000 cr "across G-Sec, GSFS and banks" [Screener; Q1 call].
- **₹2,431 cr parked as an unsecured deposit with a body corporate** (up from ₹565 cr) — management said it is Gujarat State Financial Services, a state-owned arm, at ~7.25%. An analyst on the call assumed it was secured; the annual report classifies it as unsecured [AR FY26 note on loans; Q4 call].
- Operating cash flow ₹31 cr (FY24), ₹605 cr (FY25), ₹654 cr (FY26); working-capital days rose to 157 in FY26 (from 61) because of the ICD reclassification into current assets; Acuité: gross current assets 245 days [Screener; Acuité Aug-26].
- Urea subsidy receivable ₹438 cr [AR FY26 KAM].
- Dividend ₹21/share (210%) for FY26, payout 38%; Acuité AA+/Stable, net cash accrual ₹844 cr [Q4 call; Screener; Acuité Aug-26].

## Moat evidence
- Only Indian TDI producer; runs close to the ~80% global operating factor; domestic market short of TDI [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Scale in ammonia and single-stream urea; captive ammonia feeds nitric acid, AN and TGU; GNFC and GSFC are the only TGU makers, and GNFC doubled TGU in March 2026 to keep diesel-exhaust-fluid supply going [Q4 call; Acuité Aug-26].
- Weakness in aniline: Chinese MDI makers dump aniline; India's anti-dumping duty is small ($37/t for Wanhua, $121/t others), so GNFC does aniline job work on a fixed margin instead of expanding [Q4 call].
- Stored claims of "~60% TDI market share" and TDI anti-dumping duty "through 2030" were not found in the documents read — unverified.

## Risks and red flags
- **DoT licence-fee demand of ₹21,370 cr** (after the Supreme Court AGR ruling, covering FY2005-06 to FY2019-20, over old VSAT/ISP licences of its IT arm) — more than twice the market cap. The auditor flags it as an emphasis of matter; the company has **neither provided for it nor shown it as a contingent liability**, relying on legal advice and later exemptions for non-telecom PSUs [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 43(B)].
- Related: the 2012 demerger of the VSAT/ISP business to ING Satcom (₹6 cr) is still unsettled because DoT never approved the licence transfer [AR FY26 note 43(A)].
- Treasury: ₹2,431 cr unsecured deposit with a state entity — capital allocation dictated by the state, low yield [AR FY26; Q4 call].
- Chemical prices are war-inflated; inventory built in Q1 was only ~15% liquidated by end-July at "mixed" realisations [Q1 call].
- Fertiliser losses widen until fixed-cost norms are revised; subsidy delays [Q1 call; Acuité Aug-26].
- Execution slippages at Dahej; TDI-II reliability (77% utilisation) [AR FY26].
- Contingent liabilities excluding DoT: ₹359 cr (contract claims ₹230 cr, excise/GST ₹105 cr, income tax ₹24 cr) [AR FY26 note 36].
- Auditor KAMs: urea subsidy recognition, inventory (stores & spares ₹527 cr), tax/legal litigation [AR FY26].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: pride in national service (TGU for DEF), "peak and optimised" production, ₹2,800 cr FY27 capex.
- Q1: plant shutdowns for viability (acetic acid, ethyl acetate, TDI-II partly), inventory build, capex for FY27 reset to ₹1,500–1,800 cr, energy-norm win, GMDC coal-gasification MoU; the company secretary shut down near-term realisation questions.

## My read
GNFC is a cyclical, debt-free chemical-plus-fertiliser company earning a war windfall: trailing profit of ₹1,037 cr is near peak-cycle, and about half of normal-year profit comes from treasury income. At under 9 times trailing earnings the price needs almost no growth — the real question is whether earnings fall back toward the FY24–FY25 ₹500–600 cr level once chemical prices normalise, which would roughly double the P/E. The ₹21,370 cr DoT demand, undisclosed as a contingent liability, is a tail risk that can't be sized but must be tracked. I would turn more positive if the CCPP and AN/WNA projects deliver the ₹500–600 cr contribution on time and a fixed-cost revision ends urea losses; negative on any adverse DoT/TDSAT ruling or TDI prices dropping back to FY24 levels.
