## Business and segments
- **Chemistry and products:** this is an energetic-materials business. It makes composite solid propellants (ammonium perchlorate oxidiser, which it produces itself at Peddakandukur, bound in a rubbery binder with aluminium fuel) for missile rocket motors, and was India's first private maker of solid propellant. Other products:
  - high explosives: HMX/RDX, PETN and CL-20;
  - pyrotechnics: igniters, cartridges and squibs;
  - countermeasures (**chaff and flares**), where it is the only domestic supplier to the IAF;
  - warheads, mines and ammunition;
  - commercial explosives: bulk ammonium-nitrate explosives, detonators and detonating fuse for coal mines.
  [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- **Programmes:** 100% of solid propellant for MRSAM, LRSAM and Astra; 2,500+ booster grains for Akash; pyrogen igniters for all Agni stages; rocket motors for BrahMos; fully assembled rocket motors for export. Domestic output is ~40–50 motors a month. Much domestic work depends on free-issue material from BDL and DRDO. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- **Services:** operation and maintenance of ISRO's solid-propellant plant at Sriharikota (~₹18 cr a year, through mid-FY29). The Jagdalpur (DRDO, Agni propellant) O&M contract was lost to a lower bidder after 17 years. [Q4 call]
- **Plants:** 6 sites in Telangana, MP and Maharashtra. Katepally makes propellants, HMX/RDX and rocket motors; Peddakandukur makes accessories, AP and flares; four bulk-explosive sites sit near coal mines. 850+ staff. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Revenue mix:** FY26 Defence & Space ₹315.4 cr (81%), Commercial explosives ₹72.9 cr (19%); Q1 FY27 Defence ₹79.3 cr (77%), Commercial ₹23.3 cr. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Exports:** FY26 revenue from other countries ₹51.7 cr (13%), down from ₹78.5 cr (19%). The **order book is ~54% export** (Israel, Greece, Turkey and other destinations; defence exports need Arms Act licences). [AR FY26 note; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Customer concentration:** one customer was above 10% of revenue in both FY26 and FY25 (not named). [AR FY26 note]
- **Order book:** ₹1,569 cr at Q4 (4.0x FY26 revenue; defence ₹1,491 cr) → **₹1,393 cr** at Aug 2026 (defence ₹1,309 cr, explosives ₹42 cr, services ₹42 cr). The book includes a ₹430 cr Oct 2025 chaff/flare order and a ₹350.2 cr Apr 2026 export order that still awaits its licence. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Kattupalli: RDX/HMX plant (water trials Sep 2026, then one month to production) and a 2.5 t planetary mixer for large motors, delayed by imported parts that are now being air-freighted. The rocket-motor facility rebuilt after the accident was due to be commissioned in Sep and start production in Nov 2026. Total FY26–27 capex ~₹60 cr, of which ₹28 cr was spent in FY26. CWIP was ₹26 cr at Mar-26. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- Flares plant at Peddakandukur (accident ~1.5 years earlier) has been rebuilt and is producing, which cuts import dependence. [Q4 call]
- New sites: ~400 acres in Andhra Pradesh are stuck on land price, and Odisha land was rejected. Management says these would take "1–1.5 years after acquisition", and 4–5 years for the Odisha phases. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Asset turns: FY26 sales ₹388 cr on ₹187 cr net fixed assets ≈ 2.1x. Capex is small relative to the ₹1,393 cr order book, so the bottlenecks are licences, imported inputs, free-issue material and plant qualification, not money. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- **Sole domestic source** of chaff and flares for the IAF, and single source to DRDO for pyrogen igniters. Supplies 100% of solid propellant for MRSAM, LRSAM and Astra. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Entry barriers: explosive licences (PESO/DPIIT), about five years to set up a plant and magazines, and DRDO/ARDE qualification. [Q4 call]
- **But pricing power is weak:** customers are MoD, PSU and DRDO buyers who impose LDs; domestic propellant work depends on the customer's free-issue materials. It lost the Jagdalpur O&M contract on price. Export licences can be refused: an ₹18.9 cr order was cancelled in Apr 2026 when the licence was denied. [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- Raw-material dependence: chaff payloads are imported, and the high explosive for mines came from Munitions India Ltd, which could not supply. That is why land mines and loitering-munition payloads await DRDO approval of an in-house substitute. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- No China competition or anti-dumping issue applies. Competition is domestic private entrants (Solar Industries, and others after defence liberalisation) plus PSUs. The MD says "more than 40 players" in bulk explosives. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor's qualified opinion on internal financial controls (FY26):** Majeti & Co. found a *material weakness*: "Significant procurement decisions and approvals were concentrated with a single authorised individual, resulting in inadequate segregation of duties… risk of… management override". In the same year, other income included a ₹34.2 cr purchase-discount settlement with a supplier covering the prior year's purchases. [AR FY26 auditor's report & note 22]
- **Safety:** a fire and explosion at Katepally on 29 Apr 2025 **killed four people**. The Telangana PCB issued a closure order on 7 May 2025 (revoked after environmental compensation was paid). Ex-gratia of ₹5.2 cr was booked as exceptional. An earlier flares-plant accident happened ~1.5 years before May 2026. [AR FY26 Directors' report, BRSR, note 29(d)]
- **Change of control:** under a 9 Jul 2026 SPA, Apollo Micro Systems is buying the promoters' (AKS Family Trust) **41.33% for ~₹1,550 cr (~₹698/share)**, with an open offer for 26% at ₹698 (₹976 cr). Closing is expected in Q3 FY27, subject to approvals. The stock (~₹680) trades just below the offer price. An independent director resigned on 31 Jul 2026. Strategy (AP land, product focus) is on hold "until they come into the picture". [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26; Q1 call]
- **Execution and guidance credibility:** FY26 missed its ₹500 cr target by 22%; Q1 FY27 missed badly; almost every milestone slipped between the two calls.
- **Earnings quality:** FY26 profit growth came from one-off other income; core EBITDA fell 33%. TTM operating profit is only ₹24 cr against a market cap of ~₹3,650 cr.
- Contingent liabilities are small: sales tax ₹5.8 cr, GST ₹1.0 cr, income tax ₹0.6 cr. [AR FY26 note 34]

## What changed between the two calls
- The Q4 call pitched an execution recovery (flares plant back, alternate raw material in "a month or two", ₹600–700 cr). The Q1 call was dominated by the **Apollo takeover** and delays.
- The order book fell from ₹1,569 cr to ₹1,393 cr as low inflows lagged execution.
- The DRDO approval moved out six months; Kattupalli slipped to Sep–Nov; the flares backlog moved out to about Dec 2026.
- The FY27 guide narrowed from ₹600–700 cr to "around ₹600 cr" with 15–20% EBITDA; the whole ₹430 cr order is now promised within FY27.
- New: the IFC qualification and the supplier-discount income appear only in the annual report, not on the calls.

## My read
The strategic assets are real: sole-source countermeasures, missile propellants and export rocket motors, backed by a ₹1,393 cr order book. But the reported numbers are weak and noisy. Core EBITDA fell to 10% in FY26 and 5.7% in Q1; last year's profit growth was one-off supplier discounts; there was a fatal accident; and the auditor qualified the internal controls over procurement. At ~106 times trailing earnings, the market price rests on the ₹698 Apollo offer and takeover optionality, not on fundamentals. Needed: 2–3 quarters of ₹150 cr+ revenue at 15%+ EBITDA, export licences converting, and a clean audit under Apollo. A delayed or failed takeover, or another guidance miss, would remove the price support.
