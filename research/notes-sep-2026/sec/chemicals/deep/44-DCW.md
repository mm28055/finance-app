# DCW Ltd (DCW) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: the stored **Q4 FY26** transcript is the company's official filed transcript (6 May 2026) — no issues. The stored **Q1 FY27** transcript (14 Aug 2026) matches the official BSE transcript (filed 20 Aug) in substance but misspells all three executives (official: **Saatvik Jain**, President; **Sudarshan Ganapathy**, CEO; **Pradipto Mukherjee**, CFO), drops the unit on PVC prices (official "$820 to $860" a tonne) and names the host "Aryan Capital" at the close (official: Arihant Capital). DCW reports **standalone only** — Screener's "consolidated" page is stale (data to 2015); all figures below are from Screener's standalone page.

## Business and segments
- Two sites: Sahupuram (Tamil Nadu, port-based, the larger integrated complex; ~2,900 acres of land in total, 58 MW captive power + 44.5 MW group-captive solar since Apr 2025) and Dhrangadhra (Gujarat, India's first soda-ash plant, 1939). [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]
- Chemistry: salt electrolysis → **caustic soda (90,000 tpa)** + chlorine + hydrogen; chlorine and HCl are used captively in **synthetic rutile (48–50,000 tpa;** ilmenite ore leached with HCl, 100% exported, mostly to Japan), trichloroethylene and **CPVC**. Imported **VCM** (vinyl chloride monomer, a crude/ethylene derivative, all imported) → **PVC (1 lakh tpa)**; own PVC is chlorinated into **CPVC (50,000 tpa**, Arkema licence; used in hot/cold-water pipes and sprinklers). Limestone + salt → **soda ash (1 lakh tpa)**. **Synthetic iron oxide pigments (SIOP, 28,000 tpa**, Rockwood/Italy technical tie-up; ~60% exported, mainly to the US) for construction and coatings. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Segments FY26: Basic Chemicals ₹1,538 cr revenue (71%), segment result ₹0.4 cr (FY25 −₹33 cr), EBITDA margin 2.4–3.5%; **Specialty Chemicals ₹592 cr (28%), result ₹131.8 cr, EBITDA margin 29.7–30% (FY25 35.3%), 80% of EBITDA**. [AR FY26 note 43, MD&A; Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 segments: Basic ₹360.9 cr, EBITDA margin **−5.2%** (Q1 FY26 +2.0%, Q4 +5.6%); Specialty ₹177.0 cr, margin 29.1% (33.6%, 22.9%). CPVC volume +59% YoY, SIOP +3%. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Utilisation FY26: CPVC 102% (FY25 106%, on a growing base), SIOP 83% (86%), caustic 90% (80%), PVC 100% (98%), soda ash 91% (86%) — essentially full; no volume growth left except CPVC. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- Exports **28%** of FY26 revenue; 100+ customers in 14+ countries; no customer concentration disclosed. [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener standalone, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 476 / EBITDA 54 (11%) / PAT 11; Sep-25 539 / 58 (11%) / 14; Dec-25 520 / 45 (9%) / 5; Mar-26 609 / 65 (11%) / 18; **Jun-26 542 / 36 (7%) / PBT 0 / PAT 35** (PAT is almost entirely a ₹34 cr deferred-tax write-back from moving to the 25.17% tax regime). [Screener; Q1 call]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY22 ₹2,455 cr, FY23 ₹2,634 cr, FY24 ₹1,872 cr, FY25 ₹2,000 cr, FY26 ₹2,144 cr; OPM 13% → 16% → 9% → 10% → 11%; PAT ₹108 → 192 → 16 → 30 → 48 cr. TTM sales ₹2,210 cr, PAT ₹71 cr (incl. tax credit). 3-yr sales CAGR −7%, 3-yr profit CAGR −32%. [Screener]
- Company FY26: EBITDA incl. other income ₹240 cr (FY25 ₹216 cr); PAT ₹48 cr (+60%); ROCE 8.56%; net debt/EBITDA 0.32x, net debt/equity 0.07. Q1 FY27 EBITDA incl. other income ₹41.4 cr (−28% YoY, −41% QoQ). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Unit economics/prices: CPVC net realisation fell >20% in FY26 (import pressure); PVC–CPVC spread "north of ₹25,000/t" (Aug); PVC ~$820–860/t with the new minimum import price (MIP) equal to ~₹80/kg; caustic soda ~$350/t (Q4) → >$400/t (Q1); soda ash import price ~$250/t; SIOP ~₹80,000/t at 35–36% margin. Q1 PVC contribution swung **₹50–55 cr** vs Q4 (VCM bought spot from China at high prices while import duty on PVC was suspended). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Solar saved ~₹23–24 cr of power cost in FY26 (vs ₹25–30 cr guided; ₹40 cr originally assessed); ~20% of Q1 power from solar. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- Earlier target (~2 years ago): FY27 revenue ~₹2,500 cr and **EBITDA ₹400 cr**. [Q4 call]
- Q4 call (May 2026): ₹400 cr "a bit derailed" by CPVC price erosion; declined to endorse ₹300 cr; net-debt-free by end-FY27 (₹130 cr scheduled repayment vs ₹71 cr net debt); finance cost to fall to ~₹50 cr; growth capex on hold until West Asia clears; operating profit expected to rise in FY27. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: EBITDA fell to ₹41 cr; basic chemicals lost money; finance cost ₹14.8 cr (run-rate ~₹59 cr, not ₹50 cr). **Missed.** [Q1 call; Screener]
- Q1 call (Aug 2026): ₹400 cr formally **dropped**; "assess us at ₹300 cr EBITDA steady state"; FY27 "better than FY26" (i.e. >₹240 cr) still claimed despite Q1; basic chemicals "more than break-even" in Q2; ₹250 cr capex **announced** (reversing May's pause); borrowing to exceed ₹135 cr repayments; open-market **buyback under consideration**. To beat FY26 the company needs ~₹200 cr EBITDA in the remaining nine months (~₹67 cr/quarter vs ₹41 cr in Q1). [Q1 call; derived]

## Capex and capacity
- Completed: CPVC 21,600 → 40,000 tpa (22 Jul 2025, ahead of schedule) → 50,000 tpa (end-Mar 2026); 44.5 MW group-captive solar (Apr 2025). Incremental CPVC payback ~2–2.5 years at current prices. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- New ₹250 cr programme over 2–3 years: **SIOP +15,000 tpa** (to ~45,000 tpa) — Phase I +7,000 t by **Q4 FY28**, Phase II +8,000 t after; value-added/micronised grades from FY28; plus power-plant efficiency capex at Sahupuram (Q4 FY28). Hurdle: **20% incremental ROCE**. At ~₹80,000/t and 35–36% margin, 15,000 t ≈ ₹120 cr revenue / ~₹42 cr EBITDA — the SIOP half of the spend would turn assets ~1x. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; derived]
- FY26 capex ₹93 cr; commitments ₹36.6 cr. Management rules out volume capex in basic chemicals (no VCM backward integration — capacity too small). [AR FY26 notes 35, 43; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Gross debt ₹276 cr at Mar-26 (company; Screener borrowings ₹290 cr) vs ₹426 cr a year earlier (₹150 cr repaid, no new term debt); cash + FDs ₹204 cr; net debt ₹71 cr. Company keeps cash of 5–10% of sales deliberately (negative carry). [Q4 call; Screener]
- Cash flow (Screener): CFO ₹252 cr (FY24), ₹191 cr (FY25), **₹278 cr (FY26)**; investing −₹99, −₹112, −₹63 cr; financing −₹221 cr in FY26 (debt repayment). [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 22 → 18 → 24; inventory days 139 → 143 → 96 (synthetic-rutile stock liquidated); payable days 125 → 129 → 122; cash cycle 37 → 32 → **−1 day**. [Screener]
- Dividend yield 0.62%; buyback being weighed. [Screener; Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- India's first CPVC maker and a sizeable SIOP producer in Asia; SIOP is the only product whose realisation did not fall in FY26; high entry capex for SIOP. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- Integration: chlorine-neutral since the CPVC expansion — chlorine that used to sell at a negative ₹7,000–8,000/t is now consumed in-house, lifting caustic ECU (electrochemical unit) realisation; captive salt, power and PVC for CPVC. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Scale disadvantage admitted: basic-chemical plants are in the smallest quintile; no VCM integration; CPVC faces new capacity from Epigral, Lubrizol–Grasim and Reliance (management: domestic output still below imports). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Trade protection is patchy: CPVC anti-dumping duty on China/Korea was imposed "a couple of years back"; DGTR found dumping on PVC and soda ash but duties were never imposed; PVC import duty was **suspended** during Q1 and then restored, with a new MIP; soda-ash ADD being pursued. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor's Emphasis of Matter** (FY26): (a) Tamil Nadu electricity tax on captive power ₹54.9 cr (2003–2020) — pending in the Supreme Court; (b) customs differential duty ₹12.4 cr plus interest, and ₹26 cr redemption fine/penalty on past coal imports; (c) after an **income-tax search in November 2023** at plants, offices and some directors' homes, orders for AY2015-16 to AY2024-25 cut available MAT credit by **₹28.9 cr** plus penalty notices; (d) State Government repossession order on **793 acres** at Sahupuram whose assignment deeds were never executed (High Court remanded; land price to be fixed). No provisions made. [AR FY26 auditor's report, notes 35, 40]
- Contingent liabilities **₹193.6 cr** (FY25 ₹208.5 cr) — ~13% of market cap — incl. ₹93.7 cr lease rent/cess/electricity/water (of which ₹31.8 cr is river-water charges — the withdrawal agreement expired in 1995), ₹40.3 cr customs, ₹30 cr income tax, ₹12.3 cr excise/GST, ₹10.8 cr sales tax. Auditor KAMs: provisions/contingent liabilities and deferred tax. [AR FY26 note 35, auditor's report]
- **Promoter-family pay**: FY26 remuneration to KMP and relatives ₹20.4 cr, plus ₹6.6 cr commission to the three MDs (Bakul, Vivek, Ashish Jain; nil in FY25) and a **₹10 cr performance bonus to President Saatvik Jain** (relative of an MD) — together up to ~₹37 cr against PAT of ₹48 cr. [AR FY26 note 38]
- Management change: CEO Amitabh Gupta (on the May call) replaced by COO Sudarshan Ganapathy by August; no reason given on the call. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Promoter holding-company merger (DTCPL, SBPL into DCW, effective 4 Feb 2026; share-neutral). Promoter holding 45.59%; FII holding fell from 11.35% to 6.11% in 18 months. An investor asked about links to the Times Group; management declined to answer on the call. [AR FY26 note 48; Screener; Q4 call]
- Feedstock: all VCM imported (earlier single source in Qatar, now a global distributor); VCM storage only 10–15 days. EOU export-obligation undertaking extended to 2030. [Q1 call; AR FY26 note 35]
- Credit rating IND A/Stable (India Ratings, reaffirmed in FY26); rationale page could not be opened. [AR FY26 Corporate Governance]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: May — "steady and satisfying" FY26, capex on hold, record volumes; August — "extremely difficult quarter", but growth capex launched and a buyback floated.
- Numbers: ₹400 cr EBITDA target formally replaced by ₹300 cr steady state; debt plan changed from net-cash to borrowing slightly more than repayments.
- New issues: VCM shortage and spot buying from China; PVC duty suspension then MIP; ₹34 cr deferred-tax credit; CEO change.
- Unchanged: CPVC ramp-up (volume +59%), SIOP margins 35%+, specialty-led strategy.

## My read
DCW has fixed its balance sheet (debt down from ₹605 cr in FY20 to ~₹290 cr, CFO ₹278 cr in FY26) and specialty chemicals now earn 80% of EBITDA, but the company is still 71% commodity by revenue, sub-scale, and its profit swings by ₹50 cr a quarter on VCM–PVC spreads and trade policy. Management has twice lowered its EBITDA ambition (₹400 cr → ₹300 cr) and Q1 annualises to under ₹170 cr, while the new SIOP capex only arrives in Q4 FY28. The price (market cap ₹1,438 cr) looks undemanding against ₹300 cr steady-state EBITDA, but that needs PVC spreads to normalise and CPVC realisations to hold against new Indian capacity; the ₹194 cr of contingent liabilities, the tax-search fallout and the size of promoter-family pay argue for a governance discount. Watch Q2 basic-chemicals EBITDA (promised positive), the buyback decision, and any movement in the electricity-tax and land cases.
