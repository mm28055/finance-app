# Deepak Nitrite (DNL) — deep research (A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore, consolidated, from Screener unless tagged otherwise.
Transcript check: the stored Q1 FY27 transcript (6 Aug 2026) is the official BSE filing. The stored Q4 FY26 transcript

## Business and segments
- **What it makes.** Two segments. **Phenolics** (Deepak Phenolics Ltd, Dahej): benzene + propylene → cumene → phenol and acetone, plus isopropyl alcohol (IPA), and from Q2 FY27 MIBK/MIBC and acetophenone. Phenol goes into laminates/plywood resins, pharma, agro and speciality chemicals; acetone into solvents, pharma, paints. **Advanced Intermediates (AI)** (Deepak Nitrite standalone + Deepak Chem Tech): nitration, reduction, diazotisation, oximation and fluorination chemistry — sodium nitrite/nitrate, nitrotoluidines, xylidines, cumidines, oximes, DASDA and optical brighteners, fuel additives, and new fluorinated products such as benzotrifluoride (BTF). [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- **FY26 segments:** Phenolics revenue ₹5,401 cr, EBIT ₹695 cr (13%); AI revenue ₹2,553 cr, EBIT ₹107 cr (4%), dragged by start-up losses at Deepak Chem Tech (DCTL). [AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Q1 FY27 segments:** Phenolics revenue ₹1,775 cr (+36% YoY, +24% QoQ), EBIT ₹418 cr (24%, a record); AI revenue ₹804 cr (+33%), EBIT ₹67 cr (8%). Q4 FY26: Phenolics ₹1,429 cr / EBIT ₹287 cr (20%); AI ₹708 cr / EBIT ₹34 cr. [Q1 call; Q4 call official]
- **Market share:** over 50% of Indian phenol and acetone; "the largest by a margin" optical-brightener producer outside China; a leading global producer of xylidines, cumidines, oximes. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- **Geography:** 85:15 domestic:export in Q1 FY27 (86:14 in Q4; FY26 exports ₹1,156 cr of ₹7,947 cr). Phenolics is ~97% domestic; AI is roughly 50:50. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26]
- **Capacity and utilisation:** phenol debottlenecked to "over 165%" of original nameplate, cumene 172%; run-rate near 1 lakh t a quarter in May–June; a further ~₹70 cr to reach ~4 lakh t/yr of phenol. Nitric acid plant ran at only ~45% in Q4 FY26 (technical problems) and was stabilised in Q1 FY27. [AR FY26; Q1 call; Q4 call official]
- **Raw materials:** benzene, propylene (bought; later from an own propane-dehydrogenation (PDH) unit), toluene, orthoxylene, ammonia, hydrogen, sulphur derivatives. Propylene was scarce in April 2026 (Hormuz disruption); the annual shutdown was brought forward. Management bought feedstock "at every dip" in Feb–Mar 2026 ahead of the Middle East war. [Q4 call official; Q1 call]
- **China and duties:** US removed its 45.16% anti-dumping duty on Deepak's sodium nitrite (Jan 2026); India removed phenol import duty for much of Q1 FY27 and has now reimposed it; China's new rules on hazardous nitration capacity are a tailwind. [AR FY26; Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (Screener):** Q4 FY25 revenue 2,180 / EBITDA 317 (15%) / PAT 202; Q1 FY26 1,890 / 190 (10%) / 112; Q2 FY26 1,902 / 204 (11%) / 119; Q3 FY26 1,975 / 211 (11%) / 100; Q4 FY26 2,120 / 376 (18%) / 220; Q1 FY27 2,578 / 540 (21%) / 345 (record). [Screener]
- **Annual:** revenue FY24 7,682 → FY25 8,282 → FY26 7,887; EBITDA 1,127 → 1,095 → 987; margin 15% → 13% → 13%; PAT 811 → 697 → 551. Peak was FY22 (EBITDA 1,607, 24% margin, PAT 1,067). TTM revenue 8,575, PAT 783. [Screener]
- **Plain words:** three years of falling profit as Chinese phenol capacity flooded Asia and AI chemistries turned into a "red ocean"; the turn came in Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 as the Middle East war tightened feedstock and imports, phenol duty returned, and the new nitric acid/nitration assets began to help. [Q1 call; Screener]
- **Returns:** ROCE fell from 44% (FY22) to 11% (FY26); ROE 9.8%. Capital employed ₹7,402 cr, of which ₹1,828 cr is CWIP earning nothing yet. [Screener; AR FY26]
- **Unit economics:** management refuses to discuss spreads ("I refrain from commenting on spreads"); says it buys feedstock and sells above index. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q1 vs Q4:** Q4 call — "Q1 looks on track for numbers that are better than Q4… and better than last year's Q1." Q1 delivered record EBITDA ₹540 cr (Screener) vs ₹376 cr in Q4 and ₹190 cr a year ago. **Beaten.** [Q4 call; Screener]
- **MIBK/MIBC:** Q4 — commission "tail end of Q1 or early Q2". Q1 — MIBK, MIBC and acetophenone in August, the rest within Q2; multipurpose agro-intermediate plant and alkylation plant slipped "a couple of months" (gas shortage, contract labour). **Slight delay.** [Q4 call official; Q1 call]
- **New AI products:** Q4 — 6–7 new products and fluorinated molecules to be in regular commercial production from Q3 FY27 for CY26 customer needs. Q1 — commercial allocations begin January (CY27). **Kept, back-ended.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Nitric acid:** Q4 — 45% utilisation, ₹90–100 cr EBITDA uplift expected once stable. Q1 — "successful completion and stabilisation" of ammonia-to-amines chain. **Delivered (benefit not quantified).** [Q4 call official; Q1 call]
- **Polycarbonate (PC):** Q4 — "commission by June 2028". Q1 — PC in "H2" of FY28-29 with BPA a couple of months later. **Pushed out by roughly one to three quarters.** [Q4 call official; Q1 call]
- **Margins:** Q4 — FY27 "improving trend" in standalone margins. Q1 AI EBIT margin 8% (4% in FY26). **On track.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **No numeric revenue/margin guidance is given.** [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Mega-project:** integrated propylene (PDH) → phenol/acetone → BPA → polycarbonate (1,65,000 t/yr), ₹11,500 cr, funded 60:40 debt:equity (~₹6,800 cr of debt already tied up); PC plant dismantled from Trinseo's Stade (Germany) site with Trinseo technology and CALIBRE brand; Trinseo (technology and equipment supplier) is also an anchor customer; HyCO gas supplied by Praxair India on a build-own-operate basis. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26]
- **Spend:** ~₹1,200 cr spent on the project so far; FY27 group capex ₹1,500–1,600 cr; management also said ~₹3,000 cr spent across last year and this year and "another ₹3,000 cr" in 2027–28. Peak debt ₹8,000–8,500 cr including working capital, debt/equity "will not cross 1". [Q1 call]
- **History:** CFI −₹717 cr (FY24), −₹1,489 cr (FY25), −₹669 cr (FY26); CWIP ₹774 cr → ₹1,649 cr → ₹1,828 cr; capital commitments ₹686 cr. [Screener; AR FY26 note 38]
- **Asset turns:** phenol debottlenecking from ~2 lakh t cost only ₹80–100 cr — very high return; PC project returns described only as "a formidable IRR"; no revenue potential given. [Q1 call]
- **Other:** fluorination (BTF) plant ~₹220 cr; nitric acid, nitration and second hydrogenation at DCTL commissioned in FY26. [Q4 call; AR FY26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Debt:** borrowings ₹73 cr (Mar-23) → ₹286 cr (Mar-24) → ₹1,267 cr (Mar-25) → ₹1,638 cr (Mar-26); net debt ₹1,064 cr at Mar-26; call quotes debt/equity 0.27x and net worth ₹6,214 cr at June 2026. Interest cost ₹19 cr (Q4) → ₹23 cr (Q1). [Screener; AR FY26; Q1 call]
- **Cash flow:** CFO ₹874 cr (FY24), ₹625 cr (FY25), ₹539 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹110 cr, −₹494 cr, −₹658 cr. FCF will stay negative through FY29 while the PC project is built. [Screener]
- **Working capital:** debtor days rose to 70 (56 in FY25) — management moved some customers to a dealership/consignment (CSA) model to secure dues; inventory days 57; cash conversion cycle 92 days (77 in FY24). Q1 call cites tighter inventory and collections. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Dividend:** ₹7.5/share final for FY26; payout 19%. [Q4 call; Screener]
- **Rating:** not checked (no rationale opened).

## Moat evidence
- **Scale and integration in phenol:** >50% Indian share, near-100% domestic sales, just-in-time supply to a fragmented customer base (laminates, pharma, agro) that would otherwise import in parcels with FX and freight risk. [AR FY26; Q1 call]
- **Cheap capacity creep:** phenol output up 65%+ over nameplate for ₹80–100 cr through advanced process control. [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- **Hazardous chemistry integration:** now makes its own nitric acid ("a nitrogen company that nitrates"); ammonia-to-amines chain puts it among "a select group of global players"; diazotisation with liquid, solid and gas. [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- **Policy support:** phenol import duty reimposed; US anti-dumping duty on sodium nitrite removed. [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- **Customer approvals:** PC compounds qualified with Mahindra, Panasonic, Goldmedal, Tata AutoComp; MIBK/MIBC quality "best-in-class". [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Spread-driven earnings:** Q1 record profit came with duty-free phenol imports blocked by war and feedstock disruption; Chinese phenol capacity "is now all done" but spreads remain cyclical and management won't disclose them. [Q1 call]
- **PC mega-project:** ₹11,500 cr — about half the current market cap — relocating a used German plant; timeline already slipped from June 2028 to H2 FY29; will lift debt toward ₹8,000–8,500 cr; returns undisclosed. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **DCTL losses:** AI EBIT only 4% in FY26 due to DCTL ramp-up losses; nitric acid ran at 45% in Q4. [AR FY26; Q4 call]
- **Execution frictions:** MIBK, alkylation and multipurpose plants delayed by gas shortage and contract labour. [Q1 call]
- **Receivables:** debtor days up 14 days in a year. [Screener]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion, no key audit matters; contingent liabilities negligible (₹0.5 cr). [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 38]
- **No pledge;** promoter 49.3% steady. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from crisis management (feedstock hoarding, nitric acid repairs) to "platform" talk and record results. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Nitric acid issue resolved; phenol near 4 lakh t run-rate; phenol duty reimposed. [Q1 call]
- PC timing drifted later; BPA formally announced; debt tied up; peak debt quantified for the first time (₹8,000–8,500 cr). [Q1 call]
- New commissioning delays (gas, labour) appeared. [Q1 call]

## My read
The business has turned: two quarters of sharply higher margins, the nitric acid integration is working, and phenol volumes keep creeping up for almost no capital. But much of the Q1 surge reflects an unusually tight phenol market (war-related feedstock and freight disruption, duty-free imports shut off), which may not last. The price (about 27x trailing earnings) needs the recovery to hold and the ₹11,500 cr PC project to earn well from FY29, while debt rises from ~₹1,600 cr to as much as ₹8,500 cr and free cash flow stays negative for three years. What would change my view: phenol margins holding above ~20% EBIT after the Middle East disruption fades and AI margins reaching double digits (positive); further PC slippage or cost overrun, or a slump in phenol spreads (negative).
