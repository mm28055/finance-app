# PCBL Chemical (PCBL) — deep research, Q1 FY27

Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged. Price ₹340, market cap ₹13,362 cr, dividend yield 1.77% (25 Sep 2026) [Screener]. RP-Sanjiv Goenka (RPSG) group company.

## Business and segments
- Carbon black: fine carbon powder made by partly burning heavy oil (carbon black feedstock, CBFS). Used mainly to reinforce tyres ("rubber black"), plus "performance chemicals" (industrial rubber) and "specialty black" (pigment/conductive grades for plastics, inks, coatings, cables, batteries) [IP Q1FY27].
- Capacity 900,000 t/yr after the 20,000 t Mundra specialty line (Jun 2026), including 132,000 t of specialty black; plants at Mundra, Durgapur, Chennai, Palej, Kochi; 300,000 t greenfield at Naidupeta, Andhra Pradesh planned (Phase I 150,000 t, ~₹1,100 cr) [IP Q1FY27; CARE Jul-26].
- Utilisation: FY26 sales 618,956 t on ~800,000–880,000 t through the year, i.e. roughly 70–75% (my estimate from disclosed volumes and capacity) [IP Q1FY27; CARE Jul-26].
- Volume mix FY26: tyres 354,841 t (57%), performance chemicals 194,480 t, specialty 69,635 t (11%). Q1 FY27: tyres 91,379 t, performance 42,386 t, specialty 19,748 t (+23% y/y) [IP Q1FY27].
- Specialty & Solutions = Aquapharm (bought FY24, mostly with debt): phosphonates, polymers and green chelates for water treatment, home care (detergents) and US oil & gas; capacity 167,000 t after a 37,000 t Mahad expansion; plants in Maharashtra, Jeddah and Texas. FY26 revenue ₹1,443 cr, EBITDA ₹162 cr; Q1 FY27 revenue ₹394 cr, EBITDA ₹47 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Power: 122 MW co-generation from waste gas; Q1 FY27 generation 217 MU, external sales 130 MU at ₹5.39/unit (₹3.66 in Q4) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Battery materials: Nanovace (51% JV with Australia's Kindia) for nano-silicon anode material; pilot plant at Palej got consent to operate; sampling from August 2026; commercial volumes "FY28"; plus 1,000 t super-conductive black and a planned 4,000 t acetylene black plant [Q1 call; Q4 call; CARE Jul-26].
- Exports ~38% of carbon black volume in FY26 (Q1 FY27: 50,528 t of 153,513 t, 33%), mostly South/South-East Asia; Q1 Europe ~16,000 t, US ~4,800 t [CARE Jul-26; Q1 call]. Standalone revenue 65% India, 35% global (FY25) [AR FY25].
- Customers: Indian and global tyre makers; ~one-third of volume sold on the spot market, the rest on formula contracts that pass through feedstock costs with a monthly or quarterly lag [Q1 call; Q4 call]. No customer-concentration figure disclosed.

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr) / OPM / net profit: Jun-25 2,114 / 15% / 94; Sep-25 2,164 / 12% / 62; Dec-25 1,846 / 12% / 2; Mar-26 2,066 / 12% / 40; Jun-26 2,473 / 16% / 155 [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 ₹6,420 cr (OPM 16%, PAT ₹491 cr); FY25 ₹8,404 cr (16%, ₹435 cr); FY26 ₹8,189 cr (13%, ₹198 cr). Profit more than halved in FY26 on weak carbon black spreads, surplus domestic capacity and a poor Aquapharm year [Screener; CARE Jul-26].
- Unit economics: carbon black EBITDA ~₹14,900/t average in FY26; ₹22,900/t in Q1 FY27, inflated by ~₹70 cr of low-cost inventory gains, of which ₹40–50 cr may reverse in Q2. Realisation rose ~₹27,000/t q/q: ₹11,000–12,000 from better spot pricing, the rest cost pass-through [Q1 call].
- Aquapharm EBITDA ~₹20,000/t in Q1 (₹19,500/t a year earlier); oil & gas volume –35% y/y but +50% q/q [Q1 call].
- Returns: ROCE fell 14% (FY24) → 12% → 8% (FY26); ROE 5.6% [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 included Brent averaging $97/bbl vs $78 in Q4 FY26 because of the West Asia conflict [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (30 Apr 2026): carbon black volume growth high single digit in FY27; "more than double-digit" EBITDA growth; EBITDA/t up 14–15% on ₹14,900; Aquapharm revenue +20–25%, back to ₹50–55 cr a quarter and a ₹75 cr/quarter run-rate "over the next two or three quarters"; cost savings ₹200–250 cr over 4–6 quarters; net debt to fall; FY25 profit to be surpassed; ₹40 bn (₹4,000 cr) EBITDA ambition for 2030 "on track"; battery commercial volumes FY28 [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (29 Jul 2026): FY27 EBITDA/t ₹16,500–17,000 kept; volume guide kept although Q2 volumes will be "rocky" (customer caution, destocking); gross profit ~₹36–37/kg for the next nine months; capex ~₹300 cr ± ₹50 cr, strategic capex ≤₹100 cr, Andhra spend mostly FY28; US tariff refund ₹40–45 cr expected within weeks; cost programme target intact [Q1 call].
- Delivery: EBITDA and profit beat strongly (PAT +65%), but mainly from inventory gains, spot pricing and power; carbon black volume was flat y/y (153,513 t vs 154,093 t). Aquapharm's ₹75 cr/quarter target was effectively paused — new CEO Rohit Narang (ex-Eastman) said "it is early days" and will update later [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹750 cr, including a 90,000 t brownfield at Tamil Nadu (~₹450 cr, ₹350 cr debt) [Q4 call; CARE Jul-26].
- FY27: ~₹300 cr ± ₹50 cr, mostly efficiency; Andhra Phase I (~₹1,100 cr) mostly from FY28; coal-tar distillation (backward integration to diversify feedstock) and ~10,000 t green-chelate capacity under evaluation [Q1 call; CARE Jul-26].
- Asset turns: FY26 sales ₹8,189 cr on ₹7,128 cr fixed assets = ~1.15x; the Aquapharm deal brought large goodwill/intangibles, which depresses the ratio [Screener].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings: ₹4,983 cr (FY24) → ₹5,571 cr (FY25) → ₹4,989 cr (FY26); management: net borrowings down ₹454 cr to ₹4,536 cr in FY26 while funding ₹750 cr capex [Screener; Q4 call].
- CARE: debt/EBITDA 5.02x in FY26 (above the 4x downgrade trigger); outlook cut to **Negative** from Stable in Jul 2026; FY27 repayments ~₹605 cr; cash ₹283 cr; working-capital lines ~59% used [CARE Jul-26].
- Promoter support: ₹448 cr of convertible warrants issued to promoter entities at ₹280/share (₹112 cr in FY25, ₹336 cr in FY26), used to repay debt [CARE Jul-26].
- Operating cash flow ₹1,105 cr (FY24), ₹760 cr (FY25), ₹1,576 cr (FY26); cash conversion cycle 35 days, working-capital days –40 in FY26 [Screener].
- Aquapharm shares pledged as security for an NBFC term loan (₹645 cr at Mar-25, 1.5x cover) [AR FY25 note on borrowings].
- Interim dividend ₹4.5/share declared with Q1; FY26 dividend payout was 119% of profit — dividends exceeded earnings in a year when leverage was above the rating trigger [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- Interest ₹423 cr and depreciation ₹373 cr absorbed 76% of FY26 operating profit of ₹1,043 cr [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Scale leader: India's largest carbon black producer and largest exporter, 45+ countries; capacity grew from 14,000 t to 880,000 t+ [CARE Jul-26].
- Locations near ports and tyre hubs cut freight; ~75% of feedstock comes from the US Gulf Coast; 90% of CBFS is imported [Q4 call; CARE Jul-26].
- Tyre-maker approvals take time, and formula pricing protects rupee margins per tonne over a cycle [Q4 call].
- Tailwinds: Russian carbon black exports contracting; China's "anti-involution" capacity discipline; India net exporter since FY22 (FY26 exports 4.44 lakh t, imports 1.30 lakh t) [CARE Jul-26; Q1 call]. I found no current Indian anti-dumping duty on carbon black in the documents read.
- Against: FY26 showed little pricing power — domestic surplus capacity pushed EBITDA/t down to ~₹14,900 [Q4 call; CARE Jul-26].

## Risks and red flags
- Leverage: debt/EBITDA ~5x, negative outlook; Andhra greenfield would add debt [CARE Jul-26].
- Aquapharm: margin ~11% in FY26 (–180 bp), CEO change in Q1, ₹75 cr/quarter target withdrawn in effect; European customers moving from phosphonates to green chelates [CARE Jul-26; Q1 call].
- Q1 profit quality: inventory gains (~₹70 cr), a power-price spike, and a one-off tariff refund (₹40–45 cr) coming in Q2 flatter the run-rate [Q1 call].
- Feedstock and freight: CBFS 90–95% of material cost; Red Sea/West Asia rerouting adds ≥14 days and higher freight; not always fully recovered [CARE Jul-26; Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Governance: political donations of ₹35 cr in FY25; an analyst asked about "a history of some donations" ahead of state elections — management said nothing significant in Q4/Q1 [AR FY25; Q4 call].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter was provisions for litigation; contingent liabilities small (~₹61 cr, mostly income tax) [AR FY25 KAM, note 23].
- Promoter holding 53.38% [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (Q4: cost shock, weak spreads) to confident (Q1: "structural" tailwinds from trade deals, US tariff advantage, Russian refinery damage).
- New CEO at Aquapharm and the ₹75 cr target deferred; "agentic AI" talk dropped; tariff refund and coal-tar plan added.
- Volumes flipped from the story (Q4: 90,000 t added, restocking) to a caveat (Q2 "rocky").

## My read
The carbon black core is recovering from a bad FY26, helped by trade deals and Russian supply problems, but Q1 flattered it: inventory gains, a spot-price spike and power prices explain much of the jump, and volumes did not grow. At about 50 times trailing profit the stock needs FY27 EBITDA/t of ₹16,500–17,000 on high-single-digit volume growth plus an Aquapharm recovery just to bring earnings back to FY24–FY25 levels. Leverage (~5x) and a negative CARE outlook limit room for error, and the ₹1,100 cr Andhra plan would add debt. I would get more positive on two quarters of rising volumes with EBITDA/t above ₹16,000 after inventory effects and Aquapharm back above ₹60 cr a quarter; negative if EBITDA/t slips back to ~₹15,000 or debt rises with the greenfield.
