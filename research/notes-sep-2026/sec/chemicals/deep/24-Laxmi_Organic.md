## Business and segments
- Two businesses. **Essentials** (~75% of Q1 FY27 revenue): acetyls — ethyl acetate (esterification of acetic acid with ethanol; solvent for pharma, flexible-packaging inks, coatings), acetaldehyde, acetic anhydride, butyl acetate. India's largest ethyl acetate maker, ~34% domestic share (CRISIL), "top 3 globally ex-China". **Specialties** (~25%): diketene derivatives (esters, amides, arylides made from ketene/diketene — building blocks for agro, pharma, pigments) and fluorochemicals (electrochemical fluorination at Lote — the only Indian ECF producer). Diketene: ~55% of domestic demand (CRISIL), ~14% global share, aiming to be #3 globally after Dahej. [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Mar-26; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Revenue: FY26 ₹2,846.7 cr (Essentials ₹2,027 cr, Specialties ₹820 cr vs ₹949 cr in FY25); Q1 FY27 Essentials ₹726.5 cr (+50% YoY), Specialties ₹241.8 cr (+17%). Q1 EBITDA share: Essentials ~72%, Specialties ~28% (a year earlier 11% / 89%). [IP Q4FY26; IP Q1FY27]
- Exports: 30% (FY24), 36% (FY25), 32% (FY26), 35% (Q1 FY27); USA ~10% of revenue. Top-10 customers ~20–27% of revenue; no customer above 10%. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 44; CRISIL Mar-26]
- Sites: Mahad (2 sites, 45 acres, 90% occupied), Lote (30 acres, fluorination + new world-scale ethyl acetate line, 70% occupied), Dahej (116 acres, <20% occupied; 60% specialty/40% essentials). "Large-scale operations (400 kT+)". Utilisation is not disclosed; Mahad specialty site was "fully utilised" before Dahej; Lote fluorination reached "40–45% of peak revenue" in FY26 (peak potential ~₹800 cr per stored profile — not verified). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call, AI copy]

## Moat evidence
- Scale/cost: #1 Indian ethyl acetate producer (~34% share), integrated acetaldehyde/anhydride; scale lets it keep supplying when smaller players cannot source feedstock — the Q1 volume gain (+10%) came in that setting. [CRISIL Mar-26; Q1 call]
- Specialty know-how: ketene/diketene and HF/electrochemical fluorination chemistry require hazardous-process permits and skills; 40+ diketene derivatives commercialised since buying Clariant's diketene business; ~55% of Indian diketene demand. [AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Mar-26]
- Weakness: pricing power is limited — specialty prices had to follow feedstock down ~25–30% over FY25–FY26 even without cost-plus formulas; one key agro product (~10% of specialty revenue) was phased out by a customer. Chinese acetic acid capacity keeps the chain long; ethyl acetate is imported freely. [Q1 call; Q4 call, AI copy]

## My read
Q1 FY27 is a cyclical spike in ethyl-acetate spreads on top of a business whose underlying earnings fell sharply in FY26 — and FY26 itself was propped up by a depreciation-method change and a one-off power-charge gain. The structural story (Dahej diketene doubling, Hitachi gas, fluorination) is real but keeps slipping and now reaches revenue only in FY28–FY29, while ratings, ROCE (5%) and senior-management stability have all weakened. The price needs Essentials margins well above the mid-single-digit "through-cycle" level and specialty margins back toward 20%; watch Q2 spreads, Dahej qualification in Q3, any fluorination impairment, and the MSEDCL appeal.
