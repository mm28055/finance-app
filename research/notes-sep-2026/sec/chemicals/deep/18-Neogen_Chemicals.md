# Neogen Chemicals (NEOGEN) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: both stored transcripts (Q1 FY27, 27 Jul 2026; Q4 FY26, 18 May 2026) are AI re-transcriptions. Compared with the official BSE transcripts they are accurate in substance and figures. Small errors: the stored Q1 copy says revenue grew "36%" in one place (official: **34%**); the stored Q4 copy names the chairman "Ms. Kanuru Sorana" (official: **Mr. Anurag Surana, Non-Executive Chairman**).

## Business and segments
- Two businesses. (1) Base specialty chemicals (standalone, Neogen Chemicals Ltd): 250+ bromine-based organic intermediates, organo-lithium reagents (via BuLi Chem acquisition) and inorganic lithium salts for pharma, agrochemicals, flavours & fragrances, electronics, water treatment; plants at Mahape, Vadodara/Karakhadi, Dahej SEZ. (2) Battery materials (subsidiary Neogen Ionics Ltd, NIL): lithium electrolyte salts (LiPF6-type) and additives, and liquid electrolytes for lithium-ion cells, at Dahej and a new 65-acre site at Pakhajan (Dahej PCPIR). [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Chemistry & raw materials: bromine (from Dead Sea Bromine/Israel and others; normal ₹300 ± 50/kg), lithium carbonate (company's "stable" price $15–25/kg; ~$24–25 in May 2026), both passed through with a lag. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Organic chemicals mix: bromine derivatives 40–45%, advanced intermediates ~30%, organo-lithium + lithium the rest. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 (consolidated): organic ₹194 cr (+18%), inorganic ₹57 cr (+158%); NIL (battery) ₹19 cr within these (vs ₹5 cr Q1 FY26; ₹36 cr for all of FY26, mostly salt). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Geography: Q1 FY27 ~70% domestic, ~30% exports incl. deemed exports; FY26 standalone ₹606 cr India, ₹250 cr overseas incl. deemed exports (29%). [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 37]
- Capacity/utilisation: organo-lithium raised 2.5x to 300 TPA, hit 100% in Q1 FY27 (further <₹15 cr debottleneck planned). Electrolyte: 2,000 MT existing + 30,000 MT Pakhajan (H1 FY27) = 32,000 MT (~30 GWh). Salts & additives: 5,500 MT by FY27 (≈4,000 MT salt, 1,500 MT additives; ~40 GWh of salt), plus a 500 MT intermediate unit. Of the 1,500 MTPA Dahej salt plant, 200 MTPA commissioned, 1,300 MTPA in trials. [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Dahej SEZ plant was destroyed by fire on 5 Mar 2025; production has run through toll manufacturers and rented third-party sites since; replacement plant in trial runs, commercial from Q2 FY27. [Q1 call; AR FY26 EoM]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 187 / op. profit 32 (17%) / PAT 10; Sep-25 209 / 30 (14%) / 3; Dec-25 220 / 32 (14%) / 4; Mar-26 247 / 44 (18%) / 11; **Jun-26 250 / 48 (19%) / 17**. Interest rose from ₹13 cr to ₹21 cr a quarter. [Screener]
- Company Q1 FY27: revenue ₹250.3 cr (+34%), EBITDA ₹48.2 cr (+53%, 19.3% margin), PAT ₹17.1 cr (+67%). Of the growth ~₹15 cr was price (lithium etc.), the rest volume; some gain from selling older low-cost inventory. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹691 cr → FY25 ₹778 cr → FY26 ₹862 cr; OPM 16% → 18% → 16%; PAT ₹36 cr → ₹35 cr → ₹29 cr. 3-year profit CAGR −17%. TTM PAT ₹36 cr. ROCE 6%, ROE 3.6%. [Screener]
- Unit economics (management): at $20 lithium carbonate, salt ~$20–21/kg, additives $25–30/kg, electrolyte $6–8/kg at scale ($7–9 now). Chinese salt prices were 30–50% below Neogen's formula price for most of 2025, ~20–25% below in May 2026, and "still lower" in July. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Battery ROCE target 18–20% at full use (FY29). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (May 2026): base (non-battery) FY27 revenue ₹875–950 cr (cut from ₹950–1,000 cr because the Dahej rebuild slipped from Q1 on labour shortages); battery FY27 ₹300 cr+ mostly H2; battery FY28 ₹1,200–1,400 cr; FY29 consolidated ₹3,700–4,200 cr (base ₹1,200–1,400 cr + battery ₹2,500–2,900 cr); FY31 ₹4,500–5,000 cr; Dahej replacement plant June 2026; US salt supplies from Dahej July/August; Morita $20 m equity in H1 FY27; peak debt ~₹1,700 cr. [Q4 call official]
- Q1 delivery: revenue ₹250 cr and 19.3% margin — strongest quarter yet; organo-lithium at 100%; NIL ₹19 cr. But Dahej replacement plant still in trials (commercial Q2), US customers still on "special" (below-formula) pricing, Morita money moved to Q2–Q3. [Q1 call]
- Q1 call (Jul 2026): base FY27 guidance **raised** to ₹950–1,050 cr; base margin 18% ± 1.5 (FY28 18–20%); battery FY27 ₹300 cr **kept** (₹200 cr salt + ₹100 cr electrolyte), ramp from Nov–Dec as US buyers must be non-FEOC (no Chinese-linked supply) from January 2027; base FY28 ₹1,100–1,200 cr; battery FY28 now "a strong case to cross ₹1,000 cr" (softer than ₹1,200–1,400 cr in May); FY29 battery ₹2,400–2,900 cr kept. Pre-QIP peak net debt now "~₹1,800 cr" (was ~₹1,700 cr). [Q1 call]
- Track record: repeated slips — Dahej rebuild (Q1 → June → Q2), battery capex raised by ~₹250 cr+ to ₹1,795 cr, FY26 battery revenue only ₹36 cr. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Battery project cost ₹1,795 cr: Dahej Phase 1 ₹428 cr (complete by Feb 2027) + Pakhajan Phase 2 ₹1,367 cr (by Mar 2027); ₹1,298 cr spent to date (₹218 cr in Q1 FY27). Increase blamed on switching to Japanese (MU Ionic Solutions / Morita) technology, localising parts and a 500 MT intermediate unit. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- FY27 capex ₹800–900 cr (NIL ₹700–800 cr, standalone ~₹100 cr). Room for +2,000 MT salt at Pakhajan and +500 MT additives at Dahej (12–15 months build + 3–6 months approval); decision around end-FY27. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Asset turn: management's ₹2,400–2,900 cr battery revenue on ~₹1,800 cr gross block ≈ 1.4–1.6x at full use; NIL gross block ₹1,700–1,800 cr end-FY27; standalone ₹450–550 cr. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Technology: electrolyte plant designed with MU Ionic Solutions (Mitsubishi Chemical–UBE JV); Morita (Japan) partner for salts, putting $20 m equity into the JV. [CRISIL Sep-26; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Mar-26: borrowings ₹1,395 cr (Screener), company net debt ₹1,295 cr; CWIP ₹857 cr; net worth ₹816 cr. [Screener; Q4 call; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Operating cash flow: −₹29 cr (FY24), +₹196 cr (FY25), **−₹231 cr (FY26)**; H1 FY26 −₹246 cr, H2 +₹14.6 cr. An analyst noted five-year cumulative EBITDA of ~₹582 cr but cumulative operating cash flow of about −₹93 cr; management said consistently positive operating cash flow only by FY29. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Working capital (Screener FY26): debtor days 161, inventory 445, payables 346, cash conversion cycle 260 days. Payables were stretched with supplier consent and supplier factoring ("special support ... due to the fire"). Base-business target 140 days, later 110–120; battery 90 days. [Screener; Q4 call; AR FY26 note 20]
- Equity raised: promoter preferential ₹161 cr (FY26); **QIP ₹600 cr completed** (Sep 2026, per CRISIL) for debt reduction/working capital; interest saving ₹45–50 cr a year if fully repaid; CRISIL expects net worth above ₹1,800 cr by Mar-27. Promoter stake 48.3% in Sep-26 after the QIP (53.0% in Jun-26, 51.2% in Mar-26). [CRISIL Sep-26; Q1 call; Screener]
- Insurance: fire loss ₹362.9 cr recognised with claim receivable ₹348.8 cr (net ₹14.1 cr exceptional in FY25); ₹164 cr recovered so far; ~₹186 cr still receivable. [AR FY26 EoM; Q1 call]
- Ratings: CRISIL A-/Stable (outlook raised from Negative on 25 Sep 2026 after the QIP), A2+ short term. Dividend ₹1/share. [CRISIL Sep-26; Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- Only Indian giga-scale local electrolyte supply chain today; supplies 100% of one domestic cell maker (scaling 1 → 5 GWh); working with 5 of 6 upcoming Indian giga-factories. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Provisional approvals from 4 international customers for salt; final site audits by all 4 US electrolyte makers completed; 4 of 5 major US electrolyte makers approved the site. Salt contracts lithium-formula priced with pass-through. [Q1 call]
- Policy tailwind: US 45X tax credit requires non-FEOC supply from 2027; India ACC PLI (50 GWh; 10 GWh re-bid) and a proposed battery-component PLI. [Q1 call]
- Base business: diversified (no molecule >10% of revenue); organo-lithium demand up 12x since acquisition. [Q1 call]
- Weakness: Chinese salt still cheaper; Neogen had to accept below-formula "special pricing" for 2026 trial volumes. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Auditor **Emphasis of Matter** on the Dahej fire accounting: loss ₹362.9 cr and insurance claim ₹348.8 cr booked on surveyor/CA opinion while the insurer's assessment is still in process — ~₹186 cr is still uncollected 18 months later. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Key audit matters: revenue recognition (fraud-risk presumption), and capitalisation of costs in NIL's CWIP (subsidiary auditor). [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Contingent liabilities** ₹117.9 cr (FY25 ₹28.7 cr), incl. ₹81.7 cr possible customs duty/GST on fire-damaged SEZ goods sold as scrap (company expects insurance to cover it). [AR FY26 note 35]
- Parent has given a **₹1,223 cr corporate guarantee** for NIL's borrowings and pledged 30% of its NIL shares to NIL's lenders; ICDs to NIL ₹110 cr at 9.85%. [AR FY26 related-party note; note on investments]
- Supplier-finance (factoring) arrangements keep payables high — this flatters operating cash flow and is a hidden form of borrowing. [AR FY26 note 20; Q4 call]
- Execution: several commissioning slips; salt sales depend on US policy (45X/FEOC) and Indian cell plants actually ramping. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Earnings quality: FY26 PAT ₹29 cr on ₹2,900 cr total assets; P/E ~200x on trailing profit. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- From defensive (cost overruns, guidance cut, factoring questions) to confident (record quarter, guidance raised, ₹600 cr QIP). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New: QIP (now completed), battery-component PLI, organo-lithium at full capacity. Softer: FY28 battery number, peak-debt figure raised to ~₹1,800 cr pre-QIP, Morita equity pushed out. [Q1 call]

## My read
The base business is genuinely improving (19% margin, organo-lithium full, guidance raised), and the QIP has removed the balance-sheet stress. But the valuation (~200x trailing earnings) rests almost entirely on the battery unit going from ₹36 cr revenue in FY26 to ₹2,400–2,900 cr by FY29 — at prices still being undercut by China and on timelines that have slipped repeatedly. Cash conversion has been poor for five years and the fire-insurance receivable remains large. What would make me more positive: US salt shipments at formula prices from Q4 FY27 and ₹300 cr battery revenue delivered; what would make me more negative: another commissioning slip, a US policy rollback, or the insurer settling well below ₹348.8 cr.
