# Alkyl Amines Chemicals (ALKYLAMINE) — deep research, Q1 FY27

Figures: Screener standalone (company has no subsidiaries) unless tagged. Price ₹1,848, market cap ₹9,451 cr, P/E 42 on trailing profit, dividend yield 0.54% (25 Sep 2026) [Screener]. Alkyl holds calls only half-yearly: the two latest are Q4 FY26 (6 May 2026) and Q2 FY26 (6 Nov 2025); there was no Q1 FY27 call or presentation.

## Business and segments
- Makes aliphatic amines — methylamines, ethylamines, isopropylamines — from ammonia plus methanol or ethanol, and derivatives (DEHA, DMA-HCl, DIPEA, DEK etc.), plus acetonitrile (ACN, a solvent made from acetic acid and ammonia, used in pharma and peptide synthesis). One reported segment, "specialty chemicals" [AR FY26 MD&A; Q2 call].
- End markets: pharma 50–60% of sales, agrochemicals 15–20%; also rubber chemicals, water treatment, electronics [Q4 call].
- Capacity: ~2 lakh t/yr of production (~1.5 lakh t saleable after one-quarter captive use); sold volume crossed 1 lakh t in FY25 and FY26; plants at 60–85% utilisation [Q2 call; Q4 call]. Ethylamine plant of ~30,000 t (commissioned ~2024) at 60–70%, enough for 4–5 years; ACN ~30,000 t across two plants [Q2 call; Q4 call].
- Plants: Patalganga, Kurkumbh (Maharashtra), Dahej (Gujarat) [AR FY26].
- Market structure: ethylamines a domestic duopoly with Balaji Amines ("largest or second-largest producer of ethylamines in the world"); methylamines now four players (RCF, Balaji, Alkyl, and new entrant Aarti Drugs with ~50,000 t), merchant market 80–90k t vs >1.5 lakh t capacity; ACN: Alkyl is India's leading and only pharma-grade maker, with China the main rival and Balaji building ACN capacity [Q2 call; Q4 call].
- Exports: ₹319 cr FOB in FY26 vs ₹343 cr in FY25; export share 22.7% of turnover (BRSR); direct US exposure ~1% of sales [AR FY26 MD&A, BRSR; Q2 call]. No product is more than 14–20% of sales; ~200 pharma customers; no customer-concentration figure disclosed [Q2 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr) / OPM / net profit: Jun-25 406 / 19% / 49; Sep-25 389 / 18% / 43; Dec-25 354 / 19% / 42; Mar-26 387 / 18% / 45; Jun-26 528 / 25% / 95 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: sales +30% y/y, operating profit ₹134 cr (vs ₹77 cr), net profit roughly doubled. Driver: selling prices raised 30–40% after the West Asia war doubled ammonia prices (₹50 → >₹100/kg) while cheaper stocks were still being used — a price/inventory effect, not a volume story [Screener; Q4 call; News: sahi.com, Aug 2026].
- Annual: FY24 ₹1,441 cr (OPM 17%, PAT ₹149 cr); FY25 ₹1,572 cr (19%, ₹186 cr); FY26 ₹1,536 cr (19%, ₹180 cr). FY26 volume ~–1% and price ~–1% [Screener; Q4 call].
- Long view: FY21 peak sales ₹1,242 cr at 35% OPM and ₹295 cr PAT; 5-year profit CAGR –9%, ROCE 56% (FY21) → 17% (FY26) [Screener].
- Unit prices (May 2026): methylamines ~₹100–110/kg, ethylamines ₹200+/kg, ACN "a bit over ₹200/kg" vs FY26 average ~₹140–150/kg [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q2 FY26 call (Nov 2025): walked back the usual 10–15% volume growth for FY26 to "subdued"; new import-substitute product (~₹120 cr capex, asset turn ~1.5x) with mechanical completion Feb–Mar 2026 and commercialisation Q1 FY27; ACN duty benefit from Q4 FY26 [Q2 call].
- Q4 FY26 call (May 2026): FY27 volume growth 5–10% ("normal year"), value higher on prices; "we have seen the worst"; margins "a little better" than FY26; new plant slipped to mechanical completion end-June, commissioning early Q2 FY27, economics unchanged; FY27 capex ₹80–90 cr (balance of the new plant plus engineering), maintenance ₹20–30 cr; bigger R&D projects (₹20–200 cr) on hold until volatility settles [Q4 call].
- Delivery: FY26 volume was ~flat against the original 10–15% plan (missed). Q1 FY27 margin (25%) beat the "a little better" guide by a wide margin, but management itself said the early-cycle gain "is only temporary". The new project slipped one quarter twice (Q4 FY26 → Q1 FY27 → early Q2 FY27) [Q2 call; Q4 call; AR FY26].

## Capex and capacity
- Fixed assets ₹1,015 cr, CWIP ₹130 cr (FY26); FY26 investing outflow ₹137 cr [Screener].
- New specialty project at Dahej (per AR and CFO; the Q2 call said Kurkumbh), commissioning early Q2 FY27, ~₹120 cr, import substitute for dyes, pigments, electronics; ~1.5x asset turn implies ~₹180 cr revenue at full use [AR FY26; Q2 call; Q4 call].
- Asset turns overall: FY26 sales ₹1,536 cr on ~₹1,145 cr fixed assets + CWIP ≈ 1.3x [Screener].
- A new 10–20k t ACN plant would cost ₹150–160 cr+ and take ~2 years [Q2 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free: borrowings ₹1 cr (FY26); CRISIL: zero gearing, ~₹165 cr unencumbered cash/investments, bank lines <1% used [Screener; CRISIL Dec-25].
- Operating cash flow ₹275 cr (FY24), ₹263 cr (FY25), ₹239 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹111 cr in FY26; cash conversion cycle 42 days [Screener].
- Dividend payout ~28% [Screener].

## Raw materials, China and trade duties
- Key inputs: ammonia (for all amines and ACN), methanol (methylamines), ethanol/alcohol (ethylamines), acetic acid (ACN). Ammonia is bought from distributors tied to fertiliser plants and importers; the whole aliphatic-amine industry uses only 10,000–15,000 t of ammonia a month, a tiny share of India's supply, so government allocation in a crisis is feasible [Q4 call].
- Price moves: ammonia ~₹50/kg → >₹100/kg after the Hormuz disruption (Mar 2026); methanol and acetic acid also up; the company raised selling prices 30–40% [Q4 call].
- Earlier, in Oct–Nov 2025, US sanctions on Indian distributors buying Iranian feedstock disturbed methanol/ammonia supply briefly [Q2 call].
- ACN anti-dumping duty took effect end-June 2025; importers front-loaded 1.5–2x normal volumes before it, and Chinese sellers cut prices further, so the benefit only showed from Q4 FY26. India's ACN market is ~30,000–35,000 t, of which imports were ~40% before the duty; an analyst estimated Alkyl sells ~15,000–16,000 t [Q2 call; Q4 call].
- An anti-dumping case on isopropylamine was under investigation, with a Commerce Ministry recommendation due by Dec 2025 and a Finance Ministry decision around March 2026; the outcome was not discussed on the Q4 call [Q2 call].
- China's "anti-involution" capacity discipline helps "not dramatically" — perhaps ~5% on selling prices [Q4 call].

## Moat evidence
- Duopoly in ethylamines with stable shares and protected margins; prices rose with alcohol costs [Q4 call].
- Cost leadership in ACN claimed ("we will be the lowest cost manufacturer in this product for some time") and ACN anti-dumping duty in force since June 2025; ACN market share rose in Q4 FY26 as Chinese imports fell [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Pass-through ability: products are a "C item" (small share of customers' costs), so the ammonia shock was passed on fully [Q4 call].
- Hazardous-chemical handling and pharma approvals act as barriers; CRISIL AA-/Stable [CRISIL Dec-25].
- Against: methylamines have no moat — a fourth entrant arrived despite overcapacity [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- Q1 FY27 margin spike is likely temporary; management expects margins to settle once raw material prices fall [Q4 call].
- Ammonia supply: plants were briefly shut in March 2026 when LNG shortages stopped ammonia supply (force majeure); ammonia contracts are now short term [AR FY26; Q4 call].
- Chinese competition in ACN and customers' products; ADD benefits can be eroded by price cuts; Balaji's new ACN plant [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call].
- Methylamine overcapacity (~2x demand) [Q2 call].
- Disclosure: no product volumes, prices or R&D projects disclosed until launch; only half-yearly calls [Q4 call].
- Governance: promoter family pay is high relative to profit — Executive Chairman Yogesh Kothari ₹13.6 cr, Suneet Kothari ₹6.1 cr, Kirat Patel ₹6.1 cr in FY26 (together ~₹26 cr, ~14% of net profit). Aug 2026 postal ballot re-designated Kothari as Executive Chairman and Patel/Suneet Kothari as Joint MDs, and sought 5-year commission for non-executive directors [AR FY26 corporate governance; Postal ballot notice Aug-26].
- Contingent liabilities ₹36.8 cr (tax, excise, customs); auditor's only key audit matter is litigation; unmodified opinion; related-party transactions stated as ordinary course and arm's length [AR FY26 note 36a, KAM].
- Promoters hold 72.04%, no pledge shown [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q2 FY26: tone of frustration — US tariffs, Chinese dumping outside the US, sanctions on Indian distributors buying Iranian feedstock, volume guidance cut.
- Q4 FY26: "cautious optimism… we have seen the worst"; ammonia doubling passed through; ACN duty finally working; but new R&D capex frozen and plant delayed.
- New issue in Q4: fourth methylamine player live; ammonia security.

## Best management quotes
- "We are actually a very minor cost in their cost of material. It's what you might call a C item." — Kirat Patel [Q4 call]
- "You do probably enjoy a temporary margin widening, but it's only temporary." — Kirat Patel [Q4 call]
- "It's a bit of a mystery why Aarti went into this industry when there was already overcapacity." — Kirat Patel [Q4 call]

## My read
The business is debt-free, cash-generative and well run, but profit has gone sideways for five years (₹295 cr in FY21, ₹180 cr in FY26) as Chinese dumping and new capacity hit spreads. Q1 FY27 (₹95 cr profit) is an ammonia-shock windfall that management itself calls temporary. At 42 times trailing profit — which already includes Q1 — the stock needs sustained ~20% profit growth; that requires the Dahej product to ramp and ACN share gains under the duty to hold after raw material prices normalise. I would be more positive if H1 FY27 margins stay above 21% once ammonia eases; negative if OPM falls back to ~18% by Q3 or ACN duty benefits fade.
