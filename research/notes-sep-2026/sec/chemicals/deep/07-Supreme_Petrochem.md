# Supreme Petrochem (SPLPETRO) — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. Figures: Screener standalone (history) and consolidated (FY26 on, since Xmold became a subsidiary in Apr-2025) unless tagged. Calls checked against official BSE-filed transcripts (Q1 FY27 call 29 Jul 2026; Q4 FY26 call 28 Apr 2026).

## Business and segments
- **Chemistry:** buys styrene monomer (SM, ~100% imported; traditionally from Saudi Arabia/Kuwait, now also China/Asia) and polymerises it into general-purpose and high-impact polystyrene (GPPS/HIPS), expandable polystyrene beads (EPS, "thermocol"), extruded polystyrene insulation boards (XPS), and since Sep-2025 mass ABS (acrylonitrile-butadiene-styrene, an engineering plastic; also needs acrylonitrile and butadiene). Also makes polymer compounds and masterbatches [AR FY26 MD&A p.37–38; IP Q1FY27].
- **Capacity:** PS 3,00,000 TPA; EPS 1,43,000 TPA after Phase-II (Amdoshi 1,15,000 + Chennai 33,000 per AR; commissioned 14 Apr 2026); ABS 70,000 TPA nameplate but running at ~65% (~45,000 TPA) since a critical equipment failure; XPS 72,000 m³; compounds 50,000 TPA; Amdoshi complex total 5,33,000 TPA [IP Q1FY27 p.4–5; Q4 call].
- **End markets:** appliances (refrigerators, ACs, washing machines — "practically every refrigerator manufacturer uses our polystyrene"), stationery, packaging, cold-chain and building insulation (EPS/XPS), automotive and appliance compounds (via Xmold) [Q4 call; AR FY26].
- **Customer mix:** PS demand roughly 45% OEM / 55% non-OEM processors; no customer above 10% of sales; ~17–18% of revenue is traded goods [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 Note 44].
- **Geography:** exports ₹440 cr of ₹5,322 cr standalone sales in FY26 (8.3%; ₹551 cr in FY25); exports to 100+ countries but suspended under force majeure since March 2026 [AR FY26 Note 44 and MD&A; CRISIL Feb-26].
- **One segment only (styrenics);** no product-wise revenue or ABS volume disclosed — management declined to give ABS volumes on the Q1 call [AR FY26 Note 44; Q1 call].
- **Share:** "Market leader in PS and EPS with market share exceeding 50%"; largest installed capacity in India [IP Q1FY27 p.2; CRISIL Feb-26].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (standalone, ₹ cr, revenue / operating profit / margin / net profit):** Jun-25 1,387 / 115 / 8% / 81; Sep-25 1,100 / 78 / 7% / 48; Dec-25 1,265 / 69 / 5% / 30; Mar-26 1,587 / 253 / 16% / 168; Jun-26 1,693 / 331 / 20% / 236 [Screener]. Consolidated Jun-26: 1,715 / 332 / 19% / 238 [Screener consol].
- **Annual (standalone):** FY24 revenue ₹5,253 cr, op. profit ₹473 cr (9%), PAT ₹346 cr; FY25 ₹6,023 cr / ₹535 cr (9%) / ₹391 cr; FY26 ₹5,338 cr / ₹519 cr (10%) / ₹327 cr; TTM ₹5,645 cr / ₹731 cr (13%) / ₹483 cr [Screener]. FY21–22 peak margins 18–21% show how spreads swing [Screener; CRISIL Feb-26].
- **Volumes:** FY26 manufactured sales 3,63,203 t (+2%; domestic +1.6%, exports +5.4%); Q4 FY26 1,00,664 t (+5.4%); Q1 FY27 70,842 t vs 93,853 t (−24.5%) — exports ~10–12% of normal, non-OEM PS sales down ~50% [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26].
- **Unit economics:** operating profit per tonne ≈ ₹46,700 in Q1 FY27 (₹331 cr ÷ 70,842 t) vs ≈ ₹12,300 in Q1 FY26 and ≈ ₹14,300 for FY26 as a whole [calc from Screener and call volumes; includes trading profit]. Spread (delta) over SM: GPPS normal ~$200/t, went to $300+; HIPS normal $275–300, went to $400+; by late July GP $250–275, HIPS ~$350 [Q1 call].
- **Raw material:** SM ~$1,000/t pre-war → peak $1,650 (March) → $1,500 (April) → Q1 average $1,350–1,400, peak $1,500+ → ~$1,300 CIF in late July. FY26 average SM price was ~17% lower than FY25, which is why FY26 revenue fell 11% on flat volume [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- **Returns:** ROCE 19% FY26 (23% FY25; 66–72% in FY21–22); ROE 14% [Screener].

## Guidance history
- **Volume:** Q4 call — 8–10% volume growth in FY27 *only if* normalcy returned by end-June. Q1 call — no guidance at all ("would not want to hazard any guess"). Effectively withdrawn [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Margins:** Q4 — "in normal circumstances, we should be doing better than last year because the volumes will be better." Q1 — the 19.5% margin is "an aberration… will normalize" [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Delivered far above FY26 in Q1, but on price, not volume.
- **ABS:** Q4 call — 65% of original capacity now; aim for "close to 80% or 85–90%" utilisation this year; ABS Phase-2 (second line, to 1,40,000 TPA nameplate) still targeted FY28. AR (June) — hopes for "75–80% of curtailed capacity" this year. Q1 call — no status update; board is building "ABS line 2" by March 2029 [Q4 call; AR FY26 p.38; Q1 call].
- **Capex:** Q4 call / AR — ~₹250 cr for FY27. Q1 call — new approvals ₹450 cr (XPS wide-width line 1,50,000 m³ + compounding 50→80 KTPA by June 2027; 5th PS line 80 KTPA by Dec-2028 per call / Mar-2029 per presentation), and "all capex put together… closer to ₹900 cr" including ABS line 2, all from internal accruals [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Compounds (SPC):** AR guides +30% in FY27 after +25% in FY26; Xmold utilisation 45–50% in FY26, target 65–70% in FY27 [AR FY26; Q4 call].
- **Track record:** Haryana (Munak) greenfield PS/EPS plant repeatedly deferred because IOCL's Panipat SM plant is delayed; ABS Phase-1 ran barely three months before failing [Q4 call; AR FY26].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹210 cr (ABS Phase-1, EPS Phase-II, compounding lines), all internal [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Pipeline: XPS line-2 (1,50,000 m³), compounding +30,000 TPA (by Jun-27), PS line-5 +80,000 TPA (PS to 3,80,000 TPA by Dec-28/Mar-29), ABS line-2 (to 1,40,000 TPA), Munak land nearly acquired [Q1 call; AR FY26].
- **Asset turns:** management expects ~2x on the new projects at full capacity [Q1 call]. Current: FY26 revenue ₹5,338 cr on fixed assets ₹1,474 cr + CWIP ₹74 cr ≈ 3.4x (gross block rose from ₹813 cr to ₹1,474 cr as ABS was capitalised) [Screener]. Depreciation jumped from ₹18 cr to ₹27–28 cr a quarter after ABS capitalisation [Screener].
- Xmold Polymers (Chennai, 15,000 TPA compounding) bought 80% for ₹31 cr in Apr-2025; remaining 20% due by Mar-2027 (total ₹39 cr) [AR FY26 Note 43; CRISIL Feb-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free; investable surplus ₹700 cr at Mar-26 and ₹874 cr at Jun-26 (₹463 cr at Dec-25) [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Feb-26]. Screener shows borrowings ₹126 cr (lease/other).
- FY26 CFO ₹246 cr (67% of operating profit); FCF ₹37 cr; FY25 FCF −₹36 cr [Screener].
- Working capital: inventory days 51, debtor days 34 (24 in FY25), payable days 65; cash-conversion cycle 20 days vs 8 [Screener]. Inventory ₹614 cr (17.7% of assets) — auditors made inventory valuation a key audit matter because SM prices swing [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Dividend ₹10.5/share for FY26 (60% payout) [Q4 call; Screener].
- Inventory-loss risk flagged by management: "some inventory loss might occur the moment the situation normalizes" [Q4 call].

## Moat evidence
- >50% share in PS and EPS; supplies nearly all fridge makers; specialised grades earn "at least $200" over commodity grades [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Kept 100% of domestic customers supplied through the Gulf SM shutdown by re-sourcing — reliability matters to OEMs running just-in-time [Q1 call].
- **But pricing is import-parity:** "for the commodity grades, the base price is always the landed price of the imported material" [Q4 call]. Imports from Thailand, Taiwan, Iran and China "dented the market share of local manufacturers" in FY26; ~87,000 t PS imported in FY25 [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call].
- Cost position: no backward integration into SM; only benefit is freight/proximity and scale. 50% of power from renewables [IP Q1FY27].
- Ratings: CRISIL AA-/Stable and Ind-Ra AA-/Stable — both **cut outlook from Positive to Stable** during FY26 (CRISIL on 27 Feb 2026) over margin moderation and the ABS setback [CRISIL Feb-26; AR FY26 MD&A].

## Risks and red flags
- **Earnings reversal:** Q1 profit is a war-spread windfall; spreads already narrowed by July; inventory losses when SM falls [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **ABS execution:** proprietary Versalis equipment failed in Dec-2025, ~3 months after start; running at 65%; CRISIL says plant is insured and under warranty, so loss is mainly lost sales. Management stopped giving ABS updates on the Q1 call — a disclosure gap [CRISIL Feb-26; Q1 call].
- **Duty policy:** government cut import duty on polymers to zero (to 30 Jun 2026) — management warned extensions would hurt local producers; duties restored after April per Q1 call [AR FY26; Q1 call]. No anti-dumping duty on PS in force; CRISIL cites dumping from Thailand, Korea, Taiwan, Iran, Singapore [CRISIL Feb-26].
- **China:** now self-sufficient in PS; Asian exporters redirected to India [AR FY26 MD&A].
- **Governance:** clean. Contingent liabilities tiny (₹17 cr income tax, ₹3 cr indirect tax); unqualified audit; KAMs only inventory valuation and ABS capitalisation; promoters (Supreme Industries 30.78%, R. Raheja Investments 30.78%) hold 64.24%, unchanged for two years (pledge status not checked beyond an exchange headline reporting no encumbrance at Mar-26); sales to Supreme Industries ₹129 cr at market rates [AR FY26 Notes 43, 46; Screener shareholding].
- **Import data blackout:** government stopped publication of import data in March 2026, making market share hard to verify [Q1 call].

## My read
Supreme is a well-run, debt-free converter with over half of India's PS/EPS market, but it owns no feedstock and prices at import parity, so its profit follows global SM-to-polymer spreads. FY26 (10% margin, ₹327 cr profit) is nearer the normal run-rate than Q1 FY27 (20%); TTM profit of ₹483 cr is flattered by two war quarters. At about ₹880 the market is paying for the growth pipeline (ABS to 1,40,000 TPA, PS line-5, XPS, compounds) that only lands in FY28–FY29. I would turn more positive on proof that ABS runs above 80% of nameplate and volumes recover to 90,000+ t a quarter at 9–11% margins; negative if spreads collapse with inventory losses or ABS repairs drag on.
