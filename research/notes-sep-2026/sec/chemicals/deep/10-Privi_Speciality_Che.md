# Privi Speciality Chemicals (PRIVISCL) — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged. Calls checked against official BSE-filed transcripts (Q1 FY27 call 31 Jul 2026; Q4 FY26 call 12 May 2026). The stored Q4 transcript is the official text; the stored Q1 transcript is an AI version with material errors (see below).

## Business and segments
- **Chemistry:** turns crude sulphate turpentine (CST, a by-product of kraft paper pulping, imported from the US/Europe) and gum turpentine oil (GTO) into alpha- and beta-pinene, then into pine-based aroma chemicals (dihydromyrcenol, terpineols, camphor, amber and woody notes), plus musks, citral derivatives and phenol-based molecules — 75+ products from ~60 raw materials. CST route gives a ~15–20% cost advantage over GTO; Privi is "among only four global players" with advanced CST processing [ICRA Jun-26; Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Capacity:** 48,000 MTPA finished products; CST processing 36,000 MTPA; GTO processing 12,000 MTPA; seven plants near JNPT and Ankleshwar; utilisation "around 90%" [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- **End markets & customers:** fragrance houses and FMCG — Givaudan, IFF, Symrise, P&G, Reckitt, BASF among clients; exports to 40+ countries; ~65–70% of revenue exported; ~70% of revenue under annual contracts with back-to-back raw-material sourcing; ICRA calls concentration risk low [ICRA Jun-26; Q4 call]. Sales routed through the US subsidiary were ₹409 cr in FY26 (₹330 cr in FY25) [AR FY26 Note 30].
- **Product split:** not disclosed ("we have stopped giving the product-wise… mix… as per the Board directive"); pinene products are the main contributor; camphor 4–6% of revenue [Q1 call].
- **JV PRIGIV (51% Privi / 49% Givaudan, Mahad):** makes 40+ specialty molecules exclusively for Givaudan; turned profitable in Q4 FY26; revenue ~₹55 cr in FY26, target ~₹130 cr in FY27 and ₹300 cr in 3–4 years; Q1 FY27 revenue ~₹18 cr at 14–15% EBITDA; partners adding ₹50 cr of equity [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Unit economics:** FY26 volume 42,389 t (+6.5%); realisation up from ~₹450/kg five years ago to ~₹600/kg in FY26; EBITDA per kg ≈ ₹157 (₹665 cr company EBITDA ÷ 42,389 t) [Q4 call; calc].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr, revenue / operating profit / margin / net profit):** Jun-25 559 / 132 / 24% / 58; Sep-25 679 / 182 / 27% / 90; Dec-25 605 / 151 / 25% / 75; Mar-26 722 / 180 / 25% / 94; Jun-26 666 / 152 / 23% / 83 [Screener].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹1,752 cr, op. profit ₹329 cr (19%), PAT ₹95 cr; FY25 ₹2,101 cr / ₹458 cr (22%) / ₹185 cr; FY26 ₹2,564 cr / ₹651 cr (25%) / ₹317 cr; TTM ₹2,671 cr / ₹666 cr (25%) / ₹342 cr [Screener]. FY23 margin was 12% — the margin rebuild is recent [Screener].
- **FY26 growth mix:** +6.5% volume, ~8% price, rest mix [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27:** revenue +19.2%; gross margin ~44% vs ~51% a year earlier (management confirmed raw-material cost ~4 points higher; says full-year RMC will be 52–55% of sales); EBITDA margin held by lower power/other costs; ₹2.78 cr export consignment lost in a warehouse fire near JNPT (insurance claimed) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Company "EBITDA" includes other income:** company Q1 EBITDA ₹167.5 cr (24.6%) vs Screener operating profit ₹152 cr (23%) [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- **Returns:** ROCE 22%, ROE 26% (FY26) — up from 6%/3% in FY23 [Screener; IP Q1FY27].

## Guidance history
- **FY27 (Q4 call):** 20% growth "in the coming year" on a standalone basis with similar ~24–25% EBITDA margins; merger entities would be added on completion. **Q1:** delivered ~19–20% growth and 23–24.6% margin; Chairman repeated "20% CAGR with similar EBITDA margins" — guidance kept [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Long term:** "5K1K" — ₹5,000 cr revenue and ₹1,000 cr+ EBITDA in 3–4 years (≈2x) — kept on both calls; biomass/biotech upside is "beyond 5K" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Phase-1 capacity (48,000 → 54,000 t):** Q4 call said complete by 30 Jun 2026; Q1 call moved it to September 2026 ("next 15 days or so" from 31 Jul). ICRA (30 Jun) said July 2026 — **missed** [Q4 call; Q1 call; ICRA Jun-26].
- **Phase-2/3:** 54,000 → 66,000 t by Sep-2027; new products (maltol, ethyl maltol, cyclopentanone, Musk-T, ~10 specialties) mechanically complete ~mid-2027, revenue from H2 FY28; ~72,000 t total by June–Sept 2028. Presentation instead says expansion projects "ready for commercial operations in second half of 2028"; ICRA says Phase II Q1 FY28, Phase III Q2 FY29 — timelines are inconsistent across documents [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; ICRA Jun-26].
- **Merger:** Privi Fine Sciences (PFSPL) and Privi Biotechnologies into Privi — NCLT filing promised in Q4, done in Q1; NCLT-convened shareholder meeting on 27 Oct 2026 [Q1 call; BSE filing 24-Sep-26 title on Screener].

## Capex and capacity
- Capex plan: management ₹850–900 cr over FY27–FY29 (~₹300 cr existing products, ~₹300 cr new products, ~₹300 cr specialties), "primarily funded through internal accruals" [Q1 call]. ICRA sizes the programme at **~₹1,200 cr, funded partly with ₹500–600 cr of new debt**; ~₹350 cr of Phase-1 already spent [ICRA Jun-26].
- Biomass demonstration plant (2 t/day, Navi Mumbai) ₹70–75 cr, ~12–15 months; furfural backward integration only after ~2 years (FY28–29) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Maltol/ethyl maltol: China makes >95%; Privi plans ~one-quarter of global capacity; ₹1,000 cr+ revenue potential [Q1 call].
- Asset turns: FY26 revenue ₹2,564 cr on fixed assets ₹1,169 cr ≈ 2.2x; CWIP rose to ₹314 cr [Screener]. ICRA and management both imply ~2x turns on new capex.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹865 cr (Jun-26), ₹876–887 cr (Mar-26); net debt/EBITDA 1.29x; net debt/equity 0.57x; gross borrowings ₹1,021 cr [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- FY26 CFO ₹550 cr (102% of operating profit), FCF ₹230 cr — best in years [Screener; Q4 call].
- Working-capital cycle 108 days (Q1 FY27) vs 141 days a year earlier and 117 days for FY26 [Q1 call; Q4 call]. Screener inventory days 235; inventory ~₹700 cr because of 60–130-day transit [Screener; ICRA Jun-26].
- Dividend ₹10/share for FY26 (~12% payout) [Q4 call; Screener].
- Ratings: ICRA AA (Stable) assigned 30 Jun 2026 (downgrade trigger debt/EBITDA >2x); CRISIL rating revised upward from A+/Positive during FY26 per the annual report [ICRA Jun-26; AR FY26 Board's report].

## Moat evidence
- CST processing scale and dual pinene route (cost edge ~15–20%) [ICRA Jun-26].
- Long-term contracts (~70% of sales) with the top fragrance houses; Givaudan JV making 40+ exclusive molecules; "we were born out of China Plus One" [ICRA Jun-26; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Pricing: passed on ~8% price increase in FY26 and higher alpha-pinene/GTO costs in 2026 (though with a lag — see gross margin) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Margin expansion from process work (steam/power savings, "ProMax" programme) — power and fuel costs fell year on year [Q4 call].
- China competition: in pine chemicals China relies on volatile GTO; in maltol China dominates — Privi's entry is a direct challenge [Q1 call; ICRA Jun-26]. No anti-dumping protection relevant to its exports.

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter selling:** promoter-group holding fell from 74.05% (Mar-25) to 69.89% (Jun-25) to 60.60% (Dec-25); on 31 Dec 2025 promoter entity Vivira Investment and Trading sold 36.28 lakh shares (9.28%) in a ₹1,086 cr block deal at ~₹2,799/share [Screener; News: Multibagg, Dec-25].
- **Related-party merger:** PFSPL, owned by the Babani family and promoter companies, is being merged in at 1 Privi share for every 135 PFSPL shares; it adds ~6,000 t capacity. Minority holders vote on 27 Oct 2026 — the valuation deserves scrutiny [AR FY26 Board's report; Q1 call].
- **Other related-party dealings:** purchases of ₹27.7 cr and sales of ₹15.5 cr with Prasad Organics (a KMP-relative enterprise); rent ₹3.3 cr to promoter entities Moneymart Securities and MM Infra; job work from PFSPL ₹1 cr [AR FY26 Note 30].
- **Execution slippage:** Phase-1 slipped June → September 2026; conflicting Phase-2/3 dates across call, presentation and ICRA [calls; IP Q1FY27; ICRA Jun-26].
- **Funding gap:** management says internal accruals; ICRA models ₹500–600 cr new debt on a ₹1,200 cr programme [Q1 call; ICRA Jun-26].
- **Raw materials/FX:** >55% of raw materials imported; crude-linked inputs 15–18%; alpha-pinene up sharply in 2026 [AR FY26 risk table; Q1 call].
- **Disclosure:** product and volume detail withdrawn "as per the Board directive"; quarterly volumes not given [Q1 call].
- **Audit:** unqualified; KAM on revenue-recognition timing across shipment terms; contingent liabilities small (income tax ₹18 cr, GST/customs ₹15 cr) [AR FY26 auditor's report, Note 34].

## My read
Privi has genuinely re-rated its business — margins from 12% (FY23) to ~25%, ROCE 22%, cash conversion above 100% in FY26 — on CST integration, process gains and sticky contracts with global fragrance houses. The stock at ~₹3,594 prices in the 5K1K plan (roughly doubling in 3–4 years) with little room for slippage, yet Phase-1 already slipped and the specialty/maltol capacity only contributes from H2 FY28. Governance needs watching: promoters sold ~13% of the company in 2025 and a promoter-owned company is being merged in. I would turn more positive on a fairly valued PFSPL merger, Phase-1 running full in Q3/Q4, and gross margin back near 47–48%; negative on further delays or rising debt beyond ~2x EBITDA.
