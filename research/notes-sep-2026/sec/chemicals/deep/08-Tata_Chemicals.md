# Tata Chemicals (TATACHEM) — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged. Calls checked against official BSE-filed transcripts (Q1 FY27 call 27 Jul 2026; Q4 FY26 call 4 May 2026).

## Business and segments
- **Chemistry:** soda ash (sodium carbonate) — natural (mined trona in Wyoming, USA, 2.54 MTPA; Lake Magadi, Kenya, 0.40 MTPA) and synthetic (Solvay process from salt + limestone + coal/steam at Mithapur, Gujarat, 1.09 MTPA; UK industrial soda ash now shut). Total ~3.9–4.0 MTPA, >two-thirds natural. Derivatives: sodium bicarbonate (4,40,000 TPA across India, UK, Singapore), vacuum/iodised salt (India 1.6 MTPA; UK 4.3 lakh TPA + pharma salt), precipitated/specialty silica (13,800 TPA, tyres), prebiotics (FOS, 6,770 TPA), bromine, cement (5 lakh TPA) [IP Q1FY27 p.14–15; AR FY26 MD&A].
- **End markets:** soda ash → flat and container glass, solar glass, detergents, lithium carbonate; bicarb → food, feed, pharma (haemodialysis), flue-gas treatment (NTPC is the biggest buyer); salt → sold largely to group company Tata Consumer Products, which owns the "Tata Salt" brand; agrochemicals and seeds via 55%-owned Rallis India; 1/3 stake in IMACID (phosphoric acid, Morocco) [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26].
- **New segments from Q1 FY27 (Q1 revenue / EBIT, ₹ cr):** Living Essentials (salt, bicarb, prebiotics; India, UK, Singapore) 1,064 / 188; Industrial Essentials (soda ash, silica, industrial chemicals; India, US, Kenya) 2,240 / −70; Farm Essentials (Rallis) 1,022 / 156. FY26: Living 3,633 / 749; Industrial 8,368 / −1,877 (includes ₹1,837 cr US goodwill impairment); Farm 2,897 / 210 [IP Q1FY27 p.9–10].
- **Volumes Q1 FY27:** soda ash 884 kt (US 614, India 195, Kenya 75); salt + bicarb 544 kt [IP Q1FY27 p.8].
- **Geography:** US ~1/3 of revenue (₹1,397 cr in Q1), India standalone ₹1,281 cr, UK ~5% of group (British Salt), Kenya, Singapore [IP Q1FY27 p.11; BSE filing 26-Sep-26].
- **Utilisation:** Mithapur produced 1 million t of soda ash in FY26 (≈ nameplate); UK industrial ash closed; US exports to South-East Asia cut back to break-even-or-worse levels [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr, revenue / operating profit / margin / net profit):** Jun-25 3,719 / 649 / 17% / 316; Sep-25 3,877 / 537 / 14% / 154; Dec-25 3,550 / 345 / 10% / −69; Mar-26 3,438 / 274 / 8% / −2,116 (goodwill impairment); Jun-26 4,255 / 538 / 13% / 60 (EPS −₹0.67: owners' share negative after Rallis minority) [Screener].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹15,421 cr, op. profit ₹2,847 cr (18%), PAT ₹435 cr; FY25 ₹14,887 cr / ₹1,953 cr (13%) / ₹387 cr; FY26 ₹14,584 cr / ₹1,805 cr (12%) / −₹1,715 cr. TTM: ₹15,120 cr / ₹1,694 cr (11%) / −₹1,971 cr. FY23 peak op. profit ₹3,820 cr (23%) [Screener].
- **India standalone is the profit engine:** FY26 revenue ₹4,831 cr, EBITDA ₹954 cr (FY25 ₹4,441 / ₹818 cr); Q1 FY27 revenue ₹1,281 cr, EBITDA ₹364 cr (28%) vs ₹270 cr a year ago [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27 p.7].
- **Unit economics:** consolidated EBITDA per tonne of soda ash-equivalent is not disclosed. Pricing clues: Chinese export soda ash $160–180/t FOB ($170–190 CIF South-East Asia); India took ~₹2,000/t price increase in Q1 (import parity plus weaker rupee); US domestic prices flat [Q1 call].
- **Returns:** ROCE 3% (FY26), ROE 1% [Screener]. Book value ₹832/share includes ₹9,072 cr of US mining rights and ~₹7,400 cr of quoted Tata-group shares [AR FY26 KAM; CRISIL Dec-25].

## Guidance history
- **Net debt:** Q4 call — FY27 net debt "more or less at similar levels" to Mar-26 (₹5,961 cr). Q1 delivered ₹5,692 cr — down ~₹270–300 cr, from selling land and most of its non-core shares, not operations [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Capex:** Q4 — "around ₹1,300 cr" for FY27. Q1 — capex around depreciation (~₹1,200 cr), "try not to exceed it, in fact be below that" — trimmed [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **UK:** Q4 blamed an early (March) shutdown; Q1 blamed £2.4 m of one-offs (loss on sale of EU emission allowances, prior-period items); now "EBITDA positive and tending toward PBT break-even" for the full year — the break-even target keeps slipping [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.11].
- **India margin:** Q1 said sustainable level is ~18% EBITDA (~32–33% gross contribution), so Q1's 28% is flattered by cheap coal inventory; Q2 costs rise as expensive coal arrives [Q1 call].
- **US:** exports to South-East Asia "at break-even levels… likely to remain so through the year" unless China cuts capacity [Q1 call].
- **Projects:** 82.5 KTPA iodised vacuum salt at Mithapur by end-FY27; 210 KTPA salt at Valinokkam (Tamil Nadu) and 50 KTPA silica at Cuddalore (₹775 cr) in 2028 (24-month build); 40 KTPA bicarb at Singapore; IRR hurdle "upwards of 20%", silica 15–20% [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.13].
- **Sodium-ion battery:** first pack built; 6–9 months pilot for stationary storage; customer offers in ~1 year; full plant ~2 years after that [Q1 call]. Speculative.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex: Screener shows investing outflow ₹766 cr; CWIP fell from ₹1,913 cr to ₹1,038 cr as projects were capitalised [Screener].
- CRISIL (Dec-25) assumed ~₹3,500 cr capex over FY26–FY28 [CRISIL Dec-25]. Management now caps it at depreciation (~₹1,200 cr/yr) [Q1 call].
- Asset turns are low: FY26 revenue ₹14,584 cr on fixed assets ₹20,602 cr (≈0.7x) — heavy because of acquired mining rights and goodwill [Screener; AR FY26].
- **New (Sep-2026):** TCNA won, in the Chapter 11 sale of rival Searles Valley Minerals, North American soda ash customer contracts for over 5,00,000 t to be served over two years; completed 11 Sep 2026 — adds volume to Wyoming (where fixed costs are high) [BSE filing 12-Sep-26].
- No soda ash capex until "the cycle returns" [Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt (ex-leases) ₹5,692 cr at Jun-26 (₹5,961 cr Mar-26); leases another ₹894 cr [IP Q1FY27 p.5].
- **Net debt / EBITDA ≈ 3.4x** on TTM operating profit of ₹1,694 cr (≈3.3x on company EBITDA). CRISIL's downgrade trigger is "net debt/EBITDA exceeding 3 times on a sustained basis"; CRISIL had expected 2.25x by Mar-26 — missed [calc; CRISIL Dec-25]. Rating still CRISIL AA+/Stable.
- Borrowings rose from ₹5,563 cr (FY24) to ₹8,001 cr (FY26) [Screener].
- CFO ₹1,269 cr in FY26 (67% of operating profit) vs ₹3,016 cr in FY24; FCF ₹129 cr [Screener].
- Inventory days 353, payable days 293 (Screener basis); cash-conversion cycle doubled to 113 days in FY26 [Screener].
- Dividend continues (yield ~1.7%) despite a reported loss; payout 119–143% of profit in FY24–FY25 [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Natural soda ash in Wyoming and Kenya is low-cost versus synthetic; Mithapur is among the lowest-cost synthetic plants [CRISIL Dec-25]. But China's ~2 MTPA new natural capacity in Inner Mongolia flattens that edge [AR FY26 Outlook].
- India: domestic customers asking for bigger allocations after imports halved (70,000–1,00,000 t/month → about half) during the Gulf disruption; price follows import parity [Q4 call].
- Bicarb: UK/Singapore sell into pharma/food grades where the customer must approve the plant — sticky; India bicarb capacity raised from 1.4 to 2 lakh t, sold out; but a domestic competitor added capacity [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Farm: Rallis has 7,200 dealers/95,000 retailers [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Trade protection: anti-dumping and safeguard duty (with quantity limits) on soda ash under investigation [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **China oversupply:** Chinese inventory at an all-time high of 1.73 Mt; global demand growth ~0.2% in FY27 vs ~3.8 Mt new capacity [Q1 call; AR FY26 Outlook].
- **US asset values:** goodwill impaired by ₹1,837 cr ($208 m) in Q4 FY26, but **₹9,072 cr of US mining rights remain on the books**, not written down (the business was valued as a whole and the shortfall absorbed by goodwill first; only ₹60 cr goodwill is left, so any further shortfall hits mining rights); silica and nutraceutical plants (₹426 cr) also under impairment review — both auditor key audit matters [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report; Q4 call].
- **Leverage above rating trigger** (see above). Debt cut in Q1 relied on one-off asset sales [Q1 call].
- **Contingent liabilities (Mar-26):** income-tax disputes ₹833 cr; Kenyan land-rates demand ₹891 cr (TCML won at the Court of Appeal; county has gone to the Supreme Court); indirect tax ₹150 cr; others ~₹100 cr — roughly ₹2,000 cr in all [AR FY26 Note 47].
- **Auditor change:** KPMG LLP resigned as auditor of the UK subsidiaries (incl. British Salt) in Sep-2026 because the company would not pay fees "at levels charged in previous years"; KNAV appointed; KPMG raised no concerns. BSR (KPMG India) remains group auditor [BSE filing 26-Sep-26].
- **Kenya fuel:** heavy fuel oil hedged only to October 2026; India limestone imports from the Middle East at risk if the conflict runs past Oct–Nov [Q1 call].
- **Related parties:** salt sold to Tata Consumer under a board-approved long-term arrangement; related-party balances small [Q4 call; AR FY26 Note 45].
- **Ownership:** promoters 37.98%, unchanged; FIIs fell from 13.6% to 12.1% over two years [Screener].

## My read
Tata Chemicals is two businesses: a solid India/Living Essentials franchise (India EBITDA ~₹950 cr a year, bicarb and salt growing) and a loss-making global soda ash business stuck in a Chinese oversupply cycle. The stock at ~₹644 trades below book (₹832), but book is propped up by ₹9,072 cr of US mining rights that earn little today. Leverage (~3.4x) has drifted above CRISIL's comfort level, and debt reduction now depends on asset sales. The view improves if China shuts synthetic capacity (watch its inventory falling below ~1.2 Mt), the Searles contracts lift US utilisation, and India margins hold near 18% after the cheap-coal effect fades; it worsens with a further US impairment or a rating outlook change.
