# Deepak Fertilisers & Petrochemicals (DEEPAKFERT) — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged otherwise. Calls checked against the official BSE-filed transcripts (Q1 FY27 call 31 Jul 2026; Q4 FY26 call 29 May 2026).

## Business and segments
- **What it makes (chemistry):** a gas-to-ammonia-to-nitrates chain. Natural gas (now mainly Equinor LNG) → ammonia (PCL's 500 KTPA plant at Taloja, 452 KT made in FY26, ~93% of group needs) → weak/dilute nitric acid (DNA) → concentrated nitric acid (CNA) and technical ammonium nitrate (TAN, the oxidiser in mining explosives) and nitro-phosphate fertilisers. Separately, isopropyl alcohol (IPA, 70 KTPA) is made from propylene (refinery grade propylene, RGP) [AR FY26 p.37; IP Q1FY27 p.5, p.7].
- **Three segments and end markets:** Mining chemicals (TAN, 587 KTPA; coal, limestone, metals, infrastructure; ~39% domestic share; 28% of group revenue in FY25) — Industrial chemicals (DNA 885 KTPA, CNA 231 KTPA, IPA 70 KTPA; pharma solvents, nitro-aromatics, explosives; 62% share in CNA, 27% in DNA, 10% in merchant IPA; 16% of revenue) — Crop nutrition (NP 300 KTPA, NPK 800 KTPA, Bensulf 57 KTPA; Maharashtra/Karnataka/Gujarat farmers, subsidy-linked; 44% of revenue) [IP Q1FY27 p.5].
- **Reported segments FY26 (AR):** Chemicals revenue ₹5,304 cr, segment result ₹1,147 cr (FY25 ₹5,130 cr / ₹1,412 cr — result down 19%); Fertilisers revenue ₹6,166 cr, result ₹420 cr (FY25 ₹5,120 cr / ₹425 cr). Traded fertiliser sales more than doubled to ₹1,760 cr (FY25 ₹775 cr), i.e. growth was low-margin trading [AR FY26 Note 48].
- **Q1 FY27 split:** Mining chemicals ₹911 cr (+37% YoY) on 130 KT volume (−12% YoY, −21% QoQ); Industrial chemicals ₹490 cr (−11% YoY) with nitric acid 74 KT flat and IPA just 4 KT (−76% YoY; IPA utilisation 29% because the government curtailed RGP quota); Crop nutrition ₹1,367 cr (+9%), ANP+Smartek 128 KT [IP Q1FY27 p.12–14; Q1 call].
- **Utilisation:** TAN 92% in FY26; DNA 95%, CNA 74% in Q1; ammonia plant averaged 94% in Q1 after a Q4 turnaround that lifted capacity ~10%; ANP 78%, NPK 63% [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- **Geography / customers:** almost all India; no reportable geographic segment; no single customer above 10% of revenue except Government of India subsidy [AR FY26 Note 48]. Small Australian blasting-services arm (Platinum Blasting, now 100% owned) [AR FY26 p.37]. Exports minor (TAN export volume ~0.2 KMT in Q1) [IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- **Contracts:** 65% of new Dahej CNA capacity tied up on a 20-year contract [IP Q1FY27 p.16]. Nitric acid is "long-term-contract-driven… linked to the feedstock" [Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr, revenue / operating profit / margin / net profit):** Jun-25 2,659 / 513 / 19% / 244; Sep-25 3,006 / 464 / 15% / 214; Dec-25 2,830 / 353 / 12% / 141; Mar-26 3,011 / 354 / 12% / 139; Jun-26 3,256 / 845 / 26% / 490 [Screener].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹8,676 cr, op. profit ₹1,287 cr (15%), PAT ₹468 cr; FY25 ₹10,274 cr / ₹1,925 cr (19%) / ₹945 cr; FY26 ₹11,506 cr / ₹1,685 cr (15%) / ₹739 cr. TTM Jun-26: ₹12,104 cr / ₹2,016 cr / ₹985 cr [Screener].
- **Plain words:** FY26 was a two-halves year — H1 decent, H2 hit by phosphoric acid and sulphur spikes (fertiliser subsidy lagged), IPA price pressure and a ~₹70–75 cr ammonia shutdown in Q4. Q1 FY27 then set a record on war-driven ammonia/TAN prices plus the first Equinor gas [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Unit economics:** TAN realisation ≈ ₹70,000/t in Q1 (₹911 cr ÷ 130 KT, incl. B2C services); ammonia FOB Middle East rose from ~$300/t (mid-2025) to $707–801/t (Apr–Jun 2026), ~$600 by end-July; phosphoric acid CFR $1,153→$1,360/t over a year; potash $349→$383/t [IP Q1FY27 p.15; Q1 call].
- **Returns:** ROCE 11%, ROE 10.9% (FY26); 3-year profit CAGR −16%; 5-year sales CAGR 15% [Screener].
- **B2C in TAN:** ₹151 cr in Q1 (+42%), 17% of segment revenue (16% a year earlier) [IP Q1FY27 p.12].

## Guidance history
- **Project timing (repeatedly slipped):** Gopalpur TAN + Dahej nitric acid were due Q4 FY26, then Q1 FY27, then Q2 FY27 (official Q4 transcript: "delayed by 1 quarter and now… one more quarter", blamed on election-related labour shortage) [Q4 call]. ICRA in March said "end of Q1 FY2027" [ICRA Mar-26]. Q1 call kept "towards the end of this quarter" (i.e. by Sep-26) [Q1 call]; but the FY26 annual report says "commissioning during H2 FY 2026-27" [AR FY26 p.39]. No commissioning filing found as of 27 Sep 2026 [News search, Sep-26].
- **Ramp-up:** Q4 call: Gopalpur 90–95% utilisation "by end of this year", full next year. Q1 call: ~80% in Q4 FY27 "if everything goes well" — a softer number [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Capex cost:** Gopalpur ₹2,675 cr (was ~₹2,200 cr — ~22% overrun), Dahej ₹1,983 cr; total ~₹4,650 cr; ₹3,858 cr spent by Q1 [ICRA Mar-26; IP Q1FY27 p.16; Q1 call].
- **Debt:** Q4 call declined to give an FY27 debt number; Q1 call said debt is "near peak" and deleveraging starts this year [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Margins:** no numeric guide. Q4 said growth and margins improve in FY27 — Q1 delivered (26% margin). On Q1 call management refused to extrapolate Q1 margins to the full year and said Q2 is seasonally weak for mining [Q1 call].
- **Equinor savings:** analyst quoted ~₹300 cr a year; management: "at present, this number is right" but depends on crude/Henry Hub [Q1 call].
- **Verdict:** operating guidance delivered and beaten in Q1; project timing missed twice and now ambiguous (Q2 on the call vs H2 in the AR).

## Capex and capacity
- Gopalpur, Odisha: 376 KTPA TAN, port-based, near eastern coal belt; will buy ammonia (not captive) under long-term supply contracts. Dahej: 300 KTPA WNA + 150 KTPA CNA. Post-expansion AN ~1.0 MTPA ("3rd largest pure-play TAN producer globally", ~60% of India's TAN demand) and WNA ~1.2 MTPA [IP Q1FY27 p.16; AR FY26 p.39; Q1 call].
- FY26 capex ₹1,569 cr; Q1 FY27 ₹515 cr; ₹800–1,000 cr remaining in FY27, then back to maintenance levels [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.17].
- Asset turns: FY26 revenue ₹11,506 cr on fixed assets ₹6,151 cr + CWIP ₹3,053 cr — about 1.25x gross block including CWIP. New plants (~₹4,650 cr) must earn standard spreads to lift ROCE back above ~15%. Depreciation over 25 years; interest on project loans currently capitalised, so P&L interest and depreciation will step up after commissioning [Screener; Q1 call].
- Char Dham Chemicals (explosives licence, no revenue) bought for ₹121 cr in May 2026 for the TCO (total-cost-of-ownership) blasting model [AR FY26 Note 52; Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹4,719 cr at Jun-26 (₹4,824 cr at Mar-26); gross debt ₹5,057 cr; cash ₹338 cr; net debt/equity 0.60x [IP Q1FY27 p.17].
- **The "1.4x net debt/EBITDA" is annualised on the record quarter** (₹4,719 ÷ (₹845 × 4) = 1.40x). On TTM EBITDA of ₹2,016 cr it is ~2.3x [calc from IP Q1FY27 and Screener].
- FY26 cash from operations only ₹206 cr (CFO/operating profit 28%) vs ₹1,880 cr in FY25; free cash flow −₹1,363 cr. ICRA had expected ₹650–700 cr CFO for FY26 — a clear miss [Screener; ICRA Mar-26].
- Working-capital days rose from 2 to 30; debtor days 57→74; inventory days 58→79 (fertiliser channel stock and subsidy lag). Management called it temporary [Screener; Q4 call].
- Dividend payout 17% of FY26 profit (~₹10/share) [Screener].
- DMSL (mining subsidiary) raised ₹800 cr CCDs at 12.5% XIRR — ₹500 cr third parties, ₹300 cr a promoter entity; convert in 4 years; investors hold a put option tied to exit events, parent a call option. Auditor made this a key audit matter [AR FY26 Note 21(n), KAM 1; Q4 call].

## Moat evidence
- ~40% TAN market share; India's only maker of low-density AN (LDAN, used for ANFO, sells at a premium to AN-melt and imported fertiliser-grade AN) [AR FY26 p.37; ICRA Mar-26].
- Largest nitric acid maker in South-East Asia; 62% of CNA market [IP Q1FY27 p.5–6].
- Cost position: captive ammonia (~80% consumed in-house) plus 15-year Equinor LNG (0.65 MTPA, per analyst; not confirmed by company) — management says it will cover all gas needs after phase-in [Q1 call].
- Hazardous-chemical licensing (AN Rules, PESO) and bulk-mix delivery trucks at mine sites raise switching costs [AR FY26 p.37].
- Pricing: in Q1 TAN revenue +37% on 12% less volume — price power in a tight market, but largely import-parity driven [IP Q1FY27].

## Risks and red flags
- **Cyclical peak risk:** Q1 profit rests on the US–Iran/Strait-of-Hormuz shock; management itself says prices normalise "over the medium to long term" [Q1 call]. In 2022 a similar peak (EPS ~₹34 in Q1) was followed by four weak years [Q1 call; Screener].
- **Execution:** two delays already; AR and call disagree on timing (Q2 vs H2) [AR FY26; Q1 call]. Gopalpur cost rose ~22% [ICRA Mar-26].
- **Gopalpur will buy merchant ammonia**, so it lacks the Taloja cost advantage [Q1 call].
- **Competition:** a major refiner and Coal India have signalled TAN plans [Q1 call; Q4 call]. Russian AN imports pressured prices in FY24–FY26 [ICRA Mar-26].
- **Contingent liabilities (consolidated):** ₹779 cr at Mar-26 (₹1,871 cr a year earlier; fell after MAL won an income-tax appeal). Largest items: GST input-credit disputes ₹283 cr, supplier claims ₹212 cr, sales-tax/VAT ₹157 cr. Disclosed separately: GAIL gas-pricing claim ₹357 cr against MAL (MAL won at arbitration and Delhi HC; GAIL's appeals pending in the Supreme Court) and a ₹96 cr income-tax penalty (AY 2015-16) still under appeal [AR FY26 Notes 42, 46, 49; ICRA Mar-26]. Auditor flagged contingent liabilities as a key audit matter; opinion unqualified.
- **Related parties:** promoter entity SCM Commercial holds ₹151 cr of 8% optionally convertible debentures and ₹300 cr of DMSL CCDs; subsidiary MAL committed up to ₹75 cr to an AIF (SOA Fund Series I) **managed by SCM Commercial** — promoter-managed fund receiving group money; a ₹21 cr deposit is receivable from the chairman [AR FY26 Notes 21, 41(b), 52(b)].
- **Pledges:** no promoter share pledge found; DMSL has pledged 30–51% of PCL shares to project lenders [AR FY26 borrowings note].
- **Rating:** ICRA AA- on "watch with developing implications" since 31 Mar 2026 [ICRA Mar-26].
- **Subsidy/regulation:** NBS subsidy lag, government price caps on DAP/urea limit NPK premiums; PESO portal changes cost Q1 volume [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (LPG/propane cuts, LNG delays, labour shortages, delayed subsidy, ₹70–75 cr shutdown) to "historic best" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Operating margin 12%→26%; PAT ₹139 cr→₹490 cr; net debt ₹4,824 cr→₹4,719 cr [Screener; IP Q1FY27].
- Gopalpur 95%→96% complete, Dahej 86%→93%; timing unchanged on the call but AR now says H2 [calls; AR FY26].
- Ramp-up target softened from 90–95% "by year end" to ~80% in Q4 FY27 [calls].
- New: IPA volumes collapsed (RGP quota), PESO disruption, possible Russian AN export ban from October (bullish), refiner entry (bearish), DMSL listing route still undecided (IPO vs demerger) [Q1 call].

## My read
The integration story is real: captive ammonia plus Equinor gas lowers cost, and two large plants in existing chemistry should add roughly 60% to TAN capacity and ~35% to nitric acid capacity. But Q1 FY27 is a war-price quarter, and the flattering 1.4x leverage is an annualised peak; on trailing numbers debt is still ~2.3x EBITDA and FY26 cash conversion was poor. The stock at about ₹1,364 is priced on TTM profit of ₹985 cr, so it needs the new plants to run near 80%+ and ammonia spreads to stay above pre-war levels. What would change my view positively: both plants commissioned in Q3 with steady ramp-up and Q2/Q3 margins above 18%. Negatively: further slippage, a fast fall in ammonia/TAN prices, or more promoter-linked financial dealings.
