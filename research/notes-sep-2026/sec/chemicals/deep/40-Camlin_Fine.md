- **Q4 FY26, acquisition name.** The stored copy calls the French acquisition "Alwanis", "Wintai" and "Wangree". The official name is **Vinpai** (Vinpai SA, Euronext-listed). The profile's "Winpi, Alwanis" is also wrong.

## Business and segments

**What Camlin sells.** Camlin makes antioxidants ("shelf-life solutions") and aroma chemicals. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Chemistry chain.** Phenol is converted to diphenols (hydroquinone, HQ, and catechol) at Dahej SEZ. HQ goes into TBHQ and BHA antioxidants ("straights"). Catechol goes into guaiacol and then into vanillin and ethyl vanillin.
- **Blends.** Straights are mixed into 200+ custom antioxidant, antimicrobial and feed-additive blends. The blends are made in India, Mexico, Brazil, the USA, Europe (Vinpai, France) and Belgium.
- **End markets.** Pet food, animal feed mills, rendering, biodiesel, edible oils, aquaculture, food and beverage, and flavour and fragrance houses.
- **Scale.** 1,300+ customers in 80+ countries.

**FY26 revenue mix, continuing operations (₹1,723 cr).** Blends 58%, straights 18%, aroma 14%, performance chemicals 10%. [IP Q1FY27]

**Q1 FY27 segments (new disclosure).** [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Specialty ingredients: ₹401 cr, EBITDA 6.35% (about ₹25 cr).** Straights were ₹92.7 cr (+41%) and blends ₹308.6 cr (+35%). Vinpai contributed ₹24.7 cr.
- **Aroma: ₹77.4 cr (+37%), EBITDA −4.4% (about −₹3.4 cr).**
- **Performance chemicals: ₹174.9 cr gross, EBITDA −2.5%.** About ₹130 cr of that is internal transfers, so net outside sales are only about ₹40 cr.

**Vanillin volumes.**
- Q1 FY27 sales were about 560 t: 350 t ethyl vanillin plus about 200 t methyl vanillin from channel stock. Production was about 400 t against quarterly capacity of 1,500 t. [Q1 call]
- FY26 sales were about 1,700 t. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- On annual capacity of 6,000 t (5,000 t achievable), FY26 utilisation was under 30%. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]

**Diphenol plant.**
- The Dahej diphenol plant has been **shut since about May 2026**. Phenol rose from ₹85/kg to over ₹150/kg. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- HQ is now imported from China and is secured for 2 quarters. There is about 3,000 t of catechol stock, enough for 8–9 months of vanillin. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- A decision on the plant is due by Q3 FY27: repurpose it or restart. [Q1 call]

**Geography.** Most revenue comes from the Americas (US, Mexico, Brazil). [AR FY26 MD&A]
- The European diphenol unit, CFS Europe (Italy), is in liquidation. It was deconsolidated in Q4 FY26 with a ₹85 cr book gain. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- China is also being liquidated. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Only a small India share is disclosed. There are no customer-concentration figures.

Management's breakdown of the Q1 hit: [Q1 call]
- 3–4 points from war-driven raw material, freight and rerouting via South Africa.
- 1–2 points from using costly dealer and supplier finance because of tight liquidity.
- About ₹8 cr of Brazil air freight after the fire there.
- Only about half of cost increases are passed on, with roughly a one-quarter lag.

**Vanillin unit economics.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Price:** about $13–14/kg in the US and Europe, set by market leader Solvay. That is below the $17.5–18 US list price because of tariff, holding and financing costs.
- **Raw material:** $7–8/kg before the war, now about $9/kg.
- **Conversion cost:** about ₹7 cr a month (fixed), or about $1.5/kg at 4,000 t.
- **Break-even:** at about 400 t a quarter, ethyl vanillin only breaks even.
- **Profit at 3,000 t:** about 7% EBITDA for the vertical.

## Guidance history

**Q4 FY26 call (May 2026):**
- FY27 revenue of ₹2,200–2,400 cr.
- **EBITDA margin of 12–14%**.
- Vanillin of 3,600–4,000 t (the "4,000 t mark very doable").
- Blends above ₹1,400 cr.
- Vinpai and Vitafor to break even at the EBITDA level.
- Gross margin held at about 48% in Q1.
- Debt raised "in the market"; "equity infusion is not on the table at all right now".

**Q1 FY27 delivery (Aug 2026):**
- Revenue was a record ₹520 cr (+28%).
- EBITDA margin was 1.8% reported / 3.4% adjusted, versus the implied 12–14%. Gross margin was 41.2% against the 48% promised. Both **missed**.
- FY27 margin guidance was **cut to 10–12%**, or 10–11% in Q&A, which is about ₹220–230 cr EBITDA.
- Vanillin guidance was **cut to about 3,000 t**, blamed on 4 campaign changeovers of about 1 month each.
- FY27 revenue guidance was kept.
- Management now says Q3 will return to double-digit margins, H2 will be 13–15%, and FY28 will be 12–14%.
- The remaining three quarters imply about ₹70 cr of EBITDA a quarter, against ₹9 cr in Q1.
- Equity was reopened: "we are looking at various options to raise that capital".

[Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

**Track record.** Margins were guided at 12–14% for two years running. They came in at 7% in FY26 and 1.8% in Q1 FY27. Vanillin volume guidance has been cut repeatedly. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]

## Capex and capacity

- **Capacity.** 50,000+ MTPA across 8 plants: Dahej and Tarapur in India, plus Brazil, Mexico, France and Belgium. Vanillin capacity is 6,000 TPA. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Recent spend.** FY26 investing outflow was ₹53 cr; CWIP was ₹33 cr and capital commitments ₹4.4 cr. [Screener; AR FY26 Note 44]
- **Growth plan.** No big capex is planned; growth is to come from better use of existing plants.
- **Vinpai.** Bought mainly through a **preferential share issue (share swap)** plus convertible bonds. The investment was ₹147.3 cr and the stake is 83.82% at Mar-26. The deal completed 30 Nov 2025. [AR FY26 Directors' report]
- **Clean Max Sundarban.** ₹4.9 cr invested in renewable power, now an associate. [AR FY26 Related-party note]
- **Asset turns are poor.** Sales/net fixed assets were about 1.9× in FY26 (₹1,723 cr / ₹887 cr). [Screener]
  - The vanillin plant (commissioned 2022–23) runs below 30%.
  - The diphenol plant is idle, which makes it a stranded asset until the Q3 decision.

## Cash, debt and working capital

**Debt.**
- Gross debt was about ₹670 cr at Mar-26 and about ₹640 cr at Jun-26, after IFC and EXIM repayments on the vanillin plant loans. [Q1 call]
- Consolidated short-term borrowings were ₹365.5 cr and long-term ₹302.6 cr. [AR FY26 Directors' report]
- Screener borrowings including leases: ₹720 cr. [Screener]
- Management plans a further ₹100–200 cr of credit lines "within 1–1.5 months" of August. [Q1 call]

**Working capital and cash flow.**
- Consolidated working-capital cycle is about 100 days by management's figure. [Q1 call]
- Screener FY26 cash conversion cycle is 78 days: inventory 199 days, debtors 79 days, payables 200 days. [Screener]
- **Payables of 200 days** reflect heavy supplier and dealer financing. Management admits this is buying time "through the cost of raw materials". [Q1 call; Screener]
- FY26 operating cash flow was ₹82 cr and free cash flow ₹20 cr. [Screener]
- Interest cost was ₹83 cr in FY26 (₹107 cr in FY25), and **interest now exceeds operating profit** on a trailing-twelve-month basis (₹73 cr vs ₹84 cr). [Screener]

**Rating.** India Ratings **downgraded** Camlin during FY26: bank loans to IND BBB-/Stable/IND A3, from BBB/A3+. [AR FY26 Directors' report]

**Loan covenants.** The annual report says the company will "seek any waivers, if required" on financial covenants covering net worth, liabilities and debt ratios. [AR FY26 Directors' report]

**Payouts.** No dividend.

## Moat evidence

**Integration is a real but weakened advantage.**
- Camlin is one of few integrated phenol→HQ→TBHQ/BHA→blends players.
- It supplies about 70% of Dahej output captively.
- With the diphenol plant shut, it now buys HQ from China, so the cost advantage is suspended.

[AR FY26 BRSR; Q1 call]

**Blends are the durable part.**
- 20% CAGR and 200+ formulations.
- US and Mexico run at about 14% EBITDA against a 17% target. Brazil and Vinpai are below target.

[Q1 call]

**Vanillin: trade defence helps but has not lifted prices.**
- The US and EU have anti-dumping duties on Chinese vanillin; management cites about 250%.
- A US tariff on Indian goods was cut to about 15.5% (5.5% base plus 10%). Camlin claimed a refund of about ₹9 cr of duty paid.
- Solvay caps prices at $13–18 to keep Chinese suppliers out. So Camlin is a price-taker, with a 5,000–6,000 t "import gap" to fill in the US and EU.

[Q4 call; Q1 call]

**China in straights.** Local Indian competition and Chinese HQ pricing have pushed straights sales lower. Camlin is moving that volume into its own blends. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags

**Liquidity and leverage.**
- Interest exceeds TTM operating profit.
- The company relies on supplier and dealer finance, has a rating downgrade to BBB-, and has covenant-waiver language in the annual report.
- It needs ₹100–200 cr more credit, and an equity raise has been reopened.

[Screener; AR FY26; Q1 call]

**Losses.** Continuing operations lost money in FY26. The FY26 profit is only a book gain from deconsolidating Europe (about ₹85 cr), mostly loans written off at CFS Europe. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

**Audit coverage.** The consolidated opinion is clean, with no key audit matters. But **14 foreign and 2 Indian subsidiaries** were audited by other auditors: ₹1,182.7 cr of assets and ₹1,168.7 cr of revenue (before eliminations), which is the majority of the group. [AR FY26 Consolidated auditor's report]

**Parent's exposure to subsidiaries.** On a standalone basis, this exposure is 34% of total assets (26% a year earlier) and is a key audit matter. [AR FY26 Standalone auditor's report]

**Contingent liabilities (consolidated).** [AR FY26 Note 44]
- Income tax ₹34.5 cr, up from ₹10.2 cr.
- NGT (National Green Tribunal) Tarapur environmental compensation ₹17.1 cr, stayed by the Supreme Court with 30% deposited.
- Excise and taxes ₹19.7 cr.
- GST/VAT about ₹3.6 cr.
- Bank guarantees ₹5.3 cr.

**Related parties and governance.** [AR FY26 Note 45; BSE filing 9-Sep-26]
- KMP pay was ₹10.7 cr in FY26 (₹9.6 cr in FY25) in a loss-making year.
- Sales to KMP-linked Hardware Renaissance Inc were ₹1.36 cr, with a receivable of ₹2.03 cr (more than a year's sales).
- Promoter holding is only 48.03%. It briefly rose to 49.09% in FY25, and the Vinpai share swap diluted it.
- A new independent director (Atul Pradhan, a turnaround specialist) was appointed in Sep 2026.

**Operational incidents.** A fire at a Brazil blending plant in Feb 2026 (insurance settled at a 20% haircut) and an incident at Dahej in May 2026. [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

**Aroma economics.** Aroma needs 70–80% utilisation to cover fixed costs. Utilisation has been below 30% for two years. [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]

## What changed between the two calls

- Tone moved from "consistent strategy, tariff windfall, 12–14%" to "survival mode": margins cut, vanillin volumes cut, and equity back on the table.
- New disclosures:
  - segment reporting;
  - vanillin fixed cost of about ₹7 cr a month;
  - dealer-finance cost of 1–1.5 points of margin;
  - the Brazil fire settlement at a 20% haircut.
- The diphenol shutdown moved from "planning to close" to "shut, decision by Q3".
- Unchanged: revenue guidance of ₹2,200–2,400 cr and blends growth of 20%+.

## My read

Camlin has a growing, decent blends franchise and record revenue. But it sits on a balance sheet that cannot carry an idle diphenol plant and a vanillin plant running below 30%.

Operating profit has halved and interest now exceeds it. The rating is BBB-, and management is openly seeking more credit and possibly equity. Guidance has missed badly two quarters in a row.

Even the cut FY27 target (about ₹220 cr EBITDA) needs about ₹70 cr a quarter from a ₹9 cr base.

I would only turn constructive if all three of these happen:
- Q2/Q3 margins actually reach double digits;
- the diphenol decision is made without new capex;
- funding is secured without heavy dilution.

A deeply dilutive raise or a covenant breach would make things worse.
