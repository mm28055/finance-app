# S H Kelkar & Co (SHK, "Keva") — deep research, Phase A1

## Transcript check

I compared both stored AI transcripts with the official BSE transcripts: the Q1 FY27 call of 29 Jul 2026 (filed 4 Aug) and the Q4 FY26 call of 18 May 2026. The stored copies contain **material errors**:

- **Net debt, Q1 FY27 (the most important error).** The stored copy has the CFO saying net debt rose ₹65 cr "to 370 crores". The official text says **₹852 crore** as of June 2026. The stored profile and analysis repeat the wrong ₹370 cr.
- **Promoter pledge, Q1 FY27.** A whole question from Ankur Agarwal is missing. The CEO said promoters have pledged "50 lakh shares, something like this. Rs. 30 crore is the value of the borrowing against the shares."
- **Plant names, Q1 FY27.** The stored copy calls the Q3 plant "Mahad". The official text says **Vanvate/Vanavate** (with Vashivali to follow).
- **ROCE versus ROIC, Q1 FY27.** The stored copy says "ROIC". The official text says **ROCE**: 15% target, 20% long-term, and 17–18% for Europe.
- **CFO name, Q4 FY26.** The stored copy names the CFO "Deeptish Agarwal". The official text has **Jagdish Agrawal**.
- **One-off sale, Q4 FY26.** The stored opening remarks say it was about ₹25 cr. The official text says **₹35 cr**, equal to about ₹50 cr of annual business.
- **Factories, Q4 FY26.** The stored copy mentions "Nalagarh" and "Alwar" factories. The official text refers to the Almere factory (Netherlands) and Vashivali; "Vanowrie" is Vanavate. The stored copy also says the Mulund factory closes "next year", which matches the official text.

## Business and segments

**What Keva sells.**
- Keva is India's largest Indian-origin fragrance and flavour (F&F) house.
- Fragrances are compounded blends for soaps, detergents, personal care and fine fragrance.
- Flavours go into dairy, bakery, beverages and pharma.
- Aroma "global ingredients" are specialty molecules.
- Brands are SHK, Cobra, Keva and CFF.
- It has 11 creation and development centres (CDCs) across India, Singapore, the Netherlands, Indonesia, Italy, Germany, the UK and the USA, 20 patent applications and 10 new molecules in four years.

[IP Q1FY27]

**FY26 segments.** [AR FY26 segment note]

|---|---|---|---|
| Fragrance | ₹2,126.9 cr | ₹152.6 cr | about 7% |
| Flavours | ₹241.4 cr | ₹57.7 cr | about 24% |

In FY25 fragrance was ₹1,937.2 cr and flavours ₹186.2 cr.

**Q1 FY27 by segment.** [IP Q1FY27]
- Fragrance revenue ₹539 cr (+9.0%). Fragrance EBITDA fell to ₹56 cr from ₹59 cr (−5.1%) because of CDC costs.

**Customers.**
- Over 4,100 customers. [CRISIL Sep-25]

- A large MNC account (Unilever) was won earlier but has shown "no large breakthroughs" since. [Q1 call]

**Capacity and utilisation.**
- Plants: Vashivali (rebuilt after the fire), Mulund (closing), Vapi, Vanavate (new), Almere (Netherlands, new) and Italy (CFF).
- European capacity was "approaching full capacity". Almere, running since May 2026, roughly doubles it and began with about €10m of annual sales moved from Alblasserdam.
- No group utilisation figure is given.

[Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Sep-25]

**Raw materials.**
- About 40% are directly crude-linked and 30% indirectly: solvents, aroma chemicals, citrus oils and phenol derivatives.
- Keva has shifted aroma-ingredient sourcing fully away from China ("moving completely away from the China supply chain").
- No anti-dumping duties are relevant.

[Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Moat evidence

**Switching costs.**
- Fragrance is a small share of product cost but defines the product.
- In Q1, 95%+ of clients accepted price increases. Keva held back supply to clients who resisted, which is part of why India fragrance was flat.
- Repeat, "annuity" business: products launched in the last 3–5 years are only about 10–11% of revenue.

[Q1 call]

**Scale in India.**
- Largest Indian-origin player.
- Its inventory buffers let it win share from smaller rivals during supply shocks ("we believe we gained market share in the Indian market specifically").

[Q4 call]

**Global position.**
- Keva is small against Givaudan, IFF, dsm-firmenich and Symrise.
- The antitrust probe into the global F&F majors is prompting some buyers to look at alternative suppliers, which may help Keva.

[Q4 call]

**Firmenich holds 9.80%.** Firmenich Aromatics Production (India) Pvt Ltd holds 9.80% of SHK (1.36 cr shares). That is a global major on the share register. [AR FY26 shareholders above 5%]

**Weak points.**
- Fragrance segment margin (about 7% EBIT) is thin.
- Only flavours (about 24% EBIT) shows clear pricing power.

[AR FY26 segment note]

## Risks and red flags

**Leverage and returns.** Net debt of ₹852 cr on EBITDA of about ₹240–350 cr, with ROCE at 6%. Deleveraging keeps slipping. [Q1 call; Screener]

**Promoter pledge.** About 50 lakh shares are pledged against about ₹30 cr of borrowing, per the CEO on the Q1 call (not in the stored transcript). [Q1 call official]

**Leadership changes.**
- Chairman and promoter **Ramesh Vaze died on 15 Sep 2026**.
- The group CFO changed: Rohit Saraogi left and Jagdish Agrawal came in.
- The statutory auditor changed to Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP for FY26–FY30.

[BSE filing 15-Sep-26; AR FY26 Directors' report]

**Promoter holdings.** Kedar Vaze's direct holding appears to have fallen from about 1.49 cr to 1.31 cr shares during FY26, but promoter-group holding only moved from 55.03% to 54.87%. This is probably an intra-group transfer, but I have not verified it. [AR FY26; Screener]

**Goodwill.** Goodwill of **₹337.5 cr** relates to Keva Europe, CFF (Italy) and Holland Aromatics. Its impairment test is a key audit matter, and it depends on European growth forecasts. The opinion is otherwise unmodified. [AR FY26 Auditor's report]

**Contingent liabilities.** ₹73.4 cr (₹98.8 cr in FY25), mostly income tax (₹59.7 cr) and excise/service tax (₹12.2 cr). [AR FY26 Note 39]

**Related parties and trust.**
- KMP short-term pay rose to ₹7.32 cr, from ₹2.88 cr.
- The employee benefit trust's closure is pending income-tax proceedings; the FY24 share sale booked a ₹21.95 cr loss.
- There is a ₹5.67 cr loan to associate NuTaste.

[AR FY26 related-party note]

**Restructuring.** Keva UK and Keva USA are being moved under Keva Fragrances Pvt Ltd, and SHK's 17% of CFF is being moved to Keva Italy. This is an intra-group reorganisation. [AR FY26 Directors' report]

## What changed between the two calls

- Tone moved from "no guidance, survival, exit ₹50 cr of bad business" to "double-digit growth, improved margins, normal quarter".
- Debt went the wrong way: net debt rose to ₹852 cr against the ₹800 cr gross guide, and the start of deleveraging moved to Q3.
- Flavours surprised strongly; global ingredients dropped 45%.
- New numbers: ETR target below 30%, Europe ROCE of 17–18%, CDC breakeven timelines, and the pledge disclosure.

## My read

The core India and flavours businesses are healthy, and Q1 showed real pricing power and 13% margins again. But the group is carrying heavy CDC and overseas costs, net debt of about ₹850 cr, a high tax rate and a ₹337 cr goodwill block. Reported profit is flattered by insurance receipts.

The price assumes margins recover to about 14% and debt falls. There is no track record yet of either, and ROCE is 6%.

The Chairman's death, the promoter pledge and Firmenich's 9.8% stake add uncertainty over ownership. What would change my view:
- **Positive:** two more quarters at 13%+ reported margins, with net debt actually falling by about ₹25 cr a quarter from Q3 and Germany breaking even.
- **Negative:** margins below 11%, or another inventory-driven rise in debt.
