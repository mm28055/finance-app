## Business and segments
- **What it does.** Rossari is a formulator and maker of specialty chemicals, with 4,250+ products across three verticals. [ICRA May-26; IP Q1FY27]
  - **HPPC (home, personal care and performance chemicals).** Ethoxylates and other surfactants (made by reacting ethylene oxide, EO, with fatty alcohols, phenols and amines), emulsifiers, agro-adjuvants, oil-and-gas chemicals, silicones, PEGs for pharma, and phenoxy personal-care preservatives. Rossari says it is "one of the largest globally" in phenoxy.
  - **TSC (textile specialty chemicals).** Enzymes, silicone softeners and pre-treatment and finishing auxiliaries.
  - **AHN (animal health and nutrition).** Feed enzymes, toxin binders, vitamin premixes and trace minerals.
  [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 revenue by vertical.** [IP Q1FY27]

  | Vertical | Q1 FY27 | Q1 FY26 | Growth | Share |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | HPPC | ₹553 cr | ₹431 cr | +28% | 79% |
  | TSC | ₹106 cr | ₹83 cr | +28% | 15% |
  | AHN | ₹38 cr | ₹30 cr | +27% | 6% |

  - FY26 mix: HPPC 77%, TSC 17%, AHN 6%.
  - Segment margins are not disclosed. AHN has the highest gross margin, and textiles are split between specialty and commodity products.
  [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- **Core B2B versus the loss-making consumer arm.** [IP Q1FY27]
  - The institutional and B2C businesses (Buzil Rossari and Rossari Consumer Products) had revenue of ₹71 cr in Q1 FY27, flat on the year, and EBITDA of −₹4 cr (−₹7 cr a year earlier).
  - Without them, the core B2B business had revenue of ₹626 cr (+32%) and EBITDA of ₹85 cr (14%, versus 16% a year ago).
- **Geography.** India ₹1,782.8 cr and outside India ₹613.6 cr in FY26 (26% exports, 80+ countries). Q1 FY27 exports were about ₹160 cr (+21% YoY, 23–24% of sales). Exports have grown at a 19% CAGR over FY23–26 (₹380 cr → ₹634 cr). [AR FY26 Note 42; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Customer concentration is low.** Top 10 customers are about 13% of sales, no customer is above 2%, and the accounts show no reliance on any single customer. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; AR FY26 Note 42]
- **Subsidiaries (FY26 revenue).** Unitop Chemicals (Dahej) had revenue of ₹922 cr, up from ₹554 cr in FY23; it makes non-ionic surfactants for agro and oil and gas. Tristar Intermediates (Sarigam) had revenue of ₹292 cr; it makes phenoxyethanol and other personal-care and aroma intermediates. Romakk (50.1%) makes silicones. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 Note 46]
- **Capacity.** Silvassa 120,000 MTPA. Rossari Dahej 152,500 MTPA (20,000 MTPA added in Sep 2025). Unitop Dahej 90,000 MTPA. Tristar 15,000 MTPA. Group total 387,100 MTPA including R&D. Ethoxylation capacity is 66,000 MTPA after 30,000 MTPA was added at Unitop Dahej in Q3–Q4 FY26. Thailand blending (Unistar Thai) adds 5,000 MTPA. [ICRA May-26; IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- **Utilisation.** Ethoxylation runs at "practically 90–100%" of the EO it can get, and EO, not demand, is the constraint. The new 15,000 t Unitop line ran at only 10–15% in Q3 FY26. Management expects its continuous MDEA ethoxylation plant to ramp up over the next 12 months. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Recent spend.** Investing cash outflow was ₹183 cr in FY25 and ₹220 cr in FY26. CWIP was still ₹173 cr at Mar-26 (₹140 cr at Mar-25), even though management says the capacities have been commissioned. [Screener]
- **Capital commitments** stood at ₹22.0 cr at Mar-26 (₹13.2 cr a year earlier). [AR FY26 Note 44]
- **FY27 capex** is capped at ₹50–75 cr, for pharma PEGs and aroma chemicals. The ₹10–15 cr Thailand plant is done. KSA spending so far is only pre-operative. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Asset turns.** Management targets asset turnover of 3–4× at peak utilisation. On the current base an analyst estimated ₹3,500 cr of potential sales; management did not dispute this. FY26 sales/net fixed assets was about 3.1× (₹2,396 cr / ₹784 cr). [Q4 call; Screener]
- **Why capex is on hold.** The Reliance EO expansion slipped, so Rossari slowed its capex. It is now "sweating" plants by shifting to non-EO chemistry (esters, NMMO, MDEA, NMM/NFM, spray-cooled powders, trace minerals). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Borrowings rose sharply.** Total borrowings went from ₹218 cr (Mar-25) to ₹436 cr (Mar-26): term loans ₹94 cr non-current plus ₹29 cr current maturities, and working-capital loans ₹284 cr at 5.3–7.7%. Total debt/OPBITDA rose from 0.8× to 1.5×, and interest cover fell from 14.9× to 10.0×. [AR FY26 Notes 21, 25; ICRA May-26]
- **Net debt.** ₹280 cr at Mar-26 and ₹248 cr at Jun-26. Net debt/equity rose from 0.06 to 0.21. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **A further ₹88.5 cr liability.** This is "deferred consideration" for the last 20% of Unitop, shown as a current financial liability. [AR FY26 Note on other financial liabilities]
- **Working capital (FY26).** Debtor days 85, inventory days 88, payable days 71, cash conversion cycle 103 days, compared with about 46–80 days before FY24. [Screener]
- **Cash conversion is weak.** CFO/EBITDA was 47% in FY26 (CFO ₹65 cr) and 74% in FY25. Free cash flow has been negative for three straight years: FY24 −₹83 cr, FY25 −₹21 cr, FY26 −₹175 cr. [Screener]
- **Payouts.** Dividend payout is about 2%. There is no buyback: "nothing like that on the cards". [Screener; Q4 call]
- **Credit rating.** ICRA AA- (Positive) / A1+ on ₹333 cr of bank lines. In a "material event" update on 7 May 2026, ICRA flagged the doubling of debt and said it would reassess once there is clarity. [ICRA May-26]

## Moat evidence
- **Breadth and stickiness, not scale.** Rossari has 1,750+ HPPC customers, 1,000+ textile customers and 125+ AHN customers, 100+ new HPPC products in three years, and its top 10 customers are only 13% of sales. This is a formulation-and-service moat. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- **Pass-through works.** The MD says passing on raw-material increases is "no worry at all", though textile customers resist. Freight and timing hurt margins. The steady fall in margin from 14% to 11.6% suggests pricing power is only partial. [Q1 call; Q4 call; Screener]
- **Weak on inputs.** EO comes from a single domestic supplier (Reliance), and Rossari "does not have the opportunity to negotiate". Rivals such as India Glycols are integrated, and SABIC/Petronas-type players are feedstock-integrated. That is why Rossari is pursuing KSA. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **China.** China is both a competitor (it supplies imports in HPPC) and a supplier (phenol, and blends for Thailand). No anti-dumping protection is relevant to Rossari's products. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Named competitors.** In textiles: Archroma (which owns the Huntsman and BASF portfolios), CHT, Pulcra and Croda. In AHN: Kemin, Cargill, Novozymes, Zoetis and Jubilant. In HPPC: BASF, Dow, Lubrizol and Evonik. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Unitop put-option dispute.** The sellers of Unitop invoked a put option for the remaining 20%. Rossari contested it, saying the sellers breached the share-purchase agreement. On 21 Jan 2026 the arbitral tribunal dismissed the sellers' claim. Rossari still carries a **₹88.5 cr** current liability for the tranche, and there is residual challenge risk. Unitop is the largest subsidiary, with FY26 PAT of ₹37.3 cr. [AR FY26 Note on deferred consideration, Note 46]
- **The consumer arm is losing more money.** FY26 losses were ₹12.7 cr at Buzil Rossari (₹5.6 cr in FY25) and ₹9.3 cr at Rossari Consumer Products (₹6.4 cr). Both now have negative net worth: −₹3.1 cr and −₹15.6 cr. A ₹24 cr intra-group exposure from Rossari to Buzil is listed in the annual report's loans/guarantees table; I did not verify whether it is a loan or a guarantee. Other small losses: Rossari Global FZCO −₹2.9 cr and Unistar Thai −₹1.0 cr. [AR FY26 Note 46; AOC/loan table]
- **Related parties.** Sons and a daughter of the promoters hold "office of profit" roles: Mikhail Menezes was paid ₹0.99 cr, Yash Chari ₹0.66 cr, and Yashika Chari AED 0.28m at the Dubai subsidiary. The amounts are modest but the practice is growing. [AR FY26 AOC-2]
- **Auditor.** Walker Chandiok & Co gave a clean opinion. The only key audit matter was revenue recognition (rebates and cut-off). There is a CARO/Rule 11(g) exception: the audit trail at database level for the accounting software's service provider could not be verified. No modified opinion. [AR FY26 Auditor's report]
- **Contingent liabilities.** None were disclosed as material in the consolidated notes; only capital commitments of ₹22 cr and Unitop retention pay of ₹1.6 cr.
- **Earnings quality.** Q4 FY26 profit was inflated by the office sale. Interest capitalised earlier is now flowing through the P&L at ₹9–10 cr a quarter. Profits are growing much more slowly than revenue.
- **Shareholders.** DII holding fell from 18.2% to 14.6% in Jun-26 while retail rose. Promoter holding is 68.1%, with no pledge shown. [Screener]
- **KSA.** A new greenfield in Saudi Arabia is still at the pre-operative stage and carries execution risk.

## My read
Rossari's revenue engine is working. Sales grew 28% with 10% volume growth, customer spread is wide and exports are rising. But profit is not following: FY24 to TTM PAT is about flat per rupee of sales, margins have slid from 14% to 11.6%, debt doubled and free cash flow has been negative for three years. The stock at around ₹444 prices in only modest growth. The upside case needs three things: Reliance's EO to arrive, the consumer arm to be sold or closed (worth about 2–3 points of margin), and cash flow to turn positive enough to cut net debt. I would turn more positive on a margin back above 13% with positive free cash flow. I would turn negative if the Unitop award is challenged successfully, if KSA capex starts before debt falls, or if margins drop below 11%.
