# Refex Industries (REFEX) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: both stored transcripts were compared with the official BSE filings (Q1 FY27 call, 30 Jul 2026, filed 4 Aug; Q4 FY26 call, filed Jun 2026). The stored Q1 copy has figure errors, and the stored Q4 copy is **truncated** — it stops before roughly the last third of the call, which covered the IT search, the promoter pledge, the wind margin (~8%), receivable days and the cancelled promoter warrants. Details under **Stored-data errors**.

**Classification note:** Refex is no longer a chemicals company. It started in 2002 refilling and selling refrigerant gases (HFC/HCFC gases such as R-22, R-134a, filled from imported bulk). That business was stopped in January 2026 and power trading in August 2025. What remains is coal & fly-ash logistics for thermal power plants (~88% of FY26 revenue) and wind-turbine manufacturing. So chemistry, feedstock, China dumping and anti-dumping topics mostly do not apply; I note the relevant substitutes (diesel cost, imported blades) instead. [AR FY26 note 47; IP Q1FY27]

## Business and segments
- **Ash & coal handling (core):** Refex collects fly ash and pond ash from power-plant silos and dykes and trucks it to cement plants, brick makers, road projects and mine filling. It also brings coal in on the return trip ("reverse logistics"). It is paid per tonne-km: **₹450–1,200 per tonne**, depending on distance. It works at 42 thermal plants (30–35% of Indian thermal capacity) with a fleet of 2,000+ owned/leased trucks. Volume is **65,000–70,000 t/day** (Q1 FY27). Regulation drives demand: MoEF&CC rules require 100% ash use, with penalties of ₹1,000/t, and require legacy pond ash to be cleared over 10 years. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Wind (Venwind Refex Power, ~77% owned):** assembles 5.3 MW turbines using licensed German (Vensys) technology at a leased 1 GW Silvassa plant (~₹5,000–6,000 cr revenue at full capacity per management). It is a supply-only business (no EPC): ~95% is paid on delivery and ~5% held until testing. It received RLMM approval in FY26, and India's first 5.3 MW turbine was erected at Torrent's Koppal site in Q1 FY27. Blades are still imported. [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Discontinued / demerging:** refrigerant gas (closed Jan 2026), power trading (exited Aug 2025), and green mobility (Refex Green Mobility, EV fleet, ~₹100 cr revenue), which is being demerged into Refex Mobility Ltd. The shareholder meeting was 5 Aug 2026; completion is expected by Q3 FY27. [AR FY26 note 47; IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **FY26 segment revenue (consolidated note 42):** ash & coal handling ₹2,012.7 cr (FY25 ₹2,235.6 cr, −10%, as coal trading was cut), wind ₹237.5 cr (nil), service ₹10.4 cr, solar ₹0.8 cr. On my reading of the table, ash segment EBIT was ~₹354 cr (FY25 ~₹218 cr). [AR FY26 note 42]
- **Customers:** NTPC **28%** of revenue and IL&FS Tamil Nadu Power **12%**. Others include state GENCOs (MP, TN, AP/APGENCO 3-year contract), DVC and Adani Power. Everything is domestic, with no export plans. [AR FY26 note 42; IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Order book (30 Jun 2026):** ash & coal ₹1,635 cr (₹279 cr of new orders in Q1). Wind: ₹1,860 cr in hand, of which ₹525 cr has been executed, leaving ~₹1,300 cr for FY27. Acuité puts total unexecuted orders at ₹2,518 cr. The wind book includes a 148 MW order from Jindal Steel & Power (announced Dec 2025, due for delivery by Sep 2026). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; Acuité Aug-26; News: Mercom, 19 Dec 2025]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly consolidated (Screener, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 352 / op. profit 37 (10%) / PAT 20; Sep-25 415 / 71 (17%) / 36; Dec-25 576 / 90 (16%) / 53; Mar-26 934 / 160 (17%) / 94; **Jun-26 916 / 107 (12%) / 65**. [Screener]
- Standalone continuing operations, company-reported (mostly ash): Q1 FY27 revenue ₹619.3 cr (+76%), EBITDA ₹105.4 cr (17.0%), PAT ₹73.6 cr (+123%); Q4 FY26 ₹701 cr / ₹141 cr (20.1%) / ₹94 cr. Q1 included a one-off ₹4 cr bank processing charge for moving loans to Indian Overseas Bank. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Wind:** Q4 FY26 revenue ~₹233–238 cr at ~8% EBITDA margin; Q1 FY27 revenue ₹295 cr at roughly break-even (an analyst cited a ₹0.34 cr EBITDA loss, and management did not dispute it). The consolidated Q1 margin fell to 12% because of this. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- Annual consolidated (Screener): sales FY24 ₹1,383 cr → FY25 ₹2,259 cr → FY26 ₹2,277 cr; OPM 11% → 9% → 16%; PAT ₹93 cr → ₹158 cr → ₹204 cr. TTM sales ₹2,841 cr, PAT ₹248 cr. ROCE 22.7%, ROE 17.8%. [Screener]
- FY26 standalone continuing: revenue ₹2,039 cr (−10%), EBITDA ₹350 cr (+68%, 17.2%), PAT ₹247 cr. Management says like-for-like ash growth was 28% once trading is excluded. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Cost side: diesel is the main variable cost. Contracts recover only **40–60%** of diesel price increases, and diesel supply was short at several sites in Q1 FY27. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (May/Jun 2026):** ash growth in FY27 similar to FY26 (~28%); ash EBITDA 15–18% ("20% is sustainable" when pressed); ash volume 90,000–95,000 t/day "in the current financial year"; wind to execute the whole ~₹1,500 cr open order book in FY27 at ~8% EBITDA; pledge "largely" released over 6 months; demerger done in ~90 days (by ~August). [Q4 call]
- **Q1 delivery:** standalone revenue +76% (ahead); ash margin 17% (in range); wind ₹295 cr but ~0% margin (**missed** vs ~8%); demerger slipped to Q3 FY27 (**delayed**); pledge rose rather than fell (**missed** — see risks). [Q1 call; News: Whalesbook, Sep 2026]
- **Q1 call (Jul 2026):** wind FY27 execution "close to ₹1,700 cr" at a **5–6% net (PAT) margin**, mostly in H2. Ash volume guidance was given three different ways on the same call: "close to 90,000" by Q4, "crossing 75,000–80,000 in Q4", and "yes… 90,000–95,000 in Q4". Wind EBITDA to reach 18–20% in about two years via 85% localisation within 12 months. [Q1 call]
- Track record: revenue growth has come from repeatedly re-shaping the business (refrigerants → power trading → ash → wind). Earlier guidance mixed up EBITDA and PAT margins (management had to clarify on both calls). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Ash is asset-light: trucks are leased or come from partners, "no capacity constraint… just multiplying fleet and personnel". The new rail-cum-road model uses a railway freight discount to move ash to the Northeast. [Q1 call]
- Wind: Silvassa is leased, with only ₹3–4 cr spent on repairs; capacity is 1 GW; blade manufacturing is planned in-house and 60+ vendors are being localised. Capital needs will rise with localisation, to be funded by bank loans or the holding company. [Q1 call]
- Reported fixed assets are only ₹212 cr (Mar-26) against ₹2,277 cr sales. The real capital is working capital, not plant (next section). [Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Working capital is the central issue.** Consolidated trade receivables ₹886 cr at Mar-26 (₹677 cr at Mar-25). Contract assets ₹739 cr (₹176 cr), mostly **unbilled ash revenue of ₹696 cr (₹151 cr)** — work done but not yet certified by the power plant. For the ash segment, receivables plus contract assets total ₹1,413 cr, **58% of total assets** (auditor's key audit matter). That is roughly eight months of ash revenue. [AR FY26 notes 13, 12; AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Acuité: gross current assets **374 days** in FY26 vs 212 days in FY25; debtor days 142 vs 109; bank working-capital limits **~86% used** over six months to Jun-26. Downgrade trigger: GCA above 400 days. [Acuité Aug-26]
- Cash flow (Screener): CFO −₹248 cr in FY25, +₹107 cr in FY26 (35% of operating profit); FCF +₹47 cr in FY26. The business consumed cash while profits rose. [Screener]
- Debt: borrowings ₹225 cr (Mar-26), gearing 0.15x, debt/EBITDA 0.58x, cash and bank ₹184 cr (much of it margin money for bank guarantees and LCs). Management calls debt "effectively net-zero". [Screener; Acuité Aug-26; Q1 call]
- Guarantees: ₹509 cr of corporate guarantees for subsidiaries at Mar-26 (₹46 cr a year earlier; ₹463 cr given in FY26, mostly for the wind business LCs), plus ₹37.5 cr of other corporate guarantees. [AR FY26 CARO; AR FY26 note 46]
- **Unused preferential-issue money** of ₹45 cr is parked as an inter-corporate deposit with Rudra Securities & Capital Ltd at 11%. [AR FY26 note 12]
- Dividend ₹1/share. The promoter chose not to subscribe to previously issued warrants, so they were cancelled; management says no fresh equity is needed. [AR FY26 auditor's report; Q4 call]
- Rating: Acuité upgraded to A/Stable/A1 from A-/A2+ on 7 Aug 2026 (₹660 cr rated). [Acuité Aug-26]

## Moat evidence
- Scale: management says Refex is the largest organised ash handler and the next player is about 25% of its size (their estimate; others are unlisted and serve 1–5 plants). Market share is only ~3% of a ₹75,000 cr TAM (fly ash ~₹8,500 cr/yr at ₹250/t plus a pond-ash stock worth ~₹67,000 cr at ₹400/t). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Barriers: bank-guarantee capacity, a multi-state operating network, in-house trip-tracking software (OCR, GPS) and a new integrated O&M + evacuation model with multi-year tie-ups. Management admits "a lot of the players can have execution capability". [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Pricing power is weak: only 40–60% of diesel cost passes through, and customers are state utilities (NTPC 28%) that pay slowly. [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 42]
- Wind: no moat yet. It runs on licensed technology, competes with Suzlon, Inox, Envision and Siemens Gamesa, and its margins are currently below the 18–20% EBITDA that established turbine makers earn (analyst's figure). [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter pledge rising, not falling:** 41% of promoter shares at the Q4 call; management promised a large release within six months. By 23 Sep 2026 encumbrance was **47.28% of promoter holding (26.75% of the company)**. New pledges in Sep 2026 went to little-known lenders (Frazer Goods & Supply, Infosoft Global) and earlier ones to Northern Arc Capital; the stated purpose was the promoters' "personal use". Screener shows 43.5%. [Q4 call; Q1 call; News: Whalesbook, 23 Sep 2026; Screener]
- **Income Tax search** on premises and executives' homes, 9–13 Dec 2025; the final order is still awaited. [AR FY26 Directors' report]
- **SEBI penalty** of ₹10 lakh on CMD Anil Jain (insider trading in 2023, order dated 12 Dec 2025); recovery is stayed by SAT (13 Feb 2026). [AR FY26 Directors' report]
- **Revenue quality:** ₹696 cr of unbilled revenue is estimated from the company's own daily progress reports. The auditor notes the billing unit of measure differs from the DPR unit, and the unbilled balance quadrupled in a year. An ICD from issue proceeds sits with an outside NBFC. [AR FY26 auditor's KAM; AR FY26 note 12]
- **Customer concentration:** NTPC 28%, IL&FS Tamil Nadu Power 12% (an IL&FS group entity). [AR FY26 note 42]
- Auditor: A B C D & Co. LLP, Chennai (a small firm), appointed 2022–27. The opinion is unmodified, with an emphasis of matter on the discontinued segments and the demerger, and key audit matters on receivables/contract assets and contingent liabilities. The audit trail operated. [AR FY26]
- Wind execution: first-year margins near zero, blades imported, a long localisation path; guarantees of ₹509 cr back the subsidiary.
- Coal dependence: ESG-screened money avoids the stock; FIIs cut holdings from 2.03% to 0.93% over Dec-25 to Jun-26. [Screener]

- Wind margin went from "~8% EBITDA" (Q4) to "5–6% net margin in H2" (Q1), with Q1 near zero.

## My read
The ash business is real and growing fast (standalone PAT +123% in Q1) on a regulation-driven market, and the valuation is undemanding at about 14 times trailing earnings — the price needs only ~8% annual profit growth. But the quality of those earnings is the question. Receivables plus unbilled work now equal about eight months of ash revenue, operating cash flow is a third of operating profit, the promoter keeps pledging more shares for "personal use" despite promising the opposite, and there is an unresolved IT search. Wind adds revenue but little profit so far. I would want to see unbilled revenue convert to cash (GCA days falling toward 200) and the pledge actually shrinking before trusting the numbers; a cash-flow miss or an adverse IT order would break the case.
