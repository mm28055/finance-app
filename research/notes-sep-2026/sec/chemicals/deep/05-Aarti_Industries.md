Research date: 27 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore, consolidated, from Screener unless tagged otherwise.

## Business and segments
- **What it makes.** India's largest benzene-derivatives maker. Benzene is chlorinated and nitrated into **nitrochlorobenzenes (NCB)** and **dichlorobenzenes (DCB)**, then hydrogenated, aminated, etc. into intermediates for agrochemicals, pharma (e.g. paracetamol chain), dyes/pigments and polymers (PDCB for PPS engineering plastic used in EVs, phenylenediamines). A toluene chain (nitrotoluenes, NT) feeds agro and pharma; an ethylation chain (MEA, DEA, and now PEDA) feeds agrochemicals. The fast-growing **Energy** business sells **mono-methyl aniline (MMA)** and other octane-boosting fuel additives to refiners and gasoline blenders. [AR FY26 MD&A; AGM presentation Sep-26; Q4 call]
- **End-use mix FY26:** Energy 43% (36% in FY25; long-term expected 30–40%), Agrochemicals 18%, Polymers & additives ~14%, Dyes/pigments/inks ~11%, Pharma 10%, others the rest. Energy volumes drove FY26 — CRISIL notes volume +39% but realisation −25%. [AGM presentation Sep-26; AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Sep-26]
- **Geography:** exports 59% of revenue (domestic 41%) in FY26; 57% of Q4 and 59% of Q1 revenue. Middle East was ~9–10% of FY26 revenue (mostly Energy); it fell from ~15% to 2% of Q1 FY27 revenue after the West Asia war closed shipping; volumes were redirected to the US, Europe and Africa. [CRISIL Sep-26; Q4 call; Q1 call official]
- **Customers:** no single external customer is 10%+ of revenue; ~20% of standalone revenue (₹1,650 cr) goes through its own overseas subsidiaries (UAE, UK, USA). Long-term contracts: an advanced polymer-intermediate contract; an MMA volume contract (met targets in CY25); a nitric-acid purchase contract (with Deepak Fertilisers); an agro-intermediate contract (margins below plan); plus two new ones in March 2026 — a 15-year exclusive supply contract amended to add backward integration (₹200–250 cr capex, commissioning Sep–Oct 2027) and a US$150 m supply deal with a global agro major to March 2030 (no new capex). A Bayer-linked asset is ~50% used for a Dicamba intermediate. [AR FY26 note 27; Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Sep-26]
- **Capacity and utilisation (FY26 volumes):** NCB 92.7 kt on 108 kt; DCB 96.5 kt on 120 kt (debottlenecking to 140 kt); MMA/fuel additives 237.6 kt (+93%) on 290 kt, expanded to 360 kt in Q1 FY27; PDA utilisation under pressure from US tariffs and Chinese competition. [AGM presentation Sep-26; Q1 call]
- **Raw materials:** benzene, toluene, aniline (partly sourced from China), methanol, sulphur, ethylene. Prices of benzene, sulphur, aniline, toluene and methanol rose over 60% in weeks after the West Asia war began; domestic purchases carry 7–15 days of inventory, imports 1–1.5 months. [Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Sep-26]
- **China:** China's suspension of export-tax rebates ("anti-involution") has lifted NCB-chain pricing; agro pricing still pressured by Chinese supply; MPDA and PDA hurt by Chinese competition; tighter Chinese nitration safety rules seen as a slow tailwind. Company is setting up a China subsidiary for sourcing and sales. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (Screener net sales):** Q4 FY25 revenue 1,949 / EBITDA 262 (13%) / PAT 96; Q1 FY26 1,675 / 212 (13%) / 43; Q2 FY26 2,100 / 291 (14%) / 106; Q3 FY26 2,318 / 321 (14%) / 133; Q4 FY26 2,205 / 341 (15%) / 137; Q1 FY27 2,387 / 382 (16%) / 155. [Screener]
- **Annual:** revenue FY24 6,371 → FY25 7,269 → FY26 8,286; EBITDA 978 → 997 → 1,168; margin 15% → 14% → 14%; PAT 416 → 331 → 419. Peak was FY22 (EBITDA 1,720, 28% margin, PAT 1,186). TTM revenue 9,010, PAT 531. [Screener]
- **Plain words:** volumes are up sharply (MMA almost doubled) but prices fell, so EBITDA barely moved for three years; interest (₹340 cr) and depreciation (₹474 cr) now eat most of it. FY26 tax was negative (−15%) thanks to old tax cases won and deferred-tax items; PAT is flattered. [Screener; Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 quality:** EBITDA included ~₹50–60 cr of forex (₹47 cr) and inventory gains from buying raw materials before prices spiked; management "did not say that the EBITDA run rate can be maintained". Volumes fell 12% QoQ (Energy −17%, non-energy −7%). [Q1 call official]
- **Returns:** ROCE 6.9%, ROE 7.1%. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **FY28 EBITDA ₹1,800 cr** (target set earlier; built from FY25's ₹1,016 cr plus ₹150–200 cr cost savings, ₹350–550 cr volume/margin ramp-up, ₹300–450 cr from new capex). Q4 — "not changing the EBITDA potential", but Zone IV delay "might have an impact… in the given time frame". Q1 — slower ramp than "our target aspirations for FY27, '28"; ₹1,800 cr includes Augene JV profit. **Kept on paper, timing slipping.** TTM EBITDA is ₹1,335 cr. [AGM presentation Sep-26; Q4 call; Q1 call official]
- **Zone IV (Jhagadia, ~₹1,800 cr):** Q4 — 3–4 months late (contract labour down 35%, LPG shortage, election migration), all blocks commissioned in FY27, first revenue from Q2. Q1 — delay now "3–6 months"; MPP first product in August; 97% of equipment erected, 85% of piping done; 5–10 products this year, 25–30 by FY28. **Cut/delayed.** [Q4 call; Q1 call official]
- **Capex:** FY26 ₹1,125 cr (CRISIL: ₹1,179 cr) as guided; FY27 ₹700–800 cr (₹180 cr spent in Q1); FY28 onwards ₹200–250 cr a year (CRISIL). **Kept.** [Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Sep-26]
- **Debt:** Q4 — net debt ~₹4,300 cr (3.6x EBITDA), expected to fall in FY27. Q1 — debt and finance costs rose with working capital. **Not yet delivered.** [Q4 call; Q1 call official]
- **JVs:** Augene (50:50 with Superform, amine derivatives for coatings and agro) — Q4: commission June–July; Q1: "this month or next", steady-state revenue ₹300–400 cr, reported at PAT level only. Re Aarti (plastic-recycling) — commission in FY27, not meaningful near term. **Slight slip.** [Q4 call; Q1 call official]
- **Working capital target:** 55–60 days. **Missed for now** (exports on long voyages, high raw-material prices). [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Spend history:** CFI −₹1,369 cr (FY24), −₹1,393 cr (FY25), −₹1,142 cr (FY26); CWIP ₹2,187 cr at Mar-26 (mostly Zone IV). Gross block expected ₹9,500–10,000 cr by end-FY27. [Screener; Q4 call]
- **Asset turns:** FY26 revenue ₹8,286 cr on net fixed assets ₹6,399 cr plus CWIP ₹2,187 cr — about 1.0x; ROCE under 7% shows the capex has not yet paid. [Screener]
- **Pipeline:** DCB to 140 kt; PEDA (4,000 t/yr, ethylation downstream) under trials; MMA 360 kt to be filled over ~12 months; backward-integration project (₹200–250 cr) by Sep–Oct 2027. [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Funding:** internal accruals and debt; CRISIL expects net cash accruals of ₹900–1,300 cr a year in FY27–FY29. [CRISIL Sep-26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Debt:** gross ₹3,790 cr (Mar-25) → ₹4,921 cr (Mar-26); net ₹3,495 cr → ₹4,313 cr; net debt/EBITDA 3.65x; gearing 0.86x; interest cover 3.5x; current ratio 0.83x. [CRISIL Sep-26; Screener]
- **Rating:** CRISIL AA/**Negative** (reaffirmed 11 Sep 2026) — outlook negative because geopolitics and working capital "could temper cash generation and delay expected deleveraging". Downgrade trigger: gross debt/EBITDA staying above 3.6x or margins below 12–13%. Liquidity: unencumbered surplus ₹310 cr (June 2026), bank lines 75% used, repayments ₹248–350 cr a year. [CRISIL Sep-26]
- **Cash flow:** CFO ₹1,210 cr (FY24), ₹1,238 cr (FY25), ₹781 cr (FY26); free cash flow −₹96 cr, −₹137 cr, −₹340 cr. [Screener]
- **Working capital:** inventory ₹1,732 cr (₹1,454 cr); debtor days 62 (39); payable days 137 (107); supplier-finance (reverse factoring) liabilities ₹246 cr (₹207 cr); letters of credit, bank guarantees and bills discounted ₹493 cr (₹235 cr). High payables and bill discounting are propping up the cycle. [Screener; AR FY26 notes 38, 42; auditor KAM]
- **Dividend:** 20% (₹1/share) approved at the Sep-2026 AGM; payout ~9%. [AGM presentation/news; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- **Scale and share:** CRISIL cites a "dominant market position" in NCB, DCB, nitrotoluene and aniline-based chemicals with 25–40% global share. [CRISIL Sep-26]
- **Repeat long-term contracts:** a global major came back with a 15-year contract amendment plus backward integration — "significant validation". [Q4 call]
- **Cost:** "top decile" cost claim in fuel additives; continuous nitration (safer than batch); ₹100 cr of cost savings concluded in FY26. [Q1 call; AGM presentation Sep-26]
- **Limits:** MMA already has 2–3 Indian and 2–3 Chinese competitors; PDCB is "structurally weak because of our technological disadvantage"; agro margins depend on Chinese behaviour. [Q1 call official]

## Risks and red flags
- **Leverage with negative outlook:** 3.65x net debt/EBITDA and rising working capital; FCF negative four years running. [CRISIL Sep-26; Screener]
- **Earnings quality:** Q1 helped by ₹50–60 cr forex/inventory gains; FY26 PAT helped by negative tax and ₹28.5 cr interest on tax refunds (exceptional); ₹39 cr forex revaluation loss on a US$87 m unhedged loan in Q4 interest cost. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26 note 35]
- **Auditor key audit matters:** revenue recognition (variable consideration, contract modifications and whether contract fulfilment costs are capitalised) and valuation/existence of ₹1,732 cr of inventory across many sites. Opinion unmodified. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Energy volatility:** MMA economics depend on gasoline–naphtha cracks ($15–18/bbl in Q1; negative on some days in Q4), seasonal winter dip, Middle East access. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Disclosure step back:** absolute volume figures removed from presentations because they "were being utilized in a situation that was not favorable for the company". [Q1 call official]
- **Related parties:** ₹12 cr of CSR donated to promoter-linked foundations (Aarti Foundation etc.); ₹6.7 cr land advance to a stressed NBFC written off. Contingent claims ₹110 cr (tax ₹52 cr, excise/customs ₹38 cr, GST ₹19 cr). [AR FY26 notes 34, 35, 38]
- **Promoter holding** drifting down (42.2% → 41.8% over a year); no pledge shown. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone went from defending against a 60% raw-material shock to reporting record gross income helped by inventory gains. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Zone IV delay widened from 3–4 to 3–6 months; Augene slipped a month or two. [Q4 call; Q1 call official]
- Middle East revenue share collapsed to 2%; exports redirected to longer voyages, pushing up working capital and debt. [Q1 call official]
- MMA 360 kt completed; "fuel additives" basket (3–5 new products) introduced; volume disclosure dropped. [Q1 call official]
- New China subsidiary planned. [Q1 call official]

## My read
Volumes are clearly growing (MMA nearly doubled, NCB/DCB near full), but the business has not yet turned that into returns: ROCE is under 7%, free cash flow has been negative for four years and net debt is 3.65x EBITDA with CRISIL on negative outlook. Q1's jump flattered by forex and inventory gains, and Zone IV — the main lever for the ₹1,800 cr FY28 EBITDA goal — is now 3–6 months late. At about 34x trailing earnings the price needs EBITDA to rise ~35% from TTM within two years and debt to start falling this year. What would change my view: Zone IV and Augene ramping on the revised schedule with net debt falling below ₹4,000 cr (positive); a CRISIL downgrade, an MMA crack collapse, or another quarter of rising debt (negative).
