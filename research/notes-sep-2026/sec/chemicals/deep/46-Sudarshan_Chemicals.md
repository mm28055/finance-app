## Business and segments
- World's No. 2 pigment maker (behind DIC/Sun) after buying the insolvent Heubach Group (which had absorbed Clariant's pigments) on 3 Mar 2025 for ₹1,277 cr (€139.6 mn final). 19 plants in 11 countries, ~1,600 products, 4,000+ customers in 100+ countries; 55–60% of assets in Asia vs under 1% for Western rivals. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 45; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Segments FY26: Pigments ₹9,525 cr revenue, EBITDA ₹582 cr (6.1%); Others (RIECO grinding/engineering equipment) ₹263 cr, EBITDA ₹9 cr. [AR FY26 note 48]
- Q1 FY27 split (gross, before inter-group eliminations): Legacy Sudarshan ₹768 cr (pigments ₹730 cr, +26% YoY), Acquired Group ₹1,973 cr (+4.9%), RIECO ₹38 cr (−25%). [IP Q1FY27]
- End markets: coatings (strongest for acquired group; US/EU decorative and auto "subdued"), plastics (customers buying hand-to-mouth after polymer price spike), inks (volume declining structurally; specialty packaging growing), special applications (agro, digital inks growing). [Q1 call]
- Capacity: management says no volume capex is needed to reach FY29 targets; utilisation not disclosed. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Portfolio pruning: VP4 Frankfurt GmbH (diketene/CKP plant, 90–95% of output sold to Nutrinova; FY26 revenue €15.7 mn, EBITDA €4.1 mn) is being sold to Celanese for **€76.5 mn** (≈₹840 cr per CRISIL), closing in ~2–3 months; proceeds to prepay acquisition debt. [IP Sep-26; CRISIL Sep-26]

Reported EBITDA also excludes forex on redeemable preference shares (₹25 cr gain Q4, ₹7 cr loss Q1). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- **At acquisition (Oct 2024):** acquired group EBITDA €90–100 mn within 3–4 years. Repeated on the Q4 call. [Q4 call]
- **Q3 FY26 promises vs Q4 delivery:** acquired-group Q4 EBITDA €9–10 mn promised → €11 mn business EBITDA delivered; inventory cut €20 mn promised → €29 mn delivered. **Beat.** [Q4 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (May 2026):** FY27 acquired group ~€700 mn sales, ~€35 mn EBITDA; legacy growth 8–10%; further €15–20 mn inventory reduction; RIECO turnaround "not over". Consolidated plan (IP): FY27 revenue ₹9,800–10,200 cr, EBITDA ~₹800 cr, net debt ~₹500 cr; FY29 revenue ₹12,000–14,000 cr, EBITDA ₹1,400–1,500 cr, net cash. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** acquired-group business EBITDA ₹128 cr in one quarter — annualised ~₹510 cr vs €35 mn (≈₹385 cr) guide; legacy pigment revenue +26% vs 8–10% guide; net debt ₹531 cr, nearly at the FY27 target in Q1. **Beat.** Missed: RIECO (revenue −25%, EBITDA −₹10 cr); inventory went up in subsidiaries (longer shipping), not down. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Q1 call:** guidance **kept, not raised** ("wait and watch", revisit after Q2). Management said the acquired group targets "high-single-digit or low-double-digit" margins, not legacy-like 15%. RIECO "positive by year-end". [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹270 cr (FY25 ₹95 cr), almost all in pigments. CRISIL expects ₹150–250 cr a year. Management: "moderate" capex only, for ROI-driven backward integration or special projects. [AR FY26 note 48; CRISIL Sep-26; Q1 call]
- The acquisition's economics are unusual: it was a distress purchase — identifiable net assets exceeded the price, creating a **₹1,196 cr bargain-purchase gain** booked to capital reserve (equity), not profit. [AR FY26 note 45]
- Integration spend: Project Integra (merging 4 SAP systems and ~130 applications) go-live in FY27; Global Capability Centre in Pune; second HQ in Frankfurt; commodity lines moved from Frankfurt to Roha. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt: peak ₹922 cr (Sep 2025) → ₹934 cr (Dec 2025) → ₹755 cr (Mar 2026) → ₹531 cr (Jun 2026); net debt/equity 0.2x. Gross borrowings ₹2,473 cr (FY26) against CRISIL-reported unencumbered liquidity of ₹1,401 cr. The VP4 sale (~₹840 cr) should take the group to net cash, per management. [IP Q1FY27; Screener; CRISIL Sep-26; IP Sep-26]
- CFO ₹344 cr in FY26 (₹71 cr in FY25) as inventory was cut; CFF −₹680 cr. Inventory days 515 (FY25, distorted by March acquisition) → 179; debtor days 63; NWC 23.6% of annualised sales in Q1 (25.9% FY26). [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- CRISIL A1+ (CP) reaffirmed Sep 2026; FY26 interest cover 3.9x (8.3x FY25); net debt/EBITDA 2.05x; downgrade trigger above 3.75x. Acquisition loan repayments ladder up from FY27 (₹120–345 cr a year). [CRISIL Sep-26; Q4 call]
- Dividend yield 0.4%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and breadth: 1,600 products priced from ~€1 to €130–140/kg; switching a pigment takes 1–2 years in coatings and up to 5 years in automotive — genuine switching costs. [Q1 call]
- Cost position: 55–60% of assets in Asia vs <1% for global peers; legacy business earns 15%+ margins. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Pricing: quarterly price negotiations; recent increases only passed through costs "to build trust"; the insolvency surcharge had to be rolled back to win customers back. Pricing power is moderate, not strong. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Share recapture opportunity: management says the combined business was ~€1 bn before insolvency and "lost a lot of business"; industry grows 3–4% (GDP-like), so above-market growth depends on winning back lost accounts. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Heavy reliance on other auditors:** the group auditor (S R B C & Co, EY) did not audit **57 subsidiaries** holding ₹7,060 cr of assets and ₹7,127 cr of revenue (~73% of group revenue). Opinion unmodified; key audit matters: revenue timing, hedge accounting, purchase-price allocation, and tax contingencies at Sudarshan Colorants India. [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report]
- **Inherited liabilities:** PPA provisions of ₹353 cr for contingent liabilities, of which ₹264 cr is a decommissioning liability (undiscounted ₹388 cr over ~8 years) — a future cash drain. Disputed invoices of ₹18.5 cr from the Heubach administrator's group company are not booked. [AR FY26 notes 39, 45]
- **Contingent liabilities** rising: GST/VAT disputes ₹189 cr (₹109 cr a year ago, incl. new ₹71 cr FY22 GST orders), Maharashtra captive-power electricity duty ₹36 cr, excise ₹20 cr. [AR FY26 note 39]
- **Promoter exit:** promoter holding fell from 35.8% (Sep 2023) to 8.2% (Jun 2026) via QIP dilution and reclassification of the P.R. Rathi family as public after he stepped down as Chairman (May 2025). The CMD's pay was ₹16.1 cr in FY26 — about 39% of consolidated PAT (₹41 cr). [Screener; AR FY26 related-party note; Q4 call]
- Restructuring charge coming: a European Works Council agreement signed after 30 Jun 2026; cost not yet quantified, not in guidance. [Q1 call]
- Quality of earnings: management's "business EBITDA", PPA credits, forex on preference shares and one-off depreciation adjustments make reported numbers hard to read; Q3 FY26 showed a ₹116 cr loss. Labour-code exceptional charge ₹32 cr in FY26. [Screener; AR FY26 note 50]
- RIECO keeps missing (hive-off plan on hold). Middle East disruption: 1–2 week longer shipping, customers destocking. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## What changed between the two calls (Q4 FY26 → Q1 FY27)
- Numbers moved faster than the guide: acquired-group margin 6.1% → 6.5% business EBITDA, net debt ₹755 → ₹531 cr, legacy margin 14.4% → 15.3%.
- Tone: Q4 was about "customer trust is back" and destocking ending; Q1 was about the Middle East (energy, raw-material and freight shocks) and refusing to raise guidance.
- New items: plan to buy a 70% direct stake in the Indian colorants subsidiary from overseas group entities (internal restructuring); Works Council restructuring; RIECO slump; then (1 Sep) the VP4 sale. Inventory reduction target quietly gave way to safety-stock building.

## My read
The Heubach turnaround is ahead of plan: in one quarter the acquired group earned business EBITDA that annualises well above the FY27 €35 mn guide, net debt is down 42% from peak, and the VP4 sale should make the group net cash. But most of the promised jump to ₹1,400–1,500 cr EBITDA by FY29 must come from cost-cutting and winning back lost customers in a 3–4% growth industry, and the accounting (PPA credits, "business EBITDA", other auditors covering ~73% of revenue, a ₹264 cr decommissioning liability) needs watching. The stock prices in much of the FY29 plan (Screener P/E ~96 on depressed trailing profit). The view improves if Q2 holds a ~9% business EBITDA margin and guidance is raised; it worsens if the Works Council charge is large, RIECO keeps bleeding, or European demand stalls.
