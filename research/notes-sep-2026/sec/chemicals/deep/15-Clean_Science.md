# Clean Science and Technology (CLEAN) — deep research, Q1 FY27

Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged. Price ₹807 (52-week range ₹652–1,118), market cap ₹8,580 cr, P/E ~37 (25 Sep 2026) [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Makes specialty chemicals by in-house catalytic routes (fewer steps, less effluent): MEHQ (stops acrylic monomers polymerising; used in diapers/superabsorbents), BHA, TBHQ, BHT and ascorbyl palmitate (antioxidants for food/feed), guaiacol and DCC (pharma), veratrole (agro), 4-MAP and anisole (FMCG/fragrance). Claims No.1 or No.2 global capacity in most flagship products [IP Q1FY27].
- New growth: HALS (hindered amine light stabilisers — additives that stop plastics degrading in sunlight) made in the 100% subsidiary Clean Fino-Chem (CFCL) at Kurkumbh; ~10,000 t capacity; India's first and "only the fifth globally" fully backward-integrated HALS maker; hydroquinone (HQ) and catechol plant (10,000 t, ₹260–300 cr revenue potential at full use) commissioned Dec 2025 [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call].
- Revenue mix Q1 FY27 (consolidated): performance chemicals ~81–83%, pharma/agro ~10–12%, FMCG 7% (call and slide differ slightly); HALS 22% of sales; top four legacy products 60% (85% in Q4 FY23) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Standalone FY26: performance 72%, pharma-agro 19%, FMCG 9% [Q4 call; AR FY26].
- Exports ~61–67% of consolidated revenue (AR gives both); CRISIL says 60–65%; China, Europe, Americas; HALS exports now ~50% of HALS sales versus ~20% a year earlier [AR FY26; CRISIL Sep-26; Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Customers: 500+ in 35+ countries; top 10 ~30% of revenue; one customer was 13.3% of standalone revenue in FY26 (₹106 cr; 10.8% in FY25) — likely Kemin, the US feed-additive maker that has just signed a 5-year supply contract [CRISIL Sep-26; AR FY26 note 24; Q1 call].
- Utilisation: HALS ~40% at March 2026 exit; HQ/catechol only 10–15% in Q4 FY26 [Q4 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr) / OPM / net profit: Jun-25 243 / 41% / 70; Sep-25 245 / 36% / 55; Dec-25 220 / 33% / 46; Mar-26 249 / 38% / 58; Jun-26 268 / 36% / 73 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: record consolidated sales (+10% y/y) but operating profit flat at ₹96 cr; PAT jump to ₹73 cr came from other income of ₹22 cr (₹3 cr in Q4) [Screener; IP Q1FY27].
- Standalone Q1 FY27: sales ₹203 cr (–6% y/y on volume loss from a two-week propylene-linked raw-material shutdown), EBITDA margin 42.7% (46.4% a year ago) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- HALS: ~3,200 t in FY26; ~1,000 t a quarter now; blended price up from ~₹440 to ~₹550/kg as mix moved to higher grades; CFCL turned EBITDA-positive (₹7–8 cr in Q4 FY26; implied ~₹9 cr in Q1) [AR FY26; Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Annual: FY23 ₹936 cr (OPM 43%, PAT ₹295 cr); FY24 ₹791 cr (42%, ₹244 cr); FY25 ₹967 cr (40%, ₹264 cr); FY26 ₹957 cr (37%, ₹230 cr). Standalone FY26 revenue fell 12% to ~₹796 cr after losing a key 4-MAP (FMCG) account and pricing pressure [Screener; Q4 call].
- ROCE slid from 61% (FY21) to 21% (FY26) as ~₹850 cr went into CFCL [Screener; Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (14 May 2026): no FY27 guidance ("a very tricky financial year"); PC2 (Performance Chemicals 2, water-treatment product) to commercialise Sep 2026; HQ/catechol to optimise in 1–2 quarters; FY27 capex max ₹80–100 cr plus a ₹200 cr enabling resolution for CFCL (H2 FY27); aspiration of $7–7.5/kg HALS realisation [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (1 Aug 2026): HALS revenue ₹250–300 cr in FY27 ("comfortable"); Geneus Chem (Swiss) NOR-HALS tie-up: ₹25 cr capex, production from Q3 FY27, ₹300–350 cr cumulative revenue over ~4 years (~₹100 cr in the peak year); Kemin 5-year contract lifts offtake 20–40% starting in 2–3 months but needs new capacity; HQ/catechol sales from Aug–Sep; subsidiary gross margins "sustainable" at 43–45% [Q1 call].
- Slipped: PC2 moved from Sep 2026 to Nov 2026, with revenue only from Q1 FY28 (it was originally a FY26 project); HQ/catechol ramp slower than hoped [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Met: HALS volumes held ~1,000 t, CFCL positive, exports up [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Cumulative investment in CFCL ~₹850 cr (₹100 cr in Q1 FY27; ₹200 cr in FY26) [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Fixed assets ₹760 cr + CWIP ₹118 cr (FY26); investing outflow ₹202 cr in FY26 [Screener].
- Asset turns: consolidated FY26 sales ₹957 cr on ~₹880 cr fixed assets + CWIP ≈ 1.1x (vs 3x+ in FY21). HALS at ₹250–300 cr revenue on ~₹850 cr invested is ~0.3x so far [Screener; Q1 call].
- CFCL enjoys a concessional tax rate and Maharashtra Package Scheme of Incentives (SGST refund) [AR FY26 MD&A].
- New European subsidiary (Netherlands) to start mid-September 2026 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Zero debt; ~₹480 cr unencumbered liquid investments (Aug 2026); net worth ₹1,578 cr; CRISIL AA-/Stable reaffirmed Sep 2026 [CRISIL Sep-26].
- Operating cash flow ₹236 cr (FY24), ₹214 cr (FY25), ₹275 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹74 cr in FY26 [Screener].
- Working capital lengthening: cash conversion cycle 29 days (FY21) → 118 days (FY26); inventory 157 days; CRISIL gross current assets 181 days [Screener; CRISIL Sep-26].
- Dividend payout 28%; final dividend ₹4/share for FY26 [Screener; Q4 call].

## Raw materials, China and pricing
- Main inputs are crude-linked: phenol, anisole (made captively by a vapour-phase route), hydroquinone (now partly captive), acetone and ammonia for HALS, propylene-derived inputs. Standalone raw material cost was 34.6% of sales in Q1 FY27 vs 33.3% in Q4 FY26 and 32.5% a year earlier; power and fuel 10.8% of sales [IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 supply shock: the main Indian supplier of a key input shut for lack of propylene, costing ~2 weeks of production; shipping vessels were scarce; availability normalised by July but prices stay volatile [Q1 call].
- China arbitrage: Chinese producers were less hit by the crude spike, so Clean Science bought "majority of phenol and acetone from China" in Q4 FY26; Chinese HQ capacity is diverted into MEHQ, forcing price calibration to hold share [Q4 call].
- Pass-through is partial: half-yearly and annual contracts were honoured without price increases; "price increase might be a big question" for Q2 [Q1 call].
- HALS competitors: BASF and Sabo (Europe), Rianlon and Suqian Unitechem (China); NOR-HALS is dominated by BASF [Q1 call].
- No anti-dumping duty support was cited for its products in either call or the annual report sections read.

## Moat evidence
- Process cost leadership in MEHQ/BHA/guaiacol; standalone EBITDA margin still 43–46% even in a weak year [IP Q1FY27].
- Kemin contract: "largest customer in the world" for BHA/BHT/TBHQ/AP; 5 years minimum, effectively exclusive — "I do not see any scope for any other entrant" [Q1 call].
- HALS approvals with large global accounts took ~1 year of sampling — a switching cost that now works in its favour; REACH registration for EU [Q1 call; AR FY26].
- Against: Chinese HQ capacity diverted into MEHQ forces price cuts to hold share; China buys feedstock cheaper (phenol/acetone from China were cheaper to import than to buy locally in Q4); new Indian entrants (Vinati Organics, Gem Aromatics) in anisole/MEHQ/BHA [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter selling:** Boob family sold ~24% (~₹2,626 cr block at ₹1,030 floor) in Aug 2025, cutting promoter holding from 74.97% to ~51%; earlier stake sales too [Screener; News: Business Today, 20–21 Aug 2025].
- Legacy business shrinking: standalone revenue –12% in FY26 and –6% y/y in Q1; long-term contracts prevented full cost pass-through [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Customer concentration rising: one customer 13.3% of standalone revenue [AR FY26].
- Returns dilution: ~₹850 cr in CFCL only just EBITDA-positive; repeated project slips (PC2, HQ ramp); pharma intermediate plant abandoned and repurposed for HALS intermediates [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Q1 profit quality: flat operating profit; PAT growth from other income [Screener].
- Disclosure cut: product, geography and top-product data withdrawn from presentations "for competitive reasons" [Q4 call].
- Management pay: MD Siddharth Sikchi ₹6.2 cr, Ashok Boob ₹4.0 cr in FY26 (down 27–57% after promoters forwent most of the 4%-of-PBT bonus, ~₹11 cr) [AR FY26; Q4 call].
- Auditor: unmodified; only KAM is revenue recognition/cut-off; contingent liabilities just ₹15 cr [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 34].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was defensive: loss of 4-MAP customer, Chinese arbitrage, no guidance, labour shortages from gas prices.
- Q1 is offensive: two long-term alliances (Kemin, Geneus), a HALS revenue target, exports at 50% of HALS, CFCL "monetising phase".
- Dropped: detail on repurposing the old pharma (DHDT) plant; new: European subsidiary, propylene-linked shutdown, fresh capex needs for Kemin.

## Best management quotes
- "We now shift from investing phase to monetizing phase." — Siddharth Sikchi on CFCL [Q1 call]
- "This is going to be a very tricky financial year for chemical industry in my view." — Siddharth Sikchi [Q4 call]
- "Only if we can understand Chinese and their pricing ways, I mean, life would be so easier." — Siddharth Sikchi [Q4 call]

## My read
The legacy catalytic-chemistry franchise is still highly profitable but no longer growing — standalone sales fell in FY26 and again in Q1 — so the investment case now rests on HALS, where volumes, grades and exports are clearly improving but returns on ~₹850 cr are still thin. At ~37 times trailing profit the stock needs roughly 20%+ annual profit growth, which requires HALS at ₹250–300 cr in FY27 rising further, Kemin volumes arriving on time, and PC2/HQ adding revenue in FY28. I would get more positive on two quarters of consolidated operating profit clearly above ₹100 cr with standalone volumes back to growth; negative if another quarter of flat operating profit is propped up by other income, or if promoters sell again.
