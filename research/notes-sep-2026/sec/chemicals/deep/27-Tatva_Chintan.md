# Tatva Chintan Pharma Chem (TATVA) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: both stored transcripts (Q1 FY27 call, 17 Jul 2026; Q4 FY26 call, 16 May 2026) are the official BSE filings (they carry the company's covering letters) and match the official PDFs in substance and in every figure spot-checked. Errors are in the stored **analysis**, not the transcripts — see **Stored-data errors**.

## Business and segments
- **What it makes (chemistry):** quaternary ammonium and phosphonium salts made by quaternisation/alkylation of amines, plus electrolysis:
  - **Phase-transfer catalysts (PTC)** — "detergent-like" salts that let two immiscible reactants meet, cutting solvent use and time in pharma, agro, flavour and polymer syntheses. Made since 1996. [IP Q1FY27]
  - **Structure-directing agents (SDA)** — ultra-pure quaternary hydroxides (made by electrolysis) that act as templates for zeolite catalysts used in diesel/truck exhaust NOx-removal (SCR) and refinery cracking. Tatva is the **world's #2 SDA maker** and only commercial Indian producer; approvals take 1–6 years. [IP Q1FY27]
  - **Electrolyte salts & solutions (ESS)** — salts for supercapacitors and zinc/"hybrid" batteries (explicitly **not** lithium-ion). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
  - **Pharma, agro intermediates & specialty (PASC)** — multi-step intermediates, glymes (India's largest glyme producer), new photochlorination agro intermediates and three pharma intermediates. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
  - **Semiconductor chemical** — first plant-scale batch (a key starting material, with impurities below 1 ppb) qualified by an MNC integrator in Q1 FY27; 5 products in the pipeline. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Segment revenue:** FY26 — SDA ₹204.5 cr (41%), PASC ₹162.3 cr (32%), PTC ₹117.3 cr (23%), ESS ₹16.5 cr (3%). Q1 FY27 — PASC ₹58.4 cr (34–35%), SDA ₹57.8 cr (35%), PTC ₹42.8 cr (26%), ESS ₹6.3 cr (4%). One reportable segment in the accounts; segment margins not disclosed. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 41]
- **Exports and geography:** FY26 revenue outside India ₹381.8 cr (**76%**) vs ₹235.5 cr (62%) in FY25; customers in 25+ countries; subsidiaries in the US and Netherlands. [AR FY26 note 41; IP Q1FY27]
- **Customer concentration (high):** largest customer **35%** of FY26 revenue (21% in FY25), second 19% (15%); the top customer is 43% of receivables. The top buyers are very likely SDA zeolite makers (my inference; not named). [AR FY26 note 41]
- **Capacity & utilisation:** 791 KL reactor capacity, 39 assembly lines at Ankleshwar and Dahej SEZ; FY26 utilisation 76.9% (reactors) and 58.5% (assembly lines). Management: Dahej can do ₹850–900 cr revenue at peak after debottlenecking (₹10–12 cr/yr capex) and is "nearly saturated" on land; growth beyond ~₹800–850 cr needs Jolva. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Raw materials:** amines and alkyl halides of crude and ammonia origin; ~36% of FY26 raw-material purchases imported (₹99 cr of ₹275 cr). After the West Asia war began, amines were unavailable for 2–3 weeks in March and prices rose 30–40%. [AR FY26 note 26; Q4 call]

- Company-reported Q1 FY27: revenue ₹167.1 cr (+43% YoY, +25% QoQ), EBITDA ₹32.3 cr (+86%), margin 19% vs 21% in Q4, PAT ₹16.0 cr (+140%). Growth was volume-led; price pass-through lagged. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Segment growth Q1 FY27 YoY: SDA +47%, PTC +47%, PASC +25% (+63% QoQ), ESS +76% (−52% QoQ). [Q1 call]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹394 cr → FY25 ₹383 cr → FY26 ₹506 cr (+32%); OPM 17% → 9% → 18%; PAT ₹30 cr → ₹6 cr → ₹42 cr. TTM sales ₹556 cr, PAT ₹51 cr. 5-year sales CAGR 11%, 5-year profit CAGR −4% — profits are still below FY22's ₹96 cr. [Screener]
- Returns: ROCE 7.2%, ROE 5.5% (Screener) — low because ₹600 cr+ of fixed assets are under-used after the FY22–FY25 Dahej build-out. Asset turn on net fixed assets ~0.8x (FY26 sales ₹506 cr / ₹609 cr). [Screener; derived]
- Unit economics: not disclosed per kg. Glymes: Chinese price fell from $4.6–4.8/kg to $2.1/kg within 30 days, now $3–3.5/kg. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (May 2026):** FY27 revenue growth ~25% ("20% to 25%, give or take"), EBITDA margin 20–22%; ESS 8–10% of revenue; pharma molecules ₹70–75 cr in FY27; SDA +20% to ₹250–300 cr; Jolva groundbreaking mid-July, capex ₹250–270 cr, commissioning Jan–Mar 2028, Phase-1 revenue potential ₹400–500 cr; capex ₹100 cr FY27 and ₹175 cr FY28; margin back to ~25% when Dahej is full (FY28). [Q4 call]
- **Q1 delivery:** revenue +43% YoY (ahead); EBITDA margin 19% (below the 20–22% band — **missed**, due to RM pass-through lag); ESS ₹6.3 cr (**missed** — raw-material stock-out); pharma molecule 1 commercialised (met); Jolva approved and ground broken 20 Jul (met); semiconductor batch qualified (met). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Q1 call (Jul 2026):** growth guide nudged to "25%, 30%"; margin 20–22% kept; ESS ₹40–60 cr kept; pharma ₹70–80 cr (raised slightly) with ~₹200 cr at full utilisation; 3–4 year CAGR "at least 20% to 25%"; ROIC target 20–22%. **Jolva cut** to ~₹200 cr capex with ~₹300 cr peak revenue (1.2–1.5x asset turn), vs ₹250–270 cr and ₹400–500 cr on the Q4 call; timeline 18–21 months. [Q1 call]
- **Track record:** FY24–FY25 disappointed badly (SDA destocking after COVID; OPM fell to 9%); FY26 recovered. Monoglyme capacity and flame-retardant projects were shelved when Chinese prices collapsed. [Screener; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY22–FY26: large Dahej expansion — fixed assets rose from ₹196 cr (Mar-23) to ₹609 cr (Mar-26); a new Dahej block became fully commercial in FY26 (trial production Jan 2026). [Screener; Q4 call]
- **Jolva greenfield (Gujarat):** ~₹200 cr, multipurpose + a dedicated plant for a large agro intermediate made by an in-house route; groundbreaking 20 Jul 2026; commissioning ~Q4 FY28; peak revenue ~₹300 cr. CRISIL says it will be "partially debt-funded". [Q1 call; Q4 call; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Asset turns: legacy PTC/SDA/ESS ~3x; multistage pharma/agro ~1.5x — so incremental capital earns lower turns than the old business. [Q1 call]
- Continuous-flow chemistry: 2 products now; 7–8 near-commercial products all use flow or electrochemistry, which frees reactor capacity. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹14 cr (Mar-24) → ₹36 cr (Mar-25) → **₹120 cr (Mar-26)**; unencumbered cash only ₹6 cr at Mar-26. Debt/EBITDA ~1.3x (company). Bank limits ~50% used on average over 12 months to Jul-26. [Screener; IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Sep-26]
- **Working capital is the weak spot:** inventory days 319, debtor days 86, payable days 95 → cash conversion cycle 310 days (Screener, FY26). CRISIL: gross current assets 249 days (inventory 174, receivables 87). Current ratio fell 2.76 → 1.63. [Screener; CRISIL Sep-26; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Cash flow: CFO ₹98 cr FY24, ₹25 cr FY25, ₹31 cr FY26 (only 42% of operating profit); free cash flow negative in each of FY22–FY26 (FY26 −₹82 cr). [Screener]
- Dividend paid despite rising short-term debt (≈10% payout); an investor challenged this on the Q4 call. [Q4 call]
- MAT credit of ₹41.1 cr carried as a deferred-tax asset — a key audit matter on recoverability. Cash tax low (₹7 cr) due to SEZ benefits. [AR FY26 auditor's report; Q4 call]
- Rating: CRISIL **upgraded** to A-/Stable/A2+ from BBB+/Stable/A2 on 8 Sep 2026 (₹245 cr facilities); expects cash accruals ₹75–80 cr a year; downgrade if accruals fall below ₹40 cr or working capital stretches. [CRISIL Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- **SDA duopoly:** an analyst on the Q4 call put share at roughly 85% US-based Sachem / 15% Tatva; management agreed it is a two-supplier market where customers split volume (max ~70% to one supplier) and a supplier, once approved for a vehicle platform, cannot be displaced. Tatva was late to Euro 6 but is "in the door right from the beginning" for **Euro 7** (Europe first, then US, Japan, China, India over 3–5 years). SDA pricing uses cost-pass-through formulas. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Electrolysis route for SDAs and in-house flow/electrochemistry are real process skills; but the MD concedes: "if Tatva can do, I don't believe someone else cannot do." [Q1 call]
- Semiconductor qualification after 8–9 years of work is a genuine entry barrier, but commercial scale is not expected before late 2028–2029. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **No moat in commodity-like lines:** monoglyme and flame retardants were abandoned when Chinese prices collapsed; the stock-out in ESS shows dependence on imported inputs. [Q1 call]
- China: talk of Chinese "anti-involution" subsidy removal from Jan 2027 could help, but management says "too early to predict". No anti-dumping protection relevant to its products was found. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration:** one customer = 35% of revenue and 43% of receivables; loss or destocking (as in FY24–FY25) would hit hard. [AR FY26 note 41]
- **Valuation vs returns:** Screener P/E ~80 on ROCE 7%; the price assumes the 20–25% CAGR and a margin return to ~25%.
- **Working capital and cash:** 300+ day cash cycle, negative free cash flow five years running, debt rising into the ₹200 cr Jolva build. [Screener]
- **Guidance quality:** Jolva's revenue potential was quietly cut from ₹400–500 cr to ~₹300 cr within two months; Q1 margin missed the band; ESS missed on supply. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Auditor:** NDJ & Co., a small Vadodara firm, gives an unqualified opinion with four key audit matters (revenue cut-off, capitalisation of capex, inventory valuation, MAT credit recoverability). Audit trail operated all year. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Contingent liabilities small:** ₹14.0 cr in total (indirect tax ₹2.3 cr incl. a ₹1.1 cr IGST refund dispute now at the Supreme Court, direct tax ₹0.4 cr, bank guarantees ₹3.8 cr, letters of credit ₹7.6 cr). [AR FY26 note 46]
- **Related parties:** only promoter/KMP pay (~₹1.7 cr each for three executive promoters; ₹5.97 cr total KMP benefits) and dividends — clean. Promoters hold 72.0%; no pledge reported. [AR FY26 note 42; Screener]
- **End-market risk:** SDA demand is tied to diesel trucks; China's diesel trucks are shifting to gas and EVs and Chinese SDA demand "is yet to pick up". [Q1 call]

## My read
The business has clearly turned: four straight quarters of rising revenue and margin, SDA riding Euro 7, and PASC finally producing repeat orders. But the numbers remain small (TTM PAT ₹51 cr), returns low (ROCE 7%), cash conversion poor, and one customer is over a third of sales. At about 80 times trailing earnings the price needs profits to roughly quadruple over five years — plausible only if margins return to ~25% and Jolva delivers on time. What would change the view: sustained 20%+ margins with positive free cash flow (bullish), or an SDA destocking/customer loss or another margin miss (bearish).
