# NOCIL (NOCIL) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: both stored transcripts (Q1 FY27, 4 Aug 2026; Q4 FY26, 8 May 2026) are the official BSE copies filed by the company (cover letters from the Company Secretary attached). No transcription errors to report. Errors are in the stored analysis (see Stored-data errors).

## Business and segments
- India's largest rubber-chemicals maker (Arvind Mafatlal group): 20+ products in two main families — **accelerators** (sulphenamides such as CBS and NS, which speed up vulcanisation, the heat-curing that turns raw rubber into tyre rubber) and **antioxidants/antidegradants** (TDQ, and PPDs such as Pilflex 13/6PPD, which stop rubber cracking from oxygen and ozone), plus latex chemicals and inhibitors. Claims to be the only rubber-chemicals company offering the complete range. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Chemistry and raw materials: aniline-based chains built on benzene; key inputs are aniline, amines, MIBK (imported), chlorinated aromatics, sulphur, plus gas for utilities. Aniline ran ₹100–112/kg in Jul–Dec 2025 and ~₹190/kg by Apr 2026 (analyst figures on the Q4 call, not disputed). [CARE Oct-25; Q4 call]
- Plants: Dahej (Gujarat, since 2013) and Navi Mumbai (since 1976). Installed capacity ~1.38–1.39 lakh t after the new TDQ line (analyst figure; management does not disclose capacity or utilisation "for business sensitivity"). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Customers: all domestic and international tyre majors; tyres ~65–70% of demand, non-tyre rubber goods (belts, hoses, footwear, latex) the rest. 65–70% of volume is on quarterly contracts; 30–35% spot. [Q4 call; stored profile consistent]
- Geography: FY26 exports ₹440 cr (−10%; volume −8%, hit by US tariffs and Chinese latex pricing) = 34% of turnover; domestic ~66%. Q1 FY27 volume split 67% domestic / 33% export; target 40–45% exports by FY28–29. [AR FY26 Directors' report & BRSR; Q1 call]
- Market share: ~40% of the Indian rubber-chemicals market (CARE); share gain of ~100–150 bps in Q4 FY26 per the MD. China makes ~80% of world rubber chemicals and uses only ~40% of it. [CARE Oct-25; Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Single operating segment (rubber chemicals). [AR FY26 segment note]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 336 / op. profit 31 (9%) / PAT 17; Sep-25 321 / 22 (7%) / 12; Dec-25 316 / 27 (8%) / 9; Mar-26 330 / 21 (6%) / 17; **Jun-26 403 / 45 (11%) / 28**. [Screener]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY22 ₹1,571 cr → FY23 ₹1,617 cr → FY24 ₹1,445 cr → FY25 ₹1,393 cr → FY26 ₹1,303 cr; OPM 18% → 16% → 14% → 10% → 8%; PAT ₹176 cr → ₹149 cr → ₹133 cr → ₹103 cr → ₹56 cr. ROCE 4%, ROE 2.6% (TTM). Four straight years of falling profit. [Screener]
- FY26 company figures: revenue ₹1,303 cr, operating EBITDA ₹101 cr (7.7%), PAT ₹56 cr; volume +3% (H1 −5%, H2 +12%). The MD&A blames a 200 bp margin fall on Chinese/SE Asian dumping and zero-duty imports via Thailand and Korea FTAs. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Volume index (Q1 FY20 = 100): Q1 FY26 138, Q2 140, Q3 ~140, Q4 150, **Q1 FY27 145** (+9% YoY, −3% QoQ). [IP Q1FY27]
- Unit economics (derived): Q1 FY27 value addition (revenue minus materials) ₹175 cr vs ₹143 cr a year ago; realisation up ~10% YoY (revenue +20% on volume +9%). Conversion costs rose ~₹20 cr QoQ (₹110 → ₹130 cr) on gas, freight, CSR and maintenance. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Last up-cycle reference: spreads peaked around ₹65/kg in the 2014–19 anti-dumping era (analyst figure, Q4 call).

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (May 2026): no revenue guidance; aim to lift EBITDA margin ~150 bps from the FY26 base (i.e. ~9.2%); Q4 volumes can be "the base"; new products to pick up late FY27; TDQ approvals in 6–8 months; ministry decision on TDQ and sulphenamide duties "around middle of June". [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: margin 11.2% — **beat** the implied target; volume 145 vs 150 base — **missed** (utilities and logistics); sulphenamide duty **approved** 20 Jun 2026; TDQ duty **not approved** by the Finance Ministry. [Q1 call]
- Q1 call (Aug 2026): first explicit FY27 guidance — revenue **₹1,400–1,600 cr** at current prices, EBITDA "in the region of 10%", volume growth ~10%. That implies ₹140–160 cr EBITDA vs ₹101 cr in FY26; management expects "some moderation" in prices from Q1 levels. TDQ plant revenue to start trickling in Q4 FY27, more in Q1 FY28. Specialty share to rise 5–10 points from ~15%. [Q1 call]
- Pilflex 13 (6PPD-type antioxidant): DGTR positive final finding Jun 2026; government decision expected "by the end of September". Sulphenamides + Pilflex 13 together = 25–30% of revenue (earlier: all four petitioned products ~40%). A 4-ADPA petition is still with DGTR. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- TDQ expansion at Dahej: sanctioned ₹250 cr (Mar 2024); completed, cost 10–12% under budget, trial production on, samples with customers. Adds ~20% capacity, all merchant sales. [AR FY26 Directors' report; Q4 call]
- New ₹130 cr brownfield at Dahej (approved 16 Mar 2026): specialty rubber chemicals with captive intermediate; trial production ~Q2 FY28; funded mainly by internal accruals, rest by a long-term loan (CARE noted a planned ₹100 cr term loan). [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27; CARE Oct-25]
- CWIP ₹221 cr at Mar-26 (₹60 cr a year earlier); capital commitments ₹9.8 cr (₹83 cr). [Screener; AR FY26 note 37]
- Asset turns: FY26 sales ₹1,303 cr on net fixed assets ₹831 cr ≈ 1.6x (≈1.8x in FY22). Adding ~₹380 cr of new capex to a business earning ~8% margins dilutes returns until volumes fill — ROCE fell from 13% (FY23) to 4% (FY26). [Screener; derived]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Essentially debt-free: borrowings ₹8 cr; investments ₹415 cr (incl. NCDs ₹240 cr and ICDs ₹15 cr). Gearing 0.03x (Mar-25). [Screener; AR FY26 note 42; CARE Oct-25]
- Working capital: inventory days cut from 129 (FY25) to 75 (FY26); cash cycle 156 → 84 days; CFO ₹252 cr in FY26 (₹26 cr in FY25), which funded most of the TDQ capex. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Dividend payout 45% of FY26 profit (yield ~0.8%). [Screener]
- Ratings: CRISIL AA and **CARE AA (Stable)** / A1+ (Oct 2025). CARE flags imports and says ADD "shall remain crucial for improvement in NOCIL's profitability". [CARE Oct-25; AR FY26 MD&A]

## Moat evidence
- ~40% domestic share, long approval cycles (6–8 months for tyre customers), preferred-supplier status with global tyre majors; "stickiness needle has significantly moved" in 3–5 years. [CARE Oct-25; Q4 call]
- Scale and breadth: complete product range, R&D centre recognised by the government.
- But pricing power is weak without trade protection: margins fell from 18% (FY22) to 8% (FY26) after ADD lapsed in July 2019; the company cut prices to keep volume. Chinese makers still get export subsidies and cut yuan prices, so rupee weakness did not help. [Screener; CARE Oct-25; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Trade policy dependence**: TDQ duty rejected despite DGTR recommendation; Pilflex 13 rejected once before (2021) and pending again; FTA-route imports from Thailand/Korea at zero duty. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Duty absorption**: management warns foreign exporters may absorb the new sulphenamide duty (as in the insoluble-sulphur case), so benefit is uncertain. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **6PPD regulation**: California-led scrutiny of 6PPD's toxicity to fish; NOCIL says it is in "advanced stages" on alternatives. A ban would hit a large-volume antioxidant. [Q4 call]
- **Promoter pledges**: promoter group holds 33.76%; Mafatlal Industries had 3.47% of NOCIL's equity encumbered (Apr 2026, CSB Bank), and an analyst on the Q1 call said one large promoter's stake is ~24% pledged; the CFO declined to comment ("promoters in their offices call"). [Screener; News: Whalesbook, 21-Apr-26; Q1 call]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; only key audit matter is revenue recognition (standard). Contingent liabilities small: ₹13.6 cr (₹27.5 cr). Related-party dealings immaterial (employee trusts, Vrata Tech). [AR FY26 auditor's report, notes 37, 41]
- Performance-linked ESOP targets are "a bit behind" per the MD. [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Margin rebounded from 6.4% to 11.2% as contract prices reset to higher raw-material costs; the Q4 lag reversed into a probable inventory gain (management would not quantify).
- Volume turned from +7% QoQ to −3% QoQ (gas curbs, logistics, non-tyre customers halting on labour/cooking-gas shortages).
- Trade protection: two DGTR wins (TDQ, sulphenamides) became one approval and one rejection; Pilflex 13 moved to the ministry.
- Guidance moved from a vague "+150 bps" to explicit ₹1,400–1,600 cr and ~10% margin.
- New question on promoter pledges.

## My read
NOCIL is a debt-free market leader whose profits have halved in four years because Chinese and FTA-route imports took away pricing power; FY27 should be better (Q1 margin 11%, guidance ~10%, one duty approved) but part of the Q1 jump is a raw-material price lag that management already expects to moderate. The big swing factors are external: the Pilflex 13 duty decision (due ~end-Sep 2026), whether exporters absorb the sulphenamide duty, and how fast tyre makers approve the new TDQ line. The stock is priced well above what trailing profit supports, so it needs a return to mid-teens margins, not just 10%. A Pilflex 13 approval plus clear TDQ volume in Q4 FY27 would strengthen the case; another rejection or margins slipping back below 9% would weaken it.
