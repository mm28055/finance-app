# Galaxy Surfactants (GALAXYSURF) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: both stored transcripts (Q1 FY27, 14 Aug 2026; Q4 FY26, 15 May 2026) were compared with the official BSE filings. The content matches in substance. Two errors in the stored copies: the stored Q4 transcript names the speaker as "Kanwar Bir Singh, Managing Director" — the official transcript shows the speaker is **K. Natarajan (MD)**; Kanwar Bir Singh Anand is an independent director. The stored Q1 transcript says Q1 FY26 EBITDA/MT was "approximately 20,900"; the official text says **₹20,009/MT**.

## Business and segments
- Makes surfactants (the foaming/cleansing molecules in shampoos, soaps, body wash, detergents) and specialty care ingredients (mild amino-acid surfactants, UV filters, emollients, preservatives, blends) for home and personal care brands; 215+ grades, 1,500+ customers in 95+ countries, 7 plants (5 India, 1 Egypt, 1 US via TRI-K). [IP Q1FY27]
- Chemistry: mostly oleochemical — lauryl/fatty alcohol (from palm kernel oil) is ethoxylated and sulphated (ethylene oxide and sulphur are the other key inputs) into SLES/SLS-type "performance surfactants". Petrochemical rival chain is LABSA (from crude). The relative cost of the two chains decides which one FMCG customers formulate with. [Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Oct-25]
- Q1 FY27 revenue split: Performance Surfactants ₹1,178.7 cr (66%), Specialty Care ₹603.2 cr (34%) vs 65/35 a year earlier. [IP Q1FY27]
- Customer mix Q1 FY27: MNC customers 54% of revenue (49% a year ago), regional players 39% (42%), local & niche 7% (9%). One customer is above 10% of consolidated revenue in both FY25 and FY26 (not named). [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 39]
- Geography Q1 FY27: India 43%, AMET (Africa, Middle East, Turkey) 21%, Rest of World 36% (44/22/34 a year ago). FY26 revenue: India ₹2,202 cr, overseas ₹3,025 cr (58% overseas). [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 39]
- FY26 volumes: total 2,56,942 MT (flat, +0.1%); Performance 1,59,683 MT (−4.6%); Specialty 97,259 MT (+8.8%); India 1,17,875 MT (+4.1%); AMET 62,136 MT (−10.4%); RoW 76,931 MT (+3.6%). [AR FY26 MD&A]
- Capacity/utilisation: not disclosed as a figure. Management said ₹480 cr of capex over three years, mostly specialty, was commissioned only in the last ~1.5 years and "will start coming up this year" — i.e. utilisation of new specialty lines is still ramping. [Q4 call]
- Only one reportable segment (home and personal care ingredients) in the accounts; the performance/specialty split is given only in presentations. [AR FY26 note 39]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 1,278 / op. profit 124 (10%) / PAT 79; Sep-25 1,326 / 110 (8%) / 66; Dec-25 1,329 / 119 (9%) / 59; Mar-26 1,315 / 122 (9%) / 62; **Jun-26 1,782 / 249 (14%) / 166**. [Screener]
- Company-reported Q1 FY27 EBITDA ₹252.5 cr (+87% YoY) at **₹35,458/MT**, vs ₹135.1 cr and ₹20,009/MT in Q1 FY26 — a record. PAT ₹166 cr (+109%). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Implied Q1 volume ≈ 71,200 MT (+5–5.5% YoY); implied realisation ≈ ₹250/kg vs ≈ ₹190/kg a year ago. Management attributed the price jump to doubled export freight (CIF pricing), a mix shift to 100%-active/dry grades and raw-material pass-through. [Q1 call; derived from IP]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹3,792 cr → FY25 ₹4,221 cr → FY26 ₹5,245 cr; OPM 12% → 11% → 9%; PAT ₹301 cr → ₹305 cr → ₹267 cr. TTM sales ₹5,752 cr, PAT ₹354 cr. 3-year profit CAGR −10%. [Screener]
- FY26 EBITDA ₹497 cr (FY25 ₹510 cr); EBITDA/MT ₹19,304 (FY25 ₹19,862). FY26 had a ₹30.9 cr exceptional charge for the new labour codes. [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]
- Returns: ROCE 16.5% FY25 → 13.3% FY26; RONW 13.4% → 10.5% (company definitions). Screener ROCE 13.5%, ROE 10.9%. [AR FY26 MD&A; Screener]
- Raw material: fatty alcohol averaged $2,806/MT in Q1 FY27 vs $2,751 in Q4 FY26 and $2,723 in Q1 FY26; intra-quarter it spiked from ~$2,800 to $3,300 and fell below $2,500 by end-June. Crude averaged above $100/bbl in the quarter. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Unit economics: FY26 ≈ ₹19,300 EBITDA per tonne on ≈ 2.57 lakh tonnes; each ₹1,000/MT change moves annual EBITDA by ≈ ₹26 cr. [derived from AR FY26]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (May 2026): standing FY27 guidance of 6–8% volume growth and ₹19,000–21,000 EBITDA/MT; for Q1 only, management expected "the higher end" of both ranges, refusing to guide beyond one quarter because of the West Asia war. India expected to return to 8–10%. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: volume +5% (below the "higher end" of 6–8% promised for Q1); EBITDA/MT ₹35,458 (far above ₹21,000). India +11% (beat), RoW +6%, AMET −4% (miss, supply-led). So: volume **missed slightly**, margin **beat hugely**. [Q1 call]
- Q1 call (Aug 2026): volume guidance **kept** at 6–8%; FY27 EBITDA/MT guidance **raised** to ₹24,000–25,000. Management explicitly expects only ₹21,000–22,000/MT in each of the next three quarters — so the raise is mostly Q1's one-off, not a new run-rate. [Q1 call]
- Longer term (Strategy 2030, Investor Day 2025): double volumes, 2.5x EBITDA, ROCE above 22%; ~₹25,000/MT, "backloaded" to FY29–FY30; new products >5% of revenue by 2030. Specialty share to stay 35–40%, not rise beyond 60:40. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Management admitted the "wellness" leg of Strategy 2030 is behind plan; now says an acquisition in beauty & wellness is being worked on "in the coming months/quarters". [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Run-rate capex ₹150–200 cr a year, all from internal accruals; FY27 about ₹150 cr. Maintenance capex only ₹20–30 cr a year. No new growth commitments beyond what is already under way. [Q1 call; Q4 call; CRISIL Oct-25]
- Screener: CWIP ₹262 cr (Mar-25) → ₹181 cr (Mar-26); net block ₹1,030 cr → ₹1,174 cr. Asset turn (sales/net block) ≈ 4.5x in FY26 — high, because much of revenue is raw-material pass-through. Capital commitments ₹50 cr. [Screener; AR FY26 note 41]
- Mexico: a multi-year EPC project (Galaxy building a plant for a customer; "Galaxy Surfactants Mexico") is recognising service income by milestones; amounts not disclosed "for confidentiality", called immaterial; commercialisation within 12 months (FY28). The parent has given a **₹445.7 cr performance guarantee** to the customer on behalf of the Mexico subsidiary (new in FY26). [Q1 call; AR FY26 notes 41, 43]
- AMET: capex completed; only debottlenecking; no acquisition planned there. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹235 cr (Mar-26) vs reserves ₹2,709 cr; investments ₹474 cr. CRISIL counted cash and liquid investments of ₹510 cr at Mar-25 — effectively **net cash**. Gearing ~0.09x; interest coverage fell to 11.8x in FY26 from 20.8x. [Screener; CRISIL Oct-25; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Operating cash flow fell: ₹518 cr (FY24) → ₹421 cr (FY25) → ₹333 cr (FY26); investing outflow ₹366 cr in FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 52, inventory 76, payables 62, cash conversion cycle 66 days (FY26; 73 in FY25). CRISIL notes gross current assets 130–155 days. [Screener; CRISIL Oct-25]
- Dividend payout 29% of FY26 profit (5% in FY25); yield ~1%. No buyback. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and breadth: India's largest surfactant/specialty portfolio; supplies Tier-1 FMCGs globally for 4+ decades; CRISIL AA-/Stable. [Q1 call; CRISIL Oct-25]
- Pricing: bulk of revenue is cost-plus with contractual pass-through formulas; the rupee move from 88 to 95/$ and freight were passed on without customer pushback in Q1. [Q1 call; CRISIL Oct-25]
- Switching costs: new, first-of-kind ingredients need full customer testing; incremental grades need only incremental approval. US approvals "falling into place sequentially from February". [Q1 call]
- Weak points: AMET volumes fell from ~90,000 MT to ~60,000 MT after a backward-integrated local Egyptian player took share from Galaxy's global customers — "What has changed has changed." Reformulation away from oleo-based chains cost India volumes in FY25–FY26. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Competitor exit: BASF announced closure of its care-chemicals/sulfation plant at Dahej in Aug 2026 — possible share gain, too early to quantify. [Q1 call]
- R&D spend ₹37 cr (0.7% of sales); 111 patents granted as of Mar-25. [AR FY26 note 40; CRISIL Oct-25]

## Risks and red flags
- Q1 margin is not repeatable: management itself guides ₹21,000–22,000/MT for Q2–Q4 vs ₹35,458 in Q1; part of Q1 was inventory gains and opportunistic "commercial execution" during feedstock shortages. [Q1 call]
- Feedstock: fatty alcohol tied to palm kernel oil, Indonesian biodiesel mandate (B40 → B50) and a possible El Niño; if crude falls while palm stays high, FMCG customers reformulate to LABSA and Galaxy loses volume (this happened in FY25–FY26). [Q1 call]
- Egypt/AMET: ~7 weeks of Q1 lost at the Egypt plant because raw materials were stuck at Jebel Ali/Jeddah/Port Qasim; March 2026 also mostly idle. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Contingent liability**: ₹445.7 cr guarantee to a customer for the Mexico subsidiary's performance (about 16% of standalone net worth). Tax disputes are small (customs ₹28.7 cr, excise/ST ₹3.9 cr, income tax ₹1.4 cr, sales tax/GST ₹3.9 cr). [AR FY26 note 41]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter is the fair value (DCF-based, Level 3) of ₹107 cr of preference shares held in a subsidiary. No emphasis of matter found. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Customer concentration: one customer >10% of revenue; MNCs 54% of Q1 revenue. [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27]
- Disclosure gap: EPC income not quantified; capacity/utilisation not disclosed.
- Governance: promoters hold 70.9% (Jun-26); pledge data not checked; related-party transactions stated to be in ordinary course. [Screener; AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from crisis management (Q4: Egypt idle in March, AMET −15%, RoW −7%, refusing to guide beyond Q1) to confident (Q1: record EBITDA, guidance raised, M&A talk). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Petrochemical feedstocks became scarcer/costlier than oleo, reversing the reformulation headwind — India volumes returned to double digits (+11%). [Q1 call]
- New: BASF Dahej closure; Indonesian B50 and El Niño risk; explicit M&A intent in beauty & wellness. [Q1 call]
- Unchanged: 6–8% volume guidance; ₹150 cr capex; Mexico on track.

## My read
The business is improving from a weak two-year patch: India volumes are back, US specialty approvals are flowing, and the oleo-vs-petro cost gap now favours Galaxy. But Q1's ₹35,458/MT is a windfall; management's own FY27 guide implies EBITDA of roughly ₹650–690 cr on ~2.7 lakh tonnes, i.e. ₹21–22k/MT underneath. At ~22x trailing earnings the price needs steady high-single-digit volume growth and ~₹21–22k/MT to hold, plus proof that Strategy 2030 (₹25k/MT, ROCE >22%) is real. The view would turn more positive with a sensible, accretive beauty/wellness acquisition or a clear BASF-exit share gain; more negative if crude falls sharply (reformulation risk) or the Mexico guarantee turns into a claim.
