# Le Travenues Technology (ixigo, NSE: IXIGO) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹157, market cap ₹6,916 cr, P/E 79, ROCE 6.8%, ROE 5.5%, no dividend, 52-week range ₹147–339 (down 54% from high) [Screener, 27-Sep-26].

## Business and segments
- An online travel agency (OTA) for "next billion users" in smaller towns. Brands: ixigo (trains, flights), ConfirmTkt (trains), AbhiBus (buses), Zoop (food on trains), plus hotels, Trenes.com (Spain train OTA, 60% from 1-Mar-26) and Brevistay (54.66% from Q1 FY27) [AR FY26 note 38; IP Q1FY27].
- It earns a take rate on gross transaction value (GTV): agent service charges on trains, commissions and convenience fees on flights and buses, and value-added services (refund guarantees such as ixigo Assured, seat/meal add-ons). Value-added attachment rate over 31% [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 segments [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]:
  - Trains: 2.44 cr segments (−8%), GTV ₹2,139 cr (+4%), revenue ₹141.0 cr (+9%), contribution margin (CM, revenue less direct and marketing costs of the segment) ₹52.7 cr at 37% (32% a year ago). OTA train market share 63% (60% a few quarters ago). Gross take rate 6.54%.
  - Flights: 29 lakh segments (+4%), GTV ₹2,342 cr (+27%), revenue ₹104.6 cr, CM ₹41.0 cr at 39% (42%). Gross take rate 8.41% (9.17% a year ago).
  - Buses: 89 lakh segments (+33%), GTV ₹947 cr (+39%), revenue ₹102.6 cr (+34%), CM ₹54.2 cr at 53%, now the largest profit pool (37% of group CM). Gross take rate 11.76% (12.24%).
  - Others (hotels, Zoop): CM −₹3.1 cr vs +₹1.9 cr — the hotel investment shows here.
- Group Q1: GTV ₹5,524 cr (+19%), revenue ₹356.75 cr (+13%), CM ₹144.9 cr (40.6%), adjusted EBITDA ₹29.2 cr (−7%), PAT ₹34.2 cr (+81%) [Q1 call].
- Users: 8.5 cr monthly active users, 42 lakh monthly transacting users; 92% of chat queries resolved by AI [Q1 call].
- Hotels: 0.5 million room-nights ("heads on beds") in Q1; 10,000+ directly contracted hotels in about 700 towns; 90% of hotel bookings came from existing users [Q1 call].

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly revenue: Q1 FY26 316, Q2 283, Q3 320, Q4 308, Q1 FY27 357. Operating profit: 23, −5, 24, 20, 20 (OPM 7%, −2%, 7%, 6%, 6%). Other income: 7, 5, 14, 19, 29. Net profit: 19, −3, 24, 32, 34 [Screener].
- Profit growth is driven by other income: Q1 FY27 other income ₹29 cr exceeded operating profit of ₹20 cr, thanks to interest on the Prosus cash. Management expects other income to stay "largely around that level" [Screener; Q1 call].
- Annual revenue: FY24 656, FY25 914, FY26 1,228 (+34%). Operating profit: 38, 72, 62. Net profit: 73, 60, 71. OPM: 6%, 8%, 5% [Screener].
- Company's adjusted EBITDA (excludes other income and ESOP cost): FY26 ₹120.9 cr vs ₹94.8 cr (+28%); FY26 ESOP expense ₹48.1 cr including a one-off ₹26.9 cr [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]. Screener's operating profit is after ESOP cost, hence much lower.
- FY26 GTV ₹18,693 cr (+25%); CM ₹474 cr (+18%, 39%); operating cash flow ₹196 cr (+60%) [Q4 call].
- Five-year sales CAGR 55%; three-year 35% [Screener].
- Unit economics: group take rate ≈ 6.5% of GTV (₹357 cr / ₹5,524 cr); customer-acquisition spend (brand + performance + discounts) kept near 4% of GTV. Flight take rates are falling (9.17% → 8.41%) as fares rise faster than commissions [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- ixigo gives no numeric guidance [both calls].
- **Q4 FY26 call (21 May 2026):** "Q1 FY27 does not have the same base effect as Q4 FY26, so we expect growth rates to look up." Q1 delivered: GTV growth 19% vs 9% in Q4, revenue 13% vs 8% — **delivered** [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Q4: hoped a new railway ticketing system (PRS) in Aug–Sep 2026 and Tatkal-timing changes would ease train volumes. Q1: "planning the business without assuming any immediate relief" — **dropped** [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Q4: CM floor of about 36–37%, and a willingness to take 1–2 points lower CM for share. Q1 CM 40.6% — **kept** [Q4 call].
- Q4: hotels to be disclosed only after product-market fit. Q1: first hotel volume disclosed, Brevistay bought, "we are fairly confident now" — i.e. **hotel spending stepped up**, with adjusted EBITDA down 7% as a result [Q1 call].
- Longer-term ambition: "over the next 4-5 years … number-one discovery and booking platform for India's mid-market and budget hotels" [Q1 call].
- Flights: Q1 call warns of no near-term relief — Air India cut July–September capacity about 20% and IndiGo about 10% [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is small in the usual sense; the "capex" is people, AI and acquisitions. Part of AI platform spending is capitalised and amortised over 5 years (not disclosed in rupees; "in the same range" as last quarter) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Prosus (MIH Investments) bought 10.1% for ₹1,295.6 cr at ₹280/share in FY26. Of four ₹324 cr buckets (general corporate, working capital, organic growth, inorganic), ₹554.8 cr was used and ₹740.8 cr unused at 31-Mar-26 (CARE Ratings is the monitoring agency) [AR FY26 Corporate Governance Report]. The share now trades at ₹157, 44% below Prosus's price.
- Acquisitions: Trenes.com 60% for about €11.7 mn (≈₹125 cr) (Feb 2026); Squad As A Service (Spanish AI firm) 45.02%; Brevistay 54.66% for ₹65.69 cr (FY26 revenue ₹18.1 cr), with rights to buy the rest [AR FY26; News: Business Standard 5-Jun-26; News: Entrepreneur Feb-26].
- Capital commitments ₹52 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 35].
- Payback: not disclosed. Hotels will have "a longer gestation period" [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Total cash (FDs, mutual funds, net of borrowings) ₹1,740 cr at 31-Mar-26 vs ₹337 cr a year earlier — about 25% of market cap [IP Q1FY27 p.44].
- Borrowings ₹44 cr (FD-backed overdraft) [Screener; AR FY26].
- Goodwill ₹444 cr (₹260 cr), a key audit matter [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Working capital: receivable days 14; working-capital days swung to +102 in FY26 from −25 (the cash raise sits in current assets) [Screener].
- Operating cash flow ₹196 cr FY26; FCF ₹175 cr [Screener]. No dividend.

## Moat evidence
- Train OTA share 63% (up from ~60%) [Q1 call]. Bus is "largely a duopoly-type category" online (with redBus, owned by MakeMyTrip) and AbhiBus is growing faster: bus GTV +39% vs a smaller market growth; >60% growth in 17 states [Q1 call].
- Flights: "fastest-growing flight OTA in India"; flight segments +4% when DGCA passenger growth was about 2% [Q1 call].
- Low customer-acquisition cost: 90% of hotel bookings from existing users; spend held near 4% of GTV [Q1 call].
- Weakness: it rides on regulated or concentrated suppliers — IRCTC for trains (policy changes cut volumes 8%), two airlines control most domestic capacity, and take rates are set by commissions it cannot dictate.

## Risks and red flags
- Near-zero operating margin: Q1 operating profit ₹20 cr on ₹357 cr revenue; profit now depends on interest income from raised cash [Screener].
- Rail policy: Tatkal access limits, reduced waitlist quota, Aadhaar/OTP verification — outside its control [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Airline capacity cuts and fare inflation (domestic fares +22% YoY) choke the train-to-flight upgrade funnel [Q1 call].
- Hotel push against MakeMyTrip and OYO: loss-making "Others" segment; more spend promised ("We will not be shy about investing") [Q1 call].
- Associates lose money: share of loss ₹11.2 cr in FY26 and ₹4.0 cr in Q1 FY27 (Freshbus, Sqaas) [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call].
- Management pay: KMP compensation ₹42.0 cr in FY26 vs ₹13.8 cr, mostly share-based (₹32.7 cr) [AR FY26 note 38].
- Auditor (S.R. Batliboi): unmodified opinion; key audit matter on goodwill impairment; audit trail not enabled for direct database-level changes in some software [AR FY26 auditor's report]. No contingent liabilities disclosed; no credit rating found (company is debt-free).
- No promoter group (professionally run; FIIs 61%, DIIs 13%); Prosus is a strategic holder [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- May: "AI-native reinvention" and ixigo Next launch; cautious on hotels. August: hotel investment phase, Brevistay, and detailed talk on AI cost economics.
- Hope of rail policy relief faded; aviation outlook turned more negative (capacity cuts).
- New talking points: SME/corporate travel product "in subsequent quarters"; Bharat Darshan rail packages with IRCTC.
- Adjusted EBITDA fell 7% YoY in Q1 despite 13% revenue growth — the first quarter clearly showing reinvestment.

## My read
The core marketplaces are getting stronger — share gains in trains, buses and flights, 40% contribution margin — but the group earns almost nothing at the operating line because it is funding hotels and AI, and its main suppliers (IRCTC, two airlines) are squeezing volumes. Reported profit now rests on interest from ₹1,740 cr of cash. At the current price, the market is paying about ₹5,200 cr for the operating business, which needs adjusted EBITDA to roughly triple over 3–4 years to justify it; buses alone will not do it, so the hotel bet has to work. I would turn more positive if hotels show positive contribution within 4–6 quarters or rail policy eases; more negative if adjusted EBITDA keeps falling while Prosus money is spent on acquisitions.
