# CEAT — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Price ₹3,352, market cap ₹13,560 cr, trailing P/E 21.3, book value ₹1,247, dividend yield 1.04%, ROCE 19.2% [Screener, 27-Sep-26]. RPG Group company (promoters 47.3%).

## Business and segments
- Tyres for two/three-wheelers, passenger cars/UVs, trucks and buses, LCVs, farm and off-highway (OHT), plus rubber tracks after buying **Camso**'s compact construction tyre and tracks business from Michelin for ~US$225 mn (effective 1 Sep 2025). Camso plants are in Sri Lanka (CEAT OHT Lanka). Also a Sri Lanka JV (CEAT Kelani), equity-accounted. [CARE Jan-26; AR FY26 note 49; IP Q1FY27]
- Single reportable segment (tyres). No single foreign country >10% of revenue. [AR FY26 note 41]
- Standalone product mix FY26: truck & bus 29% (FY25 30%), two/three-wheeler 27% (27%), PC/UV 22% (21%), off-highway 15% (15%), LCV/others 7% (7%). [IP Q1FY27 slide 7]
- Channel mix FY26 standalone: replacement 51% (53%), OEM 30% (28%), exports 19% (19%). Q1 FY27: replacement ~50%, OEM ~30%, international ~20% (23% including Camso). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Capacity: produced 4.5 crore tyres in FY26 (3.96 crore FY25); utilisation ">80%" (AR), "85–90%" (Q4 call), "90% plus" standalone (Q4 call). OHT and tracks ~220 t/day. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- Camso run-rate ~US$10 mn a month at customer prices (~US$120 mn a year), but CEAT currently sells to Michelin at a lower realisation during the transition. Earlier talk was ₹1,000–1,200 cr revenue at 12–13% margin. [Q1 call]
- Distribution: >5,700 dealers, >61,000 sub-dealers, >1,115 retail stores. [CARE Jan-26]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (29 Apr 2026) promised:** Q1 raw material +15%, possibly near 20%; replacement price +10% vs March (5% by April, 5% more in May–June); OEM indexed hike on 1 July; international 10–12% in stages; FY27 capex ₹1,300–1,400 cr (standalone ~₹1,350–1,400 cr, +25%) plus ~US$30 mn Camso upstream (mixers/calenders), US$20–25 mn this year; Camso customer interface 90% under CEAT by Sep 2026, upstream by Mar 2027, OEM growth from FY28; demand to moderate in Q1–Q2 but not fall; working capital back to negative. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 delivered:** raw material +16–18% (in range); replacement price ~7% in Q1 and ~11% by mid-July — the promised 10% was met; OEM double-digit from 1 July plus 3% in April; standalone margin 9.1% (14.6% Q4); Camso EBITDA negative; customer transition ~50–60% by June, 90% expected by September; capex ₹293 cr in Q1; working capital up ₹138 cr (more raw-material stock, ₹120 cr GST credit build-up); debt up ₹232 cr to ₹3,243 cr. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **New in Q1:** total price need ~16%; another 4–6% in July–August; Q2 cost +8–10%; board approved ₹1,205 cr for 53,000 two-wheeler tyres/day by FY31 (over and above Nagpur), still inside the ₹1,300–1,400 cr FY27 capex; convert US$24.5 mn of the US$80 mn loan to CEAT OHT Lanka into equity (done in Sep 2026). [Q1 call; BSE filing 23-Sep-26]
- **Verdict:** pricing promises kept; margin missed badly; Camso timing kept but economics worse than hoped; capex kept and a new project added. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹1,076 cr standalone (₹407 cr in Q4) plus ₹236 cr for Camso intangibles; consolidated investing outflow ₹2,271 cr incl. Camso. FY27 plan ₹1,300–1,400 cr. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Projects: PCUV at Chennai 95 → 130 lakh tyres a year by FY28; two-wheeler at Nagpur 80,000 → 100,000 a day; new 53,000/day two-wheeler plant (₹1,205 cr, location open, staged to FY31, debt + accruals); Camso upstream US$30 mn by Mar 2027. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Capital commitments ₹1,785 cr at Mar-26 (₹706 cr a year earlier); plus US$44 mn deferred payment for the CAMSO brand due three years after acquisition. [AR FY26 note 42]
- **Payback (my estimate):** 53,000 tyres/day × ~330 days ≈ 1.75 crore tyres a year; at an assumed ~₹1,200 per two-wheeler tyre that is ~₹2,100 cr revenue on ₹1,205 cr — asset turns ~1.7x, better than truck/car radial projects. [my calc from Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **CCI cartel penalty ₹252.16 cr** (2011–12 case) — NCLAT remanded; CCI appeal pending in Supreme Court; not provided. **Second CCI case**: Director General found CEAT and its agent S.S. Marketing cartelised a 2013 Himachal Pradesh state-transport tender; hearing pending. [AR FY26 BRSR/notes]
- Contingent liabilities (consolidated) ~₹275 cr at Mar-26 (indirect taxes the largest, ~₹164 cr) vs ~₹263 cr; table layout garbled, total approximate. [AR FY26 note 42]
- **Sri Lanka currency**: ₹48 cr loss in Q1 on the US$80 mn intra-group loan; exposure falls to ~US$56 mn after conversion. [Q1 call]
- **Camso**: EBITDA negative in Q1; depends on taking customers from Michelin and installing upstream; US tariff 10% on OHT, 25% on steel content of tracks; tracks ~50% of Camso US revenue. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Auditor B S R & Co.: unmodified opinion; key audit matters revenue recognition and Camso purchase-price allocation (consideration ₹236.5 cr for intangibles, no goodwill). [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 49]
- **Related parties**: licence fee to RPG Enterprises ~₹26 cr FY26 (~₹24 cr FY25); promoters H.V. Goenka and Anant Goenka took ₹5.0 cr and ₹4.75 cr commission as non-executive directors (₹9.75 cr of ₹10.9 cr total NED commission). A voluntary Deloitte review of related-party processes found no gaps. [AR FY26 CG report, note 40]
- US 29% tariff on on-road tyres; Middle East ~15% of international sales (near zero at end-Q4); rising Thai imports. [Q4 call; Q1 call; CARE Jan-26]
- FII holding down from 24.1% (Sep-23) to 13.8% (Jun-26). [Screener]

## My read
CEAT is the best-positioned of the three in mix — two-wheelers, passenger premium, EV fitments and OHT exports — and it has negative working capital, but it is now carrying more debt (₹3,243 cr) and a Camso integration that is losing money until at least H2 FY27. The Q1 profit collapse is mostly raw-material lag plus one-offs; if the ~16% price increase holds and rubber eases in H2, margins should return toward 12–14%. The stock is priced higher than peers on trailing earnings because trailing profit is depressed; it needs FY28 earnings to recover strongly and Camso to reach the promised double-digit margin. A second quarter of negative Camso EBITDA after September, or debt/EBITDA moving above 2.5x (CARE's trigger), would change the view.
