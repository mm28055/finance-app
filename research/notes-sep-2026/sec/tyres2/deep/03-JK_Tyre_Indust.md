# JK Tyre & Industries — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Price ₹346, market cap ₹9,970 cr, trailing P/E 13.9, book value ₹210, dividend yield 1.16%, ROCE 15.5% [Screener, 27-Sep-26]. JK Group (Singhania family; promoters 51.7%).

## Business and segments
- Truck/bus radial and bias, passenger car radials, LCV, farm, two/three-wheeler, OTR tyres. Nine plants in India (Kankroli, Banmore, Mysuru ×3, Chennai, Laksar ×3 via the Cavendish merger) and two in Mexico (JK Tornel: Tultitlan, Hidalgo). Installed capacity 38 mn+ tyres a year; TBR + PCR 210 lakh tyres a year. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- Cavendish Industries (Laksar, bought from Kesoram in 2016) merged into JK Tyre effective 22 Dec 2025, appointed date 1 Apr 2025; the auditor added an emphasis-of-matter note because FY25 figures were restated. [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 44]
- Product mix (consolidated) Q1 FY27: truck & bus 56%, passenger radial 27%, two/three-wheeler 5%, others (farm, industrial) 12%; FY26: 53% / 30% / 4% / 13%. PCR sizes 16-inch+ are 35% of PCR volume. [Q1 call; CARE Aug-26]
- Channel mix Q1 FY27: replacement 63%, OEM 27%, exports 10% (FY26: 64% / 23% / 13%). Standalone: replacement 61%, OEM 30%. [CARE Aug-26; Q4 call]
- JK Tornel (Mexico): FY26 revenue ₹2,138 cr (FY25 ₹2,147 cr), EBITDA ₹141 cr (6.6%), PAT ₹42 cr; Q4 FY26 revenue ₹378 cr, EBITDA ₹24 cr. Highest share in Mexico's mass-merchandise channel. [Q4 call]
- Utilisation Q1 FY27: ~95% in India (TBR and 2/3W nearly full, PCR 95%), ~80% consolidated (Mexico dragged). [Q1 call]
- Distribution: >6,000 dealers, 750+ Steel Wheels and 120+ Truck Wheels brand shops, exports to 100+ countries. [CARE Aug-26]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (27 May 2026) promised:** Q1 raw material +18–20% over Q4 (also "18–19%" in Q&A); replacement hikes 4–5% taken, 5–6% more planned; exports +5–7%; OEM with a lag; ₹4,980 cr new expansion on top of ₹1,130 cr under way (total ₹6,110 cr by FY29); ~₹1,200 cr cash outlay in FY27; project debt:equity 2:1; leverage to stay similar; FY27 demand buoyant; USMCA review July 2026. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 delivered:** RM +~20% (exactly as guided); price ~5% in Q1 and ~11% cumulative in replacement by August; margin 6.8% (company) vs 12.9% in Q4; net debt +₹500 cr to ₹4,945 cr; Mexico far worse than implied; India utilisation ~95% as promised; USMCA renewed for 10 years. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **New in Q1:** further 8–9% price increases planned; Q2 RM +8–10% (high-cost inventory); margins to return to "11% to 13%" in H2; FY27 margin "about 10% to 11%, if I have to make a guess"; FY27 revenue growth 10–11%; debt to rise ₹500–700 cr in FY27; +7% capacity this year (TBR, PCR balancing at Banmore). [Q1 call]
- **Verdict:** cost guidance was accurate; margin guidance reset lower for FY27; capex kept. The stored analysis that management "forecast 8–10%" RM inflation is wrong — it came from a faulty transcript copy. [Q4 call official]
- **Outside view:** CARE (27 Aug 2026) reaffirmed AA-/Stable; expects FY27 PBILDT margin 10–11%, recovery from Q3 FY27; net debt (incl. acceptances and dealer deposits)/EBITDA 3.0–3.5x over 2–3 years (2.86x FY26); downgrade if margin <10% sustained or leverage >3.5x. [CARE Aug-26]

## Capex and capacity
- **₹1,130 cr** TBR/PCR expansion under way, completion by Q3 FY28 (CARE: March 2028). **₹4,980 cr** brownfield TBR/PCR at Chennai and Mysuru, phased FY27–FY30, +~24–25% TBR/PCR capacity; total ₹6,110 cr. [Q4 call; CARE Aug-26]
- Cash outlay: company ~₹1,200 cr in FY27; CARE ₹1,600–1,800 cr in FY27 and ₹2,000–2,500 cr a year FY28–FY30; the ₹4,980 cr project alone needs ₹200–250 cr (FY27), ₹2,000–2,100 cr a year (FY28, FY29), ₹500–600 cr (FY30). Funding 2:1 debt:equity with 2–3 year moratoria. [Q4 call; CARE Aug-26]
- Recent: ₹1,398 cr of projects commissioned (Banmore PCR, Laksar TBR, all-steel radial). CWIP ₹1,100 cr at Mar-26 (₹418 cr a year earlier). FY26 free cash flow only ₹38 cr after capex. [AR FY26; Screener]
- **Payback (my estimate):** 24% more TBR/PCR capacity on a ~₹13,000–14,000 cr India base implies ~₹3,000–3,500 cr of extra revenue at today's prices for ₹4,980 cr — asset turns ~0.6–0.7x, and at 11–13% EBITDA a pre-tax payback of ~10+ years. Returns depend on truck-radial pricing staying firm. [my calc from Screener, Q4 call]

## My read
JK has the strongest volume momentum (India +25%, utilisation ~95%) and the most operational gearing, but also the weakest balance sheet, the heaviest truck-tyre exposure, a Mexican subsidiary that just broke down, and unusually high promoter pay. Management's cost forecast was accurate and the 11–13% H2 margin target is credible if rubber stays range-bound; CARE's 10–11% for FY27 is the base case. The ₹6,110 cr expansion will lift debt and depress free cash flow for three years, so the stock needs sustained 12%+ margins from FY28 to earn its cost of capital. A second weak Mexico quarter, net debt/EBITDA heading past 3.5x (CARE's trigger), or a CCI loss would weaken the case; a fast rubber decline would help JK most of the three.
