## Business and segments
- Bhartia-group holding company with six US/Canada-heavy businesses; 81% of Q1 FY27 revenue from the USA, 95% dollar-linked; specialty products 56% of revenue, specialty services (CDMO + CRDMO) 36%, generics 8%. [IP Q1FY27]
- **FY26 segment revenue / EBITDA (₹ cr):** Radiopharmaceuticals (SPECT cold kits, Ruby-Fill PET generator, MIBG pipeline) 1,178 / margin 41%; Radiopharmacy (US network of nuclear pharmacies) 2,512 / margin ~1%; CDMO Sterile Injectables (Spokane, USA + Montreal, Canada) 1,755 / 314; Allergy Immunotherapy 785 / margin 35%; Drug Discovery (CRO) 654 / 151; Generics 774 / 83 (11%); plus API (Nanjangud). [AR FY26 business reviews]
- **Inside CDMO:** Spokane revenue ₹1,715 cr (+48%), EBITDA ₹463 cr (27%); Montreal therefore ≈₹40 cr revenue and an EBITDA loss of ≈₹149 cr in FY26 (my subtraction; management said ~₹200 cr "including exceptional items"). [AR FY26; Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 segments (₹ cr, revenue / EBITDA):** Radiopharma 322 / 110 (34%, vs 46% a year ago); Radiopharmacy 700 / 12; Allergy 214 / 66; CDMO 496 / 45 — Spokane 494 / 117 (24%) and Montreal ≈2 / ≈−72 (derived); Discovery 174 / 45 (26%); API 135 / 19; Generics 173 / 4. [IP Q1FY27; own subtraction]
- **Capacity:** Spokane Line 3 (isolator fill-finish) running tech transfers for 10+ products (~80% complex biologics, incl. "one of the world's largest oncology products"); commercial batches late FY27 subject to FDA approval; Line 4 installed, tech-transfer revenue from Q4 FY27; Montreal Line 5 (CAD 145 mn project, CAD 48.8 mn government support) revenue from FY29; six PET manufacturing sites being built in the US. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 webinar promises:** "growth momentum to continue in FY27"; EBITDA margin "a story of two halves" — H1 hit by SPECT shortage, H2 margin 17–18%, representative of FY28; Line 3 revenue $60–80 mn in FY27 (mostly tech transfer), peak $80–90 mn reached 1.5–2 years earlier than planned; MIBG NDA filing in H2 FY27; Montreal FY27 losses "similar to the last" year, meaningful reduction only in FY28; FY27 capex similar to FY26's ₹1,668 cr; net debt to fall from FY28, zero by FY30. [Q4 call]
- **Clarification:** the "low double digits" growth and "38% to 40%" margin answer came from the Radiopharma CEO and refers to Radiopharma, not the whole company (the next analyst repeats it as Radiopharma's "38–42% range"). [Q4 call, official transcript]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** revenue +17% (ahead of "growth continues"); EBITDA margin 11.9% (the H1 dip was guided, but Montreal lost ≈₹72 cr in one quarter vs a guided ~₹200 cr for the full year — worse run-rate); SPECT commercial batches started, release in Q2, all SPECT products back by H2 (on track); Line 4 installation done (on track); MIBG Phase-2 dosing complete, NDA H2 FY27 (on track); net debt rose to ₹2,338 cr from ₹1,952 cr (capex phase, as flagged). [IP Q1FY27]
- **Vision 2030 (kept):** revenue ₹13,500 cr (2x FY24), EBITDA margin 23–25%, zero net debt, high-teens ROCE; CDMO >2x FY24 revenue at >25% margin; Discovery 3x; API 2x at >15%; Generics 2x at 15–17%; Allergy 1.5x at 35–40%. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹1,668 cr; FY27 "similar". Remaining spend at May 2026: Spokane Line 4 $34 mn (≈$200 mn spent), Montreal Line 5 $37 mn ($27 mn spent), PET manufacturing $85 mn ($22 mn spent). No new big projects for 12–18 months; investments must clear an ROCE hurdle. [Q4 call]
- Capital commitments ₹1,122 cr (PPE) at Mar-26 vs ₹840 cr a year earlier; CWIP ₹4,040 cr on Screener — a large pool of capital not yet earning. [AR FY26 note 37; Screener]
- Payback logic: Line 3 at $80–90 mn peak revenue; Spokane already earns 24–27% margins; management says Line 3/Line 4 margins improve once fully absorbed. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹1,952 cr (Mar-26, net debt/EBITDA 1.5x, net debt/equity 0.28) → ₹2,338 cr (Jun-26, 1.8x, 0.33). Screener gross borrowings ₹3,615 cr at Mar-26 (₹2,731 cr Mar-25). [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Operating cash flow ₹1,227 cr FY26 (CFO/operating profit 114%), but free cash flow −₹120 cr because of capex. Working capital is tight: cash-conversion cycle 47 days, working-capital days 13. [Screener]
- Dividend payout ~20% (yield ~0.5%). Promoter holding cut from 50.67% to 47.67% in Jun-2025. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Spokane: one of few North American isolator-based sterile fill-finish sites for biologics; US-based manufacturing is valued under tariff/BIOSECURE-type policies; Spokane FDA inspections closed NAI (CBER) and VAI (CDER). Switching costs are high once a biologic is filed on a line. [Q4 call; AR FY26 regulatory section]
- Radiopharma: leader in several SPECT kits (MAA, DTPA), 80% share in I-131 MIBG for neuroblastoma, Ruby-Fill install base +26% annualised with pricing and reimbursement tailwinds; MIBG has orphan status (six-month accelerated review) and will be self-commercialised. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- US radiopharmacy network is the second largest, ~1,800 customers, ~400-vehicle fleet — but SPECT radiopharmacy earns ~1% margin because of competition. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- Discovery: large-pharma share of revenue rose from <5% (FY23) to >36%. [AR FY26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Montreal FDA Warning Letter (May 2026):** after an OAI in 2024 and nine Form 483 observations in Nov 2025, the FDA issued a Warning Letter; the site runs "lean" in FY27–FY28 until Line 5 in FY29; Q1 FY27 loss ≈₹72 cr points to a worse year than the guided ~₹200 cr. The high-margin SPECT supply depends on this site until products move to third-party CMOs. [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27]
- **Goodwill and intangibles:** consolidated goodwill ₹2,826 cr plus ₹1,079 cr of intangibles under development (pipeline products) — impairment test is a key audit matter. [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report]
- **Contingent liabilities:** ~₹419 cr, mostly income tax (₹368 cr) and GST (₹35 cr); other lawsuits and investigations not quantified. [AR FY26 note 36]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinions; no key audit matters on the standalone; no Section 143(12) fraud reports; audit trail maintained. [AR FY26]
- **Execution concentration:** FY27–FY28 earnings depend on FDA approvals for Line 3 products, SPECT release from Montreal, and MIBG filing — three regulatory events in about 12 months. [Q4 call]
- **Capital intensity:** ~₹1,650 cr a year of capex with negative FCF; deleveraging only from FY28. [Q4 call; Screener]
- **Disclosure:** calls are rare (no Q1 FY27 call; the prior stored call is from FY23), so guidance is updated mainly through presentations. [Screener document list]

## My read
Two good businesses (Spokane CDMO and Radiopharma) carry the group, and Spokane is scaling well — revenue +43% and margins 24–27%. But Montreal is now under a Warning Letter and losing money faster than guided, which also starves Radiopharma of its best-margin products until H2. The stock needs H2 FY27 margins to reach the promised 17–18%, Line 3 products to win FDA approval on time, and net debt to peak by FY28. A slip in any of those three would push the FY30 targets out; a clean Montreal reinspection and on-time MIBG filing would support the view.
