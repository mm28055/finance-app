# Innova Captab (INNOVACAP) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Price ₹1,281, market cap ₹7,331 cr, trailing P/E 47.6, ROCE 15.0%, ROE 13.7% [Screener].

## Business and segments
- A domestic formulation contract maker (tablets, capsules, dry syrups, dry-powder injectables, ointments, liquids; now also blow-fill-seal parenterals and respules at Jammu) for 350+ Indian and global pharma companies, plus its own **branded generics** (domestic trade generics via Univentis Medicare, and exports to 60+ countries) and **Sharon Bio-Medicine** (acquired 2023; formulations and APIs for regulated markets — UK, Canada, Europe, Australia) [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; CARE Apr-26].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹470.9 cr: CDMO services & products ₹328.7 cr (70%, +32% YoY), branded generics ₹142.2 cr (30%, +39%) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.10]. FY26: CDMO ₹1,133 cr (+24%), branded generics ₹497 cr (+51%), total ₹1,630 cr (+31%) [Q4 call].
- Exports 32% of Q1 FY27 revenue (28% in Q4, 31% for FY26); company-level split ~70% domestic / 30% export [Q1 call; Q4 call]. CARE: FY25 domestic branded generics 18% of income, international branded 13%, exports 26% [CARE Apr-26].
- Sharon revenue ~₹240 cr in FY26, margins above group average [Q4 call].
- Plants: Baddi (3 blocks, 70–75% utilised, near saturation in general orals), Dehradun & Taloja (Sharon, near optimum), Kathua/Jammu (4 blocks: cephalosporin, penem, penicillin, general incl. injectables/BFS; ₹480+ cr invested; commercial since Jan 2025) [IP Q1FY27 pp.15–17].
- Jammu revenue: ~₹300 cr in FY26; ~₹90 cr in Q4; ₹107 cr in Q1 FY27; 25–30% annualised utilisation [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Peak ₹1,400 cr at 65–70% utilisation, asset turn "north of 3x" [Q1 call, official transcript].

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarters: Jun-25 352 / operating profit 52 (15%) / net profit 31; Sep-25 380 / 52 (14%) / 30; Dec-25 450 / 69 (15%) / 42; Mar-26 448 / 65 (15%) / 38; Jun-26 471 / 73 (16%) / 44 [Screener]. Company-reported Q1 FY27 EBITDA ₹75.1 cr (16.0%) incl. other income, PAT ₹44.1 cr (+42%) [IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- Years: FY24 1,081 / 154 (14%) / 94; FY25 1,244 / 186 (15%) / 128; FY26 1,630 / 243 (15%) / 141; TTM 1,749 / 259 / 154 [Screener]. Sales CAGR 5 yrs 32%, 3 yrs 21%; profit 3-yr CAGR 28% [Screener].
- FY26 profit grew only 10% despite 31% sales growth because depreciation rose from ₹25 cr to ₹45 cr and interest from ₹2 cr to ₹17 cr as Jammu was capitalised [Screener].
- Unit economics: Q1 FY27 volume growth 20–22% (Baddi alone 12–14%); rest from mix and price; gross margin ~35.5%, guided ±2% [Q1 call]. Ex-Jammu EBITDA margin ~18% in Q4 FY26 (agreed on call) and ~20% in Q1 FY27; Jammu EBITDA +₹1–1.5 cr in Q1 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Incentive income inside revenue:** standalone FY26 revenue includes ₹19.1 cr "GST-linked incentive" (₹3.9 cr in FY25) and other income includes ₹6.9 cr government-grant amortisation; government-grant receivable rose to ₹55.4 cr (₹26.1 cr) [AR FY26 notes 27, 28, other financial assets]. The Jammu scheme offers GST-linked incentives up to 300% of plant-and-machinery investment over 10 years plus 6% interest subvention [IP Q1FY27 p.16].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (8 May 2026): 20%+ revenue growth in FY27 (volume-based); EBITDA to grow faster than revenue and PAT faster than EBITDA; FY27 margin "should be better than the current year"; Jammu EBITDA-positive "in coming quarter"; Baddi general-block greenfield ₹150–170 cr capex over FY27–FY28 with ₹450–500 cr revenue potential [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (12 Aug 2026): delivered 34% revenue / 33% EBITDA growth; Jammu turned EBITDA-positive as promised; 20%+ volume growth kept; margin guidance restated as 15–16% ±2%, normalised 17–18% once Jammu matures ("20% would be a very high expectation"); Baddi greenfield now "at a baseline stage", no amount repeated [Q1 call].
- Verdict: delivery beat the guide in Q1; Jammu breakeven milestone met; margin language became more specific but capped at 17–18%; Baddi capex timing softened.

## Capex and capacity
- Jammu: ₹480+ cr capital investment, 4 independent blocks; ramp 5–10% (FY26) → 60–70% in 2–3 years → optimum in 5–6 years [IP Q1FY27 pp.16–17]. Management: Jammu sufficient for penem/beta-lactam/cephalosporin for 2–3 years [Q1 call].
- Fixed assets rose from ₹150 cr (FY23) to ₹780 cr (FY26) [IP Q1FY27 p.7]; Screener fixed assets ₹842 cr, CWIP ₹16 cr (Mar-26) [Screener].
- Ongoing: maintenance capex ₹20–25 cr/yr plus growth/debottlenecking ₹20–30 cr/yr until a greenfield is announced [Q1 call].
- Planned: new Baddi general oral-solid/liquid block on land bought for ~₹20 cr; ₹150–170 cr outlay, ₹450–500 cr peak revenue, 1.5 years to commercial production after start [Q4 call]. Not yet board-approved [Q1 call].
- New R&D facility coming up in Panchkula; R&D spend only 0.7–1% of revenue; 60+ scientists [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]. Semaglutide: "wave 2" follower, not first launch [Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹344 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹338 cr (Mar-25); gearing 0.36x (Mar-25); term loan ₹243.7 cr outstanding to 2032, working-capital limits ₹183 cr, utilisation ~27% [Screener; CARE Apr-26].
- Operating cash flow ₹117 cr in FY26 (59% of operating profit); free cash flow turned positive at ₹55 cr after –₹141 cr (FY24) and –₹108 cr (FY25) [Screener].
- Working capital: debtor days 98, inventory days 125 (up from 91 in FY24), payables 141, cash conversion cycle 82 days [Screener]; management targets 90 ±10 days [Q1 call].
- First dividend paid in FY26 (payout 8%) [Screener; AR FY26 note 40 dividend to promoters].
- Directors Manoj and Vinay Lohariwala give personal guarantees for bank borrowings [AR FY26 note 40].
- Rating CARE A/Positive, A1 (reaffirmed 17 Apr 2026); upgrade trigger 20% scale growth at similar margins and debt/EBITDA below 1.5x; downgrade if margin <10% or debt/EBITDA >2x [CARE Apr-26].

## Moat evidence
- Breadth and stickiness: 350+ CDMO clients, 4,200+ products; ~80% of CDMO revenue from customers with the company 5+ years (FY25) [IP Q1FY27; CARE Apr-26].
- Cost-plus/pass-through pricing on CDMO — API cost increases passed on [Q4 call; Q1 call; CARE Apr-26].
- Approvals: UK-MHRA for Baddi cephalosporin block, PIC/S (via SMDC Ukraine) for Jammu, EU-GMP, WHO-GMP; Revised Schedule M now enforced nationwide, which favours compliant contract makers [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Cost position: Jammu incentives (GST-linked + 6% interest subvention) lower effective cost vs other domestic contract makers [IP Q1FY27; CARE Apr-26].
- Weakness: low R&D (0.7–1% of sales), generic dosage forms; clients can move products at modest cost; pricing power limited (margin guide only 15–18%) [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Profit quality**: a rising share of profit comes from Jammu government incentives (₹19.1 cr GST-linked incentive in revenue, ₹55.4 cr receivable) — collection timing and scheme continuity matter; GST rate cuts (12% → 5% on many medicines) reduce GST-linked refunds [AR FY26; stored profile note on GST cut — company confirmed incentive "already factored in" on Q1 call].
- GST show-cause notice of ₹15.8 cr (excess ITC refund, May 2020–Mar 2024) was dropped on 14 Apr 2026; no contingent liabilities disclosed [AR FY26 note 44].
- Auditor (BSR & Co.): unmodified opinion; key audit matter on revenue recognition; audit-trail remark — unable to comment whether audit trail was enabled at the database layer of the two accounting packages [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Related parties: small dealings with promoter-relative entities (Azine Healthcare, Pharmatech Healthcare, Nugenic Pharma, Shubh Packaging — packing material and minor sales; exact attribution unclear in the note layout); loans to subsidiary Univentis ₹49.9 cr outstanding (₹107.1 cr prior year) [AR FY26 note 40].
- Promoter holding flat at 50.9%; FII holding has fallen from 3.6% to 0.3% since listing, DII ~20% [Screener].
- Disclosure gaps: no split of Jammu revenue between CDMO and branded; no domestic/export split by business; single operating segment [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 38].
- Execution: Jammu needs export dossier approvals (expected by end-FY27) to fill beyond domestic demand; CARE lists this as the key monitorable [Q1 call; CARE Apr-26].

## What changed between the two calls
- Jammu moved from "nearing EBITDA breakeven" to "positive ₹1–1.5 cr"; quarterly revenue from ~₹90 cr to ₹107 cr.
- Margin talk became a formula: 15–16% ±2% now, 17–18% at maturity — tempering analysts' 20% hopes.
- Baddi greenfield: from ₹150–170 cr with ₹450–500 cr potential (Q4) to "baseline stage" (Q1).
- Semaglutide/peptide talk dropped; focus on volume growth and debottlenecking.

## My read
Innova is executing well: revenue up 34% and profit up 42% in Q1, Jammu past breakeven a quarter after promising it, free cash flow positive again, and debt flat at ₹344 cr. But this is a low-margin (15–16%), low-R&D domestic contract maker whose margin ceiling management itself puts at 17–18%, and part of the profit growth is government incentive income at Jammu. At about 48x trailing earnings the price needs roughly 20%+ earnings growth for years; that is plausible for 2–3 years as Jammu fills, but depends on export approvals arriving on time. I would get more positive if Jammu reaches ₹150 cr/quarter with ~15% margin and incentives are collected in cash; negative if volume growth slips below 15% or incentive receivables keep rising.
