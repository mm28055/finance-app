# Aether Industries — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

Sources read: stored file (both call copies in full), official BSE transcripts of the Q1 FY27 call (31 Jul 2026) and Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026), Q1 FY27 results presentation and the Q1 FY27 "FAQ" document, Annual Report FY26 (MD&A, auditor's report + CARO, contingent liabilities, related parties, segment/business-model tables), Crisil rating rationale (23 Sep 2026), Screener consolidated page (raw HTML table rows).

## Business and segments
- Surat-based specialty-chemicals maker that sells "difficult chemistry": advanced intermediates and specialty chemicals made by hard processes (high pressure, hydrogenation, continuous flow, phosphorus chemistry). One reported segment; management runs three business models. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Business-model split FY26 (₹ cr):** contract/exclusive manufacturing (CEM) 523 (FY25: 263, i.e. doubled); large-scale manufacturing (LSM, its own-brand products sold at market prices) 489 (FY25: 462); CRAMS (contract research fees) 105 (FY25: 103); others 14. CEM + CRAMS = 55% of FY26 revenue vs 39% in FY24. [AR FY26 MD&A; Crisil Sep-26]
- **Q1 FY27 split:** CEM ₹165.6 cr (51.5%), LSM ₹125.8 cr (39.1%), CRAMS ₹30.1 cr (9.3%). CEM grew ~75% YoY and CRAMS ~20%; CEM + CRAMS = 60% of revenue. [IP Q1FY27; FAQ Q1FY27]
- **End-market split Q1 FY27:** pharma 32.2%, oil & gas 31.4% (crossed ₹100 cr in a quarter for the first time), material science 16.6%, agro 9.5%, photography 3.2%, coatings 1.7%. A year earlier pharma was 35.1%, oil & gas 19.2%, agro 11.1%. [IP Q1FY27; FAQ Q1FY27] Note: on the call management said oil & gas is "approximately 20%" — that is the FY26 figure (Site 4 = 21% of FY26 sales); the Q1 FY27 share is 31.4%. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Key contracts:** Baker Hughes (oilfield chemicals; dedicated Site 4 "Strata") — ₹70 cr revenue in Q1 FY27, up from ~₹45 cr in Q1 FY26 and ₹68 cr in Q4 FY26; Milliken (10-year single-product supply deal, Site 3++ "Ascend", booking revenue since Q4 FY26); Saudi Aramco (Converge polyol, target sales ₹65–75 cr in FY27); Seqens; a smaller European material-science CEM contract now commercial at Ascend. [FAQ Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26]
- **Geography:** FY26 revenue India ₹920 cr vs rest of world ₹240 cr (FY25: ₹510 cr / ₹331 cr). Exports fell because CEM customers (e.g. Baker Hughes India) buy through Indian subsidiaries. Q1 FY27: 62.5% domestic, 37.5% exports incl. deemed exports. Management expects exports to return to 50–60%. [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- **Capacity/utilisation:** Q1 FY27 — Site 2 74%, Site 3 69%, Site 4 59% (FAQ: Strata 58%, Ascend 72%). Q4 FY26 — 75% / 70% / 55%. Whole company ~65% in FY26. [Q1 call; FAQ Q1FY27; Q4 call; AR FY26]
- **Site 5 "Magnum" (Panoli):** room for 16 production blocks plus 4 on the adjoining 15-acre Site 5+ (20 in all). Phase 1 = 2 blocks, commercial production began Q1 FY27, revenue from Q2 FY27; "strong visibility for the first 10 production blocks". [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- **Quarters (sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 257 / 81 / 32% / 47; Sep-25 280 / 88 / 31% / 54; Dec-25 319 / 112 / 35% / 64; Mar-26 305 / 83 / 27% / 54; Jun-26 327 / 103 / 31% / 63. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 sales +27% YoY, operating profit +27%, net profit +33%. Company-reported EBITDA ₹102.8 cr, margin 31.5%; PAT ₹62.7 cr. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Q3 FY26 was flattered by a ₹20 cr loss-of-profit insurance receipt (₹15 cr EBITDA effect); Q4 FY26 was hit by a ₹7 cr inventory write-off (fire at an external warehouse, 11 Mar 2026) plus ~₹1 cr year-end provisions. Adjusted, the underlying margin has been ~30–31% for five quarters. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Years:** FY24 598 / 132 / 22% / 82 (fire year); FY25 841 / 243 / 29% / 158; FY26 1,160 / 364 / 31% / 219; TTM 1,231 / 386 / 235. 3-year sales CAGR 21%, profit CAGR 20%. [Screener]
- **Unit economics:** LSM prices +22.5% YoY and +1% QoQ in Q1 FY27; LSM volume −22% YoY (lines moved to CEM) but +11.7% QoQ. Gross margin 49.8% vs 47.9% a year ago. New Magnum LSM products priced at $30–40/kg; semiconductor monomer ~$50/kg. [FAQ Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Returns:** ROCE 12% FY26 (10% FY25, 7% FY24), ROE ~10% — low because ₹516 cr of capital work in progress and a large inventory earn nothing yet. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026) promises:** EBITDA margin 29–30% and PAT margin 19–20% for FY27; FY27 capex ₹300–350 cr; extra debt of ₹200–250 cr by end-FY27 "but not more"; working-capital cycle to fall from 179 days (Mar-26) to ~160 days by end-FY27, target 150; Site 5 phase-1 products commissioned by end-May/early June; fixed-asset fire claim settled by end-Q1 FY27; LSM prices to stay high "for the next 2–3 quarters"; exports back to 50–60% of sales; CEM + CRAMS to 70% of revenue by FY30. No revenue guidance ("We don't provide specific revenue guidance"). [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** EBITDA margin 31.5% (beat); PAT margin 19% (met); Site 5 phase 1 commercial in Q1 (met, on time); full fire claim received in Q1 (met); LSM prices held (+22.5% YoY); CEM + CRAMS at 60% (on track); working capital "marginally lower than March 2026" (small progress); exports 37.5% (not yet); FY27 capex guidance kept at ₹300–350 cr, Q1 spend ₹94 cr. [Q1 call; FAQ Q1FY27]
- **Kept / raised:** margin guidance kept at ~30% for FY27; core-business growth framed as "25% to 30% compounding" (Rohan Desai, Q1 call). Site 5 total investment now stated as ₹2,200–2,300 cr phased to 2030 with 1.5–1.75x asset turns. This is not an increase from the Q4 figure of ₹1,500–1,600 cr — that figure covered Site 5 plus R&D over the next four years, not the full site. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **New targets:** semiconductor block (45 t/month in 3 streams of 15 t) online "end of September" (call) / "by Q3 FY2027" (FAQ); one more block for a European major's CEM contract by Q3 FY27. First phase semiconductor volume 400 t a year, expected to triple by 2030. [Q1 call; FAQ Q1FY27; Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹383.8 cr; FY27 guided ₹300–350 cr (~₹60 cr at Site 1 "Catalyst" R&D, rest at Magnum). Q1 FY27 ₹94.3 cr, of which ₹83.3 cr at Magnum. [Q4 call; FAQ Q1FY27]
- AR FY26 plan: ₹100 cr for a new R&D building (120 fume hoods, 8 technical labs, ready Q2 FY28) and ₹250 cr for Magnum Phase 2 (4 new blocks, production in FY28). [AR FY26 MD&A; FAQ Q1FY27]
- Full Magnum build-out ₹2,200–2,300 cr by 2030 at 1.5–1.75x asset turns implies ₹3,300–4,000 cr of revenue at maturity — roughly three times FY26 sales. This is a management target, not a contract book. [Q1 call; own arithmetic]
- Funding: IPO (2022) and QIP (2025) money fully used by Q3 FY26; now term loans plus internal accruals. Crisil expects cash accruals of ₹280–300 cr a year against ₹40–60 cr yearly repayments and gearing below 0.5x. [Q4 call; Crisil Sep-26]
- Dow silicones programme (announced 30 Jul 2026): exclusive, multi-year, Dow-funded research and pilot work at Aether; possible later commercial framework and a dedicated block or site. No revenue date given — "we cannot put a revenue date on this right now". [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹458 cr at Mar-26 (₹200 cr Mar-25); at 30 Jun 2026 ₹421.5 cr working-capital loans + ₹100 cr term loans = ₹521.5 cr. Cash only ₹5.8 cr at Mar-26 (Crisil). Net debt ≈ ₹515 cr against equity of ~₹2,456 cr — low gearing (Crisil: 0.18x). [Screener; FAQ Q1FY27; Crisil Sep-26]
- Operating cash flow ₹142 cr FY26 (₹100 cr FY25; negative FY22–FY24); free cash flow negative every year: −₹481 cr FY26, −₹352 cr FY25. Cash conversion (CFO/operating profit) 54% FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital is the weak spot: debtor days 123, inventory days 345, cash-conversion cycle 341 days (Screener FY26); Crisil puts gross current assets at ~360 days (490 a year earlier), receivables 120–130 days, inventory 250–260 days; bank lines ~75% used. Company's own working-capital cycle: 179 days Mar-26 (194 Mar-25). [Screener; Crisil Sep-26; Q4 call]
- No dividend, no buyback. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- **Customer validation:** Baker Hughes grew from zero to ~₹70 cr a quarter in two years with a dedicated plant; Milliken signed a 10-year deal; Dow chose Aether as exclusive research partner for new silicone manufacturing technology; 19 marquee clients and 50+ audits in FY26, 10 more clients and 9 audits in Q1 FY27. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26]
- **Pricing power:** LSM prices up 22.5% YoY and gross margin up ~190 bps while raw-material increases were passed on; management says it "never lost market share" to China even when prices fell 30–35% after COVID. [FAQ Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Contract structure:** CEM contracts are long-term take-or-pay or minimum-volume with open-book cost pass-through ("it is an open cost P&L"). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Diversification improving:** top 5 products fell to 31.0% of revenue from 43.7%; top 10 customers 40.3% (45.9% FY25). [AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Rating:** Crisil AA-/Stable / A1+ assigned 23 Sep 2026 on ₹636 cr bank lines, citing strong market position, 300+ customers, margins of 29–32% and low leverage. [Crisil Sep-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration rising:** largest customer 18.98% of FY26 revenue (10.0% in FY24) — almost certainly Baker Hughes; management expects concentration to rise further with CEM before Site 5 dilutes it. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Working capital and cash burn:** 341-day cash cycle; FCF negative in each of the last five years; the business still depends on debt and equity raises to grow. Crisil lists further stretch in working capital as a downgrade trigger, along with margins below 25–26%. [Screener; Crisil Sep-26]
- **Safety:** major fire at Site 2 (Nov 2023) cut FY24 profit; second fire at an external warehouse (Mar 2026, ₹7 cr inventory loss, no injuries). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Related parties:** FY26 transactions with director-controlled companies ₹17.3 cr (₹11.9 cr FY25); managerial pay ₹6.7 cr; in FY25 the company bought land (₹10.9 cr) and a building (₹8.0 cr) from related parties. All approved under Sections 177/188 per CARO. [AR FY26 related-party note; AR FY26 CARO]
- **Contingent liabilities are small:** ~₹15 cr of bank guarantees plus a ₹4.3 cr GST show-cause notice alleging ineligible input credit on IPO expenses (under appeal). [AR FY26 contingent-liabilities note]
- **Auditor (Suresh I Surana & Associates):** unmodified opinion; key audit matters are revenue cut-off, capex capitalisation and inventory valuation; CARO clean — no defaults, bank stock statements agree with books, audit-trail software operated all year, no whistle-blower complaints. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Execution bandwidth:** Site 3++, Site 4 ramp, Magnum phases, a semiconductor block, the Dow programme and a new R&D centre all run at once; the family team of four runs the company. [Q1 call]
- **Promoter stake:** fell from 81.8% to 75.0% in Jun-25 (QIP), now 74.92%. No pledge disclosed. [Screener]
- **Disclosure gaps:** no order book, no contract values, no product names for Magnum or semiconductor contracts ("For competitive reasons, we do not break out our order book"). [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from war-driven pricing (Q4: "unprecedented disruption", LSM prices +20% YoY) to a technology-platform story (Q1: semiconductor monomers and the Dow silicones programme).
- Numbers improved: margin back to 31.5% from 26.7%; Baker Hughes steady at ~₹70 cr; oil & gas now 31% of sales.
- New issues: exports still only 37.5%; working capital barely moved; debt up ~₹64 cr in the quarter; semiconductor revenue is small-order only until the Magnum block starts.

## My read
The business is improving: CEM doubled in FY26, grew 75% in Q1 FY27, and margins have held around 30–31% with real pricing power in LSM. The weak points are cash — a 341-day cash cycle and negative free cash flow every year — and rising dependence on one customer. At a trailing P/E near 98 (Screener), the price assumes Magnum ramps on schedule and the semiconductor and Dow options turn into revenue; the next two quarters must show Magnum revenue, a falling working-capital cycle and exports recovering. A delay in Magnum blocks, a margin slip below ~28%, or another safety incident would change the view.
