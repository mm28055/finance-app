# Senores Pharmaceuticals (SENORES) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Price ₹1,355, market cap ₹6,241 cr, trailing P/E 49.4, ROCE 15.1%, ROE 13.4%; listed Dec 2024 (IPO ₹500 cr at ₹391) [Screener; AR FY26 IPO note].

## Business and segments
- Not a classic CDMO: roughly half own US generics (ANDAs sold via partners on a base price + **profit share**, or via its own front ends) and half CDMO/CMO for US partners (~52–53% own products, 47–48% CDMO/CMO) [Q4 call]. Plus emerging-market exports (WHO-GMP Chhatral plant, 40+ countries), India branded generics (hospitals, ~120–130 reps), and small API sales [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹180.2 cr: Regulated markets ₹127.8 cr (71%, +42% YoY, EBITDA margin ~40%), Emerging markets ₹37.6 cr (21%, +30%, margin ~14%), India branded generics ₹8.0 cr (–2.4%), others incl. API ₹6.8 cr [IP Q1FY27 segment table]. FY26: regulated ₹427.4 cr (+75%), emerging ₹145.0 cr (+20%), branded ₹40.0 cr (~5x), others ₹20.3 cr; total ₹632.6 cr [IP Q1FY27].
- Plants: USFDA-approved oral-solid plant in Atlanta, US (3 lines; controlled substances); USFDA-approved **Apnar Pharma**, Vadodara (75% acquired, consolidated from 16 Jan 2026; also UK-MHRA and Health Canada approved); WHO-GMP Chhatral (orals, injectables, liquids; PIC/S inspection done); Naroda API plant [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; AR FY26 note 46].
- Portfolio: 58 approved ANDAs (187 strengths), 23 commercialised (51 strengths), 35 approved yet to launch, 39 molecules under development/filed; approved ANDAs up from 30 in Jun-25 — partly through purchased ANDAs [IP Q1FY27 p.5; Q1 call].
- US front ends: Zoraya Pharmaceuticals (51%, own label, starting Sep–Oct 2026), Senores USA/Amerisyn JV (70%, federal/VA/military procurement), Havix, Ratnatris (73.28%) [IP Q1FY27 corporate structure; Q1 call].
- Apnar: 30 million units in Q1 FY27; 6 products commercialised; 18 mapped; 80–90% production-capacity utilisation (much of it qualification batches); USFDA inspection completed 11 Sep 2026 with one minor procedural observation [Q1 call; BSE filing 11 Sep 2026 via Screener].

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarters: Jun-25 revenue 138 / operating profit 34 (25%) / net profit 21; Sep-25 162 / 50 (31%) / 30; Dec-25 171 / 50 (29%) / 34; Mar-26 175 / 47 (27%) / 37; Jun-26 180 / 54 (30%) / 30 [Screener]. Company Q1 FY27: EBITDA ₹53.8 cr (29.8%, +87%), PAT after minority ₹31 cr (+56%), gross margin 63.5% (53.7%) [IP Q1FY27 p.8].
- Years: FY24 215 / 42 (19%) / 33; FY25 398 / 90 (23%) / 58; FY26 633 / 168 (27%) / 122; TTM 688 / 201 / 131 [Screener]. 3-yr sales CAGR 162% (IPO-era acquisitions) [Screener].
- **Other income is a large share of profit**: ₹47 cr in FY26 (30% of ₹159 cr pre-tax profit), incl. ₹18 cr in Q4 FY26 (mostly forex); only ₹3 cr in Q1 FY27 — hence Q1 net profit fell from ₹37 cr to ₹30 cr sequentially [Screener; Q1 call]. Forex is now shown below EBITDA [Q1 call].
- Company's headline "income" includes other income: FY26 "₹663–664 cr" and Q4 "₹190 cr" vs revenue from operations ₹632.6 cr and ₹175 cr [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- Unit economics: emerging-market realisation per unit up from ~₹1.20 to ~₹1.90 in 18 months; US EBITDA "upwards of 40%" [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (14 May 2026): FY27 revenue +30–40%, PAT +50–60%, blended EBITDA 29–31%; Apnar ₹80–100 cr revenue in FY27, ₹180–200 cr in 2–3 years; emerging markets ₹180 cr at 18–20% margin; India branded ₹60–70 cr; Amerisyn JV ₹50–100 cr; US aspiration ₹2,500–3,000 cr (also "₹3,000–4,000 cr") in 3–4 years; FY27 capex ~₹200 cr incl. ₹100 cr IPO money for sterile injectables at Atlanta; free cash flow positive in FY27 [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 call (27 Jul 2026): FY27 guidance kept; long-range now "₹2,500–3,000 cr revenue in 3–4 years" (company-wide, not US-only), NDAs excluded; 30% margin "as the minimum"; India branded now ₹50–60 cr (cut from ₹60–70 cr); FY27 capex ₹100–120 cr (cut from ~₹200 cr); sterile-injectable project scaled down to a pilot and ~₹100 cr IPO money redirected to oral-solid capacity (change of objects put to AGM) [Q1 call, official].
- Delivery: Q1 revenue +36% and EBITDA +87% — on track; emerging-market margin 14% vs 18–20% full-year guide; branded generics fell 2%; launches in Q1 "not significant enough" [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 spend ~₹230 cr incl. an ANDA acquisition of ~₹65 cr (the official transcript says the ₹65 cr was for ANDAs, not the Apnar plant) [Q4 call, official]. Screener investing outflow ₹121 cr (FY26) and ₹429 cr (FY25) [Screener].
- Fixed assets jumped from ₹301 cr to ₹755 cr in FY26 (Apnar and ANDA intangibles/goodwill) [Screener].
- FY27: ₹100–120 cr across subsidiaries — Apnar 3rd line under way, 4th planned; Atlanta 4th line deferred; injectable pilot; ₹60–75 cr "minimum run rate" next year [Q1 call].
- IPO money: ₹100.02 cr of ₹107 cr earmarked for the Atlanta sterile-injectable facility was unused at 31 Mar 2026 (15 months after listing); ₹105.02 cr total unutilised [AR FY26 IPO utilisation table].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹342 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹315 cr; free cash flow –₹203 cr in both FY25 and FY26; operating cash flow ₹62 cr in FY26 (company says ~₹75 cr), first positive year [Screener; Q4 call].
- Debtor days 187 (114 in FY25); working-capital days 132 [Screener]. Management: ex-Apnar net working capital ~104 days [Q4 call].
- **Unbilled profit-share receivable**: "other financial assets" rose from ₹116 cr to ₹172 cr (FY26) — revenue booked for expected profit share before partners sell the goods; management says collection takes 1–6 months and the balance will "plateau and then start decreasing" [Q4 call]. Accounting policy recognises profit share "to the extent highly probable that a significant reversal will not occur" [AR FY26 policy 3.1.3].
- No dividend [Screener]. No credit-rating rationale found on Screener's list (IPO monitoring agency is CARE) [AR FY26].

## Moat evidence
- Niche US generics: controlled substances and limited-competition products; some first-to-market/CGT launches; total accessible market for approved products ~$1 bn [Q4 call].
- Dual-shore USFDA plants (Atlanta + Vadodara) — can shift production for cost or tariffs [Q1 call].
- Partnerships: "All products in the pipeline or approved already have commercial partners mapped" [Q1 call].
- Emerging markets: 500 registered products, ~930 under registration [Q1 call].
- Weaknesses: small scale; ANDA count built partly by acquisition; value of profit-share model depends on partners' pricing; no customer concentration data disclosed.

## Risks and red flags
- **Profit quality**: 30% of FY26 pre-tax profit from other income (mostly forex); unbilled profit-share receivable of ₹172 cr ≈ 27% of FY26 revenue [Screener; Q4 call].
- **Cash burn**: two years of –₹203 cr free cash flow; promised FY27 free cash flow is unproven [Screener; Q4 call].
- **Capital-plan changes**: IPO objects changed (sterile injectables → pilot + oral solids); FY27 capex guide halved between calls [Q1 call; AR FY26].
- Auditor: Pankaj R Shah & Associates (small firm); unmodified opinion; key audit matter on revenue recognition timing; relies on other auditors for subsidiaries [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Contingent liabilities ₹30.6 cr incl. disputed income-tax ₹20.5 cr [AR FY26 note 41].
- Related parties: a web of promoter-linked entities (Remus Pharmaceuticals, Renosen, Espee Therapeutics, Senores Biologics, Aelius Projects) — amounts small (mostly <₹5 cr) but numerous; borrowings from relatives [AR FY26 note 46].
- US tariff risk on imported generics (talked of for 2028); management waiting for India–US trade deal [Q1 call].
- Minority interests in many subsidiaries (Apnar 75%, Zoraya 51%, Amerisyn 70%) leak profit [IP Q1FY27].
- Exchange sought clarification on unusual trading volume, 21 Sep 2026 — company said no undisclosed event [BSE via Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Capital plan: sterile-injectable greenfield → pilot; ₹100 cr IPO money to oral solids; capex FY27 ₹200 cr → ₹100–120 cr.
- Long-range goal restated as company-wide ₹2,500–3,000 cr (Q4 had US-only and even ₹3,000–4,000 cr).
- Focus moved from Amerisyn JV and profit-share receivables (Q4) to Apnar scale-up and Zoraya launch (Q1).
- Emerging-market margin fell 20% → 14% (seasonality claimed); branded generics declined.

## My read
Senores has grown very fast and its regulated-market business earns ~40% EBITDA, but the numbers need care: a third of last year's profit was other income, a large profit-share receivable sits on the balance sheet, free cash flow has been –₹203 cr two years running, and the stored call copies inflate Q1 EBITDA by ₹10 cr. The FY27 guide (30–40% revenue, 50–60% PAT) is achievable if the 35 unlaunched ANDAs roll out, but Q1 launches added little. At about 49x trailing earnings the price needs roughly 25%+ annual earnings growth for years. I would turn more positive on cash conversion (unbilled receivables falling, positive free cash flow in FY27) and Zoraya revenue; negative if other income keeps propping up profit or the Apnar observation escalates.
