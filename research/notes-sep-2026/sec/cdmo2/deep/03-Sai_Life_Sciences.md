# Sai Life Sciences (SAILIFE) — deep research (Phase A1)

Price ₹1,592, market cap ₹33,821 cr, 52-week ₹784–1,697, no dividend, promoters (Kanumuri family) 34.53%, FIIs 19.65%, DIIs 32.71%; no pledge shown [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Hyderabad CRDMO for small-molecule new drugs (NCEs): discovery chemistry/biology/DMPK (CRO) and process development + API manufacturing (CDMO). Sites: Hyderabad R&D campus, Bidar (Karnataka) 700 KL cGMP plant, labs in Greater Boston (US) and Manchester (UK) [IP Q1FY27].
- FY26 revenue ₹2,192 cr, +29%: CDMO ₹1,417 cr (+33%), CRO ₹775 cr (+24%). Customers: 300+, including 19 of the top 25 pharma companies; pharma = 91% of CDMO revenue; CRO split 48% pharma / 52% biotech [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27: CDMO ~60% (+6% YoY), CRO ~40% (+24% per presentation, +26% per call) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Pipeline: 33 active commercial molecules, 14 late-phase (Phase 3/pre-registration), ~155 programmes in total; six late-phase additions in 15 months, five via large-pharma FTE (dedicated-team) contracts [Q1 call]. New modalities (peptides, ADC, oligos, lipids) only 4% of FY26 revenue, down from 7% in FY24 [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Concentration: top 1 customer 12%, top 5 37%, top 10 54% of FY26 revenue [Q4 call]. One customer >10% = ₹267 cr in FY26 (two customers, ₹364 cr, in FY25) [AR FY26 consolidated note 42]. Exports ~99% [ICRA Feb-26].
- Capacity utilisation 65% (Q1 FY27) [IP Q1FY27]. Bidar 700 KL, going to 1,150 KL (225 KL in H2 FY27, 225 KL in FY28) [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM: Jun-25 496 / 121 / 24%; Sep-25 537 / 146 / 27%; Dec-25 556 / 188 / 34%; Mar-26 602 / 177 / 29%; Jun-26 554 / 148 / 27%. Net profit: 60, 84, 100, 104, 73 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: revenue +12% YoY but −8% QoQ; material margin 74%; EBITDA ₹148 cr (27% incl. forex), +18%; PAT ₹73 cr, +22% [IP Q1FY27].
- Annual: FY24 sales 1,465, OP 287 (20%), PAT 83; FY25 1,695, 410 (24%), 170; FY26 2,192, 634 (29%), 349; TTM 2,250 / 658 / 362 [Screener]. Company EBITDA FY26 ₹661 cr (30.1%) incl. forex [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Profit has compounded fast from a low base (PAT ₹6 cr in FY22) as interest fell after the Dec-2024 IPO repaid debt (interest ₹88 cr FY24 → ₹41 cr FY26) [Screener; ICRA Feb-26].
- Unit economics: management says pharma "open-book pricing" keeps margins in a 25–30% band, as at large Chinese CDMOs; 28–30% is the steady-state aim [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026):** revenue growth 15–20% and EBITDA margin 28–30% "over a three-year period"; H2 FY27 stronger than H1 (FY26 split was 48/52 vs usual 40/60); FY27 capex ₹1,100–1,300 cr (65% CDMO / 35% CRO; 75% capacity, 25% capability), funded by accruals plus debt; Bidar first 225 KL in H2 FY27; three of four new commercial molecules to produce revenue in FY27; cost inflation from West Asia, price revisions sought but recovery "may not always be contemporaneous"; net asset turn 1.2–1.4x medium term, with "a little bit of a dip" first [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (7 Aug 2026):** revenue +12% — **below** the 15–20% band but consistent with the "weaker H1" warning; EBITDA 27% — **slightly below** 28–30%; CDMO +6% — **soft**; production started on 3 of 4 new molecules — **delivered**; new discovery capacity came on stream in Q1 and is "already sold out" — **delivered**; Bidar block on schedule, "one, two months" possible slip; capex ₹263 cr in Q1 [Q1 call, official; IP Q1FY27].
- **Guidance now:** 15–20% and 28–30% **kept**; FY27 capex ₹1,100–1,300 cr **kept**; no FY28 capex guidance; "over 90% pipeline visibility on FY27 targets" [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- FY26 track record: management's 15–20% aim was beaten (29% growth, EBITDA +56%, PAT +109%) [Q4 call].
- Arithmetic (own calc): FY26 Q2–Q4 revenue was ₹1,695 cr. To reach +15% for FY27 (~₹2,520 cr) Sai needs ~₹1,970 cr in Q2–Q4 (+16% YoY); +20% needs ~+22%. Achievable only if Bidar's new block ramps on time.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹633 cr vs ₹700 cr budget [Q4 call]. FY27 ₹1,100–1,300 cr — roughly twice FY26 and ~6x the ~₹200 cr of two years earlier [Q4 call].
- Projects: Bidar 700 → 1,150 KL (two 225 KL blocks, H2 FY27 and FY28); doubled process R&D capacity; new 100,000 sq ft R&D facility at Genome Valley (operational); peptide pilot plant and a dedicated peptide development lab for a top-tier pharma; greenfield site at Choutuppal (land acquired) with commercial peptide capacity in 2028 (peptide spend "less than ₹300 cr"); early-phase formulation (oral solids up to Phase 2) in ~6 months; XDC (conjugates) centre [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Capital commitments ₹354 cr at Mar-26 (₹103 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 note 43].
- Payback: company targets net asset turn 1.2–1.4x; brownfield 12–18 months to build, greenfield 24–30 months, then a 2–3 year ramp [Q4 call]. On ₹1,200 cr at 1.3x, ~₹1,550 cr of eventual revenue (own calc) — but not before FY29.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹16 cr at Mar-26 vs net cash ₹161 cr at Mar-25 [AR FY26 consolidated capital-management note]. Borrowings ₹288 cr incl. leases [Screener]. ICRA at Sep-25: total debt ₹418 cr (lease ₹202 cr), cash ₹306 cr, debt/OPBDITA 0.8x, interest cover 12.3x [ICRA Feb-26].
- Cash flow: CFO ₹509 cr FY26 (86% of operating profit); free cash flow negative for two years (−₹55 cr FY25, −₹83 cr FY26) because of capex [Screener]. With FY27 capex of ₹1,100–1,300 cr against CFO of perhaps ₹600–700 cr (an analyst's figure the CFO did not dispute), net debt should rise by ~₹500–600 cr; the CFO only said debt/EBITDA would be "fairly healthy" [Q4 call].
- Working capital: debtor days 62, inventory days 97, payable days 182 → cash conversion cycle −24 days (FY26) [Screener]; customer advances fund part of the business.
- No dividend [Screener].
- Rating: [ICRA]AA (Stable)/A1+ assigned 26 Feb 2026; downgrade trigger is debt/OPBDITA >1.5x sustained with cash depletion [ICRA Feb-26].

## Moat evidence
- Relationship depth: 19 of the top 25 pharma companies; large-pharma share of revenue rose from 28% (FY22) to 49% (FY26); returning customers >90% of revenue in FY25 and FY26 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Switching costs: FTE model — a top-tier pharma engagement now runs end-to-end from discovery to commercial; pharma's "primary stated intent is to leave it with Sai" for manufacturing [Q1 call].
- Track record: contributed to five FDA-approved molecules in CY2025 and 17 launches in five years; 100% successful regulatory inspections claimed; USFDA/PMDA-approved sites [Q1 call, official; IP Q1FY27].
- Integration: >65% of discovery customers take more than one service [IP Q1FY27].
- Pricing power is limited by design — open-book pricing with pharma caps margins at ~28–30% [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Insider-trading code breaches**: four designated persons traded/pledged without pre-clearance; the audit committee fined them ₹11.1 lakh (deposited with SEBI IPEF on 25 May 2026); a monitoring system was installed [AR FY26 secretarial audit report].
- Heavy capex with falling free cash flow: FY27 spend is ~2x FY26 and will push the company into net debt; utilisation is only 65% today [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- CDMO growth slowed to 6% in Q1; new-modality revenue share fell from 7% to 4% in two years [IP Q1FY27].
- Contingent liabilities ₹97 cr (₹58 cr a year ago), mostly income-tax demands of ₹86 cr [AR FY26 note 43]. Not material to the balance sheet but rising.
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter is revenue recognition on fixed-price contracts measured by percentage of completion (judgement-heavy) [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Cost inflation from West Asia (inputs, logistics) with lagged pass-through [Q4 call].
- Concentration: top 10 = 54% of revenue [Q4 call].
- Unverified stored claim: the stored profile's "₹34 cr provision in March FY25, ₹16 cr reversed in Q3 FY26" — not found in the documents read; Q3 FY26's 34% OPM is consistent with a one-off write-back but I could not confirm the amount.

## What changed between the two calls
- Numbers: from +29% growth in FY26 to +12% in Q1 FY27; margin from 30% (FY26) to 27% [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Strategy: a broader "multi-modality" push — peptide commercial plant (2028), formulation (oral solids), XDC centre — added on top of the small-molecule capex [Q1 call].
- Commercial: pilot discovery collaboration converted into a long-term high-volume partnership; another large-pharma FTE deal expected to close by end-Q2, work from Q3 [Q1 call].
- Environment: biotech funding described as recovering (~18 US IPOs; YTD funding +52% by April) [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## My read
Sai is a genuine share-gainer — FY26 revenue +29% and profit doubled, big-pharma relationships deepened, and the rating is AA — but FY27 is a heavy-investment, slower year: Q1 grew 12% with CDMO at 6%, margins dipped to 27%, and capex of up to ₹1,300 cr will turn the company net-debt. Management caps its own margin at ~30% because of open-book pricing, so earnings growth must come from volume, and volume depends on Bidar's new blocks filling after H2 FY27. The price assumes the 15–20% path holds for years; I would want to see H2 FY27 revenue growth above 18% and CDMO growth recovering to double digits before getting more positive. A slip in the Bidar timeline or another 6%-type CDMO quarter would argue for caution.
