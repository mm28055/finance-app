## Business and segments
- Bengaluru CRDMO (contract research, development and manufacturing) for innovator drugs — small molecules (NCEs), biologics (NBEs), plus peptides, oligonucleotides, ADC payloads, RNAi and high-potency APIs. Customers are mostly emerging US/EU biotechs; ~3 of the top 20 big pharma plus two added directly in FY26 and one more in Q1 FY27 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Two segments: CRDMO ₹1,773 cr (83.4%, +17.7%) and Specialty Ingredients ₹352 cr (16.6%, +3.9%) in FY26 — probiotics, enzymes (serratiopeptidase), vitamin K2-7, nutraceuticals, sold mostly in India/RoW [AR FY26 segment note; Q4 call].
- FY26 revenue mix: commercial manufacturing ~60%, development/clinical batches ~15%, discovery R&D 8–9%, specialty ingredients ~17% [Q4 call]. Four molecules went commercial in FY26 (portfolio now 14 commercial molecules); they already give 8–9% of revenue from launch stocking [Q4 call]. Pipeline: 100+ early-stage programmes, 10 in Phase 3 (a couple are ADCs) [Q1 call].
- Geography FY26: Europe ₹1,145 cr (54%), USA ₹580 cr (27%), India ₹316 cr (15%), rest ₹84 cr; exports 85% of revenue [AR FY26 segment note].
- **Customer concentration is high**: three customers above 10% of revenue = 56.7% of FY26 sales (21.1%, 20.9%, 14.7%), down from 61.2% in FY25 [AR FY26 consolidated note 41]. Top 10 customers = 76.4%; top five products = 50–55% of revenue [ICRA Jul-26].
- Capacity: ~425 KL custom synthesis and ~180 KL fermentation (Unit 1 Bommasandra, Unit 2 Harohalli expanded by 130 KL to 376 KL in FY26 for ~₹150 cr, Unit 3 NeoAnthem ~₹550 cr) [AR FY26 MD&A; ICRA Jul-26]. Q1 FY27 utilisation: Unit 1 ~78%, Unit 2 ~50% (65% in FY26), Unit 2 fermentation ~50%, Unit 3 30–35% (15% in FY26) [Q1 call, official]. Unit 3 fermentation block to be functional from July 2026 [ICRA Jul-26].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (20 May 2026):** no formal guidance, but "we have delivered growth in the vicinity of 20% and more, and that's what we aspire to"; destocking "mostly behind us… they are restocking now"; EBITDA margin 38–40% "we'll continue to deliver"; Specialty Ingredients aspiration ~20% growth; FY27 capex ~₹700 cr, then ~₹500 cr; Unit 3 to turn positive in FY27; GLP-1 (semaglutide API) approval "six months… eight months"; biosimilar project to hit the P&L "next year" [Q4 call].
- **FY26 vs its own start-of-year aim:** CFO admitted FY26 aimed at ~20% revenue growth but delivered ~15%; EBITDA and PAT grew ~30% instead [Q4 call]. → revenue **missed**, profit **beat**.
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (22 Jul 2026):** revenue −22.6% YoY, blamed on customers deferring shipments (inventory re-routed between countries, slower country approvals) — the "restocking" call **missed** in Q1. Margin 39.6% — **kept**. Specialty −11.5% (war-related raw-material disruption) — **missed** vs the 20% aspiration. Semaglutide still awaiting CDSCO; now "a quarter or two" — **slipped** slightly. Unit 3 utilisation up to 30–35% — on track [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Guidance now:** full-year growth "intact… in line with our historical growth performance", but "we are not giving a guidance of any particular percentage"; Q1 "the softest quarter"; order book covers ~60% of FY27 needs (for the whole business); tax rate ~25% for the year; ESOP charge ₹9 cr in FY27 (₹16 cr FY26), ~₹5 cr FY28 [Q1 call]. The 20% number was **softened** from an "aspiration" to "historical double-digit".
- Arithmetic check (own calc from Screener): Q2–Q4 FY26 sales were ₹1,584 cr. To grow FY27 by 15% (to ~₹2,440 cr) Anthem needs ~₹2,025 cr in Q2–Q4 FY27, i.e. **+28% YoY for nine months**; +20% needs ~+35%. That is a steep H2 ramp.

## Capex and capacity
- **Unit 4 (Harohalli, 30-acre greenfield, broke ground 2025)**: Phase 1 ~₹1,200 cr split ~₹700 cr FY27 / ~₹500 cr FY28 (ICRA: ₹700 cr / ₹500 cr); adds 365 KL custom synthesis (doubling today's ~425 KL) and 100 KL fermentation, plus a dedicated food/nutraceutical plant; civil work nearly done, equipment orders now; ready by end-FY28 (March 2028). Two more phases later could triple capacity [Q1 call; Q4 call; ICRA Jul-26; AR FY26].
- Funding: internal accruals and the cash pile; ICRA notes some debt, but expects debt/OPBDITA to stay well under its 1.5x downgrade trigger [ICRA Jul-26]. Capital commitments rose to ₹450 cr at Mar-26 from ₹154 cr [AR FY26 note 40].
- Asset turns: gross fixed-asset turnover 1.10x in Q1 (annualised) [IP Q1FY27]. My estimate: ₹1,200 cr at a ~1x asset turn and ~40% margin could eventually add ~₹1,200 cr revenue and ~₹450 cr EBITDA — but only once filled, likely FY29–FY31, and the existing units are only 30–78% used today. Depreciation will step up in FY29.
- Payback logic management gives: Unit 4 is sized for the 14 commercial molecules scaling up and the 10 Phase-3 molecules converting (commercialisation "18 to 24 months" away) [Q1 call, official].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash ₹1,375 cr at Mar-26 and ₹1,720 cr at Jun-26 [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]. ICRA: free cash/FDs ₹431 cr + liquid investments ₹668 cr at Mar-26; borrowings ₹54 cr; interest cover >120x; repayments ~₹18 cr a year [ICRA Jul-26; Screener].
- Cash flow: CFO ₹844 cr in FY26 (126% of operating profit) after weak FY24 (₹140 cr) and FY25 (₹271 cr); free cash flow ₹637 cr FY26 [Screener].
- Working capital: debtor days 101 (FY26), inventory days fell to 56 from 167, cash conversion cycle 124 days from 202 [Screener]. Q1: receivables fell after the big Q4, inventory built for Q2–Q3 deliveries [Q1 call].
- First dividend: payout 19% of FY26 profit [Screener]. No buyback.

## Moat evidence
- Regulatory lock-in: once Anthem's process is in a drug's filing, switching is slow and costly. Biotechs acquired by big pharma usually keep Anthem ("they leave the team alone for a year or two"), which is how most big-pharma relationships were won [Q1 call; ICRA Jul-26].
- Margin leadership: operating margin 39% in FY26 (ICRA OPBDITA 41%) against peers in the 25–35% range, which the ICRA attributes to automation, product mix and capacity use across both segments [Screener; ICRA Jul-26].
- Funnel: 100+ early programmes → 10 Phase 3 → 14 commercial; four FY26 graduations came from its own biotech funnel (none lateral) [Q4 call].
- Regulatory record: Units 1 and 2 inspected by USFDA, PMDA, EDQM, ANVISA, TGA; no material adverse action [ICRA Jul-26; AR FY26].
- Semaglutide API: sampled to "almost all the big players" in India; claims cost "rivalling even the Chinese" — unproven until CDSCO approval [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Rating upgraded to **[ICRA]AA (Stable)** from AA- (Positive) on 2 Jul 2026 [ICRA Jul-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Concentration**: top 3 customers 56.7%, top 10 76.4%, top five products 50–55% of revenue [AR FY26; ICRA Jul-26]. One customer's inventory decision can move a quarter by 20%+, as Q1 showed.
- **Lumpiness and credibility**: management told investors in May that destocking was over; two months later Q1 fell 23%. It now asks investors to judge only full years [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Promoter and investor selling**: IPO was a 100% offer for sale (₹3,395 cr) [AR FY26]; True North's Viridity Tone LLP (the pre-IPO private-equity investor) has exited (stake size/date not verified from a filing) [BSE filing, 8 Sep 2026]; promoter Aruna Ganesh sold 3.05% (₹1,275 cr at ₹744.80) on 18 Jun 2026, taking promoters to ~71.4% [News: Upstox, 18 Jun 2026; Screener].
- **Upside-sharing payout to founders**: from True North's exit, ~₹127.7 cr was paid (by the investor, under a Dec-2024 agreement approved by public shareholders at the Jul-26 AGM) to founders Ajay Bhardwaj, Ganesh Sambasivam and K. Ravindra Chandrappa [BSE filing, 8 Sep 2026]. Not a company cost, but it rewards the founders for the PE exit price.
- **Related parties**: loan ₹7.8 cr outstanding and a ₹7.5 cr corporate guarantee to Anthem Biopharma Pvt Ltd, a promoter-group company with common directors (lease rent ₹0.3 cr); the auditor calls the terms "not prejudicial" and repayments regular [AR FY26 note 45; CARO]. Three founder-directors were paid ~₹6.6 cr each in FY26; relatives on payroll at small amounts [AR FY26 note 45.5].
- Contingent liabilities are small: tax/GST disputes of roughly ₹10–40 cr (column layout in the PDF makes the exact total hard to read — **not verified**) [AR FY26 note 40].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; only key audit matter is IT systems and controls; audit trail operated all year [AR FY26 auditor's report; CARO].
- Unit 3 (NeoAnthem) still loss-making in FY26 (hence higher group tax rate); ₹195 cr of intercompany loans to it at Mar-26 [Q1 call; AR FY26 CARO].
- Execution: Unit 4 doubles capacity while Unit 2 is 50% used and Unit 3 30–35% used — a real under-utilisation risk if the Phase-3 molecules slip [Q1 call].

## My read
The business is high quality — ~40% margins, a ₹1,700 cr net cash pile, an AA rating and a real biotech-to-big-pharma funnel — but FY27 has started badly and growth now rests on a heavy back-half: nine months of roughly +28% just to deliver 15% for the year. Concentration (three customers = 57% of sales) means quarterly swings will stay large, and management's May "restocking" comment has already been contradicted once. The price already assumes premium growth for many years; Unit 4 doubles capacity before existing units are full, so FY28–FY29 returns may dip. What would change my view up: Q2–Q3 FY27 revenue back above ₹600 cr a quarter, semaglutide CDSCO approval, and the new big-pharma contract signed. Down: another soft quarter or a Phase-3 failure at a top customer.
