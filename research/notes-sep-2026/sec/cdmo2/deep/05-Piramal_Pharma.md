## Business and segments
- Three businesses: **CDMO** (Piramal Pharma Solutions — 17 sites in India, US, UK, Canada; APIs, HPAPIs, ADC conjugation/payload-linkers, sterile fill-finish, peptides), **Complex Hospital Generics (CHG)** (inhalation anaesthesia — Sevoflurane; intrathecal baclofen; injectable pain; newly acquired Kenalog), and **India Consumer Healthcare (PCH)** (Little's, Lacto Calamine, i-pill, Saridon, Supradyn) [IP Q4FY26; CARE Jul-26].
- FY26 revenue ₹8,869 cr (−3%): CDMO ₹4,915 cr (−10%, 55%), CHG ₹2,703 cr (+3%), PCH ₹1,274 cr (+17%) [IP Q4FY26]. Q1 FY27 ₹2,270 cr (+17%): CDMO ₹1,187 cr (+19%), CHG ₹743 cr (+17%), PCH ₹347 cr (+15%) [IP Q1FY27].
- CDMO detail: >500 customers, top 10 = 36% of CDMO revenue; 47% of revenue from innovation-related work, ~$96 m from on-patent commercial manufacturing; 157 molecules in the pipeline, 25 in Phase 3; revenue split discovery 2%, development 27%, commercial 71%; ~48% of CDMO revenue from sites outside India; ADC revenue $64 m in FY26 [CARE Jul-26; Q4 call; Q1 call].
- CHG: US Sevoflurane share 48% (Q1 FY27); inhalation anaesthesia 64% of CHG sales, intrathecal 15%, injectable pain 11% [Q1 call; CARE Jul-26]. Kenalog bought from BMS for $35 m upfront plus up to $65 m contingent; supplies from Q2 FY27 [IP Q4FY26].
- Associate: AbbVie Therapeutics India (49%; FY26 revenue ₹526 cr, 23% PAT margin); Yapan Bio (33%) [IP Q4FY26; AR FY26 note 39].
- Gross margins: overseas sites 75–85%, India 55–65%; overseas sites under-utilised [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (29 Apr 2026):** FY27 revenue growth "early to mid-teens" in INR terms, excluding the destocked on-patent product; EBITDA to grow faster; H2-weighted; capex $120–135 m (FY26: $94 m vs $100–125 m guided); net debt/EBITDA ~3.6x, "range-bound" in FY27, long-term ~1x; elevated tax rate through FY27; long-range plan of 25% EBITDA margin by FY30 [Q4 call; IP Q4FY26].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (30 Jul 2026):** revenue +17% — **ahead**, but ~9–10 points were currency; EBITDA +72%, margin +400 bp to 12.5% — **beat**; Riverview payload-linker room (<$5 m) commissioned — **delivered**; Lexington sterile expansion on track for end-CY2027; no revenue from the destocked product this year, as flagged [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Guidance now:** annual guidance **kept**, "we'll come back… maybe after the September quarter results"; capex $120–135 m kept ($21 m spent); tax to stay elevated in FY27, 24–25% at scale [Q1 call].
- Track record: FY26 was guided as a recovery year a year earlier but revenue fell 3% and EBITDA fell 28% on the destocking of one large on-patent product (a migraine drug, per an analyst's question in the stored Q4 transcript) and ex-US Sevoflurane competition — **missed** [IP Q4FY26; Q4 call]. CARE notes net debt/PBILDT of 4.28x at Mar-26 was above its stated rating sensitivity [CARE Jul-26].

## Capex and capacity
- $90 m programme: Riverview (US) payload-linker capacity — done; Lexington (US) sterile injectables — completion end-CY2027 [Q4 call; Q1 call]. FY27 capex $120–135 m (CARE: ~₹1,300 cr) across CDMO capacity, debottlenecking and maintenance [CARE Jul-26].
- Capital commitments ₹583 cr at Mar-26 (₹807 cr a year earlier), plus ₹332 cr committed for Kenalog [AR FY26 consolidated note 25].
- Asset returns are weak: ROCE 3% FY26 [Screener]; CARE says overseas facilities are "yet to be fully optimised for revenue generation" and wants ROCE ≥15% for an upgrade [CARE Jul-26].
- Management's margin bridge: operating leverage at overseas sites is "the single largest driver", then mix toward differentiated offerings (ADC, HPAPI, onshore sterile) and on-patent work [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **New USFDA Form-483 at Morpeth, UK: 7 observations** after a 3–11 Sep 2026 inspection — a test of the zero-OAI record [BSE filing, 14 Sep 2026].
- **Leverage and ratings headroom**: FY26 breached CARE's downgrade thresholds; ratings held only on expected FY27 improvement [CARE Jul-26].
- **Pledged Carlyle stake (~18%)** — a forced-sale overhang if the lenders enforce [SAST filing, 26 Aug 2026].
- **Audit changes**: new statutory auditor (Suresh Surana & Associates) for FY26; 11 subsidiaries with ₹5,040 cr of revenue (over half the group) audited by other auditors; key audit matter is recoverability of goodwill (₹1,257 cr) and intangibles under development (₹301 cr) — relevant after the ₹176 cr write-off [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report]. Opinion unmodified.
- Company-secretary turnover: three office-holders between Feb and Apr 2026 [AR FY26].
- Customer concentration in on-patent CDMO: one product's destocking cut FY26 CDMO revenue 10%; no orders expected from it in FY27 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Tax leakage: losses in under-scaled overseas subsidiaries cannot yet be offset; effective tax rate stays high [Q1 call].
- Contingent liabilities small: ~₹95 cr (GST/excise ₹78 cr, TSPCB pollution-board dispute ₹12 cr) [AR FY26 note 25].
- Screener flags possible interest capitalisation [Screener].

## My read
Piramal is a recovery story, not yet a proven one: Q1 FY27 was a strong quarter, but most of the 17% growth was currency, profit after tax is still negative, and the balance sheet (net debt ~₹4,100 cr, 3.6x EBITDA) leaves little room for another setback. The long-range plan needs margins to roughly double from ~13% to 25% by FY30 through filling half-empty US/UK plants — possible, but it has been promised before. The Morpeth 483 and the pledged Carlyle stake add near-term risk. I would become more positive if FY27 EBITDA margin clears ~16% with net debt/EBITDA below 3x and Morpeth closes without escalation; negative if H2 CDMO disappoints or CARE's thresholds are breached again.
