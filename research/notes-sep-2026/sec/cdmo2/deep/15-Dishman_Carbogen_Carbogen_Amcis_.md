# Dishman Carbogen Amcis (DCAL) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Price ₹168, market cap ₹2,637 cr, trailing P/E 145 (TTM profit only ₹16 cr), book value ₹426/share (P/B ~0.39), ROCE 3.1%, ROE 1.6% [Screener].

## Business and segments
- A Swiss-led CDMO: **Carbogen Amcis** (Switzerland — Bubendorf and other sites; Netherlands; UK Manchester; Shanghai; French drug-product site at Saint-Beauzire) does process development and small-to-mid-volume GMP manufacture of APIs, highly potent APIs (HPAPI) and ADC drug-linkers/payloads; **Dishman India** (Bavla and Naroda, Ahmedabad) does larger-volume manufacturing, quaternary salts/phase-transfer catalysts, and softgels [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Two reported segments: **CDMO** (₹2,441 cr in FY26, 83%; EBITDA margin 19.4%) and **Marketable Molecules** (Vitamin D analogues, cholesterol, lanolin products; ₹490.6 cr, 17%; margin 18.8%) [IP Q1FY27 p.9; Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27: CDMO ₹534.4 cr (–12.6% YoY, margin 6.3%), Marketable Molecules ₹143.3 cr (+48%, margin 18.6%) [IP Q1FY27 pp.9–10].
- Scale: 250+ clients, 700–800 programmes in development at any time, 29 commercial molecules, 13 molecules in late Phase III, new-product development pipeline CHF 89 mn, 2,200+ staff, 950+ in R&D, 750 m³ reactor capacity [IP Q1FY27 p.3; Q1 call].
- ADC exposure: ~25% of the Swiss entity's FY26 business; ~₹150 cr of Q1 FY27 revenue from ADC-related work (linker, payload, HPAPI); anchor is a Japanese innovator's approved ADC, for which the customer is co-funding dedicated Swiss capacity (second phase due in calendar 2027) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- India standalone: ~₹400 cr revenue in FY25; guided +30–35% in FY27 at ~10% operating margin; target ₹500 cr by FY28; historically 35–40% operating margins before the EDQM problem at Bavla [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- French drug-product plant: FY26 revenue ~€8 mn with ~€9 mn EBITDA loss; FY27 guide €11–12 mn revenue, ~€6 mn loss; breakeven targeted FY28 [Q4 call].

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarters: Jun-25 708 / operating profit 141 (20%) / net profit 23; Sep-25 653 / 149 (23%) / 65; Dec-25 720 / 113 (16%) / –13; Mar-26 851 / 163 (19%) / 22; Jun-26 678 / 60 (9%) / –58 [Screener].
- Years: FY24 2,616 / 286 (11%) / –153; FY25 2,712 / 469 (17%) / 3; FY26 2,932 / 566 (19%) / 97; TTM 2,901 / 485 / 16 [Screener]. Sales CAGR 5 yrs 9%, 3 yrs 7%; ROE ~0% over 3 and 5 years [Screener].
- Cost structure: employee costs ~50% of revenue (₹1,447 cr in FY26), largely fixed Swiss salaries; COGS only ~13–17% [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.8]. A CHF 10 mn shipment slip moved Q1 from profit to a ₹51 cr pre-tax loss; management says each CHF 10 mn of revenue adds ~70% to EBITDA [Q1 call].
- Q1 FY27 also carried a ₹11.7 cr notional forex loss (USD/CHF) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- FY26 Q4 included a CHF 2.5 mn one-off pension provision and a ~₹55 cr rise in other expenses from onerous-contract provisions [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (20 May 2026): revenue CAGR ~15% over 3 years; EBITDA margin 25% by FY28 ("22–23%" for FY27 was the analyst's number — management said wait a quarter); quarterly interest ₹30–35 cr from Q2/Q3 once promoter ECB lands, full-year net interest ≤₹100 cr consolidated; net debt to fall CHF 10–15 mn a year; India ₹500 cr by FY28 [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (17 Aug 2026): FY27 revenue only **single-digit growth**; EBITDA margin "similar to last year" (~19.3%); FY28–FY29 >10% growth and 25–26% margin; interest ₹35–40 cr a quarter (₹130–140 cr a year) until the ECB arrives, ECB "in the next 60–90 days"; net debt CHF 140–150 mn at year-end [Q1 call].
- Verdict: guidance **cut** (15% CAGR → single digits for FY27; interest savings delayed); the FY28 25% margin target kept. To hit a ~19% FY27 margin after ₹60 cr in Q1, the remaining three quarters need roughly ₹500+ cr EBITDA (analyst estimate ₹550 cr, not disputed) [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is low: CHF 22.4 mn in FY26, CHF 4.9 mn in Q1 FY27; "hardcore assets and capacity in place"; spending mainly maintenance, SAP/lab software and the partner-funded Japanese co-investment [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Capital commitments rose to ₹385 cr (PPE) + ₹44 cr (intangibles) at Mar-26 from ₹69 cr — mostly the co-investment line [AR FY26 note 26].
- Payback depends on filling idle Indian capacity via tech transfers: one Swiss→Bavla transfer signed with a US company (complete within FY27), a second major one approved by a Japanese MNC (the Swiss molecule currently earns CHF 20–25 mn a year; ~30% price cut to the customer still lifts group margin), three more in advanced talks [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹3,076 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹2,389 cr (Mar-25) — gross debt rose partly because Swiss syndicated facilities were drawn and parked as deposits; net debt ex-leases CHF 146.8 mn (Mar-26) → CHF 153.6 mn (Jun-26) [Screener; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.10].
- Interest ₹174 cr in FY26 (≈31% of EBITDA); India debt ~₹800 cr at 10.5–11% [Screener; Q4 call].
- **Promoter ECB**: up to CHF 200 mn from a promoter-group company, 4% all-in, 10-year bullet, two-year interest moratorium (paid in kind), unsecured; to repay Indian debt; funded by the promoter entity using personal assets and guarantees; not yet received by mid-Aug 2026 [Q1 call; AR FY26 Schedule V note]. Management "no plan" to pledge listed shares but did not rule out a brief/small pledge [Q1 call].
- Operating cash flow ₹491 cr and free cash flow ₹225 cr in FY26 (best in years) [Screener]. Inventory days 910, cash conversion cycle 805 days on Screener's measure (distorted by work-in-progress on long projects) [Screener].
- Rating: India Ratings downgraded to IND A/Negative, A1 on 18 Feb 2026 — net adjusted leverage 3.92x (FY25), above 3x in FY26; standalone leverage 9.94x (FY25); margins below pre-EDQM levels; working capital 180 days; a further India Ratings action on 4 Jun 2026 is listed but its text could not be retrieved [News: InvestyWise, Feb 2026; web search; Screener rating list].
- No dividend [Screener]. Short-term NCDs of ~₹75 cr at 10% raised as bridge [Q1 call].

## Moat evidence
- Swiss HPAPI/ADC chemistry with long customer histories; 29 commercial molecules as base load; approvals from USFDA, Swissmedic, EDQM, PMDA, MFDS, NMPA [IP Q1FY27].
- Clean inspections: Naroda passed USFDA and Korean MFDS with no observations; Carbogen's Vionnaz (Switzerland) site passed its first USFDA inspection without a Form 483 on 11 Sep 2026 [Q1 call; BSE filing 12 Sep 2026 via Screener].
- Customer co-investment by a Japanese innovator (100% partner-funded) signals switching costs; ADC linker supplies earn "more than double" group margin [Q4 call].
- Weaknesses: biotech funding slowdown hitting early-phase demand; post-COVID destocking; high fixed Swiss cost base [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor emphasis of matter (FY26 standalone)**: (a) goodwill of ₹1,326.86 cr from the 2016–17 merger is amortised under a court scheme (now over 99 years) instead of Ind AS impairment-only — had it not been amortised, assets would be ₹745.90 cr higher; (b) **covenant breach**: at the India standalone level the company failed one financial covenant on NCD Series 2024 and 2025 (₹84.44 cr), with some waivers still awaited; covenants on NCD Series 2026 (₹48.92 cr) and a ₹63.67 cr term loan had to be modified [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Key audit matters: goodwill impairment (₹580.96 cr standalone; consolidated goodwill ~₹4,798 cr, which moves with CHF/INR), impairment of ₹2,828 cr investments in subsidiaries, uncertain tax positions [AR FY26; Q4 call].
- Contingent liabilities: disputed income tax ₹374.95 cr (AY2010-11 to AY2020-21; ₹46 cr paid under protest), excise/GST ~₹31 cr, guarantees for former subsidiaries/JV ~₹14 cr [AR FY26 note 25/29].
- Regulatory history: EDQM issues at Bavla crushed India margins (from 35–40%); the stored call copy omits this wording [Q1 call official; AR FY26].
- Related-party financing: dependence on a promoter-group ECB of up to CHF 200 mn; promoter holds 59.32%; founder Janmejay Vyas died May 2025; Arpit Vyas (Global MD) drew no remuneration in FY26 [Screener; AR FY26].
- FX: CHF/USD/INR mismatches hit reported numbers each quarter [Q1 call].
- Profitability: ROE ~0% for five years; losses in 2 of the last 5 quarters [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 "one of the best quarters ever" → Q1 "soft quarter" after a CHF 10 mn deferral.
- Guidance cut from ~15% CAGR to single-digit FY27 growth; margin "similar to last year" instead of improving; interest savings pushed out.
- New: Chief Commercial Officer (ex-Lonza) and new India sales leadership; first Swiss→India tech-transfer contract signed; a big-pharma molecule approved by USFDA in August.
- Promoter ECB timeline slipped from "Q2 or Q3" to "next 60–90 days".

## My read
Dishman is a real ADC/HPAPI franchise trapped in a weak capital structure: EBITDA of ₹566 cr in FY26 was eaten by ₹339 cr depreciation/amortisation and ₹174 cr interest, leaving ROE near zero, and Q1 FY27 swung to a ₹58 cr loss on one deferred shipment. The stock trades near 0.4x book, so it does not need growth so much as proof: the promoter ECB actually funded, interest falling to ~₹35 cr a quarter, Indian tech transfers lifting Bavla, and the covenant waivers closed. The auditor's covenant-breach and goodwill notes and the Feb-2026 rating downgrade are the key cautions. I would turn positive on two clean quarters with interest down and net debt below CHF 140 mn; negative if the ECB slips again or the rating is cut further.
