# Gland Pharma (GLAND) — deep research (Phase A1)

Price ₹2,917, market cap ₹48,137 cr, 52-week ₹1,574–3,042, dividend yield 0.69%, promoter Fosun Pharma Industrial (Singapore; ultimate parent Shanghai Fosun Pharmaceutical) 51.77%, no pledge shown [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].
Documents read: stored Q1 FY27 and Q4 FY26 transcripts (both in full); official Q1 FY27 transcript (BSE, 17 Aug 2026) and official Q4 FY26 transcript (BSE) checked against the stored copies; Q1 FY27 investor presentation (10 Aug 2026); Annual Report FY26 (chairman's letter/MD&A, consolidated auditor's report, goodwill note, related parties, contingent liabilities, segment/customer note); BSE filing of 13 Sep 2026 (Gland Pharma USA restructuring). **No live credit rating** — the last India Ratings rationale on Screener is from 2018, so none was used.

## Business and segments
- Hyderabad sterile-injectables maker (vials, ampoules, pre-filled syringes, lyophilised products, RTU bags, cartridges, ophthalmics) plus Cenexi (France/Belgium, acquired FY24, 100% CDMO). B2B model: sells to marketing partners (profit share) and does contract manufacturing [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹1,800 cr: CDMO ₹892 cr (+20% YoY, 50%), B2B ₹909 cr (+19%, 50%) [IP Q1FY27]. FY26: CDMO 46% of revenue, +28%; within the India base business CDMO was ~23% [Q4 call].
- Geography Q1 FY27: US ₹981 cr (54%, +32%; CDMO ₹265 cr +48%), Europe ₹395 cr (22%, +20% incl. Cenexi), Canada/Australia/NZ ₹53 cr (3%, −28%), RoW ₹304 cr (17%, +2% — Saudi NUPCO tender delays), India ₹67 cr (4%, +12%) [IP Q1FY27].
- FY26 revenue ₹6,431 cr, +14.5%; base business (ex-Cenexi) revenue ₹4,561 cr (+11%), EBITDA ₹1,663 cr (+15%); Cenexi revenue €182 m (+11%) with EBITDA up €16 m to about breakeven for the year [AR FY26 chairman's letter; Q4 call].
- Portfolio: 389 US ANDA filings (342 approved, 47 pending); 15 co-development products (seven 505(b)(2), eight ANDAs) from FY28; 21 RTU bags filed, 18 approved (US market ~$644 m); GLP-1 cartridge capacity raised from 40 m to 140 m units a year [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26].
- Customer concentration is moderate: **one customer >10% of revenue = ₹870 cr (13.5%) in FY26**; two customers = ₹1,389 cr (24.7%) in FY25 [AR FY26 consolidated segment note 44(c)].
- Capacity: Cenexi Fontenay ~100% used, Osny 90–100%, the other two sites 50–60% [Q4 call]. India ophthalmics and some vial/bag lines described as capacity-tight [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026):** FY27 revenue growth 12–13% in constant currency, excluding GLP-1; ~₹650 cr growth from base business plus ~₹150 cr from Cenexi; 15% CAGR "next four years"; FY28 ~15%, FY29 "19%, 20% or a bit more" when large complex products launch; base EBITDA 33–35%, consolidated 25–26%; Cenexi FY27 ~€200 m, mid-to-high single-digit EBITDA, mid-teens medium term; FY27 capex ~₹500 cr within a ₹2,000 cr programme; CDMO to add $40–50 m in FY27 [Q4 call, official].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (10 Aug 2026):** revenue +20% (+15% constant currency) vs 12–13% guided — **beat**; adjusted EBITDA 28% vs 25–26% guided — **beat**; Cenexi revenue flat YoY at €48 m, 4% margin — **on track for margin, behind on revenue** (needs ~€51 m a quarter to reach €200 m) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Guidance now:** FY27 "15% is clearly achievable" in constant currency — **raised** from 12–13%; more if the FDA approves the new bag line by Aug–Sep; four-year growth "around 20%" — **raised** from 15%, to be confirmed next quarter; consolidated EBITDA ~30% near term and 35% "in the next 3, 4 years"; FY27 capex ~₹550 cr — **raised** from ₹500 cr; Cenexi €200 m and high-single-digit margin kept; GLP-1 contribution "very limited" until FY30–31 [Q1 call, official].
- Track record: FY26 revenue growth 14.5% and adjusted EBITDA margin 26% (+360 bp) met the direction management set; Cenexi turnaround targets were met quarter by quarter in FY26 (EBITDA-positive in three quarters) [Q4 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Programme: ₹2,000 cr over five years for the base business (AR FY26 chairman's letter; the Q4 call Q&A said "next 3 years", so the pace is unclear) — vial, ophthalmic (with suspension), blow-fill-seal, liposome and new CDMO lines [AR FY26; Q1 call]. FY26 capex ₹494 cr; Q1 FY27 ₹113 cr; FY27 ~₹550 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Specific projects: ₹165 cr oncology isolator line for the new 55-SKU contract (install January); a new sterile-API block for Neuland's microparticle depot APIs; a compounding suite for the in-licensed liposomal product; Cenexi new vial/lyo block at Braine-l'Alleud and a 30 m-ampoule line at Fontenay (production early 2027) [Q1 call].
- Payback: fixed-asset turnover ~1.5–1.6x, total asset turnover ~0.5–0.6x [IP Q1FY27]. The ₹2,000 cr programme at ~1.5x would support roughly ₹3,000 cr of extra revenue at full use (own estimate).
- Large contract: 55-SKU strategic supply deal with a global specialty pharma (30–40% specialty revenue), tech transfer from Sep 2026 over 24 months, revenue from CY2029, ~$90–100 m a year at peak (~12% of current revenue); ~50–60% US, 30–35% EU, rest RoW; Cenexi does EU warehousing/QP release [Q1 call, official; IP Q1FY27]. NDDS CDMO project: $25–30 m a year, commercial 2029 [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash ₹3,547 cr and net cash ₹3,294 cr at Jun-26 (₹3,359 cr / ₹3,116 cr at Mar-26); only debt is at Cenexi (₹243 cr at Mar-26) [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Cash flow: CFO ₹1,031 cr FY26 (91% of operating profit), free cash flow ₹538 cr; Q1 FY27 CFO ₹318 cr [Screener; Q1 call].
- Working capital is heavy for a manufacturer: inventory days 284, debtor days 107, cash conversion cycle 255 days on Screener's basis (FY26); the company's own measure is 164 days average FY26 and 155 days at Jun-26 [Screener; IP Q1FY27].
- Dividend payout 32% of FY26 profit (43% FY24, 42% FY25) [Screener]. Returns are modest: ROCE 15%, ROE 10.7% [Screener]; company ROCE 11.7%, RoNW 10.5% for FY26 [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Goodwill on Cenexi ₹294 cr (grew from ₹248 cr purely on euro translation); no impairment; discount rate raised to 12.6% from 10.6% [AR FY26 note 6].

## Moat evidence
- Scale and cost: 389 ANDA filings, 342 approvals; management says it wins share in products "secured by others" through a better cost structure; European sites cost "5 to 6x more expensive than India" [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Regulatory complexity: MVI (multivitamin) has CGT exclusivity — "a very difficult product to make" [Q1 call].
- Switching costs: the 55-SKU deal consolidates a partner's supply from many sites; Cenexi's EU release role was decisive ("without Cenexi, this wouldn't have happened") [Q1 call].
- Pricing: suppliers asked 5–6% more for vials/glass; management expected 1–2% overall cost impact, with logistics costs shared with partners [Q4 call]. No evidence of Gland raising its own prices.
- Weakness: "we don't have any innovative products right now" in CDMO — it is generics-and-505(b)(2) CDMO, not innovator CDMO [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter is a Chinese state-linked group (Fosun)**; US BIOSECURE-type rules could matter if widened. Related-party dealings with Fosun companies: sales to Fosun Pharma USA (roughly ₹240 cr a year; exact column order in the note unclear — **not verified**), purchases of raw materials from Fosun Wanbang and Chongqing Carelife; receivable from Fosun Pharma USA ~₹68 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 36].
- In-licensed liposomal product comes from a China-based developer [Q1 call].
- Cenexi: revenue flat YoY in Q1; unquantified dispute at Cenexi Laboratories Thissen (Belgium) with a partner over 2016/2021 cooperation agreements — "cannot reliably estimate" [AR FY26 contingent liabilities]. Other contingent liabilities are small (tax claims ~₹10 cr) [AR FY26].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matters are revenue cut-off at year-end and Cenexi goodwill impairment; seven subsidiaries (₹2,006 cr revenue) audited by other auditors [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- No group CEO: in May the company said it was "looking at candidates… with stronger CDMO background" [Q4 call]. NSE and BSE each fined ₹70,000 for a July 18 director-composition lapse [Screener announcements, 13 Sep 2026].
- Constant-currency method is opaque (IIFL analyst queried it; management uses shipment-date rates) [Q1 call].
- Growth back-ended: big contract CY2029, complex products and GLP-1 FY29–FY31 [Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: from "12–13% constant currency, 15% CAGR" to "15% clearly achievable, ~20% for four years" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New: 55-SKU $90–100 m contract, new GLP-1 contract (semaglutide + tirzepatide, US/EU), Neuland sterile-API collaboration, China-sourced liposomal in-licence (US market ~$600–700 m) [Q1 call].
- Margin: near-term target moved up from 25–26% to ~30% consolidated [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Issues: Saudi NUPCO delays kept RoW flat; European heatwave delayed Cenexi quality releases [Q1 call].

## My read
Gland is clearly improving: FY26 profit rose 47%, Q1 FY27 beat guidance on both growth and margin, Cenexi has stopped losing money and the new 55-SKU deal gives visibility into 2029–30. Two cautions: a quarter of the Q1 growth was currency, and the step-up to "20% for four years" rests on contracts that start paying in CY2029, so FY27–FY28 are really ~15% years. Returns are still low (ROE ~11%) because of ₹3,300 cr of idle cash and a heavy inventory cycle, so earnings growth, not just revenue, must carry the price. I would turn more positive if the bag line is approved and Q2 constant-currency growth stays ≥15%; negative if Cenexi stays flat or the 20% target is not confirmed next quarter as promised.
