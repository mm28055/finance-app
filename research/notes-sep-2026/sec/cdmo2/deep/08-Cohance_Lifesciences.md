# Cohance Lifesciences — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

Sources read: stored file (both call copies in full — both are the company's BSE filings), official BSE transcript of the Q1 FY27 call (5 Aug 2026) to cross-check, Q1 FY27 investor presentation (5 Aug 2026), Annual Report FY26 (directors' report, MD&A, auditor's report + CARO, goodwill/subsidiary impairment, contingent liabilities, acquisition notes), Crisil rating rationale (12 Aug 2026), news on later FDA inspections, Screener consolidated page (raw HTML table rows).

## Business and segments
- Formerly Suven Pharmaceuticals; controlled by Advent International (via Berhyanda/Jusmiral), merged with the old Cohance on 1 May 2025 and renamed. Executive Chairman & Group CEO Umang Vohra joined in early May 2026. [AR FY26 directors' report; Q4 call]
- **Three business units (FY26 revenue ₹2,268.6 cr, −13%):** Pharma CDMO ₹889 cr (39%) — intermediates and APIs for innovator drugs, ADC payload-linkers (NJ Bio, USA, 56% owned) and oligonucleotide building blocks (Sapala, Hyderabad, 67.5% owned); API+ ₹1,088 cr (48%, −8%) — niche generic APIs (CNS, controlled substances) plus formulations; Specialty Chemicals ₹291 cr (13%, −2%) — agrochemical actives and performance materials (photochromic, OLED). "Niche technology" (ADC + oligo) = 16.2% of FY26 revenue. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Q1 FY27 split:** API+ ~62%, Pharma CDMO ~30%, Specialty Chemicals ~8%; Pharma CDMO −38.7% YoY, API+ −10.4%, Specialty Chemicals −34.7%; niche tech 15.1%. Commercial vs development share in CDMO 57:43. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Subsidiaries in Q1 FY27:** standalone revenue ₹359.9 cr with adjusted EBITDA ₹33.2 cr (9.2%); Sapala revenue ₹27.4 cr (2.5x YoY, "strong EBITDA margin"); NJ Bio revenue ₹35.0 cr with adjusted EBITDA loss of ₹32.8 cr. [Q1 call]
- **Pipeline:** 10 Phase-3 programmes; two molecules recently commercial (six intermediates to ship Q2–Q3 FY27); >140 active CDMO projects; reload conversion "above 90%". [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- **Quarters (sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 549 / 112 / 20% / 46; Sep-25 556 / 121 / 22% / 66; Dec-25 545 / 95 / 18% / 29; Mar-26 619 / 99 / 16% / 8; Jun-26 422 / 1 / 0.3% / −45. [Screener]
- Company-adjusted Q1 FY27: revenue ₹422.3 cr (−23.1%), adjusted EBITDA ₹9.2 cr (2.2%) after adding back ₹2.6 cr of ESOP/M&A costs; reported loss ₹43 cr; gross margin 71.5% (73.0%). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Years:** FY24 1,051 / 406 / 39% / 300 (Suven alone); FY25 2,608 / 797 / 31% / 484; FY26 2,269 / 427 / 19% / 150; TTM 2,142 / 316 / 15% / 59. Company adjusted EBITDA FY26 ₹477.5 cr (21.0%); standalone 24.6%. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]
- FY26 hits: destocking in two large commercial CDMO molecules (~₹260 cr of lost revenue); Nacharam formulation disruption (~₹61 cr); one-off inventory provision ₹19.5 cr and customer settlement ₹12.6 cr. Excluding destocking, revenue fell 3.5%. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Returns:** ROCE fell from 40% (FY21) and 21% (FY25) to 6% (FY26). [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (12 May 2026):** Q1 FY27 "low on both revenue and EBITDA"; Q2 "stable"; growth to return YoY from H2 FY27; Q1 gross margin hit of 100–150 bps from Middle East freight/input costs; FY27 capex ~₹300 cr; both destocked molecules expected "to return back to us in FY27"; NJ Bio needs "a little bit more than two years" to recover margins; no formal FY27 guidance. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** Q1 was the "lowest quarter ever" as warned (met); gross margin −150 bps (met the warning); restocking order received for one of the two molecules, deliveries in Q4 FY27 and FY28 (partly met — the second molecule is still unconfirmed and the ₹260 cr "a bit early... to quantify"); NJ Bio worse than internal plan. [Q1 call]
- **New guidance on Q1 call:** FY27 EBITDA margin "closer to previous year margin percentage" (i.e. ~21% adjusted), then accelerating from FY28 — implies roughly 25% in the remaining nine months after a 2.2% Q1 (my arithmetic, assuming revenue roughly flat on FY26). Sequential improvement in Q2 and YoY growth in H2 kept. [Q1 call]
- **Long-term target softened:** the earlier $1 bn FY30 revenue goal is now "an aspiration... we may be a little bit away from it over the next 3 to 4 years"; a fresh plan by December 2026. The CFO did not commit to returning to 35% margins. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- Relationships: API+ has products where Cohance holds "more than 50% of the global market share" with innovators; top 15 products are about half of API revenue; eight of top-10 APIs hold leadership positions; two-thirds of API revenue from innovators/large generics. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Differentiated platforms: ADC payloads (three payload DMFs filed in FY26; MMAE and exatecan drawing interest as alternatives to Chinese suppliers), oligonucleotide amidites (Sapala), integrated bioconjugation at NJ Bio. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Execution: commercial OTIF (on-time-in-full) ~100%; Q1 innovator audits with no critical findings; 11 API customer audits in Q1. [Q1 call]
- Weakness in the moat: heavy past dependence on a few commercial molecules ("we have had by the past reliance on a few molecules... that is the reason why we are in the situation we are in today"). [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Crisil rating collapse:** on 12 Aug 2026 Crisil moved Cohance from AA-/Positive to "BB/Stable, Issuer Not Cooperating" because the company did not provide information after repeated requests (24 Jul, 30 Jul, 5 Aug), then withdrew the rating at the company's request. Crisil notes non-cooperation "may be a result of deterioration in its credit risk profile". The company has little debt, so the practical effect is small, but it is a disclosure/governance warning sign. [Crisil Aug-26]
- **Auditor fraud report:** in FY26 the statutory auditors (Walker Chandiok) reported a suspected fraud to the Audit Committee under Section 143(12), identified through a whistle-blower complaint; the company says it investigated, tightened controls and there was no material financial impact. Amount not disclosed. [AR FY26 directors' report]
- **Audit trail:** SAP S4 HANA audit trail not enabled at database level; auditors could not comment on the third-party-hosted SAP Rise system. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Regulatory:** US FDA Warning Letter for the Nacharam formulations site (Jan 2026) after an OAI; Form 483 with 5 observations at Pashamylaram (27 Jul–5 Aug 2026); after the call, a 483 with 4 observations at a Telangana formulations unit (27 Aug–4 Sep) and 5 observations at the Jaggaiahpet API site (14–19 Sep). [AR FY26; Q1 call; News: Business Standard 5 Sep 2026; News: Whalesbook, Sep 2026]
- **Promoter pledge:** promoters (57.49%) have pledged 94.6% of their holding (Screener) — typical of private-equity holding-company financing, but a forced-sale risk if the price keeps falling. [Screener]
- **Management churn:** new CEO (May 2026), CFO Himanshu Agarwal left on 13 Sep 2026, earlier MD changes; an analyst on the Q4 call raised concerns about "selective" information flow around investor exits and re-entries. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26]
- **NJ Bio losses and goodwill:** NJ Bio lost ₹32.8 cr at EBITDA in one quarter; consolidated goodwill ₹1,284 cr; impairment of investments in subsidiaries is a key audit matter. [Q1 call; AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Contingent liabilities** are modest: ₹53 cr (tax and other). [AR FY26 note 37]
- **Disclosure gaps:** no molecule/customer concentration, no ADC revenue split, no quantified restocking value, FY27 guidance only qualitative. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: new CEO listening; "bottoming out"; both destocked molecules expected back. Q1: strategy set (unify nucleic acids under Sapala, reposition agrochem to innovators, right-size NJ Bio costs); one restocking order confirmed.
- Numbers worsened as warned (EBITDA margin 16% → 2.2% adjusted), but cash improved (net cash ₹153 cr → ₹251 cr).
- New issues: Pashamylaram 483, CFO exit, $1 bn FY30 goal downgraded to "aspiration", Crisil non-cooperation.

## My read
The core (standalone API+ and small-molecule CDMO) still earns and converts cash, and the balance sheet is net cash. But the business is in a deep trough — Q1 FY27 adjusted margin 2.2%, NJ Bio bleeding, two regulated sites with fresh FDA observations and one under a Warning Letter — and governance signals are poor (Crisil non-cooperation, auditor fraud report, 94.6% promoter pledge, CFO exit). Meeting the FY27 "close to last year's margin" promise needs a steep H2 recovery that is only partly backed by confirmed orders. I would want to see Q2 revenue up clearly, the second restocking order, clean FDA outcomes and NJ Bio losses shrinking before trusting a recovery.
