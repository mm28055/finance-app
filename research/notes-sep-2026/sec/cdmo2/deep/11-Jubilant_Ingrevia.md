# Jubilant Ingrevia — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

Sources read: stored file (both call copies in full), official BSE transcripts of the Q1 FY27 call (Jul 2026) and Q4 FY26 call (May 2026), Q1 FY27 results presentation, Annual Report FY26 (directors' report, MD&A highlights, standalone auditor's report + CARO/audit-trail note, contingent liabilities, BRSR export figure), Crisil rating rationale (11 Aug 2026), Screener consolidated page (raw HTML table rows).

## Business and segments
- Bhartia-group chemicals company (demerged from Jubilant Pharmova in 2021) built on a vertically integrated chain: molasses/ethanol → acetyls → pyridine & picolines → derivatives, vitamins and CDMO. Global leader in bio-pyridine and bio beta-picoline and "the only scaled non-Chinese player"; top-2 globally in vitamin B3 and in merchant acetic anhydride; top-3 in diketene derivatives. [Crisil Aug-26]
- **Three segments (Q1 FY27, ₹ cr, revenue / EBITDA):** Specialty Chemicals 533 / 139 (26%; +11% / +7% YoY) — pyridine/picolines, fine chemicals, CDMO; Nutrition & Health 243 / 36 (15%; +36% / +45%) — vitamin B3, choline, premixes; Chemical Intermediates 524 / 57 (11%; +38% / +240%) — acetic anhydride, ethyl acetate. Corporate cost −₹23 cr. Total revenue ₹1,300 cr (+25%), EBITDA ₹209 cr (+37%), PAT ₹106 cr (+41%). [IP Q1FY27]
- **FY26:** Specialty revenue ₹1,937 cr (+7%), EBITDA ₹510 cr (+21%); Nutrition ₹790 cr / ₹100 cr; Specialty + Nutrition = 62% of revenue (49% in FY23) and ~85% of EBITDA. Exports 44.3% of FY26 turnover. [Q4 call; Crisil Aug-26; AR FY26 BRSR]
- **CDMO:** all reported inside Specialty; size not disclosed, "growing at 30–40% every year". Flagship $300 mn, five-year agro-CDMO contract with a global agrochemical innovator from a new Bharuch plant (commissioned in 14 months, shipping since Mar 2026). Funnel 100+ molecules / ₹3,500 cr+ peak potential; 25+ confirmed molecules with ₹1,500 cr+ peak potential; ~10 advanced-stage molecules (₹1,100 cr). End-market mix of the business: agro 20–25%, pharma ~30%, industrial 10–15%, nutrition 15%, animal 10%. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Capacity/utilisation:** pyridine & picolines ~50,000 t, running at 90–95%+ (China has 2.5–3 lakh t at 30–40%); new 5,000 t human-grade niacin/niacinamide plant at ~60% of run-rate, target 70%+ by year-end. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

(Company "reported EBITDA" includes other income: 153 / 146 / 136 / 172 / 209.) [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- **Years:** FY22 4,949 / 841 / 17% / 477 (peak cycle); FY24 4,136 / 427 / 10% / 183; FY25 4,178 / 519 / 12% / 251; FY26 4,388 / 568 / 13% / 278; TTM 4,651 / 624 / 13% / 309. [Screener]
- **Drivers of Q1:** Q1 EBITDA rose ~₹56 cr YoY, of which ~₹40 cr came from Chemical Intermediates (war-driven acetyl pricing and cost pass-through); Specialty EBITDA rose only ₹9 cr. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Pricing:** pyridine at "rock bottom" while beta-picoline is at multi-year highs; pyridine/picoline long-run average $2.5–4/kg. Vitamin B3 prices up sharply in Q1, may soften from end-Q2. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Returns:** ROCE 11% (FY26); 29% in FY22. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 (May 2026):** "at least 20% year-on-year growth on EBITDA" for FY27; sequential growth from Q1; Specialty sustainable margin 23–25% after corporate costs; capex ₹400–500 cr; Gajraula multipurpose plant (MPP) to finish this year and "commence production in Q4"; Pinnacle 2030 aspiration of ~3x revenue and ~4x EBITDA needs 20–25% EBITDA growth a year. [Q4 call]
- **Investor day at Bharuch (Jun 2026) / Q1 call:** FY27 EBITDA ₹750–800 cr (≈+26–34% on FY26 company EBITDA of ~₹595 cr); H1 "around ₹400 crore plus"; ₹100 cr of new lean savings. Kept after the Q1 beat, citing acetyl price volatility. [Q1 call; own arithmetic from Screener]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** EBITDA ₹209 cr (+22% QoQ, +37% YoY — ahead of the run-rate needed); Nutrition margin 15% (3-year high); Specialty margin 26% (above the 23–25% "sustainable" band); but the big agro-CDMO customer asked for a "temporary pause" in volumes after raw-material prices spiked — management says the contract gives "full protection, which will more than cover" its EBITDA expectation, and Q2 volumes will be higher than Q1. [Q1 call]
- Verdict: guidance kept (not raised); CDMO volume ramp delayed; the rest on or ahead of plan.

## Capex and capacity
- ~₹2,000 cr deployed on new plants over the last two years (Pinnacle); FY27 capex ₹400–500 cr, mainly the Gajraula MPP (fine chemicals/CDMO; commissioning end-CY2026, contribution from FY28 per Crisil) plus a semiconductor/electronics R&D and clean-room facility at Gajraula. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; Crisil Aug-26]
- Acquisition of Remidex Pharma (100%, effective 30 Mar 2026) — premixes and formulations plant in Bengaluru — for human nutrition. [AR FY26 directors' report]
- Asset turns: CWIP fell from ₹525 cr to ₹154 cr as the Bharuch CDMO plant was capitalised; fixed assets rose to ₹3,183 cr. [Screener; Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹602 cr at Mar-26 (₹694 cr Mar-25), net debt/EBITDA ~1.0x, interest cover ~12x; gross borrowings ₹792 cr; net worth ₹3,126 cr (Screener equity + reserves). [Crisil Aug-26; Screener]
- Operating cash flow ₹524 cr FY26 (CFO/operating profit 111%); FCF ₹235 cr. Net working capital 59 days; cash-conversion cycle 55 days (Screener). [Screener; Q4 call]
- Dividend ₹5/share for FY26 (payout ~28%). Crisil A1+ on ₹600 cr commercial paper reaffirmed Aug 2026. Promoters sold down from 51.47% to 45.22% in Jun-2025; no pledge disclosed. [Q4 call; Crisil Aug-26; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Integration and cost: ~40% of acetyl volume and 40–45% of pyridine/picoline output are consumed internally; management claims costs below Chinese rivals despite China's 5–6x capacity. [Crisil Aug-26; Q4 call]
- Share: global #1 in bio-pyridine/beta-picoline, #2 in vitamin B3, #2 in merchant acetic anhydride; pyridine plants at 90–95% utilisation while Chinese plants run at 30–40%. [Crisil Aug-26; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- CDMO credibility: two agro-innovator molecules delivered "first time right"; Bharuch plant passed a US FDA audit; take-or-pay "make-whole" clause on the $300 mn contract. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Weakness: base pyridine pricing is set by Chinese overcapacity; intermediates are commodity-priced. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Anchor CDMO customer under strain:** the agro innovator is "going through some tough times" and paused volumes; protection is contractual, so a long pause could turn into a negotiation or dispute rather than revenue. No firm volume timeline yet ("they have not given us a firm timeline"). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Earnings quality:** Q1 growth leaned on a cyclical spike in Chemical Intermediates (+240% EBITDA); Crisil flags the sustainability of the CI recovery as "monitorable". [IP Q1FY27; Crisil Aug-26]
- **Contingent liabilities ₹454 cr** (₹352 cr a year earlier): income tax ₹176 cr (mainly transfer pricing), state excise ₹167 cr (up from ₹72 cr), customs ₹33 cr, central excise ₹30 cr, others ₹32 cr — about 15% of net worth. [AR FY26 note 38]
- **Audit trail:** database-level audit trail enabled only from Jan/Mar 2025, and no assurance on a third-party-hosted revenue system; otherwise unmodified opinion, revenue recognition as key audit matter, no fraud reports, clean secretarial audit. [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 46]
- **Related parties:** Bhartia-group links (common directors with Jubilant Pharmova); CARO confirms transactions comply with Sections 177/188; the company is still "updating documentation" for transfer-pricing-specified transactions. [AR FY26]
- **Trade protection:** some businesses (e.g. choline) benefit from EU/US anti-dumping duties on China; removal would hurt. [AR FY26 risk section; stored profile]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: CDMO plant started shipping, "at least 20%" EBITDA growth, sequential growth from Q1. Q1: guidance quantified at ₹750–800 cr; anchor CDMO volumes paused; intermediates became the swing factor; Nutrition hit a 3-year high.
- Tone: confident on the portfolio, cautious on predicting CDMO volumes.

## Stored-data errors
- Stored quarterly EBITDA (209 / 172 / 136 / 146) is the company's reported EBITDA including other income; Screener operating profit is 199 / 163 / 126 / 135.
- "ob=3500" is not an order book — it is the peak-revenue potential of the 100+ molecule funnel; confirmed molecules are ₹1,500 cr+.
- Stored profile: "integration of Chemadex" — the acquisition is Remidex Pharma.
- Stored profile: Specialty margin "heavily supported by make-whole financial protections" — management said the plant made positive EBITDA on actual volumes; no make-whole payment was stated as booked in Q1.
- Stored profile: "Exports typically ~50%" — FY26 exports were 44.3% (BRSR).

## My read
Jubilant Ingrevia is slowly becoming more specialty-led — Specialty + Nutrition now give ~85% of EBITDA and debt is falling — and FY27 guidance of ₹750–800 cr looks reachable after a ₹209 cr Q1. But the Q1 beat came mostly from a cyclical acetyl spike, while the structural story (the $300 mn agro-CDMO contract) is paused at a customer in difficulty. The view improves if the innovator restores volumes by Q3, the Gajraula MPP starts on time and Specialty margins hold 24–26% as pyridine prices stay weak; it worsens if acetyl prices fall back and the CDMO "protection" has to be enforced rather than earned.
