# Indian Pharma CDMO / CRDMO — sector research, 28 Sep 2026

Note: live web search/fetch was unavailable for part of this session; sources below are the ones actually opened (ICRA sector note, EY-Parthenon–OPPI release, IBEF, and the FY26 annual reports of Anthem, Sai Life, Innova and Windlas, which quote Frost & Sullivan, IQVIA, Fortune Business Insights and IMARC), plus peer earnings calls stored in this folder.

## Market size and growth
- **Global CRDMO**: US$213 bn in 2024 → US$330 bn by 2029, 9.1% CAGR (2019–24: 9.4%) [Frost & Sullivan via Anthem AR FY26]. Alternative estimate US$260.5 bn (2025) → US$540.5 bn (2034), 8.5% CAGR [Fortune Business Insights via Sai Life AR FY26]. EY-Parthenon–OPPI: US$303 bn by 2028, 9.0% CAGR [EY-OPPI, Nov 2025].
- **Global CDMO (manufacturing-led)**: ~US$199 bn in 2025 [Fortune BI via Sai AR FY26]; biologics CRDMO US$48 bn (2024) → US$86 bn (2029), 12.5% CAGR; small-molecule share 77.5% in 2024, growing 8.1% [Frost & Sullivan via Anthem AR FY26].
- **India's share is small**: Asia-Pacific holds 21–26% of a US$180–200 bn global CRDMO market; China 18–21%, India only 2–3% [ICRA, Feb 2026].
- **India CRDMO size — two definitions**:
  - ICRA (narrow, services-led): US$3.4–4.0 bn in CY2025 → US$7–9 bn by CY2030 → US$14–18 bn by CY2035; **15–16% CAGR CY2026–35** [ICRA, Feb 2026].
  - Frost & Sullivan (broad, incl. API/CMO): US$8 bn (2024) → US$15 bn (2029), **13.4% CAGR**, ~1.5x global growth; India CDMO US$6 bn → US$12 bn, CRO US$2 bn → US$4 bn; Indian biologics CRDMO US$1 bn, 15.5% CAGR; small molecules >92% of Indian CRDMO [Frost & Sullivan via Anthem AR FY26].
  - Domestic-formulation CDMO (IMARC, quoted by Windlas): US$8.52 bn (2025) → US$16.53 bn (2034), 7.4% CAGR [Windlas AR FY26].
- **Near-term growth**: ICRA forecasts its CRDMO sample's revenue growth at only **8–10%** (FY2026, with Sai Life's AR applying it to FY2026-27), operating margin stable at **21–23%**, capex ₹5,000–6,000 cr, debt/EBITDA 1.3–1.5x [ICRA, Feb 2026; Sai AR FY26].
- **Domestic market (for formulation contract makers)**: Indian pharma market ₹5.30 lakh cr (US$60 bn) in FY26 → US$130 bn by 2030 (government/PIB target) [IBEF, updated Sep 2026]. IPM volume grew only ~2.7% in FY26 while value grew 8–9% (IQVIA); Q1 FY27 volume +3.4% [Windlas AR FY26; Windlas Q1 call]. Pharma exports US$31.1 bn in FY26 (US$30.4 bn FY25) [IBEF].

## Demand drivers
- **Big-pharma outsourcing and patent cliff**: innovator drugs are expected to rise from 51.3% to 53.9% of pharma revenue by 2029; small/mid biotech share rising to 26.1% by 2029, growing 8.5% a year — the biggest outsourcers [Frost & Sullivan via Anthem AR FY26]. Global R&D spend US$289 bn (2024) → US$343 bn (2030) [Frost & Sullivan via Anthem AR].
- **China+1 / BIOSECURE**: the US BIOSECURE Act restricts US firms' use of certain Chinese biotech service providers; Anthem's FY26 report refers to its "implementation" and says it is prompting sponsors to evaluate India; Sai Life says it has "reinforced the focus on supply-chain diversification" with customers moving to multi-source strategies [Anthem AR FY26; Sai AR FY26]. Sai Life's management says WuXi-style integrated FTE relationships "are starting to migrate here" [Sai Life Q1 FY27 call]. (Exact statutory status and effective dates not re-verified in this session.)
- **Biotech funding**: CY2025 funding squeeze (NIH cuts, weaker VC) hit early-stage demand; ICRA expects recovery to take time [ICRA, Feb 2026]; Sai reports "encouraging signs of recovery" [Sai AR FY26]; Dishman still cites a funding slowdown in Aug 2026 [Dishman Q1 FY27 call].
- **GLP-1 / peptides**: semaglutide's 2026 patent expiry opens generic GLP-1 demand in India and elsewhere [Innova AR FY26]. Gland Pharma has 8 GLP-1 CDMO contracts (6 more in pipeline) and is expanding cartridge capacity to 140 mn units, but excludes GLP-1 from guidance [Gland Q4 FY26/Q1 FY27 calls]. Anthem, Aarti and Innova are building peptide/oligo capability; Windlas can fill GLP-1 vials but sees demand still in-house [stored calls].
- **Advanced modalities**: ADCs, peptides, oligonucleotides, HPAPI and sterile fill-finish are the high-growth pockets [Sai AR FY26; Anthem AR FY26]. Dishman's ADC work was ~25% of its Swiss business in FY26 [Dishman Q4 call].
- **Domestic formulation outsourcing**: Revised Schedule M (enforced nationwide) raises compliance costs, pushing small non-compliant units out and work to organised CDMOs (Innova, Windlas, Akums) [Windlas AR FY26; Innova Q4 call]. Trade generics ₹240 bn (2024) → ₹680 bn (2030) [UJA via Windlas AR FY26]; Jan Aushadhi 16,912 stores (Jun 2025), target 25,000 by Mar 2027 [IBEF].

## Policy
- **India**: PLI for pharma ₹15,000 cr (2020-21 to 2028-29); ₹60,000 cr API push announced 5 Dec 2025; Biopharma SHAKTI ₹10,000 cr over 5 years (Budget 2026-27) for biologics, biosimilars and 1,000+ clinical-trial sites; bulk-drug parks ₹1,400 cr; 100% FDI automatic for greenfield pharma [IBEF].
- **US tariffs**: talk of a two-year 0% window for India followed by possible tariffs on generics; CDMOs on ex-works contracts pass freight/tariff costs to customers; US-sited plants (Piramal, Senores) seen as hedges [Piramal Q1 FY27 call; Onesource Q1 FY27 call; Senores Q1 call].
- **State incentives**: J&K's GST-linked incentive (up to 300% of plant investment over 10 years) plus 6% interest subvention materially support Innova's Jammu plant [Innova IP Q1FY27].
- **Codeine cough-syrup crackdown** removed a ₹50–60 cr/yr product from Windlas' trade generics [Windlas Q4 call].

## Cost drivers
- **Raw materials/energy**: the 2026 West Asia war doubled some solvent/urea-linked inputs (Aarti: 50–100% spike, easing to 25–50% by July); API prices "volatile, stability has not arrived" [Aarti Q1 call; Windlas Q1 call]. Cost-plus/pass-through contracts (Innova, Windlas, most CDMOs) protect margins but not always inventory timing.
- **People**: employee costs ~50% of revenue at Swiss-based Dishman; state minimum-wage hikes (Uttarakhand) at Windlas [Dishman Q1 call; Windlas Q1 call].
- **FX**: CHF/USD/INR swings hit Dishman and Aarti's reported profits [Dishman Q1 call; Aarti AR FY26].
- **Freight**: Suez/Hormuz disruption lengthened shipping; ex-works terms shift cost to customers [Onesource Q1 call].

## Competitive structure and where profit sits
- China dominates Asia's share; India is 2–3% of global CRDMO revenue — the opportunity is share gain, not market growth alone [ICRA, Feb 2026].
- Profit pools (my synthesis):
  - Integrated innovator CRDMOs (Sai, Anthem, Syngene-type): EBITDA ~25–30% (Sai targets 28–30%, noting large Chinese peers run 25–30%); ICRA sample 21–23% [Sai Q4 call; ICRA].
  - Specialty/ADC/HPAPI (Dishman/Carbogen): high gross margins but heavy European fixed costs — FY26 EBITDA 19.3%, ROE ~0% [Dishman Q4 call; Screener].
  - Hybrid API/chemistry players (Aarti Pharmalabs): company margin 20–25%, commercial CDMO gross margin 60–65%, Xanthine commodity cycle [Aarti Q1 call].
  - Domestic formulation contract makers (Innova, Windlas, Akums): EBITDA 11–16%, but asset-light and cash-generative (Windlas negative working capital) [Screener; company calls].
  - US-generics hybrids (Senores): ~40% EBITDA in regulated segment but profit-share receivables and other income cloud quality [Senores IP Q1FY27].
- Capex intensity high: ICRA sample capex ₹5,000–6,000 cr in FY2026; ramp-up costs depress ROCE for 1–2 years (Aarti ROCE 17% → 11%) [ICRA; Screener].

## Cycle position (Sep 2026)
- Post-COVID destocking and the 2025 biotech-funding trough are ending slowly; late-phase/commercial work (Phase 3 → launch) is healthy, early-phase remains soft [Dishman Q1 call; ICRA].
- Many Indian CDMOs just finished large capex (Aarti Atali, Innova Jammu, Windlas Plant 6, Anthem/Sai expansions) — FY27 is a utilisation/absorption year; margins improve if volumes arrive.
- Valuations: sector trades at a premium on the China+1/BIOSECURE narrative, while FY27 growth for most is guided at 8–20%.

## Outlook FY27–FY29 (forecaster named)
- ICRA: Indian CRDMO industry 15–16% CAGR CY2026–35; FY2026-27 sample revenue +8–10%, OPM 21–23%, leverage 1.3–1.5x [ICRA Feb 2026; Sai AR FY26].
- Frost & Sullivan: India CRDMO 13.4% CAGR 2024–29 to US$15 bn; global 9.1% [via Anthem AR FY26].
- EY-Parthenon–OPPI: global CRDMO 9.0% CAGR to US$303 bn by 2028 [EY, Nov 2025].
- IQVIA/industry for domestic formulations: IPM value 8–9%, volume ~3%; IMARC domestic CDMO 7.4% CAGR [Windlas AR FY26].
- Company guidance for our five names: Aarti 15–18% revenue/EBITDA CAGR for 3–4 years; Innova 20%+ volume growth, margin 15–16% ±2%; Senores FY27 revenue +30–40%, PAT +50–60%; Dishman single-digit FY27, >10% FY28–29 with 25–26% margin; Windlas no numbers (₹1,100 cr capacity) [company calls].
