# Aarti Pharmalabs (AARTIPHARM) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Price ₹940, market cap ₹8,520 cr, trailing P/E 39.6, ROCE 10.7%, ROE 8.6% (FY26) [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Three businesses. **Xanthine derivatives** (caffeine, theophylline and relatives; 74% of volume sold to beverage makers, rest to pharma/nutraceutical), **API and intermediates** (steroids, oncology/HPAPI, cardiovascular, anti-diabetic generics; 61 APIs commercialised, 60 US DMFs, 44 CEPs) and **CDMO/CMO** (small-molecule intermediates and GMP APIs for innovators, mostly Phase 3 and commercial) [IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 standalone revenue split: Xanthine 57%, API & intermediates 30%, CDMO/CMO 7%, others (trading, scrap, export incentives) 6% [Q1 call, official transcript; IP Q1FY27 p.13]. On ₹535 cr standalone revenue that is roughly Xanthine ₹305 cr (confirmed on the call), API ~₹160 cr, CDMO ~₹37 cr.
- Q4 FY26 split was very different: Xanthine 43% (₹227 cr), API 28%, CDMO 29% (record ₹155 cr) [Q4 call]. FY26 full year: Xanthine ₹792 cr, CDMO ₹276 cr, API ~₹600–650 cr (management said ₹600 cr; an analyst's reading of the deck gave ₹647 cr), trading ~₹50 cr [Q4 call].
- CDMO history: ₹32 cr (FY22) → ₹93 cr → ₹161 cr → ₹208 cr (FY25) → ₹276 cr (FY26, +32%) [IP Q1FY27 p.12]. 22 customers, 57 active projects, 37 commercial, 20 in development (Q1 FY27); commercial projects up from 21 three years ago [Q1 call].
- Geography: Xanthine 79% export in Q1 FY27 (63% in Q4); API 48% international / 52% domestic; API market mix 58% regulated, 14% RoW, 28% non-regulated [IP Q1FY27]. Company-level Q1 FY27 split 60% international, 40% domestic [IP Q1FY27 p.4].
- Capacity: Xanthine expanded from 5,000 to 9,600+ MTPA (Unit 5, Tarapur added 3,600 TPA in 2026), running near 6,000 t/yr (~500 t/month) before the new block; target 80%+ utilisation by FY28 and 20–25% global share (from 15–20%) — management calls itself the world's third-largest (deck) or second-largest (call) producer [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]. Multipurpose reactors 1,500+ KL; Atali Block 1 (440 KL) fully operational Q2 FY27 [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Consolidation change: Ganesh Polychem (50%) became a joint venture from 1 Apr 2025 after a shareholder-agreement addendum, so FY26 consolidated revenue is not comparable with FY25 (FY25 consolidated ₹2,115 cr vs standalone ₹1,771 cr) [IP Q1FY27 p.19 note; Q4 call; AR FY26 related-party note].

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarters: Jun-25 revenue 386 / operating profit 93 (24%) / net profit 50; Sep-25 418 / 74 (18%) / 28; Dec-25 432 / 102 (24%) / 48; Mar-26 583 / 113 (19%) / 61; Jun-26 536 / 136 (25%) / 76 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 revenue +39% YoY, profit +52% YoY; company's standalone figures: revenue ₹535 cr (+42%), EBITDA ₹133 cr (24.8%), PAT ₹71 cr (+49%) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]. The quarter was flattered by Xanthine spot prices after the West Asia war and by a ₹3.5 cr forex gain (vs ₹17 cr loss in Q4) [IP Q1FY27 p.18].
- Years: FY24 1,853 / 386 (21%) / 217; FY25 2,115 / 466 (22%) / 272; FY26 1,819 / 369 (20%) / 175; TTM 1,969 / 426 / 213 [Screener]. FY26 fell because API degrew (inventory issues at customers, price erosion), Ganesh Polychem left consolidation, new plants added cost ahead of revenue, and a ₹33 cr forex loss (incl. ₹29.7 cr mark-to-market on a forward contract) [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 41(n)].
- Unit economics: Xanthine Q1 volume +25% YoY; realisation fell after June as raw materials came off the peak (from +100% to +25–50% over pre-war) [Q1 call]. API gross margin ~45–50%; company gross margin ~50% ±; commercial CDMO gross margin 60–65% [Q1 call].
- Return on capital has halved: ROCE 19% (FY22) → 17% (FY25) → 11% (FY26) [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (26 May 2026): 15–18% revenue and EBITDA CAGR for 3–4 years; CDMO +40–50% in FY27; API to "surpass FY25" (~₹700 cr); Xanthine "well beyond ₹1,000 cr" from full capacity; margins will not fall below 20%; capex ~₹400 cr in FY27 [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (10 Aug 2026): standalone EBITDA margin 22–25% for FY27 (new); Xanthine FY27 revenue ₹900–1,000 cr (range first given as ₹900–1,100 cr); CDMO +40–50% kept, but "H2-heavy"; ₹1,000 cr CDMO goal moved from "FY28 or FY29" to "'29 or '30"; API goal cut — "we will not be able to cross it, but should be close" to FY25 [Q1 call].
- Verdict: Xanthine guidance raised; API guidance cut; CDMO long-range target pushed out by about a year; FY27 CDMO growth kept but now depends on H2 after only ~₹37 cr in Q1 (vs ~₹84 cr needed per quarter for +40%) [Q1 call; my arithmetic from ₹276 cr FY26].

## Capex and capacity
- Spent ~₹200 cr (FY25) and ~₹400 cr (FY26); ~₹400 cr planned for FY27, split roughly equally between Xanthine completion, Atali Phase 1 capitalisation/Phase 2, debottlenecking and maintenance [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.16]. Screener shows investing outflow of ₹412 cr (FY25) and ₹431 cr (FY26) [Screener].
- Atali greenfield Phase 1 cost ~₹450 cr for 440 KL; innovator-audited (no USFDA audit needed as it makes intermediates) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New Atali Block 2: ₹149 cr for ~405 KL dedicated CDMO block (civil works sized for 600 KL), groundbreaking Q3 FY27, commercialisation H2 FY28, for 3–4 projects from multiple customers [IP Q1FY27 p.15; Q1 call].
- Payback language: dedicated blocks 1.5–2x asset turns (Q4 call), but on the Q1 call the combined ~₹600–650 cr Atali investment was put at "around 1x" asset turn — a lower figure [Q4 call; Q1 call]. A single dedicated block could deliver ₹250–300 cr revenue [Q4 call].
- Debottlenecking: steroid block +33% capacity (6-week shutdown hit Q1); oncology block and Block 5 line next; management says no new API capex needed to reach ₹1,000 cr API revenue [Q1 call].
- R&D: 7.4% of API+CDMO sales (₹65 cr in FY26), 250+ scientists over three centres; new peptide/oligonucleotide lab — early [IP Q1FY27 p.8; Q1 call].
- Gross fixed assets + CWIP rose to ₹1,641 cr (standalone) with ₹404 cr capitalised in FY26 [AR FY26 auditor KAM].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹754 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹413 cr (Mar-25) and ₹285 cr (Mar-24); net debt/equity 0.32x [Screener; IP Q1FY27]. Finance cost ₹47 cr in FY26 vs ₹27 cr [Screener].
- Free cash flow negative two years running: –₹114 cr (FY25), –₹186 cr (FY26); operating cash flow ₹262 cr in FY26 (84% of operating profit) [Screener].
- Working capital is heavy: debtor days 117, inventory days 280, cash conversion cycle 251 days (FY26) [Screener]. CRISIL: gross current assets 219 days (Mar-25), inventory 130–150 days by its measure [CRISIL Jul-25]. Management: large CDMO customers pay no advances and ship once or twice a year, so inventory builds for quarters [Q4 call].
- Dividend ₹3.50/share for FY26 (payout 18%) [Q4 call; Screener].
- Rating CRISIL AA-/Stable (upgraded Jul-2025); downgrade trigger: operating margin below 18% or bigger debt-funded capex; bank lines ₹450 cr cash credit + ₹106.55 cr ECB + ₹100 cr term loan [CRISIL Jul-25; CRISIL Mar-26 bulletin].

## Moat evidence
- Xanthine: fully backward-integrated, non-Chinese source; 80–90% of competing capacity is in China; China removed ~11–13% export rebates and cut production quotas, and the US levies 20–25% tariffs on Chinese product — management calls the rebate/quota changes structural and the raw-material spike temporary ("50-50") [Q1 call; Q4 call]. Target 20–25% world share [IP Q1FY27].
- API: top 10 products are 60–70% of API sales with 10–50% market share each; 60 US DMFs and USFDA-approved Tarapur Unit 4 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]. But pricing power is weak: "pricing pressures in existing molecules remain" [Q1 call].
- CDMO: 37 commercial projects, nearly all revenue from Phase 3/commercial molecules; two sites USFDA-approved for intermediates; innovators have audited Atali [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Customers flat at 20–22 for five-plus quarters — breadth is not yet widening [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Cost position: in-house solvent recovery; 21 MW solar project [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor emphasis of matter (FY26):** a target-redemption forward contract taken in FY25 was "inadvertently not included" in fair-value calculations; ₹29.73 cr loss (₹22.24 cr after tax) booked in FY26, FY25 not restated (FY25 impact ₹6.02 cr judged immaterial). Opinion unmodified [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 41(n)]. This is a control lapse around derivatives, which were also a key audit matter.
- **Audit-trail modification:** the auditor modified its books-of-account statement because database-level audit trail on the SaaS accounting system could not be verified [AR FY26 auditor's report para 2(b), 2(i)(vi)].
- **Related parties:** ₹15 cr deposit with promoter-group **Valiant Organics** plus ₹15.5 cr "conducting cum loan & license fees" and ₹13 cr reimbursements in FY26 (likely the leased 250 KL site); ₹7.8 cr ICD to Aarti Ventures; ₹15 cr advance received from JV Ganesh Polychem at 8%; ₹41.9 cr sales to Aarti Drugs (₹92.9 cr in FY25); ₹85.5 cr purchases from promoter-linked Prozeal Green Energy in FY25 (solar) [AR FY26 note 38].
- Contingent liabilities are small: ₹7.9 cr bank guarantees; capital commitments ₹28.9 cr [AR FY26 note 35]. No pending litigation affecting financials [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Promoter holding down** from 46.3% (Sep-23) to 42.86% (Jun-26); DIIs down from 13.7% to 7.2% [Screener]. Management change: Rajendra Gogri ceased as director; Rashesh Gogri becomes MD and Hetal Gogri Gala whole-time director from 1 Oct 2026 [BSE filing 23 Sep 2026 via Screener].
- Xanthine prices already cooling by July; if China recovers or new capacity is sold into the spot market, margins normalise [Q1 call].
- Disclosure gaps: no segment P&L (single "Pharmaceuticals" segment in the accounts), segment revenue given only as % of standalone revenue with trading mixed in — caused visible confusion on the Q4 call [AR FY26 note 36; Q4 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "CDMO-led growth" (Q4, record ₹155 cr quarter) to "Xanthine carries the year" (Q1, record ₹305 cr Xanthine, CDMO 7%).
- New numbers: FY27 standalone EBITDA margin 22–25%; Xanthine ₹900–1,000 cr; Atali Block 2 approved (₹149 cr).
- Walk-backs: API FY25 level "will not be crossed"; ₹1,000 cr CDMO goal to FY29–FY30; dedicated-block asset turn 1.5–2x (Q4) vs "around 1x" for the whole Atali spend (Q1).
- New issue: CDMO delay blamed on "accounting standard" and non-deliveries — revenue is recognised on dispatch, so a missed shipment moves a quarter.

## My read
Aarti Pharmalabs is a sound, mid-quality chemistry business whose headline growth this year comes from a commodity (Xanthine) lifted by Chinese policy and a war-driven price spike, not from CDMO. FY26 profits fell 36% while debt nearly doubled to ₹754 cr and free cash flow was –₹186 cr; ROCE is now 11%. The price (about 40x trailing earnings) needs the 15–18% CAGR plus CDMO delivering ~₹390–415 cr in FY27, which requires roughly ₹350–380 cr in the last three quarters after ₹37 cr in Q1. I would turn more positive if H2 CDMO lands, Atali lifts ROCE back toward 15%, and customers grow beyond 22; I would turn negative if Xanthine prices fall faster than volumes rise, or if the derivative/audit-trail lapses recur.
