# Akums Drugs & Pharmaceuticals — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

Sources read: stored file (both call copies in full), official BSE transcript of the Q1 FY27 call (Aug 2026) to cross-check, Q1 FY27 investor presentation, Annual Report FY26 (MD&A/capital sections, directors' report incl. fraud and secretarial remarks, standalone and consolidated auditor's reports, contingent liabilities, contract balances), Screener consolidated page (raw HTML table rows). ICRA rationales (Apr 2026) are listed on Screener but the ICRA page did not render; not read.

## Business and segments
- India's largest domestic pharma CDMO (contract manufacturer of finished formulations for Indian pharma companies and MNCs), Haridwar-based, 50.6 bn-unit capacity across oral solids, liquids, injectables, hormones, penems, oncology, cosmetics. [AR FY26]
- **FY26 segment revenue / EBITDA (₹ cr):** CDMO 3,485 / 467 (+8.6% / +2.9%); domestic branded formulations (Akumentis) 446 / 90; international branded (Unosource) 143 / 36; trade generics 100 / −10; API 184 / −40. Total ₹4,359 cr (+5.8%), adjusted EBITDA ₹522 cr (12.0%). [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 segments:** CDMO ₹964 cr (+18.6% YoY), EBITDA ₹163 cr (~17%); Akumentis ₹115 cr (+7.3%), EBITDA ₹12 cr (−25% YoY, −46.5% QoQ — ~200 new medical reps); Unosource ₹35 cr (−1.5%), EBITDA ₹6.9 cr; API ₹32 cr (−29.7%), EBITDA −₹4 cr (−₹12 cr in Q4); trade generics ₹21 cr (−9.5%), EBITDA ~₹0.1 cr. CDMO = 82.6% of revenue. [Q1 call official; IP Q1FY27]
- **Utilisation:** CDMO capacity use 42.9% in FY26 (39.4% FY25, 24.6% FY24); ~50% by Q1 FY27; management treats 55–60% as practical peak. New injectable plant in "early teens" utilisation. [AR FY26; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Export build-up:** EU-GMP for Plants 1, 2, 3 and ANVISA for Plant 3; a European CDMO contract (fixed price, ~€35 mn a year from FY28 to 2032, "high teens" margin) for which €100 mn was received upfront; Zambia government supply of $25 mn a year in FY27 and FY28 from India, then a local plant ($45 mn, 51% Akums) from ~FY29–30. [Q4 call; AR FY26]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- **Quarters (sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 1,024 / 129 / 13% / 65; Sep-25 1,018 / 94 / 9% / 43; Dec-25 1,160 / 147 / 13% / 68; Mar-26 1,158 / 152 / 13% / 81; Jun-26 1,167 / 175 / 15% / 101. Q1 FY27 revenue +13.9%, EBITDA +35.4%, PAT +56%. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- **Years:** FY24 4,178 / 123 / 3% / 1; FY25 4,118 / 465 / 11% / 344 (incl. ₹106 cr deferred-tax credit); FY26 4,359 / 522 / 12% / 256; TTM 4,502 / 568 / 13% / 293. Other income ₹108 cr in FY26 (interest on deposits). [Screener; Q4 call]
- **Unit economics:** cost-plus model — a percentage mark-up on input cost, so when API prices fall ~20–25% (FY26), absolute profit falls; when they rise (from Mar 2026), profit rises. Gross margin 44.7% in Q1 FY27 (43.2% a year ago). CDMO volume growth "high teens" for three straight quarters, ~20%+ in H2 FY26. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Returns:** ROCE 15% FY26 (16% FY25); ROE ~8% because of the large cash pile. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 (18 May 2026) promises:** CDMO margins "similar"; double-digit CDMO volume growth in H1; Zambia $25 mn supplies to "commence by the end of Q2 FY27"; Europe from FY28 at €35 mn/yr; API losses to fall sharply but full year still negative; trade generics flat and near break-even; domestic branded double-digit growth; international branded double-digit growth; FY27 capex ~₹300 cr; tax rate 32% now, ~29% target; no revenue guidance ("We don't want to state future numbers"). [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** CDMO +18.6% with 17% EBITDA margin (beat); consolidated margin 15.0% (beat); API loss cut to ₹4 cr (ahead); trade generics at break-even (met); domestic branded +7.3% but margin down (missed on profit); international branded −1.5% (missed); Zambia start slipped to "Q3 or maybe Q4" though $25 mn for FY27 is kept. [Q1 call]
- **New on Q1 call:** blended CDMO margin 14–15% for FY27, "lean toward the upper bracket"; Q2 volume growth "high teens"; volume growth at least double digits for FY27; API monthly EBITDA break-even by Feb–Mar 2027; Akumentis to improve from Q3. [Q1 call]
- Verdict: kept, CDMO slightly raised; Zambia timing slipped one to two quarters.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹222 cr; FY27 target ~₹300 cr; new oral-solid plant at Baddi to go live by end-FY27 (cost "still discussing"); Oriflame India's two factories (Noida skincare, Roorkee tablets) bought for ₹56 cr in Jul 2026; Zambia plant $45 mn (Akums 51%); capital commitments ₹80 cr at Mar-26. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 38]
- With utilisation ~50% vs a 55–60% peak, the existing base can still absorb growth; management says spare capacity is a deliberate competitive tool. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- "Cash surplus" ₹1,616 cr at Jun-26 (₹1,682 cr gross cash at Mar-26), 98% in nationalised-bank FDs. Borrowings ₹157 cr (Screener). [Q1 call; Q4 call; Screener]
- **Important caveat:** about ₹1,033 cr of the cash is a customer advance — contract liabilities (advances from customers) were ₹1,076 cr at Mar-26 vs ₹65 cr a year earlier, mainly the €100 mn European prepayment, which will be worked off against supplies to 2032. Operating cash flow of ₹1,181 cr and FCF of ₹958 cr in FY26 were "majorly attributable to the European contract". Excluding the advance, underlying net cash is closer to ₹600 cr, and FCF ex-advance ≈₹0 cr (my arithmetic). The ₹94 cr interest cost in FY26 largely reflects the financing charge on this advance. [AR FY26 contract balances; Q4 call; Screener]
- Working-capital days rose from 91 to 105 (inventory built ahead of war-time supply risk). Dividend: ₹1 final + ₹2 special (18% payout). [Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and breadth: 1,035 DCGI product approvals; serves "all the large MNCs in India" for 15+ years; four R&D centres; >₹100 cr R&D over three years. [AR FY26; Q4 call]
- Share gains: volume growth ~20% vs Indian pharma market volume growth of 1–3%, coming from existing customers moving in-house or other CDMOs' volumes to Akums. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Regulatory upgrade: EU-GMP on three plants; chosen by a European partner for a €200 mn fixed-price mandate after due diligence — "India's first domestic CDMO to win a major European formulation mandate". [AR FY26; Q4 call]
- Limits: pricing is cost-plus, so pricing power is modest; CDMO margin sits at 13–17%. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Income Tax search (Jan 2025):** the IT Department searched offices, plants and KMP residences on 15–21 Jan 2025 under Section 132 and cloned electronic books; after year-end the company and some subsidiaries received show-cause notices under Section 158BC (block assessment). The auditors (Walker Chandiok) carry this as an **emphasis of matter** in both standalone and consolidated reports; three subsidiary auditors do too. Impact "pending conclusion" — not quantified. [AR FY26 auditor's reports; note 52(d)]
- **Fraud reported by auditors (Sec 143(12)):** ₹0.45 cr misappropriation via fictitious/inflated IT procurement by subsidiary employees; disciplinary action taken and insurance claim filed. Small, but a control lapse. [AR FY26 directors' report; CARO xi]
- **Board compliance lapse:** board composition breached SEBI LODR after an independent director left in Feb 2025 until Jun 2025; management blamed the IT search workload. [AR FY26 secretarial remarks]
- **Contingent liabilities ₹99 cr:** income tax ₹76.6 cr (mostly a ₹73.4 cr pre-acquisition demand on Parabolic Drugs, contested), indirect tax ₹10.4 cr, NPPA drug-pricing recovery claim ₹12.2 cr. [AR FY26 note 37]
- **Fixed-price European contract:** a fixed price to 2032 exposes margins to input inflation; management says costing already builds in inflation. [Q4 call]
- **Capital allocation:** API business has lost ~₹40 cr a year for three years; a minority shareholder on the Q1 call asked management to exit it; ₹1,600 cr "cash" invites M&A risk. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- No promoter pledge; promoters hold 75.26% (Screener). [Screener]

## My read
The core CDMO is in a genuine up-cycle — three quarters of ~20% volume growth, rising API prices that lift absolute profit under the cost-plus model, and margins climbing to 15%. Exports (Europe from FY28, Zambia from H2 FY27) add higher-margin layers, and spare capacity means little new capex is needed. But the headline net cash is mostly a customer prepayment, the tax-search matter is unresolved with fresh show-cause notices, and API remains a drag. I would watch the Zambia start, the API break-even by March 2027, and the outcome of the Section 158BC proceedings.
