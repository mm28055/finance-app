## Business and segments
- Hyderabad API maker with two businesses: **CMS** (custom manufacturing — APIs and intermediates for innovator drugs, mostly for venture-backed biotechs) and **GDS** (generic APIs: "Prime" high-volume molecules like Levetiracetam, Mirtazapine, Ezetimibe; "Specialty" complex generics like Aripiprazole sterile, Donepezil, Apixaban, Paliperidone) [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Aug-26].
- FY26 revenue mix: CMS 61% (+94% YoY), Prime + Specialty APIs 33% (Prime −5%, Specialty −16% on Paliperidone customer issues), other 6% [CRISIL Aug-26]. Q1 FY27: CMS ~67% of revenue; CMS commercial ₹418 cr vs ₹120 cr a year earlier; CMS development only ~₹20 cr [IP Q1FY27].
- CMS pipeline: 99 active projects (Q1 FY27) — 17 pre-clinical, 23 Phase 1, 20 Phase 2, 13 Phase 3, 7 pre-registration, 19 commercial [IP Q1FY27].
- Geography FY26: US ~35%, Europe ~33%; regulated markets >94% [CRISIL Aug-26]. Exports 92% [IP Q1FY27].
- **Concentration is high in CMS**: CMS top-5 customers = 93% of segment revenue and top-5 products = 94%; company-wide top 10 customers ~73%, top 5 products ~65% [CRISIL Aug-26]. Three customer groups each >10% of revenue = ₹1,088 cr (~53% of FY26 revenue), up from ₹504 cr in FY25 [AR FY26 consolidated segment note, major customers]. CEO: "maybe about 3 of them … really drive our business" [Q1 call].
- Capacity: three USFDA-inspected units, ~1,226 KL (Unit 1 256 KL, Unit 2 389 KL, Unit 3 581 KL); two units at 85–90% utilisation, one ~65% (May 2026) [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]. Peptide module 1 (7,000 L; ~$30 m) operational from Sep 2026 [BSE press release, 15 Sep 2026].

- Currency helped: CEO said FY26 was "slightly better than expected, aided by the favorable exchange rates" [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (12 May 2026):** no formal guidance; 18–20% CAGR "a fair assumption not necessarily linearly", described as a five-year aspiration; EBITDA 25%+; ROCE "expected to moderate" as capex rises; one commercialisation in FY27 and 1–2 later; peptide facility "ready by July"; working capital days (137) "should normalize in FY27" [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (5 Aug 2026):** revenue +116%, EBITDA 35.5% — **beat** on both; working capital days fell to 84 from 137 — **delivered**; peptide plant slipped from July to commissioning "next month" (module 1 declared operational on 15 Sep) — **slightly late**; two new advanced-clinic projects won (some peptides) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; BSE press release].
- **Guidance now:** "we've always aspired to grow at about 20%, and we expect that FY27 & FY28 also"; margin "25% plus… anything above that is kind of a bonus"; do not treat Q1 as a base; capex intensity to rise further, possibly overseas or via M&A for new modalities [Q1 call]. CRISIL (Aug-26) expects FY27 revenue growth "above 15%" [CRISIL Aug-26].
- Arithmetic (own calc from Screener): FY26 Q2–Q4 sales were ₹1,730 cr. With Q1 FY27 at ₹642 cr, FY27 needs only ~₹1,785 cr in Q2–Q4 (+3%) to hit +20% — so the 20% aspiration looks achievable even with flat later quarters; the risk is margin, not growth.

## Capex and capacity
- Approved capex ₹1,460 cr over the last 13 quarters, ₹870 cr spent to date; ₹203 cr approved in Q1 FY27 (₹196 cr for Unit 1 capacity) [Q1 call]. Capex cash outflow ₹397 cr FY26, ₹122 cr Q1 FY27 [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Peptides: module 1 (7,000 L: 750 L solid-phase + 6,250 L liquid-phase) operational; module 2 (up to 18,000 L) to be pulled forward, configured with an anchor customer; four modules planned [BSE press release, 15 Sep 2026]. CRISIL expects peptide revenue from FY28 [CRISIL Aug-26].
- New R&D centre at Genome Valley (140,000 sq ft), cost ₹189 cr (₹130 cr debt) [CRISIL Aug-26; IP Q1FY27].
- Land: ₹126 cr for 134 acres at Kakinada (Andhra Pradesh) with first refusal on 66 more — a new large site, funded from accruals [BSE filing, 7 Sep 2026].
- Capital commitments ₹154 cr at Mar-26 (₹83 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 note 40].
- Returns: company ROCE 26.1% FY26, 35.8% Q1 FY27 annualised; fixed-asset turnover 2.6x FY26 [IP Q1FY27]. Management warns ROCE will dip as gestation periods lengthen [Q1 call].

## Moat evidence
- Regulatory record: no USFDA OAI classification, warning letter or import alert ever; 18 USFDA inspections cleared; 1,010+ DMFs filed [CRISIL Aug-26; IP Q1FY27].
- Stickiness: supply agreements usually survive customer M&A ("change of ownership does not necessarily annul… the supply agreement") [Q4 call].
- Capability: 16–18 years of peptide process chemistry; supplying 3 NDA filings and 18 IND filings; sole supplier for the first USFDA-approved deuterated molecule [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Scale of opportunity: five years ago the largest visible molecule was ~₹50 cr a year; today management can "visualize ₹500 crores per year or even ₹1,000 crores per year" molecules [Q1 call].
- Partnership with Gland Pharma for sterile APIs (asset-light; niche generics) [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Concentration**: ~3 commercial CMS molecules drive the business; three customer groups = ~53% of revenue [Q1 call; AR FY26]. An analyst linked one key molecule to bempedoic acid (Esperion, subject to a buy-out bid by ArchiMed) and its later-decade patent expiry; management declined to confirm the customer [Q4 call].
- **Transcript integrity question**: an investor alleged on the Q4 call that the Q3 FY26 transcript had altered wording of a question on "inventory manipulation" versus the audio; on the Q1 call the CFO said the agency version and uploaded version matched and the full audio is public [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Not proven, but worth noting given the Q3→Q4 swing.
- Leadership change: from 1 Apr 2026 Saharsh Davuluri became CEO & MD and Sucheth Davuluri moved to Executive Vice Chairman — "part of a preplanned transition" [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 39].
- Related parties: CSR routed through the promoter-linked Neuland Foundation (about ₹14 cr contributed in FY26); rent of ₹3.6 cr a year to Mrs. D. Vijaya Rao (promoter relative) [AR FY26 note 39]. Promoter-director pay figures could not be read reliably from the PDF layout — **not verified**.
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter is the carrying value of goodwill from the Neuland Health Sciences merger [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Contingent liabilities small: disputed income tax ₹7.5 cr, GST/customs ₹1.1 cr, a ₹4.5 cr power surcharge and old land-title disputes at Bonthapally [AR FY26 note 41].
- GDS weakness: Prime −5% and Specialty −16% in FY26 [CRISIL Aug-26].
- Capex step-up (new land, peptides, R&D centre, possible overseas assets) with longer payback; ROCE will fall [Q1 call].

## My read
Neuland's earnings have stepped up sharply — TTM profit ~₹500 cr vs ₹260 cr in FY25 — on a handful of commercial CMS molecules, and Q1 FY27 already secures most of a 20% growth year. The business is improving (net cash, A+/Positive rating, peptide plant live), but it is concentrated: three customer groups are over half of revenue and margins swing 18%→40% between quarters. Management itself guides margin back towards 25%, so today's 35% should not be capitalised. What would make me more positive: a second commercial molecule of scale in FY27–FY28 and peptide revenue in FY28; what would worsen the view: any sign of destocking or generic entry in the top molecules.
