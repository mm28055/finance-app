# OneSource Specialty Pharma — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

Sources read: stored file (both call copies in full), official BSE transcripts of the Q1 FY27 call (25 Jul 2026) and Q4 FY26 call (13 May 2026), Q1 FY27 investor presentation (24 Jul 2026), Annual Report FY26 (auditor's report + CARO, goodwill/impairment note, contingent liabilities, related-party note, borrowings), CareEdge rating release (5 Mar 2026), India Ratings upgrade (Aug 2026, via news headline only — the agency page did not load), Screener consolidated page (raw HTML table rows).

## Business and segments
- Bengaluru-based CDMO (contract development and manufacturing for other drug companies) formed from Stelis Biopharma plus the soft-gel business of Strides and the injectables business of Steriscience (merger effective Nov 2024, listed Jan 2025). Promoter group led by Arun Kumar (founder of Strides). One reported segment (CDMO). [AR FY26 note 39; CareEdge Mar-26]
- **Four offerings:** (1) drug-device combinations (DDC) — filling GLP-1 drugs such as semaglutide into cartridges and assembling pens; (2) biologics — integrated drug substance + drug product (microbial 1 KL, mammalian 4 KL; +5 KL and +4 KL planned); (3) soft-gelatin capsules — 2.4 bn capsules a year, +1.6 bn targeted; (4) sterile injectables incl. a dedicated penicillin plant. Five sites, all in Bengaluru. [IP Q1FY27; CareEdge Mar-26]
- **Mix:** no revenue split is disclosed ("we don't really break down business-wise revenue"). The chairman said soft gels and injectables together are "trending closer to the $100 million mark" (≈₹945 cr at ₹94.5/$) — i.e. more than half of today's revenue is the base business, not GLP-1. [Q4 call]
- **GLP-1 position:** the only CDMO behind all three generic semaglutide approvals in Canada (two partners launched: Dr. Reddy's, and Orbicular with a Canadian partner); in India supplies 10 of 21 generic pen brands and "more than 40%" of generic pens sold (end-June 2026). India demand rose from under 2,000 pens a month a year ago to 150,000–160,000. Two customers hold US first-to-file status on tirzepatide. Hikma (MENA) launching soon. 20+ DDC customers, 80+ customers overall. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Capacity/utilisation:** cartridge line 1 (~225 sterile days a year, 200-litre batches, ~60,000 pens a day) was full in Q1 FY27 and "reached full contribution". Line 2 commercial in Q2 FY27, line 3 installed within FY27 → 675 sterile days in FY28; a fourth line later in FY28. New lines take 500–1,000-litre batches (2.5x output per day at 500 L). CareEdge: cartridge capacity to rise from 40 mn to 220 mn by FY28. Soft gels to be fully used within 12–15 months; pre-filled-syringe capacity still mostly free. [Q1 call; Q4 call; CareEdge Mar-26]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- **Quarters (sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 327 / 88 / 27% / −0; Sep-25 376 / 106 / 28% / 10; Dec-25 290 / 17 / 6% / −89; Mar-26 428 / 92 / 21% / 5; Jun-26 449 / 123 / 27% / 25. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹449 cr (+37% YoY, +5% QoQ); EBITDA ₹123.3 cr (+39% YoY, +34% QoQ), margin 27.5%; reported PAT ₹25 cr; "adjusted PAT" ₹63.7 cr (excludes ₹4.3 cr exceptional items and ₹34.4 cr a quarter of amortisation of intangibles created by the merger). In dollars: $47.5 mn revenue, $13.1 mn EBITDA. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- **Years:** FY24 172 / −82 (Stelis alone); FY25 1,445 / 467 / 32% / −18; FY26 1,422 / 304 / 21% / −74; TTM 1,543 / 339 / 22% / −49. FY26 fell because Canadian semaglutide approvals slipped and Q3 FY26 collapsed (6% margin). Adjusted PAT FY26 ₹73.9 cr per company. [Screener; Q4 call; AR FY26]
- Depreciation ~₹280 cr a year includes ~₹138 cr of merger-intangible amortisation; interest ₹122 cr FY26. These two lines are why reported profit is near zero. [Screener; AR FY26]
- **Unit economics:** margin is driven by DDC mix and operating leverage — opex for lines 2 and 3 is already in the cost base ("the opEx has to be upfronted"). Base business is seasonal (soft gels peak in H2 flu season). [Q1 call]
- **Returns:** ROCE 1% FY26 on Screener (net worth ~₹5,831 cr incl. ₹1,976 cr goodwill). Company targets ROCE above 50% excluding goodwill and scheme intangibles and capital work in progress. [Screener; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 4D]

## Guidance history
- **Standing target (reiterated on both calls):** FY28 organic revenue $400 mn with ~40% EBITDA margin ($160 mn). At ₹94.5/$ that is ≈₹3,780 cr revenue and ≈₹1,510 cr EBITDA — 2.4x TTM revenue and 4.5x TTM EBITDA in under two years. No FY27 guidance ("we are reiterating FY28"). [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; own arithmetic]
- **Q4 FY26 promises:** line 2 available in Q2 FY27; three lines by end-FY27; sequential QoQ growth in revenue and EBITDA through FY27; working capital to normalise in FY27; cost of borrowing below 9%; Steriscience/Brooks related-party merger deferred about two years. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** sequential growth delivered (revenue +5%, EBITDA +34%); line 2 "set for commercialisation" in Q2 (on track, not yet proven); 80% of the $100 mn capex committed; nine launches, six new logos, 12 inspections incl. two surprise FDA audits. Dr. Reddy's paused its semaglutide supplies, but other customers filled the capacity. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Longer term:** Europe opens for semaglutide from 2029–30 and the US later; biologics commercial manufacturing from FY29; greenfield soft-gel site update "over the next quarter or so". [Q1 call]
- Verdict: guidance kept, not raised. The FY28 goal has been held "for the last couple of years" (analyst on Q4 call), including through the FY26 miss. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- $100 mn (~₹870 cr) programme over FY26–FY28, mostly DDC; ~80% committed. CareEdge expected ~₹400 cr spent by end-FY26. Capital commitments ₹282 cr at Mar-26 (₹58 cr a year earlier). [Q1 call; CareEdge Mar-26; AR FY26 note 28]
- Next: biologics expansion (+5 KL microbial, +4 KL mammalian) "significantly lower" in cost than DDC; greenfield soft-gel plant (+1.6 bn capsules); injectables line shut in Q2–Q3 FY27 to add lyophilisation and high-viscosity pre-filled syringes (benefit "FY30 numbers"). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Funding: all by borrowing — "fully funded through incremental borrowings". [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹1,276 cr at Mar-26 vs ₹772 cr at Mar-25 (CareEdge: ₹1,353 cr at Dec-25 excl. leases); CWIP ₹529 cr. Four rating upgrades since listing; cost of debt below 9%. [Screener; CareEdge Mar-26; Q4 call]
- Cash from operations only ₹9 cr in FY26 (−₹68 cr FY25); free cash flow −₹561 cr FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital ballooned: inventory ₹440 cr at Mar-26 vs ₹158 cr at Mar-25; debtor days 177, inventory days 438 (Screener). Management says the inventory is customer-funded GLP-1 material backed by ~₹250 cr of customer advances ("It is paid for by the customers already"). [Q4 call; Screener]
- No dividend. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- First and only CDMO with three G7 semaglutide approvals; >40% of India generic pens; customers pay capacity-reservation fees and sign take-or-pay contracts ("both by paying upfront fee as well as take-or-pay kind of contract"). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Switching costs: a drug's filing names the filling site, so moving supplier needs a new regulatory approval. Batch-size changes need approvals in some markets. [Q1 call]
- Compliance: 49 inspections/audits in FY26, 12 in Q1 FY27, surprise FDA inspections passed with EIRs, EU GMP renewed, ANVISA (Brazil) and TGA approvals. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Biologics funnel 4x FY25, 70+ active RFPs, Formycon and a US biosimilar major signed. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Rating momentum: India Ratings upgraded to IND A/Positive from IND A- in Aug 2026, citing commercial GLP-1 supplies. CareEdge BBB+/Stable was withdrawn at the company's request in Mar 2026. [News: Business Standard, 18 Aug 2026; CareEdge Mar-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Contingent liability of ₹1,286 cr:** subsidiary Biolexis faces a $136.3 mn claim from Prestige Biopharma over a terminated Sputnik Light vaccine manufacturing deal, now at the Singapore International Arbitration Centre; the company calls it "frivolous" and seeks a $13.6 mn refund. The claim is about a fifth of net worth. CareEdge flagged "risk of contingent liability materialising". [AR FY26 note 28; CareEdge Mar-26]
- **Goodwill of ₹1,976 cr** from the merger; impairment test is a key audit matter (Deloitte) and depends on long-range forecasts ("projection period exceeds five years"). A delay in GLP-1 volumes could force a write-down. [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 4D/4G]
- **Related parties:** roughly ₹400 cr of FY26 revenue (~28%, my sum of the note) came from Strides-group and Steriscience entities, the largest being Strides Pharma Inc. (~₹274 cr). The proposed merger of promoter-owned Steriscience/Brooks (valued at ~₹2,200 a share) was pulled after minority pushback and "deferred" about two years. [AR FY26 note 32; Q4 call]
- **Auditor remarks:** Deloitte's opinion is unmodified, but it notes audit trail (edit log) was "not enabled for certain critical tables" in one accounting software, and independent-director commission awaits shareholder approval. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Customer concentration and dependence on semaglutide approvals:** Dr. Reddy's paused semaglutide supplies in Q1 FY27; FY26 already showed how an approval delay can wipe out a quarter's profit (Q3 FY26 margin 6%). [Q1 call; Screener]
- **Leverage and cash burn:** debt up ₹500 cr in a year with negative free cash flow. [Screener]
- **Promoter pledge:** promoters hold 30.5% and have pledged 37.2% of their holding (Screener). The company's own share pledge to lenders was released in Sep 2024, so this is a promoter-level pledge, not a company loan. [Screener; AR FY26 note 12]
- **Disclosure gaps:** no segment, customer or line-level revenue; no FY27 guidance. [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was about recovery and the dropped related-party deal; Q1 was about full capacity despite Dr. Reddy's pause, and new biologics customers.
- Numbers: EBITDA margin 21.5% → 27.5%; revenue flat in dollars QoQ ($47.5 mn vs ~$48 mn) — growth now waits on line 2.
- New issues: injectables plant shutdown in Q2–Q3; US tariff talk; freight delays via Suez/Hormuz (contracts are ex-works, so customers bear the cost).

## My read
The business turned the corner in Q1 FY27: line 1 is full, margins are back to 27.5% and demand for GLP-1 filling outruns supply. But the FY28 target needs revenue to rise about 2.4 times and EBITDA 4.5 times in under two years, so everything rides on lines 2 and 3 filling on time and on partners' approvals in new markets. Reported profit will stay small because of merger amortisation and interest; the ₹1,286 cr arbitration claim and the ~28% related-party revenue are real governance overhangs. The view improves if Q2–Q3 show revenue stepping up with line 2 and margins above 30%; it worsens on any approval slip, a further Dr. Reddy's delay or an adverse arbitration ruling.
