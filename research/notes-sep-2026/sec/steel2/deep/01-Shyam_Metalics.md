# Shyam Metalics and Energy — deep research (Phase A1)

Screener code SHYAMMETL (consolidated). Price ₹1,086, market cap ₹30,303 cr, 52-week range ₹746–1,148, book value ₹413/share, ROCE 13.0%, ROE 9.7%, dividend yield 0.41% (Screener, 28 Sep 2026). **Transcript verdicts:** the stored Q1 FY27 transcript (call 21 Jul 2026, filed 24 Jul) and the stored Q4 FY26 transcript (call 12 May 2026, filed 16 May) both **match official** — they carry the BSE cover letters, and key figures were checked against the BSE copies. The official Q4 copy itself contains two slips: "crossing the INR8,000 crores of revenue threshold" (should read ₹18,000 cr) and stainless capacity "to 6 million tonnes" (the presentation shows a 0.6 MTPA melt shop).

## Business and segments
- Integrated "ore to metal" producer in east India: iron pellets, sponge iron, pig iron, billets, TMT/wire rods/structurals (carbon steel long), CR coil/colour-coated/galvalume sheets (carbon steel flat), ferro alloys ("speciality alloys"), stainless steel long products (Pithampur, MP; acquired from Mittal Corp, Oct 2023) and aluminium foil (Pakuria, WB; Sambalpur, Odisha). Plants at Sambalpur, Jamuria, Mangalpur, Kharagpur (Ramsarup Industries), Pakuria, Giridih and Indore/Pithampur. [IP Q1FY27 p.14–15, 19–22]
- Claims: 6th-largest Indian steel producer, 2nd-largest coal-based sponge-iron maker, 16.93 MTPA combined capacity, 467 MW captive power (79% of power needs at ₹2.65/kWh vs grid ₹5–7). [IP Q1FY27 p.5, 35]
- The standalone company reports one segment (iron and steel); no customer is 10% of revenue. CRISIL says no customer is above 5% of consolidated revenue. [AR FY26 note 46; CRISIL Nov-25]
- Revenue mix Q1 FY27: steel products about 77%, carbon steel the largest single line (about 39% of FY26 revenue per the Q4 call). Exports were 21% of FY26 revenue; more than 60% of foil output is exported. [IP Q1FY27 p.16–17, 39; Q4 call]
- FY26 sales volume 49.46 lakh t (+26%); Q1 FY27 13.83 lakh t (+14% YoY). Company projects FY27 at 61.62 lakh t (+25%). [IP Q1FY27 p.45]
- EBITDA per tonne Q1 FY27 (Q4 FY26): metallics ₹2,906 (₹3,238); carbon steel ₹7,688 (₹7,460); stainless ₹10,194 (₹8,512); speciality alloys ₹21,809 (₹18,780); aluminium ₹53,661 (₹36,562). [IP Q1FY27 p.38]
- Realisations Q1 FY27: stainless ₹1,75,925/t (+27% YoY); aluminium foil ₹4,83,467/t (+32%); CR coil/sheet ₹86,815/t (+21%); speciality alloys ₹1,06,613/t (+22%); carbon steel ₹45,777/t (+2%). [IP Q1FY27 p.40–41]
- August 2026 monthly release (YoY): stainless volume +16% at ₹1,83,032/t; foil +27%; speciality alloys +28%; carbon steel long only +2.8% (and −7% month on month); carbon steel flat +114%; metallics +28%. The new 0.018 MTPA foil plant at Sambalpur was commissioned in July 2026 and is "stable". [BSE sales release 7 Sep 2026]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (12 May 2026):** the CMD said FY27 growth would be "close to 30%" and agreed with an analyst's ₹3,000 cr-plus FY27 EBITDA "maybe 5% here and there". CRM EBITDA ₹10,000–11,000/t and aluminium ₹35,000–40,000/t for FY27; aluminium benefits "in the second half". FY27 capex about ₹2,900 cr, FY28 about ₹3,000 cr, balance over two more years (total about ₹10,000 cr). Debt capped at 0.5x equity. Inventory had risen to 123 days; the CFO agreed it would be liquidated in Q1 or Q2. New ₹2,700 cr capex: ₹900 cr 8 lakh t long/speciality wire-bar mill at Kharagpur and ₹1,800 cr stainless expansion with cold-rolling and bright-annealing at Sambalpur, both by March 2029. Galvalume line "within this month end or early next month". [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (21 Jul 2026) — the current guidance:** FY27 EBITDA growth "more than 20%, 25%", with internal projections "more than 25%"; revenue and volume growth also "more than 20%". The CMD called 20% a deliberately low public number. Long-term EBITDA margin aspiration 14–15% (opening remarks), and "15% to 17% or 16%" for the whole business by 2031 (Q&A). Vision 2031: revenue about ₹42,500 cr (about 18% a year), EBITDA about ₹6,200 cr (about 22% a year), ROCE 22% and ROE 20% on the company's basis (+600–700 bp). ₹575 cr capex spent in Q1; ₹9,580 cr balance over 3–4 years, "primarily" from internal accruals. Inventory to stay at a "2- to 3-month" level. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.9, 48]
- **What changed:** the FY27 growth number fell from "close to 30%" (May) to "more than 20–25%" (July). Management framed this as conservatism, not a cut. The inventory unwind promised in May was dropped: in July the CFO said 2–3 months of inventory is normal. Stainless ambition moved from "₹300 cr a month in 3 years" (May) to a "₹600–700 cr" run-rate once the new plant runs at 70–80% (July; unit unclear on the call). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Q1 delivery vs May promises:** operating profit +32% YoY (ahead of the 30% pace); aluminium ₹53,661/t (well above the ₹35,000–40,000/t guide); stainless ₹10,194/t. The flat/CRM business made about ₹8,500/t per an analyst on the call, below the ₹10,000–11,000/t guide. Volume +14%, well short of the 25% annual plan. Capex of ₹575 cr is about 20% of the ₹2,900 cr FY27 plan. **Verdict: profit guidance delivered for Q1, volume behind, headline FY27 number trimmed, inventory promise dropped.** [Screener; IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Commissioning promised for FY27: colour-coated line (done April 2026; CRM capacity 0.25 → 0.4 MT); aluminium foil stock and foil plant (commissioned July 2026, "3–4 months" to regularise, value from Q3); 0.5 MT iron-making (end of Q2/early Q3); power plants in Q2; aluminium FRP "on track for Q2"; wagon plant Phase 1 operations from September 2026. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.21, 23; BSE sales release Aug-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Since listing the company has spent ₹9,205 cr of a total planned ₹18,785 cr (49%); ₹6,286 cr has been capitalised. [IP Q1FY27 p.37]
- Pending projects at 30 Jun 2026: ₹13,695 cr budget, ₹3,654 cr spent, **₹10,041 cr pending**. The largest items: hot strip mill (HR coil, about 1.58 MTPA) ₹5,400 cr (only ₹169 cr spent); stainless flat chain ₹2,940 cr (₹755 cr spent: 0.6 MTPA melt shop, 0.6 MTPA hot strip mill, HRAP, 0.4 MTPA CRM, precision CRM, bright annealing); SBQ mill 0.8 MTPA ₹900 cr (₹75 cr spent); captive power 100 MW ₹930 cr (₹545 cr spent); solar ₹450 cr (₹25 cr spent); aluminium ₹800 cr (₹744 cr spent); railway wagons 4,800 units ₹200 cr (₹82 cr spent). [IP Q1FY27 p.26–29]
- Most big projects commission in FY28–FY29 ("most ... majorly '28, '29"), so FY31 targets rest on plants not yet built. [Q1 call]
- A 2 MTPA HR plant would normally cost ₹16,000–18,000 cr; the CMD says brownfield integration cuts that to about a third. [Q4 call]
- Solar capex is switching to an "opex model": in Q1 a 26% stake was bought in Emerge Green Power (management says it is a PE-backed platform and not a related party). Separately, the subsidiary SSPL bought 26% of Emerge Solar Projects in Aug 2025, which the AR lists as an associate and as a KMP-influenced entity. [Q1 call; AR FY26 AOC-1 and note 41]
- Another downstream capex (after the HR plant) is under study; a decision is due "by third quarter". [Q1 call]
- The Vision 2031 mix shift is striking: stainless revenue ₹1,322 cr → ₹11,969 cr and EBITDA ₹77 cr → ₹1,108 cr; SBQ ₹0 → ₹1,019 cr EBITDA; CRM ₹106 cr → ₹645 cr; carbon steel ₹1,706 cr → ₹2,866 cr; speciality alloys EBITDA *falls* ₹367 cr → ₹143 cr as alloy output moves to captive stainless use. [IP Q1FY27 p.49]
- CRISIL expects a ₹6,500–7,000 cr outlay over FY26–FY28, funded from accruals of about ₹2,000–2,500 cr a year. [CRISIL Nov-25]

- Cash flow: CFO ₹2,024 cr FY26 (₹1,713 cr FY25); investing −₹1,939 cr; Screener free cash flow −₹614 cr FY26 and negative in FY23–FY26. Capex was 101% of CFO in FY26. [Screener; IP Q1FY27 p.44]
- **The Vision 2031 slide shows gross debt rising from ₹957 cr to about ₹4,226 cr by FY31**, offset by about ₹4,958 cr of cash/accruals, so net cash of about ₹732 cr. That sits awkwardly with "no external debt" and "debt only for working capital" on other slides. [IP Q1FY27 p.9, 11, 48]

## Moat evidence
- **Cost position:** captive power covers 79% of needs at ₹2.65/kWh against grid power at ₹5–7; raw materials within 250 km; two captive railway sidings and 19 rakes. [IP Q1FY27 p.5, 15, 35]
- Integration from pellets to finished products lets the company switch between selling intermediates and using them captively. CRISIL calls its market position "established" in east India. [CRISIL Nov-25]
- Never loss-making in 20+ years; revenue, EBITDA and PAT compounded about 20% a year over two decades (company claim). [IP Q1FY27 p.8]
- Pricing power is limited in carbon steel (realisation +2% YoY); it is stronger in foil (exports, "oversold") and speciality alloys. [IP Q1FY27 p.40; Q4 call]
- The stainless plan uses about 80% in-house inputs (alloys, power, pig iron), which management says is a cost edge over scrap-based rivals paying ₹6–7/kWh. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **ED attachment (material):** on 15 Apr 2026 the Enforcement Directorate provisionally attached ₹152.48 cr of corporate bonds and AIF units held by subsidiary Shyam SEL and Power under PMLA. The probe concerns coal bought from illegal mining and pilferage in Eastern Coalfields areas; the filing put the total at ₹159.51 cr. **The auditor added an Emphasis of Matter** to the consolidated report. On the Q4 call the CMD played it down as "some letter" that applied to many steel firms. [AR FY26 note 51 and auditor's report; Q4 call; News: TradeBrains/ScanX, Apr 2026]
- A Central Pollution Control Board inspection found a non-compliance; management says it was fixed in 4–5 days. [Q4 call]
- Consolidated contingent liabilities ₹723 cr (FY25 ₹560 cr): indirect-tax claims ₹138 cr, customs ₹15 cr, VAT ₹15 cr, income tax ₹5 cr, bank guarantees ₹227 cr, bills discounted ₹323 cr. A subsidy of ₹41.83 cr has long been receivable from the West Bengal government (a key audit matter). [AR FY26 note 40; auditor's report]
- The auditor notes that audit trail was not enabled at database level for one accounting software; for a second, third-party-managed software it could not assess audit-trail preservation. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Related parties are mainly intra-group: sales to subsidiaries ₹624 cr and advances of ₹3,333 cr given (₹3,170 cr recovered). Dealings with promoter-linked firms are small (about ₹3 cr of sales). No promoter pledge found. [AR FY26 note 41]
- Execution risk is very large: ₹10,000 cr pending, including a ₹5,400 cr HR mill with almost nothing spent, and new product lines (stainless flat, SBQ, wagons, battery foil) where the company has no track record. [IP Q1FY27 p.26–29]
- Demand and cycle: secondary rebar prices fell in July; carbon-long volumes grew only 2.8% in August. [Q1 call; BSE sales release Aug-26]
- ROE on Screener's basis (9.7%) is well below the company's presented 13%, because a large book of cash, AIFs and CWIP earns little. [Screener; IP Q1FY27 p.43]

New items: the Emerge Green stake, the ₹4,500 cr enabling resolution, and a further downstream capex under study. The ED matter was not raised on the Q1 call. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]

## My read
The operating business is improving: Q1 operating profit rose 32% and per-tonne profit rose in stainless, alloys and aluminium, and the balance sheet is still net cash. But this is still mostly a carbon-steel and intermediates business, and the FY31 story depends on about ₹10,000 cr of projects that mostly commission in FY28–FY29. Free cash flow has been negative for four years, and interest and depreciation are rising. At the current price it needs about 17% a year earnings growth. That is plausible if FY27 lands near management's "25%+", but not if carbon-steel spreads soften. The ED attachment and the growing Level-3 AIF book are governance points to watch. What would change the view: confirmation or release of the ED attachment, and whether the HR mill and stainless flat projects stay on their FY28–FY29 schedule without net debt.
