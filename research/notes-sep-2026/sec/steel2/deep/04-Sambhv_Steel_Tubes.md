# Sambhv Steel Tubes — deep research (Phase A1)

Screener code SAMBHV (consolidated; one wholly owned subsidiary, Sambhv Tubes Ltd). Price ₹159, market cap ₹4,689 cr, 52-week range ₹80.7–162, book value ₹35.8/share, ROCE 19.4%, ROE 18.7%, no dividend (Screener, 28 Sep 2026). Listed July 2025 after a ₹440 cr IPO. **Transcript verdicts:** the stored Q1 FY27 transcript (call 4 Aug 2026) and Q4 FY26 transcript (call 11 May 2026) both **match official**. Both carry the company's BSE/NSE cover letters, and key figures (debt, guidance ranges, capex, PLI) were checked against the BSE copies.

## Business and segments
- Raipur (Chhattisgarh) maker of carbon-steel ERW black pipes and tubes, pre-galvanised (GP) coils and pipes, and narrow-width stainless-steel (SS) CR coils. It is backward-integrated from iron ore and coal: sponge iron 2.8 lakh t, MS slabs, narrow HR coil 3.7 lakh t, and SS slabs/HR 80,000 t each via an AOD route. Captive power is 25 MW (16 MW waste-heat, 9 MW AFBC). [IP Q1FY27 p.19–25]
- It calls itself the only Indian ERW maker at a single site that refines steel from iron ore, rather than buying coil. It uses a secondary route (DRI + induction furnace) but makes pipes to primary-grade IS standards. [AR FY26 MD&A SWOT; Q1 call]
- Finished capacity 0.68 MTPA: ERW pipes 3.5 lakh t, GP coils/pipes 1.16 lakh t (doubled in FY26), SS CR coil 1.16 lakh t (doubled from 58,000 t, consent to operate received in Q1 FY27). [IP Q1FY27 p.6, 8; Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 sales 1,07,771 t: structural pipes 56,617 t; GP 29,814 t; SS CR coil 14,760 t; intermediates 6,580 t. FY26 sales 3,96,731 t (+37%), with value-added products 3,56,000 t (+51%). [IP Q1FY27 p.7, 32; Q4 call]
- Utilisation (Q1 FY27): MS pipes above 65% (70% is the norm); GP above 90%; SS about 60%. [Q1 call]
- Realisations Q1 FY27: MS pipe about ₹60,000/t, GP ₹75,000/t, SS 200-series ₹1.40 lakh/t, SS 300-series ₹2.0–2.1 lakh/t. Q2 FY27 outlook: ₹58,000; ₹72,000–73,000; ₹1.30 lakh; ₹2.0 lakh. [Q1 call]
- About 90% of SS output is low-nickel 200-series. It sells coils only, not SS pipes. [Q1 call]
- Distribution: 47 distributors and 1,000+ dealers in 15 states and one UT. Pipes are strongest in the north; the far south is not targeted. SS coil is sold pan-India. [IP Q1FY27 p.28; Q1 call]
- Market share in ERW pipes is only about 3% (company's own SWOT). [AR FY26 MD&A]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (11 May 2026):** Q1 FY27 EBITDA/t ₹7,500–8,000. FY27 EBITDA/t ₹7,000–8,000; volume +10–15%; EBITDA margin 10–12% (10% floor, 12–12.5% upside); PAT margin 5% ±1%. Kesda Phase 1 (3.6 lakh t SS) commissioning in Q4 FY27, with first-year utilisation at most 40%. ₹930 cr project with ₹300 cr spent; ₹675 cr term loan tied up. New ₹200 cr capex: ₹50 cr DFT pipe mill (+1.5 lakh t, by Q4 FY27) and ₹150 cr 30 MW Sarora power plant; claimed operating benefit ₹100–120 cr a year. Peak long-term debt about ₹800 cr plus ₹200–300 cr working capital. Debt rules: long-term debt at most 1.5x net worth and at most 1.5x forward EBITDA. Working-capital cycle to rise from 17 days to 25–30 days once Kesda SS runs. 2030 target 2 MTPA finished capacity (10 lakh t MS pipes, 3 lakh t coated, 7 lakh t SS) and a 10–12% share in each. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (4 Aug 2026) — the current guidance:** Q1 delivered ₹10,000+/t (ex-sponge) against the ₹7,500–8,000 guided. Q2 EBITDA/t ₹7,500–8,500 and Q2 volume about equal to Q1. FY27: EBITDA/t ₹7,500–8,500 (**raised** from ₹7,000–8,000); revenue, volume and EBITDA each +10–15% over FY26 (base ₹270 cr operating EBITDA); EBITDA margin about 12% ±1–2 pts; **PAT margin 6% ±1% (raised from 5% ±1%)**. FY27 segment volumes: MS pipes 2.3–2.4 lakh t, GP about 90,000 t, SS about 60,000 t (about 4 lakh t of value-added products). FY28: EBITDA/t above ₹8,000 and revenue "around ₹4,500" cr. Kesda trial run and production "by Q4 '27"; average utilisation about 35% in FY28 and 60–65% in FY29, needing ₹200 cr and ₹300 cr of extra working capital. Peak debt ₹800–850 cr term plus ₹200–300 cr working capital by end-FY27, at 7.5–8%. SS margin ₹15,000–16,000/t now, ₹12,000–13,000/t once Kesda sells some HR; floor about ₹10,000/t. [Q1 call]
- **Internal inconsistency:** FY27 guidance of ₹7,500–8,500/t on about 4.4–4.6 lakh t (FY26's 3.97 lakh t +10–15%) implies roughly ₹330–390 cr of EBITDA, or +20–40%, not the stated +10–15%. Q1 alone made ₹95 cr, about 35% of FY26's full year. **Verdict: guidance raised on margin and per-tonne profit, deliberately low on growth; Q1 beat the Q4-call guide by about 25–30%.** [Q1 call; Q4 call; Screener]
- Price outlook (Q1 call): MS pipe prices to ease a further 3–5% this year. SS prices are "looking good" for now, but QCO relaxation means Chinese imports without BIS certification could soften SS margins once shipping normalises. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Kesda Phase 1 (greenfield SS, 3.6 lakh t):** ₹810 cr plant + ₹125 cr 25 MW power plant = about ₹935 cr, funded by a ₹675 cr term loan plus internal accruals. SS capacity would rise to 4.76 lakh t. [IP Q1FY27 p.8; CRISIL Aug-26; Q1 call]
- **Timing risk:** management says commissioning in Q4 FY27 is "on time in terms of our bar chart". **CRISIL (17 Aug 2026) says commencement is estimated from Q3 FY28, with about 30% completion to date.** The company's own schedule as of 26 Jul 2026 shows melt-shop civil work at about 30–40%, equipment erection not yet started, and hot-rolling-mill erection at about 20%, all against a Q4 FY27 testing target. [CRISIL Aug-26; IP Q1FY27 p.11–12; Q1 call]
- Brownfield: DFT pipe mill ₹50 cr (+1.5 lakh t ERW, to 5 lakh t; the Q4 call said Q4 FY27, the Q1 call "Q2 2028"); 30 MW Sarora power ₹150 cr (₹100 cr spent in FY27, ₹100 cr in FY28 for power + DFT); 8 MW rooftop solar ₹25 cr. Debt for the brownfield projects is not yet tied up. [Q1 call; Q4 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Kesda Phases 2–3 (to reach 1.2 MTPA at the site) are not defined yet and will be announced after Phase 1 starts. Management flagged ₹300–400 cr of further capex by FY28. A seamless-pipe project (approved under PLI 1.2) awaits approvals. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- PLI 1.2 for thin-gauge SS CR coil: incentive of 13–15% of eligible revenue until about 2030–31, capped at about ₹200 cr. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Power: 88–90 MW by end-2027 and 150 MW by 2030. Management claims ₹180–200 cr of annual savings once live (the Q4 call gave ₹60–70 cr for the Sarora plant alone). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Standalone capital commitments were ₹612 cr at March 2026. [AR FY26 note 40]
- 15% of Vajra Alloys (a planned super-alloy maker founded by ex-MIDHANI staff) was bought for ₹7.5 cr on 1 Apr 2026. [AR FY26 note 48; Q1 call]

- **Promoter warrants:** 86.95 lakh warrants at ₹115 (₹100 cr) to promoters and management, about 3% dilution. That price is about 28% below today's ₹159 (set on the 90-day/10-day VWAP of ₹112/₹114). On 17 Sep 2026 the company answered NSE observations: the notice's reference to ICDR Reg. 166A was not applicable, and it confirmed the VWAP rounding. Money goes to capex, working capital and a project in the subsidiary still to be announced. [BSE filing 17 Sep 2026; Q1 call]
- Rating: CRISIL A+/Stable assigned 17 Aug 2026 (₹162 cr cash credit). Gearing 0.4x and TOL/TNW 1x at March 2026; bank limits about 40% used; "strong" liquidity. [CRISIL Aug-26]

## Moat evidence
- Cost: iron ore (NMDC) and coal (SECL, long-term linkage) within about 250 km, captive power, and domestic coal (not imported coking coal). The switch from LPG to piped gas in January 2026 shielded it from war-related fuel shocks. [IP Q1FY27 p.26; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Pricing: black pipes sell at par with or 1–2% (about ₹1/kg) below the market leader. [Q1 call]
- SS niche: narrow-width (330 mm, 650 mm at Kesda), thin-gauge coil (0.1–0.4 mm planned), a mostly imported/unorganised segment of about 70,000–80,000 t a month. This is not head-on competition with Jindal Stainless's wide coils. [Q1 call]
- Co-branding: 28 SS pipe-maker partners (10 at March 2026), buying about 1,200 t/month; target 2,500 t/month by year-end. Approvals from Engineers India and Chennai Metro. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.6]
- Evidence against a moat: 3% ERW share; profits track HR coil prices (Q3 FY26 fell to ₹5,245/t). CRISIL lists end-market cyclicality as a key weakness. [AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Aug-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Project timing:** CRISIL's Q3 FY28 start vs management's Q4 FY27 is the biggest disagreement; a slip pushes the FY28 revenue target (about ₹4,500 cr) out. [CRISIL Aug-26; Q1 call]
- Leverage rises just as a new, larger product line ramps at 35% utilisation, and working capital needs ₹200–300 cr more. [Q1 call]
- SS import competition after QCO relaxation (non-BIS imports allowed until a cut-off date); MS pipe prices easing. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Governance: family business with no formal succession plan ("not yet there", MD). Promoter warrants are priced well below market. Part of the warrant money goes to a subsidiary project not yet disclosed. [Q1 call; BSE filing 17 Sep 2026]
- Related parties (FY26): sales to promoter-linked Sadhguru Pipes LLP ₹15.5 cr (₹7.5 cr in FY25); purchases from Anjaneya Minerals ₹4.5 cr; job work by Ganpati Sponge Iron ₹3.4 cr. Group-company corporate guarantees were released (Ganpati Sponge ₹879 cr, S. Pyarelal Ispat ₹198 cr), which is a clean-up. The subsidiary repaid ₹34 cr of loans. [AR FY26 note 38]
- Audit: clean opinion (S S Kothari Mehta & Co.); the only key audit matter is revenue recognition. The CARO report is clean: bank returns agree with books, no defaults, audit trail preserved. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Contingent liabilities are tiny: claims ₹0.19 cr and income tax ₹2.6 cr. [AR FY26 note 40]
- Shareholder base: FIIs fell from 3.61% to 1.53% and DIIs from 6.82% to 3.17% since June 2025; holder count fell from 1.31 lakh to 0.70 lakh. Promoters hold 56.1%. [Screener shareholding]

## What changed between the two calls
- Margin guidance went up (EBITDA/t floor ₹7,000 → ₹7,500; PAT margin 5% → 6%), but growth guidance stayed at +10–15%. The co-branding count rose from 10 to 28. New items: the ₹100 cr promoter warrant issue, the 8 MW solar plant, the Vajra stake, and explicit caution on SS prices from QCO relaxation. Peak term-debt guidance edged up from "₹800 cr at max" to ₹800–850 cr. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## My read
Execution so far has been good. FY26 volume rose 37%, per-tonne profit rose from about ₹5,300 to ₹7,000+, and Q1 FY27 beat guidance by a wide margin. The low-cost integrated model looks real. But the next two years depend on one project (Kesda SS) whose timing management and CRISIL now see differently by at least two quarters, while debt roughly trebles. At the current price it needs about 17% a year earnings growth. FY27 guidance on its face gives only 10–15%, although the per-tonne numbers imply 20%+, and FY28 needs Kesda to arrive on time. What would change the view: visible Kesda equipment erection and trial production by March 2027 (positive), or a slip into FY28 with SS prices softening from imports (negative).

## Stored-data errors
- DB Q1 FY27 "ebitda=100, mgn=13.7%" is total EBITDA including other income. Screener operating profit is ₹95 cr (13.0%).
- DB Q4 FY26 "pat=56" is the standalone figure (₹55.8 cr). Screener consolidated net profit is ₹53 cr.
- DB pe 28.2 vs Screener 28.0; DB roce 19.1% vs Screener 19.4% (minor).
- The stored profile presents Kesda Phase 1 as "firmly on schedule for Q4 FY27". CRISIL's August 2026 rationale puts commencement from Q3 FY28 at about 30% completion.
- The stored profile says Q1 margins were "partially aided by utilizing low-cost Q4 raw material inventory". Management gave that explanation for Q4 FY26 on the May call; on the Q1 call it credited market pricing and value-added mix.
- The stored analysis describes the ₹100 cr warrants as being "for subsidiary investments". The stated uses are capex, working capital, the subsidiary project and general purposes.
