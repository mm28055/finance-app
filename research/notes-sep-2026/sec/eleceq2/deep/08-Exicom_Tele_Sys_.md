# Exicom Tele-Systems (EXICOM) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Transcript check: **Q1 FY27 stored copy — materially wrong.** The official BSE transcript (10 Aug 2026) says "Q1 FY27", "fiscal '27", "calendar '27" and "October '26"; the stored copy rewrites these as "Q1 FY25", "FY25", "2025" and "October 2025" throughout. It also gives the TRI-FLEX prize as "$22–23 million" (official: "USD20 million to USD30 million contract for calendar '27"), names the hyperscaler product "Bit-Flex" (official and presentation: GRID-FLEX, ~USD 20m for CY27), and drops the CFO's "3x production capability" for Hyderabad. Core rupee figures (₹331 cr, ₹21.9 cr loss, ₹8.7 cr, ₹370 cr debt, ₹1,400 cr order book) match. **Q4 FY26 stored copy — tidied copy (figures match; gaps):** condensed Q&A; key figures (₹388 cr, 30 lakh EBITDA, ₹1,000 cr critical-power book, 3x Tritium, 25% loss cut, $30–35m GRID-FLEX, ₹850 cr FY28 potential, ₹50 cr BESS) agree with the official text.

## Business and segments
- Two businesses: **Critical Power** (DC power systems, Li-ion batteries, smart enclosures and hybrid solar power for telecom towers, BharatNet, small commercial BESS) and **EV chargers (EVSE)** — AC home "wallbox" chargers for car makers, DC fast chargers for charge-point operators, plus the US/Australian DC fast-charger maker **Tritium**, bought out of administration in FY25. [IP Q1FY27; CARE Feb-26]
- Q1 FY27 consolidated segment revenue: Critical Power ₹177.2 cr (+73% YoY, −11% QoQ), EVSE ₹153.9 cr (vs ₹102.8 cr). Mix 54% critical power / 46% EVSE. FY26 consolidated: Critical Power ₹641.8 cr, EVSE ₹509.9 cr. [IP Q1FY27 segment table]
- Standalone (India) Q1 FY27: Critical Power ₹176.0 cr (+80%), EV ₹60.8 cr (+15%); FY26 standalone EV ₹277.1 cr (~30% of standalone). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Only the EVSE segment and Tritium lose money: consolidated EV segment result was a loss in Q1 FY27 and FY26 (FY26 segment loss ₹235 cr before unallocated items per the presentation's table, which is poorly laid out; treat as indicative). [IP Q1FY27]
- Geography of EV business: India 50%, UK/Europe 22%, Australia/NZ 16%, US 10%. Critical-power exports ₹15 cr in Q1 (8% of the segment); Q4 FY26 exports ~₹30 cr (15%). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Capacity/utilisation (Q1 call): AC-charger lines ~100%, DC power systems 90–100%, PCBA ~100%, batteries ~100%, DC chargers ~65%. A new AC line was ordered; monthly AC output to rise ~50% in three months. Tritium's Tennessee plant can make roughly 2.5x current revenue (~$100m+ a year); it runs at about a quarter of that. [Q1 call]
- Wallbox share: CEO estimates "above 50%" of Indian EV home chargers (DRHP said 60%); "no research report" behind it. [Q1 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales: Jun-25 205, Sep-25 282, Dec-25 277, Mar-26 388, Jun-26 331. Operating profit: −39, −33, −32, 0, −22 (OPM −19%, −12%, −12%, 0.1%, −7%). Net profit: −83, −69, −68, −54, −74. [Screener]
- Annual: sales FY24 1,020 → FY25 868 → FY26 1,152 (+33%); operating profit 113 → −37 → −103; net profit 64 → −110 → −274. TTM sales ₹1,277 cr, TTM loss ₹265 cr. [Screener]
- Depreciation jumped from ₹19 cr (FY24) to ₹116 cr (FY26) and ₹39 cr in Q1 FY27 alone — much of it Tritium's capitalised R&D and leases; interest ₹56 cr in FY26. [Screener; Q1 call]
- The Indian business is profitable: standalone Q1 FY27 revenue ₹236.8 cr (+57%), EBITDA ₹20.9 cr (8.8% margin, +137%), PAT ₹4.9 cr; FY26 standalone revenue ₹895 cr, EBITDA ₹70 cr (7.8%). Standalone gross margin Q1 29.1% (+2 pts QoQ, −3.6 pts YoY on input costs). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Tritium: revenue ~$10.4m in Q1 FY27 (Q4 $10.3m; a year earlier ~$5m), bookings $20.8m (about 2x the prior quarter), backlog ~$23m. H1 FY26 Tritium revenue ₹81 cr with ₹91 cr EBITDA loss (CARE). FY25: ~₹100 cr revenue, ₹67 cr EBITDA loss. [IP Q1FY27; CARE Feb-26]
- Unit economics: the Tritium drag is roughly the whole group loss — standalone EBITDA +₹21 cr vs consolidated −₹22 cr in Q1 implies roughly ₹40 cr+ quarterly overseas EBITDA loss. Part of Q4's break-even came from selling cheap inventory bought with Tritium; margins "normalised" in Q1. [Q1 call]
- Return ratios: ROCE −14.7%, ROE −40.8% (FY26). [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (19 May 2026):** Tritium revenue to grow ~3x in FY27 and its EBITDA loss to shrink ~25%; Tritium EBITDA break-even in Q4 FY27; critical-power exports to 20% of segment in FY27; non-telecom BESS ₹40–50 cr in FY27; GRID-FLEX hyperscaler test could unlock $30–35m in FY28; three new products ~₹850 cr potential in FY28. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (10 Aug 2026):** consolidated EBITDA fell back to −₹21.9 cr. CEO: consolidated break-even "over the next 2 quarters… quarter 2 or 3"; Tritium break-even Q4 FY27 and 3x revenue "I'm sure". Export target restated as doubling 8% to ~15% of critical-power sales within FY27 (lower than Q4's 20%). GRID-FLEX prize restated as ~$20m for CY27 (was $30–35m for FY28); TRI-FLEX $20–30m for CY27. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Verdict: **Mixed** — Tritium bookings and India revenue beat; consolidated profit reversed; export and product-prize targets quietly trimmed. A new near-term goal (consolidated break-even by Q2/Q3 FY27) was added.
- Earlier record: CARE notes FY25 and H1 FY26 "deterioration" and slower-than-expected Tritium ramp versus what was envisaged. [CARE Feb-26]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹144.6 cr standalone, ₹239.3 cr consolidated (mostly the Hyderabad/Telangana plant, capitalised in FY26, plus Tritium development). [AR FY26 Board's report; note 73]
- Hyderabad plant: 18.4 acres; operational March 2026; CFO says it gives "3x production capability"; AC-charger capacity quoted in the annual report as rising from ~42,000 to 2,22,000 units a year (management could not reconcile this with "2x" on the call). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Running Gurgaon and Hyderabad together added ~₹8.7 cr to fixed costs in Q1; to fade "over the coming quarters". Depreciation up 67% standalone YoY. [Q1 call]
- Capex now mostly lines as needed; no large new plant announced. Payback depends on filling Hyderabad and Tritium's US plant (running at ~25%). [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Consolidated net debt ₹370.1 cr (30 Jun 2026). Screener borrowings ₹713 cr at Mar-26 (includes ~₹250 cr lease liabilities, mostly Tritium). [IP Q1FY27; Screener; CARE Feb-26]
- Cash flow from operations: FY25 −₹169 cr, FY26 −₹87 cr; free cash flow FY25 −₹773 cr (Tritium purchase), FY26 −₹405 cr. [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 130, inventory days 193, payable days 171 (FY26); inventory built at both plants "by design" and receivables up with sales. Trade receivables ₹414 cr at Mar-26 (auditor key audit matter). [Screener; AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Funding: rights issue ₹259.6 cr in July 2025 (net inflow ~₹155 cr after converting promoter loans); promoter unsecured loans ₹67 cr and ₹27 cr OCDs in 9M FY26, to convert to equity. Promoter Nextwave converted a ₹55 cr loan into rights shares. [CARE Feb-26; AR FY26 note 50]
- No dividend. Promoter holding fell from 69.57% to 65.20% (Jun-26); DII holding collapsed from 10.19% (Mar-24) to 0.31% (Jun-26). [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- ~50–60% share of Indian EV wallbox home chargers (management estimate); 100% share of business won with a leading international car brand in Q1. [Q1 call]
- Telecom power: 60% share of supplies in BharatNet via system integrators; ~₹700 cr open BharatNet orders plus ~₹800 cr 10-year service contract; a 140 cr+ DC power order from a leading telco in Q2 FY27. [IP Q1FY27]
- Consolidated order book ₹1,400 cr+ (1 Jul 2026); ~₹1,000 cr of that is critical power; EV charger book ~₹200 cr. CARE counted ₹1,461 cr at Dec-25, with US$46m Tritium orders to be executed by FY27. [IP Q1FY27; CARE Feb-26]
- 130+ R&D engineers, 200+ service staff in 25 states; Tritium adds liquid-cooled high-power DC technology and a Brisbane R&D centre. [Q4 call]
- Limits: fixed-price PSU contracts mean commodity spikes cannot be passed on ("limited project supply quantity and margins"); AC chargers are commoditising — the annual report cites "industry-wide pricing pressure in the domestic EV charging segment". [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 Board's report]

## Risks and red flags
- **Credit rating:** CARE BBB/Negative, reaffirmed Feb 2026 — "stretched liquidity", "weak coverage indicators", continued Tritium losses; interest cover −0.69x in FY25; ratings depend on "continued timely promoter support". [CARE Feb-26]
- **Promoter pledge:** 25,66,585 Exicom shares held by promoter Nextwave Communications are pledged to working-capital lenders (security for borrowings). [AR FY26 borrowings note]
- **Related parties (HFCL group):** Exicom belongs to the HFCL group (MD Anant Nahata is Mahendra Nahata's son; HFCL owns ~5.4%). FY26 sales to HFCL about ₹60 cr (vs ~₹3 cr in FY25) and advances received from HFCL about ₹46 cr, per the related-party table (the PDF layout is jumbled; figures should be re-checked). Material related-party approvals were sought by postal ballot in January 2026. [AR FY26 note 50; CARE Feb-26]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matters — revenue recognition, ₹95.9 cr GST input credit recoverable (continuous reconciliation, auditor "relied upon the management's assessment"), and receivables of ₹414 cr concentrated on government/large customers. All 8 overseas subsidiaries (incl. Tritium) were audited by other auditors. CARO notes "delays in few cases for PF, TDS and GST". [AR FY26 auditor's report & CARO]
- **Contingent liabilities (Mar-26):** bank guarantees ₹57.6 cr, letters of credit ₹11.6 cr, tax demands ~₹2.7 cr. After year-end, a customs order (31 Aug 2026) demanded ₹14.49 cr duty plus interest and penalties; the company will appeal. [AR FY26 note 48; BSE filing 31 Aug 2026 via Screener]
- Exceptional items FY26: VRS at the Solan plant ₹7.0 cr, labour-code gratuity ₹0.9 cr (standalone). [AR FY26 note 72]
- Screener flags "company might be capitalizing the interest cost". Heavy capitalised Tritium R&D means depreciation (₹130 cr TTM) now exceeds the Indian business's EBITDA. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Input-cost and supply risk: semiconductors, copper and plastics disruptions limited output in Q1; no formal forex hedging (CARE). [Q1 call; CARE Feb-26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone went from "inflection point" (Q4) to "transition quarter" (Q1). Consolidated EBITDA swung from +₹0.3 cr to −₹21.9 cr on seasonality, loss of cheap Tritium inventory and ₹8.7 cr dual-plant cost.
- Tritium bookings doubled to $20.8m; new near-term promise of consolidated break-even by Q2/Q3 FY27.
- Export target cut from 20% to ~15% of critical power; GRID-FLEX prize trimmed from $30–35m (FY28) to ~$20m (CY27).
- New: supply-chain constraints (chips, copper, plastics), an analyst query on unexplained depreciation, and inconsistent capacity numbers.

## My read
The Indian business is genuinely improving: standalone revenue +57% and EBITDA more than doubled, backed by a ₹1,400 cr order book and the new Hyderabad plant. But the group is still losing about ₹70 cr a quarter after interest and depreciation, is rated BBB with a negative outlook, and relies on promoter money and a pledged promoter stake. The price (₹2,239 cr market cap, ~1.75x TTM sales) is pricing in a Tritium turnaround that has been promised before. What would change my view: two consecutive quarters of consolidated EBITDA break-even (Q2/Q3 FY27), Tritium converting the GRID-FLEX/TRI-FLEX trials into signed contracts, and operating cash flow turning positive.
