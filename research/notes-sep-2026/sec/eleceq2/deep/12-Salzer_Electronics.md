## Business and segments
- Coimbatore-based maker of low-voltage industrial switchgear (cam-operated rotary switches — market leader with ~25% share — load-break switches, isolators, contactors, relays, toroidal/three-phase transformers, wire harnesses, cable ducts, terminal connectors), wires and cables (70% white-labelled B2B for large brands/OEMs on cost-plus), and building electrical products (B2C, south and now east India). Newer lines: smart meters, EV DC fast chargers (associate Ultra Fast Chargers), energy-management (Bengaluru BBMP street-light project via associate Effilume). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Q1 FY27 segment mix: industrial switchgear 54% (+10% YoY, EBITDA margin 8%), wires & cables 40% (+11%, 5%), building products 6% (+48%). FY26: switchgear 56% (+20%, 11% margin), W&C 39% (+30%, 5%), building 5% (+16%). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Exports 19% of Q1 FY27 revenue (21% in FY26, 28% in FY25); North/South America and Europe are about half of exports; US tariffs and West Asia cut exports. Exports partly routed through promoter-group firm Salzer Exports Ltd (CRISIL). [Q1 call; Q4 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Distribution: ~15% of revenue goes through Lauritz Knudsen (ex-L&T switchgear, now Schneider); Schneider is the largest single customer at ~12% of revenue. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Capacity: switchgear now 80–85% utilised; wires & cables ~65%; smart-meter plant 4 million meters a year (~₹70 cr cost, ₹400–450 cr revenue potential). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Subsidiary Kaycee Industries (71.7%): FY26 revenue ₹60 cr, PAT ₹5 cr; Q1 FY27 revenue +8%. [Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]

## Guidance history
- **Earlier (FY26 calls):** 9.5–10% EBITDA margin "definitely"; smart meters once expected to reach a few hundred crore of sales by FY24–FY25 (per analyst on Q1 call). Neither happened. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (26 May 2026):** FY27 EBITDA 9–9.5% for the whole year (after ~15% price rise in Feb and 7–10% in June); margins to "stabilize in Q2"; revenue ₹2,000–2,100 cr (~14–19% growth); switchgear margin 12–13% from Q2; EV charger revenue ~₹25 cr; debt to fall back to ≤25% of revenue; Saudi plant delayed by West Asia war. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (Aug 2026):** FY27 EBITDA cut to **8–8.5%**; Q2 still under pressure; Q3–Q4 at 9–9.5%; growth "close to 23% to 25%"; Saudi plant installation Sep–Oct 2026 (presentation still says "Jun-26"); Bengaluru energy-management annuity ~₹2 cr a month from mid-August (Salzer's share ~50%, booked through the associate); FY27 capex only ₹15–16 cr maintenance plus Hosur and Saudi. The Q1 presentation still showed a "~10% FY27" margin target — management said it would correct it. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Verdict: **Missed** — Q1 margin 6.3% vs the promised Q2 stabilisation; margin guidance cut twice (10% → 9–9.5% → 8–8.5%); Q1 revenue growth (12.9%) below the new 23–25% aim; smart-meter plan stalled.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹62 cr (new building at Coimbatore and maintenance). FY27: ₹20–25 cr for a new plant at Shoolagiri (Hosur) and a Saudi assembly unit, plus ~₹25 cr a year maintenance (CRISIL); management says ₹15–16 cr maintenance. Switchgear capacity expansion needed in FY28. [CRISIL Aug-26; Q1 call]
- Saudi Arabia (Salzer Electronics Arabia): starts with cable ducts and terminal connectors; FY28 incremental revenue ~₹25 cr. [Q1 call]
- Smart meters: ~₹70 cr plant (Annur) commissioned FY25; FY26 sales ₹52 cr (CRISIL; management said ~₹45 cr over the last year), Q1 FY27 ~₹3.5 cr; ₹22 cr of finished meters awaiting customer clearance; management "will take a call" in 2–3 quarters if business does not come. [CRISIL Aug-26; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Associates: Effilume (47%, ₹4.2 cr invested) for the ₹200 cr BBMP project; Salzer EV Infra (100%, ₹0.93 cr). [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹312 cr (FY24) → ₹461 cr (FY25) → ₹535 cr (FY26); CRISIL total debt ₹531 cr, gearing 0.9x, interest cover 3.1x, NCA/debt 0.20x; fund-based limits ₹480 cr used >90% on average. CRISIL expects debt to stay ₹500–550 cr. [Screener; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Operating cash flow FY25 −₹11 cr, FY26 +₹46 cr; free cash flow negative every year since FY21 (FY26 −₹17 cr). [Screener]
- Working capital: inventory days 137, debtor days 92, cash conversion cycle 177 days (FY26); gross current assets 206 days; ~₹40 cr smart-meter receivables stuck. [Screener; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Analysts on the Q4 call estimated ~₹250 cr of debt funds the 5%-margin wires business, leaving ~2% PAT; management agreed ("close to 2–2.5% PAT") and "took the suggestion" of a demerger. [Q4 call]
- Dividend payout ~9%; promoter holding only 37.47%; FIIs 4.5%. [Screener]
- Rating: CRISIL A/Stable / A1, reaffirmed 4 Aug 2026; downgrade trigger if operating margin falls below 8% — Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 were already below 8%. [CRISIL Aug-26]

## Moat evidence
- ~25% share and leadership in cam-operated rotary switches; bought nearest competitor Kaycee in FY20. [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Long OEM relationships and the Schneider/Lauritz Knudsen distribution tie-up (>350 dealers). [CRISIL Aug-26; IP Q1FY27]
- Pricing power is limited: needed three price increases (~24% cumulative in six months per CRISIL) and still lost 4–5 points of switchgear margin (12% → 7.5–8%) because of a 2–3 month pass-through lag. Wires & cables are cost-plus but earn only ~5%. [Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Gross margin on switchgear 35–40%. [Q4 call]
- CRISIL: industry has "low entry barriers" and faces cheap Chinese imports. [CRISIL Aug-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Credibility:** investor presentation showing a 10% FY27 margin while the call guided 8%; the company secretary argued with an investor group before management conceded (Q1 call). Saudi start date in the presentation (Jun-26) already missed. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Smart-meter drag:** ~₹70 cr plant, ₹22 cr inventory and ~₹40 cr receivables with little new business; Tamil Nadu tender cancelled. [Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Related parties:** sales of materials to promoter-group Salzer Exports Ltd ~₹39 cr a year; purchases, labour, rent and electricity from Salzer Spinners and SRVE Industries (a few crore each); services from Quebec Information Services (₹0.85 cr). Amounts are small relative to sales but show a web of promoter entities. [AR FY26 note 51; CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Contingent liabilities (Mar-26):** EPCG export obligations ₹42.9 cr, letters of credit ₹15.2 cr, bank guarantees ₹13.9 cr, CESTAT customs appeal ₹0.4 cr; disputed income-tax demands of ~₹0.9 cr and a ₹0.55 cr penalty under appeal. [AR FY26 note 45; CARO]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matter only revenue recognition; CARO clean (bank returns agree with books, no defaults). [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Leverage and rating headroom:** margin below CRISIL's 8% downgrade trigger for two quarters. [CRISIL Aug-26; Screener]
- Low promoter stake (37.5%) with limited institutional ownership. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Margin guidance cut from 9–9.5% to 8–8.5%; stabilisation pushed from Q2 to Q3.
- Revenue ambition phrased as 23–25% growth (vs ₹2,000–2,100 cr, i.e. 14–19%, in Q4) even though Q1 grew 12.9%.
- Switchgear utilisation up to 80–85%; FY28 capex signalled.
- Smart meters went from "very good news… by this year" (Q4) to "a drag on our balance sheet" (Q1).
- New: annuity from the BBMP energy project starting August; Effilume stake raised to 47%.

## My read
Demand is fine (revenue +24% in FY26, switchgear running at 80–85%), but profit has not followed: PAT grew 3% in FY26 and fell 52% in Q1 FY27 as copper, silver and plastic costs outran price increases. About ₹530 cr of debt funds a low-margin wires business and a stalled smart-meter bet, so interest takes a third of operating profit. At ~₹960 cr market cap (≈0.5x sales, ~21x trailing earnings) the stock is cheap only if margins really return to 9%+ in H2 FY27. I would change my view on evidence of Q3 FY27 margin above 9%, a decision on smart meters, and debt falling towards 25% of sales.
