# KP Green Engineering (BSE SME 544150) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Note on periods: KP Green is on the BSE SME platform and reports **half-yearly**. The two calls are the **H2 FY26 call (13 May 2026)** and the **H1 FY26 call (14 Nov 2025)** — the stored file labels them "Q4 FY26" and "Q2 FY26". There is no Q1 FY27 result or call; the next result (H1 FY27) is due around November 2026.

Transcript check: **both stored copies — match official.** They reproduce the BSE-filed transcripts (cover letters included) word for word.

## Business and segments
- Designs, fabricates and hot-dip galvanises heavy steel structures: transmission-line towers, solar module-mounting structures and tracker parts, substation structures, telecom towers and monopoles, high-mast and lighting poles, crash barriers, cable trays, pre-engineered buildings (PEB), railway/highway bridges, windmill tubular towers. ~23 products. Also services (optical-fibre fault rectification, galvanising job work, rooftop solar). [ICRA May-26; IP H2FY26]
- Part of Dr Faruk Patel's KP Group (KPI Green Energy, K.P. Energy). Reports one segment; 100% domestic. [AR FY26 note 45; ICRA May-26]
- Capacity ~4,00,500 MTPA (Matar, Bharuch 3,47,500 MTPA + Dabhasa, Vadodara 53,050 MTPA), including a 90,000 MTPA galvanising plant ("Asia's largest", 15-tonne single dip, LPG blended with 20–25% green hydrogen). FY26 output 1,24,500 MT — about 31% utilisation. [H2 call; ICRA May-26]
- Approvals: transmission towers approved in 16+ states (GETCO up to 400 kV, MSETCL 220 kV); Nextracker and GameChange Solar approvals; RDSO approval for railway work; NATRAX crash-barrier certification. [H2 call; ICRA May-26]
- Customer concentration: top five customers ~59–60% of FY26 sales; **KP Group companies ~47% of FY26 revenue** (ICRA). Related-party sales in FY26: K.P. Energy ₹370.0 cr, KPI Green Energy ₹175.5 cr, Sun Drops Energia ₹53.6 cr, KPIG Energia ₹15.4 cr — about ₹617 cr of ₹1,246 cr. [ICRA May-26; AR FY26 note 46]

- ROCE 36.7%, ROE 34.8% (FY26). [Screener]

GST) BSNL 4G-saturation order (telecom towers on infrastructure-as-a-service basis + 5–10 year O&M); ICRA: ~₹1,800 cr at May 2026, execution 1–1.5 years. Bidding pipeline "above ₹3,000 cr"; claimed 60–70% tender win rate. [H2 call; ICRA May-26]

## Guidance history
- **H1 FY26 call (Nov 2025):** FY26 growth "minimum 60% to 70%", possibly "up to" 100%; capacity 4,00,500 MTPA by end-FY26; galvanising plant to start by end-December 2025; order book ~₹1,100 cr, 50% internal/50% external; aim to move to 30% internal / 70% external. [H1 call]
- **Delivered:** FY26 revenue +79%, EBITDA +117%, PAT +85% — **beat** the 60–70% floor; capacity and galvanising plant commissioned. [H2 call; Screener]
- **H2 FY26 call (May 2026):** FY27 growth "40 to 50%" minimum ("maximum is no limit"); the CMD's vision is 40–50% a year to 2030; EBITDA margin "16 to 20%"; whole ₹1,831 cr order book to be executed in FY27; group share of order book 20–25%; new capex mainly backward integration (hot rolling mill), plus cables and conductors, containers, aluminium extrusion, tubular towers, fasteners. [H2 call]
- Verdict: **Delivering or beating** on growth; guidance kept at 40–50% for FY27. The H1 FY27 result (Nov 2026) will be the first test.

## Capex and capacity
- FY25–FY26 capex ~₹450 cr, funded mainly by the ₹189.5 cr IPO (March 2024) and internal accruals; fixed assets rose from ₹36 cr (FY24) to ₹543 cr (FY26). [ICRA May-26; Screener]
- Sale and leaseback: in FY26 the company sold "its major plant and machinery" and leased it back (auditor key audit matter); the cash-flow statement shows roughly ₹150 cr of sale proceeds (PDF columns jumbled — treat as approximate). This turns owned machines into lease liabilities — effectively a financing. [AR FY26 auditor's report; cash-flow statement]
- Related-party capital purchases FY26: ₹41.3 cr from KPGenix Sunray and ₹12.8 cr from KPI Green Hydrogen & Ammonia (group firms). [AR FY26 note 46]
- Next phase: rolling mill and other product lines "at a nascent stage"; timing and cost not given. With 31% utilisation there is room to grow without big new plants. [H2 call]
- Asset turn: FY26 sales ₹1,246 cr on ₹543 cr fixed assets ≈ 2.3x. [Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹100 cr (Mar-25) → ₹349 cr (Mar-26), mostly short-term working capital; long-term loans only ₹35 cr. CFO: average borrowing cost 8.5–9%. Gearing 0.76x; TOL/TNW 2.47x (vs 1.27x); interest cover 5.91x (vs 11.53x). [Screener; H2 call; ICRA May-26]
- Operating cash flow FY26 ₹155 cr (CFO/operating profit 75%) — good; but free cash flow −₹211 cr after capex. [Screener]
- Inventory days jumped 96 → 195 (stock-up of steel and zinc against West Asia disruption); debtor days improved 142 → 93; payable days 138; cash conversion cycle 150 days. "Other current liabilities" include TReDS bill-discounting by suppliers. [Screener; H2 call]
- Payable of ₹113 cr to KPI Green Energy and purchases of ₹215.8 cr from it in FY26 (new) — group company acting as a supplier/financier. [AR FY26 note 46]
- Dividend payout ~2%; promoter holding 66.61%; institutions <0.5%. [Screener]
- Rating: ICRA A- (Stable)/A2+, reaffirmed 21 May 2026; liquidity adequate; repayments only ~₹9 cr a year FY27–FY28. [ICRA May-26]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and one-stop range (23 products) plus approvals (16+ state utilities, Nextracker, GameChange, RDSO, NATRAX) — the approvals take years to win. [H2 call; ICRA May-26]
- EBITDA margin 20% in FY26 vs 16% before — above typical fabricators; management attributes it to customisation and product mix. [H2 call; Screener]
- Assured internal demand: group IPP/EPC capex (KPI Green, K.P. Energy) — infrastructure is 15–20% of a renewable project's cost. This is both an advantage and a dependence. [H1 call]
- Weakness: ICRA calls the industry "highly fragmented and competitive"; galvanising is "a part of the process", not a margin driver (CFO). [ICRA May-26; H1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party dependence and pricing:** ~47–50% of FY26 revenue from group companies, making margins sensitive to "the transfer pricing policy among the Group entities" (ICRA). Management's "20–25% group" figure refers to the order book, not revenue. [ICRA May-26; AR FY26 note 46; H2 call]
- **Royalty to promoter:** 2% of turnover paid to Dr Faruk Patel for the "KP" brand — ₹19.2 cr in FY26 (₹13.9 cr in FY25), ₹7.7 cr payable at year-end; management says brand costs (IPL, cricket sponsorship) are borne by him personally. [AR FY26 note 46; H2 call]
- **Money to the promoter family:** "Advance for property" to Faruk G. Patel ₹32.9 cr outstanding at Mar-26 (₹19.1 cr given in FY26); ₹42.1 cr deposit given to group firm Quyosh Energia; small stale receivables from Faaiz Money Changer (₹1.3 cr) and Renewable Minds LLP (₹1.4 cr). [AR FY26 note 46]
- **Auditor observations:** unmodified opinion; key audit matters on revenue recognition, PPE and the sale-and-leaseback gain. The auditor reported that the fixed-asset module of the new SAP S/4HANA system was not configured "till the date of signing this report", so it relied on management workings for the fixed-asset register. CARO: "slight delays" in depositing TDS, TCS, GST, PF, ESIC; some small income-tax demands unpaid >6 months. [AR FY26 auditor's report & CARO]
- **Contingencies:** company states no contingent liability; a ₹5.47 cr property advance (FY16–17) to Mukesh Engineering is in litigation at the Gujarat High Court; interest on delayed MSME payments not provided. [AR FY26 note 48]
- Working-capital debt quadrupled; inventory 195 days; fuel/gas costs for galvanising exposed to West Asia. [Screener; H2 call]
- SME listing (limited liquidity, half-yearly reporting); main-board migration pending eligibility. [H2 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- H1 FY26 → H2 FY26: growth delivered above guidance; galvanising plant and 4 lakh MTPA capacity commissioned; order book up from ~₹1,100 cr to ₹1,831 cr with the BSNL win.
- Group share of the order book fell from ~50% to 20–25% (ICRA: 48% → 23%).
- New concerns raised by analysts in H2: inventory build-up, 4x interest cost, cash reclassification, the promoter royalty.
- Guidance stepped down from "60–70% minimum" (FY26) to "40–50% minimum" (FY27).

## My read
The operating story is real: revenue nearly doubled for two years, margins rose to 20%, capacity is only ~31% used and the order book (₹1,831 cr) covers FY27. But roughly half of FY26 revenue came from promoter-group companies, the promoter takes 2% of sales as royalty and has ₹33 cr of company money as a property advance, and the balance sheet now carries ₹349 cr of debt plus a sale-and-leaseback. At ~11x trailing earnings the price already discounts governance risk; the business needs about 40% growth with rising external share to justify more. What would change my view: external revenue rising above 60% of sales in H1 FY27, the property advance being returned or explained, and inventory days falling back towards 100.
