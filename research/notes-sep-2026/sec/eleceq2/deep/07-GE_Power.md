## Business and segments
- **What it sells now:** services for coal-fired (steam) power plants — "core services" (repairs, spares, maintenance, generator and turbine repairs, control systems such as ALSPA and Mark VIe), "upgrades" (life extension and efficiency upgrades), and completion of legacy new-build boiler and flue-gas desulphurisation (FGD) projects that are in close-out [Q4 call; AR FY26].
- **Exited:** hydro and gas businesses (slump sale, FY25); construction scope of EPC; gas turbines/HRSG are "not in the objects for GEPIL" (they belong to GE Vernova) [Q4 call; ICRA Mar-26].
- **Durgapur factory (West Bengal, ~661 acres):** being demerged to JSW Energy — shareholders get 10 JSW Energy shares for every 139 GEPIL shares and keep their GEPIL shares; effective retrospectively from 1 Jul 2025 once the NCLT sanctions it. The plant lost ~₹27 cr a year on average in FY23–FY25. A five-year manufacturing services agreement with JSW Energy reserves capacity at pre-agreed prices [Investor meet Jul-26; Q4 call].
- **Customers:** NTPC, state utilities, BHEL, private IPPs; exports of boiler spares to Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Austria/Australia, UAE, Malaysia, Indonesia, Morocco; geographic mandate is India plus ~13 boiler markets [Q4 call; Investor meet Jul-26].
- **Market and share:** addressable Indian thermal services market ₹3,500–4,000 cr a year; GEPIL has "roughly 18%" of it [Q4 call]. Non-GE ("other OEM") fleet orders ~₹320 cr, doubled from ₹162 cr [Investor meet Jul-26].
- **Joint venture:** NTPC-GE Power Services (NGSL, 50%) — FY26 revenue ₹766 cr, PBT ₹43 cr, order book ₹2,183 cr, ICRA A+ [AR FY26 Directors' report].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (12 May 2026):** no revenue or margin guidance; the underlying 11% EBITDA is "the base... at par if not more" for coming years; Durgapur demerger to close "within this calendar year", at worst within 12 months of March 2026; alternative supply chain in ~18 months [Q4 call].
- **Investor meet (6 Jul 2026):** no new targets; restated FY26 core-order growth (+34%), EBITDA ₹277 cr, liquidity ₹880 cr, net worth ₹483 cr (standalone), and the demerger terms; ICRA BBB+ (Stable) [Investor meet Jul-26].
- **Delivery:** Q1 FY27 sales +8% and 14% OPM — consistent with the "11% base, not lower" message. Demerger still pending NCLT; ICRA expects approval in Q3 FY27 [ICRA Mar-26]. **Mixed:** turnaround delivered; growth modest.

## Capex and capacity
- Almost none: fixed assets ₹40 cr, CWIP ₹3 cr [Screener]. The strategy is asset-light — after the demerger GEPIL owns no major plant, buys fabrication from JSW for five years and builds an independent supply chain [Q4 call].
- FY26 investing outflow ₹436 cr is the ₹450 cr inter-corporate deposit into the group cash pool, not capex [Screener; AR FY26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Liquidity:** ~₹880 cr at March 2026, of which ₹450 cr is an inter-corporate deposit to **LM Wind Power Blades (India) Pvt Ltd**, the GE Vernova group's cash-pool entity, repayable on demand at 5.49–6.35% [AR FY26 note 16; Q4 call]. Borrowings only ₹18 cr [Screener]. Undrawn cash-pool borrowing line ₹286 cr [ICRA Mar-26].
- **Cash flow:** operating cash ₹469 cr in FY26 and ₹379 cr in FY25 [Screener]; FY26 includes ₹343 cr collected under the BHEL settlement [Q4 call].
- **Working capital:** debtor days 219 (597 in FY24), inventory days 107; bank-guarantee exposure cut by ₹1,364 cr over two years [Screener; Investor meet Jul-26].
- **Dividend:** ₹7 per share for FY26 (70% of face value) — first in four years; payout 19% [Q4 call; Screener].
- **Rating:** ICRA BBB+/Stable/A2, upgraded from BBB/A3+ on 3 Mar 2026 [ICRA Mar-26].
- **Other assets:** FY25 hydro and gas slump sale added ~₹295 cr to EBITDA; the BHEL settlement brought ~₹116 cr to P&L, ₹343 cr of cash and released ₹423 cr of bank guarantees; a contract-manufacturing and leave-and-licence arrangement at Vadodara is also in place [IP Jul-26].

## Moat evidence
- OEM know-how and IP on the GE/Alstom installed base; "the only company" in GE Vernova working on both GE and non-GE fleets [Q4 call].
- 18 first-of-its-kind products won in FY26; non-GE orders doubled [Q4 call; Investor meet Jul-26].
- Services orders are short-cycle (under 12 months) and plant operators rarely switch servicers on critical equipment [Q4 call].
- **Against it:** ~18% share means strong incumbents (BHEL and others); addressable market capped at ₹3,500–4,000 cr; coal's long-term decline [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party cash:** ₹450 cr (roughly half of liquidity) lent on demand to a GE Vernova group entity; analysts pressed on this on the Q4 call; management says it is board-approved and benchmarked to HSBC rates [Q4 call; AR FY26].
- **Contingent liabilities are large relative to equity:** sales tax ₹203.7 cr, GST ₹13.3 cr, income tax ₹110.6 cr, other claims not acknowledged ₹130.1 cr — about ₹458 cr in total vs net worth of ~₹580 cr [AR FY26 note 39; Screener].
- **Earnings quality:** FY26 PBT of ₹340 cr included ~₹156 cr of one-offs; FY25 profit came from other income. Q1 FY27 PAT of ₹54 cr is the cleaner run-rate [Q4 call; Screener].
- **Policy:** the July 2025 FGD notification exempted Category C plants (>50% of the fleet, >200 GW) and made Category B optional; only ~8 GW (Category A) must fit FGD [Q4 call].
- **Demerger:** needs shareholder, creditor and NCLT approval; any delay prolongs the Durgapur drag [Q4 call; ICRA Mar-26].
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matter is revenue recognition on long-term fixed-price contracts (estimates of cost to complete) [AR FY26 auditor's report]. No emphasis of matter found.
- **Terminal risk:** a coal-services business with no gas or nuclear exposure [Q4 call].

## My read
The turnaround is real — operating losses to ~11–14% underlying margins, ₹880 cr of liquidity, a dividend and a rating upgrade — but reported FY26 profits flatter the business because of settlement one-offs. What remains is a small, cash-rich thermal-services company growing core orders ~30% but with total revenue likely to grow only modestly as legacy projects close. At ~12 times trailing (flattered) earnings the valuation depends on the clean run-rate (Q1 FY27 PAT ₹54 cr) holding and the JSW share entitlement. Watch NCLT sanction of the demerger, whether the ₹450 cr group loan is recalled or grows, and core-services order intake in FY27.
