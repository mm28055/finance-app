# Inox Wind (INOXWIND) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Figures from Screener's consolidated page unless tagged otherwise.
- Price ₹74.8; market cap ₹12,919 cr; 52-week range ₹68–159 (53% below the high); trailing P/E 37.5; ROCE 10.6%; ROE 7.5% [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].
- Promoters (INOXGFL group) 44.18%, down from 48.27% in Mar 2025; no dividend [Screener].

## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** wind turbines (2 MW and 3.3 MW platforms; a 4.4 MW "4X" prototype due for installation in August 2026, commercial launch "within CY26"), turnkey wind-farm EPC, and through subsidiaries: Inox Green (listed O&M company, ~13.3 GWp portfolio including ~6.5 GW being acquired) and Inox Renewable Solutions/IRSL (EPC, evacuation infrastructure, cranes, transformers) [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Jun-26].
- **Plants:** Una (Himachal), Rohika (Gujarat), Barwani (Madhya Pradesh), a new nacelle unit at Bhuj; claimed capacity >2.5 GW across four facilities; technology tie-up with AMSC Windtech [CRISIL Jun-26; IP Q1FY27].
- **Customers:** NTPC, CESC, NLC India, Aditya Birla, Amplus/Gentari, Hero Future Energies, Jakson, and the group's own IPP, **Inox Clean Energy** [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]. India only.
- **Mix:** O&M ~10% of consolidated revenue, expected to rise to 18–20% after the Inox Green acquisitions consolidate [Q4 call]. Management says the order book moved from 100% turnkey two years ago to 75–80% equipment supply [Q4 call]; third-party book at July 2026 was 59% equipment supply / 41% turnkey [Q1 call].
- **Segment disclosure:** product vs EPC revenue split is not disclosed; megawatts executed stopped being disclosed from FY26 [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM / PAT (₹ cr) [Screener]:**
  - Q1 FY26: 826 / 184 / 22% / 97
  - Q2 FY26: 1,119 / 228 / 20% / 121
  - Q3 FY26: 1,207 / 282 / 23% / 127
  - Q4 FY26: 1,244 / 200 / 16% / 106
  - **Q1 FY27: 814 / 152 / 19% / 64** (sales −1%, PAT −34% year on year)
- **Company's "EBITDA"** includes other income and excludes expected-credit-loss (ECL) provisions and one-offs: ₹237 cr, 27.2% of ₹872 cr total income in Q1 FY27 [IP Q1FY27]. The gap to Screener's ₹152 cr is large and should not be ignored.
- **Annual [Screener]:** FY24 sales ₹1,746 cr, OPM 15%, loss ₹48 cr; FY25 ₹3,557 cr, 21%, PAT ₹438 cr; FY26 ₹4,397 cr (+24%), 20%, PAT ₹449 cr (+3%). TTM PAT ₹417 cr. EPS fell from ₹3.23 to ₹2.34 due to share issuance.
- **FY26 vs promise:** guided ₹5,000 cr revenue, achieved ~₹4,600 cr (total income) — a ~₹400 cr shortfall blamed on a delayed imported component, commodity costs and a PSU contract [Q4 call].
- **Order book:** ~3.1 GW at March 2026 → ~4.4 GW at July 2026, including a 1.5 GW MoU with group company Inox Clean (500 MW firm, "up to Rs. 3,500 crs" — about ₹7 cr/MW) and a 200 MW NLC India turnkey award [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]. Acuité puts the book at over ₹25,000 cr (4.2 GW of turbines plus ₹7,000 cr of EPC) [Acuité Aug-26].
- **Industry:** India added a record ~6 GW of wind in FY26 and 1.4 GW in Q1 FY27; installed base 57.4 GW; 2.35 GW of pure wind awarded in Q1 FY27 tenders [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q3 FY26 (as recalled on Q4 call):** FY26 revenue ₹5,000 cr; net working-capital days ~200 [Q4 call]. **Missed** both (revenue ~₹4,600 cr; debtor days 353).
- **Q4 call (29 May 2026):** FY27 consolidated revenue +~75% (to ~₹7,500 cr+); EBITDA margin per the company's slide 20–22% (the official transcript reads "20% to 20%"); Inox Green EBITDA "upwards of 600 crores" on consolidation; FY26 shortfall of ₹400 cr to be recovered in Q1–Q2; receivables to "fall off sharply" [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Q1 call (7 Aug 2026):** "we maintain that revenue guidance of 75% growth... and an EBITDA margin of 20% to 22%"; 70–75% of the year falls in H2; the equipment-supply pivot to show "from Q3 onwards"; Inox Green ₹600 cr EBITDA is an annualised run-rate from H2 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Delivery:** Q1 total income ₹872 cr (+1%) — the remaining three quarters need roughly ₹6,500–7,100 cr, about double last year's run-rate. The ₹400 cr catch-up was only "partly recognized". **Missed so far; guidance kept.**
- **Track record:** analysts on both calls said guidance has been missed "in every conference call" for three quarters; management blamed force majeure [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex about ₹648 cr (operating cash −₹598 cr minus free cash −₹1,246 cr); investing outflow ₹850 cr including Inox Green's O&M acquisitions [Screener].
- **Under way:** blade capacity for the 4X turbine; Jaipur transformer plant moving to 4.9 MVA, then 8–20 MVA and 100 MVA+ units; in-house cranes (4 operating, more this year); power electronics (inverters, unit substations) in IRSL [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]. No rupee figure or payback given; management says "relatively short payback periods" [Q1 call].
- **Funding:** ~₹1,600 cr equity raised in FY26 [CRISIL Jun-26]; new enabling resolutions for a further raise, not discussed ("silent period") [Q1 call]. The group says it infused over ₹2,000 cr in three years [Acuité Aug-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Receivables are the core problem:** ₹4,249 cr at March 2026 (353 days) vs ₹2,687 cr (280 days) a year earlier; inventory 186 days [CRISIL Jun-26]. Screener cash conversion cycle 443 days [Screener]. Acuité calls the gross current-asset cycle 592 days [Acuité Aug-26].
- **Cash flow:** operating cash −₹598 cr in FY26, −₹366 cr in FY24, +₹138 cr in FY25; free cash negative every year for five years (FY26 −₹1,246 cr) [Screener].
- **Debt:** borrowings ₹1,587 cr at March 2026; cash ₹612 cr [Screener; CRISIL Jun-26]. Net debt/EBITDA 0.43x (CRISIL). The company's slide calls it "net cash" — not consistent with these figures.
- **Management on receivables:** not disclosed quarterly; "would have shown a downward trajectory"; improvement promised from Q2–Q3 [Q1 call].
- **Ratings:** CRISIL put its A rating on Watch Developing and **withdrew it at the company's request** on 16 Jun 2026, citing the group's heavy acquisition spending at Inox Clean [CRISIL Jun-26]. Acuité reaffirmed AA−/Stable on 20 Aug 2026 [Acuité Aug-26].

## Moat evidence
- **Plug-and-play common infrastructure** (land, evacuation, substations) at wind sites, which IRSL owns — management calls it a "critical moat" [IP Q1FY27].
- **Localisation:** 80–90% of turbine components already made in India, aiming for ~100% by year-end; new ALMM rules for wind should favour localised makers for "at least 3 years" [Q1 call].
- **Captive demand:** Inox Clean targets 14 GW of renewable capacity by FY29, 20–30% wind — "almost 1/3 of our annual execution targets" [Q4 call].
- **O&M scale:** Inox Green's ~13.3 GWp portfolio after buying Wind World India's 4.5 GW O&M book (FY26 revenue ₹580 cr) [IP Q1FY27].
- **Against it:** competitors are moving to 5 MW+ turbines while Inox's 4.4 MW is still at prototype stage [Q1 call]; the wind OEM market is competitive with global players [CRISIL Jun-26]; group-company orders are not a sign of market preference.

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor emphasis of matter:** (1) funds invested in six SPVs set up by Inox Green for SECI wind projects — completion dates expired, extensions rejected, bank guarantees of ₹55.8 cr invoked, CERC appeal dismissed on 17 Nov 2025, APTEL appeal pending; (2) ₹114.8 cr of O&M services rendered but not yet billed. Key audit matters include revenue recognition, receivables impairment (receivables mainly from state-owned enterprises) and litigation [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 54/55].
- **Contingent liabilities:** contractor claims ₹95.9 cr and customer claims ₹121.5 cr (the latter limited to outstanding balances) [AR FY26 note 40].
- **Related parties:** corporate guarantee of ₹180.5 cr for a bank loan of Inox Clean Energy; group company GFL guarantees ₹498 cr of Inox Wind borrowings; ₹13.5 cr expensed for doubtful inter-corporate deposits owed by related parties [AR FY26 RPT note]. A growing share of the book (1.5 GW MoU) is from the promoter's own IPP; management says it is at arm's length [Q1 call].
- **Group capital allocation:** CRISIL flagged Inox Clean's acquisitions (Vena Energy ~6 GW, Vibrant Energy, SkyPower, Boviet Solar) as critical to group credit [CRISIL Jun-26].
- **Disclosure:** no MW execution, no receivable days, no EPC vs supply revenue split; "adjusted EBITDA" excludes ECL provisions.
- **Dilution:** repeated equity raises (equity capital ₹391 cr in FY24 → ₹1,728 cr in FY26) [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- **Tone:** Q4 blamed war-hit logistics and a PSU contract but promised a clean FY27; Q1 was defensive, with management complaining of no "word of appreciation" for margins.
- **Numbers:** order book 3.1 GW → 4.4 GW (mostly the Inox Clean MoU); Q1 total income flat; company EBITDA 27.2%.
- **New issues:** fund-raise enabling resolutions; IRSL demerger completed 1 Aug 2026; NCLT approval for Wind World India.

## Best management quotes
- "We maintain that revenue guidance of 75% growth over the previous year and an EBITDA margin of 20% to 22% on a consol basis." [Q1 call]
- "As against the guideline of INR5,000 crores... we have achieved INR4,600 crores." [Q4 call]
- "24 months ago, 100% of our order book was turnkey... now 75% to 80% being equipment supply." [Q4 call]

## My read
The order book and the sector backdrop are real, but Inox Wind converts orders into cash poorly: 353 receivable days, negative free cash every year, repeated equity raises and three quarters of missed guidance. FY27 needs revenue to roughly double in nine months, and a rising share of orders comes from the promoter's own IPP. The auditor's emphasis on the SECI SPVs and the CRISIL withdrawal add to the governance discount. At ~37 times trailing earnings the price needs the 75% growth to arrive with cash; watch Q2 revenue (needs a sharp jump), any disclosure of receivable days, the 4X commercial launch, and whether the fund-raise dilutes again.
