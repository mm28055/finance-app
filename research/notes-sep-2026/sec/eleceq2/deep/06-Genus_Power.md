# Genus Power Infrastructures (GENUSPOWER) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Figures from Screener's consolidated page unless tagged otherwise. The stored calls are Q4 FY26 (19 May 2026) and Q3 FY26 (11 Feb 2026); no Q1 FY27 call transcript is on file with the exchange, so Q1 FY27 comes from the August 2026 investor presentation.
- Price ₹306; market cap ₹9,301 cr; 52-week range ₹207–359; trailing P/E 14.3; ROCE 25.3%; ROE 30.9% [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].
- Promoters (Kailash group) 39.34%; GIC's affiliate Chiswick holds ~15% [Screener; CRISIL Nov-25].

## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** smart electricity meters (single/three-phase, prepayment, DT, ABT), plus its own head-end system (HES), meter-data management (MDMS) and RF-mesh communications. Mainly acts as an **AMISP** — installing and running smart meters for state power distributors (discoms) under the government's RDSS scheme on a design-build-finance-own-operate-transfer basis, with 8–9-year concessions [Q4 call; IP Aug-26].
- **Structure:** projects sit in SPVs under the **Gemstar platform** — GIC 74%, Genus 26% — with Genus as exclusive meter supplier, EPC contractor and O&M provider [CRISIL Nov-25].
- **Scale:** 18+ million meters a year of capacity (Jaipur, Haridwar, Guwahati); 87 lakh smart meters installed in FY26 (30 lakh in Q4); crossed 1 crore cumulative RDSS installations; all 24 AMISP projects (3.61 crore awarded meters) have reached operational go-live [Q4 call; IP Aug-26].
- **Share:** ~22–23% of AMISP awards; over 30% of meter manufacturing [Q4 call]. Genus says it has supplied 2.5 crore of India's ~5.6 crore installed smart meters (as of Feb 2026) [Q3 call].
- **Order book:** ₹24,020 cr at 30 Jun 2026, down from ₹25,173 cr at March and ₹27,000 cr at December (no large new wins) [IP Aug-26; Q4 call; Q3 call]. Of ~₹23,000 cr from the platform, ~₹16,000 cr is capex (installation) and the rest O&M; ~₹2,000 cr is other meters, exports, gas and water [Q4 call].
- **Other lines:** gas meters (India opportunity ₹35,000–36,000 cr over 3–4 years), water meters, exports (target ~₹500 cr in 2–3 years) — all small today [Q4 call; IP Aug-26].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM / PAT (₹ cr) [Screener]:**
  - Q1 FY26: 942 / 199 / 21% / 137
  - Q2 FY26: 1,149 / 234 / 20% / 143
  - Q3 FY26: 1,122 / 212 / 19% / 140
  - Q4 FY26: 1,537 / 267 / 17% / 172
  - **Q1 FY27: 1,365 / 260 / 19% / 197** (sales +45%)
- **Q1 FY27 standalone:** revenue ₹1,365 cr, EBITDA ₹260 cr (19.1% vs 21.2%), PAT ₹163 cr; gross margin 37.0% vs 39.6% a year earlier because of raw-material costs and West Asia supply disruption [IP Aug-26]. Consolidated PAT is higher (₹197 cr) because of mark-to-market gains on shares of Genus Paper & Boards — notional, not operating [IP Aug-26].
- **Annual [Screener]:** FY24 sales ₹1,201 cr, OPM 11%, PAT ₹87 cr; FY25 ₹2,442 cr, 19%, ₹311 cr; FY26 ₹4,751 cr (+95%), 20%, ₹592 cr (+90%). TTM PAT ₹652 cr.
- **Unit economics:** realisation per meter is not disclosed [Q4 call]. As a rough cross-check (my estimate), FY26 revenue of ~₹4,750 cr on 87 lakh installs is about ₹5,500 per installed meter, but this mixes supply to other AMISPs and utilities, so it is only indicative.
- **O&M annuity:** ~₹150 cr in FY26; guided to ₹800–900 cr a year within 2–3 years [Q4 call], later restated as ₹600–700 cr from FY28 rising to ₹1,100–1,200 cr [IP Aug-26].

## Guidance history
- **Q3 call (Feb 2026):** FY26 installs 80–90 lakh; FY27 revenue ₹6,000 cr at ~20% EBITDA; positive operating cash flow "by the end of 2027"; peak gross debt ₹2,100–2,200 cr; platform investment capped at ₹1,100 cr to FY28 [Q3 call].
- **Q4 call (May 2026):** FY26 delivered — 87 lakh installs, revenue ₹4,738 cr standalone (+94%), EBITDA 20.3%. FY27 revenue **raised** to ₹6,000–6,500 cr; EBITDA margin **cut** to 18% (from 20–21%) because fixed-price contracts have no pass-through for chips, components and the rupee; ≥1 crore installs; peak net debt ~₹1,900–2,000 cr; cash flow "at par or maybe a little negative" for FY27, positive only from Q1–Q2 FY28 — **pushed back**; working-capital days to fall another 50–75 days [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 (Aug 2026 presentation):** FY27 revenue guide of ₹6,000–6,500 cr reaffirmed; H2-weighted; Q1 EBITDA 19.1% was above the 18% guide [IP Aug-26].
- **Track record:** installation and revenue promises met; cash-flow promise slipped a year; margin guide lowered.

## Capex and capacity
- Manufacturing capex is small — ₹10–20 cr a year for dies and moulds; capacity of 18 million meters is ample (~80% use expected in FY27) [Q4 call; Q3 call].
- The real capital need is equity/loans into the Gemstar platform: ~₹487 cr invested by March 2026, another ₹600–700 cr over FY27–Q1 FY29 [Q4 call]. FY26 investing outflow ₹459 cr [Screener].
- Funding: bank debt, a $49.5 million (₹413 cr) ECB from the US International Development Finance Corporation, and internal cash [IP Aug-26; CRISIL Apr-26 bulletin].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Debt:** borrowings ₹2,302 cr at March 2026 (₹1,366 cr a year earlier); net debt ₹1,573 cr, cash ₹719 cr [Screener; Q4 call].
- **Cash flow:** operating cash negative three years running — −₹152 cr (FY24), −₹443 cr (FY25), −₹219 cr (FY26); free cash −₹514 cr in FY26 [Screener]. Contract assets absorb the cash until meters are billed [Q4 call].
- **Working capital:** company debtor days fell from 187 to 89; total working-capital days including unbilled from 343 to 275 [Q4 call]. Screener: debtor days 115, inventory days 170, cash conversion cycle 188 days [Screener]. Working capital used per rupee of revenue growth fell from ~80% (FY25) to ~40% (FY26), guided to ~20% [Q4 call].
- **Related-party lending:** loans and advances rose from ~₹75 cr to ~₹528 cr in FY26, mostly to the Gemstar platform [Q4 call]; CRISIL flagged the "increase in related party exposure" and made further increases a rating sensitivity [CRISIL Nov-25].
- **Rating:** CRISIL AA−/Stable/A1+ (reaffirmed Nov 2025; bank limits updated Apr 2026); working-capital limits ~93% utilised [CRISIL Nov-25].
- **Dividend:** payout 2% in FY26 [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Integrated stack (meter + communications + HES/MDMS software + field O&M) and ~550 R&D staff; RF mesh is its own [Q4 call; IP Aug-26].
- Scale: largest meter maker in India; 30%+ manufacturing share; neck-and-neck with Adani in installs [Q3 call; Q4 call].
- Financing edge via GIC — lets Genus bid for capital-heavy AMISP projects [CRISIL Nov-25].
- **Against it:** a tender business — "it is difficult to comment" on future margins as new entrants arrive; fixed-price contracts with no escalation [Q3 call; Q4 call]. Order book shrinking while tenders slip.

## Risks and red flags
- **ED/PMLA case (material and escalating):** the Enforcement Directorate searched the corporate office and the chairman's residence on 3 Dec 2024. In February 2026 the company received a notice from the PMLA Court, Patna, to appear in a pre-cognizance hearing on the ED's complaint against it. The auditor carries this as an **emphasis of matter** in both standalone and consolidated reports [AR FY26 note 38; auditor's report]. On the 11 Feb 2026 call management said there had been "no further development" [Q3 call].
- **Related-party exposure** to the GIC platform (loans, 26% equity, exclusive supply) [CRISIL Nov-25; Q4 call].
- **Cash burn and debt** until FY28; net debt to peak ~₹2,000 cr [Q4 call].
- **Input costs:** 40–45% of meter components imported; fixed-price contracts [CRISIL Nov-25; Q4 call].
- **Accounts:** key audit matter is revenue, receivables and contract assets on AMISP contracts (allocation across supply, installation and O&M is judgmental) [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Consolidated profit includes notional MTM swings on Genus Paper & Boards shares [IP Aug-26]. Contingent liabilities: bank guarantees ₹119.9 cr; tax disputes ~₹19.5 cr [AR FY26 note 35].
- **Discom counterparty risk** and RDSS deadline (programme extended to 2028) [Q4 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- **Tone:** Q3 was about go-live milestones and confidence on cash flow; Q4 turned to input-cost pressure and pushed cash-flow timing out.
- **Numbers:** FY27 revenue ₹6,000 cr → ₹6,000–6,500 cr; EBITDA ~20% → 18%; positive cash flow end-FY27 → Q1–Q2 FY28; peak debt now framed as net ₹1,900–2,000 cr.
- **New issues:** all 24 projects at go-live; order book drifting down; after the calls, the PMLA court notice surfaced in the annual report.

## Best management quotes
- "There is no pass-through in our contracts... These are all fixed-price contracts." [Q4 call]
- "We will be almost either at par at cash flow or maybe a little negative, not surely not positive." [Q4 call]
- "For Genus, it is actually good if the tendering happens slowly." [Q3 call]

## My read
Genus has executed superbly — revenue up ~4x in two years, 87 lakh installs, all projects live — and at ~14 times trailing earnings the market is not paying for growth. The discount reflects real issues: negative cash flow until FY28, rising debt and related-party lending to the GIC platform, a shrinking order book, lower FY27 margins, and an ED/PMLA case that has moved to a court hearing. If cash flow turns positive in FY28 and O&M annuity income builds as guided, the stock is cheap; an adverse court step or a new margin cut would change that. Watch Q2 installs and margin, new AMISP wins from the 8–9 crore meters due for tender, and any development in the Patna PMLA case.
