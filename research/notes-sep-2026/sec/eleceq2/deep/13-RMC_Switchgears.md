# RMC Switchgears (RMC) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Note on calls: RMC does not hold a quarterly call. The two stored transcripts are the **FY26 Analyst Day (11 Jun 2026)** — labelled "Q4 FY26" — and the **H1 FY26 Investor Meet (21 Nov 2025)** — labelled "General 21 Nov 2025". Q1 FY27 results (Aug 2026) came with a presentation but no call.

Transcript check: **both stored copies — match official.** They reproduce the transcripts RMC filed with the exchanges (15 Jun 2026 and 24 Nov 2025 cover letters), including ₹401.59 cr revenue, the ₹7.07 cr Q3 loss / ₹9.3 cr Q4 profit, ₹850 cr+ order book, ₹1,500 cr pipeline, "3% to 5%" of receivables over six months, and the ₹5,000 cr 2030 aspiration.

## Business and segments
- Jaipur-based. Three segments (company presentation; reported as one segment in the accounts): Electrical Products (distribution boxes, smart-meter enclosures, FRP, panels) ₹96.5 cr in FY26; Electrical EPC (utility distribution/transmission works, automation) ₹96.1 cr; Solar EPC (rooftop and ground solar, PM-KUSUM type) ₹209.0 cr. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 32]
- Customers are overwhelmingly government: all EPC revenue is B2G and ~30% of products; overall only ~5–10% B2B. Top five customers ~83% of sales; Maharashtra's MSEDCL alone ~55% of FY25 revenue. [Analyst Day; Infomerics Feb-26]
- New product: PulseBox — an IoT monitor for distribution transformers (theft, overload, safety). Proof of concept done, pilots on 10 transformers in 5+ locations; one utility is drafting tender specifications. Management's addressable-market claim: ~75 lakh transformers, "Rs. 50,000 crore plus" — "This is not guidance." No order yet. [Analyst Day; IP Q1FY27]
- Smart-meter enclosure capacity raised to 3,00,000 units. [IP Q1FY27]
- Subsidiaries: RMC Green Energy (planned 1 GW solar-module plant, ₹80.2 cr, **deferred** Nov 2025), RMC Solar Park, RMC Solar One/Two/Three/Five (project SPVs). Intelligent Hydel Solutions (51%) was deconsolidated on 31 Mar 2026 (see risks); water-management venture dropped. [Infomerics Feb-26; AR FY26 note 5; Analyst Day]

Net profit Q1 4.1, Q3 −7.1, Q4 9.3. H1 FY26 (company): revenue ₹221.6 cr (+111%), EBITDA ₹34 cr (15.3%), PAT ₹20 cr. [Screener; H1 Investor Meet]

- Annual: sales FY24 172 → FY25 318 → FY26 402 (+26%); operating profit 34 → 52 → 47 (OPM 20% → 16% → 12%); net profit 15 → 31 → 22 (−27%). FY22→FY26 revenue ₹41.6 cr → ₹401.6 cr. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]

Infomerics' Dec-25 split: solar EPC ₹270.9 cr, electrical EPC ₹415.3 cr, products ₹85.5 cr. Tender pipeline ₹1,500 cr+. [IP Q1FY27; Analyst Day; Infomerics Feb-26]

## Guidance history
- **H1 FY26 meet (Nov 2025):** no definitive H2 FY26/FY27 guidance; "Vision 2030" aspiration ₹5,000 cr sales; order pipeline ~₹825 cr "to be delivered over the next 12 months"; 1 GW module plant deferred, equity raise dropped as "value destructive"; historic ~10% net margin referenced by investors. [H1 Investor Meet]
- **Delivered:** H2 FY26 revenue ~₹180 cr and PAT only ~₹2 cr (Q3 loss); FY26 PAT −27%; operating cash flow negative. The ₹825 cr book was not delivered within 12 months. [Screener; derived]
- **Analyst Day (Jun 2026):** "not supposed to give guidance"; FY27 "better than the last year, that is for sure"; will add execution buffers; positive operating cash flow expected in FY27, retention money to be collected in H1 FY27; ₹5,000 cr by FY30 "remains intact"; promoter buying "you might see in coming months". [Analyst Day]
- **Q1 FY27 (Aug 2026):** revenue −56% YoY; order book up to ₹1,188 cr. No promoter purchase visible (promoter 51.97% in Jun-26, unchanged). [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Verdict: **Missed** — FY26 profit and cash targets missed, Q1 FY27 revenue collapsed; order intake is the only strong point. The ₹5,000 cr aspiration (~12x FY26 revenue) is not credible on this record.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex modest: fixed assets ₹34 cr → ₹56 cr. The ₹80.2 cr solar-module plant is deferred; "no planned capex in next 2-3 years" (Infomerics). [Screener; Infomerics Feb-26]
- Loan of ₹10.34 cr given to subsidiary RMC Green Energy in FY26; total investment in subsidiaries ₹11.9 cr. [AR FY26 Board's report; note 5]
- PulseBox development spending hit Q3 FY26 profit; next R&D project under way (undisclosed). Management: "the technology will always be from a Western country." [Analyst Day]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹59 cr (FY25) → ₹105 cr (FY26); includes promoter unsecured loans and inter-corporate loans (Infomerics). Gearing 0.59x at Sep-25. Additional ₹35 cr working-capital limit taken in FY26; limit use >90% historically. [Screener; Infomerics Feb-26]
- Operating cash flow FY26 −₹12 cr; free cash flow −₹34 cr. [Screener]
- Receivables: consolidated trade receivables ₹148 cr → ₹205 cr (analyst on Analyst Day); debtor days 186. Standalone ageing at Mar-26: not due ₹70.7 cr, <6 months ₹84.4 cr, **6–12 months ₹20.7 cr** (~12% of ₹175.8 cr) — higher than the CEO's "3% to 5%" (which excluded retention). Retention money ~₹58 cr (10% withheld until project completion). Payment terms 120–150 days. [Analyst Day; AR FY26 note 5(iii)]
- No dividend. Promoter holding 51.97%; FIIs 2.7%. Stock down ~73% from its high. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Weak in EPC: management calls its EPC business a "red ocean market… where the price decides everything"; FY26 solar orders had to be cancelled, reworked or deferred when input costs rose. [Analyst Day]
- Some edge in certified utility products (smart-meter enclosures) and bundled product + EPC bids; utility empanelments across Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Gujarat. [IP Q1FY27; Infomerics Feb-26]
- PulseBox claimed to be unique ("right now, we are the only one") — unproven commercially. [Analyst Day]

## Risks and red flags
- **Legacy default-grade ratings:** CARE kept RMC's facilities at **CARE D; Issuer Not Cooperating** (12 Nov 2025) because the company did not supply information; Brickwork's **BWR D (Issuer Not Cooperating)** was reaffirmed and withdrawn on 10 Apr 2026. Only Infomerics rates it currently: IVR BBB/Stable, A3+ (upgraded 25 Feb 2026). [CARE Nov-25; Brickwork Apr-26; Infomerics Feb-26]
- **Insider-trading lapse:** the secretarial audit found a promoter (a designated person) "executed a contra trade in violation of the Company's Code of Conduct and the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations", not reported to the exchange; the company-secretary post was vacant for a period and there were instances of UPSI sharing not recorded in the digital database. [AR FY26 secretarial audit report & management reply]
- **Deconsolidation of Intelligent Hydel:** on 31 Mar 2026 RMC pledged its entire 51% stake in Intelligent Hydel Solutions to Saatvik Cleantech EPC as security for "financial assistance", concluded it had lost control, and stopped consolidating it (CEO: "we have sold the IHSPL"). The terms and any gain/loss are not clearly explained. [AR FY26 note 5 footnote (ii); Analyst Day]
- **Intra-group sales:** standalone sales to own subsidiaries in FY26 — ~₹106 cr to RMC Green Energy and ~₹27 cr to RMC Solar Park — plus large related balances (receivable/advance from RMC Green Energy ~₹64 cr per the table; layout jumbled). Consolidated revenue eliminates these, but standalone figures are inflated by them. Related-party trade with How Sweet and Acme Metawires (KMP relatives) is small. [AR FY26 note 31]
- **Audit report format:** the audit report reproduced in the FY26 annual report is the Regulation 33 report on the year's results (unmodified); I could not find a CARO annexure or key audit matters in the filed document. [AR FY26]
- Concentration (MSEDCL ~55% of FY25 sales), government payment delays, commodity exposure (raw materials 80–90% of cost). [Infomerics Feb-26]
- Contingent liabilities small: pending litigation/GST/EPCG items ~₹1.0 cr at Mar-26. [AR FY26 note 30]

## What changed between the two calls
- Nov 2025: growth story (H1 +111%), module-plant capex deferred, equity raise dropped.
- Jun 2026: "learning year" — Q3 loss, cancelled/deferred solar orders, negative cash flow; pivot to "profitable, sustainable growth" and technology (PulseBox); water business dropped; IHSPL "sold".
- After June: Q1 FY27 revenue halved, but order book jumped to ~₹1,188 cr with Gujarat awards.

## My read
RMC grew revenue ten-fold in four years by taking on government solar and electrical EPC, but FY26 showed the cost: margins fell, profit dropped 27%, cash flow turned negative and receivables ballooned; Q1 FY27 revenue then halved. Governance signals are poor — two agencies still carry "D / issuer not cooperating" ratings, a promoter breached insider-trading rules, and a subsidiary was pledged and deconsolidated with little explanation. At ~₹233 cr market cap (~11x trailing profit) the ₹1,188 cr order book offers upside if executed profitably, but the record says be sceptical. I would want positive operating cash flow in H1 FY27, collection of the ~₹58 cr retention money, and a first paid PulseBox order before trusting the story.
