# Servotech Renewable Power System (SERVOTECH) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Transcript check: **Q1 FY27 stored copy — tidied copy (figures match; gaps).** The official NSE transcript (call of ~22 Jul 2026, filed 28 Jul) is a short, retail-investor call; the stored copy smooths the MD's broken English and garbled numbers (e.g. official "EBITDA increased by 62.85 percent from 23.18 crore to 47 crore, standing at 11.10 crore" is a mis-read of standalone EBITDA ₹23.18 cr / PAT ₹11.10 cr) and gives consolidated EBITDA as ₹20.91 cr (official call: ₹20.94 cr). ## Business and segments
- Makes and sells solar inverters (hybrid, on-grid, off-grid), solar panels via channel, AC and DC EV chargers (7 kW to 360 kW; working on 1 MW), lithium battery packs (solar storage, e-rickshaws), small BESS, servo stabilisers; also executes government EPC (rooftop solar for railways, state EV-charging tenders, OMC fuel-station chargers). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Reports one segment. MD's FY26 split: solar ~51%, AC/small chargers ~27%, DC chargers ~15%, installation/AMC ~4%, energy storage ~1%, power ~1%; long-run aim ~60% solar / 40% EV. [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 32]
- Almost all sales in India: FY26 standalone export income ₹1.4 cr vs ₹24.3 cr in FY25. [AR FY26 note 32]
- Channel: 800+ distributors, 6,000+ retailers, 370+ towns; retail channel ~₹25 cr a month vs ₹2 cr in FY22; target >50% of revenue from channel. Brand ambassador Sonu Sood, TV campaigns, a cricket franchise (Siliguri Strikers) and a T10 league. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Group: Rebreathe Medical (95%; oxygen concentrators, revenue cut from ₹98 cr to ₹32 cr in FY26), Servotech EV Infra (94.28%, charge-point operator), Servotech Sports & Entertainment (61.73%, loss-making), Hertz & Pixelz (51%), 27% associate Rhine Solar (solar module and cell maker). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Capacity/utilisation: inverter output ~12,000–15,000 units a month (Q4); FY26 new lines "above 60%" used; Q1 call: "utilising only 50%" of capacity, yet also "not able to make as much material as there are orders"; battery-pack capacity "fulfilled". Absolute capacity not disclosed ("giving the idea in the public domain is very dangerous"). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Company gives no numeric revenue/margin guidance, citing "SEBI guidelines". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (7 May 2026) promises:** FY27 "a year of operational consolidation"; "no fresh long-term debt"; capex to "moderate significantly", funded from internal accruals; restore positive operating cash flow; gearing "below half a turn"; receivables to "industry typical" levels; working-capital days to 60–70; new capacity 100% used from Q2 FY27; a QIP being worked on "if things get better"; channel >50% of revenue.
- **Q1 FY27 call (late Jul 2026):** revenue +58% consolidated delivered; but MD now says "the debt can increase", "no logic to go towards equity" (QIP shelved), and announces BESS capacity 2x in 6 months, 3x by 31 Mar 2027, 10x in two years, with a new Haryana plant under a ₹400 cr MoU ("its building and structure is also starting to be built"). DC/AC charger demand "a little bit stagnant". [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Verdict: **Mixed** — top line beat; balance-sheet promises (no new debt, capex moderation, equity raise) were reversed within one quarter. No Q1 update on receivables or cash flow was given.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex/asset purchases ~₹79 cr (incl. ₹12.2 cr for 27% of Rhine Solar); fixed assets rose from ₹69 cr to ₹156 cr (Screener). MD: "Total CapEx for the year was 64 crore." [Q4 call; Screener; AR FY26 related-party note]
- New term loans: HDFC ₹30 cr (Jun-25, machinery), ICICI ₹12 cr (Oct-25). [AR FY26 borrowings note]
- FY27: Haryana MoU "₹400 crore" to expand manufacturing (headline from the presentation; amount, timing and funding not explained); BESS capacity 3x by Mar-27, 10x in two years; MD says capital will not be 10x. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Payback: not quantified; MD says BESS "is very helpful to improve our EBITDA". [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- Weak. Products are largely assembled power electronics; MD himself says "99% of batteries are just assembly". Patents: four filed, three granted (charger/solar topologies); BEE 5-star rating for 60 kW and 120 kW DC chargers. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Channel reach (800+ distributors) and brand spending are the main differentiators; >70% partner retention claimed. [IP Q1FY27]
- Customers: OMCs (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL), railways, state nodal agencies (UPNEDA, BREDA). Government work brings long payment cycles. [Infomerics Sep-25; Q4 call]
- No disclosed market share. Charger market "stagnant" per MD in Q1. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Balance-sheet U-turn:** in May the plan was no new long-term debt and a QIP; in July debt "can increase" and equity was ruled out, while a ₹400 cr MoU plant is started. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Disclosure quality:** the consolidated segment note in the FY26 annual report says the company's operations "primarily comprise of Dealing in Commodities, Commodity Derivatives" and cites Ind AS 109 — boilerplate copied from another company. Management refuses to give capacity, order book or product mix in presentations. [AR FY26 consolidated note 32; Q1 call]
- **Related parties:** purchases from associate Rhine Solar ₹36.4 cr and investment ₹12.15 cr; trade with Bhatia Electronics Products Pvt Ltd (director-interested) — sales ~₹7.7 cr, purchases ~₹2.4 cr (table layout unclear). Group spends on cricket franchises and event companies that lose money. [AR FY26 related-party note]
- **Contingent liabilities:** ₹82.4 cr at Mar-26 (mostly ₹81.4 cr bank guarantees for EPC work), about a third of net worth. [AR FY26 note 34]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion by M/s Rohit KC Jain & Co; key audit matter on revenue recognition (discounts and rebates). CARO: bank returns agree with books; no defaults; unspent CSR transferred to the special account on 29 Apr 2026. Subsidiaries/associate audited by other auditors. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Rating: Infomerics IVR BBB+/Stable (upgraded Sep 2025) on FY25 data (gearing 0.39x then); gearing has since roughly doubled. [Infomerics Sep-25; Q4 call]
- Stock −50% from 52-week high; FII exit. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "consolidation and normalisation" to "expansion": BESS 3x/10x targets and a new Haryana plant.
- QIP dropped; more debt accepted.
- Receivables, cash flow and the ₹100 cr stuck with OMCs/railways were not discussed in Q1.
- Charger demand described as "stagnant"; growth now pinned on solar (PM Surya Ghar) and BESS.

## My read
Revenue is growing fast again (+58% in Q1) and the H2 FY26 margin of ~12% was the best in its history, so the business is not broken. But cash conversion is poor — two years of negative free cash flow, debt nearly tripled, receivables at ~120 days — and management reversed its own balance-sheet promises within a quarter. Disclosure is thin and sometimes careless. At ~₹1,600 cr market cap and ~42x trailing earnings the price needs sustained 25%+ growth with cash coming in. I would want to see operating cash flow turn positive, receivables fall below 90 days and proper disclosure of the Haryana plant's cost and funding before taking a more positive view.
