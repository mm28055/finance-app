# Indian electrical equipment — sector research, 28 Sep 2026

Scope: smart meters, wind turbines, steam/gas turbines, gearboxes, T&D equipment (transformers, switchgear, conductors, towers), EV chargers, and the power-capex cycle. Note on method: the web-search tool hit its usage limit during this run, so sources were opened directly (curl) from known industry, government and rating-agency pages; company documents read for the company files are used where they carry industry data. Every source below was opened.

## Market size and growth
- **Industry growth FY26: +12.6%** on the IEEMA Electrical Equipment Growth Index (FY25 +14.1%, FY24 +17.5%). Momentum picked up late: Q4 FY26 +18.5%, led by power transformers (+58.5%), insulators (+42.7%), cables (+24.1%), conductors (+16.4%), HV switchgear (+15.5%) and LV switchgear (+12.0%). [IEEMA FY26 update]
- **FY26 growth by product (IEEMA index):** energy meters +54.9% (FY25 +73.7%), power transformers +31.6% (+34.8%), insulators +27.4%, HV switchgear +23.6% in the table (the text says MV-HV switchgear +7.9% — IEEMA's own page is inconsistent), conductors +18.4%, transmission towers +12.6%, capacitors +11.3%, cables +10.0%, LV switchgear +7.9% (FY25 +14.8%), distribution transformers +3.4%, rotating machines (motors) +1.1%, instrument transformers −35.1%, surge arresters −8.7%. Within HV: GIS +41.8%, HV circuit breakers +28.2%; MV GIS −11.9%, RMUs −4.2%. [IEEMA FY26 update]
- **Smart meters are past peak growth:** meter growth ran +88.3% (Q1 FY26), +96.7% (Q2), +53.6% (Q3), then **+11.3% in Q4 FY26**. [IEEMA FY26 update]
- **Trade:** electrical imports jumped 33.9% to ₹2,02,093 cr (US$23.1 bn) in FY26; exports rose 11.5% to ₹1,30,659 cr (US$14.8 bn) — the trade deficit widened sharply, i.e. much of the demand boom is being met by imports (mostly Chinese equipment and components). [IEEMA FY26 update]
- **T&D is the core:** IEEMA estimates transmission & distribution is ~65% of the Indian electrical and allied electronics industry (quoted by Salzer's management). [Salzer Q1 FY27 call]

## Demand drivers (with numbers)
- **Generation additions:** record 54,597 MW added in FY26, 96% non-fossil; installed base 5,32,740 MW at 31 Mar 2026, 53% non-fossil. [IEEMA FY26 update] IBEF puts capacity at 542.36 GW (May 2026) with renewables 225.61 GW; FY26 generation 1,840 BU (+0.6% only — weak demand year). [IBEF Power, Sep-26]
- **Renewables pace (MNRE, 31 Aug 2026):** solar 168.0 GW, wind 58.5 GW; FY27 to date (Apr–Aug 2026) +17.8 GW solar and +2.4 GW wind; total non-fossil 304.3 GW. Rooftop solar 32.6 GW (PM Surya Ghar). [MNRE physical progress]
- **Wind:** a record ~6 GW was added in FY26 and 2.35 GW of pure-wind capacity was awarded in Q1 FY27 tenders (Inox Wind presentation, as read for the companion Inox Wind file). CEA's 2035–36 plan needs wind at 155 GW — roughly 10 GW a year from ~58.5 GW today; the wind makers' body IWTMA targets 100 GW by 2030. [Powerline on CEA plan, May-26; IBEF Power; MNRE]
- **Transmission:** only 12,139 ckm of lines and 1,13,013 MVA added in FY26 against targets of 15,382 ckm and 1,26,007 MVA. [IEEMA FY26 update] ICRA (Jul 2026): only ~12% of TBCB transmission projects commissioned by March 2026 met their scheduled date (median delay >10 months); curtailment of renewable power under T-GNA runs 50–60% in solar hours in Rajasthan/Gujarat. ICRA says the sector needs ~20,000 ckm and 120 GVA a year — "an investment opportunity of at least Rs. 5-6 trillion between 2026-27 and 2031-32" — and calls it a "multi-year capex upcycle"; ~107 GW of already-connected projects are to be integrated into ISTS in FY27–FY31. [SaurEnergy on ICRA, 8 Jul 2026]
- **CEA long-range plan (Mar 2026):** transmission for 900 GW+ of non-fossil capacity by 2035–36; installed capacity ~1,121 GW (786 GW non-fossil), solar 509 GW and wind 155 GW — i.e. 40–45 GW of solar+wind additions a year; peak demand ~459 GW and energy 3,365 BU by 2035–36; 10 GW offshore wind evacuation (Gujarat, Tamil Nadu); GEC-III to evacuate 134.7 GW RE plus 25.2 GW pumped storage. [Powerline on CEA plan, May-26]
- **National Electricity Plan to 2032:** ₹9.15 lakh crore of transmission investment, network to 6.48 lakh ckm, for 458 GW peak demand by 2032; a PLI-like scheme for transmission equipment is being considered. [IBEF Power, Sep-26]
- **Smart meters (Parliament reply, mid-2026):** 7.24 crore smart meters installed nationally by 30 Jun 2026, of which 5.73 crore under RDSS; RDSS has sanctioned 20.33 crore meters (19.79 crore consumer, 52.5 lakh DT, 2.05 lakh feeder); RDSS sunset **31 Mar 2028**. Slow progress blamed on early RFP/test-bed delays and consumer resistance (rebates for prepaid meters, no maximum-demand penalties now offered). [T&D India on Parliament reply] My arithmetic: ~14.6 crore RDSS meters remain for ~21 months — about 0.7 crore a month versus a recent pace nearer 0.3 crore a month (RMC's presentation cited 4.05 crore RDSS installs in early 2026), so either the deadline slips again or installation must roughly double. [RMC IP Q1FY27; derived]
- **Industrial capex:** gearbox demand from steel, cement and power is "favourable" (ICRA, Jul 2026; Elecon FY26 order inflow ₹2,660 cr, +11.8%). Steam turbines: Triveni's FY26 order inflow of ₹2,326 cr was "supported entirely by exports and the aftermarket" — domestic industrial captive-power demand was weak. [ICRA Elecon Jul-26; ICRA Triveni Sep-26]
- **EV charging:** Q1 FY27 registrations ~86,000 electric passenger vehicles, ~1,400 e-buses, 6,500 e-goods carriers; e-4W penetration ~5%, e-bus 7%, e-3W 32% (FY26); Delhi's ₹15,000 cr 4-year EV policy targets 30,000+ charging points; PM E-DRIVE incentives go to charger users, not makers. Utilisation of public chargers is a known problem (30–35% of deployed DC chargers said to be non-functional, per a study cited by Exicom). [Exicom Q4 & Q1 calls; Salzer Q1 call]
- **Data centres, BESS, nuclear:** Viability Gap Funding of ₹5,400 cr for 30 GWh of battery storage with ISTS waiver to June 2028; SHANTI Act (Dec 2025) opens nuclear to private capital (100 GW target by 2047); PM-KUSUM budget raised to ₹5,000 cr in FY27. [IBEF Power, Sep-26]

## Policy
- RDSS (₹3.03 lakh crore; smart meters, loss reduction) runs to March 2028. [T&D India; RMC IP Q1FY27]
- ALMM-style localisation rules extended to wind turbines should favour local makers "for at least 3 years" (Inox Wind management, per companion file). Solar PLI and ALMM keep module/cell supply domestic but with cost volatility; safeguard-duty and China supply shocks hurt solar EPC margins in FY26 (RMC Analyst Day). [RMC Analyst Day]
- FGD rule relaxation (July 2025) cut the retrofit market for thermal-plant pollution control — only ~8 GW of Category A plants must fit FGD (GE Vernova T&D/GE Power companion file). 
- US tariffs (50% → 18% reset) and West Asia disruption cut Indian electrical exports in FY26 (Salzer exports fell to 21% of sales from 28%). [Salzer IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Salzer Aug-26]

## Cost drivers
- Copper, silver, aluminium, zinc, steel and plastics spiked through H2 FY26 and Q1 FY27 on the West Asia conflict; silver went from ~₹70/g to ~₹400/g before easing to ~₹250/g (Salzer). Switchgear makers without cost-plus contracts lost 4–5 points of margin (Salzer switchgear 12% → 7.5–8%) because price rises lag 2–3 months; CRISIL counted ~24% cumulative price increases at Salzer in six months. [Salzer Q1 call; CRISIL Salzer Aug-26]
- Fuel/gas costs hit galvanisers (KP Green) and plastics-heavy product makers; semiconductor and copper shortages limited EV-charger output (Exicom). [KP Green H2 call; Exicom Q1 call]
- Fixed-price government contracts (BharatNet, RDSS, state solar EPC) absorb commodity spikes — the common reason for FY26 margin misses at Exicom, RMC and Genus-type players. [Exicom IP Q1FY27; RMC Analyst Day]

## Competitive structure and share
- Concentrated niches with pricing power: industrial gearboxes (Elecon ~38–40% domestic share, ICRA), sub-100 MW steam turbines (Triveni "dominant" domestically, ICRA), power transformers and HV equipment (large MNC-linked players; demand far ahead of supply — IEEMA +31.6%/+58.5% in Q4).
- Fragmented, tender-driven segments: structures/towers (KP Green — ICRA calls it "highly fragmented and competitive"), utility EPC ("red ocean", RMC), LV switchgear and wires (Salzer — CRISIL notes "low entry barriers" and cheap Chinese imports), EV chargers (AC chargers commoditising; Exicom cites "industry-wide pricing pressure"), smart meters (new AMISP entrants; fixed-price TOTEX contracts). [ICRA KP Green May-26; RMC Analyst Day; CRISIL Salzer Aug-26; Exicom AR FY26]
- Imports rising 34% in FY26 shows domestic capacity is short in high-end equipment and components. [IEEMA FY26 update]

## Cycle position
- **Grid/T&D: early-to-mid upcycle.** Transmission additions are running below target, ICRA sees a ₹5–6 trillion spend in FY27–FY32, and Q4 FY26 transformer growth was +58.5%. Risk is execution (ROW, transformer shortages), not demand. [ICRA via SaurEnergy; IEEMA]
- **Smart meters: past peak growth, entering the back half.** Meter growth fell to +11% in Q4 FY26; RDSS deadline March 2028; installation pace must double to finish. [IEEMA; T&D India]
- **Renewables equipment (wind, solar structures): strong but lumpy.** Record FY26 wind; FY27 to August only +2.4 GW wind; curtailment and transmission delays are the brake. [MNRE; ICRA]
- **Industrial capex (turbines, gears, motors): mixed/soft domestically.** Motors +1.1% (IEEMA), Triveni's domestic inflow nil in FY26, while Elecon sees healthy steel/cement/power orders. [IEEMA; ICRA]
- **Consumer/retail electrical and EV chargers: demand growing, margins squeezed** by commodities and competition. [Salzer; Exicom]

## Outlook FY27–FY29 (forecaster named)
- **ICRA (Jul 2026):** transmission "multi-year capex upcycle"; ≥₹5–6 trillion investment FY27–FY32 (~₹1 trillion a year); ~107 GW to be integrated FY27–FY31; slippages likely. [SaurEnergy on ICRA]
- **CEA (Mar 2026 plan):** 40–45 GW a year of solar+wind additions to 2035–36; wind 155 GW, solar 509 GW; peak demand ~459 GW by 2035–36. [Powerline]
- **Ministry of Power / NEP (IBEF summary):** ₹9.15 lakh crore transmission to 2032; peak 458 GW by 2032. [IBEF Power]
- **CRISIL (Aug 2026, on Salzer):** switchgear/wires revenue +16–20% in FY27 and 8–10% a year over the medium term, operating margin recovering to 9–9.5%. [CRISIL Salzer Aug-26]
- **ICRA (Sep 2026, on Triveni):** margins to recover in the rest of FY27 on exports and aftermarket; revenue growth "comfortable". **ICRA (Jul 2026, on Elecon):** favourable medium-term gearbox demand; outlook revised to Positive. [ICRA Triveni; ICRA Elecon]
- **Government (Parliament reply):** RDSS completion targeted by 31 Mar 2028 — implies smart-meter volumes stay high through FY28, then fall sharply unless RDSS-II or a successor scheme follows. [T&D India]
- **IWTMA (via IBEF):** wind toward 100 GW by 2030. [IBEF Power]
- My synthesis: FY27–FY29 demand is strongest in transmission equipment (transformers, HV switchgear, conductors, towers) and grid-scale renewables/storage; smart meters stay busy to FY28 but with falling growth and tough cash terms; EV chargers grow in volume but remain low-margin; small-cap EPC-heavy names carry the most execution and receivable risk.
