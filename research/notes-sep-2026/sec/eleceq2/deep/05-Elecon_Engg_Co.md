# Elecon Engineering (ELECON) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Figures from Screener's consolidated page unless tagged otherwise.
- Price ₹458; market cap ₹10,283 cr; 52-week range ₹352–626 (27% below the high); trailing P/E 34.0; ROCE 20.9%; ROE 16.4% [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].
- Promoters 59.28% (unchanged); FIIs down to 7.0%, DIIs up to 7.2% [Screener].

## Business and segments
- **Gears (≈80% of Q1 FY27 revenue):** industrial gearboxes — catalogue products (54% of Q1 gear sales, priced off a monthly list) and engineered/custom products (46%), plus marine gearboxes for the Indian Navy. End markets: power, steel, cement, sugar, mining, rubber, marine [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Material handling equipment, MHE (≈20%):** wagon tipplers, stacker-reclaimers, crushers, conveyors, port equipment for power, cement, mining and ports. EPC has been exited; now products and after-sales only [Q1 call; ICRA Jul-26].
- **Market position:** 38–40% share of India's industrial gearbox market [ICRA Jul-26]; "only company in India" able to make complex naval gearboxes [IP Q1FY27].
- **Geography:** international 22% of FY26 revenue [ICRA Jul-26]; 29% in Q1 FY27 (₹151 cr, +21.9%) [Q1 call]. FY26 overseas revenue: Benzler Europe ₹171 cr, Radicon USA ₹102 cr, UK ₹102 cr, Middle East ₹66 cr, Singapore ₹22 cr [Q1 call]. Assembly/repair centres in UK, Sweden, USA, Netherlands; a new step-down subsidiary in Mexico [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- **Plant:** Vallabh Vidyanagar (Gujarat), ~3,35,000 sq m, with in-house foundry. Capacity utilisation only "around 56% to 60%" after recent capex [Q4 call].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM / PAT (₹ cr) [Screener]:**
  - Q1 FY26: 491 / 130 / 27% / 175 (inflated by a ₹25 cr arbitration award in revenue, ₹10 cr in other income and an ₹80 cr post-tax exceptional gain)
  - Q2 FY26: 578 / 126 / 22% / 88
  - Q3 FY26: 552 / 109 / 20% / 72
  - Q4 FY26: 746 / 158 / 21% / 6 (after a ₹102 cr goodwill impairment)
  - **Q1 FY27: 521 / 109 / 21% / 70**
- **Q1 FY27 on the company's adjusted basis:** revenue +11.9% vs adjusted ₹465 cr; EBITDA ₹109 cr (+3.9%); PAT +2.3%. Gear revenue ₹416 cr (+16.3%), EBIT ₹75 cr, 17.9% margin; MHE revenue ₹105 cr (−2.9%), EBIT ₹27 cr (−25.3%) [Q1 call].
- **Annual [Screener]:** FY24 sales ₹1,937 cr, OPM 24%, PAT ₹356 cr; FY25 ₹2,227 cr, 25%, ₹415 cr; FY26 ₹2,366 cr (+6%), 22%, ₹341 cr (−18%). TTM PAT ₹236 cr (hit by the impairment). Adjusted FY26 revenue ₹2,341 cr, EBITDA ₹498 cr (21.3%) [Q4 call].
- **Margin drift:** gear EBIT margin 18.8% in FY26 vs mid-20s three years ago; management blames the catalogue-heavy mix, higher R&D and overseas hiring (employee cost +16% in FY26) [Q1 call; ICRA Jul-26].
- **Order book (30 Jun 2026):** ₹1,518 cr (+36.8%) — gears ₹1,043 cr (+46.9%), MHE ₹475 cr (+18.8%); overseas ₹256 cr (+73%). Q1 intake ₹755 cr (+23%), including the largest single industrial-gear order the company has received, from the power sector (~27% of gear intake). FY26 intake ₹2,660 cr (+11.8%) [Q1 call; Q4 call; ICRA Jul-26].
- **Unit economics:** gear order book is ~73% engineered in Q1 intake; engineered margins are 2–3 points above catalogue [Q1 call; Q4 call]. About ₹160 cr of the gear book runs beyond FY27 [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Earlier:** FY26 guidance cut in the December quarter (Q4 revenue at least 5% lower) [Q4 call]. FY30 target ₹5,000 cr revenue (about 21% a year from FY26).
- **Q4 call (16 Apr 2026):** FY27 guidance **withheld**; ₹77 cr of March orders pushed into Q1; EBITDA could return to "close to 22% to 24%" if domestic orders flow; MHE sustainable margin 20–22%; Navy order expected in Q4 FY27; capex ₹400 cr over FY26–FY28 (₹95 cr spent) [Q4 call].
- **Q1 call (13 Jul 2026):** guidance reinstated — "low double-digit consolidated revenue growth" with EBITDA margin held at FY26 levels; gear EBITDA ~24%; MHE EBITDA 22–24%; exports to grow double digits; FY30 ₹5,000 cr "challenging but still achievable"; Navy RFP still expected to yield orders in Q4 FY27; possible upgrade next quarter [Q1 call].
- **Delivery:** the ₹77 cr carry-over was booked, but another ~₹70 cr of finished goods dispatched before 30 June missed revenue recognition [Q1 call]. MHE revenue fell despite a record book. **Mixed:** guidance reinstated but modest; execution timing still slipping.

## Capex and capacity
- ₹400 cr programme over FY26–FY28, ~₹95 cr spent in FY26 (80% gears) [Q4 call; Q1 call]. CWIP ₹23 cr; fixed assets ₹930 cr [Screener]. Depreciation jumped from ₹61 cr (FY25) to ₹104 cr (FY26) as earlier capex was commissioned [Screener].
- Further capex "which we will seek the board's approval in a near future's time" [Q1 call].
- With utilisation at 56–60%, near-term growth does not need new capacity; the capex builds capability (engineered, marine) and lifts depreciation ahead of revenue. Mexico: little capex planned [Q4 call].
- Funding: internal cash; no debt except leases [ICRA Jul-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Net cash ~₹700 cr;** investments ₹743 cr at March 2026 yielding ~8% [Q4 call; Screener]. Borrowings ₹273 cr are lease liabilities [ICRA Jul-26].
- **Cash flow:** operating cash ₹314 cr in FY26 (81% of operating profit) vs ₹425 cr (101%) in FY25; free cash ₹218 cr [Screener].
- **Working capital:** debtor days 111 (84 in FY24), inventory days 98; receivables rose from ~₹610 cr to ~₹720 cr, mostly Feb–March sales [Screener; Q4 call].
- **Dividend:** ₹1.5 per share (payout 13%) [Q4 call; Screener].
- **Rating:** ICRA AA/A1+, outlook raised to Positive on 15 Jul 2026 [ICRA Jul-26].

## Moat evidence
- 38–40% share of India's gearbox market; broadest range; in-house foundry and design [ICRA Jul-26].
- Naval gearbox capability — first order executed at 1–2 points lower margin because of learning; repeat orders expected to carry better margins [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Catalogue price list refreshed monthly, which shields ~55% of gear sales from cost spikes [Q1 call].
- **Against it:** "for the new projects, definitely there will be a price pressure from the MNCs" setting up local plants; rivals with cheap inventory undercut in Q1; large PSU customers dictate dispatch timing [Q1 call]. Gear margins have not returned to earlier levels.

## Risks and red flags
- **Execution timing:** recurring end-of-quarter slippage (₹77 cr in Q4, ₹70 cr in Q1); MHE design clearances delayed by EPC contractors [Q1 call].
- **Input costs:** ~5% average bill-of-materials inflation (steel 5–7%, bearings 6–7%, tooling 6–10%) cut MHE margin by 2.5–3 points in Q1 [Q1 call].
- **Low returns on the cash pile:** ROE fell from ~29% (FY24) to 16% as ₹750–800 cr sits in investments [Q4 call; Screener].
- **Accounts and auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matters are **related-party transactions** (sales, purchases and services with several promoter-group companies such as Power Build, Emtici Engineering, Eimco Elecon, Elecon Hydraulics, Akaaish Mechatronics) and receivables impairment (ECL) [AR FY26 auditor's report; RPT note]. FY26 carried a ₹102 cr non-cash goodwill write-off on the 2010-11 Benzlers-Radicon acquisition and ₹80 cr of mark-to-market exceptional gains [Q4 call]. Contingent liabilities: excise/service-tax disputes ₹49.3 cr and GST ₹10.4 cr [AR FY26 note 41/42].
- **Defence timing:** Navy aircraft-carrier gear order expected in Q4 FY27; Project 17A pushed to ~Q3 FY28 [Q4 call].
- **US tariff:** 50% Section 232 duty on Indian gear exports to the US; Mexico unit to serve Latin America [Q4 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- **Tone:** from guidance withheld and "limited near-term visibility" to cautious reinstatement.
- **Numbers:** order book ₹1,292 cr → ₹1,518 cr; MHE sustainable-margin talk 20–22% → 22–24%.
- **New issues:** input-cost inflation; another ₹70 cr of stranded finished goods; European recovery "two quarters minimum" away.

## Best management quotes
- "We are targeting to have a low double-digit consolidated revenue growth while maintaining the EBITDA margin as it we had it in the last year." [Q1 call]
- "For the new projects, definitely there will be a price pressure from the MNCs and all the competitions." [Q1 call]
- "Having 750-800 crores in investments is a good problem to have." [Q4 call]

## My read
Elecon is a strong franchise (40% share, net cash ~₹700 cr, AA rating now on Positive outlook) whose earnings have stalled: FY26 adjusted revenue +5%, margins down about 3 points from the FY25 peak, and FY27 guided to only low double-digit growth despite a record ₹1,518 cr book. The reasons are partly cyclical (PSU dispatch delays, war, input costs) and partly structural (MNC competition, catalogue-heavy mix, rising depreciation and overseas costs). At ~34 times trailing (impairment-depressed) earnings the price needs a return to 15%+ growth; watch Q2 conversion of the gear book, gear EBIT margin back toward 20%, and whether the Navy order lands in Q4 FY27.
