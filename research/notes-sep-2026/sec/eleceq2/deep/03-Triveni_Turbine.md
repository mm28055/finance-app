# Triveni Turbine (TRITURBINE) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Figures from Screener's consolidated page unless tagged otherwise.
- Price ₹554; market cap ₹17,618 cr; 52-week range ₹428–788 (30% below the high); trailing P/E 50.8; ROCE 34.1%; ROE 25.2% [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].
- Promoters 55.84% (stable). FIIs have cut from 28.0% (Mar 2025) to 20.6% (Jun 2026); DIIs up from 10.6% to 16.0% [Screener].

## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** industrial steam turbines below 100 MW (captive power, co-generation, biomass, waste-to-energy, waste-heat recovery, sugar, steel, cement, oil and gas "API" drive turbines), plus aftermarket — spares, service and refurbishment of its own and other makers' rotating equipment. New lines: organic Rankine cycle (ORC) turbines, CO2 heat pumps, MVR compressors and a CO2-based long-duration energy storage system (with Energy Dome technology) [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Position:** "Top 2 globally in industrial steam turbines" in the <100 MW segment; 6,000+ installations in 80+ countries, 16+ GW [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]. ICRA calls its domestic position "dominant" [ICRA Sep-26].
- **Mix FY26:** exports 57.7% of revenue (₹1,258 cr, +30.1%), domestic 42.3%; product 73%, aftermarket 27% [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]. Aftermarket fell to 25% of Q4 FY26 sales from 33% [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 mix:** domestic sales ₹240 cr (+27.4%), exports ₹203 cr (+10.8%), export share 45.8% (49.3% a year ago) [Q1 call].
- **Plants:** Peenya and Sompura (Bengaluru), plus service workshops in Houston (Texas) and Pretoria (South Africa) [AR FY26]. Execution cycle: 7–8 months for <15 MW up to ~18 months for 100 MW [Q1 call].
- **Market:** the <100 MW steam-turbine market outside China and Japan was flat at ~7 GW between 2021 and 2025, while >100 MW grew 26.8% a year [IP Q1FY27, citing McCoy 2025]. Triveni's core market is not growing fast; growth comes from share, exports, aftermarket and new products.

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM / PAT (₹ cr) [Screener]:**
  - Q1 FY26: 371 / 74 / 20% / 64
  - Q2 FY26: 506 / 115 / 23% / 91
  - Q3 FY26: 624 / 133 / 21% / 92
  - Q4 FY26: 680 / 128 / 19% / 102
  - **Q1 FY27: 443 / 51 / 12% / 51** (sales +19%, PAT −20%)
- Company-reported EBITDA (includes other income) was ₹79.7 cr, 18.0%, against 25.8%; PBT ₹69.7 cr, 15.7% vs 23.5% [Q1 call]. ICRA's operating margin: 11.6% vs 19.8% [ICRA Sep-26].
- **Why Q1 was weak:** export dispatches deferred by customers after freight rose "3-4x" (orders are FOB, so the client chooses when to ship); more low-margin domestic execution, including the balance of bought-out items on large projects; the near-zero-margin NTPC CO2 storage order; and orders booked a year earlier at lower prices [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Annual [Screener]:** FY24 sales ₹1,654 cr, OPM 19%, PAT ₹269 cr; FY25 ₹2,006 cr, 22%, ₹359 cr; FY26 ₹2,181 cr (+9%), 21%, ₹349 cr (−3%, after a ₹15.7 cr wage-code exceptional charge [Q4 call]). TTM sales ₹2,252 cr, PAT ₹336 cr.
- **Order book and intake:**
  - Closing order book ₹2,180 cr at 30 Jun 2026 (+5.1%), of which aftermarket ₹624 cr (+115%, 29% of the book) and exports 57% [Q1 call; ICRA Sep-26].
  - Q1 FY27 intake ₹568 cr (+6.1%): exports 68% (+53.4%), aftermarket 39%; domestic intake −35.4%, product intake −11.6% [Q1 call].
  - FY26 intake ₹2,326 cr, slightly below FY25's ₹2,363 cr [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Sep-26].
- **Enquiry book (Q4 call):** product enquiries ~18 GW, nearly double FY25; North America ~3 GW (up ~1,000% from a low base; data centres over half of the enquiry book); India ~7 GW (+100%) [Q4 call]. Not updated on the Q1 call; domestic enquiries "have seen weakness" [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (19 May 2026):**
  - EBITDA margin volatility "around sort of a mean 25% EBITDA level"; "comfortable with a 25% plus EBITDA level" [Q4 call].
  - FY27 growth indicated by the ~8% growth in the FY26 closing order book ("gives you an idea" of execution); H2-weighted [Q4 call].
  - US subsidiary (loss ~₹8 cr in FY26) to be "profitable" in FY27 [Q4 call].
  - API drive turbines to return to 10%+ of order booking and revenue; exports to exceed domestic revenue [Q4 call].
- **Q1 call (11 Aug 2026):**
  - Margin anchor changed to "a PBT margin of over 20%" over the medium and long term; no EBITDA number [Q1 call].
  - US subsidiary now to "break even" in FY27; it made a loss in Q1 [Q1 call].
  - Full-year revenue and profit growth still promised, back-ended [Q1 call].
- **Delivery vs promise:** margin 18% (company EBITDA) vs 25% mean — **missed**; US profitability **cut** to break-even; exports below domestic in Q1 — **behind**; order-book growth 5% vs ~8% at year-end — **behind**. The margin target was effectively lowered.

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: FY26 capex about ₹72 cr (operating cash ₹111 cr less free cash ₹39 cr); fixed assets ₹363 cr; CWIP ₹17 cr [Screener]. Asset turnover 5.74x [IP Q1FY27].
- Spending is on the Houston refurbishment workshop (upgraded in FY26), the Pretoria unit, R&D and digital tools; no large expansion announced [AR FY26]. ICRA sees "no major debt-funded capital expenditure" [ICRA Sep-26].
- Capacity is not a constraint; the bottleneck is order conversion and dispatch timing.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Debt-free;** cash and liquid investments ~₹970 cr at March 2026 and ₹1,020 cr at 30 Jun 2026 [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Sep-26].
- **Receivables jumped:** ₹639 cr at March 2026, 84 days vs 49 days a year earlier, because billing bunched at year-end; management expects a similar level at March 2027 [Q4 call]. Screener debtor days 107 (66 in FY25); working-capital days 71 (47) [Screener]. Q1 saw receivables fall and "a reversion to negative working capital" [Q1 call].
- **Cash conversion fell:** operating cash ₹111 cr in FY26 (53% of operating profit) vs ₹187 cr in FY25 and ₹271 cr in FY24; free cash ₹39 cr [Screener].
- **Dividend:** payout 39% in FY26 [Screener].
- **Rating:** ICRA AA+ (Stable)/A1+ reaffirmed 18 Sep 2026; non-fund limits raised to ₹920 cr [ICRA Sep-26].

## Moat evidence
- Global top-two share in <100 MW industrial turbines; ICRA cites "technical and cost competitiveness", value engineering and faster turnaround [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Sep-26].
- Management: "We generally do not lose international orders based on price… This is largely an oligopolistic market globally" [Q4 call].
- Aftermarket and refurbishment of other makers' machines broaden the annuity: aftermarket order book more than doubled to ₹624 cr [Q1 call].
- Customer advances fund part of working capital (negative working capital on non-balance-sheet dates) [ICRA Sep-26; Q4 call].
- **Against it:** orders are mostly fixed-price; competition from global parents' subsidiaries; exposure to sugar and cement customers with moderate credit [ICRA Sep-26]. "What we get is what our competitors get" on domestic pricing [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin reset:** from a 25% EBITDA anchor to ">20% PBT" in one quarter; Q1 operating margin 11.6% [Q1 call; ICRA Sep-26].
- **Domestic slowdown:** domestic intake −35.4% in Q1; product intake −11.6% [Q1 call].
- **Export timing:** FOB contracts let clients delay shipment when freight spikes [Q1 call].
- **New-technology bets:** the NTPC CO2 storage pilot was taken at ~3% PBT and saw cost increases; commissioning end-Q2/early-Q3 FY27 [Q4 call; Q1 call]. US subsidiary lost ~₹8 cr in FY26 and again in Q1 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Accounts and auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matter is revenue from contracts [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Contingent liabilities small — ₹17.2 cr consolidated, mostly income tax [AR FY26 note 41]. FY26 exceptional ₹15.7 cr for employee benefits under the new wage code [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]. Related-party dealings are routine; the chairman and vice chairman also run group company Triveni Engineering [AR FY26].
- **Governance note:** the COO was moved to an AI-focused "Business Transformation" role in Q1 [Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- **Tone:** from "enquiry book doubled, US booming, 25% EBITDA mean" to "challenging quarter, back-ended year, >20% PBT".
- **Numbers:** Q1 company EBITDA margin 18% vs 25.8% a year ago; order book ₹2,180 cr (+5.1%) vs ~8% growth at year-end.
- **New issues:** freight-driven dispatch deferrals, domestic intake collapse, US subsidiary guided only to break-even.

## Best management quotes
- "That degree of volatility will always exist around sort of a mean 25% EBITDA level." [Q4 call]
- "We believe that a PBT margin of over 20% is something that we can maintain in the medium and long-term." [Q1 call]
- "Freight rates have gone up 3-4x, the availability of vessels has become a little bit less easily available for our customers." [Q1 call]

## My read
Triveni is a high-quality, debt-free franchise (top-two global share, ~₹1,000 cr cash, ROCE mid-30s), but growth has stalled: FY26 sales +9%, profit −3%, intake flat, and Q1 FY27 margins halved. The US data-centre story is real in enquiries but converts slowly (12+ months, water permits), and the domestic market has weakened. At ~51 times trailing earnings the price needs a clean H2 margin recovery and double-digit growth resuming in FY28. Watch Q2–Q3 export dispatches and margin back above 20%, the first US combined-cycle product order, and whether domestic intake stabilises.
