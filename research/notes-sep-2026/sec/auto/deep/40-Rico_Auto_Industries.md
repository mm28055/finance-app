# Rico Auto Industries Ltd (RICOAUTO / BSE 520008) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Consolidated (Rico Auto + Rico Jinfei Wheels 96.3%, Rico Friction Technologies 70%, AAN Engineering 100% (defence), US and UK trading subsidiaries). Price ₹132, market cap ₹1,789 cr, trailing P/E 50.0, 52-week range ₹78–158 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026]. Calls: Q4 FY26 (1 Jun 2026) and Q1 FY27 (14 Aug 2026); Q1 FY27 presentation (August 2026).

## Business and segments
- Aluminium die-casting and machining (89% of Q1 FY27 revenue; FY26 ₹2,155 cr, 88%) and ferrous castings (11%; FY26 ₹322 cr) — engine, transmission, EV/hybrid housings, brake parts; plus oil/water pumps and alloy wheels (Rico Jinfei), friction materials, defence fuses and shooting-range containers (AAN) [IP Aug-26; Q4 call].
- Capacity: aluminium 1,10,000+ t a year (120+ die-casting machines up to 2,700 t, more than half above 800 t); ferrous 75,000+ t; 3,000+ CNC/special machines; 16 plants [IP Aug-26]. Utilisation ~65–75% on both lines at Q4 FY26, with small machines near 95% [Q4 call].
- Customers: BMW, Toyota (and the Toyota–Aisin hybrid ecosystem), Ford, Maruti Suzuki, Hero, Kia, Tata, Mahindra, Bosch, Cummins, Knorr-Bremse [IP Aug-26; Q4 call; CRISIL Dec-25]. Single source on the new programmes [Q1 call].
- Geography: exports 15% of Q1 FY27 revenue (18% a year earlier); FY26 exports ₹395 cr (16%) [IP Aug-26; Q4 call]. Management said on the same Q1 call that exports were "around 10–12%" this quarter — an inconsistency [Q1 call].
- EV and hybrid ~7–9% of the business mix; the new Hosur plant (commercial production from September 2026) is aimed at hybrid/EV parts for the Toyota–Aisin ecosystem [IP Aug-26; Q1 call].
- Non-auto: railways (RDSO approvals; FY26 revenue only ₹3–4 cr), defence (150 shooting-range containers supplied in FY26; revenue ₹20–30 cr), and external sales of its own CNC machines (target 100 machines, ~₹35–40 cr in FY27) [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Aug-26].

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Annual: FY22 sales 1,860, OP 158 (8%); FY23 2,302 / 221 (10%); FY24 2,160 / 222 (10%); FY25 2,212 / 195 (9%); FY26 2,478 / 225 (9%); net profit FY23 51, FY24 39, FY25 21, FY26 52; TTM sales 2,689, NP 32 [Screener]. Five-year sales growth 11% a year, ROE 6–8% for a decade [Screener].
- Quarters (sales / OP / OPM / NP): Q1 FY26 543 / 53.9 / 9.9% / 16.7; Q2 627 / 61.0 / 9.7% / 17.7; Q3 629 / 60.6 / 9.6% / 11.1; Q4 677 / 47.8 / 7.1% / 6.9; Q1 FY27 755 / 34.8 / 4.6% / −3.4 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 revenue +39% YoY, but about ₹100 cr (standalone) to ₹110–115 cr (consolidated) of that was higher aluminium prices passed through; volume-led growth was roughly 20% [Q1 call].
- Q1 FY27 margin hit: ~₹13 cr air freight and sorting (rusted parts after 9-week sea transits; a measurement-correlation quality issue put parts on hold), ~₹10 cr raw-material settlement lag (aluminium +42–57% YoY for some grades), plus manpower (Haryana raised wages ~40%), power, gas and tooling inflation; other expenses rose ₹24 cr [Q1 call; IP Aug-26].
- FY26 one-offs cited: Labour Code ₹11 cr and RM lag ₹19 cr; "adjusted" FY26 margin 10.25% [Q4 call].
- Asset turns: fixed assets ₹1,206 cr + CWIP ₹173 cr vs sales ₹2,478 cr; the presentation shows an asset-turnover ratio of both "2.10" (slide 25) and "1.2" (slide 33) for FY26 — internally inconsistent [IP Aug-26; Screener].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 (1 Jun 2026): FY27 revenue "cross ₹3,000 cr"; EBITDA margin above 10.25% "starting from Q1 itself"; exports +32% (US and Germany to double in two years); railways ₹100 cr and defence ₹50 cr in FY27; new orders ~₹2,500 cr over five-year programme lives; Hosur from September 2026; net-debt repayments ~₹110–120 cr a year [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 (14 Aug 2026): revenue raised to ₹3,200–3,250 cr (Q2 >₹840 cr, Q3 >₹850 cr, Q4 ~₹900 cr; July alone ~₹300 cr); Q2 margin "in between" 4.6% and 10%, back to normal from Q3; "our target should be around 12%. We will be achieving the 12% target" (no year stated); railways RDSO approvals "in the cycle" of 2–3 months; defence ~₹5–7 cr; ~₹4,000 cr revenue possible from existing assets; capex to be restrained for a year [Q1 call, official].
- Delivery against the Q4 promise: Q1 margin 4.6% vs ">10% from Q1" — **missed badly**; revenue **beaten**. Railways slipped again (FY26 target of ₹60–70 cr from railways and defence was missed, per the Q4 call; RDSO approval described as received in June, "in the cycle" in August) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Long-range: an analyst cited a ₹7,500 cr 2030 target that management did not dispute [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Capex: FY22 ₹187 cr, FY23 ₹236 cr, FY24 ₹193 cr, FY25 ₹151 cr, FY26 ₹212 cr — about ₹1,000 cr in five years while revenue rose ~₹600 cr [IP Aug-26; Q1 call].
- Hosur greenfield (three phases), hybrid/EV parts; Tamil Nadu subsidy of ~₹39 cr accruing over 10 years from FY27 [Q4 call; IP Aug-26]. Capital commitments outstanding ₹27.3 cr at March 2026 [AR FY26 note 41].
- A 2,700-tonne die-casting cell costs ~₹25 cr plus ₹10–15 cr infrastructure; each die ~₹8–8.5 cr; Maruti and Hero pay for tooling through the piece price, others upfront [Q1 call].
- Land: Haridwar plot (~2 acres, ~₹10 cr) held for sale, payment due by 30 December 2026; Gurugram land — a ₹700 cr offer was refused as too low; a "Vatika" land parcel under discussion [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 note 5E].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹728 cr (Mar-26); net debt ₹686 cr (₹653 cr a year earlier); leverage ~3.75x; term-debt repayments ₹110–130 cr a year [Screener; Q4 call; CRISIL Dec-25]. Borrowings include ₹147 cr of sales-bill discounting and ₹147 cr of TReDS purchase-bill discounting [AR FY26 borrowings note].
- Cash flow from operations ₹326 cr FY26 (₹193 cr FY25); free cash flow ₹33 cr [Screener]. Working-capital days fell from 33 to 7 [Q4 call]; bill discounting helps this.
- Working capital (Screener): debtor days 52, inventory 67, payables 111, cash cycle 7 days [Screener].
- Rating: CRISIL A/Stable, A1 (reaffirmed Dec 2025; September 2026 bulletin only updated lenders). CRISIL's downgrade trigger is an operating margin **below 8% sustained**; Q1 FY27 was 4.6% [CRISIL Dec-25; Screener]. CRISIL noted promoters may support via equity and unsecured loans; directors' interest-free short-term loans appear in the borrowings note [CRISIL Dec-25; AR FY26].
- Dividend payout 15% FY26 [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Single-source status on 55 new programmes (28 launched and ramping), 7–8-year lives, with Toyota, BMW, Ford [Q1 call].
- Scale in large-tonnage die casting and in-house die/machine building (250 HPDC dies a year, up to 16-axis CNC machines built in-house) [IP Aug-26].
- Pricing: aluminium is pass-through but with a lag; 75% of customers by value now on monthly settlement (standalone lag hit fell to ₹3.3 cr in Q1) [Q1 call]. Freight and inflation claims are negotiated after the fact — one customer agreed to pay ~50% of air freight [Q1 call].
- Weakness: returns have stayed at 6–9% ROCE for years; CRISIL says ROCE may reach ~9.5% but lag its earlier 11–13% expectation [Screener; CRISIL Dec-25].

## Risks and red flags
- **Guidance credibility**: margin promised above 10% from Q1 came in at 4.6% ten weeks later; management said the air-freight surge came after the June call [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Rating trigger**: margin well below CRISIL's 8% downgrade threshold in Q1 FY27 [CRISIL Dec-25].
- Debt ₹686 cr net against thin profits; net profit fell to a loss in Q1 FY27 [Screener].
- Related parties: promoter-group firm ASN Manufacturing & Services supplies raw material/job work, services, rent (about ₹38 cr of transactions with KMP-controlled enterprises in FY26 vs ₹26 cr; table layout garbled in the text) and a loan balance of ~₹10 cr; total managerial remuneration ₹15.3 cr against net profit of ₹52 cr [AR FY26 note 53]. Two of five independent directors (Sarita Kapur, Yogesh Kapur) share the promoter family's surname; the filings do not state a relationship [AR FY26 note 53; IP Aug-26].
- Auditor: key audit matter revenue recognition; contingent liabilities ₹15.4 cr (income tax ₹12.4 cr) [AR FY26 note 40].
- CFO changed in November 2025 (Rakesh Kumar Sharma → Naveen Sorot) [AR FY26 note 53].
- Export logistics: 9–10 week sea transits to the US; US tariffs [Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- From "adjusted margin 10.25%, above 10% from Q1" (June) to "Q1 4.6%, normal from Q3" (August).
- Revenue guidance lifted to ₹3,200–3,250 cr, but ~₹100–250 cr of FY27 revenue is metal-price inflation [Q1 call].
- Railway story walked back from "RDSO has given us approval" (June) to "in that cycle" (August); defence now quoted at ₹5–7 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New: CNC-machine sales, a one-year pause on big capex, and a ₹4,000 cr revenue capacity claim [Q1 call].

## My read
Revenue is finally growing fast (Q1 FY27 +39%, about half of it real volume) as new single-source programmes for Toyota, BMW and Ford ramp up. But the company has spent ~₹1,000 cr of capex in five years for thin returns, net debt is ₹686 cr, and a margin promise made in June failed within weeks. At a trailing P/E of 50 on depressed earnings, the price needs about 24% a year of profit growth ((50 × 0.1726)^0.1 − 1) — achievable only if margins really return to ~10% from Q3 FY27 while revenue reaches ₹3,200 cr+. Hidden land value (a ₹700 cr offer for Gurugram) is a genuine cushion. The view changes on two clean quarters at ≥9–10% margin, or on a land sale used to cut debt.
