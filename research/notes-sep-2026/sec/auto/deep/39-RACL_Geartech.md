# RACL Geartech Ltd (RACLGEAR / BSE 520073) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Consolidated (India + RACL Geartech GmbH, Austria). Price ₹1,686, market cap ₹1,988 cr, trailing P/E 40.0, 52-week range ₹922–1,933, stock +52% in a year [Screener, 27 Sep 2026]. Calls: Q4 FY26 (12 Jun 2026) and Q1 FY27 (25 Aug 2026); Q1 FY27 presentation filed 25 Aug 2026. The exchange sought a clarification on unusual trading volume on 15 Sep 2026 (company letter filed the same day) [Screener documents list].

## Business and segments
- Precision transmission gears, shafts, gearbox sub-assemblies, chassis and steering parts, industrial gears. Plants at Gajraula (main, 100% green power) and Noida; a trading subsidiary in Austria with five European warehouses; 29 active customers, 1,600+ SKUs, 1,200+ staff [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- FY26 revenue split by segment: commercial vehicles 20%, recreational vehicles 18%, passenger cars 13% (nil in FY22–23), tractors/agri 10%, two-wheelers ~30% (was 40–45%) [Q4 call].
- Geography: exports 66% and domestic 34% of Q1 FY27 net sales; Europe 59%, India & Asia-Pacific 36%, North America 5% [IP Q1FY27]. FY26: exports 75%, Europe 69% [Q4 call]. Standalone FY26 export sales ₹336 cr (+15.5%), domestic +18.5% [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Customers: KTM (Austria; 15-year relationship; largest customer ~16% of FY25 revenue), ZF (BMW X5 platform; electric power steering), MAN Trucks (full resourcing of MAN's in-house gear lines completed in 2025), BMW (EV projects), TVS/Norton (single source for the entire gear train), Royal Enfield (new, 350cc gears), Kawasaki Japan (new), BHEL (industrial gears, supplies started) [Q4 call; Q1 call; CARE Oct-25]. Top five customers ~58% of FY25 revenue; policy that no customer exceeds 20% [CARE Oct-25].
- In export programmes RACL is the sole supplier of each part for the life of the model, by contract; domestic OEMs (e.g. Royal Enfield) multi-source [Q1 call].

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr; Screener "sales" includes other operating income)
- Annual: FY23 sales 358, operating profit 82 (23%), NP 37; FY24 410 / 96 (23%) / 39; FY25 415 / 85 (20%) / 24; FY26 490 / 107 (22%) / 49; TTM 522 / 117 / 50 [Screener].
- **FY26 profit was flattered by currency gains**: other income ₹22.5 cr, of which foreign-exchange gain ₹21.7 cr (₹8.6 cr in FY25) — about a third of FY26 pre-tax profit of ₹66 cr [AR FY26 other-income note; Screener]. The company's "EBITDA ₹126 cr, 25.3%" (standalone) and "consolidated revenue ₹512 cr" both include this other income; operating margin on Screener's basis was 22% [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call].
- Quarters (sales / OP / OPM / other income / NP): Q1 FY26 100.7 / 18.8 / 18.7% / 8.1 / 8.3; Q2 120.3 / 24.4 / 20.3% / 5.6 / 12.1; Q3 137.3 / 31.3 / 22.8% / 3.9 / 16.3; Q4 131.7 / 29.3 / 22.2% / 4.8 / 12.4; Q1 FY27 132.4 / 31.9 / 24.1% / 0.3 / 8.9 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: operating revenue +31.5% YoY, operating margin a record 24.1%; but pre-tax profit (₹16.8 cr) was flat on Q4 because forex gains vanished, and tax was 47% (a deferred-tax hit from a surcharge-rate change), so net profit fell to ₹8.9 cr [Screener; Q1 call]. Management guided a 25.62% full-year tax rate [Q1 call].
- Cost structure Q1 FY27 (standalone): raw material ~28%, staff ~12%, finance cost 4.8%, depreciation 7.2%, manufacturing expenses 33.5% [Q1 call].
- Returns: ROCE 17% FY26 (12% FY25), ROE 17% [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 (12 Jun 2026): growth "20%" a year (15–20% band); ₹1,000 cr revenue "in next three to five years"; FY27 capex ₹77.45 cr with matching bank debt; BMW Titan/Venus SOP October–November 2026 after an August sign-off; Royal Enfield commercial production August–September; Kawasaki mass production from January 2027; ZF truck steering SOP September–October 2026; shift drum SOP "next month" at 150,000 a year [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 (25 Aug 2026): management confirmed guidance "closer to 570 crores" of revenue (16–17% growth), ±5%, and refused to raise it mid-year; 15–20% growth "next year as well"; capex ₹77 cr = ~₹40 cr heat-treatment replacement + ~₹35 cr capacity [Q1 call, official].
- Delivery: Q1 operating revenue +31.5%, ahead of the 16–17% pace. BMW final sign-off slipped from August (Q4 call) to 24 October (Q1 call), with pilot shipments already going [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Royal Enfield: official Q1 call says commercial supplies "started from January" and run at 7,500–8,000 sets a month against a nomination of ~10,000 — which conflicts with the Q4 call's "commercial production August–September" for the new 350cc project (these may be different part sets; not clarified). **Guidance kept; timing on BMW slightly later.**

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex ₹77.45 cr: new all-electric heat-treatment plant (~₹40 cr) replacing 30-year-old LPG furnaces — building done by October 2026, equipment September–October, commissioning October–December, trial production January 2027; ~₹35 cr capacity additions, part for FY27 and part FY28 [Q1 call]. Management calls the heat-treatment spend "not capex, a replacement programme" [Q1 call].
- Next capex (announced each January) will include a large outlay for the ZF "Crystal" electric-power-steering project for a US OEM (commercial supplies end-2027/mid-2028) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- ZF X5 line runs at 50–60% of installed capacity; the programme's peak moved from 2028 to 2030 [Q1 call].
- CARE: four strategic projects with ~₹350 cr of combined revenue potential over four years [CARE Oct-25].
- Asset turns: fixed assets ₹342 cr vs sales ₹490 cr (~1.4x) — a capital-heavy business (my calculation from Screener).

## Cash, debt and working capital
- ₹80 cr preferential issue in May 2025 (Malabar India Fund, White Oak funds, Dr Aniruddha Malpani) used to prepay debt; promoter stake fell from 53.3% to 42.7% [CARE Oct-25; AR FY26; Screener].
- Borrowings ₹236 cr (Mar-26) from ₹317 cr; gearing debt ₹225 cr; debt/equity 0.63x (1.3x before) [Screener; AR FY26 note 41; Q4 call].
- Cash flow from operations ₹92 cr FY26 (₹56 cr FY25); free cash flow +₹39 cr, the first sizeable positive year in the series [Screener].
- Working capital is heavy: inventory ~3.5 months (European warehouses near customers), export collections 3–4 months, cycle ~5 months, bank-line use ~80% [CARE Oct-25]. Screener shows debtor days 109 and inventory days 375 (inventory days is inflated because raw material is only ~28% of sales) [Screener].
- Dividend payout ~4% [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Sole-source, life-of-model contracts on export parts; customers support suppliers when a model fails (cash, alternate business or price increases) [Q1 call].
- Premium niche: gears for KTM (including the new Duke 790 kinematics), BMW EV reduction gearboxes, TVS Norton; "barring one or two brands... most are buying gears... from us" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Process evidence: zero-paper, end-to-end digital traceability for BMW; TISAX and IATF certifications [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Customer stickiness: no project has ended in 10–15 years without a successor of equal or higher value [Q1 call].
- Rating: CARE A-/A2+ with outlook revised to **Positive** (Oct 2025) [CARE Oct-25].

## Risks and red flags
- **Earnings quality**: a third of FY26 pre-tax profit came from forex gains; Q1 FY27 net profit fell to ₹8.9 cr as those gains disappeared and tax spiked [AR FY26; Screener].
- Europe exposure (59–69% of sales) and KTM concentration (KTM's 2025 insolvency cut FY25 revenue by ₹35–40 cr) [CARE Oct-25; Q1 call].
- Capital intensity and working capital (5-month cycle); new debt for FY27 capex [CARE Oct-25; Q4 call].
- Domestic multi-sourcing (Royal Enfield): management deliberately limits capacity for such customers [Q1 call].
- Governance: related-party dealings small — CMD remuneration ₹2.72 cr, wife's retainership, son (COO) ₹0.58 cr, CSR trust ₹0.87 cr, ₹0.12 cr to a pickleball company [AR FY26 note 40]. Auditor unqualified; key audit matter is revenue cut-off [AR FY26]. Contingent liabilities negligible (₹0.03 cr tax; small bank guarantees and a €1.1 mn SBLC for the Austrian subsidiary) [AR FY26 note 49]. No pledge [Screener].
- Disclosure: no per-kit value, no project sizes; revenue figures quoted on calls often include other income [Q1 call; Q4 call].

- KTM confirmed back to near pre-COVID volumes; ZF X5 programme recovering; ZF shifting assembly to Eastern Europe [Q1 call].
- BMW final sign-off moved from August to 24 October 2026 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Kawasaki and ZF US truck pilot not updated on Q1 [Q1 call].

## My read
The operating business is improving: sales +31% in Q1 FY27, operating margin at a record 24%, debt cut with fresh equity, and a pipeline (BMW EV, Royal Enfield, Kawasaki, ZF steering) that supports 15–20% growth for several years. But the headline profit growth of FY26 leaned on ₹22 cr of currency gains, and Q1 FY27 net profit fell as those disappeared. At a trailing P/E of 40, the price needs about 21% a year of profit growth ((40.0 × 0.1726)^0.1 − 1) — at the top of what management guides and above it once forex gains are stripped out. I would watch the BMW SOP after the 24 October sign-off, the heat-treatment plant starting in January 2027, and whether operating (not forex-aided) profit keeps compounding.
