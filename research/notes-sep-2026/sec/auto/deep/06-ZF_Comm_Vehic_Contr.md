# ZF Commercial Vehicle Control Systems India (ZFCVINDIA) — deep research

Research date 27 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore unless stated. Screener = consolidated page, read from the raw HTML table rows.

## Business and segments
- Formerly WABCO India; the Indian arm of ZF's Commercial Vehicle Solutions division. Makes air-braking and vehicle-control systems for trucks, buses, trailers, tractors and off-highway machines: ABS (anti-lock brakes), EBS (electronic braking), ESC (electronic stability control), compressors, actuators, valves, AMT (automated manual transmission) parts, ECAS (electronically controlled air suspension), door-control systems, e-compressors for electric buses. [IP Sep-26]
- FY26 revenue mix (management): domestic OEM ₹1,978 cr (+17.5%), aftermarket ₹583 cr (+15.5%), goods exports ₹1,025 cr (−11%), service exports (engineering/software for ZF's global centres) ₹509 cr (+10%). Roughly 48% OEM / 14% aftermarket / 25% goods exports / 12% services. [Q4 call]
- Claims ~80% share of India's CV braking market and ~50% of pneumatic braking in construction equipment; supplies pneumatic brakes to most tractor OEMs and to defence vehicle programmes. [IP Sep-26]
- Geography (standalone FY26): product sales India ₹2,499 cr, overseas ₹1,022 cr; services India ₹44 cr, overseas ₹462 cr. [AR FY26, note 38]
- The company reports one segment (automotive) under Ind AS 108, so no segment margins are disclosed. [AR FY26, note 38]
- Footprint: 6 plants (Ambattur, Mahindra World City SEZ, Oragadam, Jamshedpur, Lucknow, Pantnagar), 3 engineering centres, a 200-acre proving ground, ~550 aftermarket dealers, 5,560+ employees, 1,100+ engineers. [IP Sep-26]
- Utilisation: Mahindra World City (export plant) is "at almost peak level"; further expansion will go to Oragadam. [Q4 call]
- A wholly owned subsidiary, ZF CV Control Systems Manufacturing India Pvt Ltd, earned total income of ₹83.9 cr in FY26 (FY25 ₹33.1 cr) and PAT ₹10.5 cr. The parent lent it ₹10 cr and the board approved a further ₹30 cr investment in preference shares. [AR FY26, Board's report §7–8]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM / PAT (Screener): Jun-25 ₹976 / ₹132 / 13% / ₹122 · Sep-25 ₹913 / ₹134 / 15% / ₹108 · Dec-25 ₹1,075 / ₹199 / 19% / ₹140 · Mar-26 ₹1,155 / ₹190 / 16% / ₹146 · Jun-26 ₹1,066 / ₹138 / 13% / ₹104. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 sales grew 9.2% YoY; PAT fell 14.7% because Q1 FY26 carried a ₹39 cr foreign-exchange gain plus actuarial one-offs (₹43.7 cr in total). Excluding these, PBT was ₹140.8 cr, up 16.9%. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹3,816 → FY25 ₹3,831 → FY26 ₹4,119 (+7.5%); operating profit ₹567 → ₹632 → ₹657; OPM 15% → 16% → 16%; PAT ₹406 → ₹461 → ₹517; EPS ₹45.44 (pre-bonus basis). 3-year sales CAGR 6%, profit CAGR 17%. [Screener]
- Other income is large: ₹173 cr in FY26, about 25% of PBT (₹693 cr), mostly interest on the cash pile. Core operating profit grew only 4% in FY26 while sales grew 7.5%. [Screener]
- Unit economics: OEM content per vehicle about ₹45,500 in Q3 FY26 and ₹39,500 in Q4 FY26; the Q3 figure was inflated by retrospective price settlements, so underlying content was "more or less flattish". [Q4 call]
- Industry: CV >6-tonne production 463,000 in FY26 (+16.6%); Q4 FY26 a record 151,000. ZF's OE sales grew 17.6%, slightly ahead. Q1 FY27 industry +8.4%, ZF +8.6%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Aftermarket Q1 FY27 ₹158.4 cr (+15.6%); record month June 2026 at ₹63.06 cr. Goods exports Q1 FY27 ₹271.4 cr (+9.7%). Services +12.5% YoY. [Q1 call]
- Return ratios: ROCE 19.4%, ROE 14.6% (diluted by cash). [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (15 May 2026): no revenue/margin guidance. On FY27 margins the CFO said she expected them "more or less flattish with maybe single-digit small growth" and would be "cautious" given the West Asia war. FY27 capex guided at ₹180–190 cr. Management said April–May volumes looked good. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: sales +9.2%, but OPM slipped to 13% (Screener) as aluminium rose from ~₹260/kg to >₹360/kg; only part of the cost has been recovered from OEMs, recovery is taking longer than the usual two-quarter lag; selective price increase effective July 2026. So "flat margin" is **behind plan** so far; no guidance number was withdrawn because none was given. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Q4 said ADAS full-suite business had been won from 2 OEMs; Q1 added pneumatic ESC nominations from 3 major OEMs, SOP (start of production) Q3 FY28 ahead of the October 2027 truck mandate; localisation 40–50% now, >75% by SOP. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Exports: Q4 expected "moderate improvement"; Q1 delivered +9.7% and management declined to promise double-digit growth for the year. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Longer-term: ZF Group's FY30 India growth target "definitely stands"; an LCV (light CV) braking portfolio was earlier targeted at ~US$90 mn revenue by 2030 — management did not repeat the number on the Q1 call. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex plan ₹180–190 cr (new products plus replacement). [Q4 call]
- Actual capex has been modest: FY26 ~₹143 cr and FY25 ~₹165 cr (cash from operations minus free cash flow, Screener); gross block barely moved (fixed assets ₹736 → ₹732 cr, CWIP ₹68 → ₹61 cr). [Screener]
- Recent additions: lines for ABS/ASP cartridges, vacuum pumps, crankshaft machining at Oragadam; new assembly lines for actuators and valves; assembly at Jamshedpur, Lucknow, Pantnagar to sit near OEM plants. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Asset turns are high (sales ~5.6x net fixed assets), so growth needs little capital; the ESC ramp relies on local EMS (electronics contract manufacturing) partners rather than big in-house capex. [Screener; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash about ₹2,175 cr at Mar-26: cash ₹191.7 cr + bank deposits ₹1,434.9 cr + current investments ₹603.5 cr, against lease-type borrowings of ₹55 cr. Up from ~₹1,496 cr of cash and deposits a year earlier. About 8% of market value. [AR FY26 consolidated balance sheet; Screener]
- Cash from operations jumped to ₹810 cr in FY26 (144% of operating profit) from ₹286 cr in FY25; free cash flow ₹667 cr. [Screener]
- The jump is largely a one-time release of receivables from the parent group: amount due from ZF CV Systems Global GmbH fell from ₹436.9 cr to ₹191.6 cr. Debtor days fell from 107 to 83. [AR FY26 note 37; Screener]
- Inventory days rose from 31 to 43 (inventory ₹191 cr → ₹281 cr), consistent with buffer-stocking during aluminium shortages. Cash conversion cycle 56 days (FY25 80). [Screener; AR FY26]
- Dividend: ₹4 per share post-bonus (≈₹45 cr, ~9% of FY26 PAT) after a 5:1 bonus issue (record date 24 June 2026). Payout remains low given the cash pile. [Q4 call]
- No credit rating is current (no borrowings; last rating actions listed on Screener are Fitch/India Ratings 2015–16). MD&A: "does not foresee a need to borrow or raise capital". [Screener documents; AR FY26 MD&A]

## Moat evidence
- ~80% share of CV braking; nominated by "most of the major OEMs" for ESC, so management expects to keep its "majority position" as ESC becomes mandatory on all >6-tonne vehicles from October 2027. [IP Sep-26; Q1 call]
- Regulation ladder already paid off once: ESC became mandatory on buses (except Type 1 city buses) from September 2025, lifting content in FY26. [Q4 call, official transcript]
- ZF proving ground "fully booked until Oct'2027" for ~150 vehicle models being homologated for ESC rules — a practical bottleneck for competitors. [IP Sep-26]
- Tata Motors gave ZF its Atmanirbhar award (Sep 2026) for localising the e-compressor used in electric buses and trucks. [IP Sep-26]
- Access to ZF's global technology (royalty-based), and a captive export channel to ZF's global plants; switching costs are high because brakes are safety-critical and homologated per vehicle model. [AR FY26 note 37; Q4 call]
- Limits: in ADAS/AEBS (automatic emergency braking) OEMs are unbundling from braking suppliers and "many service providers" compete; ZF has full-suite wins with only 2 truck OEMs and a few bus makers. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Parent dependence and royalty leakage:** FY26 royalty to group companies ≈₹101 cr (FY25 ≈₹90 cr) plus IT support charges from ZF AG ₹45.3 cr (FY25 ₹34.7 cr) — together ~3.5% of sales and rising faster than sales. [AR FY26 note 37.2]
- **Group concentration:** sales of goods to ZF CV Systems Global ₹781 cr (FY25 ₹876 cr) plus software services ₹324 cr, R&D services ₹60 cr and business support ₹74 cr — roughly 30% of revenue comes from ZF group entities at transfer prices set by the parent. Purchases from ZF AG more than doubled to ₹70.8 cr. [AR FY26 note 37.2]
- **Promoter selling:** ZF cut its stake from 75% to 67.49% (Jun 2024), 63.16% (Dec 2024) and 60.00% (Jun 2025), citing its own "liquidity and financial flexibility"; ZF AG's 2025 adjusted EBITA margin was 4.5%, so further sales are possible. [Screener shareholding; News: Autocar Professional, Jun 2024; IP Sep-26]
- **Contingent liabilities:** a new GST claim of ₹57.0 cr appeared in FY26 (nil in FY25); income-tax ₹1.36 cr, property tax ₹0.32 cr, labour ₹0.08 cr. Management says the claims are not tenable. [AR FY26 note 35, standalone]
- **Auditor:** unqualified opinion; one key audit matter (revenue cut-off). The audit trail was not enabled at database level for direct data changes — a common, minor remark. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Management churn:** MD P. Kaniappan left 30 June 2025; CFO Sweta Agarwal left in Q1 FY27; new CFO Rakesh Mishra from 1 Sep 2026; new company secretary from 25 July 2026. [AR FY26 note 37; Q1 call]
- **Related party:** Pinnacle Mobility Solutions (a company in which a director is interested) became a related party from Jan 2026; ₹2.7 cr sales, ₹2.0 cr receivable. Small. [AR FY26 note 37]
- **Margins:** aluminium is the main raw material; OEM pass-through lags 2+ quarters; FX gains swing profits (₹39 cr in Q1 FY26). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Cycle:** CV demand is cyclical; a diesel price increase in May 2026 could weigh on freight rates and fleet purchases. [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from celebrating a record year (FY26 total income ₹4,302 cr, bonus 5:1) to managing cost inflation and labour shortages (April–May blue-collar shortage). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Aluminium moved from ₹233 → ₹274/kg (Q4) to ~₹260 → >₹360/kg (Q1), easing by ~₹25/kg in July. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Exports flipped from −6% (Q4) to +9.7% (Q1) as the US ramped up; services growth stayed strong (+22.5% in Q4, +12.5% in Q1). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New: 3 OEM pneumatic ESC nominations; July price increase; CFO exit. Near-term demand strong — July CV production ~40–42k units, ~10k above a normal July. [Q1 call]

## My read
The business is steadily improving in the right places: aftermarket and services grow mid-teens, exports have turned, and the October 2027 truck ESC mandate — where ZF is nominated at most major OEMs — should lift content per vehicle meaningfully from FY28. Near-term margins are the weak point: operating margin fell to 13% in Q1 and profit growth is flattered or hurt by FX and interest income. With the stock pricing in high-teens earnings growth, it needs margins back to 16%+ by H2 FY27 and a clean ESC ramp; a failure to recover aluminium costs, further ZF stake sales, or rising royalties would change the view. A large net-cash balance (~₹2,175 cr) is a cushion but also drags ROE.
