# SJS Enterprises (NSE: SJS) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Transcript check: both stored transcripts (Q1 FY27 call of 7 Aug 2026; Q4 FY26 call of 6 May 2026) were compared word-by-word with the BSE filings. Verdict for both: **matches official** (word-sequence match ~98%; no figure in the stored copy is absent from the official PDF; differences are page headers only).

## Business and segments
- Makes the "look and feel" parts of vehicles and appliances: decals, 2D/3D dials and appliques, 3D lux badges, aluminium badges, overlays, chrome-plated and painted plastic parts (SJS Decoplast, formerly Exotech), and in-mould decoration/labelling/electronics parts (Walter Pack India, WPI). [IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 revenue by entity: SJS standalone 54.3%, SJS Decoplast (SDPL) 28.0%, WPI 17.7%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 revenue by end market: passenger vehicles 44.6%, two-wheelers 36.6%, consumer appliances 14.5%, others (sanitaryware, electricals, medical) 4.3%. FY26: PV 41.7%, 2W 38.3%, consumer and others ~20%. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Geography Q1 FY27: domestic 90.2% (₹235.4 cr, +20.3% YoY), exports 9.8% (₹25.6 cr, +83.2% YoY). FY26 exports ₹91.1 cr (+60.5%). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Customers: M&M, Tata Motors and TVS Motor were each 12–15% of consolidated FY25 revenue; others include Bajaj, HMSI, Hero MotoCorp (added FY26), Suzuki, Whirlpool, Samsung, Visteon (10 plants worldwide), Autoliv USA, FCA USA, Yazaki, John Deere, Royal Enfield, Ather. [ICRA Feb-26; Q4 call]
- Capacity and utilisation at FY26 end: SJS Bangalore ~75% (₹45 cr expansion adds ~20%); SDPL 95%+ with outsourcing (new ₹100 cr Pune greenfield roughly doubles/1.75x capacity, commercial operations from Aug 2026); WPI ~75%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Kit value per car (company's own figures): legacy ₹1,200–1,500; after WPI ₹3,500–5,000; target 5–8x legacy once displays arrive. Two-wheeler legacy kit ₹300–500 with 1.5–2x upside. [IP Q1FY27]
- Only one reported operating segment (aesthetics for auto and appliances), so no audited segment margin split. [AR FY26 note 40]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (Screener, ₹ cr): Mar-25 201, Jun-25 210, Sep-25 242, Dec-25 244, Mar-26 260, Jun-26 261. Operating margin (Screener) rose from 25% to 29% over those six quarters. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹261 cr (+24.5% YoY, +0.3% QoQ); company EBITDA ₹80.0 cr, margin 30.0% (Screener operating profit ₹75 cr, 29%, because it excludes other income); reported PAT ₹74.4 cr includes a one-time post-tax gain of ₹24.2 cr from selling the old Bangalore plant; normalised PAT ₹50.3 cr (+45.2%), margin 19.3%. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Annual (Screener, ₹ cr): sales FY24 628 → FY25 760 → FY26 955 (+26%); operating profit 153 → 196 → 271; OPM 24% → 26% → 28%; net profit 85 → 119 → 172. Three-year sales CAGR 30%, profit CAGR 38%. [Screener]
- Company FY26: revenue ₹955.1 cr (+25.6%), EBITDA ₹288.0 cr (29.6% margin), PAT ₹171.8 cr (+44.6%); ROCE 35.5%, ROE 19.5%. Auto business grew 41.1% in Q4 FY26 vs industry production +18.9%. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 outperformance: auto business +32.4% vs 2W+PV industry production +21.7% (~1.5x); PV +45.4%, 2W +19.5%. 27th straight quarter ahead of industry. [Q1 call]
- Gross margin: 56.6% in Q4 FY26, 56.7% in Q1 FY27 (CFO); input-cost inflation cost about 50–60 bps of margin in Q1 not yet recovered. [Q1 call]
- New-generation products ~24% of revenue; CEO expects 25–30% within five years. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Industry outperformance: 1.5–2x industry volume growth for FY27, stated on Q4 call and repeated on Q1 call. The FY26 guide had been 2.5x (tied to Hero and export wins); the CEO denied the lower range was a cut. Q1 delivered ~1.5x, the bottom of the range. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Margins: long-run guide 27–28% EBITDA ("benchmark more than 25%"); actual 30.3% Q4 FY26, 30.0% Q1 FY27. Guidance kept conservative. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Order book: "over 85% of FY27 forecast revenue" on Q4 call; "over 88%" in Q1 presentation. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Exports: 14–15% of consolidated revenue by FY28, kept on both calls; Q1 was 9.8%, so exports must grow much faster than the rest to get there. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Displays/cover glass: Q4 call first said supplies "early FY27", corrected to "end FY27, early FY28"; Q1 call fixed it at Q2 FY28. Target: 10% of a ₹5,000–7,000 cr Indian display market by 2030 (today ₹500–1,000 cr). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- SDPL: double SDPL sales in 3–4 years; new plant can add ₹200–250 cr revenue at 2–2.5x asset turns, 85–90% utilisation in ~3 years, EBITDA breakeven at 1–1.25x asset turn. [Q1 call]
- Delivery vs promise: Pune chrome plant was "final stages of commissioning" in May; commercial operations started Aug 2026 (met). Bangalore expansion "fully ready by end of Q1" — not explicitly confirmed on Q1 call. WPI consumer-business ramp promised "next couple of quarters" in May; in Aug still "ramp-up phase", no numbers (slipping). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Three-year special capex (FY26–FY28) ~₹220 cr (Bangalore expansion ₹45 cr, SDPL chrome greenfield ₹100 cr, cover glass ~₹40 cr + display ~₹25 cr = ~₹65 cr) plus maintenance ₹15–20 cr a year; total ₹260–270 cr, of which ~₹80 cr spent in FY26. All three projects to finish capex in FY27. [Q4 call]
- Unexecuted capital contracts (consolidated) ₹45.8 cr at Mar-26 vs ₹29.3 cr at Mar-25. CWIP ₹60 cr at Mar-26 vs ₹6 cr a year earlier. [AR FY26 note 37; Screener]
- Hosur display plant: leased, ~97,000 sq ft, equipment ordered for Q2 FY27 installation; SJS will localise ~50% of display value (TFT glass stays imported). Phase 1 optical bonding; phase 2 in-house cover glass with anti-glare/anti-reflection coatings; ambition to add backlights. Exclusive BOE Varitronix licence for four-wheeler displays (Pricol's BOE deal is 2W-only). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- A wholly owned subsidiary for cover glass/displays was approved in Q1 FY27 to allow a future joint venture. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Funded entirely from internal cash. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash ₹328.8 cr at 30 Jun 2026 (cash ₹338.1 cr, debt ₹9.3 cr), up from ₹243.7 cr at Mar-26 and ₹99.2 cr at Mar-25. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Cash from operations (Screener): FY24 ₹109 cr, FY25 ₹163 cr, FY26 ₹224 cr; free cash flow ₹79 cr → ₹126 cr → ₹142 cr; CFO/operating profit 110% in FY26. Q1 FY27 CFO ₹80.9 cr (101% of EBITDA), FCFF ₹83.8 cr (includes land-sale proceeds). [Screener; Q1 call]
- Working capital (Screener): debtor days 85, inventory days 76, payable days 81, cash conversion cycle 81 days in FY26 (84 in FY25, 108 in FY24). [Screener]
- Dividend: ₹3.5 a share for FY26; payout only ~7% of profit. Cash is being kept for "bite-size" acquisitions (North America, SE Asia, India). [Q4 call; Screener]
- ICRA AA- rating, outlook raised to Positive (Feb 2026); gearing 0.01x, interest cover 65.9x in H1 FY26. [ICRA Feb-26]

## Moat evidence
- Margin leadership: 29.6% EBITDA in FY26, gross margin >50% for four years; peers in auto components mostly run mid-teens. [ICRA Feb-26; Q4 call]
- Pricing: aesthetic parts "get refreshed every year and new prices get priced in automatically"; chrome plating has back-to-back cost pass-through with a one-quarter lag. [Q4 call]
- Share of business: high single-supplier share with M&M, Tata and TVS in SDPL/WPI product lines (ICRA). SDPL sales up ~4x and EBITDA margin from 12% to 20% since the Exotech acquisition. [ICRA Feb-26; Q1 call]
- Cross-selling proof: in Q1 SDPL won Tata business and WPI won Mahindra business. [Q1 call]
- Global customers: Visteon (from one Indian plant to 10 worldwide), Whirlpool (global supplier). [Q4 call]
- In-house R&D centre recognised by DSIR (Govt of India) in Q1 FY27. [IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- **Regulator inquiry at subsidiary WPI**: WPI's secretarial audit (annexed to SJS's FY26 annual report) says the Registrar of Companies, Pune issued notices under Section 206 (Sep 2025, Nov 2025, Mar 2026) in an inquiry started Jan 2024 into "Form ADT-4 filed by the auditor under Section 143(12)" — i.e. a statutory auditor's report of suspected fraud at WPI. WPI was still preparing its reply to the March 2026 notice. The nature and amount are not disclosed anywhere I could find; the calls never mentioned it. [AR FY26, Annexure V, WPI secretarial audit]
- **Tax disputes**: consolidated contingent income-tax liabilities ₹25.6 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹1.8 cr a year earlier, mainly a ₹23.0 cr demand for AY 2024-25 (ESOP expenses, metro-land compensation, and a Section 56(2)(x) adjustment on the subsidiary acquisition) and a ₹2.4 cr demand on non-compete fees. The tax department surveyed the Bengaluru office in Jan 2025 and issued reopening notices for AY 2019-20 and 2020-21. No provision made. [AR FY26 note 37/38]
- **Promoter**: K.A. Joseph and family hold only 20.15% (Jun-26), down from 21.15% in Mar-26 (about 1 percentage point sold in the quarter; no reason given). 2.9 lakh shares (0.91% of the company, ~4.5% of promoter holding) are pledged to Bajaj Finance. [Screener; Trendlyne shareholding; ScanX/SAST disclosure]
- Execution on displays: no customer purchase orders yet; orders expected only after plant PPAP (customer approval) in FY28; margin on the business "cannot say — ask in Q2 FY28". [Q1 call]
- Walter Pack Spain non-compete (no competing for BMW, Mercedes in Europe) until Jan 2027. [Q1 call]
- Consumer segment has lagged (programme shed at WPI; ramp-up slow). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Auto cyclicality (79% of revenue from 2W+PV), raw materials 44–46% of costs, 16–17% imported. [ICRA Feb-26]
- Auditor (S.R. Batliboi) unqualified opinion; key audit matter only revenue recognition/price-variation provisions. Minor: SDPL/WPI filed some forms late. [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone steady and confident; Q1 was the first quarter with flat sequential revenue (+0.3% QoQ) after five quarters of increases, blamed on a 7.5% QoQ fall in PV industry volumes.
- New: display subsidiary formed; DSIR recognition; Pune plant live; land-sale gain; remaining WPI stake bought.
- Display timeline firmed to Q2 FY28 and management said orders come only after local PPAP — slightly more distant than the Q4 "very advanced discussions" wording.
- Analysts pushed on input costs (answer: 50–60 bps drag, recovery in 1–2 quarters) and on flat export and WPI run-rates (~₹25 cr a quarter exports for four quarters); answers were qualitative.
- M&A talk quieter than on Q4 call ("a few in mind, in discussions").

## My read
SJS is a genuinely high-quality, cash-rich niche business: 26% revenue growth, 28–30% margins, net cash ₹329 cr and cash conversion above 100%. The near-term growth engine (SDPL's new chrome plant, Hero and PV cross-selling) is in place, but Q1 showed growth slowing to ~1.5x industry and flat revenue quarter on quarter, and the display story will not produce revenue before Q2 FY28. At a P/E around 39 on Screener (TTM earnings include a ₹24 cr one-off gain), the price already assumes 20%+ growth for years. What would change my view: disclosure on the WPI auditor-fraud inquiry (a negative surprise there would hurt trust), export share failing to move from ~10% toward 14–15%, or a firm display order from a global Tier-1 (positive).
