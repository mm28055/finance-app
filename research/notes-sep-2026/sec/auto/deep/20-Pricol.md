# Pricol (PRICOLLTD) — deep research, Sep 2026

Figures from Screener's consolidated page unless tagged otherwise. Screener "sales" = the company's revenue from operations plus "other operational revenue" (Q1 FY27: ₹1,083.58 cr + ₹21.86 cr = ₹1,105 cr), so Screener revenue runs ~₹20 cr a quarter above the company's headline.

## Business and segments
- Coimbatore-based maker of instrument clusters and connected-vehicle products (DICVS: LCD/TFT/hybrid clusters, telematics, sensors), actuation/control/fluid-management parts (ACFMS: oil and coolant pumps, fuel pumps, cabin tilt, disc brakes, switches) and, since Feb-2025, injection-moulded plastics via Pricol Precision Products (P3L, bought from TVS group's Sundaram Auto Components for ₹197.5 cr on a slump-sale basis). 16 plants incl. Indonesia; 470+ engineers; ~4.5% of revenue on R&D. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 Directors' Report]
- FY26 mix: DICVS ~60%, ACFMS ~20% (~₹750 cr, +~30%), P3L ~20% (₹924 cr revenue, 9.24% EBITDA; ~₹65 cr foam business handed back to TVS in Feb-2026). DICVS projected at ₹2,424.6 cr = 61.17% of FY26 consolidated turnover of ₹4,052.4 cr (demerger scheme). [Q4 call; BSE demerger filing 27-Jun-26]
- Q1 FY27: DICVS and ACFMS each grew ~25%; Pricol's 2W business +28% vs industry +23%; weighted industry +22% vs Pricol +26%. P3L revenue ₹249 cr at 7.8% EBITDA (hit by polymer and LPG prices, and capacity-capped). [Q1 call]
- Market share: 2W clusters 30–35% by volume (largest in India) per the official Q1 transcript; ~two-thirds in CVs and off-road; 8–9% in PVs (only Tata — 8 of 10 Tata cars). CRISIL cites 55–60% of domestic clusters and 65% of 2W clusters (likely value-based) and "second-largest global instrument cluster maker by volume". [Q1 call; CRISIL Jul-26]
- Customers: 2W OEMs ~70% of revenue; domestic OEMs 89–90%; exports ~7% of revenue (target 10%; earlier 20% goal "failed to deliver"). New: Honda (clusters, pumps, plastics — plastics orders partly put on hold for lack of capacity), Suzuki (first switches), Mahindra (early talks), EV makers (Ather, River, Simple, Raptee) and Royal Enfield in plastics. [CRISIL Jul-26; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- TFT penetration in 2Ws ~7–8%, expected to double in three years; hybrid LCD+TFT clusters growing for cost reasons. [Q1 call]

## Key numbers
- Annual (Screener): revenue FY24 ₹2,272 cr → FY25 ₹2,692 cr → FY26 ₹4,041 cr (+50%, mostly the P3L acquisition). Operating profit ₹285 cr (13%) → ₹313 cr (12%) → ₹469 cr (12%). Net profit ₹141 cr → ₹167 cr → ₹251 cr. [Screener]
- Company FY26: revenue from operations ₹3,963.9 cr (+51%), EBITDA ₹492.9 cr (12.44%, +47.5%), PAT ₹250.8 cr, EPS ₹20.57. The 2022 promise of ₹4,000 cr revenue by FY26 was met, with little debt. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Quarters (Screener; revenue / op. profit / OPM / net profit): Q1 FY26 ₹895 / 99 / 11% / 50; Q2 ₹1,007 / 118 / 12% / 64; Q3 ₹1,039 / 121 / 12% / 64; Q4 ₹1,099 / 131 / 12% / 73; Q1 FY27 ₹1,105 / 124 / 11% / 67. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 company: revenue from operations ₹1,083.58 cr (+23.46%), EBITDA ₹123.69 cr (+21.4%), margin 11.41% (13.29% in Q4), PBT ₹87.5 cr (+32%), PAT ₹67.02 cr (+34%). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Cost shock drivers: polymer prices +55%, aluminium +62%, semiconductors +35%, memory devices +28% (as of May); rupee at record lows; freight high; minimum-wage increases in three states cost ~₹21 cr a year. Management says ~1.5–2 points of margin were lost in Q1. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- ROCE 20% FY24 → 21% FY25 → 24% FY26 (flattered by "sweating" full capacity); management expects it to dip during capex, P3L to settle at 16–18%. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (15 May 2026): very cautious — "softening of earnings" and slower auto sector; cannot recover all cost increases from OEMs ("I do not think so"); ACFMS 30% growth "not sure"; still grow faster than market; P3L to double (or more) within three years of acquisition; capex "this year" ₹680–700 cr; debt/equity at most 0.5–0.6; acquisitions under "active negotiations and due diligence"; BOE backlight-module line in 10–12 months; Domino switches 18–24 months; content per vehicle +50% in three years with ~10 strategic customers. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: revenue +23.5% with share gains (**better than the Q4 tone**); margin 11.41% (**down, as warned**); acquisitions **dropped** — "paused" for a year after due diligence found poor asset quality; capex timetable **stretched** to ₹700 cr over 18–24 months. [Q1 call]
- Q1 call (31 Jul 2026): margin recovery through indexation — ~75% of revenue re-priced in Q2, the rest in Q3 — worth at least ~0.5 point if the rupee holds; steady-state margin 12.5–13% ("beyond that, competition will undercut us"); DICVS to grow 5 points above market, ACFMS 10 points above; P3L to 2.5x FY25 annualised revenue, with capacity rising from ~₹1,000 cr to ~₹2,000 cr of turnover; disc brakes and switches meaningful only from FY28; BMS dropped; FY31 revenue ₹8,000 cr "fully intact". [Q1 call]
- Demerger (board 26–27 Jun 2026): DICVS to be demerged into Pricol Autotech Ltd, 1 new share per Pricol share, to be listed; minimum four quarters, CRISIL notes an appointed/effective date of 1 Apr 2027; divisions run as separate entities internally from Oct–Dec 2026. Rationale: raise equity and bring in technology/market partners for integrated cockpits (clusters + infotainment + climate control). [Q1 call; CRISIL Jul-26; BSE demerger filing 27-Jun-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex plan ₹700 cr: ~₹400 cr P3L (new capacity and moving out of TVS campuses where a lock-in period ends), ₹150–180 cr DICVS, ~₹120 cr ACFMS. [Q1 call]
- Five new plants: Hosur, Mysuru, Aurangabad (greenfield), Sanand (for Honda), Bhiwadi (construction started); polymer centre of excellence by May 2027; own tool room; BOE backlight module in progress. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- CRISIL assumes ~₹500 cr capex in FY27, gearing below 0.5x. [CRISIL Jul-26]
- Earlier cycle: investing outflow ₹377 cr FY25 (incl. P3L purchase) and ₹298 cr FY26; CWIP ₹112 cr at Mar-26. [Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings (Screener) ₹61 cr Mar-24 → ₹279 cr Mar-25 → ₹379 cr Mar-26 — inflated because customer and vendor bill discounting (formerly in trade payables) was reclassified as borrowings under new rules; underlying net debt was ₹63.1 cr at Mar-26. Cash ~₹126 cr (CRISIL). [Q4 call; Screener; CRISIL Jul-26]
- Cash flow: CFO ₹255 cr FY24, ₹223 cr FY25, ₹281 cr FY26 (78% of operating profit); free cash flow −₹38 cr FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital FY26: debtor days 52, inventory 70, payables 71, cash cycle 51 days. Inventories of ₹425.9 cr pledged to lenders. [Screener; AR FY26 standalone notes]
- Dividend: 200% interim "Golden Jubilee" dividend (₹2 a share) for FY26; no final, to conserve cash (Screener shows 0% payout — appears to miss the interim). [Q4 call; Screener]
- Rating: CRISIL AA−, placed on Watch Developing (Jul-2026) because of the demerger; bank limits ~43% used; cash accrual ₹370–390 cr a year. [CRISIL Jul-26]

## Moat evidence
- Leading cluster share (30–35% of 2W volume per management; 55–65% per CRISIL), two-thirds of CV/off-road clusters; confirmed LOIs for most programmes over the next three years; an external study says volume share held and value share rose. [Q1 call; Q4 call; CRISIL Jul-26]
- Pricing: FX and commodity indexation (quarterly or half-yearly) recovers most cost increases with a lag; but management concedes a 12.5–13% ceiling because rivals undercut above it. [Q1 call]
- Technology: in-house cluster/telematics tech, awards from Tata (Sierra), Suzuki (5 years best supplier), Ashok Leyland; BOE (displays) and Domino (switches) tie-ups for backward integration. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Weakness: in integrated e-cockpits, global rivals (Nippon Seiki, Continental/Aumovio, Denso, Visteon) have 40–50x Pricol's volume and a cost edge; 45–50% of raw materials imported (65–70% of that from China), exposing margins to the rupee. [Q1 call; CRISIL Jul-26]

## Risks and red flags
- Auditor change: VKS Aiyer & Co resigned as statutory auditor by letter of 10 Jul 2025 "for the reasons mentioned in their letter" (reason not reproduced in the AR sections read; a secretarial compliance summary says no concerns were raised to the audit committee). Sundaram & Srinivasan appointed from 1 Aug 2025. [AR FY26 Directors' Report; News: InvestyWise]
- New auditor's FY26 opinion unmodified; key audit matters: revenue recognition (cut-off, rebates, price revisions) and disputed taxes/litigation. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Contingent liabilities small: taxes ₹8.6 cr; labour cases ₹35.0 cr (₹48.9 cr a year earlier); LCs, guarantees and EPCG ₹43.9 cr. [AR FY26 note 44]
- Related parties: payments to promoter-linked entities (services, reimbursements) ~₹36 cr in FY26 (₹54 cr FY25); MD Vikram Mohan paid ₹8.07 cr in FY26 (107x the median employee, +13.9%); Chairperson Vanitha Mohan 59x the median (+10%) before she stepped back. [AR FY26 standalone related-party note; remuneration annexure]
- Demerger execution: splits a ~61%-of-revenue cluster business from pumps and plastics; timing ≥12 months; could bring a partner at the subsidiary level (dilution of the cluster business). Rating on watch. [Q1 call; CRISIL Jul-26]
- Heavy capex across five plants at once while margins are squeezed; P3L margin already down to 7.8%; FII and DII holdings fell (FII 16.9% → 14.0%, DII 16.5% → 11.8% over two years); promoter stake only 38.5%. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Currency and chip-cost risk; a large share of imports from China. [CRISIL Jul-26; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone from "situation is quite bleak" to confident on growth, though margins fell 190 bps QoQ as warned.
- Strategy: demerger announced (not mentioned in Q4); acquisitions moved from active due diligence to a one-year pause.
- Capex: from ₹680–700 cr "this year" to ₹700 cr over 18–24 months.
- New numbers: steady-state 12.5–13% margin, ₹21 cr wage hit, P3L capacity ₹1,000 cr → ₹2,000 cr, ROCE path.

## My read
Pricol keeps gaining share and has integrated the P3L plastics business well, delivering the ₹4,000 cr revenue it promised in 2022; revenue growth is running mid-20s. The weak spots are margins that management itself caps at 12.5–13% (and that fell to 11.4% in Q1), heavy dependence on imported electronics priced in dollars, and a big multi-plant capex plus a demerger happening at the same time. At ~35x earnings the price needs the indexation recovery to lift margins back toward 12.5% by Q3 and P3L to scale without further margin erosion. An unexplained auditor exit in 2025 is a governance point to keep watching; a clean demerger with a credible technology partner would be the main upside trigger.
