# Fiem Industries (FIEMIND) — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. Figures: Screener (Fiem's "consolidated" page; subsidiaries are tiny) unless tagged. Both calls read in full. The stored Q4 FY26 transcript is the official BSE filing (matches official). The stored Q1 FY27 transcript (13 Aug 2026) is a **tidied copy (figures match; gaps)** of the official BSE transcript filed 18 Aug 2026: all financial figures, guidance, capex and cost numbers match; gaps are names — it writes Ather's new model as "Monarch" (official text: "Konarc"), "Norton Menex" (official: "Norton Manx"), "Acer" for Ather, and "more than 1,000, more than 100 models" (official: "more than 100 models"). The stored analysis's claim that management "cited unexpected procedural delays" for the 4-wheeler slip is wrong: "procedural delays" was the analyst's phrase and management replied "There are no procedural delays."

## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** 2-wheeler lamps (LED and conventional), rear-view mirrors, plastic moulded parts, sheet-metal parts, bank-angle sensors (Toyodenso technology), canisters (Aisan tie-up); tiny LED luminaire/railway display business [IP Q1FY27 p.2, 6].
- **Product mix Q1 FY27 (automotive revenue):** LED lighting 48.2%, conventional lighting 28.8%, rear-view mirrors 10.7%, plastic parts 8.8%, others 3.5%; LED = 63% of lighting (flat in a 61–64% band for ~7 quarters) [IP Q1FY27 p.4; Q1 call].
- **FY26 revenue by product (₹ cr):** lighting 2,095, mirrors 298, plastic parts 254, others 144 [AR FY26 MD&A p.75].
- **Vehicle split:** 2W 97.7%, 4W 2.3% (Q1 FY27); FY26 2W 97.15%, 4W 2.85% [IP Q1FY27 p.4; IP p.3].
- **Channel:** domestic OEMs 94.0%, domestic replacement 4.6%, exports only 1.4% (Q1 FY27); FY26 exports 1.85% [IP Q1FY27 p.4].
- **Customers (share of sales, Q1 FY27):** TVS ~33.7% and Honda ~25.2% are the two largest (together ~59%); then Yamaha, Suzuki, Royal Enfield, Hero (each ~5–14%) [IP Q1FY27 p.33]. Hero is the fastest-growing account (Vida EV; all Vida lamps for Hero's new Andhra Pradesh plant from Hosur from Q2) [Q1 call].
- **EV wins:** supplies to TVS iQube, Honda Activa EV, Ather's new model (sole supplier on that model; earlier Ather models were with a competitor), River RX02, Royal Enfield Flying Flea, Hero Vida [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Capacity:** nine plants (Haryana, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Himachal, Rajasthan, Gujarat); utilisation ~75% [Q4 call; IP].
- **4W business:** M&M (second then first source on some parts), Force Motors, Tata developing; ~2.5% of sales; Mercedes prototype samples under 1.5–2 years of validation before any RFQ [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr: revenue / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 659 / 89 / 14% / 58; Sep-25 715 / 99 / 14% / 64; Dec-25 690 / 98 / 14% / 63; Mar-26 751 / 110 / 15% / 71; Jun-26 775 / 104 / 13% / 65 [Screener]. Company-reported Q1 FY27 net sales ₹769.9 cr (+18.6%), EBITDA ₹104.06 cr (13.5%), PAT ₹65.2 cr (+16.3%) [Q1 call].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹2,029 cr, op. profit ₹269 cr (13%), PAT ₹166 cr; FY25 ₹2,423 / ₹322 (13%) / ₹205 cr; FY26 ₹2,816 / ₹396 (14%) / ₹256 cr; TTM ₹2,932 / ₹410 / ₹263 cr [Screener]. Company FY26: net sales ₹2,790.65 cr (+16%), EBITDA ₹393.3 cr (14.09%), PAT ₹253.9 cr (+24%) [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- **Returns:** ROCE 29%, ROE 23% (FY26); sales CAGR 5 yrs 18%, profit CAGR 5 yrs 39% [Screener].
- **Unit economics:** an LED lamp set is 3–4x the value of a conventional one; margins at product level "pretty much similar", so rising LED share lifts revenue, not margin % [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 costs:** raw material +80 bps, total cost +120–130 bps q/q; import content ~20%; Haryana minimum wage up ~35%, employee cost to stay at Q1 run-rate [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Standing guidance:** revenue growth 15–20% a year and EBITDA margin ~14% ("14% EBITDA plus" on Q4 call) — **kept** on Q1 call, and said to apply to "this year and the years to come" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **4W target (Q4 call, 1 Jun 2026):** "₹100–150 crores for the current year" and ₹200–250 cr for FY28 [Q4 call]. **Q1 call (13 Aug): cut** — 4W to stay ~2.5% of revenue in FY27 ("slightly lower than what we had indicated"), the target "pushed out by 2 quarters" into FY28; 4W is now "a 10-year lens" business [Q1 call]. At ~2.5% of ~₹3,300 cr, FY27 4W revenue would be ~₹80 cr, below the ₹100–150 cr promised just ten weeks earlier.
- **RFQ claim walked back within the Q4 call:** management first said 70% of ~₹700 cr of 4W RFQs had been "converted into a business", then clarified these are RFQs "going into the next phase… not converting into an order" [Q4 call].
- **Capex:** Q4 call "₹200-odd crores over the next 2 years"; Q1 call "around ₹100-odd crores" for FY27, ₹41.15 cr spent in Q1 — consistent [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **LED share:** Q4 call "progressively keep moving up"; Q1 call "around 70% over the next 24–30 months" — but it has been stuck at 61–64% [Q1 call].
- **Q1 delivery vs promises:** revenue +18.6% (in the 15–20% band, below 2W industry production +22.8%); margin 13.5% vs ~14% guide (Q1 seasonally weak, cost lag); 4W missed [Q1 call; Screener].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹108.3 cr (debottlenecking); FY27 ~₹100 cr, mostly Hosur (Kelamangalam and Thally Road plants) for TVS/Hero EV demand, some at Tapukara [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Capital commitments ₹41.6 cr at Mar-26 (₹7.1 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 note 34].
- No greenfield plant planned; "under no scenario will we have a situation where there is demand and we do not have the capacity" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Asset turns:** FY26 revenue ₹2,816 cr on fixed assets ₹604 cr ≈ 4.7x [Screener] — high for an auto part maker.
- New EMI/EMC electronics test lab at Gurugram (commissioned FY26) to develop light-control modules and hands-off-detection sensors; both at proof-of-concept stage with no orders yet [AR FY26 MD&A p.74; Q1 call]. The hub-motor project with Gogoro has been stopped [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Effectively debt-free: cash ₹276 cr on the Q4 call ("roughly ₹280 cr" per analyst on Q1 call); Screener borrowings ₹66 cr (mostly lease liabilities) [Q4 call; Screener].
- CFO ₹233 cr (FY25) → ₹161 cr (FY26); FCF ₹132 cr → ₹33 cr [Screener]. Reason: Fiem stopped discounting TVS and another major customer's bills — the "vendor discounting scheme" contingent liability fell from ₹85.0 cr to ₹0.9 cr — so receivables rose (debtor days 37 → 50) [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 33; Screener]. Underlying cash generation is therefore about ₹85 cr better than the reported CFO.
- Working capital days rose from 29 to 47 [Screener].
- Dividend ₹40/share for FY26 (₹105.3 cr, payout ~41%), up from ₹30 [AR FY26 note 52; Screener].
- Insurance: ₹53.1 cr insurance claim recoverable at Mar-26 (₹5.1 cr a year earlier). Rai plant fire (June 2023): ₹50 cr interim received, final assessment still pending nearly three years later. Tapukara fire (23 Aug 2025): ₹48.7 cr of assets booked as recoverable; ₹82.3 cr claim filed 30 Jan 2026 [AR FY26 note 51; balance sheet].
- No credit rating: the company has no rated debt [AR FY26 corporate governance].

## Moat evidence
- Long-standing top-2 2W lighting position with TVS and Honda; wallet share "remains similar" across the top 4 customers; >100 models in development with customers [Q1 call]. ICRA (Lumax rationale) names Fiem as the key 2W lighting competitor [ICRA Lumax Apr-26].
- Cost position: integrated mirrors, plastics and sheet-metal plus in-house tooling; FY26 EBITDA margin 14.1%, higher than larger lighting peers such as Lumax (~10%) [AR FY26; Screener].
- Pass-through: raw-material changes are passed to OEMs "both ways… with a lag of a quarter here and there" [Q4 call] — margin has stayed 13–15% for 11 quarters [Screener].
- Technology: design centres in Italy and Japan, technical tie-ups with Ichikoh (lighting, 2005), Toyodenso (sensors), Aisan (canisters) [IP Q1FY27 p.10].
- **Limits:** analyst mapping of Fiem's sales to customers' output suggests share loss at HMSI; management declined to restore annual wallet-share disclosure [Q1 call]. Honda revenue grew only ~5% in FY26, below HMSI volumes [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration:** TVS + Honda ≈ 59% of sales; top six customers >85% [IP Q1FY27 p.33].
- **Promoter selling:** promoters sold 4.45% (11.7 lakh shares) in the open market on 5 and 17 Sep 2025; holding fell from 58.96% to 54.51% [Screener; News: ScanX, Sep 2025].
- **Management change:** CEO Vineet Sahni (hired ~2023 to build 4W) left around the Q4 results; 4W now run by MD Rahul Jain and JMD Rajesh Sharma; no replacement CEO sought. The 4W target was cut one quarter later [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Two plant fires in 26 months** (Rai 2023, Tapukara 2025); insurance recovery of the first still unsettled; auditor made the Tapukara claim a key audit matter because the recoverable amount "involves significant management judgment" [AR FY26 auditor's report KAM 1; note 51].
- **Related parties:** lease rent to MD Rahul Jain and JMD Aanchal Jain ₹2.5 cr a year; brand royalty ₹0.5 cr a year to Fiem Auto & Electrical Industries (promoter-controlled); design fees to Italian subsidiary ₹13.8 cr and R&D fee to Japan subsidiary ₹2.1 cr; KMP pay ₹17.1 cr (incl. ₹3.6 cr to the departed CEO); CSR to promoter-controlled Fiem Foundation ₹4.6 cr [AR FY26 note 41]. Small relative to PAT but worth noting.
- **Contingent liabilities:** small — GST disputes ₹1.2 cr [AR FY26 note 33]. Auditor opinion unmodified; KAMs are the insurance claim and revenue recognition.
- **Stagnant LED mix** (61–64% for seven quarters) and exports only ~1.5% — the growth levers beyond the 2W cycle are not yet visible in numbers [Q1 call; IP].

## What changed between the two calls
- The 4W target went from "₹100–150 cr for the current year" to "slightly lower" / pushed to FY28 in ten weeks; tone shifted from "significant traction" to "it is never going to be easy" and "a 10-year lens".
- Margin dipped to 13.5% with a new 35% Haryana wage increase and rupee/input inflation; guidance of ~14% kept.
- New talking points: EV wins (Ather, River, Royal Enfield, Hero Vida AP plant), TVS capacity expansion to 8.3 mn units, Hosur expansion; Gogoro hub-motor project confirmed dead.
- Cash of ~₹280 cr: management says it is kept for organic/inorganic or electronics opportunities, and debt could be raised later.

## Best management quotes
- "What we expected to fully materialize in FY27 will spill over into FY28" (on 4W) [Q1 call]
- "There are no procedural delays… It is an ongoing journey for us that is taking longer than we had expected." [Q1 call]
- "Our guidance remains the same. The top line, we guide around 15% to 20%… and an EBITDA margin of around 14%." [Q1 call]

## My read
The core 2-wheeler lamp business is excellent and steady: 18% sales CAGR over five years, 13–15% EBITDA margin for three years, ROCE near 29%, no debt and a ~41% payout. But the diversification story has not delivered — 4W is still ~2.5% of sales after three years, the 4W target was cut ten weeks after being given, the CEO who was building it left, and LED share has stalled. So the price should be paid for a 15–20% 2W grower, not for a 4W optionality. The view improves if LED mix moves toward 70% and 4W orders (M&M first-source, Mercedes RFQ) turn into named SOPs; it worsens on further HMSI share loss, margin below 13.5% for two quarters, or more promoter selling.
