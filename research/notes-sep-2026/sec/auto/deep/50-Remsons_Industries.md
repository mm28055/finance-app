## Business and segments
- India's largest and one of the earliest control-cable makers (brake, clutch, throttle, hood, parking-brake cables, push-pull cables), plus gear shifters, pedal boxes, winches, jacks and two-wheeler combi-brake mechanisms [Q3 call; IP Q1FY27 slides 11, 34–37].
- "Remsons 2.0" adds: sensors (Uni Automation, Pune — majority stake 2024), premium lighting (BEE Lighting UK, 51% in 2024 — Aston Martin, Lamborghini, JLR, Ford GT), tyre-mobility kits (Aircom JV, 26%), electronics JV (Daiichi Remsons), rail brake parts and defence sub-assemblies (Remsons Edge Tech; new 30,000 sq ft Chakan plant) [IP Q1FY27 slides 24–33; AR FY26 AOC-1].
- Plants: Gurugram, Pardi (Gujarat), Daman, Pune/Shirwal/Chakan and two in the UK (Stourport, Redditch — ex-Magal Cables, bought 2020) [IP Q1FY27 slide 10].
- Customers: Hero MotoCorp, Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, Force Motors, M&M; abroad Ford, JLR, Ineos, Stanley Black & Decker; Stellantis from FY27 [ICRA Jul-26; IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 mix (slide values; labels partly garbled): India ~62% / rest of world ~38%; OEM 94% / aftermarket 6%; by vehicle roughly passenger cars ~40%, 2W/3W ~35%, CV ~19%, farm ~5% [IP Q1FY27 slide 10]. Exports were 33% of 9M FY26 sales [Q3 call].
- No segment reporting beyond geography.

## Guidance history
- **Q3 FY26 call (13 Feb 2026, the latest call):** FY27 consolidated revenue ₹520–570 cr excluding acquisitions; EBITDA margin rising "steadily" to 11–12% next year and 13–14% in 2–3 years; FY27 capex "₹20 crore plus"; Stellantis (₹300 cr+, 7 years, North America) to start in FY27 with ₹15–20 cr in the first half-year and a ₹40–50 cr annual run-rate after; railway revenue ₹25–35 cr "this year", ~₹150 cr in 3–4 years; order book at least ₹500 cr with ₹800–900 cr pipeline; ₹900–1,000 cr revenue by FY29–FY30 (₹800–850 cr organic, ₹150–200 cr from acquisitions) on ~₹100 cr of investment; ₹50–70 cr set aside for an acquisition; debt/equity to stay 0.6–0.8x [Q3 call, official].

## Moat evidence
- Scale in control cables; 15–30 year OEM relationships with limited churn [ICRA Jul-26].
- Export wins against Chinese suppliers: Stellantis North America ₹300 cr+/7 years; global CV OEM pedal-box ₹160 cr/10 years; CV OEM gear-shifter ₹60 cr/5 years; hood-rod ₹30 cr/5 years; BEE Lighting ₹12 cr for a German OEM [IP Q1FY27 slide 6; Q3 call]. Management says China holds "a very big share" of control-cable exports to the US/Mexico [Q3 call].
- Weak pricing power: ICRA cites "low bargaining power and intense competition" limiting pass-through of labour and overheads; the replacement market is largely unorganised [ICRA Jul-26].
- Margin history is thin: EBITDA 6% in FY19 to ~10–11% now; ROCE 14–15% [IP Q1FY27 slide 8; Screener].

## Risks and red flags
- **Flood at Pardi (Valsad) plant:** operations disrupted from 23 Jul 2026; fully restored only by 18 Sep 2026; loss of output and possible asset/inventory damage in Q2 FY27 (insurance position not disclosed) [BSE filing 18-Sep-26, which refers to the 23-Jul-26 intimation].
- **Regulatory product risk:** if the long-delayed two-wheeler ABS mandate is enforced, ~8% of consolidated revenue (combi-brake mechanisms/cables) is at risk [Q3 call].
- **Acquisition-heavy strategy:** many small, unrelated bets (lighting, sensors, tyre kits, rail, defence, Brazil licence); goodwill valuation in a subsidiary is an auditor key audit matter; the Astro Motors stake purchase was cancelled and management declined to explain [AR FY26 auditor's report (Kanu Doshi Associates LLP); Q3 call].
- **Promoter holding fell** from 65.53% (Mar-26) to 62.83% (Jun-26) with no change in share count — a sale or transfer I could not trace to a disclosure [Screener shareholding].
- **Contingent liabilities** ₹7.8 cr (₹7.2 cr) [AR FY26 consolidated note].
- **Disclosure quality:** the FY26 annual report text is not machine-readable (custom font encoding), so related-party amounts could not be verified; no earnings calls since February 2026 [AR FY26; Screener concall list].
- **UK unit:** Magal/Remsons Automotive UK "stable, no high growth" in FY27 after lost EV programmes; JLR cyber-attack shut a key customer for months [Q3 call].

## My read
Remsons is a small, family-run cable maker trying to become a diversified supplier through a string of small acquisitions; sales have grown ~24% a year, but margins are stuck near 9–11% and Q1 FY27 profit fell. The export wins (Stellantis, a global CV pedal-box order) are real evidence of competitiveness against China, and FY26 cash flow was the best in years. At a P/E in the low 20s the price assumes the ₹520–570 cr FY27 revenue and a climb toward 12% margins; the flood makes FY27 harder, and Q1 was already behind. The view would improve with Stellantis revenue showing up and margins back above 11%; it would worsen with more unrelated acquisitions, further promoter selling or an ABS mandate.
