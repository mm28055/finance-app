# Sterling Tools (NSE: STERTOOLS) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Price ₹239, market cap ₹870 cr, P/E 45.2, ROCE 7.2%, ROE 3.1%, 52-week range ₹155–335 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- India's second-largest automotive fastener maker (high-tensile cold-forged bolts: engine, axle, wheel, special fasteners); plants at Faridabad, Prithla, Bengaluru plus a wire-drawing unit; 18 warehouses, 100+ aftermarket distributors [IP Q1FY27 slides 5–7; ICRA Nov-25].
- Standalone fastener mix FY26: OEM 88%, retail 11%, exports 1%; by vehicle roughly CV 25%, 2W 26%, PV ~30%, farm/off-road ~15–17% (slide layout garbled; ICRA FY25: CV 24%, PV 27%, 2W 26%, tractor 10%) [IP Q1FY27 slide 20; ICRA Nov-25]. Largest customer ~20%, top five ~61% [ICRA Nov-25].
- Claimed share: 25–30% of domestic automotive fastener buying [Q4 call, official].
- EV subsidiaries: **Sterling E-Mobility (SEM)** — motor controllers, integrated motor+controller, on-board/off-board chargers, DC-DC converters, magnet-free motors (tech partners Jiangsu Gtake, Landworld, Advanced Electric Machines UK); **Sterling Tech-Mobility (STML)** — high-voltage DC contactors and relays (Kunshan GLVAC), fully automated Bengaluru plant; plus ADAS (Minieye) and two-wheeler rider-assist (Nanjing Haohang) tie-ups [IP Q1FY27 slides 5–9].
- SEM+STML were 6.8% of consolidated income in Q1 FY27 [IP Q1FY27 slide 19]; SEM was 37% (₹380 cr) in FY25 before its anchor customer insourced [ICRA Nov-25; Q1 call].
- Utilisation: fasteners 90–95% in Q1 FY27 ("fully utilised") [Q1 call, official]. SEM capacity >₹300 cr revenue, STML ~₹140 cr — together ~₹440 cr of capacity vs ~₹60 cr annualised current revenue [Q1 call, official; IP Q1FY27].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (18 May 2026):** fastener capex ₹75 cr in FY27, commercialised by Q4 FY27; existing capacity supports ~₹800 cr revenue, rising to ₹900–1,000 cr with this and next year's capex; asset turn 1.8–2x; long-term fastener EBITDA "15% plus"; Q1 to face "special pressure"; SEM not profitable in FY27, profitable in FY28; OBC/DC-DC lines by end-Q2 FY27, supplies from Q3; STML serial production July–August 2026; EV timeline "shifted by nearly 3–5 years", pushing the 50% non-fastener revenue target back [Q4 call, official].
- **Q1 FY27 call (6 Aug 2026):** capex raised to ~₹80 cr (the filed transcript corrects a spoken "₹88 crore" to ₹80 cr), plus ₹25–30 cr next year to reach ₹1,000 cr capacity; full-year fastener margins to hold despite steel; EV revenue +20–30% in FY27 and +30–40% in FY28; SEM break-even at ~₹175 cr revenue and STML at ~₹70 cr, both in FY28; OBC/multi-function lines commissioned by end-Q2, meaningful revenue in FY28 after ~4 months of road trials [Q1 call, official; IP Q1FY27 slide 18].
- Delivery: the feared Q1 margin squeeze did not happen (15.4% standalone vs 15.3% FY26) — **beat**. Subsidiary losses unchanged (~₹10 cr a quarter). STML supplies on track for July–August; OBC lines on track.
- Verdict: fastener guidance **kept/raised** (capex up, growth strong); EV break-even **kept** at FY28 but the larger diversification target was **pushed back** in May.

## Capex and capacity
- Fasteners: ~₹80 cr FY27 brownfield (Bengaluru and NCR plants), mostly H2, benefits from Q4 FY27; another ₹25–30 cr in FY28 for ₹1,000 cr revenue capacity [Q1 call, official].
- EV: cash invested so far ~₹50 cr in STML and ~₹48–49 cr in SEM [Q1 call, official].
- Asset turns: standalone net fixed-asset turnover 2.9x (FY26); management uses 1.8–2x for new capex [IP Q1FY27 slide 27; Q4 call].
- Capital commitments ₹8.2 cr at Mar-26 (₹16.3 cr Mar-25) [AR FY26 Note 41]. Customs duty deferred under the MOOWR scheme on imported machines: ₹7.9 cr contingent [AR FY26 Note 41].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹147 cr (consolidated, Mar-26) vs ₹155 cr; the standalone fastener business is net debt-free [Screener; Q4 call]. ICRA put consolidated cash and liquid investments at ₹160 cr (Mar-25) and net cash ₹5.8 cr [ICRA Nov-25].
- Cash flow weakened: CFO ₹56 cr in FY26 (₹124 cr FY25); FCF ₹5 cr [Screener]. Inventory days rose to 172 (124) and debtor days to 44 (23) — the EV subsidiary's stock and receivables [Screener].
- Standalone CFO ₹83.3 cr in FY26 [Q4 call].
- Dividend payout 34% of consolidated FY26 profit [Screener].
- Rating: ICRA AA-/Stable, A1+ (Nov-2025) — outlook cut from Positive because SEM's scale collapsed after Ola Electric insourced motor controllers [ICRA Nov-25].

## Moat evidence
- Second-largest domestic fastener maker; healthy share of business with Maruti, HMSI, Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, M&M; new entries with Tata PV and Hyundai [ICRA Nov-25; Q4 call].
- Pricing: steel is a contractual pass-through (1–4 month lag); no annual price-down contracts; management is now asking OEMs to also compensate for non-steel inflation (chemicals, tungsten, wages) and reports positive responses [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Growth ahead of industry: standalone +23.7% in Q1 FY27 vs PV sales +25.9% and 2W +20.3% (SIAM) — in line to slightly ahead; FY26 +11.4% vs industry ~10% [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Business wins FY26: ~₹64 cr of new fastener business [Q4 call].
- EV side is weak on moat: the anchor customer (Ola, per ICRA) insourced, wiping out ~₹280 cr of revenue in a year; the products rely on Chinese technology partners [ICRA Nov-25; Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **EV losses and write-offs:** ~₹10 cr quarterly loss; ₹21 cr bad-debt provision in Q4 FY26 on a large EV customer, which management still hopes to recover [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Management pay vs profit:** key management compensation ₹18.3 cr in FY26 (₹22.2 cr FY25), incl. ₹5.8 cr share-based; director Jaideep Wadhwa (heads SEM) ₹7.6 cr (₹12.5 cr in FY25) — against consolidated PAT ₹29.3 cr [AR FY26 Note 43].
- **Related parties:** rent ₹1.3 cr to promoter entity Sterling Technologies; CSR ₹1.1 cr to the promoter family foundation; small legal fees to Swarup & Company, the firm of independent director Shailendra Swarup [AR FY26 Note 43].
- **Contingent liabilities** small: service tax ₹1.1 cr plus interest ₹0.9 cr, income-tax appeals ~₹6 cr across four years, MOOWR customs ~₹7.9 cr [AR FY26 Note 41].
- **Auditor:** Walker Chandiok & Co (Grant Thornton); unqualified; key audit matter on revenue recognition (discounts/variable consideration) [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Concentration/cyclicality:** ~99% domestic; top five customers ~61% of standalone sales [ICRA Nov-25].
- **China-linked technology:** EV, ADAS and rider-assist products all rely on Chinese licensors — geopolitical and localisation risk [IP Q1FY27 slide 6].
- **Management churn:** CFO Pankaj Gupta left Dec-2025; two company secretaries in FY26; Anish Agarwal (promoter family) now Director & CFO [AR FY26 Note 43].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: May was defensive on EV ("timeline shifted by 3–5 years"); August was confident, with numeric EV growth (20–30%, 30–40%) and break-even revenue levels for the first time.
- Fasteners: from "Q1 will face special pressure" to a 15.4% Q1 margin and capex raised to ₹80 cr.
- New: 33 active SEM programmes (from 28), four new OEM confirmations, ADAS regulation for trucks/buses from Oct-2027 (~5 lakh vehicles a year).
- Dropped: the ₹21 cr bad-debt provision and the 50% non-fastener target were not discussed.

## My read
The fastener business is doing well — 24% growth, 15%+ margins, full plants and a capex step-up — and would on its own earn roughly ₹65–75 cr a year. The consolidated picture is dragged down by an EV arm that lost its anchor customer, burns ~₹40 cr a year, and carries ₹440 cr of capacity for ~₹60 cr of sales; management pay is also high relative to group profit. The price needs the EV losses to shrink visibly in H2 FY27 (OBC/multi-function supplies, STML contactors) and FY28 break-even to hold. The view would improve on two quarters of falling subsidiary losses; it would worsen on further write-offs or a fastener margin slip below 14% as steel costs catch up.
