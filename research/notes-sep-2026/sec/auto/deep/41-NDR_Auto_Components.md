# NDR Auto Components — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Screener code NDRAUTO (consolidated). Price ₹747, market cap ₹1,776 cr, 52-week range ₹605–1,082 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Makes seat frames (metal) and seat trims/covers for cars and two-wheelers; also body-in-white (BIW) sheet-metal parts for the Jimny. Reports a single segment ("manufacture of different seating systems") and a single geography, India [AR FY26 note e, Segment Information].
- It is mostly a Tier-2 supplier: it sells frames and trims to seat assemblers, above all group associate **Bharat Seats** (BSL), which supplies finished seats to Maruti Suzuki; and to Toyota Boshoku for Toyota models (Urban Cruiser, Hyryder) [ICRA Jul-25].
- Customer concentration is extreme. Sales to Bharat Seats were ₹486.6 cr in FY26 (₹388.4 cr in FY25), about 59% of ₹822.5 cr revenue from operations [AR FY26 related-party note d(ii)]. Two customers together gave ₹729.7 cr, about 89% of revenue [AR FY26 segment note]. ICRA estimates ~70% of revenue ends at Maruti and 85–90% at Maruti plus Toyota; two-wheelers (Suzuki Motorcycle) were 5.5% of FY25 revenue [ICRA Jul-25].
- Share of Maruti: company says it supplies ~30% of Maruti's seat frame/trim requirement [IP Q1FY27 p.14]; ICRA puts it at 30–35% [ICRA Jul-25]. It supplies 100% of the new Brezza, as on the old model [Q1 call].
- Plants: Gurgaon (trim), Pathredi (sheet metal), Bangalore and Surendranagar, Gujarat (frame and trim); new NDR Auto South plant at Anantapur (0.17 mn pieces a year) inaugurated, sales from Q2 FY27 [IP Q1FY27 p.7]. Unused land: 26 acres at Aurangabad and 9 acres at Kharkhoda [Q4 call].
- Group structure: 28.66% of listed Bharat Seats (associate); 50% JVs Toyota Boshoku Relan India and Toyo Sharda India; 50:50 JV NDR Hayashi Automotive (with Hayashi Telempu, Japan) for sunshades, NVH carpets and ambient lighting; 100% subsidiaries NDR Auto Components Safety Systems (seat-insert fabric, from May 2025) and NDR Auto Components South (from Nov 2025) [IP Q1FY27 p.21; AR FY26 note d(i)].
- EV content: management says EV seats give "a slight increase" in revenue per car with similar margins and ROCE [Q1 call]. It supplies the Maruti e-Vitara (removed from order book once production started) [Q4 call]. No exports.

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales: Jun-25 185 → Sep-25 200 → Dec-25 208 → Mar-26 229 → Jun-26 221. Operating profit 20 → 22 → 23 → 26 → 25; operating margin 11%, 11%, 11%, 12%, 11%. Net profit 14 → 15 → 15 → 18 → 16 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 as reported: revenue from operations ₹221.45 cr (+19.6% YoY), company "EBITDA" ₹26.44 cr (11.88% of total income), PBT ₹21.10 cr (vs ₹17.43 cr), PAT ₹16.40 cr (vs ₹13.60 cr, +21%) [IP Q1FY27 p.3, p.6]. Note: the company's EBITDA adds other income (₹1.10 cr); excluding it, operating profit is about ₹25 cr, 11.4% [IP Q1FY27 p.6; Screener].
- Sales fell ~3% quarter-on-quarter from the Q4 peak (₹229.07 cr) — seasonal plus model mix; an analyst noted growth is now roughly in line with Maruti's production, where it used to be well ahead [Q1 call].
- Annual trend: sales FY24 602 → FY25 713 → FY26 823 (+18%, +15%); operating profit 56 → 73 → 91; margin 9% → 10% → 11%; net profit 39 → 53 → 62; EPS ₹16.3 → ₹22.4 → ₹26.0 [Screener]. Six-year story: sales ₹114 cr (FY21) to ₹823 cr (FY26).
- Returns: ROCE 19% (FY24), 23% (FY25), 22% (FY26), ROE 18.9% [Screener]. Management quotes ROCE of 35–36% "excluding non-productive land and surplus cash" (35.34% at Jun-26) and 28.4% unadjusted [IP Q1FY27 p.5].
- Other income is a meaningful ~₹13 cr a year (FY26), about 16% of pre-tax profit [Screener].
- Share of associate/JV profit Q1 FY27: +₹2.36 cr from Bharat Seats (associate), −₹0.58 cr from JVs [IP Q1FY27 p.6; Q1 call].
- Unit economics: not disclosed. Company says it makes "more than a million seats" a year [Q1 call]; that implies very roughly ₹8,000 of NDR revenue per seat set — my estimate, not a company figure.

## Guidance history
- Long-term target (repeated both calls): revenue ₹3,000 cr by FY30 for NDR, ROCE ~35%+, "gradually improving margin" [IP Q1FY27 p.11]. Bharat Seats target raised to "about ₹3,500 cr" by FY30 on the Q4 call and repeated on Q1 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- How the ₹3,000 cr is built: Q4 — current ~₹830–850 cr plus ₹650 cr order book ≈ ₹1,500 cr; "₹500 cr from existing customers and products"; "₹1,000 cr… give us more time" [Q4 call]. Q1 — "our goal is to do about ₹2,000 cr from our organic business. The ₹1,000 cr inorganic part is something we are still working on" [Q1 call]. So one-third of the target now depends on an acquisition not yet identified.
- Margins: Q4 — 11.5% (ex other income) is "sustainable"; do not expect higher yet because of start-up costs [Q4 call]. Q1 — "11% to 12%", commodities indexed [Q1 call]. Q1 delivered 11.4% (ex OI) — **kept**.
- FY27 revenue: no annual guidance; Q4 said "we should likely have the similar revenue growth" (FY26 was +15%) with eVitara full year and three projects starting [Q4 call]. Q1 grew 19.6% — **delivering**. Q1 said growth "should have a slight uptick" from here [Q1 call].
- Timelines: Hayashi sunshade plant slipped from April to June 2026 "because of some operational issues" [Q4 call]; it started in June [Q1 call] — **met the revised date**. Anantapur plant: SOP Jul-26 per plan, inaugurated, production "in 10–15 days" after the 11 Aug call [Q1 call] — **roughly on time**. Ambient lighting: Q4 said two small orders worth ₹10–20 cr combined; Q1 said they start in **2028** [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Aurangabad (Toyota): land bought for the Toyota expansion; Q1 conceded Toyota now targets calendar 2029, so little business there before FY30 — a **pushed-out** growth leg [Q1 call].
- EBITDA margin at ₹3,000 cr: management declined to give any [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Current project list totals ₹149.8 cr: seat insert fabric, Manesar, ₹21.89 cr, 6 mn pcs, Jan-27; seat trim & frames, Anantapur, ₹22.56 cr, Jul-26; Hayashi JV (ambient light, carpet, sunshades), Bengaluru, ₹80.49 cr project cost, sunshades Jun-26 / ambient lighting Jan-27; seat latch, Pathredi, ₹17.43 cr, 3 mn pcs, Jan-27; seat belt reminder, Gujarat, ₹7.43 cr, 1.5 mn pcs, Jan-27 [IP Q1FY27 p.7].
- Expected asset turn on the ₹150 cr: "approximately 4x" → about ₹600 cr of revenue at maturity [Q1 call]. Anantapur alone: ₹70–80 cr revenue potential "subject to how the model performs" [Q1 call].
- Hayashi JV: ₹80 cr is the approved project cost; only ~₹30 cr invested so far (₹10–15 cr each partner), to be added as business comes [Q4 call]. Break-even needs ₹100–150 cr of sales; target potential ₹200–300 cr "over five to six years"; losses "will continue for some quarters" [Q1 call]. ₹20 cr of NDR's existing sunshade business was transferred into the JV [Q4 call].
- Extra capex beyond these projects: "₹40–50 cr annually for the next couple of years" [Q1 call]; Q4 said ₹30–40 cr plus ₹40–50 cr for the new order book [Q4 call] — the wording is loose. FY26 cash capex was ₹73.4 cr (FY25 ₹48 cr) [AR FY26 cash flow; Q4 call].
- Funding: internal accruals; "ZERO debt company" [IP Q1FY27 p.3]. NDR South was first funded by (intra-group) debt, now converted to equity [Q1 call]. Capital commitments fell to ₹5.6 cr at Mar-26 from ₹33.5 cr [AR FY26 note 27].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt: no bank borrowings at Mar-26; Screener's "borrowings" of ₹62 cr (₹32 cr at Mar-25) are lease liabilities (including a lease from Bharat Seats of ₹6.3 cr) [Screener; AR FY26 balance sheet and note d]. ICRA: gearing 0.1x, total debt/OPBDITA 0.4x, interest cover 22.7x (FY25) [ICRA Jul-25].
- Cash: cash and equivalents fell to ₹15.1 cr at Mar-26 from ₹50.8 cr; other bank balances ₹8.2 cr (₹12.1 cr); a ₹17 cr loan to Bharat Seats was repaid in FY26 [AR FY26 cash flow and balance sheet]. ICRA had cash of ₹60–65 cr at Mar-25 [ICRA Jul-25]. Net cash has shrunk sharply because of capex and JV investment.
- Cash flow: operating cash flow ₹37 cr in FY26 vs ₹84 cr in FY25; free cash flow −₹36 cr (FY25 +₹36 cr); CFO/operating profit 61% [Screener]. Management blamed ₹12 cr paid for a Delhi office, ₹6 cr support to subsidiaries and faster payment of MSME vendors; "it should normalize" [Q4 call]. The first filed consolidated cash flow wrongly showed negative operating cash flow and was refiled on 13 May 2026 [Q4 call].
- Working capital: debtor days 45, inventory days 28, payable days 74, cash conversion cycle −1 day (FY26) vs −11 (FY25) [Screener]. Company ratios: debtor 43 days, inventory 18 days [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Dividend payout 15% of FY26 profit; yield 0.54% [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Share: ~30–35% of Maruti's seat frame and trim needs [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Jul-25]; the ₹200 cr jump in order book (₹450 cr → ₹650 cr in Q4) was entirely Maruti new models, and management said "there is someone else who has been losing some share" [Q4 call].
- Pricing: raw materials are indexed; "we are not facing any pressure from customers to reduce selling price" [Q1 call]. Year-end price variations are a key audit matter, i.e., material and judgment-based [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Cost: "probably the most backward integrated seat player", >1 mn seats a year [Q1 call]; gross margin gained 100 bp in Q4 partly from vendor volume discounts [Q4 call].
- Relationship moat: 35+ years with Maruti via Bharat Seats (a Maruti/Suzuki JV since 1988), JVs with Toyota Boshoku and Toyo Seat [IP Q1FY27 p.14, p.21]. Suzuki's global CEO attended Bharat Seats' 40th anniversary [Q1 call].
- Weakness: seat frames are metal fabrication; new non-seat products enter as "second or third player" [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Income-tax search emphasis of matter**: auditors (S.R. Batliboi) flag the uncertainty from an Income Tax search under Section 132 in FY24 at company premises, plants and some employees'/KMP residences; opinion not modified [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Demands of ₹5.02 cr (AY21–AY25) plus ₹1.42 cr penalty; CIT(A) reduced demand to ₹3.67 cr, AY23 (₹1.34 cr) pending [AR FY26 note 27(i)].
- **GST dispute**: classification of two-wheeler seats — ₹4.96 cr tax already deposited under protest plus an equal ₹4.96 cr penalty and interest; a further ₹1.55 cr notice from Tirupati (Nov-24 to Sep-25) [AR FY26 note 27(ii); CARO annexure]. Total contingent liabilities ₹12.3 cr (₹5.5 cr income tax, ₹6.8 cr GST) — small vs ₹358 cr net worth [AR FY26 note 27].
- **Related-party dependence**: ~59% of revenue is sales to Bharat Seats, a family-controlled associate; receivable from BSL ₹61.7 cr at Mar-26; NDR also leases premises from and buys capital work from BSL (₹2.8 cr) [AR FY26 note d]. Company states sales are at arm's length and benchmarked; the approval of material RPTs with BSL was put to shareholders [AR FY26].
- **Customer/OEM concentration**: ~70% Maruti end-exposure; Maruti's market share loss would hit NDR directly [ICRA Jul-25].
- **Diversification slow**: Toyota Aurangabad pushed to CY29; Kia/Hyundai Transys onboarded Dec-24 but no material orders disclosed; order book flat at ₹650 cr in Q1 [ICRA Jul-25; Q1 call].
- **Rising overheads**: other expenses up on marketing, prototypes and hiring; management calls it "the new normal" [Q1 call]. Employee cost rose 33% in FY26 [Q4 call].
- **Disclosure gaps**: no product-mix split despite two requests (promised "in the next presentation", not given in Q1 deck) [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]; order-book split not given; no margin guidance for FY30.
- **Stored Q1 transcript errors** listed below. Promoter holding steady at 73.13%, no pledge disclosed [Screener; AR FY26 CARO (ix)(f)].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from order-win excitement (Q4: record ₹650 cr book, share gains from a rival) to execution and cost absorption (Q1: flat book, "bidding for a lot more business") [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- The ₹3,000 cr FY30 target was explicitly split into ₹2,000 cr organic + ₹1,000 cr inorganic in Q1 [Q1 call].
- New facts in Q1: Hayashi break-even at ₹100–150 cr sales; Toyota Aurangabad delayed to CY29; ambient lighting only from 2028; NDR South debt converted to equity; ESOP grant to key staff approved [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.3].
- Margin line held at 11–12% despite commodity inflation — a positive surprise flagged by several analysts [Q1 call].

## My read
The core business is steadily improving: sales up 15–20% a year, margin up from 9% to 11–12%, no bank debt, and a stronger grip on Maruti's seat supply. But the growth path is narrower than the ₹3,000 cr headline suggests — management itself now says only ₹2,000 cr is organic, the Toyota leg has slipped to CY29, and the ₹650 cr order book is mostly Maruti and spreads over several years to 2030. The ₹150 cr of new projects (4x asset turn) should add roughly ₹500–600 cr of revenue by FY29–FY30 if orders follow, but the Hayashi JV will lose money for several quarters and free cash flow turned negative in FY26. At about 27x trailing earnings the price needs mid-to-high-teens earnings growth for several years; a real non-Maruti order (Toyota, Kia/Hyundai), a disclosed product mix, or a clean outcome to the tax search would raise my confidence; a Maruti share loss or a pricey acquisition would lower it.
