# Sona BLW Precision Forgings (Sona Comstar) — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Screener code SONACOMS (BSE 543300).

## Business and segments
- Four verticals: (1) Driveline — precision-forged differential gears, differential assemblies, steering bevel boxes, epicyclic gear trains, reduction gearboxes; (2) Motors — EV traction motors and controllers (2W/3W), suspension motors (a China-launched premium-car product), starter motors, BSG; (3) Sensors & software — Novelic (Serbia) mmWave radars, in-cabin and short-range exterior radar; (4) Railway — brakes, couplers, air springs, dampers, and new electric control panels and HVAC, bought from Escorts Kubota. A fifth vertical, Robotics & Physical AI, was launched in Jul 2026. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- Customers: global PV and EV OEMs (North America, Europe, China), Indian PV/CV/tractor OEMs, Indian e-2W/e-3W makers, Indian Railways. Management will not name customers. [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 mix: BEV revenue ₹436 cr (+107% YoY), 44% of automotive product revenue (39% in Q4 FY26). Eastern markets (India plus Asia) 59% of revenue (56% a year ago); India crossed 50% of FY26 revenue. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- New products matter: >35% of revenue now comes from products that did not exist seven years ago (19 products developed in-house), an annualised ₹1,800 cr business earning "over 230 crores" of profit; acquired businesses (Comstar, Novelic, railway; ₹2,750 cr invested) are ~40% of revenue. [Q1 call]
- Railway division: bought from Escorts Kubota as a going concern on a slump-sale basis for ₹1,643 cr (including licences valued at ₹855 cr), effective 1 Jun 2025. Management calls it "slightly margin-dilutive but return-accretive". [AR FY26 KAM; Q4 call]
- Capacity: about 1 million electric motors a year, described as "fungible" across customers. Management says demand is not the constraint; customers' supply chains are. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- No formal segment reporting in the results; the company gives product, geography and powertrain splits in presentations only.

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarters (sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 854 / 206 / 24% / 122; Sep-25 1,138 / 284 / 25% / 170; Dec-25 1,200 / 296 / 25% / 150; Mar-26 1,258 / 296 / 24% / 187; Jun-26 1,301 / 293 / 23% / 179. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 YoY: sales +52%, operating profit +42%, net profit +47% (the base quarter included exceptional costs). The railway business was consolidated from 1 Jun 2025, so the base quarter held only one month of it; part of the 52% is acquired, and management gave no organic growth figure. [Screener; AR FY26 KAM]
- Company-reported Q1 FY27: revenue ₹1,310 cr (+54%), EBITDA ₹303 cr (+49%, 23.1% margin, down 0.7 pts), PAT ₹181 cr (+45%, 13.6% margin). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Annual: FY24 sales 3,185 / OP 902 (28%) / PAT 518; FY25 3,546 / 967 (27%) / 600; FY26 4,449 / 1,081 (24%) / 629. TTM sales 4,897, PAT 686. [Screener]
- FY26 company view: revenue ₹4,475 cr (+26%), EBITDA ₹1,107 cr (+13%, 24.7%), PAT hit by a one-time ₹30 cr labour-code charge; adjusted PAT ₹670 cr (+11%). FY26 BEV revenue ₹1,154 cr, down 6% because of a weak Q1 FY26 (rare-earth magnet ban, US tariffs). [Q4 call]
- Margin drivers: traction motors and railway are lower-margin assembly businesses, so margin falls as they grow; Q4 FY26 margin also carried a 1.9-pt base effect from PLI income booked in Q4 FY25. [Q4 call]
- ROCE has fallen from 24% (FY24) to 14% (FY26) because of the ₹2,400 cr QIP in Sep 2024 that is still partly in cash, and the railway purchase. [Screener; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (30 Apr 2026): EBITDA margin band 23–25% (set earlier, after the railway deal; the pre-acquisition core band was 24–26%) — "we should be able to continue being in that band"; margin pressure from commodities, Haryana minimum-wage increase from 1 Apr 2026 and gas shortage expected "in the foreseeable future"; suspension motors to grow 3–4x in FY27 and could reach double-digit % of revenue; traction motors second fastest; railway new products (HVAC ₹2,000–2,500 cr market, electric panels ₹1,500 cr market) meaningful only from year three. No revenue guidance. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (23 Jul 2026): margin 23.1% — inside the band but at its bottom; "April was tough, May was tougher", June better; recovery expected "from Q2 onwards". New long-term goal: "another 10x company" over the next decade (Sona Comstar 2.0). [Q1 call]
- Track record: revenue far ahead of what management expected a year ago ("if you had asked me in June ... I would have thought you were being overly optimistic"); margin guidance kept but only just met; suspension motors confirmed as fastest-growing product. Guidance status: margin band kept; no numbers raised or cut. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹369 cr plus ₹110 cr for extra land; FCF ₹290 cr on management's definition (Screener shows ₹180 cr). [Q4 call; Screener]
- Denso partnership (signed 22–23 Jul 2026): JV-A for 4-wheelers and larger (high-voltage liquid-cooled inverters, motors, generators) — Denso 51%, management control, licenses its IP for a royalty. JV-B for 2/3-wheelers — Sona will move its existing 2W/3W traction motor and controller business into a subsidiary by slump sale and sell 49% to Denso at an enterprise value of ₹1,750 cr; Sona keeps 51% and control, and the JV pays Sona royalties. [IP Q1FY27; News: Autocar Professional/Energetica, 23 Jul 2026]
- Capex intensity for motors is low: ₹8–9 of revenue per ₹1 of capex in 2-wheelers, ₹11–12 in high-voltage systems. [Q1 call]
- Addressable market claimed for JV-A: Indian hybrid/EV PV and CV market of 2.3 million vehicles, about ₹24,000 cr in 2030 (S&P Global Mobility), "many times" that by 2035. SOP timelines not disclosed. [Q1 call]
- Robotics & Physical AI: three orders worth ₹600 cr added in Q1, taking this vertical's order book to ₹800 cr (3% of total); SOPs "1 is this quarter, 1 is next quarter, 1 within 15 months". [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and investments ₹1,269 cr at Mar-26 after paying ~₹1,800 cr for the railway business, land and the final Novelic payment; borrowings ₹442 cr (short-term borrowings of ₹226 cr added in FY26). Net cash about ₹830 cr. Management says net debt stayed negative in Q1. [Q4 call; Screener; Q1 call]
- Cash flow: CFO ₹659 cr in FY26 (₹775 cr FY25); CFO/operating profit 78% (98% FY25). [Screener]
- Working capital worsened with the railway business: debtor days 94 (73), inventory 107 days (82), cash conversion cycle 121 days (78). Q1 FY27 was "our best quarter from an operational cash flow perspective" on lower debtor days (no figure given). [Screener; Q1 call]
- Dividend payout 33%; about ₹200 cr paid in FY26. [Screener; Q4 call]
- The Denso stake sale should bring in cash (49% of a ₹1,750 cr enterprise value); timing and tax not disclosed.
- Rating: India Ratings action on 25 Jun 2026 (listed on Screener); the rationale page did not load, so not read.

## Moat evidence
- Net order book ₹24,000 cr (5.4x FY26 revenue): EV ₹15,400 cr (64%), non-EV auto ₹6,600 cr (28%), railway ₹1,200 cr (5%, 12-month POs), robotics ₹800 cr (3%). 69 EV programmes across 36 customers (20 fully ramped, 20 ramping, 29 not yet in production). [IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 wins: hybrid differential assembly from an existing North American OEM (₹640 cr, SOP H2 FY29); two hub-wheel traction motor programmes from a new Indian e-2W OEM (₹90 cr); ICE differential gears from a North American OEM (₹210 cr, SOP H2 FY28) and ₹40 cr of CV/OHV gear programmes. [IP Q1FY27]
- Q4 FY26: three European driveline orders in one quarter — the first European EV order in almost four years. [Q4 call]
- Technology validation: Denso will pay Sona royalties for 2W/3W technology; management believes it is the first Indian auto-part maker to earn royalty from a global Tier-1. [Q1 call]
- Rare-earth resilience: moved to light rare-earth magnets five quarters ago; traction-motor growth shows no magnet shortage. [Q1 call]
- Customer concentration falling: top 4 products were 86% of revenue in FY25; the same 86% now needs the top 8. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Promoter dispute: Rani Kapur (mother of late chairman Sunjay Kapur, died 12 Jun 2025) alleges fraud and forgery over the family trusts that control Aureus Investment, the 28% promoter. The RK Family Trust case is in the Delhi High Court; Priya Sachdev Kapur (Sunjay's widow, non-executive director since 2025) issued notice removing Rani Kapur as trustee from 25 Mar 2026. The company says Rani Kapur has held no shares since 2019. Risk: promoter-level litigation and board distraction, not an operating issue so far. [News: Outlook Business; Whalesbook 2026; AR FY26 governance report]
- Management pay: MD & Group CEO total pay ₹15.0 cr in FY26 vs ₹3.4 cr in FY25 — includes ₹6.0 cr share-plan perquisite, ₹5.5 cr commission (new: 1% of consolidated PAT, capped at 5% of net profit) and a 15% raise. A new 15 lakh-share performance plan (PSP-2025) was approved in Dec 2025. [AR FY26 remuneration table]
- Profit leakage from JV-B: Sona's fastest-growing product line (2W/3W traction motors) will be 49% owned by Denso, so almost half of its future profit goes to a minority holder. [News: Autocar Professional, Jul 2026]
- Margin trend: OPM down from 28% (FY24) to 23% (Q1 FY27) with mix shifting to lower-margin assembly businesses. Commodity pass-through lags. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Execution breadth: two JVs, railway integration, robotics and eVTOL R&D at the same time; the CEO himself said it takes "4-5 years for a new product to reach hundreds of crores". [Q4 call]
- Auditor (Walker Chandiok): unmodified; KAMs are railway business-combination accounting and impairment of intangibles; audit trail was not enabled at database level for the whole year. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Contingent liabilities are small: GST writ (Rule 96(10) export refund) ₹28 cr, income-tax/transfer-pricing ~₹11 cr, service tax negligible. [AR FY26 note 39]
- US tariffs: Section 232 auto-parts tariffs unchanged; company says US revenue and margins have not fallen. [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: from "anti-fragility" and cost inflation (Q4) to a 10-year "Sona Comstar 2.0" ambition with Denso and robotics (Q1).
- Numbers: margin from 24.4% to 23.1%; BEV share from 39% to 44%; order book ₹23,700 cr to ₹24,000 cr (EV share down from 70% to 64% as non-EV and robotics grew).
- New: Denso JVs, the 49% sale of the 2W/3W motor business, robotics order book ₹800 cr, first CV radar order.
- Dropped: European supplier bankruptcies and the railway HVAC/panel market sizes got little airtime.

## My read
Sona is growing fast (sales +52% in Q1) but part of the jump is the railway purchase; organic growth is healthy, led by suspension and traction motors, while margin is sliding toward the bottom of the 23–25% band as lower-margin assembly products grow. The order book (₹24,000 cr, 5.4x revenue) and new European and North American wins give unusual visibility, and the Denso tie-up validates the technology — but it also hands 49% of the 2W/3W motor profits to Denso. The stock price asks for sustained high growth for years; I would get more confident if Q2 FY27 margins recover above 24% as promised and the Denso cash comes in, and more cautious if margins fall below 23%, the promoter dispute spills into the board, or robotics spending grows without revenue.
