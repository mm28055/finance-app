# Gabriel India (GABRIEL) — deep research

Research date 27 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore. Screener = consolidated page, read from the raw HTML rows. Caution: after "Project Rise" (merger of Anand group businesses, effective 22 May 2026) the company restated past periods; Screener mixes restated (Jun-25, Mar-26, Jun-26) and old (Sep-25, Dec-25, FY24–FY26 annual) figures.

## Business and segments
- Flagship listed company of the Anand Group. Core: shock absorbers, struts and front forks (ride control) for 2-wheelers, cars, CVs and railways, plus aftermarket. [IP Q1FY27]
- Standalone Q1 FY27 sales split: 2W/3W 64% of sales (32% market share), passenger vehicles 21.5% (23% share), CVs and railways 12.5% (88% share, per company); electric 2W/3W share 57%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Project Rise (effective 22 May 2026) moved into Gabriel: the Anchemco automotive-fluids business (DEF, coolants, brake fluid — merged into standalone), 76% of Anand CY Myutec (synchroniser rings, aluminium forgings), 25.1% of Dana Anand India (axles, driveshafts; FY26 revenue ₹2,987 cr, EBITDA 16.1%, PAT ₹352 cr, exports 40%), and 49% of Henkel Anand India (body-in-white adhesives, NVH; FY26 revenue ₹956 cr, EBITDA 21.5%, PAT ₹142 cr). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Other units: Inalfa Gabriel Sunroof Systems (sunroofs; FY26 revenue ₹434 cr, EBITDA ₹65.4 cr, 15.1%; ~170,000 sunroofs sold in FY26), the suspension business bought from Marelli Motherson (MMAS; near break-even), SK Enmove Gabriel (lubricants, 49%, started sales), Jinhap Gabriel (auto fasteners, 51%, SOP Q3 FY27), Gabriel Europe Engineering Centre (R&D, Belgium). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Project Jupiter (board approval 21 July 2026): 28.99% of HL Mando Anand India (brakes, steering, some suspension; FY26 revenue ~₹5,886 cr, PAT ₹358 cr, net worth ₹1,924 cr) bought from promoter company Asia Investments Pvt Ltd for ₹2,231 cr — ₹1,881 cr in new Gabriel shares (1.44 cr shares at ₹1,305.89) plus ₹350 cr cash; and 30% less one share of HL Klemove India (ADAS/electronics; FY26 revenue ₹1,000 cr+, adjusted EBITDA ₹129 cr, unaudited) for US$98.44 mn in two tranches ($73.83 mn by 15 Sep 2026, $24.61 mn within 18 months). [Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Exports are small: ~₹36 cr in Q1 FY27 (~3% of standalone sales); target 10% via shock absorbers, solar-tracker dampers and e-bike forks. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (28 May 2026): FY27 standalone capex ₹160–190 cr; margin goal "closer to 10%" not changed by the war; Hero MotoCorp SOP in Q2 FY27; Jinhap SOP Q3 FY27; sunroof new-business EBITDA 12–14%; ₹50,000 cr Anand Group revenue target for 2030 with Gabriel as the vehicle; restructuring to finish by June. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: Rise completed (listing approval pending); revenue +15.5% but margins **fell** (standalone 8.4%, consolidated 8.7%) on commodity inflation and pass-through lag; sunroof lost 15,000–20,000 units to an operating problem at a Hyundai Mobis supplier that halted Creta output, said to be a timing effect only; capex ₹32.3 cr in the quarter. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- New in Q1: Project Jupiter; debt to rise to ~₹800–1,000 cr (shareholder approval sought for ₹1,600 cr limit); management said debt-to-equity "should be 1:1" after closing. CRISIL instead expects ₹1,000–1,200 cr of debt with adjusted gearing below 0.3x because net worth rises to ~₹4,000 cr. [Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Klemove: content per vehicle ₹20,000–70,000; orders with Mahindra and Tata at ₹50,000–60,000 per vehicle set. HL Mando: ~40% of business now from non-Korean OEMs; exports 7–8%. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Standalone capex ₹190 cr in FY26; FY27 plan ₹160–190 cr for Hosur, Khandsa (Gurugram) and Sanand plant expansions and technology. CRISIL models ₹200–230 cr a year group capex. [Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Fixed-asset turnover ~6.8x (standalone), so capex is light; growth capital is now going into acquisitions rather than plants. [IP Q1FY27]
- Acquisition outlay: ₹350 cr cash (Mando) + ~₹850 cr (US$98.44 mn, Klemove), funded by new NCDs (CRISIL AA+ on ₹1,000 cr) and internal cash. [Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Pre-deal: consolidated borrowings ₹148 cr at Mar-26 (₹69 cr a year before), including ₹84.4 cr of Anchemco debt that came with the merger; standalone liquidity ₹253.6 cr at Jun-26 (₹297.4 cr at Mar-26). [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- Cash from operations ₹345 cr in FY26 (97% of operating profit); free cash flow ₹108 cr. Cash conversion cycle only 18 days — working capital is light. [Screener]
- Dividend ₹5 per share for FY26 (₹4.7 in FY25), ~28% payout. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Rating: CRISIL AA+/Stable on ₹170 cr bank lines, and assigned to ₹1,000 cr NCDs (27 Aug 2026). CRISIL expects interest cover above 12x and debt/EBITDA below 1.9x. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- Associates upstream ~45% of their profit as dividends — important because Mando and Klemove will be equity-accounted (profit share, not revenue). [CRISIL Aug-26]

## Moat evidence
- Share: ~32% of 2W/3W shock absorbers, ~23% of PV, dominant in CV/rail dampers; 57% of electric 2W/3W. [IP Q1FY27]
- 30-year partnership with HL Mando (since 1996) and technology licences (Yamaha, KONI, Inalfa, Dana, Henkel, CY Myutec, SK Enmove, Jinos) give access to technology Gabriel could not build alone. [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- New 2W wins: Hero MotoCorp (first programme SOP Q2 FY27), LOIs from TVS, Suzuki, Honda and Mahindra 2W; semi-active suspension development with two 2W customers. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Aftermarket: ₹487 cr in FY26 (8% CAGR), ~30,000 retailers, 829 business partners. [IP Q1FY27]
- Weakness: PV share is model-dependent (lost share in Q1 as customers' SUVs underperformed). [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party acquisitions from the promoter:** both Rise and Jupiter buy Anand-group assets from promoter entities, paid largely in new Gabriel shares. Promoter stake rose from 55.0% to 63.55% (Jun-26) and 66.29% (Sep-26); share count rose from ~14.4 cr to ~17.7 cr. The Mando price (₹2,231 cr for 28.99%) implies ~21.5x Mando's FY26 PAT — cheap versus Gabriel's own multiple, which is why it is EPS-accretive — but minority holders depend on independent valuations. [Screener shareholding; Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Equity-accounted growth:** Dana, Henkel, Mando and Klemove stakes are minorities (25–49%); Gabriel will book profit share and dividends, not control their cash. Klemove FY26 figures are unaudited. [Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Leverage turn:** from near-zero to ₹1,000–1,200 cr of debt; interest cost starts from Q2 FY27. Management's "1:1" debt-equity remark conflicts with CRISIL's <0.3x — clarity needed. [Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Margin squeeze:** consolidated margin down ~110 bps YoY in Q1; recovery depends on OEM price settlements, now monthly. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Auditor:** unqualified; key audit matter is revenue (price variations passed to customers); the audit trail was not enabled in one software. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Exceptional:** ₹13.3 cr labour-code charge in FY26. [AR FY26 note 35B]
- **Contingent liabilities** modest: direct tax ₹4.3 cr, indirect tax ₹7.2 cr (incl. ₹4.4 cr GST TRAN-1 disputes), claims ₹24.7 cr; an old tax case concerns management fees paid to a group company. [AR FY26 note 46]
- **Exchange query:** NSE/BSE sought clarification on price movement on 23 Sep 2026; the company said it knew of no reason. [BSE filing 23-Sep-26 via Screener]
- **Customer concentration** in 2W (TVS, HMSI, Yamaha, Bajaj); sunroof dependent on Hyundai/Kia. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: consolidation of four businesses; commodity squeeze flagged; no mention of Mando or Klemove. Q1: Project Jupiter announced the day before the call, lifting Anand-group revenue inside Gabriel from ~58% to ~70%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Balance-sheet stance flipped from cash-funded to ₹800–1,000 cr debt. [Q1 call]
- Operating tone softer: PV underperformance, sunroof production loss, margin down; 2W strong. [Q1 call]

## My read
Gabriel is turning from a mid-margin shock-absorber maker into the Anand Group's holding platform, and most future earnings growth will come from equity-accounted stakes (Dana, Henkel, Mando, Klemove) bought from the promoter at multiples far below Gabriel's own. That is accretive for earnings per share, but it makes the stock a group-restructuring story whose quality depends on deal pricing and on minority cash flows it does not control. The core business is healthy (2W share gains, Hero entry, light working capital) but margins slipped to 8.7% in Q1. The price needs roughly 30%+ yearly profit growth; watch Q2 margins, the first quarter of Mando profit share and interest cost, and whether the remaining ~30% of group businesses come in on similarly fair terms.
