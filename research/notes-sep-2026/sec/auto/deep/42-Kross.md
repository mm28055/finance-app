# Kross — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Screener code KROSS (standalone; no subsidiaries, so no consolidated page). Price ₹268, market cap ₹1,728 cr, 52-week range ₹151–283 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026]. Listed Sep 2024 (IPO fresh issue ₹250 cr).

## Business and segments
- Jamshedpur-based maker of safety-critical forged, cast and machined parts for medium and heavy commercial vehicles (M&HCV) and tractors (axle shafts, companion flanges, anti-roll bars, differential spiders, PTO shafts, universal-joint crosses), plus complete **trailer axles and suspensions** sold to trailer fabricators across India; new hydraulic **tipping jacks** from Q4 FY26 [AR FY26 MD&A; Ind-Ra Jun-26].
- Reports one operating segment; management mix: trailer axles & suspension 43% of FY26 revenue (42.6% per MD&A), components 57% [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]. Q1 FY27: trailer axles & suspension 40.7%, CV components 42.9%, tractor ~9.9%, exports 4.5%, tipping jacks ~2.0% [IP Q1FY27 product-wise chart]. Q4 FY26 trailer share was 45.2% [IP Q1FY27].
- Customers: Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, TAFE, Sonalika, International Tractors, Dana India, Eaton; 200+ customers [Ind-Ra Jun-26; IP Q1FY27]. Top-5 customers ~59.5% of Q1 FY27 revenue (56.3% a year earlier) [IP Q1FY27]; CFO said top-5 fell from ~60–63% at IPO to ~58–59% [Q1 call].
- Market position: management calls itself "a #2 player" in trailer axles that cannot price above the #1 [Q4 call]; Ind-Ra calls it one of the top three trailer-axle makers, competing with Tata Motors and York Transport Equipment [Ind-Ra Jun-26].
- Geography: FY26 revenue outside India ₹20.2 cr of ₹673.2 cr (3.0%), FY25 ₹19.7 cr [AR FY26 note 45]. Management said exports were 4% of FY26 revenue [Q4 call] — the audited geographic split shows 3.0%.
- Capacity: trailer axles raised from 5,000 to 7,500 a month with the ₹25 cr extrusion plant; tipping jacks 800 kits a month; forging presses 400–2,500 t, another 1,000 t press ordered; heat-treatment 100 t/day [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]. Q1 FY27 volumes: ~9,500 axles and ~3,200–3,300 suspensions; Q4 FY26: 10,500+ axles, 3,500+ suspensions [Q1 call; Q4 call]. That puts Q1 axle output at ~42% of the new 22,500-a-quarter capacity (my arithmetic). Forging/casting run at ~70% utilisation [Q1 call].
- EV content: none material; the business is tied to diesel trucks, trailers and tractors (axle and driveline parts are powertrain-neutral).

## Key numbers (Screener, standalone, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales: Jun-25 139 → Sep-25 131 → Dec-25 177 → Mar-26 225 → Jun-26 184. Operating profit 16 → 15 → 23 → 34 → 23; margin 12%, 11%, 13%, 15%, 12%. Net profit 11 → 8 → 14 → 22 → 13 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 as reported: revenue ₹184.3 cr (+32.3% YoY), EBITDA ₹22.55 cr (+39.5%), margin 12.23% (+63 bp YoY), PBT ₹17.83 cr, PAT ₹13.31 cr (+24.4%), PAT margin 7.2% [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]. Gross margin slipped to 44.8% from 45.5% (Q4) and 46.0% (Q1 FY26) — steel and conversion-cost inflation before OEM settlements [IP Q1FY27].
- Annual: sales FY24 620 → FY25 620 → FY26 673 (flat, then +8.5%); operating profit 81 → 81 → 88; margin 13% each year; net profit 45 → 48 → 55 [Screener]. H2 FY26 revenue was 49% above H1 [Q4 call].
- Returns: ROCE 33% (FY24) → 22% (FY25) → 16% (FY26) — diluted by IPO cash and ₹100 cr of FY26 capex not yet earning; ROE 12% [Screener].
- EPS: ₹8.56 (FY26), TTM ₹8.96 [Screener]. No dividend.
- Unit economics (my estimate): Q1 trailer revenue ≈ 40.7% × ₹184 cr ≈ ₹75 cr on ~9,500 axles plus ~3,250 suspensions — roughly ₹75,000–80,000 per axle including suspension share. Tipping jacks are expected at ~15% margin [IP Q1FY27]; exports carry the best margins, tractors the thinnest [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- FY27 revenue: Q4 — about 22% growth "subject to demand being consistent" [Q4 call]. Q1 delivered +32% and management said Q1 was "in line with our targets" [Q1 call] — **delivering**.
- Margin: Q4 — "between 14% to 15% margin is what we are looking at", with an explicit warning that Q1 would suffer from delayed, retrospective OEM settlements [Q4 call]. Q1 printed 12.23% — **missed** the band (though the Q1 dip was flagged). Q1 — steel settlement given mid-July retrospectively; conversion-cost settlement pending; margins to expand "quarter to quarter" [Q1 call]. The explicit 14–15% number was not repeated.
- Tipping jacks: Q4 — "targeting 300 units by the end of Quarter 1 and subsequently 500 units in Q2 and Q3"; in Q&A "in Quarter 1, we would be doing approximately 150 to 200 kits" [Q4 call]. Q1 — 226 sold (220 produced per deck); new target 65–70% of 800/month capacity by Q4 FY27 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27] — **slower than the 300 target**, roughly in line with the 150–200 Q&A figure.
- Extrusion line: Q4 — axles from it to sell "this month, May 2026" / "by end of this month or June" [Q4 call]. Q1 call — "commissioned and productionized" [Q1 call]; Q1 deck — "commercial production to start soon in August 2026" [IP Q1FY27] — **slipped ~2–3 months**.
- High-pressure moulding line (doubles casting): Q4 — "operational by September 2026" [Q4 call]; Q1 — "expected to start in Q3" [Q1 call] — **slight slip**.
- Robotic axle-shaft forging: September 2026 on both calls [Q4 call; Q1 call] — **kept**.
- Seamless tube plant: commissioning Q4 FY27, trials by year-end, revenue from FY28 [Q4 call; Q1 call] — **kept**; Ind-Ra says "fully operational by FY28" [Ind-Ra Jun-26].
- Exports: 8% of revenue in two years; +40–45% exports in FY27; new European Tier-1 parts under validation, orders from H2 FY27 [Q1 call] — **kept**. Tractor share to ~15% in two years (from 9–10%), third tractor OEM starting from Q1 [Q4 call] — **kept** (tractor revenue +~40% YoY in Q1 per deck).

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹100.2 cr (FY25 ₹28.4 cr) — tipping jacks, extrusion plant (₹25 cr), forging presses, seamless-tube shed (~₹60 cr spent) [Ind-Ra Jun-26; Q4 call].
- FY27 plan: ~₹100 cr on seamless tubes plus ₹20–25 cr other [Q4 call]; Ind-Ra quotes ₹100–150 cr for FY27, normalising to ₹50–60 cr a year from FY28 [Ind-Ra Jun-26]. Seamless tube total cost ~₹167 cr, 115–220 mm diameter, mix of debt and accruals; captive use for axles plus oil & gas market sales [IP Q1FY27].
- Funding: term loan ~₹100 cr tied up for seamless tubes (Ind-Ra: ₹110 cr), drawn as machines arrive; no other debt planned [Q1 call; Ind-Ra Jun-26]. Capital commitments ₹68.8 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 34].
- Payback: extrusion margins "improve significantly above 50% utilisation" [IP Q1FY27]; seamless tubes replace imported tubes (rupee depreciation makes imports dearer) [Q4 call]. No asset-turn target given.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt: long-term ₹29.1 cr + short-term ₹23.2 cr + leases ₹1.4 cr = ₹53.7 cr at Mar-26 (₹34.0 cr at Mar-25) [AR FY26 note 38]. Adding ₹34.9 cr of bills discounted with recourse, Ind-Ra's gross adjusted debt is ₹88.6 cr; net adjusted leverage 0.72x EBITDA (FY25 net cash); interest cover 10.9x [Ind-Ra Jun-26; AR FY26 note 34].
- Cash fell to ₹25.4 cr at Mar-26 from ₹85.4 cr as IPO money was spent [Ind-Ra Jun-26].
- Cash flow: operating cash flow ₹28 cr (Screener; Ind-Ra ₹22.9 cr) vs ₹9 cr in FY25; free cash flow −₹72 cr (FY25 −₹20 cr) [Screener]. Ind-Ra expects FCF negative in FY27, positive from FY28 [Ind-Ra Jun-26].
- Working capital is the weak spot: debtor days 107, inventory days 105, payable days 60, cash conversion cycle 153 days (74 in FY23) [Screener]; Ind-Ra's cycle 175 days (163 FY25, 117 FY24), blamed on product and customer mix [Ind-Ra Jun-26]. And this is after ₹34.9 cr of receivables were discounted with banks.
- No dividend since listing [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Backward integration from forging and casting to machining and assembly; "among the few players with a full product range of axles and suspension" [Ind-Ra Jun-26]. India's first axle-beam extrusion plant (single-piece beam, lighter for the same tonnage) [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Share gains: trailer axle volumes +34% YoY in Q1 vs single-digit industry growth, though management would not claim share gain ("spillover" of Q4 orders) [Q1 call]. Q4 sales were "capped by our capacities" [Q1 call].
- Pricing power is limited in trailers: 3–5% hike from 1 April, another 1–2% planned, "in line with competition"; "We are a #2 player. So, we cannot price our product more than the #1 player" [Q1 call; Q4 call]. With OEMs, steel and conversion costs are passed through retrospectively with about a one-quarter lag [Q4 call; Ind-Ra Jun-26].
- Relationships: ~two decades with Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, TAFE; "Super 8 Supplier" award from Ashok Leyland [Ind-Ra Jun-26; IP Q1FY27].
- Import dependence low: 12% of purchases, mainly China [Ind-Ra Jun-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Working capital stretch**: ~150–175-day cycle and ₹35 cr of recourse bill discounting; a CV slowdown would lock up cash quickly [Screener; Ind-Ra Jun-26; AR FY26 note 34].
- **Bank stock-statement mismatches**: CARO says quarterly returns to banks "are not in agreement with the books"; e.g., Mar-26 receivables ₹197.2 cr per books vs ₹208.7 cr reported to banks; Sep-25 receivables ₹164.1 cr vs ₹144.8 cr [AR FY26 CARO ii(b), note 53]. Explained as period-end adjustments, but recurring for eight quarters.
- **Past non-cooperation with raters**: CARE had Kross at "CARE BB; Issuer Not Cooperating" from 2022 to Oct 2024 before withdrawing in Dec 2025; Ind-Ra also had it "IND BB (Issuer Not Cooperating)" in Jul 2023 before upgrading [CARE Dec-25; Ind-Ra Jun-26 rating history]. Pre-IPO disclosure habits; now IND A/Stable.
- **Related parties**: sales of ₹9.35 cr in FY26 (₹12.1 cr FY25) to Bull Auto Parts, a firm where a director is proprietor; ₹4.9 cr outstanding; small unsecured loans from promoters (~₹1.7 cr) [AR FY26 note 51]. Auditor confirms Sections 177/188 compliance [AR FY26 CARO xiii].
- **Contingent liabilities** ₹39.7 cr: bills discounted ₹34.9 cr, excise/service tax ₹2.8 cr, income tax ₹1.7 cr, GST ₹0.3 cr [AR FY26 note 34]. Emphasis of matter on a favourable Jharkhand High Court order quashing electricity duty — a ₹4.6 cr contingent asset, not a liability [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Cyclicality and concentration**: M&HCV trucks and trailers dominate; top-5 customers ~59% [IP Q1FY27].
- **Execution**: extrusion commercial start slipped to August; tipping-jack ramp below the first target; seamless tube is the largest project in the company's history (~₹167 cr vs ₹207 cr of gross fixed assets at Mar-26) [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- **Disclosure consistency**: exports "4%" on call vs 3.0% audited; the Q1 deck says tipping jacks "220 produced", call says "226 sold" / "around 200" [Q4 call; AR FY26; IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Promoter holding rising slightly to 68.57%; no pledge disclosed [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 tone: capacity-constrained, 22% growth and 14–15% margin talk, LPG shortage handled [Q4 call]. Q1 tone: record revenue but margin squeeze; focus on OEM settlements (steel settled 15 July, conversion pending) and the new **Parivartan** scrappage-linked scheme (from 30 Oct 2026: no BS-IV-or-older trucks into Delhi NCR; 10-year road-tax holiday, free registration, ~13% total discount on replacement) as a demand driver [Q1 call].
- OEMs gave strong schedules from September to March, "first time supported by healthy order books" [Q1 call].
- New: ~₹100 cr term loan confirmed for seamless tubes; European Tier-1 (Swedish customer) already ~all of current exports; four part families, one in mass production, three from H2 [Q1 call].

## Best management quotes
- "We are a #2 player. So, we cannot price our product more than the #1 player." [Q4 call]
- "Quarter-to-quarter we will be expanding on margins and this will not only be compensated by the steel price, but also by the conversion increase." [Q1 call]

## My read
Kross is in a real upswing: the CV cycle turned in H2 FY26, trailer axle volumes are up a third, tractors and exports are growing, and Q1 revenue grew 32%. But this growth is expensive in capital: FY26 capex was ₹100 cr, FY27 will be similar, free cash flow is deeply negative, ROCE has fallen to 16%, and receivables plus inventory now take ~150+ days. Margins have been stuck at ~13% for three years; the 14–15% hope depends on OEM conversion-cost settlements and on filling the extrusion line. At about 30x trailing earnings the price needs roughly 20%+ earnings growth for several years; watch Q2 margins (should recover to 13–14% if settlements come through), working-capital days, and seamless-tube commissioning in Q4 FY27. A CV downturn after the Parivartan pull-forward is the main risk.
