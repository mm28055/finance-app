## Business and segments
- Part of the TSF group (the T.S. Santhanam branch of the old TVS family). The promoters held 58.31% until September 2026. [Q4 call; ICRA Feb-26]
- Products:
  - steel wheels for trucks, tractors and cars;
  - earthmover (construction) wheels;
  - cast and forged aluminium wheels;
  - air suspensions and lift axles for buses and trucks;
  - fabricated parts and hydraulic cylinders for construction equipment;
  - machined parts for wind turbines.
  - A subsidiary, WIL Car Wheels (a joint venture with Topy of Japan), makes car steel wheels.
  - [Q1 FY27 presentation; Q4 call]
- Reported segments, FY26 consolidated:
  - Automotive: revenue ₹4,526 cr (+16%), EBIT ₹287.6 cr (6.4% margin).
  - Industrial: revenue ₹938.5 cr (+12%), EBIT ₹41.3 cr (4.4%, +15%).
  - [AR FY26 note 43; Q4 FY26 presentation]
- Q1 FY27 consolidated:
  - Automotive: revenue ₹1,254 cr, EBIT ₹75.6 cr.
  - Industrial: revenue ₹237 cr, EBIT ₹7.8 cr (3.3%).
  - [Q1 FY27 presentation]
- Q4 FY26 revenue by business (standalone): trucks and buses 22%, tractors 16%, construction wheels 17%, cars 14–15%, fabrication 14%, air suspension 10%, cylinders 5%. [Q4 call]
- Market shares, H1 FY26, from ICRA:
  - steel rims: medium and heavy trucks 37%, light trucks 76%, tractors 52%, cars 35%;
  - about 20% of world construction-equipment and tractor wheels (company's claim).
  - [ICRA Feb-26; Q4 call]
- Exports:
  - FY26: ₹1,342 cr (26%).
  - Q1 FY27: ₹379.7 cr (27.4% of standalone sales).
  - Main markets: USA, Europe, Brazil. Customers include Caterpillar, John Deere and Komatsu.
  - [Q1 FY27 presentation; Q4 call]
- Capacities stated in May 2026 [official Q4 transcript]:
  - car steel wheels 11–12m a year, rising to 13.5–14m;
  - large tractor wheels 50,000 a month;
  - truck wheels (including light trucks) 250,000 a month, rising to 300,000;
  - aluminium wheels 42,000 a month, rising to 60,000 and then 80,000;
  - heavy construction wheels 16,000 a month.

## Capex and capacity
- Capex history (standalone, ₹ cr): FY22 129.6, FY23 148.1, FY24 141.5, FY25 249.0, FY26 261.8. FY27 is expected at ₹400–450 cr. [Q1 FY27 presentation]
- Where the money went: in FY26 about 40% went to wind (machining large castings; ~₹90 cr of machines, each "the size of a building", taking 12–15 months to commission). The rest went to aluminium wheels (₹300 cr+ committed in total), the Mambattu tractor-export plant, and bus air suspensions. [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- Expected return: management expects ₹500–600 cr of revenue from FY26's ₹250 cr of capex, since wind machining turns over about 1x a year and aluminium about 2x. [Q2 call]
- Wind: the partner casting maker produces about 3,500 tonnes a month and aims for 10,000 in 3–4 years. Wheels India does 85–90% of its machining. [Q4 call]
- Hydraulic cylinders: revenue about ₹200 cr. The SH Pack (Korea) alliance targets about $15m of business within 24 months, starting slowly. [Q2 call]
- Capital commitments ₹73.8 cr at Mar-26. [AR FY26 note 26]
- **Equity raised in September 2026**:
  - A ₹180 cr preferential issue to promoters at ₹1,461 a share (TSF Investments ₹150 cr, which takes it to 27.81%; the Ram family ₹30 cr), allotted 21 Sep.
  - A ₹250 cr QIP (share placement to institutions) at ₹2,088 a share, allotted 25 Sep; the stated use is repaying borrowings.
  - Share count rises from 2.443 cr to 2.686 cr (+9.9%).
  - The promoters paid about 30% less a share than the institutions (pricing formula, earlier relevant date).
  - [BSE filings 21, 24, 25 Sep 2026; SAST 23 Sep 2026; Business Today 25 Sep 2026]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Consolidated debt: ₹710 cr at Jun-26 (₹690 cr at Mar-26). Debt/equity 0.68. Debt/EBITDA 1.53x. [Q1 FY27 presentation]
- **Bill discounting**: ₹469 cr of supplier-finance bill discounting plus ₹23.1 cr of buyers' credit sit inside trade payables. Including these, debt/EBITDA is 2.75x at Q1 FY27 (2.88x in FY26). Management treats total debt as about ₹1,150 cr. ICRA measured debt/operating EBITDA at 3.4x in 9M FY26. [AR FY26 borrowings note; Q1 FY27 presentation; Q2 call; ICRA Feb-26]
- Cash flow (Screener): cash from operations FY24 ₹329 cr, FY25 ₹400 cr, FY26 ₹477 cr (124% of operating profit). Free cash flow ₹179 cr, ₹192 cr, ₹203 cr. In Q1 FY27 free cash flow was "close to breakeven". [Screener; Q1 FY27 presentation]
- Working capital (Screener, FY26): debtor days 55, inventory 73, payables 131, giving a cash cycle of −3 days. Management says inventory days fell from 76 to 63 and debtor days from 61 to 54. Payables are inflated by the bill discounting. [Screener; Q4 call]

- Dividend payout 23% in FY26. [Screener]
- Ratings: ICRA A (Stable)/A2+, reaffirmed 18 Feb 2026, supported by "exceptional financial flexibility" from the TSF parentage. India Ratings also rates the company (Feb 2026). [ICRA Feb-26; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Dominant domestic shares: light-truck rims 76%, tractor rims 52%, heavy-truck rims 37%, car rims 35%. [ICRA Feb-26]
- Global position in construction and tractor wheels (~20% share). Some export customers go back 30+ years, and a new supplier needs years of qualification. [Q4 call; Q2 call]
- Only machining partner for a large wind-casting maker (85–90% of its machining), which it describes as a conversion business with double-digit returns. [Q4 call]
- Air suspension supplier to all Indian e-bus makers (JBM, Switch, EKA, Olectra), replacing imports. Q2 FY26 growth was 29%. [Q2 call]
- Pricing: steel and aluminium costs pass through over time. Wage and energy increases must be negotiated. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Thin margins: operating margin ~8%, industrial segment EBIT margin 3–4%. Double-digit EBITDA has been "1–2 years away" for over a year. [Q1 FY27 presentation; Q4 call]
- Dilution and pricing: equity grows ~10% in one month. The promoters bought at ₹1,461, while the market was at ~₹2,300 and the QIP was at ₹2,088. The pricing is legal under SEBI formula, but new money is being raised just after capex guidance rose from ₹280–300 cr to ₹400–450 cr. [BSE filings; Q1 FY27 presentation]
- Hidden debt: ₹469 cr of bill discounting. It is disclosed properly in the notes, but "debt ~₹700 cr" statements leave it out. [AR FY26]
- Related parties:
  - bought Axles India shares worth ₹22.68 cr from promoter entity TSF, taking the stake from 9.5% to 12.5%, and aims for 15–25%, with a possible later combination;
  - buys goods and services from group companies such as Brakes India and Sundaram Business Services;
  - the MD's pay was ₹6.05 cr.
  - [AR FY26 note 45; Q2 call; Q4 call]
- Governance change: Chairman S. Ram stepped down on 30 Mar 2026. [AR FY26 note 45]
- Auditor: unqualified, no key audit matters. Disputed taxes of ₹47.0 cr (₹19.7 cr a year earlier) are not provided for. [AR FY26 note 26]
- Tariffs: US tariffs were 25% on auto parts and 33–35% effective on construction and tractor wheels (Oct 2025), mostly paid by customers. EU CBAM carbon tax is a recurring cost. [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- Cyclical demand; management admitted visibility of "about 1 month". [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone shifted from "steady 8–10% growth" (Oct 2025) to a record year: 16% growth, ROE 15.7%, first consolidated quarterly PAT above ₹50 cr.
- New headwinds: West Asia war, aluminium re-sourcing away from the Middle East, fuel and wage inflation, CBAM.
- Capex rose from ₹250 cr to ₹280–300 cr (May) to ₹400–450 cr (July), followed by a ₹430 cr equity raise in September.
- The stricter inventory-provisioning rule appeared (₹26 cr hit).
- "India plus one" overseas manufacturing is now under consideration, with a new US country manager and a German sales subsidiary.

## My read
The self-help turnaround is real:
- operating margin up from 5% to 8% in two years;
- ROCE 18.7%;
- operating cash flow above operating profit;
- dominant shares in truck and tractor rims.

But Q1 FY27 shows the margin stalling at 7.8%. Capex has nearly doubled to ₹400–450 cr, and the company just issued ~10% more shares, partly to promoters at a much lower price.

After a 166% one-year rise, the stock trades at ~36x trailing profit (Screener). That price needs the double-digit margin to arrive on time and the wind, aluminium and hydraulics investments to turn at the promised asset turns.

What would change my view:
- positive: EBITDA margin above 9% by H2 FY27, or aluminium wheels reaching 80,000 a month;
- negative: another capex increase, or bill discounting rising above ~₹450 cr.
