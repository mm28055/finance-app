# Studds Accessories (NSE: STUDDS, BSE: 544599) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Price ₹410, market cap ₹1,613 cr, P/E 21.6, ROCE 22.9%, 52-week range ₹403–600 (at the low) [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- India's largest two-wheeler helmet maker; listed 7 Nov 2025 through an IPO that was entirely an offer for sale by existing shareholders, so the company received no money [AR FY26 MD&A; AR FY26 CARO 3(x)(a)].
- Two brands: **Studds** (mass/mid, MRP ₹925–4,195) and **SMK** (premium, ₹3,000–15,350); also private-label helmets for Daytona and O'Neal, bicycle helmets and accessories (boxes, jackets, gloves) [IP Q1FY27 slides 11, 20].
- FY26 revenue mix by product: Studds helmets 74.1%, SMK 15.7%, private labels 3.1%, other accessories 7.1% [AR FY26 MD&A]. Q1 FY27 mix: Studds 72.2%, SMK 16.6% (18.1% a year ago), private labels 7.2%, accessories 4.1% [IP Q1FY27 slide 6].
- Channels FY26: distributors and exclusive brand outlets 55.2%, exports 20%, OEMs (Hero, Honda, Suzuki, Royal Enfield, Yamaha) 13.7%, others (e-commerce, government canteens) 11.1% [AR FY26 MD&A; CARE Apr-26]. Q1 FY27: exports 20.9%, OEM 13.4% [IP Q1FY27].
- Export revenue FY26 ₹128.6 cr, up 32.3%; exports went from 16.75% of sales in FY25 to 20% in FY26 [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]. Sells in 70+ countries; ~25% share of the Indian helmet market per CARE [CARE Apr-26].
- Single reportable segment (helmets and accessories), so no segment margin disclosure [AR FY26 Note 34]. Management's own split: Studds brand EBITDA margin ~17% vs SMK ~27% ("slightly upwards of 10%" gap); SMK exports 30–35% EBITDA [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Capacity (30 Jun 2026): 2.38 mn helmets+boxes a quarter installed across three plants, Q1 FY27 production 1.95 mn, utilisation ~81% (Q4 FY26: 89%); EPS liners 85%, water decals 66% [IP Q1FY27 slide 16; Q1 call]. Annual installed ~9.5 mn going to ~12.5 mn with Facility V [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Indian helmet market: 26 mn units CY2024 to 35 mn by CY2029, 6.1% a year (company's RHP-sourced industry report) [AR FY26 MD&A].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (25 May 2026):** FY27 revenue growth ~17–18% "while maintaining EBITDA margins at broadly similar levels" (FY26: 19.3%); exports to 23–24% of sales in FY27 and ~30% in 2–3 years; 1.5 mn capacity "by Q2 FY27"; Decathlon supplies from July 2026; Italy sales from mid-Q2; riding jackets and Bluetooth from H2 FY27; ad spend up to ₹30 cr (FY26 ₹23 cr); 22–23% EBITDA margin pushed to "the year after next" [Q4 call, official].
- **Q1 FY27 call (10 Aug 2026):** margin guidance cut hard — Q2 FY27 EBITDA 14–15%, back to 18–20% run-rate by Q4 FY27 "subject to raw material prices remaining broadly stable" [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 slide 5]. Revenue: "high teens" kept, built as ~10% volume + 7–8% price. FY28: "mid to high teens" (13–14% volume + 3–4% price, standalone), Italy extra [Q1 call].
- Delivery vs promise: revenue growth 13.7% (below 17–18% run-rate but price hike only 5% realised); margin 11.5% vs "similar levels" — **missed badly**. Capacity slipped from "by Q2" to "October"; Decathlon from July to October; Italy from mid-Q2 to October. Jackets moved earlier (Q2), mesh Bluetooth Q3 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Verdict: revenue guidance **kept**, margin guidance **cut**, three launch dates **slipped by about a quarter**.

## Capex and capacity
- Facility V (Faridabad): total planned ~₹160 cr, two phases of 1.5 mn units each; takes annual capacity from ~9.5 mn to ~12.5 mn [AR FY26 MD&A]. Phase 1 from October 2026; phase 2 over the following 15–18 months [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Spend: ₹48 cr capex in FY26 [Q4 call; AR FY26]; ₹76 cr spent on the project till 30 Jun 2026 plus ₹10 cr vendor advances; ₹7.5 cr in Q1 FY27; FY28 plan ₹31 cr (phase 2 construction) [Q1 call]. CWIP ₹58 cr at Mar-26 (₹29 cr Mar-25) [Screener].
- Funding: internal cash; no borrowings of note (₹8 cr) [Screener].
- Asset turns: FY26 sales ₹634 cr on fixed assets ₹338 cr ≈ 1.9x. ₹160 cr for 3 mn units (~₹530 per unit of capacity) against ~₹780 revenue per unit means the new plant should turn ~1.5x at full use (derived).
- Capacity headroom is needed: Q4 FY26 utilisation was 89% [Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹8 cr; CARE put gearing at 0.01x and cash at ₹81.5 cr (Sep-25), with ~₹21–22 cr fixed deposits lien-marked against working-capital lines; working-capital use below 5% [Screener; CARE Apr-26].
- Operating cash flow ₹86 cr in FY26 (95% of operating profit), free cash flow ₹40 cr; FY25 CFO ₹63 cr / FCF ₹21 cr [Screener].
- Inventory days jumped: 62 (FY24) → 80 (FY25) → 107 (FY26) per Screener; management said 91 vs 68 on its basis, blamed a new Faridabad warehouse and more US stock, and called 85–90 days "comfortable" [Screener; Q4 call]. Inventory turnover fell 26.6% [AR FY26 key ratios]. Debtor days 24; cash conversion cycle 62 days vs 34 in FY24 [Screener].
- Dividend ₹3 per ₹5 share for FY26. Management called it a "payout of 60%" — that is 60% of face value; as a share of profit the payout is ~14% [Q4 call; Screener dividend payout 14%].
- CARE withdrew its A+/A1+ rating in Apr-2026 at the company's request after bank lines were closed — consistent with no debt [CARE Apr-26].

## Moat evidence
- Scale and share: ~25% of the Indian helmet market; ~77 lakh helmets a year; organised players Studds, Steelbird and Vega dominate [CARE Apr-26; Q4 call].
- OEM approvals: supplies Hero, Honda, Suzuki, Royal Enfield, Yamaha; CARE notes OEM approval is "time-consuming and costly", a real entry barrier [CARE Apr-26].
- Pricing power (tested in 2026): ~9% list price hike from 1 Apr 2026 accepted with no volume loss in general trade (volume +8.5% in Q1); all OEMs but one agreed to the increase by August [Q1 call]. But the company could not pass the full cost at once — gross margin fell ~600 bps [IP Q1FY27].
- Brand ladder: SMK volumes ~52% CAGR (FY24–FY26), private labels ~22%, Studds brand ~3% [IP Q1FY27 slide 20; Q4 call].
- Vertical integration: in-house moulds, EPS liners, decals, painting, VCA-certified test lab; ECE 22.06 and IS 4151 certified; Brazil and Indonesia local certifications held [IP Q1FY27 slides 14–16; Q4 call].
- Formalisation tailwind: management estimates unorganised share 20–30% today, falling to 10–15% in 2–3 years; proposed rule of two BIS-certified helmets with each new two-wheeler would widen the market [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A].

## Risks and red flags
- **Raw-material shock:** India imports ~98% of its styrene via the Gulf; the Hormuz disruption pushed the company's styrene basket from ~₹135 to a peak ~₹225/kg, still ~₹185 in August [Q1 call]. Any renewed Gulf disruption repeats the Q1 hit.
- **Structural wage cost:** Haryana minimum wage up ~35% from 1 Apr 2026 (unskilled ₹11–12k → ₹15–16k/month), ~200 bps margin hit; management says it is permanent and must be offset through price/mix [Q1 call].
- **Guidance credibility:** the Q4 "similar margins" promise was made on 25 May, after the April styrene peak was known, then cut to 11.5% actual. Launch dates for capacity, Decathlon and Italy all slipped about a quarter [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Inventory creep:** 107 inventory days on Screener's basis; auditors made inventory valuation a key audit matter (provision ₹0.22 cr, reversal ₹0.14 cr) [AR FY26 auditor's report; Screener].
- **Related parties:** promoter family pay FY26 — Madhu Bhushan Khurana ₹2.80 cr, Sidhartha Khurana ₹2.73 cr, Shilpa Arora (daughter, whole-time director) ₹1.49 cr (up from ₹0.83 cr), plus family salaries; sales to US subsidiary Bikerz US ₹4.7 cr with ₹3.2 cr receivable. Bikerz US was bought from promoter-linked Bikerz Inc for ₹2.56 cr in 2024 [AR FY26 Note 36]. Modest in size; no loans to promoters.
- **Contingent liabilities** small: ₹3.7 cr total (service tax ₹0.32 cr, GST ₹0.26 cr and ₹0.33 cr, income tax ₹0.42 cr, bank guarantees ₹2.1 cr) [AR FY26 Note 37]. Disputed statutory dues listed in CARO match [AR FY26 CARO vii(b)].
- **Auditor:** Rajan Chhabra & Co. (small firm); clean opinion, key audit matters only revenue cut-off and inventory valuation; no qualification or emphasis of matter; Deloitte appointed internal auditor for FY27 [AR FY26].
- **Europe start-up:** Italy subsidiary losses ~₹2–2.5 cr in FY27 and ~₹2 cr in FY28; revenue only €100–125k in FY27 and ~€1 mn in FY28 [Q1 call]. The switch from Spain to Italy followed pushback from the second-largest European distributor [Q4 call, official].
- **Stock:** 32% below its high; BSE sought a clarification on a volume spurt on 22 Sep 2026 — the company said nothing undisclosed [BSE filing 22-Sep-26]. Promoter holding steady at 61.75% [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "structural transformation… one of the most exciting periods" (May) to cost defence (August).
- Numbers: margin guide went from ~19% for FY27 to 14–15% for Q2 and 18–20% only by Q4; styrene detail (₹135 → ₹225 → ₹185) and the wage hike were new.
- MotoGP/Moto4 brand talk disappeared; Decathlon, Italy and Bluetooth were recast as H2 FY27 growth engines. Bluetooth/jacket revenue quantified for the first time (₹15–20 cr in FY27).
- Price-cut risk flagged: if styrene falls, OEM prices may be cut slightly; distributor prices will not [Q1 call].

## My read
Studds is a good, cash-rich, debt-free business whose margin story just took a hit it did not see coming, even though the April styrene spike was already visible when it guided "similar margins" in late May. The core Studds brand grows only ~3% a year in volume; the investment case rests on SMK, exports (20% of sales, target 30%) and the new 3 mn-unit plant filling up. At ₹410 the price assumes margins recover to roughly the high teens; if Q2 prints near 14–15% and Q3 moves toward 18%, earnings power of ~₹95–100 cr on FY27 sales of ~₹740 cr is plausible. What would change the view: a second Gulf supply shock, OEMs forcing price cuts as plastics soften, or inventory days staying above 100 while the new plant ramps.
