# Menon Bearings — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Screener code MENONBE (consolidated). Price ₹298, market cap ₹1,671 cr, 52-week range ₹101–330 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Kolhapur maker of bi-metal engine bearings, bushes and thrust washers (parent), plus two wholly owned subsidiaries: **Menon Alkop** (aluminium die-casting and machining) and **Menon Brakes** (brake linings/shoes); a third subsidiary, Menon Bearings New Ventures, has no operations [CRISIL Apr-26; AR FY26 note 16]. Reports one segment, "Auto Components" [AR FY26 note 14].
- Size by division: Q1 FY27 standalone (bi-metal) sales ₹67.06 cr (+40.4% YoY), PAT ₹11.23 cr [Q1 call]; Alkop runs at ~₹21 cr a quarter per an analyst, and management targets ₹100 cr in FY27 and ~₹125 cr in FY28 [Q1 call]; brakes is the small remainder (~₹4–6 cr a quarter, my arithmetic from consolidated minus standalone).
- End markets (bi-metal): tractors 35%, HCV/LCV 25%, brake systems 20–25% (bushes supplied to Brakes India), transmissions 15% (Allison, Magna), industrial/other 10% [Q4 call, official]. Auto is 80–90% of revenue per CRISIL; management says ~75% auto on a consolidated basis [CRISIL Apr-26; Q4 call].
- Customers: John Deere (single source for 20+ years), Tata Motors, International Tractors, Brakes India, Cummins, Eaton, Allison Transmission, Magna, Taco Prestolite; no customer above 10–15% of sales [CRISIL Apr-26; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Exports ~34% of FY26 revenue (target 37% in FY27), 24+ countries, majority to the USA; ~80% of export orders now on ex-works terms [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]. Aftermarket through 10,000+ retail outlets; merchant exports to Africa via Dubai (₹7–8 cr a year) halted for three months by the West Asia conflict, now being redirected direct to Africa on 100% advance terms [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Capacity: bi-metal 486 → 580 lakh units (+19%) in FY26; brakes 30 → 50 lakh pieces (+67%); Alkop 1,450 t a year [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]. Utilisation in Q1: bearings ~80%, Alkop and brakes 65–70%; third shift runs only bottleneck operations [Q1 call]. Management says bi-metal peak revenue on present capacity is "above ₹400 cr" [Q1 call].
- EV content: Tier-2 to Tesla (via Concentric pumps) and Porsche e-mobility (via Eaton); Alkop supplies Tata EV parts (via Taco Prestolite), up to 8-kg high-pressure castings on a 1,600-t machine [Q1 call; Q4 call]. EV share of Alkop: "around 10%" on the Q4 call but "4% to 5%" on the Q1 call; target 8–10% by FY27 end [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].

Division margins: bi-metal 21%+, Alkop ~21%, brakes 25% in Q1 (historically 12–14%) on a favourable mix [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- FY27 revenue: Q4 — "more than ₹360 cr" (earlier ₹350 cr) [Q4 call]; Q1 — ₹360 cr is "a conservative figure and we will stick to that" [Q1 call]. Q1 run-rate ₹92–94 cr — **on track to beat**.
- Margin: Q4 — "20% to 22%" for the year "on a conservative side"; analyst's "at least 50 bp a year" improvement answered "Right" [Q4 call]. Q1 — 21.5–22% consolidated, "sustain around 20% to 21% over a period of time" [Q1 call]. Q1 delivered 22% (Screener) / 23.8% (company) — **delivering**.
- Growth: Q4 — 25% a year for two years, "₹500 cr by 2028" [Q4 call]; Q1 — "20% plus year on year… it can be 25% also"; "we were supposed to reach ₹500 crore in 2030… in the next 2–3 years, it's our internal target" [Q1 call] — **kept, slightly softened in wording**.
- New business: Q4 — 51 new parts worth ~₹30 cr (Alkop) plus >₹50 cr bi-metal pipeline over two years [Q4 call]; Q1 — RFQs of ~₹75 cr at a 75–80% hit rate, plus ₹65–75 cr potential from the June US/Canada trip (Magna, Linamar, Allison) over FY27–FY28 [Q1 call].
- Brake dynamometer (needed for railway and OEM brake approvals): originally due Aug 2025; Q4 — by end-Aug 2026 after two suppliers failed [Q4 call]; Q1 — commissioned end-Aug, running by end-Sep 2026; railways first-year ₹4–6 cr rising to ₹25–30 cr in two years [Q1 call] — **a year late, date held since Q4**.
- PTFE bushings for a European EV maker: Q4 — one part approved, expected order of 1 lakh a month rising to 6 lakh [Q4 call]; not mentioned on the Q1 call — **unconfirmed**.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex deployed: bi-metal ₹6.74 cr, Alkop ₹19.59 cr, brakes ₹0.58 cr [IP Q1FY27].
- Plan: bi-metal ₹25 cr over two years (₹9–10 cr in FY27, adding 25–30% capacity worth ~₹25 cr of revenue); Alkop ₹7 cr (₹4 cr this year); brakes ₹2.5–3 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]. Expected asset turn on new capex ~2.5x [Q1 call]. The deck mentions solar power and energy-efficiency investments; the stored profile's "3.8 MW solar saving ~₹2.25 cr a year" was not found in the Q1 deck or either official transcript — unverified.
- Land: 7 acres empty at Alkop; 65,000 sq ft new Alkop building half-used; looking for a new plot for bi-metal in a 2-year horizon [Q1 call].
- Funding: management says internal accruals, "no fresh loan" [Q1 call]; CRISIL describes FY26–FY27 capex as partly **debt-funded** [CRISIL Apr-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹47 cr at Mar-26 (₹43 cr at Mar-25, ₹15 cr at Mar-23) [Screener]; term loans from Shri Mahalaxmi Bank, Bajaj Finance, HDFC Bank, Federal Bank, secured on Kolhapur assets and a personal guarantee of chairman Nitin Menon [AR FY26 note 11]. CRISIL: gearing 0.27x, interest cover 12.3x (FY25), cash ₹18.1 cr (Sep-25), term debt repayment ₹9–9.6 cr a year, cash accrual >₹31 cr a year [CRISIL Apr-26]. Company deck: net debt/equity 0.15 [IP Q1FY27].
- Cash flow is weak relative to profit: operating cash flow ₹23 cr in FY26 (₹27 cr FY25) against ₹64 cr operating profit — CFO/OP 36%; free cash flow ₹5 cr [Screener].
- Working capital: debtor days 111 (91 in FY25), inventory 93, payables 60; cash conversion cycle 144 days [Screener]. US customers pay in "more than 180 days" [Q4 call, official]; ₹14.6 cr of ₹66.5 cr consolidated receivables are 6–12 months old [AR FY26 receivables ageing]. Management is using ₹6 cr (rising to ₹25 cr) of export packing credit at 4.70% with a 2.75% interest subvention [Q4 call].
- Dividend ₹2 a share declared with Q1 (face value ₹1); payout 29% of FY26 profit [IP Q1FY27; Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Critical, low-cost parts with high failure cost; zero-PPM certificates from several OEMs; single source to John Deere for 20+ years [Q1 call].
- Pricing: raw-material indexation with a quarterly lag; copper rose from ~₹900 to ~₹1,210–1,275/kg in a year and was passed through [Q4 call]. Carbide tool costs up 300% were absorbed, yet margin rose [Q1 call].
- Niche positioning: heavy-duty tractor/CV/industrial bearings rather than passenger cars, where large global rivals compete [Q1 call].
- CRISIL: "sole supplier or a major supplier of bearings for most customers"; established position supports "higher margins" of 18.7–20.4% (FY24–FY25) [CRISIL Apr-26].
- Management estimates the domestic engine-bearing market at ~₹1,000 cr a year [IP Q1FY27].

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter-family selling while promoting the story**: Aditya, Anshul and Sucheta Menon (promoter group) sold 9,96,909 shares (1.77%) in the open market between 18 Sep 2024 and 14 Aug 2026; promoter holding falls to 66.67% from 68.44% [SAST Reg 29(2) disclosure, 14 Aug 2026]. Aditya Menon spoke on the 17 Jul 2026 call.
- **Related party**: sales of ₹19.47 cr (FY26; ₹20.77 cr FY25) plus ₹2.51 cr service charges to Mani Auto Components, a firm in which chairman Nitin Menon is a partner (₹4.37 cr outstanding) — ~6.5% of revenue; small dealings with other Menon entities (Flyga Resorts, Sunshine Acres, Menon United) [AR FY26 note 16]. Parent's corporate guarantee to Menon Alkop ₹29 cr [AR FY26 CARO/loans note].
- **Cash conversion**: CFO only 36% of operating profit in FY26; debtor days rising to 111 [Screener].
- **Management change**: MD R.D. Dixit served until 3 Mar 2026; Arun Aradhye became MD on 4 Mar 2026 [AR FY26 note 16].
- **Disclosure consistency**: EV share of Alkop given as ~10% (Q4) then 4–5% (Q1); export destination "80%… to Europe" in the official Q4 text vs "majority… to the United States" on Q1; quarterly vs annual-report line items reclassified (tooling cost moved into raw materials), flagged by an investor [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Cyclicality**: tractors and trucks ~60% of bi-metal demand; monsoon and US truck cycle matter [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Brake division execution**: dynamometer a year late; railway approvals need a further ~year of testing [Q1 call].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion, no key audit matters, no emphasis of matter; no contingent liabilities disclosed beyond the subsidiary guarantee [AR FY26 auditor's reports].

## My read
The business is clearly improving: sales up 37% and profit up two-thirds in Q1, margins holding at 22%+ despite steep input inflation, and a credible pipeline of US and European orders on existing capacity. The weak spots are cash — receivables and inventory absorb most of the profit, so free cash flow is tiny — and governance optics: promoter-family members sold ~1.8% while talking up the story, and a chairman-linked firm buys ~₹20 cr a year. At ~38x trailing earnings the price already needs ~25% earnings growth for several years; beating ₹360 cr comfortably, a working dynamometer with first railway orders, and a turn in operating cash flow would support that, while further promoter selling or a stretch in debtor days would argue caution.
