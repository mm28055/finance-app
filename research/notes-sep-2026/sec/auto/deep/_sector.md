# Indian auto components — sector research (Phase A2), 27 Sep 2026

Scope: the 50 companies in `sec/auto/` (engine parts, EV/driveline, batteries, forgings/castings, wiring and electricals, lighting, suspension, brakes/steering, wheels, exhaust, fasteners/springs, decorative parts, helmets, tractor/off-highway parts). Every figure carries its source tag; the URL list is at the end.

## 1. Market size and growth
- **FY26 industry turnover ₹7.60 lakh cr (US$85.9 bn), +12.7%**; supplies to vehicle makers (OEM) ₹6.63 lakh cr (+16.3%); aftermarket ₹1.08 lakh cr (+9%); exports US$24.0 bn (+5%), with Europe the strongest growth; imports US$25.4 bn (+13%) [ACMA, Jul-2026].
- Turnover has more than doubled since FY21 (17% a year FY21–FY26) [ACMA, Jul-2026].
- The industry ran a **trade deficit for the first time in two years**; China supplied ~36% of imports, mainly electronics and EV parts [ACMA via BizzBuzz, 7-Jul-2026].
- EV components (ex-lithium cells) were only **4.6% of domestic OEM supplies** in FY26 [ACMA, Jul-2026].
- CareEdge sizes the wider "auto ancillary" market (incl. tyres and batteries) at ₹9.84 lakh cr in FY26 → ₹10.68 lakh cr in FY27 (+8–9%); revenue mix OEM ~67%, exports ~22%, aftermarket ~11%; exports ~₹2.3 lakh cr in FY27 [CareEdge, 13-Aug-2026].
- Listed-company results: 59 listed component makers grew revenue 12.5% and EBITDA 13.3% in FY26, margin flat at 13.6%; suspension/braking revenue +16%, multi-product +15%; tyres, lighting and suspension EBITDA +17%; **forgings EBITDA −4% and batteries −1%**; 25 of 59 saw margins shrink [Elara Capital, 29-Jun-2026].

## 3. Policy
- **GST 2.0 (22 Sep 2025):** small cars and two-wheelers under 350cc cut from 28% to 18% (plus cess removal); bikes above 350cc raised to 40%; tractors to 5%; vehicle prices fell 10–15%; expected to add ~200 bps to 2W and ~100 bps to PV demand growth [ClearTax; Autocar Professional; Kotak Securities summaries]. ICRA credits most of FY26's surprise to the GST cut [ICRA via Business Standard, Feb-2026]. The effect becomes a high base from Q3 FY27.
- **PLI-Auto:** ₹25,938 cr outlay [Studds AR FY26 citing PIB]; only **two of ACMA's ~1,100 members have received PLI money** [ACMA DG via BusinessToday, 7-Jul-2026].
- **ACC battery PLI (50 GWh):** only ~1.4 GWh (2.8%) commissioned on time, all by Ola Electric; no incentives disbursed to any beneficiary as of Feb-2026 [IEEFA, 2026]. Exide and Amara Raja lost the PLI bids and are building cells outside the scheme [Zetwerk/IEEFA summary]. **Exide commissioned Phase-I of its 6 GWh Bengaluru cell plant on 23-Sep-2026 (revenue from Q3 FY27, cylindrical NMC for 2W)**; Amara Raja pushed its cell plant start to Q2–Q3 FY27 (4 GWh first phase) [Whalesbook/S&P AutoTechInsight/Autocar Pro, 2026].
- **PM E-DRIVE:** e-2W incentives end 31 Jul 2026; framework extended to 31 Mar 2028 for select categories (buses, trucks, charging) [Studds AR FY26 citing PIB].
- **Fuel-efficiency and safety rules:** CAFE III from 1 Apr 2027, with two-wheelers proposed to be brought in [Cartoq/BEE draft]. ABS for all L2 two-wheelers was proposed from 1 Jan 2026 (3–5% price rise), but was still "hanging… delayed" in Feb-2026 per Remsons — ~8% of Remsons' revenue (combi-brake parts) is exposed [ICRA 2W note Jun-2025; Remsons Q3 FY26 call]. Two BIS helmets with every new 2W proposed [Tribune] — a tailwind for Studds. ADAS for M&HCVs and buses notified from Oct-2027 (may slip to Jan-2028), ~5 lakh vehicles a year; PV Level-2 draft for 2029 [Sterling Tools Q1 FY27 call].
- **Rare-earth magnets:** China has required export licences since Apr-2025; light magnets partly released to a few Indian firms, heavy magnets still restricted [Outlook Business; Deccan Herald]. India approved a **₹7,280 cr sintered-magnet scheme (26-Nov-2025) for 6,000 t/yr**, five bidders, production in 2–3 years [PIB/PM India]. CRISIL flagged rare-earth shortages as a hit to Alicon's H1 FY26 [CRISIL Aug-2026 rating on Alicon]. Sona BLW, Sterling (magnet-free motors via AEM, UK) and Sedemac are the most exposed/most active on alternatives.

## 4. US tariffs, export demand and China+1
- **US exposure:** ~US$6.24 bn of FY26 component exports go to the US (27% of exports); about half fall under the **25% Section 232 tariff** (up from a 10% duty) [BusinessToday, 7-Jul-2026; Business Standard, Feb-2026].
- **Interim trade deal (6–7 Feb 2026):** reciprocal tariff on India cut from 50% to 25% and then 18%; **preferential tariff-rate quotas for auto parts** otherwise under Section 232 [White House joint statement; Thompson Hine; AIR].
- **New overhang:** two US Section 301 probes — labour practices (concluded: 12.5% tariff, not stacked on Section 232) and "overcapacity" (open) — covering 16 countries [BusinessToday, 7-Jul-2026].
- CRISIL expects component exports +8–9% in FY27, "aided by tariff corrections in the United States, the sector's largest export destination" [CRISIL, 3-Jun-2026].
- **US heavy trucks (forgings, castings, axles, gears):** Class 8 orders finished the 2026 order season +39%; trailing-12-month orders to Aug-2026 were 350,677 (+111%); H2 2026 build slots oversubscribed by ~35,000; the **EPA 2027 NOx "pre-buy" has ended**, and 2027 compliance adds US$8,000–12,000 per truck [ACT Research; FleetOwner, Sep-2026]. Read-through: strong H2 CY2026 for Ramkrishna Forgings, Bharat-Forge-type exporters, Alicon's CV programmes; risk of a 2027 air-pocket.
- **Europe:** weak. EU e-truck registrations only 4.8% in H1 2026 [Precision Camshafts Q1 FY27 call citing ACEA]; Alicon's Slovak unit revenue −34%, PCL's Europe revenue −14%, EMOSS halved [deep files 47–48]. EU–India FTA signed early 2026; management teams expect only gradual RFQ flow [Remsons Q3 FY26 call].
- **China+1 is real but slow:** Remsons won Stellantis North America cables (₹300 cr+/7 years) displacing Chinese supply; Alicon got tooling transfers from Indian OEMs; ACMA notes FTAs and "growing global confidence" [Remsons, Alicon deep files; ACMA Jul-2026]. At the same time, imports from China (36%) keep rising in electronics/EV parts.

## 5. Input costs
- Raw materials are **~three-quarters of sector costs; steel and aluminium are 50–60% of inputs**; OEMs pass through with a 1–2 quarter lag, "not always in full" [CRISIL, 3-Jun-2026].
- **Aluminium:** LME ~US$3,750/t in early June 2026 (highest since 2022) on West Asia/Hormuz disruption; US$3,282/t on 21-Sep-2026; Goldman sees US$3,300/t Q3 2026 and US$2,950/t average 2027 [AlCircle/Discovery Alert/metalcharts]. Alicon saw a ₹30–35 cr quarter-on-quarter cost hit in Q4 FY26 [Alicon Q4 call].
- **Steel:** Indian HRC ~₹64,000/t and CRC ~₹75,000/t in Sep-2026, a four-year high, expected to stay firm through H2 FY27 [BigMint via Business Standard, 22-Sep-2026]. Sterling Tools: low-cost steel inventory exhausted by Q2 FY27 [Sterling Q1 call].
- **Lead:** ~US$1,905/t (4-Sep-2026), range-bound near US$2,000 [metalcharts; Fastmarkets via INN] — neutral for Exide/Amara Raja.
- **Petrochemicals:** styrene-based plastics up ~65% (₹135 → ₹225/kg) at the April peak because India imports ~98% of styrene via the Gulf [Studds Q1 FY27 call].
- **Labour:** Haryana minimum wages +35% from 1-Apr-2026 (Studds, Alicon's Binola plant, Sterling's NCR plants); new Labour Codes (Nov-2025) forced one-time gratuity charges (Alicon ₹5 cr) [deep files 46, 47, 49].
- **Margin outlook:** CRISIL cuts FY27 component EBITDA margin to **10.5–11% from ~12%** (−100–150 bps); inventory up 15–20 days from 80–85 days as firms build buffers [CRISIL, 3-Jun-2026]. CareEdge flags a Q1 FY27 margin drag from copper, aluminium, steel, rubber, crude-linked inputs, freight and energy [CareEdge, Aug-2026].

## 6. EV penetration
- **FY26 EV retail 24.5 lakh (+24.6%)**; electric 2W 14.0 lakh (+21.8%), 2W penetration 6.54% for the year and 9.79% in March-2026 [FADA, Apr-2026]. CareEdge: overall EV share 8.28% in FY26 (0.71% in FY20) [CareEdge, Aug-2026].
- **August 2026:** overall EV share 12.3% (9.5% a year earlier); 2W 10.7%, 3W 65.3%, PV 7.6%, CV 5.18% [FADA via Punjab Kesari, Sep-2026]. High fuel prices after the West Asia shock are pushing adoption [Alicon Q4 call; Sterling Q4 call].
- Component-level reality is slower: EV parts were 4.6% of OEM supplies [ACMA]; Sterling Tools lost ₹280 cr of EV-controller revenue in a year when Ola in-sourced; Alicon, PCL and Sterling all say EV programmes slipped 18 months to "3–5 years" [deep files 47–49]. Hybrids (Maruti/Toyota) are the near-term winner for engine/casting suppliers — Alicon sees hybrids growing 25–30% a year [Alicon Q1 call].

## 7. Replacement vs OEM mix
- Aftermarket is ~14% of ACMA turnover (₹1.08 lakh cr, +9%) and CareEdge's 11%; it grows steadily with the vehicle parc and formalisation [ACMA; CareEdge]. CRISIL earlier put replacement growth at 8–9% a year [CRISIL, May-2025].
- High-replacement names in our list: Exide and Amara Raja (batteries), Studds (helmets, distributors 55% of sales), Gabriel (shock absorbers), Suprajit/Remsons (cables); replacement cushions them when OEM volumes slow in H2 FY27 [deep file 46; company descriptions].
- OEM-heavy names (>85% OEM: Sterling 88%, Remsons 94%) carry the most exposure to the H2 FY27 volume slowdown [deep files 49–50].

## 8. Capex cycle and asset turns
- Industry capex ~₹27,000 cr in FY27, +10% [CRISIL, 3-Jun-2026]; ICRA had pencilled in ₹25,000–30,000 cr for FY26 [ICRA, Feb-2025].
- Plants are full after the FY26 volume surge: Sterling fasteners 90–95%, Alicon "close to 100%" (slide: ~80%), Studds 81–89%, PCL 80–85% [deep files].
- Typical new-capex asset turns quoted: Alicon 2.5–3x target (~4x claimed on the ₹125 cr leased plant); Sterling 1.8–2x; PCL 1.5–2x; Studds ~1.5x (derived) [deep files 46–49]. Brownfield/leased sites are favoured to cut lead times.
- Risk: capex committed at peak utilisation just as ICRA forecasts H2 FY27 volume declines in CVs and a high base in 2W/PV.

## 9. Competitive structure and where profit sits
- Profit sits with (a) electronics/ECU and EV-content makers where content per vehicle rises (Sedemac, Sona, Minda, Pricol — high multiples, 30%+ ROCE for Sedemac [dashboard]); (b) brand/replacement franchises with pricing power (Exide, Amara Raja, Studds 19% EBITDA in FY26); (c) niche export forgers with sticky global contracts. It is thinnest in commodity metal conversion (castings, basic machining) where margins are 9–11% and returns 7–11% ROCE (Alicon, PCL, Sterling consolidated) [Elara; deep files 46–49].
- Commodity pass-through (metal indexation monthly/quarterly) protects gross profit in rupees but compresses margin percentages when prices spike [Alicon, CRISIL].

## 10. Cycle position
- Domestic: late upcycle — record FY26 and H1 FY27 volumes, capacity near full, margins squeezed by inputs, forecasts pointing to low single-digit volume growth and a high base from Q3 FY27 [ICRA; CRISIL].
- Exports: US trucks in a pre-buy-driven peak ending in 2026; Europe weak; tariff overhang reduced by the Feb-2026 deal but Section 301 still open [ACT/FTR; BusinessToday].

Company targets imply faster growth than the industry: Remsons ₹900–1,000 cr by FY29–FY30 (~20% a year), Alicon "double by 2030", Studds mid-to-high teens in FY28, Sterling EV revenue +30–40% in FY28 [deep files 46–50]. My working assumption: 7–10% nominal industry growth in FY28–FY29 (vehicle volumes 4–6% plus content and exports), with margins recovering toward 11.5–12% if aluminium eases as Goldman projects for 2027.

## 12. Five key findings
1. **Volume growth halves in FY27, and H2 is the weak half.** Record Q1 (PV +26%, 2W +20%) came off the GST-cut base; ICRA sees FY27 at 3–6% with CV volumes falling in H2 [SIAM; ICRA].
2. **Margins are the story, not revenue.** Aluminium near four-year highs, HRC at a four-year high, styrene +65% and a 35% Haryana wage hike push CRISIL's FY27 margin call down 100–150 bps to 10.5–11%; reported revenue growth is flattered by metal pass-through [CRISIL; BigMint; company calls].
3. **EV content is growing more slowly than EV sales.** EV share hit 12.3% in August 2026, but EV parts are only 4.6% of OEM supplies and China supplies 36% of imports; cell plants (Exide Sep-2026, Amara Raja FY27) are just starting and ACC-PLI delivery is ~3% of target [FADA; ACMA; IEEFA].
4. **Exports: US trucks peak, Europe weak, tariffs eased but not solved.** Section 232 still covers half of US-bound parts (with a promised quota), a Section 301 overcapacity probe is open, and the EPA-2027 pre-buy has ended [BusinessToday; White House; ACT/FTR].
5. **Capex is rising at full utilisation.** ~₹27,000 cr of FY27 capex (+10%) is being committed while volume growth slows — returns depend on content gains, exports and hybrids, not on vehicle volumes [CRISIL; company calls].

## Suggested buckets
1. **EV, electronics and electrical content (10):** Sona BLW Precision, Sedemac Mechatronics, Minda Corp, Pricol, Motherson Sumi Wiring, Dhoot Transmission, Varroc Engineering, Divgi TorqTransfer, Lumax Industries, Fiem Industries — earnings driven by rising electronic/LED/EV content per vehicle rather than vehicle counts; highest multiples, rare-earth and China-sourcing risk.
2. **Batteries and brand-led replacement (3):** Exide Industries, Amara Raja Energy, Studds Accessories — consumer brands with distributor/aftermarket channels and pricing power; commodity inputs (lead, styrene) and new-energy capex (lithium cells, new helmet plant) set returns.
3. **Forgings, castings and precision metal parts (17):** Shriram Pistons, Craftsman Automation, Sundram Fasteners, CIE Automotive, Ramkrishna Forgings, ASK Automotive, Rolex Rings, Talbros Automotive, OBSC Perfection, Rajratan Global Wire, RACL Geartech, Rico Auto, Menon Bearings, Poojaa Precision, Alicon Castalloy, Precision Camshafts, Sterling Tools — metal conversion with indexation pass-through; capex-heavy, export- and ICE/hybrid-linked, margins 9–15%.
4. **CV, tractor, off-highway and rail systems (8):** ZF CV Control Systems, Automotive Axles, Wheels India, Steel Strips Wheels, Carraro India, Uniparts India, Kross, Frontier Springs — tied to truck/tractor/rail/construction cycles (domestic CV turning down in H2 FY27, US trucks post-pre-buy), heavier assets.
5. **Vehicle systems and assemblies for 2W/PV OEMs (12):** Gabriel India, Tenneco Clean Air, Sharda Motor, JTEKT India, Rane (Madras), Lumax Auto Tech, Sandhar Technologies, Suprajit Engineering, SJS Enterprises, NDR Auto Components, Belrise Industries, Remsons Industries — domestic OEM-supplied systems (suspension, exhaust, steering, cables, seating frames, decoratives); growth follows 2W/PV volumes plus content/regulation (BS/CAFE, safety), moderate margins.
