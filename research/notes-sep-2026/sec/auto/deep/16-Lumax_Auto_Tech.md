# Lumax Auto Technologies (LUMAXTECH) — deep research, Sep 2026

Figures from Screener's consolidated page unless tagged otherwise. Screener's "operating profit" excludes other income; the company's "EBITDA" includes it (management confirmed this on the Q1 call), so company EBITDA runs about ₹10–15 cr a quarter above Screener's number.

## Business and segments
- Multi-product auto-parts maker for Indian OEMs: advanced plastics and interiors (IAC India, now merged in), structures and control systems (2W frames, gear shifters via Lumax Mannoh, Cornaglia), mechatronics (sensors, switches, antennas, telematics through JVs with Alps Alpine, Yokowo, Ituran, FAE), alternate fuels (Greenfuel CNG systems, 60% bought in FY25) and an aftermarket arm. 28 plants in 7 states, 7 JVs/alliances, >500 engineers. [IP Q1FY27]
- FY26 segment revenue: advanced plastics ₹2,566 cr (+25%), structures and control ₹816 cr (+17%), Greenfuel ₹383 cr, mechatronics ₹281 cr (+~150%, from ₹115 cr), aftermarket +15%. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 segment revenue: advanced plastics ₹769 cr (+47%; IAC is ~60% of it), structures and control ₹220 cr (+21%), Greenfuel ₹111 cr (+17%), aftermarket ₹104 cr (+6%), mechatronics ₹84 cr (+56%). Advanced plastics is 56% of revenue. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Vehicle mix FY26: passenger vehicles 53%, 2/3-wheelers 24%, aftermarket 10%, commercial vehicles 9%. [Q4 call]
- Customers: Maruti Suzuki is the largest (Q1 growth 48%), then Mahindra (dominant at IAC; >90% of IAC's order book), Bajaj (Q1 revenue +64% on Chetak frames and lighting). CRISIL says two customers give 40–45% of group revenue. [Q1 call; Q4 call; CRISIL May-26]
- Capacity: some verticals run with 20–30% spare capacity; mechatronics and IAC are capacity-constrained, hence new plants. [Q4 call]
- Segment margins (management, not audited): advanced plastics 16–20% EBITDA; Greenfuel ~20% underlying (23% in Q1 incl. ~₹3 cr one-off tooling); mechatronics target 14–15% within 12–24 months. [Q1 call]
- Content per vehicle: ₹70–75K per 4W and ₹22–25K per 2W, both said to have grown 5x in five years (company claim). Portfolio pitched as "EV agnostic". [IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers
- Annual (Screener): revenue FY24 ₹2,822 cr → FY25 ₹3,637 cr → FY26 ₹4,870 cr (+34%). Operating profit ₹369 cr → ₹465 cr → ₹659 cr; OPM 13% → 13% → 14%. Net profit (before minority) ₹167 cr → ₹229 cr → ₹337 cr. [Screener]
- Company-reported FY26: revenue ₹4,870 cr, EBITDA ₹705 cr (14.5%), PAT ₹337 cr (+47%). FY26 tax rate 17% on Screener includes a one-off deferred-tax reversal in Q3 (Greenfuel merger with its SPV); underlying ~26%. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Quarters (Screener; revenue / op. profit / OPM / net profit): Q1 FY26 ₹1,026 / 125 / 12% / 54; Q2 ₹1,156 / 155 / 13% / 78; Q3 ₹1,271 / 176 / 14% / 108 (tax −7%); Q4 ₹1,417 / 203 / 14% / 98; Q1 FY27 ₹1,364 / 190 / 14% / 99. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 company view: revenue +33%, EBITDA ₹205 cr (+51%), margin 15.1% (+190 bps YoY, +40 bps QoQ), PBT ₹132 cr (+78%), PAT before minority ₹99 cr (+83%). Minority share 12%. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Interest cost rising with acquisition debt: ₹69 cr FY24 → ₹80 cr → ₹106 cr FY26; depreciation ₹179 cr FY26 (Q4 included a Greenfuel intangible life reclassification). [Screener; Q4 call]
- ROCE 18% FY24 → 19% → 21% FY26; ROE 26.4%. [Screener]
- Industry backdrop: Q1 FY27 production +22% (PV +17%, 2W +23%, CV +15%, 3W +39%) off a weak pre-GST-cut base; management expects growth to slow sharply in H2 as the base rises. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (1 Jun 2026): no hard revenue number; "outperform industry", some businesses 2–3x industry growth; mid-term 20% CAGR over 3–5 years. Margin to "sustain or go up by at least 30 bps" from 14.5% despite commodity, wage and energy inflation recovered with a 3–6 month lag. Capex ₹275–300 cr. Order book ₹1,450 cr (25% FY27, 54% FY28, 21% FY29). [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: revenue +33%, margin 15.1% (already +60 bps vs FY26), order book up to ₹1,601 cr (24% FY27 / 56% FY28 / 20% FY29). Capex only ₹23 cr in Q1 vs ₹300 cr full-year guide. **Delivered or beat on growth and margin; capex back-ended.** [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 call: guidance kept, not raised. "Unfair" to assume ₹6,000 cr revenue in FY27; 20% CAGR over FY27–FY28 "very safe". Margins at Q1 level sustainable for the year. FY27 EBITDA of roughly ₹800–850 cr and FY28 ~₹1,000 cr (15.5–16% margin, including other income). [Q1 call]
- Long-term "20.20.20.20" plan (FY26–31): ≥20% revenue CAGR, "inching closer" to 20% EBITDA margin in 5–7 years, 20% ROCE, 20% revenue from future/clean mobility (15% in FY26, 6% in FY25); revenue >₹10,000 cr by FY31. MD walked the margin back: 17–17.5% would make them "quite happy". Of the 20%, ~15% organic and the rest acquisitions. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Mechatronics: ~₹400 cr revenue in FY27, ~₹1,000 cr by FY30–31; order book ₹400–500 cr. [Q1 call]
- Aftermarket: guided 15%+ growth after a strategy change (retail pull, new products such as BlueChem); Q1 delivered only 6%. **Missed** for the quarter; full-year guide kept. [Q1 call]
- Minority interest guide changed: Q4 call said 15–17% of PAT for the year; Q1 call said 11–13% after Lumax FAE became 100% owned. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Product milestones: Q4 said body control modules (BCM) to start production in 45–60 days — Q1 confirmed BCM launched. Ituran's second telematics product (regulation-driven, new OEM) promised in ~3 months. RFID/telematics orders being finalised with all top four CV makers, launch by end-Q3 FY27. ADAS proof-of-concept results in about two quarters. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹233 cr, incl. ₹45 cr land in Gujarat and Kharkhoda and ~₹100 cr IAC and Lumax Alps Alpine expansions. [Q4 call]
- FY27 capex guide ₹300 cr (Q1 spend ₹23 cr): new IAC plant at Chakan (Mahindra demand), mega mechatronics plant at Manesar consolidating Lumax Yokowo, Alps Alpine, Ituran and FAE (commissioning Q3 FY27), new Greenfuel plant at Nashik for Mahindra's first CNG passenger model. [Q1 call]
- Funding: mostly internal accruals; 10–12% as debt at JV level. Management said the ₹300 cr covers delivery of the ₹1,600 cr order book and FY28–29 needs; more capex only if the order book rises. [Q1 call]
- Capital commitments ₹96.6 cr at Mar-26 (₹42.8 cr a year earlier). [AR FY26, cons. note 41]
- SHIFT software centre in Bengaluru: ~20 engineers, ₹5–7 cr a year. China representative office (~8 people, rising to ~15) to source technology tie-ups. [Q4 call]
- CRISIL expects ₹200–250 cr capex a year against accruals over ₹350 cr. [CRISIL May-26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Mar-26: free cash ₹396 cr; long-term debt ₹553 cr; total debt ~₹1,000 cr (company). Screener borrowings ₹1,231 cr (likely includes leases). >90% of long-term debt financed the IAC and Greenfuel acquisitions; repayments start FY27 and debt should "die down" in 3–4 years. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Jun-26: free cash ₹415 cr, long-term debt ₹508 cr, net long-term debt ₹93 cr, debt/equity 0.32 (Q4 said 0.46 — the basis changed or debt fell sharply; not reconciled). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Cash flow: CFO ₹265 cr FY24 → ₹290 cr → ₹460 cr FY26; free cash flow ₹214 cr FY26; CFO/operating profit 86%. [Screener]
- Working capital FY26: debtor days 76, inventory days 52, payable days 108, cash cycle 20 days; working-capital days −18. Suppliers fund the business. [Screener]
- Non-current investments jumped (₹162 cr → ₹313 cr, standalone, per analyst) mainly on mark-to-market of its 5% stake in sister firm Lumax Industries. [Q4 call]
- Dividend: Screener payout fell to 13% in FY26 (from 34% in FY22) because dividend was set on budgeted pre-merger standalone profit; board to review the policy for FY27. Stated internal floor is 35% of (standalone) profit. [Q4 call; Screener]
- CRISIL: AA/Stable reaffirmed May 2026 (upgraded from AA− during FY26); bank lines raised to ₹681 cr from ₹332 cr; cash surplus ~₹400 cr; long-term debt repayment ~₹150 cr a year from FY26; downgrade trigger net debt/EBITDA above 2x. [CRISIL May-26]

## Moat evidence
- Pricing pass-through: plastics (up 30–40% in Q1) are back-to-back with most OEMs, 80–90% recovered in the same quarter; margin still rose 190 bps. Electronics costs (up 30–50% for some parts) recover more slowly but mechatronics is only 6–7% of sales. [Q1 call]
- Leadership claims: Lumax Mannoh is the PV gear-shifter leader; already in production on three shift-by-wire platforms as automatic shifters migrate (seen 50:50 in ~36 months). Target top-2 in shark-fin antennas and in switches/sensors within 3–5 years. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Greenfuel: only 2–3 large players in CNG delivery systems; localising imported parts (ferrule tubes, sub-tanks); new Mahindra PV CNG win. [Q1 call]
- Customer awards FY26: Maruti vendor awards for Lumax Alps Alpine (localisation) and IAC (part development); Mahindra supplier excellence award for IAC. [Q4 call]
- Order book ₹1,601 cr = ~33% of FY26 revenue, of which 40% in "future and clean mobility"; mostly new business on top of base volumes (some replacement overlap). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Weakness: in the aftermarket, non-lighting products lost share when Lumax passed on price rises that rivals absorbed; MD admits it "cannot compete with the OEMs" in aftermarket pricing. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Concentration: two customers 40–45% of revenue (CRISIL); IAC is >90% Mahindra and a new IAC OEM is not expected before FY28–29. [CRISIL May-26; Q4 call]
- Auditor (Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP): unmodified opinion; emphasis of matter on the NCLT-approved mergers of Lumax Ancillary and IAC India (prior year restated). Key audit matters: price-adjustment liabilities owed to customers (judgement-heavy), and ₹39.8 cr of investments in three subsidiaries whose accumulated losses leave net worth below carrying value (no impairment taken). [AR FY26, standalone auditor's report]
- Audit trail: core accounting software lacked application-level logs for some privileged users and database-level audit trail; a third-party system could not be assessed. Common but a control gap. [AR FY26, auditor's report para vi]
- Contingent liabilities (group, Mar-26): income tax ₹30.6 cr, GST ₹4.5 cr, legal ₹1.1 cr — about ₹36 cr total, small against ₹1,211 cr net worth. A DRT bill-discounting case (₹7.8 cr decree) was won on appeal in Apr-2025 and the deposit refunded. [AR FY26, cons. note 41]
- Related parties: sizeable dealings with sister company Lumax Industries (promoter-controlled) — material RPTs approved by shareholders; transactions of roughly ₹379 cr in FY26 appear in the table (layout garbled in the PDF, row not certain), plus royalty ₹4.0 cr and rent paid to Lumax Industries and to the chairman. Loans to KMP (small). [AR FY26, cons. note 42]
- Deposits of ₹38.9 cr pledged as security for a subsidiary's loan. [AR FY26, standalone notes]
- JVs: one JV still loss-making at PBT; Lumax JOPP stake sold to partner because it dragged profit. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Acquisition-led growth: the 20% CAGR needs ~5% a year from deals while management admits private-equity activity has pushed up valuations. [Q1 call]
- Other: labour shortages in Apr–May 2026 (negligible impact claimed); electronics inflation; high base in H2 FY27. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Promoter holding flat at 55.98% for nine quarters; no selling seen. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "watchful" (inflation lag of 3–6 months, labour shortages, portfolio clean-up) to confident (same-quarter pass-through, record margin).
- Order book +₹150 cr QoQ to ₹1,601 cr; advanced plastics' order book up to ₹787 cr from ₹700 cr; mechatronics ~₹483 cr vs ₹400 cr.
- New themes: BCM launch, RFID/telematics for CV makers, ADAS proof of concept, FY28 EBITDA ~₹1,000 cr. Dropped: JOPP exit, labour.
- New weak spot: aftermarket +6% after +15% in FY26.

## My read
The business is improving on every visible measure: revenue up 34% in FY26 and 33% in Q1, margin up to ~15% (company basis), ROCE 21%, and an order book that grew to ₹1,601 cr. Much of the jump came from acquisitions (IAC, Greenfuel) and a low pre-GST-cut base, so organic growth will look slower in H2 FY27; the real test is whether mechatronics reaches ~₹400 cr this year and whether IAC ever wins a second big customer. At ~42x trailing earnings the price assumes the 20% CAGR plus slow margin gains hold for years, and that acquisitions are made at sensible prices. What would change the view: aftermarket growth staying in single digits, mechatronics margins stuck below 10%, a Mahindra slowdown, or a costly acquisition funded with debt.
