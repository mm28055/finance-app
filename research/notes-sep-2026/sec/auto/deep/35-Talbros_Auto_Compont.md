# Talbros Automotive Components (NSE: TALBROAUTO) — deep research, Sep 2026

Figures: Screener consolidated (Talbros standalone plus equity-accounted 50:50 JVs — Marelli Talbros Chassis Systems (MTCS) and Talbros Marugo Rubber (TMR) — whose revenue is **not** in Talbros sales; their profit share sits in profit). Management's "group turnover" adds 100% of JV revenue and is not comparable with Screener sales. Price ₹457, market cap ₹2,823 cr (Screener, 27 Sep 2026).

## Business and segments
- Four businesses: (1) gaskets and heat shields — ~50% share of India's gasket market, "3 times the nearest competitor", single source for five customers; (2) hot forgings (750–2,500 tonne presses), ~56% exported to JCB, Dana, Carraro, GKN, Musashi, JLR, BMW; (3) MTCS 50:50 JV with Marelli Suspension Systems Italy — control arms, axles, chassis parts, >90% PV (Maruti, Tata, JLR); (4) TMR 50:50 JV with Marugo Rubber Japan — anti-vibration rubber, ~90% Maruti. New: Lohum Talbros CarbonTech (49:51 JV with Lohum Cleantech) for recovered carbon black and devulcanised rubber. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- Q1 FY27 (company figures): total income ₹242 cr (+15%); gaskets & heat shields ₹164 cr (+21%, EBITDA ₹29 cr, +32%); forgings ₹78 cr (+4%, EBITDA ₹14 cr); MTCS ₹105 cr (+43%, EBITDA ₹17.5 cr); TMR ₹40 cr (+31%, EBITDA ₹6 cr, +57%). Group turnover ₹387 cr (+23%). Heat shields ₹17.2 cr in the quarter. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- FY26: gaskets ₹595 cr (+7%, EBITDA ₹105 cr); forgings ₹295 cr (+2%, EBITDA ₹51 cr, flat); MTCS ₹346 cr (+21%, EBITDA ₹62 cr, +35%); TMR ₹147 cr (+13%, EBITDA ₹19 cr); heat shields ₹58 cr; group turnover ₹1,383 cr. [Q4 call]
- Exports ~25% of revenue (Europe + UK ~80% of exports; target 35% by FY28). Maruti ~19–20% of the group's business, Tata Motors ~9%. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- New data-centre angle: gaskets in Cummins and Kirloskar generator engines; Cummins sales ₹81 cr → ₹90 cr (FY26) → ₹25 cr in Q1 FY27; data-centre-linked business "about ₹30–40 cr" a year, ~5% of gaskets. [Q1 call]
- EV sales ₹12.5 cr in Q1 FY27 (3.27% of sales) vs ~₹9 cr a year earlier; target ≥5% in two years. [Q1 call]

Company FY26: revenue ₹889 cr, EBITDA ₹155 cr (17.5%), PAT ₹104 cr. [Screener; Q4 call; CARE Dec-25]

- Other income is large (₹40 cr FY26 on Screener), and profit includes JV share; ~30%+ of PBT depends on non-operating/JV lines. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- Returns: ROCE 18% FY26 (21% FY24), ROE 15%. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (May 2026): FY27 growth "15% to 20%" (analyst confirmed "16% to 20%"; group aspiration 18–20%: ~₹150–160 cr from new orders = 11–12% + 6–7% market); "cross ₹1,000 cr" this year (earlier "₹1,100 cr" not repeated); EBITDA 17–18% ("definitely around 17%"); capex ₹103 cr (FY26 ₹51 cr); Q1 revenue "in line with the last quarter"; Stellantis chassis order (₹1,000 cr over years, ~₹150 cr a year) starts June with ~₹100 cr in FY27; forging ₹500 cr order from October (20% this year); Cummins billing from June; JLR EV from September; Kia heat shields full from April; MTCS +35–40%, TMR ~15%; Lohum JV operations July–October 2026. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue ₹242 cr ≥ Q4 (**met**); Stellantis SOP in Q1 (**met**); MTCS +43% and TMR +31% (**beat**); forging +4% (**missed** the "recovery"); margin 17.6% (at the low end). [Q1 call]
- Q1 guidance: group growth 18–20% kept; margin trimmed to "about 17-odd percent, maybe 17.5%"; gaskets +17% for FY27 (~₹680–700 cr); forgings ₹340–350 cr (+15–20%); FY30 targets gaskets ₹850 cr, forgings ₹600 cr (earlier FY27 forging hope of ₹400 cr abandoned — lost ₹30–40 cr Meritor business to US tariffs, plus weak Europe). [Q1 call, official]
- Pattern: large orders announced (~₹4,000 cr over three years) convert slowly — FY24's ₹326 cr order book gave ₹27 cr in year 1 and ₹51 cr in FY26; FY25's ₹431 cr gave ₹37 cr in FY26. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex ₹51 cr FY26 → ₹103 cr FY27: gaskets ₹16 cr (asset turn >3x), forgings ~₹60 cr (₹45–47 cr plant for the Marelli ~₹90 cr, Dana, GKN business; rest infrastructure), MTCS ~₹20 cr, TMR ~₹7 cr; to be done by Sep–Oct 2026, 25% done by May. MTCS's Gujarat plant pushed out to FY27–28 as Maruti/Tata SOPs moved. Funded from accruals. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Lohum JV: ~₹70 cr total investment over two years (CARE); Talbros 49%. [CARE Dec-25; AR FY26]
- Capital commitments only ₹4.1 cr at Mar-26 (standalone). [AR FY26 note 39]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹84 cr (Mar-26), falling; adjusted gearing 0.22x (Mar-25); interest cover 9.7x; cash ₹35.8 cr (Mar-26). [Screener; CARE Dec-25; IP Q1FY27]
- Operating cash flow ₹85 cr FY26 (CFO/OP 82%); FCF ₹46 cr. [Screener]
- Working capital is heavy and lengthening: debtor days 118 and inventory days 118 in FY26 (99 and 110 in FY24); working-capital days 71 (45 in FY24). CARE: 90–120 days credit to customers, ~30% of raw materials imported. [Screener; CARE Dec-25]
- Dividend payout only 4% of profit (yield 0.16%). [Screener]
- Investments ₹247 cr (JV stakes, fair-valued in standalone books). [Screener; AR FY26 auditor's report]

## Moat evidence
- ~50% gasket share; single source to five OEMs; "virtually the single source for Cummins" on the engine gasket. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Price edge in exports: "We give them a solution at a 15–20% cheaper price" than European vendors, some of which went bankrupt. [Q4 call]
- Order wins: TACL + JVs >₹1,000 cr of new orders over five years (~₹700 cr export, ~₹100 cr EV); earlier ₹1,000 cr Stellantis (via MTCS) and ₹500 cr forging (mainly JLR). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Margins steadily up: OPM 10% (FY15–FY20) → 16% (FY25–FY26). [Screener]

## Risks and red flags
- **Marelli JV partner distress**: Marelli is in court proceedings; a stake sale/restructuring update was promised "by 30th September". MTCS is the fastest-growing profit contributor (FY26 EBITDA ₹62 cr); a change of partner could affect technology, customers (Stellantis came via Marelli) or require cash. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Related party: sales of ₹75.4 cr in FY26 to QH Talbros Pvt Ltd (associate; Talbros owns 5.83%; promoter-family firm — Umesh Talwar is a promoter director there); shareholders asked to approve up to ₹125 cr in FY27, including royalty and dividend receipts. [AR FY26 AGM notice; CARE Dec-25]
- Slow order conversion and forging stagnation (FY24–FY26 forgings ₹277 → ₹290 → ₹295 cr). [CARE Dec-25; Q4 call]
- Europe weakness (BMW, GKN schedules cut), migrant-labour shortage in Haryana in Q1. [Q1 call]
- Working capital days rising; heavy dependence on JV profits and other income for reported profit. [Screener]
- Auditor's key audit matter: valuation of unquoted, fair-valued investments in group companies (high estimation uncertainty). Contingent liabilities small (₹4.5 cr; ₹2.55 cr Faridabad development charges). [AR FY26]
- Promoter holding 58.4%, stable; no pledge noted. Ratings: CARE A+ (Stable)/A1+ reaffirmed Dec 2025.

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: "the pain is over", order conversion story, record quarter. Q1: data-centre gaskets as a new theme, Marelli stake sale as a live issue, forging recovery pushed to H2. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Numbers: margin guidance softened from 17–18% to ~17–17.5%; FY30 divisional targets introduced (gaskets ₹850 cr, forgings ₹600 cr; "3–4 years" also quoted as gaskets ₹850–900 cr, forgings ₹650–700 cr). [Q1 call]
- New CEO Ashish Gupta (ex-CEO of MTCS) from 1 April 2026, spending ~50% of time on forgings. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## My read
The gasket franchise is strong and getting better (share, heat shields, data-centre engines), and the JVs — especially MTCS on the Stellantis order — are driving group growth of ~20%. But the listed company's own sales grew only ~5% in FY26; forgings have been flat for three years; announced order books convert at a fraction of their headline value; and roughly a quarter to a third of pre-tax profit comes from other income and JV share. The price needs mid-teens profit growth, which is achievable if forgings finally grow in H2 and the Marelli partner issue resolves cleanly. A messy Marelli exit or another flat forging year would cut the view.
