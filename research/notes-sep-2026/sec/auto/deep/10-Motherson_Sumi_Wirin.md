# Motherson Sumi Wiring India (MSUMI) — deep research

Research date 27 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore (company documents use ₹ million; converted). Screener = **standalone** page (the company has no subsidiaries, so Screener has no consolidated page), read from the raw HTML rows.

## Business and segments
- India's largest wiring-harness maker, a 2022 demerger from Motherson (SAMIL); jointly controlled by SAMIL and Sumitomo Wiring Systems (SWS, Japan); promoters hold 61.72%. Makes low-voltage and high-voltage harnesses, data/video cables and components for Indian OEMs only (no exports business of note). [AR FY26 MD&A; Screener]
- FY26 revenue mix: passenger cars 64%, two-wheelers 12%, commercial vehicles 10%, off-road/tractors 6%, others (Tier-1/2 suppliers) 8%. EV share of revenue 8.5% in Q1 FY27 (6.6% in FY26). [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]
- Deeply integrated with its promoters: wires, terminals, connectors and EV components come from SAMIL, child parts from SWS; land and buildings are leased from SAMIL; design, procurement, HR and infrastructure are shared central functions billed by SAMIL. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- Capacity: existing plants ran at ~80% utilisation at Q4; three greenfields — Kharkhoda (Haryana, ~80%), Navagam (Gujarat, ~60%, new model ramping), Pune (~50%, EV programme volumes below forecast). Greenfields did ₹443 cr revenue in Q4 FY26 and ~₹450 cr in Q1 FY27 against an original ~₹2,000–2,100 cr a-year expectation. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener): Jun-25 sales ₹2,494 / operating profit ₹244 / OPM 10% / PAT ₹143 · Sep-25 ₹2,762 / ₹280 / 10% / ₹165 · Dec-25 ₹2,887 / ₹262 / 9% / ₹149 · Mar-26 ₹3,335 / ₹274 / 8% / ₹167 · Jun-26 ₹3,407 / ₹258 / 8% / ₹145. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +37% (7 points from copper pass-through, the rest volume, content, premiumisation and new model launches) vs PV industry +17%; EBITDA ₹258 cr (+6%), margin 7.6% (9.8% a year ago); PBT ₹195 cr (+3%); PAT ₹145 cr (+1%). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹8,327 → FY25 ₹9,319 → FY26 ₹11,478 (+23%); operating profit ₹1,012 → ₹996 → ₹1,060; OPM 12% → 11% → 9%; PAT ₹638 → ₹606 → ₹625. 3-year sales CAGR 18% but profit CAGR only 9%. [Screener]
- Material cost rose to 67.5% of revenue in FY26 from 65.2%; copper is 24–28% of raw-material cost. Copper averaged ₹1,348/kg in Q1 FY27, up ~7% QoQ and ~53% YoY (₹883/kg in Q1 FY26). [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Unit economics: revenue grows with content per vehicle, but profit per rupee of sales is falling — operating profit barely moved in three years while revenue rose ~38%. [Screener]
- Q4 FY26 detail: revenue +33% YoY while the market grew in single digits; gross margin compressed ~2.9 points QoQ, mostly copper (copper up ~18% QoQ added ~2% to revenue); gross margin fell ~600 bps from Q1 to Q4 FY26 as copper rose three quarters in a row. [Q4 call]
- Employee cost: FY26 manpower cost grew ~25% (analyst estimate, not disputed) as three greenfields hired ahead of volumes; Q1 FY27 employee cost ~₹600 cr after the NCR wage hike. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Margin arithmetic: even full cost recovery lowers the percentage margin (a "denominator effect"), because pass-through adds the same rupees to sales and costs. Management concedes the old 34–35% gross margin will not simply return. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- ROCE 39% (FY26; 48% in FY24), ROE 32%. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (28 Apr 2026): FY26 capex ~₹190 cr and FY27 "on similar lines" (analysts quoted ~₹200 cr) for greenfield expansion, automation and replacement; copper lag cost 2–2.5% at the bottom line in Q4, to be recovered in Q1 "as per the contractual terms"; greenfields to reach company-level margins "in a couple of quarters"; new capacity once utilisation passes 80%, backed by firm orders. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: revenue beat, but margin fell further to 7.6% because a new shock arrived — minimum-wage increases of 30–40% in the NCR region, fully booked in Q1 employee cost (~₹600 cr); a 7% copper lag still sits in the quarter (vs 17% in Q4). Greenfield revenue flat QoQ at ~₹450 cr, at break-even only before copper-lag effects; EBITDA contribution pushed out "one or two quarters". [Q1 call]
- No numeric margin or revenue guidance; recovery of wage costs is "constructive discussions" with no timeline; ROCE target above 40% reiterated; new expansion plans to be announced "in the next quarters", funded internally. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex ~₹190 cr in FY26; FY27 similar. Screener: cash used in investing ₹200 cr; fixed assets ₹779 cr — asset-light (sales ~15x fixed assets). [Q4 call; Screener]
- Next wave: new greenfields to follow OEM expansions (Aurangabad/Sambhajinagar PV hub was raised by analysts); each greenfield front-loads manpower before volumes arrive, as FY26 showed (₹127 cr net greenfield start-up cost, per an analyst on the Q4 call, not disputed by management). [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹40 cr at 30 Jun 2026 (external debt ₹61 cr, cash ₹21 cr) plus lease liabilities ₹199 cr, versus net cash ₹56 cr at Mar-26 — working capital absorbed cash as copper rose. [IP Q1FY27]
- Cash from operations ₹761 cr in FY26 (92% of operating profit; FY25 only 56%); free cash flow ₹561 cr. Debtor days rose from 39 (FY24) to 60 (FY26); inventory days 82. [Screener]
- Dividend payout 62% of FY26 profit; yield 1.69%. A 1:2 bonus issue was made in FY26. [Screener; AR FY26]
- Rating: India Ratings reviewed on 2 Jul 2026 (page could not be read); CRISIL last reviewed 1 Oct 2025. Not material — debt is negligible. [Screener documents]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and customer lock-in: supplies nearly every major Indian OEM; harnesses are designed in 2–3 years before a model launches, and MSWIL is a design partner for a Japanese OEM's future vehicles. [Q1 call]
- Technology and components from Sumitomo (world's #2 harness maker) and SAMIL — hard for local rivals to match. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- Growth far above the industry (+37% vs PV +17% in Q1; +33% in Q4 FY26 vs single-digit industry) shows share gains and rising content per vehicle. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Contractual copper and currency pass-through (3-month lag for most customers, 6 months for a few). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Readiness for new architectures: supplies data and video cables used in zonal designs; management says content keeps rising as OEMs add features, and that full simplification is seen in "very few vehicles" globally. [Q1 call]
- Limits: labour-intensive product; wage and other non-copper costs are negotiated, not automatic; zonal/48V architectures could simplify harnesses long term (management sees no de-contenting yet). [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party dependence (auditor's key audit matter):** FY26 purchases of goods from the jointly-controlling group ₹5,243 cr (FY25 ₹3,887 cr) plus ₹216 cr from other related parties — about 70% of the ₹7,742 cr material bill; rent/lease payments to it ~₹99 cr. The auditor flagged arm's-length pricing of these transactions as a key audit matter. [AR FY26 note 36; auditor's report]
- **Royalty/management fees:** management fee ₹60.9 cr (FY25 ₹54.5 cr) to SAMIL, equal to the royalty SAMIL pays SWS; shareholders were asked to approve FY27 transactions with SWS including royalty of up to ₹75 cr and purchases of ₹922 cr. [AR FY26 MD&A; AGM notice in AR FY26]
- **Structural margin decline:** OPM from 14% (FY21) to 9% (FY26) to 7.6% (Q1 FY27); management says it is "a return on capital employed focused company", not margin-focused, and that even after cost recovery the material ratio "will not be the same". [Screener; Q1 call]
- **Wage-cost shock:** 30–40% NCR minimum-wage hike absorbed; other states may follow; recovery from OEMs is uncertain in timing and amount. [Q1 call]
- **Greenfield under-utilisation**, especially Pune (EV volumes below forecast). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities** modest: indirect-tax claims ₹37.5 cr (FY25 ₹40.5 cr), workmen claims ₹3.3 cr. [AR FY26 note 39]
- **Disclosure gaps:** no plant-level or high-voltage vs low-voltage split; management declined to quantify the wage impact or the copper element separately beyond the 7%. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: copper lag (−2 to −2.5% at bottom line) and slow Pune ramp were the issues; tone "transitional", greenfields "contributing". Q1: copper still rising, plus a new wage shock; greenfields stalled QoQ; closing remark called it "a work in progress quarter". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- EV share disclosed at 8.5% (up from 6.6% FY26). [IP Q1FY27]
- Net cash flipped to small net debt. [IP Q1FY27]

## My read
Revenue is compounding fast and share gains are real, but the business is converting less and less of it into profit: operating profit is roughly where it was in FY24 on ~38% more revenue, and Q1 margin hit 7.6%. Much of the cost base (materials, rent, royalty, shared services) flows to the promoter group, which the auditor highlights, so minority holders depend on arm's-length pricing. The price needs roughly mid-teens profit growth; that requires margins to recover toward 9–10% as copper lags unwind, wage costs are passed on and greenfields fill. Watch Q2–Q3 margins, any OEM wage settlement, greenfield revenue moving past ₹500 cr a quarter, and further state wage hikes.
