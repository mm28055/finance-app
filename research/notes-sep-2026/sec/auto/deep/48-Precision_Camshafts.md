# Precision Camshafts (NSE: PRECAM, BSE: 539636) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Price ₹121, market cap ₹1,146 cr, P/E 36.4, ROCE 7.3%, ROE 3.0%, 52-week range ₹104–208 (−42% from high) [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- One of the few makers worldwide of all four camshaft types (cast iron, ductile iron, hybrid, assembled); 150+ variants, mainly passenger-vehicle engines; also balancer shafts and fuel-injector parts [CARE Sep-25; AR FY26].
- Customers: Ford, GM, Bosch, Hyundai, Maruti Suzuki/Suzuki Gujarat, Tata Motors, Mahindra, Renault-Nissan; top five ~40% of FY25 consolidated sales; two customers each above 10% of group revenue in FY26 [CARE Sep-25; AR FY26 Note 34].
- Group: standalone camshafts (Solapur); MEMCO Engineering (Nashik, precision parts for Bosch, Delphi, Endress+Hauser); EMOSS (Netherlands, electric drivelines for trucks/buses); e-HCV retrofit business in India. German subsidiary MFT (acquired 2017) filed for insolvency and was deconsolidated in FY26 [AR FY26; Q4 call].
- One reportable segment; geography FY26: India ₹438 cr (+4.8%), outside India ₹335 cr (−25%) — Europe ₹172 cr, Asia ₹42 cr (half of FY25), others ₹122 cr [AR FY26 consolidated Note 34].
- Utilisation: 80–85% overall — foundry ~80%, machine shop ~90% [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 split: standalone total income ₹173.3 cr, MEMCO ₹14.1 cr (EBITDA ₹1.1 cr), EMOSS ₹13.8 cr (EBITDA loss ₹4.6 cr; Q4 revenue was ₹29.5 cr) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (22 Jun 2026):** new orders worth ~₹1,500 cr cumulative lifetime revenue over 5–6 years, above the existing business; capex ₹100–120 cr over three years, incremental revenue 1.5–2x capex at peak in 2.5–3 years (the opening remarks said "more than 2x"); new Solapur plant (10 lines, ~2 lakh machined camshafts a month) to start production in Q1 FY27, "big jump" from April 2027; e-HCV homologation in FY27 and sales from April 2027 (one MOU worth ₹60–70 cr a year); 29 MW solar saving ~₹24 cr a year; EMOSS "stable" but no growth expected [Q4 call, official].
- **Q1 FY27 call (28 Aug 2026):** new programmes with Mahindra, Tata Motors and Maruti Suzuki started production; ₹1,500 cr order book repeated (now "four to five years"); EMOSS revenue halved and management would not rule out winding it up; looking for acquisitions in India; no new numbers on capex, margins or growth [Q1 call, official].
- Delivery: standalone revenue grew 6.6% q-o-q; consolidated operating margin fell to 3.8% (Screener) — EMOSS loss and cost inflation. No numeric guidance was given, so nothing formally cut, but the EMOSS "stable" line from June was abandoned within 10 weeks.

## Capex and capacity
- Capex plan ₹100–120 cr over three years (foundry, machine shop, automation, Solapur plant) [Q4 call]. Capital commitments ₹50.1 cr at Mar-26 (₹44.0 cr Mar-25) [AR FY26 Note 32]. CWIP ₹65 cr [Screener].
- Solapur plant civil works complete; machines for first two projects arriving; phased up to 10 lines [Q4 call].
- Expected asset turn 1.5–2x on new capex → ₹150–250 cr extra revenue at peak (FY29–FY30) [Q4 call].
- Funding: internal cash; consolidated borrowings cut from ₹120 cr to ₹54 cr in FY26 [Screener].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash: investments ₹357 cr (quoted mutual funds ₹356.7 cr) vs borrowings ₹54 cr — cash and funds are roughly a quarter to a third of market cap [Screener; AR FY26 fair-value note].
- Operating cash flow ₹120 cr (FY26), FCF ₹52 cr; CFO has exceeded operating profit for three years [Screener].
- Inventory days 113 (139 in FY25), debtor days 59, cash conversion cycle 64 days (92) [Screener].
- Dividend ₹1 per share; standalone payout 164% of FY26 profit (129% in FY25) [Screener standalone].
- Rating CARE A/Stable, A1 (Sep-2025): strong liquidity, but "moderate profitability and modest return ratios" [CARE Sep-25].

## Moat evidence
- Scale and range in camshafts: among the few global suppliers of all four types; long OEM relationships (most customers >10 years) [CARE Sep-25].
- "Last man standing" in ICE parts: management says several big American OEMs have reversed EV plans and few suppliers are adding camshaft capacity [Q4 call].
- New awards from Maruti, Hyundai, Mahindra, Tata, Renault-Nissan (₹1,500 cr lifetime) show continued wins; programmes started in Q1 FY27 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Against: operating margin has fallen from 27% (FY15) to 9% (FY26) consolidated; exports shrinking; pass-through of raw material (pig iron, MS scrap, resin sand) lags 3–6 months [Screener; CARE Sep-25].

## Risks and red flags
- **MCA investigation:** CARE (Sep-2025) notes an investigation by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs under Section 210 of the Companies Act, with no formal order yet, and lists an adverse outcome as a downgrade trigger [CARE Sep-25]. I found no mention of it in the FY26 annual report — a disclosure gap to check.
- **Overseas capital destruction:** standalone investments in subsidiaries ₹162.7 cr, of which ₹103 cr already provided for impairment; loans to subsidiaries ₹92.6 cr with ₹27.5 cr provided — both are auditor key audit matters (MSKA & Associates) [AR FY26 auditor's report]. PCL Netherlands had negative net worth of ₹70 cr at Mar-25 [CARE Sep-25].
- **EMOSS:** revenue ₹13.8 cr in Q1 FY27 with ₹4.6 cr EBITDA loss; European e-truck share only 4.8% of registrations in H1 2026; possible wind-down [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Contingent liabilities** (consolidated): income-tax demand ₹17.0 cr on ESOP disallowance (₹3.4 cr paid under protest), GST ₹2.0 cr, stamp duty/PF/excise small; corporate guarantee for MEMCO ₹3.2 cr [AR FY26 Note 32].
- **Related parties:** small — purchases from promoter-linked Cams Technology (~₹0.7 cr) and Core Power (~₹1.4 cr); donation ₹0.35 cr to promoter trust; KMP pay ₹9.5 cr [AR FY26 Note 33].
- **Earnings dependence on treasury income:** other income (₹52 cr TTM) is close to the whole of pre-tax profit (₹64 cr TTM) [Screener].
- **Terminal-technology risk:** product is an ICE-only part; e-HCV retrofit and EMOSS attempts to diversify have not worked so far (Tata Ace retrofit dropped) [Q4 call].

## My read
PCL is a shrinking, cash-rich camshaft maker whose reported profits rest on treasury income and one-offs rather than operations — consolidated operating margin was 3.8% in Q1 FY27. The India business is the bright spot (new Mahindra/Tata/Maruti programmes, ₹1,500 cr lifetime orders), and the ~₹300 cr of net cash protects the downside. At a P/E in the mid-30s the price needs standalone growth to show up clearly from April 2027 and the European losses to stop. The view would improve with a clean exit from EMOSS and standalone margins back in the mid-teens; it would worsen with any adverse MCA finding or more money sent overseas.
