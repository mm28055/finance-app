# SPR Auto Technologies (formerly Shriram Pistons & Rings) — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Screener code SHRIPISTON (BSE 544344). Renamed SPR Auto Technologies Ltd effective 2 Apr 2026.

## Business and segments
- Legacy (standalone) business makes pistons, piston pins, piston rings and engine valves for 2W, PV, CV, tractors, gensets, compressors, marine, railways and defence; sold to OEMs, the aftermarket (brands SPR and USHA) and exports to 30+ countries. Claims #1 exporter of these parts from India. [IP Q1FY27]
- Standalone FY26: revenue ₹3,527 cr (+10.9%); domestic revenue ₹3,031 cr, exports ₹495 cr. Exports have been flat for four years (₹484 cr FY23, ₹518 cr FY24, ₹484 cr FY25, ₹495 cr FY26). Europe is the largest export region (about 46% of exports per the FY26 pie). [IP Q1FY27]
- Powertrain-agnostic subsidiaries: SPR Takahata (62%, precision injection-moulded parts, Neemrana), SPR TGPEL (100%, moulds and moulded parts, Noida), SPR EMFi (72.58%, EV motors and controllers, Coimbatore), Karna Intertech (100%, die-casting moulds, bought 1 Apr 2025), and three ex-Antolin interior and lighting companies (100%, bought 8 Jan 2026: headliners, door panels, sun visors, consoles, pillar trims, ambient lighting; 5 plants in Chakan, Pune and Chennai). [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 AOC-1]
- Management says powertrain-agnostic businesses are "over 35%" of consolidated total income, and "nearly 60%" of the business is insulated from EV risk (counts non-auto, aftermarket and exports as insulated). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- No segment reporting: the company reports a single "automotive components" segment and does not give quarterly subsidiary revenue or margin; the CEO refused a breakup on both calls. One customer accounted for ₹581 cr (13%) of FY26 group revenue (₹458 cr in FY25). [AR FY26 note 38; Q1 call]
- Technology partners: Kolbenschmidt (pistons, since 1972), Riken/NPR-Riken (rings, since 1978), Honda Foundry (pistons), Fuji Oozx (valves), Takahata (moulding), Antolin (interiors, long-term licence). NPR-Riken Niigata owns 21.30% of the company. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note on shareholding]
- Capacity: 14 manufacturing plants, 8 assembly units. Management will not give utilisation or peak revenue ("capacity is a dynamic number"). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarters (sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 963 / 195 / 20% / 135; Sep-25 1,016 / 207 / 20% / 142; Dec-25 1,023 / 206 / 20% / 126; Mar-26 1,456 / 268 / 18% / 159; Jun-26 1,474 / 258 / 18% / 148. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 vs Q1 FY26: sales +53%, operating profit +32%, net profit only +10%, because interest rose from ₹9 cr to ₹34 cr and depreciation from ₹32 cr to ₹53 cr after the Antolin purchase. [Screener]
- Company's own Q1 FY27 numbers: consolidated total income ₹1,499 cr (+51%), EBITDA incl. other income ₹283 cr (+27%, margin 18.9% vs 22.5%), PBT ₹195 cr (+7%), PAT ₹148 cr (+9%). Management put the hit from commodity and supply disruption at ₹30 cr of EBITDA and extra finance cost at ₹25 cr. [IP Q1FY27]
- Standalone (legacy) Q1 FY27 is weak underneath: revenue ₹942 cr (+11.7%), EBITDA incl. other income ₹202 cr (flat), margin 21.0% vs 23.5%, PAT ₹112 cr (−14%), with ₹23 cr extra finance cost carried in the parent for the Antolin NCDs. [IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: FY24 sales 3,089 / OP 642 (21%) / PAT 439; FY25 3,550 / 724 (20%) / 516; FY26 4,459 / 876 (20%) / 561. TTM sales 4,970, PAT 575. 5-year sales CAGR 23%, profit CAGR 45%. [Screener]
- FY26 includes a ₹27 cr exceptional charge for the new labour codes (standalone ₹24 cr). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- ROCE fell to 21% in FY26 from 26–28% in FY24–FY25 as ₹1,708 cr of acquisition capital came in. [Screener; AR FY26 KAM]
- Unit economics: management says Q1 production volumes rose 22% while standalone revenue rose about 12%, blaming mix (smaller vehicles post-GST cut) and lower exports; realisation per piece fell. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (12 May 2026): no numeric revenue guidance. Promised to "outgrow the end market" (FY26 legacy growth ~11% vs weighted market 6–7%); capex of about ₹200 cr a year for 2–3 years ("more than less"); Antolin margins (said to be 9–10% at purchase) to move towards standalone levels (20%+) in "even lower than 3 years"; ₹1,000 cr QIP for growth and M&A, NCDs of ₹500 cr (18 months) and ₹500 cr (24 months) to be repaid on schedule, not early; commodity costs are 100% pass-through with a one-quarter lag. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (5 Aug 2026): delivered 51% total-income growth (Antolin-led); organic (ex-Antolin) growth "over 16%"; Antolin margin now "early mid-teens" from 7–8% (note the base was stated as 9–10% on the Q4 call); commodity lag cost ₹30 cr EBITDA, expected to be recovered "within this next quarter"; net debt ₹550 cr at June, net debt/equity 0.2x and never above 1x; consolidated margins aim for "high teens". [Q1 call]
- Track record: capex promise on track (Sunbeam lines bought and commissioned, Takahata new plant started, TGPEL second plant filling); QIP delivered (₹1,000 cr raised in Aug 2026); margin promise mixed — consolidated EBITDA margin fell from 22.5% to 18.9% YoY and standalone margin fell 250 bps, so the "we are not going to lose on our margins" claim still needs Q2 proof. Exports promise ("we will continue to grow") not delivered: flat for four years and weaker in Q1. [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Guidance status: kept (qualitative); no numbers raised or cut.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex close to ₹200 cr in capacity (Ghaziabad, Pathredi, Pithampur Phase 3, Takahata Neemrana, TGPEL Noida); similar or higher each year for FY27–FY29. [Q4 call]
- Antolin purchase: 100% of Antolin Lighting India and Grupo Antolin India and 99% of Grupo Antolin Chakan for ₹1,708 cr, funded by ₹1,000 cr NCDs plus cash; goodwill on the group balance sheet now ₹1,323 cr. [AR FY26 KAM]
- Sunbeam Lightweighting (a Craftsman Automation subsidiary) piston plant and machinery bought and commissioned in Q1 FY27; price not disclosed on the call. [Q1 call]
- Takahata Phase 4 plant (called Phase 3 on the Q4 call) being built next to Neemrana plant on 5 acres; revenue expected from early 2027. TGPEL second Noida plant filling up. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- EMFi Coimbatore motor/controller plant commissioned Dec 2025; sales doubled in FY26; "adding one winding machine raises capacity 15%+". Technology from EMFi Singapore, Wuxi Lingbo (controllers), Shenzhen Greatland (motors); products 2 kW–250 kW, up to 800 V, hairpin winding. [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Funding: ₹1,000 cr QIP closed 20 Aug 2026 — 23,36,448 shares at ₹4,280 (3.6% below the ₹4,438.20 floor), about 5% dilution; promoter holding fell to 41.55% from 43.75%. [News: ScanX/InvestyWise, Aug 2026; Screener shareholding]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings rose to ₹1,968 cr at Mar-26 from ₹508 cr (₹1,000 cr NCDs for Antolin). India Ratings put consolidated cash at ₹1,160 cr at FYE26, so net debt was roughly ₹810 cr in March; management said ₹550 cr at June. After the Aug-26 QIP the group is close to net cash. [Screener; News: India Ratings via search, Feb 2026; Q1 call]
- Consolidated debt/equity 0.62x at Mar-26 vs 0.19x; interest cover still about 13.5x. [IP Q1FY27]
- Cash flow: CFO ₹625 cr in FY26 (₹434 cr FY25), FCF ₹437 cr; CFO/operating profit 91%. Investing outflow ₹1,815 cr (acquisition). [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 68 (60 in FY25), inventory 111 days, payables 121 days, cash conversion cycle 58 days (75). Trade receivables are pledged for bank working-capital lines (normal). [Screener; AR FY26 receivables note]
- Dividend: ₹5 interim + ₹5 final for FY26; payout only 8%. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Rating: India Ratings upgraded to IND AA+/Stable (Feb 2026). SPR Takahata separately rated IND AA-/Stable. [IP Q1FY27; News: India Ratings]

## Moat evidence
- Leading share in Indian pistons, rings, pins and valves; CEO claims "we lead the stack with a fairly good market share across all customers"; Antolin plus one peer hold about 75% of the headliner market (analyst claim, not denied). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Five decades of licensed technology (Kolbenschmidt, Riken, Honda Foundry, Fuji Oozx) with a 21.3% strategic holder (NPR-Riken) — hard for new entrants to copy. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- Content gains from regulation: CAFE-3 friction-reduction coatings and flex-fuel (E20–E85) pistons/rings carry higher prices ("the realization is also more"); hybrid engines redesigned with SPR on programmes launching 2029–2030. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Consolidation of a shrinking ICE supply base: bought Sunbeam's piston lines; Eaton closed a US valve plant (Eaton's business sold to Dana). [Q1 call]
- Standalone EBITDA margin rose from 15.9% (FY22) to 23.3% (FY26), a cost position few Indian auto-part makers match. [IP Q1FY27]
- Pass-through: commodity clauses with "all our customers" cover 100% of changes with a one-quarter lag. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Margin dilution from acquisitions: group margin now 18% vs 20–21% before Antolin; Antolin still well below standalone. Management's own starting margin for Antolin changed between calls (9–10% in May, 7–8% in Aug). [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Legacy growth lagging volumes: standalone revenue +12% against 22% production growth; standalone PAT −14% in Q1. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Exports stuck near ₹500 cr for four years; Europe (largest region), US and Middle East weak with "no immediate recovery". [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Disclosure: no subsidiary revenue/margin in quarterly reporting, no capacity or utilisation numbers, no customer names for EMFi, no QIP use-of-proceeds split. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Serial M&A appetite: "It could be INR 300 crores or ... INR 1,500 crores", fresh ₹1,000 cr equity raised with no stated target. Goodwill ₹1,323 cr is a key audit matter (impairment testing) along with Antolin purchase-price allocation. [Q1 call; AR FY26 KAM]
- Contingent liabilities ₹64 cr at Mar-26 (₹68 cr prior), including disputed income tax ₹27 cr (up from ₹3 cr), GST ₹11 cr and labour cases ₹19 cr. Small vs net worth of ₹2,900 cr. [AR FY26 note 39]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; KAMs are revenue recognition, Antolin business-combination accounting and goodwill impairment. Standalone report notes audit-trail could not be confirmed for one third-party-hosted software at database level. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Related parties: royalty/technical fees to collaborators (group royalty expense about ₹69 cr in FY26 vs ₹55 cr; column layout in the PDF is garbled, treat as approximate); KMP short-term pay ₹21 cr plus director commission ₹7.7 cr. Nothing unusual found. [AR FY26 cons. notes 31, 35; standalone RPT note]
- No promoter pledge found; promoter stake diluted only by QIP.

## What changed between the two calls

- New numbers: ₹30 cr commodity/logistics hit, ₹25 cr extra interest, Antolin margin "early mid-teens", net debt ₹550 cr.
- New issues: standalone margin down ~250 bps; exports explicitly worse (Europe, US, Turkey, Egypt); minimum-wage increases; analysts questioned why legacy revenue lagged volume.
- Positive: EMFi "continuously exceeding all targets"; Antolin won new programmes; hybrid and flex-fuel wins "from all our customers".

## My read
The legacy piston-and-ring franchise is still excellent (23% standalone margin in FY26, AA+ rating, strong cash flow), but Q1 FY27 showed its growth lagging vehicle production and its margin slipping 250 bps, which the company blames on a one-quarter commodity lag. Group growth is now bought: Antolin doubled the revenue base but at half the margin and brought ₹1,323 cr of goodwill, so earnings grew only 10% on 53% sales growth. At a price that needs strong growth, the case rests on Antolin reaching mid-teens margins, EMFi scaling, and the QIP money earning its cost. I would turn more positive if Q2 FY27 shows standalone margin back above 22% and group margin near 20%; negative if another large, low-margin acquisition is announced or exports keep shrinking.
