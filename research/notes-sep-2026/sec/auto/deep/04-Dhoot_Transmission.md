# Dhoot Transmission — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Screener code DHOOTTRANS (BSE 544867). Listed 17 Aug 2026; only one earnings call so far (Q1 FY27, 4 Sep 2026). No annual report as a listed company yet, so the IPO prospectus (Aug 2026) stands in for the annual report.

## Business and segments
- Makes wiring harnesses (low- and high-voltage), and "non-wiring" products: 2W EV battery-pack assemblies, controllers, sensors, relays, switches, EV cord sets and charging guns, DC-DC converters; backward-integrated into terminals, connectors, cables and moulded parts through subsidiary Dhoot Autocomponents (about ₹1,200 cr of internal, eliminated sales). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Q1 FY27 revenue split: wiring harness ₹1,090 cr (+44.6% YoY, from ₹753 cr), non-wiring ₹358 cr (+67.7%, from ₹213 cr; +55% excluding Multilink). By vehicle: 2W 67%, 3W 22%, others 11%. By powertrain: ICE 63%, EV 27%, others 10%. India 92%, overseas 8%. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Customers: top customer (Bajaj Auto) 31.8% of FY26 revenue, TVS Motor 19.6%; top five 72%, top ten 81%. Battery packs go to essentially one EV customer (a second, "down South", started supplies in the last month of Q1). [Prospectus; CRISIL Aug-26; Q1 call]
- Market position: about 41% of the domestic 2W/3W wiring-harness market (CRISIL), number one in 3W; the company cites ~38% (top-two) harness share and 70%+ in its leading category. [CRISIL Aug-26; IP Q1FY27]
- Capacity: 22 plants, 3 engineering centres; utilisation 74% in FY26 (64% in FY25). Management keeps harness utilisation near 75% so it can meet the festive-season peak. [CRISIL Aug-26; Q1 call]
- Ownership: promoters are BC Asia Investments XV (Bain Capital) and founder Rahul Dhoot; promoter group 82.78% after IPO. [Prospectus; Screener]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarters available (sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 966 / 169 / 18% / 96; Mar-26 1,277 / 179 / 14% / 95; Jun-26 1,446 / 218 / 15% / 133. Sep-25 and Dec-25 quarters are not on Screener (pre-listing). [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 vs Q1 FY26: revenue +49.7%, EBITDA +29% (margin 15.1% vs 17.5%), PAT +37.8%; Multilink added about 3 points of growth (20 days of ownership). EV revenue +79%. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Annual: FY24 sales 2,798 / OP 517 (18%) / PAT 299; FY25 3,445 / 594 (17%) / 354; FY26 4,525 / 713 (16%) / 397. Margin has slipped about one point a year. [Screener]
- FY26 includes exceptional costs of ₹20 cr: ₹12 cr Bain Capital advisory fee, ₹4.3 cr write-off of an investment in Evon SA (in liquidation), ₹4 cr labour-code impact. Q1 FY27 has a further ₹3 cr exceptional. [Prospectus note 33B; IP Q1FY27]
- Unit economics: EV content per vehicle is 1.5–2.5x ICE for harnesses alone and "up to 4x" if a customer buys the full product set. Copper is 20–23% of bill of materials. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- First call, so no earlier promises to test. Guidance given on 4 Sep 2026: FY27 revenue growth 25–30% (after ~50% in Q1, because H2 FY26 had a GST-led high base); EBITDA margin 15–16% for the year; Multilink to grow 25–30% at Dhoot-level margins, integration done by end-Q3/early-Q4; EV share of revenue above 30–32% in 2–3 years; a high-voltage PV harness JV to be disclosed "in our second earnings call". [Q1 call]
- Pre-IPO track record: revenue grew 23% in FY25 and 31% in FY26; CRISIL expected margins above 15%, which held in Q1 (15.1%) but is at the bottom of the guided band. [CRISIL Aug-26; Screener]
- Copper lag: prices rose 3–4% a month in July–August, so recovery is "probably no, not immediately in Q2. But in Q3". [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- IPO (10–12 Aug 2026) raised ₹3,067 cr at ₹871 a share: fresh issue ₹1,400 cr, offer-for-sale ₹1,667 cr by BC Asia Investments XV (Bain) and Mangalam Capital. Use of fresh money: ₹465 cr debt repayment, ₹302 cr to subsidiaries for their debt, ₹150 cr for two new harness plants (Jhajjar, Haryana; Shoolagiri/Hosur, Tamil Nadu — adding 15–20% capacity this year), ₹410 cr for unidentified acquisitions and general purposes. [News: Chittorgarh, Aug 2026; Prospectus; Q1 call]
- Multilink (Karnataka partnership; switches, relays, sensors, chargers; adds Hero MotoCorp) bought on a slump-sale basis for ₹435 cr, control from 11 Jun 2026; ₹421 cr already paid. Management expects 20–25% of the price to become goodwill. [Prospectus; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- FourFront Ltd merger into a subsidiary agreed for ₹400 cr (Feb 2026) but conditions were still unmet at Mar-26 with no extension agreed. [Prospectus note 43B]
- Ride Vision (Israel) 2W ADAS JV: Dhoot 67%, Ride Vision 33%, US$3 m; revenue depends on future Indian ADAS rules. [Prospectus note 43B; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt about ₹220 cr at June 2026 (after the Multilink payment); after the IPO money arrived in August, net cash "around 1,000 crores". Borrowings were ₹918 cr at Mar-26; CRISIL expects gearing below 0.1x by Mar-27. [Q1 call; Screener; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Bain's March 2026 equity infusion lifted net worth to ₹2,414 cr at Mar-26 from ₹977 cr; the IPO adds about ₹1,400 cr more. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- Cash flow: CFO ₹348 cr in FY26 (CFO/operating profit 67%); FCF only ₹19 cr after heavy capex; FY25 FCF was −₹65 cr. [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 64, inventory 78, payables 73, cash conversion cycle 69 days (64 in FY25). [Screener]
- No dividend. Rating: CRISIL upgraded to AA/Stable from AA- on 25 Aug 2026. [CRISIL Aug-26]

## Moat evidence
- ~41% share of 2W/3W wiring harnesses and number one in 3W; relationships with top-five customers average 13 years; no top-five customer lost in FY24–FY26. [CRISIL Aug-26; Prospectus]
- Supplies both LV and HV harnesses on "99%" of EV 2W programmes it serves; 40–45% share of new EV cord-set business for next year. [Q1 call]
- Integration: 75–80% of harness components now localised (imports down from 30–35% to 20–25%). [Q1 call]
- Returns: adjusted ROCE about 25% in FY26 (CRISIL); ROE 24% (Screener).
- Weakness in the moat: battery-pack assembly uses customer-supplied cells, so value added is limited, and a single customer takes most of it — management will not disclose its margin. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Customer concentration: Bajaj Auto 31.8%, TVS 19.6%; the fortunes of two OEMs drive half of revenue. [Prospectus]
- Payments to the promoter's adviser: under an April 2025 agreement Dhoot pays Bain Capital Advisors (India) ₹12 cr a year for five years, and on an IPO must pay a lump sum of ₹60 cr less fees already paid — so about ₹48 cr became payable on listing (shown as a ₹48 cr contingent liability at Mar-26). This is a related-party cost borne by all shareholders for services to a promoter. [Prospectus notes 36.2, 33B]
- Pledge at the promoter level: BC Asia Investments XXIII has pledged all its shares in BC Asia XV (the promoter) and BC Asia XVI to lenders (Deutsche Bank as agent); enforcement could change control of the promoter stake. [Prospectus risk factor 64]
- Other related-party dealings: ₹21.7 cr of plant and equipment bought from promoter-group Mangalam Capital in FY26, plus ₹2 cr rent. Small but worth tracking. [Prospectus RPT summary]
- Acquisition appetite: ₹410 cr earmarked for unidentified acquisitions plus a pending ₹400 cr FourFront deal, on top of Multilink. [Prospectus]
- Margin drift: OPM 18% (FY24) → 16% (FY26) → 15% (Q1 FY27); copper lag hurts in rising markets. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Contingent liabilities ₹60 cr at Mar-26 (₹48 cr Bain fee, ₹8.8 cr income tax, ₹2.8 cr GST). Auditor's FY24 emphasis of matter only concerned the special-purpose (IPO) accounts; opinion not modified. The FY25–FY26 restated numbers include a common-control merger of Dhoot Holdings. [Prospectus]
- Disclosure: presentation and call on the same day as results (an analyst complained; company promised to change); no battery-pack margin; ICE growth quoted as both 63% and 52.6% on the same call. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Only one call exists. Compared with the prospectus, the Q1 call added: a second battery-pack customer, Multilink closed and being integrated, net cash after the IPO, the HV PV harness JV in "advanced stage", and a firm 25–30% FY27 growth target.

## My read
Dhoot is a well-run, high-share harness maker riding the 2W/3W boom and electrification — Q1 revenue grew 50% and EV revenue 79% — and the IPO has turned a leveraged balance sheet into net cash. But margins have slipped each year to about 15%, half of revenue depends on two OEMs, and the Bain era has brought fees, a promoter-level pledge and an acquisition pipeline that could dilute returns. The stock now trades near 80% above its IPO price, so it needs the 25–30% growth to hold for years. I would watch Q2–Q3 FY27 for margin at or above 15% as copper stabilises, the size and price of the next acquisition and the HV PV JV, and how the ₹48 cr Bain payment is booked.
