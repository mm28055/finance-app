# Sedemac Mechatronics (NSE: SEDEMAC) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Pune engineering firm founded by IIT-Bombay technologists (Shashikanth Suryanarayanan, Amit Dixit, Manish Sharma). Listed 11 Mar 2026 through a 100% offer for sale of 80.4 lakh shares (₹1,087 cr, ≈₹1,352/share); the company raised no money [AR FY26 Board's report]. Promoters 26.2%, DIIs 39.8%, FIIs 4.3% (Jun-26) [Screener]. Price ₹3,445, market cap ₹15,216 cr, 52-week range ₹1,413–3,545 [Screener, standalone — no consolidated accounts; no subsidiaries].

**Only one earnings call exists** (Q4 FY26, 18 May 2026). Management said it will hold calls every six months, so Q1 FY27 has a presentation (28 Jul 2026) but no call [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Business and segments
- Designs and makes "control-intensive" electronic control units (ECUs): integrated starter-generator (ISG) controllers for 2W/3W, engine fuel-injection (EFI) controllers, combined ISG+EFI units, motor controllers (MCUs) for electric 2W/3W, and genset controllers. All technology in-house, no foreign technical tie-up [Q4 call; AR FY26].
- Core IP: "sensorless" motor control — runs the motor without a position sensor; it matters most on "wet magneto" motorcycles where a Hall-sensor ISG cannot be fitted [Q4 call].
- FY26 revenue ₹1,058 cr: **Mobility ₹911 cr (+61%)**, **Industrial ₹148 cr (+57%)**. EV MCU revenue ₹68 cr (7.4% of Mobility; ₹9.7 cr in FY25). Exports ₹86 cr (+89%, all gensets, mainly US) [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Volumes: 3.9 million control-intensive ECUs in FY26 (2.4 m FY25); TTM to Jun-26 4.2 million [IP Q1FY27]. ECUs are 85–90% of revenue [Q4 call].
- Market position: ISG used by 3 of the top-4 2W/3W OEMs and on 4 of the top-10 2W models; Sedemac supplied over 80% of India's FY26 ISG volume growth (ISG-fitted 2W/3W: 8.4 m in FY26 vs 5.1 m three years earlier, out of ~27 m produced) [Q4 call; AR FY26].
- **Concentration:** largest customer (a leading domestic 2W/3W OEM) 77% of FY26 revenue (80% FY25); ISG alone 67–68% of revenue [ICRA Sep-26]. The company's own metric (largest customer minus next four) was restated on 2 Jul 2026 from 49% to **58%** for FY26 (FY25 66%→68%, FY24 70%→72%) [Corrigendum to IP Q4FY26].
- Capacity: ~40 lakh units a year at MF1 (Chakan) and MF2 (Dhayari), running 80–85% [ICRA Sep-26; Q4 call]. MF3 (1.2 lakh sq ft, 3x MF1) started limited commercial production on 25 Sep 2026; MF4 (motors) due Q3 FY27; land bought at Shoolagiri, Hosur (MF5) [BSE filing 25-Sep-26; Q4 call].

- Unit economics: ~₹20 cr of plant capex and ₹12–15 cr of working capital per ₹100 cr of extra revenue (₹227 cr cumulative plant capex supported ₹1,058 cr) [Q4 call]. Industrial revenue is lumpy (fell 10% FY23→FY24) [Q4 call].
- R&D: ₹79 cr spent in FY26 (7.5% of revenue), of which ₹40.9 cr capitalised as an intangible asset (₹33 cr in FY25) — equal to about 39% of FY26 net profit [AR FY26 note 4D].

## Guidance history
- Company gives no revenue, capex or margin guidance and no order book ("this is not a project business") [Q4 call].
- FY27 outlook (May-26): drivers — ISG on variants of 3 top-10 motorcycles at 3 of the top-4 OEMs (two launches in Q1, one in Q4 FY27); further e2W MCU ramp-up; export 3W ISG ramp-up. Dampeners — semiconductor tightening and commodity inflation ("mild EBITDA % pressure"), El Niño risk to India 2W demand and US standby-generator demand [IP Q4FY26; Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 update (Jul-26): one motorcycle launch done, second "imminent" (so one of two Q1 launches slipped); e2W MCU and export 3W ISG "ramp-up in play"; RM costs did cut EBITDA margin; management expects margin "to hold or improve for rest of FY27 vs Q1FY27"; no El Niño effect yet [IP Q1FY27].
- New markets: CV after-treatment controller SOP H2 FY27; first power-tool motor-controller win (a concrete grinder) with SOP in 4–5 quarters; EV MCUs for LCVs [Q4 call].
- Verdict: **growth delivered (+43%), margin dampener materialised as flagged, launch timing slightly behind (1 of 2 Q1 launches)**.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 investing outflow ₹148 cr; free cash flow −₹47 cr [Screener]. FY27 capex ₹150–200 cr per ICRA; company declined to disclose [ICRA Sep-26; Q4 call].
- MF3 is "infrastructure first" — line capex added to match demand; would allow roughly 3x current output with the same mix [Q4 call].
- Capital commitments and funding: internal accruals plus small term loans; rated facilities raised to ₹214.95 cr [ICRA Sep-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹72 cr at Mar-26; gearing 0.2x; debt/EBITDA 0.3x; interest cover 26.5x (FY26) and 25.5x (Q1 FY27) [Screener; ICRA Sep-26].
- Cash and liquid investments ₹65 cr at Mar-26, of which ₹51 cr earmarked for IPO expenses (recoverable from selling shareholders); undrawn lines ~₹57 cr [ICRA Sep-26].
- Cash from operations ₹128 cr in FY26 (71% of operating profit) [Screener]; ICRA expects ₹120–150 cr a year.
- Working capital: inventory 106 days (semiconductor stocking), debtors 46 days (up from 24), payables 128 days; cash cycle 25 days [Screener]. ~75% of raw material is imported [ICRA Sep-26].
- No dividend.

## Moat evidence
- Patented sensorless ISG with "no material competition" in that segment (ICRA); the only global rivals named are Shindengen and, partly, Denso, mainly at Honda [ICRA Sep-26; Q4 call].
- Share: >80% of India's FY26 ISG volume growth; most domestic ICE 3W already use Sedemac ISG (all but one big maker) [Q4 call].
- Sticky, application-critical parts: OEMs invest heavily to integrate them; the ISG+EFI combo cuts OEM cost versus two separate units [Q4 call].
- Scale in motor controllers: >3 million a year — "nobody else except Shindengen" makes as many for mobility [Q4 call].
- Margin trend: EBITDA margin 15% (FY24) → 21% (FY26) with pricing described as "ossified" over long negotiations [Screener; Q4 call].
- Rating: ICRA upgraded to [ICRA]A (Positive)/A1 from A-(Stable)/A2+ on 15 Sep 2026 [ICRA Sep-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration:** 77% from one OEM; the company's own concentration chart in the May-26 presentation understated it and was corrected on 2 Jul 2026 [ICRA Sep-26; Corrigendum].
- **Capitalised R&D:** ₹40.9 cr of FY26 development cost sits on the balance sheet (KAM in the audit report); if expensed, FY26 pre-tax profit would be ~27% lower [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 4D].
- **GST search:** Maharashtra GST department searched the registered office and plants on 8–11 Sep 2026 under Section 67; company says no material impact; no demand disclosed yet [BSE filing 12-Sep-26].
- **Investor selling:** A91 Emerging Fund II sold 24.03 lakh shares (about 5.4%) on 25 Sep 2026, cutting its stake from 12.74% [SAST filing 25-Sep-26]. More pre-IPO investors may exit.
- Semiconductor/import dependence (~75% of RM), forex; El Niño exposure (India 2W and US standby gensets) [ICRA Sep-26; Q4 call].
- Auditor (standalone): unmodified opinion; KAMs on product-development capitalisation and revenue recognition; audit trail not enabled at database level [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Contingent liabilities small: ₹2.83 cr income-tax matter before Bombay High Court [AR FY26 note 41].
- Disclosure: no product-level margins, no capex guidance, calls only half-yearly [Q4 call].
- Tax rate fell to 19% in Q1 FY27 from 43% a year ago — flatters PAT growth [Screener].

## What changed between the two reporting points (Q4 call → Q1 presentation)
- Revenue growth slowed from +60% (Q4) to +43% (Q1), margin fell from 21.3% to 19.4% [IP Q4FY26; IP Q1FY27].
- Concentration metric corrected upward (Jul-26) [Corrigendum].
- After Q1: ICRA upgrade, GST search, MF3 start, A91 stake sale (all Sep-26) [BSE filings].

## My read
Sedemac is a genuinely differentiated electronics maker growing 40–60% a year with 20% margins, little debt and a strong patent position in sensorless control. But it is effectively a single-product (ISG ~68%), single-customer (77%) business, about ₹41 cr of R&D (≈39% of FY26 profit) is capitalised rather than expensed, and the stock already prices in years of fast growth — the price needs well over 30% earnings growth for a long time. What would change the view: ISG wins at a second large OEM moving the top-customer share below 60%, e2W MCU scaling past ~15% of Mobility, and a clean end to the GST search; a slowdown at the anchor OEM or further PE selling would weigh.
