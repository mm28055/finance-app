# Rane (Madras) (RML) — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. Figures: Screener consolidated (RML plus its small Netherlands holding company and Mexico plant) unless tagged. Both calls read in full against the official transcripts on the company site (the BSE filing of 27 Aug 2026 is only a cover letter pointing to the website).

**Important structural fact.** The calls are **Rane Group** calls. They also cover Rane Holdings (RHL, the listed parent), Rane Steering Systems (RSSL, formerly Rane NSK, an RHL subsidiary) and the ZF Rane / ZF Lifetec Rane joint ventures (RHL's 49% JVs). The FY26 annual report says RML "does not have any associate or joint venture". So the ZF seat-belt warranty provision (€23 mn) and RSSL's weak margins are **not RML's** [AR FY26 Board's report §1.7; RPT note 35].

- **Q1 FY27 stored transcript (21 Aug 2026): tidied copy (core figures match; gaps and three wrong peripheral figures).** Revenue, order wins (lifetime value ₹2,040 cr), capex ₹270–300 cr, peak debt ~₹1,100 cr, the 0.5x debt-to-equity target by March 2028 and the €23 mn provision all match. Wrong: RHL's effective tax rate is given as "almost 25%" (official: "almost 28%"); prior-year professional fees are given as ₹35 cr (official: ₹45 cr); the ₹70 cr capex is attributed to "RBSL, the subsidiary company" (official: RSSL, which is RHL's subsidiary, not RML's).
- **Q4 FY26 stored transcript (18 May 2026): tidied copy (figures match; gaps).** It omits an exchange on dividend policy (RML pays ~40% of profit, RHL 50–70%) and on group trademark and technology fees to RHL (~₹43 cr in FY26, "by and large" 0.5% of revenue). ZF Rane Q4 revenue is given as ₹765 cr; official ₹763 cr.

## Business and segments
- **What it sells (after the April 2025 merger of Rane Brake Lining and Rane Engine Valve into RML):** steering and suspension linkages and steering gears (~51% of Q1 FY27 sales), brake linings/pads/friction materials, engine valves and guides, and aluminium high-pressure die-castings (Light Metal Castings, LMC, ~8%); 17 plants (16 India, 1 Mexico) [IP Q1FY27 p.3–5, 14; AR FY26 Board's report].
- **Channel mix Q1 FY27:** India OEM ~56%, international OEM/OES ~30% (about 35% of which is "deemed exports" billed in rupees), India aftermarket ~14% [IP Q1FY27 p.14]. FY26: domestic OEM 57%, exports 27%, aftermarket 16%; within OEM, PV >55%, CV 22%, tractors 12%, 2W/3W 9% [CRISIL Jul-26].
- **Customers:** Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors PV, TAFE, M&M, plus export customers in Europe/US; competitors ZF Steering Gear (India) and JTEKT India [CRISIL Jul-26].
- **Friction acquisition:** Hindustan Composites' friction business bought on a slump-sale basis for ₹370 cr (enterprise value); FY26 revenue ~₹315 cr, PBT ~₹40 cr, operating margin ~13–14%; ~40–42% railways, ~40% CVs; two plants in Maharashtra; completed Aug 2026. Combined friction revenue to exceed ₹1,000 cr [IP Q1FY27 p.11; CRISIL Jul-26; Q1 call].
- **Order wins:** FY26 record ₹712 cr annualised sales (FY25 ~₹574 cr); from Q1 FY27 reported as lifetime value — ₹2,040 cr over 24 programmes, 54% export; lifetime value ≈ 6.2–6.4x annual revenue, so Q1 wins ≈ ₹320–330 cr a year, starting in 18–24 months [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.16; CRISIL Jul-26].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr: revenue / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 881 / 75 / 8% / 19; Sep-25 919 / 79 / 9% / 21; Dec-25 1,015 / 91 / 9% / 31; Mar-26 1,048 / 96 / 9% / 37; Jun-26 1,042 / 87 / 8% / 30 [Screener]. Company-reported Q1 FY27 total revenue ₹1,050.6 cr (+18.8%), EBITDA ₹95.8 cr (9.1%, incl. other income), PAT ₹30.1 cr (+62.5%) [IP Q1FY27 p.13].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹3,463 cr, op. profit ₹270 cr (8%), PAT ₹55 cr; FY25 ₹3,406 / ₹284 (8%) / ₹38 cr; FY26 ₹3,863 / ₹341 (9%) / ₹107 cr; TTM ₹4,024 / ₹352 / ₹119 cr [Screener]. Standalone FY26 PAT ₹111.4 cr, EPS ₹40.32; dividend ₹16/share [AR FY26 Board's report].
- **Returns:** ROCE 14%, ROE 15% (FY26) [Screener].
- **Q1 FY27 growth:** domestic OE +13%, international +24%, India aftermarket +28%; LMC exports ~+50% and domestic +20% (partly aluminium price pass-through) [IP Q1FY27 p.14; Q1 call].
- **Margin drag Q1:** EBITDA margin down ~90 bps q/q (9.5% → 8.4% by an analyst's reckoning) on commodity, logistics, LPG and premium freight/overtime costs [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (18 May):** double-digit EBITDA margin "during next year" (FY27), then 11–12%; FY27 capex ₹240–250 cr (15–20% maintenance, ~50% growth); debt "will only continue to come down", target debt-to-equity 0.5x; Velachery land money mostly in FY27 [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** margin fell to ~9% (company) / 8% (Screener) — missed; revenue +18.8% — ahead of CRISIL's ~10% medium-term expectation [IP; CRISIL Jul-26].
- **Q1 FY27 call (21 Aug):** double digits now an "aspiration" for the full year, possibly reached in Q3; capex **raised** to ₹270–300 cr ("advancement of an order" in steering); debt to peak at ~₹1,100 cr then end FY27 below ₹1,000 cr; 0.5x debt-to-equity pushed to March 2028; consultant-led cost programme to pay off in FY28 [Q1 call].
- **Track record:** the 11–12% margin aspiration dates from the 2024–25 merger ("12% in 18 months") and has not been met; margin has stayed ~8–9% for three years [Q4 call; Screener; CRISIL Jul-26]. CRISIL assumes only 9.0–9.2% for FY27 and 9.5–10% over the medium term [CRISIL Jul-26].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹191 cr; FY27 ₹270–300 cr plus ₹370 cr acquisition ≈ ₹600+ cr outflow, funded by internal accruals, Velachery proceeds and debt [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Capital commitments ₹74.6 cr at Mar-26 (₹31.5 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 note 40A].
- A new corporate office on the retained 1.02 acres at Velachery is extra capex (~12 months away) [Q4 call].
- Asset turns: revenue ₹3,863 cr on fixed assets ₹739 cr ≈ 5.2x [Screener]. No payback figures given for the acquisition beyond "margin-accretive" and 12–18-month integration.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹752 cr at Mar-26 (Screener) / total debt ₹634 cr (CRISIL: ₹586 cr short-term, ₹48 cr long-term); net debt ₹705.8 cr per management (₹779.2 cr a year earlier) [Screener; CRISIL Jul-26; Q4 call]. Long-term debt cut from ₹336 cr using ₹145 cr of Velachery advances [CRISIL Jul-26].
- CRISIL expects debt of ₹900–950 cr at FY27-end, gearing ~1.2x and net debt/EBITDA ~2.3x (from 0.85x and 1.73x) before improving [CRISIL Jul-26].
- Velachery land: 3.48 acres sold to Canopy Living (Arihant–Prestige JV) for ₹361.18 cr; ₹145 cr received by Mar-26; balance over FY27–28, dependent on Tamil Nadu approvals [AR FY26 PPE note; Q4 call].
- CFO ₹316 cr (FY25) → ₹219 cr (FY26); FCF ₹84 cr (FY26) [Screener]. Cash-conversion cycle 67 days; working-capital days −17 [Screener].
- Rating upgraded to CRISIL AA-/Stable/A1+ on 9 Jul 2026 (from A+/A1); CRISIL deducts ~₹300 cr of acquisition intangibles from net worth [CRISIL Jul-26].
- Dividend payout ~41% (FY26) [Screener]; policy ~40% [Q4 call].

## Moat evidence
- Leading domestic position in mechanical and hydrostatic steering gears; long relationships with Maruti, M&M, Tata, TAFE; record order wins two years running [CRISIL Jul-26; Q4 call].
- Friction: leadership in PV/2W brake linings and aftermarket; with Hindustan Composites adds CV and railway friction (railways tender-based, 12–14 months visibility) [CRISIL Jul-26; Q1 call].
- Export wins against strained European suppliers — 54% of Q1 lifetime-value wins are exports [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Limits:** "no loss of share" claimed, but domestic growth lagged industry for several quarters due to model mix; margins remain "modest" (CRISIL) at ~9% versus peers; raw-material pass-through has a lag and wage/overtime/freight are not recoverable [Q1 call; CRISIL Jul-26]. Mexico satellite plant depends on US–Mexico trade terms [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Leverage rising again** after a year of reduction: acquisition + higher capex; the deleveraging target slipped from "debt will only come down" (May) to March 2028 (Aug) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Auditor key audit matters:** (1) impairment of the Light Metal Castings unit — carrying value ₹250.1 cr; ₹4.1 cr goodwill impaired in FY26 and headroom is now nil ("any adverse movement in a key assumption would lead to further impairment"); (2) recoverability of ₹62.8 cr deferred tax assets on past losses [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 4]. Opinion unmodified.
- **Related parties — payments to parent RHL:** professional charges ~₹20.8 cr, information-systems ~₹13.6 cr, trade-mark fee ~₹1.5 cr and reimbursements ~₹19 cr in FY26 (read from the RPT table; column alignment in the PDF is imperfect), plus purchases and professional charges with RSSL and the ZF JVs [AR FY26 note 35]. Group-wide trademark/technology fees to RHL were ~₹43 cr, about 0.5% of revenue [Q4 call]. RML also paid ₹49–50 cr of professional fees in FY26 (₹45 cr FY25) to consultants [Q1 call].
- **Contingent liabilities:** ₹87.6 cr (income tax ₹30.1 cr, GST/excise/sales tax ₹55.9 cr, labour ₹1.7 cr) [AR FY26 note 40].
- **Equity:** a ₹40 cr preferential warrant issue was proposed in May 2026 (details not seen in the filings read); promoter RHL holds 70.45% [Q4 call; Screener].
- **Disclosure:** switch from annualised order wins to lifetime value makes comparisons harder (≈6.2–6.4x) [Q1 call].
- **Stored-profile error:** the ZF JV warranty (€23 mn, ~₹230 cr provision, ₹75 cr insurance cover) is a Rane Holdings/JV risk, not an RML liability.

## What changed between the two calls
- From "margins to double digits next year and debt only down" (May) to "aspiring" to double digits by Q3 with debt peaking at ~₹1,100 cr (Aug).
- New: ₹370 cr Hindustan Composites friction deal (signed 30 Jun, closed Aug), lifetime-value order reporting, capex raised by ₹30–50 cr.
- Cost shock from West Asia (commodities, LPG, freight) cited for the Q1 margin dip.

## Best management quotes
- "I see double digits happening during next year and then going to that 11-12% level." — Harish Lakshman [Q4 call]
- "The aspiration is for the full-year margin to be a double-digit number… we should be able to try and achieve it in Q3." [Q1 call]
- "It should be around INR1,100 crores for a brief period of time, but by year end, we should come below INR1,000 crores." [Q1 call]

## My read
RML is a growing (16–19% revenue growth), newly consolidated component maker with good order wins and a steering/friction franchise, but its core problem is margin: ~9% for three years against an 11–12% promise, and Q1 FY27 slipped again. The friction acquisition should add ~₹300 cr of revenue at ~13–14% margins, which mechanically lifts the blend, but it and higher capex push debt back toward ₹1,000 cr and depend on the Velachery land cash arriving. The stock needs both growth and a real move to double-digit margins in H2 FY27; failure to reach ~10% by Q3–Q4 or further LMC impairment would weaken the case. The ZF warranty issue should not be charged to RML.
