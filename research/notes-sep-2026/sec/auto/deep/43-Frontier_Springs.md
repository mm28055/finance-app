# Frontier Springs — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Screener code FRONTSP (standalone; BSE-listed, scrip 522195). Price ₹1,439, market cap ₹1,700 cr, 52-week range ₹1,150–1,870 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026]. Note: this is a **railway** supplier sitting in the auto list; almost none of its revenue is automotive.

## Business and segments
- Kanpur-based maker of hot-coiled compression (coil) springs, air springs and forged parts for Indian Railways' wagons, coaches and locomotives; springs are fitted on Vande Bharat [AR FY26 MD&A]. Plants at Rania (Kanpur Dehat, UP) and Paonta Sahib (HP); installed spring capacity 9,900 MT [AR FY26 note on capacity; CRISIL May-25].
- Three divisions, no segment reporting. FY26 gross sales by division per MD: coil springs ~₹175 cr, air springs ~₹150 cr, forging "₹60-plus cr" (gross, i.e., including GST) [Q4 call]. Q2 FY26 run-rate: coil ~₹35 cr, air ~₹35 cr, forging ₹16–17 cr a quarter [Q2 call].
- Customers: Indian Railways production units (ICF, RCF, MCF, CLW, BLW, PLW) and wagon builders (Titagarh, Texmaco, Jupiter); wagon makers are only 10–15% of revenue [Q2 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Market share (management's claim): ~40% of Indian Railways' coil spring demand, "broadly similar" in air springs, 20–25% in forgings; 1–2 other approved coil-spring and air-spring makers, 7–8 in forgings [AR FY26 MD&A; Q2 call].
- Air springs: technology MoU with ContiTech (Continental), Germany; capacity ~300 coach sets a month (final testing is the bottleneck) vs output 180–200 sets a month; one coach uses four air springs; realisation ~₹1.25 lakh per air spring "a little bit improved" [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]. "Almost 15,000 air springs" supplied with "not a single failure" [Q2 call].
- Forging: 1-, 3- and 6-tonne hammers; the 6-tonne hammer runs one 8-hour shift [Q4 call].
- Exports: nil in practice — FY26 foreign-currency earnings were ₹0.71 lakh (air-spring samples) [AR FY26 note 30]. Management does not plan to export coil/air springs; exploring forging exports [Q4 call].
- EV content: not applicable (rail).

## Key numbers (Screener, standalone, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales: Jun-25 75 → Sep-25 83 → Dec-25 81 → Mar-26 83 → Jun-26 78. Operating profit 20 → 22 → 20 → 24 → 19; margin 27%, 27%, 25%, 29%, 25%. Net profit 15 → 16 → 14 → 17 → 12 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 as reported: revenue ₹78.46 cr (+4.1% YoY, −4.9% QoQ), EBITDA ₹19.24 cr (−5.8% YoY), margin 24.52% (27.11% a year ago, 28.51% in Q4), PBT ₹18.05 cr, PAT ₹12.01 cr (−18.5% YoY) [IP Q1FY27 p.23]. The tax rate jumped to ~33% from ~25% (PBT ₹18.05 cr → PAT ₹12.01 cr) with no explanation in the deck [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- Why Q1 was weak: "an acute constraint in the availability of industrial gas… combined with logistics-related delays in the procurement of air spring bellows" cut utilisation in the second half of the quarter; management says both are "firmly behind us" [IP Q1FY27 p.20].
- Revenue has been stuck at ₹75–83 cr a quarter for five quarters; the MD on the Q4 call hoped to "break that 82 crore" in Q1 — it fell instead [Q4 call; Screener].
- Annual: sales FY24 135 → FY25 231 → FY26 322 (+71%, +39%); operating profit 21 → 50 → 87; margin 16% → 22% → 27%; net profit 13 → 35 → 61; EPS ₹51.9 (FY26, after a 2:1 bonus lifted equity capital from ₹4 cr to ₹12 cr) [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A: revenue ₹322.06 cr, EBITDA ₹86.31 cr, PAT ₹61.31 cr].
- Returns: ROCE 21% → 42% → 51%, ROE 39% [Screener]; company ROCE 43.6% [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Unit economics: air spring ~₹1.25 lakh each, ~₹5 lakh per coach set; 180–200 sets a month ≈ ₹110–120 cr a year of air-spring sales (my arithmetic, consistent with the ~₹150 cr gross figure) [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- FY26: Q2 call targeted "₹375 cr gross revenue" and "at least" 26–27% EBITDA margin for the year [Q2 call]. Delivered revenue from operations ₹322.06 cr (management says gross target "surpassed") and 26.8% margin [Q4 call; AR FY26] — **delivered**.
- FY27 revenue: "₹500 cr gross revenue" first stated in Nov 2025 and repeated on the Q4 call ("we will try to achieve 30% growth") [Q2 call; Q4 call]; reaffirmed in the Q1 deck: "We remain fully committed to our FY27 gross revenue guidance of 500 crores. The execution deferred in Q1 will be recovered" [IP Q1FY27 p.20]. Q1 net revenue was ₹78.5 cr, +4% — **behind**; the rest of the year needs roughly ₹110–115 cr of net revenue a quarter if "gross" means including 18% GST (my estimate of the definition — management has not defined "gross").
- FY27 margin: Q4 — "between 23%-24% definitely this year also, if not 26%-28%" [Q4 call]; Q1 delivered 24.5% — **in line with the cautious guide**.
- Longer term: "if not 30%, we will definitely have a 25% growth" beyond FY27; ₹1,000 cr "in the next 5 years", needing new products [Q4 call; Q2 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- FIBA (failure indication and brake application for air springs): Q2 — samples by Dec-25, then 6–8 months of trials, supplies from about Dec 2026, ₹40–50 cr business [Q2 call]. Q4 — basic RDSO approval and patent done, 6-month (~20,000 km) trial, some orders late FY27, "₹20–25 crores… from 27-28" [Q4 call]. Q1 deck — RDSO approval only as a **developmental vendor**, samples for 25 coach sets entering a **twelve-month** trial [IP Q1FY27 p.20] — **slipped** by about six months.
- Forging: Q2 — FY26 forging ₹75–80 cr, FY27 ₹100–115 cr [Q2 call]; Q4 — FY26 forging "₹60 plus crore" gross [Q4 call] — **missed**.
- Alstom: Q2 — air-spring talks "on the verge of completion… orders from Alstom" within 1–1.5 months [Q2 call]; not mentioned on the Q4 call; FY26 exports nil [AR FY26 note 30] — **dropped/undelivered**.
- NSE listing: planned for "next financial year's first quarter" [Q2 call]; no listing seen in the filings I read — **unverified**.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹20 cr (Q2 plan was ₹15 cr), funded internally; FY27 plan ₹20–25 cr across three divisions including a more automated coil-spring line from China and air-spring test machines [Q4 call; Q2 call]; Q4 deck said ₹15–20 cr [IP Q4FY26]. FIBA testing kit ~₹2 cr [Q2 call].
- Utilisation ~70% overall [Q4 call]; air springs ~60–67% (200 of 300 sets); 6-tonne hammer one shift; 3-tonne and 2-tonne hammers 60–70% [Q4 call; Q2 call].
- Asset-light: net block ₹65.5 cr at Mar-26 vs ₹322 cr revenue [IP Q1FY27 p.24].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹10.8 cr (mostly short-term); cash and bank ₹3.5 cr; but non-current investments ₹48.3 cr, of which mutual funds ₹38.6 cr and listed equities ₹6.1 cr [AR FY26 MD&A; note 2]. So effectively net cash of ~₹40 cr.
- Operating cash flow ₹31 cr (FY26) vs ₹22 cr; free cash flow ₹17 cr; CFO/operating profit only 60% [Screener]. Much of the surplus goes into mutual funds and shares rather than dividends: payout ~1% (₹0.70 a share proposed) [Screener; AGM outcome 22 Sep 2026].
- Working capital: receivables rose to ₹60.9 cr from ₹38.4 cr (debtor days 69 vs 61); inventory days fell to 83 from 126; **payable days collapsed to 33 from 94**, lifting the cash conversion cycle to 119 days from 92 [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]. Railway retention money of ₹26.7 lakh sits in receivables [AR FY26 auditor's KAM].

## Moat evidence
- RDSO approval and 40+ years of supply; new entrants "wait for 2 years to get a regular order" [Q2 call]. No new competitor approved in its products [Q4 call].
- Share ~40% in coil and air springs (management claim) [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Margin expansion from 16% (FY24) to 27% (FY26) shows pricing on new tenders; but prices are fixed for the life of each tender, so cost spikes hurt for 6–8 months [Q4 call; Screener].
- Quality: zero field failures claimed on ~15,000 air springs; competitors "importing from China… getting a lot of failure" [Q2 call].
- Weakness: a single buyer (Indian Railways) that buys by lowest-bid tender.

## Risks and red flags
- **Past non-cooperation with CRISIL**: rated "Crisil B/Stable; Issuer Not Cooperating" from 2022 to March 2025, then withdrawn at the company's request (May 2025). CRISIL notes non-cooperation "may be a result of deterioration in its credit risk profile" [CRISIL May-25]. A disclosure-culture flag, not a credit one.
- **Related-party dealings** with promoter-linked "associate concerns" of roughly ₹48 cr in FY26 (~15% of revenue): sales of ₹13.4 cr to Frontier Alloy Steels (also shown as a "marquee client" in the deck); job work ₹10.8 cr and components ₹4.9 cr bought from Vishpa Rail Equipments (plus rent); and ~₹13.4 cr of purchases and ~₹5.1 cr of sales with NWFP Equipments / P.C. Packagers (the table's alignment is unclear) [AR FY26 note 32; IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- **Family pay**: five Bhatia family directors took about ₹7.2 cr (₹2.04 cr Kapil, ₹2.03 cr Neeraj, ₹1.44 cr each Mamta and Manju, ₹0.26 cr K.L. Bhatia) — ~12% of FY26 profit [AR FY26 note 32].
- **Treasury in equities**: ₹6.1 cr of listed shares and ₹38.6 cr of mutual funds held by an engineering company [AR FY26 note 2].
- **Execution**: Q1 revenue fell; FIBA trial doubled to 12 months; forging and Alstom promises missed; the ₹500 cr target now needs a steep H2 [IP Q1FY27; Q2 call; Q4 call].
- **Single customer**: Indian Railways budget and tender timing; revenue lumpy by delivery schedules [Q2 call].
- **Unexplained Q1 tax rate** of ~33% vs ~25% norm [IP Q1FY27].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; KAMs on revenue timing/railway retention, tax provisions and gratuity [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Contingent liabilities tiny: bank guarantees ₹0.34 cr, LCs ₹3.27 cr [AR FY26 note 29(5)].
- Promoter holding flat at 51.76%, no pledge disclosed [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q2 FY26 (Nov 2025): exuberant — margins "at least" 26–27%, forging to ₹100–115 cr, Alstom orders imminent, FIBA supply from Dec-26, NSE listing [Q2 call].
- Q4 FY26 (Jun 2026): cautious on margin (23–24% guide on steel/energy inflation), FIBA revenue pushed to FY28, forging came in at ~₹60 cr, no Alstom mention; first disclosure of a ₹370 cr order book; revenue plateau acknowledged [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 (deck only; no call held): gas shortage and bellows delays hit the quarter; FIBA trial now 12 months; ₹500 cr gross guidance reaffirmed [IP Q1FY27].

## My read
Frontier has been a spectacular railway-capex winner: revenue up 2.4x and margin from 16% to 27% in two years, with little debt. But momentum has stalled — revenue has been flat at ~₹80 cr a quarter for five quarters, Q1 FY27 profit fell 18%, and several promises (forging scale, Alstom, FIBA timing) slipped. The ₹500 cr gross FY27 target looks hard from here. Governance hygiene is middling: past non-cooperation with CRISIL, sizeable dealings with family-linked firms, and surplus cash parked in equities and funds. At ~29x trailing earnings the price assumes the growth resumes at 20%+; I would want to see Q2 revenue back above ₹90 cr, the air-spring rate move to 250+ sets a month, and FIBA trial progress before trusting the target.
