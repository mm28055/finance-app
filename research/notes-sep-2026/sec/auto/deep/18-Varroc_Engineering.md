# Varroc Engineering (VARROC) — deep research, Sep 2026

Figures from Screener's consolidated page unless tagged otherwise. Company "EBITDA" is close to Screener's operating profit (Q1 FY27: ₹222.8 cr vs ₹222 cr).

## Business and segments
- Pune-based parts maker, mainly for two- and three-wheelers: polymer body parts, ICE powertrain (valves, crankshafts, forgings), lighting (2W, and now 4W from Thailand), aftermarket, e-mobility (traction motors, controllers, DC-DC converters), HMI/connectivity, plus overseas 2W lighting (Vietnam), forging (Italy) and electronics (Romania). The global 4W lighting arm (VLS) was sold to Plastic Omnium/OPmobility in 2022. 37 plants, 8 countries, ~6,100 staff, 130+ patents. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- Q1 FY27 revenue mix by product: body parts 34.8%, ICE powertrain 21.5%, lighting 16.5%, aftermarket 10.6%, e-mobility 7.6%, HMI/connectivity 3.9%, overseas forging 3.3%, Romania electronics 1.8%. Vehicle mix: 2W/3W 74%, 4W 26%. Geography: India 87%, overseas 13%. Customers: Bajaj 44%, non-Bajaj 56%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Revenue from EV models: ~16% of Q1 FY27 revenue (+87% YoY); ~13% in FY26, 9.8% FY25, 5.3% FY24. Content per EV is 5–6x an equivalent ICE model (company claim). About three-quarters of EV revenue is from Bajaj. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD letter; Q1 call]
- Only ~70% of the business is "powertrain agnostic"; the rest is ICE or EV powertrain. [Q1 call]
- India vs overseas: India EBITDA margin 11.8% and PBT 7.4% in FY26 (11% / 5.7% in FY25); Q1 FY27 India EBITDA 10.6%, PBT ~7%. Overseas (2W lighting profitable; Romania electronics and Italy forging loss-making at EBITDA) plus overseas R&D drag of roughly ₹70 cr a year. FY26 was the third straight year of overseas revenue decline before a Q4 revival (+24%), then +45.6% in Q1 FY27. [AR FY26 MD letter; Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (27 May 2026): FY27 growth "mid to high teens" (answered "Yes"); FY27 margins "higher than FY26" (9.4%); capex ₹450–500 cr (incl. land), FY28 ₹300–400 cr; net debt not to fall in FY27 (average ₹500–600 cr), zero net debt by end-FY28; Romania EBITDA breakeven by Q4 FY27, Italy forging a year later, whole overseas business breakeven within two years; overseas order wins ~₹1,400 cr in FY26 (programmes starting in FY27, full effect FY28). [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery vs those promises: revenue +29.9% (**beat**); EBITDA 8.5%, below FY26's 9.4% (**missed so far**; management expects most war-related under-recovery back in Q2–Q3); net debt ₹527 cr (**within** the ₹500–600 cr range); Q1 capex ₹160 cr and the full-year capex guide **raised** to ₹500–550 cr; overseas losses narrowing (**on track**). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 call (6 Aug 2026): 20–25% growth for FY27 as an "ambition", not guidance; FY31 revenue ₹20,000 cr (about double FY27's ~₹10,000 cr), ~20% overseas, ~10% of it from acquisitions; 10% PBT margin in about 3 years (overseas too in 3–4 years); zero net debt by FY28 kept "for now". Open to acquisitions in electronics, e-powertrain and aftermarket, mainly in India. [Q1 call]
- Order book: outstanding annual peak revenue ₹3,509 cr at end-FY26; Q1 wins ₹599 cr (two-thirds e-mobility volume increases, rest mainly 4W lighting in Thailand); ₹499 cr moved to production; ₹3,609 cr outstanding, of which ₹2,316 cr SOP in FY27, ₹1,167 cr FY28, ₹127 cr FY29. Q1 wins: Bajaj 58%, EV customers 72%. FY26 wins ₹3,289 cr, a record, >65% EV-linked. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Traction-motor SOP with a second EV maker (backed by a global OEM) expected in Q2; two more in advanced talks. [Q1 call]
- Earlier record: the AR letter admits overseas targets were missed for three years and says Romania breakeven by Q4 FY27 is "a commitment against which we should be held accountable". [AR FY26 MD letter]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 tangible capex up 45% on FY25; ₹1,040 cr of capital deployed in FY26 including proceeds from exiting the China JV (May 2025). [AR FY26 MD letter]
- FY27 capex ₹500–550 cr (India and overseas), front-loaded: ₹160 cr in Q1, mostly e-mobility capacity and capacity for EV models; land acquisition for new plants partly included. FY28 lower (₹300–400 cr per Q4 call). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Board sought enabling approval for up to ₹500 cr of NCDs and a ₹3,000 cr borrowing limit — "just an enabling resolution". [Q4 call; News: Whalesbook, May-2026]
- VRS in FY26 cost ₹33.3 cr (voluntary retirement compensation). [AR FY26 notes]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt: ₹983 cr Mar-24 → ₹748 cr Mar-25 → low of ₹380 cr Sep-25 → ₹495 cr Mar-26 → ₹527 cr Jun-26. Net debt/equity 0.28, net debt/EBITDA 0.59. Screener gross borrowings ₹925 cr Mar-26 (₹3,695 cr in Mar-21). [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Q1 debt rise (₹32 cr) from capex and ₹44 cr more working capital (inventory for festive season, war-cost claims still to be invoiced). [Q1 call]
- Cash flow: CFO ₹638 cr FY24, ₹737 cr FY25, ₹537 cr FY26; FCF ₹465 cr FY25 → ₹87 cr FY26. Company claims ~84% EBITDA-to-CFO conversion over three years. [Screener; AR FY26 MD letter]
- Working capital FY26: debtor days 30, inventory 60, payables 97, cash cycle −8 days (suppliers fund the business). [Screener]
- Dividend ₹1.50 a share (150% of face value) for FY26, up from ₹1.00. [Q4 call]
- Promoters hold 75% with no pledge. [Screener; AR FY26 Corporate Governance 3.3.5]

## Moat evidence
- Primary supplier of EV powertrain parts to Bajaj, the leader in combined 2W+3W EVs; business relationships with all top-three 2W and 3W EV makers; one more e-powertrain customer starting Q2. [Q1 call]
- Order wins: record ₹3,289 cr of annual peak revenue in FY26; overseas wins of inverters, wall chargers (bi-directional, for a global EV maker, from Romania), 4W headlamps (Thailand). [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- Indian 2W content across body parts, lighting, powertrain, HMI and aftermarket gives breadth few rivals match; 7 technical centres (India, Poland, Italy, China). [IP Q1FY27]
- Weakness: pricing pressure is real — EV average prices falling high single digits; margins stuck at 8.5–9.7% company-wide; Bajaj is ~44% of revenue and 58% of Q1 wins. Management says its strategy is "not necessarily a customer diversification strategy". [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- Qualified audit opinion (FY26, S R B C & Co LLP, standalone and consolidated): ₹20.99 cr of income recognised in FY26 revenue from the former China JV (Chongqing Varroc TYC) is disputed by TYC Parties under the Transition Management Agreement; auditors could not obtain sufficient evidence. The Q1 FY27 limited review is also qualified (₹21.0 cr standalone / ₹44.2 cr consolidated of prior-year income unverified). [AR FY26 auditor's report; News: multibagg.ai, Aug-2026]
- Emphasis of matter: OPmobility (buyer of the VLS lighting business) has started ICC arbitration alleging breaches of the 2022 sale agreement, with claims quantified at US$66.41 mn (roughly ₹550 cr) plus costs for some breaches and unquantified for others; no provision. Second EOM on GST orders (classification of goods, 2017–2023): demand cut on appeal to ₹28.4 cr; further appeals on interest and penalty. [AR FY26 auditor's report; notes 50, 55]
- Contingent liabilities (group) ₹98.1 cr at Mar-26 vs ₹156.4 cr a year earlier, plus EPCG export obligations of ₹164.5 cr. [AR FY26, consolidated note 38]
- Related parties: sales of ₹36.9 cr to Endurance Technologies (Jain-family company) and purchases of ₹11.5 cr; ₹21.0 cr royalty from Varroc TYC (the disputed income). [AR FY26, consolidated note 46]
- Customer concentration (Bajaj 44% of revenue); overseas losses; Italy forging for sale "if the right price is offered"; EV price deflation; war-related input costs. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Transcript/data quality: the company's Q4 transcript itself contains typos (e.g., "INR 32,889 billion", "net worth ... INR 4,952 million" for net debt). [Q4 call official]

## What changed between the two calls
- Growth ambition raised from mid-to-high teens to 20–25% for FY27; ₹20,000 cr FY31 target and ~10% inorganic share introduced; M&A appetite (electronics, e-powertrain, aftermarket) new.
- Margin moved the wrong way: 9.7% in Q4 to 8.5% in Q1, blamed on tooling, war costs and casual labour.
- Capex raised from ₹450–500 cr to ₹500–550 cr; net debt drifted up; zero-debt-by-FY28 target kept but hedged.
- New CTO for BU1 (Eric Hammond, ex-Ford) for software-defined vehicle, e-powertrain and higher-voltage systems. Italy forging sale talk dropped.

## My read
Varroc is growing faster than at any time since the VLS sale — +30% in Q1 on EV parts and an overseas revival — and the balance sheet is now light (net debt ~0.6x EBITDA). But profitability has not followed: EBITDA margin is stuck around 8.5–9.7% and falling average EV prices plus Bajaj's weight cap pricing power. The legal overhang (qualified opinion on TYC income, OPmobility's ~₹550 cr claim) is small relative to the ₹12,800 cr market value but is unresolved and unprovided. The price needs the 10% PBT target and overseas breakeven to arrive roughly on time; proof would be Romania at EBITDA breakeven by Q4 FY27 and India margins back above 11% in H2. A second year of overseas slippage, or an adverse arbitration award, would change the view.
