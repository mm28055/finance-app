# Minda Corporation (NSE: MINDACORP) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Spark Minda group flagship (Ashok Minda family). Promoters 64.84%, unchanged for 3 years, no pledge disclosed. Price ₹679, market cap ₹16,230 cr, 52-week range ₹468–769 [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Two verticals: **Mechatronics & Aftermarket** (vehicle access — locks, keys, handles, latches; die-casting; aftermarket) and **Information & Connected Systems** (wiring harnesses, instrument clusters/TFT displays, sensors, antennas) [IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 product mix: wiring harness 32%, vehicle access 25%, clusters 16%, die-casting 15%, others 12%. End-market mix: 2W/3W 46%, CV 27%, PV 19%, aftermarket 8% (Q4 FY26: 48/28/14/10) [Q1 call; Q4 call]. PV share jumped because **Minda VAST** (50:50 JV with VAST, ~₹500 cr FY26 revenue, >90% PV, ~7% EBITDA margin) is consolidated from Q1 FY27 after a change in the shareholder agreement [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Associates/JVs: **Flash Electronics** 49% (EV motors, controllers, magnetos, converters; FY26 revenue ₹1,803 cr, EBITDA ₹310 cr, PAT ₹137 cr; Q1 FY27 revenue ₹533 cr, +42%, EBITDA margin 15.4%); Furukawa Minda Electric (17.5%, Japanese-OEM wiring harness); Minda Infac 51%; EVQ Point 29.5% (being sold); new JVs Turntide (UK, axial-flux motors/controllers), Toyo Denso (switches), Minda-HCMF (sunroofs) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Board outcome 24-Sep-26].
- Geography: India ₹5,479 cr, outside India ₹707 cr (11.4%) in FY26 [AR FY26 consolidated segment note]. Group (incl. Flash, VAST) revenue about ₹9,000 cr [Q4 call].
- EV share: ~10% of Minda Corp revenue (+40% YoY), ~30% of Flash, ~14% at group level [Q1 call]; EV was over 15% of Q1 order wins [IP Q1FY27].
- Capacity utilisation 77–80% on average [Q1 call]; 42 plants, ~23,000 people [Q4 call].

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly revenue: Jun-25 1,386 | Sep-25 1,535 | Dec-25 1,560 | Mar-26 1,704 | Jun-26 1,846. Operating profit: 156 | 178 | 184 | 203 | 212. Net profit: 65 | 85 | 84 | 124 | 206 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: revenue +33% (about ₹125 cr from VAST, so ~24% organic), EBITDA ₹212 cr (+35%), margin 11.5% (+19 bps YoY, −40 bps QoQ). PAT ₹206 cr includes a ₹106 cr one-time gain from re-measuring VAST on consolidation; underlying PAT about ₹100 cr, roughly +54% [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Screener other income ₹126 cr].
- Annual: revenue FY24 4,651 | FY25 5,056 | FY26 6,185 (+22%); operating profit 517 | 576 | 722; margin 11% | 11% | 12% (11.7% reported); net profit 227 | 255 | 358 (+40%) [Screener; Q4 call].
- Interest cost nearly doubled to ₹123 cr in FY26 (₹69 cr FY25) — the price of the debt-funded 49% Flash stake bought in Jan-2025 [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A 6.4.1].
- ROCE 13% (Screener); the AR's Vision-2030 table quotes a FY25 ROCE "baseline" of 23.1%, which does not match Screener's 13% — a different definition, not explained [AR FY26 MD&A; Screener].
- Growth drivers: of the 29% Q4 growth, 12–13 points came from premiumisation/new products; 2W sales grew more than 26%, "outperforming the industry by about 12%" (the same call put Q4 2W industry growth at ~21%, so the claim does not reconcile) [official Q4 transcript]. Wiring-harness kit value rises about 20% a year [Q4 call]. VAST PV kit value ₹8,000–13,000 today, could double [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (22 May 2026): grow "at least 50% more than the industry"; to reach Vision 2030 needs ~19–20% a year, and management agreed FY27 growth should be at least 20%; FY27 capex ₹400–450 cr; lifetime order book ₹10,000 cr for FY26 (20% export), executing over 48–60 months; sunroof (lifetime order ~₹350 cr) SOP in 4–5 months; switches JV SOP by Mar-27; Turntide SOP in 5–6 months [official Q4 transcript].
- Q1 call (13 Aug 2026): revenue +33% — beat; EBITDA margin guided at 11.5–12% for the rest of FY27 (FY26: 11.7%); Flash growth 20–24% with long-run margin 16–17% (was 18% in Q4); capex "about 400-odd crores"; new orders ₹2,500 cr in the quarter; sunroof trials cleared, SOP on track for Q2; Turntide SOP Oct–Nov 2026; switches SOP Q4 FY27/Q1 FY28, ~₹150 cr revenue in FY28, peak FY29 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Vision 2030: ₹17,500 cr revenue, EBITDA >12.5%, net debt/equity 0.3x, ROCE >25%, exports ₹1,500 cr [AR FY26 MD&A]. **Inconsistency:** the annual report calls ₹17,500 cr "consolidated revenue… 3.5x growth; ~28% CAGR" from FY25's ₹5,056 cr, but on the Q4 call management said it is a group-level figure including Flash and VAST (≈2x from ₹9,000 cr) [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call].
- Verdict: **growth guidance beaten; margin guidance trimmed (Flash 18% → 16–17%; group 11.5–12% vs Q4's 11.9%); capex kept at ~₹400 cr (lower end of the Q4 range)**.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹413 cr, a record (plan ₹400 cr) — first tranche of a five-year ₹2,000 cr organic plan (FY26–FY30) [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A 3.3].
- FY27: ~₹400 cr spread across ICE and EV; plus ₹63 cr put into group JVs (green mobility, sunroof, switches) in Q1 [Q1 call].
- Projects: second Minda Instruments plant (clusters/cockpits) due Q1 FY27; fifth die-casting plant in Pune (export-led, ~18 months from May-26); land bought in several clusters [Q4 call].
- 24-Sep-26 board: up to ₹500 cr NCDs by private placement "for various funding requirements"; a China subsidiary (up to US$1 m); sale of the 29.5% EVQ Point stake [Board outcome 24-Sep-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt including leases ₹1,369 cr at Mar-26 (₹1,534 cr Mar-25); net debt-to-capital 51.5% vs 69.6% on the company's measure [AR FY26 capital-management note]. The MD&A quotes net debt ~₹1,165 cr and 0.4x — a different basis [AR FY26 MD&A 6.4.1]. Screener borrowings ₹1,476 cr [Screener].
- CRISIL expected adjusted gearing 0.55x and net debt/EBITDA 1.68x by end-FY26; bank-line use ~49%; cash >₹118 cr; accruals ₹510–610 cr vs repayments ₹107–150 cr [CRISIL Apr-26].
- Cash from operations FY26 ₹676 cr (110% of operating profit); free cash flow ₹311 cr [Screener].
- Working capital: debtor days 59, inventory 66, payables 107; cash cycle 18 days (31 in FY25) [Screener].
- Dividend ₹1.40/share for FY26 (payout ~9%) [Q4 call; Screener].
- CARO flag: the parent used ₹312 cr of short-term funds for long-term purposes [AR FY26 CARO clause ix(d)] — consistent with the Flash purchase and explains the new NCD plan.

## Moat evidence
- Share gains: Minda's 2W revenue grew >26% in Q4 (industry ~21%); CV +25% (industry ~20%); wiring harness +30% and clusters +35% in Q1 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Order wins: ₹10,000 cr lifetime in FY26 (₹3,500 cr in Q4 alone), ₹2,500 cr in Q1 FY27 [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Backward integration: in-house connectors now meet 18–19% of harness needs [Q4 call].
- Technology access via partners (VAST, Toyo Denso, Turntide, HCMF) plus 335 patents filed, 150 granted [Q1 call].
- Pricing: metals (copper, zinc, aluminium up 30–40% in FY26) are passed through with a 1–2 quarter lag; the pass-through carries no margin, so margin % is diluted [Q4 call]. Labour and freight are not indexed.
- Rating: CRISIL upgraded to AA/Stable from AA-/Positive in April 2026 [CRISIL Apr-26].

## Risks and red flags
- Contingent liabilities jumped to about ₹105 cr (Mar-25: ~₹17 cr), mainly a ₹67.1 cr income-tax demand for AY2023-24 (incl. ₹17.8 cr interest) under appeal, and GST/VAT ₹32.1 cr [AR FY26 consolidated note 2.37].
- Auditor (S.R. Batliboi & Co.): unmodified opinion; KAM on revenue recognition (year-end price-variation estimates). CARO adverse remarks: parent clause ix(d) (short-term funds used long-term); two subsidiaries (Spark Minda Green Mobility, Spark Minda-Toyodenso) clause xvii (cash losses). 7 subsidiaries (₹327 cr revenue) audited by other auditors. Audit trail not enabled at database level [AR FY26 auditor's reports].
- Related parties: rent paid to promoter company Minda Capital ₹27.8 cr (₹24.5 cr FY25); purchases from Minda Silca ₹29.9 cr; chairman's pay ₹14.3 cr [AR FY26 note 2.39]. Modest but growing.
- Earnings quality: Q1 PAT flattered by ₹106 cr non-cash gain; share of associate profit fell to ₹18 cr from ₹31.5 cr QoQ (Flash depreciation, Furukawa ₹0.8 cr vs ₹5 cr) [Q1 call].
- Leverage and new NCDs (up to ₹500 cr) against a stated goal of halving net debt/equity to 0.3x [Board outcome 24-Sep-26; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Fire at the Noida plant (D-6-11, Sector 59) on 31-May-26; plant ~4–5% of group production; insured; impact not quantified on the Q1 call [CRISIL Jun-26].
- Compliance slip: ₹2,000 BSE fine for one-day-late shareholding filing (Aug-26) — trivial [Board outcome 24-Sep-26].
- Concentration in 2W/3W (46%) and dependence on EV subsidies for Flash growth.

## What changed between the two calls
- From "record everything" and margin defence to acknowledging 1–2 quarter lags on commodity, wage (UP/Haryana minimum wage hikes from 1-Apr-26) and freight costs [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Flash margin reset from 18% (Q4) to 15.4% (Q1), long-run guide 16–17% [Q1 call].
- PV share lifted to 19% by VAST; VAST margin improved to 8.4–8.5% from 6.5% [Q1 call].
- Sunroof: from "SOP in 4–5 months" to "trials approved first time, on track" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- After the calls: ₹500 cr NCD approval, EVQ exit, China subsidiary, new CS and CPO (Sep-26) [Board outcome 24-Sep-26].

## My read
Minda Corp is growing well above the industry (organic ~24% in Q1) and its move into clusters, VAST's PV access systems and Flash's EV electronics is real and backed by ₹12,500 cr of orders in five quarters. The weaker parts are the balance sheet and earnings quality: the Flash stake left net debt ~₹1,370 cr, interest cost doubled, CARO flags short-term money used long-term, and a new ₹500 cr NCD is coming. The price needs about 20% earnings growth for several years; that holds if margins get to 12%+ and Flash recovers to 16–17%. The view would weaken if contingent tax demands grow, margins stall at 11.5%, or management keeps redefining the Vision 2030 base.
