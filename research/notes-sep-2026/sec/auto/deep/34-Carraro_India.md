# Carraro India (NSE: CARRARO) — deep research, Sep 2026

Figures: Screener consolidated (Carraro India plus its 99% subsidiary Carraro Technologies India). Price ₹535, market cap ₹3,042 cr (Screener, 27 Sep 2026). Note the company's "EBITDA" and margins **include other income and are calculated on total income** (stated on its slides); Screener's operating profit excludes other income. The gap matters in Q1 FY27.

## Business and segments
- Makes axles and transmissions (drivelines) for tractors and construction equipment, plus gears; part of Italy's Carraro Group (Carraro International S.E. holds 68.77%). Two plants at Ranjangaon (Pune): drivelines and gears; one R&D centre; ~1,800 employees, 245+ suppliers, 50+ OEM customers. [IP Q1FY27]
- Market position (company, citing Markets and Markets CY23): leading supplier in the non-captive tractor transmission market; 60–65% share of non-captive construction-vehicle transmissions. Supplies every Indian backhoe-loader maker except JCB. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- FY26 revenue ₹2,255.5 cr: agriculture ₹1,019 cr (+19%), construction ₹984 cr (+31%), rest spares/gears/other. Domestic ₹1,443 cr (64%), exports ₹812.5 cr (36%). [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹544.7 cr: agriculture ₹255.9 cr (+15%), construction ₹226.4 cr (+4%), others ₹62.4 cr; domestic ₹379.5 cr (+26%), exports ₹165.2 cr (−14%). Domestic split: agri ₹213.5 cr (+32%), construction ₹139.3 cr (+20%), other ₹26.7 cr. Export split: construction ₹87.1 cr, agri ₹42.4 cr, other ₹35.7 cr. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Exports are almost all sold to the parent group: sales of goods to Carraro Drive Tech Italia (CDTI) ₹774 cr in FY26 (₹550 cr in FY25) — about 34% of revenue; the company says it has "less visibility" of end demand because it exports to its parent. [AR FY26 RPT note 36.2; Q1 call]
- Capacity FY26: axles 1,17,898 and transmissions 37,605 units at 79.8% use (weight-based); gears ~1.89 mn at 76%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Mix: TBH (telescopic handler) axles for a major international OEM ~₹160 cr and backhoe-loader parts ~₹66 cr to the main export customer in FY26 (analyst figures, not disputed). [Q1 call]

- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Jun-25 493; Sep-25 586; Dec-25 570; Mar-26 607; Jun-26 545 (+10% YoY). Operating profit (ex other income): 48, 52, 55, 63, 44; OPM 10%, 9%, 10%, 10%, **8%**. Net profit: 29, 32, 28, 42, 31. [Screener]
- Company basis Q1 FY27: total income ₹558.7 cr; EBITDA ₹57.9 cr (+6%), margin 10.4% on total income vs 11.0%; PAT ₹31.4 cr (+8%). Other income ₹14.0 cr included a one-time ₹8.8 cr customs provision write-back. Stripping all other income, EBITDA was ~₹43.9 cr (8.1% of revenue) vs ~₹47.8 cr (9.7%) a year earlier — core margin fell ~160 bp. Gross margin 28.0% vs 28.7%. [IP Q1FY27; derived]
- Annual (Screener): FY24 sales ₹1,789 cr, OPM 7%, profit ₹63 cr; FY25 ₹1,808 cr, 9%, ₹88 cr; FY26 ₹2,255 cr (+25%), 10%, ₹131 cr (+48%); TTM ₹2,307 cr, ₹133 cr. Company FY26 EBITDA ₹247.5 cr (10.8% on total income, incl. ₹28.5 cr other income of which ₹8.4 cr was a vendor-provision reversal); FY26 also had a ₹9.5 cr exceptional charge for the new labour codes. [Screener; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Returns strong: ROCE 29% FY26 (17% FY23), ROE 27%. [Screener]
- Unit economics: volume growth in Q1 "around 8% to 10%" per sales head (to be checked), little price in it yet. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (May 2026): FY27 revenue growth 8–12% in normal conditions, but "8% to 12% can become probably 4% to 8%" given West Asia; margin "we reached 10.8%, we will move upward, we're not going to slide backward"; capex ₹130–140 cr ("more on the ₹130 crores"); Turkey high-HP transmission SOP in FY27, Indian programme SOP FY28; localisation 78% → 86–88% in 2–3 years; FY30 revenue ₹3,500–4,000 cr ("exceeding our earlier target"); new bull-gear order ~₹15 cr a year from FY28; Montra Electric e-transmission engineering deal ₹17.5 cr. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +10% (within the normal-case range); Turkey SOP **met**; margin **slid backward** (10.4% vs 10.8%; core 8.1%); localisation fell to 74% (emergency imports); exports −14% (container/vessel shortages). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 call guidance (official transcript): if no new shocks, FY27 growth "in the range of maybe up to 10%"; EBITDA up "probably by half a point" if price increases are fully recovered; full-year cost recovery "zero-sum" target; exports back on track "already from Q2"; FY30 ₹3,500–4,000 cr kept. [Q1 call, official]
- Net: revenue guidance edged down (8–12% → "up to 10%"), margin guidance moved from "only upward" to "+0.5 point if we recover everything". [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- Switching costs: 12–18 month validation cycles; supplies all Indian backhoe-loader OEMs except JCB, so market-share shifts among them do not hurt Carraro. [Q1 call]
- Share gains: domestic 4WD agri growth ~30–35% vs 4WD market ~20–22% (FY26); BHL drivelines +18% vs market +14% (Q1 FY27) and +9% vs market −2% (FY26). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- 36 tractor customers — "we are covering everybody"; two new prototypes in Q1 = two new projects (revenue in 1.5–2 years). [Q1 call]
- Group technology: IPR for domestic products transferred to Carraro India in 2023; access to group R&D. [IP Q1FY27]
- Structural tailwind: 4WD share of 40HP+ tractors ~24% in FY26, expected 40–45% in 3–5 years; GST cut narrowed the 2WD–4WD price gap; ~60–65% of 4WD axles are outsourced. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Income-tax disputes ₹210 cr** (contingent liability, ₹206 cr a year earlier) — about 37% of net worth; the auditor (Deloitte Haskins & Sells) made direct-tax litigation its key audit matter. Transactions with group firms are subject to transfer-pricing studies. [AR FY26 note 33/34; auditor's report]
- Parent dependence and payments: ~34% of revenue sold to CDTI; payments to the group in FY26 — royalty ₹9.9 cr, corporate service charges ₹27.7 cr (+38%), professional fees ₹20.0 cr (₹12.4 cr to Carraro SpA, new), purchases ₹85 cr. Royalty + service + professional fees ≈ ₹57.5 cr, ~44% of FY26 net profit. Shareholders asked to approve material RPTs with CDTI (₹155.7 cr already in Q1 FY27). [AR FY26 note 36.2; AGM notice]
- Customer concentration: major customers 48.9% of revenue (43.0% in FY25). [AR FY26 note 34.3]
- Quality of reported margin: two consecutive periods flattered by one-off other income (FY26 ₹8.4 cr reversal; Q1 FY27 ₹8.8 cr customs write-back). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Supply chain: Tier-2/3 migrant-labour shortages, ad-hoc supplier premiums, 3–4 month pass-through lag; energy costs. [Q1 call]
- Turkey market "in turmoil", Europe agri subdued, US decisions delayed. [Q1 call]
- Latest credit rating found is India Ratings (Apr 2025), older than 12 months; not read.

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 cautious ("4% to 8%"), Q1 "We feel the worst is over. We have bottomed out." [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Numbers: margin slipped instead of rising; exports fell 14%; localisation 78% → 74%; new disclosure of domestic/export segment split after analysts asked. [Q1 call]
- New issues: supplier labour shortages and a "bidding" war for components; container shortages; customs write-back. [Q1 call]

## My read
Carraro is a well-run, high-return business riding a genuine structural shift (2WD to 4WD tractors) with strong domestic share gains; returns of ~29% ROCE and positive free cash flow are real. But Q1 FY27 showed the margin is thinner than the headline — core EBITDA was ~8% once one-offs are removed — and growth guidance has drifted down to "up to 10%". The parent relationship cuts both ways: it supplies technology and export demand, but takes a rising share of profit in fees and carries ₹210 cr of tax disputes. The price needs ~10% growth with margins recovering to 10%+; I would turn more positive on a clean (ex-one-off) margin back above 10% and exports recovering in Q2.
