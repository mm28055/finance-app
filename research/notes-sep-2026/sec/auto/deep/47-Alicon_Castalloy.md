# Alicon Castalloy (NSE: ALICON, BSE: 531147) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Price ₹730, market cap ₹1,198 cr, P/E 32.3, ROCE 10.6%, ROE 7.0%, 52-week range ₹580–1,025 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- India's largest aluminium-casting foundry: gravity and low-pressure die castings (GDC/LPDC), machined and painted — cylinder heads, intake manifolds, brackets, EV motor/battery/inverter housings, e-axle housings, charge-cooler tanks [IP Q1FY27 slides 4–8; CRISIL Aug-26].
- Plants: Shikrapur and Chinchwad (Pune), Binola (Haryana), and Illichmann Castalloy s.r.o. in Slovakia; tool room and technology centre in Austria [IP Q1FY27 slide 8]. 92 customers, 809 live parts [IP Q1FY27 slide 5].
- Customers: Hero, Bajaj, Maruti Suzuki, Daimler India CV, Mahindra, Honda, Toyota Industries Engine India, Ashok Leyland; JLR (via e-axle), Lamborghini/Audi parts in Europe [CRISIL Aug-26; Q4 call].
- Single reportable segment (aluminium castings) [AR FY26 Note 42]. Standalone FY26 sales: domestic ₹1,537 cr, exports ₹124 cr (FY25: ₹1,347 cr / ₹184 cr) — **exports fell ~33%** [AR FY26 Note 42].
- FY26 mix by vehicle: two-wheelers ~42%, passenger vehicles 34%, commercial vehicles 17–18%, non-auto 5–6%; exports incl. deemed exports ~20% [Q4 call, official]. Q1 FY27: auto 96% / non-auto 4%; domestic 88% / exports 12% [IP Q1FY27 slide 15].
- Tonnage: FY26 ~34,000 tonnes; Q1 FY27 9,124 tonnes (Q1 FY26 ~9,000); Europe only 150–200 tonnes a quarter [Q1 call, official].
- Utilisation: "around 80%" in the Q1 presentation, but the CEO said "close to 100%… more than 90% for sure" on the call — the two do not match [IP Q1FY27 slide 11; Q1 call]. Q4 call analyst cited 78%.
- Europe (Illichmann s.r.o.): revenue ₹122 cr in FY26 vs ₹186 cr in FY25; pre-tax profit ₹0.74 cr vs ₹10.4 cr [AR FY26 Directors' report].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (13 May 2026):** FY27 revenue +8–10% ex-aluminium ("modest"); absolute EBITDA +20%, i.e. margin up ~1.5 pts to "12.5% to 13%"; capex ₹130–150 cr (₹50 cr maintenance); at least one new factory in FY27; "not looking for any further write-offs"; Daimler order peak ₹80–90 cr a year from H2 FY27; order book ₹7,600 cr over FY26–FY31 after removing stalled programmes [Q4 call, official].
- **Q1 FY27 call (14 Aug 2026):** revenue growth raised to 12–15% ex-aluminium; margin guidance cut to "at least 1%" improvement; capex ₹150 cr incl. ~₹70 cr of the ₹125 cr new Shikrapur facility; order book ₹8,450 cr (the slide shows ₹8,094 cr); inorganic move promised "by the fourth quarter" [Q1 call, official; IP Q1FY27 slide 14]. Note the CFO's prepared remarks still said "8% to 10%".
- Delivery: Q1 ex-aluminium growth 17.5% beat the new range; margin 9.5% reported (11.4% "adjusted" by management for aluminium) vs a promised step-up to 12.5–13% — **behind**. JLR e-axle finally shipping at 600 sets/week after an 18-month customer delay [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Verdict: growth guidance **raised**, margin guidance **cut**; the multi-year order-book number has been cut once (₹9,100 cr → ₹7,600 cr in Q4) and rebuilt.
- Longer term: management says revenue should "almost double" by 2030 [Q1 call]; CRISIL (Aug-26) assumes only 6–8% growth and 10.5–11.5% margins over the medium term [CRISIL Aug-26].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹141 cr (₹14,090 lakh standalone); FY25 ~₹181 cr investing outflow [AR FY26 Directors' report; Screener cash flow]. Three-year spend of ~₹500 cr was questioned by analysts; management said ~50% is maintenance [Q4 call].
- New leased 1.35 lakh sq ft plant ~5 km from Shikrapur: ₹125 cr over 2–3 years, possession 1 Sep 2026, SOP by March 2027, starting at 3,000 tonnes rising to 7,000 tonnes; revenue ~₹500 cr a year in 4–5 years; all initial business already committed [Q1 call, official; IP Q1FY27 slide 17].
- Implied asset turn ~4x on the new plant at peak (₹500 cr / ₹125 cr), vs management's usual 2.5–3x target and <2x on the existing base [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Funding: internal accruals plus borrowings "as required" [Q1 call]. CRISIL expects ₹100–150 cr capex a year and debt under ₹400 cr [CRISIL Aug-26].
- Order book: ₹8,094 cr of booked incremental revenue scheduled FY26–FY31 (₹991 cr, ₹1,021 cr, ₹1,276 cr, ₹1,376 cr, ₹1,629 cr, ₹1,798 cr) [IP Q1FY27 slide 14]. Mix: EV ~16%, hybrid ~12%, structural ~10%, rest ICE; non-auto 2% (~₹126 cr); new business ~40% export-linked [Q1 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 wins: 8 new parts from 5 customers (2 two-wheeler, 2 CV, 3 non-auto, 1 PV via Illichmann); 6 domestic, 2 export; entry into two large Indian PV/CV OEMs with ~₹850 cr five-year visibility; data-centre HVAC compressor housing; first aluminium tractor cylinder head for the world's largest tractor maker [IP Q1FY27 slide 11; Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹354 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹366 cr; standalone net debt ₹291 cr vs ₹312 cr [Screener; AR FY26 Note 43]. Cash only ~₹20 cr; working-capital lines ~85% used over the 12 months to May 2026 [CRISIL Aug-26].
- Operating cash flow strong: ₹239 cr in FY26 (128% of operating profit), FCF ₹93 cr [Screener]. Part of this came from stretching suppliers: payable days rose from 99 to 135; management cited vendor-financing arrangements [Screener; Q4 call].
- Debtor days 110, inventory 62, cash conversion cycle 37 days (61 in FY25) [Screener].
- Dividend FY26: ₹2 interim + ₹3 final proposed = ₹5 per share; payout 24% [AR FY26; Screener].
- Rating: CRISIL A/Stable, outlook cut from Positive on 12 Aug 2026 for weaker-than-expected revenue and margins; A1 short term [CRISIL Aug-26].

## Moat evidence
- Scale and process range in aluminium castings (GDC, LPDC, machining, painting) with in-house tooling (20 tools/month) and validation labs [IP Q1FY27 slide 8].
- Switching evidence: some new Indian OEM wins were "immediate transfers of tooling" to Alicon because others could not make the part; single-source supplier to India's largest hybrid maker; ~1 million PV cylinder heads a year [Q1 call].
- Pricing: aluminium is passed through, now monthly with most OEMs ("no loss, not benefit"); labour and overhead recovery needs negotiation [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Weakness: return on capital has been 10–15% for five years; ROCE 11% in FY26 [Screener]. Management admits two-wheeler parts are "mostly commoditized" and deliberately cut 2W share by 1–2% [Q1 call].
- Renewable power >50% of electricity (captive solar associates) — lowers energy risk and helps with European customers [Q4 call; AR FY26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party capex:** Alicon bought ₹36.2 cr of plant/equipment (net) from promoter group company Atlas Castalloy in FY26 and ₹43.5 cr in FY25 — about a quarter of FY26 capex; also pays it rent (₹4.0 cr), purchases (₹6.6 cr) and expenses (₹6.3 cr) [AR FY26 Note 44]. The board report says RPTs are at arm's length; worth watching given weak returns on that capex.
- **Management cost:** key management pay ₹22.2 cr in FY26 (incl. ₹5.3 cr share-based, ₹4.9 cr commission) vs consolidated PAT ₹34.4 cr [AR FY26 Note 44E; consolidated P&L].
- **Contingent liabilities:** corporate guarantees ₹54.6 cr (up from ₹46.2 cr, likely for the European subsidiary), customs interest on unmet export obligations ₹9.4 cr, bank guarantees ₹9.7 cr, court cases ₹2.5 cr [AR FY26 Note 48].
- **Auditor:** Kirtane & Pandit LLP; unqualified; key audit matters on capitalisation of plant and on contingent liabilities [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Guidance and disclosure gaps:** order book quoted as ₹8,450 cr on the call vs ₹8,094 cr on the slide; utilisation 80% vs "close to 100%"; FY26 PAT given as "₹24 crore" on the official Q4 call vs ₹34.4 cr audited; the prior ₹9,100 cr order book was cut by removing four stalled programmes [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26].
- **Europe:** Slovak unit revenue −34% and near break-even; recovery promised from Q4 FY27 [AR FY26; Q1 call].
- **Cost inflation:** Haryana minimum wage +35% at Binola from April 2026; aluminium spike after the Middle East war (₹30–35 cr quarter-on-quarter cost hit in Q4) [Q4 call].
- **EV bets slow:** Gogoro and other EV programmes cut or delayed; management is pivoting back to ICE/hybrid cylinder heads [Q1 call].
- Promoter holding drifted from 55.72% (Sep-23) to 53.79% (Jun-26) [Screener]. CEO change on 1 Apr 2026 (Rajeev Sikand → Sumit Bhatnagar) and heavy senior hiring [AR FY26 Note 44].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: May was defensive ("I do not want to make promises of an extraordinary bumper year"); August was expansive ("multifold growth", inorganic plans, a second new site hinted).
- Numbers: growth guide 8–10% → 12–15%; margin step-up 1.5 pts → "at least 1%"; order book ₹7,600 cr → ₹8,450 cr; new plant quantified (₹125 cr, ₹500 cr revenue).
- New: non-auto wins (data-centre HVAC, tractor aluminium head), two new Indian OEMs, JLR shipping; explicit admission that EV bets underdelivered.

## My read
Alicon is growing again at the top line — Q1 FY27 was its first ₹500 cr+ quarter and new Indian OEM wins look real — but it has not yet shown it can turn sales into profit: margins fell from 13% (FY24) to 9.5%, depreciation keeps climbing, and ROCE is stuck near 11%. CRISIL's outlook cut in August (6–8% growth, 10.5–11.5% margins) is a sober counterweight to management's "double by 2030". At a P/E in the low 30s on depressed earnings, the price needs margins back above 11% and the Europe/JLR ramp to show up by Q4 FY27. What would change the view positively: two quarters of 11%+ EBITDA with tonnage growth; negatively: another order-book cut, more related-party capex with Atlas Castalloy, or debt rising above ₹400 cr.
