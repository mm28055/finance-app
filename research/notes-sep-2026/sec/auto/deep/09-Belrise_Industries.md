# Belrise Industries (BELRISE) — deep research

Research date 27 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore (company documents use ₹ million; converted). Screener = consolidated page, read from the raw HTML rows. Listed May 2025 (formerly Badve Engineering).

## Business and segments
- Sheet-metal and polymer parts maker, strongest in 2-wheeler (2W) chassis, exhaust systems, fuel tanks, BIW (body-in-white) parts; growing proprietary lines — suspension (5 OEM customers), steering columns (4), high-tensile steel parts (4), braking. [IP Q1FY27]
- Manufacturing revenue by vehicle type, Q1 FY27: 2W/3W ₹1,788.7 cr (81.4%, +18%), 4W passenger ₹101.2 cr (4.6%, +22%), 4W commercial ₹187.5 cr (8.5%, +16%), others (aerospace, defence, renewables, subsidy) ₹120.5 cr (5.5%, +69%). Exports 4.5% of manufacturing revenue. [IP Q1FY27]
- A **trading** arm (Badve Engineering Trading FZE, Ras Al Khaimah, UAE) trades steel and other metals: ₹1,774 cr in FY26 (18.7% of revenue, +4.6%); Q1 FY27 ₹348.6 cr (−19%, Middle East disruption). Low-margin and dilutes consolidated margin. [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]
- Customers: Bajaj Auto (largest, dependent "over the medium term"), HMSI, Hero, Tata Motors, a Japanese 4W OEM, and newer wins at "a fast-growing 2W/3W OEM" and "a large Japanese 2W OEM". Top three customers are 55–60% of revenue. [CRISIL Feb-26; Q4 call]
- Footprint: 27 plants in 13 cities, plus Belrise SDM (France) and Chester Hall (UK, aero-engine machining); 890+ robots, 90,000+ tonnes a year capacity. [IP Q1FY27]
- Acquisitions: H-One India (high-tensile steel, Japanese parent), Mag Filters, SDM (France, at book value), Chester Hall (UK; £13.2 mn for £18.5 mn revenue, £2.1 mn EBITDA), Hyva India tipper-body business (US$5.65 mn ≈ ₹54 cr at ~3.6x EV/EBITDA; three plants; closing Q3 FY27). [Q4 call; Q1 call; News: Autocar Professional, Aug 2026]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener): Jun-25 sales ₹2,262 / operating profit ₹281 / OPM 12% / PAT ₹112 · Sep-25 ₹2,354 / ₹296 / 13% / ₹133 · Dec-25 ₹2,341 / ₹287 / 12% / ₹122 · Mar-26 ₹2,553 / ₹290 / 11% / ₹130 · Jun-26 ₹2,546 / ₹293 / 12% / ₹122. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹2,546.5 cr (+13%); manufacturing revenue ₹2,197.9 cr (+20%); EBITDA ₹293.3 cr (+5%), margin 11.5% (12.4% a year ago); manufacturing EBITDA ₹279.3 cr (+10%), margin 12.7% (13.8%); PAT ₹121.7 cr (+9%). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- FY26: revenue ₹9,509 cr (+15%); manufacturing ₹7,735 cr (+17%); EBITDA ₹1,154 cr (12.1%); manufacturing EBITDA ₹1,058 cr (13.7%); adjusted PAT ₹502 cr (+41%, helped by interest falling from ₹307 cr to ₹232 cr after IPO debt repayment). [Q4 call; Screener]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹7,484 → FY25 ₹8,291 → FY26 ₹9,509; OPM 12% each year; PAT ₹314 → ₹355 → ₹497. 3-year sales CAGR 13%. [Screener]
- Unit economics: 2W content per vehicle rose from ~₹12,000 to ~₹20,000 in 18 months — but management said this figure **includes** merging two promoter companies (Badve Autocomps, Eximius); 4W content ₹25,000 → ₹40,000–45,000 including H-One. [Q4 call]
- 2W/3W grew 18% in Q1 against industry production of 20–25%; management blamed customer mix (faster-growing OEMs where it was small). [Q1 call]
- ROCE 14.5%, ROE 12.6% — modest for the multiple. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (25 May 2026): FY27 mid-teens revenue growth; EBITDA margin "broadly stable" vs FY26 (12.1%); capex 6–6.5% of manufacturing revenue (≈₹500–580 cr); double the 4W/CV business vs FY25 — needs ~40–45% growth this year; SDM to be EBITDA-positive in 2027; aerospace and defence to reach 10%+ of revenue in the medium term; QIP enabling resolution up to ₹2,000 cr. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: revenue +13% (inside "mid-teens"), but margin 11.5% (below FY26) and 4W/CV growth **18%**, far short of the 40–45% pace needed. Management "maintain[ed]" guidance, said "the worst is behind us" on costs and that pass-through will lift margins in coming quarters. [Q1 call]
- QIP done: ₹1,700 cr raised at ₹220 a share (7.73 cr shares, July 2026); "significant majority" to be deployed this fiscal year, mainly on acquisitions in aerospace and 4W/CV. The only deal named so far (Hyva) costs ~₹54 cr. [Q1 call; News: Business Standard, Jul 2026]
- Order wins (peak annual revenue): Q4 — ₹90 cr exhaust/fuel tanks (fast-growing 2W/3W OEM, SOP Q2 FY27), ₹220 cr exhaust and metal parts (large Japanese 2W OEM, SOP Q4 FY27); Q1 — ₹65 cr+ chassis (same fast-growing OEM, SOP Q4 FY27), ~₹50 cr chassis for a large Indian 2W OEM (Q4 FY27), ₹150 cr+ sheet-metal assemblies for a US solar-tracker maker (2.5 GW, Q4 FY27), 59 assemblies plus tooling for an Indian commercial EV, suspension/braking for a Vietnamese 2W entrant, 3W brake assemblies. Run-rate with the fast-growing OEM ~₹200 cr. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex ~₹618 cr in FY26 (cash from operations ₹876 cr minus free cash flow ₹258 cr, Screener); FY27 guide 6–6.5% of manufacturing revenue, including R&D (163 people). [Screener; Q4 call]
- New capacity: Bangalore-2 brownfield (SOP Q2 FY27), Bhiwadi-2 brownfield (Japanese OEM, SOP Q4 FY27), Haridwar ramp-up, solar-tracker line, Hyva's three plants. Four new facilities are adding staff costs ahead of revenue. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Most growth orders start only in Q4 FY27, so FY27 revenue will be back-end loaded. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹597.7 cr at Mar-26 (borrowings ₹1,521 cr, down from ₹2,964 cr after the ₹2,150 cr IPO fresh issue). The July QIP (₹1,700 cr) should leave the company net cash until deployed. [Q4 call; Screener; CRISIL Feb-26]
- Cash from operations ₹876 cr in FY26 (90% of operating profit); free cash flow ₹258 cr. Cash conversion cycle 48 days; working-capital days rose to 59. [Screener]
- Dividend ₹0.55 per share final for FY26 (~10% payout). [Q4 call; Screener]
- Rating: CRISIL AA-/Stable (long term), A1+ (short term), Feb 2026. CRISIL expected gearing 0.2–0.3x at Mar-26 and cash accrual ₹800–900 cr a year. [CRISIL Feb-26]

## Moat evidence
- Among the top three 2W metal-component makers with ~24% share (prospectus data, Mar-24). [IP Q1FY27]
- Vertical integration and automation in exhaust systems; claims to be one of the only Indian firms able to plate stainless-steel exhausts. [Q4 call]
- Broke a three-decade single-supplier hold on 2W/3W steering columns; rescued a Japanese OEM's supply within eight weeks, which led to a ₹220 cr order. [Q4 call]
- High-tensile steel up to 1,470 MPa through the H-One Japan tie-up (industry ~600 MPa) — enables EV/PV localisation wins. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Aerospace certifications (via Chester Hall/SDM) are hard to get (12–24 months); single-source reverse-thruster parts for a major aircraft platform. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Heavy related-party trading with promoter companies:** the annual report lists ~50 promoter-linked entities. FY26 purchases from Badve Autocomps ≈₹477 cr and sales to it ≈₹314 cr; purchases from Eximiius Autocomps ≈₹344 cr and sales ≈₹162 cr; Shreepriya Auto purchases ≈₹63 cr, sales ≈₹68 cr, rent ≈₹9 cr (read from a poorly formatted table; treat as approximate). Badve Autocomps and Eximius Infra Tech are being merged in "at close to book value" (8.3x P/E), which management says will "materially reduc[e] RPTs"; Eximiius Autocomps stays outside. [AR FY26 RPT note; IP Q1FY27]
- **Guarantees for promoter companies:** a corporate guarantee to Eximiius Autocomps' Bandhan Bank loan (₹41.3 cr outstanding) and land worth ₹32.0 cr pledged for Eximius Infra Tech's Bank of Maharashtra loan (₹214 cr outstanding). [AR FY26 note 35]
- **Trading subsidiary guarantees:** US$30 mn SBLC and a US$68.75 mn guarantee to a trade-finance lender for the Dubai trading arm (US$34.3 mn ≈ ₹325 cr drawn); a £13.86 mn guarantee for the UK acquisition loan (Apr 2026). [AR FY26 note 35]
- **Promoter selling then company raising:** promoter-group Sumedh Tools sold 6.56% (~₹900 cr) at ₹153.7 in Dec 2025; the company raised ₹1,700 cr at ₹220 seven months later. Promoter stake 73.0% → 66.5% → 61.1%. [News: TradingView/Moneycontrol, Dec 2025; Screener shareholding]
- **Growth quality:** part of the content-per-vehicle gain comes from merging related parties, not new business. [Q4 call]
- **Customer concentration:** 55–60% from Bajaj, HMSI and Hero. [CRISIL Feb-26]
- **Governance churn:** company secretary resigned 7 June 2026; the H-One merger scheme was withdrawn and refiled. [AR FY26 Board's report]
- **Auditor:** unqualified; key audit matter is revenue (year-end price variations). Contingent liabilities small (~₹21.9 cr, mainly indirect tax ₹12.1 cr unpaid). [AR FY26]
- **Capital deployment:** ~₹1,650 cr of QIP money is still to be spent; aerospace deals abroad carry integration risk (SDM lost ₹9.5 cr one-time in Q4 FY26). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 focus: aerospace (Chester Hall), Japanese OEM rescue, stable-margin promise. Q1 focus: ₹1,700 cr QIP, Hyva, solar trackers, many new 2W orders. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Numbers: manufacturing growth held at ~20% but consolidated margin slipped 90 bps YoY; trading fell 19%. [IP Q1FY27]
- The 4W/CV doubling target was not repeated in Q1; aerospace talk shifted to "localising high-volume aero-engine components in India". [Q1 call]

## My read
The core 2W business is growing ~20% with real new-customer wins, debt is gone, and the order book for H2 FY27 (Japanese OEM, solar trackers, new chassis) is visible. But reported margins are drifting down, the promised 4W/CV doubling is well behind, ROCE is only ~14%, and governance needs watching: large purchases and sales with dozens of promoter companies, guarantees for them, and a promoter sale shortly before a company raise. The price needs roughly 20% earnings growth for years; watch Q2–Q3 margins (back to ≥12%), how the ₹1,650 cr of unspent QIP cash is used and priced, and whether related-party flows actually shrink after the merger.
