## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** hot-forged and machined bearing rings (inner/outer rings, 20 mm ID to 900 mm OD) and auto components (gear blanks, ring gears, hubs, shafts, transmission and chassis parts); ~85% of revenue is machined, 15% forged-only [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.10].
- **Q1 FY27 segment revenue (₹ cr):** export auto components 118 (Q1 FY26: 90, +31%); domestic bearing rings 86 (90); domestic auto components 45 (53); export bearing rings 32 (35); scrap ~20 + export incentives ~3 = 23 (21). Auto components ₹163 cr (+13%), bearing rings ₹118 cr (−6%) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.5].
- **FY26 segment revenue (₹ cr):** domestic bearing rings 386 (FY25: 329), domestic auto 170 (177), export bearing 154 (155), export auto 350 (399), scrap 71 (78), incentives 13 (16); total ₹1,143 cr [Q4 call].
- **Geography FY26:** India 54%, US 22–23%, Europe ~22%, Mexico/Canada/others a few % [Q4 call]. US exports fell ~30% in FY26 after tariffs hit 53%; Europe +25%, domestic +50% [Q4 call].
- **End markets:** ~50% passenger vehicles, ~20% CV/heavy-duty, 14–16% industrial, ~7% EV/hybrid [Q1 call]. Industrial share fell from ~26% (FY22) to 18% (FY26) [Q1 call].
- **Customers:** top 10 customer groups ~84% of FY25 sales [CARE Aug-25]; top 5 groups 65–70% across 15+ plants; wallet share 45–50% with the three largest bearing makers and 35–40% with some auto-component customers [Q4 call]. The large bearing customer whose business keeps declining is Timken ("That is the major factor") [Q1 call].
- **Capacity:** 26 forging lines, 1,65,000 tonnes a year, 625 CNC spindles; utilisation 63–65%, target 70–72% by FY27-end [IP Q1FY27 p.11; Q1 call]. Domestic hot-forged bearing ring market in its size range ₹1,600–1,800 cr, Rolex share ~30% [Q4 call].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr: revenue / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 292 / 62 / 21% / 49; Sep-25 271 / 55 / 20% / 44; Dec-25 275 / 58 / 21% / 48; Mar-26 306 / 56 / 18% / −0.3 (after the ₹50.4 cr RoR charge); Jun-26 304 / 69 / 23% / 60 [Screener].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹1,222 cr, op. profit ₹262 cr (21%), PAT ₹156 cr; FY25 ₹1,155 / ₹241 (21%) / ₹174 cr; FY26 ₹1,144 / ₹230 (20%) / ₹141 cr (after exceptional ₹51.6 cr); TTM ₹1,156 / ₹237 / ₹152 cr [Screener]. Sales have been flat for three years (3-yr CAGR −1%) [Screener].
- **Q1 FY27 detail:** gross margin 55.6% (50.7% a year ago); EBITDA ex-other income ₹69 cr (22.6%, +150 bps per presentation); other income ₹19.8 cr; PAT ₹60.2 cr (19.8%) [IP Q1FY27 p.6].
- **Returns:** ROCE 21%, ROE 16% (FY26), down from 32% ROCE in FY23 [Screener].
- **One-offs in FY26:** ₹50.4 cr Right of Recompense (RoR, lenders' claw-back from the 2013 debt restructuring) exceptional charge; ₹1.2 cr labour-code charge; ~₹22 cr US customs duties expensed in Q4 of which ~half is recoverable from customers; ~₹6 cr legal fees on the RoR settlement [AR FY26 note 32; Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (18 May):** FY27 revenue growth 15–17% ("mid-teen"), FY28 "high teens or beyond"; EBITDA margin "not less than 20.5%, 21%"; FY27 new-programme revenue ₹160–165 cr on top of existing business; capex ₹30–40 cr plus maintenance (~₹50 cr total); monthly dispatch order book ₹115–125 cr for H1; US refund on IEEPA tariffs expected by Sep-2026 [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** revenue +4.3% (₹304 cr), well below the 15–17% run-rate — blamed on a shop-floor labour shortage in April–June; margin 22.6% beat the 20.5–21% floor [Q1 call].
- **Q1 FY27 call (6 Aug):** "mid-teen" FY27 growth kept, FY28 "approaching 20%"; FY27 margin 21–22% (raised floor), +50 bps in FY28; export auto components ₹425–450 cr in FY27 (FY26 ₹350 cr); capex ₹30–40 cr a year; July 2026 was the highest-ever monthly revenue [Q1 call].
- **Track record:** the ₹175 cr of new orders flagged in Dec-2024 for FY26 delivered only ₹75–80 cr because of US tariffs [Q4 call]. Growth guidance has been missed for three years (revenue ₹1,222 cr in FY24 → ₹1,144 cr in FY26) [Screener]. To hit ~15% in FY27 after a 4% Q1, the rest of the year must grow ~19%.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹36 cr; FY27–28 ₹30–40 cr a year (plus maintenance); forging takes 70–75% of each rupee of capex [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Capital commitments only ₹2.8 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 34].
- Spare capacity (63–65% utilised) means growth needs little capex; fixed assets ₹447 cr vs revenue ₹1,144 cr ≈ 2.6x turns [Screener].
- New directions: a joint venture with an overseas partner for value-added machining in India (global agency appointed; "6–9 months"), registrations for defence (business hoped within 12 months) and aerospace; 9 MW more solar by mid-2026 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.17].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free since FY22; borrowings nil at Mar-26 [Screener]. Cash and liquid investments ~₹367 cr before the buyback [Q4 call]; ₹180 cr buyback of 1 crore shares at ₹180 (3.67% of equity; promoters did not tender) completed, shares extinguished 28 Jul 2026 [IP Q1FY27 p.18; News: Whalesbook, Jul 2026]. RoR fully settled: ₹101 cr paid 31 Mar 2026 [AR FY26 note 32].
- Cash flow: CFO ₹190 cr, FCF ₹155 cr in FY26 (₹177 cr FY25) [Screener].
- Working capital is heavy: inventory days 155, debtor days 70, cash-conversion cycle 164 days, working-capital days rose from 89 to 121 in FY26 [Screener]. Including goods in transit/consignment stock in US warehouses, inventory is 150–160 days [Q4 call].
- No dividend yet (payout 0%); management says more buybacks or dividends are "on the agenda" [Q1 call; Screener].
- Rating: CARE A-/Stable, A2+ (Aug-2025): gearing 0.04x, interest cover ~107x, free cash and liquid investments ₹310 cr at Mar-25 [CARE Aug-25].

## Moat evidence
- Long qualification: new customers/programmes take 15–18 months of validation; programmes run 5–7 years [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Share: ~30% of the domestic addressable hot-forged bearing-ring market; 45–50% wallet share with three biggest bearing makers; seven of the top ten customers associated for >15 years [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.14].
- Scale: claims competitors have 10–20% of its capability (26 forging lines, 625 spindles under one roof) [Q4 call].
- Pass-through: raw material and currency pass-through quarterly (half-yearly for some) with overseas customers; value-added/freight costs are not covered [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Limits:** as a tier-2 supplier to large tier-1s it has "limited bargaining power" [CARE Aug-25]; revenue has not grown in three years despite the moat; freight costs (2.5–3x, containers scarce) are absorbed until customers agree to reimburse [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter pledge:** CMD Manesh Madeka pledged 73 lakh shares (2.68% of equity, ~5% of his holding) to Equitus Finance and Tata Capital on 31 Dec 2025 [News: ScanX, Jan 2026]. The stored profile's account of promoters buying and then selling shares at a loss "for urgent need of funds" could not be verified from filings read.
- **Customer concentration:** top 10 groups ~84%; one US customer (Allison, per analysts) cut volumes 35–40% in FY26 and one bearing customer (Timken) keeps shrinking [CARE Aug-25; Q1 call].
- **Trade and logistics:** US Section 232 tariff 25% on its products; refund of excess IEEPA tariffs (paid until Oct-2025) only partly received; ocean freight 2.5–3x in Q2 FY27 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Revenue recognition:** consignment stock in US warehouses means duty is paid on entry but revenue is booked only when customers lift goods — auditor made revenue timing the key audit matter [AR FY26 auditor's report; Q4 call].
- **Contingent liabilities:** income-tax disputes ₹23.6 cr, GST ₹2.4 cr, electricity ₹1.3 cr (total ₹27.2 cr, up from ₹20.2 cr) [AR FY26 note 34]. Auditor (S R B C & Co) opinion unmodified.
- **Related parties:** small — family remuneration ~₹7 cr, subcontracting to relatives' firms Rapid Cut and Revolve Engineering ~₹1 cr, CSR ₹0.3 cr to promoter's NeoRajkot Foundation [AR FY26 note 36].
- **Labour:** dependence on migrant semi-skilled labour; the Q1 shortfall is a recurring seasonal risk [Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was about closing the CDR chapter (RoR paid, buyback, US refunds); Q1 about execution — labour shortage, freight shock, and the first sign of US recovery (export auto components +31% y/y, Allison business back near FY25 levels).
- Margin guidance raised (from ≥20.5–21% to 21–22%) but revenue guidance left unchanged despite a weak Q1.
- New items: JV search for value-added machining, defence/aerospace registrations, Dana (acquired by Allison) opportunity 12–18 months away.

## My read
A very clean, cash-rich forging business (20–23% EBITDA margin, no debt, CDR fully behind it) whose problem is growth: revenue has been flat around ₹1,150 cr for three years because of Timken, US tariffs and weak industrial demand. Q1 FY27 margins were excellent but revenue grew only 4%, so the full-year "mid-teen" guide needs ~19% growth for nine months. The price needs the growth story (FY28 "approaching 20%") to come true; watch Q2 revenue (July was a record), export auto components toward ₹425–450 cr, and the fate of the promoter pledge. A second weak quarter or no new customer to replace Timken would argue for a lower growth estimate.
