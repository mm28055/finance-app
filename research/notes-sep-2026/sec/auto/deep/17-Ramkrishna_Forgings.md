# Ramkrishna Forgings (RKFORGE) — deep research, Sep 2026

Figures from Screener's consolidated page unless tagged otherwise. Company "EBITDA" excludes other income, so it matches Screener's operating profit closely.

## Business and segments
- Forgings and castings maker (Jamshedpur-based): front axle beams, crankshafts, knuckles, ring-rolled parts, machined and assembled parts, now castings (via merged JMT Auto / Mal Metalliks, renamed Ramkrishna Casting Solutions), aluminium forgings, railway bogie frames, trailer axles. Mexico machining plant (bought Aug-2024) and a 51% rail-wheel JV with Titagarh (RTRWL, equity-accounted). [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27]
- Standalone revenue mix Q1 FY27: domestic auto 48.8%, railways 6.0%, mining/earth-moving/farm 5.7%, misc industrial 7.2% (domestic 67.7%); export auto 28.3%, export others 4.0% (exports 32.3%). FY25 exports were 40.8% and FY22 45.2% — the export share has fallen sharply. [IP Q1FY27]
- Export geography (standalone): North America ₹688 cr in FY26 vs ₹1,056 cr in FY25 (−35%); Europe ₹490 cr vs ₹414 cr (+18%). Q1 FY27: NA ₹222 cr, Europe ₹128 cr, total ₹354 cr (+11% YoY). [IP Q1FY27]
- Capacity ~3,95,800 t (press 2,39,050 t; forging 70,350 t; ring rolling 24,000 t; casting 62,400 t). Q1 FY27 utilisation: total 68% (74% in Q4), press 60%, forging 64%, ring rolling 127%, casting 79%. New press capacity of 43,000 t added Jan–Mar 2026 and 28,800 t of casting on 31 Mar 2026 explain the lower percentages. [IP Q1FY27]
- Volumes and price: forging volume up 4.0% YoY in Q1 FY27 (company chart); casting volume 8,593 t (+85% YoY). Realisation ~₹2.4 lakh/t export, ~₹1.8 lakh/t domestic; casting ~₹1.3–1.5 lakh/t. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Railways: 7.5% of FY26 revenue (4.6% FY25); bulk dispatch of assembled bogie frames started; company sees a ₹2,000 cr+ a year addressable market. Target: double-digit share of revenue in FY27. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- Trailer axles ~₹120 cr in FY26 at 4–5% share, target ~₹250 cr and ~10% share in FY27. [Q4 call]
- Customer concentration: top ten customers ~60% of revenue. [CRISIL Dec-25]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (4 May 2026): refused to repeat the earlier 10–15% growth guide "because of geopolitical risks". Promised: Q4 margin "here to stay", +100–150 bps if energy costs are passed on; casting 85–90% utilisation by H1; press ~85% by end-FY27; cold forging 75–80% by year-end; debt cut of ₹400–500 cr; capex ₹300–400 cr (incl. ~₹50 cr JV equity); 40,000 rail wheels (₹400–450 cr revenue) in FY27 with plant start end-May/June and 300 trial wheels in June/July; exports above 40% of volumes within two years; PV 10% of revenue in two years; railways double-digit share. [Q4 call]
- Q1 call (24 Jul 2026): targets revived — ₹8,000 cr revenue by FY29 ("one year delayed" from FY28), 22–25% CAGR; ROCE 12–15% in FY27 and 20% in FY28; exports ~35% of consolidated revenue in FY27, 20%+ export growth; capex ~₹350 cr; net debt ₹1,900 cr (from ₹2,000 cr), heading to ~₹1,500 cr; working-capital days to fall ~10 a year. No margin range given. [Q1 call]
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: margin +85 bps (**met**); debt −₹100 cr (**on track**); rail wheels — only trial production, 300 samples now in August and bulk supply Sept/Oct (**slipped ~1 quarter**); casting utilisation 79% vs 85–90% target (**behind**, on a larger base); press utilisation 60% (**far from 85%**); railways share 6.0% in Q1 vs double-digit target (**behind**); Mexico ~₹6 cr revenue, "significant" only from Q3 (**slipped**). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Earlier history: the ₹8,000 cr by FY28 target was set before FY25; FY25 and FY26 growth were 9% and 5%. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Order wins: Q4 FY26 ₹594 cr (4-year life; ₹323 cr CV — over half export — ₹258 cr energy storage in North America); Q1 FY27 ₹278 cr auto (82% PV — a domestic EV brand — 18% 2W crankshafts) plus ₹15 cr metro. Order book to be executed: ₹1,550 cr of new orders in FY27; FY28 ~₹2,800 cr (₹1,100 cr incremental) per the Q4 presentation. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- ~₹800 cr+ spent over FY25–FY26 (press lines, casting, aluminium, Mexico, JV). CWIP down to ₹337 cr from ₹498 cr as projects were capitalised; fixed assets ₹3,762 cr. [Q4 call; Screener]
- FY27 capex ~₹350 cr (incl. ₹20–30 cr more JV equity); next big capex only by end-FY28 once aerospace/other orders are visible. Aerospace/titanium/inconel needs just ₹10–20 cr for heating equipment now; "zero" aerospace orders; meaningful revenue at least 2 years away (Q4 call: not before FY29). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Management's asset-turn claim: ~2.5x on net block at full use (~₹9,000 cr revenue at 100%); realistically 75–80% utilisation before the next capex. Screener shows FY26 revenue/fixed assets ~1.1x. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Aluminium forgings: bulk supplies to global EV makers started; margins ~14–15% but on ~₹400/kg metal, so high absolute profit per piece. [Q4 call]
- Capital commitments ₹183.7 cr at Mar-26 (₹295.8 cr a year earlier). [AR FY26, standalone note 35B]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and range: one of India's larger forging groups with hot, warm and cold forging, ring rolling, casting, machining, assembly and aluminium ("press to paint"). [IP Q1FY27]
- Railway approvals: bulk supply of assembled bogie frames and a contracted 80,000-wheel Railways order through the JV (plus ~1.2 lakh wheels from the Titagarh partner). [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Pass-through: steel passed on with a one-quarter lag; forex passed on through contracts; gas/energy not automatically — being negotiated under force majeure clauses. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Ring rolling at 121–130% utilisation, no plans to add. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Weakness: FY25–26 showed limited pricing power — margins fell from 21% to 14% when US Class 8 demand and tariffs (26% US reciprocal tariff) hit; exports fell from 41% to 32% of revenue. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- Accounting failure: an external fact-finding study (report 14 Jun 2025) confirmed inventory overstatement of ₹220.5 cr at Mar-25 and ₹50.2 cr at Mar-24 from "erroneous accounting entries and non-recording of rejections"; FY25 and FY24 figures restated. Management called it "internal upheavals" on the Q1 call. [AR FY26 Corporate Governance para j; Q1 call official]
- Auditors (joint: S.R. Batliboi & Co. LLP and S.K. Naredi & Co. LLP): FY26 standalone opinion unmodified; key audit matter on revenue recognition timing and recoverability of ₹867 cr of trade receivables. Audit-trail feature not enabled for some software. [AR FY26, auditor's report]
- Off-balance-sheet support: shortfall undertaking for the rail-wheel JV of ₹524.8 cr (₹176.7 cr a year earlier) against JV loans of ₹1,029 cr; corporate guarantees for subsidiaries ₹642.5 cr (Casting Solutions ₹417 cr, Mexico ₹178 cr, US ₹47 cr); ₹37.5 cr bank guarantee for the JV. The JV's debt is not in consolidated net debt. [AR FY26, standalone notes 35A and 39]
- Other contingent liabilities are small: excise/service tax ₹6.7 cr, GST ₹0.8 cr. [AR FY26, note 35A]
- Governance lapses: board-meeting gap over 120 days (Jan–May 2025), fined ₹10,000 by both exchanges. Promoter-group entity Ramkrishna Rail & Infrastructure (3.59%) asked to be reclassified as public. [AR FY26 Secretarial Audit]
- ECL provision of ₹42 cr in Q4 FY26 described as "headroom" — earnings management risk. [Q4 call]
- Execution load: wheel JV, Mexico, casting, aluminium and aerospace all ramping at once; wheel timeline already slipped. [Q1 call]
- Exposure to US Class 8 cycle and tariffs; energy and freight costs not fully passed through. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- FII holding fell 24.5% → 20.4% over the year. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone from guarded (no numbers, 42 cr ECL, shipping costs) to bullish: "best period for RKFL has just started".
- New numeric targets: ₹8,000 cr by FY29, ROCE 20% FY28, exports 35% of FY27 revenue.
- New theme: non-ferrous (titanium, inconel, Nimonic) for aerospace, robotics and semiconductors — with zero orders so far.
- Wheel plant slipped from May/June start to Sept/Oct bulk supply; Mexico "significant" revenue pushed to Q3.
- Order intake fell from ₹594 cr (Q4) to ₹293 cr (Q1) and shifted from CV/export to domestic PV/EV.

## My read
The operating recovery is real — margin up from 13% in Q2 FY26 to 18% in Q1 FY27 and a record ₹840 cr operating cash flow — but it has not yet reached shareholders: interest and depreciation absorb most of it, ROCE is 6%, and trailing earnings are small. The FY25 inventory overstatement, the negative CRISIL outlook, over ₹1,100 cr of JV and subsidiary guarantees, and a record of missed targets (₹8,000 cr was once an FY28 goal) mean management's 22–25% growth path should be discounted. The price already assumes a steep earnings rebound; what would change the view positively is wheel-JV bulk supply by Q3, net debt near ₹1,500 cr by March 2027 and exports back above 35%. A slip in any of these, or another accounting surprise, would argue for caution.
