# Lumax Industries (LUMAXIND) — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged. Both calls read in full. The stored Q4 FY26 transcript is the official BSE filing (matches official). The stored Q1 FY27 transcript (11 Aug 2026) is a **tidied copy (figures match; gaps)** of the official BSE transcript filed 18 Aug 2026: it says connector localisation is "approximately 20%" where the official text says 24%; it rounds SIAM data (93.6 vs 93.5 lakh units; 3W 3.6 vs 3.5 lakh; CV growth 16% vs 15%); and it says "five plants" won GreenPro certification where the official text says five products from those plants. All company financial figures, guidance and order-book numbers match.

## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** automotive lamps — headlamps (front lighting 68% of Q1 FY27 revenue), tail lamps (23%) and other lamps (9%); plus moulds/tooling sold to OEMs at each new model launch (₹63 cr in Q1 FY27 vs ₹75 cr a year earlier; ~₹180–185 cr in FY26) [IP Q1FY27 p.23, 25; Q1 call].
- **Vehicle mix Q1 FY27:** passenger vehicles 64%, 2/3-wheelers 31%, CV and others 5% (FY26: 65/29/6) [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A p.82].
- **Technology mix:** LED 63% of Q1 FY27 revenue (61% a year ago; 35% in FY23, ~39% FY24, 58% FY25, ~61% FY26); target 65–70% [IP Q1FY27 p.23; AR FY26 MD&A p.80].
- **Customers (Q1 FY27, manufacturing revenue ₹1,160 cr):** Maruti ~₹306 cr (26%, +43% y/y), M&M ~₹187 cr (16%, roughly flat), HMSI ~₹160 cr (14%), Tata Motors ~₹131 cr (11%, +68%); Maruti+M&M+HMSI = 56% [IP Q1FY27 p.24; Q1 call]. Top 3 OEMs ~61% of FY26 revenue [AR FY26 MD&A p.85]; top 8 ~85% [ICRA Apr-26].
- **Wallet share (management):** Maruti "less than 30%" today heading to 35–40%; M&M 40–50% of its lighting; HMSI tail lamps to rise 2–3x in FY28 [Q1 call]. ICRA: leading PV lighting supplier and third largest in 2W [ICRA Apr-26].
- **Ownership/technology:** Stanley Electric (Japan) owns 37.5%, the D.K. Jain family 37.5%; technical tie-up since 1984; Lumax owns 21.28% of SL Lumax (Hyundai-Kia supplier; FY26 turnover ~₹2,900 cr, EBITDA margin ~14%) [AR FY26 MD&A p.80; Q4 call].
- **Capacity:** 12 plants (Haryana, Uttarakhand, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat); capacity utilisation 70–80% [AR FY26 p.~20; ICRA Apr-26]. Exports minimal [ICRA Apr-26].
- **Order book:** ₹2,500 cr at Q1 FY27 (₹2,200 cr at Q4; ₹1,760 cr at Q3 FY26) — ~90% LED, 12% EV, 60% PV / 40% 2-3W; ~60% (≈₹1,500 cr) starts production in FY28 [IP Q1FY27 p.22; Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr: revenue / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 923 / 82 / 9% / 36; Sep-25 1,009 / 89 / 9% / 36; Dec-25 1,053 / 111 / 11% / 47; Mar-26 1,200 / 124 / 10% / 54; Jun-26 1,223 / 110 / 9% / 51 [Screener]. Company-reported Q1 FY27 EBITDA ₹113 cr (9.2%), Q4 ₹124.9 cr (10.4%) [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹2,637 cr, op. profit ₹231 cr (9%), PAT ₹111 cr; FY25 ₹3,400 / ₹280 (8%) / ₹140 cr; FY26 ₹4,184 / ₹406 (10%) / ₹172 cr; TTM ₹4,485 / ₹433 / ₹187 cr [Screener]. FY26 PAT absorbed a ₹17.8 cr one-off for the new labour codes [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A p.83].
- **Growth:** sales CAGR 3 yrs 22%, 5 yrs 24%; profit CAGR 3 yrs 21%; ROE 22%, ROCE 18% (FY26) [Screener].
- **Below the operating line:** depreciation up from ₹93 cr (FY24) to ₹152 cr (FY26) and interest from ₹49 cr to ₹75 cr — so PAT margin stays ~4% even as EBITDA margin rises [Screener]. A large part of PAT is the SL Lumax associate share (Q1 FY27 ₹12.1 cr of ₹51 cr) and SL Lumax dividends [IP Q1FY27 p.26].
- **Unit economics:** PV lamp content ₹15,000–20,000 per car on average (up to ~₹30,000 on premium models), expected ₹22,000–25,000 in 4–5 years; an LED headlamp carries 4–6x the rupee content of a halogen one [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A p.80].
- **Margin drags:** Q1 FY27 had 120–130 bps net commodity/other cost impact (CMD said ~150 bps from delayed recoveries); Q4 FY26 had 90 bps forex hit; Haryana minimum-wage rise ~30–35 bps [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (1 Jun 2026) promised:** FY27 EBITDA margin 10.5–11%; FY27 capex ₹100–150 cr (maintenance ₹40–50 cr); growth "at least 2x of the industry"; margin ~13% in 3–4 years; "significant increase" in mould revenue; trying to move OEMs to monthly cost-recovery amendments; interest cost ~₹80 cr [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 delivered:** revenue +32.6% (industry production +22%) — beat; EBITDA margin 9.2%, below Q4's 10.4% and below the full-year band — missed for the quarter; monthly amendments **refused** by most OEMs (only aluminium, not relevant to Lumax) — dropped [Q1 call].
- **Q1 FY27 guidance:** margin 10.5–11% **kept**, Q2 "definitely above 10%"; revenue 15–20% for FY27 (new, explicit); capex **raised** to ₹200–250 cr on new wins; mould sales ₹250–300 cr (vs ₹180–185 cr), mostly H2; FY28 capex ₹150–200 cr (tentative); revenue ₹9,000 cr+ by FY30–31 (15–20% CAGR) and "teen" EBITDA of ~13% in 3–4 years [Q1 call].
- **Inconsistency to note:** the FY26 annual report MD&A speaks of a "12% EBITDA target within two years" and "early teens within two–three years", while the call says 13% in 3–4 years [AR FY26 MD&A p.82, 83; Q1 call].
- **Earlier record:** FY26 capex was guided at ₹180–220 cr and ended at ₹350–410 cr (AR says ₹350–400 cr, presentation "₹410+ cr"); capex guidance has been raised twice in a row [AR FY26 MD&A p.80; IP Q1FY27 p.4].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26: ~₹350–410 cr committed (Chakan Phase 2 commercialised; Sanand 2 operational Q1 FY26; Bengaluru greenfield ₹140 cr approved Q2 FY26) [AR FY26 MD&A p.81; IP Q1FY27 p.4]. Screener FY26 investing outflow ₹265 cr; fixed assets rose from ₹1,106 cr to ₹1,434 cr [Screener].
- Bengaluru plant (Maruti and Toyota models) to be commissioned Q4 FY27; brownfield additions at Sanand and Bawal for new wins [Q1 call].
- FY27 capex ₹200–250 cr (was ₹100–150 cr); capital commitments ₹172.6 cr at Mar-26 (₹54.5 cr a year earlier) [Q1 call; AR FY26 note 42].
- **Asset turns:** FY26 revenue ₹4,184 cr on gross block-ish fixed assets ₹1,434 cr ≈ 2.9x [Screener]. Payback: management has not given one; order book of ₹2,500 cr (~60% SOP in FY28) is the justification.
- **Funding claim vs record:** MD&A says the FY26 capex was "internally accrued, not debt-funded", but borrowings rose from ₹888 cr to ₹973 cr (Screener, incl. leases) and ICRA attributes debt growth to "debt-funded capex undertaken over the last three financial years" [AR FY26 MD&A p.80; Screener; ICRA Apr-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹973 cr at Mar-26 (₹723 cr Mar-24) [Screener]; ICRA puts debt excl. leases at ~₹800 cr, debt/OPBITDA ~2.5x, TOL/TNW ~2.6x [ICRA Apr-26]. Net long-term debt ₹235 cr (Mar-26) → ₹209 cr (Jun-26); ₹85–90 cr repayment in FY27; short-term (working-capital) debt will rise with growth [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Cash flow: CFO ₹212 cr (FY25) → ₹373 cr (FY26); FCF −₹124 cr (FY24), −₹110 cr (FY25), +₹86 cr (FY26) [Screener].
- Working capital: debtor days 55, inventory days 90, payable days 145, cash-conversion cycle ~0 (FY26) [Screener]. The business is funded heavily by suppliers; ICRA flags "high reliance on short-term borrowings" and asset–liability mismatch [ICRA Apr-26]. Management expected working-capital days to rise in Q1–Q2 because of recovery lags [Q4 call].
- Dividend payout ~30–35% (Screener FY26 30%; management "35%") [Screener; Q4 call]. Rating upgraded to ICRA AA- (Stable)/A1+ on 27 Apr 2026 [ICRA Apr-26].

## Moat evidence
- Technology access: exclusive lighting partnership with Stanley Electric (a global top lighting maker); Czech and Taiwan design centres; first localised Adaptive Driving Beam in India; 5 patents granted, 36 filed [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.11].
- Share: leading PV lighting supplier; with associate SL Lumax covers customers making ~92% of Indian PV output and ~85% of 2W makers across the group [ICRA Apr-26; Q1 call]. Single-source win on Honda Activa and Shine [AR FY26 MD&A p.84].
- Switching costs: lamps are designed-in per model; tooling is model-specific and paid by OEM (mould revenue) — once awarded, supply runs for the model life [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call].
- Localisation as cost moat: SMT (circuit-board assembly) 100% in-house at Bawal; bare PCBs 40–50% local heading to 70–80%; connectors 24% heading to 40–50%; 70–90 bps margin gain expected over 2–3 years [Q1 call].
- **Limits:** no pricing power over OEMs — cost recovery lags a quarter and OEMs refused monthly resets [Q1 call]. ICRA: PV lighting is "highly competitive", keeping margins "moderate" (competitors Uno Minda, Fiem, India Japan Lighting, Marelli Motherson) [ICRA Apr-26]. Management expects competition "will increase and become stronger" [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration:** top 3 ~61% of revenue; M&M revenue flat for ~4 quarters despite a claimed 40–50% wallet share, because Lumax is absent from the XUV 7XO-type fast growers such as the XUV700 EV platform [AR FY26 MD&A p.85; Q1 call].
- **Import and currency exposure:** 25–30% of raw material/bought-out parts imported; significant China sourcing; tooling fully imported; laminate shortages for 7–9 layer PCBs [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Contingent liabilities (Mar-26):** customs demand for not adding Stanley design/drawing/testing fees to imported mould values — ~₹26.4 cr plus penalty ₹13.2 cr confirmed by the Commissioner in Mar-2024, under appeal at CESTAT (₹5 cr deposited); customs classification ₹26.9 cr; transfer-pricing additions AY22 ₹26.7 cr and AY23 ₹24.0 cr; new GST notices (interest on supplementary invoices; e-way bills) in FY26; Sanand property tax ₹2.5 cr [AR FY26 consolidated note 42].
- **Related parties:** royalty to Stanley ₹57.8 cr in FY26 (₹39.3 cr FY25), ~1.4% of revenue and ~34% of PAT; purchases from Stanley group ~₹322 cr; purchases from promoter-group Lumax Auto Technologies and Lumax Ancillary ₹478 cr and sales to Lumax Auto Technologies ₹204 cr; service charges to Lumax Management Services ₹31.6 cr [AR FY26 consolidated RPT note]. These are approved by the audit committee but are large relative to profit.
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matter is revenue recognition around price-variation accruals with OEMs (the estimate of recoveries/passes-through at year-end) — relevant because the margin story depends on these recoveries. CARO lists only small disputed customs dues [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Management:** Stanley nominee Tetsuya Hojo resigned from the board 26 May 2026 (routine rotation, no concern stated) [AR FY26 RPT list]. No promoter pledge disclosed.
- **HVAC diversification failed to scale:** ~₹18–20 cr a year against a ₹35–40 cr peak expected, because the Honda model sold poorly [Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from celebrating two double-digit-margin quarters to explaining a 9.2% quarter; the monthly price-reset plan was abandoned.
- Order book up ₹300 cr in one quarter (to ₹2,500 cr), prompting a doubling of FY27 capex guidance.
- New specifics: FY27 revenue guidance 15–20%, mould revenue ₹250–300 cr, FY30–31 revenue ₹9,000 cr+, content-per-vehicle roadmap, China "Global Resource Center" for sourcing.
- New risks: West Asia freight and commodity inflation, PCB laminate shortage; M&M model-mix weakness.

## My read
The business is improving on volume and content: revenue compounded ~22% a year for three years, LED share has nearly doubled since FY23 and the ₹2,500 cr order book gives FY28 visibility. The weak point is conversion to profit — EBITDA margin has only moved from ~9% to ~10%, depreciation and interest absorb much of the gain, royalty to Stanley is a third of PAT, and the Q1 dip shows the company cannot pass on costs quickly. For the stock to work, the price needs roughly the promised path (15–20% revenue growth plus ~100 bps a year of margin), so FY27 must land in the 10.5–11% band. Two quarters above 10.5% with debt flat would raise conviction; another quarter below 10% or a third capex upgrade funded by short-term debt would cut it.
