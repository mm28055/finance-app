# JTEKT India (NSE: JTEKTINDIA) — deep research, Sep 2026

Figures: Screener **standalone** (JTEKT India has no subsidiaries since merging JTEKT Fuji Kiko in Jan 2024; Screener's "consolidated" page is stale and stops at Sep 2023 — do not use it). Price ₹118, market cap ₹3,275 cr (Screener, 27 Sep 2026). JTEKT holds calls only twice a year (after Q2 and Q4). The two calls here are Q2 FY26 (18 Nov 2025) and Q4 FY26 (20 May 2026); there was no Q1 FY27 call, so Q1 delivery comes from the results filing and press reports.

## Business and segments
- India's leading steering-system maker for passenger vehicles: column electric power steering (C-EPS, 45.5% of FY26 sales), manual rack-and-pinion gears (26.5%), hydraulic steering (6.5%), columns (6.2%), others 10.5%; driveline (CVJs, case differentials, axles) is small — CVJ 4.9% of sales. Steering ~94%, driveline ~6%; exports 2.8% of sales. [IP May-26]
- Promoters: JTEKT Corporation Japan 66.02% plus JTEKT Column Systems (together ~69.6%) and Maruti Suzuki (total promoter 74.98%). Toyota owns ~20% of JTEKT Japan. [AR FY26; ICRA Jul-26; Q4 call]
- Customer mix FY26: Maruti Suzuki 60% (56% in FY25), Toyota 10% (12%), M&M + Tata 10%, Honda 5% (8%), exports 3% (2%), others incl. Renault-Nissan export models. ~100% share of business with Maruti (except select models) and nearly 100% with Toyota Kirloskar. [Q4 call; ICRA Jul-26]
- Plants: Gurgaon, 3 at Dharuhera, Chennai, 2 at Bawal; new Gujarat plant under construction (building completion early 2028). [IP May-26; Q2 call]
- Capacity and use FY26 (2-shift): CVJ line 1 3.94 lakh units, 83%; CVJ line 2 (Nov-25) 3.71 lakh, 27%; manual gear line 5 106%; line 6 (Aug-25) 21%; Chennai export gear line 4 (3.4 lakh) SOP May-26; C-EPS line 3 (Jul-25, 4.41 lakh) 48%. Manual gear capacity 32 lakh, C-EPS 15 lakh. [IP May-26; Q2 call]
- Unit prices: C-EPS ₹11,000–18,000+; manual gear ₹2,500–3,700; CVJ ₹2,800–3,900. [Q2 call]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Jun-25 566; Sep-25 639; Dec-25 680; Mar-26 780; Jun-26 718 (+27% YoY). Operating profit: 30.9, 46.3, 52.3, 71.1, 38.2; OPM 5.5%, 7.2%, 7.7%, 9.1%, 5.3%. Net profit: 10.8, 18.2, 20.4, 27.5, 6.2. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 is the worst margin in the table despite record Q1 sales: PAT fell ~43% YoY to ₹6.2 cr; depreciation (₹28.3 cr vs ₹21.0 cr) and interest (₹5.2 cr vs ₹4.2 cr) jumped with new capacity. The company held no call and the press reports give no reason (search snippets mention steel/aluminium costs and logistics, unverified). [Screener; News: Business Upturn, Aug-26]
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹2,245 cr, OPM 10%, profit ₹107 cr; FY25 ₹2,399 cr, 8%, ₹75 cr; FY26 ₹2,666 cr (+11%), 7.5%, ₹77 cr; TTM ₹2,818 cr, ₹72 cr. [Screener; IP May-26]
- Margins have drifted down for a decade: OPM 13% in FY18, 7–8% since FY20 except FY24; gross margin fell from ~29% to ~27% in two years; management said gross margin is ~660 bp lower than FY19. [Screener; Q4 call; Q2 call]
- Returns: ROCE 10% FY26 (company: 7.87%; FY24 17%), ROE 7.4–7.8%. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]
- FY26 EBITDA bridge (company): +0.15 pt from exports (₹55.1 cr → ₹66.4 cr), +0.23 pt other; −0.33 pt mix (Honda −33%, Renault-Nissan exports −16%), −0.23 pt forex gain booked in other income, −0.24 pt US tariff (₹6.3 cr), +0.18 pt employee cost. [IP May-26]

## Guidance history
- Q2 FY26 call: H1 margin 6.3% blamed on 1.6% PV market growth, delayed SOPs and low US exports; "these factors will improve in the next 6 months' time or the next 2 to 3 quarters" and margins return to original levels. Brazil (Stellantis platform via JTEKT Brazil) to start "June or July" 2026 at ~1 lakh units, potential 5 lakh; exports potential 8–10% of sales and ~₹150 cr within 2–3 years; new capex (₹460 cr) could add ~₹1,000 cr revenue. [Q2 call]
- Q4 FY26 call: H2 margin 8.48% delivered (vs 7.71%), full year 7.5%. Brazil dispatches started May 2026 (earlier than promised), 70,000 units in FY27, 1–1.5 lakh in FY28 (₹50–60 cr). Incremental revenue "conservative ₹800 cr" (₹300 cr already in FY26 + ~₹500 cr more), capacity fully used "within 18 months"; CVJ to ~₹250 cr from ₹130 cr at 90% use once Maruti's MPV EV launches (Oct 2026); Honda SUV EV mid-FY27 (~₹100 cr potential). Capex to "reduce definitely" — ~₹100 cr more for Gujarat plus normal capex. [Q4 call]
- ICRA (Jul 2026) expects margin 8–9% in FY27 but capex of ₹250–300 cr a year in FY27 and FY28 — more than management's "reduce" message implied. [ICRA Jul-26]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: sales +27% (ahead of the ₹500 cr-more path); but OPM 5.3% — below Q1 FY26 (5.5%) and far below the 8.5% H2 exit. Margin guidance (improvement in FY27) is so far **missed**. [Screener]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex ~₹800 cr over FY24–FY26: FY26 ₹444 cr (company), FY25 ~₹270 cr. CWIP ₹400 cr at Mar-26 (₹411 cr per management) against fixed assets ₹826 cr. [AR FY26 MD&A; ICRA Jul-26; Screener; Q4 call]
- Capital commitments ₹204.7 cr at Mar-26 (₹155.5 cr a year earlier). [AR FY26 note 37]
- Rights issue ₹249.9 cr at ₹108.10 per share (FY26), promoters took full entitlement; ~₹114 cr for Gujarat building/utilities, ~₹55 cr for CVJ forging machines (backward integration, 4–5 lakh units). ₹0.6 cr+ still in the rights-issue account at year end. [Q2 call; AR FY26]
- Asset-turn targets: C-EPS 2.5–3x, manual gear 2–2.5x, CVJ ~1.5x; blended target 3x vs ~2.2x now (company calculation includes CWIP). [Q4 call; IP May-26]
- Gujarat plant: ₹250 cr committed, ~₹130 cr spent, building done early 2028; built to serve Suzuki Gujarat and a future Toyota Maharashtra plant (~2028–29). [Q2 call; Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Bank borrowings rose from ₹153 cr (Mar-25) to ₹337 cr (Mar-26); debt/equity 0.29x; debt-service cover fell to 2.97x from 4.32x; total debt/OPBITDA 1.7x (0.8x in FY25). JTEKT Japan guarantees ₹59.8 cr of the bank debt. [AR FY26 MD&A; ICRA Jul-26; AR FY26 AGM notice]
- Free cash flow: −₹343 cr FY26, −₹82 cr FY25, −₹14 cr FY24 (Screener); operating cash flow halved to ₹99 cr (CFO/OP 64%). [Screener]
- Working capital tight: debtors 52 days, inventory 43, payables 58 (cash cycle 37 days). [Screener]
- Liquidity: ~₹72 cr cash (Mar-26), ₹111 cr unused WC lines, ~₹250 cr undrawn term loans; repayments ₹60–80 cr in FY27. [ICRA Jul-26]
- Dividend payout 27% of FY26 profit; yield ~0.6%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- ~100% share with Maruti on most models and with Toyota; full-system supplier (manual gear + C-EPS + CVJ) on e-Vitara, Victoris and the coming MPV EV; "we supply 100% of Maruti's EV business currently". [ICRA Jul-26; Q4 call]
- Manual gear share 40–45% of India; CVJ share small (GKN >60%), target 15%. [Q4 call]
- Parent technology and backing: ICRA now rates [ICRA]AA (Stable)/A1+ explicitly factoring parent support; access to unsecured Japanese bank loans. [ICRA Jul-26]
- Limits: pricing is "a very, very transparent and a very, very competitive process" with OEMs; "stiff competition… resulted in loss of business for a few key models in the past". [Q2 call; ICRA Jul-26]

## Risks and red flags
- Royalty to the parent is heavy and rising: ₹47.8 cr in FY26 (1.79% of turnover; 2.13% on the 84% of sales covered), equal to **62% of the previous year's net profit** (54.9% in FY25, 34.4% in FY24). Royalty grew 15.7% in FY26 vs turnover +11.1% and PAT +2.2%. Shareholders are asked to approve up to ₹550 cr a year of transactions with JTEKT Japan (20.6% of turnover: royalty, development cost, components, capital goods). [AR FY26 AGM notice]
- Tax disputes on disallowed royalty/expenses: ~₹37.5 cr (incl. interest) pending for FY20–FY23, part paid under protest. [AR FY26 CARO]
- Customer concentration: Maruti 60% and rising; Honda (≈100% supplier) volumes fell ~30–33%. [Q4 call]
- Capex overshoot vs returns: ROCE ~8–10% after ~₹800 cr capex; ICRA says capex will "constrain any material improvement in leverage and coverage metrics". [ICRA Jul-26]
- Margin credibility: H1 FY26 recovery promise was met in H2, then Q1 FY27 fell back to 5.3% with no call to explain.
- Auditor B S R & Co. LLP: unmodified opinion; key audit matter revenue recognition; audit trail operated. [AR FY26 auditor's report]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q2: defensive — margin down, SOPs delayed, US tariffs; Q4: confident — H2 margin 8.48%, Brazil started early, "conservative ₹800 cr" extra revenue. [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- Customer mix shifted further to Maruti (56% → 60%); Honda fell (8% → 5%). [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- Capex message softened from "₹1,000 cr revenue potential" to "fixed asset turnover 3x within 18 months" and "capex will reduce", later contradicted by ICRA's ₹250–300 cr a year. [Q2 call; Q4 call; ICRA Jul-26]

## My read
Volume growth is real — new Maruti full-system models, CVJ and the Brazil export order lifted Q1 sales 27% — but the conversion to profit is poor and getting worse: margins are in a decade-long drift down, depreciation and interest are rising faster than EBITDA, and the parent's royalty now takes a larger slice of profit than it did three years ago. The stock's price needs a margin recovery to 8–9% on ~₹3,300 cr sales in FY28; Q1 FY27 at 5.3% makes that harder. What would change my view: two quarters at 8%+ margin with CVJ/line-6 utilisation climbing, and capex actually falling as promised.
