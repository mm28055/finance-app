# Sandhar Technologies (NSE: SANDHAR) — deep research, Sep 2026

Figures: Screener consolidated unless stated. Price ₹574, market cap ₹3,455 cr (Screener, 27 Sep 2026). No Q1 FY27 earnings-call transcript had been filed on BSE or found online by 27 Sep 2026 (Screener lists only the Q1 presentation), so the "two calls" here are Q3 FY26 (16 Feb 2026) and Q4 FY26 (25 May 2026); Q1 FY27 delivery is taken from the Q1 presentation.

## Business and segments
- Maker of lock sets and mirrors (proprietary), aluminium die-castings, sheet-metal parts (frames, wheel rims, fuel tanks), operator cabins for excavators, and small EV electronics (battery chargers, motor controllers). 34 plants in India, 4 overseas (Spain, Romania, Poland, Mexico), 6 JV plants with Korean/Taiwanese partners. [IP Q1FY27]
- Product mix Q1 FY27: aluminium die-casting 34.4%, sheet metal 18.6%, locking systems 17.2%, cabins & fabrication 10.2%, assemblies 9.6%, vision (mirrors) 5.2%, others 4.8%. Two-wheelers 69.5% of revenue (66.1% a year ago). Geography: standalone 48.2%, Indian subsidiaries 42.5%, overseas 9.3%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Sole/single-source supplier of lock sets and mirrors to Hero MotoCorp, TVS (motorcycles) and Honda Cars; single source for wheel assemblies to TVS and Royal Enfield and for excavator cabins to JCB India. Top two customers (Hero, TVS) were 56.1% of 9M FY26 revenue (51.4% in FY25); top ten 79.8%. [India Ratings May-26]
- FY26 split (company): India business revenue ₹4,384 cr (+28%) with EBITDA ₹472 cr; five JVs revenue ₹257 cr, EBITDA ₹28.3 cr on ~₹62 cr invested; overseas lost €2.56m (₹26.2 cr) at EBT. India 2W revenue grew 35.1% against industry 12.9%. [Q4 call]
- New projects (Sundaram-Clayton aluminium business acquired March 2025, Khed City PV die-casting, Sanaswadi cabins, Chennai sheet metal, EV): ~₹341–342 cr invested, ₹468 cr revenue in FY26, ₹130 cr in Q1 FY27 with EBITDA ₹0.9 cr and PBT loss ₹7.4 cr. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 1,090; Sep-25 1,270; Dec-25 1,185; Mar-26 1,307; Jun-26 1,382 (record, +27% YoY). Operating profit: 82, 118, 108, 129, 105; OPM 8%, 9%, 9%, 10%, 8%. Net profit: 28, 73, 33, 64, 37. Sep-25 profit includes a ₹34 cr one-off gain on a Bengaluru land sale. [Screener; Q3 call]
- Company basis Q1 FY27: revenue ₹1,381.9 cr (+26.8%); EBITDA ₹117.3 cr (+15%), margin 8.49% vs 9.34% a year ago and 11.08% in Q4; PBT ₹51.1 cr (+41%); PAT ₹37.3 cr (+33%). Hits named: minimum-wage increases in Haryana and Uttarakhand ₹5.84 cr, energy cost ₹7.50 cr, new-project PBT loss ₹4.81 cr. [IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹3,521 cr, OPM 10%, profit ₹110 cr; FY25 ₹3,884 cr, 10%, ₹142 cr; FY26 ₹4,852 cr (+25%), 9%, ₹199 cr (+40%); TTM ₹5,144 cr, ₹208 cr. Sales CAGR 5 years 21%, profit 20%. [Screener]
- The company's FY26 "EBITDA ₹513 cr, 10.6%" includes other income (₹75 cr); Screener operating profit is ₹438 cr (9.0%), which matches India Ratings' ₹437.6 cr. [Q4 call; Screener; India Ratings May-26]
- Returns modest: ROCE 12% FY24–FY26 (16% in FY19), ROE 11.5%. The company says the existing India business earns ~21% ROCE (annualised, 9M FY26), which is dragged down by new projects and Europe. [Screener; Q3 call]
- Standalone is now under half the group: after moving castings and sheet metal into subsidiaries, standalone Q1 revenue fell 8.5% to ₹666 cr. [IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- Q3 FY26 call (Feb 2026) promised: overseas break-even in Q4 and no losses from April 2026 (back to 9–10% margin "starting" FY27); Sundaram-Clayton (SC) plant switchover by end-April/early May 2026, then 7–7.5% EBITDA margin and ~₹500 cr revenue in FY27; new projects turning around from April 2026; EV "close to break-even" next year; electronic mirrors SOP 1 April. [Q3 call]
- Q4 FY26 call (May 2026) reset: FY27 revenue growth "15% to 16% plus" (about ₹5,500 cr), excluding price re-triggers; margin up 0.25–0.5 points for the existing business; SC shift by end of Q2 with EBT losses for the whole of FY27 and turnaround from Q3; Khed City and Sanaswadi turning EBT-positive from Q2; EV and Romania profitable only in FY28; new projects to reach ~₹750 cr revenue (2–2.5x on ₹342 cr invested); capex 5–7% of revenue, i.e. ₹275–310 cr; aspiration to double revenue every 3–4 years and PAT ~₹450 cr at ₹10,000 cr revenue. [Q4 call]
- So between the two calls the SC timeline slipped a quarter, the SC profit target turned into a loss year, and the EV break-even moved from FY27 to FY28. Overseas break-even in Q4 was delivered (EBITDA 14.6%, EBT nil). [Q3 call; Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +27% (well ahead of 15%); margin down 85 bp YoY to 8.49% (guidance was up); overseas EBT −€0.37m (better than −€1.19m a year ago, but back in loss as warned for aluminium lag); presentation now shows SC turnaround in Q4 FY27 (was Q3) and Sanaswadi in Q3 FY27 (was Q2); Chennai sheet metal and EV in FY28; the new Hosur (Avigna) die-casting plant Phase I to start end-Aug 2026, completion March 2027. Guidance kept on revenue; timelines cut again. [IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex ₹1,406 cr over FY22–FY26 (India Ratings), including ₹299 cr in FY26 and ₹316 cr in FY25. FY27 plan: ₹275–310 cr per the CFO (5–7% of revenue), ₹200–250 cr per India Ratings, "largely funded through internal accruals". [India Ratings May-26; Q4 call]
- CWIP ₹115 cr at Mar-26; capital commitments (standalone) ₹22.4 cr (₹40.5 cr a year ago). [Screener; AR FY26 note 31]
- Asset turn target on new projects 2–2.5x (₹342 cr invested → ₹700–750 cr revenue). Group fixed assets ₹1,652 cr against FY26 sales ₹4,852 cr (≈2.9x). [Q4 call; Screener]
- Phase-wise: Hosur Avigna ADC (one of Sandhar's largest casting plants, includes 1,250-tonne HPDC), Chennai Oragadam sheet metal Phase II by March 2027. [IP Q1FY27]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹897 cr, gross debt ₹948 cr at Mar-26: ₹564 cr working-capital debt, ₹384 cr term loans; term repayment ~₹103 cr in FY27 (India Ratings: ₹146 cr FY27, ₹103 cr FY28). Screener borrowings ₹1,148 cr (includes leases). [Q4 call; India Ratings May-26; Screener]
- Subsidiaries hold 79% of group debt but earn ~36% of EBITDA (9M FY26). Overseas borrowings ~€47–49m against ~€46m annual revenue; part of the rise is reclassifying bill discounting into loans and euro translation (€1 = ₹90 two years ago, ~₹108–111 now). [India Ratings May-26; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Net adjusted leverage 2.53x at 9M FY26 — above India Ratings' 2.0x downgrade trigger for five straight years (FY22–FY26); interest cover 6.4x. [India Ratings May-26]
- Operating cash flow ₹210 cr FY26 (CFO/OP 60%, weakest in years); free cash flow −₹71 cr (Screener), −₹169.5 cr (India Ratings); FCF negative in FY25 and FY26. India Ratings expects +₹45–50 cr FCF in FY27. [Screener; India Ratings May-26]
- Working capital lengthened: debtor days 62 (52), inventory 67 (63) per Screener; CFO attributes it to one-off receipts timing and overseas inventory, normal 40–45 days receivables. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Dividend payout only 12% of FY26 profit (yield ~0.7%); no buyback. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Single-source status on locks/mirrors at Hero, TVS and Honda Cars; wheel assemblies at TVS and Royal Enfield; JCB cabins. [India Ratings May-26]
- Market-share gain: India 2W revenue +35.1% vs industry +12.9% in FY26. [Q4 call]
- Casting breadth: high- and low-pressure aluminium, zinc and magnesium, plus machining (TVS machining business bought earlier, SC aluminium business in 2025), from small parts to engine blocks — management calls itself "the only player" doing HPDC zinc too. [Q4 call]
- Smart locks at ~5,000 sets a month but adoption "slower than projected"; FY27 still 2–3% of sales. [Q4 call; Q3 call]
- Weakness: no pricing power on commodities — pass-through lags ~90 days in India and ~180 days overseas, which is why margins dip whenever aluminium jumps. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Customer and segment concentration rising: Hero + TVS 56%; 2W ~70%; India Ratings says new plants mostly serve Hero and TVS, so concentration will rise. [India Ratings May-26; IP Q1FY27]
- Serial timeline slips on new projects (SC: April → end-Q2 → Q4 FY27 turnaround; EV: FY27 → FY28). [Q3 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Europe: Barcelona holding company and its Romania/Poland/Mexico units — 9M FY26 revenue €34.3m (down from €37.5m), EBITDA ~6.9%, Romania project late; the parent supports it with an SBLC, and management openly says it will "relook at this business". [India Ratings May-26; Q4 call]
- Guarantees given by the company ₹624 cr at Mar-26 (₹411 cr a year earlier), mostly for subsidiaries' loans; GST disputes ₹15.6 cr (₹7.8 cr). [AR FY26 note 31; AR FY26 Directors' report p.45]
- Related parties: purchases of goods ₹60.7 cr in FY26 (₹53.4 cr) from Swaran Enterprises, a firm in which Mrs. Santosh Davar (promoter family) is a partner; lease rentals to promoter entities Sandhar Estates, Jubin Finance and Shorah Realty. Auditor and board say arm's length, but it is a recurring family-firm supply line. [AR FY26 RPT note]
- Auditor B S R & Co. (KPMG network): unmodified opinion; key audit matters are revenue recognition and impairment of ₹365 cr investments in subsidiaries/JVs; eight subsidiaries audited by other auditors; minor audit-trail reporting gap at one subsidiary and one JV. [AR FY26 auditor's reports]
- Labour: 30–50% state minimum-wage increases and ~20% absenteeism in April 2026 — ₹5.84 cr hit in Q1 alone. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Promoter holding 70.38% unchanged; Jayant Davar moved 4.36% to son Neel Jay Davar (intra-family). No pledge disclosed. [AR FY26; Screener]
- Ratings: India Ratings IND AA-/Stable, A1+ (affirmed May 2026); ICRA also rates (June 2026 update; rationale page did not load for me).

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "fixing the ₹50 cr drag" (₹25.8 cr overseas + ₹25 cr new-project losses in 9M FY26) to growth: "double our revenues every three to four years" and ₹450 cr PAT at ₹10,000 cr revenue. [Q3 call; Q4 call]
- New in Q4: labour "polycrisis", energy and wage inflation, and a hoped-for industry-wide price re-trigger; telematics via royalty-based technology transfer within 12 months. [Q4 call]
- Q3's overseas "9–10% margin from April" became "a little bit of a dip in Quarter 1" in Q4 — and Q1 did show an EBT loss. [Q3 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## My read
Sandhar is a volume story that is working on the top line: 25–27% growth for five quarters, driven by 2W share gains at Hero and TVS. The weak spot is conversion to cash and returns — margins are stuck around 9%, free cash flow has been negative for two years, leverage sits above its rating trigger and every new-project timeline has slipped at least once. If the Hosur plant, Sanaswadi and SC turn EBT-positive by Q4 FY27 and the price re-trigger arrives, FY28 margin could reach 10%+ and ROCE mid-teens; the price mainly needs ~15% growth with steady margins. I would change view on another timeline slip, on a further rise in guarantees to subsidiaries, or if Europe needs fresh equity from India.
