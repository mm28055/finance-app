## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** engineered off-highway components — 3-point linkage systems (3PL, the hitch that attaches implements to tractors; 47% of FY26 finished-goods sales) and precision machined parts (PMP: pins, bushings, articulated-joint parts for construction and agriculture machines; 51%), plus power take-off (PTO) parts and a new fabrication line [IP Q1FY27 p.13; Q1 call].
- **End markets Q1 FY27:** construction ~45% of revenue; aftermarket ~12% (15% in FY26, 20% in FY25, flat in absolute terms); large agriculture ~20% (FY26); rest small agriculture [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Channels:** warehouse sales (made in India, stocked in US/Europe/Mexico warehouses, delivered just-in-time) 56% in Q1 FY27 vs 50–52% a year earlier; locally made-locally sold 22% (25%); rest direct exports. FY26: warehouse 51%, direct exports 26%, India local 14%, US local 8%. Margin is set by channel — direct exports ~20% EBITDA "base", warehouse higher, local lower [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Geography FY26:** North America ~53%, Europe ~25%, India ~14%, Japan ~5%, Asia ~1.5% [Q4 call]. US ≈ 45–50% of revenue [ICRA May-26].
- **Customers:** top 10 of the global agriculture OEMs and the top 3 construction OEMs; largest customer ~26% of 9M FY26 revenue [ICRA May-26; Q1 call]. Presentation shows top-customer share 28%/27%/31% and top-10 76%/78%/84% (FY25/FY26/Q1 FY27) [IP Q1FY27 p.15].
- **Market share:** "dominant" global share in <70 HP tractor linkages; single-digit share in >70 HP (large ag) — the main runway; a fraction of top construction OEMs' relevant spend [Q1 call].
- **Capacity:** 7 plants (3 Ludhiana, 2 Noida, 1 Visakhapatnam, 1 US), 4 warehouses (2 US, 1 Europe, 1 Mexico), 67,320 tonnes a year installed [IP Q1FY27 p.14].

## Guidance history
- **Earlier:** FY26 guided "mid-teens" growth — delivered 21% [Q4 call].
- **Q4 FY26 call (26 May):** FY27 growth "in line with FY26" (~21%); Q1 in line with Q4; H2 > H1; EBITDA "comfortably above" 20% through-cycle; capex 2.5–3.5% of revenue; Ludhiana finishing-shop fire rebuild by year-end, fully insured incl. loss of profit [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** revenue +27%, Q1 ≈ Q4 as promised (₹347 cr vs ₹339 cr), operating margin 23.6% [Q1 call; IP].
- **Q1 FY27 call (5 Aug):** growth **raised** to "a couple of percentage points better" than FY26's 21% (≈23%); Q2 "in line with Q1"; FY28 growth to continue as large ag recovers in CY2027; warehouse share 52–56%; Mexico warehouse first deliveries Q3 FY27, "mid-single-digit million-dollar" revenue in FY27 [Q1 call].
- **M&A:** promised since the Dec-2022 IPO; ~12 targets evaluated, two near-deals collapsed; ~6 under review; no deal yet [Q4 call; Q1 call]. The Oct-2025 ₹101 cr special dividend was paid partly because a target was dropped [Q4 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Capex held at 2.5–3.5% of revenue (~₹30–45 cr a year); ₹12 cr in Q1 FY27 and ₹12 cr in Q4 FY26 [Q1 call; Q4 call]. Capital commitments ₹21.9 cr at Mar-26 (standalone) [AR FY26 note 32].
- Growth is done by "balancing capex" on bottleneck machines; US plant in-sourcing and Mexico warehouse funded within this [Q4 call].
- Fabrication: small/medium fabrication facility built ~2.5 years ago; "meaningful" in 18–24 months [Q1 call].
- Fixed-asset turnover ~1.7x gross block (FY26) [IP Q1FY27 p.16].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash ₹160 cr (Mar-26) → ₹190 cr (Jun-26); gross borrowings ₹155 cr at Mar-26 (incl. ₹25 cr lease liabilities) [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]. ICRA: cash and liquid investments ₹210–220 cr, debt ~₹80 cr, interest cover 23x [ICRA May-26].
- Cash flow: CFO ₹174 cr, FCF ₹148 cr (FY26); Q1 FY27 CFO ₹44 cr [Screener; Q1 call].
- Working capital: 136 days of revenue (Mar-26, from 155 in FY25), 139 days (Jun-26); inventory ~134 days by company measure, 409 days on Screener's cost-based measure; for warehouse sales, stock is held 5–6 months [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener; ICRA May-26].
- Dividends: FY26 total ₹170 cr incl. ₹101 cr special — payout 108% of profit [Q4 call; Screener].
- Rating: ICRA AA-(Stable)/A1+ reaffirmed 7 May 2026 [ICRA May-26].

## Moat evidence
- Global delivery model (India cost + US/EU/Mexico warehouses) lets OEMs choose price vs lead time; ICRA calls it a key strength [ICRA May-26].
- Share: dominant in <70 HP linkages; "100% supplier or large share" on the parts it supplies to top-3 construction OEMs [Q4 call].
- Stickiness: maintained share through a three-year downturn in which 3PL revenue fell ~30% [Q4 call]; order wins steady at ₹200–225 cr a year (annualised potential, rolling 12 months) [Q4 call].
- Pass-through: freight and raw material passed to customers; FY26 tariff impact largely passed on [Q4 call; ICRA May-26].
- **Limits:** earnings swing hard with the cycle (margin 22% FY23 → 15% FY25 → 24% Q1 FY27); single-digit share in large ag [Screener; Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Cyclicality:** large ag down ~30% in CY2025 and a further 15–16% expected in CY2026; recovery only in CY2027 [Q1 call].
- **Concentration:** largest customer ~26–31%; US ~50% of revenue; US tariffs on PMP/3PL remain a watch item [ICRA May-26; IP Q1FY27 p.15].
- **Margin quality:** Q1's headline 25.3–25.9% margin includes ₹7.8 cr other income; material cost at 33.3% is below the "normal" 34–37%, and ~2% of sales came from currency in some FY26 quarters — so ~24% is above the company's own 20% through-cycle anchor [IP Q1FY27 p.8; Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matters include inventory valuation, revenue timing, receivables and **related-party transactions** ("significant related party transactions, including cross-border transactions with subsidiaries…royalty payments, management service charges") [AR FY26 standalone auditor's report]. Dealings with promoter-controlled entities are tiny (rent ₹0.16 cr to Soni Holdings); the bulk is intra-group sales to US/German subsidiaries (₹330 cr) and ₹84 cr dividends from subsidiaries [AR FY26 note 40].
- **Guarantee:** corporate guarantee to banks for subsidiary rose to ₹21.9 cr (₹1.8 cr) [AR FY26 note 32]. Contingent tax/GST claims small (~₹9 cr).
- **Fire:** Ludhiana finishing-shop fire (Q3 FY26); insurance claim receivable ₹22.1 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 standalone notes; Q4 call].
- **Management change:** CFO Rohit Maheshwari left 12 Mar 2026; Sandeep Taneja from 16 Mar 2026 [AR FY26 note 40].
- **M&A risk:** three years of unfulfilled acquisition talk; a large deal could dilute returns [Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Growth guide raised from "in line with FY26" to "a couple of points better"; construction momentum stronger than expected (US data-centre/AI and EU infrastructure).
- Large ag recovery explicitly pushed to CY2027 (Q4 said Q4 FY26 was the trough); aftermarket weakness admitted (flat y/y).
- New: fabrication as a growth pillar while M&A stalls; Mexico first deliveries dated to Q3.

- "When we talk about 20% margins, we are talking about 20% margins over a cycle that is peak to trough." [Q1 call]

## My read
The business is in a genuine cyclical upswing with self-help: revenue +21% in FY26 and +27% in Q1 FY27 even with large ag still falling, driven by construction wins and a richer warehouse mix. But current ~24% operating margins are near the top of its range (22% at the FY23 peak) and partly helped by a weak rupee and low material costs, so the market should not capitalise them as normal; management itself anchors at 20%. The price needs ~20%+ growth for two to three years, which is plausible if large ag turns in CY2027. Watch Q2 (guided ≈ Q1), whether large-ag new wins show in revenue, and whether cash (₹190 cr) goes to a sensible acquisition or back to shareholders.
