# ASK Automotive (ASKAUTOLTD) — deep research, Sep 2026

Figures from Screener's consolidated page unless tagged otherwise. Company EBITDA includes other income (Q1 FY27 ₹164 cr vs Screener operating profit ₹158 cr).

## Business and segments
- Gurugram-based maker of two-wheeler brake shoes and pads (advanced braking systems, "ABS" in company usage — not anti-lock brakes), aluminium die-cast parts (crankcases, covers, housings, now alloy wheels) and control cables; 18 plants, 9,000+ workforce; JVs with Fras-le (Brazil), AISIN (Japan) and a new cable JV with TD Holding (sunroof cables, ASK 49%). Listed Nov 2023. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 Directors' Report]
- Q1 FY27 revenue by product: aluminium lightweighting (ALPS) 57.7%, braking 35.5%, cables 2.9%, others 3.9%, wheel assembly nil (exited from 1 Apr 2026; it was 5.3% a year earlier). By channel: 2W ICE OEMs 74.6%, 2W EV 5.7%, independent aftermarket 7.0%, PV 3.5%, exports 2.9%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Segment revenue Q1 FY27: braking ₹482 cr (+48%), ALPS ₹784 cr (+75%), cables ₹39 cr (+20%). Much of the ALPS rise is aluminium price pass-through (alloy up from ~₹285 to ₹365/kg). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Market position: ~50% share of India's 2W braking systems (company slide); supplies 70–80% of 2W EV makers; top three customers 60–65% of revenue; 2W >89–90% of revenue; OEMs 70–80% of sales. Honda is the largest customer with the highest content per vehicle. [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Jul-26; Q1 call]
- Capacity: Bangalore (18th plant) full (90% per the official Q4 transcript); Karoli (Rajasthan, ₹750 cr invested, 23 acres) 65% in Q4 → ~75% in Q1 → ~80% by Q4 FY27; Karoli monthly revenue up from ₹60 cr to ₹110 cr, seen at ~₹1,500 cr next year. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Exports: ₹141 cr in FY26 (₹147 cr FY25; target missed); ₹39 cr in Q1 FY27 vs ₹33 cr. Ford export order ₹40–45 cr in FY27, ~₹60 cr in FY28. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Key numbers
- Annual (Screener): revenue FY24 ₹2,995 cr → FY25 ₹3,601 cr → FY26 ₹4,176 cr (+16%). Operating profit ₹301 cr (10%) → ₹432 cr (12%) → ₹531 cr (13%). Net profit ₹174 cr → ₹248 cr → ₹297 cr. [Screener]
- Company FY26: revenue growth 16.2% reported, 20.1% "net" (excluding +3.1% alloy pass-through and −7% wheel-assembly exit); EBITDA ₹551 cr (13.1%, +24%); ROACE 26.9%, ROE 25.3%; dividend ₹1.85. Independent aftermarket +24.7% after GST on its products fell from 28% to 18%. [Q4 call]
- Quarters (Screener; revenue / op. profit / OPM / net profit): Q1 FY26 ₹891 / 120 / 13% / 66; Q2 ₹1,054 / 137 / 13% / 80; Q3 ₹1,084 / 141 / 13% / 80; Q4 ₹1,147 / 132 / 12% / 72; Q1 FY27 ₹1,358 / 158 / 12% / 85. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 company: revenue +52.1% reported, +25.3% net (alloy pass-through +33.4%, wheel-assembly exit −6.6%) vs 2W production +22.8%; EBITDA ₹164 cr (+32.7%), 12.0% margin (13.8% a year ago); PAT ₹85 cr (+28.8%); EPS ₹4.32. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Unit economics: management agrees EBITDA on "pass-through-adjusted" revenue is ~15%+ but says 15% is "not sustainable in our industry"; normal reported margin 13.5–14%. [Q1 call]
- Interest rising: ₹29 cr FY24 → ₹48 cr FY26 → ₹16 cr in Q1 FY27 alone; depreciation ₹69 cr → ₹114 cr. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (20 May 2026): FY27 growth "around mid-teens"; hold the 13.1% EBITDA margin except for the aluminium denominator effect; capex ~₹400 cr for FY27 (₹40–50 cr maintenance); exports +20%; alloy wheels for the Japanese customer from H2 with revenue ₹90–100 cr in FY27 and ~₹220 cr in FY28; sunroof cables from H2; minimum-wage increases to be compensated by OEMs. [Q4 call official]
- Q1 delivery: net growth 25.3% (**beat**); alloy wheel supplies began in Q1, a quarter early (**beat**); EBITDA margin 12.0% vs 13.1% (**missed on paper**, absolute EBITDA +33%); exports +18% (**near target**); all aluminium increases passed through with no lag pending. [Q1 call]
- Q1 call (5 Aug 2026): FY27 growth raised to "high teens"; capex raised to "something like ₹700 cr" for an unplanned new plant in the South (Bangalore) "at a war footing", to run before March 2027, funded by accruals plus term loans; debt/equity to stay below 0.5; margins 13.5–14% "should come" if aluminium eases and Hormuz clears; alloy-wheel confirmed orders ₹70–90 cr FY27 and ₹250 cr FY28; AISIN aftermarket JV to show small profit by year-end. [Q1 call]
- Inconsistency: on the Q1 call the CMD said the earlier capex guidance was "₹450 crore to ₹500 crore", but the Q4 transcript and CRISIL (Jul-26) both say ₹400 cr. Relative to ₹400 cr, the new ~₹700 cr is +75%. [Q1 call; Q4 call; CRISIL Jul-26]
- Taiwanese (LIOHO) alloy-wheel collaboration still "in final stages of testing" — guided for H2 for several quarters. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex: investing outflow ₹339 cr FY25 and ₹479 cr FY26; FY27 now ~₹700 cr (vs ₹400 cr planned in May). CWIP ₹152 cr at Mar-26. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Karoli: alloy wheels (Kyushu Yanagawa technology) started; ~20% land left for expansion. New South plant for a new order from an existing customer (unnamed; "broad-based, ICE and EV", mainly ALPS). [Q1 call]
- Solar: 9.9 MW at Sirsa running; 11.55 MW at Bikaner to commission in Q2 FY27; payback 5–5.5 years. [Q1 call]
- Asset turn: revenue/fixed assets ~2.7x in FY26 (₹4,176 cr / ₹1,566 cr). [Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹346 cr Mar-24 → ₹412 cr Mar-25 → ₹679 cr Mar-26; cash ~₹20 cr (CRISIL). Debt rose again in Q1 on aluminium-driven working capital; the company would not give the figure until Q2. Company debt/equity 0.50 for FY26. [Screener; CRISIL Jul-26; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Cash flow: CFO ₹303 cr FY24, ₹360 cr FY25, ₹301 cr FY26 (CFO/op. profit 71%); free cash flow −₹175 cr in FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital FY26: debtor days 26, inventory 41, payables 49, cash cycle 18 days (11 in FY25); the Mar-26 jump was blamed on the March aluminium spike. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Corporate guarantees of ₹934 cr for the wholly-owned subsidiary ASK Automobiles (₹604 cr drawn) — intra-group, already in consolidated debt. [AR FY26 standalone note 37]
- CRISIL AA/Stable/A1+ reaffirmed Jul-2026 on ₹364 cr bank lines; fund-based limits 70.6% used; expects net cash accrual ₹450–500 cr a year and interest cover 9–10x. [CRISIL Jul-26]
- Dividend payout 12%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Share: ~50% of India's 2W braking systems; long relationships (Hero 33 years, others 19–25 years). Safety parts need OEM homologation, which makes switching slow. [IP Q1FY27]
- Outgrowth: net revenue +20.1% in FY26 vs 2W production +11.8%; +25.3% in Q1 FY27 vs +22.8%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Pricing: aluminium is fully passed through back-to-back ("No lag pending, we have received everything"); the aftermarket passed on April increases after a conscious ₹5 cr hit in March. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Aftermarket: GST cut from 28% to 18% narrowed the price gap with grey-market and duplicate parts; aftermarket +24.7% in FY26. [Q4 call]
- Weakness: CRISIL notes OEM pricing pressure and constant need for new EV products; EV growth lagged because a large EV customer (Ola) shrank. [CRISIL Jul-26; Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Auditor (Walker Chandiok & Co LLP): unmodified opinion; key audit matter on revenue recognition (volume/scheme discounts, material-price accruals). Audit trail not enabled at database level for the accounting software. [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 46]
- Enforcement Directorate FEMA inquiry: order of 23 May 2023 sought FY2016-17 import/export and foreign-investment details; company says information was supplied and nothing further received. Disclosed as a contingency. [AR FY26 standalone note 37]
- Other contingent liabilities tiny (GST demand ₹1.18 cr). [AR FY26 note 37]
- Related parties small: purchases from promoter-linked AA Friction Materials ~₹17 cr (₹33 cr FY25); rent paid to promoter entities; executive remuneration ~₹7 cr each to the CMD and joint MDs. [AR FY26 consolidated note 39]
- Promoter selling: 85% → 78.95% (Sep-2024) → 74.95% (Dec-2025, 4% sold in the open market by K.S. Rathee to meet minimum public shareholding). Required by law, but further supply is possible. [Screener; AR FY26 Directors' Report]
- Independent director Arun Duggal resigned 13 Aug 2025 (no reason found in the AR sections read). [AR FY26 Corporate Governance]
- Business: heavy 2W and top-3-customer dependence; ICE-heavy braking (EVs use regenerative braking); aluminium spikes swell working capital and debt; capex jump to ~₹700 cr raises execution risk; draft ABS mandate uncertain. [CRISIL Jul-26; Q1 call]
- Disclosure gap: Q1 debt level withheld until Q2 results. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Growth guidance up from mid-teens to high teens; capex up from ~₹400 cr to ~₹700 cr for a new South plant decided "not even 15 days" before the call.
- Alloy wheels moved from "H2" to supplies already started; FY28 alloy-wheel orders now ₹250 cr (Q4: ~₹220 cr expected).
- Margin language shifted from "sustain 13.1%" to "13.5–14% should come" but dependent on aluminium and Hormuz.
- Debt: from "contained within limits" to "it will certainly increase", cap of 0.5x debt/equity.

## My read
ASK is a well-run, high-share franchise in 2W brakes and aluminium castings that keeps beating industry growth by 3–8 points, with ROCE around 25%. The reported numbers are distorted by aluminium pass-through: revenue growth looks huge and margins look low, while absolute EBITDA grew ~33% in Q1. The worry is the balance sheet direction — free cash flow was negative in FY26, debt nearly doubled in two years and capex is jumping to ~₹700 cr on short notice. At ~40x earnings the price needs mid-to-high-teens growth for several years and margins back near 13.5–14%; the view would change if the new plant slips, aluminium stays high and debt/equity heads above 0.5, or the top customers' volumes stall.
