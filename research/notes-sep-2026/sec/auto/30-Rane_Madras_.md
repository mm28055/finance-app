## PROFILE (Q4 FY26)
# Rane (Madras)

## Executive Summary
Rane (Madras) Limited (RML) exists to engineer and manufacture critical, highly engineered automotive components—spanning steering and linkage systems, engine valves, light metal castings, and safety products. By acting as a localized, high-quality manufacturing partner, RML solves a core problem for global and domestic OEMs: securing a reliable, cost-efficient supply chain for safety-critical components without sacrificing technological adaptability. 

Historically operating as a cyclical, commoditized supplier, RML is currently transitioning into a **Challenger** category, aiming for leadership in the Indian steering and friction space. This pivot is anchored by significant corporate restructuring (demerging the ZF JV, exiting the NSK joint venture while retaining technology rights), record-high order book accretion, and the recent strategic acquisition of Hindustan Composites' friction business.

## The Business Model

### Revenue Drivers
- **What do they sell?** Tier-1 automotive components, prominently manual and electric steering gears, suspension linkages, light metal die-castings, engine valves, brake components, and occupant safety systems (via its ZF joint venture). 
- **Who pays them?** Major domestic OEMs (Maruti Suzuki is a marquee client, alongside Mahindra and Tata), international automotive giants (HKMC, GM, Honda), and aftermarket distributors. 
- **How do they make money?** Revenue is generated through long-term, multi-year supply contracts linked to specific vehicle platform life cycles. Pricing is generally structured with raw material pass-through clauses, though lag effects exist. The company also generates higher-margin spot and recurring sales via an increasingly integrated aftermarket channel.

### Segment & Geographic Mix
Based on management disclosures through FY26, the revenue profile demonstrates heavy domestic OEM reliance with a growing export foothold.

| Category | Contribution / Key Dynamics |

### Competitive Moat (or Lack Thereof)
- **Is the moat Durable or Fleeting?** The moat is **transitioning to Durable**.
- **Evidence:** High switching costs heavily insulate the business. Steering systems and occupant safety products are highly regulated, safety-critical components that OEMs rarely re-source mid-platform life. Furthermore, RML has executed a masterful technology hedge: despite taking full control of the Rane NSK entity, they secured continuous lifetime licensing for NSK's legacy technology while simultaneously securing next-generation Electric Power Steering (EPS) technology from ZF. This dual-threat engineering capability has already yielded early non-Maruti OEM wins.
- **The Bear Case:** The moat remains vulnerable to client concentration (heavy reliance on Maruti's platform success) and severe product liability risks. Because RML essentially acts as a build-to-print or licensed manufacturer rather than an IP creator, they are exposed to localized OEM volume fluctuations and warranty liabilities caused by Tier-2 supplier failures. Legacy NSK steering contracts are still running at low single-digit margins.

## Growth Vectors
- **Where is the next $100M coming from?** 
  1. **Record Order Book:** RML closed FY26 with its highest-ever annualized new business booking of ₹712 crore across domestic and international markets, backed by another ₹55 crore in recent JV wins, setting up a strong ramp-up through FY27/FY28. 
  2. **Inorganic Margin Accretion:** The recent ₹370 crore acquisition of Hindustan Composites' friction business provides immediate scale and margin expansion in the non-steering portfolio. 
  3. **EPS Ramp-up:** Leveraging the new ZF technology license to capture the domestic transition toward electric steering.
- **Management Execution:** Leadership is demonstrating strict capital discipline following a strong Q4 FY26 (₹1,051.7 crore in revenue, +16.2% YoY). They resumed deleveraging this quarter, reducing net debt by ₹73.4 crore to close at ₹705.8 crore and pushing the debt-to-equity ratio below 1x. Instead of ballooning debt for their ₹191 crore FY26 capex, they are utilizing advances from non-core real estate monetization (Velachery land sale). Margin expansion closing at 9.5% proves post-merger synergy extraction is working despite geopolitical commodity friction, keeping their 11-12% structural margin target intact. 

## Key Risks & Governance
- **Forensic Red Flags:** The board proposed a ₹40 crore preferential allotment of warrants in Q4. Management positions this as a signal of promoter conviction and a minor capital optimization alongside pending real estate transactions. Raising minor equity while sitting on incoming land-sale cash and improving cash flows still warrants mild scrutiny regarding capital allocation efficiency.
- **Business Risks:** 
  - **Catastrophic Warranty Liabilities:** The ZF JV recognized a massive ₹230 crore provision (₹172 crore net of tax, RML share is 49%) in late FY26 for a potential 9.6-lakh vehicle recall in North America (HKMC Palisade seat belt unlatching in extreme cold). Final clarity is highly opaque and delayed by another 3-6 months. The provision explicitly assumes a targeted inspection rather than a 100% replacement rate, and insurance coverage is capped at just ₹75 crore.
  - **Macro Sensitivities:** Heavy exposure to global crude and surging steel prices tied to geopolitical tensions, which threatens near-term margin realization. 
- **What could derail the thesis?** A slower-than-expected recovery in specific OEM SUV/Tractor models, geopolitical commodity shocks obliterating margin expansion, or further slippages in the ZF warranty provision if the customer mandates full replacement.

## Investment Thesis
- **The Bull Case:** Rane (Madras) is emerging from a multi-year restructuring cycle leaner and highly capable. Peak consolidation pains are over, the D/E ratio is trending toward a healthy 0.5x target, and peak capex (revised upwards to ₹240-250 crore for FY27 to support heavy new order wins) is fully supported by internal accruals and land monetization. With an unprecedented ₹712 crore annualized order book, robust European export market share capture, access to ZF's EPS technology without equity dilution, and the margin-accretive Hindustan Composites acquisition, RML offers a compelling cyclical upturn play with a clear line of sight to double-digit EBITDA margins by FY27. 
- **The Bear Case:** Investors should steer clear if they are uncomfortable with the opacity of automotive warranty liabilities. The 9.6-lakh vehicle HKMC recall overhang at the ZF JV is severely delayed and could easily exceed current provisions if the customer mandates full vehicle replacements. Furthermore, domestic margin targets depend heavily on successfully passing surging commodity inflation onto OEMs and sustaining momentum in specific Maruti and Mahindra platforms, leaving RML highly sensitive to external macroeconomic shocks.
