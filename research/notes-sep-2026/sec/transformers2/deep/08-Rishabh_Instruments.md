# Rishabh Instruments (RISHABH) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Context: this is **not a transformer maker**. It makes electrical measuring instruments, low-voltage current transformers (CTs, small metering devices), solar inverters and, through Poland's Lumel Alucast, aluminium die-castings. It sits in the grid-equipment group because of CTs, metering and grid demand. Sources read: Screener consolidated page (raw rows), official Q1 FY27 transcript (BSE, 20 Aug 2026), official Q4 FY26 transcript (BSE), Q1 FY27 earnings presentation (BSE), Annual Report FY26 (BSE; MD&A, standalone and consolidated auditor's reports, CARO, notes 9, 46, 48), CRISIL rationale of 2 Mar 2026, the company's 21 Aug 2026 clarification on a CNBC-TV18 stake-sale report, and the stored input file.

## Business and segments
- **Two segments.** (1) Electrical and Electronic Instruments (EEI). This covers panel meters, multifunction and energy meters, CTs, test and measurement tools (multimeters, insulation testers), power-quality analysers, medium-voltage relays and controllers (Lumel SA, Poland), EMS/PCB assembly, solar string inverters, and SCADA software (Microsys, Czech). (2) High-Pressure Die Casting (HPDC), which is Lumel Alucast in Poland. [AR FY26 MD&A] [IP Q1FY27]
- **FY26 split:** EEI revenue ₹536.9 cr (+17.5%), 69.3% of the group, with adjusted EBITDA ₹133.0 cr (24.8%). HPDC revenue ₹238.3 cr (−9.6%), 30.7%, with adjusted EBITDA ₹3.3 cr (1.4%, up from −₹15.0 cr in FY25). [IP Q1FY27 p.11–12]
- **Entities (FY26):** standalone India ₹267.6 cr (EBITDA 22.6%); Lumel SA ₹228.6 cr (adjusted EBITDA 23.4%); Lumel Alucast ₹238.3 cr; USA, UK and China together about 13% of EEI (growth of 53%, 25% and 23%). [Q4 call]
- **Geography (FY26):** Europe is the largest region (roughly 62–71% across the periods on the presentation's geography chart), with Asia and "others" making up the rest. The US business is small at about ₹30 cr ($3 m) in FY26. [IP Q1FY27 p.12] [Q1 call]
- Management calls itself "the largest manufacturer of CTs in the world by value". It makes about **6,000 low-voltage CTs a day and is expanding to 10,000 a day**, with demand from data centres and metering. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Solar inverters were ₹8–9 cr in FY26, with ₹24–25 cr targeted for FY27. They are operationally profitable, but gross margin is only 15–20% against 60–70% on core instruments. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr; OP excludes other income)
- **Annual:** FY24 sales 690 / OP 72 (10%) / PAT 40 → FY25 720 / 49 (7%) / 21 → FY26 775 / 127 (16%) / 82. Revenue growth is slow (3-year CAGR 11%). The profit jump is a margin recovery, mainly Alucast going from a loss to breakeven plus EEI mix. [Screener]
- **Quarterly sales:** Jun-25 190, Sep-25 196, Dec-25 184, Mar-26 205, Jun-26 198. **OPM:** 15%, 17%, 17%, 16%, 17%. **PAT:** 20, 22, 21, 20, 19. So consolidated earnings have been flat for five quarters. [Screener]
- **Q1 FY27 (company):** revenue ₹198.3 cr (+4.2%), reported EBITDA ₹33.3 cr (16.8%, +17.3%), adjusted EBITDA ₹35.3 cr (17.8%, before ₹2.0 cr of ESOP/SAR cost), PAT ₹19.4 cr (−1.4%). [IP Q1FY27 p.8]
- **Why PAT fell while EBITDA rose:** depreciation was up ₹2.0 cr (Nashik plant, SMT line), other income down ₹0.7 cr, and **tax up ₹2.9 cr** (effective rate 18% → 27%). PBT actually grew 10.8%. Management blamed only depreciation. [IP Q1FY27 p.8] [Q1 call]
- **Segments Q1 FY27:** EEI ₹154.0 cr (+34.0%), adjusted EBITDA ₹38.2 cr (24.8%, +69%). HPDC ₹44.3 cr (−41.2%), adjusted EBITDA −₹2.8 cr (−6.4%), PAT −₹5.9 cr. Lumel SA ₹63.9 cr (+39%), EBITDA ₹15.3 cr (24%). Standalone India ₹77.6 cr (+25.6%), EBITDA 22.9%. [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Unit economics:** consolidated gross margin is 62.4% (Q1 FY27). Core instruments earn 60–70% gross and about 40–45% contribution; solar and part of EMS earn 15–20% contribution. [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- ROCE FY26 14% (FY25 5%), ROE 12%. Returns remain mediocre because HPDC ties up capital. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **FY26 promise (start of year):** consolidated EBITDA of about ₹110 cr. **Delivered:** reported ₹126.4 cr, adjusted ₹136.3 cr. This was **beaten**. [Q4 call]
- **Q4 call (18 May 2026) for FY27:** EEI revenue growth of 20–25% with EBITDA of 20–22%. Alucast "not to lose money", revenue about ₹180 cr (flat in euros), double-digit margin "next year" (FY28). Group adjusted EBITDA of about ₹150–160 cr is a "realistic guideline". US revenue of ₹100 cr in "3–4 years", with FY27 +40–50%. Solar ₹24–25 cr. The new Nashik facility to be commissioned by June–July. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 delivery (17 Aug 2026):** EEI beat (+34%, 24.8% margin). **Alucast missed**: −6.4% adjusted EBITDA against the "not lose money" promise. Breakeven was moved to "by end of FY27" and double-digit margins to "about 2 years". The US target was **tightened** to ₹100 cr in "2–3 years", with ₹45 cr this year. Nashik is partly commissioned, with full operation in 1–2 months. [Q1 call]
- **Guidance kept, not raised:** management refused to lift the EEI guide ("2%, 3% more. But we want to stick to the guidelines as we projected"). [Q1 call]
- **Arithmetic:** EEI +20–25% on ₹537 cr gives ₹645–670 cr. Alucast at about ₹180 cr takes the group to about ₹825–850 cr in FY27. Adjusted EBITDA of ₹150–160 cr would be about 18–19%. Q1's ₹35 cr adjusted is running just under that pace because of Alucast. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- **Nashik expansion:** two multi-storey buildings, built for "the next 4–5 years" and able to take output to **more than 2.5x** today. One floor is dedicated to automated solar-inverter lines (up to about ₹100 cr of solar revenue). Status: buildings complete, partly operational, fully live by about Sep–Oct 2026. The rupee cost was not disclosed on the calls. Screener shows ₹74 cr of investing outflow in FY26, CWIP up to ₹52 cr and fixed assets up ₹60 cr. [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [Screener]
- **CT capacity** is going from 5,000–6,000 to 8,000–10,000 a day, with machinery ordered. **SMT lines:** three at Lumel (the newest is high-end and 40% EU-funded), now assembling PCBs including Intel-chip motherboards; a fourth line will be added once three-shift utilisation is reached. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **M&A:** ₹50–200 cr targets are being assessed in the US, Europe and India. Nothing is definitive. [Q1 call]
- Asset turns: consolidated sales/fixed assets are about 2.1x (₹775 cr / ₹371 cr). EEI is asset-light, and Alucast is the heavy part. [Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Net cash:** ₹160.6 cr at 30 Jun 2026 (₹127.6 cr at Mar-26). Gross borrowings are ₹81 cr (Screener, Mar-26), mostly working-capital lines at subsidiaries. CRISIL cites ₹188 cr of unencumbered cash at Sep-25 and gearing of 0.17x. [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [Screener] [CRISIL Mar-26]
- **Working capital is heavy:** inventory 213 days, debtors 62 days, payables 91 days, cash conversion cycle **184 days** in FY26 (165 in FY25). CRISIL flags "large working capital requirement" as a key weakness. [Screener] [CRISIL Mar-26]
- **Cash flow:** operating cash flow ₹108 cr in FY26 (CFO/OP 105%). But free cash flow was −₹2 cr after capex (FY25 −₹11 cr). [Screener]
- **Dividend:** ₹2/share for FY26, the first since listing (payout about 9%). ESOP costs continue for three more years at a smaller scale. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [AR FY26 note 67]
- **Rating:** CRISIL upgraded it to **BBB+/Stable / A2** on 2 Mar 2026 (from BBB/A3+). Upgrade trigger: margin sustained above 14.5%. Downgrade trigger: margin below 11%, or a large debt-funded capex or acquisition. [CRISIL Mar-26]

## Moat evidence
- **Scale in niche instruments:** "one of the world's largest manufacturers of panel meters" (CRISIL). It has 200+ product groups, in-house R&D in India, Poland and China, and about 15 launches in two years with 15+ planned for FY27. [CRISIL Mar-26] [Q1 call]
- **Pricing power:** consolidated gross margin rose from 58.2% (FY25) to 62.1% (FY26). As management put it, "we have our own brand and price list," so cost increases pass through. [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call]
- **Customer wins:** €5 m, then €3 m, grid-upgrade orders from one German customer (about 20–25% of Lumel SA's current business). It has 4–5 data-centre projects in India (e.g. Sify), with about 10 more being quoted. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Weak spots:** solar inverters are "not yet" cost-competitive with Chinese products. The US needs UL/ANSI redesigns, and test-lab queues slow this down. Alucast competes on cost in a shrinking European auto die-casting base. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Stake-sale report:** on 20 Aug 2026 CNBC-TV18 reported that the promoters (who own about 69.4%) are talking to suitors, including a German firm, to sell control at a valuation above ₹3,000 cr. **The company said it has "no material event" to disclose; it did not deny the report.** This is a control-change and open-offer wildcard. [Company clarification 21 Aug 2026]
- **Alucast** is about 30% of revenue and is losing money again (Q1 PAT −₹5.9 cr). Management has discussed a demerger but decided nothing. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Auditor findings:** unmodified opinions. **Key audit matters:** impairment of investments in subsidiaries (standalone; ₹95.2 cr of investments, ₹0.29 cr impaired in FY26 for Energy Solutions Labs) and **goodwill of ₹27.0 cr** (consolidated). **Other matter:** six subsidiaries (₹200.8 cr of assets, ₹130.1 cr of revenue) were audited by other auditors, and one small subsidiary and an associate are **unaudited**. **Audit trail** was not enabled at database level for one accounting software. [AR FY26 auditor's reports; note 64]
- **Contingent liabilities (standalone, Mar-26):** an inverter customer (Ambit Energy) claims **₹6.58 cr** for faulty inverters (open since 2022; the company rejects it, and the matter is before a Rajkot court). A PF demand of ₹0.61 cr (2006–09). A corporate guarantee for the China subsidiary with ₹0.63 cr of exposure. [AR FY26 note 48]
- **Pledges:** current assets of about ₹140 cr are hypothecated to working-capital lenders. There is **no promoter share pledge** disclosed. Promoter holding drifted from 70.68% (Sep-23) to 69.40% (Jun-26). [AR FY26 note 46] [Screener]
- **Europe exposure:** about two-thirds of revenue is European, where industry is weak and Polish wage inflation is high. CRISIL notes margin volatility (7.7–14.3% over FY23–FY25). [CRISIL Mar-26]
- **Concentration inside Lumel SA:** one German grid customer supplies 20–25% of current business. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- **Better:** EEI grew 34% (against 17.5% in FY26), Lumel SA grew 39%, the US and UK grew over 40%, and domestic order bookings were up about 20%. The US ₹100 cr target was pulled in from 3–4 years to 2–3 years.
- **Worse:** Alucast went from breakeven to −6.4% adjusted EBITDA. The double-digit-margin date slipped from FY28 to "about 2 years". The demerger talk went quiet.
- **New:** high-end PCBA (Intel motherboards), hybrid inverters, experience centres, a CT capacity push, an M&A pipeline of ₹50–200 cr, and (after the call) the stake-sale press report.

## Stored-data errors
- **Q1 FY27 "ob=0"** and **Q4/Q3 "ob=0"**: the company does not disclose an order book, so the value should be null, not zero.
- **Q4 FY26 margin stored as 16.3%.** That is the FY26 full-year margin; Q4 was 16.2%.
- **The stored profile says PAT fell "driven entirely by higher depreciation".** In fact the higher tax charge (₹2.9 cr, rate 18% → 27%) was bigger than the ₹2.0 cr depreciation rise.
- **The stored profile says "50+ new products"**; the official figure is about 15.
- **The stored profile says the balance sheet is "pristine … zero net debt".** Net cash is correct (₹160.6 cr), but gross debt of ₹81 cr and a 184-day cash cycle are omitted.
- **The stored analysis says "Nashik doubling capacity … newly commissioned"**; it was only partly commissioned at Q1.
- Other stored quarterly figures (₹198.3 / ₹204.9 / ₹183.6 / ₹196.3 cr revenue; ₹33.3 / ₹33.3 / ₹31.4 / ₹33.4 cr EBITDA) match the company and Screener. Market cap, P/E (33.9 vs Screener 34.1) and TTM ₹783 cr match.

## My read
The instrument business (70% of revenue) is improving fast: 34% growth, a 25% margin, CTs sold into data centres and German grid-upgrade orders. Net cash of ₹160 cr funds capacity with no strain. But the consolidated picture is held back by Alucast, which has again slipped into losses, and earnings have been flat at about ₹20 cr a quarter for five quarters. The price assumes EEI keeps compounding at 20%+ and Alucast stops bleeding by FY28. The live stake-sale report adds a possible open-offer upside that the company has not denied. I would get more positive on two consecutive quarters of Alucast at breakeven or a clear exit, and more negative if EEI growth fades to the low teens once the German order ends (FY27).
