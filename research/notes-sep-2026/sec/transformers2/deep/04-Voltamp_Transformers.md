## Business and segments
- **What it makes:** oil-filled power transformers (currently up to 160 MVA; up to 315 MVA from the new plant), distribution, dry-type and unitised substation transformers. It reports a single segment [CARE Aug-26; AR FY26].
- **Customers:** more than 1,000, mostly private-sector industry. End markets include power, refineries, textiles, chemicals, real estate, autos, infrastructure and steel. There is little exposure to state utilities, which pay slowly [CARE Aug-26].
- **Concentration:** the top 10 customers were about 26% of FY26 sales, down from about 33% in FY25 [CARE Aug-26].
- **Exports:** 3.24% of FY26 turnover [AR FY26 BRSR].
- **Volumes:**
  - FY26: 16,514 MVA (FY25: 15,460 MVA), with capacity utilisation above 100% for the second year running [AR FY26 Directors' report].
  - Q1 FY27: 4,215 MVA, up 29% from 3,260 MVA [CARE Aug-26].

## Guidance history
- There is no formal guidance and there are no calls. What the annual report and rating note say:
  - **Margins:** "margins are expected to get normalized going forward" as industry capacity and competition rise [AR FY26 Directors' report; MD&A]. Q1 FY27's 14.8% operating margin confirms the trend [CARE Aug-26].
  - **New power-transformer plant:**
    - Annual report: completion "targeted by June 2026", operational "from July 2026" [AR FY26].
    - CARE: commercial operations from **October 2026**, a delay of about two months "owing to delay in delivery of few import equipment" [CARE Aug-26].
    - Cost revised to about ₹215 cr from about ₹200 cr [CARE Aug-26].
    - **Slipped about one quarter; cost up 7.5%.**
- **Dividend:** ₹100 per share for FY26 (₹101.17 cr, about a 33% payout), up from about 31% [AR FY26; Screener].

## Capex and capacity
- **Greenfield EHV power-transformer plant, Vadodara:**
  - Capacity 6,000 MVA a year, costing about ₹215 cr, all from internal accruals.
  - It lifts the unit rating Voltamp can make from 160 MVA to 315 MVA. The annual report says "up to 250 MVA and 220 KV" [CARE Aug-26; AR FY26 BRSR].
  - Commercial operations are due in October 2026, so FY27 gets only about half a year of partial output.
  - On FY26 volume of 16,514 MVA, the plant adds about 36% capacity in MVA terms, at larger ratings.
- **Dry-type transformer plant at Karjan, Vadodara:** 2,300 MVA for about ₹90 cr, plus land of ₹25 cr approved on 5 May 2026. Commercial operations in Q4 FY28 [CARE Aug-26; AR FY26].
- **Spending pace:** capital work in progress was ₹108 cr at March 2026. FY26 capex was ₹95.5 cr (FY25: ₹43 cr). Unfinished capital contracts stood at ₹85 cr [Screener; AR FY26 BRSR; AR FY26 note 45].
- **Payback:** not disclosed. On FY26 revenue per MVA (about ₹13 lakh), 6,000 MVA at full use could add about ₹780 cr of revenue on ₹215 cr of capex (my estimate, about 3.6 times asset turns).

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Debt:** no fund-based debt. Gearing, including leases and customer mobilisation advances, is 0.07x. Fund-based bank limits are unused [CARE Aug-26].
- **Treasury:**
  - Unencumbered liquid investments: ₹667 cr (CARE). Total investments on Screener: ₹995 cr.
  - Policy: up to 15% in equity funds, the rest in government securities and high-grade bonds.
  - CARE expects the cash to "remain within the company" with no near-term use [CARE Aug-26; Screener].
- **Cash flow:**
  - Operating cash flow fell to ₹140 cr in FY26 from ₹219 cr in FY25. Free cash flow was only ₹15 cr (₹159 cr in FY25). Operating cash was 68% of operating profit [Screener].
  - Inventory days jumped to 126 from 85. Debtor days improved to 46 [Screener].
- **Bank guarantees:**
  - Guarantees outstanding: ₹398 cr [AR FY26 note 45].
  - Average use of non-fund limits (bank guarantees, including long-term performance guarantees): about 81% [CARE Aug-26].
  - No guarantee has been invoked "in the last many years" [CARE Aug-26].
- **Credit rating:** CARE AA/Stable/A1+, reaffirmed 19 Aug 2026 [CARE Aug-26].

## Moat evidence
- **Customers:** a 1,000-plus private customer base and repeat orders. It has avoided slow-paying state utilities, which keeps debtor days at 46–55 against 85–130 at utility-heavy peers [CARE Aug-26; Screener].
- **Quality:** low failure rates, reflected in low repair spending, and no performance guarantee invoked [CARE Aug-26].
- **Supply chain:** a reliable vendor base helps it "avoid late delivery penalties" [AR FY26 MD&A]. Contrast TARIL, which paid late-delivery penalties in FY26.
- **Evidence against the moat:**
  - Orders are **mostly fixed-price**; only copper is hedged [CARE Aug-26].
  - The operating margin fell from 20% to 16% in two years, and to 15% in Q1 FY27.
  - The company expects "aggressive pricing trends" from rivals with big expansion plans [AR FY26 MD&A].
  - Its maximum rating is only 160 MVA until the new plant runs.

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter selling:** promoter Kunjal L. Patel sold 7.8% (7.88 lakh shares) in a block deal on 9 Sep 2025 at ₹7,605.91, raising about ₹600 cr. The promoter stake fell to 30.01% [News: Upstox, 9-Sep-25; Screener]. At 30%, promoter ownership is low for a family-run firm.
- **Management pay:** key managerial pay was ₹21.0 cr in FY26 (₹16.8 cr in FY25), about 7% of PAT. The two MDs received ₹6.7 cr and ₹8.2 cr [AR FY26 note 40]. There is also small CSR giving to trusts where management serves as trustees or directors [AR FY26 note 40].
- **Treasury volatility:** investment income swings reported profit. Q4 FY26 other income was −₹10 cr; Q1 FY27 was +₹42 cr [Screener]. About 23% of FY26 profit before tax (₹67 cr of ₹406 cr) came from other income.
- **Contingent items:**
  - Tax demands of about ₹8.5 cr (excise, income tax, GST) [AR FY26 note 45].
  - The only key audit matter is litigation, provisions and contingent liabilities; the audit opinion is unmodified [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Small order book:** only 0.74 times revenue. Growth depends on the new plant filling quickly while competitors add capacity.
- **Capex delay:** the new plant slipped from June/July to October 2026 because imported equipment arrived late [CARE Aug-26].
- **Disclosure gap:** no calls, presentations or quarterly order-book updates. Investors rely on the annual report and rating notes.

## My read
Voltamp is the cleanest balance sheet in the group:
- no debt
- about ₹667–995 cr of investments
- 46 debtor days
- a 33% payout

But it is a business with its margin being squeezed. Prices are flat while input costs rise, orders are fixed-price, and the operating margin has fallen from 20% (FY24) to 15% (Q1 FY27). Capacity is maxed until the 6,000 MVA plant starts in October 2026, and the order book (0.74 times sales) is thin.

At about 34 times trailing earnings, the price needs about 19% earnings growth a year for a decade (the note's formula). That is a stretch unless the new plant ramps up in H2 FY27–FY28 and margins stabilise near 15%. Three things would change the view:
- The new plant's first-half revenue.
- Whether the order book climbs above ₹2,000 cr.
- Any further promoter selling after the lock-up.
