# Atlanta Electricals (ATLANTAELE) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Figures come from Screener's consolidated page and from company filings. Price ₹1,626, market cap ₹12,503 cr, 52-week range ₹708–2,200. Promoters hold 87.28% [Screener, 27 Sep 2026]. The company listed through an IPO in September 2025 with a ₹400 cr fresh issue [Q4 call].

## Business and segments
- **What it makes:** power transformers from 5 MVA/11 kV up to 500 MVA/765 kV, plus auto, inverter-duty (IDT, used in solar plants), furnace, generator and special-duty transformers. It does not make distribution transformers [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- **Q1 FY27 product mix:** power transformers 79%, auto transformers 14%, IDT 6%, others 1% [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- **Q1 FY27 voltage mix:** 220 kV class 56%, 66 kV 25%, 132 kV about 5.5%. In FY26, 220 kV was about 52% of revenue [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 end markets:** transmission and distribution 66%, renewables about 19%, the rest thermal and industrial [Q1 call].
- **Customers:** 273 customers in Q1 FY27, up from 77 in FY22 [IP Q1FY27]. State utility boards make up 60–75% of the order book (e.g. GETCO, RVPNL, PSTCL), alongside private power producers and EPC contractors [Q4 call]. Q1 orders included ₹291.68 cr from RVPNL and ₹285.15 cr from PSTCL (23 units of 160 MVA, 220/66 kV) [Q1 call].
- **Exports and data centres:** no export revenue in Q1 FY27, and no data-centre orders [Q1 call].
- **Capacity:** 63,060 MVA across five plants [IP Q1FY27]:
  - Anand Unit I: 9,360 MVA
  - Anand Unit II: 6,660 MVA
  - Bengaluru: 720 MVA
  - Vadod (Unit 4): 30,540 MVA
  - Atlanta Trafo at Ankhi (ex-BTW, bought April 2025): 15,780 MVA
- **Utilisation:**
  - FY26 production was 22,943 MVA [Q4 call].
  - Q1 FY27 sales were 4,381 MVA (Vadod 1,520, Ankhi 320), about 28% of capacity on an annualised basis [Q1 call].
  - Vadod ran at about 39% of nameplate over its first seven months. Management targets 65% in FY27 and 100% in FY28 [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- **Revenue:** the standing target is 40% a year for three years from the IPO. FY26 delivered 48.8% [Q4 call]. On the Q1 call this was repeated as "40% on INR1,851" for FY27, which means about ₹2,590 cr. Management expects ₹2,400 cr of the June order book to be executed in FY27 [Q1 call]. **Kept.**
- **Margin:** on the Q4 call management would not endorse the analyst's "18–20%" band and said only that margins would be "stable" [Q4 call]. On the Q1 call the CFO guided "around 17 to 18%", and the CMD pointed back to the 16–16.5% margin at IPO time [Q1 call]. This is a **quiet step down** from the FY26 actual of 18.6%. The stated reason is tie-up and development costs.
- **400 kV:**
  - Q4 call: the short-circuit test would come in "coming quarter" (Q1 FY27) [Q4 call].
  - Q1 call: the first unit will be ready at the end of Q2 FY27, with the test "early next part of the quarter". Meaningful 400 kV revenue is expected only in FY28 [Q1 call].
  - **Slipped about one quarter.**
- **765 kV technology tie-up:**
  - Q4 call: expected to close in "next couple of months" [Q4 call].
  - Q1 call: target is to close in Q2, with a prototype at Ankhi in Q3–Q4 and bidding from Q4 FY27. It needs government approval on the partner's side. Cost is a USD 3–5 million one-time fee plus a 2–4% royalty for 3–4 years [Q1 call].
  - **Slipping.**
- **Other promises:**
  - All term debt was repaid by March 2026, as promised on the Q3 call [Q4 call]. **Met.**
  - Unit 6 (IDT) targeted for December 2026 [Q4 call; Q1 call]. **On track.**
  - Exports at 15% of revenue within three years [Q4 call; Q1 call]. **Kept.**

## Capex and capacity
- **Vadod (Unit 4):** about ₹200 cr invested. It earned about ₹495 cr of revenue in its first seven months. Management claims a payback of 3–4 years, against an industry norm of 5–7 years [Q4 call].
- **Atlanta Trafo (ex-BTW Atlanta):** bought in April 2025 at an enterprise value of about ₹260 cr, including about ₹80 cr of BTW debt. The funding mix was debt plus internal accruals [IP Q1FY27]. Consolidated goodwill was about ₹24.4 cr [AR FY26 consolidated acquisition note]. The plant was built by BTW (Baoding Tianwei) and has previously supplied 400/765 kV units to PGCIL and GETCO [IP Q1FY27].
- **Unit 6 (IDT):** about ₹65 cr for 5,000 MVA next to Vadod, due by December 2026 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Tank and radiator backward integration:** ₹170–180 cr. Only ₹15–20 cr had been spent by July 2026. Tanks and radiators are just 4–5% of a transformer's cost, and the CMD says the aim is supply security and export-grade quality, not margin [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Funding:**
  - Management says internal accruals, with a board-approved term loan available if needed [Q4 call].
  - **CRISIL (Feb 2026) expected the ₹180 cr to be funded "75% debt or a QIP"**, which means possible dilution [CRISIL Feb-26].
- **Capital committed:** ₹42.2 cr at March 2026 [AR FY26 note 45]. ₹182.91 cr moved from capital work in progress to fixed assets in FY26, and the auditor made this a key audit matter [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Spend so far:** investing cash outflow was ₹141 cr in FY25 and ₹403 cr in FY26 (capex plus acquisition) [Screener].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Term loans of ₹340 cr (₹130 cr for Vadod, ₹210 cr for the BTW acquisition) were fully repaid by March 2026. The money came from IPO proceeds, internal accruals and general-purpose IPO funds [Q4 call].
- Of the IPO money: ₹395.46 cr was used, including ₹210 cr for working capital, and ₹4.54 cr is unspent. The monitoring agency reports no deviation [Q4 call].
- Screener borrowings were ₹51 cr at March 2026. Cash was about ₹93 cr [Q4 call], so the company is roughly net cash [Screener].
- Operating cash flow was ₹84 cr (FY25) and ₹184 cr (FY26). Free cash flow was −₹30 cr and +₹68 cr. Operating cash was 77% of operating profit in FY26 [Screener].
- **Working capital:**
  - FY26: debtor days 84, inventory 102, payables 112 [Screener].
  - Q1 FY27: inventory 105 days, receivables 88, payables 110. The CFO put net working capital at 72 days, with a cash-conversion cycle of "about 83" [Q1 call].
  - Management expects net working capital to rise to 80–90 days in FY27–28 [Q4 call].
- **Bank facilities** grew from ₹910 cr to ₹1,320 cr, mostly non-fund-based (such as bank guarantees). CRISIL rates ₹1,460 cr of facilities at A/Stable and A1, upgraded from BBB+ in October 2025 [Q4 call; CRISIL Feb-26].
- **Dividends:** none paid [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- It got PGCIL approval for 400 kV at Vadod on 2 April 2026, about two years after breaking ground. Management calls this among the fastest in the industry, though it is still subject to the short-circuit test [Q4 call].
- It took part in PGCIL's vendor-development programme for 500 MVA, 400 kV units [Q1 call].
- Eight NABL-accredited test labs can test up to 500 MVA/765 kV [IP Q1FY27].
- It won the Atlanta Trafo (BTW) plant by exercising a right of first refusal, outbidding CG Power and TRIL [IP Q1FY27].
- **Pricing power is contract-based:**
  - About 75% of the order book carries price-variation clauses (all state-utility orders, and some longer private ones) [Q4 call].
  - Management says there has been no price correction in 220/132 kV and no hit on margins [Q1 call].
- **Cost position:**
  - Management describes itself as having "very lean" fixed costs [Q4 call].
  - The top 10 suppliers provide 69% of raw materials, with imports at 13.9% [IP Q1FY27].
- **Evidence the moat is thin:**
  - CRISIL lists "intense competition" and tender risk as weaknesses [CRISIL Feb-26].
  - About 60,000 MVA of peer capacity (CG, TRIL) is coming online [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Related party, Amod Stampings (promoter-influenced):**
  - FY26 purchases from Amod were ₹114.6 cr (₹111.0 cr in FY25), and sales to it were ₹39.5 cr [AR FY26 note 48].
  - CRISIL used to combine Amod with Atlanta. It now excludes Amod, citing management's "revised stance" and arm's-length dealing [CRISIL Feb-26].
  - This is about ₹115 cr a year of core material bought from a promoter company, so it needs watching.
- **Promoter guarantee:** the promoter LLP Atlanta UHV Transformers gave a free corporate guarantee of ₹1,320 cr on the SBI consortium limits [AR FY26 note 48]. This is supportive, but it is a dependence on the promoter.
- **Bank stock statements:**
  - The CARO report shows the inventory in quarterly statements to banks was ₹44.5–68.1 cr higher than in the books each quarter of FY26.
  - Creditor balances also differed, by up to ₹40 cr.
  - The company's explanation is goods in transit and overheads that were not counted [AR FY26 CARO clause ii].
  - This is common, but the gaps are large relative to about ₹400 cr of inventory.
- **Contingent liabilities:** ₹36.6 cr (₹15.1 cr a year earlier). Of this, ₹33.5 cr is bills discounted but not yet due, plus small tax and claim amounts [AR FY26 note 44].
- **Audit:** the auditor's opinion is unqualified. The key audit matters are revenue cut-off, provisions and contingencies, and the capitalisation of capital work in progress [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Promoter holding and free float:** promoters own 87.28%, so only about 13% of shares trade freely. More shares may be sold to meet listing rules (minimum public shareholding) [Screener].
- **Execution risk on new products:** 400 kV revenue depends on a short-circuit test that has not yet happened. 765 kV depends on a foreign partner that needs government clearance [Q1 call].
- **Chinese competition:** Chinese transformer makers have been allowed into PSU tenders. Management says only one of the four approved firms makes transformers, and it has not bid yet [Q1 call].
- **Anti-dumping probe on CRGO steel:** the trade regulator (DGTR) is investigating imports of CRGO electrical steel, with a decision due around March 2027 [Q1 call].
- **Data inconsistency:** the CFO said Q4 FY26 production was "13,000 MVA". That does not fit FY26 total production of 22,943 MVA or the Q4 revenue-per-MVA pattern, and the figure is unverified [Q1 call; Q4 call].

- **Order book:** up 25% quarter on quarter, to ₹3,117 cr.

## My read
The business is improving fast. Revenue grew 49% in FY26, margins rose from 16% to 19%, the balance sheet is now roughly net cash, and the order book is 1.6 times TTM sales. So FY27 growth near the guided 40% looks well covered by the ₹2,400 cr executable backlog. But about half of Q1's growth came from higher price per MVA (commodity pass-through), not volume. Guidance has already slipped to a 17–18% margin, and the move to 400/765 kV (the source of future margin) is running a quarter late. At around 57 times trailing earnings, the price needs about 26% earnings growth a year for a decade (the note's formula). The guided 40% covers the next few years, but the price also assumes margins hold after the cycle turns. Watch three things: the 400 kV short-circuit test result in Q2/Q3 FY27, whether the 765 kV tie-up is signed, and whether the tank/radiator capex is funded by a QIP. Growing Amod purchases or wider bank-statement gaps would weaken the case.
