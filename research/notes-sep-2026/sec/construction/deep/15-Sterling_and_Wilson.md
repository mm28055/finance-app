# Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy (NSE: SWSOLAR)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Consolidated figures in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.*

## Business and segments
- The company does pure-play solar EPC: utility-scale PV, floating solar, hybrid and standalone battery storage (BESS), and one wind project. It also runs an O&M business covering its own and third-party plants. [AR FY26] [Q4 call]
- FY26 revenue by segment:
  - Domestic EPC: ₹5,836 cr.
  - International EPC: ₹1,444 cr.
  - O&M: about ₹268 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- In FY26 the company commissioned about 4.5 GW AC (5.9 GW DC). Management puts this at about 15% of India's 28.3 GW AC of utility-scale solar installed that year, citing MNRE data. [Q4 call]
- The EPC portfolio is 27.3 GWp across 20 countries, with about 10 GW+ under execution in India. [AR FY26]
- O&M portfolio: 18.3 GWp at Q1 FY27, up from 13.5 GWp at Q4 FY26 and 8.7 GWp a year earlier. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Shareholders at 31 Mar 2026:
  - Reliance New Energy (a wholly owned subsidiary of RIL): 32.49%.
  - Shapoorji Pallonji & Co: 6.94%.
  - Kainaz Daruvala: 5.57%. [AR FY26]
- The board is fully non-executive. [Infomerics Apr-26]
- Order mix: turnkey (module in scope) was about 70% of FY26 inflows and about two-thirds of the unexecuted order value (UOV). The rest is balance-of-system (BoS) work. [Q4 call]
- Named clients include NTPC Green, Coal India (L1), Adani Green (5-year framework), JSW, Aditya Birla, Serentica, Sembcorp, Brookfield, GIPCL and Engie. [Infomerics Apr-26] [AR FY26]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue, Jun-25 → Jun-26 (₹ cr): 1,762, 1,749, 2,092, 1,946, 1,590. Q1 FY27 was down 10% YoY. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Quarterly operating margin (OPM), same quarters: 4.8%, 0.2%, 3.2%, 7.0%, 5.0%. [Screener]
- Quarterly net profit, same quarters (₹ cr): 39, −478, 2, 142, 53. Q1 FY27 PAT was up 36% YoY, helped by a lower tax rate. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- FY22–FY26 revenue (₹ cr): 5,199, 2,015, 3,035, 6,302, 7,548. FY26 grew 20%. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- FY22–FY26 net profit (₹ cr): −916, −1,175, −211, 86, −296. [Screener]
- FY26 had exceptional items of ₹611 cr (₹610.94 cr), driving the annual loss of ₹296 cr. [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- The standalone FY26 loss was ₹2,510 cr, after ₹2,802 cr of exceptional items. These were an impairment of the investment in, and loans to, a wholly owned subsidiary. [AR FY26]
- FY26 gross margin was 10.5% against 10.1% in FY25. International EPC gross margin rose to 13.2% from 8%. Q1 FY27 gross margin was about 9.9%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Operational EBITDA (gross margin less recurring overheads):
  - FY26: ₹444 cr, a 5.9% margin, up 53% YoY.
  - Q1 FY27: ₹78 cr, a 4.9% margin. Reported Q1 EBITDA was ₹96 cr including forex gains.
  - Recurring overheads stayed flat at ₹349 cr in FY26. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Market data:
  - Market cap about ₹4,125 cr; price ₹177 against a 52-week range of ₹148–261.
  - P/E 13.2 on trailing figures, flattered by Q4/Q1 profits.
  - Reported ROE 40.1%, which is distorted by the shrunken equity base. [Screener]
- Promoter holding is 45.73%, of which 27.6% is pledged per Screener. Promoter holding is down 23.3 percentage points over 3 years, mainly from the QIP. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (24 Apr 2026):**
  - About 15% revenue growth in FY27 on the existing UOV. Reliance orders are excluded.
  - About 15% growth in order book.
  - EPC gross margin 8–10%; O&M gross margin about 20%.
  - Overheads at most 4–4.5% of revenue, implying 4–5% EBITDA.
  - Management expected BESS to make up about 20% of new orders. [Q4 call]
- **Earlier guidance:** analysts said the company had guided to a ₹2,000–2,500 cr quarterly revenue run-rate. The CEO confirmed it: "we were geared up to achieve over INR2,000 crores of revenue in this quarter." [Q1 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (17 Jul 2026):**
  - Revenue growth trimmed to "10% to 15%", still excluding Reliance.
  - Q2 is guided flat with Q1, at about ₹1,600 cr. So H2 must run at about ₹2,600–3,000 cr a quarter; one analyst worked this out and management accepted it.
  - Margin guidance unchanged.
  - O&M revenue guided to ₹400–450 cr in FY27 against ₹268 cr. [Q1 call]
- **Order inflow:** FY26 inflows were ₹10,062 cr, up 43%, against the 15% growth guided at the start of the year, so order-book guidance was beaten. [Q4 call]
- **Reliance:**
  - Q4 FY26: orders promised "this financial year" with "no comment" on the quarter. An earlier call had pointed to Q4 FY26.
  - Q1 FY27: "timing and quantum ... will necessarily follow their rollout schedule." Terms are still not closed. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
  - Reliance has been a recurring promise that keeps slipping.

## Capex and capacity
- This is an asset-light EPC model with no manufacturing capex. Capex (fixed assets and cash flow) is not material in Screener data. [Screener]
- The scale-up is in people: headcount went from about 2,500 to about 3,459. About 100 graduate engineer trainees were hired. Management says the additions are "project-specific" rather than overhead. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [AR FY26]
- About 10.5 GW is under execution. [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 order backlog:
  - UOV is a record of about ₹13,000 cr, against ₹11,813 cr at Q4 and ₹9,096 cr a year earlier.
  - About ₹9,000 cr of the UOV sits in six turnkey projects that have not started (three in India, three international).
  - This includes the Egypt West Minya project: 1 GW AC plus 600 MWh BESS, about US$560 m, in a 50:50 JV with Hassan Allam. [Q1 call]
- The Coal India 1.2 GW DC order (L1, domestic-content DCR modules) was still awaiting its LOA at the Q1 call. [Q1 call]
- Bid pipeline: 27.7 GW, about 90% India, per the Q1 call. Management sees ₹45,000–50,000 cr of market ordering in H2: about 25 GW of utility solar plus about 35 GWh of BESS. [Q1 call]
- Recent wins announced: Rajasthan 534.3 MWp and South Africa 616 MWp. [Screener ANN]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings at FY26 were ₹1,191 cr. FY26 operating cash flow was −₹257 cr. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 gross borrowings fell about ₹130 cr through term-loan repayments; net debt was "largely stable". [Q1 call]
- Net working capital:
  - Q3 FY26: −₹407 cr.
  - Q4 FY26: −₹329 cr.
  - Q1 FY27: −₹260 cr.
  - It is negative but has been shrinking for two quarters. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Infomerics (27 Apr 2026) reaffirmed IVR BBB+/**Negative** and IVR A2 on ₹6,465 cr of bank lines. [Infomerics Apr-26]
  - Adjusted tangible net worth fell to ₹415.92 cr at Sep-25 from ₹966 cr, and gearing rose to 2.87x from 0.93x.
  - Liquidity is rated "stretched". Term-loan repayments are ₹615 cr in FY27 and ₹377.6 cr in FY28.
  - Downgrade trigger: gearing at or above 3x.
- The CFO said on the Q4 call that the company is rated BBB+, upgraded two notches from BBB− during the year. That is true, but it omits the Negative outlook. [Q4 call] [Infomerics Apr-26]
- Fresh credit lines total more than ₹3,200 cr cumulatively. The lead bank raised its limits, and local South African banks were added. [Q1 call]
- Receivables aged over 1 year are about ₹392 cr, including about ₹145 cr aged over 3 years (analyst's figure from the AR). The CFO said these include retention money and litigated amounts. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- By management's claims, it has about 15% of India's utility solar commissioned in FY26 and a 25–28% hit rate. It says its "market share in the solar space is around 25%, more than 25%" (basis not defined). [Q4 call]
- It has repeat clients across PSUs (NTPC, Coal India, SJVN) and conglomerates (Adani Green's 5-year framework, Reliance as parent). [AR FY26] [Q4 call]
- It uses back-to-back supplier pricing and avoids land and right-of-way risk. [Q1 call]
- Against that:
  - Gross margins of 8–10% and heavy competition show a thin moat. Management admits "some of the new entrants have gone aggressive." [Q1 call]
  - Only about ₹6,400 cr of orders were finalised industry-wide in Q1, against more than ₹20,000 cr expected. [Q1 call]
- O&M is the stickier piece: 2–3-year contracts at 20–25% gross margin. [Q4 call]

## Input costs
- Module prices have risen since January 2026. Drivers are Chinese production cuts, the end of Chinese export tax rebates from 1 April, and silver prices. [Q4 call]
- Domestic DCR/ALMM modules trade at a large premium; an analyst cited 130–160%. Management says the Coal India DCR bid price is locked with suppliers. [Q1 call]
- Commodity volatility (copper, aluminium) forced the company to defer supplies in Q4 FY26, cutting revenue. It also slowed developer ordering for two straight quarters. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- BESS cells are imported; "Make in India" packs are mostly assembled locally. The company has no battery tie-up yet. In the current BESS order, the client supplies the batteries free of cost. [Q1 call]
- Forex: US-dollar revenue gives a natural hedge on international work. The Q4 and Q1 results included forex gains (about ₹35 cr in Q4). [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Legacy litigation, auditor emphasis of matter:**
  - Consolidated: a US subcontractor arbitration went wrong. The ₹485.64 cr the company had treated as recoverable was dismissed, the subcontractor was awarded ₹57.85 cr plus ₹36.61 cr interest, and ₹30.84 cr of costs followed. The total ₹610.94 cr charge was **not indemnified**. [AR FY26] [Q4 call]
  - Standalone emphasis of matter: exposure to a wholly owned subsidiary (investment, loans and receivables) of ₹3,654.75 cr "may have an adverse effect on the functioning of the Company." [AR FY26]
  - Also under emphasis of matter: wrongfully invoked bank guarantees of ₹441.33 cr (US$47.04 m) and ₹106.98 cr (A$16.59 m), plus ₹24.86 cr of remediation costs. All are carried as recoverable with no provision. [AR FY26]
- **Indemnity dependence:**
  - The former promoters (SP Group and the Daruvala family) indemnify net claims above ₹300 cr. [AR FY26]
  - About ₹800 cr is cash-out and covered. Only ₹120–130 cr is expected to be recovered in FY27. [Q1 call]
  - ₹174.54 cr was received in Sep-25. [Infomerics Apr-26]
  - The indemnifier's own rating is IVR BBB/Negative (SPCPL). [Infomerics Apr-26]
- Other open claims:
  - About ₹1,800 cr of claims are open. US court cases will take 2–3 years. [Q1 call]
  - Management calls about ₹3,800 cr of AR-disclosed counter-claims "frivolous" and could not quantify indemnity cover for them. [Q1 call]
  - An Australian arbitration of about ₹200 cr, of which about ₹110 cr is indemnified. [Q1 call]
- **Execution:** six large turnkey projects (about ₹9,000 cr) have not started, so H2 must be about 60% above Q1's run-rate. [Q1 call]
- Negative rating outlook and gearing close to the 3x trigger; liquidity rated "stretched". [Infomerics Apr-26]
- The CEO's remuneration exceeded the Schedule V limit because of losses. Shareholder approval is being sought. [AR FY26]
- Other items:
  - A CGST Jaipur demand of ₹13.86 cr plus penalty. [Screener ANN]
  - The company declined a JM Financial conference slot. [Screener ANN]
  - Auditor rotation: Kalyaniwalla & Mistry leaves at the 2026 AGM, and Deloitte continues. [AR FY26]
- **Related party:** Reliance orders will need arm's-length related-party approval, and their terms are undisclosed. [Q1 call]

## My read
Sterling and Wilson has become a domestic solar/BESS EPC business with a record ₹13,000 cr backlog. It has a credible market position (about 15% of FY26 utility commissioning) and a growing, higher-margin O&M annuity. The equity story still rests on three things: H2 execution of roughly ₹2,600–3,000 cr a quarter, a Reliance Kutch order that has slipped every quarter, and legacy international claims. The last came back to hit the company in FY26 with a non-indemnified ₹611 cr loss and a standalone ₹2,510 cr loss. With 8–10% gross margins, net worth that fell to about ₹416 cr in H1 FY26, a Negative rating outlook and "stretched" liquidity, one bad claim outcome matters a lot. I'd treat it as a high-beta, low-margin energy EPC name and watch the Q2 LOAs and indemnity inflows.
