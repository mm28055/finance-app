# Dilip Buildcon Ltd (DBL, ISIN INE917M01012)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Figures are consolidated (Screener) unless marked "standalone". ₹ cr = rupees crore.

## Business and segments
- Bhopal-based infrastructure group. Management ("DBL 2.0") presents it as three engines:
  - **EPC:** roads, irrigation and water, tunnels, metro, transmission, solar, mining infrastructure; 12 verticals.
  - **MDO (coal/bauxite mine development and operation):** Siarmal, Pachhwara, Pottangi.
  - **Asset platform:** road HAMs recycled into Anantam Highways InvIT (sponsored with Alpha Alternatives) and Shrem InvIT; solar and transmission SPVs.
  
  Management expects about three-quarters of profit to come from MDO and assets by FY29. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Order book ₹27,691 cr (30 Jun 2026). The MDO portion is counted as only a 3-year rolling ₹5,224 cr; the full remaining MDO contract value is ~₹1.03 lakh cr. FY26 order inflow was a record ₹18,548 cr (1,977 MW solar ~₹5,500 cr, Bihar road ~₹3,500 cr, Mekhali transmission ~₹1,700 cr, Rajasthan ERCP canal ~₹2,000 cr). Q1 FY27 inflow was only ₹268 cr. L1 positions: Sikasar–Kodar canal ₹2,524 cr and a Solapur/Paranda transmission project ₹1,265 cr (Sep). [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [BSE filing, 21 Sep 2026]
- Coal: FY26 production 28.72 million tonnes (Siarmal 22.35 Mt, Pachhwara 6.37 Mt); Q1 FY27 4.79 Mt. Target ~57 Mt a year by FY29. Revenue per tonne: ~₹550–600 at Siarmal (78% fee until the coal-handling plant is ready, then 100%) and ~₹1,150–1,200 at Pachhwara. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Management says only ~10% of revenue over 20 years came from its home state, Madhya Pradesh. [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY26 order inflow:** guided ~₹15,000 cr (per CRISIL Jul-25); achieved ₹18,548 cr. **Beaten.** FY26 revenue fell 21% (CRISIL had expected −5%). **Missed.** [CRISIL Jul-25] [Q4 call]
- **FY27 revenue growth of 30–40%** (standalone, per Q4): reaffirmed at Q1, with the ramp-up from Q3 to Q1 FY28. Q1 was flat to down. **Kept, but back-ended.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 EBITDA margin:** Q4 said 11–12% (standalone); Q1 said "around 10% to 12%". **Softened at the low end.** Standalone Q1 was 10.3%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 order inflow ₹10,000–12,000 cr:** kept. Bids of ₹15,000–20,000 cr are awaiting opening; the pipeline is ~₹1.5 lakh cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Debt reduction ₹600–800 cr in FY27** and standalone net-debt-free by FY28: kept, though standalone net debt rose to ₹2,106 cr in Q1 from ₹1,880 cr. Working capital to fall to ~120 days by year-end (133 now) and ~90 days next year. FY27 finance cost ~₹350 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **MDO revenue:** ₹2,500 cr in FY27, ₹3,000+ cr in FY28, ~₹4,000 cr in FY29 (Q4). Q1 MDO revenue was ₹362 cr, well below run-rate. Management blamed slow evacuation (~6 Mt of stock at Siarmal at Q4) and a strike at Pachhwara, and kept the targets. **Kept, at risk.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **InvIT flips:** 11 remaining HAMs to transfer through Mar 2027 for ~₹1,750–1,800 cr of units, with <₹81 cr of incremental equity (<₹200 cr at Q4). One tranche slipped a quarter. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Standalone capex <₹100 cr a year. The equipment fleet (gross ~₹3,600 cr, net ~₹800 cr) is described as debt-free. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Siarmal coal-handling plant: total mine capex ₹2,730 cr, 40–45% done. It is funded by SPV cash (~₹300 cr) and a ₹2,000 cr sanctioned loan. Completion in ~1.5 years; commercial operation expected in FY29. [Q1 call]
- Solar and transmission SPVs need ~₹1,650 cr of equity. Alpha Alternatives will co-invest 49%, and DBL has arranged ~₹900 cr of "structured equity", so DBL's own share is ~15%. On 21 Sep 2026 DBL signed definitive agreements to sell stakes in the solar portfolio to Alpha (project cost ~₹8,400 cr, including transmission). [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [BSE filing, 21 Sep 2026]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Standalone net debt ₹2,106 cr (Jun-26), net D/E 0.31. Consolidated net debt ₹7,801 cr, mostly SPV and MDO project debt. Screener consolidated borrowings ₹8,041 cr (Mar-26). [Q1 call] [Screener]
- InvIT units held: ~₹1,521 cr face value (Anantam ₹1,314 cr, Shrem ₹207 cr), plus ~₹1,750–1,800 cr expected. Distributions were ~₹100 cr in FY26. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Working capital: 133 days (Jun-26); Screener shows 122 days (FY26) against 44 days (FY24). Inventory days rose to 474. JJM receivables are ~₹400 cr. [Q1 call] [Screener] [Q4 call]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 1,624 → 2,845 → 1,080 → 131 → 1,204. Interest cover is low (Screener flag). [Screener]
- Dividend payout ~1%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Large road-HAM experience: over ₹45,000 cr of HAM projects, with ~90% completed early, and three Bengaluru–Vijayawada packages (₹1,700 cr) finished ahead of schedule in Q1. [Q1 call]
- MDO contracts are long-dated and fixed-rate, with ~₹1.03 lakh cr of contracted value. Management targets 24–25% MDO margins, rising after the coal-handling plant. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- It owns a large equipment fleet and has in-house execution. [Q4 call]

## Input costs
- Crude-linked diesel and bitumen: the government reimburses ~60–65% of the abnormal increase. Cement and steel costs also rose in the 2026 disruption. MDO contracts are fixed-rate per tonne with inflation adjustment. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Wayanad tunnel landslide (7 Jul 2026):** a slope failure hit DBL's ₹1,341 cr Anakkampoyil–Kalladi–Meppadi twin-tunnel project. Management says a committee found it "purely natural calamity". A Kerala amicus curiae report instead called it foreseeable (unscientific earth dumping) and questioned DBL's selection, citing the earlier Anakapalle flyover collapse. [Q1 call] [News: Onmanorama, 19 Jul 2026; Mongabay, Jul 2026]
- **Earnings quality:** FY26 PAT (₹1,398 cr) depended heavily on other income (₹1,396 cr, including asset-sale gains) and a very low tax rate. Operating revenue fell 21%, and ROE is ~5–6%. [Screener]
- **Leverage:** consolidated net debt ~₹7,800 cr against a market cap of ~₹6,700 cr. Group structure (SPVs, InvIT units, structured equity) is complex. [Q1 call] [Screener]
- **Contingent liabilities and commitments (standalone, Mar-26):**
  - bank guarantees ~₹1,214 cr;
  - corporate guarantees for subsidiaries ~₹846 cr;
  - claims ~₹71 cr;
  - GST ~₹3 cr;
  - capital commitment to invest in subsidiaries ~₹2,612 cr.
  
  The auditor gave an unmodified opinion; no emphasis of matter was found in the extracts reviewed. [AR FY26]
- **Dilution:** 16.23 million warrants to non-promoter entities were converted in FY26 (equity ₹146 cr → ₹162 cr). The promoter stake fell from 70.15% to 63.14% (Jun-25 quarter). No pledge was found. [AR FY26] [Screener]

## My read
DBL is in transition. The EPC business shrank sharply in FY24–FY26, while coal MDO, InvIT units and a now-rebuilt order book (₹27,700 cr plus ~₹1 lakh cr of MDO backlog) offer long-dated cash flows. Reported profits have been flattered by asset-sale gains and low tax, and Q1 FY27 did not yet show the promised 30–40% revenue ramp or MDO run-rate. Consolidated leverage and the Wayanad controversy add risk. The view would improve with quarterly revenue rising sharply in Q3–Q4 FY27, Siarmal evacuation normalising, the Alpha deal closing with cash to cut standalone debt, and InvIT flips on schedule. It would weaken if MDO volumes stay low or debt fails to fall by ₹600–800 cr.
