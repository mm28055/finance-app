# Power Mech Projects Ltd (POWERMECH, ISIN INE211R01019)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Figures are consolidated (Screener) unless marked. ₹ cr = rupees crore.

## Business and segments
- Hyderabad-based power-plant services company. Its businesses are:
  - erection, testing and commissioning (ETC) of boilers, turbines and generators;
  - civil works (roads, railways, water);
  - operations and maintenance (O&M) of power plants, now also Mumbai Monorail;
  - balance-of-plant (BOP) EPC;
  - coal mine development and operation (MDO) through subsidiaries.
  
  [Q1 call] [CARE Aug-26]
- Q1 FY27 revenue mix (₹ cr): civil 796 (+28%), O&M 431 (+8%), industrial construction/ETC 217 (−13%), industrial EPC 96, mining 84 (+223%). By sector, power was 54% and non-power 46%; by geography, 96% domestic. [Q1 call]
- Order book: ₹16,229 cr executable (Jun-26), or ₹55,398 cr including long-dated MDO. At Mar-26 the book was 47% civil, 23% O&M, 15% mechanical, 10% water pipelines and 6% electrical/MDO/other. [Q1 call] [CARE Aug-26]
- Key customers: BHEL ~₹5,300 cr across 5 projects (including an 800 MW Singareni BOP EPC package of ~₹2,550 cr, the company's first big BOP job); Adani ~₹2,400 cr across 14 projects; Vedanta ~₹1,551 cr across 6 projects. On 9 Sep 2026 it won a ₹970 cr O&M contract for Vedanta's 2×600 MW Sakti plant. [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [BSE filing, 9 Sep 2026]
- MDO: KBP mine (CCL) producing since Nov 2025; Tasra (SAIL) awaits a washery, with hot commissioning around Nov–Dec 2026. There is also a road HAM project due Jan 2028 and a 16 MW solar plant in Bihar. [Q1 call] [CARE Aug-26]

## Guidance history
- **FY26 revenue ~₹6,500 cr:** delivered ₹6,062 cr. **Missed**, blamed on uncertified water-division (Jal Jeevan Mission) bills. [Q4 call]
- **FY27 revenue:** 21% growth, ~₹7,300 cr, set at Q4 and reaffirmed at Q1 ("we are on track"). Q1 grew 26%. **Kept.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 EBITDA margin 12.5%** (including other income), rising ~0.5 points a year to ~14% by FY30. Q1 was 10.8%, hit by war-related material costs, higher royalty on the KRBM (royalty monitoring) contract and high overburden cost at the KBP mine. Management said it is "confident of maintaining its full year target". **Kept, but Q1 well below.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 order inflow ₹12,000 cr:** kept at Q1 (15.5% achieved). About 60–65% hit rate on power bids. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **MDO revenue:** ₹500 cr in FY27 (KBP ₹350 cr + Tasra ₹150 cr) and ₹1,100–1,200 cr in FY28. Q4 had given FY28 as KBP ₹500 cr + Tasra ₹750 cr. MDO margins: 15–17% in FY28, rising to 21–23% at peak (FY29+). **Kept.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **KRBM (Karnataka royalty enforcement) contract:** revenue ~₹700–750 cr in FY27, margin cut to 10–11% from 14% after the government began keeping 50% of penalties on seized quantities. **Cut.** [Q1 call]
- **Capex:** ~₹400 cr in FY27 (Tasra washery) plus ~₹400 cr at the Tasra SPV (coal-handling plant, rail siding). Core business needs ₹100–120 cr a year. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Headcount has grown from ~40,000 to ~50,000 workers and ~4,000 engineers and supervisors. 80–90% of cranes and equipment are owned. Management says current working-capital limits support up to ₹10,000 cr of revenue. [Q1 call]
- ETC capacity is effectively maxed at 14–15 simultaneous 800 MW boilers; growth will come from BOP EPC (20–22%) and O&M. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Gross debt ₹622 cr and net debt ₹163 cr at Mar-26; D/E 0.32. CARE: total debt/PBILDT 1.59x, interest cover 6.1x (FY26). [Q4 call] [CARE Aug-26]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 175 → 182 → 205 → 1 → 387 (the company says ₹430 cr in FY26). Debtor days ~93. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- Payment terms: private clients (Adani and others) pay 70% on bill and the balance within the month. BHEL pays in 30 days. Retention (5%) is replaced by bank guarantees. [Q1 call]
- Water division: of a ₹2,700 cr order, ₹1,800 cr has been executed. The ₹900 cr balance is on hold pending central funds; ₹128 cr of work is uncertified and ₹90 cr receivable (Mar-26). [Q4 call]
- Dividend payout ~1%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- It is the largest third-party erection and O&M service provider for Indian thermal plants. Strong ties with BHEL, Adani, JSW and Vedanta; private developers prefer it for "delivery management". High hit rates in power (60–65%). [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- It is riding the thermal revival: the national plan is +63 GW coal capacity by 2032. Management maps an opportunity of INR 60,000 crores for itself (installation, civil, BOP) and INR 800–1,000 crores a year in new O&M. [Q1 call]
- O&M margins are ~18% and MDO margins 15% now, rising to 21–23%, both above construction's 10–11%. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Steel and alloy steel, industrial gases (the LNG spike) and diesel (mining). Price-variation clauses cover most of this, but with a one-quarter lag. Private clients sometimes compensate directly. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin slippage:** Q1 margin of 10.8% against the 12.5% guide. There is a regulatory hit on the KRBM contract, and the ETC business was hit by an accident at the Vedanta Athena project (work stopped for 3–4 months). [Q1 call]
- **Guidance record:** it missed the FY26 inflow target (72%) and revenue guidance (₹6,062 cr vs ₹6,500 cr). [Q4 call]
- **Concentration:** thermal power (54–70% of the book) and a few customers (BHEL, Adani). [Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities:** Screener shows ₹2,814 cr, mostly bank guarantees backed by FDs. Tax claims (VAT and GST) are small. The auditor gave an unmodified opinion with standard key audit matters. A revised annual report was filed on 13 Sep 2026 for editorial changes only. [Screener] [AR FY26] [BSE filing, 13 Sep 2026]
- **MDO execution:** delayed approvals held back the start. Tasra depends on washery commissioning. [CARE Aug-26]
- **Promoter:** holding 58.41%, down ~5.7 points over three years (reason not checked). No pledge flagged. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- FY27 guidance (₹7,300 cr revenue, 12.5% EBITDA, ₹12,000 cr inflow) was unchanged, but Q1 margins undershot. New explanations: KRBM royalty-sharing, war costs and KBP overburden.
- New entries: Mumbai Monorail O&M (urban mobility) and JSW Salboni civil and structural work (₹850 cr). A ₹970 cr Vedanta O&M order followed in September.
- The Executive Director (Rohit Sajja) joined the Q1 call to discuss MDO ramp-up.

## My read
Power Mech is a well-run beneficiary of India's thermal-power capex revival. Revenue has grown ~22% a year since FY22, ROCE is above 20%, leverage is modest, and there is a credible move into higher-margin O&M, BOP EPC and MDO. The catch is that management's guidance tends to run ahead of delivery: the FY26 targets were missed, and Q1 FY27 margins were 170 bps below the 12.5% guide. The view would improve with Tasra washery commissioning on time, a margin recovery towards 12% in H2, and a second BOP EPC win. It would weaken if thermal ordering by BHEL or Adani slows or water-division dues stay stuck.
