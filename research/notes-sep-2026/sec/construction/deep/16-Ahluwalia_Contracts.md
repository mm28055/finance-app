# Ahluwalia Contracts (India) (NSE: AHLUCONT)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Consolidated figures in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise. Subsidiaries are immaterial, so standalone and consolidated are nearly the same.*

## Business and segments
- Ahluwalia is a pure building-construction EPC contractor. Work spans residential towers, commercial offices, institutional buildings, hospitals, airports and stations, metro depots and hotels, for both government and private clients. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- Unexecuted order book at 31 Mar 2026, by segment (excluding GST): residential 39.7%, infrastructure 29.9%, commercial 17.6%, hospital 6.3%, institutional 5.9%, hotel 0.6%. [AR FY26]
- By client: private 61.2%, central government 28.5%, state government 9.5%, overseas 0.8%. [AR FY26]
- By region: North 53.6%, West 22.2%, East 15.9%, South 7.5%. NCR alone is about 50% of the book. [AR FY26] [Q1 call]
- Largest projects:
  - Central Vista Div-12, about ₹3,070 cr including GST; a design-build job at the Nirman/Udyog Bhawan sites.
  - CSMT station redevelopment in Mumbai, ₹2,076 cr excluding GST.
  - DLF Dahlias, ₹2,000 cr+; DLF Downtown, about ₹1,600–1,700 cr.
  - India Jewellery Park in Navi Mumbai, about 12% of the book at Sep-25.
  - Varanasi, Darbhanga and Kota airports, plus Signature Global and Whiteland residential. [AR FY26] [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [CARE Apr-26]
- Promoters hold 55.32%, with no pledge shown on Screener. Bikramjit Ahluwalia (CMD, 87) holds 16.34% and Shobhit Uppal (Deputy MD) holds 6.43%. [Screener] [AR FY26]
- Named competitors: L&T B&F, NCC (Nagarjuna), Shapoorji, Kalpataru and Tata Projects. [Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue, Mar-25 → Jun-26 (₹ cr): 1,216, 1,005, 1,177, 1,061, 1,322, 1,126. [Screener]
- Quarterly operating margin (OPM), same quarters: 10%, 9%, 11%, 9%, 9%, **4.2%**. [Screener]
- Quarterly net profit, same quarters (₹ cr): 83, 51, 79, 54, 82, 10. The company reports Q1 FY27 PAT as ₹11.42 cr, down 77.65% YoY. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin was 4.29% against 8.59% a year earlier; management called it one of its lowest ever. [Q1 call]
- FY21–FY26 revenue (₹ cr): 1,982, 2,692, 2,838, 3,855, 4,099, 4,565. FY26 was up 11.4%. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- FY26 EBITDA margin was 9.52% (FY25: 8.34%). FY26 PAT was ₹264.3 cr, up 31%. Net worth crossed ₹2,000 cr. [Q4 call]
- FY24 PAT of ₹375 cr included about ₹232 cr of other income. [Screener]
- Depreciation rose from ₹67 cr in FY25 to ₹98 cr in FY26, and was ₹32 cr in Q1 FY27 alone. [Screener]
- Market data:
  - Market cap ₹3,922 cr; price ₹586 against a 52-week range of ₹559–1,078. The stock is down 41% over one year.
  - P/E 17.4; book value ₹308; ROCE 20.4%.
  - An analyst on the Q1 call put the price at ₹795, so the stock has fallen about 26% since the call. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Ten-year stock CAGR is 7%, and the payout ratio is about 2%. Shareholders pressed twice for buybacks, and management refused both times. [Screener] [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q3 FY26 call:** management guided to double-digit EBITDA (per the Q4 call's recap). [Q4 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (1 Jun 2026):**
  - FY27 revenue growth of 15–20%, raised from the usual 10–15%.
  - "We will be into double-digit margins in this year."
  - Order inflow target of about ₹8,000 cr.
  - Capex of about ₹300 cr.
  - Execution targets: CSMT about ₹600 cr, Central Vista about ₹1,000 cr, Dahlias about ₹400 cr, IJP about ₹100 cr. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (17 Aug 2026):**
  - Revenue growth "about 12% to 15%".
  - Double-digit FY27 EBITDA is now "ruled out". The hope is to regain Q1 FY26-type margins (about 8.6%) "over the next three quarters", with NGT as a rider.
  - Order inflow target cut to about ₹4,000–5,000 cr.
  - Capex cut to ₹220–250 cr.
  - Project targets lowered: Central Vista about ₹700 cr (was ₹1,000 cr) and CSMT about ₹500 cr (was ₹600 cr). [Q1 call]
- **Track record:**
  - An analyst told management its guidance had "no relevance", and the Deputy MD pushed back sharply.
  - CARE had also expected margins above 10%. [Q1 call] [CARE Apr-26]
  - The prior pledge of a return to 12–13% margins once private orders dominated has not happened, even with private orders at 61–62%. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex was ₹264.6 cr, against the roughly ₹500 cr guided "a couple of quarters ago" and the ₹274 cr cited on the call. FY27 capex has been cut to ₹220–250 cr. [AR FY26] [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- The spending goes on mechanisation (tower cranes, hoists, batching plants) to offset labour shortages. Management claims a 4–5 year payback and a "7% to 10%" return uplift, without defining the metric. [Q4 call]
- Fixed assets rose from ₹293 cr in FY23 to ₹634 cr in FY26. [Screener]
- Order book:
  - At 31 Mar 2026: ₹21,096 cr net unexecuted (excluding GST), about 4.6x FY26 revenue; gross book ₹29,676 cr.
  - At 30 Jun 2026: ₹20,664 cr.
  - FY26 inflow was ₹10,257 cr, including ₹4,300 cr in Q4. Q1 FY27 inflow was only ₹513 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [AR FY26]
- Two L1 positions totalling ₹1,621 cr were on hand at Q4. The Odisha university job fell through because Ahluwalia refused to extend a fixed-price bid. RML hospital (₹500 cr) is still L1. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Headcount: 3,978 permanent employees, plus 10,000–12,000 labourers on sites. [AR FY26] [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Balance sheet at 30 Jun 2026:
  - Gross debt about ₹2.3 cr; cash and bank ₹920 cr.
  - Mobilisation advances ₹924 cr, about 31% of them interest-bearing at about 8%.
  - Unbilled revenue ₹946 cr (₹688 cr at Mar-26).
  - Retention ₹401 cr; trade receivables ₹632 cr; trade payables ₹776 cr; inventory, including real-estate inventory, ₹391 cr. [Q1 call]
- Q1 interest rose to ₹16 cr on the new interest-bearing Central Vista mobilisation advance. Management expects about ₹15–16 cr a quarter. [Q1 call] [Screener]
- Working capital days were 104 at Mar-26 (company measure) and 119 in Q1 FY27, when Assam payments were delayed. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Screener's working capital days were 55 in FY26; the difference is in definition. [Screener]
- FY26 operating cash flow was ₹281 cr and free cash flow only ₹16 cr, as capex absorbed most of the cash. [Screener]
- Other financial and other assets rose ₹391 cr in FY26 (unbilled and retention). [AR FY26]
- CARE AA-/Stable/A1+ was reaffirmed on 9 Apr 2026, with facilities up to ₹3,140 cr. [CARE Apr-26]
  - Gross current asset days: 210 (FY25).
  - Collection days rose from 77 in FY22 to 136 in FY25.
  - Six-month-plus debtors are about 60% of receivables.
  - Downgrade triggers: gross current asset days above 275, or margin below 8% on a sustained basis.
- Contingent liabilities at 31 Mar 2026: bank performance guarantees ₹902.8 cr, other bank guarantees ₹1,510.5 cr, and surety or indemnity bonds to clients ₹573.7 cr (up from ₹224.5 cr). Tax and other claims total about ₹171 cr. [AR FY26]
- About ₹221 cr of deposits are pledged as margin for guarantees. [AR FY26]

## Moat evidence
- The balance sheet is the moat. The company has been net cash for years and uses its fund-based limits at about 7%, so it survived the Jaypee/HDIL-era downturn in which peers failed. [CARE Apr-26] [Q4 call]
- It has a large, repeat blue-chip client roster: DLF, Godrej, Signature Global, Brigade, CPWD/Central Vista, AAI and Indian Railways. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Only 10.34% of the book is fixed-price, and about 89% carries material escalation clauses. Cement and steel are a pass-through on private jobs with about a 30-day lag; government jobs use a WPI-linked quarterly escalation. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- The weak point is labour. Many large private NCR contracts lack a labour-escalation clause, and management conceded that clients "may not" compensate the company contractually. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Labour: minimum wages in Haryana and UP rose about 35–40%.
  - NCR sites ran at 40–50% labour strength for about two months, and skilled-labour rates are above even the mandated levels.
  - Company-level EBITDA hit was about 1.5% in Q1. If no escalation is granted, the ongoing hit is about 1%.
  - Management calls the new wage levels a "new base". [Q1 call]
- The AIIMS Jammu final bill was cut by ₹29 cr, shaving 2.6 points off Q1 EBITDA. The company is going to arbitration. [Q1 call]
- The Iran war disrupted things since Feb 2026:
  - LPG shortages drove labour away from sites.
  - Switchgear and panel lead times stretched from 4–6 weeks to 3–4 months.
  - Metal prices rose ahead of the WPI escalation. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Haryana shut about 90% of its ready-mix concrete plants, and NGT bans may affect Q3 FY27. [Q1 call]
- Project-staff cost has risen to more than 5% from 3.5–4.5%, and safety spending is about 3% from about 1%. Management says these costs are now priced into new bids. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Credibility gap on guidance:** within about 11 weeks, guidance went from "double-digit margins in this year" to "ruling out" double digits, with revenue growth cut from 15–20% to 12–15%. Management's tone with an analyst was combative. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **NCR concentration:** about 50% of the book is exposed to NGT bans, labour shocks and state actions such as the concrete-plant shutdown. CARE flags this as well. [Q1 call] [CARE Apr-26]
- Residential exposure is about 40% of the book, mostly large Gurgaon towers. Developers have "slowed down the launches" and sentiment is "muted". [AR FY26] [Q4 call]
- Execution slippage:
  - The order book doubled in two years, but revenue grew only about 9–11% a year.
  - India Jewellery Park has been delayed repeatedly by redesign; groundwork is now expected in Q3 FY27 and ₹100 cr of billing in FY27. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [CARE Apr-26]
- Central-government payments: bills on a Himachal central university project have gone unpaid for 5 months because the project is over budget. Assam payments stalled before the elections. [Q1 call]
- Fixed costs are building up: depreciation and staff costs are rising, while revenue from the big projects lags. [Q1 call] [Screener]
- Governance: the CMD is 87, and succession is not disclosed. The audit report is clean, with no emphasis of matter and no CARO remarks. [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Margin guidance: from "double-digit margins in this year" to "ruling out" double digits in FY27 and "aspire" for FY28. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Revenue growth guidance: from 15–20% to 12–15%. Management claims the base guidance "was 15%". [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Order inflow target: from ₹8,000 cr to about ₹4,000–5,000 cr. Management is now "not very aggressive" given cost volatility. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Capex: from about ₹300 cr to ₹220–250 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Central Vista FY27 billing: from ₹1,000 cr to about ₹700 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- New issues since the Q4 call: the labour-wage shock, the AIIMS Jammu dispute, the central-university non-payment and the Haryana RMC shutdown. [Q1 call]

## My read
Ahluwalia is the cleanest balance sheet in building construction: net cash of about ₹900 cr, CARE AA-, a clean audit and a ₹20,700 cr book. But it keeps failing to turn backlog into margin. Q1 FY27 showed how exposed a labour-intensive NCR builder is to wage and regulatory shocks when contracts lack labour escalation. The 11-week reversal from "double digits this year" to "ruled out" damages the credibility of management's guidance. The stock has roughly halved from its high. I'd want to see compensation from NCR clients and Central Vista billing actually ramping before trusting a rebound to 9–10% margins.
