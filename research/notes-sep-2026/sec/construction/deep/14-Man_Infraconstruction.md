# Man Infraconstruction Ltd (MANINFRA, ISIN INE949H01023)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Figures are consolidated (Screener) unless marked. ₹ cr = rupees crore.

**Classification note:** despite its name and "construction" tag, Man Infra is now mainly a **Mumbai residential real-estate developer** (redevelopment, joint ventures and development-management deals) with a small captive and port EPC arm and a Florida (US) luxury portfolio. EPC order book is only ₹392 cr. [Q4 call]

## Business and segments
- Real estate: group GDV >₹17,500 cr across ongoing and upcoming projects (Mar-26), of which ~₹13,300 cr is still unsold. Projects are across Tardeo and Marine Lines (South Mumbai), Bandra (BKC, Pali Hill, Mount Mary), Vile Parle, Mulund, Ghatkopar and Mira-Bhayandar/Dahisar. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Brands: "Aaradhya" for gated communities and a new "MS Collection" for ultra-luxury (Marina Vista at Pali Hill, Berkeley House at Mount Mary). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Deal types: equity JVs (typically 30–70% stakes), development-management (DM) deals where costs sit in the landowner's books and MICL earns fees plus a profit share, and interest income on ~₹1,461 cr lent to project entities. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- EPC: executes the group's in-house construction (~1 crore sq ft of construction area, which management frames as ₹9,000–10,000 cr of internal EPC) plus ports (Vadhvan). The external EPC order book is ₹392 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- US (MICL Global, 100%): Florida luxury projects with GDV ~US$1.4 billion (Ritz-Carlton Residences Fort Lauderdale, 1250 West Avenue minority stake, villas). About US$35 million has been sent so far. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY27 PAT growth >25%** over FY26: stated with Vision 2031 and kept at Q1 ("we continue to maintain"). Q1 attributable PAT was +29%. **Kept.** [Q1 call]
- **Pre-sales ₹5,000 cr over FY27–FY28** (roughly ₹2,500 cr a year): kept at Q1 despite only ₹290 cr in Q1. Management says launches are lumpy and that it "might even surpass" the target. **Kept, back-ended.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 launches:** Q4 said ~₹5,500–5,600 cr GDV; Q1 said 1.1 million sq ft and ~₹6,600 cr GDV. Pali Hill and Mulund commercial were launched in Q1. Marine Lines was a Diwali 2026 target at Q4 and is now "by this March"; Tardeo 2.0 was brought forward into FY27. **Mixed: Marine Lines slipped, Tardeo 2.0 advanced.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 revenue recognition +35–40%** (Q4), from completions at Ghatkopar (Mar 2027), Dahisar and Mulund Tower G (Dec 2027). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **GDV target:** double to >₹35,000 cr by 2031 (Vision 2031), "much prior" per management. Goregaon SRA could add ~₹10,000 cr GDV in about two years. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Minimal fixed assets (₹61 cr). Capital goes into land and society redevelopment deals, JV equity, and loans to project entities. Management says ~₹200 cr of cash buys ₹2,500–3,000 cr of GDV. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- The 306 m Aaradhya Avaan tower (Tardeo) has reached ~40 floors, with the first phase (~32 habitable floors) to be handed over by Mar 2028, against a RERA date of Dec 2030. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and equivalents ₹768 cr (Jun-26; ₹686 cr at Mar-26). Borrowings ~₹78 cr, mostly partner contributions. Net cash. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 187 → 448 → 573 → 133 → −50 (acquisitions and construction outlays in FY26). Working-capital days rose to 740 (Screener). [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Equity: of 3.50 crore preferential warrants (proceeds up to ₹543 cr), 3.24 crore converted and 26.3 lakh cancelled. Promoters and directors held warrants. The equity base rose to ₹81 cr of share capital. A share buyback is under way (daily purchases reported, e.g., 3.85 lakh shares on 25 Sep 2026). [AR FY26] [BSE filing, 25 Sep 2026]
- Dividend payout ~18%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Mumbai redevelopment track record, and execution speed from in-house construction (e.g., a 38-storey members' tower in under 2.5 years). High sell-through: Parkwood 90%, Mulund Tower G 75%, Avaan >60%, Pali Hill 30% within two months. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Feasibility is underwritten at "0% price increase" and a 10–15% haircut to market prices, targeting a 20%+ bottom line. DM deals return 2.5–3x the money invested. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Management says marble, tile and steel costs are up 13–15% on war-related effects and expects developers to pass this through in prices. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Business mix:** earnings depend on Mumbai luxury housing, lumpy launches and the timing of revenue recognition (reported revenue fell 43% in FY26 while sales held up). Much profit sits in associates and DM fees, which are less visible in revenue. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- **Disclosure:** management repeatedly deferred data questions (revenue reconciliation, segment revenue, area split) to "after the call". The Q1 "call" was a Zoom analyst meet. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Overseas exposure:** Florida projects in USD (it holds ~US$35 million there, with a local JV partner); tax and currency questions were deferred. [Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities (group, Mar-26):** bank and corporate guarantees ~₹341 cr (₹167 cr a year earlier); service-tax appeal at CESTAT; commitments to associates ~₹49 cr. The auditor's key audit matter is construction revenue recognition. No qualification found. [AR FY26]
- **Promoter:** holding fell from 66.65% to 62.52% over the year even as warrants converted. No pledge flagged. FIIs cut from 3.81% to 1.92%. [Screener]
- **Rating:** CARE A+/Stable (Nov 2025), citing strong liquidity from warrant money and DM income. It flagged revenue-recognition delays. [CARE Nov-25]

## What changed between the two calls
- The launch pipeline grew (₹5,600 cr → ₹6,600 cr GDV) and Mount Mary (Berkeley House, ₹1,000 cr+ GDV) received its IOA. Marine Lines slipped again, from Diwali to "by this March", after plot additions and FSI changes.
- Q1 pre-sales (₹290 cr) were weak against the ₹2,500 cr-a-year target. Management answered with the launch calendar.
- A buyback started after Q1.

## My read
Man Infra is a net-cash, fast-executing Mumbai developer with a large luxury launch pipeline (~₹6,600 cr GDV in FY27) and a credible record of selling out projects. Reported numbers are volatile and lag the underlying sales, and disclosure is thin, with most data questions deferred. The US foray adds currency and governance complexity. The view would improve if FY27 pre-sales approach ₹2,500 cr as Marine Lines, Tardeo 2.0 and Mount Mary launch, and Ghatkopar and Dahisar completions lift cash flow. It would weaken if launches slip further, cash is used heavily in the US, or pre-sales stay near the Q1 run-rate.
