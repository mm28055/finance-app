# EPack Prefab Technologies (NSE: EPACKPEB)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Consolidated figures in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.*

## Business and segments
- EPack Prefab is a Noida-based pre-engineered building (PEB) and prefab company with two divisions:
  - **Pre-Fab**: turnkey design, manufacture and erection of PEB, steel structures and insulated sandwich panels.
  - **EPS**: expanded polystyrene blocks and moulded products for packaging and construction. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- Capacity: 147,122 t a year of PEB, and 1.3 million m² of sandwich panels at FY26. Three plants are under construction:
  - Mambattu (AP) line 2 is commissioned.
  - The Ghiloth greenfield continuous sandwich-panel line (North India) is due in Oct–Nov 2026.
  - A Gujarat greenfield 50,000 t PEB plant is due by about Q3 FY27. [AR FY26] [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Customer mix: about 35–38% of the order book comes from renewables, data centres, semiconductors and logistics (at 1 Apr 2026). Logistics and warehousing are 22–25%, and auto about 10%. Clients named include Waaree Energies and Horizon, plus an Adani data centre (panels). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Data centres: a new subsidiary, **EPack Data Center Solutions**, was formed with a ₹75 cr equity earmark to make design-for-manufacture-and-assembly (DFMA) products such as containment zones, pipe spools and modules. The company lost two turnkey data-centre bids in Q1. [Q1 call]
- IPO: listed on 1 Oct 2025 at ₹204 a share, with a ₹300 cr fresh issue plus an offer for sale. Promoters hold 64.95%. [AR FY26] [Screener]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue, Mar-25 → Jun-26 (₹ cr): 331, 295, 434, 325, 471, 366. [Screener]
- Quarterly operating margin (OPM), same quarters: 11%, 10%, 12%, 10%, 10%, 9%. [Screener]
- Quarterly net profit, same quarters (₹ cr): 20, 16, 29, 17, 30, 18. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹366 cr (+25%), EBITDA about ₹35 cr (9.4% vs 10.5%), PAT margin 5.0% (vs 5.4%). [Q1 call]
- FY26: revenue ₹1,525 cr (+34%), EBITDA ₹160 cr (about 10.5%), PAT ₹93 cr (+56%). About ₹107 cr of debt was repaid. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- Revenue history, FY21–FY26 (₹ cr): 241, 450, 657, 905, 1,134, 1,525. The five-year revenue CAGR is 45%. [Screener]
- Market data:
  - Market cap ₹2,468 cr; price ₹245 against a 52-week range of ₹132–344.
  - P/E 26; ROCE 21.7%; ROE 17.0%. No dividend is paid. [Screener]
- Capacity utilisation: prefab division 75%+; sandwich-panel line about 45% (from 25% in FY26). [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (18 May 2026):**
  - Prefab division growth of about 30% in FY27.
  - EBITDA "at least 10% plus", with a long-run band of 10.5–11.5%.
  - A possible temporary dip of about 200bp from the steel spike caused by the West Asia war.
  - Order book ₹1,117 cr. FY27 capex of about ₹150 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (3 Aug 2026):**
  - FY27 revenue of **₹1,900–1,950 cr** (+~30%).
  - EBITDA of 10.5% for the full year, recovering from Q2.
  - FY27 order inflow target of ₹2,000 cr (₹580 cr won in Q1).
  - Peak revenue potential after all expansions of ₹2,700–2,900 cr. [Q1 call]
- **Track record:** management says "every commitment that we made while going for the IPO has been met". The admitted miss is under-use of the sandwich-panel line. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex is about ₹150 cr across the three plants. Another ₹20 cr is available to add 11,000 t at Ghiloth. [Q4 call]
- Investing cash outflow was ₹303 cr in FY26 and ₹151 cr in FY25. Capital work in progress was ₹60 cr at Mar-26. [Screener]
- The data-centre subsidiary's ₹75 cr is earmarked for a manufacturing facility. Land and a detailed plan are pending; management said "by the end of this quarter" (Q2) it should have real numbers. [Q1 call]
- Exports have started: about ₹2.5 cr of sandwich panels shipped to Africa in Q1. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings fell from ₹216 cr to ₹115 cr in FY26 after the IPO. The company says it is "heading towards becoming a debt-free company". Interest is still about ₹8 cr a quarter, and Screener flags the cost of borrowing as high. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- FY26 operating cash flow was ₹136 cr (CFO/OP 106%) and free cash flow about ₹3 cr after capex. The cash conversion cycle is 26 days (payables 143 days). [Screener]
- Contingent liabilities at Mar-26:
  - Bank guarantees ₹212.4 cr (from ₹172.2 cr).
  - Corporate guarantees ₹140.3 cr (from ₹92.3 cr).
  - Tax and other claims are small. [AR FY26]
- Rating: ICRA A+/A1 (per the AR). The ICRA rationales (latest 21 Jul 2026) were not read. [AR FY26] [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and footprint: four operating plants plus three coming, with integrated steel fabrication and sandwich panels. [AR FY26]
- Execution speed is the main pitch: "our speed of execution remains the forte". The company books orders almost weekly, so new contracts are repriced to current steel prices. [Q1 call]
- The largest order to date, ₹165 cr for a solar cell/module plant, and the first order from an auto maker show movement into bigger projects. [Q1 call]
- Limits: margins are "more or less similar" across sectors "because ... we have a competition in all the ... bits that we do". This is a tonnage-margin business with limited pricing power. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Steel is the main input. Steel prices rose about 12–15% in the four to five months after the war began. Most contracts are **fixed-price**, and the P&L hit was about 100bp against the 200bp feared. [Q1 call]
- New orders are booked at current prices. Management says the commodity impact is "now behind us". [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Fixed-price exposure:** most orders carry no pass-through, so another steel spike would hit margins directly. [Q1 call]
- **Capacity roll-out risk:** three plants arrive in FY27. The new sandwich-panel line had low utilisation (25%) in its first year. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Data-centre diversification:** early stage, with no DFMA product revenue yet and two turnkey bids lost. [Q1 call]
- **Related-party and group context:** the promoter chairman is also Executive Chairman of EPack Durable (a listed consumer-electronics ODM). Related-party transactions are governed by policy; the amounts were not extracted. [AR FY26]
- **Rising guarantees:** corporate guarantees rose to ₹140 cr and bank guarantees to ₹212 cr. [AR FY26]
- **Valuation:** 26x earnings for a business with about 10% EBITDA and 5–6% PAT margins. [Screener]
- The FY26 audit had no emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Order book: from ₹1,117 cr (mid-May) to about ₹1,376–1,380 cr (30 June), on record Q1 bookings of ₹580 cr (against ₹240 cr a year earlier). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Margin: the feared 200bp hit turned out to be about 100bp (9.4% in Q1). The 10.5% full-year target is maintained. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Revenue: FY27 guidance made explicit at ₹1,900–1,950 cr. [Q1 call]
- Data-centre subsidiary: formed with a ₹75 cr earmark. [Q1 call]

## My read
EPack Prefab is a fast-growing PEB and panel maker riding the factory, renewables and warehouse capex boom. Revenue has compounded at 45% over five years, and FY27 is guided to +30% on a record ₹1,380 cr order book. It also has a post-IPO, nearly debt-free balance sheet. Its model is fixed-price and tonnage-margin, so EBITDA stays around 10% and moves with steel prices, as the 100bp Q1 dip showed. Three new plants and a data-centre venture add execution risk in FY27. At 26x earnings, the stock needs the 10.5% margin recovery and new-capacity absorption to happen on schedule.
