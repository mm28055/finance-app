# IRB Infrastructure Developers Ltd (IRB, ISIN INE821I01022)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Figures are consolidated (Screener) unless marked. ₹ cr = rupees crore. The share count is 12,078 million after a bonus issue, so per-share figures are on the post-bonus base.

## Business and segments
- India's largest private toll-road platform. It builds, operates and recycles highway concessions through a "B.E.S.T." model (Bid, Execute, Stabilise, Transfer). The group has 17,355 lane-km in 13 states, an asset base of ~₹94,000 cr, about 10% of India's toll revenue and ~43% of TOT projects awarded so far. [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call]
- **Three segments:**
  - **BOT/TOT** (100%-owned Mumbai–Pune Expressway TOT and Ahmedabad–Vadodara BOT, plus two HAM projects under construction): Q1 FY27 revenue ₹733 cr, EBITDA margin 91%.
  - **InvITs & related assets** (51% of the private IRB Infrastructure Trust, ~17% of the listed IRB InvIT Fund, plus fair-value gains): Q1 revenue ₹437 cr, margin 94%.
  - **Construction/O&M** (EPC and fixed-price O&M for all group assets): Q1 revenue ₹967 cr, margin 15%.
  
  [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- Private InvIT: 14 assets, 10,674 lane-km, average daily toll ₹13.35 cr in Q1 FY27 (+45% like-for-like), cumulative distributions ₹1,268 cr. Partners GIC and Cintra co-invest. Public InvIT: 10 assets, 4,445 lane-km. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- Shareholders: Cintra (Ferrovial) 19.9% and GIC affiliate Bricklayers 16.9%. Promoter (Mhaiskar family) 30.81%. [IP Q1FY27] [Screener]
- Order book ~₹44,100 cr (Jun-26), of which only ₹1,700 cr is EPC. The rest is long-dated O&M for InvIT assets. Management expects ₹4,000–4,300 cr of construction revenue over the next two years, mostly O&M. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY27 construction revenue:** Q4 said it "should comfortably… cross the ₹3,000 crore mark". Q1 construction revenue was ₹967 cr, which annualises to ~₹3,900 cr. **On track.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Toll revenue:** Q4 targeted ~10% growth and a "5-digit" (≥₹10,000 cr) gross toll figure for IRB plus the private InvIT in FY27. Q1 showed +26% combined, helped by new assets. **On track.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Profit:** 25% PAT CAGR target (FY30 plan); Q1 PAT +51%. **Kept.** [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Balance sheet:** net debt/equity 0.6x → 0x by FY30; cash ROE from 8% to 14%+. Q4 expected ₹150–200 cr lower annual interest in FY27. Q1 underlying interest was ₹401 cr vs ₹462 cr a year earlier. Refinancing saved ~₹25 cr a year (Mumbai–Pune, ₹3,654 cr, −65 bps) and ~₹180 cr a year at the private InvIT (₹11,000 cr, −160 bps). **Kept, delivering.** [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- **Asset rotation:** Q4 said two assets (Solapur–Yedeshi, Chittorgarh–Gulabpura, EV ~₹4,500 cr) would move to the public InvIT "in the first half". A binding term sheet was signed in Q1 (EV ₹4,605 cr) and the SPA on 22 Sep 2026. **Delivered on time.** A further ~₹30,000–35,000 cr EV is to move over 2–3 years. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [BSE filing, 22 Sep 2026]
- **AUM target:** ₹80,000 cr → ~₹1,40,000 cr in 3–4 years (restated as ₹1.4 lakh cr at Q1). **Kept.** [IP Q1FY27]
- **Order wins:** Q4 expected TOT bidding traction in 3–4 months. Q1 had no new orders, only ₹800–900 cr of change-of-scope work. TOT-20/21/22 bids were postponed to late August after NHAI raised the upfront capex. **Delayed.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **No equity raise:** "no capital raising at IRB" (Q4). Dividend policy is 20%+ of profit. **Kept.** [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- IRB itself does little capex now. New TOT/BOT assets are housed in the private InvIT, with equity from IRB, GIC and Cintra in proportion to their units. The ₹4,905 cr of equity released in Nov 2025 funds new assets such as TOT-17 and TOT-18 without fresh IRB money. [CRISIL Dec-25] [IP Q1FY27]
- Wholly owned under-construction assets: two HAM projects (Pathankot–Mandi ₹800 cr, Chittoor–Thachur ₹900 cr). These are to be offered to the public InvIT on completion. [IP Q1FY27]
- Non-core monetisation: on 22 Sep 2026 the board approved phased development of 350 acres (of 1,100 acres at Taje/Pimpaloli, Pune) and 3,500 sq m at Chandivali, Mumbai. No values were given. [BSE filing, 22 Sep 2026] [News: Sahi/HDFC Sky, 23 Sep 2026]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Consolidated borrowings ₹20,027 cr (Mar-26), including a US$ bond (₹4,502 cr at 7.11%). Net debt/equity 0.53x (Mar-26) and 0.51x (Jun-26), on company figures. [Screener] [IP Q1FY27] [CRISIL Dec-25]
- Distributions from the InvITs are now a cash engine. Q1 FY27: ₹102 cr from the private InvIT (vs ₹27 cr) and ~₹35 cr from the public InvIT (vs ₹19 cr). FY26 received ~₹300 cr in total. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 364 → 1,764 → 4,054 → 1,971 → 2,098; free cash flow −1,010 → 1,354 → 3,816 → 1,776 → 1,417. Interest cover is low (Screener flag). [Screener]
- Receivable of ₹4,132 cr from a joint venture (the private InvIT) for the earlier transfer of 9 project companies, carried at fair value. The auditor lists this as a key audit matter. [AR FY26]
- Dividends: ₹187 cr for FY26 (payout 15%); ~₹60 cr interim for Q1 FY27. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and cost position in TOT: ~43% of TOT awards to date. In-house tolling and O&M (FASTag ~97%). AI-assisted O&M on ~21,000 lane-km. Rated partners (GIC, Cintra) lower its cost of capital. Both InvITs are rated AAA. [IP Q1FY27]
- Inflation-linked tariffs: toll rates reset each April with WPI. FY27 revision was ~2.5–3%. With WPI at ~8%, management expects a much higher revision in April 2027. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Pipeline: the government monetisation pipeline is ~₹4.4 lakh cr. TOT awards of ~₹40,000–50,000 cr are expected a year. IRB bids at 15–16% IRR and transfers at 12–12.5%. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- Competition: 5–7 bidders typically on BOT. NHAI's new net-worth rules (25%, and 50% for PE-backed bidders) may not reduce competition much. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- The construction book is small, so exposure to steel, cement and bitumen is limited ("minimal capex balance"). HAM escalation is passed through. [Q4 call]
- The main sensitivity is interest rates. Most rates have been locked through refinancing, and management does not foresee a rate cycle moving up for 2–3 years. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Concession cliff:** the wholly owned Mumbai–Pune TOT (1,014 lane-km, the group's flagship cash generator) ends in April 2030. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Accounting complexity:** profits include fair-value gains on InvIT units and a ₹4,132 cr fair-valued JV receivable. FY25's ₹6,481 cr PAT was ~90% non-cash revaluation. ROE is ~4%, even though the P&L looks profitable. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- **Related parties:** IRB is sponsor, project manager and exclusive fixed-price O&M/EPC contractor to both InvITs, and holds units in both. Pricing of asset transfers between the private and public InvITs sets IRB's reported gains. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Contingent liabilities** are small: ₹184 cr (guarantees for JVs ₹121 cr, bank guarantees ₹63 cr) at Mar-26, down from ₹360 cr. Pending litigation is mostly from commuters and regulators, deemed remote. [AR FY26]
- **Auditor (joint audit):** unmodified. Key audit matters: recoverability of loans and fair value of the JV receivable; construction revenue; impairment of toll-collection rights (₹22,946 cr). A ₹43 cr labour-code exceptional charge was taken in FY26. [AR FY26]
- **Pledges:** borrowings are secured by pledges of InvIT units held by IRB. No promoter share pledge is flagged on Screener. The promoter stake is 30.81%, down 3.4 points over three years on Screener. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- **Order-flow dependence:** growth depends on NHAI tendering TOT/BOT. BOT awarding has "hardly" happened despite a ₹2 lakh cr pipeline, which management conceded at Q4. [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Refinancing was completed (₹14,650 cr in total), and underlying interest fell ~13% YoY.
- Asset rotation moved from "triggered" to a binding term sheet and then an SPA. InvIT distributions to IRB rose ~3.8x YoY.
- Still no new TOT wins. TOT-20/21/22 were postponed and made more capex-heavy. MLFF (barrier-free tolling) clarifications are pending.
- Management said revenue mix should move towards ~50:50 construction/O&M versus BOT/InvIT over 2–3 years, which lifts group margins.

## My read
IRB is turning into a capital-light sponsor and O&M platform. Toll growth (+26%), falling interest costs and sharply higher InvIT distributions are pushing reported profit up 38–51% a quarter, and the Q4 promises on refinancing and asset transfer were met on time. The catch is that economic returns are still thin: ROE ~4%, a large fair-valued receivable, and non-cash revaluation gains in revenue. The Mumbai–Pune concession ends in 2030. The view would improve with TOT wins at the stated 15–16% IRR, a strong April 2027 tariff reset, and net debt falling as promised. It would weaken if NHAI's TOT/BOT awarding stays stalled or if transfer pricing between the InvITs starts to look generous to IRB.
