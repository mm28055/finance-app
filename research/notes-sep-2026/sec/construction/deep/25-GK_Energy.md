# GK Energy (NSE: GKENERGY)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Standalone figures in ₹ crore (Screener has no consolidated page).*

## Business and segments
- GK Energy is a Pune-based EPC contractor for **solar-powered agricultural water pumps**, mainly under PM-KUSUM Component B and state schemes such as Maharashtra's Magel Tyala (MTSKPY), MP's PMKMSY and Chhattisgarh's Saur Sujala. It is expanding into residential rooftop solar and, most recently, battery storage (BESS). [CARE Feb-26] [Q1 call]
- Scale:
  - More than 164,500 systems and 726 MW installed cumulatively, across more than 7,500 villages.
  - Q1 FY27: 24,118 systems installed (against 10,827 a year earlier) and 109 MW commissioned.
  - Management says monthly installation bandwidth is 15,000+ systems. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Operating model: "low capex". The company sources pumps, panels and structures from third-party OEM/ODM manufacturers under forward price agreements, and runs decentralised warehouses plus its own fleet of 40+ vehicles. It has about 1,500 staff. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Revenue mix in Q1 FY27: pumps about 95%, rooftop about 5%. The order book is about 80% pumps and 20% rooftop. [Q1 call]
- Management says Maharashtra accounts for about 70% of India's solar-pump business. GK is empanelled in Maharashtra, Haryana, Rajasthan, UP and MP, which together make up about 82% of PM-KUSUM's sanctioned pump volume. [Q1 call] [CARE Feb-26]
- New BESS win: an LoA from MSEDCL on 21 Sep 2026 for 150 MW / 300 MWh with viability-gap funding. It pays monthly revenue for 15 years; the filing summary cites about ₹42.8 cr a year. [BSE 21-Sep-26] [Screener ANN]
- IPO: listed on 26 Sep 2025 at ₹153 a share, raising ₹400 cr fresh plus a ₹64.26 cr promoter offer-for-sale. Promoters (Gopal Kabra and Mehul Shah) hold 79.2%. [AR FY26] [CARE Feb-26] [Screener]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue, Mar-25 → Jun-26 (₹ cr): 353, 295, 358, 460, 419, 505. [Screener]
- Quarterly operating margin (OPM), same quarters: 19%, 19%, 20%, 20%, 20%, **16%**. [Screener]
- Quarterly net profit, same quarters (₹ cr): 45, 37, 46, 59, 59, 60. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹505 cr (+71.1%), EBITDA ₹86.1 cr (17.05%), PAT ₹59.7 cr (+61.6%). [Q1 call]
- FY26: revenue ₹1,533 cr (+40%), EBITDA ₹313 cr (20.44% vs 18.64%), PAT ₹201 cr (+51%). [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- Revenue history, FY22–FY26 (₹ cr): 70, 285, 411, 1,095, 1,533. PAT: 2, 10, 36, 133, 201. [Screener]
- Market data:
  - Market cap ₹2,512 cr; price ₹124, below the ₹153 IPO price, against a 52-week range of ₹87–240.
  - P/E 11.2; ROE 36.8%; ROCE 40.9%. [Screener]
- Pump volumes doubled in Q1, but revenue grew only about 71% as the chairman confirmed that "realization has been low per pump". [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY26:** the company guided to about 70,000 pumps and delivered 60,000+. Management blamed material-supply problems in the last weeks of March. [Q4 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (13 May 2026):**
  - "Double" FY26 revenue in FY27, i.e. about ₹3,000 cr (as an analyst restated it).
  - "Two-digit" PAT margin.
  - Order book ₹710 cr, including a ₹350 cr Magel Tyala Phase 4 order. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (7 Aug 2026):**
  - Revenue doubling reiterated ("definitely on that track").
  - PAT to stay "in the same line" as Q1 (about 12%).
  - PM-KUSUM 2.0 expected by Q3 FY27, with the announcement around September and work starting by the end of Q3.
  - Q1 is typically 15–20% of annual volume and Q4 35–40%. [Q1 call]
- **The gap:** doubling needs about ₹2,500 cr in the next three quarters, against an order book of only ₹541 cr (including post-June orders). The target depends heavily on PM-KUSUM 2.0 and Magel Tyala Phases 6 and 7. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- The company is asset-light: fixed assets are ₹106 cr at FY26 (up from ₹14 cr), and it has no manufacturing. The chairman is "not very clear" on backward integration. CARE notes a nascent plan for PV-module manufacturing through a subsidiary. [Screener] [Q1 call] [CARE Feb-26]
- BESS (150 MW / 300 MWh) will need project capex and financing. Details were not disclosed beyond the LoA. [BSE 21-Sep-26]
- Long-term vision: 1 million homes and a "$1 billion" enterprise by 2030. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- After the IPO, the company moved to a **net cash surplus of ₹240.6 cr** (FY26). Borrowings were ₹203 cr at Mar-26. Q1 interest fell to ₹5 cr from ₹10–11 cr because IPO money funds working capital. [AR FY26] [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Working capital is the weak spot:
  - Debtor days were 140 in FY26 (120 in FY25).
  - The cash conversion cycle is 112 days, and working capital days rose from 44 to 80. [Screener]
- Operating cash flow: FY25 −₹99 cr; FY26 +₹53 cr, before IPO inflows. Free cash flow was negative in every year shown. [Screener]
- CARE assigned BBB+/Stable/A2 on 5 Feb 2026 on ₹300 cr of facilities. [CARE Feb-26]
  - Downgrade triggers: PBILDT margin below 12%, TOL/TNW above 2x, or operating cycle above 150 days.
  - ICRA (Nov 2025) and Infomerics (Dec 2025) ratings are listed but were not read. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Market position: one of the leading PM-KUSUM-B EPC players, with 18 years of rural distribution built up. Scale in Maharashtra, the largest market, is a real advantage in execution and service. [CARE Feb-26] [Q1 call]
- Procurement: management fixes supplier prices on day one for 3–6 months of supply. "We are happy to have 1% less profit, but we want to [be] clear what we are going to earn". [Q4 call]
- Limits to the moat: realisations per pump are falling, and competition is intense. CARE flags "intense competition in the industry" and "tender-driven nature of operations". [Q1 call] [CARE Feb-26]

## Input costs
- Solar modules, pumps and structures are bought from OEMs. The company uses forward agreements to limit price risk. [Q4 call]
- The company expects realisation per pump to stay flat unless PM-KUSUM 2.0 changes specifications. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Policy dependence:** growth hinges on PM-KUSUM 2.0 launching by about Q3 FY27. An analyst cited a legislative reply indicating that funds had been spread across ministries. The chairman disputed its relevance. [Q1 call]
- **Order book too thin for guidance:** ₹541 cr against about ₹2,500 cr of revenue needed in three quarters. The order book fell from ₹710 cr at the Q4 call. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Customer and geographic concentration:** high exposure to Maharashtra government schemes and state distribution companies. CARE flags this as a key constraint. [CARE Feb-26]
- **Receivables:** 140 debtor days on government-subsidy schemes. [Screener]
- **Margin trend:** Q1 OPM of 16% is down from 20%, and CARE's downgrade trigger is 12%. [Screener] [CARE Feb-26]
- **Share performance:** the stock trades below its ₹153 IPO price 12 months after listing. The promoter sold ₹64 cr in the IPO. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- **BESS diversification:** a new technology for GK, with VGF and tariff risk over 15 years. [BSE 21-Sep-26]
- The FY26 audit had no emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Order book: from ₹710 cr at the Q4 call to ₹541 cr at the Q1 call, despite new orders. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Margin: from 20.4% in FY26 to 17.05% in Q1. The chairman had "given the indication that we may expect little bit down". [Q1 call]
- PM-KUSUM 2.0 timing: still "Q3", with no slippage admitted. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Interest cost: halved as IPO proceeds replaced bank debt. [Q1 call]
- New BESS LoA from MSEDCL after the Q1 call. [BSE 21-Sep-26]

## My read
GK Energy is a high-return, asset-light EPC play on farm solar pumps: 37% ROE, net cash after the IPO, and FY26 profit up 51%. Its fortunes are tightly tied to government schemes, above all the delayed PM-KUSUM 2.0 and Maharashtra's Magel Tyala. The FY27 "double revenue" target (about ₹3,000 cr) is backed by an order book of only ₹541 cr, and per-pump realisations and margins are already slipping. Receivables of 140 days on subsidy schemes are the other key risk. The stock sits below its IPO price, so the market doubts the guidance. A formal PM-KUSUM 2.0 launch and new Magel Tyala phases are the catalysts to watch.
