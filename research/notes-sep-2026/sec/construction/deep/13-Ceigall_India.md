# Ceigall India Ltd (CEIGALL, ISIN INE0AG901020)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Figures are consolidated (Screener) unless marked "standalone". ₹ cr = rupees crore.

## Business and segments
- Punjab-origin (Ludhiana) road contractor, listed in Aug 2024. Its specialities are elevated corridors, flyovers, bridges, tunnels and expressways. Examples: the Danapur–Bihta 19.87 km four-lane elevated corridor, Delhi–Saharanpur elevated highway, the Ramban–Banihal twin tunnel and Kanpur Metro. Promoter (Sehgal family) holds 82.05%. [IP Q1FY27] [Screener]
- Since the IPO it has diversified into renewables (solar, solar-plus-BESS, standalone BESS, PM-KUSUM in Maharashtra and MP), transmission and distribution (the Velgaon 400 kV substation), metro, wind turbine civil works and industrial infrastructure. Renewables were ~35% of FY26 inflow and 19% of the order book at Mar-26. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Order book ₹18,568 cr (30 Jun 2026): 19 EPC, 10 HAM, 1 DBFOT and 9 tariff-based renewable and transmission projects; 39 ongoing projects. Its largest single project is the Sahebganj–Areraj–Bettiah HAM (Bihar, TPC ₹2,160 cr). The remaining HAMs are all NHAI except one MPRDC project. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- International: subsidiaries in Singapore and Dubai. Bids included a ~₹13,000 cr, 17 km highway-and-tunnel project in Romania (qualified alone) and an AED 250 million Sobha job in Dubai; the approach is "very conservative". [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY26:** inflow ₹11,332 cr against ₹5,000 cr guided (**beaten**); revenue growth 10–15% guided, 17% delivered consolidated (**met**). [Q4 call]
- **FY27 revenue growth:** "minimum 15%" (Q4), with renewables 20–25% of revenue; repeated at Q1. **Kept.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 EBITDA margin 11–12.5%:** kept at Q1 despite 13.4–14.8% in Q1. Margins exclude bonus and royalty income. **Kept (conservative).** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 order inflow:** Q4 said "minimum INR 5,500 crores"; Q1 said ₹6,000 cr. **Slightly raised.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **HAM monetisation:** at Q4, three assets had been sold to Neo Asset Management (Malout–Abohar–Sadhuwali, Bathinda–Dabwali, Jalbehra–Shahbad) for >₹400 cr, expected in Q1, Q2 and Q3. Malout–Abohar closed in Q1 at an IRR above plan. **On track.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Equity commitments:** Q4 said ₹1,937 cr over 3 years (~₹800 cr renewables). Q1 said ₹859 cr in FY27 (₹550 cr HAM + ₹310 cr solar) and ₹744 cr in FY28. ₹439 cr has been invested since the IPO. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Capex:** ₹30–35 cr in FY27. [Q1 call]
- **Q4 correction:** the unencumbered cash figures quoted on the Q4 call (₹266 cr cash, ₹146 cr FDs) were later corrected in the transcript to ₹166 cr and ₹75 cr. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Fixed assets are only ₹341 cr; more than ₹100 cr of IPO money was spent on machinery. The model is asset-light, with specialised equipment (e.g., launchers) bought as needed. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Renewables: Maharashtra solar (land for 50+ MW leased; piling started) and Morena (MP) solar-plus-BESS (PPA signed, awaiting a transmission line). An L1 was won in standalone BESS in Q1. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Standalone D/E ~0.2x; consolidated 0.6x (Mar-26). Borrowings ₹1,311 cr. ~₹320 cr in FDs. Commercial paper of ₹100 cr approved (at ~6.8–7% vs WCDL at 7.5–7.8%). [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [Screener]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: −135 → −73 → −211 → −520 → −91; free cash flow negative every year since FY22, reflecting HAM funding. [Screener]
- Unbilled revenue days rose from 94 to 133 and payable days from 79 to 138 at Mar-26. Milestone billing, after the April 2024 withdrawal of monthly billing, was blamed; monthly billing has now been restored by the Road Transport Ministry. [Q4 call]
- Dividend payout ~3%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Niche in complex structures (elevated corridors, tunnels) and a record of early completion bonuses. Tighter NHAI eligibility (higher net worth, extra performance security) favours established bidders. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- It targets ~25% project IRR on HAM and recycles capital by selling to Neo. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Steel, cement, bitumen and diesel. The Road Transport Ministry's 2026 circulars compensate escalation monthly and cover abnormal bitumen costs. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Internal-control failure:** the auditor gave a **qualified opinion on internal financial controls** (standalone) for FY26. A ₹8.985 cr "bogus purchase" fraud by certain employees colluding with vendors at four sites was found in a January 2026 stock-take. No cash was lost (it was recovered from vendor balances), but some remediating controls were still untested at the report date. [AR FY26]
- **Terminated HAM:** the step-down subsidiary Ludhiana–Rupnagar Greenfield Highway had its concession terminated by NHAI. Its accounts are prepared on a non-going-concern basis (emphasis of matter in the subsidiary audit; deemed immaterial to the group). [AR FY26]
- **Cash flow and HAM equity:** operating cash flow has been negative for five years, with ~₹1,600 cr of equity still to be invested in FY27–FY28. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- **Land and approvals:** Ludhiana bypass has only 62% of land; Morena solar awaits transmission; appointed dates for two new HAMs are expected in Q3–Q4 FY27. [Q1 call]
- **Promoter concentration:** 82.05% promoter holding and a limited free float. No pledge flagged. [Screener]
- **Disclosure:** the Q4 cash figures were misstated on the call and corrected later. The FY26 annual report on BSE is a scanned (image) PDF; I read it via OCR. [Q4 call] [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- The first HAM monetisation (Malout–Abohar) completed. Appointed dates arrived for VRK-11, VRK-12 and Indore–Ujjain after quarter-end.
- The inflow guide was nudged up to ₹6,000 cr. Q1 margins (13.4% standalone) ran above the 11–12.5% guide.
- Renewables moved from "LOA" to execution (Maharashtra PM-KUSUM, Morena PPA). A BESS L1 and an Arunachal JV highway L1 were added.

## My read
Ceigall has grown fast: revenue is 3.5x FY22, order book ~4.6x FY26 revenue, standalone margins 13%+, and HAM recycling has now been demonstrated with its first asset sale. Diversification into solar, BESS and transmission lowers road dependence but adds execution and tariff-asset risk. The negatives are negative cash flow, heavy upcoming equity commitments, a terminated HAM subsidiary, and a qualified internal-controls opinion after a vendor fraud. The view would improve with the remaining Neo sales closing, operating cash flow turning positive, and renewable projects commissioning on time. It would weaken if more control lapses emerge or HAM land delays pile up.
