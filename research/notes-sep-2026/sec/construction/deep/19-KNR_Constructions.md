# KNR Constructions (NSE: KNRCON)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore. "SA" means standalone EPC; "consol" means consolidated with HAM SPVs. Screener consolidated numbers include SPV construction and annuity income.*

## Business and segments
- KNR is a Hyderabad-based roads EPC contractor and HAM developer, with irrigation (mostly Telangana) and urban water pipeline work. It is now diversifying into coal mining as an MDO contractor, plus flyovers, railways and metro. [AR FY26] [Q1 call]
- Order book at 30 Jun 2026: ₹8,667 cr executable, or ₹15,234 cr including the two new HAMs and the Kusmunda mining order.
  - By segment: roads 38%, irrigation 11%, pipeline 6%, **mining 45%**.
  - By client: third-party 71% (central government 46%, state 24%, private 1%) and captive HAM 29%. [Q1 call]
- New wins:
  - Chennai ECR elevated HAM (about ₹2,150 cr) and the Gudebellur–Mahabubnagar HAM in Telangana (₹1,734 cr). Together the two need about ₹510 cr of equity.
  - Kusmunda (SECL) coal mining with Sushee Infra, ₹3,361 cr over 8 years; KNR does 100% of the EPC back-to-back.
  - Banhardih (Jharkhand) mining, ₹3,552 cr, awaiting stage-II forest clearance.
  - GHMC flyovers of about ₹1,400–1,500 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [CRISIL Aug-26]
- HAM monetisation to Indus Infra Trust:
  - Palani: ₹205 cr consideration plus a ₹90 cr cash upstream.
  - Ramagiri: ₹227 cr.
  - Guruvayur: completed 17 Sep 2026 for ₹485.86 cr, against ₹193.32 cr invested.
  - The agreed total for four assets is about ₹1,543 cr; Ramanattukara is still pending. [Q1 call] [BSE 18-Sep-26] [CRISIL Aug-26]
- Promoters hold 48.81%, with no pledge shown. DIIs have cut their holding from 28.3% to 17.3% over five quarters. [Screener]

## Key numbers
- Consolidated quarterly revenue, Mar-25 → Jun-26 (₹ cr): 975, 613, 646, 743, 696, 588. [Screener]
- Consolidated quarterly operating margin (OPM), same quarters: 23%, 30%, 30%, 22%, 24%, 16%. [Screener]
- Consolidated quarterly net profit, same quarters (₹ cr): 8, 123, 105, 103, 106, 81. [Screener]
- Standalone FY26: revenue ₹2,097 cr, EBITDA ₹178 cr (8.5%), PAT ₹116 cr. In Q4 FY26 the EBITDA margin was only 5.3%. [Q4 call]
- CRISIL-adjusted FY26 operating income was ₹2,111 cr (−38% YoY), with EBITDA margin down to 9.1% from 19.1%. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- Standalone Q1 FY27: revenue ₹436.7 cr, EBITDA ₹65 cr (15%), PAT ₹282.3 cr (boosted by monetisation gains).
  - The EBITDA includes about ₹46 cr net of one-off Palani cash-surplus EPC claims. **Excluding that, the margin was about 5.5%.** [Q1 call]
- Consolidated revenue, FY21–FY26 (₹ cr): 2,904, 3,606, 4,062, 4,429, 4,753, 2,698. Consolidated net profit, FY24–FY26 (₹ cr): 752, 1,002, 437. [Screener]
- Market data:
  - Market cap ₹3,375 cr; price ₹120 against a 52-week range of ₹109–205, down 40% in one year.
  - Price/book 0.68x; P/E 9.9. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Earlier margins:** CRISIL says margins used to run at 15–22% but should now settle at 11–13%. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Q4 FY26 call (1 Jun 2026):**
  - Revenue was "quite difficult to say"; management would "try to touch around 2,000 plus".
  - Order inflow ₹8,000–10,000 cr.
  - Margin about 10% overall on the new mix, with the Telangana HAM at 11–12% EBITDA.
  - Management said "whatever the order which is coming up it's coming at a tight competition." [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (14 Aug 2026):**
  - FY27 standalone revenue ₹2,200–2,300 cr, **EBITDA 8–9%**. Q2 margins are expected similar to Q1's underlying level, and H2 at 10–12%.
  - FY28 revenue more than ₹3,000 cr at 11–12%, sometimes stated as 12–13%.
  - Order inflow ₹8,000–10,000 cr maintained.
  - Mining revenue about ₹150 cr in FY27 and about ₹400 cr in FY28 (Kusmunda only). [Q1 call]
- **Track record:** CRISIL notes the start-FY26 order book of only ₹5,052 cr, right-of-way (RoW) constraints, higher subcontracting and one-offs. These drove the FY26 collapse from what had been 20%-margin norms. [CRISIL Aug-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Mining is transforming capital intensity:
  - Kusmunda needs about ₹500–600 cr of equipment (100-tonne dumpers, surface miners at about ₹13 cr each, dozers) for only about ₹400 cr a year of revenue.
  - Banhardih needs another ₹300–400 cr, but its land and forest stage-II clearance are pending 8–10 months. [Q1 call]
- FY27 capex was guided at ₹350–400 cr (₹14 cr in Q1). The ED separately cited Kusmunda at ₹400–450 cr plus Banhardih at about ₹200 cr. [Q1 call]
- Management plans dumper buybacks from Caterpillar and Komatsu every two years at about 75% residual value. It targets a PAT margin of "6%-plus" on mining. [Q1 call]
- HAM equity:
  - Revised requirement ₹805 cr, of which ₹595 cr has been invested. The balance is ₹125 cr in FY27 and ₹85 cr in FY28.
  - The two new HAMs need about ₹510 cr more.
  - CRISIL estimates ₹400–450 cr of investment in FY27. [Q1 call] [CRISIL Aug-26]
- Execution: the Mysore–Kushalnagar packages 4 and 5 are only about 18–20% done, after local protests blocked land until about June 2026. The new HAMs' appointed dates are expected in Q3 FY27. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Standalone debt nil; standalone cash ₹310 cr at 30 Jun 2026. [Q1 call]
- Consolidated debt (the SPV project loans) fell from ₹2,438 cr at Mar-26 to ₹1,975 cr at Jun-26 after monetisation, with consolidated cash of ₹435 cr. [Q1 call]
- Standalone working capital days jumped to **133** from 78 at Mar-26. Screener's consolidated working capital days are 312 for FY26. [Q1 call] [Screener]
- **Telangana irrigation receivables:**
  - About ₹1,300 cr from Kaleswaram Package 4 alone, and about ₹1,450 cr from irrigation overall.
  - Unbilled irrigation revenue that is already recognised is ₹825 cr. [Q1 call]
  - The auditor's **emphasis of matter**: dues of ₹1,363.3 cr on Kaleswaram Package 4 "were considered as good for recovery by the management." [AR FY26]
  - CRISIL cites ₹673 cr of pending irrigation receivables plus more than ₹1,340 cr of unbilled revenue at Mar-26. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- Operating cash flow was negative for three years: FY24 −₹297 cr, FY25 −₹567 cr, FY26 −₹149 cr. [Screener]
- CRISIL AA/Stable/A1+ was reaffirmed on 3 Aug 2026 on ₹2,350 cr of bank lines. TOL/ANW is 0.13x; net worth ₹4,055 cr, with about 23% locked in HAM/annuity investments. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- Contingent liabilities (standalone, Mar-26):
  - Claims ₹646 cr, against ₹464 cr a year earlier. This includes income tax ₹287.9 cr, indirect taxes about ₹176 cr, and **civil cases ₹182.2 cr (up from ₹0.26 cr)**.
  - Plus corporate guarantees given for SPVs. [AR FY26]
- In September 2026, CIT(A) orders deleted income-tax penalties of ₹18.2 cr (AY2007-08) and ₹9.35 cr (AY2021-22). [BSE 18-Sep-26] [BSE 24-Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- A long record of on-time and early completion: more than 9,000 lane-km across 12 states, with early-completion bonuses. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- A large owned equipment fleet and in-house EPC for its own HAMs. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- A strong monetisation record: repeated sales to InvITs at about 2.5x invested equity (for example, Guruvayur ₹485.86 cr against ₹193.32 cr). [BSE 18-Sep-26]
- The moat is eroding. EBITDA margins fell from 19% to 9% on competition. The company lacks greenfield-expressway qualification for UPEIDA bids, and its BESS bids went "very bad". [CRISIL Aug-26] [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Bitumen rose with West Asia crude. MoRTH moved to monthly price adjustment. [Q4 call]
- Mining cost structure: equipment, labour and diesel (HSD) are "all three major components". Management had not yet computed EBITDA on the mining work. [Q1 call]
- Management expects BESS equipment risk from the weaker rupee. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Telangana concentration:**
  - About ₹1,450 cr of irrigation dues (about 30% of net worth) under an auditor's emphasis of matter.
  - The state has proposed ₹650 cr in 5–10 instalments of ₹70–90 cr a month. The ED said "unless you don't push, things will not happen." [Q1 call] [AR FY26]
  - A further revised estimate of more than ₹700 cr needs government approval. [Q1 call]
- **Underlying profitability:** Q1 underlying EBITDA of about 5.5% and FY27 guidance of 8–9% compare with a 20%+ history. Profits now depend on monetisation gains and one-off claims. [Q1 call]
- **Mining execution:** a new business line, now 45% of the order book, with ₹900 cr–₹1,000 cr of capex. The client insisted on an appointed date during the monsoon. Banhardih clearances are uncertain. [Q1 call]
- **Order book quality:** HAM and mining make up most of the book. The headline ₹15,234 cr overstates near-term revenue: mining yields only about ₹350–400 cr a year per project. [Q1 call]
- **Buyback:** repeatedly "under consideration" with no decision. Management is keeping cash for equity and capex needs. [Q1 call]
- The rise in civil-case contingent liabilities to ₹182 cr is not explained in the extracted text (not disclosed). [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Revenue: from "try to touch around 2,000 plus" to ₹2,200–2,300 cr, helped by new projects starting in Q3. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Margin: from about 10% to **8–9% for FY27**, since Q1 and Q2 are weak and H2 is at 10–12%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Order book: added the Kusmunda mining order (₹3,361 cr) after the Q4 call. [Q1 call]
- Monetisation: Ramagiri and Palani closed in Q1 FY27, and Guruvayur on 17 Sep 2026. [Q1 call] [BSE 18-Sep-26]
- Telangana: moved from "within 1, 2 months" (Q4 call) to a concrete instalment proposal that is still unpaid at the call. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Standalone working capital days: from 78 to 133. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## My read
KNR has moved from a best-in-class road contractor earning 20% EBITDA to a sub-10% business. FY26 standalone EBITDA was 8.5%, Q1 FY27's underlying margin about 5.5%, and FY27 guidance is 8–9%. Meanwhile about ₹1,450 cr is stuck with the Telangana irrigation department, under an auditor's emphasis of matter. The balance sheet is still strong (standalone debt-free, CRISIL AA) because the company keeps selling HAM assets well. The order-book refill leans on capital-hungry coal mining, where KNR has no track record and margins are unproven. At 0.7x book, the stock prices in much of this. The re-rating depends on Telangana paying the promised instalments and on Kusmunda ramping without eating the cash pile.
