# J Kumar Infraprojects (NSE: JKIL)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore. Screener shows standalone numbers; the calls cite consolidated figures, which are nearly identical because only one associate is equity-accounted.*

## Business and segments
- J Kumar is a Mumbai-based urban infrastructure EPC contractor. Its work covers elevated corridors and flyovers, metro (elevated and underground), road tunnels, bridges, and civil and building work. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- Order book mix at 30 Jun 2026 (₹22,246 cr): elevated corridors and flyovers 48%, roads and road tunnels 20%, metro 9%, others 23%. [Q1 call]
- At 31 Mar 2026 (₹18,554 cr) the mix was: elevated corridors 51%, roads and tunnels 18%, metro 11%, others 20%. [Q4 call]
- Anchor projects:
  - Goregaon–Mulund Link Road (GMLR) twin tunnel for BMC. Two TBMs; about 4 km of tunnel segments cast; a ₹800 cr change of scope added.
  - Chennai elevated corridor: four NHAI packages worth ₹3,570 cr plus one ₹580 cr state package. About 21 km of the 25 km route runs along the Cooum river.
  - Anand Nagar–Saket elevated road.
  - Vadhavan port connectivity, where land acquisition is pending.
  - An NBCC GPRA colony project, a Lucknow convention centre, and a Karnataka stadium. [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- Management says the company has taken pan-India work for 7–10 years and now works in seven states, with Maharashtra still preferred. [Q1 call]
- Management: joint MDs Kamal Gupta and Dr Nalin Gupta; CFO Vasant Savla. Promoters hold 46.64%, with no pledge shown. [Q1 call] [Screener]
- Workforce: about 6,955 people, including 1,100+ engineers, across 40+ ongoing projects. [AR FY26]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue, Mar-25 → Jun-26 (standalone, ₹ cr): 1,633, 1,479, 1,337, 1,306, 1,573, 1,507. [Screener]
- Quarterly operating margin (OPM) held at 14–15% throughout. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 consolidated: revenue ₹1,511 cr (+2% YoY), EBITDA ₹215 cr (14.1% vs 14.6%), PAT ₹97 cr (−6%). [Q1 call]
- FY21–FY26 revenue (standalone, ₹ cr): 2,571, 3,527, 4,203, 4,879, 5,693, 5,694. FY26 was flat. [Screener]
- FY26 consolidated:
  - Revenue ₹5,723 cr (+1%), EBITDA ₹823 cr (14.4%), PAT ₹387 cr (−1%).
  - A ₹12.4 cr exceptional charge for the new Labour Codes.
  - Other income more than doubled to ₹78 cr from ₹33 cr. [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- Net profit, FY21–FY26 (₹ cr): 64, 206, 274, 329, 390, 384. [Screener]
- Market data:
  - Market cap ₹3,586 cr; price ₹474 against a 52-week range of ₹425–672.
  - P/E 9.2; price/book 1.07x; ROE 12.3%; ROCE 18.3%. The stock is down 24% over one year. [Screener]
- Dividend payout is about 8%. Buybacks are "on our cards" but not imminent. [Screener] [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY26 (from the Q1 FY26 call, as recalled by an analyst):** 15% revenue growth. Actual growth was about 1%. [Q1 call]
- **The ₹7,500 cr target:** an earlier "vision" of ₹7,500 cr revenue in FY27 has now been pushed to FY28. [Q1 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (20 May 2026):**
  - FY27 revenue growth of about 15% (more than ₹6,500 cr), with bottom-line growth to match.
  - EBITDA margin of 14–15%, aspiring to 15–16%; PAT margin of about 7%.
  - Order inflow of ₹9,000–10,000 cr; the MD said "we'll cross" ₹10,000 cr.
  - Capex of ₹200–250 cr a year for FY27 and FY28.
  - "zero impact" from geopolitics. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (7 Aug 2026):**
  - 15% revenue growth reiterated, H2-weighted.
  - EBITDA margin of 14–15%.
  - Order inflow target ₹8,000–10,000 cr. About ₹5,500 cr has been won so far, plus the ₹1,500 cr DMRC L1.
  - Capex trimmed to about ₹150 cr a year, including maintenance.
  - Working capital guided to 100–110 days. [Q1 call]
- **Order inflow history:** only about ₹2,500 cr in FY25 and ₹1,039 cr in FY26. Management calls this deliberate margin discipline. [Q4 call] [AR FY26]

## Capex and capacity
- The TBMs are about ₹300 cr, sitting in capital work in progress (CWIP) at Mar-26. They are due to be capitalised when GMLR boring starts, which was expected within days of the Q1 call. [Q1 call] [Screener]
- FY26 capitalisation was about ₹480 cr, so depreciation will rise. FY26 depreciation was ₹196 cr against ₹169 cr; the Q4 FY26 charge of ₹65 cr included write-offs of unusable machinery. Depreciation is guided at ₹50–60 cr a quarter. [Q1 call] [AR FY26]
- Q1 FY27 capex was ₹34 cr. Management expects no big capex for two years unless a special project is won. [Q1 call]
- Project timelines:
  - GMLR: the contractual date was Nov 2028, and the company is entitled to a 2-year extension. The internal target is June 2029, with Dec 2029 at the latest.
  - Chennai: about 20% done; completion extended to Dec 2028, with prolongation costs paid by NHAI.
  - Anand Nagar–Saket: about 15% done, targeted for Oct–Nov 2028. [Q1 call]
- Pipeline:
  - The management team has bid about ₹2,000 cr so far.
  - It sees ₹50,000 cr–₹1 lakh cr of Mumbai-region opportunities in the next 9–12 months: Uttan–Virar (about ₹15,000–16,000 cr for MMRDA), Shaktipeeth, metro lines 5/10/13/14, and DMRC. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt:
  - Mar-26: −₹264 cr (net cash). Gross debt/equity was 0.18 and net debt/equity −0.08.
  - Jun-26: −₹45 cr. Gross debt rose to about ₹840 cr from about ₹580 cr as the final TBM instalment was paid. Gross debt/equity is 0.24. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [AR FY26]
- Management expects gross debt below ₹800 cr by March 2027. [Q1 call]
- Working capital days:
  - FY25: 112. FY26: 99. Q1 FY27: 103.
  - At Mar-26, unbilled revenue was ₹578 cr, mobilisation advances ₹706 cr and retention ₹464 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Mobilisation advances at Jun-26: ₹470 cr, all interest-bearing at about 8.5–10%. Borrowing cost is 9–10%. Screener flags the cost of borrowing as "high". [Q1 call] [Screener]
- FY26 cash flow: operating cash flow jumped to ₹1,116 cr (from ₹375 cr) and free cash flow to ₹653 cr. Investments rose to ₹314 cr from ₹2 cr as surplus cash was parked in debt securities. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Bank lines:
  - Fund-based: ₹1,300 cr (₹530 cr term loans plus about ₹800 cr working capital), 39% utilised.
  - Non-fund-based: about ₹5,000 cr, 65% utilised. [Q1 call]
- Contingent liabilities: bank guarantees and surety bonds of ₹4,363.8 cr at Mar-26, up from ₹2,945.2 cr. Claims not acknowledged total about ₹19 cr. [AR FY26]
- Rating: ICRA A+/Positive (fund and non-fund based limits) and A1 on commercial paper. The ICRA rationale page (27 Feb 2026) could not be parsed, so the rating is taken from the AR. [AR FY26]
- Vizag land held as investment property (about ₹100–106 cr):
  - Management says it has a ₹180 cr deal for 30 acres.
  - Cash was expected in Q2–Q3 FY27, though it had been "by Q2" on the Q4 call.
  - How the land was acquired is not disclosed. On the Q4 call management mentioned a ₹106 cr loan against the asset, of which ₹90 cr has been repaid. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- It has specialist urban-infrastructure capabilities: twin-tunnel TBMs, "first road-on-road" elevated work in Chennai with special launchers, metro stations and cut-and-cover. Few mid-caps bid for such work. [Q1 call] [AR FY26]
- Its margins have held at 14–15% EBITDA across cycles, even in the flat FY26, which is above most EPC peers. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- All EPC contracts carry price-variation clauses covering steel, cement, fuel, labour and others; management calls the net impact "zero". [Q4 call]
- Payments are prompt from DMRC, MMRDA, MSRDC, BMC and NHAI. Maharashtra's payment stress is limited to irrigation and PWD, where J Kumar has little exposure. [Q1 call]
- The weaknesses are high dependence on Mumbai agencies, and repeated slippage in site handover and approvals.

## Input costs
- Price-variation formulas are index-based, so they don't track actual costs exactly. Management says the gap was "0.5% here and there" even after the post-COVID steel spike. [Q4 call]
- Labour: a 10–15% shortage at sites in April–May 2026 around the elections, which management called routine. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 was hit by BMC's restrictions on water use at construction sites and by the US–Iran war. Management called both temporary. [Q1 call]
- The Q4 call's "zero impact" line on geopolitics was contradicted in Q1, where the war was cited as a drag on growth. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Execution slippage:** the order book doubled while revenue stayed flat for two years.
  - GMLR was delayed by tree-cutting approval (Supreme Court permission came in Aug 2025) and land issues.
  - Chennai was delayed by restrictions along the Cooum river.
  - The Orange Gate project was cancelled, and VDCR is still slow.
  - Management frames this as "momentary". [Q1 call]
- **Guidance reliability:** FY26's 15% growth guidance turned into about 1%. FY27's 15% needs about 20% growth over the remaining nine months, with H2 carrying the load. [Q1 call]
- **Vadhavan:** a greenfield project with land acquisition on the first 6–7 km. Earthwork starts only after the monsoon (October). [Q1 call]
- **Rising guarantee exposure:** bank guarantees and surety bonds rose ₹1,400 cr (+48%) in a year, and non-fund lines are 65% utilised. More large wins will need more limits. [AR FY26] [Q1 call]
- **Accounting:** management says the TBM will be amortised over the project in its internal costing, while the books follow standard asset lives. So reported profit may differ from the project-level economics. [Q4 call]
- **Auditor change:** Todi Tulsyan & Co completed two terms. S P M L & Associates was appointed at the 22 Sep 2026 AGM. The FY26 audit was clean, with no emphasis of matter or qualification. [AR FY26] [Screener ANN]
- Other: a DMRC order was first announced at ₹1,770 cr, including GST, and corrected to ₹1,500 cr excluding GST, which management called a "typo error". [Q1 call]

## My read
J Kumar is a well-run, margin-disciplined specialist in urban infrastructure. It has held 14–15% EBITDA, a net-cash balance sheet and an ICRA A+/Positive rating through two years of flat revenue. FY27 order wins (about ₹7,000 cr including L1) have refilled the book to a record. The problem has been turning backlog into revenue: GMLR, Chennai and VDCR all slipped on approvals and land, and management's growth guidance has repeatedly overshot. At about 9x earnings and near book value, the market is pricing in more slippage. The GMLR TBM starting, Vadhavan beginning after the monsoon, and H2 revenue running above ₹1,800 cr a quarter are the markers to watch.
