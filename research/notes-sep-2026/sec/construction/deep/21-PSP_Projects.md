# PSP Projects (NSE: PSPPROJECT)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Consolidated figures in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.*

## Business and segments
- PSP is an Ahmedabad-based building-construction EPC contractor. It builds industrial, institutional, government, residential and religious or heritage buildings, and runs its own precast plant. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- The operational footprint is mainly Gujarat plus Mumbai. Management says it will stay in buildings only (industrial, data centre, commercial, hotel, hospital). [Q1 call]
- Ownership change: **Adani Infra (India) Ltd (AIIL) acquired 34.41%** in 2025.
  - 11.32% came through an open offer in June 2025 and 23.09% from the promoter in Q2 FY26.
  - AIIL is now a co-promoter with joint control alongside founder Prahaladbhai Patel (CMD). [AR FY26] [CARE Dec-25]
- Promoter group holding is 68.82%, up from 60.14%. FII plus DII holdings fell from about 15.8% to about 5.0%. [Screener]
- Order book at 30 Jun 2026: **₹13,245 cr** (+103% YoY).
  - Group (Adani) projects about 70%, external about 30%.
  - By segment: industrial 39%, residential 37%, government 23%, institutional about 1%.
  - About ₹3,000 cr relates to Dharavi rehabilitation (Adani). [Q1 call]
- Key external projects:
  - Ambaji Mata temple, ₹962 cr.
  - SMC (Surat Municipal) high-rise, ₹693 cr.
  - GIFT City fintech building, ₹259 cr.
  - Science City gallery, ₹248 cr.
  - Dharoi dam region, ₹198 cr.
  - Sabarmati Riverfront Phase 2, ₹187 cr. [Q1 call]
- Adani projects include SVPI Ahmedabad airport development, Mumbai T1 office, the Matunga and Mahim rehab (Dharavi), Mundra port buildings, and an Adani Healthcare project in Mumbai. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue, Mar-25 → Jun-26 (₹ cr): 673, 518, 703, 813, 1,115, 853. [Screener]
- Quarterly operating margin (OPM), same quarters: 4.8%, 4.8%, 7%, 7%, 5%, 6%. [Screener]
- Quarterly net profit, same quarters (₹ cr): 6, 0, 16, 18, 21, 18. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹853 cr (+65% YoY), EBITDA ₹55 cr (6.42%), PAT ₹18 cr (2.12%). [Q1 call]
- FY26: revenue ₹3,149 cr (+25%), EBITDA ₹189 cr (6.0% vs 7.14%), PAT ₹55 cr (−2%).
  - Q4 FY26 absorbed a provision of about ₹30 cr, related to "Kashi" according to the CMD. [Q4 call]
- Revenue and profit history, FY21–FY26 (₹ cr):
  - Revenue: 1,241, 1,748, 1,938, 2,506, 2,512, 3,149.
  - OPM: 11%, 15%, 12%, 10%, 7%, 6%.
  - PAT: 81, 167, 132, 123, 56, 56. [Screener]
- Market data:
  - Market cap ₹3,192 cr; price ₹807 against a 52-week range of ₹569–1,143.
  - **P/E 43.5**; price/book 2.5x; ROE 4.6%. No dividend in the last three years. [Screener]
- Employees rose from about 2,400 to about 2,600, and quarterly employee cost from ₹35 cr to ₹46 cr (5.39% of sales). About 16,000 labourers are deployed on sites. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Earlier guidance (analyst recap):** FY27 revenue of ₹4,500–5,000 cr, then 20–25% annual growth to reach ₹9,000–10,000 cr by FY30. [Q4 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (30 Apr 2026):**
  - FY27 revenue of ₹4,500 cr.
  - EBITDA margin "7% to 8%".
  - Order inflow of at least ₹5,000–6,000 cr a year.
  - Management said that excluding the ₹30 cr provision, Q4 EBITDA would have been about 8%. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (30 Jul 2026):**
  - Revenue of ₹4,400–4,500 cr, or "more than 25%" growth.
  - EBITDA of 7–8% for the full year, but realistically reached only from H2 (Q1 was 6.4%).
  - Order inflow about ₹4,000–5,000 cr, "plus-minus INR400-500 crores" (the analyst had cited about ₹6,000 cr, of which about ₹5,000 cr from Adani).
  - Capex 3–4% of revenue.
  - Net debt-free within 2–3 quarters. [Q1 call]
- **Track record:** EBITDA fell from 10.66% in FY24 to 7.43% in FY25 (CARE basis) and 6% in FY26. This was blamed on out-of-scope work on UP's Bhavan Prakoshtha project and working-capital drag. [CARE Dec-25] [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Q1 FY27 capex was ₹28 cr. Gross block is ₹793 cr and net block ₹470 cr at Jun-26. Depreciation rose from ₹17 cr to ₹26 cr YoY on asset additions. [Q1 call]
- The precast facility supports speed: for example, "Project 90" (3 basements plus G+18) was erected in 148 days. [Q4 call]
- Management is building the team to take on 1.5–2x the previous year's work. It expects to add 3,000–4,000 more labourers in the next 1–2 quarters. [Q1 call]
- Order inflow:
  - FY26: ₹10,925 cr, a record, with 85% from Adani.
  - Q1 FY27: ₹630 cr, with 93% from Adani.
  - Bid pipeline: ₹6,200 cr+ (61% group). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Standalone at 30 Jun 2026:
  - Long-term borrowing ₹38 cr; short-term ₹217 cr.
  - Fixed deposits ₹324 cr, of which ₹139 cr is lien-free.
  - Mobilisation advance **₹836 cr, all interest-free**.
  - Trade receivables ₹745 cr (₹928 cr at Mar-26); unbilled ₹473 cr; retention ₹222 cr; inventories ₹362 cr. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Bank limits: ₹1,497 cr sanctioned, with ₹678 cr of non-fund and ₹166 cr of fund-based lines utilised. [Q1 call]
- FY26 operating cash flow was ₹323 cr, a turnaround from −₹224 cr in FY24, helped by the Adani advances. [Screener]
- **Rating:** CARE downgraded the short-term rating to CARE A1 from A1+ on 22 Dec 2025, citing weaker liquidity: gross current asset days rose from 237 to 278 and bank borrowing increased. The long-term rating of CARE A+/Stable was reaffirmed. [CARE Dec-25]
- Contingent liabilities at Mar-26: bank guarantees ₹735.4 cr (down from ₹896.4 cr) and tax disputes ₹16.1 cr. [AR FY26]
- Legacy receivables:
  - Surat Diamond Bourse: about ₹90 cr outstanding. The CMD had been "called ... for some discussion" with no resolution.
  - UP medical projects: ₹60 cr unbilled plus ₹40 cr receivable. Extension-of-time approvals were described as near signing, with the account expected to close by August–September 2026. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- A captive order pipeline from the Adani group: 70% of the order book, pass-through cost structures, and interest-free advances without bank guarantees (per CARE). [CARE Dec-25] [Q1 call]
- PSP has a right of first refusal on Dharavi rehabilitation phases. That programme involves about 2 lakh houses over about 5–6 years. [Q1 call]
- Its execution record includes the Kashi Vishwanath corridor, Surat Diamond Bourse and Vishwa Umiya Dham (a record 24,000 m³ continuous pour). It has precast capability. [AR FY26] [Q4 call]
- But margins have halved since FY22 to about 6% EBITDA. Group work appears to be priced as a pass-through rather than at premium margins (inference). [Screener]

## Input costs
- Adani projects are "passthrough cost", so management sees little impact from commodity moves. [Q1 call]
- Aluminium and copper price rises affected some activities on the SMC and RVNL (external) projects. [Q1 call]
- Labour shortages in April–May cut revenue in those months to about ₹270 cr each, against ₹319 cr in June. This inflated employee cost as a share of sales by about 1 point. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party concentration:** about 70% of the order book and about 45% of Q1 revenue come from the new co-promoter group. Pricing and terms are related-party transactions. Management targets a long-run 70–75% share of Adani work. [Q1 call]
- **Margin compression despite growth:** OPM fell from 15% (FY22) to 6% (FY26). The 7–8% target has slipped from full-year to H2. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- **Valuation:** a P/E of about 43x on ₹73 cr of trailing PAT prices in Adani-driven growth. [Screener]
- **Legacy disputes:** Surat Diamond Bourse (₹90 cr), UP government medical projects (₹100 cr) and the ₹30 cr "Kashi" provision. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Liquidity:** the short-term rating downgrade to A1 (Dec 2025). Receivable days rose to 108 in FY26 (Screener). [CARE Dec-25] [Screener]
- **Governance:** joint control between the founder family and AIIL. The founder sold 23.09% to AIIL. Institutional investors largely exited. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- A senior MEP general manager resigned in September 2026 (minor). [Screener ANN]
- The FY26 audit was clean, with no emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Revenue guidance: from ₹4,500 cr to ₹4,400–4,500 cr, essentially unchanged. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Margin: the 7–8% guidance now explicitly depends on H2; Q1 was 6.42%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Order inflow expectations: eased from ₹5,000–6,000 cr towards about ₹4,000–5,000 cr, with only ₹630 cr booked in Q1. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Balance sheet: finance cost fell and short-term borrowing dropped from ₹274 cr to ₹217 cr. The net debt-free target is now 2–3 quarters away. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Group share: Adani projects were 67% of the order book at March and about 70% at June. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## My read
PSP has turned into the Adani group's in-house building contractor. The order book doubled to ₹13,245 cr, and interest-free advances have repaired a liquidity profile that had cost it its A1+ rating. Revenue is growing quickly (+65% in Q1), but margins are about 6%, less than half of FY22. The market is paying about 43x earnings, betting that Dharavi and group volumes will restore 8%+ margins. The main risks are related-party concentration and margin dilution. I'd want to see H2 FY27 margins actually reach 7–8% and the legacy receivables (Surat Diamond Bourse, UP) collected before accepting that valuation.
