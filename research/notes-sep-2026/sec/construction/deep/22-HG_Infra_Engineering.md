# H.G. Infra Engineering (NSE: HGINFRA)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore. "SA" means standalone EPC; "consol" includes HAM, solar, BESS and transmission SPVs.*

## Business and segments
- HG Infra is a Jaipur-based highway EPC and HAM developer. It is diversifying into railways and metro, solar (KUSUM projects in Rajasthan), battery storage (BESS, with GUVNL and NVVN) and transmission (Odisha, plus two RECPDCL projects). It works in 14+ states. [Q1 call]
- Order book at 30 Jun 2026: **₹14,502 cr**.
  - Roads ₹9,386 cr (65%); rail and metro ₹3,054 cr (21%).
  - Transmission ₹1,457 cr, BESS ₹461 cr and solar ₹144 cr (together 14%). [Q1 call]
- Key new wins:
  - Pune–Shirur elevated road, an EPC subcontract from Welspun, ₹3,931 cr.
  - Gobindpur–Tangi (Odisha capital ring road) HAM.
  - Two rail sidings for Adani thermal plants (Anuppur ₹340 cr, Mirzapur ₹440 cr).
  - RECPDCL transmission projects (Mirzapur ₹320 cr EPC, Jamshedpur ₹843 cr EPC). [Q1 call]
- Asset base:
  - 11 HAM projects.
  - 3 BESS projects (735 MW / 1,470 MWh). DC blocks are ordered from CATL, with commissioning targeted for Feb/Mar/Jun 2027.
  - Solar: 137 KUSUM plants commissioned; total project about 94% complete. [Q1 call]
- Monetisation: five HAMs are being sold to Neo Infra Income Opportunities Fund for ₹1,384 cr. About ₹659–660 cr has been received and about ₹725–729 cr is due in FY27. [CARE Sep-26] [Q1 call]
- Promoters hold 71.78%. FII plus DII holdings fell from 14.9% to 11.5%. [Screener]

## Key numbers
- Consolidated quarterly revenue, Mar-25 → Jun-26 (₹ cr): 1,361, 1,482, 904, 1,421, 1,427, 1,101. [Screener]
- Consolidated quarterly net profit, same quarters (₹ cr): 147, 99, 52, 94, 85, **−45**. [Screener]
  - The Q1 FY27 loss came after an exceptional charge: accrued HAM financial income was discounted on sale of the Raipur–Visakhapatnam OD5/OD6 SPVs, plus an impairment of about ₹20–25 cr on AP-1. [Q1 call] [Screener]
- Standalone Q1 FY27: revenue ₹907 cr, EBITDA ₹77 cr (**8.49%**), PAT ₹28 cr (against ₹125 cr a year earlier). [Q1 call]
- Standalone FY26: revenue ₹5,667 cr, EBITDA ₹733 cr (12.94%), PAT ₹389 cr (FY25: ₹577 cr). Q4 FY26 EBITDA was 9.37%. [Q4 call]
- CARE puts the PBILDT margin at 14.49% in 9M FY26, 9.84% in Q4 FY26 and 8.78% in Q1 FY27. [CARE Sep-26]
- Consolidated FY22–FY26 (₹ cr):
  - Revenue: 3,751, 4,622, 5,378, 5,056, 5,235.
  - PAT: 380, 493, 539, 505, 330.
  - Interest: 118, 154, 217, 265, 469. [Screener]
- Market data:
  - Market cap ₹2,943 cr; price ₹453 against a 52-week range of ₹430–976, down 53% in one year.
  - Price/book 0.9x. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **FY26 miss:** the year started with ₹10,000 cr of expected order inflow, but "we could secure only new orders of INR1,300 crores". [Q4 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (29 May 2026):**
  - FY27 revenue of "INR6,500 crores plus"; the CMD said "Yes" when asked to confirm ₹7,000 cr.
  - Order inflow ₹11,000–12,000 cr (about 70% roads and rail).
  - The margin could not be "guarantee[d] ... accurately". [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (14 Aug 2026):**
  - FY27 revenue ₹6,000–6,500 cr, "at least" matching FY25's ₹6,100 cr. Q2 is expected at about ₹1,000 cr, which implies about ₹4,000 cr in H2.
  - FY28 revenue about ₹7,000 cr.
  - FY27 EBITDA 13.5–14%, with H2 at about 15%.
  - Order inflow ₹11,000–12,000 cr, with about ₹5,500 cr already received.
  - Standalone external debt targeted to fall to ₹900 cr by year-end, from ₹1,834 cr. [Q1 call]
- An analyst on the Q1 call said it "seems very, very difficult even if we reach INR5,000 crores, INR5,200 crores". [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Equity commitments across HAM, BESS and transmission:
  - Total ₹3,019 cr, of which ₹1,665 cr has been infused.
  - Remaining: ₹583 cr in the rest of FY27 (HAM ₹309 cr, BESS and transmission ₹275 cr), ₹625 cr in FY28 and ₹146 cr in FY29. [Q1 call]
- CARE cites ₹785 cr of equity commitments in FY27. [CARE Sep-26]
- Expected annuity-like SPV revenue:
  - Solar: ₹250 cr+ a year.
  - BESS: about ₹225 cr a year.
  - Three transmission projects: about ₹215 cr a year over a 35-year concession. [Q1 call]
- Solar: about ₹300 cr+ of project debt is to be released only after commissioning, and the MNRE deadline for KUSUM in Rajasthan was extended to 31 Mar 2027. Transmission-line costs rose and some plants had to be relocated, which hit margins. [Q1 call]
- Appointed dates for Pune–Shirur and the Odisha HAM are expected in October 2026. The DLF project is only 3% done because of land constraints. The Thane metro project is facing land and local disruption. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Standalone gross debt:
  - Mar-26: ₹1,627 cr.
  - Jun-26: ₹1,834 cr, made up of ₹910 cr working capital, ₹400 cr NCDs and ₹524 cr term loans. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Consolidated borrowings rose from ₹1,513 cr (FY24) to ₹5,029 cr (FY26) as SPV capex was debt-funded. Consolidated interest cost rose from ₹217 cr to ₹469 cr over the same period. [Screener]
- Free cash flow was negative for five straight years; FY25 was −₹2,415 cr and FY26 −₹702 cr. [Screener]
- Gross current asset days rose from 218 (FY25) to 277 (FY26). CARE attributes this to:
  - HAM unbilled revenue;
  - pending change-of-scope payments on large EPC projects;
  - receivables from a solar EPC client whose debt tie-up is pending. [CARE Sep-26]
- CARE reaffirmed CARE AA on 3 Sep 2026 but **revised the outlook to Negative from Stable**. [CARE Sep-26]
  - Downgrade triggers: total debt/PBILDT above 2x, or gross current asset days not falling below 220.
  - CARE also cites two large MSRTC EPC projects in Maharashtra that were cancelled.
- ICRA rationales dated 28 Apr and 21 Aug 2026 are listed on Screener but were not read, because ICRA pages load via JavaScript. [Screener]
- Contingent liabilities (standalone, Mar-26):
  - Claims ₹64.1 cr, direct tax ₹10.0 cr, indirect tax ₹41.8 cr.
  - Corporate guarantees for SPVs, including ₹452 cr sanctioned for AP-1. [AR FY26]
- A new Karnataka GST show-cause notice (FY2022-23 audit) for ₹94.0 cr covers input tax credit and turnover reconciliation. The company calls it "not sustainable". [BSE 16-Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- HG built its franchise on fast, on-budget highway execution with a large owned fleet, and has a record of early-completion bonuses. [Q1 call]
- The moat has weakened: roads face "intensifying competition and lower margins, as always". The CMD admits "1 or 2 projects where the margins are not that encouraging". [Q1 call]
- CARE says its ability to deliver solar and BESS projects on time "remains to be established". [CARE Sep-26]

## Input costs
- Q1 FY27 disruption: bitumen and diesel (HSD) supply disrupted; shortages of metal commodities and long-lead items for BESS and transmission. [Q1 call]
- Q4 FY26: higher costs of metals, diesel, bitumen, cement and logistics from the West Asia crisis. [Q4 call]
- BESS exposure: batteries sourced from CATL, which carries forex and technology-obsolescence risk. [Q1 call] [CARE Sep-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **CBI/ACB Patna case, under auditor emphasis of matter:**
  - On 21 Jan 2026, four employees were taken into custody under the Prevention of Corruption Act (sections 7, 7A, 8 and 9), relating to railway projects.
  - Offices and the CMD's residence were searched. ₹0.093 cr was seized from the CMD's home (personal funds) and ₹0.077 cr from an employee.
  - The employees are on bail and no chargesheet has been filed. An external firm reviewed the matter at the audit committee's request. [AR FY26] [CARE Sep-26]
- **Profit collapse:** standalone margins fell from about 14.5% (9M FY26) to about 8.5% (Q1 FY27), and Q1 FY27 had a consolidated loss. [CARE Sep-26] [Q1 call]
- **Leverage:** consolidated debt of about ₹5,000 cr against equity of about ₹3,265 cr. Deleveraging depends on the ₹725 cr monetisation balance, the Karnal HAM sale, solar debt release and change-of-scope collections, all of which have slipped before. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- **Guidance credibility:** FY26 order inflow was ₹1,300 cr against ₹10,000 cr targeted. FY27 now needs an H2 of about ₹4,000 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Order-book quality:** the largest new order is a subcontract from another developer (Welspun). The DLF, Thane metro and New Delhi station projects are stuck on land and approvals. [Q1 call]
- The ₹94 cr GST notice adds to the tax overhang. [BSE 16-Sep-26]

## My read
HG was one of the best-executing road EPC companies (about 20% ROCE and double-digit PAT margins as recently as FY24). Since then it has taken on heavy SPV investment (HAM, solar, BESS, transmission) just as road awards dried up and margins compressed. The result is consolidated debt of about ₹5,000 cr, standalone EPC margins of about 8.5%, a Q1 loss, a CARE Negative outlook, and a CBI corruption probe under an auditor's emphasis of matter. Management's H2 guidance (about ₹4,000 cr of revenue at about 15% margin, and debt halved) looks very ambitious. At 0.9x book, the stock partly reflects this. I'd treat any recovery as contingent on monetisation cash actually arriving and the CBI matter staying contained.
