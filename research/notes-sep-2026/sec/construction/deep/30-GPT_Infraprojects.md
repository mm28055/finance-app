# GPT Infraprojects (NSE: GPTINFRA)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Consolidated figures in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.*

## Business and segments
- GPT is a Kolkata-based civil EPC contractor focused on railway and road bridges, rail infrastructure, flyovers and roads, mainly in Eastern and Northern India. It also makes **concrete railway sleepers** in India and in Africa (South Africa, Namibia and Ghana). [Screener] [CRISIL Mar-26] [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 segment revenue: infrastructure ₹283 cr and sleepers ₹19 cr. [Q1 call]
- Order book about ₹4,303 cr at the Q1 call (₹4,476 cr at end-FY26, about 3.5x revenue). [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Clients include RVNL, NHAI, NHIDCL and Indian Railways. Key projects include:
  - NHAI Ganga Bridge in Prayagraj.
  - Rupnarayan Bridge in Kolaghat for South Eastern Railway.
  - Kona Expressway (NHAI).
  - Byculla Bridge in Mumbai, near completion. [Q1 call] [CRISIL Mar-26]
- **Acquisition of Alcon Builders & Engineers:** a Delhi-based railway signalling EPC company (electronic interlocking, Kavach-type work), bought for ₹151.83 cr in cash. It has been a wholly owned subsidiary since 1 Jan 2026.
  - Accounting recognised customer-relationship intangibles of ₹87.5 cr and goodwill of ₹30.2 cr.
  - A scheme of amalgamation of Alcon and Jogbani Highway into GPT is under NCLT process; the NCLT-convened shareholder and creditor meetings were ordered on 16 Sep 2026. [Q4 call] [AR FY26] [BSE Sep-26]
- New verticals: power EPC (first order of about ₹53 cr for a Power Grid substation in Kurnool) and a 51% HAM SPV in Rajasthan (GPT ISC JU Highway). [Q1 call] [CRISIL Mar-26]
- Recent orders:
  - ₹483.72 cr RVNL railway bridge (17 Sep 2026).
  - ₹85.53 cr Eastern Railway signalling contract through Alcon (12 Sep).
  - An L1 position of ₹21.21 cr through Alcon (22 Sep). [Screener ANN]
- **Promoters hold 69.37%, and 50.8% of that holding is pledged.** [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **FY26:** order inflow guided at ₹2,000 cr and delivered at ₹2,422 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (21 May 2026):** EBITDA margin of 13%+ as the long-term hurdle rate. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (3 Aug 2026):**
  - FY27 revenue growth of about 30%, i.e. about ₹1,700 cr. This needs about ₹1,400 cr in Q2–Q4 on a Q1 base of ₹302 cr.
  - FY27 EBITDA 14–15%, with a long-term target of 13–14%.
  - Order inflow ₹3,000 cr+ (only about ₹130 cr won in Q1).
  - Power EPC revenue of ₹150–200 cr a year in about two years.
  - No increase in debt; debt to fall during the year. [Q1 call]
- **Credit rating:** CRISIL reaffirmed A/Stable and A1 on 25 Mar 2026 (₹490 cr of lines). A credit bulletin followed on 21 May 2026. [CRISIL Mar-26] [CRISIL May-26]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 investing outflow was ₹170 cr, mainly the Alcon purchase plus Ghana sleeper plant capex. Fixed assets rose from ₹164 cr to ₹332 cr, including acquired intangibles. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- The Ghana sleeper factory started in FY26 and lifted consolidated margins. Management expects more African orders over the "next couple of quarters". [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Alcon gives access to a market management describes as a "USD1.5 billion" signalling opportunity, citing about ₹1 trillion of planned railway outlay over six years on Kavach, interlocking and related work. [Q1 call]
- Management expects to bid for large EPC packages of ₹1,500–2,000 cr, each running 3–4 years. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings rose from ₹129 cr to ₹294 cr in FY26, funding the Alcon acquisition and working capital. The CFO says there will be no further increase. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- FY26 operating cash flow was ₹64 cr and free cash flow ₹10 cr. Working capital days are 81 and debtor days 36. [Screener]
- Contract assets were about ₹430 cr at March 2026, up about 5% in Q1. About ₹200 cr has since been billed and collected. [Q1 call]
- CRISIL notes "large working capital requirement and sizeable investment commitment towards the HAM Project". [CRISIL Mar-26]
- The FY26 audit had no emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]

## Moat evidence
- It has more than four decades of railway relationships (sleepers plus bridges) and an established Eastern India presence. [CRISIL Mar-26] [Q1 call]
- Alcon adds a signalling credential with high entry barriers, and the merger is meant to let GPT's balance sheet qualify it for larger contracts. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Margins have steadily improved: standalone EBITDA rose from 12.3% to 13.3% in FY26, and consolidated EBITDA was 15.7% in Q1 FY27. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- It is regionally concentrated in West Bengal and the East, where it is exposed to state events such as elections. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Cost inputs are steel, cement and labour. No specific commodity margin issues were flagged in Q1; the main disruption was election-related labour shortages. [Q1 call]
- African sleeper operations add forex and country risk. [CRISIL Mar-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter pledge of 50.8%** of holdings, and promoter holding down 5.6 points over three years. [Screener]
- **Back-ended guidance:** 30% FY27 growth requires about 55% YoY growth in the rest of the year after a flat Q1. Order inflow of ₹130 cr in Q1 against a ₹3,000 cr target. [Q1 call]
- **Acquisition integration:** Alcon adds amortisation (about ₹3 cr a quarter) and goodwill. The merger is still pending NCLT approval. [Q1 call] [AR FY26] [BSE Sep-26]
- **HAM exposure:** a 51% stake in a Rajasthan HAM SPV with equity commitments. [CRISIL Mar-26]
- **Regional and political risk:** the West Bengal concentration showed up in the April–May 2026 slowdown. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Order book: from ₹4,476 cr to ₹4,303 cr, with Q1 inflow of about ₹130 cr. Large orders arrived after the quarter (RVNL ₹484 cr, Alcon ₹86 cr). [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [Screener ANN]
- Margin guidance: raised for FY27 to 14–15% (long-term 13–14%), helped by signalling and Africa. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Integration: the merger scheme advanced to NCLT-convened meetings in September. [BSE Sep-26]
- New segment: entry into power EPC. [Q1 call]

## My read
GPT is a small, steady railway and bridge EPC company with rising margins, a 20%+ ROCE, CRISIL A and a generous dividend. It is buying its way into higher-margin railway signalling (Alcon) and growing an African sleeper business. The weak points are a 50.8% promoter pledge, a heavily back-ended FY27 target (₹1,400 cr needed in nine months after a flat Q1), and a still-thin Q1 order intake, although September's RVNL and Alcon wins help. At about 15x earnings, delivery of the H2 ramp-up and a clean Alcon merger are the checkpoints.
