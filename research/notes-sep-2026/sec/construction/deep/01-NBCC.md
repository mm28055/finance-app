# NBCC (India) Ltd (NBCC, ISIN INE095N01031)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Figures are consolidated (Screener) unless marked. ₹ cr = rupees crore.

**Document note:** NBCC held no Q4 FY26 earnings conference call. Instead it held an in-person investor meet in Mumbai on 1–2 June 2026; only the presentation and a video link were filed, with no transcript. This file therefore uses the Q1 FY27 call transcript (12 Aug 2026), the Q3 FY26 call transcript (16 Feb 2026) for earlier guidance, and the June 2026 investor-meet presentation in place of Q4 call material.

## Business and segments
- NBCC is a Navratna central PSU under the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA). It is mainly a **project management consultant (PMC)**: government clients hand it building projects, often on nomination, and it passes the construction to third-party contractors back-to-back, earning a fee. It also has small EPC and real-estate arms. [AR FY26] [CRISIL Dec-25]
- FY26 segment revenue (consolidated): PMC ₹12,375 cr (96%), EPC ₹382 cr (vs ₹736 cr in FY25), real estate ₹100 cr (vs ₹259 cr), unallocated ₹31 cr. Standalone PMC revenue was ₹9,393 cr. [AR FY26]
- "Redevelopment" PMC is the growth engine. NBCC redevelops old government colonies and land on a self-financing basis: part of the new built-up area or land is sold, and the proceeds pay for construction. NBCC earns a PMC fee and a marketing fee. Examples: 7 GPRA colonies in Delhi (World Trade Centre at Nauroji Nagar, Sarojini Nagar, Netaji Nagar etc.), East Kidwai Nagar, and Moti Bagh. [IP Jun-26]
- It completes stalled private housing under Supreme Court orders. Amrapali: >32,550 homes completed, Phase 2 FAR work of ₹12,500 cr, 8% PMC fee plus 1% marketing fee. Supertech: 16 projects in Noida and Greater Noida, not yet awarded. [IP Jun-26] [Q1 call]
- Subsidiaries: HSCC (100%, healthcare consultancy), HSCL (51%, Kolkata construction), NBCC Services (100%, maintenance), overseas LLCs in Dubai and REDCCOR (a joint venture with Rajasthan). In July 2026 the board approved merging HSCC into NBCC, with an appointed date of 1 Apr 2026. [AR FY26] [News: Indian Masterminds, Jul-26]
- Geography: almost all revenue is from India. The Maldives contributed ₹116 cr of FY26 revenue (₹386 cr in FY25). New work in Seychelles (~₹620 cr housing), a branch in Australia (Sep 2026) and a 66-unit housing plot in Dubai. [AR FY26] [Q1 call] [BSE filing, 14 Sep 2026]
- Order book (consolidated) was ₹1,26,964 cr at 30 Jun 2026: NBCC ₹1,12,305 cr, HSCC ₹6,679 cr, HSCL ₹7,639 cr, NSL ₹341 cr. Only ₹33,241 cr of this is "running". ₹93,723 cr is "yet to award/start" — about 74%. The standalone split is ~60% redevelopment and ~40% PMC. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]

- **FY26 order inflow:** "minimum ₹20,000 cr" (Feb 2026). Actual: ₹18,780 cr. **Missed narrowly.** [Q3 call] [IP Jun-26]
- **FY27 revenue:** Feb 2026 said FY27 "should end at about ₹18,000 cr… conservative". Q1 FY27 guided ₹15,000–17,000 cr ("precisely ₹16,000–17,000 cr"), with standalone at ₹13,000–14,000 cr. **Cut.** Q1 revenue of ₹2,260 cr means the remaining three quarters must average about ₹4,600 cr. [Q3 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 PAT:** Feb 2026 guided ₹1,000–1,200 cr. Q1 guided ₹1,100–1,200 cr consolidated, and the CMD said he has a "backup plan". Q1 PAT of ₹158 cr annualises to ~₹630 cr. **Kept, but back-end loaded.** [Q3 call] [Q1 call]
- **Longer term:** FY28 revenue ~₹21,000 cr with PAT ₹1,300–1,400 cr; FY29 revenue ₹24,000–25,000 cr with PAT ~₹2,000 cr, relying on the Gurugram 37-D (₹2,300 cr revenue / ₹1,200 cr profit claimed in Feb) and Ghitorni real-estate projects. **New numbers on the Q1 call.** [Q1 call] [Q3 call]
- **Margins:** Q1 guided EBITDA of at least 6.5–7% and PAT margin of 6–6.5%, versus 5–6% EBITDA guided in Feb. **Raised.** [Q1 call] [Q3 call]
- **FY27 order inflow:** ₹50,000–60,000 cr, including ~₹30,000 cr from the 5 GPRA colonies (cleared by PIB, awaiting Cabinet) plus 2–3 large state orders. The ~₹2 lakh cr year-end order book mentioned earlier was not repeated. [Q1 call]
- **Awarding to contractors:** the investor day talked of ₹30,000–32,000 cr in FY27. Q1 said ₹20,000–25,000 cr, including Supertech ~₹10,000 cr, J&K ₹3,500 cr and Rajasthan Mandapam ₹3,500 cr. **Cut.** [Q1 call]
- **Delayed large projects:** MAHAPREIT (~₹25,000 cr), J&K satellite city (~₹15,000 cr) and Supertech (~₹10,000 cr), about ₹50,000 cr in total. In Feb all three were said to "start next year". At Q1, J&K had moved and Supertech was cleared by the Supreme Court, but MAHAPREIT was "still stuck up with approvals" and has been pushed to Q3. [Q3 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is small by design (asset-light model). Fixed assets rose to ₹614 cr (Mar-26) from ₹185 cr (Mar-24) after NBCC bought a tower in Bharat Business Park, Sarojini Nagar, at the base price of ₹39,100/sq ft. It pays on a construction-linked plan over about two years (10% paid so far). The CMD said later auctions fetched ₹50,000–66,000/sq ft. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Real estate: Gurugram 37-D rebuild (contract ₹821 cr awarded, launch expected Q2/Q3 FY27, RERA registration pending), Ghitorni 21-acre land (lease agreement in process), a Jaipur property and a 66-unit Dubai project. Revenue from these is recognised on handover, so mostly after about two years. [IP Jun-26] [Q1 call]
- CPSE REIT: the board has approved in principle an SPV holding ~75,000 sq ft of NBCC's rentable assets, as sponsor of a proposed national CPSE REIT (Budget 2026). No timeline. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free: borrowings are nil. Consolidated cash and bank balances were ~₹4,866 cr (Sep-25), but only ~₹220 cr of that was unencumbered; most is client money. On the Q1 call the finance director gave "cash" of ₹666 cr at 30 Jun 2026. [CRISIL Dec-25] [Q1 call] [Screener]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 80 → -374 → -6 → 661 → 439; free cash flow 74 → -385 → -50 → 342 → 402. [Screener]
- Debtor days rose to 144 (FY26) from 93 (FY25). Group trade receivables were ₹5,092 cr at Mar-26, flagged by the auditor as a key audit matter. Working-capital days are negative (-119) because client advances fund the work. [Screener] [AR FY26]
- Dividends: ₹1.00/share for FY26 (payout 38%); interim of ₹0.15 for FY27 declared with Q1. No buyback. [Screener] [IP Q1FY27]
- Inventory (land bank and real-estate work-in-progress) was ₹1,194 cr at Mar-26. [AR FY26]

## Moat evidence
- Government status: it receives work on nomination from central ministries and state governments. It is the designated agency for Delhi GPRA redevelopment and the Supreme Court's choice for stalled housing (Amrapali, then Supertech). The PSU scorecard rated it "Excellent" with a score of 97.5. [Q1 call] [CRISIL Dec-25]
- Proven ability to monetise redevelopment land: WTC Nauroji Nagar ₹13,884 cr, 100% sold; Bharat Business Park ~₹10,000 cr sold in three auctions; Amrapali FAR units in FY26: 4,625 units worth ~₹9,366 cr. [IP Jun-26] [Q1 call]
- Weak points: PMC fees on government-funded projects fell to 5–8% from 7–10% after a cost-efficiency drive. HSCC and HSCL quoted about 2% fees against 3–4% overheads, so loss-making mandates are being dropped (e.g. a Maharashtra health project; ~₹300 cr of lost revenue). [CRISIL Dec-25] [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- NBCC does not carry commodity risk directly. Cost and execution risk is passed to subcontractors through back-to-back contracts and bank guarantees. Its main cost is staff overhead against fee income. [CRISIL Dec-25]
- On real-estate projects (37-D, Ghitorni, Dubai) NBCC does carry construction-cost and price risk. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Execution/conversion:** 74% of the order book has not started. Delays at MAHAPREIT, J&K and Supertech come from state approvals and a shortage of "seed money". NBCC has signed an MoU with HUDCO for seed funding backed by state guarantees. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- **Seasonal Delhi disruption:** GRAP pollution curbs halt construction around Nov–Jan, putting the back-ended FY27 guidance at risk. [Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities (standalone, Mar-26):** claims not acknowledged as debt ₹1,670 cr (up from ₹1,522 cr); GST demands ₹219 cr; VAT ₹58 cr. A DVAT demand of ₹405 cr has been remanded and is "not ascertainable" (payable by the client if confirmed). [AR FY26]
- **Auditor emphasis of matter (not qualifications):**
  - Gurugram 37-D structural-crack project: cumulative provisions of ₹469 cr, a ₹750 cr recovery suit and 16 other cases;
  - Faridabad land ₹132 cr without a conveyance deed;
  - Kochi project ₹87 cr unsold, pending environmental clearance;
  - NBCC Plaza demand ₹32 cr;
  - Naya Raipur land ₹21 cr;
  - at HSCC: unconfirmed and unreconciled balances, and revenue on the "Test to Treat Arogya Yojna" project not recognised after irregularities by a subcontractor;
  - at HSCL: receivable and payable ageing prepared manually.
  
  Key audit matters were revenue recognition, receivables (₹5,092 cr), inventory valuation and provisions. [AR FY26]
- **Governance:** the stock exchanges levied penalties for board-composition non-compliance (independent directors not yet appointed by the government); a waiver has been sought. [AR FY26]
- **Related parties:** related-party dealings are with subsidiaries and government companies, which are exempt under SEBI LODR 23(5). Nothing unusual found. [AR FY26]
- **Pledges and promoter:** Government of India holds 61.75%, steady, with no pledge. FIIs 4.77%, DIIs 11.28%. [Screener]
- **Disclosure:** management guidance is given verbally and has a record of slipping (FY26 revenue and inflow both missed). Cash disclosures mix restricted client funds with own funds. [Q3 call] [Q1 call]
- Loss-making subsidiaries: only minor. CRISIL notes NBCC has fully provided for losses at the NBCC-RK Millen and HSCL-SIPL JVs. [CRISIL Dec-25]

## What changed between the two calls
(Comparing the Q3 FY26 call in Feb 2026 with the Q1 FY27 call, as there was no Q4 call.)
- The FY27 revenue target fell from "~₹18,000 cr conservative" to ₹15,000–17,000 cr. The PAT target held at ₹1,100–1,200 cr and the EBITDA-margin target rose to 6.5–7%.
- Monetisation moved faster than expected: Bharat Business Park sold out (~₹10,000 cr), and the Africa Avenue (262 units, ~2,900 applicants), Vinayak Mandir Marg and Scindia Marg sales were lined up for Aug–Oct.
- New items: the HSCC merger, the CPSE REIT SPV, the HUDCO seed-money MoU, and Supertech cleared by the Supreme Court. MAHAPREIT slipped again.
- Analyst tone turned sceptical about converting orders into revenue. Several questioners pressed on repeated delays.

## My read
The standalone business is improving in quality: redevelopment and marketing fees are lifting margins (standalone EBITDA 8.8% in Q1), the balance sheet is debt-free and ROCE is ~30%. But reported growth is not keeping pace with the ₹1.27 lakh cr order book. Consolidated revenue fell in Q1, three-quarters of the book has not started, and management has missed its own revenue and inflow targets for FY26 and has now cut FY27. The PAT goal of ₹1,100–1,200 cr needs about ₹950 cr in the remaining three quarters, against ₹158 cr in Q1. The view would improve with Cabinet approval of the 5 GPRA colonies, awarding of the Supertech and J&K packages, and quarterly revenue above ₹4,000 cr in Q2–Q3. It would weaken if FY27 PAT stays near the ₹750 cr run-rate or the 37-D launch slips again.
