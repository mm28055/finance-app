# Solarworld Energy Solutions (NSE: SOLARWORLD)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Consolidated figures in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise. No Q1 FY27 earnings call was found on BSE or the company website (only the Q1 investor presentation and press release). The Q1 section therefore uses the presentation, tagged [IP Q1FY27].*

## Business and segments
- Solarworld is a Noida and New Delhi-based solar EPC contractor, mostly building utility-scale ground-mounted plants for PSUs under the CAPEX model, with some RESCO projects. It is expanding into battery storage (BESS) EPC and manufacturing, TOPCon module manufacturing, and junction boxes. [IP Q1FY27] [CRISIL Feb-26]
- Portfolio:
  - 47 projects completed (264 MW AC / 348 MW DC) and 13 ongoing (1,405 MW AC / 1,809 MW DC of EPC).
  - Plus 582 MW / 1,184 MWh of ongoing BESS projects. [IP Q1FY27]
- Order book at 30 Jun 2026: **₹2,727.5 cr**, made up of solar EPC and O&M ₹1,588.6 cr and BESS EPC and IPP ₹1,138.9 cr. [IP Q1FY27]
- Clients are almost all PSUs: NTPC REL, SJVN Green, NTPC, RRVUNL, GUVNL, plus one "leading renewable energy company". [IP Q1FY27]
- Manufacturing:
  - A 1.552 GW TOPCon module line in Roorkee, commissioned July 2025, which received ALMM approval only in December 2025.
  - A 3.4 GWh BESS line, ready for launch.
  - A 5 GW junction-box JV, now producing.
  - A 1.2 GW cell line targeted for June 2027, now being redirected (see below). [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call]
- **Change in IPO objects (7 Sep 2026):** the board approved moving IPO proceeds away from the company's own 1.2 GW TOPCon cell plant at Pandhurana (through subsidiary KSPL). The money would instead go into a 50:50 JV, Rays Green Energy Manufacturing, with Rays Power Infra, for a 2.4 GW cell plant at Mohasa (MP). The change needs shareholder approval at the AGM. [BSE 7-Sep-26] [BSE 9-Sep-26]
- IPO: September 2025 at ₹351 a share, oversubscribed 68.5x. Net proceeds were ₹521.7 cr (fresh plus pre-IPO). Promoters hold 65.77%. [IP Q1FY27] [Screener]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue, Mar-25 → Jun-26 (₹ cr): 176, 68, 138, 578, 592, **168**. Revenue is very lumpy. [Screener]
- Quarterly operating margin (OPM), same quarters: 7.4%, 12.7%, 10.9%, 11.2%, 9.8%, **6.6%**. [Screener]
- Quarterly net profit, same quarters (₹ cr): 9.4, 12.9, 9.3, 49.2, 49.1, 9.5. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: total income ₹178 cr (+121% YoY), EBITDA ₹20.7 cr (−2% YoY), PAT ₹9.5 cr (−26% YoY). [IP Q1FY27]
- FY26: total income ₹1,416 cr (+157%), EBITDA ₹187.9 cr (13.3%), PAT ₹120.4 cr (8.5%). Net worth ₹844.8 cr; debt/equity 0.3x. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- Revenue history, FY23–FY26 (₹ cr): 232, 501, 545, 1,376. PAT: 15, 52, 77, 120. [Screener]
- Market data:
  - Market cap ₹1,167 cr; price ₹135, about 62% below the ₹351 IPO price, against a 52-week range of ₹128–389.
  - P/E 10.0; ROE 20.8%. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **After the IPO (as an analyst recalled on the Q4 call):** FY26 revenue of about ₹1,500 cr at an 11% PAT margin. The actual result was ₹1,376 cr of revenue at 8.5% PAT. [Q4 call]
- **CRISIL (Feb 2026):** FY26 revenue of about ₹1,400 cr, 20–25% growth in FY27, and about 12% operating margin. [CRISIL Feb-26]
- **Q4 FY26 call (26 May 2026):**
  - Execute "70%, 75%" of the roughly ₹2,800 cr order book in FY27, which the analyst framed as ₹1,900–2,000 cr of revenue.
  - Margins of 8–11%, with "a slight downside" possible if the West Asia war persists.
  - A 60:40 BESS-to-solar revenue mix as a medium-term target. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 (presentation only):** revenue of ₹168 cr, less than 9% of the implied full-year run-rate, and PAT down 26%. No updated guidance was published. [IP Q1FY27] [Screener]

## Capex and capacity
- FY25–FY26 investing outflow was about ₹750 cr, covering the module line, the BESS line and the junction-box JV. Fixed assets rose to ₹145 cr, with ₹43 cr of capital work in progress. [Screener]
- The cell plant (1.2 GW Pandhurana) was an IPO object. The proposal now is to invest instead in a 50% JV for 2.4 GW at Mohasa. The JV terms (governance and funding) are set by the SSA/JVA and were not fully extracted. [BSE 7-Sep-26]
- Management targets captive use of its own modules and cells to lift EPC margins. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings rose to ₹255 cr (FY26) from ₹115 cr. The IPO lifted net worth to about ₹845 cr. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- FY26 operating cash flow was **−₹50 cr** and free cash flow −₹109 cr. Debtor days are 92 and payables days 150. [Screener]
- **SJVN dispute (auditor emphasis of matter):** SJVN suspended two GUVNL Phase XIII/XIV projects (100 MW and 260 MW) in Kutch, citing land issues.
  - SJVN sought to invoke ₹13.8 cr of bank guarantees. The company went to the Delhi HC, and the guarantees were extended to 31 Jul 2026.
  - Claims filed before the Dispute Adjudication Board (DAB) total ₹219.3 cr. ₹51.5 cr is carried as receivable and treated as fully recoverable. [AR FY26]
  - The DAB awards (18 Sep 2026) came to ₹13.77 cr on the 100 MW project (against ₹56.5 cr claimed) and ₹26.50 cr on the 260 MW project, together about ₹40 cr. [BSE 18-Sep-26]
- CRISIL upgraded Solarworld to A-/Stable and A2+ on 26 Feb 2026 (lines raised to ₹420 cr). A CARE rating (Mar 2026) also exists but was not read. [CRISIL Feb-26] [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Management claims it is among the top three solar EPC companies by profitability, with EPC-only PAT margins of about 10.5%. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- It has repeat PSU relationships (NTPC, SJVN), and an independent director is a former NTPC Green/NTPC REL CEO. [IP Q1FY27]
- It is moving towards vertical integration (modules, cells, BESS, junction boxes), but the manufacturing pieces are new and not yet profitable. The module line ran only about 2.5 months after ALMM approval in FY26. [Q4 call]

## Input costs
- Raw material prices rose significantly because of the West Asia war, hitting Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 margins. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- Module, cell and battery-cell prices are volatile. BESS cells are imported. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **IPO-object variation:** within a year of listing, IPO money is being redirected from the company's own cell plant to a 50:50 JV with another company (Rays Power). This affects the use-of-proceeds case investors relied on. [BSE 7-Sep-26]
- **Guidance misses:** FY26 revenue and margin missed the post-IPO guidance, and Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹168 cr is far below a ₹1,900–2,000 cr full-year run-rate. [Q4 call] [Screener]
- **Customer concentration:** almost the whole order book is with PSUs, and one client (SJVN) is in a DAB dispute. [IP Q1FY27] [AR FY26]
- **Cash burn:** negative operating cash flow in FY26 despite ₹120 cr of PAT. [Screener]
- **Stock collapse:** down about 62% from the IPO price. FIIs cut their holding from 3.4% to 0.8% and DIIs from 11.8% to 9.3%. [Screener]
- **Minor tax items:** a GST penalty of ₹1.22 cr against a subsidiary (the Uttarakhand appeal was allowed on 23 Sep 2026). [Screener ANN]

## My read
Solarworld grew quickly into a ₹1,400 cr PSU solar EPC contractor and raised IPO money to integrate backwards into modules, cells and BESS. The first year after listing has been rough:
- FY26 missed guidance;
- Q1 FY27 revenue collapsed to ₹168 cr, with PAT down 26%;
- operating cash flow turned negative;
- an SJVN dispute is recovering only part of its claims;
- the board now wants to redirect IPO funds into a 50:50 cell JV with another company.

At about 10x trailing earnings and roughly 60% below the IPO price, the stock is cheap for a reason. I'd want H2 execution of the ₹2,700 cr book, the outcome of the shareholder vote on the change of IPO objects, and positive cash flow before trusting the story.
