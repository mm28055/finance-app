# Capacit'e Infraprojects (NSE: CAPACITE)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Consolidated figures in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.*

## Business and segments
- Capacit'e is a Mumbai-based building EPC contractor. It builds high-rise and super-high-rise residential towers, mass housing, speciality buildings and some urban infrastructure. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Order book at 30 Jun 2026: **₹13,535 cr** (about 5x revenue); public sector 55%, private 45%. [Q1 call]
- Key contracts:
  - CIDCO mass housing in Navi Mumbai: about ₹2,000 cr of scope still to be handed over, plus escalation. The client extended completion to March 2028.
  - MHADA BDD Worli redevelopment: a ₹17,000 cr parent contract held by TCC, a Tata Projects–Capacit'e JV. Capacit'e takes 35% as a subcontractor in its standalone accounts, and TCC's profit share is consolidated.
  - NBCC (building ramp-up).
  - IIT Bombay (₹550 cr): delayed by tree-cutting permissions, and started only in Q2 FY27.
  - Signature Global, Raymond Wadala, and a downtown project. [Q1 call]
- New order: an LOI from Mahindra Lifespace worth ₹741 cr for Kanjurmarg and one other site (27 Aug 2026). [Screener ANN]
- Two customers each contributed more than 10% of FY26 revenue: ₹525 cr and ₹332 cr. Government revenue was ₹785 cr and non-government ₹1,450 cr (standalone). [AR FY26]
- **Promoters hold 31.69%.** The pledge fell from 85.5 lakh to 50 lakh shares, and full release is targeted by the end of FY27. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Management change: Director–Operations & CEO Rajendra K. Jain resigned on 18 Aug 2026. Four business-unit CEOs were appointed as senior management on 9 Sep 2026. [BSE 18-Aug-26] [Screener ANN]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue, Mar-25 → Jun-26 (₹ cr): 671, 589, 646, 675, 712, 629. [Screener]
- Quarterly operating margin (OPM), same quarters: 13%, 17%, 17%, 16%, 15%, 16%. [Screener]
- Quarterly net profit, same quarters (₹ cr): 53, 47, 51, 50, 45, 40. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹629 cr (+7%); EBITDA ₹99 cr (15.7% vs 17.2%), after an extra ₹10 cr provision for non-ferrous price escalation not yet covered by indices; PAT ₹40 cr (6.2%). [Q1 call]
- FY26: revenue ₹2,623 cr (+12%); EBITDA ₹427 cr (16.3%); PAT ₹193 cr (7.3%). Other income fell to ₹21 cr from ₹58 cr. [Q4 call]
- Revenue, FY21–FY26 (₹ cr): 880, 1,340, 1,799, 1,932, 2,350, 2,623. PAT: 2, 48, 95, 120, 204, 193. [Screener]
- Market data:
  - Market cap ₹1,614 cr; price ₹191 against a 52-week range of ₹179–314, down 36% in one year.
  - Price/book 0.85x; P/E 8.7. No dividend is paid. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (May 2026):**
  - Revenue growth of 20% a year for two years.
  - EBITDA 15.5–16.5%, or 16.5–17.5% if global uncertainty eased.
  - A ₹10 cr escalation provision was taken in Q4 FY26.
  - FY26 order inflow was ₹4,446 cr against ₹3,500 cr guided. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (10 Aug 2026):**
  - 20% growth reaffirmed ("101%").
  - Q3 and Q4 revenue of ₹850 cr+ each.
  - Order inflow ₹4,500–5,000 cr (₹1,071 cr so far).
  - Net debt-free within 8 quarters.
  - Both ₹10 cr provisions (₹20 cr in total) hoped to be reversed in Q3–Q4. [Q1 call]
- **Track record:** revenue growth has slowed from 22% (FY25) to 12% (FY26) to 7% (Q1 FY27), even though the order book is about 5x revenue. An analyst raised this directly. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex target is ₹193 cr, with ₹52.2 cr spent in Q1. This covers formwork (aluminium and tunnel), towers and cranes for high-rises. [Q1 call]
- Non-core asset sales: ₹44 cr realised in FY26, with ₹50 cr targeted in FY27. [Q4 call]
- Labour: the workforce dipped to about 50% in May, with a shortfall of about 30% in June. Management says it is "fully in place" now. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Gross debt was ₹522 cr at Jun-26, up from about ₹483 cr at Mar-26, because about ₹150 cr of collections slipped by 10 days. At Mar-26, gross debt/equity was 0.25x and net debt/equity 0.10x. [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [Screener]
- Working capital days fell by 43 days in FY26. Operating cash flow was ₹224 cr (₹52 cr in FY25). Debtor days are still **151** per Screener. [Q4 call] [Screener]
- Infomerics upgraded Capacit'e to IVR BBB+/Stable and A2 on 19 May 2026, on ₹2,193 cr of bank lines. It flags "geographical, sectoral and customer concentration risks". [Infomerics May-26]
- Contingent liabilities (standalone, Mar-26):
  - Tax: income tax ₹22.7 cr; GST ₹89.4 cr.
  - Guarantees given: ₹213.7 cr (₹168.5 cr in FY25), plus another ₹331 cr category (₹274 cr in FY25) whose label is garbled in the extracted table. [AR FY26]

## Moat evidence
- It specialises in high-rise and super-high-rise towers using aluminium and tunnel formwork. It has repeat developer clients (Mahindra Lifespace, Raymond, Signature Global) and is a JV partner with Tata Projects on MHADA. [Q1 call] [Screener ANN]
- The JV with Tata Projects on a ₹17,000 cr redevelopment programme gives multi-year visibility. [Q1 call]
- The moat is modest: the credit rating is only BBB+ and there is concentration in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, as Infomerics notes. [Infomerics May-26]

## Input costs
- Price escalation is linked to published indices, which lag actual cost changes:
  - Aluminium prices rose 35–40%, and electrical items 18–20% against an 8% WPI move.
  - Management says escalation typically catches up over one or two quarters.
  - Price variation on the CIDCO contract is currently about 28%. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Steel prices fell about 20% and then rose about 3%. [Q1 call]
- Delhi NCR construction bans (GRAP) have been budgeted at 20 days in FY27. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Qualified audit opinion (running since the Sep-2023 quarter):**
  - Receivables of ₹11.56 cr from one party were previously written off or provided for. Management reversed the write-off through other income and the credit-loss allowance in FY24, based on "future recoverability projections".
  - The auditor is "unable to comment on the recoverability".
  - The predecessor auditor had also qualified FY25, and there has been an auditor change. [AR FY26]
- **CEO exit:** the Director–Operations & CEO resigned in August 2026, one week after the Q1 call. [BSE 18-Aug-26]
- **Low promoter stake with a pledge:** promoters hold 31.7% and still have 50 lakh shares pledged. Their holding fell about 7 points over three years. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- **Execution:** only about ₹8,000–9,000 cr of the ₹13,535 cr order book was actually executable in the last period, because of site handover delays (IIT, CIDCO, Great Value). [Q1 call]
- **Receivables:** debtor days of 151, and working capital days of 128. [Screener]
- **Escalation provisions:** ₹20 cr taken over Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27, with reversal dependent on the indices catching up. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Margin: from the FY26 level of 16.3% to 15.7% in Q1, after a second ₹10 cr provision. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Growth: 20% guidance maintained, but Q1 was only +7%, so H2 must deliver about ₹850 cr+ a quarter. [Q1 call]
- Pledge: fell from 85.5 lakh to 50 lakh shares. [Q1 call]
- Debt: rose to ₹522 cr on delayed collections. [Q1 call]
- After the call: the CEO resigned, a Mahindra Lifespace LOI worth ₹741 cr arrived, and the management structure was reorganised into four BU CEOs. [BSE 18-Aug-26] [Screener ANN]

## My read
Capacit'e has a large, mostly Mumbai-centred order book (₹13,535 cr, about 5x revenue), solid 15–16% EBITDA margins and a deleveraging balance sheet, recently upgraded to BBB+. It trades below book at about 9x earnings. The discount reflects real issues:
- slowing conversion (7% growth in Q1 despite the huge backlog);
- a qualified audit opinion, running since 2023, over a receivable written back into income;
- a low, still-pledged promoter stake;
- the CEO's exit right after the Q1 call.

H2 execution of about ₹850 cr a quarter, clearance of the audit qualification and full release of the pledge are the checkpoints.
