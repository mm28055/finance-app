# RITES Ltd (RITES, ISIN INE320J01015)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Figures are consolidated (Screener) unless marked "standalone". ₹ cr = rupees crore.

## Business and segments
- RITES is a Navratna-class PSU under the Ministry of Railways (government holds 72.20%). It is a transport infrastructure consultant: project management consultancy, design, quality assurance and inspection. It has 13 verticals including railways, buildings, airports, ports, highways and urban transport. It is also the export arm of Indian Railways for rolling stock and leases locomotives. [CARE Jul-26] [Q4 call]
- **Standalone segments in Q1 FY27:**
  - Consultancy: revenue ₹272 cr (+4%), margin 31.4%.
  - Turnkey: revenue ₹176 cr (+19%), margin 1.4%.
  - Leasing: revenue ₹49 cr (+14%), margin 38.1%.
  - Export: revenue ₹1 cr (spares only; the first Bangladesh rake shipped in Q2).
  - Subsidiary REMC Ltd (power trading and renewable consultancy for Railways): Q1 profit ~₹22 cr; FY26 revenue ₹163 cr, profit ₹90 cr, dividend to RITES ₹42 cr.
  
  [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Turnkey is accounting-heavy but low-value. RITES's role is the same as a PMC, but the whole project cost flows through revenue ("we are not a construction company"). Turnkey margins are 1.5–2%. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Order book ₹9,445 cr (30 Jun 2026, a record):
  - by segment: turnkey ₹4,718 cr (50%), consultancy ₹2,597 cr (26%), exports ₹1,775 cr (19%), leasing ₹235 cr (4%), REMCL ₹120 cr (1%);
  - by award route: competitive 62%, nomination 38%.
  
  About 70%+ of fresh inflows are now competitive. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- Exports: ~₹900 cr for 200 coaches to Bangladesh (10 rakes; the first shipped around Aug 2026). The rest is Mozambique locomotives and refurbished Indian Railways diesel locomotives converted to Cape gauge for Africa (order for ~30). A South Africa order for nine locomotives (~US$35 million) was won in July 2026. [Q1 call]
- About 3,125 employees at Q1 FY27, up ~450 in a year to execute the larger book. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY26:** "double-digit growth and growth in profit" with exports of ~₹300 cr. Revenue rose 10% and profit 7%; exports ~₹300 cr. **Met.** [Q4 call] [Screener]
- **FY27 revenue:** Q4 promised "substantial, disruptive growth" and an attempt at an all-time high revenue, above FY22's ₹2,662 cr. Q1 said "double-digit growth on the revenue" and that execution must "step on the gas". Q1 was only +8.6%. **Kept, but lagging.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 profit:** will grow, but will not beat the all-time high (₹571 cr) "for at least two to three years". **Kept.** [Q4 call]
- **Margins:** "red lines" of 20% EBITDA and 15% PAT (consolidated). Q1 delivered 22% and ~17%. **Kept.** Management expects margin pressure from low-margin competitive orders, travel costs and the coming pay revision. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Exports:** Q4 said "much higher than ₹300 crore" in FY27. Q1 said "at least ₹300 crores plus", about 15% of revenue together with overseas consultancy. The Bangladesh order will spill into FY28 (early Q2–Q3), and Mozambique deliveries may begin only at the end of FY27. **Effectively trimmed.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Employee cost:** FY28 increase of 8–10% (not 20%) including the pay revision. [Q1 call]
- **Dividend:** 90%+ payout to continue. Q1 FY27 interim dividend was ₹1.40/share (93.7% payout). **Kept.** [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is low. Fixed assets rose to ₹867 cr (Mar-26) from ₹535 cr (Mar-24), mainly leasing locomotives. There is no major expansion plan. Capacity is people-based, and hiring continues (200+ more in the pipeline). [Screener] [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free (borrowings ₹7 cr, mostly leases). Large cash balances include client funds; CARE calls liquidity strong. [Screener] [CARE Jul-26]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 310 → 560 → 432 → 637 → 327; free cash flow 168 → 424 → 295 → 505 → 266. [Screener]
- Collection period is high: CARE puts it at 125 days in FY26 (135 in FY25); Screener shows 141 debtor days. [CARE Jul-26] [Screener]
- Dividend payout 85–95% for five years (FY26 85%). A bonus issue in FY25 doubled the share count (equity ₹481 cr). [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Institutional position: technical wing of the Railways for inspection and quality assurance. It still receives nomination orders for safety-critical work. Quality assurance revenue is back to FY24 levels after a competitive shock; the non-Railway share is now ~60%. [CARE Jul-26] [Q4 call]
- Sole export channel of Indian Railways rolling stock, except to Thailand, Malaysia and Indonesia. It has won one export order a quarter for 7–8 quarters and is pursuing a standard-gauge Vande Bharat prototype for export. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- The moat is weakening on price: 62% of the book is competitive, and consolidated OPM has fallen from 29% (FY23) to 22–24%. [IP Q1FY27] [Screener]

## Input costs
- Consultancy fees are a percentage of project cost, and execution contracts carry price-variation clauses, so commodity exposure is minimal. The main costs are people (the pay revision is due) and travel. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin dilution:** turnkey is half the book at ~1.5% margin, and competitive consultancy wins are at lower fees. [Q1 call]
- **Export execution:** delays on the first Bangladesh rake pushed Q1 export revenue to near zero, and Mozambique timing is uncertain. [Q1 call]
- **Working capital:** 125–141 receivable days. [CARE Jul-26] [Screener]
- **Contingent liabilities (standalone, Mar-26):** small. Claims not acknowledged ₹39 cr. Another ₹39 cr has been disclosed for a building declared unsafe on a PMC job, where the contractor is in insolvency: reconstruction costs ₹99 cr, of which ~₹60 cr is expected from insurance, bank guarantees and contract dues. [AR FY26]
- **Auditor:** unmodified. Emphasis of matter: the IRSDC JV's accounts are on a liquidation basis; an Israeli associate (MMG) was fully provided and struck off in Apr 2026. Key audit matters include revenue estimates and contingent liabilities. [AR FY26]
- **Subsidiary clean-up:** closure of RITES (Afrika) Pty Ltd initiated 25 Sep 2026. [BSE filing, 25 Sep 2026]
- **Pledges/promoter:** none; government stake steady at 72.20%. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was confident ("disruptive growth"). At Q1 the CMD conceded the company "need[s] to really step on the gas" after +8.6%.
- Export guidance was softened from "much higher than ₹300 crore" to "at least ₹300 crores plus"; Bangladesh completion moved to FY28.
- New items: the South Africa locomotive order (July), a standard-gauge Vande Bharat export exploration, an MoU with CRISIL, and later (23 Sep) an MoU with NHIDCL for highways consultancy.

## My read
RITES is a cash-generating, debt-free consultancy that pays out about 90% of profit. Its record ₹9,445 cr order book should lift revenue in FY27–FY28. But the growth is lower quality: half the book is 1.5%-margin turnkey work, fresh wins are 70% competitive, and profits will lag revenue, as management openly admits. Q1 growth of ~9% was below the tone of the Q4 call. The view would improve with export deliveries picking up in H2 (Bangladesh rakes, Mozambique) and consultancy growth back in double digits. It would weaken if margins approach the 20% EBITDA "red line" or receivable days keep rising.
