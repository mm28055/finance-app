# Welspun Enterprises Ltd (WELENT, ISIN INE625G01013)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Figures are consolidated (Screener) unless marked. ₹ cr = rupees crore.

## Business and segments
- Infrastructure developer and EPC contractor of the Welspun group (B.K. Goenka). It runs three verticals. It bids selectively for HAM/BOT and technically complex EPC projects, executes them largely through sub-contractors ("asset-light"), and sells the assets once they are operational. [Q1 call] [CRISIL Sep-26]
- Q1 FY27 revenue mix: water 41%, transport 35%, tunnelling/rehabilitation 24%. FY26 tunnelling revenue +37%; water −3% (slow UP Jal Jeevan Mission); transport −17% (completions; Pune–Shirur award delayed). [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Water:**
  - Dharavi 418 MLD multi-level wastewater plant, ~70% complete, commissioning Jul 2027, followed by 15 years of O&M;
  - Bhandup 2,000 MLD water treatment plant, ~20% complete at Q4, due Jun 2029;
  - Panjrapur 910 MLD plant, won Dec 2025;
  - UP Jal Jeevan Mission schemes, >80% complete.
  
  The water order book is ~₹14,000 cr including ~₹5,000 cr of O&M. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Welspun Michigan Engineers (WMEL, 60.09% owned):** micro-tunnelling, segmental TBM tunnels, pipe rehabilitation (geopolymer lining), pumping stations and the "Smart Ops" wastewater technology. FY26 revenue ₹874 cr (+31%), EBITDA margin 21%. Q1 FY27 revenue ₹179 cr (21.3% margin). Order book ₹2,135 cr. On 20 Sep 2026 it won two Ahmedabad sewer-rehabilitation jobs worth ₹351 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [BSE filing, 20 Sep 2026]
- **Transport:**
  - Pune–Shirur elevated road (DBFOT toll: 53.4 km, ~36 km elevated; project cost ~₹7,300–7,900 cr; EPC ~₹5,400 cr; 4 years' construction + 25 years' tolling);
  - Aunta–Simaria HAM (complete; being sold at EV ~₹1,000 cr);
  - Sattanathapuram–Nagapattinam (SNRP) HAM, PCC expected Q3 FY27;
  - Varanasi–Aurangabad nearing PCC;
  - 51% of Mukarba Chowk–Panipat (MCP), classified as a discontinued operation.
  
  [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [CRISIL Sep-26]
- Oil and gas: a stake in offshore block MB-OSN-2005/2 with ~₹500 cr invested. The revised field development plan (FDP) has been submitted to the regulator (approval hoped for in 4–6 weeks from Aug 2026). Production would take about two years after approval, and the company now leans towards developing rather than exiting. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Consolidated order book: ~₹20,000 cr at Mar-26; >₹18,700 cr at Jun-26. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY26 revenue ₹3,600 cr:** delivered ₹3,615 cr. **Met.** [Q4 call]
- **FY26 EBITDA margin 18–20%:** delivered ~23% (company). **Beaten.** [Q4 call]
- **FY26 order inflow ~₹10,000 cr:** met (Pune–Shirur and Panjrapur). [Q4 call]
- **FY27 revenue growth:** Q4 said "15%, 20% as we have always maintained". Q1 said "more closer to 15% than 20%", after a −8% Q1. **Softened.** CRISIL assumes 10–15%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [CRISIL Sep-26]
- **FY27 EBITDA margin:** "18% plus" at both calls (conservative, given the geopolitical cost risk). Q1 delivered 22.9%. **Kept.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 order inflow:** ₹8,000–10,000 cr at both calls. Q1 said wins expected in H1 "may move on to H2". **Delayed.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Pune–Shirur:** Q4 expected financial closure in Oct/Nov and ₹500–600 cr of FY27 revenue. Q1 said appointed date in Q3 FY27 and ~₹500 cr of revenue. **Kept, trimmed at the top end.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Aunta–Simaria sale:** targeted within H1 FY27 at Q4. An agreement was signed (EV ~₹1,000 cr), with completion expected in Q2 FY27. It will remove ~₹800 cr of debt. **On track.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **WMEL:** 20% growth in FY27, 25% CAGR over 3 years, 21–22% EBITDA (Q4). Q1 revenue fell as some orders were delayed; 15–20% was "still maintaining". **Softened.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Equity commitment to Pune–Shirur is about ₹1,500 cr. Funding: ₹250 cr already received from preferential warrants, ₹750 cr more to be called within 18 months, and proceeds from asset sales (Aunta–Simaria, SNRP, MCP). [CRISIL Sep-26] [Q4 call]
- A further ₹1,000 cr enabling fund-raise resolution was passed, described as "just an enabling approval… no proposal to raise this fund" at present. [Q4 call]
- Own fixed assets are small (₹288 cr). Execution is sub-contracted; WMEL's tunnelling machines are "100% occupied". [Screener] [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- Niche technical positioning: multi-level treatment plants in space-constrained Mumbai; micro-tunnelling and rehabilitation via WMEL; complex elevated corridors (a Ganga bridge on Aunta–Simaria). Management says water margins of 20–25% are a "technology play" that is not available on "run-of-the-mill" projects. [Q1 call]
- Proven asset recycling: six road assets sold in 2022 at EV ~₹9,000 cr; Aunta–Simaria now being sold. It targets equity IRR above 18%. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Credit strength: CRISIL upgraded the long-term rating to AA/Stable on 21 Sep 2026 (ICRA AA/Stable). [CRISIL Sep-26]

## Input costs
- Most contracts carry WPI/CPI escalation. The Road Transport Ministry has moved bitumen to actual cost and HAM payments to monthly. BOT projects (Pune–Shirur) carry no escalation, so contingency buffers are built in. [Q4 call]
- Q1 was hit by supply-chain disruption linked to the West Asia war, a weeks-long Mumbai construction and debris-transport stoppage (pollution/AQI), and labour shortages around elections. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Revenue stagnation:** revenue has been ~₹3,600–3,700 cr for two years, and Q1 FY27 fell 8%. Growth depends on Pune–Shirur (appointed date in Q3) and on new orders that have slipped to H2. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- **Working capital/collections:** receivables from state water schemes (UP JJM) are slow, and gross current assets are at 271 days. [CRISIL Sep-26]
- **BOT traffic/toll risk** on Pune–Shirur, and a large equity outlay relative to the company's size. [CRISIL Sep-26]
- **Contingent liabilities** are modest: claims not acknowledged as debt ~₹160 cr (income tax ₹71 cr, GST ₹59 cr, service tax ₹23 cr). Guarantees for SPVs are ₹1,372 cr. [AR FY26]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion. Key audit matters: revenue on long-term construction contracts and asset impairment/valuation. No emphasis of matter found. [AR FY26]
- **Promoter dealings:** the promoter holding rose from 55.31% to 56.13% during the period in which ₹1,000 cr of preferential warrants were issued; I did not verify who the allottees were. SPV shares are pledged to project lenders as usual. No promoter share pledge is shown on Screener. [Screener] [AR FY26]
- **Related parties:** there are about ₹1,000–1,100 cr of inter-company contracts between WEL and WMEL. Oil-and-gas capital is outside the core business. [Q4 call]
- **Disclosure:** several answers on project-level revenue (Pune–Shirur FY28, Panjrapur) were deferred to "connect with the team", and the Q1 transcript header is misdated (15 May 2026). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was upbeat (FY26 guidance met, Pune–Shirur won). Q1 was a "soft" quarter, and the FY27 growth guide moved to the low end (~15%).
- The key approval arrived: the high court cleared the Dharavi–Ghatkopar tunnel on 22 Jun 2026, and shaft work has started.
- The Aunta–Simaria sale moved from "advanced stages" to a signed agreement.
- Oil and gas moved from a possible exit to "very high likelihood that we will develop the field", pending FDP approval.

## My read
The balance sheet (net cash-neutral, AA rating) and margins (~20%+) are clearly better than most peers. The water and tunnelling niche via WMEL is a genuine differentiator. But the business has not grown revenue for two years, operating cash flow has been weak, and Q1 FY27 was soft again. Much now rides on Pune–Shirur, a big BOT bet carrying toll risk, and on the ₹8,000–10,000 cr inflow target. The view would improve with Aunta–Simaria closing at a good multiple, Pune–Shirur financial closure on time, and quarterly revenue back above ₹1,000 cr in H2. It would weaken if water-project collections stretch further or more capital goes into oil and gas.
