# Engineers India Ltd (ENGINERSIN, ISIN INE510A01028)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Figures are consolidated (Screener) unless marked "standalone". ₹ cr = rupees crore.

## Business and segments
- Engineers India (EIL) is a Navratna PSU under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas; the Government of India owns 51.32%. It is India's leading engineering consultant for refineries, petrochemicals, pipelines, fertilisers and offshore facilities. Oil and gas has been ~70–80% of the order book over the past three years. [Screener] [CARE Sep-26]
- **Two segments (standalone):**
  - **Consultancy & Engineering** (PMC, EPCM and FEED; high margin): FY26 revenue ₹1,782 cr, segment margin 24% (30% in FY25).
  - **Turnkey/LSTK** (usually on an open-book-estimate basis): FY26 revenue ₹2,068 cr, segment margin 17%, inflated by a ₹221 cr change order in Q3 FY26 (8% in FY25).
  
  In Q1 FY27 consultancy revenue was ₹499 cr (+22%) and turnkey ₹302 cr (−33%). [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Order book (standalone) was ₹14,424 cr at 30 Jun 2026: consultancy ₹10,498 cr (73%), turnkey ₹3,926 cr. At Mar-26 the book was ₹15,109 cr, a record: domestic consultancy ₹4,374 cr, overseas consultancy ₹6,491 cr and turnkey ₹4,245 cr. [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- Overseas: Dangote (Nigeria) refinery expansion PMC/EPCM of US$360 million, won Jan 2026, ~4 years; Dangote fertiliser (four lines) US$70 million; Dangote Ethiopia fertiliser ₹286 cr (May 2026). On 22 Sep 2026 EIL signed a PMC/EPCM contract worth over US$450 million for Dangote's 700,000 bpd refinery in Kenya. The Middle East is 10–15% of the order book (Abu Dhabi office; Saudi Aramco long-term agreement). [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27] [BSE filing, 22 Sep 2026]
- Non-oil diversification: infrastructure (data centres, IIM/IIT campuses, NTPC townships, convention centres) was ~25% of FY26 inflow. Other lines: nuclear balance-of-plant and environmental studies, coal gasification, CBG (its own plant in Maharashtra) and SAF for MRPL. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Investments: stakes in Ramagundam Fertilizers (RFCL) and Numaligarh Refinery (NRL). The JV/associate contributed ₹42.5 cr profit in Q1 FY27, against a ₹7.4 cr loss a year earlier when the RFCL plant was shut. Subsidiary Certification Engineers International (CEIL) earned ₹24.7 cr in FY26. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY27 revenue:** Q4 said "minimum 10% to 15% increase". Q1 said "at least 10%" (to ~₹4,200 cr), with more than 50% (possibly 55%) from consultancy. **Kept; slightly softer wording.** Q1 fell 6%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 order inflow:** ~₹8,000 cr at both calls. **Kept.** It needs ~₹5,250 cr more from mid-August. The Kenya Dangote contract (>US$450 million) signed on 22 Sep should cover a large part of that gap. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [BSE filing, 22 Sep 2026]
- **Margins:** consultancy segment margin 20–25% and LSTK 5–7% (Q4). At Q1: consultancy 24%, LSTK 7.5%, overall operating margin target 16% for FY27, with upside if pending change orders settle. **Kept.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **FY28 revenue ₹5,000 cr:** "still maintaining" (Q1). **Kept.** [Q1 call]
- **Consultancy growth:** 15–20% "on the conservative side" (Q4). The Q1 answer implies FY27 consultancy revenue of ₹2,300–2,400 cr (+30%). **Raised implicitly.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Big domestic awards:** the IOCL Paradip phase-2 balance (Rs 8–10 billion) was expected by end-FY27 (Q4). At Q1 it was "under their approval… land issues". BPCL Andhra is expected at end-FY27 or in Q1 FY28. **Slipping.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is minimal (fixed assets ₹338 cr). The business is people-based and has no term debt. CARE notes "no firm investment commitments" but says heavy investment would be a monitorable. Capacity is set by engineering manpower; mid-level lateral hiring is under way. [Screener] [CARE Sep-26] [Q1 call]
- Management said "certain other investment plans are also under consideration" for surplus cash, and ruled out distributing reserves beyond dividends. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free (borrowings ₹17 cr, mainly leases). Liquid investments and cash were ₹1,359 cr at Mar-26. Investments on the balance sheet total ₹1,606 cr. [CARE Sep-26] [Screener]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 48 → −113 → 222 → 117 → 319; weak relative to profit (CFO/OP 70% in FY26). [Screener]
- Debtor days 45 (FY26). Working-capital days have risen from −179 (FY22) to −60 (FY26) as client advances shrank relative to revenue. [Screener]
- Dividend: ₹5/share for FY26 (interim ₹2.5 + final ₹2.5; payout 41%). [Q4 call] [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Domestic oil-and-gas design leadership over six decades, with proprietary in-house technologies. It is typically chosen on nomination for critical jobs such as the Euro-VI fuel upgrades and refinery energy optimisation. Most PSU work is now competitive or negotiated. [Q4 call] [CARE Sep-26]
- International credibility: empanelled with major national oil companies. Middle East business grew from ₹30 cr to ~₹1,000 cr in 3–4 years. Won the Dangote Nigeria, Ethiopia and Kenya mandates. Hit rate is ~20–25% of bids. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Pricing and mix: a consultancy-heavy book (73%) supports ~24% segment margins, against ~7% on turnkey. [IP Q1FY27]

## Input costs
- Consultancy is manpower cost (the 8th Pay Commission for PSUs "will come later"). Turnkey jobs are mostly open-book, so commodity risk is limited. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Middle East conflict and the Hormuz crisis: existing jobs continue, but new mega-projects are on hold. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor emphasis of matter (FY26):**
  - (a) a contractor's claim of ₹410 cr against EIL's counter-claim of ₹129 cr, pending in the Supreme Court over a 2016 termination;
  - (b) ₹86 cr of FY25 revenue recognised on an unapproved HRRL change order. The auditor says it "remains unapproved and unsettled" and that disclosure was inadequate;
  - (c) an Angola receivable of ₹59 cr from FY2016-17, now fully provided. The CAG had criticised the earlier accounting.
  
  These are serious revenue-recognition remarks for a PSU. [AR FY26]
- **Contingent liabilities (standalone, Mar-26):** commercial claims ₹198 cr; tax disputes of about ₹543 cr (Karnataka VAT SLPs ₹424 cr + ₹53 cr + ₹9 cr, and others) that the note says are reimbursable by clients; total ~₹742 cr, against net worth of ~₹3,146 cr. [AR FY26]
- **Revenue lumpiness:** Q3 FY26 profits were inflated by a ₹221 cr change order. Turnkey revenue fell 33% in Q1 as projects tapered. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Concentration:** hydrocarbon capex cycle (70–80% of the book); Dangote group is a big share of the overseas book (Nigeria, Ethiopia, Kenya). [CARE Sep-26] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Governance/disclosure:** the Q4 transcript header is misdated (August 22 instead of May 22). An analyst complained that the results filed with the exchange were illegible. The statutory auditor for FY27 has been changed by CAG (S C V & Co LLP). [Q4 call] [BSE filing, 9 Sep 2026]
- **Related parties:** mainly the JVs (RFCL, NRL) and PSU clients. Nothing unusual found. No pledge; GoI stake steady at 51.32%. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone on the Middle East moved from "hope it resolves in a month or so" (Q4) to "still very grim… new mega projects are under hold" (Q1). Even so, ~₹400–500 cr of orders came from the region in the quarter.
- The RFCL JV turned consistently profitable (~₹40 cr a quarter), and EIL expects a dividend from it in FY27.
- Consultancy margin rose to 24% in Q1 (from 17% a year earlier). Management began talking about FY27 consultancy revenue of ₹2,300–2,400 cr.
- Nuclear enquiries (environmental studies, NPCIL consulting) and coal-gasification bids after the ₹34,000 cr viability-gap funding scheme were given more airtime.

## My read
EIL's earnings quality is improving. The mix is shifting to consultancy (73% of the order book, ~24% margins), overseas mandates (Dangote ×3) are multi-year, and a debt-free balance sheet with a stable JV now adds profit. But top-line growth is patchy: Q1 revenue fell and turnkey is tapering. The auditor's emphasis on an unapproved ₹86 cr change order and the ₹410 cr contractor claim are real blemishes. The view would improve if consultancy revenue grows 25%+ in FY27, large domestic refinery and petchem awards (BPCL Andhra, IOCL Paradip-II) arrive, and the HRRL claim is settled. It would weaken if mega-projects in the Middle East stay frozen or cash is put into low-return investments.
