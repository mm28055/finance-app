# Pennar Industries (NSE: PENIND)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Consolidated figures in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.*

## Business and segments
- Pennar is a Hyderabad-based diversified engineered-steel company with eight divisions:
  - Pre-engineered buildings (PEB) in India and the US (the Ascent subsidiary).
  - Precision tubes, process heating (boilers), hydraulics, engineering services, automotive body-in-white (BIW), steel, and railways. [CARE Mar-26]
- It has 14 plants: 11 in India and 3 overseas (Tennessee and Alabama in the US). [CARE Mar-26] [AR FY26]
- Segment revenue, Q1 FY27:
  - "Custom design building solutions" (PEB India plus US): ₹507 cr, up from ₹411 cr.
  - Diversified engineering: ₹385 cr, down from ₹450 cr, as steel, hydraulics and exited lines shrank. [Q1 call]
- Management describes the steel and railways "legacy businesses" as about ₹1,300 cr of annual revenue that is being deliberately run down (no new capital). [Q1 call]
- Portfolio moves in FY26:
  - Acquired Telco Enterprises' structural-steel assets in Alabama for about US$14 m (now Ascent Structural).
  - Divested its 51% defence subsidiary.
  - Formed a solar-module JV with Zetwerk, where Pennar holds about 45%. The JV is not consolidated; management says the 2 GW plant has about ₹4,000 cr of revenue potential.
  - Acquired Agile Traders FZCO for West Asia distribution. [AR FY26] [Q1 call]
- Promoters hold 39.67%. A promoter has committed about ₹50.4 cr through convertible warrants, with ₹20 cr brought in so far. [Screener] [AR FY26] [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Earlier PAT-margin targets (as recounted by an investor on the Q1 call):**
  - 2024 target: 5% PAT margin by FY26. It was missed at 3.83%.
  - A later target of 7% PAT by FY28–29 is now phrased by management as "7% PBT in 3 years". Management acknowledged that "it would be extremely inappropriate if we gave you guidance, then we switched that around". [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (27 May 2026):**
  - PAT margin trajectory to keep improving through FY27.
  - Working capital to fall from 82 to 75 days.
  - Order books: PEB India ₹810 cr; PEB US US$63 m; boilers ₹128 cr. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (13 Aug 2026):**
  - No numeric revenue guidance, but "double-digit" group growth from Q2.
  - PEB margins to recover in Q2 after a pass-through lag of about 200bp.
  - ROCE of 25% for FY27, with a stated long-term target of about 30%.
  - Debt/equity at most 0.7 by year-end.
  - PBT margin of 7% over three years.
  - Segment-level EBITDA disclosure promised by next quarter. [Q1 call]
- **Rating agency view:** CARE's upgrade trigger is a PBILDT margin above 10% with total debt/PBILDT below 2.5x. [CARE Mar-26]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 investing outflow was ₹349 cr, including the Telco acquisition, the AGT robotic welding line in the US and the Raebareli plant in India. Capital work in progress was ₹136 cr at Mar-26. [Screener] [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- The BIW Hyundai plant was being commissioned in August 2026, and management says it should double BIW revenue over three months. [Q1 call]
- Order books at the Q1 call were at record levels:
  - PEB India ₹1,008 cr.
  - PEB US more than US$100 m. The call transcript says "USD 100 billion", an evident slip.
  - Boilers ₹150.75 cr. Hydraulics about ₹30 cr, where US tariff uncertainty has put the business on "wait-and-watch". [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings rose from ₹812 cr to ₹1,162 cr in FY26. Short-term borrowings rose ₹145 cr for working capital; long-term debt rose for Telco and capex. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- FY26 operating cash flow was ₹202 cr and free cash flow −₹126 cr. [Screener]
- Working capital:
  - Inventory days 176 and debtor days 74; cash conversion cycle 93 days, close to CARE's 95-day negative trigger.
  - Management says it is "deliberately stocking a little more raw material" and that some debtors "are stuck". [Screener] [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [CARE Mar-26]
- Cash and equivalents are about ₹206 cr (Mar-26). [Q4 call]
- CARE reaffirmed A/Stable and A1 on 31 Mar 2026 on ₹1,767 cr of bank facilities. It notes "debt-funded capex, moderate debt coverage indicators". [CARE Mar-26]
- Title deeds for ₹115.9 cr of immovable property are pledged with banks. [AR FY26]

## Moat evidence
- It has sticky, customised engineering relationships: "clients share related designs ... the likelihood of switching to some other supplier is less." [CARE Mar-26]
- The US PEB and structural platform (Ascent) is a differentiator, benefiting from US reshoring. The AGT robotic line cut welding labour from 20–30 people to 2 per product. [AR FY26]
- The margin mix is improving as higher-margin PEB US and engineering services grow and legacy steel and rail shrink. [Q1 call]
- Management notes that peers passed on steel price rises faster, and PEB EBIT fell by more than 200bp in Q1. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Steel is the dominant input. PEB margins were hit for "a month or 2" before pass-through clauses took effect. Management says most of the increase has now been passed through. [Q1 call]
- US hydraulics face tariff uncertainty, and bookings are running below expectations. [Q1 call]
- US employee costs rose 37% (₹16.2 cr) in Q1 on sales and execution hires. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Goalposts keep moving:** PAT-margin targets have slipped repeatedly (5% by FY26 missed; 7% moved out). There is still no segment disclosure, which is promised for next quarter. [Q1 call]
- **Leverage creep:** debt up 43% in FY26 and finance cost above 4% of revenue. The cash conversion cycle is near CARE's 95-day trigger. [Screener] [Q1 call] [CARE Mar-26]
- **Earnings quality:** reported EBITDA includes other income (forex, write-backs, gains on investments), which management calls recurring. [Q1 call]
- **Legacy drag:** about ₹1,300 cr of steel and rail revenue is declining, which masks growth in the priority units. [Q1 call]
- **US exposure:** tariffs and hydraulics weakness; the Ascent integration is still "taking time to fully materialize". [Q1 call]
- **Unconsolidated JV:** the Zetwerk solar JV's numbers are not disclosed. [Q1 call]
- The FY26 audit had no emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]

## My read
Pennar is shifting from a steel and rail commodity processor towards PEB (India and US), engineering services and boilers. That mix shift is steadily lifting margins (PBT 5.4% in Q1 against 4.8% a year earlier) and has built record order books. The execution record on targets is poor, however: PAT goals have been missed and redefined. Debt is rising with acquisitions and inventory build, working capital sits near CARE's trigger, and there is no segment disclosure. At about 18x earnings and 2x book, the market is giving partial credit for the transformation. Delivery on the promised Q2 margin rebound and ROCE of 25% will show whether management can now meet its numbers.
