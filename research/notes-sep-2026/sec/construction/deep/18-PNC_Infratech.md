# PNC Infratech (NSE: PNCINFRA)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore. "SA" means standalone (the EPC business); "consol" means consolidated, which includes HAM/BOT SPVs and PNC Infra Holdings.*

## Business and segments
- PNC is an Agra-based roads and highways EPC contractor and HAM developer. It has diversified into Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) water supply, Andhra Pradesh irrigation, coal-mining EPC, airport and railway EPC, and a solar-plus-battery (BESS) PPP project with NHPC. [AR FY26] [Q1 call]
- Order book of ₹19,100 cr at 31 Jul 2026:
  - Roads 64.3%, of which NHAI/MoRTH is 31.7%.
  - Coal mining 14.9%, JJM 12.1%, and canal, airport and rail 8.8%. [CARE Sep-26]
- Asset portfolio:
  - 1 BOT toll and 2 BOT annuity projects.
  - 14 HAM projects with a total bid project cost of more than ₹17,200 cr: 6 operational, 5 under construction, 1 awaiting financial closure (MPRDC), and 2 with concession agreements signed on 16 Jul 2026. [Q1 call]
- Asset monetisation: 12 road assets (11 HAM plus 1 state BOT) were sold to Vertis Infrastructure Trust (formerly Highways Infrastructure Trust, KKR-sponsored) at an enterprise value of ₹9,005.7 cr and an implied equity value of ₹2,902 cr. Tranche I completed in May 2025, and the Challakere asset in March 2026. [AR FY26] [Q4 call]
- Promoters hold 56.07%, with no pledge shown. [Screener]
- Management: CMD Pradeep Kumar Jain; MDs Chakresh Kumar Jain and Yogesh Jain; Director (Infra) T.R. Rao. [AR FY26]

## Key numbers
- Consolidated quarterly revenue, Mar-25 → Jun-26 (₹ cr): 1,704, 1,423, 1,128, 1,201, 1,617, 1,688. [Screener]
- Consolidated quarterly net profit, same quarters (₹ cr): 75, 431, 216, 77, 108, 332. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 consolidated operating margin was 31%, inflated by ₹217.66 cr of arbitration claims. Excluding claims, the standalone PBILDT margin was 12.10%. [Screener] [CARE Sep-26]
- Standalone Q1 FY27: revenue ₹1,518 cr (+34%), EBITDA ₹375 cr (24.7%), PAT ₹271 cr (+235%). [Q1 call]
- Standalone FY26: revenue ₹4,633 cr (−9% per CARE), EBITDA ₹583 cr (12.58%), PAT ₹344 cr. [Q4 call] [CARE Sep-26]
- FY26 also included ₹435.19 cr of arbitration claims and bonus: ₹515.75 cr was received under NHAI settlement agreements, plus a ₹56.39 cr early-completion bonus. [CARE Sep-26] [AR FY26]
- Consolidated revenue, FY22–FY26 (₹ cr): 7,208, 7,956, 8,650, 6,769, 5,368, a 38% fall from the peak. [Screener]
- Consolidated PAT, same years (₹ cr): 580, 658, 909, 815, 832. FY26 was flattered by other income of ₹634 cr, which includes monetisation gains and arbitration receipts. [Screener]
- Market data:
  - Market cap ₹3,469 cr; price ₹135 against a 52-week range of ₹116–311, down 55% in one year.
  - P/E 5.7; price/book 0.51x; consolidated ROE 2.4% per Screener. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Earlier guidance:**
  - EBITDA margin had been targeted at 13–13.5%, per the Q4 FY26 call's recap. [Q4 call]
  - Before the Q4 FY26 call, FY27 standalone growth was expected at about 25%. [Q4 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (20 May 2026):**
  - FY27 revenue of about ₹6,000 cr (+30%); FY28 about ₹7,500 cr (+25%).
  - EBITDA margin of about 12%, with management admitting "margin pressure would be there" in H1 because of bitumen and commodity costs.
  - Order inflow of about ₹15,000 cr.
  - Coal mining revenue of ₹600 cr in FY27 and ₹1,400 cr in FY28. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (10 Aug 2026):**
  - FY27 and FY28 revenue guidance maintained; order inflow ₹12,000–15,000 cr, with about ₹5,900 cr secured.
  - Coal mining trimmed to about ₹500 cr in each of FY27 and FY28, because the land handed over was discontinuous and faced local resistance.
  - JJM guided to ₹700–800 cr in FY27 and about ₹1,000 cr in FY28; the AP canal project to about ₹150 cr in FY27.
  - Capex of about ₹150 cr a year. [Q1 call]
- **After the Q1 call:** NHAI extended the debarment to PNC (letter dated 11 Sep 2026), which blocks MoRTH/NHAI bidding for three years. The inflow guidance made on 10 Aug did not account for this. [BSE 14-Sep-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Standalone capex is about ₹150 cr in FY27, of which about ₹70 cr was spent in Q1. The coal-mining contract needs about ₹350 cr in total over its early years, and new surface miners have been deployed. [Q1 call]
- HAM equity:
  - Required: ₹1,623 cr. Infused by June 2026: ₹1,187 cr. Balance: ₹436 cr, with ₹226 cr due in FY27.
  - The two new HAMs (combined bid project cost ₹3,483 cr) need about ₹400 cr more, mainly in FY28–29.
  - The solar/BESS project needs about ₹400 cr of equity. [Q1 call]
- Execution was held back by missing appointed dates on three NHAI HAMs and one MPRDC project (more than ₹4,400 cr in total, concessions signed Jul-23/Mar-24). This was the main cause of FY26's decline. [Q4 call] [CARE Sep-26]
- Bid pipeline: 24 bids submitted worth ₹32,000 cr, including 10 NHAI/MoRTH HAMs. Another 78 projects worth about ₹1.7 lakh cr are to be bid. The debarment now cuts off much of the NHAI part. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Standalone at 30 Jun 2026:
  - Net worth ₹6,084 cr.
  - Bank debt ₹428 cr, excluding ₹485 cr of inter-corporate deposits.
  - Cash and investments ₹1,046 cr, so net cash of ₹133 cr. [Q1 call]
- Consolidated at 30 Jun 2026: net worth ₹7,147 cr, debt ₹5,448 cr (0.76x) and cash of ₹2,870 cr. Consolidated borrowings fell from ₹9,364 cr to ₹5,170 cr in FY26 through the monetisation. [Q1 call] [Screener]
- Working capital:
  - Net working capital days were 110 at Jun-26. Gross current asset days rose to 289 in FY26 from 158 in FY24. [Q1 call] [CARE Sep-26]
  - Debtors ₹1,900 cr, of which HAM debtors are ₹479 cr and **water debtors ₹925 cr**.
  - JJM dues pending from the government are ₹741 cr, excluding GST. AP irrigation receivables are ₹153 cr. [Q1 call]
- CARE says NHAI's reversion from monthly to milestone-based HAM payments raised unbilled revenue. [CARE Sep-26]
- Contingent liabilities at Mar-26: indirect-tax disputes of about ₹61 cr and letters of credit of ₹38.6 cr. Screener shows ₹3,729 cr, which likely includes guarantees for SPVs. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- Rating (CARE, 23 Sep 2026): CARE AA+ and CARE A1+, both placed on Rating Watch with Negative Implications because of the debarment. On 25 Sep, the short-term rating was reaffirmed and taken off watch, while the long-term rating stays on negative watch. [CARE Sep-26] [BSE 25-Sep-26]
- CARE's downgrade triggers: net debt/PBILDT above 1x, or PBILDT margin below 12%. [CARE Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- A long record of early completions and bonuses, including the ₹56.39 cr MSRDC Samruddhi package bonus. It has executed about 100 major projects in 16 states. [AR FY26] [CARE Sep-26]
- It recycles capital well: exiting HAM assets to KKR's InvIT at an equity value of ₹2,902 cr freed the balance sheet. [AR FY26]
- It has an arbitration and settlement pipeline:
  - ₹235 cr received from NHAI under Vivad-se-Vishwas in May 2026.
  - A ₹244 cr award won against UP PWD in July 2026. [Q1 call]
- It has its own quarrying and mining capability, which management argues carries into coal. [Q4 call]
- Management concedes "very unhealthy competition" in NHAI bidding for 2–3 years. [Q4 call]

## Input costs
- Bitumen spiked with crude during the West Asia conflict. MoRTH/NHAI rebased bitumen prices from 1 April 2026 and cut the price-adjustment cycle from three months to one. [Q4 call]
- The FY27 margin of about 12% assumes relief in H2; management says Q1–Q2 are pressured. [Q4 call]
- CARE notes NH construction pace fell from 34 km/day in FY24 to 25 km/day in FY26, with about 21 km/day expected in FY27. [CARE Sep-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **NHAI debarment:**
  - The problem started with pavement failure on the Kanpur–Lucknow Expressway: an approximately 300 m stretch of the 45.24 km Awadh Expressway HAM after July rains. [CARE Sep-26]
  - NHAI then extended the concessionaire's debarment to PNC for three years (letter dated 11 Sep 2026). PNC "will not be able to participate in any bid of MoRTH/NHAI and their executing agencies." PNC is contesting it. [BSE 14-Sep-26]
  - On the Q1 call, management refused to discuss the show-cause notice. Analysts cited press reports of a ₹42 lakh/day toll-loss claim, which management did not confirm. [Q1 call]
- **Concentration:** NHAI/MoRTH was PNC's core client and makes up 31.7% of the current book. New orders must now come from states, railways, coal and power, where PNC is newer and competition is intense. [CARE Sep-26]
- **Receivables:**
  - Water debtors of ₹925 cr, as states slowed JJM funding. The mission was extended to December 2028.
  - Payment delays in AP irrigation. [Q1 call]
- **Earnings quality:** FY26 and Q1 FY27 profits leaned heavily on arbitration and settlement income (₹435 cr and ₹218 cr). Core standalone EBITDA is about 12%. [CARE Sep-26]
- **Execution in the new segments:** coal-mining land issues, and the solar project's land and PPA still pending (revenue expected only from Q4 FY27). [Q1 call]
- **Guidance risk:** the ₹15,000 cr FY27 inflow and ₹7,500 cr FY28 revenue targets now look very stretched. [Q1 call] [BSE 14-Sep-26]
- The FY26 audit was clean, with no emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Order inflow: from about ₹15,000 cr to ₹12,000–15,000 cr, and then the debarment arrived after the call. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Coal-mining revenue: from ₹600 cr to about ₹500 cr in FY27, and from ₹1,400 cr to about ₹500 cr in FY28. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- New issue: the Kanpur–Lucknow (Awadh) HAM stretch failed and NHAI issued a show-cause notice, which escalated into the debarment by September. [Q1 call] [BSE 14-Sep-26]
- Standalone revenue swung from −9% in FY26 to +34% in Q1 FY27, as appointed dates arrived (three HAMs in Q3 FY26) and mining started. [CARE Sep-26] [Q1 call]
- Working capital days improved from Q4 to 110 on JJM and Maharashtra receipts. [Q1 call]

## My read
PNC went into FY27 with a monetised, net-cash standalone balance sheet and a refilled ₹19,100 cr order book. A three-year NHAI/MoRTH bidding ban, imposed over one HAM stretch's failure, now strikes at the core of the franchise. Roads are 64% of the book, and PNC's main edge was fast NHAI execution. The rest of the book (JJM, AP irrigation and coal) has slow-paying or teething counterparties. Reported profits are also inflated by one-off arbitration income, with core margins about 12%. At 0.5x book, the market is pricing in a long freeze. The outcome of the legal challenge and the speed of non-NHAI order wins will decide whether the ₹6,000 cr and ₹7,500 cr revenue guidance survives.
