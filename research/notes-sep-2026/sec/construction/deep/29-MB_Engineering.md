# M&B Engineering (NSE: MBEL)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Consolidated figures in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.*

## Business and segments
- M&B is an Ahmedabad-based steel-building company with two divisions:
  - **Phenix**: pre-engineered buildings (PEB) and heavy structural steel, with plants at Sanand (Gujarat) and Cheyyar (Tamil Nadu).
  - **Proflex**: self-supported steel roofing, installed with mobile on-site manufacturing units. [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹291 cr: Phenix ₹214 cr (74%, +22%) and Proflex ₹77 cr (26%, +25%). Domestic sales were ₹263 cr (90%) and exports ₹28 cr (10%). [Q1 call]
- Order book at 30 Jun 2026: **₹1,053 cr** (+25%), made up of Phenix ₹837 cr (including ₹278 cr of exports) and Proflex ₹216 cr. [Q1 call]
- Exports go mainly to North America through a US subsidiary (Phenix Construction Technologies Inc). A large US order announced in October 2025 has received all approvals, and about 28% had been dispatched by the Q1 call. Both plants are now AISC-certified, and Cheyyar can serve the US West Coast from FY28. [Q1 call] [CRISIL Aug-26]
- Capacity:
  - Sanand PEB: 72,000 t, rising to 92,000 t with a 20,000 t brownfield expansion in Oct 2026.
  - Heavy structural steel: 12,000 t, plus a new ₹30 cr, 10,000 t automated line expected in Q1 FY28.
  - Cheyyar: +20,000 t by Q3 FY28.
  - Total PEB and structural capacity to reach about 154,000 t by Q3 FY28.
  - Proflex: 21 lakh m² a year.
  - A third plant in North India is being scouted. [Q1 call]
- IPO: listed in August 2025 at ₹385 a share, a ₹650 cr issue made up of ₹275 cr fresh and ₹375 cr offer-for-sale. Promoters hold 70.61%; CRISIL says the family of Hasmukh Patel manages the company. [AR FY26] [CRISIL Aug-26] [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (12 May 2026):**
  - FY27 revenue growth of about 25%.
  - A softer H1 expected from the Iran conflict (margins and execution), Q1 labour constraints and the Q2 monsoon. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (10 Aug 2026):**
  - FY27 revenue growth of "over 25%".
  - More than 20% compound growth over three to four years.
  - **Margin guidance withheld** for one more quarter because of freight and cost uncertainty.
  - Export EBITDA of about 15% at peak freight, against 16–17% normally (17–18% was earlier guided). Domestic margin is about 11%. [Q1 call]
- **CRISIL's view:** revenue growth of 15–20% and operating margin of 10.5–11.5% over the medium term. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Earlier target:** an order-inflow run rate of "INR100 crores a month" (analyst recap). Q1 inflow was about ₹260 cr. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Q1 FY27 capex was about ₹27 cr, covering the Sanand expansion, two Proflex mobile units and a 2.5 MW captive solar plant. [Q1 call]
- IPO fund use: ₹146.69 cr of ₹259.32 cr net proceeds (57%) used by 30 Jun 2026. [Q1 call]
- Utilisation: Sanand is "chock-a-block full" at 75–80% (the practical maximum). Cheyyar is targeted at about 60% in FY27. FY26 PEB volume was 72,000 t, and the FY27 target is 85,000–90,000 t. [Q1 call]
- Capital commitments rose to ₹49.5 cr from ₹4.6 cr. [AR FY26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings fell from ₹191 cr to ₹84 cr in FY26 after the IPO. CRISIL puts gearing at 0.12x, TOL/TNW at 0.77x and interest coverage at 8.2x. [Screener] [CRISIL Aug-26]
- FY26 operating cash flow was **−₹31 cr** and free cash flow −₹62 cr. Inventory days are 175 and the cash conversion cycle 137 days. [Screener]
- Management gives net working capital as 39 days. CRISIL says gross current asset days improved to 189 from 221. [Q4 call] [CRISIL Aug-26]
- CRISIL reaffirmed A/Stable and A1 on 31 Aug 2026 (bank lines enhanced to ₹515 cr). Net worth is ₹652.7 cr. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- Contingent liabilities are bank guarantees of ₹112.9 cr (₹103.3 cr in FY25). [AR FY26]

## Moat evidence
- It is a "design-led" provider of PEB plus heavy structures plus self-supported roofing (Proflex is a niche). It has 60–70% repeat customers. [Q1 call]
- It has an export capability, with AISC certification at both plants, that most Indian PEB peers are only starting to build. [Q1 call]
- The company chooses not to chase volume: its hit rate is 12–15%, against about 20% at peers, to protect margins, and it is capacity-constrained. Management said "I will not run after orders just to make my hit rate 20%". [Q1 call]
- Each plant economically serves a 1,000–1,200 km radius, which limits growth to regional expansion. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Domestic steel prices rose more than 20% in Q4 FY26, hurting procurement costs on contracts not yet covered for raw material. [Q4 call]
- Freight to the US has doubled (US$10,000–12,000 per container), plus higher US inland costs. Other expenses rose by about 3% of revenue in Q1. [Q1 call]
- Offsetting this, US Section 232 duties fell from 50% to 25%. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin drift:** EBITDA fell from 13.6% (FY25) to 12.5% (FY26) to 11.4% (Q1 FY27), and margin guidance is now withheld. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Cash conversion:** negative operating cash flow in FY26 despite a record PAT, with high inventory days. [Screener]
- **US exposure:** export margins depend on US tariffs (Section 232), freight and forex. FY26 had a forex loss. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Capacity bottleneck:** growth is gated by the Sanand expansion landing on time in October 2026. [Q1 call]
- **Share price:** 33% below the ₹385 IPO price, with FIIs falling from 4.75% to 0.84%. The IPO was mostly an offer-for-sale by promoters (₹375 cr). [Screener] [AR FY26]
- The FY26 audit had no emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Order book: from ₹1,083 cr (end-FY26) to ₹1,053 cr (Q1), as inflow of about ₹260 cr lagged execution. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Margin: from about 13% adjusted (FY26) to 11.4% operating (Q1). Management now declines to give margin guidance. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Capacity: a new ₹30 cr heavy-structural line was approved, and the Cheyyar expansion is scheduled. [Q1 call]
- Exports: approvals received for the large US order; about 28% dispatched. [Q1 call]

## My read
M&B is a well-run, mid-sized PEB and roofing maker with a real export angle, A-rated credit, 23% ROCE and a net-cash post-IPO balance sheet. Growth of 25%+ is constrained more by capacity than by demand. The issues are margin erosion (from 13.6% to 11.4%) from steel, freight and forex, negative operating cash flow in FY26, and dependence on US trade policy for the higher-margin export book. At about 15x earnings and a third below the IPO price, the stock needs October's Sanand expansion and an H2 margin recovery to prove the growth story.
