# Cemindia Projects Ltd (CEMPRO, formerly ITD Cementation India; ISIN INE686A01026)

Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Figures are consolidated (Screener) unless marked. ₹ cr = rupees crore.

## Business and segments
- Heavy-civil EPC contractor with 90+ years of history (Cementation UK → Trafalgar House → Kvaerner → Skanska → ITD Thailand in 2005). The Adani group bought control in May 2025 through Renew Exim DMCC, which now holds 67.46%. The company was renamed Cemindia Projects. [IP Aug-26] [CARE Sep-26] [Screener]
- The company reports a single segment ("Engineering and Construction"). Order book by sector at 30 Jun 2026 (₹31,307 cr):
  - maritime structures ₹7,258 cr (23%);
  - urban infra/metro/airports ₹6,494 cr (21%);
  - industrial structures and buildings ₹5,314 cr (17%);
  - data centres ₹4,154 cr (13%);
  - water and wastewater ₹3,414 cr (11%);
  - highways and bridges ₹2,617 cr (8%);
  - foundation/specialist ₹1,296 cr (4%);
  - hydro/dams/tunnels ₹760 cr (3%).
  
  [IP Aug-26] [AR FY26]
- Client mix of the order book: private >60% (mostly Adani entities). Adani group is ~53% of the order book, per management and CARE. Top-5 states are ~65%. [IP Aug-26] [Q1 call] [CARE Sep-26]
- Geography: FY26 revenue 98% India, 2% overseas (UAE/Abu Dhabi marine job, Bangladesh transmission-tower foundations, Sri Lanka). Exports are ~2–3% of the order book. [IP Aug-26] [Q1 call]
- Key equipment fleet: 8 tunnel-boring machines (4 recently ordered, for the Pune and Delhi metros), 5 micro-TBMs, 46 hydraulic piling rigs, 279 cranes and 4 jack-up barges. Chennai Metro uses 4 TBMs. [IP Aug-26] [Q4 call]
- Data centres (new since FY26): about 320–400 MW of civil work for the group in Navi Mumbai (three ~130–140 MW and two 30 MW halls) and Vizag, worth ~₹3,000 cr at Q4. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 2,576 → Sep-25 2,195 → Dec-25 2,315 → Mar-26 2,973 → Jun-26 2,721 (+6% YoY). [Screener]
- Quarterly operating profit (₹ cr) / OPM: 240 / 9% → 202 / 9% → 222 / 10% → 358 / 12% → 275 / 10%. Company EBITDA (including other income) for Q1 FY27 was ₹285 cr, a 10.5% margin. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Quarterly net profit (₹ cr): 137 → 108 → 111 → 242 → 141 (Q1 +3% YoY). Q4 FY26 was lifted by ~₹100 cr of old-claim realisation (mostly booked as interest in other income) and release of provisions on completed metro jobs. Management called this "not a regular phenomenon". [Screener] [Q4 call]
- Annual revenue (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 3,809 → 5,091 → 7,718 → 9,246 → 10,061. OPM: 8% → 8% → 10% → 9% → 10%. Net profit: 69 → 125 → 274 → 373 → 598. FY26 claims realised totalled ~₹150 cr. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- ROCE improved from 14% (FY22) to 33% (FY26); ROE 27.8%. [Screener]
- Valuation (25 Sep 2026): market cap ₹21,578 cr, price ₹1,257 (52-week range ₹481–1,650), P/E 35.9, price/book 9.0x. [Screener]
- Order inflow (₹ cr): FY22 8,841; FY23 6,915; FY24 7,174; FY25 ~9,993; FY26 ~19,918 including L1 and April wins (CARE: ~₹10,000 cr from Adani in FY26); Q1 FY27 8,519 (₹6,000 cr from Adani). July 2026 added ₹1,247 cr, and the company is L1 on ~₹990 cr more. [IP Aug-26] [Q1 call] [CARE Sep-26]

## Guidance history
- **FY27 revenue growth:** Q4 said "at least 25% more than this year"; Q1 said "25%" for FY27 and FY28, and 20–25% for "a few years". **Kept.** Q1 grew only 6%. FY27 needs ~₹12,500 cr, which implies ~₹3,260 cr per quarter over Q2–Q4. Management blames the Vadhvan port job (zero output, local issues), a ₹10,000–12,000 cr block of new orders (Munger, DMRC, Pune Metro, Morsagar) still in design, and a slowdown at the Abu Dhabi job linked to the war. It expects Q3–Q4 to be much stronger. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 order inflow:** "maybe ₹25,000 cr" (Q4) became "around ₹25,000 cr" (Q1), with ₹8,519 cr already booked. **Kept.** The bid pipeline rose from ₹70,000 cr (35–40% group) at Q4 to ₹90,000 cr (~50% group) at Q1, with a 15% hit rate (down from ~20%). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **EBITDA margin:** after Q4's 12% the MD said 10–10.5% "going forward"; Q1 said "10% plus… double digit". Q1 came in at 10.5% (company) / 10% (Screener OPM). **Kept and met.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Capex:** FY26 was ₹260 cr. FY27 guidance of ₹350–400 cr was kept at Q1, with ₹81 cr spent in Q1. Capex could be much higher if a large-diameter road tunnel is won. **Kept.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Fund-raising:** Q4 (30 Apr) said "No… no plan as yet". On 23 Jul the board approved raising up to ₹5,000 cr by QIP or other routes (EGM 17 Aug 2026). On the Q1 call this was described as an enabling resolution for capex (TBMs), inorganic growth and working capital, not needed for the current book. **Reversed.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [News: Business Today, 26 Aug 2026]
- **Bangladesh job:** receivables of ~₹178 cr; completion by Sep–Oct 2026. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Fixed assets were ₹1,168 cr at Mar-26 (₹655 cr at Mar-22). Routine capex is ₹300–400 cr a year. New TBMs have been bought for the Pune and Delhi metros, and the Bangalore Metro TBM has been sold. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- The proposed ₹5,000 cr raise is about twice the current net worth of ₹2,400 cr (Mar-26). Management gave organic capex needs of "anything in the range of, say, 4,000–5,000" (crore), depending on wins. [Q1 call] [IP Aug-26]
- Morsagar (Rajasthan irrigation): a joint venture with the group in which Cemindia holds 27%. Cemindia is also bidding for group BOT projects as a back-to-back EPC contractor, but does not plan to put in equity itself "unless there is some special interest by the group". [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Gross debt ~₹1,000 cr (Jun-26), net debt ~₹700 cr, net debt/equity 0.28. Screener borrowings ₹1,001 cr (Mar-26). [Q1 call] [Screener]
- Mobilisation advances were ~₹1,400 cr at Mar-26, of which ~90% were interest-free (largely group). CARE: total debt/PBILDT improved to 1.94x (FY26) from 2.64x; interest cover 4.97x (FY26) and 5.5x (Q1 FY27). [Q4 call] [CARE Sep-26]
- Working capital: receivables ~69 days including retention, inventory ~30 days, WIP ~107 days, net working capital ~120 days (Jun-26), guided at 110–120 days. Screener's cash conversion cycle is -110 days (FY26), because payables are large (238 days). [Q1 call] [Screener]
- Operating cash flow (₹ cr) FY22→FY26: 339 → 471 → 704 → 184 → 500; free cash flow 244 → 69 → 369 → 12 → 274. [Screener]
- Dividend payout ~9–11%. The FY26 dividend was declared late (a process delay, per the Q4 call). No buyback. [Screener] [Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- Technical niche: one of about 3–5 serious marine contractors in India (breakwaters, jetties, ship-lift at Karwar). Also skilled in underground metro (~40 km tunnelled) and specialist foundations. [Q1 call] [IP Aug-26]
- Parent pull: ~₹16,000 cr of Adani orders over FY26–Q1 FY27 (ports, airports, data centres, roads, water). CARE and ICRA both upgraded the rating in Sep 2026, citing group linkages. Management says group jobs are "competitive bidding" with "no special preference". [CARE Sep-26] [Q4 call]
- Margin improvement: OPM rose from ~8% (FY22) to ~10% (FY26) after legacy low-margin jobs finished ("no legacy job left out"). [Screener] [Q4 call]

## Input costs
- Main inputs: steel, cement, fuel/diesel and bitumen; plus labour and subcontractors. [AR FY26]
- Pass-through: about 30% of jobs have fixed "star price" commodity clauses, and ~80% have some escalation cover for "normal inflation", but not for "abnormal inflation cost because of war". Some group jobs supply materials free of cost. The MD warned of "some effect" on margins from the 2026 oil-price spike. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Labour shortage was acknowledged as an industry-wide constraint. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Group concentration:** 53% of the order book and ~50% of the pipeline come from Adani entities. CARE lists "weakening linkages with Adani group" as a downgrade trigger. The annual report lists revenue from ~25 Adani group entities as related-party transactions (I did not total them). [CARE Sep-26] [AR FY26]
- **Execution:** CARE says 70% of the order book is at a nascent stage. Vadhvan port has had no start for more than a year ("not in our control"). [CARE Sep-26] [Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities (Mar-26):** claims not acknowledged as debt ₹686 cr (vs ₹470 cr), indirect tax ₹105 cr, income tax ₹38 cr, property tax ₹50 cr. Total is ~₹880 cr, about 37% of net worth. [AR FY26]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion. Key audit matters: estimation of contract revenue, cost and claims; recoverability of receivables and contract assets. No emphasis of matter found. [AR FY26]
- **Equity dilution risk:** the ₹5,000 cr enabling resolution came only three months after management said there was no fund-raising plan. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Pledges:** none flagged on Screener. Promoter holding rose from 46.64% to 67.46% through the Adani open offer in Jun-25 quarter. FIIs fell from 13.95% to 7.26%. [Screener]
- **Disputes:** small arbitration award (₹4.71 cr) enforcement petition withdrawn 23 Sep 2026, to be refiled; no material impact. [BSE filing, 24 Sep 2026]
- **Management/disclosure:** the CFO (Nitesh Sharma) was absent from the Q4 call. The Q1 transcript contains minor inconsistencies (July inflow given as ₹1,247 cr, while the results summary said ₹447 cr). Cash flow from operations for Q1 was not given on the call. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Fund-raising went from "no plan" to a ₹5,000 cr enabling resolution, which has become the main investor debate.
- The pipeline grew from ₹70,000 cr to ₹90,000 cr. The group share of the pipeline rose from 35–40% to ~50%.
- Q1 FY27 growth slowed to 6% (Q4 had grown 17%). Margins returned to ~10.5% from the one-off-boosted 12–15% in Q4.
- New sectors are being targeted: high-speed rail and nuclear power; road tunnels (Mumbai, Bengaluru, below the Brahmaputra).

## My read
The business has clearly moved up a level. Revenue has grown 2.6x since FY22 to ₹10,061 cr, margins are ~10% and ROCE is 33%. Adani ownership has doubled order inflow and lifted ratings to AA-/AA. The flip side is dependence: half the book is from the parent, related-party revenue is large, and 70% of the book has not started. Near-term growth (Q1 +6%) also lags the 25% guidance. The view would improve with visible Q3–Q4 revenue of ₹3,000–3,500 cr a quarter, a Vadhvan start, and a larger non-group win share. It would weaken if the QIP is priced to fund group-linked capex or acquisitions with thin returns, or if margins fall below 10% as commodity inflation bites.
