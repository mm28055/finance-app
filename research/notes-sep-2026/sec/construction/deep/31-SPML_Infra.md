# SPML Infra (NSE: SPMLINFRA)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Consolidated figures in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.*

## Business and segments
- SPML is a Kolkata-based EPC contractor that has emerged from a long debt restructuring. It works in three segments:
  - **Water**: drinking water, irrigation, river-linking and sewerage.
  - **Power substations and transmission**.
  - **Battery energy storage (BESS)**: both as an EPC contractor and as a battery-pack OEM. [Q1 call]
- Order book about **₹5,100 cr** at the Q1 call. About ₹1,251 cr is low-margin "legacy" work (mostly back-to-back joint-control contracts); the rest is new "SPML 2.0" orders taken at 10%+ margins. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- New orders since FY25 total more than ₹4,280 cr, including SPML's JV shares. The largest is an **NTPC BESS order of ₹1,128 cr** (Barauni, with 15-year O&M), for which Energy Vault (US) provides technical support. [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- Q1 FY27 inflow was ₹1,293 cr (Konar, Kekri and Indore water projects, among others), and SPML is L1 on another ₹212 cr. [Q1 call]
- BESS manufacturing at Supa MIDC, Pune:
  - Phase 1 (2.5 GWh battery-pack line) is ready.
  - Expansion to 5 GWh plus 600 containers a year is due by H1 FY28, at a total cost of about ₹236–238 cr, which is said to be fully funded.
  - Cells are imported from China, and the container technology comes from Energy Vault under an exclusive tie-up.
  - A 104.4 kWh pack cleared international safety testing (September 2026). [Q1 call] [AR FY26] [Screener ANN]
- Promoters hold 40.51%, up from 35.12% after warrant conversions. They have put in about ₹400 cr over three years (₹313.5 cr recently, plus ₹112.5 cr earlier). [Screener] [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue, Jun-25 → Jun-26 (₹ cr): 156, 190, 230, 291, 284. [Screener]
- Quarterly operating margin (OPM), same quarters: 4.6%, 6%, 11%, 7%, 9%. [Screener]
- Quarterly net profit, same quarters (₹ cr): 12, 15, 20, 27, 23. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹286 cr (+74% YoY), EBITDA ₹28 cr (about 10%), PAT ₹22.7 cr (+87%). Tax was near zero in recent quarters (Mar-26 −33%, Jun-26 0%). [Q1 call] [Screener]
- FY26: revenue ₹868 cr (+12%, a turnover shortfall), EBITDA about ₹76 cr per management, PAT ₹75 cr. Q4 FY26 EBITDA was 8.4%, after one-off legal and arbitration costs and credit-loss provisions. [Q4 call] [Screener]
- Revenue history, FY21–FY26 (₹ cr): 683, 952, 883, 1,319, 777, 868. PAT: −117, 0, 0, −2, 48, 75. [Screener]
- Market data:
  - Market cap ₹1,407 cr; price ₹166 against a 52-week range of ₹151–261, down 35% in one year.
  - P/E 16.5; **ROE 8.3%, ROCE 6.5%**. No dividend is paid. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (1 Jun 2026):**
  - The company will "remain focused on execution rather than building the order book".
  - The BESS line would start by the end of June 2026, with 5 GWh plus containers by the end of calendar 2026.
  - FY26 turnover fell short, which management blamed on calibrating execution to available funds during the March West Asia disruption. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 call (17 Aug 2026):**
  - More than 25% growth in revenue, EBITDA and PAT in FY27.
  - Order intake of more than ₹5,000 cr (₹1,293 cr in Q1).
  - BESS revenue of ₹200–300 cr in Q4 FY27, subject to NTPC design and sample approvals by December.
  - The 5 GWh expansion now slips to H1 FY28 (it was "end of this year" on the Q4 call).
  - Potential BESS revenue: ₹2,000–2,500 cr at 2.5 GWh and ₹4,500–5,000 cr at 5 GWh (management claim). [Q1 call]
- **Track record:** FY26 turnover missed guidance, while order intake and margin guidance were met, per management. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capital work in progress was ₹64 cr (the BESS plant), and BESS capital commitments were ₹55 cr at Mar-26 (₹173 cr a year earlier). [Screener] [AR FY26]
- Management says BESS capex is fully financed through equity plus a ₹10 cr term loan for the container line. [Q1 call]
- Pipeline tracked: 134 projects worth about ₹98,725 cr across 11 states, of which irrigation and water are more than 80%. Examples are Wainganga–Nalganga (about ₹1 lakh cr), the Marathwada Water Grid and Ken-Betwa. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings were ₹358 cr at FY26, down from ₹1,771 cr in FY23 after the restructuring. Management puts debt/equity at 0.4x, down from 1.1x. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- **Legacy debt:** about ₹700 cr in total, of which about ₹325 cr has been repaid. The remaining ₹375 cr is described as "fully backed" by an arbitration award of about ₹678 cr. CRISIL's facility list includes a ₹380 cr term loan from **India Debt Resolution Company**. [Q1 call] [CRISIL Sep-26]
- Arbitration claims outstanding are about ₹4,526 cr. Management expects about 40% to become awards, based on its history. [Q1 call]
- Cash flow and working capital:
  - Operating cash flow was **negative** in FY25 (−₹85 cr) and FY26 (−₹113 cr).
  - Debtor days 216 and working capital days 159.
  - Trade receivables rose from ₹299.6 cr to ₹417.5 cr in FY26. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- Ratings:
  - ICRA upgraded SPML to BBB/Stable (3 Jul 2026, per Screener), and CRISIL rates it BBB/Stable and A3+ (rationales dated 18 Jun and 22 Jul 2026). The rationale texts were not read; the credit bulletin lists facilities.
  - Indian Overseas Bank has sanctioned ₹200 cr of cash credit and ₹650 cr of non-fund-based lines.
  - Management says bank limits are being enhanced from ₹505 cr to ₹860 cr, plus a surety line of about ₹300 cr. [Q1 call] [CRISIL Sep-26]
- Screener flags that the company "might be capitalizing the interest cost". [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- It has four decades of water and power EPC experience and pre-qualifications. [Q1 call]
- In BESS it combines an OEM role and an EPC role, with an exclusive Energy Vault technology tie-up. The NTPC order is a key reference project. [Q1 call]
- Its new orders are said to carry price-variation clauses, 10%+ margins and escrow-based supplier payments in water projects. [Q1 call]
- There is no proven BESS manufacturing record yet, and cells are imported from China like everyone else. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- BESS depends on Chinese cells, so rupee depreciation matters. Management says it inserts price-variation clauses in its contracts. [Q1 call]
- Water and power EPC costs (pipes, steel, cables) are covered by price-variation clauses on new orders (management claim). [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **History:** heavy losses (FY21: −₹117 cr) and a debt restructuring. Legacy debt is still being serviced from arbitration proceeds, so recovery timing matters. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- **Earnings quality:** a near-zero tax rate, reliance on arbitration outcomes, and negative operating cash flow for two years. [Screener]
- **Receivables:** 216 debtor days with state water utilities. [Screener]
- **BESS execution:** first revenue depends on NTPC approving designs and samples by December 2026. The expansion timeline has already slipped. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Audit scope:** the standalone accounts include three joint operations with ₹89.4 cr of revenue whose financial information is **unaudited** and furnished by management. [AR FY26]
- **Low returns:** ROE of about 8% and ROCE of about 6.5%, even after the turnaround. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- BESS: the 2.5 GWh line is ready. The 5 GWh expansion moved from "end of this year" to H1 FY28, and first NTPC billing is now expected in Q4 FY27. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Orders: ₹1,293 cr won in Q1, and the order book is about ₹5,100 cr (₹5,369 cr at the start of Q4 FY26). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Ratings: the ICRA upgrade to BBB and a new CRISIL BBB rating came between the calls. [Q1 call]
- Growth guidance of more than 25% was made explicit. [Q1 call]

## My read
SPML is a turnaround: promoters injected about ₹400 cr, debt fell from about ₹1,800 cr to about ₹360 cr, credit ratings are back to BBB, and the new order book is priced at 10%+ margins. It is also making a bold pivot into BESS manufacturing, anchored by a ₹1,128 cr NTPC order. The quality of earnings is still weak: negative operating cash flow, 216 debtor days, a near-zero tax rate, legacy debt repaid out of arbitration awards, and unaudited joint-operation accounts. At about 17x earnings with single-digit ROE, the stock needs NTPC BESS approvals in December and a turn to positive operating cash flow to justify the multiple.
