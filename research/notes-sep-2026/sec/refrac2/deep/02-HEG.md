# HEG — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

**Read this first — the company has split.** The composite scheme became effective on 1 Sep 2026 (record date 7 Sep). The graphite-electrode business, the captive power plants and the 9.98% GrafTech stake moved to **HEG Graphite Ltd** (1 new share per HEG share; to be renamed HEG Ltd and listed "in the second half of October 2026"). The old listed company (BSE 509631, Screener code now **HEGAM**) was renamed **HEG Advanced Materials Ltd**, absorbed Bhilwara Energy Ltd (8 new shares for every 7 BEL shares) and now holds TACC (battery anode), Replus (battery packs/BESS, 74%) and hydro/solar power. The stock fell from ~₹728 (6 Sep) to ~₹260 at the ex-date open; it was ₹244 on Screener on 27 Sep. [News: Kotak Neo, Sep 2026] [BSE SAST 21 Sep 2026] [HEG Greentech presentation, Jul 2026 p.6] [Screener]

Consequence for the note: the ticker the dashboard calls "HEG" no longer owns an electrode business; the pure electrode company is not yet listed. Everything below on electrodes is about HEG Graphite; the Greentech section is about the listed HEGAM.

Figures in ₹ crore unless stated; history is Screener consolidated (pre-split HEG, continuing operations), read from the raw page.

## Business and segments
- **Graphite electrodes (HEG Graphite):** ultra-high-power electrodes for electric arc furnaces (EAF). Capacity 100,000 t/yr at Mandideep near Bhopal — "by far the world's largest single location plant"; next two plants ~70,000 t. Utilisation >90% in FY26 and Q1 FY27 (production 95% in Q4 FY26). Captive power ~80 MW (66 MW thermal + 13.5 MW hydro). [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27 p.3] [Greentech IP p.6]
- Two reported segments in FY26: Graphite (segment result ₹270 cr, FY25 ₹217 cr) and Power (₹15 cr). [AR FY26 Note 37]
- Geography FY26 (standalone ₹2,569 cr): India ₹847 cr (33%), **USA ₹440 cr (17%)**, Turkey ₹183 cr, Egypt ₹95 cr, Korea ₹89 cr, Spain ₹81 cr, Japan ₹67 cr, UAE ₹30 cr, others ₹616 cr. No customer >10%. [AR FY26 Note 37]
- FY26 sales volume 92,800 t (+20%); EBITDA margin 16% (Ind-Ra basis); ~20% of annual volume goes to the Middle East/North Africa. [Ind-Ra May-26] [Q4 call]
- **Greentech (HEG Advanced Materials, listed):** TACC anode plant 20,000 t (start of production Q1 FY28), graphene (150 t, under development), Replus battery energy solutions (1 GWh → 6 GWh target), operating green power 292 MW (hydro 192 MW + 86 MW in Himachal, wind 14 MW), with a 76 MW hydro in Uttarakhand and 300 MWp solar under construction (target 754 MW by FY28); promoter holding 60.72%. [Greentech IP p.6–7] [Screener shareholding Sep-26]

## Capex and capacity
- **Electrodes:** +15,000 t brownfield to 115,000 t, capex ₹650 cr, started Nov 2025, operational early 2028, funded from internal accruals. At ~₹2.8 lakh/t realisation, 15,000 t adds ~₹400 cr revenue at full use (derived) — roughly 1.5 years of revenue per rupee of capex, a good asset turn for a brownfield. [Ind-Ra May-26] [Q1 call]
- **Anode (TACC):** Phase 1 20,000 t, cost ₹2,200–2,300 cr; 40% spent by July, 90–95% to be spent in FY27; SBI loan ₹1,240 cr, rest equity/accruals; "85% procurement complete, 100% engineering complete"; payback "4 to 5 years". Phase 2 +10,000 t for ~₹800 cr by 2029 (full 30,000 t ≈ ₹3,100 cr, 70% debt). Stated ambition 60,000 t by FY32. HEG (pre-split) had put ₹180 cr equity and ₹400 cr optionally convertible debentures into TACC by end-FY26 and guaranteed ₹1,230 cr of TACC's SBI facilities. [Q1 call, official] [Greentech IP p.15] [Ind-Ra May-26] [AR FY26 Note 36]
- Offtake: presentation lists non-binding MOUs/discussions, e.g. a European automotive OEM >40,000 t and a North American automotive company >70,000 t over 3–5 years; the company itself warns there is "no assurance" these convert. [Greentech IP p.16]
- **Power:** 76 MW hydro (bought from Statkraft, ~30% built, ~2.5 years) and 300 MWp solar (18 months) to add ~₹200 cr EBITDA. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Pre-split standalone treasury ₹858 cr at Jun-26 (₹792 cr at Mar-26), but **short-term borrowings ₹783 cr** at Jun-26 (export packing credit) — so the electrode business is roughly net-cash-neutral, not "debt-free" in the everyday sense. Management's "debt-free" means no long-term loans. [IP Q1FY27 p.17–18] [Q1 call]
- Screener consolidated borrowings ₹796 cr at Mar-26; Ind-Ra: debt ₹793 cr, liquid cash/investments ₹463 cr at FYE26; net leverage 0.98x (FY25 0.75x); interest cover 10.9x. [Screener] [Ind-Ra May-26]
- Working capital is heavy: inventory 389 days, cash conversion cycle 323 days (FY26); electrodes take 4–5 months to make. CFO FY24 612 → FY25 280 → FY26 213; free cash flow 244 → 102 → −35. Ind-Ra expects FCF negative again in FY27. [Screener] [Ind-Ra May-26]
- Greentech entity: ~₹1,500 cr gross debt expected by 31 Mar 2027; ₹600 cr of Malana-acquisition debt in BEL to be retired. [Q1 call]
- Dividend: final ₹3.4/share for FY26. [Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and cost: single largest plant; ran >90% while Western peers ran 60–65%; management claims "lowest-cost producer". [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- High entry barriers: HEG was "the last new entrant in the Western world in 1976"; no new Western capacity announced in 25 years except HEG's. [IP Q1FY27 p.2–3] [Ind-Ra May-26]
- Customer spread: ~35 countries, top-20 global steelmakers. [IP Q1FY27 p.3]
- Weak point: no captive needle coke (unlike GrafTech, ~75–80% integrated); only 3–4 needle-coke suppliers globally. HEG's GrafTech stake was explicitly bought as a hedge on a needle-coke squeeze. [Q4 call, official]
- Pricing: peers announced increases — one of $600–1,200/t, another $930/t; HEG "will follow suit" from Q3–Q4. [Q1 call, official]

## Risks and red flags
- **US trade case:** Commerce preliminary countervailing duty on HEG **6.99%** (Graphite India 3.68%, others 5.87%), 30 Jul 2026; petitioners are Resonac and Tokai Carbon's US units. Anti-dumping preliminary was due by 22 Sep 2026 (the petition alleged dumping margins of 42.59–73.40% for India); result not found in public sources by 27 Sep. Finals due by 7 Dec 2026. The US was **17% of FY26 revenue**, not the "<10%" management said on the Q1 call. [US Federal Register 30 Jul 2026 & 13 Jul 2026] [AR FY26 Note 37] [Ind-Ra May-26]
- **Needle coke:** up $200–300/t; total electrode cost up 10–15%; lands in P&L from ~Nov–Jan. If price hikes lag, H2 margins fall back. [Q1 call]
- **GrafTech stake (9.98%, ₹283 cr cost in May 2025)** stays with HEG Graphite and swings reported profit through fair-value changes (Q4 loss, Q1 gains). [Ind-Ra May-26] [Q1 call]
- **Scheme/related-party:** the merger issued ~13 cr new shares; promoter holding rose from 56.27% to 60.72% (LNJ Spark Advisory got 4.05 cr shares, 12.29%). The BEL swap ratio (8:7) is favourable to whoever held BEL (promoters ~42%) and should be judged against BEL's value; Ind-Ra kept HEG on "Rating Watch with Developing Implications" (IND AA-) pending clarity on how treasury and liabilities are split. [BSE SAST 21 Sep 2026] [Ind-Ra May-26]
- **Contingent liabilities:** ~₹110 cr (income tax ₹81 cr, up from ₹72 cr); auditor's only key audit matter is provisions/contingent liabilities; opinion unmodified. Corporate guarantee to SBI for TACC ₹1,230 cr — goes with Greentech. [AR FY26 Note 38, auditor's report]
- Single-plant concentration (Ind-Ra). [Ind-Ra May-26]

## My read
The electrode business is in its best shape since 2019: full plants, a 15% capacity addition that is cheap and on time, rising global EAF capacity and peers pushing $600–1,200/t price rises. But H2 FY27 is a race between price hikes (not visible until October bookings) and needle-coke inflation, and the US duty case is a real hit to the highest-price market (17% of sales). For the note, the bigger issue is structural: the listed "HEG" is now a battery-materials and power company whose value rests on an unproven anode plant, non-binding MOUs and a promoter-favourable merger; the electrode company lists separately around late October. The note should treat them as two different stocks and should not use the pre-split P/E or market cap for either.
