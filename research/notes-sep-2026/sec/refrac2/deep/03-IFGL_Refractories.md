# IFGL Refractories — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Figures in ₹ crore unless stated; history is Screener consolidated (raw page, read 27 Sep 2026). Company "EBITDA" and "total income" include other income, so they run slightly above Screener's operating profit and sales.

## Business and segments
- Kolkata-based maker of flow-control and specialised refractories for steel (slide-gate plates, nozzles, purging plugs, tundish products, tap-hole masses) plus foundry ceramics. Promoter S.K. Bajoria group holds 72.44% (Krosaki Harima/Nippon Steel exited in FY24). 10 plants: India (Kandla, Rourkela, Vizag), UK (Monocon, Sheffield Refractories), Germany/Czech (Hofmann Ceramic), USA (EI Ceramics, Mono Ceramics), China (Tianjin). [ICRA Aug-26] [IP Q1FY27 p.12–17]
- Reported by location of operations (FY26 external sales, approximate from the AR table): **India ~₹1,102 cr (~58%)**, **Europe ~₹429 cr (~23%)**, **Americas ~₹338 cr (~18%)**, Asia ex-India (China) small. Americas grew ~25% and Europe ~15% in FY26. [AR FY26 Note 36] [IP Q4FY26 p.9]
- Standalone (India) business: Q1 FY27 revenue ₹297 cr = domestic ₹228 cr (+7%) + exports ₹69 cr (+9%); mix 77:23. FY26 domestic ₹864 cr (+20%); exports fell 11%. Top five customers 30–35% of India sales. [IP Q1FY27 p.6] [Q4 call, official] [ICRA Aug-26]
- Total Refractory Management (TRM — the supplier runs the refractory work inside the steel plant and is paid per tonne of steel) is "gaining acceptance"; FY26 wins include a 1,000+ heat EOF campaign at Vizag and a record porous-plug life at Tata Steel Kalinganagar. [Q4 call, official]
- The US subsidiaries now earn "high-teen" margins; Europe (Monocon UK, Sheffield) and Hofmann are loss-making or near break-even. [Q4 call] [ICRA Aug-26]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue: Q1 FY26 454 → Q2 489 → Q3 469 → Q4 483 → Q1 FY27 512 (+13% YoY). Operating profit 36 → 38 → 23 → 39 → 37; OPM 8% → 8% → 4.9% → 8% → 7%. Net profit 11 → 13 → −3 → 14 → 17. [Screener]
- Company figures Q1 FY27: consolidated total income ₹515 cr, EBITDA ₹40 cr (7.7%, vs 8.5%), PAT ₹17 cr (+58%, helped by the end of ₹26.7 cr/yr goodwill amortisation — depreciation fell to ₹13.6 cr from ₹19.2 cr). Standalone EBITDA ₹31.4 cr, margin 10.5% (13.5% a year ago); gross margin 43% (47%). [IP Q1FY27 p.5–9] [Q1 call, official]
- Annual: revenue FY24 1,639 → FY25 1,653 → FY26 1,894; operating profit 155 → 130 → 137; OPM 9% → 8% → 7%; net profit 82 → 43 → 35. Company FY26 EBITDA ₹146 cr (7.7%); adjusted PAT ₹40 cr. Standalone FY26 EBITDA ₹126 cr (11.3%). [Screener] [Q4 call, official]
- Longer view: 5-year sales CAGR 13% but 5-year profit CAGR −11%; ROCE fell from 14% (FY21) to 5% (FY26). An investor on the Q4 call called the "5-year downward trajectory in our return metrics" the core concern. [Screener] [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (2 Jun 2026): domestic "at least… double-digit growth" in FY27; US margins "sustainable"; Hofmann "closer to a breakeven" in FY27; Monocon UK break-even by Q4 FY27; Sheffield technology products "ready by end of this year"; Khurda greenfield work "has picked up"; Gujarat JV awaiting Press Note 3 approval. [Q4 call, official]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: domestic +7% (below double-digit, management repeats the full-year double-digit target); consolidated revenue +13%; standalone EBITDA −17% on raw-material and LPG costs; Europe segment loss widened from ~₹3 cr to ~₹7 cr QoQ (Sheffield hit by British Steel's blast-furnace outage). Price increases are "temporary" and only cover input costs — "no additional margin on this account". Long-term consolidated target softened from 12% EBITDA to "double-digit… depends on how the geopolitical situation stand". [Q1 call, official]
- Verdict: revenue **delivering**, margin **missed**, Europe **mixed**, capex plans **stalled** (see below).

## Capex and capacity
- Two big projects announced (Q4 FY26 presentation): **Khordha/Khurda, Odisha** greenfield dolomite bricks, ₹300–350 cr, "end of FY28"; **Bhachau, Gujarat** basic bricks JV with China's Marvels group, ₹300 cr, FY29. [IP Q4FY26 p.12]
- Both are now on hold. ICRA (25 Aug 2026): the JV application "has been closed by the appropriate authority of Government of India due to concerns regarding proposed project location", and Khordha "has been put on hold due to the ongoing geopolitical tensions in the Middle East". Management on the Q1 call said only that the government "advised us to change location and apply for the same again". Money spent so far on the JV is "mostly land". [ICRA Aug-26] [Q1 call, official]
- FY27 capex commitment only ₹50–60 cr (ICRA); standalone capital commitments ₹35 cr at Mar-26 (₹6 cr a year earlier). [ICRA Aug-26] [AR FY26 Note 34]
- Past capex: Vizag and Rourkela expansions and an R&D centre (~₹20 cr on R&D per CFO); Mag-carbon bricks and casting flux lines could add ₹150–200 cr revenue at peak. Capacity/utilisation figures are not disclosed. [Q1 call, official]
- Growth drivers that need little capex: Sheffield technology transfer (plastic ramming mass now made at Vizag; next wave of trials), US products made locally (thin-slab tundish SEN designed at the India R&D centre), Monocon's move into foundries, Australia and Saudi offices, British Steel restart and Speciality Steel (ex-Liberty) Rotherham melt-shop restart expected Nov–Dec 2026. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Consolidated debt ₹195.6 cr, cash ₹122 cr at Mar-26 (net debt ~₹74 cr); gearing 0.2x; interest cover 7.9x; DSCR 3.5x; debt repayments ~₹16 cr in FY27. ICRA reaffirmed [ICRA]AA- (Stable)/A1+. [Q4 call] [ICRA Aug-26]
- Cash from operations FY24 152 → FY25 28 → FY26 63; free cash flow 18 → −44 → 0. Working capital: debtor days 82, inventory days 152, payable days 86, cash conversion cycle 148 days (FY26). [Screener]
- The company moved from net cash to net debt over FY22–FY26 while building Vizag/Rourkela; management calls the debt "very negligible". [Q4 call]
- Dividend ₹2.15/share for FY26 (after a 1:1 bonus; equity capital doubled to ₹72 cr). [Q4 call] [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Technical record: zircon nozzles and porous plugs with long campaign lives; first all-Vizag EOF lining passed 1,000 heats; record porous-plug life in a 300-t ladle at Tata Steel Kalinganagar. [Q4 call]
- Share gains in India: domestic revenue grew ~20% in FY26 and ~22% CAGR over seven years (investor's figure, not disputed), far above steel output growth; gains come from "smaller and more specialized players". [Q4 call]
- ICRA: pricing power is "limited owing to intense competition from large and established global refractory manufacturers"; but imports are "not prevalent" in IFGL's Indian segment, "which helps maintain the price discipline to an extent". [ICRA Aug-26]
- Weak point: it accepts "a relatively competitive pricing environment" to win brick and casting-flux accounts ("margins… may initially be lean"). [Q4 call]
- US: local manufacturing is the edge — "we are manufacturing in US for the US". [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Stalled growth capex**: both greenfield projects on hold; the JV location was rejected. The stored note's growth story leans on these plants. [ICRA Aug-26]
- **Management churn**: previous CEO (Arasu) left, followed by others including the R&D head (raised on the Q4 call); Mihir Bajoria (joined the board 24 May 2025) is now MD; Mukesh Rawal returns from the US as CEO India from 16 Aug 2026; Manoj Rakhecha is CEO International. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27 p.19]
- **Governance lapse**: the corporate-governance certificate is *qualified* — shareholder approval for M.P. Bajoria's appointment came four days after the deadline under SEBI rule 17(1C). Statutory audit opinion is unmodified. [AR FY26 CG certificate]
- **Auditor emphasis of matter**: (a) the 2016 amalgamation was accounted by the purchase method creating ₹267 cr goodwill that Ind AS would not have allowed (now fully amortised); (b) an income-tax appeal on Section 10AA (tax ₹8.3 cr) pending in Calcutta High Court. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Contingent tax claims**: income-tax disputes ₹11.2 cr; goodwill-depreciation claims disallowed/reopened for AY2018-21 with tax impact ~₹40 cr; 10AA ₹8.3 cr. [AR FY26 Note 33]
- **Europe losses** and exposure to UK steel (British Steel, Speciality Steel) — old Liberty dues unsecured with administrators. [Q1 call] [ICRA Aug-26]
- **Raw materials**: large share imported from China; LPG shortages in Q1. [ICRA Aug-26] [Q1 call]
- No material related-party transactions reported; no promoter pledge. [AR FY26 Directors' report]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 tone: resilient, leadership reset, projects progressing. Q1 tone: defensive on margins, JV "on hold", Europe loss up, reliance on UK steel restarts.
- Numbers: standalone margin down to 10.5% from 11.8% in Q4; the 12% consolidated margin goal replaced by "double-digit"; staff cost ~10% of standalone revenue flagged.
- New issue after the Q1 call: ICRA's disclosure that Khordha is on hold and the JV application was closed — neither said plainly on the call.

## Best management quotes
- "these are all temporary price increase… no additional margin on this account we see." — Amit Agarwal, Q1 call
- "they have advised us to change location and apply for the same again." — Q1 call, on the China JV
- "we are targeting breakeven performance by Q4 FY27" — Manoj Rakhecha, Q4 call, on Monocon

## My read
IFGL is two businesses: a good, share-gaining Indian flow-control franchise (double-digit growth, ~11% standalone margin) and an overseas portfolio where the US is now strong but Europe keeps losing money. Consolidated margin has slid from 15% (FY21) to 7% and returns to ~5%, and the two capex projects that were meant to lift growth are both on hold, so FY27–FY29 growth must come from share gains, Sheffield products and a European recovery. The stock needs Europe to break even and standalone margins back above 12% to justify earnings growth in the mid-teens; if Q2–Q3 show margins still near 7% consolidated, a lower growth estimate is warranted. A restart of the Odisha project with a clear funding plan, or UK steel restarts lifting Europe to profit, would change the view upward.
