# Eldeco Housing & Industries Ltd (ELDEHSG, BSE 523329)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** the listed Lucknow arm of the Eldeco group (CMD Pankaj Bajaj; Group CEO Manish Jaiswal). It builds townships, plotted developments, villas, group housing and small commercial projects. Promoters hold 54.83%. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- **Formats:** horizontal (plots and villas, gross margin 50–60%) and vertical (apartments, lower margin). Solano Gardens' blended margin is 35–40%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Key projects:**
  - Eldeco City (Imperia Phases 1–2, Latitude 27, Trinity, Skywalk, Twin Towers).
  - Solano Gardens: FY26 launch, 343 of 433 units sold, over Rs 384 cr booked.
  - A commercial strip in Eldeco City (Imperia Avenue / City Courtyard).

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Forthcoming projects (3.39 mn sq ft):** Eldeco City (1.57 lakh), Solano group housing (5.18 lakh), City Courtyard (0.37 lakh), LDA GH-7 (5.62 lakh), LDA GH-8 (7.35 lakh), Shaheed Path (10.07 lakh), Palm Springs (3.73 lakh). [IP Q1FY27]
- **Land bank:** 95.5 acres, including a 50.3-acre plot under a legally binding contract (Q1 FY27) and 32 acres at an undisclosed site. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- **Accounting:** revenue is booked on completion/possession, so it is lumpy and lags bookings. [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **No explicit bookings guidance** on either call.
  - Q4 call: unsold plus to-launch GDV of about Rs 4,000 cr, to be sold "over the next 5 to 7 years".
  - Q1 call: FY27 bookings should show "strong growth" over Rs 744 cr, and "FY28 onwards is going to be a pivotal change".

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Margins (Q4 call):** FY27 EBITDA 30–35% and PAT about 25%, driven by Imperia 2 (about Rs 140 cr of recognition). Q1 delivered 37% and 30%, **ahead**. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Launch timing drifted, in the more optimistic direction:**
  - Q4 call: "no major launches" in Q1/Q2; the Rs 2,000 cr of new parcels would launch "towards the end of the year, if at all"; designs were not final.
  - Q1 call: "almost all" of forthcoming projects 4–7 (about 26.8 lakh sq ft) to launch within FY27.
  - The Q1 deck still shows these as "under planning" or "final approval awaited".

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Latitude 27** (Rs 270–300 cr):
  - Q4 call: possessions "later this year… or maybe early next year".
  - Q1 call: 15–20% recognised in Mar–May 2027, partly slipping to FY28. The war-related four-month RERA extension was cited.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Legacy ready stock** (about Rs 75 cr): 40–60% to be sold in FY27. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Land additions:**
  - FY26: three Lucknow parcels (two Lucknow Development Authority auctions plus Shaheed Path), about 2 mn sq ft, GDV about Rs 2,000 cr.
  - Q1 FY27: a 50.3-acre contract and about 15 acres aggregated.
  - GDV of the 50-acre site not disclosed; the vertical/horizontal mix is "under internal debate".

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Execution:** construction spend of Rs 178 cr in FY26 and Rs 58 cr in Q1 FY27; all projects got a four-month RERA extension because of Middle East war disruption (commodities, labour). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Borrowings:** up from about Rs 3 cr (FY23) to Rs 154 cr (Mar-26) to fund land. Cash from operations was +Rs 104 cr in FY26 after two negative years. [Screener]
- **Customer advances:** other liabilities rose to Rs 499 cr from Rs 322 cr, reflecting Solano bookings. [Screener]
- **No current credit rating:** the last Screener-listed rating is from India Ratings in 2016. [Screener]
- **Buyback / capital return:** "under active consideration" after investor pressure. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Brand and execution record in Lucknow:** Solano Gardens sold 79% of launched units at launch. Plotted formats earn 50–60% gross margin. [Q4 call]
- **Low-cost land from government auctions**, which management says gives lower risk and faster monetisation. [Q4 call]
- **Market share:** Eldeco holds under 10% of a Lucknow market of about Rs 5,000 cr a year, per a Q4 call summary; rising construction cost is its main concern. [News: GuruFocus/Investing.com Q4 summary]
- **Weak spots:**
  - Single-city exposure (Lucknow).
  - Small scale (FY26 revenue Rs 176 cr).
  - Some vertical projects sell slowly (Trinity's Faith tower had 2–3 bookings in its first two weeks).

  [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- War disruption (April–May 2026) hit commodities and labour; RERA granted extensions. Cost impact was not quantified. [Q1 call]
- Realisations on new projects are about Rs 8,000–11,000 per sq ft, above the portfolio average. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party loan, now repaid:**
  - Term loans to promoter company Eldeco Infrastructure & Properties (Rs 27.5 cr from 2019 plus Rs 11.5 cr from 2022, at 8%) were extended again in Aug 2025.
  - The outstanding balance of Rs 52.3 cr (maximum Rs 54.5 cr) was **fully repaid during FY26**, clearing a long-running governance concern.

  [AR FY26]
- **Unconfirmed balances:** a note to the accounts (note 39) says receivables, payables and advances are "subject to reconciliation and confirmation"; the impact is "unascertainable". [AR FY26]
- **Contingent liabilities:** small (income tax AY 2016-17 Rs 1.8 cr; bank guarantees Rs 1.3 cr). [AR FY26]
- **Management continuity:** the CMD missed the Q1 call because of a medical emergency. Guidance is vague ("educated guess"), and slide terminology changed from "area booked" to "area allotted". [Q1 call]
- **Launch dependence:** the FY27 story needs approvals on four large parcels; the Q4 call said "if at all". [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **ROE** has been below 7% for three years, with low asset turnover. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- **Bookings** fell back to Rs 106 cr (Q1) after the Rs 383 cr Solano-led Q4; collections improved (+68%).
- **Margins** rose sharply (EBITDA 37%) on Imperia Phase 2.
- **Land:** a 50.3-acre contract was added; the forthcoming pipeline is now 3.39 mn sq ft.
- **Launch timing** moved from "end of year, if at all" to "almost all within FY27".
- **Latitude 27** revenue partly slipped to FY28.

## My read
Eldeco is a small, high-margin Lucknow developer that has shifted from a sleepy, cash-rich balance sheet to active land buying (about Rs 2,000 cr of GDV plus 50 acres). Solano Gardens showed it can sell plotted townships quickly. The repaid promoter loan removes a governance overhang, but guidance is deliberately vague, the new parcels are still in planning, and Latitude 27 revenue is slipping. FY27 profit should look strong on Imperia 2 margins; the real test is whether the four big parcels launch by March 2027 as now implied.
