# Raymond Realty Ltd (RAYMONDREL, BSE 544420, ISIN INE1SY401010)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million. Demerged from Raymond Ltd (appointed date 1 Apr 2025); listed 1 Jul 2025, so history is short.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** a Mumbai-region (MMR) residential developer controlled by the Raymond group (Gautam Singhania family; promoters 50.93%). It began in 2019 on Raymond's former factory land in Thane. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- **Two "engines":**
  1. **Own land:** 100 acres at Pokhran Road, Thane, with GDV about Rs 25,000 cr. About 65 acres are under development (6.7 mn sq ft, GDV Rs 16,500 cr). So far Rs 9,400 cr is sold, Rs 7,460 cr collected, and 11 towers (about 4,000 homes) delivered. Prices there are about Rs 23,000–25,000 per sq ft. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
  2. **Asset-light joint developments (JDAs):** eight projects with GDV about Rs 27,000 cr (52% of total).
     - Launched: Bandra East, BKC, Wadala, Sion — GDV Rs 11,500 cr, Rs 2,900 cr sold.
     - Not yet launched: two in Mahim, Kandivali (70% revenue share to Raymond), and Parel (a slum-redevelopment project, GDV Rs 8,500 cr).

  [Q1 call] [Q4 call] [News: Business Standard, 15 Jul 2026]
- **Total GDV:** Rs 52,000 cr, of which launched Rs 28,000 cr and to be launched Rs 24,000 cr. Unsold launched stock is Rs 15,700 cr. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- **Brands:** TenX (aspirational), Address by GS (premium), Invictus by GS (luxury), plus small retail launches each year. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Mix shift:** JDAs were 64% of Q1 FY27 bookings (54% in FY26, 22% in FY25); Thane is now about one-third. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Accounting:** revenue is recognised on the **percentage-of-completion method** (as construction progresses), which smooths reported revenue. [Q4 call] [AR FY26]

## Guidance history
- **FY27 (Q4 call):**
  - pre-sales and revenue growth of at least 20%;
  - EBITDA margin **16–18%** ("flat to marginally upward").

  [Q4 call]
- **FY27 (Q1 call):**
  - pre-sales and revenue growth of at least 20%, **kept**;
  - EBITDA margin now described as **17–19%**, "committed to the market over the last few interactions". That is higher than the 16–18% given in May;
  - ROCE of at least 20%.

  [Q1 call]
- **Land additions (business development):** FY26 guidance was Rs 5,000–10,000 cr of new GDV; about Rs 3,000 cr was delivered, a **miss**. In FY27 so far Parel (Rs 8,500 cr) and Kandivali are signed. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Launches:** two Mahim JDAs (about Rs 2,500 cr and about Rs 2,000–2,200 cr) in Nov–Dec 2026 and Feb–Mar 2027. Kandivali in FY28; Parel about 18 months away. Mahim was signed in 2023 and has taken about 2.5 years. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **50:50 JDA/own-land mix** target (for FY27) was reached a year early, in FY26. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **JDA capital:** about Rs 300–350 cr per Rs 2,000 cr project, mostly approval costs paid to government plus a 10–15% deposit to the landowner.
  - Parel: management says peak Rs 450–500 cr.
  - The press reported about Rs 700 cr peak investment and about 40% revenue share to the development partner.

  [Q1 call] [News: Business Standard, 15 Jul 2026]
- **Thane cash:** releases about Rs 450–500 cr a year; JDAs add about Rs 100–150 cr. Management says the company overall will be **cash-negative for the next two years** while approval costs for new launches are paid. [Q4 call]
- **Thane commercial:** an area is earmarked but not yet activated. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Jun-26:** gross debt Rs 1,095 cr; cash Rs 271 cr; net debt Rs 824 cr; net debt to equity 0.7x (internal cap 1x). Blended cost 9.6%. [Q1 call]
- **Mar-26:** net debt Rs 656 cr. [Q4 call]
- **Operating cash flow:** -Rs 910 cr in FY26 (Screener).
- **Interest:** FY27 guided at about Rs 100–120 cr. That includes interest on government approval-premium instalments (about Rs 45 cr in FY26, charged at 8–9%) as well as bank term loans (about Rs 50 cr). [Q1 call]
- **Dividend:** small (payout 4%). [Screener]
- **Credit rating:** CARE A+/Stable, reaffirmed 6 Aug 2026, on Rs 1,000 cr of bank facilities. [CARE Aug-26]
- **Funding options:** AIF money at project (SPV) level; new equity is the "last resort". [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Brand and land:** Raymond brand plus low-cost legacy land at a prime Thane location. TenX Habitat (3,100 homes) is sold out and fully handed over. [Q4 call]
- **Sales record:** over 85% of revenue potential sold in projects launched before FY26 (CARE). Rs 2,900 cr sold across four new JDAs within about a year. [CARE Aug-26] [Q1 call]
- **JDA structure:** the company takes 100% development control, with step-in rights on rehabilitation work. [Q1 call]
- **Weak spot:** Thane competition is intense (Lodha, Adani, Oberoi, Kalpataru, Piramal), with "very marginal" price rises. [Q4 call]

## Input costs
- War-related cost pressure is seen as temporary. Scenario analysis suggests a 3–4% cost impact if prolonged, which management expects to pass on to buyers. [Q4 call]
- Suppliers (for example ceramics, despite gas shortages at Morbi) are holding contracted rates. [Q4 call]
- JDA projects start at single-digit margins and build to about 20% as prices rise. Own-land margins are 25–26%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Cash burn and leverage:** operating cash flow -Rs 910 cr in FY26; debt more than doubled year-on-year; interest is now eating most operating profit (Q1 net profit Rs 13 cr). Management guides cash-negative for two years. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- **Income-tax survey:** at company offices in Sep 2025; CARE is monitoring. [CARE Aug-26]
- **Execution:** slum-redevelopment (Parel) and society-redevelopment JDAs carry long timelines (Mahim about 2.5 years). CARE flags a "limited track record in large-scale real estate development". [CARE Aug-26] [Q4 call]
- **Guidance consistency:** the EBITDA margin range drifted from 16–18% to 17–19% between calls without explanation. The FY26 land-addition target was missed. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities:** GST about Rs 19.8 cr. A tenancy-rights claim over part of the Thane land is unquantified; revenue courts have ruled for the company. [AR FY26]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion. The emphasis of matter only covers demerger accounting; the key audit matter is percentage-of-completion revenue. [AR FY26]
- **Related parties:** no material related-party transactions; no AOC-2 filing needed. [AR FY26]
- **Pledges:** none disclosed. Promoter holding rose from 48.87% to 50.93%.
- **Institutions leaving:** FII plus DII holdings fell from about 22% at listing to about 8.8%. Management blames index and size limits and has hired a new IR head. [Screener] [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- **Land deal:** Parel JDA signed (GDV Rs 8,500 cr); total GDV rose from Rs 42,000 cr to Rs 52,000 cr.
- **Debt:** net debt rose from Rs 656 cr to Rs 824 cr; quarterly interest jumped.
- **Margin guidance:** restated from 16–18% to 17–19%.
- **Management:** new investor-relations head; a new SPV ("TenX Mahalakshmi Ltd") incorporated for a possible deal.

## My read
- **Scaling fast:** from one Thane township to a Rs 52,000 cr pipeline, largely through capital-light JDAs in prime Mumbai areas. Bookings are growing strongly.
- **The trade-off is cash:** operating cash flow is deeply negative, net debt is rising, and interest is compressing profit. Management says this will last two more years.
- **What would improve the view:** EBITDA margin reaching 17% or more in FY27; the Mahim launches on time; debt staying below 1x equity.
- **What would worsen the view:** JDA launch delays, a weak Thane market, an adverse outcome from the tax survey, or net debt/equity near 1x before JDA cash arrives.
