# Sri Lotus Developers & Realty Ltd (LOTUSDEV, BSE 544469, ISIN INE0V9Q01010)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million. Listed Aug 2025, so there is little history.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** a Mumbai luxury and ultra-luxury developer. It focuses on **redevelopment** of old housing societies and commercial buildings in the western suburbs (Juhu, Versova, Andheri West, Bandra) and increasingly in south Mumbai (Prabhadevi, Napean Sea Road). [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- **Asset-light model:** it does not buy land. It signs development agreements with societies and, per the CEO, invests roughly 10% of GDV. Construction is about 20% of the selling price. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Pipeline:**
  - 22 projects (17 residential, 5 commercial), 17 of them redevelopment; GDV about Rs 17,500–18,000 cr including the new Juhu commercial project.
  - A GIFT City mixed-use project (about 1 mn sq ft carpet area, freehold, GDV Rs 2,000–2,200 cr) is awaiting mixed-use zoning, expected by year-end.

  [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Pricing:** average selling price about Rs 86,000 per sq ft (Q1 FY27). Management claims a 10–25% price premium to peers in the same micro-market. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Recent launches:**
  - FY26: Amalfi (Versova), The Arcadian (Juhu), Varun (Bandra), Celestia (Versova, GDV Rs 1,400–1,500 cr).
  - Q1 FY27: Lotus Trident (Andheri West) and Lotus Aquaria (Prabhadevi, from about Rs 85,000 per sq ft); combined GDV Rs 1,350 cr.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Accounting:** revenue is recognised on the **percentage-of-completion** method, unlike most peers, which recognise at handover. Reported revenue therefore tracks construction progress. [AR FY26]

## Guidance history
- **FY26:** pre-sales of Rs 1,157 cr met guidance. The Q3 call's promise that Q4 collections would be "much better" was **missed** (Rs 82 cr). [Q4 call]
- **FY27 (given on the Q4 call, repeated on the Q1 call):**
  - pre-sales Rs 1,800–2,000 cr;
  - revenue and PAT growth of 55–60%;
  - EBITDA margin 35–40%, PAT margin 25–30%.
  - **Kept.** On the Q1 call the CEO spoke of an EBITDA range of "33–36%", slightly softer.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Launches: slipped.**
  - Q4 call: six launches in FY27 (GDV Rs 5,000–5,500 cr). Aquaria, Trident and Sky Plaza in H1; the other three "next quarter" (i.e. Q2).
  - Q1 call: only Aquaria and Trident launched. Sky Plaza (about Rs 1,500 cr) moved to end-H1 or Q3; Odyssey (Rs 1,000 cr) to Q4; Portofino (about Rs 500 cr) within 3–4 months; Aurelia (Rs 600–700 cr) undated.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Collections:** Q4 call said collections would pick up from Q1. They did rise (Rs 150 cr), but the FY27 target set on the Q1 call is only about Rs 1,000 cr, well below pre-sales. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Delayed projects:** Monarch, Avalon and Solana are each 3–4 quarters behind. One involves rebuilding an existing hospital; Monarch has "paperwork… many stakeholders". [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Business development:** plan to sign 4–6 new projects in FY27 (Q4 call). One so far: the Juhu commercial redevelopment (GDV Rs 1,600 cr; construction from about FY28; 3–4 years to build). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- There is no fixed-asset capex. Spending goes into projects: Rs 718 cr in FY26 (Rs 287 cr in Q4). [Q4 call]
- **IPO (Aug 2025):** fresh issue about Rs 792 cr (net Rs 732 cr), of which Rs 550 cr was earmarked for the Amalfi, Arcadian and Varun subsidiaries. Rs 271 cr of that Rs 550 cr was deployed by Jun-26. The Q4 call gave Rs 394 cr deployed overall by Mar-26. [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call]
- **Completion schedule:** Arcadian and Varun are expected to be about 90% complete by Mar-27, and Amalfi 60–70%. Varun had about 20 slabs done by Aug 2026. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Jun-26:** cash Rs 776 cr, debt Rs 153 cr, net cash Rs 623 cr (Mar-26: Rs 697 cr). [Q1 call]
- **Promoter loan:** borrowings of Rs 75 cr are owed to the promoter, Anand Pandit, at Mar-26. FY25 saw Rs 170.6 cr of promoter loans repaid. [AR FY26]
- **Equity:** rose from Rs 933 cr to Rs 1,918 cr in FY26, mainly from IPO money. [Q4 call]
- **Working capital:** days rose from 392 to 520 (Screener), because receivables and inventory are building ahead of collections. [Screener]
- **Dividends:**
  - FY26: dividend at a 50% payout to non-promoters only; the promoter group waived its share.
  - FY27: a 30% interim dividend declared in Sep 2026.
  - No dividend policy was formalised yet as of May 2026.

  [Q4 call] [Screener announcements]
- **Credit rating:** none found on Screener; the company has little external debt.

## Moat evidence
- **Redevelopment know-how in niche sea-facing and garden-facing plots**, where fresh land is almost unavailable. Management points to "blue and green" views as a scarcity premium. [Q1 call]
- **Sales speed:** Celestia booked Rs 155 cr in 7 days. Within one year: Amalfi 62% sold, Arcadian 41%, Varun 38%. Trident did about Rs 150 cr in its first month. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Pricing:** 5–10% price rises over the last year; 10–25% premium to other developers. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Record:** "Zero RERA cases" is claimed in the IP; not independently verified. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Scale is small** (Rs 769 cr revenue), and the claimed premium rests heavily on the founder's reputation in Juhu.

## Input costs
- Management said input costs were up about 7% and labour about 5% after the Middle East conflict. That translates to about 1% of project cost, or up to 1.5–2.5% overall, because construction is only about 20% of the selling price. [Q4 call]
- There are no imported materials. [Q4 call]
- Because margins are very high (about 36% EBITDA), cost inflation has a limited effect; the bigger risk is the pace of sales.

## Risks and red flags
- **Cash conversion:** collections are far below revenue and pre-sales; FY26 operating cash flow was -Rs 326 cr. Because of percentage-of-completion accounting, profit is booked well before cash comes in. [Screener] [AR FY26]
- **Concentration:** everything is in the Mumbai western suburbs plus a few south Mumbai projects. Ultra-luxury buyers are a narrow group, largely HNIs and NRIs.
- **Promoter selling:**
  - The promoter holds 81.87% and must reach 75% (the minimum public shareholding rule) within about two more years.
  - Anand Pandit sold 2.00% (97.7 lakh shares) in the open market on 10 Sep 2026, cutting the promoter holding to 79.87%.
  - More stake sales are certain, which is an overhang on the stock.

  [BSE filing 10 Sep 2026] [Q1 call]
- **Related parties:**
  - Borrowing of Rs 75 cr from the promoter.
  - Office rent of about Rs 0.96 cr to promoter-linked entities.
  - Many promoter-family entities, including film production LLPs (Aish Motion Pictures, Bombay Masti Films).
  - Management says there are no material related-party transactions.

  [AR FY26]
- **Disputes:**
  - A GST show-cause notice of Rs 10 cr (FY21, FY22, FY24) under Section 74 (the fraud or suppression section) was received after year-end.
  - Bank guarantees of Rs 15.7 cr.
  - The Ghatkopar project (GDV about Rs 600 cr) is stuck in the Supreme Court between a society and bungalow owners.

  [AR FY26] [Q4 call]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matter on percentage-of-completion revenue recognition; no emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]
- **Pledges:** none. [AR FY26]
- **Disclosure gaps:** IPO deployment figures differ between calls (Rs 394 cr vs Rs 271 cr, on different bases). The GDV shown is the company share only; free area given to tenants is excluded. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- **Launches:** from "six launches, three in H1" to two done, with Sky Plaza and Odyssey pushed out.
- **Business development:** the Juhu commercial redevelopment (Rs 1,600 cr) was added.
- **Collections:** improved (Rs 150 cr against Rs 82 cr), and a FY27 collections target of about Rs 1,000 cr was introduced.
- **EBITDA guidance:** effectively softened from 35–40% to "33–36%".
- **Ownership:** the promoter began selling down for the public-shareholding rule (after the call, in Sep 2026).

## My read
- **A high-margin, fast-growing niche developer** with a net-cash balance sheet and strong early sales in scarce sea-facing redevelopment projects.
- **The weak points:**
  - cash lags reported profit badly, because of percentage-of-completion accounting and slow collections;
  - launches are already slipping against the May guidance;
  - the promoter must sell about 5% more stock.
- **What would improve the view:**
  - collections of Rs 1,000 cr or more in FY27;
  - operating cash flow turning positive;
  - Sky Plaza, Portofino and Odyssey launching by Q4.
- **What would worsen the view:**
  - pre-sales below Rs 1,500 cr;
  - further project delays (Monarch, Ghatkopar);
  - margins falling toward 30%.
