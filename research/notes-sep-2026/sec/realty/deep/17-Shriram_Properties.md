# Shriram Properties Ltd (SHRIRAMPPS, NSE)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** a South India-focused mid-market and affordable housing developer, part of the Shriram group. Markets: Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata and (since FY26) Pune. Management: CMD M. Murali, CEO Gopalakrishnan, CFO Ravindra Pandey. Promoters own only 27.89%. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- **Operating model:** "asset-light", with joint ventures, joint development agreements and development-management deals making up over 60% of the portfolio (CRISIL). Revenue is recognised mainly on project completion (Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata), so quarterly numbers are lumpy. [CRISIL Oct-25] [Q4 call]
- **Pipeline:**
  - Ongoing: 16 mn sq ft, with 2.9 mn sq ft unsold (unsold GDV about Rs 1,970 cr).
  - Upcoming: 17.7 mn sq ft (GDV about Rs 11,560 cr).
  - Total about Rs 13,530 cr, plus 7.3 mn sq ft (over Rs 6,000 cr) at an advanced stage.

  [Q1 call]
- **Kolkata (Uttarpara) land bank:** settled with the West Bengal government in FY26 by surrendering 42 of 314 acres. About 105–110 developable acres remain, plus 2.3 mn sq ft of approved apartments. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Product mix:** moving from low-ticket Kolkata and Chennai legacy stock to mid-premium homes, villas, and "branded land" plots. Average mid-market price has risen from about Rs 5,000 per sq ft (pre-COVID) to about Rs 7,600–7,700. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY26:** sales guidance Rs 2,600 cr, **missed** (Rs 2,354 cr) on launch delays. Bengaluru e-Khata/Kaveri portal problems and occupancy-certificate delays were blamed. CRISIL had expected over Rs 2,800 cr of sales and about Rs 1,800 cr of collections. [Q4 call] [CRISIL Oct-25]
- **FY27 (Q4 call):**
  - sales **Rs 3,300–3,500 cr** (5–5.5 mn sq ft);
  - collections Rs 2,100–2,200 cr;
  - handovers 3,750–3,800 units;
  - pipeline addition 7–8 mn sq ft, GDV Rs 5,000–6,000 cr.

  [Q4 call]
- **FY27 (Q1 call):** "guidance remains unchanged".
  - About 6 mn sq ft of launches (GDV about Rs 6,000 cr), mostly in Q3–Q4.
  - Sustenance sales Rs 1,400–1,500 cr.
  - Revenue +20%+, PAT +20–25%, PBT margin 8–9%.
  - Pune Manjari (2.3 mn sq ft, bought as floor-space rights) is now shown as a Q3 launch, versus Q2 hopes.

  [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **FY26 business development:** Rs 372 cr invested across seven projects, the most in any single year. About 80% of four years' operating cash flow (Rs 900 cr+) has been reinvested. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27:** Rs 88 cr invested; added a Doddagubbi (Bengaluru) JDA of about 0.7 mn sq ft (GDV about Rs 650 cr). [Q1 call]
- **Kolkata land economics:** plots and villas earn about Rs 6.5–7.5 cr net contribution per acre, against Rs 3.5–4.5 cr per acre from a bulk sale to a warehousing buyer. Site cash potential is Rs 1,200–1,400 cr over about five years; strategy expected within a quarter. [Q1 call]
- **Handover pipeline for the rest of FY27:** about 2,900 units, revenue about Rs 1,560 cr (410 units / Rs 400 cr already completed; Q2 400 units / Rs 160 cr; H2 2,100 units / Rs 1,000 cr). [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Jun-26:** gross debt Rs 651 cr; cash Rs 219 cr; net debt Rs 432 cr; net debt/equity 0.29x; cost of debt about 11%. Net free cash flow in Q1 was Rs 47 cr. [Q1 call]
- **Mar-26:** net debt Rs 438 cr; Rs 358 cr of undrawn lines. [Q4 call]
- **Gearing may rise temporarily in FY27** to fund growth; long-term comfort level is 0.5x. [Q1 call]
- **CRISIL A-/Positive**, reaffirmed 28 Oct 2025 (Rs 263 cr of bank facilities).
  - Expected bank debt Rs 850–950 cr including JVs.
  - Refinancing risk flagged.
  - The company has given corporate guarantees for subsidiary debt.

  [CRISIL Oct-25]
- **Screener cash from operations** was -Rs 16 cr in FY26, against the company's Rs 271 cr, which is before business-development spend and on the company's share. [Screener] [Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Brand and launch absorption:**
  - Spectrum, Pune: 300+ units in under a year, in a micro-market of about 850 units a year.
  - Songs of the Earth, Bengaluru: 85%.
  - Southbrook plots: about 55% in 30 days.
  - Stellar, Chennai: about 20% at launch.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Delivery:** eight projects (about 4 mn sq ft) delivered in FY26, "ahead of RERA timelines" per the company. [Q4 call]
- **Weak spots:** low reported margins, dependence on other operating income, and a low promoter stake (27.9%). [Screener]

## Input costs
- Construction-cost pressure of about 8–10%; sustenance prices rise only about 4–5% a year. Management expects margin protection, not expansion, from pricing. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- The margin recovery depends on legacy low-price projects (Grand One, Sunshine One at Rs 18–24 lakh tickets; Shankari at Rs 4,500–5,000 per sq ft) finishing their handovers. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Enforcement Directorate search** of company premises on 23 Oct 2024. The auditor has an **emphasis of matter**; no findings have been communicated to the company. [AR FY26]
- **Income-tax contingent liabilities:** about **Rs 628 cr** at the parent plus Rs 92 cr at subsidiaries. These include disallowed losses and a section 153C (search-linked) assessment. Together they are about half of net worth; the company is contesting them. [AR FY26]
- **Other items:** RERA customer compensation cases, land litigation and an environmental land case. The company is committed to funding two loss-making JVs. [AR FY26]
- **Key audit matter:** recoverability of investments, loans and receivables in joint ventures. [AR FY26]
- **Guidance record:** FY26 sales missed; FY27 is back-ended (Q3–Q4 launches); Q1 revenue and margins were weak. Management admitted "some disappointment last year". [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Earnings quality:** fair-value gains and reversals of constructive obligations inside "other operating revenue", tax write-backs, and FY26 legacy provisions (Rs 7 cr impairment, Rs 10 cr landowner-recoverable provision). [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Bengaluru approvals** (e-Khata) and **Maharashtra RERA** timing remain launch risks. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- **Sales:** Q1 was a record for a first quarter (Rs 484 cr); guidance kept, with no upgrade.
- **Kolkata:** moved from "evolving strategy" to a tested plotted and villa model (55% of plots sold in 30 days); bulk land sale effectively ruled out.
- **Pipeline:** net debt steady (Rs 438 cr to Rs 432 cr); Doddagubbi JDA added.
- **Disclosure:** management committed to resume quarterly operational updates after investor criticism.
- **Tone:** more cautious on FY28 ("deliver FY27 and then talk about FY28").

## My read
Shriram trades below book value because its reported margins are thin and it has a history of missing guidance, even though the balance sheet is sound (net debt/equity 0.29x, CRISIL A-/Positive) and handovers are building. FY27 hinges on H2 launches and roughly Rs 1,560 cr of handovers; its Kolkata land is now a real cash lever (Rs 1,200–1,400 cr potential). The ED search and the large contested tax claims are governance overhangs worth tracking. Credibility rests on hitting Rs 3,300–3,500 cr of sales, which would need about Rs 2,850 cr+ in the remaining nine months.
