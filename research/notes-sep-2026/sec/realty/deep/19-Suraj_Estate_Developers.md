# Suraj Estate Developers Ltd (SURAJEST, NSE)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** a redevelopment-focused developer in South Central Mumbai (Mahim, Dadar, Prabhadevi, Shivaji Park, Parel). It was founded in 1986 and listed in Dec 2023; promoters hold 69.8%. Management: WTD Rahul Thomas, CFO Shreepal Shah. [Screener] [Q1 call]
- **Specialty:** tenant-occupied ("cessed") building redevelopment. The company claims a 61% share of redevelopment in its market and over 1,011 homes rebuilt free for tenants. [Screener]
- **Three segments:** value luxury, luxury, and commercial. Commercial now dominates:
  - Suraj One Business Bay, Mahim: about 33% sold at about Rs 50,000 per sq ft. An adjoining plot adds about 1.5 lakh sq ft carpet and Rs 800 cr of GDV, taking combined GDV above Rs 2,000 cr.

  [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Recent land:**
  - Hally Pacific Pvt Ltd (Sayani Road, Prabhadevi), about Rs 30.4 cr, GDV Rs 200 cr.
  - Dadar West plot, Rs 18 cr, GDV about Rs 100 cr.
  - Bandra (Mount Mary) land: 2.5–2.8 lakh sq ft at Rs 1–1.5 lakh per sq ft, with two conveyances still pending.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Accounting:** percentage-of-completion method. [Q4 call]

- **Bandra approval premiums:** Rs 300–350 cr, "through our internal accruals", with construction finance later. [Q1 call]
- **Unsold ongoing inventory:** residential 22,000 sq ft (about Rs 109 cr); commercial 1.4 lakh sq ft (about Rs 841 cr); about Rs 950 cr in total. Residential sales are limited by thin stock. [Q1 call]
- **Business Bay:** a target to sell at least another 1 lakh sq ft in FY27. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Tenant-redevelopment know-how** and long presence in South Central Mumbai, where land is scarce. The company says societies prefer an "area-known" builder and deal terms are "pretty standard". [Q1 call] [Screener]
- **High margins on legacy land:** EBITDA 37–40%. New commercial projects on purchased land earn only 25–30%, so the mix will dilute margins. [Q4 call]
- **Weak spots:** very small stock of saleable residential units; reliance on a single commercial project for FY27 pre-sales. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Not quantified on either call.
- Commercial land bought outright raises land cost as a share of revenue. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Liquidity strain:** overdue statutory income tax and unpaid advance tax flagged by the auditor; falling collections (Q1 Rs 86 cr); rising net debt; negative cash from operations. [AR FY26] [Q1 call] [Screener]
- **Contested dues (CARO):** GST Rs 35.2 cr (Apr 2018 to Mar 2023), income tax about Rs 18.7 cr (FY18 Rs 6.3 cr; FY24 Rs 12.2 cr), service tax Rs 1.1 cr. [AR FY26]
- **Execution concentration:** Business Bay Phase 2 (Rs 800 cr) needs RERA amendment; Bandra needs plots tied up and Rs 300–350 cr of premiums. [Q1 call]
- **Guidance drift:** the launch pipeline fell from about Rs 2,000 cr to Rs 1,600 cr, and the debt outlook flipped from "normalising" to "rising", within one quarter. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Market signal:** the stock rose 13% on 21 Sep 2026. The exchange asked for clarification on the price movement (23 Sep 2026); the company replied that no material information was undisclosed. [BSE filing 24 Sep 2026] [News: Whalesbook, Sep 2026]
- **Auditor:** no emphasis of matter or qualification on the financial statements themselves. The key audit matter is revenue recognition. [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- **FY27 pre-sales guidance** issued at Rs 700 cr (deferred in May). Q1 pre-sales rose 74%, but collections fell 25%.
- **Launch pipeline** cut from about Rs 2,000 cr to Rs 1,600 cr and phased by quarter; Business Bay Phase 2 plan approved.
- **Net debt** rose from Rs 580 cr to Rs 614 cr; the debt outlook turned from "back to normal" to a temporary increase.
- **Dadar West land** added (Rs 18 cr); acquisition of the Mahim development rights completed (per a later filing).

## My read
Suraj has a real niche in South Central Mumbai redevelopment, earns high margins on old land, and hit its FY26 sales guidance. But it is now levering up to buy expensive land (commercial Mahim, Prabhadevi, Bandra), while collections are slipping and the auditor reports late income-tax payments. Those are early liquidity warnings for a company with no credit rating. FY27 depends on Business Bay Phase 2 launching in Q3 and on commercial buyers paying on schedule. I would watch net debt, statutory dues and collections closely before trusting the growth plan.
