# Mahindra Lifespace Developers Ltd (MAHLIFE, BSE 532313, ISIN INE813A01018)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** the real estate arm of Mahindra & Mahindra (M&M holds 52.41%). It has two businesses. [Screener] [AR FY26]
  - **Residential:** focused on three cities — Mumbai region (MMR), Pune and Bengaluru, with a small presence in Chennai. It is leaving affordable housing and moving to "mid-premium / premium" homes. It avoids luxury homes priced above about Rs 60,000–70,000 per sq ft or tickets above about Rs 10 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
  - **Integrated cities and industrial clusters (IC&IC):** Mahindra World City in Chennai and Jaipur, and "Origins" industrial parks in Chennai, Ahmedabad and Pune. It leases industrial land, often to Japanese clients through its partner Sumitomo. [Q4 call]
- **Development pipeline (GDV) and targets:**
  - about Rs 50,000 cr in total (Jun-26);
  - regional target split 60% Mumbai / 20% Pune / 20% Bengaluru;
  - currently about Rs 35,000 cr is in Mumbai and about Rs 5,000 cr each in the other two cities;
  - much of Mumbai is society redevelopment, which is slow.

  [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Large projects:**

| Project | Size / GDV | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Mahindra Rainforest, Bhandup (Kanjur) | GDV above Rs 12,000 cr | Phase 1 launched late Q1 FY27 |
| Thane (Ghodbunder) land | about 6–6.5 mn sq ft mixed use, GDV about Rs 7,500 cr | re-zoned residential |
| K2 Kandivali East | 15 acres, 1.8 mn sq ft, GDV about Rs 5,600 cr | bought Jun 2026 |
| Mahalakshmi (south Mumbai), Beacon Hill, WestEra | pricing Rs 50,000–60,000 per sq ft | launched or launching |

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [News: Business Standard, 19 Jun 2026]
- **Partners:**
  - **Mitsui Fudosan** (Japan's largest real estate company) has a multi-project residential partnership, starting with 49% of Mahindra Blossom in Bengaluru, and has committed to another deal.
  - **Sumitomo** is the partner in the Origins Chennai phases (OC1, OC2A, OC2B).

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY26 promises:** eight occupancy certificates planned and eight received (two slipped into early April). Five of eight planned launches happened; Rainforest slipped from FY26 to Q1 FY27. [Q4 call]
- **FY27 residential pre-sales: Rs 4,500–5,000 cr** (+32–47%). First given on the Q3 FY26 call, repeated on the Q4 call, and **kept** on the Q1 call. Q1 delivered about 20%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Launches FY27:** about Rs 10,000 cr, of which Rainforest is about Rs 3,000 cr and seven or eight other launches about Rs 6,700–7,000 cr. Schedule per the IP:
  - Beacon Hill and Citadel Phase 3 in Q1;
  - Mahalunge Phase 1 and Lakewoods F&G in Q2;
  - Saibaba Phase 1 in Q3;
  - Navrat and WestEra in Q4.

  [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Longer-term:** residential pre-sales of Rs 10,000 cr by FY30 (5x FY25). IC&IC guided at Rs 400–500 cr of business a year, giving Rs 100–150 cr PAT; about Rs 1,500 cr cumulative PAT over the next few years. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- **Land additions (business development):** Q4 call said "north of Rs 10,000 cr" for FY27; Q1 call said Rs 10,000–20,000 cr, with Rs 5,600 cr already done. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Origins Ahmedabad: softened.** Q4 call: "this year Ahmedabad should kick in". Q1 call: awaiting a large anchor client, "hoping in the next few quarters or years". [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Pricing outlook:** 4–6% a year for the next two years, against 7–8% (and about 20% in peak years) earlier. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Land spending:** about Rs 900 cr in FY26, adding Rs 10,560 cr of GDV. Kandivali K2 was won in a competitive bid (Jun 2026); launch expected in 12–15 months. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [News: Business Standard, 19 Jun 2026]
- **Annuity plan:** hold (not strata-sell) commercial space at Bhandup, Thane and Citadel (Pune), targeting Rs 150–200 cr of rent within 4–5 years. [Q4 call]
- **IC land bank:** Origins Pune about 400–500 acres aggregated, with approvals in FY27–28. Only 3–4 of six IC locations are currently "firing". [Q4 call]
- **Asset sale:** the entire stake in four Ample Parks logistics associates is being sold to Omega Warehouse Holdings 2 for up to Rs 80 cr, completing by Dec 2026. [BSE filing 27 Aug 2026]

- **Jun-26:** net debt to equity -0.20; cost of debt 7.5% (was 8.1%). The debt is mainly inter-corporate deposits (ICDs) from the Mahindra group plus commercial paper. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Parentage:** the Mahindra brand and M&M support; CRISIL describes it as one of M&M's "growth gems". [CRISIL Apr-26]
- **Foreign partners:** Mitsui and Sumitomo give capital and a pipeline of foreign industrial clients; the top three Origins Chennai customers are Japanese. [Q4 call]
- **Launch success:** Blossom (Bengaluru) was 60% sold within a week. Rainforest took about Rs 600 cr in five weeks at a price premium to its micro-market. Cancellations are below 1%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Better approvals process:** RERA filed within a day of commencement certificate; society redevelopment cycle cut from about 2 years to about 400 days. [Q4 call]
- **Cost control:** portfolio IRR about 17% across 26 projects. Projected costs have not moved more than Rs 10 cr over 8 quarters. [Q4 call]

## Input costs
- Aluminium and aluform are up (about 10% of cost structure); steel is down. [Q1 call]
- Contracts are awarded in roughly 20% slices each year, which spreads the effect of price spikes. An extra contingency of just under 1% has been added. [Q1 call]
- Management expects wage inflation of 8–10% and home-price growth of 4–6% to act as a rough natural hedge on costs. [Q1 call]
- Affordable-housing legacy projects (Tathawade, Palghar, Kalyan) run at single-digit or negative margins and will drag FY27 profit. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Execution backlog:** the big Mumbai pipeline depends on society redevelopments and approvals. Rainforest, Thane and K2 are large bets in one region (MMR). MMR inventory overhang is 16–16.5 months, against 8–10 months in Pune and Bengaluru. [Q1 call]
- **Earnings quality:** profit has relied on other income and JV share. Q1 FY27 other income fell to Rs 24 cr, from about Rs 107–137 cr in prior quarters, now that consolidated projects are being handed over. [Screener]
- **Contingent liabilities (Mar-26):**
  - welfare-association claim Rs 45 cr;
  - consumer, arbitration and civil cases Rs 59 cr;
  - energy dues Rs 23 cr;
  - income tax Rs 34 cr;
  - indirect tax Rs 65 cr.
  - The Parekh & Brothers arbitration (claim Rs 33 cr) was settled on 27 Jul 2026 with "no material impact".

  [AR FY26] [BSE filing 21 Aug 2026]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matter on the carrying value of inventory (Rs 5,175 cr). No emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]
- **Related parties:**
  - ICDs and funding support from M&M, with AGM approval sought for material related-party transactions with M&M in FY27.
  - A comfort letter given for a Rs 23.5 cr ICD rollover at JV Mahindra Happinest.

  [AR FY26]
- **Pledges:** none; M&M is a stable holder at 52.41%. **FII holding** fell from 9.6% to 7.4% over the year. [Screener]
- **Management:** CEO Amit Sinha (three years in the role) and CFO Sriram Kumar were on both calls. No exits noted.

## What changed between the two calls
- **Rainforest:** moved from "RERA received, EOIs underway, walk-ins slow" to launched, with about Rs 600 cr sold.
- **Land additions:** K2 Kandivali added (Rs 5,600 cr); FY27 target now Rs 10,000–20,000 cr.
- **Market tone:** more cautious. Management now says "we've seen the best of the current cycle" and expects price growth of only 4–6% for two years.
- **Ahmedabad:** timeline pushed back again.
- **P&L:** first quarter of consolidated operating profit (Eden Phase 2 and Luminare handovers).

## My read
- **Direction:** the business is improving. The balance sheet was repaired by the rights issue (net cash), the land pipeline has grown to about Rs 50,000 cr, and project handovers are finally producing operating profit.
- **Main test:** delivering the FY27 target of Rs 4,500–5,000 cr of pre-sales, which is about 40% growth, against a market management itself calls past its peak.
- **What would improve the view:**
  - Rainforest and Mahalakshmi selling well through H2;
  - IC closures in Q2;
  - an Origins Ahmedabad anchor client.
- **What would worsen the view:** a pre-sales miss (below Rs 4,000 cr), MMR approval slippage, or margin pressure from affordable-housing legacy projects.
