# Sobha Ltd (SOBHA, BSE 532784, ISIN INE671H01015)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- Bengaluru-based developer with an unusual, fully backward-integrated model. It runs its own design, concrete-products, glazing, metal-works and interiors plants on more than 45 acres, with over 10,300 technicians. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Segments:**
  - Real estate: residential plus a small amount of commercial rental, about Rs 52 cr a year.
  - Contractual and manufacturing (C&M): construction and glazing for outside clients, plus interiors, concrete and mattresses.

  [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- FY26 gross segment revenue: real estate Rs 4,420 cr, C&M Rs 1,028 cr. Segment results were Rs 383 cr for real estate and Rs 115 cr for C&M. [AR FY26]
- **Q1 FY27 sales by region:**

| Region | Sales | Share | Price per sq ft |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bengaluru | Rs 2,068 cr | 56.5% | Rs 12,780 |
| NCR (Gurgaon and Greater Noida) | Rs 1,384 cr | 37.9% | Rs 24,667 |
| Kerala | Rs 134 cr | 3.7% | — |
| Tamil Nadu and others | small | — | — |

  [IP Q1FY27]
- **Ticket sizes in Q1 FY27:** homes above Rs 5 cr were 42% of sales (Rs 1,539 cr, +324% YoY, driven by Sobha Crescent in Gurgaon). Rs 2–3 cr was 26%, under Rs 2 cr 19%, and Rs 3–5 cr 13%. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Scale:**
  - 154 mn sq ft completed to date;
  - 33.64 mn sq ft of saleable area under construction;
  - forthcoming pipeline 20.77 mn sq ft across 17 projects (sale potential Rs 29,557 cr);
  - land bank 361 acres with 36.94 mn sq ft potential (Sobha's share 76%);
  - a further 1,749 acres are "under various stages of consolidation".

  [IP Q1FY27]
- Delivery capacity is about 7 mn sq ft a year on average over the last decade. Completions were 5.4 mn sq ft in FY26 (3,188 homes, +19%). FY27 target is 6–6.5 mn sq ft. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY27 pre-sales:** Q4 call guided "similar" growth of about 30%. The Q1 call **kept** it: "at least 30%, maybe better if launches are on time". Q1 alone delivered 36% of FY26's full-year pre-sales. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Launches:** about 10 mn sq ft guided for FY27 (GDV about Rs 15,000 cr). Q1 delivered 6.89 mn sq ft (GDV about Rs 10,000 cr), with a further 8.2 mn sq ft across 9 projects promised in the next nine months (about Rs 12,000 cr). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
  - The launch target is effectively **raised** to about 15 mn sq ft.
  - FY26 had missed its launch plan: 6.04 mn sq ft was launched after delays.
- **Margins:** Q4 call said projects recognised in the next 12 months would earn 24–26% EBITDA, and more than 30% on the whole unrecognised book. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
  - On the Q1 call, Q2 is expected to be "similar", with EBITDA reaching 17–20% by Q4 FY27.
  - The recovery keeps being pushed out; margin revival has been "coming" for several quarters.
- **Operating cash flow:** the Q4 call targeted about Rs 2,000 cr for FY27. On the Q1 call the MD called it "on an average for the next few years" and said "we should be able to touch that number". **Softened.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Land spending:** Q4 call said Rs 1,100–1,200 cr, similar to FY26 (Rs 1,160 cr). Q1 call **raised** it to Rs 1,500–1,600 cr if new deals close. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- The business is capital-light on fixed assets; money goes into land. Q1 FY27 spend: land Rs 370 cr (+31%), capex Rs 70 cr. [Q1 call]
- **New land in Q1:**
  - a 1.3-acre Mumbai plot bought outright for Rs 180 cr;
  - a Greater Noida joint development.
  - Combined GDV of the two is Rs 2,700–3,000 cr. The Greater Noida project is to launch in FY27; Mumbai has no firm date.

  [Q1 call]
- **Funding:** the board approved up to Rs 1,000 cr of NCDs on private placement, to be issued in at least two tranches as acquisitions firm up. [Q1 call] [News: Zee Business, Jul 2026]
- **Rental plans:** about 2–2.5 mn sq ft of future rental assets on land already held in Hoskote and Gurgaon, but no dedicated commitment yet. A 6 lakh sq ft Gurgaon commercial project was removed from the sale pipeline while leasing is evaluated. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Hoskote "Sobha One World":** 5.39 mn sq ft, 3,484 homes, GDV about Rs 7,500 cr. Phase 1 launched in Q1 FY27 with 3.4 mn sq ft released and about 40% of that sold. Construction starts around Aug 2026. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Jun-26:** gross debt Rs 1,110 cr, cash Rs 1,769 cr, net cash Rs 659 cr; net debt to equity -0.14; average borrowing cost 7.62%. [Q1 call]
- **Mar-26:** gross debt Rs 1,002 cr and net cash about Rs 800 cr. Management expects net debt of about zero at the end of FY27 because of land spending. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Deleveraging history:** debt fell from Rs 3,052 cr (Mar-21) to Rs 1,057 cr (Mar-26), helped by a Rs 1,984 cr rights issue in FY25. About 46% of it went to repaying debt. [Screener] [ICRA Dec-25]
- **Ratios:** debtor days 19 (FY26); working-capital days 35. Cash from operations (Rs 430 cr) was 213% of operating profit, because collections run far ahead of profit recognition. [Screener]
- **Dividend:** payout about 33% of FY26 profit. A dividend yield of 0.49% at Rs 1,227 implies about Rs 6 per share. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- **Execution:** in-house manufacturing and technicians give control of quality and delivery.
  - 7 mn sq ft a year delivery track record; 595 projects completed.
  - Completed-but-unsold stock is only 0.26 mn sq ft, so it has almost no finished inventory.

  [IP Q1FY27]
- **Launch absorption:**
  - Crescent (Gurgaon, Rs 4.8–7.9 cr homes) sold about 50% within a month and about 60% by the end of Q1.
  - One World sold about 40% of released stock at launch.
  - Rivana (Greater Noida) was weaker, at about 25% early.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Pricing:** realisation up 9.4% in FY26. Management says prices have been "stable" for 3–4 quarters, so growth will come from volume. [Q4 call]
- **Credit:** ICRA AA-/Stable, reaffirmed Dec-25. India Ratings moved its AA- outlook to Positive, as reported in the IP. [ICRA Dec-25] [IP Q1FY27]

## Input costs
- Main costs: land (Rs 2,023 cr in FY26 real estate segment cost), subcontractors (Rs 1,153 cr), project materials (Rs 1,157 cr), labour. [AR FY26]
- **Commodity inflation (Q4 call):** management saw "significant price increases for a short while" in commodities. It is still within built-in escalation buffers and is "wait-and-watch" on passing costs to buyers. [Q4 call]
- **Karnataka minimum wage** rose about 60% in May 2026. The impact is still being assessed; management expects to absorb it within budgets. [Q1 call]
- **Labour shortages** in Apr–May, around the four state elections, delayed construction milestones and so billing and collections. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Cost pass-through is slow. Fixed-price sales from years ago mean inflation shows up as lower margins at handover.

## Risks and red flags
- **Enforcement Directorate (PMLA) case:**
  - The ED provisionally attached land worth Rs 201.6 cr held by Technobuild Developers, linked to a Gurgaon joint development. The allegation concerns plot allotment, pricing and fees under Haryana rules.
  - The adjudicating authority confirmed the attachment. Sobha has appealed to the Appellate Tribunal.
  - This is also a key audit matter, because Sobha's land advance to Technobuild depends on it.

  [AR FY26]
- **Income Tax:** search in March 2023; demands of Rs 67.2 cr for AY2015-16 to AY2023-24 are under appeal. The auditor flags this as an emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]
- **Contingent liabilities (standalone, Mar-26):** about Rs 394 cr in total, including the PMLA attachment of Rs 201.6 cr and indirect taxes of Rs 122.9 cr. There is also a criminal complaint by a unit holder and land-acquisition and forest/pollution-board cases, not quantified. [AR FY26]
- **Audit trail:** auditors could not confirm that the audit-trail feature was enabled throughout the year for a third-party-hosted software. This is a CARO/reporting remark, not a qualification. [AR FY26]
- **Concentration:** Bengaluru was 51–61% of pre-sales in FY25–H1FY26. Q1 FY27 was 45% from a single project (One World). [ICRA Dec-25] [Q1 call]
- **Margin risk:** EBITDA of 6% on Screener for FY26 is very low. The promised 17–20% by Q4 FY27 needs high-margin completions to arrive on time.
- **Related parties:**
  - The group does business with promoter-linked Technobuild through land advances under an MoU.
  - Nearly all other related-party transactions are with subsidiaries.
  - No AOC-2 transactions were reported, meaning none that were not at arm's length or were material.

  [AR FY26]
- **Pledges and promoter holding:** none reported. Promoter holding is flat at 52.88%. [Screener]
- **Investor exit:** FIIs cut their holding from 8.91% (Mar-25) to 6.12% (Jun-26). [Screener]
- **Management:** MD Jagadish Nangineni and CFO Yogesh Bansal were on both calls. No exits were noted.
- **Disclosure gap:** in the Q1 call the CFO said residential revenue from delivered homes was "INR63 crores". This is likely a transcription error (real estate revenue was Rs 1,107 cr), so it is not used here.

## What changed between the two calls
- **Launches:** from "Hoskote in Q1" to delivered, plus Sacred Grove; the Q1 record came from launches after a slow start.
- **Land spending:** plan lifted to Rs 1,500–1,600 cr, with entry into Mumbai (outright purchase) and more Greater Noida.
- **Funding:** Rs 1,000 cr NCD approved. Net cash expected to fall to about zero.
- **Margins:** timing slipped again; Q2 is to be flat, and 17–20% is now framed as a Q4 exit rate.
- **Operating cash flow:** the Rs 2,000 cr target was softened into a multi-year average.
- **Costs:** new cost headwind from the Karnataka minimum wage hike.

## My read
- **Operations are improving strongly:** record pre-sales, collections up 26% and a net-cash balance sheet.
- **Accounting profit is still poor:** 6% operating margin and about 3–4% ROE.
- The investment case rests on the promised margin step-up as FY23–FY25 sales (sold at higher prices) are handed over from H2 FY27. That promise has already slipped once.
- **What would confirm the thesis:**
  - EBITDA of 15% or more by Q4 FY27;
  - full-year pre-sales above Rs 10,500 cr.
- **What would break it:**
  - another margin delay;
  - One World sales stalling after the launch rush;
  - an adverse Appellate Tribunal ruling in the ED land case;
  - land spending pushing the company back into net debt.
