# Arkade Developers Ltd (ARKADE, BSE 544261)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** a Mumbai residential developer (CMD Amit Jain; promoters 71.19%). It focuses on premium, mid-to-aspirational homes in the western and eastern suburbs. [Screener] [IndRa Feb-26]
- **Two engines:**
  - Housing-society redevelopment, which needs little capital.
  - Greenfield land: Thane Kasarvadavali (6.28 acres, bought for Rs 172.5 cr), Filmistan Goregaon, and Woollen Mills Bhandup (3.55 acres, Rs 148 cr by MoU).

  [AR FY26] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Track record:** about 5.48 mn sq ft completed. Eight ongoing projects with GDV Rs 2,300 cr, and 12 upcoming projects with GDV Rs 12,785 cr (4.25 mn sq ft carpet).
  - A 24 Sep 2026 press release says upcoming projects have grown from 8 to 15, and GDV from Rs 2,800 cr (Aug 2024) to Rs 18,000 cr (57 lakh sq ft carpet).

  [IndRa Feb-26] [IP Q1FY27] [BSE filing 24 Sep 2026]
- **Side businesses:** Arkade Finroot (home-loan assistance, earns commissions from non-Arkade projects too) and Assist360 (facility management). A commercial/annuity vertical is planned. [Q1 call]
- **Listing:** IPO in Sep 2024, net proceeds Rs 381 cr, used for construction and land. [IndRa Feb-26]

## Guidance history
- **FY27 growth:**

  - Q1 call: **FY27 pre-sales about Rs 1,000 cr** (Rs 500 cr from launches plus Rs 500 cr from ongoing stock), roughly +11% on Rs 901 cr. That is **below the 20–25% implied in May**; the CFO cited "a brief delay for the launch".

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **EBITDA margin:**
  - Q4 call: 27–28% (CFO) or about 25% (CMD).
  - Q1 call: 25–26%, a **trim**.
  - PAT margin 18–19% at both calls.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Filmistan launch** (GDV Rs 3,500 cr; expected profit Rs 1,000–1,200 cr over 3–5 years):
  - Q4 call: "by this financial year-end".

## Capex and capacity
- **FY26 land purchases:**
  - Filmistan: land Rs 165 cr (from Aspen Properties and Kamanwala Housing, paid Jan 2025) plus the Filmistan Pvt Ltd shares for Rs 170 cr (agreement dated 3 Jul 2025, covering tenancy rights).
  - Thane: Rs 172.5 cr.
  - Woollen Mills, Bhandup: Rs 148 cr by MoU.

  [AR FY26] [Q4 call]
- **Redevelopment pipeline:** Kandivali Ashok Nagar cluster (nine societies, 3.25 lakh sq ft, GDV Rs 1,100 cr, MoU signed May 2026). Another pipeline of similar size is at letter-of-intent stage. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Land cost** ranges from 15–20% of GDV in the northern suburbs to about 50% in South Mumbai; the IRR hurdle is about 20%. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Net debt:** Rs 5 cr at Jun-26 (net debt/equity 0.01x); borrowings Rs 99 cr at Mar-26. [IP Q1FY27] [Screener]
- **Cash from operations** was negative in FY25 (-Rs 218 cr) and FY26 (-Rs 119 cr) because of land buying funded by the IPO. [Screener]
- **India Ratings: IND BBB+/Stable**, first assigned 6 Feb 2026.
  - Debt repayments Rs 59.8 cr (FY26) and Rs 106 cr (FY27).
  - Committed receivables Rs 390 cr against pending construction cost Rs 626 cr.
  - Undrawn limits Rs 89 cr.
  - Negative trigger: net debt above 3.5x adjusted cash from operations.

  [IndRa Feb-26]
- **Funding plan:** construction finance if needed; no equity plan stated. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Presence in established suburbs:** Arkade sells about 95% of stock before the occupancy certificate. Collections average 89% of pre-sales, per India Ratings. [IndRa Feb-26]
- **Redevelopment relationships** with societies and on-time delivery, as claimed by management. [Q1 call]
- **Weak spots:**
  - Scale is small: pre-sales about Rs 900 cr.
  - Launches are lumpy; FY26 had almost none, and sales came from Q4 sustenance.
  - Most future GDV sits in two big greenfield projects (Thane and Filmistan), which is new territory for a redevelopment-led company.

  [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]

## Input costs
- Construction inflation is "absorbed by the incremental rate", in management's words; no numbers given. [Q1 call]
- Q4 call: May sales were "a little slow because of the war impact and the fuel prices". [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Filmistan write-off:** Rs 182 cr of tenancy rights (bought through Filmistan Pvt Ltd) were written off after the demerger order of Mar 2026.
  - FY26 PAT fell to Rs 5 cr.
  - The auditor added an **emphasis of matter** on the Filmistan investment and impairment.
  - Management says it lowers future project cost.
  - The acquisition price effectively was Rs 335–348 cr for one site.

  [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- **Guidance drift within a quarter:** growth trimmed from 20–25% to about +11% pre-sales; EBITDA from 27–28% to 25–26%; Filmistan launch pushed out. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities:** GST demands for FY18–FY25 of **Rs 54.3 cr** (Rs 53.5 cr a year earlier), about 6% of net worth; bank guarantees Rs 3.6 cr. [AR FY26]
- **Concentration and execution:** everything is in MMR. Thane and Filmistan are far larger than any past project (past annual launches were at most about Rs 1,500 cr). [Q1 call]
- **Margin compression** from a larger team (employees +30%) and lower treasury income after the IPO cash was spent. [Q1 call]
- **Related parties:** none material (AOC-2 "NA"). Promoter holding is stable. [AR FY26] [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- **FY27 pre-sales** now quantified at about Rs 1,000 cr (flat-ish), against "20–25% growth" in May.
- **EBITDA guidance** fell from 27–28% to 25–26%; Q1 EBITDA margin was 18.9%.
- **Filmistan** moved from "FY27 year-end" to FY28 (Q1 FY28 in the deck).
- **Pipeline GDV** rose from about Rs 12,000 cr to Rs 12,800 cr; the 24 Sep release claims Rs 18,000 cr.
- **Net debt** stayed near zero (Rs 5 cr).

## My read
Arkade is a small, near debt-free Mumbai redevelopment specialist that used its IPO money to buy two large greenfield sites (Thane and Filmistan) and now carries a Rs 12,800 cr pipeline, about 14 times FY26 pre-sales. The Rs 182 cr Filmistan write-off, the auditor's emphasis of matter, and guidance cuts within one quarter (pre-sales growth, margin, launch date) point to execution stretch. FY27 is a bridge year with about Rs 1,000 cr of pre-sales. The investment case rests on Thane (Q4 FY27) and Filmistan (FY28) launching on time and selling at premium prices. India Ratings' BBB+ reflects the small scale.
