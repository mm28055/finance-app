# Ajmera Realty & Infra India Ltd (AJMERA, BSE 513349)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** the flagship of the Mumbai-based Ajmera group, which has five decades of history and about 155 projects (20.7 mn sq ft) delivered. Management: Rajnikant Ajmera (Chairman), Manoj Ajmera (MD), Dhaval Ajmera (Director, Corporate Affairs), Nitin Bavisi (CFO). Promoters hold 68.56%. [CRISIL Aug-26] [Screener] [Q1 call]
- **Core asset: the Wadala township** (Manhattan 1 and 2, Greenfinity). Remaining GDV is about Rs 18,000 cr, including a boutique-office Phase 1. It was Rs 13,194 cr in May; about 1 mn sq ft of extra floor-space rights (FSI) added about Rs 3,000 cr. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Other projects:**
  - Mumbai: Vihara (Bhandup), Solis (Vikhroli), 33Fifteen (Bandra, commercial), Vann/Ajmera One (Versova luxury).
  - Bengaluru: Iris, Marina, and a new Whitefield project.
  - A Kanjurmarg land bank (11 acres plus 7 acres) awaiting conversion from leasehold to freehold.

  [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Scale:** 12 ongoing projects of about 25.6 lakh sq ft (developer share), total project cost about Rs 5,081 cr, 72% sold. Land bank about 104 lakh sq ft in Mumbai. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Growth model:** increasingly "asset-light" redevelopment and joint development. FY26 business development was Rs 2,433 cr across five projects. [Q4 call]
- **Revenue recognition:** percentage-of-completion, once a project is 25% complete and 10% collected. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **FY27 launches:** about 36.85 lakh sq ft at an estimated project cost of Rs 4,865 cr, funded partly by about **Rs 1,465 cr of incremental debt** over project life (CRISIL). [CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Wadala:** Manhattan 1 is 93% sold; Manhattan 2 over 50% sold (excavation stage); Greenfinity 94% sold and nearing its occupancy certificate. The 55-acre master plan is frozen, for an FY28 launch. [Q1 call]
- **Kanjurmarg:** a US master planner has been appointed and police housing (a regulatory condition) is under construction. Funding is being arranged at SPV level with funds. [Q4 call]
- **Formwork capex:** fixed assets doubled (about Rs 35 cr to Rs 60 cr+) on aluminium (Mivan) formwork. [Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Township premium at Wadala:** a 6–7 acre car-free podium with amenities. Manhattan 1 sells at about Rs 37,000 per sq ft and Manhattan 2 at about Rs 35,000. Management targets 10–15% annual price rises despite competition from Raymond nearby. [Q4 call]
- **Launch absorption:** Manhattan 2 sold about 40% (about Rs 700 cr) in 15–20 days; Solis 86%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Brand:** most revenue comes from repeat and referral customers, per CRISIL. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Weak spot:** the Versova luxury project (Vann) sold three units at launch and **none in Q1**; management expects "subdued" FY27 sales there. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Input costs
- Management cites cost escalation from Middle East-related supply disruption; no quantified impact given. [Q4 call]
- Project margin claim: EBITDA sustainable, with a Rs 6,300 cr launch pipeline expected to generate Rs 1,400 cr+ of cash. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Pre-sales guidance at risk:** Q1 was Rs 146 cr against Rs 2,200 cr for the year. The guidance depends on a Q3 boutique-office launch (Rs 3,600 cr, the largest single launch the company has attempted) and on Q4 launches that have already slipped. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Kanjurmarg:** the leasehold-to-freehold conversion has been "next quarter" since at least May 2026, with a December deadline. A 7-acre sale or JV depends on it. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Leverage path:** CRISIL flags "moderately high" debt reliance and about Rs 1,465 cr of new debt for FY27 launches; guidance allows debt/equity up to 1x. [CRISIL Aug-26] [Q4 call]
- **Slower buyers:** decision cycles lengthened from 30–45 days to 50–60 days (geopolitics). [Q4 call]
- **Concentration:** Mumbai and Bengaluru micro-markets, with Wadala dominant. [CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Disclosure inconsistency:** launch-pipeline figures differed within the same Q1 call (Rs 3,000 cr vs Rs 6,500 cr). [Q1 call]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion, no emphasis of matter. The key audit matter is revenue recognition under Ind AS 115. No material related-party transactions needing AOC-2. [AR FY26]
- **Share price** down 41% in a year. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- **Pre-sales:** from a record FY26 (Rs 1,701 cr) to a weak Q1 (Rs 146 cr); the FY27 Rs 2,200 cr target was not repeated.
- **Wadala GDV** raised from Rs 13,194 cr to about Rs 18,000 cr (extra FSI); the boutique office became a single Rs 3,600 cr phase.
- **Kanjurmarg 7-acre:** moved from "offers… not narrowed down" to active outright/JV talks, closing after conversion.
- **Debt** fell by Rs 57 cr; debt/equity guidance softened from 1x to "much lower".
- **Launch dates slipped** (Borivali, Pune); SV Concrete swapped for Whitefield.

## My read
Ajmera had a strong FY26 on the back of Wadala and fast-selling redevelopment launches, and it beat both its sales and leverage guidance. FY27 depends almost entirely on H2 launches, above all a Rs 3,600 cr boutique-office phase and deals that need the long-delayed Kanjurmarg conversion. The Rs 146 cr Q1 and management's failure to restate the Rs 2,200 cr target are the warning signs. The balance sheet is adequate (CRISIL A-, debt/equity 0.47x), but planned launches need about Rs 1,465 cr of new debt. Execution on the Q3 launch and the Kanjurmarg conversion are the swing factors.
