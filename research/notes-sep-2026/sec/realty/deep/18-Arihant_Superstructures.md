# Arihant Superstructures Ltd (ARIHANTSUP, BSE 506194)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** a Navi Mumbai-based developer of affordable, mid-income and (increasingly) premium housing in the Mumbai region ("Mumbai 3.0": Panvel, Kharghar, Vashi, Shilphata, Chowk/Karjat) and Jodhpur.
  - Chhajer family control: CMD Ashok Chhajer, Joint MD Parth Chhajer, WTD Bhavik Chhajer. Promoters hold 71.10%.

  [Q1 call] [Acuite Feb-26] [Screener]
- **Portfolio:** GDV about Rs 14,000 cr (up from about Rs 6,000 cr in five years), to be executed over 6–7 years. Luxury is 41% of the project mix; target mix is 40–45% premium, 30–35% mid-income, 20% affordable. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Premium bets:** World Villas at Chowk (77 acres, 362 villas, a 221/227-key hotel and a 10.5-acre gymkhana) and Town Villas.
  - Between Q3 and Q4 FY26 their stated areas were **restated from carpet to saleable area** (World Villas Phase 1: 4.14 to 10 lakh sq ft; Town Villas: 30 to 58 lakh sq ft), partly also a design change.

  [Acuite Feb-26] [Q4 call]
- **Hospitality pivot:**
  - A subsidiary (Dwellcons) signed a hotel operating agreement with **ITC Hotels** on 24 Sep 2026 for a 227-room "Mementos by ITC Hotels" at World Villas.
  - A second hotel is planned near Imagicaa (Khopoli); Radisson operates the existing hotels.

  [BSE filing 25 Sep 2026] [Q1 call]
- **Accounting:** percentage-of-completion method. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Hospitality and club:** a Rs 500 cr investment programme over three years (about Rs 350 cr for two hotels), "by debt or by internal resources". Rs 35–40 cr is deployed so far. [Q1 call]
  - Management targets about Rs 50 cr a year of hospitality PAT from year 3–4, with an 8–9 year payback thanks to low land cost.
- **World Villas:** about 26% of cost incurred by Sep-25 (Acuite). Phase 1 completion targeted Oct 2027, the full project 2030; 65–70 villa sales targeted in FY27, but only four villas sold in Q4 FY26. [Acuite Feb-26] [Q4 call]
- **Land deals** in 2023–24 (World Villas, Town Villas, Thane, Shilphata) have, per management, roughly tripled in land value. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Early land buying** in Navi Mumbai and Chowk ahead of the Atal Setu and the Navi Mumbai airport. Management claims its entry cost gives villa margins of 45–47%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Delivery:** 1,721 units delivered in FY26; about 1,495 more received occupancy certificates in Q1. Unsold completed stock is only 71 units (book value Rs 14 cr). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Weak spots:** a single region (MMR); affordable-segment margins of about 9–10% PAT; small scale relative to a Rs 14,000 cr GDV claim. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Costs up 3–5% because of the war and the rupee; labour shortages. Management says price increases on the balance stock will offset this. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Mid-income realisations are expected to rise about 10%; ticket size heading to Rs 95 lakh–1 cr. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Leverage and coverage:** net debt about 1.8x equity; interest now eats most operating profit; cash from operations negative; BBB- rating with debt-service cover of 1.0x. Rs 500 cr of hospitality capex will add debt. [Q1 call] [Acuite Feb-26] [Screener]
- **Guidance softening:** FY27 pre-sales growth cut from 25–30% to "a little higher" within one quarter. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Promoter loans:** Rs 361 cr of unsecured promoter money at 13–13.5% sits behind secured lenders, a related-party exposure. [Q4 call]
- **Metric restatement:** villa areas switched from carpet to saleable basis, which inflated GDV and area figures. [Q4 call]
- **Governance changes:**
  - New statutory auditors (K J K & Associates) appointed at the 24 Sep 2026 AGM for five years; the reason was not stated in the filing read.
  - An independent director (Namrata Thakker) resigned on 7 May 2026.

  [BSE filing 24 Sep 2026] [AR FY26]
- **Data gap:** the FY26 annual report's financial statements (auditor's report, contingent liabilities, related-party notes) are image pages and could not be extracted. Auditor remarks are **not verified**. [AR FY26]
- **Share price** down 40% in a year. [Screener]

## My read
Arihant has built a large land-led GDV in Navi Mumbai's growth corridor, but it has done so with debt. Net debt is about 1.8x equity, including Rs 361 cr of expensive promoter loans; interest exceeds pre-tax profit; cash from operations has been negative for four years; the rating is BBB-. It is now adding a Rs 500 cr hotel programme just as pre-sales guidance has been cut back. Upside depends on World Villas and Town Villas delivering the promised 45%+ margins and on the Q1 wave of occupancy certificates turning into cash that repays debt. Until then, the equity story is highly leveraged, and an auditor change plus an unreadable annual report add uncertainty.
