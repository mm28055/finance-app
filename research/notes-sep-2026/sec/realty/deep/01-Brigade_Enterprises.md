# Brigade Enterprises (BRIGADE, BSE 532929, ISIN INE791I01019)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- Bengaluru-based developer, founded in 1986. It runs three businesses: residential and commercial property for sale (real estate), leased offices and Orion malls (leasing), and hotels through listed subsidiary Brigade Hotel Ventures (BHVL). [IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 revenue split: real estate Rs 707 cr (60%), leasing Rs 328 cr (28%), hospitality Rs 144 cr (12%). Total Rs 1,179 cr. [IP Q1FY27]
- Geography of residential pre-sales: in FY26 and Q4 FY26, about 65% came from Bengaluru, 20% from Chennai and 15% from Hyderabad. [Q4 call]
- Ongoing projects total 32.54 mn sq ft, of which Brigade's share is 26.42 mn sq ft. Real estate is 25.39 mn sq ft and leasing is 7.15 mn sq ft. [IP Q1FY27]
- Leasing portfolio in operation: 9.36 mn sq ft leasable, with 8.31 mn sq ft leased and 1.05 mn sq ft vacant. The largest assets are Brigade Tech Gardens (3.0 mn sq ft), WTC Chennai (1.98 mn sq ft) and Orion Mall at Brigade Gateway (0.83 mn sq ft). [IP Q1FY27]
- Office occupancy is 88%. GCCs are 58% of the leased portfolio and IT/ITES 26%. Tenants taking more than 1 lakh sq ft make up 65% of the lease portfolio. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- Hospitality: nine operating hotels. In Q1 FY27 the average room rate (ARR) was Rs 7,241 (+7% YoY) and occupancy 76%. BHVL targets 3,300 keys by FY31, with a 1,700-key pipeline. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call] [CRISIL Jun-26]
- Land bank: 543 acres. Land cost Rs 4,672 cr, of which Rs 938 cr is still payable. Development potential is 57 mn sq ft (Brigade share 43 mn sq ft), of which 45 mn sq ft is residential. By city: Bengaluru 29, Chennai 16, Hyderabad 6. [IP Q1FY27]
- Bengaluru market share: 12–13%, per CRISIL. [CRISIL Jun-26]

## Guidance history
- **FY26 promise vs delivery:** the plan was 12 mn sq ft of launches; actual was 8.3 mn sq ft (GDV Rs 10,000 cr). Pre-sales fell 5%. Management blamed approval delays in Bengaluru. [Q4 call]
- **FY27 pre-sales:** guided at "at least 20% growth", aiming for Rs 9,000 cr (Q4 call). **Kept** on the Q1 call despite Q1 pre-sales of only Rs 1,061 cr. That leaves about Rs 7,900 cr for the next nine months. CRISIL expects Rs 8,500–9,000 cr. [Q1 call] [CRISIL Jun-26]
- **FY27 residential launches: cut.** The Q4 call gave 11.6 mn sq ft (GDV Rs 11,900 cr). On the Q1 call this became 9.36 mn sq ft for FY27: 2.36 mn sq ft in Q2 and about 7 mn sq ft in H2. About 3 mn sq ft moves to Q1 FY28. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
  - About 1 mn sq ft of the cut is Morgan Heights, now removed from the pipeline.
  - About 1 mn sq ft is "sales phasing".
  - No launches happened in Q1, although about 1.5 mn sq ft had been planned.
- **Morgan Heights relaunch: dropped for now.** On the Q4 call it was promised for Q1 FY27; it did not happen (see Risks). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Timing of two big launches brought forward:** Hyderabad Neopolis 2 (about 2 mn sq ft) now targeted in Q2/Q3, from Q3/Q4 earlier. Whitefield–Hoskote is now Q3. [Q1 call]
- **Real estate EBITDA margin:** guided "into 20s" for FY27. Q1 delivered 21%. [Q1 call]
- **Operating cash flow:** the Q4 call said it should grow close to pre-sales growth, a few hundred basis points lower. Q1 company-defined operating cash flow was Rs 354 cr, +10%. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Leasing:** targets about 20% annual growth in leasing revenue over 5–6 years. FY26 office leasing revenue was about Rs 765–877 cr; management gave both figures on different calls. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Committed commercial capex:** 12 projects, 7.15 mn sq ft, estimated cost Rs 4,814 cr. Rs 1,216 cr is spent; Rs 3,598 cr remains. The biggest is WTC and Orion Mall Hyderabad (Rs 1,809 cr). [IP Q1FY27]
- **Wider plan:** about 10 mn sq ft of commercial launches over FY27–28, costing about Rs 6,000 cr over 4 years (Rs 1,200–1,700 cr a year). [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 commercial launches: 4.01 mn sq ft (HRC Atrium 1.44, WTC and Orion Hyderabad 1.72, and others). [IP Q1FY27]
- **Funding:** mix of rental cash flow and lease-rental discounting (LRD) debt. Management says debt to equity will stay "well under 1x". [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Bain Capital joint venture (announced Apr 2026):** 50:50 JV on a 10.8-acre Whitefield site opposite ITPL. Plan is about 2 mn sq ft of offices plus a 250-key five-star hotel; completion about 40 months after approval. The press put total investment at Rs 2,200 cr. [Q4 call] [News: Business Standard, 27 Apr 2026]
- Residential business development added Rs 15,000 cr GDV (13 mn sq ft) in FY26 and Rs 2,400 cr GDV (2.7 mn sq ft) in Q1 FY27. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Brand and market position:** 12–13% of the fragmented Bengaluru market, with over 100 mn sq ft delivered. [CRISIL Jun-26] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Pricing power:** like-for-like price rises of 8–9% a year; 7–9% planned after each launch. [Q4 call]
  - Brigade Lumina sold more than 85% at launch (Mar 2026).
  - Belvedere sold 150 of 760 units in its launch month.
- **Rent-backed annuity:**
  - Leasing and hospitality give steady income; rent collections are at 99%.
  - Re-letting vacated space earns 10–15% higher rents (mark-to-market).
  - Management says leasing EBITDA margin is above 80% once fit-out and facility-management pass-through revenue is excluded.

  [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Partners:** marquee capital partners GIC (on three land parcels) and Bain Capital. [CRISIL Jun-26] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Credit:** CRISIL AA-/Positive, reaffirmed Jun-26; ICRA AA/Stable. [CRISIL Jun-26] [IP Q1FY27]

## Input costs
- Main costs are construction (cement, steel, labour), payments to landowners and approval costs.
- Q1 FY27 construction cash outflow was Rs 763 cr and landowner payments Rs 207 cr. [IP Q1FY27]
- Real estate margins are set by prices fixed when units were sold years earlier. FY26 reported margins were weak because older, lower-priced inventory was being handed over. Newer sales carry "higher 20s" operating margins. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- There was no specific discussion of cement or steel inflation on either call. Disclosure gap.

## Risks and red flags
- **Morgan Heights, Chennai (regulatory):**
  - SEIAA revoked the environmental clearance over Pallikaranai marshland concerns. Brigade refunded buyers.
  - The High Court has ordered status quo.
  - Unsold value attributable to Brigade is Rs 650 cr (0.7 mn sq ft), still counted in the Jun-26 inventory; it will be removed next quarter if unresolved.

  [Q1 call]
- **Approval risk in Bengaluru:** caused the FY26 miss.
  - The Kengeri land has landowner litigation.
  - The residential part of the 75-acre KIADB site shrank to 3–4 lakh sq ft after bylaw changes.

  [Q1 call] [CRISIL Jun-26]
- **Office vacancy:**
  - Amazon vacated about 630,000 sq ft at WTC Bengaluru. About 0.34 mn sq ft of Brigade's share is still vacant.
  - Twin Towers A and C are 0.51 of 0.57 mn sq ft vacant, but are being sold.
  - Large leasing enquiries were postponed because of the West Asia crisis.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Tax (auditor emphasis of matter):**
  - Income Tax Department survey under Section 133A in Dec 2025 at group premises. Management made an "ad-hoc" provision it says is not material.
  - BHVL property-tax demand of Rs 92.2 cr (2011-12 to 2021-22); Rs 46 cr paid under protest.
  - Arbitration over an Rs 8.6 cr JDA deposit.

  [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- **Morgan Heights:** from "relaunching in Q1" to clearance revoked, buyers refunded and removed from the pipeline.
- **FY27 launch plan:** cut from about 11.6 mn sq ft to 9.36 mn sq ft. The Rs 9,000 cr pre-sales target was kept, so reliance on H2 is higher.
- **Real estate margin:** improved as promised, from 12% to 21%.
- **WTC Bengaluru leasing:** tone moved from "next couple of quarters" to "3–4 quarters". Leasing is now in smaller floor-by-floor deals.
- **Hospitality:** about 10% of business was lost to the West Asia crisis in Q1; a recovery is expected in H2.
- **Bain Capital:** JV announced; its first accounting gain (Rs 36.6 cr) came through in Q1.

## My read
- The business is healthy but in a slower patch.
- **Positives:** real estate margins are recovering as promised; net debt is low and mostly backed by rent; the land bank is large (57 mn sq ft).
- **Negatives:** pre-sales have fallen for five straight quarters year-on-year. FY27 depends on about 7 mn sq ft of H2 launches that need approvals, which is exactly what failed in FY26. The Rs 9,000 cr target looks stretched.
- **What would improve the view:**
  - Neopolis 2 and the Bengaluru launches landing on time in Q2–Q3;
  - WTC Bengaluru re-let;
  - a Morgan Heights court win.
- **What would worsen the view:**
  - another quarter of launch slippage;
  - FY27 pre-sales below about Rs 7,500 cr;
  - tech-sector job cuts hitting Bengaluru demand.
