# Max Estates Ltd (MAXESTATES, BSE 544008, ISIN INE03EI01018)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** the real estate arm of the Max Group (chairman Analjit Singh; vice-chairman and MD Sahil Vachani). It operates only in Delhi NCR (Noida, Gurugram, Delhi). [Q1 call] [ICRA Aug-26]
- **Two businesses:**
  - Residential and mixed-use for sale, under the "LiveWell" and intergenerational-community theme. Senior-living parts are branded Antara, which belongs to the sister company Max India.
  - Grade-A offices for lease ("WorkWell").

  [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Offices in operation:** Max Towers (Noida), Max House (Okhla, Delhi) and Max Square (Noida). About 1.2 mn sq ft, 100% occupied. FY26 rent Rs 150–155 cr: Max Towers Rs 53 cr, Max House Rs 45 cr, Max Square Rs 60 cr. [Q4 call]
- **Offices under construction:**
  - Max Square Two (Noida, about 1 mn sq ft, occupancy certificate Q2 FY28, about Rs 125 cr rent a year);
  - Max District (Gurugram Sector 65, 1.6 mn sq ft, occupancy in Q3 FY28 and Q3 FY29, Rs 200–225 cr rent);
  - the office part of Max One.

  Peak rent across all assets is about Rs 700 cr on a 100% basis; Max Estates' share is about Rs 350 cr. [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Partner:** New York Life (NYL) owns about 20.4% of Max Estates and 49% of the commercial and mixed-use projects. Its cumulative commitment is about Rs 1,770 cr. [ICRA Aug-26]
- **Residential projects:**

| Project | GDV / sales | Status |

| Estate 128, Noida | Rs 2,700 cr sold | sold out |
| Estate 360, Gurugram | Rs 4,800 cr sold | under construction |

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Delivery record:** only about 2 mn sq ft of residential delivered so far; the first residential handover (Estate 128) is due in FY28. [ICRA Aug-26] [IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- **Track record on pre-sales guidance** (from the company's own slide):
  - FY24: guided Rs 1,300 cr, achieved Rs 1,844 cr;
  - FY25: guided Rs 4,000 cr (later raised), achieved Rs 5,321 cr;
  - FY26: guided Rs 6,000–6,500 cr, achieved Rs 5,305 cr. That is a **miss**, and it includes Rs 1,221 cr of inherited bookings.

  [IP Q1FY27]
- **FY27: pre-sales guidance dropped.** On both calls management refused to give a pre-sales number, citing a "volatile" macro environment. The slide now shows only a launch calendar: Rs 11,900 cr GDV to be launched across Gurugram and Noida. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Collections:** the Q4 call guided Rs 2,500–3,000 cr for FY27; the Q1 call narrowed it to Rs 2,500–2,700 cr. Project spending is Rs 1,500–1,800 cr, leaving Rs 750–1,000 cr of operating cash for land. ICRA expects collections up 50–55%. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [ICRA Aug-26]
- **Launch changes between calls:**
  - Estate 105 Phase 2 moved **earlier**, from FY28 (Q4 call) to FY27 (Q1 call).
  - Sector 59 GDV was quoted at about Rs 3,900 cr (Q4) and then "more than Rs 3,500 cr" (Q1); launch still Q3 FY27.
  - H2 launches Rs 5,000–5,500 cr plus Rs 3,000–4,000 cr of older unsold stock.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Estate 128 delivery:** "calendar 2027" on the Q4 call; the Q1 IP says FY28. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Standing targets:** add about 2 mn sq ft of residential and 1 mn sq ft of commercial a year; about Rs 700 cr of rent at peak. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Commercial capex:** Rs 1,500–1,800 cr still to spend on Max Square Two, Max District and Max One. Funding is "fully tied up": about 40% equity from Max Estates and NYL, and construction finance from SBI and ICICI that converts to lease-rental discounting (LRD) loans on completion. [Q1 call]
- **Target returns:** 12–14% yield on cost at underwriting, against REIT cap rates of 6.5–7%. [Q1 call]
- **Land (Aug–Sep 2026):**
  - **Najafgarh, Delhi:** 84.7 acres under Delhi's land-pooling policy, bought from **promoter-group companies** through a preferential share swap. About 70.33 lakh shares at Rs 597.50, worth Rs 420.2 cr (land valued at Rs 4.95 cr per acre), about 4% dilution. Approved at the EGM on 24 Sep 2026. Press reports put GDV at Rs 10,000–12,000 cr.
  - **Ghaziabad (Indirapuram):** binding MoU for a 9.76-acre JDA (revenue share, about 1.5 mn sq ft, GDV Rs 2,500–3,000 cr), subject to due diligence.

  [NSE filing 28 Aug 2026] [BSE EGM outcome 24 Sep 2026] [BSE press release 23 Sep 2026] [News: Business Standard, 29 Aug 2026]
- **Delhi land pooling:** DDA cleared the land-pooling guidelines under the Master Plan 2047 in Aug 2026. Management called it "transformational". [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Jun-26:** gross debt Rs 1,960 cr (LRD Rs 934 cr), cash Rs 1,727 cr, net debt Rs 234 cr. Mar-26 net debt was about Rs 100 cr. The increase is construction finance on the office projects. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Screener figures:** borrowings Rs 2,412 cr (Mar-26); FY26 operating cash flow -Rs 616 cr. The company's own definition gave FY26 operating cash flow of Rs 450–500 cr. The gap likely reflects land and approval payments classified as operating costs. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- **Funding policy:** no debt for land, which is paid from internal accruals or promoter money; about Rs 1,200 cr surplus sits in RERA accounts. [Q4 call] [ICRA Aug-26]
- **Credit:** ICRA A+/Stable, a first-time issuer rating assigned 13 Aug 2026.
  - Committed receivables Rs 9,455 cr; cash-flow adequacy about 105%.
  - LRD debt about 5.5–6x rent; DSCR 1.30–1.35x in FY27.

  [ICRA Aug-26]
- **Deferred tax asset:** about Rs 195 cr, from accumulated tax losses (AR FY26). **Dividends:** none. [AR FY26] [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- **Office quality:** 100% occupancy. The latest Max Towers lease was at Rs 156 per sq ft per month against a portfolio average of Rs 132.
  - Pre-leases at 25–35% premiums to micro-market rents (Max District about 2 lakh sq ft; Max Square Two about 80–90k sq ft).
  - WALE (weighted average remaining lease term) 3.4–6.5 years.

  [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Residential brand:** new launches sold quickly.
  - Estate 105: about Rs 1,800 cr in 10 days.
  - Terraces Phase 1: sold out in the quarter.
  - Estate 361: 68% of launched stock sold in FY26.
  - Cancellations are "negligible".

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Partners:** NYL co-invests, and Max Group's name gives access to capital. [ICRA Aug-26]
- **No execution record at scale yet:** only about 2 mn sq ft of residential delivered. [ICRA Aug-26]

## Input costs
- Management says budgets include escalation and contingency buffers, and inflation so far has been absorbed. A prolonged West Asia conflict would need reassessment. [Q4 call]
- It avoids turnkey contracting and uses in-house project management and OEM (direct-from-manufacturer) procurement. [IP Q1FY27]
- Margins on Estates 360 and 361 are already the lowest (20–25%), so cost overruns matter most there. [IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party land purchase:**
  - The Najafgarh land is bought from companies owned by the promoter group (Analjit Singh, Piya Singh, Tara Singh Vachani, Sahil Vachani), paid in newly issued shares.
  - Independent valuers (KPMG for the share exchange; Cushman & Wakefield and iVAS for the land) were used.
  - Land pooling in Delhi has a long history of delays; value depends on approvals.

  [NSE filing 28 Aug 2026]
- **Antara fee:** Antara (Max India) receives a development-management fee of about 9–9.5% of sales value on the senior-living parts (about Rs 1,200–1,500 cr at Estate 360; 15–20% of Estate 361). Management says Antara units sell at a 7–10% premium that offsets the fee. Material related-party transactions with Antara were approved in FY26. [Q1 call] [AR FY26]
- **Guidance credibility:** missed FY26 pre-sales guidance, then withdrew guidance. FY26 pre-sales were flattered by Rs 1,221 cr of inherited Max One bookings, which bring only about Rs 550–600 cr of new cash. [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call]
- **Concentration and execution:** everything is in NCR; limited delivery history; heavy parallel construction of residential and commercial. [ICRA Aug-26]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matters on impairment of investment property and on capitalisation of project costs, including borrowing costs. No emphasis of matter. [AR FY26]
- **Contingent liabilities:** negligible (Uttarakhand VAT Rs 0.2 cr; income tax Rs 0.5 cr). [AR FY26]
- **Pledges:** none. Promoter holding about 45.3%. FIIs fell from 27.4% to 25.8% over the year (includes NYL). [Screener]
- **Management:** no exits noted. The same CFO (Nitin Kansal) has been on both calls.

## My read
- **What looks good:** a strong brand; about Rs 13,500 cr of sold-but-unrecognised sales at 20–45% margins; a quality office book with a partner (NYL); net debt still low.
- **What is weaker:**
  - FY26 guidance was missed and guidance then withdrawn;
  - most of the value sits in future deliveries by a company with little delivery history;
  - the new Delhi land comes from the promoter group and depends on the slow land-pooling process.
- **What would improve the view:** on-time Estate 128 delivery (FY28); FY27 collections of Rs 2,500 cr or more; Sector 59 selling at least 40% at launch.
- **What would worsen the view:** further related-party asset transfers, launch slippage, or net debt rising well above Rs 500 cr before handovers.
