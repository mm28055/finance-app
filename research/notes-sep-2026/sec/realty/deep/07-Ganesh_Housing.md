# Ganesh Housing Ltd (GANESHHOUC, BSE 526367, ISIN INE460C01014)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million. Screener code: GANESHHOU.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** an Ahmedabad (Gujarat) developer run by the Patel family. MD and CEO Shekhar G. Patel holds 34.97%; promoters hold 73.06% in total. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- **Main asset is land:** about 510–518 acres of fully paid land. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
  - 411 acres at Godhavi ("Smile City" township), where 46 acres were sold earlier at an average of Rs 14.1 cr per acre;
  - about 65 acres at Million Minds Tech City (an IT/ITeS SEZ);
  - the balance at Thaltej and Malabar.
- **How it makes money:** mostly by **selling land**, plus occasional residential projects. FY24–FY26 profits came mainly from land sales, per the Q4 call. [Q4 call]
- **Million Minds Tech City:**
  - Phase 1 is a Grade-A office of about 0.85 mn sq ft leasable, with 42,000 sq ft floor plates, built with Tishman Speyer as development partner. It was inaugurated on 17 May 2026 by Union Home Minister Amit Shah.
  - Stated long-term potential for the 65 acres is 15–18 mn sq ft across offices, homes, hotels and retail.

  [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Residential:** Malabar Retreat (sale value about Rs 450 cr), 83% built. It is the only ongoing residential project. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Delays before guidance:** on the Q4 call an analyst complained guidance had been deferred "4 quarters running", and that One 91 Thaltej had been pushed back by a quarter four times. Management promised FY27 guidance and "clarity" on Thaltej at Q1. [Q4 call]
- **One 91 Thaltej – reversed:**
  - Q4 call: to be developed as a 30-plus-floor commercial tower (sale value Rs 2,100 cr, cost about Rs 1,500 cr) funded from internal cash.
  - Q1 call: the land was **sold instead** to an unnamed listed buyer. Price not disclosed; management said it would disclose once the buyer agrees.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 guidance (first given on the Q1 call):**
  - revenue Rs 1,000–1,200 cr;
  - PAT Rs 300–325 cr (flat to +10%).
  - Mix: about Rs 550–600 cr from land (including Thaltej and possibly Godhavi) and about Rs 470 cr from projects (Malabar Retreat handover).

  [Q1 call]
- **Million Minds leasing – slipped and walked back:**
  - Q4 call: 60–65% "leased or in final stages"; rent from Q3 FY27 (October), about Rs 72–77 cr a year.
  - Q1 call: signed LOIs only 43% (2.64 lakh sq ft), with 15–20% more under negotiation; rent now from **Q4 FY27**; full-year rent above Rs 70–75 cr in FY28.
  - The Q1 IP still says "by Q3 FY27" and "~55%".

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Launches – slipped:** Q4 call put Million Minds Phase 2 (commercial) in Q2 and Million Minds residential Phase 1 in Q3. The Q1 call moved them to Q3 and Q4. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Malabar Retreat:** handover and revenue recognition targeted in FY27. Bookings rose from about Rs 175 cr (Q4) to Rs 183 cr (Q1); 73 units, about 45% sold. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Million Minds Phase 1:** funded from internal accruals. A lease-rental discounting (LRD) loan of about Rs 150 cr was taken in Mar-26 against LOIs. Management says no more debt is needed for the rest of Phase 1. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Projects under construction (Screener):** CWIP (capital work in progress) of Rs 540 cr in Mar-26, up from Rs 148 cr in Mar-24.
- **Land buying:** about 7 acres bought in Q4 FY26; further parcels "under negotiation" with partial payments made. Management says cash from Thaltej will fund more land. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- **Future:** Million Minds Phase 2 (commercial) is expected to take about 3 years to build. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Borrowings:** Rs 526 cr (Mar-26), up from Rs 248 cr. That is about Rs 150 cr of LRD plus Rs 149 cr of short-term unsecured loans from **group (promoter) companies**. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- **Dividend cut:** 110% (FY24), 50% (FY25), then 15% (Rs 1.5 per share, FY26), "to conserve cash" for land and projects. [Q4 call]
- **Operating cash flow:** Rs 213 cr in FY26 (Rs 410 cr in FY25); free cash flow only Rs 30 cr after spending on Million Minds. [Screener]
- **Cash-flow projection:** management projects Rs 9,950 cr of free cash flow from planned projects, using "conservative" prices of about Rs 5,300 per sq ft. [Q4 call]
- **Credit rating:** no current rating. The last one listed on Screener is Brickwork, Mar 2023. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- **Cheap land:** historic, low-cost land in Ahmedabad's growth corridors (SG Highway, Thaltej, near the Ahmedabad–GIFT City belt) is the core advantage. Past Godhavi sales averaged Rs 14.1 cr per acre. [Q4 call]
- **Office tenants:** demand at Million Minds comes from GCCs, tech firms and co-working operators. Management frames Ahmedabad as an emerging GCC hub. [Q1 call]
- **Market backdrop:** Ahmedabad is among India's most affordable big housing markets (EMI-to-income about 18% in 2025, per the IP). Residential launches were 6,745 units in Q1 CY26, +29% YoY (Cushman & Wakefield, quoted on the Q4 call). [IP Q1FY27] [Q4 call]
- **Limited development record:** few projects launched in recent years. An analyst noted a listed Ahmedabad peer launched 4+ projects in 18 months while Ganesh launched none. [Q4 call]

## Input costs
- Management flagged "emerging cost pressures" and higher input costs after the West Asia crisis, but gave no numbers. [Q4 call]
- For land sales, cost is the old book value of the land. For Malabar Retreat and Million Minds, construction cost matters, but most is already spent.

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party loans (key audit matter):**
  - Loans and advances are 33.86% of group total assets: Rs 957 cr in all.
  - Of that, Rs 385 cr is lent to **related parties**, Rs 519 cr is "for purchase of land on behalf of the Group", and Rs 50 cr to others.
  - The auditor highlights uncertainty over whether the related-party advances will be recovered.

  [AR FY26]
- **Promoter property deals:** the AGM asked approval for a material related-party transaction of up to **Rs 200 cr** with MD and promoter Shekhar G. Patel in FY27, covering purchase of immovable property and other dealings. A similar resolution covers his son Amanvir Patel. [AR FY26]
- **Loans from promoter companies:** short-term unsecured borrowings of Rs 149 cr from group companies. [Q4 call]
- **Undisclosed land sale:** the buyer and price of One 91 Thaltej are undisclosed, and the plan was reversed within two months of saying it would be developed. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Disclosure quality:**
  - the official Q1 transcript is dated "July 27, 2027";
  - Million Minds potential is given as "15 billion sq ft" on one call and 18 mn sq ft on another;
  - leasing percentages differ between the call and the IP.
  - There is no separate pre-sales disclosure yet (promised "from Q3").

  [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Contingent liabilities:** long-running income-tax disputes (AY2007-08 and AY2008-09 in the Supreme Court and Gujarat High Court; AY2015-16 demand Rs 1.55 cr, already recovered by the tax department). Amounts are not fully quantified in the note. [AR FY26]
- **Concentration:** a single city (Ahmedabad) and a single office asset. Earnings depend on lumpy land deals.
- **Pledges:** none reported. Promoter holding is flat. FII and DII holdings are negligible (under 1% each). [Screener]
- **Management:** the calls were led by an external "Corporate & Financial Advisor" (B. Ravi) rather than the promoters.

## What changed between the two calls

- **Guidance:** FY27 guidance finally given: revenue Rs 1,000–1,200 cr, PAT Rs 300–325 cr.
- **Million Minds:** rent start pushed from Q3 to Q4; leasing now described as 43% signed LOIs rather than 60–65%.
- **Launches:** Million Minds Phase 2 and the residential launch each pushed back one quarter.

## My read
- **The assets are real:** a huge, cheaply held land bank in a growing city, and an office campus close to earning rent.
- **The governance and disclosure picture is poor:**
  - a third of assets are loans and advances, over Rs 380 cr of them to related parties;
  - promoter property transactions of up to Rs 200 cr were approved;
  - borrowing from group companies;
  - an undisclosed land sale;
  - repeatedly slipping launch and leasing dates.
- **Profit depends on one-off land deals** rather than a repeatable development business.
- **What would improve the view:**
  - Million Minds fully leased with rent flowing by Q4;
  - Malabar Retreat handed over;
  - related-party advances coming down.
- **What would worsen the view:** further promoter-related transactions or a miss on FY27 guidance.
