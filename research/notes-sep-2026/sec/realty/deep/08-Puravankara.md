# Puravankara Ltd (PURVA, BSE 532891, ISIN INE323I01011)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** a Bengaluru-headquartered developer founded in 1975, controlled by the Puravankara family (promoters own 75.00%). It sells under three brands: [Screener] [IP Q1FY27] [India Ratings Nov-25]
  - **Puravankara** (premium/luxury);
  - **Provident** (mid-income/affordable);
  - **Purva Land** (plotted developments).
- **Other businesses:**
  - Starworth, an in-house construction arm (order book above Rs 2,000 cr);
  - commercial offices: Purva Aerocity (north Bengaluru, 2.2 mn sq ft, with 1.3 mn sq ft completed and occupancy certificate received) and Purva Zentech (being sold).

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Geography:**
  - Ongoing projects by value (H1 FY26): Bengaluru 53%, Mumbai and Pune 25%, Chennai 13%, Kochi 6%.
  - Mumbai is now a big growth area, with about Rs 25,000 cr of GDV (redevelopment-led).
  - Entered Delhi NCR in Sep 2026: a 13.44-acre plot in Greater Noida, about 4.57 mn sq ft, GDV about Rs 5,200 cr.

  [India Ratings Nov-25] [Q1 call] [BSE press release 22 Sep 2026]
- **Scale:** land bank 56.48 mn sq ft; portfolio of about Rs 58,500 cr; planned launches of 20.48 mn sq ft (GDV about Rs 27,300 cr). [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- **FY26:** the previous year's launches slipped (Bengaluru approval problems: e-Khata property records and the Greater Bengaluru Authority split). FY26 still hit a record because of two big Q4 launches: Northern Lights (about Rs 1,100–1,200 cr in Q4) and Estrella/Lokhandwala (more than Rs 800 cr). [Q4 call]
- **FY27 guidance (first given on the Q4 call):**
  - pre-sales Rs 11,200 cr (+51%), 48% from sustenance and 52% from new launches; about Rs 7,000 cr from the South;
  - gross debt reduction about **Rs 750 cr**, excluding new land borrowing.

  [Q4 call]
- **Q1 call:**
  - Pre-sales guidance Rs 11,200 cr **kept**; Q1 delivered about 13% of it.
  - Debt-reduction target restated as **Rs 700 cr** (slightly lower).
  - EBITDA margin guidance 25–30% kept.

  [Q1 call]
- **Launch slippage continues:**
  - Hennur Road delayed by the change of government in Karnataka.
  - Cityspire and Winworth (Kochi) awaiting RERA.
  - Some Q1 launches moved to Q2.
  - Mumbai: Miami (Breach Candy) RERA received in June; Pali Hill launch Oct–Nov; Apna Ghar 3 and Deonar Baug in Q4.
  - Management admitted "some projects may be coming in Q2 may go to Q3".

  [Q1 call]
- **Collections:** no numeric guidance; management expects double-digit growth. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Land (Q1 FY27):** four Bengaluru deals — 41.93 acres, 4.23 mn sq ft, GDV Rs 5,200 cr. Land payments of Rs 574 cr were funded from March cash. [Q1 call]
- **Land (FY26):** six projects, more than 12 mn sq ft, GDV about Rs 15,200 cr, including:
  - Chembur redevelopment (Rs 2,100 cr);
  - Malabar Hill (Rs 2,700 cr at about Rs 1.15–1.2 lakh per sq ft of carpet area);
  - Attibele (53.5 acres, Rs 4,800 cr);
  - a north-Bengaluru JDA with KVN (Rs 3,300 cr).

  [Q4 call]
- **Commercial:** Aerocity Phase 2 (0.9 mn sq ft) will start only after 70–80% of Phase 1 is leased. A new 1.3 mn sq ft office at Hebbal is to start construction by Q4 FY27. [Q1 call]
- **Asset sale:** Purva Zentech sold to ICICI Prudential AMC at an enterprise value of about Rs 625 cr; about Rs 250 cr of debt on the asset will be repaid. Closing was expected in Aug 2026. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Net debt:** Rs 2,836 cr in Jun-26 (net debt to equity 1.57x), up from Rs 2,321 cr (1.31x) in Mar-26 after land payments. Cash Rs 1,106 cr. Average cost of debt **11.12%**, among the highest of listed developers. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Gross debt:** Screener shows Rs 5,595 cr (Mar-26), up from Rs 2,627 cr (Mar-22). Management says the extra debt was taken for land. [Screener] [Q4 call]
- **Hidden project debt:** about Rs 1,400 cr of project-linked NCDs are equity-like (repayment tied to project performance). Their fair-value interest is charged to the P&L at more than 11.05%. [Q4 call]
- **Management stance:** no aim to become debt-free: "debt is an important part of the business". Scheduled repayments in FY27 are Rs 836 cr. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Dividend:** none in FY25 or FY26. [Screener]
- **Credit rating:** India Ratings IND A-/Stable, affirmed Nov 2025; NCD, CP and bank-loan ratings withdrawn. [India Ratings Nov-25]
- **Cash still to come:** estimated surplus of Rs 19,290 cr — Rs 8,816 cr from ongoing projects, Rs 2,131 cr from commercial exits and Rs 8,343 cr from the pipeline. [Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Brand and scale:** 50-year brand; strong launch take-up in FY26 (Northern Lights sold about 30% at launch). [Q4 call]
- **Mumbai redevelopment:** growing position across price points, from Dombivli to Breach Candy. [Q1 call]
- **In-house construction:** Starworth. [Q4 call]
- **Weak cost position:** 11%+ cost of debt and thin reported profit. Several projects missed RERA deadlines (see risks).

## Input costs
- Construction costs were up 6–7% on higher diesel prices (Q4 call); management says existing contingencies cover it. [Q4 call]
- Margins at redevelopment and JDA projects are lower (about 20%), so there is limited room to absorb inflation. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor emphasis of matter:** covers ongoing legal proceedings. The auditor states these "may have an adverse effect on the functioning of the Group". [AR FY26]
  - Deposits and advances of Rs 61.4 cr on land/JDA plots with title disputes;
  - a Rs 35 cr claim from minority shareholders of a subsidiary;
  - Rs 82.7 cr of inventory under government land acquisition;
  - Rs 58.2 cr of customer receivables and Rs 17.6 cr of other balances under litigation.
- **Income Tax search (Oct 2023)** on the company, group companies and promoters:
  - assessment orders with a potential tax impact of Rs 45.1 cr (AY2016-17 to AY2024-25);
  - in FY26 a **Benami Property Act show-cause notice** asking why the company should not be treated as beneficial owner of **43.5 acres** of land (carrying value Rs 13.2 cr). The company has replied.

  [AR FY26]
- **RERA delays:** projects in Goa, Pune (two) and Bengaluru missed RERA completion deadlines; extensions were taken. These total 2.1 mn sq ft (Jun-25). [India Ratings Nov-25]
- **Leverage and interest:** net debt/equity 1.57x at 11%+ cost. The debt-reduction target competes with land buying (Rs 574 cr in Q1 alone, plus the NCR entry). [Q1 call]
- **Execution:** large pipeline in Bengaluru, where approvals are slow, and in Mumbai redevelopment, which is also slow. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Related parties:** transactions are mainly with subsidiaries (guarantees, security pledges). No material related-party transactions flagged outside the group. [AR FY26]
- **Pledges and promoters:** no promoter share pledge disclosed; promoter holding flat at 75%. FIIs about 16.6%. [Screener]
- **Management consistency:** on the Q4 call the South CEO said the company would "definitely participate" in senior living. On the Q1 call the CFO said "I don't think we have spoken about senior living". [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- **Operations:** Q1 pre-sales and collections grew and handovers rose; the P&L swung to a profit.
- **Debt:** the reduction target was trimmed from Rs 750 cr to Rs 700 cr. Net debt actually rose by about Rs 515 cr in Q1 because of land buying.
- **Asset recycling:** the Purva Zentech sale was signed (about Rs 625 cr enterprise value).
- **Launches:** some slipped by a quarter (Karnataka government change, Kochi RERA).
- **New market:** NCR entry followed in September (Greater Noida, GDV Rs 5,200 cr).

## My read
- **Sales engine improving:** record FY26 pre-sales, +28% in Q1, higher prices, a profitable P&L again.
- **Balance sheet is the weak link:** 11% debt, net debt/equity rising to 1.57x, and aggressive land buying (Bengaluru, Mumbai, now NCR) while promising debt reduction.
- **Legal overhang:** the tax search, Benami notice and auditor emphasis of matter need watching.
- **What would improve the view:**
  - Rs 11,200 cr of pre-sales delivered;
  - the Zentech sale closed with debt actually falling by about Rs 700 cr;
  - Aerocity leased.
- **What would worsen the view:** further launch slippage, net debt/equity above 1.75x, or an adverse Benami ruling.
