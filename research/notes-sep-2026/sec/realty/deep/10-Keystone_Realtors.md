# Keystone Realtors Ltd ("Rustomjee"; RUSTOMJEE, BSE 543669, ISIN INE263M01029)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million. Screener code: RUSTOMJEE.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** the flagship of the Rustomjee Group (chairman and MD Boman Irani; promoters 74.59%). It is a Mumbai-region (MMR) developer best known for **society and cluster redevelopment**. [Screener] [ICRA Sep-26]
- **Scale:**
  - more than 29 mn sq ft completed;
  - 8.7 mn sq ft saleable area under construction (17 projects, about 12 mn sq ft of construction area);
  - about 22.8 mn sq ft forthcoming.

  [ICRA Sep-26] [Q1 call]
- **Land additions:** since FY23 it has added 27 projects with GDV of Rs 31,079 cr. 21 of them are redevelopments, and five are cluster redevelopments, including GTB Nagar (11.5 acres) and Dindoshi. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Market share:** about 2% of MMR (target 5%). [Q4 call]
- **Price segments:**
  - emerging premium (Rs 1–3 cr), for example the Thane Urbania township;
  - premium (Rs 3–7 cr), the "sweet spot";
  - super-premium and luxury (Rs 8–150 cr): Panorama (Pali Hill), Ocean Vista (Versova), Cliff Tower (Bandra), Bandstand, Crescent.
  - 94% of the forthcoming pipeline is emerging premium to super-premium.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **New verticals:**
  - Plotted land (Igatpuri, about 62 acres, added in Q1 FY27): target Rs 500–750 cr of pre-sales a year.
  - Commercial offices (33Fifteen, 28 HQ Prabhadevi, 2 mn sq ft in Thane in phases): target about Rs 100 cr of rent by 2030.
  - A captive precast-concrete plant, with technical support from a Singapore partner.

  [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Accounting:** switched to **percentage-of-completion** revenue recognition for new projects from 1 Apr 2025. Legacy projects stay on completed-contract. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **FY26: all targets met or beaten.**
  - Pre-sales Rs 4,022 cr, on guidance.
  - Launches Rs 9,813 cr, 1.4x the Rs 7,000 cr guidance.
  - Land additions Rs 10,400 cr, 1.74x the Rs 6,000 cr guidance.
  - Net cash kept all year.
  - Management: "We have always met or surpassed every single guidance metric."

  [Q4 call]
- **FY27 (Q4 call):**
  - pre-sales Rs 5,000 cr (+25%);
  - launches about Rs 8,000 cr;
  - land additions about Rs 8,000 cr or more;
  - operating cash flow about Rs 1,000 cr;
  - gross debt/equity cap tightened from 1:1 to 0.75:1.
  - FY30 target: **Rs 10,000 cr** of pre-sales (about 26% CAGR).

  [Q4 call]
- **Q1 call: all kept.** Q1 pre-sales were only about 12% of the full-year target, so about Rs 4,300–4,400 cr is needed in nine months. More than Rs 2,000 cr of launches (28 HQ, Ozone Skye) have already happened in July. Operating cash flow will pick up "noticeably" in Q3–Q4. [Q1 call]
- **Launch timing:** GTB Nagar Phase 1 (a MHADA cluster) in Q2/early Q3; Dindoshi cluster about 2.5–3 months after its HPC (high-power committee) hearing. Management says "in this financial year for sure". [Q1 call]
- **Rating:** ICRA upgraded to AA-/Stable (from A+) and CRISIL assigned AA-/Stable. [Q1 call] [ICRA Sep-26]

## Capex and capacity
- **Investment rule:** about 10% of GDV invested before launch (asset-light redevelopment); each project underwritten at about 35% gross margin. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Q1 FY27 spending:** land and approvals Rs 232 cr (+54%); construction Rs 299 cr (+26%). FY26 construction spend was Rs 997 cr. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Virar:** floor-area rights (FSI) are being monetised through JDAs with other developers at about 60% margin. [Q1 call]
- **New vehicles:** One Landmark House LLP formed in Sep 2026 for a possible new project; a Palghar tie-up is flagged as coming "very soon". [BSE filing 9 Sep 2026] [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Jun-26:** gross debt Rs 876 cr; free cash about Rs 803 cr; gross debt/equity 0.3; net debt/equity 0.02 (the first net-debt figure after being net cash). [Q1 call]
- **Mar-26:** gross debt Rs 755 cr and free cash Rs 818 cr. [Q4 call]
- **Screener borrowings:** Rs 1,484 cr (Mar-26), higher than the company's gross debt. The gap likely reflects project-level NCDs and other items; ICRA rates about Rs 670 cr of NCDs. Not reconciled here. [Screener] [ICRA Sep-26]
- **Management stance:** net debt "is bound to go into the positive territory" as cash is deployed into projects. [Q1 call]
- **Dividend:** small (FY25 payout 11%; nil in FY26). [Screener]
- **Payment plans:** 10:90 plans (10% now, 90% at possession) are mostly backed by bank subvention, with the bank paying the developer as construction progresses. Management says no more than about 30% of buyers choose them. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Redevelopment record:** 25 years of redevelopment; more than 2,500 families rehoused. Knight Frank sizes the Mumbai redevelopment opportunity at about Rs 1.3 lakh cr by 2030. [Q4 call]
- **Cluster advantage:** Maharashtra gives additional incentives for large cluster schemes, and adjoining plots can be added later to improve margins. [Q1 call]
- **Sales velocity:** FY24 launches 66% sold and FY25 launches 49% sold by Mar-26. Sales pattern is about 35% by plinth level and 75% during construction. [Q4 call]
- **Credit:** dual AA-/Stable ratings (CRISIL, ICRA) — among the best among mid-size Mumbai developers. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Management says unsold stock can be repriced to cover cost inflation. The precast plant should cut material wastage by about 30% and labour needs by about 40%. [Q4 call]
- Legacy low-margin revenue shrinking from 62% to about 12% of the mix is the main driver of the margin step-up, not input costs. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Rustomjee Crown dispute (new, Sep 2026):**
  - BMC demolished alleged unauthorised alterations (foyers) in flats at Crown, Prabhadevi, in Aug 2026.
  - About 140 flat owners (246 plaintiffs) sued the company and BMC, seeking damages reported at **Rs 1,000 cr**.
  - The Bombay High Court ordered status quo on further demolition; the company says Crown was built as sanctioned (1991 DCR rules).
  - ICRA calls the financial impact "not ascertainable".

  [ICRA Sep-26] [News: Free Press Journal, Sep 2026]
- **Contingent liabilities (standalone, Mar-26):** indirect tax Rs 84.4 cr; income tax Rs 23.4 cr; other Rs 4.6 cr. [AR FY26]
- **Auditor:** no qualification, reservation or adverse remark, no emphasis of matter; key audit matter on revenue recognition. The statutory auditor changes to Walker Chandiok for FY27–FY31 after the previous term ended. [AR FY26] [BSE filing 18 Sep 2026]
- **Promoter selling:** promoter holding fell from 78.25% to 74.59% in the Dec-25 quarter, bringing it to the 75% minimum public shareholding level. [Screener]
- **Execution:** heavy dependence on MHADA/HPC approvals for cluster projects; nine months left to deliver 88% of the FY27 pre-sales target. [Q1 call]
- **Related parties:** Kapstone Constructions is a JV (construction); the group also has an AIF (Kapstone Opportunities Fund). No material related-party transactions were flagged. [AR FY26]
- **Pledges:** none reported.
- **Management:** CFO Sajal Gupta and the ED team were on both calls. No exits.

## What changed between the two calls
- **Launches:** Q1 had no launches, so pre-sales dipped; big launches started in July (28 HQ, Ozone Skye).
- **Margins:** reported EBITDA margin jumped to 21%, confirming the legacy roll-off.
- **Rating:** ICRA upgraded to AA-.
- **Net cash:** the company went from net cash to marginal net debt, as flagged.
- **After the call:** the Crown litigation (Sep 2026) is the main new negative.

## My read
- **Credible on execution:** a clean guidance record, AA- ratings, a low-debt balance sheet, and margins finally showing through as legacy projects roll off.
- **FY27 is back-loaded:** the Rs 5,000 cr target depends on H2 cluster launches that need government approvals.
- **New legal overhang:** the Crown litigation (a Rs 1,000 cr damages claim) could hurt brand perception in Mumbai even if the financial hit is small.
- **What would improve the view:** GTB Nagar and Dindoshi launched on time; FY27 operating cash flow of about Rs 1,000 cr; a favourable Crown ruling.
- **What would worsen the view:** launch delays leaving pre-sales below Rs 4,500 cr, or an adverse court finding on Crown's construction.
