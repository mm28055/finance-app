# Signatureglobal (India) Ltd (SIGNATURE, BSE 543990, ISIN INE903U01023)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million; Rs 1 bn = Rs 100 cr.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** a Gurugram-focused residential developer. It began in affordable housing (Haryana Affordable Housing Policy) and low-rise DDJAY floors. Since Feb 2024 it has moved up to mid-income and premium high-rise "group housing". [IP Q1FY27] [CARE Sep-26]
- **Track record:** delivered 19.2 mn sq ft. Portfolio of 50.2 mn sq ft saleable area:
  - 9.2 mn sq ft ongoing, of which affordable is 30% and mid-income Gurugram 64%;
  - 23.2 mn sq ft recently launched;
  - 17.8 mn sq ft forthcoming.

  [IP Q1FY27]
- **Three micro-markets:**

| Micro-market | Saleable area | Recent selling price |
|---|---|---|
| Sector 71 / Southern Peripheral Road (SPR) | 17.8 mn sq ft | about Rs 22,000 per sq ft |
| Sector 37D / Dwarka Expressway | 9.5 mn sq ft | about Rs 16,500 per sq ft |
| Sohna corridor | 7.5 mn sq ft | about Rs 15,000 per sq ft |

  [IP Q1FY27]
- **Geography:** all completed projects are in Gurugram and Sohna. About 62% of ongoing area is in Gurugram, the rest in Sohna and Manesar. [CARE Sep-26]
- **Construction model:** outsources construction to EPC contractors: Ahluwalia Contracts (Deluxe DXP), Capacit'e (Titanium and Cloverdale SPR) and Arabian Construction Co (Twin Tower DXP). [IP Q1FY27]
- **New lines of business:**
  - Branded residences (Tonino Lamborghini, Q1 FY27).
  - Commercial through a 50:50 JV with RMZ (Gurugram Commercity Ltd): about 17 acres on SPR, about 5.6 mn sq ft, indicative value Rs 14,000–15,000 cr.
  - Ultra-luxury farmhouse villas (Farrukhnagar, Sep 2026).

  [IP Q1FY27] [BSE filing 25 Sep 2026]

- **FY26 profit is almost entirely one-off.** FY26 net profit of Rs 1,095 cr includes an exceptional gain of Rs 1,267 cr from selling 50% of Gurugram Commercity to RMZ. Before that item, profit before tax was only Rs 43 cr (against Rs 105 cr in FY25). [AR FY26] [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **FY26 promises vs delivery:**
  - Collections were guided at about Rs 6,000 cr and came in at Rs 4,022 cr. Management blamed slower construction.
  - Completions slipped because of heavy rains and long NGT (National Green Tribunal) construction bans.
  - Pre-sales fell 19%. The IP footnote admits: "earlier pre-sales guidance reflected stronger visibility at the time".

  [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27] [CARE Sep-26]
- **FY27 guidance (Q4 call), all kept on the Q1 call:**
  - launches over Rs 15,000 cr;
  - pre-sales about Rs 10,000 cr;
  - revenue recognition Rs 5,000 cr (needing completions worth Rs 6,000–6,500 cr);
  - collections above Rs 5,000 cr. The CEO said "once bitten, twice shy" about setting this target.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Q1 FY27 progress:** pre-sales at about 20% of target; collections Rs 670 cr, about 13% of target and called an "aberration". Launches reached about Rs 4,400 cr (Lamborghini). [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Launch plan (Q4 call):** Lamborghini in Q1 (delivered), a second SPR tower around Diwali (Q2–Q3), and a 2 mn sq ft SPR residential launch next to the RMZ site in Q4. The Q1 call said the H2 launches are "most likely" in SPR as well. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Land spending: raised.** Q4 call said Rs 1,000–1,500 cr for FY27; Q1 call said Rs 1,500–1,800 cr, now including large low-rise townships outside NCR. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Land:**
  - Rs 700 cr spent in FY26, adding 2.5 mn sq ft in Sohna.
  - On 25 Sep 2026 it bought 25 acres (about Rs 150 cr, per press) and signed a collaboration agreement for 169.22 acres in Farrukhnagar (Gurugram West).
  - That adds 6.77 mn sq ft with GDV of Rs 5,500–6,000 cr.

  [Q4 call] [BSE filing 25 Sep 2026] [News: Business Standard, 25 Sep 2026]
- **RMZ JV:** total capex about Rs 3,500–4,000 cr over 4–4.5 years for both partners combined. Debt is to be raised inside the JV and kept off Signatureglobal's consolidated balance sheet; equity goes in as an investment. [Q4 call]
- **Construction:** 9.2 mn sq ft is ongoing, with most of it due in 4–6 quarters. About 46% of total cost on ongoing projects has been incurred. [IP Q1FY27] [CARE Sep-26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Jun-26:** cash and bank Rs 2,522 cr, but about Rs 1,700 cr of that sits in RERA and escrow accounts. [IP Q1FY27] [CARE Sep-26]
- **Net debt:** Rs 390 cr (Jun-26), up from Rs 200 cr (Mar-26). Management expects it to rise with land purchases. Internal cap: net debt below 0.5x the year's operating surplus. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- **Gross debt:** above Rs 2,900 cr at Mar-26 (Rs 2,300 cr at Mar-25). Screener shows borrowings of Rs 2,979 cr. CARE says the cost of debt "continues to remain elevated". [CARE Sep-26] [Screener]
- **Where FY26 operating cash went:** operating cash flow about Rs 2,100 cr (company definition), split roughly one-third land, one-third debt reduction, one-third approvals and interest. RMZ also paid about Rs 1,250 cr. [Q4 call]
- **Cash still to come:** committed receivables above Rs 19,600 cr. Unsold stock on ongoing launched projects is worth over Rs 11,100 cr, about 6 quarters of sales at the current pace. [CARE Sep-26]
- **Dividends and buybacks:** none. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- **Local land bank and approvals:** 93 acres on SPR Sector 71 with licensed, DTCP-paid land. Management argues Gurugram had almost no group-housing supply between 2014 and 2022, so delivered stock is scarce. [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Launch absorption:**
  - FY26 launches worth about Rs 10,000 cr sold more than 40% (over Rs 4,000 cr).
  - Lamborghini sold over 300 of about 400 released units, more than Rs 1,500 cr, at over Rs 22,000 per sq ft.
  - Internal target is 40% sold at launch, which covers construction cost.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Pricing:** realisation per sq ft up from Rs 11,762 (FY24) to Rs 17,093 (Q1 FY27), mostly from mix and some market price increases. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Credit:** CARE A+/Stable, reaffirmed on 22 Sep 2026. [CARE Sep-26]
- **No cost advantage:** construction is outsourced; the edge is the land bank and speed of approvals.

## Input costs
- Construction cost inflation has run at 7–8% a year over a decade; management budgets for it. No unusual spike was seen in Q1 FY27 despite the Middle East conflict. [Q1 call]
- Because land is largely paid for and sold early, rising construction costs are the main margin risk. The company funds construction from customer advances. [CARE Sep-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Weaker operations:** FY26 pre-sales -19%, collections -8% and Q1 FY27 down again year-on-year. Collections are far behind sales. CARE flags reliance on customer advances. [CARE Sep-26]
- **Concentration:** almost the whole business is in Gurugram, and FY27 launches are concentrated in one micro-market (SPR Sector 71) at Rs 5–5.5 cr ticket sizes. [Q1 call] [CARE Sep-26]
- **Quality of earnings:** FY26 profit is a one-off gain from selling a JV stake; the core P&L lost money in three of the last four quarters. [AR FY26] [Screener]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion. Key audit matters cover revenue recognition and valuation of investments and loans. The BRSR assurance carries an emphasis of matter on ESG data systems, which is non-financial. [AR FY26]
- **Contingent liabilities:** small, mostly income tax. The largest items are Rs 6.6 cr (AY2015-17) and Rs 3.9 cr (AY2022-23). There are also unquantified customer and contractor cases. [AR FY26]
- **Related-party dealings:** no material related-party transactions; AOC-2 filed. [AR FY26]
- **Loss-making subsidiaries:** Signatureglobal Business Park lost Rs 47.6 cr in FY26; Forever Buildtech lost Rs 17 cr. [AR FY26]
- **Pledges:** none disclosed. Promoter holding is flat at about 69.5%. [Screener]
- **Management:** the same team (four Aggarwal promoters, CEO Rajat Kathuria, CFO Sanjeev Kumar Sharma) was on both calls. No exits noted.
- **Regulation:** Gurugram construction is regularly halted by NGT and air-quality (GRAP) bans. RERA granted a 4-month extension advisory tied to the Middle East conflict. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- **Launch pipeline:** the Lamborghini launch happened as promised.
- **Pre-sales:** weaker year-on-year, but the Rs 10,000 cr target was held.
- **Collections:** missed again; framed as "lumpy milestones slipped".
- **Land appetite:** raised to Rs 1,500–1,800 cr, now including large-format low-rise mid-income townships outside NCR. The Farrukhnagar deal followed in September.
- **Net debt:** nearly doubled to Rs 390 cr in the quarter.

## My read
- **Direction:** the business is moving upmarket successfully. Selling prices are up about 45% in two years, and branded, farmhouse and commercial projects widen the product range.
- **Operating trend is weaker:** pre-sales and collections have fallen for over a year, and reported profit before the one-off RMZ gain is about nil.
- **Balance sheet:** still sound (net debt Rs 390 cr, A+ rating), but more aggressive land buying will push debt up.
- **What would improve the view:**
  - collections back above about Rs 1,100 cr a quarter;
  - Rs 5,000 cr of FY27 revenue recognition actually delivered;
  - the H2 SPR launches selling 40% or more.
- **What would worsen the view:** pre-sales below Rs 8,000 cr again, or debt to collections approaching CARE's 0.8x trigger.
