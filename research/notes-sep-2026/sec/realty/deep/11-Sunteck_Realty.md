# Sunteck Realty Ltd (SUNTECK, BSE 512179, ISIN INE805D01034)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** a Mumbai-region (MMR) residential developer run by chairman and MD Kamal Khetan (promoters 63.14%). It sells in three "luxury" bands: [Q1 call] [India Ratings Jun-26]
  - **Uber luxury:** BKC (Signature Island/Signia) and Nepean Sea Road in south Mumbai.
  - **Premium luxury:** Oshiwara District Centre (ODC, Goregaon), including Sunteck City and 5th Avenue; Sky Park in Mira Road; Beach Residences in Vasai.
  - **Aspirational luxury:** large low-cost townships at Naigaon (Sunteck World, One World) and Kalyan.
- **Pre-sales mix:**
  - Q1 FY27: uber luxury 29%, premium 50%, aspirational 21%.
  - Q4 FY26: about 50% uber luxury plus commercial, 40–45% premium, 10–15% aspirational.

  [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Rental:** two debt-free office assets earn Rs 60–70 cr a year. A large ODC commercial/retail cluster is planned from about FY29; the IP targets about Rs 450 cr of rent by FY29. [India Ratings Jun-26] [Q1 call]
- **Dubai:** a 50% partner in a Downtown Dubai plot next to Dubai Mall, bought at AED 385 mn. GDV is about Rs 9,000 cr. The project is launch-ready but deferred because of the West Asia war. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **GDV:** about Rs 44,100 cr in total at FY26 (gross of pre-sales); Rs 42,700 cr at Jun-26 (net of sales). By model, JDAs are about 50%, owned land 26% and JVs the rest. "To-be-launched" is about Rs 16,100 cr including Dubai, or about Rs 7,100 cr excluding it. Trailing twelve months to Jun-26: pre-sales Rs 3,287 cr against collections of only Rs 1,491 cr. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Accounting:** revenue is recognised mainly at possession, with percentage-of-completion for some projects. [AR FY26]

## Guidance history
- **FY26:** pre-sales growth of about 25% was guided and delivered (Rs 3,157 cr). [Q4 call]
- **FY27 pre-sales growth:** the Q4 call guided "similar" (about 25%) growth, "irrespective of Dubai". The Q1 call restated it as **25–30%**. Q1 delivered 20% growth and management says it is confident on the full year. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 launches:** about Rs 7,000 cr, including:
  - an extra tower at ODC 5th Avenue (Altavia already launched);
  - the Andheri (Western Express Highway) redevelopment;
  - Sky Park 2 in Mira Road;
  - Vasai and Naigaon towers;
  - Sunteck City Tower 2.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Nepean Sea Road: slipping.**
  - Q4 call: "100%" RERA and construction start "in the first two quarters".
  - On the Q1 call an analyst asked about RERA; management spoke only of large collections "once we start the construction".
  - Tanirika Infrastructure (Rs 22.4 cr) was bought in Apr 2026 to consolidate the Nepean Sea plot.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [News: ScanX, Apr 2026]
- **Dubai:** launch deferred from "ASAP" (Q4 call) to "timing recalibrated" (Q1 call). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Collections:** Q1 call said they should grow 25–30%, with the big step-up when Nepean Sea construction starts. [Q1 call]
- **Land spending:** FY26 Rs 810 cr (FY25: Rs 180 cr), the highest ever. FY27 is expected to be "much more". [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **FY26 land additions:** three projects, GDV about Rs 5,000 cr:
  - Andheri redevelopment near the Western Express Highway;
  - Mira Road JDA (3.5 acres);
  - Andheri JB Nagar near the airport (1.75 acres, bought outright; residential or commercial still undecided).

  [Q4 call]
- **Dubai:** AED 70 mn invested at purchase (about Rs 12 per dirham) plus about AED 60 mn in FY26. The company puts it at "not more than Rs 200–225 cr". [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **ODC commercial:** construction starting soon, with completion expected 24–30 months later. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Leverage:** net debt/equity 0.06–0.07x. Gross debt doubled to Rs 774 cr in FY26 (Rs 387 cr in FY25). India Ratings expects gross debt to peak at Rs 1,100–1,200 cr because of front-loaded land spending, and notes cash of only about Rs 90 cr at FY26 end. [Screener] [Q1 call] [India Ratings Jun-26]
- **Equity raise under way:**
  - Preferential convertible warrants of Rs 500 cr issued in Dec 2025; about 65% taken up by the promoters.
  - 25% (Rs 125 cr) was received upfront; the remaining Rs 375 cr is due by about mid-2027.
  - There is also an annual enabling resolution for about Rs 2,000 cr of fund-raising; management says there is "no planning" to use it.

  [India Ratings Jun-26] [Q1 call]
- **Operating cash flow:** -Rs 432 cr in FY26 (Screener), because of land spending. [Screener]
- **Working capital:** debtor days fell to 37. [Screener]
- **Dividend:** payout about 11%. [Screener]
- **Credit rating:** India Ratings IND AA/Stable and A1+, affirmed Jun 2026. [India Ratings Jun-26]

## Moat evidence
- **Luxury addresses:** BKC and Nepean Sea Road. BKC plus Nepean Sea sold about Rs 1,500 cr in FY26 (Rs 609 cr in Q4). [Q4 call]
- **Low inventory:** unsold stock in projects under construction is under 12 months of sales. [Q4 call]
- **Pricing:** average selling price above Rs 25,000–30,000 per sq ft, so a 2–3% cost rise barely moves margins. Management says no discounts are being given. [Q4 call]
- **Execution record:** committed receivables are 1.5x the remaining construction cost; over 65% of developed area is already sold. [India Ratings Jun-26]
- **ESG:** GRESB score 99/100; DJSI ESG score 78. [Q1 call]

## Input costs
- Tiles and imported finishing goods rose in price; labour was short around the state elections. Cement, steel and ready-mix concrete were not in short supply. [Q4 call]
- Management expects prices to stay "stable", not rising. Margin expansion should come from the richer mix and newer launches. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Cash conversion:** FY26 collections were only about 45% of pre-sales; debt doubled; India Ratings flags that debt taken for land has no receivables behind it. [India Ratings Jun-26] [Q4 call]
- **Concentration:**
  - geography: MMR only;
  - segment: heavy reliance on ultra-luxury (BKC, Nepean Sea) and a Dubai bet;
  - a deferred Dubai launch ties up capital.

  [India Ratings Jun-26]
- **Auditor emphasis of matter:**
  - Rs 14.0 cr receivable from a former partnership firm, in dispute; the arbitration award in the company's favour is challenged in the Bombay High Court, and the firm's accounts are unavailable.
  - Recoverability questions over the investment in the Piramal Sunteck Realty JV (Rs 17.3 cr).

  [AR FY26]
- **Tax:** GST show-cause notice (DRC-01 under Section 73) received 24 Sep 2026 over FY23 TDR (transferable development rights) transactions; the company expects no material impact. [BSE filing 25 Sep 2026]
- **Contingent liabilities:** group share of claims not acknowledged as debts is about Rs 0.8 cr in the JV note; no large items seen. [AR FY26]
- **Promoter holding:** slipped from 67% three years ago to 63.14%; promoters are now putting money back in through the warrants. [Screener] [India Ratings Jun-26]
- **Disclosure quality:**
  - short 30-minute calls;
  - the CMD acknowledged a mislabelled GDV table in the IP;
  - Dubai investment figures were given loosely.

  [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Pledges:** none reported.

## My read
- **Operations are improving:** steady 20–25% pre-sales growth, rising margins, a strong AA rating and a light net-debt position.
- **Swing factors:** the two big value drivers — Nepean Sea Road and Dubai — are both delayed. Collections lag sales, and debt is rising with land buying, partly offset by promoter warrant money.
- **What would improve the view:** Nepean Sea RERA and construction start in H2; collections above Rs 1,800 cr in FY27.
- **What would worsen the view:** further delays at the flagship projects, weak luxury demand in Mumbai, or gross debt heading above Rs 1,200 cr without a matching rise in collections.
