# Arvind SmartSpaces Ltd (ARVSMART, BSE 539301)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** the real-estate arm of the Lalbhai group (Arvind Ltd), set up in 2008 and headquartered in Ahmedabad. It builds residential projects for middle- and high-income buyers: plotted developments and villas (horizontal) plus apartments (vertical). Commercial and industrial projects are selective. [IndRa Jun-26] [Screener]
- **Scale:** completed 9.8 mn sq ft; 57.9 mn sq ft under construction; about 29.9 mn sq ft in pipeline (Ind-Ra's figures). [IndRa Jun-26]
- **Three core markets:**
  - Ahmedabad and Gujarat: leader in horizontal (plotted) development; the Aqua City plotted project is the current star.
  - Bengaluru: 31% of FY26 bookings (Rs 485 cr); about Rs 3,000 cr of stock.
  - Mumbai region: over Rs 4,000 cr of GDV locked. Three projects: Pen-Khopoli (plotted, about Rs 1,500 cr), Santacruz redevelopment, and Goregaon (a MHADA redevelopment with Oxford Sigma; 44% profit share for Arvind).

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Operating model:** a mix of outright land, joint developments (JDs) and JVs, with HDFC Capital platform money via optionally convertible debentures (OCDs) in three Bengaluru projects. Revenue is booked on completion (occupancy certificate or building-use permission), so reported numbers are lumpy. [Q4 call] [AR FY26]
- **Mumbai share of portfolio:** Ind-Ra says it is 20% of portfolio value, moving toward a roughly equal split across the three cities. [IndRa Jun-26]

## Guidance history
- **FY27 bookings growth of 35–40%** (Rs 2,100–2,200 cr), given at the Q4 call and **kept** at Q1. The 22 Sep Sylva launch and Q1's Rs 432 cr put the company on track. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **FY27 land deals (business development): Rs 4,000–5,000 cr GDV**, kept. Rs 2,600 cr was done in Q1 (Goregaon plus South Ahmedabad JD); management now aims at "the higher band". [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **EBITDA margin on new sales: 22–25%**, kept. JDs sit at the low end and outright land at the high end. Reported margins (49% in Q1) are project-specific. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Operating cash flow:**
  - Q4 call: FY27 roughly flat at about Rs 417 cr.
  - Q1 call: Rs 400–500 cr, which management described as what it had said "in our previous call". It was described as "largely" flat.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Launches:** six projects, about Rs 3,000–3,500 cr, in FY27, weighted to H2.
  - Ahmedabad: Vastrapur (H1).
  - Bengaluru: three, including Sarjapur, approved.
  - Mumbai: two, of which Pen-Khopoli is ahead. Goregaon was newly named as a candidate at the Q1 call.

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Long term:** pre-sales CAGR of 25–30% over 4–5 years. There is no revenue guidance because revenue depends on approvals. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Surat project dropped** after diligence, citing technical and legal complexity. [Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Group backing:** Lalbhai group parentage, and HDFC Capital as a long-standing capital partner. [IndRa Jun-26]
- **Plotted-development niche:** a leading position in Ahmedabad, where fast sales have kept operating cash flow at 36–37% of collections (management expects 25–30% going forward). [Q4 call]
- **Fast launch sales:** 42–60% of launched inventory sold within days (Skycrest, Greenfields, Sylva). [Q4 call] [BSE filing 22 Sep 2026]
- **Sustenance sales are growing** (over Rs 600 cr in FY26; the whole of Q1), which reduces dependence on launches. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Weak spot:** Forest Trails (Bengaluru villas) is over 50% unsold and saw net cancellations (-Rs 11 cr, 5 units) in FY26. Management says the show-villa experience will be ready and sales will restart. [Q4 call]

## Input costs
- About 4% cost increase comparing before and after the Middle East war; the budget contingency covers it without price hikes "if this is not very, very long drawn". [Q4 call]
- Labour costs are rising gradually but are not yet hurting project profitability. [Q1 call]
- Price growth is expected to moderate; land deals are underwritten without large price increases. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Leverage is rising fast:** gross debt doubled in FY26, and Ind-Ra sees net debt reaching Rs 800–850 cr. **Company net-debt figures exclude platform OCDs** (Rs 167 cr vs Ind-Ra's Rs 330 cr). [IndRa Jun-26] [Q4 call]
- **Mumbai execution:** redevelopment and MHADA projects carry approval and society risk. Goregaon uses a two-layer JV (44% profit share) with Oxford Sigma, which handles approvals. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Launch timing:** H2-heavy launches and approval slippage risk; FY26 revenue fell 21% on delayed certificates. [Q4 call]
- **Ind-Ra's listed weaknesses:** moderate concentration by project, ticket size and geography. [IndRa Jun-26]
- **Institutional churn:**
  - The HDFC Capital Affordable Real Estate Fund-1 sold down from 8.79% to 1.00% in Feb–Mar 2026.
  - Pirojsha Godrej (Godrej Properties chairman) personally bought 1.78% at Rs 498 on 9 Mar 2026.
  - Promoters rose from 49.83% to 53.83% in the Mar-26 quarter, while DII holdings fell from 15.8% to 8.6%.

  [News: Business Standard, 10 Mar 2026] [Screener]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion, no emphasis of matter. Key audit matters are revenue recognition and inventory carrying value. [AR FY26]
- **Contingent liabilities:** tiny (GST Tran-1 credit dispute, Rs 2.5 cr).
- **Related parties:** no AOC-2 transactions. [AR FY26]

## My read
Arvind SmartSpaces is scaling from a Gujarat plotted-development specialist into a three-city developer, with good launch absorption and a credible guidance record: FY26 bookings met the growth guide, and FY27 is on track after a strong sustenance-only Q1. The Ind-Ra upgrade to AA- helps funding, but debt is now rising deliberately (towards Rs 800–850 cr net) to buy land, including in Mumbai redevelopment, where the company has no delivery record yet. Reported profit will stay lumpy; the Q1 margin of 49% is not the run-rate (22–25%). Key watch items are Mumbai launches in H2 FY27, operating cash flow of Rs 400–500 cr, and whether net debt tracks Ind-Ra's band.
