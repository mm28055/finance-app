# Marathon Nextgen Realty Ltd (MARATHON, BSE 503101)

*Research date: 25 Sep 2026. Latest reported quarter: Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026). Crore = Rs 10 million.*

## Business and segments
- **What it is:** the listed arm of Mumbai's Marathon group (Shah family). The promoter holding company is Marathon Realty Pvt Ltd (MRPL). The company develops residential and commercial projects across the Mumbai region (MMR); revenue is booked on the **percentage-of-completion method**. [Q4 call] [Infomerics Aug-25]
- **Key projects:**
  - **Monte South, Byculla:** luxury joint venture; the company takes a 40% revenue share. Four 65-storey residential towers plus a commercial tower.
  - **Nexzone, Panvel:** 25 acres, 19 towers, mid-income and affordable homes.
  - **Neo series, Bhandup:** NeoValley, NeoPark, NeoSquare and NeoHome.
  - **Commercial:** Futurex (Lower Parel, ready offices sold and leased) and Millennium (Mulund, small offices).

  [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **New verticals in FY26:**
  - Kanjurmarg: controlling interest in three entities for about Rs 70 cr, covering six projects with GDV over Rs 840 cr. The company has a one-third economic interest and 50% control.
  - Permanent Transit Camp (PTC) business: the company builds transit housing and sells it to other developers, who get extra development rights (FSI) in return.
  - Sunset Spaces: 90% stake bought (Versova redevelopment).

  [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Proposed group merger:** a composite scheme would bring promoter entities owning about 400–418 acres (Panvel, Bhandup, Dombivli) and Futurex inventory into the listed company.
  - The scheme covers Matrix Water, Sanvo Resorts, MRPL, Matrix Enclaves, Matrix Land Hub and Marathon Energy.
  - Shareholder and creditor meetings were held on 7 Sep 2026; the second NCLT hearing is pending.

  [Q1 call] [AR FY26] [BSE filing 7 Sep 2026]

## Moat evidence
- **Futurex pricing:** prices up about 15% YoY and 50–60% over 5–6 years. The company sells and leases, and has 300+ corporate clients (Nykaa, ZEE, L'Oréal, SBI, HDFC). Remaining inventory is 12–15 months. [Q4 call]
- **Monte South:** a scarce-supply luxury address; ready towers sell at a premium. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Infrastructure tailwinds:** Atal Setu, the Navi Mumbai airport, and Panvel–Karjat rail (deadline Dec 2026) for Panvel; the Goregaon–Mulund Link Road for Bhandup. These are cited, not proven, advantages. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Weak spot:** sales volumes are small; Q1 bookings on the company's share were Rs 86 cr. Nexzone and Neo sales are modest. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Loans to the promoter company:**
  - Loans to **Marathon Realty Pvt Ltd (holding company)** were **Rs 822.9 cr at Mar-26, up from Rs 605.9 cr**. They are repayable on demand at 12–13.5%, "for execution".
  - The company also gives a corporate guarantee to MRPL.
  - The auditor's CARO report states loans were given to the promoter and related parties "without specifying terms or period of repayment": Rs 1,094 cr standalone (Rs 586 cr promoter, Rs 508 cr related parties).
  - Both are key audit matters: loans and investments in group entities are 49.6% of standalone assets, and loans to related entities are 38.1% of consolidated assets.

  [AR FY26]
- **More promoter lending sought:** the AGM notice asked for approval of loans, guarantees and security to MRPL of **up to Rs 1,000 cr**, equal to 201.6% of the company's turnover.
  - Transactions were Rs 173 cr in FY26 and Rs 20 cr in Q1 FY27.
  - MRPL's standalone turnover is Rs 117.5 cr, with PAT Rs 49.5 cr and net worth Rs 519 cr.
  - In effect, QIP-era cash is funding promoter-entity projects that the merger is meant to absorb.

  [AR FY26]
- **Emphasis of matter:** the pending merger scheme (appointed date 1 Jan 2025); the accounts will be restated on approval. The swap terms and valuation were not reviewed here. [AR FY26]
- **Earnings quality:** heavy reliance on other income and one-off investment-property sales; negative cash from operations; ROE below 5%. [Screener]
- **Contingent liabilities:** "Others" Rs 50.5 cr; GST/excise/service tax Rs 14.3 cr (up from Rs 8.2 cr); RERA cases Rs 4.8 cr. [AR FY26]
- **Disclosure:** no numeric guidance; bookings are reported both on the company's share and "post-merger", which complicates comparison. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## My read
Marathon now reports a clean, near debt-free balance sheet and a big pipeline, but the listed company is effectively financing its promoter: Rs 823 cr is lent on demand to MRPL, and shareholders were asked to allow up to Rs 1,000 cr. The group merger is the real thesis because it would fold in about 400 acres and the loan book. Its terms, NCLT timing and valuation are the key unknowns. Operating momentum is thin (Q1 bookings Rs 86 cr on its share) and profits lean on other income. I would treat this as a governance-sensitive special situation until the merger closes and cash from operations turns positive.
