# Aimtron Electronics — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

NSE SME (Emerge) company; reports half-yearly. Latest results: H2 FY26 (to 31 Mar 2026), call 28 Apr 2026. No Q1 FY27 numbers exist; next results are H1 FY27 (Sep-26 half).

## Business and segments
- Vadodara (Gujarat) based contract manufacturer and designer of electronics: PCB assemblies, complete box builds and some design-to-order (ODM) work for industrial, telecom/networking, power/data-centre, IoT/robotics, automotive/BMS, medical and small defence/railway customers. About 45–50 in-house designers (150 with partners), ~80 ready reference platforms. [H2 call; IP H2FY26]
- FY26 revenue mix: box build ~69%, PCBA ~29%, ODM/design small (management: under 5%). Customers ~70% domestic; exports 20–25% of the order book. [IP H2FY26; H2 call]
- Plants: Vadodara (9 SMT lines across the group before AIC; 37 through-hole lines; 3 box-build lines) and a small Bengaluru unit. Management says 9 SMT lines can support ₹800–900 cr of revenue; each line ₹80–100 cr. No utilisation figure for India. [H2 call; IP H2FY26]
- US: Aimtron Electronics LLC (Texas) and, from 1 Feb 2026, **Aimtron International Controls (AIC)**, the former International Control Services in Decatur, Illinois — rugged electronics for agriculture, mining and fire-safety OEMs; 58,000–60,000 sq ft on ~4 acres, 4 SMT lines, ~54–55% utilised when bought; ~$17m annual revenue; bought at "5–6x" or "6–7x" EBITDA (both said on the same call; price not disclosed). [ICS call Jan-26; Business update 17-Sep-26]
- New subsidiary Aimtron Mechatronics (Vadodara greenfield): injection moulding, sheet metal and later 2–4 (up to 6) SMT lines. [IP H2FY26; H2 call]

## Key numbers
- Screener consolidated (half-years, revenue / operating profit / OPM / PAT): H1 FY25 ₹58 cr / ₹15 cr / 25% / ₹11 cr; H2 FY25 ₹102 / ₹19 / 19% / ₹14; H1 FY26 ₹123 / ₹30 / 25% / ₹20; H2 FY26 ₹179 / ₹36 / 20% / ₹26. [Screener]
- Full years: FY24 ₹93 cr / ₹24 cr (26%) / PAT ₹14 cr; FY25 ₹159 cr / ₹32 cr (20%) / ₹26 cr; FY26 ₹301 cr / ₹61 cr (20%) / ₹46 cr. FY26 revenue +89%, PAT +79%. [Screener]
- Company's EBITDA definition includes other income: FY26 ₹65.8 cr (21.8%), H2 FY26 ₹35.7 cr (20.0%). Screener's operating margin is ~2 points lower. [IP H2FY26]
- AIC contributed about $1.6m in its first two months (Feb–Mar 2026) at ~11% EBITDA. [H2 call]
- Open order book ₹521 cr at 31 Mar 2026 (FY25: ₹189 cr), about 1.7x FY26 revenue, plus a ₹57.7 cr IoT/AI-surveillance order on 6 Apr 2026; management calls it "~₹600 cr" including AIC. RFQ pipeline ₹900–950 cr. Execution typically 12–16 months. [IP H2FY26; H2 call]
- AIC booked $11.8m of orders in the Jul–Sep 2026 quarter (to 17 Sep) vs a historical average under $4m a quarter; five new customers. [Business update 17-Sep-26]
- Unit economics not disclosed; management says single orders have grown from ₹5–10 cr to ₹25–50 cr.

## Guidance history
- Before the ICS deal (Nov 2025 / Jan 2026): FY26 revenue guidance raised from ₹225 cr to ~₹280 cr; ₹1,000 cr revenue "within three years" (FY28–FY29). [ICS call Jan-26]
- Delivered FY26: ₹301 cr — **beat** the raised guide. EBITDA margin held ~20–22%. [Screener; H2 call]
- H2 FY26 call (28 Apr 2026): FY27 revenue ₹450–550 cr (organic 40–50% plus AIC); consolidated EBITDA 20–22% "±1–2%"; PAT ~15%; 40–50% CAGR over 3–5 years toward ₹1,000 cr; AIC margin 11% → 15% quickly and 18–20% in 1–2 years; AIC revenue ~$17m in FY27, $25–30m peak. [H2 call]
- No Q1 FY27 delivery check possible (half-yearly reporting). The Sep-26 AIC order update is consistent with the guide.

## Capex and capacity
- Existing plants: no major capex needed for two years apart from small machine additions; nine SMT lines support ₹800–900 cr revenue. [H2 call]
- AIC: ₹10–12 cr planned for ERP/MRP, tooling and working capital to lift utilisation from ~54% to ~90% over ~3 years; revenue capacity ₹280–300 cr. [ICS call Jan-26; IP H2FY26]
- Mechatronics greenfield: construction under way; mechanical shop first, SMT lines in phase 2; ready Q4 FY27 or Q1 FY28 at the latest. Cost not disclosed. [H2 call]
- Funding: ₹94.6 cr from convertible warrants (Sep 2025; conversion price ₹680.64); a further ₹205 cr preferential issue approved 25 Sep 2026 to AIFs (Clarus Capital ₹120 cr, 360 ONE ₹40 cr, Niveshaay ₹20 cr, Alchemy ₹15 cr, AAA Apex ₹10 cr) at ₹1,674.47 per share, "for strategic acquisitions (mergers and JVs across the globe), working capital and general corporate purposes"; EGM 22 Oct 2026. [Board outcome 25-Sep-26; IP H2FY26]
- Management says further acquisitions (design firms in North America/Europe) were "on hold" in April; the September fund-raise explicitly names acquisitions again. [H2 call; Board outcome 25-Sep-26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Operating cash flow negative in two of the last three years: FY24 +₹7 cr, FY25 –₹19 cr, FY26 –₹40 cr; free cash flow FY26 –₹87 cr (includes the AIC purchase). [Screener]
- Borrowings rose from ₹1 cr (Mar-25) to ₹55 cr (Mar-26), mostly long-term (₹12.3 cr India long-term plus US acquisition debt). Equity ₹235 cr. [Screener; IP H2FY26]
- FY26 debtor days 164, inventory days 176, payable days 86; working-capital days 256 (FY25: 195). Management: ODM work and proactive stocking of scarce components need more inventory; 30–40% of March receivables collected since. [Screener; H2 call]
- Standalone lent ₹16.8 cr to Aimtron Electronics LLC for the AIC deal and ₹7.8 cr to Aimtron Mechatronics. [AR FY26 note 35; H2 call]
- No dividend. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Approvals: ISO 13485 and CDSCO (medical), IATF 16949 (auto), AS9100 (aerospace), telecom partner status, RDSO (railways) expected H1–Q3 FY27. [H2 call]
- Wins: $12m (~₹100 cr) data-centre UPS design-and-build contract with a US Fortune-500 firm (six variants, 1–3 kVA, potential to 20 kVA); ₹97.6 cr ODM contract with a US infrastructure firm (H1 FY26); 50,000-unit AI/IIoT box-build order worth ₹46 cr; Navratna PSU communication order; SIL-4 railway signalling work. [IP H2FY26; H2 call]
- Management frames the edge as speed and complexity in high-mix, low-volume work ("speedboat vs Titanic" vs L&T). Evidence of pricing power is thin: it is cost-plus and the box-build shift has raised revenue per order more than margin. [H2 call]
- IP usually belongs to the customer; Aimtron takes a royalty or 5–7-year supply contract when it funds design. [H2 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Cash burn:** negative operating cash flow while profits rise; working-capital days at 256 and rising; growth is being funded by equity (warrants, ₹205 cr preferential) and new debt. [Screener]
- **Related-party trading with promoter-owned US companies:** sales to Aimtron Corporation USA, American Pinball USA, Aimtron Systems USA and Aimtron Technologies LLC (all "foreign company with same management") were roughly ₹44 cr in FY25 (about 27% of FY25 revenue) and ~₹11 cr in FY26, as the related-party table reads; the company also pays ~₹4 cr a year for services to promoter-owned Aimtron Technologies Pvt Ltd. An income-tax transfer-pricing notice (s.92CA(3)) was received for FY24 international transactions. [AR FY26 note 35 & 31]
- **Auditor's key audit matters** read like control gaps: no policy yet to classify slow-moving/obsolete inventory; scrap policy not implemented; fixed-asset tagging incomplete; loans and advances carried without confirmations; no definitive policy yet for consolidating the foreign subsidiaries; a receivable from a bankrupt international customer written off (~₹1.3 cr). Overseas subsidiaries audited by other auditors. Opinion unmodified. [AR FY26 auditor's reports]
- **Contingent liabilities:** income-tax demand ₹7.9 cr (10% paid) and GST ₹13 cr — ₹20.9 cr in total (FY25: ₹7.9 cr), ~45% of FY26 PAT; EPCG export obligation stated as ₹187.3 cr to be met by FY28. [AR FY26 note 31]
- **Acquisition risk:** AIC margin (~11%) is half the India level; the deal price was not disclosed and management gave two different multiples. More deals are being funded. [ICS call Jan-26; H2 call]
- **Disclosure:** half-yearly reporting only; presentation and results released close to the call; H2 gross margin fell from 31% to 28%. Main-board migration expected after three years of listing (mid-2027). [H2 call]
- Promoter holding 71.4% (Sep-25) → 68.6% (Aug-26) from warrant conversion dilution; no pledge disclosed. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Jan-26 (ICS acquisition call) was about the US "Buy America, Build America" hedge; Apr-26 moved to the domestic order book, box-build mix and the ₹450–550 cr FY27 guide.
- Numbers improved: FY26 revenue ₹301 cr beat the ₹280 cr raised guide; order book ₹521 cr vs ₹189 cr a year earlier.
- New issues in April: negative ₹40 cr operating cash flow, inventory build, L&T's entry, lower H2 gross margin; margin guidance widened to "20–22% ±1–2%".
- Acquisitions: January said "not in a rush"; April said deals "on hold"; September's ₹205 cr raise names acquisitions first.

## My read
Aimtron is growing very fast (revenue +89% in FY26, order book ₹521 cr, AIC bookings tripling) and keeps beating its own revenue targets, so the growth story is real. But the quality of that growth is weaker than the margin suggests: operating cash flow has been negative for two years, working-capital days are 256, the auditor lists basic control gaps, and a meaningful slice of past sales went to promoter-owned US companies. The share price already discounts ₹450–550 cr in FY27 at 20%+ margins and continued 40–50% growth; it needs the H1 FY27 results (due Oct–Nov 2026) to show revenue of ₹200 cr+ with positive operating cash flow. A second year of cash burn, a margin slip toward the AIC level, or a large acquisition funded by the new ₹205 cr would change the view for the worse.
