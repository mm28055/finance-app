# Vinyas Innovative Technologies — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

NSE SME (Emerge) company; reports half-yearly. Latest results H2 FY26 (to 31 Mar 2026), call 3 Jun 2026. H1 FY27 results due after 1 Oct 2026 (trading window closed). Only one stored transcript; I also read the official H1 FY26 transcript (Nov 2025) for the guidance track.

## Business and segments
- Mysuru (Karnataka) contract manufacturer of high-reliability electronics: PCB assemblies, cable harnesses, electromechanical assemblies, sub-systems and system-level integration, mostly high-mix, low-volume. [IP H2FY26; CRISIL Aug-26]
- End markets: defence & aerospace ~70–75% of revenue (H1 FY26 call) and 85–90% of the order book; industrial ~15%; medical, transport (rail HVAC/power) the rest. Medical targeted at ~5% of FY27 revenue. [H1 call; H2 call]
- Programmes: naval multi-function and AESA radar, missile launcher control units, smart munition kits, EO/IR payloads, software-defined radios, UAV payloads, loitering munitions. [IP H2FY26 p.7]
- Exports ~50% of FY26 revenue (Europe a little larger than the US). 70–75% of raw materials imported (Israel, US, Taiwan, Singapore); Israel 10–15% of supplies. [H2 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Certifications: AS9100D, NADCAP for electronics/PCB assemblies (June 2025), ISO 13485, IATF 16949, Government of India defence industrial licence. 1.5 lakh sq ft production area; ~500 staff. [IP H2FY26 p.4]
- New structures: 26% associate **Nexus Advanced Technologies Pvt Ltd** (the joint venture with an unnamed Israeli defence prime, to make defence systems in India); 49% associate **United Vinyas Technologies Inc.** (US); wholly-owned Vinyas Innovative Technologies Inc. (US, incorporated 3 Feb 2026, not operating; ODI filing not completed at 31 Mar 2026). [AR FY26 AGM notice items 6–7; note 37]

## Key numbers
- Screener standalone full years (revenue / operating profit / OPM / PAT): FY23 ₹235 cr / ₹21 cr / 9% / ₹7 cr; FY24 ₹317 / ₹33 / 11% / ₹15; FY25 ₹397 / ₹41 / 10% / ₹19; FY26 ₹514 / ₹61 / 12% / ₹31. Three-year revenue CAGR 30%, profit CAGR 61%. [Screener]
- Half-years: H1 FY25 ₹150 cr / ₹17 cr / 11%; H2 FY25 ₹247 / ₹24 / 10%; H1 FY26 ₹212 / ₹21 / 10% / PAT ₹9 cr; H2 FY26 ₹302 / ₹40 / 13% / PAT ₹22 cr. [Screener]
- Company headline "revenue ₹518 cr" is total income including other income; revenue from operations was ₹514.3 cr. Company EBITDA ₹64.8 cr (12.5%) also includes other income; Screener operating profit ₹61 cr (12%); CRISIL puts FY26 operating margin at ~11%. [IP H2FY26; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Order book: ₹863 cr (Mar-25) + ₹960 cr inflow – ₹514 cr executed = ₹1,309 cr (Mar-26), 2.5x FY26 revenue; ~₹1,400 cr by July 2026 per CRISIL. Execution 18–24 months (CRISIL: 18–36 months). [IP H2FY26 p.6; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Interest is heavy: ₹16 cr in FY26 against ₹42 cr PBT. [Screener]
- Unit economics not disclosed; margin depends on programme mix.

## Guidance history
- H1 FY26 call (Nov 2025): 30–35% growth "every year"; EBITDA 9–11%, rising ~300–400 bp over four years via system integration; existing capacity supports ₹1,100–1,200 cr revenue and the capex doubles it; utilisation 50% by year-end FY26 and 70–75% by end FY27. [H1 call]
- Delivered FY26: revenue +30% (₹514 cr) — **met** the bottom of 30–35%; margin 12% (Screener) — **beat** the 9–11% guide on H2 mix.
- H2 FY26 call (Jun 2026): FY27 EBITDA 11–12% (margin guide **raised**); growth "25–30% year-on-year… about 30%" (**slightly lower** than the 30–35% of November, though the presentation still says "targeting 30–35%"); FY27 order inflow 25–30% above FY26's ₹960 cr, large orders in H2; exports ~50%. [H2 call; IP H2FY26 p.5]
- CRISIL's upward trigger is 20–30% growth at 11–12% margin — broadly the company's plan. [CRISIL Aug-26]

## Capex and capacity
- ₹30 cr capex (from the preferential raise) for SMT lines, test/inspection equipment and an extra 25,000 sq ft Class-3 system-integration area; due ~Aug–Sep 2026. [H2 call; H1 call]
- After expansion, management puts peak revenue capacity at ₹2,000–2,100 cr and utilisation at 35–40%; says each SMT line supports ₹500–600 cr (as stated; unusually high per line). New lines need 8+ months of customer qualification. [H2 call]
- Asset-light: fixed assets ₹65 cr against ₹514 cr revenue. [Screener]
- Shareholders approved raising the borrowing limit to ₹600 cr at the 25 Sep 2026 AGM. [AR FY26 AGM notice item 4]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Operating cash flow erratic: FY24 –₹29 cr, FY25 +₹9 cr, FY26 –₹32 cr; free cash flow FY26 –₹67 cr. Over FY20–FY26 cumulative CFO is roughly zero. [Screener]
- Borrowings ₹131 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹104 cr (Mar-25); net worth ₹236 cr; gearing 0.55x (CRISIL). Unencumbered cash over ₹18 cr; bank-limit use averaged 76% (12m to May-26). [Screener; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Debtor days 161 (FY25: 163), inventory days 114, payable days 43, cash-conversion cycle 232 days; CRISIL: gross current assets 282 days, receivables high because of Q4 billing. [Screener; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Preferential issue of convertible warrants at ₹1,100 (₹150 cr total); ₹61.25 cr received by Mar-26 (₹53.25 cr used); balance expected by March 2027. [AR FY26 MD&A & Board's report; CRISIL Aug-26]
- No dividend. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- NADCAP and AS9100D plus a defence licence are hard, slow approvals; new programmes take 8+ months before revenue. [H2 call; IP H2FY26]
- Order book 2.5x revenue with 85–90% defence and long programme lives; added four defence/aerospace, three industrial and one medical customer in FY26. [IP H2FY26 p.6]
- Pass-through: most contracts carry price-parity and forex clauses (CRISIL); defence pricing protected on inputs (management). [CRISIL Aug-26; H2 call]
- Weakness: it builds to others' designs; margins (~11–12%) are those of a contract manufacturer, not a product owner.

## Risks and red flags
- **Low promoter holding:** 29.4%, unchanged since listing; management has no plan to raise it. [Screener; H2 call]
- **Very large related-party approvals:** shareholders were asked to approve transactions of up to ₹400 cr with Nexus (26% associate) and ₹150 cr with United Vinyas Technologies Inc. (49% associate) until the next AGM — 78% and 29% of FY26 turnover. Pricing to be "arm's length/transfer-pricing" with no external valuation. Sales through part-owned associates are harder for minority shareholders to monitor. [AR FY26 AGM notice items 6–7]
- **Cash conversion:** negative operating cash flow in two of the last three years; growth funded by warrants and bank debt; borrowing limit raised to ₹600 cr. [Screener; AGM notice]
- **Rating appeal:** CRISIL reaffirmed BBB+ (outlook Positive) on 10 Aug 2026 while the company appealed; after "additional information on working capital and revenue visibility from strategic partnerships" it upgraded to A-/Stable on 25 Aug 2026 and raised rated limits to ₹275 cr. The fast reversal rests on management's forward assurances. [CRISIL 10-Aug-26 and 25-Aug-26]
- **Contingent liabilities (Mar-26):** GST demand ₹3.7 cr (new), labour charter-of-demands litigation ₹6.5 cr, income tax ₹0.2 cr; bank guarantees ₹35.7 cr (₹19.4 cr a year earlier). [AR FY26 note 36]
- **Compliance:** US subsidiary's FEMA ODI filing incomplete at year-end. Auditor's opinion on results unmodified. [AR FY26 note 37]
- **Geopolitics/imports:** 70–75% imported inputs; Israel dependency for critical parts. [CRISIL Aug-26; H2 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- November: focus on the ₹150 cr raise, 30–35% growth "every year", 9–11% margins, ₹1,062 cr order book.
- June: FY26 delivered (+30%), H2 margin 13%, order book ₹1,309 cr; margin guide lifted to 11–12%; growth language softened to 25–30%; new Israeli JV and US subsidiary; capex nearly done; supply-chain disruption acknowledged but easing.

## My read
Vinyas is a steady 30%-a-year grower with a defence-heavy order book of 2.5x revenue and margins that finally moved up in H2 FY26; CRISIL's upgrade to A- confirms the lenders' comfort. The weak spots are cash (negative operating cash flow in FY26, 230+ day cash cycle), a promoter owning only 29%, and newly approved related-party channels (₹550 cr of limits through two part-owned associates) that could blur where profit is booked. The price needs FY27 revenue around ₹650–670 cr at 11–12% margin and a clear H1 FY27 collection of the March receivables. A half with positive operating cash flow and transparent associate dealings would firm up the view; any sign that growth is booked through Nexus or UVT at thin margins would weaken it.
