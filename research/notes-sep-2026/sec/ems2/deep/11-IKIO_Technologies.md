# IKIO Technologies — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Noida-based design-and-manufacture (ODM) house, historically LED lighting: functional/decorative home lighting built for Signify (Philips), retail in-store and commercial-refrigeration lighting, RV lights for the US; now also hearables/wearables (contract and ODM), public-address/fire-alarm products for Honeywell, automotive LED lamps for the aftermarket (production from May–June 2026), solar inverters/batteries, and small EMS work. 3,000+ SKUs, ~2,500 staff (1,500–1,600 at the 2023 IPO). [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Reported split: "ODM home lighting" (the Signify-type business) vs "Other business". Q1 FY27: other ₹124 cr (+53% YoY, 73%), ODM home lighting ₹45 cr (+16% YoY, 27%). FY26: other ₹426 cr (71%), ODM home lighting ~₹170 cr. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Wearables ~15–16% of revenue (guided 16–18% for FY27, ~₹100 cr); non-lighting overall ~25% of FY26 revenue, guided 30–32% in FY27. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Geography: outside India ₹116 cr in FY26 (19.5% of ₹595 cr; company says ₹110 cr/18%, +53%); Middle East (UAE, via Royalux FZCO) the main export market, US slowed by tariffs. [AR FY26 note 42; Q4 call]
- Capacity: five plants, ~5 lakh sq ft; new IPO-funded campus: Block I (~2 lakh sq ft, May 2024), Block II (~2 lakh sq ft, partly commercialised Q2 FY27, two floors for wearables), Block III (~1 lakh sq ft, under construction). Mature units ~70%+ utilised, new ones lower. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- Screener consolidated quarters (revenue / operating profit / OPM / PAT): Q1 FY26 ₹120 cr / ₹11 cr / 9% / ₹2 cr; Q2 ₹164 / ₹18 / 11% / ₹11; Q3 ₹146 / ₹22 / 15% / ₹11; Q4 ₹165 / ₹26 / 16% / ₹18; Q1 FY27 ₹169 / ₹22 / 13% / ₹11. [Screener]
- Full years: FY23 ₹359 cr / 22% OPM / PAT ₹50 cr; FY24 ₹438 cr / 21% / ₹61 cr; FY25 ₹486 cr / 12% / ₹32 cr; FY26 ₹595 cr / 13% / ₹42 cr (+23% revenue). TTM ₹644 cr revenue, ₹50 cr PAT. [Screener]
- Gross margin: Q1 FY27 41.2% vs 44.6% in Q4 FY26 and 36.6% in Q1 FY26; FY26 40.2% (FY25 42.1%). Pre-IPO gross margins were ~63–64%. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Segment margins (management, Q1 FY27): ODM home lighting EBITDA ~8.4%; other businesses ~15%. [Q1 call]
- Depreciation rose from ₹6 cr (FY23) to ₹31 cr (FY26) with the new campus, and will rise again as Block II floors start. [Screener; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (4 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth 20–22%; EBITDA "in line with what we achieved" (FY26 ~13%) — though the CEO also answered "yes… 15% to 16%" to an analyst's leading question; long-term target 18–20% (later 17–18%); full-utilisation revenue ~₹1,500 cr on ~₹300 cr investment (4.5–5x asset turn); Block II by end-Q1 FY27; US solar expansion "in the next two or three quarters". [Q4 call, official transcript]
- Q1 FY27 delivery (11 Aug 2026): revenue +41% YoY (ahead), EBITDA 13.0% (in line with the "in line with FY26" version, below the 15–16% version). Management restated guidance as **18–20%** revenue growth (a slight **cut** from 20–22%) and **13–14%** EBITDA (the CEO said this is what he had said in Q4). Long-term margin target lowered to **17–18%** at 50–60% utilisation of new plants, within 3–3.5 years. Block II **late** (Q2 not Q1). US solar opportunity **delayed** by the war; strategy "shifting". [Q1 call, official transcript]

## Capex and capacity
- IPO (June 2023) raised ~₹350 cr; ₹212 cr allocated to IKIO Solutions' new Noida facility, ₹50 cr debt repayment, rest general purposes. Up to Mar-26 ₹287 cr used; ₹39 cr unused and the deadline extended to FY27 (board, 2 May 2026). [AR FY26 Board's report / monitoring agency]
- Remaining capex ₹20–25 cr (Block III), from IPO money; capital commitments ₹21.9 cr at Mar-26. [Q1 call; AR FY26 note 39]
- Asset base ~₹300 cr; management targets a return to 4.5–5x asset turns (₹1,300–1,500 cr revenue) — current turn is ~2x (₹595 cr on ₹300 cr). [Q4 call; Screener]
- Acquired 88% of Gravus/"Gravis" Tech (Dec 2025) for high-end architectural lighting distribution; Royalux General Trading LLC (UAE) added Feb 2026. [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 45]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹70 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹53 cr (Mar-25); equity ₹602 cr; CRISIL (Sep 2025) expected TOL/TNW ~0.1x and interest cover 9–10x; cash ₹113 cr at Mar-25 (mostly earmarked for capex). [Screener; CRISIL Sep-25]
- Operating cash flow weak: FY24 –₹5 cr, FY25 +₹35 cr, FY26 +₹16 cr (37% of operating profit); free cash flow negative three years (–₹86 cr, –₹59 cr, –₹65 cr). [Screener]
- Inventory days 218 (FY26) vs 184 (FY25); debtors 82 days; cash-conversion cycle 253 days. Management: carrying extra inventory and spot-buying because component lead times have jumped (some semiconductors >1 year, prices doubled). [Screener; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- No dividend since FY24 (13% payout then). [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Deep in-house integration (tool room, injection moulding, sheet metal, powder coating, SMT); can redesign boards when a chip is unavailable — the same approach that won share during COVID. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Frost & Sullivan (IPO time): ~23–24% share of India's functional-decorative home lighting (downlights/spotlights) in 2023; largest supplier to Signify in that category; ~200 customers in commercial/refrigeration lighting. [Q4 call]
- Pricing is "BOM-plus" with a 2–3 month lag for raw-material and forex pass-through; no annual price-down contracts. [Q4 call]
- Evidence against a strong moat: EBITDA margin halved from 22% (FY23) to 12–13%, and gross margin fell from ~63% to ~41%, as mix shifted to lower-margin categories. [Screener; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration:** in the parent, one customer (Signify) contributed ₹146 cr in FY26 (₹191 cr in FY25) — about a quarter of group revenue; Signify's lighting JV with Dixon is a live risk. IKIO Solutions (the wearables subsidiary) gets over 93% of its ₹93.5 cr revenue from its major customers; Royalux Lighting >38% from two customers. [AR FY26 note 42(C); Q4 call]
- **Margin reset:** OPM fell from 21–23% (FY21–FY24) to 12–13%; guidance for FY27 now 13–14%; the long-term target has drifted down from 18–20% to 17–18%. [Screener; Q1 call]
- **Capital efficiency:** ROCE 9% vs 31–41% pre-IPO; IPO money partly unspent three years on (deadline extended). [Screener; AR FY26]
- **Related parties:** several promoter-linked firms (Raina Metal Tech, Life Tek Solutions, Singh Engineering, Singh Enterprises, Safe Solutions, IKIO Led Lighting LLC and Bright Future USA in the US) trade with the group — purchases, rent and sales (e.g., sales to Krishna Computech ~₹1.2 cr, Bright Future USA ~₹1.0 cr in FY26); promoters also lent money to the group during FY26 (the table's column alignment in the PDF is unclear, so amounts not quoted). [AR FY26 consolidated note 45]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion, no key audit matters; modified remark on audit-trail (edit-log) compliance under Rule 11(g). Contingent liabilities small: customs/indirect-tax demand ₹1.3 cr, income tax ₹1.4 cr, corporate guarantee ₹9.0 cr. [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 39]
- Tax rate volatile (35% in Q1 FY27; 181% in Q4 FY25). Promoter holding 72.6%, no pledge; DIIs have exited (7.5% → 0.9%). [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from expansion (US solar, new verticals, 20–22% growth) to resilience (war-driven raw-material and lead-time shock, spot buying, minimum-wage revision raising staff cost).
- Numbers: revenue growth strong (+41%) but margin slipped from 15.7% (Q4) to 13.0%; gross margin down 3.4 points QoQ.
- Guidance trimmed: revenue 20–22% → 18–20%; margin "15–16%" (one Q4 answer) → 13–14%; long-term 18–20% → 17–18%. Block II late; US solar parked.
- New: automotive aftermarket production with five Tier-1 brands; Honeywell SKUs to rise 3–4x by year-end; ODM home lighting now has 3–4 customers (was >95% one customer).

## Stored-data errors
- DB quarterly figures (Q2 FY26–Q1 FY27 revenue, EBITDA, margin, PAT) and TTM ₹644 cr match Screener. DB P/E 34.2 ≈ Screener 34.1.
- Stored analysis overstates the margin "walk-back": the official Q4 transcript twice says FY27 margins would be "in line with what we have achieved" (~13%); the 15–16% came as a "yes" to an analyst's leading question. The revenue guide was 20–22% in Q4, not 18–20% as the Q1 CEO recalled.
- Stored profile: "Signify-dependent label shed; non-legacy 73% of revenue" is right for the reporting split, but "other business" still includes lighting — non-lighting is only ~25% of revenue (management, Q4 call). "PLI adds 400–450 bp on eligible revenue (₹5–6 cr)" — not found in either official transcript.

## My read
IKIO is growing again (Q1 FY27 +41%) and has diversified away from one Signify product line, but it is now a lower-margin, more capital-heavy business than the one that listed in 2023: operating margin has settled at 12–16% against 21–23% pre-IPO, ROCE is 9%, free cash flow has been negative for three years and inventory days are 218. Guidance was trimmed in Q1 on raw materials and lead times. The price needs 18–20% growth with margins holding at 13–14% and the new blocks filling toward the promised 4.5x asset turn; evidence of that would be rising asset turns and positive free cash flow in H2 FY27. A further margin slip, a Signify volume loss via the Dixon JV, or another delay in using the IPO money would argue for caution.
