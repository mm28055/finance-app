# Cyient DLM (NSE: CYIENTDLM / BSE: 543933) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹931 (52-week ₹265–1,010 — the stock more than tripled from its low), market cap ₹7,394 cr, P/E 90.0, book value ₹128, ROCE 9.9%, ROE 7.5%, no dividend [Screener consolidated, 27-Sep-26]. Parent **Cyient Ltd holds 52.11%**; FIIs 0.24%, DIIs 29.2% [Screener shareholding]. CRISIL reaffirmed its bank-loan ratings (A+ category) and then **withdrew them at the company's request** in Jul-26 (loans repaid) [CRISIL Jul-26]. Auditor S.R. Batliboi & Associates (EY) — unmodified [AR FY26].

## Business and segments
- Hyderabad/Mysuru/Bengaluru EMS maker for **high-mix, low-volume, high-reliability** work: aerospace, defence, medtech, industrial; plus a US unit (Altek Electronics, bought Oct-24) and a growing "build-to-spec" (B2S, design-owned) line [Official Q1 transcript; AR FY26].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹373.8 cr (+34%): **Aerospace 42% (+40%)**, Industrial 32% (+90%), Medtech 16% (flat), Defence 9% (+35%), Auto & others 1%. Product mix: PCBA 48% (+21%), box build 21% (+85%), mechanical & others 10% [Official Q1 transcript; IP Q1FY27].
- **Exports 94%** of revenue (North America, Europe, Israel, Japan); India 6% [Official Q1 transcript].
- Order book **₹2,599 cr** at Jun-26 (₹2,417 cr Mar-26; ₹1,906 cr Mar-25); Q1 order intake ₹551.9 cr, book-to-bill 1.5x; ~70% of intake from existing customers, 30% from customers added in the last 4–6 quarters [Official Q1 transcript; CRISIL Jul-26].
- Named anchor customers: Honeywell Aerospace and Thales (both expected to ramp "extraordinarily" over 18 months); a Japanese eVTOL customer on B2S (production in 12–18 months) [Official Q1 transcript].
- **Customer concentration:** customers each above 10% of revenue together = ₹702.8 cr, **55.7% of FY26 revenue** (FY25: ₹1,051.7 cr, 69.2%) [AR FY26 note 34].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 278 → Sep-25 311 → Dec-25 303 → Mar-26 369 → **Jun-26 374 (+34%)**. Operating profit 25 → 31 → 28 → 43 → **39 (+56%)**; OPM 9% → 10% → 9% → 12% → **10%**. Net profit 7 → 32 → 11 → 22 → **16 (+118%)** [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 revenue ₹1,192 cr, OPM 9%, PAT ₹61 cr; FY25 ₹1,520 cr, 9%, ₹68 cr; **FY26 ₹1,261 cr (−17%), 10%, ₹73 cr** [Screener].
- **FY26 PAT was flattered by a ₹19.6 cr non-taxable reversal of the Altek earn-out** (the seller missed targets); normalised PAT ₹56.3 cr (−24%); normalised EBITDA ₹130.2 cr (10.3%) [Official Q4 transcript; AR FY26 MD&A].
- FY26 revenue fall came from the end of one large defence order in FY25 (defence revenue −68%), plus West Asia shipping delays and Israeli approval delays [Official Q4 transcript; CRISIL Jul-26].
- Q1 FY27 material cost rose to 62% of revenue (60% a year earlier) on mix and B2S buying; other income turned slightly negative on forex [IP Q1FY27; Screener].

## Guidance history
- **Q3 FY26 call:** chairman indicated ~25% growth for FY27 [Official Q4 transcript, analyst recap].
- **Q4 call (Apr-26):** no revenue number, but "year-over-year growth in all four quarters" and "within the ballpark range" for FY27; EBITDA margin 10–11% sustainable; NWC target 100–120 days "in a couple of years" [Official Q4 transcript].
- **Q1 delivery (23-Jul-26):** revenue +34%, EBITDA +56%, margin 10.5% (fourth straight double-digit quarter), record order book — **ahead of the promise**; but **NWC rose to 161 days** (145 in Q4), inventory 162 days, operating free cash flow −₹17.1 cr — **away from** the 100–120-day goal [Official Q1 transcript; IP Q1FY27].
- **New medium-term frame ("Strengthen–Expand–Transform")**: core 9–11% EBITDA; FY27–FY29 "Expand" into robotics and AI/data-centre hardware at **11–13%**; FY30+ "Transform" with B2S products at **13–18%**; B2S could add 250–300 bps to group margin in 12–18 months; book-to-bill ~1.5x for FY27 [Official Q1 transcript].

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: CRISIL expects ~₹40 cr capex a year; management says current capacity supports ~1.75–2x today's revenue and no special capex is needed for AI/robotics [CRISIL Jul-26; Official Q1 transcript].
- B2S lab expanded from 6,000 to 15,000 sq ft; Nadcap audit for cable harness (Mysuru) completed; ITAR-certified US facility (Altek) for US defence work [Official Q1 transcript].
- IPO proceeds (FY24) now fully used; capital commitments ₹7.8 cr [Official Q4 transcript; AR FY26 note 28].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- CFO ₹54 cr (FY23) → −₹71 cr (FY24) → −₹62 cr (FY25) → **+₹54 cr (FY26)**; FCF +₹9 cr in FY26 [Screener].
- Total debt (incl. ₹66 cr leases) ₹301 cr → **₹206 cr** (Mar-26) after repaying loans from Cyient; free cash ~₹136 cr [CRISIL Jul-26]. Screener borrowings ₹301 cr → ₹172 cr [Screener].
- Working capital: Screener FY26 inventory **314 days**, debtors 89, payables 133, cash conversion **270 days**, working-capital days 169. Company Q1 FY27: DIO 162, DSO 77, DPO 63, customer advances 15 days, NWC **161 days** [Screener; Official Q1 transcript].
- No dividend [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Aerospace qualification is slow and sticky: "very, very difficult to get into. But once you are in you're in" — present with most major aerospace OEMs/Tier-1s [Official Q1 transcript].
- Order book up 36% in a year (₹1,906 cr → ₹2,599 cr in 15 months) [CRISIL Jul-26; Official Q1 transcript].
- Parent support: Cyient provides guarantees, inter-company loans and customer access (Cyient DLM is ~17% of Cyient's revenue) [CRISIL Jul-26].
- Weak points: 94% export-dependent, high customer concentration, and a history of lumpy defence orders (FY25 spike, FY26 collapse) [Official Q1/Q4 transcripts; AR FY26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Working capital is the chief risk**: 270-day cash cycle (Screener), inventory building again in Q1; operating cash flow negative in FY24, FY25 and Q1 FY27 [Screener; IP Q1FY27].
- **Earnings quality**: FY26 reported PAT (₹73 cr) includes ₹19.6 cr earn-out reversal — Altek missed its targets; goodwill of ₹71.8 cr on Altek tested as a key audit matter [AR FY26 MD&A, note 4, auditor's report].
- **Management churn**: CEO Anthony Montalbano resigned May-25; new CEO Rajendra Velagapudi (from parent Cyient) from Jul-25; CFO Shrinivas Kulkarni resigned Oct-25, replaced by R.M. Subramanian (Avalon's CFO until Nov-24); two independent directors resigned in 2025 [AR FY26 note 30].
- Customer concentration (>55% from a handful of customers) and Israel/Middle-East exposure (Cyient Israel advance ₹44 cr on the balance sheet) [AR FY26 notes 30, 34].
- US tariff uncertainty weighs on Altek cross-selling [Official Q4 transcript].
- Governance otherwise clean: unmodified audit, audit trail operated all year, **no contingent liabilities** disclosed at Mar-26 [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 28].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was defensive (17% revenue fall, earn-out reversal, analyst asked if there was a "structural issue"); Q1 was confident — growth back, record orders, new CEO's three-phase plan with margin targets.
- New: AI/data-centre and robotics as target sectors, Chief Strategy & Growth Officer hired, margin ladder 9–11% → 11–13% → 13–18%.
- Working capital went backwards (NWC 145 → 161 days) as inventory was built against Middle East supply risk.

## My read
Cyient DLM has turned the corner on growth (+34% in Q1) and orders (record ₹2,599 cr, 1.5x book-to-bill), with steady ~10% EBITDA margins and a debt-light balance sheet backed by Cyient. But it earns thin profit (PAT margin 4.4%), ties up huge working capital (270-day cash cycle), and FY26 profit leaned on an earn-out reversal. At ~90x trailing earnings the price assumes both 25–35% growth and the promised step-up to 11–13% margins; watch NWC days (need a fall toward 120), the Honeywell/Thales ramp, and first AI/robotics wins in the next 2–3 quarters.
