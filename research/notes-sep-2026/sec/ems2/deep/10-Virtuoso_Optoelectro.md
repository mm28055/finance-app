# Virtuoso Optoelectronics — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

Migrated from BSE SME to the main boards (BSE and NSE) in 2026; now reports quarterly. FY26 is its first Ind AS year (FY25 restated).

## Business and segments
- Nashik-based contract manufacturer (OEM and, since FY26, ODM) of room-AC indoor and outdoor units, plus deep freezers/commercial refrigeration, water dispensers, semi-automatic washing machines (from FY27), LED lighting/EMS boards, AC components (heat exchangers, cross-flow fans, EPS/plastics) and, since January 2026, reciprocating (refrigerator) compressors. [ICRA Sep-26; Q4 call]
- Subsidiaries: Virtuoso Compressors Pvt Ltd (VCPL) and Virtuoso Polymers Pvt Ltd (VPPL; Sanand and Chennai plants, ~85% of sales to outside customers). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Revenue mix: management says AC ~60% of FY26 revenue (from ~75% in FY25), EMS ~15%, commercial refrigeration ~10%, components ~7%, compressors ~8%; FY27 plan AC 55–60%, compressors ~15%, EMS ~10%, refrigeration ~10%, other ~5%. Q1 FY27 actual: AC ~70%, compressors 7–8%, EMS 7–8%, deep freezers similar. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- ICRA's figures differ: RAC ~72% of FY26 revenue and non-AC only ~28% (23% in FY25). [ICRA Sep-26]
- Capacities (current → planned): EMS 4 lakh → 8 lakh CPH (Sep 2026) → 12 lakh; AC 1.0m → 1.3m → 1.8m sets within 12–15 months; deep freezers 1.5 lakh → 2.5 lakh (Q3 FY27) → 4 lakh (FY28); compressors 2.8m → 6m (Dec 2026–Mar 2027), long-run 8–8.5m. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Customers: 4 AC customers; 8–9 compressor customers; 3 main deep-freezer customers; Voltas is the anchor. Average AC realisation ~₹20,000 per unit. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- Screener consolidated quarters (revenue / operating profit / OPM / PAT): Q1 FY26 ₹203 cr / ₹20 cr / 9.9% / ₹6.4 cr; Q2 FY26 ₹98 cr / ₹10 cr / 10.3% / –₹3.9 cr; Q3 FY26 ₹206 cr / ₹24 cr / 11.8% / ₹8.0 cr; Q4 FY26 ₹317 cr / ₹29 cr / 9.2% / ₹4.5 cr; Q1 FY27 ₹376 cr / ₹34 cr / 9.1% / ₹9.1 cr. [Screener]
- Full years: FY24 ₹531 cr / ₹52 cr (10%) / PAT ₹10 cr; FY25 ₹698 cr / ₹55 cr (8%) / ₹12 cr; FY26 ₹824 cr / ₹84 cr (10%) / ₹15 cr (+18% revenue). TTM ₹996 cr revenue, ₹18 cr PAT. [Screener]
- Company Q1 FY27: net sales ₹375.9 cr (+85% YoY, +18.6% QoQ), EBITDA ₹35.1 cr (9.3%, includes other income), PAT ₹9.05 cr (2.4%). [IP Q1FY27]
- The thin bottom line: interest ₹33 cr and depreciation ₹28 cr in FY26 (Ind AS leases added right-of-use depreciation) against operating profit of ₹84 cr; PAT margin 1.8%. [Screener; ICRA Sep-26]
- Unit economics: AC ~₹20,000 realisation; EBITDA by line (company): AC 6–8%, components 10–12%, other non-AC 10–15%; compressors 6–7% now (value addition only 5–10%). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (1 Jun 2026): 35–40% revenue CAGR for 3–5 years (one answer said "30–40%" for RAC); EBITDA 9–10% for FY27; PAT margin +50–100 bp over two years; compressor utilisation ~60%, expected ~80% within 2–3 months; peak revenue from current expansion "beyond ₹2,500 cr"; main-board migration by August. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery (Aug 2026): revenue +85% — well ahead; EBITDA 9.3% (company) — within 9–10%; PAT margin 2.4%. Guidance **kept** (35–40% growth, 9–10% margin, PAT 2.5–3%), with a possible upgrade after Q3. Compressor utilisation now ">50%" — **below** the ~60% stated in June and the ~80% expected. Peak-revenue talk **raised** to ₹3,500–4,000 cr after the expansion. Main-board migration **done**. [Q1 call]
- ICRA expects FY27 revenue growth above 50%. [ICRA Sep-26]

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex ₹80–100 cr in the listed company (Q1 call); ICRA puts group capex at ₹200–220 cr in FY27 and ₹80–100 cr in FY28. [Q1 call; ICRA Sep-26]
- Pieces stated on the Q4 call: compressors ₹150 cr (phase 1, 2.8m → 6m), EMS ~₹25 cr, AC ₹40–50 cr, commercial refrigeration ₹20–25 cr in each of two phases. Full compressor plan (capacity plus shell and motor backward integration) earlier put at ~₹500 cr. A 22-acre site was bought to consolidate compressors over 1–3 years. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Asset turns: EMS 3–5x, AC 4–5x, refrigeration 4–5x, compressors 3.5–4x. [Q4 call]
- Funding: ₹150 cr optionally convertible debentures in VCPL (Q1 FY27, cost "mid-teens"); ₹106 cr equity infused in FY26; blanket approval for ₹250 cr equity; preferential issue to up to five QIBs approved at the 24 Sep 2026 EGM (~₹85 cr: ₹46 cr working capital, ₹18 cr Chinese machinery for deep-freezer expansion, ₹21 cr general purposes). ICRA expects ₹170–180 cr equity in FY27. [Q1 call; ICRA Sep-26; EGM notice update 21-Sep-26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹345 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹181 cr (Mar-25) and ₹135 cr (Mar-24); plus ₹68 cr lease liabilities; the ₹150 cr OCD came after March. Total debt/OPBDITA 5.5x; interest cover 2.5x; TOL/TNW 1.6x. [Screener; AR FY26 note 40; ICRA Sep-26]
- Free cash flow negative every year: FY24 –₹47 cr, FY25 –₹71 cr, FY26 –₹167 cr. CFO ₹16 cr in FY26 (24% of operating profit). [Screener]
- Working capital: net working capital 40.7% of revenue (ICRA); management quotes ~85 days at Q1. Screener: inventory 120 days, debtors 33 days (from 16), payables 81 days. Fund-based limit use averaged 70% (12m to Jul-26), buffer ₹14 cr. [ICRA Sep-26; Q1 call; Screener]
- Repayments ₹33–35 cr a year to FY28. No dividend. [ICRA Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- Only independent (non-captive) refrigerator-compressor maker in India today; the government's import cap (40% of FY25 import volume, extended to March 2027) forces brands to buy ~60% locally. Two competitors expected to start between late FY27 and mid-FY28; industry gap ~14–15m units vs 15–18m units of planned capacity. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- ODM in ACs (40–50% of AC revenue in FY26) won a new marquee customer; ODM earns only ~1–1.5% more margin than OEM. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Backward integration (EPS, plastics, CFF, heat exchangers, tool room) helped hold margins in FY26. [Q4 call]
- Pricing power is limited: realisations flat, cost pass-through with a quarter's lag, PLI benefit (~1% of AC EBITDA) ends after FY27 and will be negotiated with customers. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration:** one customer ~50% of FY26 revenue (above 75% in FY25) and one product (RAC) ~72% (ICRA). Management's numbers on the Q1 call were inconsistent (largest customer "40–45% of AC", "last year in excess of 60%", "25–35% of total this year"). [ICRA Sep-26; Q1 call]
- **Leverage and cash burn:** debt nearly doubled in FY26 to ₹345 cr before a ₹150 cr OCD; debt/OPBDITA 5.5x; ICRA rating only BBB (Stable); negative free cash flow three years running; continuous equity issuance (promoter holding 73.5% at listing → 46.2% by Aug-26). [Screener; ICRA Sep-26]
- **Related-party items:** an ₹8.61 cr advance "for purchase of capital goods" paid in FY25 to Mrs. Nikitha Shravan Poddatur (close member of KMP) is still outstanding at 31 Mar 2026; she also receives rent (₹36 lakh in FY26) and remuneration; advances for raw material to Techmark International FZE (UAE, promoter-influenced) of ₹0.35 cr (FY25) and ₹0.07 cr (FY26); rent to Sukrit Bharati HUF. [AR FY26 consolidated note 39]
- **Policy dependence:** compressor economics hinge on the import cap and its March 2027 review; PLI for ACs ends. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Execution:** simultaneous expansions across EMS, AC, freezers, compressors and a new site; Q4 call admitted earlier freezer expansion (1.5 → 4 lakh) did not happen as planned last year. Compressor utilisation slipped from ~60% to ">50%". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Auditor (SKVM & Co.): unmodified; key audit matters revenue recognition and inventory valuation; comparatives restated for first-time Ind AS. Contingent liabilities small: EPCG customs ₹6.2 cr, GST demand ₹4.8 cr under appeal. [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 45]
- Segment disclosure: reports a single segment, so the segment margins quoted on calls cannot be checked. [AR FY26 note 39 (standalone)]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: story of recovery after a weak H1 FY26 (Q2 revenue only ₹98 cr), 35–40% growth, 9–10% margin, capex list, main-board move.
- Q1: revenue jumped 85%; guidance kept; peak-revenue ambition raised to ₹3,500–4,000 cr; raw-material (copper, aluminium, compressor) and logistics pressure acknowledged, margin improvement pushed to FY28; commercial refrigeration demand soft in Q1; funding of the remaining compressor plan (debt or equity) undecided; another preferential issue launched.

## My read
Virtuoso is growing fast (Q1 FY27 revenue +85%) and has a genuine early lead in refrigerator compressors protected by an import cap, but it earns very little: 9–10% operating margin becomes a ~2% net margin after interest and depreciation, free cash flow has been negative for three years, and debt is 5.5x operating profit before the new ₹150 cr OCD. The equity story needs the 35–40% growth to arrive with a clear step-up in net margin toward 3% and without endless dilution. Watch compressor utilisation (it slipped in Q1), the March 2027 import-cap review, and whether the ₹8.6 cr advance to a promoter relative is recovered.
