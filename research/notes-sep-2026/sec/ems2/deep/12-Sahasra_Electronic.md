# Sahasra Electronic Solutions — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

NSE SME (Emerge) company, listed Oct 2024; reports half-yearly. Latest results H2 FY26 (to 31 Mar 2026), call late May 2026. Only one stored transcript; I also read the official H1 FY26 transcript (Nov 2025) for the guidance track.

## Business and segments
- Noida/Bhiwadi group making PCB assemblies and box builds (EMS), memory products (microSD, USB drives, SSDs, DDR modules), server/desktop motherboards and tablets (IT hardware, from a 2016 JV with Taiwan's MiTAC), and, via 100% subsidiary Sahasra Semiconductors (Bhiwadi), memory-chip assembly, test, marking and packaging (ATMP) — billed as "India's first operational OSAT". [IP Sep-26; H2 call]
- FY26 consolidated revenue ₹138.8 cr: EMS 66.0%, memory solutions 18.9%, semiconductor packaging 11.3% (₹15.7 cr, +71%), IT hardware 3.8% (₹5.2 cr; a European partner collapsed). [IP May-26; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Exports 56% of FY26 revenue (standalone: overseas ₹68.8 cr, domestic ₹54.8 cr); was 85–90% exports two years ago. Noida (4 SMT lines, SEZ) serves exports; Bhiwadi (4 lines, twice Noida's capacity) serves India. 113–140 customers; repeat business 84–91%. [AR FY26 Board's report; H2 call; IP May-26]
- Capacity and use (company, FY26): Noida ₹78.7 cr revenue vs ₹100 cr peak (~79–80%); Bhiwadi EMS ₹44.9 cr vs ₹200 cr (~22%); semiconductor unit ₹15.3 cr vs ₹250 cr peak (~6%). Two SMT lines added April 2026 (8 total; target 10). [IP May-26; H2 call]
- Pending merger (filed with NSE 24 Mar 2026, effective date 1 Apr 2026): promoter-owned Sahasra Electronics Pvt Ltd (EMS, ~₹97 cr FY26 revenue), Infopower Technologies Pvt Ltd (PCB fabrication, ~₹16 cr) and Sahasra Sambhav/Sanskar Sambodh Skill Development into the listed company; swap 226 shares per share of Sahasra Electronics Pvt, 55 per share of the skill company, 5 per share of Infopower. Needs NSE, SEBI and NCLT approval. [AR FY26 Board's report; H2 call]

## Key numbers
- Screener consolidated halves (revenue / operating profit / OPM / PAT): H1 FY25 ₹46 cr / ₹8 cr / 17% / ₹2 cr; H2 FY25 ₹50 / –₹1 / –1% / –₹4; H1 FY26 ₹61 / ₹11 / 17% / ₹6; H2 FY26 ₹78 / ₹8 / 10% / ₹6. [Screener]
- Full years: FY25 ₹96 cr / ₹8 cr (8%) / PAT –₹2 cr; FY26 ₹139 cr / ₹18 cr (13%) / ₹12 cr. Other income ₹10 cr in FY26 (FD interest on IPO money plus ~₹2 cr of the capital subsidy) was a large part of PBT of ₹13 cr. [Screener; H2 call]
- Standalone FY26 (company): revenue ₹123.6 cr (+42%), EBITDA ₹18.8 cr (15.2%), PAT ₹19.7 cr (includes other income). [IP May-26]
- Executable order book ₹68.5 cr at Mar-26 (₹42.2 cr a year earlier); top-5 clients 55.6% of consolidated revenue (37.9% in FY25). [IP May-26]
- Semiconductor unit still loss-making at EBITDA level in FY26 (losses "reduced significantly"); received a ₹21.8 cr capital subsidy in Q4 FY26, booked as deferred income over 15 years (~₹1.6 cr a year). [H2 call]

## Guidance history
- H1 FY26 call (Nov 2025): FY26 revenue guidance ₹130 cr; semiconductor FY26 had been projected at ~₹50 cr but management conceded ~₹10 cr; FY27 semiconductor again ₹50 cr; semiconductor EBITDA margin 16–20% at scale; ₹200 cr semiconductor expansion under ISM 2.0 (50% grant, ₹50 cr promoter/internal, ₹50 cr debt). [H1 call]
- Delivered FY26: consolidated revenue ₹138.8 cr — **beat** the ₹130 cr guide; semiconductor ₹15.7 cr — **missed** the original ₹50 cr badly. [IP May-26]
- H2 FY26 call (May 2026): FY27 target ₹275–300 cr for the merged entity (listed ~₹160 cr + semiconductor ~₹50 cr + unlisted ₹85–90 cr) with ~15% PBT margin; semiconductor EBITDA break-even in FY27 and ₹150 cr in FY28 at 10% PAT; group PAT 16–17% later; listed entity alone ~₹300 cr by FY28; ₹600–650 cr consolidated in 3–5 years; operating cash flow positive only "by the end of FY28". [H2 call, official transcript]
- No H1 FY27 numbers yet (trading window closed from 1 Oct 2026).

## Capex and capacity
- ~₹40 cr of IPO money still in fixed deposits for machinery. [H2 call]
- Semiconductor: ₹200 cr expansion and ₹50 cr R&D planned, contingent on ISM 2.0 and the Rajasthan Semiconductor Policy 2026 (applications being made). [IP May-26; H1 call]
- EMS: 8 SMT lines sufficient for guidance; running 1.5 shifts; Bhiwadi can triple output by adding shifts and lines. [H2 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Operating cash flow negative both reported years: FY25 –₹6 cr, FY26 –₹10 cr; free cash flow –₹70 cr and –₹19 cr. Management blames inventory build-up (2–3 months), slow payers and stocking memory for price rises. [Screener; H2 call]
- Borrowings ₹67 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹52 cr; long-term debt only in the semiconductor subsidiary; net debt/equity ~0.2x (company). [Screener; IP May-26]
- Inventory days 196, debtor days 115 (Screener; company says 93), payable days 108; cash-conversion cycle 203 days (Screener) vs 140 days (company). [Screener; IP May-26]
- Standalone lent ₹19.75 cr to the semiconductor subsidiary (H1 FY26) and guarantees ₹27.5 cr of its bank borrowings. [H1 call; AR FY26 notes 40, 49]
- Maiden dividend yield 0.23%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Among very few Indian memory ATMP units; 24–25 active semiconductor customers; consumer memory sold via distributors in Nepal, Germany and soon the US; memory shortage from AI demand helped in FY26. [H2 call]
- 25-year EMS history; memory customers/retail partners listed include Sony, HP, Silicon Power, Centon, Delkin and Amazon; certifications IATF 16949, AS9100D, ISO 13485, IRIS rail. [IP Sep-26]
- Weak points: EMS is high-mix, medium-volume contract work with modest margins; memory prices are volatile ("prices in the consumer segment have recently gone down"). [H2 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Record-keeping:** the FY26 annual report says a quantitative reconciliation of raw materials and finished goods was **not prepared** (note 54), and that after an SAP disruption the physical raw-material quantity **did not tally** with regenerated stock records at 31 Mar 2026, "subsequently rectified" by 8 May 2026 (note 56). Inventory valuation was the auditor's key audit matter. In the H1 FY26 call, an investor spotted a ₹4.27 cr "fixed assets written off" line in the cash flow that management said was an entry error. [AR FY26 notes 54, 56, auditor's report; H1 call]
- **Promoter-group merger:** folding three promoter-owned private companies into the listed one at a share-swap ratio set by the promoters' valuer; revenue between the entities (e.g., the semiconductor unit sold to the unlisted company that is an Amazon vendor) will be eliminated. [H2 call; AR FY26]
- **Related-party flows:** loan of ₹6.6 cr to Infopower Technologies (a merging promoter company) with interest; sales/purchases with Sahasra Electronics Pvt Ltd and "Sahasra Electronics" (a sole proprietorship of CMD Amrit Lal Manwani) — sales, purchases, rent and multi-crore expense reimbursements (exact split unclear because the PDF table columns are misaligned); group properties and promoter guarantees secure the bank loans. [AR FY26 note 48]
- **Guidance credibility:** semiconductor revenue was guided at ~₹50 cr for FY26 and came in at ₹15.7 cr; the same ₹50 cr is now the FY27 target. [H1 call; IP May-26]
- **Earnings quality:** FY26 PBT of ₹13 cr leaned on ₹10 cr of other income; ROCE 6%. Contingent liabilities small (income-tax ₹0.26 cr) apart from the ₹27.5 cr subsidiary guarantee. [Screener; AR FY26 note 40]
- **Concentration:** top five customers 55.6% of revenue, up from 37.9%. [IP May-26]
- Auditor's opinion unmodified; CARO reports no material discrepancies. Promoter holding 69.9%, no pledge disclosed; institutions have exited (DIIs 8.4% → 0.9%). [AR FY26; Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Nov 2025: FY26 ₹130 cr guide; semiconductor ~₹10 cr vs ₹50 cr hoped; merger scheme being drafted; questions on a ₹4.27 cr write-off and ₹19.75 cr loan to the subsidiary.
- May 2026: FY26 beat (₹138.8 cr), margins up to 13%, ₹21.8 cr subsidy received; big FY27 target (₹275–300 cr merged) leans on the merger and a 3x semiconductor jump; cash-flow turnaround pushed to FY28; European IT partner lost; peak-revenue talk of ₹600–750 cr.

## My read
Sahasra recovered well in FY26 (revenue +45%, back to profit, domestic mix up to ~45%) and owns a rare memory-packaging asset, but almost everything in the FY27 story is still to be proven: the ₹275–300 cr target depends on a promoter-group merger and on semiconductor revenue tripling after it missed the same ₹50 cr goal last year. Cash flow is negative, other income props up profit, and the FY26 annual report admits stock records did not tally at year-end. The price needs the merger approved on fair terms, the semiconductor unit at ₹50 cr and break-even, and positive operating cash flow sooner than FY28; an H1 FY27 with semiconductor revenue near ₹20–25 cr and clean inventory accounting would help, while another semiconductor shortfall or merger-related dilution would hurt.
