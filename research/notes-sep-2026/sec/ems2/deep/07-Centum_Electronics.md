# Centum Electronics — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Bengaluru maker of high-reliability electronics in two businesses: **EMS** (contract manufacturing of boards and box builds for global industrial, semiconductor-equipment, energy, transport, medical and defence OEMs; customer owns the design) and **Build-to-Specification (BTS)** (Centum designs and builds radar, electronic-warfare, satellite-payload and missile subsystems, mostly for DRDO, ISRO, HAL, BEL). [IP Sep-26; AR FY26 MD&A]
- FY26 standalone revenue ₹973 cr: EMS 72% (₹696 cr), BTS 28% (₹277 cr). Q1 FY27: EMS ₹148 cr, BTS ₹57 cr (still 72:28). [IP Sep-26 p.13, 16, 29]
- FY26 industry split: defence, space & aerospace 54%, industrial & energy 28%, transport & automotive 11%, healthcare 7%. Geography: India 48%, Europe 32%, North America 7%, rest of world 13%; exports about 53–54% of revenue. [AR FY26 MD&A p.51; IP Sep-26]
- Margin by model, per management: BTS 18–20%+ EBITDA, EMS 9–10% ("10–11% if lucky", cost-plus, resets at next price negotiation). Working-capital days: BTS 225–350, EMS 85–110. [IP Sep-26 p.21; Q4 call]
- Footprint 3.5 lakh sq ft; EMS plant at KIADB Aerospace Park, Devanahalli; BTS at Yelahanka. About 1,650 staff. No utilisation figure disclosed. [IP Sep-26 p.2, 9]
- The overseas arm (Centum T&S, ex-Adetel, bought 2016, France/Canada) was put into court restructuring in March 2026, operating businesses sold to MBDA and SII, and deconsolidated from 4 June 2026; judicial liquidation from 2 July 2026. Centum is now an India-only business. [Q1 call; CARE Sep-26]

## Key numbers
- Standalone (the business that remains), Screener: FY24 revenue ₹633 cr / operating profit ₹79 cr; FY25 ₹750 cr / ₹102 cr (14%); FY26 ₹973 cr / ₹121 cr (12%). Three-year revenue CAGR 25%. [Screener standalone]
- Standalone quarters (revenue / operating profit / OPM): Q1 FY26 ₹185 cr / ₹26 cr / 14%; Q2 ₹206 / ₹25 / 12%; Q3 ₹238 / ₹25 / 11%; Q4 ₹344 / ₹45 / 13%; Q1 FY27 ₹205 / ₹23 / 11%. Q1 FY27 PAT ₹14 cr (6.6% margin). [Screener standalone; Q1 call]
- Consolidated quarters (Screener): Q1 FY26 ₹179 cr / ₹28 cr; Q2 ₹277 / ₹20; Q3 ₹237 / ₹32; Q4 ₹340 / ₹49; Q1 FY27 ₹204 / ₹24 (12%). Q1 FY27 consolidated net profit ₹106 cr includes a one-time ₹94 cr gain on deconsolidation; continuing-operations PAT was ₹11 cr. [Screener consolidated; Q1 call]
- FY26 standalone booked ₹203 cr of exceptional charges (₹153.8 cr provision on the investment in Centum UK, ₹39.5 cr receivables written off from the Canadian subsidiary, plus inventory provisions), taking reported standalone PAT to a ₹117 cr loss; profit before exceptional items was ₹100 cr, up 63%. [AR FY26 note 41; CARE Sep-26; Q4 call]
- Order book (standalone): ₹1,341 cr Mar-25 → ₹1,645 cr Mar-26 → ₹1,797 cr Jun-26 (+31% YoY). Split Jun-26: BTS ₹925 cr, EMS ₹872 cr. Q1 order inflow ₹360 cr (+70%), BTS inflow ₹120 cr (+150%). [IP Sep-26 p.29; CARE Sep-26; Q1 call]
- Semiconductor-equipment EMS revenue: roughly zero in FY25, over ₹100 cr ($10m+) in FY26; target $25–30m in 1–2 years, from one anchor global OEM. [Q1 call]
- Unit economics not disclosed (no price/volume data); margin is driven by BTS share.

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026): medium-term standalone growth 25–30% a year; EBITDA margin 13–15% "in the next 1–2 years"; FY26 margin (12.4%) missed the 13–15% band because of EMS product mix; BTS inflow of ₹100–150 cr slipped into FY27 and would be booked in Q1/Q2; French sale to conclude by July 2026 with no further losses; FY27 capex ₹40–45 cr. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery (14 Aug 2026): revenue +11% and margin 11.3%, both below the run-rate needed; management called Q1 "somewhat muted" and blamed BTS phasing. Growth guide restated as "about 25%" for FY27 and FY28 (the lower end of 25–30%); margin guide softened from 13–15% to "above about 13%". **Guidance effectively trimmed at the top end; kept at the bottom.** [Q1 call]
- Delivered: French exit completed a month early (deconsolidated 4 June); BTS inflow of ₹120 cr in Q1 captured part of the spillover. [Q1 call]
- CARE's own view (Sep 2026): revenue CAGR ~20–25% medium term, margins up 50–100 bp from rising BTS share — a notch below management. [CARE Sep-26]

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex ₹40–45 cr, split roughly evenly between EMS lines and BTS R&D/test capability (Q4 call). [Q4 call]
- New facility at KIADB Aerospace Park: design done, construction to start; the land belongs to a promoter group private company, so Centum spends nothing this year; ₹50–70 cr outflow (clean room, plant, HVAC) from late FY28. [Q1 call]
- Funding: ₹210 cr QIP (Mar 2025, ₹1,160/share). ₹110 cr repaid debt in FY25; FY26 used ₹4.8 cr more on debt and ₹25.7 cr on capex; **₹60 cr unused at 31 Mar 2026**. [AR FY26 QIP note]
- Asset turn: standalone revenue ₹973 cr on ~₹134 cr of PP&E — about 7x — so growth needs working capital far more than plant. [IP Sep-26 balance sheet]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Standalone borrowings ₹123 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹96 cr (Mar-25); debt/equity 0.28x (company's figure); ₹60 cr of QIP money still unspent at Mar-26. Consolidated borrowings fell from ₹308 cr (FY23) to ₹123 cr as subsidiaries left. [Screener; IP Sep-26]
- Standalone inventory ₹457 cr at Mar-26 (254 inventory days on Screener), receivables ₹307 cr (115 days), payables 142 days; cash conversion cycle 227 days. Adjusted net working-capital days 142 (FY25: 141; FY24: 159). [Screener standalone; IP Sep-26; AR FY26 KAM]
- Customer advances ~₹210 cr, about two-thirds from BTS. [Q1 call]
- Operating cash flow weak: standalone CFO ₹28 cr in FY26 on ₹121 cr operating profit (40%); FY25 was negative ₹20 cr. Free cash flow negative in FY25 and FY26. [Screener standalone]
- CARE: gearing including advances 0.80x at Mar-26 (0.55x Mar-25) after the write-offs; inventory rose further by June 2026 due to the West Asia crisis; working-capital limit use averaged 67% (12m to Jul-26). [CARE Sep-26]
- Dividend payout ~17–21% of standalone profit historically; yield 0.1%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- DRDO SAMAR Level-5 certification; supplied to almost every ISRO satellite programme including Chandrayaan and Mangalyaan. [IP Sep-26; CARE Sep-26]
- Sticky customers: over 30% of FY26 revenue from customers of 20+ years, over 40% from 10–20 years; ~70–80% of products single-sourced. [CARE Sep-26; IP Sep-26 p.10, 15]
- System wins: HAL AESA radar for the Utility Helicopter–Maritime (UHM) — ₹66 cr development phase, ₹500 cr+ production potential to FY31, won L1 among 3 shortlisted of 10+ bidders; second space-debris tracking radar (~₹30 cr); Virupaksha radar subsystems (<₹10 cr development); GRSE naval navigation ₹29.4 cr; a customer indication for a ~₹350 cr three-satellite constellation under the Space Based Surveillance programme. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Sep-26 p.20]
- Semiconductor EMS: says it is the anchor OEM's first major India supplier, competing mainly with Malaysia. [Q1 call]
- Limits: EMS is cost-plus with low entry barriers (CARE); customer owns the IP, so Centum cannot sell the same product to rivals. [CARE Sep-26; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Concentration:** two customers above 10% each contributed ₹424 cr (44%) of FY26 standalone revenue; top five over 65% (59% in FY25). The semiconductor ramp is one OEM. [AR FY26 note 43(c); Q4 call]
- **Capital-allocation history:** the 2016 Adetel purchase lost money for a decade; FY26 wrote off ₹203 cr standalone and consolidated net loss was ₹52 cr. [AR FY26; Screener]
- **Working capital:** 250+ inventory days and rising; auditor made inventory obsolescence (₹457 cr balance) the key audit matter; CARE expects needs to "remain elevated, and potentially increase". [AR FY26 auditor's report; CARE Sep-26]
- **Promoter selling:** CMD sold 4.53% (6.65 lakh shares, ₹153 cr at ~₹2,301) in August 2025; promoter holding 58.8% (Sep-23) → 46.8% (Jun-26) after QIP dilution and this sale. No pledge. [News: ScanX, Aug-25; Screener shareholding; AR FY26 CG report]
- **Related parties:** new plant to be built on land owned by a promoter group private company (lease terms not yet disclosed); Centum Industries Pvt Ltd (promoter entity) is a related party from a 2016 slump-sale. The KIADB land lease deed expired Dec 2023; title transfer still pending. [Q1 call; AR FY26 CARO & note 6]
- Auditor's opinion unmodified; audit-trail logging not enabled for privileged-access changes (a common CARO remark). Contingent liabilities small: disputed income tax ₹4.6 cr, indirect tax ₹9.4 cr. [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 44]
- Forex: imports large; exports 50–55% give partial natural hedge. [CARE Sep-26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "cleaning up" to "focus on India growth"; the French exit closed early and management says no further liabilities.
- Numbers softened: Q1 revenue +11% and 11.3% margin; margin target language slipped from "13–15%" to "above 13%"; growth from "25–30%" to "about 25%".
- New talking points: design-led manufacturing (first wins hoped "in coming quarters"), electrification/grid-automation NPIs, an export electronic-warfare BTS opportunity, and the new plant (₹50–70 cr from FY28). Supply-chain stress (copper-clad laminate, memory lead times) discussed in Q4 was not raised in Q1.
- Semiconductor target restated: "$30m in 2–3 years" (Q4) became "$25–30m in 1–2 years" (Q1) — slightly firmer timing.

## My read
Centum's India business is genuinely better than its reported accounts: standalone revenue has compounded 25% for three years, the order book is up 31% to ₹1,797 cr and the loss-making overseas arm is finally gone, which CARE rewarded with an upgrade to BBB+. But Q1 FY27 was weak (+11%, 11.3% margin), the margin target has quietly slipped, and the business eats cash — CFO was 40% of operating profit in FY26 and inventory is 250+ days. The price already assumes the 25% growth and a margin march toward 13–15%; it needs BTS revenue to jump in H2 FY27 (UHM, SBS satellites) and semiconductor EMS to double. A year of 25% growth with margin above 13% and positive free cash flow would support the story; another half with low-teens growth or a stall at the semiconductor OEM would not.
