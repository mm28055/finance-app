## Business and segments
- Contract manufacturer (ODM/OEM) of **room ACs, washing machines, air coolers**, plus plastic moulding (consumer durables, sanitaryware, auto) and electronics (PCB assemblies, TV job-work); 70+ brand customers, 11 plants (Greater Noida, Bhiwadi, Ahmednagar/Supa, Roorkee), 10,000+ staff [IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹2,034 cr (+35%): products 80% (+40.7%) — **RAC ₹1,401 cr (+38%)**, washing machines ₹211 cr (+67%), coolers ~₹19 cr; electronics 5.3% (+6.3%); plastic moulding & components ₹294.6 cr (+7%) [Official Q1 transcript].
- FY26 revenue ₹5,288 cr (+9%): RAC +9% for the year (Q4 −12%), washing machines +52%, coolers −8% [Official Q4 transcript]. CRISIL: RAC 78–80% and washing machines 19–20% of product revenue; products 70–73% of group [CRISIL Feb-26].
- RAC volume +20–22% and ASP +10–12% in Q1; average RAC realisation ~₹21,000; per-unit manufacturing charge ~₹2,200–2,300 [Official Q1 transcript].
- **Goodworth Electronics** (TV joint venture): Q1 sales ₹177 cr, EBITDA ₹6.3 cr [Official Q1 transcript].
- AC is 60–65% of sales; target 50–55% in 2–3 years [Official Q1 transcript].
- Order book is not disclosed (brands give seasonal forecasts) [Official Q1 transcript].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 1,504 → Sep-25 655 → Dec-25 1,412 → Mar-26 1,717 → **Jun-26 2,034 (+35%)**. Operating profit 121 → 30 → 117 → 119 → **148 (+22%)**; OPM 8% → 4.6% → 8% → 7% → **7%**. Net profit 67 → 3 → 62 → 65 → **76 (+13%)** [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 revenue ₹2,746 cr, OPM 10%, PAT ₹135 cr; FY25 ₹4,870 cr, 10%, ₹288 cr; **FY26 ₹5,288 cr (+9%), 7%, ₹197 cr (−32%)**; TTM ₹5,818 cr, PAT ₹206 cr [Screener].
- Company Q1 EBITDA ₹156.2 cr (7.7%, incl. other items) vs ₹139.4 cr; gross margin 14.5% vs 15.9% a year ago; raw material 85.5% of sales [IP Q1FY27].
- **FY26 was hit by a weak AC season** (industry RAC volumes −15%: early monsoon, BEE rating change, GST-cut wait), a ~20% rupee fall (forex loss ₹38.8 cr vs gain ₹18.0 cr), and a March LPG shortage + truck shortage costing ~₹420 cr of Q4 sales [Official Q4 transcript].
- PLI: ₹37.5 cr for FY26 plus washing-machine PLI → ~₹71 cr to be recognised in FY27 [Official Q4 transcript].

## Guidance history
- **FY26:** began with "strong double-digit growth" (industry-wide), delivered +9% revenue and −32% PAT — **missed**; PGEL has since **stopped giving revenue guidance** [Official Q4 transcript; Official Q1 transcript].
- **Q4 call (28-May-26) for FY27:** revenue growth "better than industry"; EBITDA margin "towards 8%"; inventory to fall from ₹1,601 cr (Mar-26) to **under ₹900 cr by June-end**; washing machines +30–35%; capex ~₹400 cr; PBT to grow with sales; hope to beat FY25 PAT (~₹288 cr) [Official Q4 transcript].
- **Q1 delivery (7-Aug-26):** revenue +35% (well ahead of industry ~15% volume); washing machines +67%; operating margin ~7.3% (below 8%), 8% kept as an "aspiration, not guidance"; **inventory still above ₹1,000 cr** at June-end — **missed the <₹900 cr target** (blamed on higher commodity values and QCO-driven strategic stocking); RAC volume +20% or more for FY27; 25–30% growth possible in FY27–FY28; FY29 may be a "consolidation year"; fixed-asset turns >4x [Official Q1 transcript].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹785 cr (₹500 cr land & buildings, ₹165 cr RAC machinery, ₹70 cr washing-machine machinery); FY27 ~₹400 cr to finish projects, then 1.5–2 years of "sweating assets"; gross block to more than double vs three years ago [Official Q4/Q1 transcripts].
- **Washing machines**: new 1.8 mn-unit plant at DMIC Greater Noida live (total ~3 mn; ~72% annual utilisation before) — target 70–80% by FY28 [Official Q1 transcript].
- **Refrigerators**: 1.2 mn-unit plant at Sri City (AP), commercial production Q4 FY27; anchor customer committed 30–35% of capacity; 600–700k units possible in FY28 [Official Q1 transcript].
- **Compressors** (Supa, Maharashtra): first 2 mn-unit line, mass production Dec-26/Jan-27, ~₹2,850–2,900 per unit (≈₹450–500 cr revenue at 80% use), room for four lines; mostly for in-house ACs. Compressor imports are capped at 25% of FY25 levels until 31-Mar-27 and then barred under a Quality Control Order [Official Q1 transcript].
- New PG Technoplast plant at Salarpur (Rajasthan) — consolidating some Greater Noida units [Official Q1 transcript].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- CFO ₹188 cr (FY24) → −₹77 cr (FY25) → ₹70 cr (FY26); FCF −₹37 cr → −₹564 cr → **−₹713 cr** [Screener].
- Gross debt ₹409 cr (Jun-25) → ₹500 cr (Mar-26) → ₹417 cr (Jun-26); cash ₹911 cr → ₹389 cr → ₹491 cr; **net cash ₹501 cr (Jun-25) → net debt ₹110 cr (Mar-26) → net cash ₹74 cr (Jun-26)** [IP Q1FY27].
- Inventory ₹1,601 cr at Mar-26 (AC ₹1,300 cr incl. ₹450 cr finished goods); Screener FY26 inventory 134 days, debtors 82, payables 147, cash conversion 69 days [Official Q4 transcript; Screener].
- Raised ₹1,500 cr via QIP at ₹699 a share on 10-Dec-24 [AR FY26 Board's report]. Interest ₹102 cr in FY26 (₹89 cr FY25) [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Share gains: RAC revenue +38% vs industry primary ~15% volume (+10–12% price) in Q1 FY27; outsourcing share rising as brands find it cheaper than in-house plants [Official Q1 transcript].
- One-stop supplier across ACs, washing machines, fridges (from FY28) and TVs (JV), with in-house plastics, sheet metal, PCBAs and soon compressors [IP Q1FY27; Official Q1 transcript].
- Pricing is **per unit, not percentage** — commodity pass-through protects rupee profit but lowers margin %; pass-through lags a season [Official Q1 transcript].
- Weak points: entry-level/low-end models are the ones outsourced; brand-level price wars flow upstream; CRISIL flags intense competition [Official Q1 transcript; CRISIL Feb-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Seasonality and cyclicality**: RAC is ~60–65% of sales; one bad summer (FY26) cut PAT by a third [Screener; Official Q4 transcript].
- **Forex**: unhedged import liabilities caused ₹38.8 cr loss in FY26; only $15–20 mn hedged; guidance assumes rupee ≤₹97/$ [Official Q4 transcript].
- Working capital: inventory target missed; cash conversion weakens in bad seasons; free cash flow negative three years running [Screener; Official Q1 transcript].
- Execution on three new plants (washing machines, fridges, compressors) at once; compressor technology developed without a Chinese partner [Official Q4/Q1 transcripts].
- Audit: most of the business sits in subsidiaries (PG Technoplast et al.: ₹3,956 cr revenue, ₹4,977 cr assets) audited by another auditor; database-level audit trail was missing at PG Technoplast until 30-Sep-25 and at PG Plastronics all year [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report].
- Contingent liabilities small (~₹17 cr: central excise ₹7.7 cr, anti-dumping duty ₹7.4 cr at the Supreme Court, third-party claims ₹2 cr) [AR FY26 note 40].
- Investor-relations complaint on the Q1 call (unanswered emails, plant-visit requests) — minor but notable [Official Q1 transcript].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was about a "perfect storm" (LPG, trucks, rupee, weather) and a 43% EBITDA fall; Q1 was a record ₹2,000 cr+ quarter with confidence on share gains and new plants.
- Revenue guidance formally dropped; 8% margin recast as an "aspiration".
- New: QCO-driven compressor shortage (and PGEL's line as a hedge), IGCT copper-tube import ban from November, new CEO with AC experience, Big-Four-assisted SOP project, SAP rollout.

## My read
Q1 FY27 shows the FY26 slump was mostly weather, inventory and rupee — revenue is back to +35% and PGEL keeps gaining share as brands outsource. But margins remain ~7%, profit growth (+13%) lags revenue, free cash flow has been negative for three years, and inventory is still high. The price (~72x trailing) assumes FY27 PAT at least recovers to FY25's ~₹288 cr and new plants add growth in FY28; watch Q3/Q4 price pass-through, compressor start-up in Dec-Jan, and whether inventory falls below ₹900 cr. Another weak summer or a sharp rupee fall would break the recovery story.
