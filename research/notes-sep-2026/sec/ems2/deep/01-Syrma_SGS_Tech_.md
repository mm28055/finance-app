# Syrma SGS Technology (NSE: SYRMA / BSE: 543573) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹1,733 (52-week ₹634–1,804), market cap ₹33,410 cr, P/E 90.0, book value ₹148, ROCE 16.8%, ROE 14.0%, dividend yield 0.09% [Screener consolidated, 27-Sep-26]. Promoter holding 42.28% (46.53% Mar-25; the fall is mainly dilution from the ~₹1,000 cr QIP of Aug-25), DIIs 15.9%, FIIs 7.5% [Screener shareholding; Ind-Ra May-26]. Rating: **Ind-Ra upgraded bank lines to IND AA/Stable (from AA-), CP IND A1+** [Ind-Ra May-26]. New CEO **Jaidit Singh Brar** (ex-McKinsey senior partner) from 29-Jun-26; previous CEO Satendra Singh moved to Senior Operating Director [Official Q1 transcript; News: TradingView/Reuters, Jun-26].

## Business and segments
- Engineering-led EMS/ODM maker of PCB assemblies, box builds, RFID tags and magnetics for ~350 customers in 35+ countries from 17 domestic plants plus Germany; >10,000 employees [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Q1 FY27 revenue from operations ₹1,588.6 cr by vertical (YoY): **Consumer ₹533 cr (+68%, 34% of mix)**, Auto ₹395 cr (+78%, 25%), Industrial ₹377 cr (+31% YoY but −18% QoQ, 24%), IT & Railways ₹150 cr (+199%, 9%), Healthcare ₹134.5 cr (+100%, 8%) [IP Q1FY27 slide 7].
- FY26 mix: consumer ~31% (₹1,452 cr), industrial ~28–30%, auto ~24–25%, healthcare ₹395 cr (8%), IT & rail ₹476 cr (~10%) [Q4 call; AR FY26].
- **Exports** ₹1,200+ cr in FY26 (+41%, ~25% of revenue); Q1 FY27 ₹387 cr (+61%, 24%): ~40% Europe, ~22% USA (~37% incl. Mexico/Canada) [Q4 call; Official Q1 transcript].
- **ODM (own-design)** ₹825 cr in FY26 (+80–82%, 17% of revenue, from 12%); Q1 FY27 ₹270 cr vs ₹125 cr (17%), down from ₹345 cr in Q4 because defence, maritime and MedTech are back-ended [Q4 call; Official Q1 transcript]. MedTech is almost all ODM (~₹395 cr of the ₹825 cr) [Q4 call].
- **Elcome** (naval/maritime electronics, founded 1978): 60% bought for ₹235 cr in FY26; remaining 40% to be bought in phases over ~3 years under an earn-out; revenue ₹200–300 cr, EBITDA margin 20–25%; added ₹50.8 cr to FY26 operating EBITDA on part-year consolidation [AR FY26 pp.~6, note 7; Ind-Ra May-26]. Defence/maritime contributed ~₹140 cr in Q4 FY26 [Official Q1 transcript].
- New entities: Elemaster JV (60%, ₹33 cr paid Apr-26; Bengaluru high-reliability plant opened 2-Sep-26), Kaga JV (60%; ~₹24–25 cr total, Syrma ~₹15 cr) for Japanese customers' Indian EMS needs, potential ₹300–500 cr over 3–5 years [Ind-Ra May-26; Official Q1 transcript; BSE filing 2-Sep-26]. KSolare solar-inverter JV with Premier Energies was **dropped** (seller missed conditions); a greenfield inverter plan is being evaluated [Q4 call].
- Capacity utilisation 65–70%; expansion at Bengaluru (incl. data-centre power/cooling), Pune, and Jodhpur MedTech (120,000 sq ft plant inaugurated 22-Sep-26) [Official Q1 transcript; BSE filing 22-Sep-26].
- Customer concentration: top-5 38%, top-10 51%, top-20 66% in Q1 FY27 (FY26: 34/47/63%). **Largest customer fell from ₹976 cr (26% of revenue) in FY25 to ₹618 cr (13%) in FY26** — the consumer "cap" at work [Official Q1 transcript; Q4 call; AR FY26 note 44.3].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 944 → Sep-25 1,146 → Dec-25 1,264 → Mar-26 1,465 → **Jun-26 1,589 (+68% YoY)**. Operating profit 87 → 115 → 159 → 174 → **162**; OPM 9% → 10% → 13% → 12% → **10%**. Net profit 50 → 66 → 110 → 119 → **106 (+112%)** [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 revenue ₹3,154 cr, OPM 6%, PAT ₹124 cr; FY25 ₹3,787 cr, 9%, ₹184 cr; **FY26 ₹4,819 cr (+27%), 11%, ₹346 cr (+88%)**; TTM ₹5,464 cr, PAT ₹402 cr [Screener].
- Gross margin: 25.4% (Q1 FY26) → 25.9% (Q4 FY26) → **24.5% (Q1 FY27)**; FY26 gross margin 25.6% vs 22.6% in FY25 [IP Q1FY27 slide 5; Q4 call].
- Unit economics: the business is volume × mix. Management's worked example: a chip rising from ₹60 to ₹100 passed through at cost leaves absolute rupees intact but cuts the margin percentage — so component inflation (memory, metals) will depress reported margin % even with full pass-through [Official Q1 transcript].
- Other income is falling (FY25 ₹47 cr → FY26 ₹31 cr); PLI (net of sharing with customers) was ~₹38 cr in FY26 (gross ~₹80 cr), and the electronics PLI scheme ended Mar-26 [Screener; Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A].

## Guidance history
- **FY26 (set May-25):** EBITDA ₹400 cr+, revenue +30–35%, exports ₹1,100 cr, positive cash flow. **Delivered:** operating EBITDA ₹545 cr (₹490 cr+ ex-Elcome), revenue +27% reported (+38% ex-consumer), exports ₹1,200 cr+, OCF ₹290 cr (53% of EBITDA). Beat on profit, slight miss on reported top line [Q4 call].
- **FY27 (Q4 call, 12-May-26):** revenue growth 30–35% ("35%"), operating EBITDA margin **10.5–11%** (CFO said "at least 10% to 10.5%" in prepared remarks; MD said 10.5–11%), ~₹700 cr total EBITDA / 30% absolute EBITDA growth, exports ~₹1,500 cr, MedTech >₹500 cr, consumer ~30% of sales, working-capital days to fall further; organic capex ₹100–150 cr plus ~₹250 cr on PCB [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (30-Jul-26):** revenue +68% (far ahead), exports +61%, EBITDA +69%; but **operating margin 10.2% was below the 10.5–11% band** (blamed on 34% consumer mix), and **working-capital days rose from 63 to 71** with ₹340 cr of new short-term debt — the opposite of the Q4 promise. Guidance **kept**, with management saying it expects to "exceed" on revenue and EBITDA; a formal raise deferred by a quarter because of supply-side risk [Official Q1 transcript; IP Q1FY27].
- Longer term: 30–35% growth for 2–5 years organically; FY28 growth "superior to 30–35%" once PCB revenue is added; ODM to 25% of sales over "several years" (17–19% near term); exports +25% a year minimum [Q4 call; Official Q1 transcript].

## Capex and capacity
- **Multilayer PCB (JV Shinhyup Syrma Circuits, Syrma 75% / Korea's SH Electronics 25%) at Naidupeta, Andhra Pradesh.** Phase 1 ~₹400 cr: ₹50 cr spent by Mar-26, ~₹130 cr funded by Jun-26 (₹50 cr in Q1), rest in FY27, funded 50–60% by debt; building 65–70% complete, equipment from Oct-26, power-on Jan–Mar 2027, **commercial production from Apr-27**. Phase 2 (~₹400 cr) from end-2027/early-2028 into FY29 [Q4 call; Official Q1 transcript; AR FY26 note 7].
- Separate ECMS approvals for copper-clad laminate, HDI and flex PCB: another ~₹800 cr over FY28–FY30. Ind-Ra puts the approved total PCB + CCL plan at **₹1,595 cr over ~6 years**; AR calls it a "₹1,600 crores greenfield facility" [Q4 call; Ind-Ra May-26; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Economics: asset turns ~1.5x gross at 80%+ utilisation (40–50% in year one); EBITDA 15–18% at full ramp excluding incentives, **below 10% in the first year**; ~50% capex subsidy (ECMS + state) claimable roughly a year after start-up [Official Q1 transcript; Q4 call].
- Organic capex ₹100–150 cr in FY27 (₹40 cr in Q1); total Q1 capex ~₹90 cr; FY26 capex ~₹140 cr. Capital commitments at Mar-26 ₹158 cr [Official Q1 transcript; Q4 call; AR FY26 note 40].
- Board passed an enabling resolution for up to ₹1,000 cr QIP (described as routine, "war chest" for semiconductors/ISM 2.0 partner or M&A) [Official Q1 transcript].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Mar-26: total debt ₹353 cr (term ₹68 cr, working capital ₹286 cr), cash + investments ₹820 cr, **net cash ₹467 cr**. Jun-26: debt ₹686 cr (working capital ₹619 cr), cash + investments ₹808 cr, **net cash ₹122 cr** [IP Q1FY27 slide 9].
- Screener borrowings (incl. leases) ₹665 cr (Mar-25) → ₹400 cr (Mar-26); investments ₹59 cr → ₹547 cr (QIP money parked) [Screener].
- Cash flow: CFO −₹109 cr (FY24) → ₹176 cr (FY25) → **₹290 cr (FY26)**; FCF ₹4 cr → ₹114 cr; FY26 investing outflow −₹736 cr (Elcome, PCB, treasury) funded by the QIP [Screener].
- Company NWC days 69 (FY25) → 63 (FY26; 58 ex-Elcome) → **71 (Jun-26)**. Screener FY26: debtor 139 days, inventory 108 days, payables 199 days (cash conversion 48 days). Ind-Ra's own measure: 79 days in FY25, **92 days in H1 FY26** [IP Q1FY27; Screener; Ind-Ra May-26].
- Dividend payout 9% in FY26; no buyback [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Certifications and qualification time: mature products take 12–18 months to transfer, new ones 18–30 months; TISAX automotive information-security certification (first in India per MD) required by an overseas auto customer [Official Q1 transcript; Q4 call].
- Wallet share: "number one or number two vendor" at most domestic customers; 1–2% share at global customers, aiming for 4–6% [Official Q1 transcript].
- Margin mix shift is real: gross margin +300 bps in FY26 to 25.6% as ODM went from 12% to 17% of sales and consumer was held ~30% [Q4 call; AR FY26].
- Customer adds: 32 in FY26 (potential ₹2,500 cr/yr at maturity), 18 in Q1 FY27 (5 auto; ₹1,000 cr+ at full ramp, mostly from FY28) [Q4 call; Official Q1 transcript].
- Weaknesses: EMS is price-competitive (Ind-Ra: "limits its bargaining power"), 60% of materials imported, and new large entrants (e.g., L&T's electronics foray) raise competition [Ind-Ra May-26; Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Working capital and debt went the wrong way in Q1**: +₹340 cr short-term debt, net cash down ₹345 cr in a quarter, framed as a deliberate "strategic inventory" build until end-CY26 [Official Q1 transcript; IP Q1FY27].
- **Execution load:** PCB fab (complex chemistry, customer approvals), Elcome integration and 40% buy-out, Elemaster, Kaga, possible inverter greenfield and ISM 2.0 — plus a brand-new CEO from consulting [Official Q1 transcript; AR FY26].
- **Goodwill ₹454.7 cr** (Johari MedTech CGU ₹203.9 cr) — a key audit matter; Elcome purchase-price allocation and the liability to buy the remaining 40% also a key audit matter [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report].
- Auditor (Walker Chandiok & Co): **unmodified opinion**; standalone report carries an emphasis of matter only on the SGS Tekniks/SGS Infosystems amalgamation (appointed date 1-Apr-23, NCLT order 7-Oct-25). **Audit-trail exception**: the edit log was not enabled at database level for the holding company's accounting software [AR FY26 auditor's reports; note 57].
- Contingent liabilities are small (tax/GST demands totalling roughly ₹5 cr) [AR FY26 note 40].
- Related parties: modest — sales to Infinx (promoter-linked) ~₹0.9 cr, travel from Jetways, rent/services from Tandon Holdings; ESOP perquisite of ₹14.0 cr to director Jayesh Doshi in FY26; FY25 ₹47 cr upfront lease payment to Ebony Electronics (promoter-linked) for a site [AR FY26 note 46].
- Consumer (~30–34%) is thin-margin and lumpy; smart metering (~₹250–260 cr in FY26) has only ~12–15% material margin with long receivables — management is deliberately slowing it [Q4 call].
- Middle East/Red Sea disruption, specialty-chemical shortages, component lead times; PLI scheme ended Mar-26 [Official Q1 transcript; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Valuation: ~90x trailing earnings leaves little room for a margin miss [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from **balance-sheet celebration** (net cash ₹467 cr, WC days 63, "we will not let this slip") to **supply-chain defence** ("war room", inventory as a "strategic asset", WC 71 days).
- Revenue momentum accelerated (Q4 +56%, Q1 +68%) and management moved from "30–35%" to "35% plus" and "exceed".
- Margin fell (Q4 11.9% → Q1 10.2%) on consumer mix; guidance band held.
- New: CEO change, Kaga JV, data-centre power/cooling, C-DAC server motherboard approval, QIP enabling resolution. Dropped: KSolare.

## My read
The business is clearly improving: revenue has grown 68% YoY, ODM and exports are rising, the largest-customer dependence has halved, and FY26 beat its own EBITDA target by a wide margin. The weak spots are margin percentage (10.2% in Q1, below guidance) and the reversal in working capital only one quarter after management promised further discipline. At ~90x trailing earnings the price assumes 30%+ growth for several years plus a successful PCB ramp from FY28; watch Q2 working-capital days (back towards 63?), whether margin gets back into the 10.5–11% band, and PCB power-on in Jan–Mar 2027. A second quarter of rising debt or a PCB delay would weaken the case; a formal guidance raise with falling inventory would strengthen it.
