# Amber Enterprises India (NSE: AMBER / BSE: 540902) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹6,960 (52-week ₹5,400–8,974), market cap ₹24,546 cr, P/E 119 (on reported TTM profit depressed by one-offs), book value ₹1,242, ROCE 10.3%, ROE 6.0%, no dividend [Screener consolidated, 27-Sep-26]. Promoters 38.09% (39.72% Mar-25; diluted by the ₹1,000 cr QIP in FY26); FIIs 30.6% (Sep-25) → 20.5%, DIIs 17.8% → 30.5% [Screener shareholding; CRISIL Dec-25]. Rating **Crisil AA-/Positive/A1+** (outlook raised to Positive Nov-25) [CRISIL Dec-25]. Auditor **S.R. Batliboi & Co (EY)** — **qualified the FY26 consolidated accounts** (see Risks) [AR FY26].

## Business and segments
- Three divisions (Q1 FY27): **Consumer Durables** ₹2,758 cr (+8%), operating EBITDA ₹214 cr (7.8%); **Electronics** ₹985 cr (+29%), EBITDA ₹107 cr (+117%, 10.8%); **Railway Sub-systems & Defence** (Sidwal) ₹144 cr (+18%), EBITDA ₹16 cr (−26%, 11.3%) [Official Q1 transcript; IP Q1FY27].
- FY26: Consumer Durables ₹8,383 cr (+14%), EBITDA ₹593 cr (+6%, ~7.1%); Electronics ₹3,268 cr (+49%), EBITDA ₹287 cr (+89%, 8.8%); Railways ₹535 cr (+19%), EBITDA ₹90 cr (+8%, 16.8%). Group ₹12,186 cr, operating EBITDA ₹970 cr, adjusted PAT ₹338 cr (all +22%) [Official Q4 transcript].
- **Consumer Durables:** India's largest room-AC contract maker/ODM — **26–27% share of the Indian RAC market** (FY25), supplies brands covering ~75% of the market; RAC ~75% of division (finished units ~55–60%, rest components), non-RAC 25% (washing machines, fridges components, motors) [CRISIL Dec-25; Official Q1 transcript].
- **Electronics** (IL JIN: Amber holds 90.22% of shares, but only ~60% on a diluted basis after ₹1,750 cr of CCPS issued to PE investors): FY26 PCBA ~₹2,281 cr (~5% margin), bare PCB ~₹600 cr (Ascent + Shogini; 12–13% margin now vs 16–17% normal), power electronics/industrial ~₹400 cr (Power-One ~15% margin, Unitronics Israel 50.4%) [Official Q4 transcript; CRISIL Dec-25; AR FY26 Board's report].
- **Railways:** HVAC, pantry, doors, gangways, brakes for coaches/metros; order book ₹2,750 cr+ (Jun-26), ₹2,600 cr+ (Mar-26); new Faridabad greenfield plant now running [IP Q1FY27; Official Q4 transcript].
- **Mobile phones (new):** manufacturing collaboration (not a JV) with Oppo India for Oppo, OnePlus and Realme in a sub-leased Oppo facility; trial production Q4 FY27, commercial Q1 FY28; ~8 mn units year 1, 13–16 mn year 2; capex under ₹50 cr; EBITDA 1.5–2% (industry, ex-PLI); NWC 4–10 days [Official Jun-26 business call; Official Q1 transcript].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (18-May-26) for FY27:** consolidated **margin pressure of 50–100 bps** (wages +35% Haryana/+22% UP, copper-clad laminate +60%, gold +60%, rupee); Consumer Durables growth in line with industry 12–14%; Electronics **+40%** at **9.5–10% margin**; Railways +30–35% (FY27 and FY28) at **16–17% margin**; capex **₹1,800–2,000 cr** (cash ₹1,100–1,200 cr); **net debt ₹700–800 cr by March 2027** (₹511 cr at Mar-26) [Official Q4 transcript].
- **Q1 delivery (13-Aug-26):** operating EBITDA +28%, margin 8.7% vs 7.6% — **better than guided**, and the 50–100 bps cut was **withdrawn** ("margins are normalized at the current level"); Electronics margin 10.8% (above 9.5–10%); CD growth 8% (high base; guidance 13–15% kept); Electronics 40% **kept**; Railways margin 11% vs 16–17% guided — **now 15–16%** for FY27 (cut); **net debt already ₹1,225 cr at Jun-26**, above the year-end guide [Official Q1 transcript].
- Longer term: CCL (copper-clad laminate) plant by 2029–30; electronics margin "endeavour" to rise from 10.8%; railways +30–35% in FY28; Jewar mega-AC plant construction FY28, trial FY29 [Official Q1 transcript].

## Capex and capacity

- ECMS approvals for HDI/multilayer PCB: **Jewar (UP) ~₹3,200 cr** with Korea Circuit (trial ~18 months from Aug-26), **Hosur (TN) ~₹1,000 cr** (running in FY27), Shogini Pune ~₹500 cr; Ascent ~48% capital subsidy on plant & machinery from the Centre plus 42% from UP; asset turns only 0.8–1x [Official Q1/Q4 transcripts].
- Acquisitions: Shogini (80%, ₹506 cr), Power-One, Unitronics (50.4%), Ascent raised to 98.5% (Jul-26; ex-MD stepped down), Amber Resojet (remaining 50% for ₹1.74 cr, Apr-26) [CRISIL Dec-25; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26].

## Moat evidence
- Scale and share in RAC (26–27%), ~70% of an AC's bill of materials made in-house, plants next to customers; top-5 customers 38% of FY25 revenue [CRISIL Dec-25; Official Jun-26 call].
- Pricing: commodity/FX passed on with a one-quarter lag as a tier-1 supplier; bare-PCB (tier-2) lags two quarters; PCB price hikes of ~15% and ~18% obtained in FY26 [Official Q4/Q1 transcripts].
- Electronics margin 2.8% (2018) → 10.8% (Q1 FY27) through mix toward PCB and power electronics [Official Q1 transcript].
- Weak spots: Indian Railways contracts are fixed-price (no pass-through); mobile assembly is a 1.5–2% margin business [Official Q1 transcript; Jun-26 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Qualified audit opinion (first time):** S.R. Batliboi qualified FY26 consolidated accounts because they include unaudited figures of one subsidiary and the step-down JV **Shivaliks Mercantile**, whose ₹81.9 cr share of loss was recognised; Amber then sold its 49% in Shivaliks to J P Fincap on 30-Mar-26 for ~₹1.1 lakh after writing off the investment and loan (₹112 cr in FY26) [AR FY26 auditor's report, Board's report].
- **Audit-trail gaps**: holding company and six subsidiaries on Microsoft D365 without database-level logging; two step-down subsidiaries with incomplete logs [AR FY26 note 71].
- Recurring "exceptional" items (₹26 cr FY25, ₹112 cr FY26, ₹123 cr Q1 FY27) and heavy use of "adjusted PAT" — reported earnings are well below adjusted [AR FY26; Screener].
- Minority interests are growing (IL JIN PE investors hold ~39% on a diluted basis; Mr. Sim ~8.6%) — more of electronics profit belongs to others [Official Q1 transcript].
- Leverage rising fast (net debt more than doubled in one quarter) while capex peaks; interest + depreciation already ~₹193 cr a quarter [Screener; Official Q1 transcript].
- Contingent liabilities are modest: income-tax ₹80 cr, GST ₹14 cr, corporate guarantee ₹2.9 cr [AR FY26 note 45].
- Execution across many fronts (mobile, HDI PCB, CCL, railways, acquisitions) [Official Q1 transcript].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from **defensive** (50–100 bps margin cut, wage hikes, CCL inflation) to **expansive** (Oppo, HDI groundbreaking, $300 bn market talk).

- New: Oppo deal (announced 18–20 Jun-26), IL JIN ₹5,000 cr enabling resolution, CCL plan, BEE tonnage-label rule from 1-Jul-26 causing channel confusion.

## My read
Operations are improving: electronics is now a ₹4,000 cr+ run-rate business at double-digit margin and Q1 group EBITDA grew 28%. But reported earnings are much weaker than "adjusted" earnings, FY26 accounts carry a first-time audit qualification, net debt jumped to ₹1,225 cr, and minority holders own a growing slice of electronics. The price assumes the PCB and mobile bets deliver from FY28; watch net debt against the ₹700–800 cr year-end guide, railways margin recovery, and a clean FY27 audit. Fewer exceptional items and positive free cash flow would improve the view.
