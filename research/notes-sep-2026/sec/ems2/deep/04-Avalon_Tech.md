# Avalon Technologies (NSE: AVALON / BSE: 543896) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹2,328 (52-week ₹777–2,620), market cap ₹15,574 cr, P/E 117, book value ₹108 (P/B ~21.5), ROCE 19.3%, ROE 16.5%, no dividend [Screener consolidated, 27-Sep-26]. Promoters 44.39% (50.57% Mar-25) — co-promoter **Bhaskar Srinivasan and family trusts sold 5.71% in a ₹303 cr block deal on 24-Jun-25** (holding 21.22% → 15.51%) and left the board 10-Feb-26; DIIs 17.3% → 24.7% [Screener shareholding; News: Trade Brains, Jun-25; AR FY26 note 32A]. Rating **Crisil A/Stable/A1** (assigned Apr-26) [CRISIL Apr-26]. Auditor Varma & Varma — unmodified opinion [AR FY26].

## Business and segments
- Chennai-based EMS company focused on complex, high-mix **box builds** (59.9% of Q1 FY27 revenue, from 44.5% in FY22) plus PCBA, cables/harnesses, sheet metal, plastics and magnetics; 16 plants, 615k+ sq ft across India (Chennai incl. MEPZ SEZ) and the US (Sienna Corp, California) [IP Q1FY27; Official Q1 transcript].
- **Build-to-print, not ODM** — customers own the IP even when Avalon helps design [Official Q1 transcript].
- Q1 FY27 verticals: Industrial 32% (+52%), Clean energy 29% (energy-storage systems for US customers), Mobility 25% (+36%; rail ~15% +37%, aerospace ~8% +47%), communications declining, medical +15% [Official Q1 transcript]. FY26: Industrial 34% (+65%), Mobility 28% (+50%), Clean energy 20% (+45%), Communications 8% (+58%) [Official Q4 transcript].
- Customer geography: US 59%, India 41% (Q1 FY27); FY26 US 62% / India 38%. Manufacturing location: India 72%, US 28% (Q1 FY27; FY26 79/21) [IP Q1FY27; Official Q4/Q1 transcripts].
- **India manufacturing: EBITDA 16.7%, PAT 11.1%** (Q1 FY27). US manufacturing lost ~₹4 cr at PAT in Q1 (₹5 cr Q4; ~₹9 cr a year ago; ~₹14 cr two years ago); target EBITDA break-even by end-FY27 [Official Q1 transcript].
- Order book ₹2,208 cr at Jun-26 (+23.4%; 14-month average execution), plus ₹1,256 cr of 15–36-month contracts; ₹2,196 cr at Mar-26; ₹2,016 cr at Dec-25 [Official Q1/Q4 transcripts; CRISIL Apr-26].
- New: HVDC/power systems for data centres and semiconductor wafer-fab equipment (revenue started Q1 FY27), Kavach rail anti-collision (testing done), aerospace cabin sub-assemblies to volume, locomotive engine sub-systems [Official Q1 transcript].
- Largest customer ₹190.6 cr = **11.9% of FY26 revenue** (FY25 ₹121.4 cr, 11.1%); top-3 <25% of order book [AR FY26 note 28.3; CRISIL Apr-26].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 323 → Sep-25 382 → Dec-25 418 → Mar-26 480 → **Jun-26 484 (+50%)**. Operating profit 30 → 39 → 48 → 57 → **58 (+94%)**; OPM 9% → 10% → 11% → 12% → **12%**. Net profit 14 → 25 → 33 → 41 → **35 (+145%)** [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 revenue ₹867 cr, OPM 7%, PAT ₹28 cr; FY25 ₹1,098 cr, 10%, ₹63 cr; **FY26 ₹1,603 cr (+46%), 11%, ₹113 cr (+78%)**; TTM ₹1,764 cr, PAT ₹134 cr [Screener].
- Gross margin held in the 33–35% band: 35.5% (Q1 FY26), 33.7% (Q4 FY26), 34.7% (Q1 FY27); FY26 34.3%. Tariff pass-through inflated revenue and cut EBITDA % by ~0.9 pts in Q1 [IP Q1FY27; Official Q1 transcript].
- About 45–50% of costs below gross margin are fixed/semi-fixed, so growth lifts margin (EBITDA 9.2% → 12.0% YoY) [Official Q1 transcript].

## Guidance history
- **FY26:** started at 18–20% growth, delivered **46%**; NWC guided 120–130 days, delivered 112 days [Official Q4 transcript].
- **Doubling FY24 → FY27 (~₹1,725–1,734 cr):** achieved on a trailing-12-month basis in Q1 FY27, about a year early [Official Q1 transcript].
- **FY27 (Q4 call, 7-May-26):** revenue +24–27%; gross margin 33–35%; capex ₹50–60 cr; US break-even later in FY27; next doubling to **~₹3,200 cr by FY29** [Official Q4 transcript].
- **Q1 FY27 (5-Aug-26):** revenue +50%, gross margin 34.7%, US loss narrowed to ₹4 cr — **guidance raised to 26–30%**; ₹3,200 cr FY29 target kept; NWC 117 days (inside 120–130 guide) [Official Q1 transcript]. Implied growth for the remaining three quarters is only ~20–25% — management calls the guide conservative because large programmes can slip by a quarter [Official Q1 transcript].
- Track record: beat or met every stated target in the last two years.

## Capex and capacity
- Capex ₹58 cr (FY25), ₹56 cr (FY26), ₹16 cr (Q1 FY27); asset turns ~9.9x; aim 8–10x and ROCE >20% [Official Q4/Q1 transcripts].
- New Chennai plant for domestic demand complete; commercial production from Q2 FY27 [Official Q1 transcript].
- **Buying a large land parcel in Chennai** for semiconductor-equipment and advanced-electronics work over the next decade (ISM 2.0 opportunity); size and cost not yet disclosed — could lift capex above the ₹50–60 cr run-rate [Official Q1 transcript].
- One new box-build capability area to be announced within ~2 quarters [Official Q1 transcript].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- CFO ₹17 cr (FY24) → ₹25 cr (FY25) → ₹57 cr (FY26); FCF −₹18 cr → −₹21 cr → **+₹4 cr** — profit growth has not yet turned into much cash [Screener]. Q1 FY27 CFO ₹32 cr [Official Q1 transcript].
- Debt ₹196 cr, cash & investments ₹171 cr, **net debt ₹24 cr** (Jun-26); net debt/equity 0.03 [Official Q1 transcript].
- Company NWC 142 days (Jun-25) → 112 (Mar-26) → 117 (Jun-26): inventory 94 days, receivables 74, payables 51. Screener FY26: debtor 87, inventory 161, payables 87, cash conversion **160 days** — still a working-capital-heavy model [Official Q1 transcript; Screener].
- No dividend [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Long product lives (10–15 years) and ~9-year average relationships with top customers [CRISIL Apr-26]; a further ₹3,465 cr of 3–15-year orders is not counted in the order book [Official Q1 transcript].
- **Dual-shore model**: US plant wins and qualifies new customers (and hedged US tariffs), then volume moves to India at 16.7% EBITDA [Official Q1/Q4 transcripts].
- Vertical integration (metal, cable, plastics, magnetics) lets it take the whole box — box-build share 44% → 60% [Official Q1 transcript].
- Pricing: tariffs passed "substantially all" to customers; gross margin stable in a 33–35% band for five years [Official Q4/Q1 transcripts].
- Weak points: import dependence and FX; clean-energy revenue tied to US energy-storage incentives (company says support runs to 2030+) [CRISIL Apr-26; Official Q1 transcript].

## Risks and red flags
- **Valuation**: P/E ~117 and P/B ~21.5 assume 26–30% growth continues for years [Screener].
- **Co-promoter exit**: Bhaskar Srinivasan sold 5.71% (Jun-25) and left the board (Feb-26) [News: Trade Brains, Jun-25; AR FY26 note 32A].
- US dependence: ~60% of revenue from US customers; clean energy (29%) linked to US subsidies; US policy/tariff swings [IP Q1FY27; Official Q1 transcript].
- Working capital (~160 days cash cycle per Screener) and near-zero free cash flow despite rapid profit growth [Screener].
- Supply chain: bare-PCB and fibreglass/high-purity material shortages; company pre-bought supplies [Official Q1 transcript].
- Governance looks clean: unmodified audit (KAM: revenue recognition), audit trail operated all year, contingent liabilities only ~₹2 cr (tax/PF), related-party dealings limited to KMP pay and ₹0.5 cr consultancy to a relative's firm [AR FY26 auditor's report, notes 32B, 35].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was about finishing the "double by FY27" promise early and resetting to ₹3,200 cr by FY29; Q1 raised FY27 guidance and shifted the story to new markets (Europe, Southeast Asia), semiconductor equipment, HVDC/data-centre power and a big Chennai land buy.
- US share of manufacturing rose to 28% (from ~21–23%) — management says it will settle near 20%.
- New concern: bare-PCB/material shortages; new plan: VP-level hiring, automation, AI.

## My read
Avalon is the cleanest execution story in this group: eight straight quarters of improvement, targets beaten, margins rising with scale, clean audit and a near-debt-free balance sheet. What it lacks is cash conversion (FY26 free cash flow only ₹4 cr) and cheapness — at ~117x trailing earnings the price needs the ₹3,200 cr FY29 target and more. Watch the size and funding of the Chennai land/semiconductor capex, US break-even by March 2027, and whether FY27 growth lands above 30%. A sharp slowdown in US clean-energy orders would hurt most.
