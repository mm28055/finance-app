# Kaynes Technology India (NSE: KAYNES / BSE: 543664) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹3,650 (52-week ₹2,995–7,705, **−53% from high**), market cap ₹24,536 cr, P/E 70.6, book value ₹708, ROCE 12.7%, ROE 8.7%, no dividend [Screener consolidated, 27-Sep-26]. Promoters 53.46% (57.75% Mar-25; fall is mainly the ₹1,600 cr QIP of Jun-25); **FIIs 11.2% → 5.8% and DIIs 23.7% (Sep-25) → 11.8% (Jun-26)** while retail holders doubled to 4.49 lakh — institutions have exited [Screener shareholding; CRISIL Mar-26]. Rating **Crisil A/Stable**; placed on "watch developing" 12-Dec-25 after "accounting and reporting lapses in fiscal 2025", watch removed and rating reaffirmed Mar-26 [CRISIL Mar-26].

## Business and segments
- Mysuru-based EMS/ODM maker (box builds, PCBA, cable harness) for automotive/EV, industrial, railways, aerospace/defence/space, medical and IT; 18 plants, 26 SMT lines, 500+ customers [IP Q1FY27 slides 8–9].
- **Three businesses in one:** (1) core EMS — FY26 revenue ₹2,655 cr (Q1 ₹442 cr, Q2 ₹683 cr, Q3 ₹667 cr, Q4 ₹862 cr) incl. ~₹300 cr from US/Canada units (Digicom, August Electronics); (2) **smart metering** via Gridcrest (ex-Iskraemeco India, bought Sep-24) — FY26 revenue ₹971 cr (~25% of sales), a supply-install-maintain (AMISP) model paid only after installation; (3) new **OSAT** (chip packaging, Sanand, Gujarat — inaugurated by the PM) and **bare PCB** (Kaynes Circuits, Chennai) — pre-revenue [Official Q4 transcript; AR FY26 AOC-1].
- Q1 FY27 split: standalone EMS ₹639 cr (+53% from ₹418–480 cr), overseas ₹102 cr (from ₹24 cr, includes August acquisition), metering ₹204 cr (−12% from ₹231 cr) — deliberately cut because metering collections were only ₹88 cr against ~₹240 cr billed [Official Q1 transcript].
- Vertical mix FY26: industrial ~49% (incl. metering), automotive (incl. EV) ~25–28%, aerospace/strategic ~7%, railways ~6%, medical ~2%; exports ₹351 cr (9.7%) vs ₹202 cr (7.4%) in FY25 [IP Q1FY27 slide 20; AR FY26 note 31].
- Order book ₹8,904 cr (Jun-26) vs ₹7,401 cr (Jun-25); "~₹9,000 cr" flat since Dec-25 (₹9,072 cr per CRISIL) [IP Q1FY27 slide 17; CRISIL Mar-26].
- **Customer concentration:** one customer = ₹844 cr, **24% of FY26 revenue** (FY25: 13%) [AR FY26 note 31]. An EV two-wheeler OEM for whom Kaynes was sole supplier cut volumes ~90% in FY26 [Official Q4 transcript].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 673 → Sep-25 906 → Dec-25 804 → Mar-26 1,243 → **Jun-26 946 (+40% YoY)**. Operating profit 113 → 148 → 119 → 194 → **148 (+31%)**; OPM 17% → 16% → 15% → 16% → **16%**. Net profit 75 → 121 → 77 → 91 → **56 (−25%)** [Screener].
- Why profit fell while revenue rose: other income down (₹27 cr → ₹14 cr as QIP cash was spent), depreciation up (₹16 cr → ₹37 cr; Q4 included ~₹32 cr amortisation of acquired order-book intangibles), interest ₹28 cr → ₹37 cr, tax rate 22% → 36% (loss-making new units, capitalised inter-company interest, amortisation) [Screener; Official Q1 transcript].
- Annual: FY24 revenue ₹1,805 cr, OPM 14%, PAT ₹183 cr; FY25 ₹2,722 cr, 15%, ₹293 cr; **FY26 ₹3,626 cr (+33%), 16%, ₹364 cr (+24%)**; TTM PAT ₹346 cr [Screener]. Company FY26 EBITDA ₹574 cr (15.8%) [IP Q1FY27 slide 22].
- Rolling-12-month ROCE 15.8% → **11.6%**; asset turnover 3.8x → 2.0x; net debt/equity 0.1 → 0.2 [IP Q1FY27 slide 18].
- Unit economics: FX hit ~3.3% of input cost YoY; component prices +30–35% (PCB ~3x, other parts +10–12%, lead times 6–8 months) — pass-through lags a quarter [Official Q1 transcript; IP Q1FY27 slide 4].

## Guidance history
- **FY26:** started at ₹4,500 cr revenue (~55% growth), cut to ₹4,000 cr at Q3, delivered ₹3,626 cr (**missed by ~20%**); OCF was guided "neutral or marginally negative", came in at **−₹600 cr**. Management held the guidance on TV 75 days into Q4 [Official Q4 transcript; CRISIL May-26].
- **FY27 (Q4 call, 14-May-26):** no rupee figure — "2x the market growth" (market 15–18%); metering receivables to fall 70–80% by end-Q3 and the metering unit to turn cash-positive by year-end; OSAT Unit 2 commercial by "Q2"; PCB ₹300–400 cr and OSAT ₹250–300 cr revenue in FY27; rail +20–25% at >30% margin; NPI/value-added to ~30% of revenue "in coming years" [Official Q4 transcript].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (8-Aug-26):** revenue +40% (EMS +48%) vs market +17% — **meets "2x"**; but metering receivables **rose** from ₹1,158 cr to ₹1,311 cr (total receivables ₹1,765 cr → ₹1,925 cr), Q1 OCF about **−₹259 cr** (inventory +₹177 cr), net debt ~₹800 cr; OSAT/PCB **slipped to Q3 FY27** ("minor timing slippage"), combined FY27 target restated as ₹450–500 cr; the metering "derisking/divestment" plan pushed to the Q3/Feb-27 call. ~₹200 cr metering cash came in first week of July [Official Q1 transcript].
- Verdict: revenue promise kept; **cash and timing promises missed or deferred** again.

## Capex and capacity
- OSAT + PCB spent ~₹1,200–1,250 cr so far (OSAT ~₹700 cr, PCB ~₹500 cr; FY26: ₹473 cr OSAT, ₹324 cr PCB) plus ~₹250 cr of equipment in transit [Official Q1 transcript].
- FY27 capex plan: ~₹300 cr OSAT + ₹300 cr PCB + ₹250 cr EMS = ~₹850 cr; Q1 spent ~₹230 cr (cash-flow fixed assets ₹360 cr) [Official Q1 transcript].
- CRISIL: ~₹4,700 cr capex planned in HDPCB and OSAT over three fiscals, **~55% from government grants**, 25–30% internal [CRISIL Mar-26]. OSAT subsidy received ₹170 cr to Jul-26 [Official Q1 transcript].
- Capital commitments jumped to **₹810 cr** (Mar-25: ₹29 cr); **corporate guarantees to subsidiaries ₹2,390 cr** (Mar-25: ₹211 cr) [AR FY26 note 29].
- OSAT revenue visibility claimed ₹2,500 cr over 5 years; Mitsui partnership for Japan; PCB capacity "requested" in full by one global customer (vendor code issued); talks with "one of the largest EV manufacturers globally" [Official Q4 and Q1 transcripts].
- Subsidiary balance sheets at Mar-26: Kaynes Semicon assets ₹615 cr vs liabilities ₹626 cr; Kaynes Circuits ₹359 cr vs ₹362 cr — both pre-revenue and financed largely by liabilities [AR FY26 AOC-1].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash flow: CFO +₹88 cr (FY24) → −₹82 cr (FY25) → **−₹600 cr (FY26)**; FCF −₹295 cr → −₹1,031 cr → **−₹1,841 cr** [Screener]. Standalone EMS OCF ~₹250 cr positive in FY26 (₹65 cr in FY25) per management [Official Q4 transcript].
- Borrowings ₹903 cr (Mar-25) → ₹913 cr (Mar-26); ~₹800 cr at Jun-26 per management (not reconciled on the call) [Screener; Official Q1 transcript].
- Screener FY26: debtor days **154** (77 in FY25), inventory 169, payables 131, cash conversion **193 days**; company 12-month rolling NWC 122 → **163 days**; core EMS alone ~53–54 days [Screener; IP Q1FY27; Official Q4 transcript].
- Metering receivables ₹521 cr → ₹1,365 cr in FY26 (~₹250 cr non-current), ₹1,311 cr at Jun-26; only ~₹40 cr securitised per quarter [Official Q4/Q1 transcripts].
- **Bills discounted with banks ₹455 cr** (Mar-25: ₹115 cr) — receivables already turned into cash but still a contingent liability [AR FY26 note 29].
- No dividend; growth funded by equity — ₹1,600 cr QIP in Jun-25 [Screener; CRISIL Mar-26].

## Moat evidence
- Long record (since 1988) with defence/space/rail approvals; Kavach rail product has initial approvals; ISRO/DRDO titanium gas-bottle work via Cryo-Precision/Aerocaliph (76% owned) [Official Q1/Q4 transcripts; AR FY26].
- 72 new customers in Q1 FY27; serial supplies started to India's No. 2 EV two-wheeler maker after an 8-month development [Official Q1 transcript].
- Backward integration (OSAT, PCB) could raise switching costs if it works; today it is capital tied up, not earnings [AR FY26 AOC-1].
- Weak points: margin (~16%) is good for Indian EMS but has not translated into cash; CRISIL notes 20–30% of materials come from China [CRISIL Mar-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Governance/disclosure:** Dec-25 Kotak report found FY25 related-party items missing from standalone accounts — ₹180 cr purchases from Kaynes Electronics Manufacturing, ~₹320 cr and ₹180 cr payables and ₹190 cr receivables — plus contingent liabilities of ₹520 cr (18% of net worth), ₹180 cr of capitalised technical know-how and a 17.7% average borrowing cost; Kaynes said the items were eliminated on consolidation and "inadvertently not disclosed", since rectified. Stock fell 12.7% on 5-Dec-25; CRISIL cited "accounting and reporting lapses" [News: Business Today, 5-Dec-25; CRISIL Mar-26].
- **Auditor:** K.P. Rao & Co (Mysuru) signed an unmodified FY26 opinion, but **8 overseas subsidiaries' accounts were not audited** and 3 subsidiaries were unaudited (assets ₹31 cr); KAMs were revenue recognition and IT controls in a heavily customised ERP. **Walker Chandiok proposed as new auditor** from the Sep-26 AGM [AR FY26 auditor's report; Board's report].
- Management churn: CFO absent from the Q4 call (health); CEO Rajesh Sharma left 8-Nov-25; an independent director left 10-Nov-25; company secretary changed Mar-26; new MD from 24-Sep-25 [Official Q4 transcript; AR FY26 note 30].
- Metering model: receivables ~₹1,300 cr against ~₹970 cr annual metering sales; performance bank guarantees ₹191 cr (Mar-25 ₹104 cr); an NCLT claim of ₹6 cr against Gridcrest [AR FY26 note 29; Official Q1 transcript].
- Large contingent exposures: corporate guarantees ₹2,390 cr, bills discounted ₹455 cr, LCs ₹69 cr, customs bonds ₹103 cr [AR FY26 note 29].
- Execution: OSAT and PCB already slipped from Q2 to Q3 FY27; new-unit losses and depreciation will weigh on profit before revenue arrives [Official Q1 transcript].
- Related-party dealings outside the group are small (services to Kaynes Technology Inc./Kemsys Inc. ~₹5 cr) [AR FY26 note 30].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was defensive and apologetic about a 20% revenue miss and −₹600 cr OCF; Q1 was "earn back trust" — focus on speed, balance sheet and a deliberate cut in metering.
- Metering receivables promise (reverse "this quarter") failed: they rose ₹153 cr; the plan to derisk/divest was deferred to Q3.
- New: global component shortage (PCB prices ~3x), inventory build (+₹177 cr), Mitsui OSAT tie-up, space satellites (launch mid-2027), rolling-12-month disclosure of working capital.
- OSAT/PCB timeline slipped a quarter; FY27 target narrowed to ₹450–500 cr combined.

## My read
Core EMS is growing well (+48–53% in Q1) at ~16% operating margin, and the order book (~₹8,900 cr) is solid. But the group keeps missing on cash: FY26 OCF −₹600 cr, FCF −₹1,841 cr, metering receivables still rising, and ₹2,390 cr of guarantees behind pre-revenue OSAT/PCB units. Reported profit is falling (Q1 PAT −25%) as depreciation, interest and tax catch up. The price (~70x trailing earnings despite a 53% fall) still assumes OSAT/PCB work and metering cash comes back; watch metering receivables in Q2/Q3, first OSAT/PCB revenue in Q3, and the first audit by Walker Chandiok. A clean new-auditor year and positive group OCF would change the view; another receivable increase would not.
