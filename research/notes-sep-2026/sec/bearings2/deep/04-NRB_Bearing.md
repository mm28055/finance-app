# NRB Bearings — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Screener code NRBBEARING (consolidated). Price ₹535, market cap ₹5,183 cr. The stock is up 91% in a year and near its 52-week high of ₹549 [Screener].

## Business and segments
- The company is India's leader in needle and cylindrical roller bearings: about 65%+ share in needle and 75%+ in CRB by volume, per the company's own deck. CRISIL puts it at 65–70% share. It has 7 plants in India and Thailand and over 3,000 product designs. About 90% of Indian vehicles use NRB parts [IP Q2FY26; CRISIL Jul-26].
- Revenue mix: domestic OEMs about 65–70%, aftermarket about 10–12%, exports 20–25%. No single customer (with its tier-1) exceeds 7% of revenue [AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Jul-26].
- Segments by end-use (Q1 FY27 call): industrial 14% of revenue, up from 11%, growing 34% YoY. Two/three-wheelers about 30%, commercial vehicles 27–30%, cars about 12% (mostly via tier-1s). About 70% of revenue is in applications common to ICE, hybrid and EV, such as transmissions, chassis and steering, so the company calls itself "EV-agnostic" [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Subsidiaries (FY26): Europe GmbH revenue ₹170 cr, PAT ₹3.2 cr; Thailand ₹149 cr, PAT ₹7.8 cr; USA ₹59 cr, PAT ₹6.3 cr; SNL Bearings (73.45% owned) ₹56.6 cr, PAT ₹10.9 cr; NRB Holdings (Dubai) ₹13 cr, loss ₹0.3 cr; Mahant Tool Room Pvt Ltd loss ₹0.1 cr [AR FY26 Board's report].
- Standalone FY26: revenue ₹1,185.7 cr (+10%). Domestic sales ₹823.6 cr (+6.2%). Exports ₹253.9 cr, up only **2.7%** [AR FY26 MD&A].
- New verticals: aerospace (Mahant Tool Room business, bought Jan 2026, all HAL orders); an industrial-CRB joint venture with Italy's Unitec/Mondial (75% NRB); US production at Columbia, SC (first win: GM Corvette planet pins); plus robotics, data centres and off-highway, with JCB UK a new customer [AR FY26; Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Revenue target.** On the Q4 call: ₹2,500 cr by FY31, "aspirational… increasingly concrete". In Q1: "if you compound the last 12 months' growth of 14.38% you get ₹2,730 cr", with ₹3,000 cr as the "aspirational vision". **Raised, but only by extrapolation.** No FY27 number was given [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Margins.** On the Q4 call: an aspiration of "18% to 21%", with 18–20% required for any new area. In Q1: "our margins are between 18% to 20%… over an annual period". **Slightly softened** [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Exports.** On the Q4 call, international growth was 4% in FY26 and 10–14% (maybe 15%) was expected in FY27. No Q1 update was given [Q4 call].
- **Capex.** On the Q4 call: about ₹120 cr in FY27 within a ₹240 cr announcement (₹200 cr brownfield over 18 months plus up to ₹40 cr land). In Q1: the programme is ₹270 cr, with ₹60 cr spent and ₹100 cr ordered. The rule of thumb is ₹100 cr of capex giving ₹130 cr of sales. CRISIL says about ₹400 cr over three fiscals, with ₹150 cr of debt. **The capex budget keeps growing** [Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Jul-26].
- **Unitec JV.** ₹110 cr investment for ₹130 cr of capacity revenue, commissioning April 2027. The site moved from Hyderabad to Aurangabad (Shendra), where a partly built facility was bought. **Timeline kept; location changed** [Q1 call; AR FY26: up to ₹67 cr equity plus debt from NRB].
- **Mahant order book.** On the Q4 call: "close to ₹50 crores" (after ₹25 cr at acquisition). In Q1: Mahant about ₹30 cr, plus ₹20 cr of NRB's own non-aerospace defence business, about ₹50 cr in total. **Restated lower for Mahant alone.** The target is ₹300 cr revenue and ₹90 cr profit in aerospace by 2031 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Lifetime nominations.** This was ₹800 cr (earlier). On the Q1 call, the MD first said the Corvette win "has taken the ₹800 crores to ₹1,100 crores". At the end of the call she **corrected herself**: she had meant ₹800 cr plus a US$300,000 US programme. She said only that ₹800 cr "has moved ahead", with no new figure [Q1 call, official].

## Capex and capacity
- Reported utilisation is about 85%, but management says effective utilisation is "close to full". Brownfield machines are arriving, with commissioning from Jun–Jul 2026 through Q1 2028. The expansion adds 15–25% capacity across TRB, ball, wide-inner-ring, spherical, CRB, needle and thrust bearings [Q4 call; AR FY26].
- Investments: Mahant Tool Room up to ₹37.5 cr, Unitec JV up to ₹67 cr, land up to ₹40 cr. Maintenance and new-product capex is about 10% of turnover each year on top [AR FY26; Q4 call].
- Open capital commitments ₹21.6 cr (standalone, Mar-26) [AR FY26 Note 48].
- CRISIL expects gearing to rise from 0.16× to about 0.3× on about ₹150 cr of new debt [CRISIL Jul-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Consolidated debt ₹154 cr (Mar-26, down from ₹187 cr). Unencumbered cash ₹77 cr. Gearing 0.16×, interest cover 25.3×. Working-capital lines about 9% used [CRISIL Jul-26; Screener].
- **Inventory is the structural weakness.** Screener inventory days are 302 on a cost basis (FY24: 364), CRISIL says 130–170 days on a sales basis, and the cash conversion cycle is 281 days. Year-end inventory fell ₹20 cr+ in FY26 [Screener; CRISIL; Q4 call].
- **The auditor's key audit matter is inventory.** Standalone inventory of ₹271.9 cr is 24% of total assets, with a ₹81.4 cr allowance for slow-moving and obsolete stock. Work-in-progress and finished goods are valued "in excel spreadsheets" across many locations. The opinion is unqualified [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Operating cash flow was ₹241 cr in FY26 (126% of operating profit) against ₹84 cr in FY25. Free cash flow was ₹136 cr [Screener].
- Dividend: two interims of ₹2.50 and ₹3.20 for FY26, ₹5.70/share (39% payout) [AR FY26; Screener].
- Corporate guarantees for subsidiaries total about ₹203 cr (Thailand ₹112 cr, Europe ₹43.6 cr, USA ₹47.3 cr). There is also a USD 6 m loan to NRB Holdings [AR FY26 Board's report].

## Moat evidence
- It supplies transmission bearings to every BMW (1 to 7 Series) and every Mercedes (A-Class to Maybach). It is designed into a new BMW "i" e-steering via Magna for a 2027 launch. It holds a patented kingpin bearing for Mercedes, Mack, Freightliner, Scania and Volvo trucks [Q4 call].
- Share of business is 60–70% on its core products and 100% on some. CRISIL confirms market leadership [Q4 call; CRISIL Jul-26].
- A Make-in-USA plant won a Corvette programme open only to US bearing makers [Q1 call].
- A Sukhoi-30 plain spherical bearing order. Management says "not even five" companies globally can make it [Q1 call].
- Pricing: EV-specific bearings are priced to keep the margin profile, and the company avoids selling commodity bearings at a loss to OEMs [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter pledge and selling.** In FY25, TSS Trust 1 borrowed against pledged promoter shares to fund the family settlement with the NIBL branch. **77.7% of the promoter stake was pledged at Mar-26, cut to 14.99% by Jun-26** after the promoters sold **6.47%** of the company in Q1 FY27. Promoter holding fell from 51.20% to 44.73% [CRISIL Jul-26; Screener shareholding].
- More selling in Sept 2026: the MD sold 2,47,630 shares on 18 and 21 Sep and 18,450 on 23 Sep. She holds 38.81% directly plus 2.37% still pledged to Tata Capital (down from 3.10%) [BSE SAST filings 22 and 25 Sep 2026].
- BSE asked the company to explain a volume spike on 21 Sep 2026. The company cited normal market forces [BSE clarification 21 Sep 2026].
- **Inventory risk:** ₹81 cr of obsolescence allowance against ₹272 cr of net standalone stock. Valuation is spreadsheet-based [AR FY26 KAM].
- **Standalone exports grew only 2.7% in FY26**, and consolidated international growth was about 4%, against the 10–14% FY27 target [AR FY26; Q4 call].
- The MD&A contradicts itself on economic value added (EVA, profit left after charging for all the capital used). The text says "EVA is negative in the current year resulting from the decline in operating profits". The table, garbled in extraction, appears to show FY26 EVA positive and FY25 negative, and operating profit actually rose. This is probably wording carried over from last year, a small disclosure-quality point [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Contingent liabilities (standalone): income tax ₹49.8 cr (₹12.4 cr paid under protest) and sales tax/VAT ₹1.8 cr. The PF "basic wages" impact is not ascertainable [AR FY26 Note 48].
- Disclosure changes: management declines to split exports, aftermarket or capex by entity ("watched by our competitors"). The nomination figure was misstated and then corrected on the call [Q4, Q1 calls].
- Execution load is heavy: six "vectors", a JV relocation, US ramp-up, aerospace certification (AS9100 pending, "next six months" as of May) and ₹270–400 cr of capex, all with a single MD-led management voice [Q4, Q1 calls; CRISIL].

## What changed between the two calls
- The tone moved from "quality of FY26 growth, inventory defended" (Q4) to "six-vector transformation, ₹2,730–3,000 cr" (Q1).
- New in Q1: GM Corvette/US plant, JCB UK, the JV move to Aurangabad, data centres and humanoids (R&D stage), and a ₹10 cr cost spike.
- The Mahant order book was restated, the ₹1,100 cr nomination figure was retracted, and the margin band narrowed from 18–21% to 18–20%.
- An analyst noted "the pledging has finally been reduced" and referred to the "transition in the ownership control" [Q1 call].

- The profile calls governance "clean". It misses the 77.7% promoter pledge (Mar-26), the 6.47% promoter sale in Q1 FY27 and the September 2026 sales.

## My read
The operating business is in its best shape in years. Revenue is growing 13–19%, margins are at 17–18% on Screener's basis, debt is down, and there are real wins: BMW/Magna steering, the Corvette in the US, HAL/Sukhoi, JCB. Capex of ₹270–400 cr plus the Unitec JV should support 15%+ growth into FY28. The governance picture is weaker than the stored note says. The family settlement was funded with pledged promoter shares. Promoters sold 6.47% in Q1 FY27 and more in September, and they now hold under 45%. Inventory (with an ₹81 cr obsolescence allowance) and flat standalone exports remain soft spots. After a 91% rally, the price needs about 15% compounding and margins held near 18%. I would turn more cautious if promoter selling continues after the pledge is cleared, or if Q2 margins slip below 16% on costs.
