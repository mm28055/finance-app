# Harsha Engineers International — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Screener code HARSHA (consolidated). Price ₹462, market cap ₹4,207 cr. Promoters hold 75.0%, FIIs 1.8% [Screener].

**Important on "revenue":** the company reports two businesses. **Engineering** (bearing cages, bushings, stampings, castings) was ₹421 cr in Q1 FY27. **Solar EPC** (a business that designs and installs solar plants) was ₹36 cr. Screener's sales figure is the total, ₹457 cr. The dashboard stores the Engineering figure only [IP Q1FY27 p.9].

## Business and segments
- It is the largest maker of precision bearing cages in India, the part that holds the rolling elements in a bearing. It has over 50% domestic share and about 6.5% of the organised global cage market (a CY2021 estimate). It makes cages in brass, steel and polyamide from 20 mm to 2,000 mm across 7,000+ product codes [IP Q1FY27 p.12; CARE Apr-26].
- Plants: Changodar and Moraiya (Ahmedabad), Harsha Advantek at Bhayla (a new greenfield plant; commercial production from 26 Jun 2025), Changshu (China) and Brasov (Romania) [CARE Apr-26].
- Customers: Timken, Schaeffler and SKF together are about 60–75% of standalone sales. The top 10 are about 80% of revenue, across 80+ customer plants. Wallet share is 80–90% in India but only 10–20% at the big three overseas and 2–3% at Japanese bearing makers [CARE Apr-26; Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Geography (FY26): 58% of revenue is from outside India. Europe is about 30% (including Romanian castings), China a little over 10%, the US 6–7% and Japan about 2% [Q4 call].
- FY26 product lines: bushings ₹127 cr (+25%), stampings about ₹60 cr, large-size cages about ₹49 cr (+14%), Japanese customers about ₹72–73 cr (+12%). Engineering consolidated was ₹1,444 cr (+13.8% from ₹1,269 cr) and Solar EPC ₹183 cr [Q4 call].
- Subsidiaries (FY26): Romania revenue about ₹247 cr, **loss about ₹14 cr**. China about ₹120 cr, PAT about ₹5 cr. Combined foreign loss ₹9 cr (the Q1 deck says "around ₹10 cr"). Advantek revenue ₹43 cr, loss ₹11.4 cr [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].

  Jun-25 365 / 55 (15%) / 38 · Sep-25 378 / 54 (14%) / 36 · Dec-25 409 / 57 (14%) / 34 · Mar-26 474 / 73 (15%) / 47 · Jun-26 457 / 67 (15%) / 37 [Screener].
- Company "Engineering EBITDA" (defined as PBT plus depreciation plus finance cost, so it includes other income): Q1 FY26 ₹65.3 cr (18.7%), Q4 FY26 ₹77.1 cr (20.2%), Q1 FY27 ₹69.8 cr (16.6%) [IP Q1FY27 p.9].
- Q1 FY27: total revenue +25.2% YoY. Engineering +20.7%, India Engineering +21% (and +6% QoQ). Engineering EBITDA margin fell 210 bps YoY and 360 bps QoQ. Management gave three causes: raw materials up 6–8% not yet passed through, a ₹4 cr forex loss as cash-flow hedges were settled, and ₹3 cr of extra indirect material costs. PAT was ₹37 cr, −1.5% YoY [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Annual: FY24 sales 1,392 / OP 171 (12%) / PAT 111 · FY25 1,408 / 175 (12%) / 89 (after a ₹28 cr goodwill impairment on Romania) · FY26 1,627 / 240 (15%) / 155 [Screener; CARE].
- ROCE is about 13% and ROE about 11%. Returns are held down by Romanian losses, the Advantek ramp-up and idle cash [Screener].
- Unit economics: raw materials are passed through with about a 4-month lag. A 6–7% raw-material rise means about a 3–4% price rise [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- **Revenue.** On the Q4 call (May): FY27 "overall double-digit", India Engineering "mid-teens", minimum 15% a year for India Engineering in normal conditions. In Q1 (Aug): India "higher-teens" (mid-to-high teens in Q&A), consolidated "low-to-mid teens" or 12–15%, and 20% "a very tough stretch". **Kept, with India nudged up.** Q1 delivered +21% India and +20.7% Engineering [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Margins.** On the Q4 call: "at least maintain" the current margin, with +100–200 bps at the consolidated level over 2–3 years. In Q1: the aim is only to get back to last year's 18–18.7% "provided metal prices stabilise". Standalone Engineering sustainable margin is 20–22% (FY26 was about 24%). **The expansion talk was dropped; the target was cut to recovery** [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Advantek (Bhayla).** On the Q4 call: sales at least 3× in FY27, current capacity able to deliver ₹250–300 cr of revenue. In Q1: ₹140 cr+ target for FY27 and PAT-positive by the end of FY27; Q1 sales were ₹30 cr with a ₹4.3 cr loss and 9% EBITDA; ₹300–400 cr expected by year 3 across both phases. **On track so far, but back-ended.**
- **Foreign subsidiaries.** On the Q4 call: combined loss of ₹9 cr in FY26, to improve. In Q1: combined loss guided at ₹2–4 cr for FY27, **but Q1 alone lost about ₹3.3 cr**. **At risk.**
- **Product lines.** Bushings: Q4 +25–30%; Q1 +34.7% YoY, "over 30%" for the year. **Beating.** Stampings: Q4 "at least 20%"; Q1 +31% at ₹19 cr, target 30%. **Beating.** Large-size cages: Q4 "15–20%"; Q1 flat at ₹10 cr with a new target of 30–50%. **Missed in Q1, target raised anyway.** Japan: Q1 ₹21 cr (+25%) with +10% guided for the year.
- **Solar.** On the Q4 call: more than 25% growth and EBITDA held. In Q1: about ₹200 cr at 7–8% EBITDA [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Capex.** On the Q4 call: FY27 maintenance ₹30–40 cr plus China about ₹70 cr (₹20 cr in FY28), with Advantek Phase 2 still to be set. In Q1: ₹180–200 cr over FY27–FY28, of which ₹50–80 cr in FY27. CARE puts FY26–FY28 capex at about ₹435 cr including about ₹130 cr routine [Q4 call; Q1 call; CARE Apr-26].

## Capex and capacity
- **Advantek (Bhayla):** outlay ₹300–350 cr, funded by about ₹250 cr of term loans with Gujarat state interest subsidy. Phase 2 (bushings, stampings, large cages) has been approved; civil work was delayed by rain, and a third building is due by end-FY27 [CARE Apr-26; Q1 call].
- **China brownfield (steel cages):** ₹90–100 cr (about US$10 m) funded by local debt. The timeline moved from "FY28" and "H2 FY28" to **Q3 FY28**, with full benefit from FY29 [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- FY26 capex was ₹120 cr (FY25 about ₹210 cr). Q1 FY27 capex was ₹37 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Management says existing assets could support about ₹2,400–2,500 cr of India revenue and about ₹2,700–3,000 cr consolidated [Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Consolidated borrowings rose from ₹200 cr to ₹372 cr at Mar-26 (Advantek loans). Standalone cash and liquid investments were about ₹475 cr at Mar-26 (₹368 cr at Mar-25), so the group is roughly net cash. Working-capital lines are about 10% used [Screener; CARE Apr-26].
- **Cash conversion weakened.** FY26 operating cash flow was ₹68 cr, 54% of operating profit, against ₹206 cr in FY25. Free cash flow was −₹47 cr [Screener].
- The working-capital cycle was 130 days at Mar-26 and 116 days at Jun-26. Q1 FY27 receivables were 74 days, inventory about 86 days in total, payables 42 days [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.10].
- Dividend payout is about 9% [Screener]. IPO proceeds are fully used [AR FY26].
- Rating: CARE AA−/Stable/A1+, reaffirmed 27 Apr 2026, with limits raised to ₹493 cr. The downgrade trigger is a PBILDT margin (CARE's term for operating margin) below 12% or an operating cycle above 150 days [CARE Apr-26].

## Moat evidence
- It is the only outsourced supplier with all three cage materials at scale. Wallet share at Indian plants is 80–90%, and an analyst said a customer plant quoted 95–97% [Q1 call].
- It has long-term contracts with some global majors. Cages are about 5% of bearing cost but carry long lead times and tooling, so customers "prefer continuing with reliable vendors" [IP Q1FY27; CARE].
- Pass-through clauses protect absolute profit over time, but CARE notes "limited bargaining power" against three giant customers [CARE Apr-26].
- India exports from HEIL were ₹139 cr in Q1 FY27 (+22% YoY). The US additional tariff on cages has fallen to zero [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration:** the top 3 are 60–75% of standalone sales, and the top 10 about 80% [CARE; Q1 call].
- **Romania:** revenue about ₹247 cr, loss about ₹14 cr in FY26. A ₹28 cr goodwill impairment was taken in FY25 (₹95 cr of standalone investment impaired). A new CEO is in place, with a plan to lift the cage share from about 22% to 30–35%, but the main semi-finished-casting customer's offtake is still weak [CARE; Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Guidance slippage:** the margin-expansion talk was dropped within one quarter, and the foreign-subsidiary loss in Q1 alone (₹3.3 cr) nearly equals the full-year guide (₹2–4 cr) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Cash:** operating cash flow of ₹68 cr in FY26 and negative free cash flow while capex continues [Screener].
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion, but with an **audit-trail exception**: the edit log is "not enabled at the database level" for the accounting software [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Contingent (standalone):** letters of credit, corporate guarantees and SBLCs of ₹377 cr (up from ₹246 cr), mainly for subsidiary borrowings. EPCG export obligations ₹34 cr. Tax claims are small [AR FY26 contingent note].
- **Management exit:** the Head of the Brass SBU resigned on 21 Aug 2026 [BSE filing 20 Aug 2026].
- **Disclosure gap:** Romania and China are reported only as a combined figure each quarter [Q1 call].
- Solar EPC is low-margin (7–8%), lumpy and competitive, and CARE calls it a constraint [CARE; Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- The tone shifted from "margins up 100–200 bps over 2–3 years, US tariffs gone" (Q4) to "raw material +8%, hedge loss, indirect costs; hope to return to 18%" (Q1).
- The growth guide was raised for India but held at low-to-mid teens overall.
- China commissioning was pinned to Q3 FY28, Advantek Phase 2 was confirmed, and a Romania CEO change was announced.
- The large-cage slip in Q1 came from capacity ramp-up problems at Advantek.

## My read
The Indian cage franchise is strong: 80–90% wallet share, India Engineering growing about 20%, and bushings and stampings growing 30%+. Harsha is one of the clearest beneficiaries of bearing MNCs expanding in India. Consolidated returns are still diluted by three things: Romania (about ₹14 cr loss on about ₹247 cr revenue), the Advantek ramp-up (₹4 cr loss in Q1) and weak cash conversion (FY26 operating cash flow only ₹68 cr). At about 27× earnings, the price needs about 12–15% compounding. That looks achievable if Advantek turns PAT-positive and margins return to about 18% as pass-through catches up in H2 FY27. I would turn more positive on a clean Q2–Q3 with Engineering EBITDA of 18%+ and foreign losses under ₹1 cr a quarter. I would turn more negative if Romania needs another impairment or operating cash flow stays under 60% of operating profit.
