# Aegis Logistics — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Price ₹1,365, market cap ₹47,926 cr, P/E 38.3 on TTM EPS ₹35.65, ROCE 13.3%, ROE 16.3%, dividend yield 0.64% [Screener, 27-Sep-26].

## Business and segments
- Two divisions. **Gas** (LPG and propane, now ammonia): port terminalling for others ("logistics"), sourcing LPG cargoes for customers (thin trading margin), and its own **distribution** of LPG to industrial, commercial and autogas buyers. **Liquids**: tank storage for chemicals, petroleum products and edible oils at seven ports. [AR FY26 segment note; IP Q1FY27]
- FY26 segment split: Gas revenue ₹7,689 cr (+26%), Gas EBITDA ₹1,127–1,131 cr (+68–69%); Liquids revenue ₹644 cr (flat vs ₹650 cr), Liquids EBITDA ₹472 cr (−5%). EBITDA mix LPG 70% / Liquids 30%. [AR FY26 MD&A p.6; Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27: Gas EBITDA ₹591 cr (+296% YoY, +8% QoQ); Liquids revenue ₹178 cr (+24%), EBITDA ₹136 cr (+28%), fifth straight quarter of Liquids EBITDA growth. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Volumes Q1 FY27: distribution 2.77 lakh t (+91% YoY, +19% QoQ); terminal throughput 1,124 kt (**−3% YoY** from 1,161 kt, and below Q4's 1.23 mt); sourcing 121 kt (+1%). FY26: throughput 5.15 mt (+14%), distribution 7.54 lakh t (+45%), sourcing 6.07 lakh t (+2%). [IP Q1FY27 slide 8, 15]
- Capacity: LPG static ~249,000 t, potential throughput ~18.6 mt/yr, liquids ~2.01 million kl (CARE). Kandla is the largest hub (952,000 kl liquids, 48,000 t LPG); Mumbai 334,000 kl + 21,000 t LPG (running at full use); Mangalore 82,000 t cryogenic LPG + 193,000 kl; Pipavav 70,800 t LPG + 36,000 t ammonia; Haldia 25,000 t LPG (HPCL exclusive to 2038) + 226,890 kl; JNPA 101,900 kl; Kochi 82,545 kl. [CARE Mar-26; Q1 call; AR FY26 p.5–7]
- Downstream network: 140+ autogas stations across ~10 states and 200+ LPG distributors in ~140 cities (brands Aegis Puregas, Magna, Chhota Cikander). Distribution assets are franchise-financed, so little capex or working capital. [CARE Mar-26; Q1 call]
- Structure matters: most new terminals sit in **Aegis Vopak Terminals (AVTL)**, listed June 2025, where outside holders own **55.29%** (Vopak plus public). Aegis builds assets through its 100% EPC arm and sells them to AVTL. FY26 profit to minorities ₹208 cr of ₹1,107 cr. [AR FY26 NCI note; Q4 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly revenue / operating profit / net profit: Jun-25 1,719 / 240 / 175; Sep-25 2,294 / 291 / 244; Dec-25 1,725 / 297 / 233; Mar-26 2,594 / 624 / 455; Jun-26 2,357 / 714 / 545. OPM went from 14% to 30% in four quarters. [Screener]
- Annual: FY24 revenue 7,046, OP 923, NP 672, EPS ₹16.22; FY25 6,764 / 1,098 / 787 / ₹18.90; FY26 8,333 / 1,452 / 1,107 / ₹25.59. TTM NP ₹1,476 cr, EPS ₹35.65. [Screener]
- Company "normalised EBITDA" (before forex/hedging) is higher than Screener's operating profit: Q1 FY27 ₹727 cr vs ₹714 cr; FY26 ₹1,599 cr vs ₹1,452 cr. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Profit to Aegis shareholders is smaller than headline profit: FY26 EPS ₹25.59 × ~35.1 cr shares ≈ ₹898 cr vs ₹1,107 cr reported; Q1 FY27 ≈ ₹484 cr vs ₹545 cr. [Screener; AR FY26]
- Unit economics: distribution margin about ₹7,000/t in FY26 vs ~₹4,000/t up to FY25; terminal throughput earns close to ₹1,000/t EBITDA; sourcing is "a very low contributor" (~$85–90/t revenue-side reference). Q1 FY27 distribution margin was above ₹7,000/t (CFO: Q4 and Q1 were "3x" the ₹5,000 an analyst suggested). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- The FY26–Q1 FY27 jump was driven by the West Asia war: LPG prices moved from ₹54,000–60,000/t to ₹1,50,000/t and back to ₹80,000–90,000/t; India's LPG shortfall was 50% in April and 30% in May 2026. [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **EPS growth:** "25% plus" CAGR, kept on both calls; delivered 32% five-year EPS CAGR. Management calls itself conservative. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Distribution volumes:** Q4 call — 2 million t "by '28", including ammonia. Q1 call — "2 million in probably next year or the year next"; path given as 0.75 mt FY26 → above 1 mt FY27 → ~1.5 mt FY28, growth "closer to 50%". Slight softening of the date (FY28 → FY28/FY29). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Distribution margin:** ₹7,000/t "sustainable" on both calls; FY27 blended "₹7,000 plus" called "almost a certainty"; from FY28 the war premium is meant to be replaced by freight savings (VLGC freight ~$15/t vs ~$50/t on medium carriers, worth ~₹3,000/t — CFO said "please don't take me on that"). [Q1 call]
- **Terminal throughput:** "worst-case" ~25% a year growth. Q1 delivered **−3% YoY**. This is the clearest miss against a stated number. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Ammonia:** Q4 call — ~25% utilisation in year one, then +30–40% a year; distribution ~200,000 t to begin, +20–30% a year; throughput margin ₹2,500–3,000/t, distribution "up to ₹5,000/t". Q1 call — declined to give margins: "wait for a quarter". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Project dates vs Q4 promises:** ammonia terminal (H1 FY27) — **met**, commissioned 10 Aug 2026. Mumbai 64,000 kl (H1 FY27) — on schedule. JNPA liquids phase 1 (~100,000 kl) — Q4 call and AR said H1 FY27; Q1 call says **Q3 FY27 (slipped one quarter)**. Kandla–Gorakhpur pipeline hook-up — Q4 said Q2 FY27; Q1 said H1 at Kandla but "during this year" at Pipavav (soft slip). Kochi 60,000 kl "evaluating" → approved at 49,577 kl for early FY28. JNPA extra LPG tank "36,000–50,000 t evaluating" → approved at 52,000 t. [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 p.7; BTA filing 24-Aug-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Cumulative capex ~$1.2 bn by March 2027; up to ~₹5,000 cr more in FY28; pipeline ~$5 bn by December 2030, back-ended into FY29–FY31, organic and inorganic. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- FY26 capex ₹964 cr (Gas ₹739 cr, Liquids ₹220 cr) vs ₹1,732 cr in FY25; capital commitments ₹1,379 cr at March 2026. [AR FY26 segment note, contingent-liabilities note]
- Big projects: JNPA 318,100 kl liquids + 77,236 t LPG + 35,000 t/yr bottling, ₹1,675 cr; new 52,000 t refrigerated LPG tank at JNPA (EPC framework ₹142.5 cr advance from AVTL); Kandla CRL-4 94,148 kl (FY28); Kochi 49,577 kl (₹37.2 cr advance); Mangalore rail gantry + bottling ₹52.5 cr; Mumbai 64,000 kl ₹125 cr; Pipavav liquid rail gantry with a 15-year take-or-pay above 0.5 mt/yr. [Q1 call; JNPA and Kochi framework filings Aug-26]
- Ammonia terminal: 36,000 t, cost ₹525 cr, sold by Aegis to its step-down subsidiary Aegis Terminal (Pipavav) for ₹525 cr on 24 Aug 2026; one-third under 15-year take-or-pay with Hindustan Zinc; ITOCHU owns 10% of that subsidiary and intends 25% within three years. Further terminals with L&T (non-binding MoU) would cost multiples of ₹525 cr. [BTA filing; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Vadhavan port: non-binding MoU, ~₹20,000 cr potential; only part included in the $5 bn. [Q4 call]
- Returns: ROCE has drifted down from 20–24% (FY15–FY19) to 13% (FY25–FY26) as capital piled into terminals ahead of volume. [Screener ratios]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- March 2026: borrowings ₹4,150 cr vs cash and investments ₹5,939 cr → net cash ~₹1,790 cr. Borrowings were ₹4,606 cr in FY25. Company became net-debt-negative in December 2025 after prepaying term loans with AVTL IPO money; raised ₹1,690 cr via two NCD issues in FY26. [Screener; IP Q1FY27; CARE Mar-26]
- The cash is spread across entities (standalone ~₹2,700 cr; Sea Lord ₹410 cr; Aegis Gas ₹840 cr; Aegis International ₹306 cr), and AVTL funds its own capex through equity dilution. [Q1 call, analyst question]
- FY26 cash from operations ₹2,028 cr (CFO/OP 167%) and free cash flow ₹1,212 cr, the best in the company's history; FY25 FCF was −₹386 cr. Working-capital days −113 in FY26 (helped by customer advances/payables and minority capital). Debtor days 21. [Screener]
- Target gearing ~0.6x. Dividend FY26 ₹8.70/share (payout 34%). [Q4 call; Screener]
- **New:** board meets **28 Sep 2026** to consider raising funds by equity, QIP, FCCB, ADR/GDR or debt. With ~₹5,900 cr on hand, this signals the $5 bn plan needs outside money and may dilute holders. [Company filing 23-Sep-26; News: Whalesbook, Sep-26]
- Ratings: CARE AA/Stable reaffirmed March 2026; India Ratings IND AA/Positive affirmed September 2026. [CARE Mar-26; Company filing 18-Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- Largest independent LPG terminal operator in India; claims to be the only third-party terminal with rail connectivity for LPG. Scarce waterfront land at seven ports. [IP Q1FY27 slide 20]
- Long contracts: HPCL exclusive terminalling at Haldia to 2038; Hindustan Zinc 15-year take-or-pay (ammonia); 15-year take-or-pay >0.5 mt/yr on Pipavav rail gantry. [Q1 call]
- Large cryogenic tanks (Mangalore 82,000 t, Pipavav 48,000 t, Kandla) can unload a whole VLGC in one go, cutting demurrage and freight; this matters more as US cargoes on very large carriers rise (India's US LPG imports near 1 mt). [Q1 call]
- Distribution volume nearly doubled when rivals struggled for supply; management credits integration (sourcing JV with ITOCHU in Singapore, storage, rail/pipeline, retail). [Q1 call]
- Pricing: storage contracts are largely fixed or index-linked (CARE). Liquids EBITDA margin ~62% vs ~10% for Gas (FY25). [CARE Mar-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin reversion:** the ₹7,000/t distribution margin and 30% OPM are war-inflated. If LPG prices normalise before freight savings arrive, FY28 profit could fall back. The "procurement efficiency" offset is an estimate, not contracted. [Q1 call]
- **Throughput stalled:** Q1 logistics volume fell 3% YoY despite new Mangalore and Pipavav capacity. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Minority leakage and related-party transfers:** 55.29% of AVTL belongs to others; Aegis's own EPC arm builds and sells assets to AVTL and its subsidiaries (ammonia terminal ₹525 cr; JNPA tank ₹142.5 cr; Kochi ₹37.2 cr) at prices set within the group and called arm's length. Aegis also gave ITOCHU a ₹80.3 cr corporate guarantee for AVTL's obligations. [BTA and framework filings; guarantee filing 7-Apr-26]
- **Related parties:** CMD Raj Chandaria's pay rose to ₹10 cr (₹6.6 cr in FY25). Promoter entities Trans Asia Petroleum and Huron received dividends of ~₹55.6 cr and ~₹66.7 cr (final) plus interim. Vopak (holder in AVTL) received ₹27.9 cr interest; closing payable to related parties ~₹413 cr. [AR FY26 note 40 — column layout in the PDF is hard to read; treat Vopak line items as indicative]
- **Contingent liabilities (March 2026):** GST/sales-tax demands ₹98 cr (up from ₹77 cr); air-pollution matter before the Supreme Court ₹142 cr; electricity dispute ₹0.8 cr. New GST penalties: ₹2.9 cr (Jan 2026, Gujarat) and ₹0.12 cr (Aug 2026, Maharashtra), both on input-tax-credit disallowance, under appeal. [AR FY26 contingent-liabilities note; filings 5-Jan-26, 21-Aug-26]
- **Customer concentration:** one Gas customer was 22% of revenue in FY26 (10% in FY25). [AR FY26 segment note]
- **Auditor:** C N K & Associates — unmodified opinion; key audit matter is capitalisation of projects in progress. CARO: subsidiary Aegis Terminal (Pipavav) flagged under clause 3(xvii) (cash losses). Audit trail operated all year. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Policy:** government cut subsidised LPG cylinders and is pushing piped gas; Morbi ceramics cluster switches between LPG and natural gas on price. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Execution:** $5 bn capex (4x the current asset base) with Vadhavan still a non-binding MoU. [Q4 call; CARE Mar-26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "breakout year" to "record quarter". The war was discussed heavily in June; by August it was "life beyond Morbi" and "we are not traders". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New numbers: distribution ramp now framed as ~50% a year; the 2 mt target moved from "by '28" to "next year or the year next". JNPA phase 1 slipped from H1 to Q3 FY27. [Q1 call]
- New approvals: 52,000 t JNPA LPG tank, 49,577 kl at Kochi, possible 60,000 kl at Mangalore. [Q1 call]
- Ammonia: from detailed margin guidance in June to "I don't think I should be saying anything" in August. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- After the Q1 call, the fund-raising board meeting was called (28 Sep). [Filing 23-Sep-26]

## My read
The business is clearly stronger: distribution has doubled, cash flow is the best ever, and the company is net cash with AA ratings. But roughly half of the last four quarters' profit jump is a war windfall in LPG margins, and throughput — the steady toll business — actually fell in Q1. At about 38 times trailing earnings the price assumes the ₹7,000/t margin holds and volumes near 2 mt arrive by FY28–FY29; management's own 25% EPS target is roughly what is needed. The view changes if FY27 H2 margins drop towards ₹5,000/t, if throughput stays flat after the Kandla–Gorakhpur hook-up, or if the 28 September fund-raise is large and priced low. Watch minority leakage too: more growth is being housed in AVTL, where outsiders own 55%.
