# TVS Supply Chain Solutions — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Price ₹130, market cap ₹5,747 cr, P/E 81.1 on TTM profit ₹68 cr, ROCE 10.1%, ROE 9.7%, book value ₹46 [Screener, 27-Sep-26]. Promoters (TVS Mobility group) 43.0%, unchanged; FIIs 2.4%, DIIs 2.2% [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Asset-light contract-logistics company operating in 26 countries (India, Europe/UK, North America, Asia-Pacific) with 435 warehouses, 25.1 million sq ft managed, ~16,500 employees; 100 Fortune 500 customers (91 a year ago). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- **ISCS** (Integrated Supply Chain Solutions: sourcing, in-plant logistics, warehousing, aftermarket spares, UK defence logistics): FY26 revenue ₹8,239 cr (75%), segment result ₹734 cr. Q1 FY27 revenue ₹2,417 cr (+21.9% YoY, +5.9% QoQ), adjusted EBITDA ₹196 cr (8.1%; 8.3% a year ago, 9.3% in Q4). [AR FY26 segment note; Q1 call]
- **GFS** (Global Forwarding: ocean/air freight): FY26 revenue ₹2,764 cr (25%), segment result ₹61 cr. Q1 FY27 revenue ₹918 cr (+50.6% YoY, +22.6% QoQ), adjusted EBITDA ₹38 cr (4.1%; 2.1% a year ago, 2.4% in Q4). [AR FY26; Q1 call]
- Geography: India 30% of Q1 revenue (₹998 cr, +43.9% YoY; ISCS India +30%, GFS India +84%); rest of world ₹2,338 cr (+23%). FY26 India ₹3,037 cr, RoW ₹7,966 cr. [IP Q1FY27 p.12–13; AR FY26]
- Customers by industry (FY26): automotive 32.1%, consumer 25.2%, tech & tech infra 14.4%, industrial 12.3%, rail & utilities 7.5%, healthcare 2.4%. [IP Q1FY27 p.3]
- Contract length averages ~4 years in India, 5+ in North America, ~7.5 in Europe. Warehouses ~85% utilised. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Revenue:** Q4 call — "double-digit maybe an early teen" growth for FY27; ISCS "early teens or even mid-teens", aspiring to 18–20%. Q1 — "mid-teen growth in FY27" (IP and call). Q1 delivered +28.7% (helped by GFS freight rates). **Raised.** [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.5]
- **ISCS margin:** Q4 call — "range-bound between 9.5% and 10% moving forward". Q1 delivered 8.1%; now "9% in Q2" and 9.5–10% "by Q4". Medium-term 10.5–11%. **Pushed back.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **GFS margin:** aim 5%, "hopefully settle at about 6%, 6.5%" over several quarters; Q1 at 4.1%; management expects it to hold "around this number". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Group adjusted EBITDA FY27:** 7.3–7.4% (Q4 call). Q1 7.0%. [Q4 call]
- **PBT margin:** long-standing 4% PBT aspiration. Q1 call: "Whether we will reach that 4% in this year-end... it's our aspiration. But FY'28, definitely." **Moved from FY27-exit to FY28.** Q1 at 1.0%. [Q1 call]
- **Pipeline and conversion:** pipeline ₹6,100 cr (Q4) → ₹7,500 cr+ (Q1); historic conversion 20–25% over 12–18 months; new wins ₹524 cr (Q4) → ₹543 cr (Q1); new-business target 12–15% of prior-year revenue (FY26: 12.1%). [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.16]
- **ALA Group JV** (Italy, aerospace/defence): MoU in Q4 → JV signed; warehouse certification under way; revenue from H2 FY27; "almost ₹2,000 crores in year 5". [Q1 call]
- **Swamy & Sons 3PL** (FMCG, India): definitive agreement Q4 → completed 22 May 2026; "accretive". [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: capex mostly racking, automation and warehouse fit-out; Screener FCF ₹481 cr in FY26 (CFO ₹746 cr) — but lease payments (~₹400+ cr a year of ROU depreciation plus interest) sit below FCF. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Growth comes through leased space tied to customer contracts; short-term lease rentals rising (other expenses ₹329 cr in Q1 vs ₹257 cr a year ago). [Q1 call]
- Oracle ERP live in India ISCS; patent accepted for unified logistics platform; robotics used in a North America project. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Capital commitments small (~₹10 cr). Goodwill ₹659 cr. [AR FY26 note 37; consolidated balance sheet]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and relationships: 100 Fortune 500 clients; "invitee for almost every large MNC's foray into India". [Q4 call]
- Switching costs: multi-year embedded contracts (4–7.5 years), in-plant and aftermarket processes, system integration; ~2/3 of new wins come from existing customers. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Win rate: new wins of 21% of prior-year quarterly revenue in each of the last two quarters vs ~₹160–320 cr per quarter earlier. [IP Q1FY27 p.16]
- Niche certifications: UK defence logistics; ALA aerospace JV brings OEM contracts (Boeing, Airbus, Dassault per management). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Weakness: GFS has little moat; margins driven by freight rates and lane choices. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Thin profits:** 1% PBT margin; FY26 statutory profit ₹117 cr includes a ₹177 cr one-off InvIT gain; ex-gain the business earned ~₹69 cr. ECL provisions (~₹56 cr) and labour-code exceptional costs (₹5.2 cr in Q4, more in Q3) eat into it. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- **Churn:** ₹126 cr of revenue lost to churn in one quarter; GFS churn is "constant". [IP Q1FY27 p.14; Q4 call]
- **GFS cyclicality:** Q1 growth leaned on higher freight rates and India ocean volumes during the war; could reverse. [Q1 call]
- **Recession risk** flagged by the MD as the biggest risk. [Q1 call]
- **Auditor (S.R. Batliboi & Associates, Chennai):** unmodified. Key audit matters: revenue recognition (principal-vs-agent judgements, variable consideration) and goodwill impairment (₹659 cr). CARO adverse at the holding company under 3(iii)(e): a ₹5.63 cr loan to Thai subsidiary TVS Logistics Siam was renewed to settle overdue dues. Five subsidiaries (assets ₹1,085 cr, revenue ₹1,275 cr) audited by other auditors. Audit-trail gap at database level for one consolidation software. [AR FY26 auditor's reports]
- **Contingent liabilities (March 2026):** PF demand ₹17.2 cr (April 2011–Feb 2015; ₹7.7 cr deposited, pending at EPF Tribunal), plus tax and guarantee items each under ~₹40 cr. Disputed statutory dues not deposited: income tax ₹9.5 cr, GST ~₹20 cr across states. [AR FY26 note 37; CARO vii(b)]
- **Related parties:** intra-group loans (₹96.2 cr repayable-on-demand loans to wholly owned subsidiaries; ₹40 cr borrowed from subsidiaries); promoter-group transactions are small. [AR FY26 CARO iii; related-party note]
- **Leadership change:** MD Ravi Viswanathan retired after the Q4 call; Vikas Chadha became MD. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Disclosure quality:** presentation uploaded after 7 pm for a 9 am call (analyst complaint, accepted by MD). [Q1 call]

## My read
Revenue momentum is the best since listing — record wins, a bigger pipeline and 29% growth — and the balance sheet is steady with an AA/Positive rating. But the business still converts almost none of it into profit: 1% PBT margin, statutory profit flattered by a one-off InvIT gain, and heavy lease, credit-loss and labour-code costs. At ~81 times trailing earnings the price assumes the PBT margin climbs from 1% towards 4% (roughly a 3–4x profit rise) by FY28; management has just pushed that goal back a year. Watch ISCS margin recovering to 9%+ in Q2, GFS holding ~4% as freight rates normalise, and quarterly churn staying well below new wins.
