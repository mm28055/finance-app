# Delhivery — deep research (as of 27 Sep 2026)

Price ₹423, market cap ₹31,673 cr, P/E 266 on TTM profit ₹93 cr, ROCE 1.0%, book value ₹129/share [Screener, 27-Sep-26]. No dividend. No promoter; FIIs 41.9%, DIIs 43.1% (June 2026) — DIIs up from 29.6% a year earlier as FIIs sold [Screener].

## Business and segments
- India's largest third-party logistics company. Reports one Ind AS segment ("Logistics Services") but discloses service lines. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Express parcel** (e-commerce, D2C, SME): Q1 FY27 322 million shipments (+55.2% YoY, +5.2% QoQ; YoY inflated by Ecom Express, consolidated from Q2 FY26), revenue ₹1,869 cr (+33.2%). FY26 ~1 billion parcels, revenue ₹6,685 cr. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- **Part truckload (PTL)** freight: Q1 FY27 542,000 t (+18.4% YoY, −1.4% QoQ), revenue ₹633 cr (+24.5%); yield near ₹12/kg, up 37 paise of which only ~6 paise from fuel. FY26 ~2 million t, revenue ₹2,254 cr. Delhivery says it is the No. 2 PTL player. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Supply chain services (SCS)** (warehousing/fulfilment): FY26 revenue ₹729 cr, service EBITDA ₹79 cr (10.9%, 4x FY25). Q1 FY27 revenue ~₹200 cr; margin dipped as two large new contracts (an industrial and a consumer-durables client) started. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Others** (full truckload, cross-border) ~₹197 cr/quarter at roughly break-even to small loss. **New initiatives** (Delhivery Local intra-city trucks, Rapid, Financial Services): Q1 revenue ₹32 cr, adjusted EBITDA loss ₹32 cr. [IP Q1FY27]
- Network (June 2026): 18,810 pin-codes, 54,096 active customers, 23.3 million sq ft, 136 automated sort centres, 75,074 team members, 82,768 partner agents, daily fleet 20,663. [IP Q1FY27 appendix]
- Service-line margins, Q1 FY27: Express service EBITDA ₹292 cr (15.6%; 16.3% a year ago; FY26 17.2%); PTL ₹71 cr (11.2%; FY26 exit 13.4%); transport total 14.5%; total service EBITDA 13.1% vs 13.0%. Corporate overheads ~9.3% of revenue. [IP Q1FY27 p.7]

## Guidance history
- **Express volume FY27:** +20–30%; Q1 call: "towards the better end of the range". [Letter Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **PTL volume FY27:** +18–22% with rising yields; Q1 delivered +18.4%. [Letter Q1FY27]
- **Express margin:** 16–18% service EBITDA "normative" band. Q4 call: "firmly at the upper end". Q1 FY27 delivered **15.6% (below band)**; CFO now says H2 FY27 will be in range, "closer to the higher end". [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **PTL margin:** exit FY26 13.4% → exit FY27 target 15–15.5%; long-term 16–18%. Q1 at 11.2% (seasonally low). [Q1 call]
- **Long-run:** transport adjusted EBITDA from 6.3% to "at least 10%" (to ~11% if corporate overheads fall to 7% of revenue); transport ROIC from 16% to 25%+. Corporate overheads have stayed ~9.3–9.4% of revenue for FY25, FY26 and Q1 FY27 — an analyst flagged no progress. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Capex:** 4.7% of revenue in FY26 (7.8% in FY23); target <4.5% / 4%; Q1 FY27 3.1%. [Q4 call; Letter Q1FY27]
- **Free cash flow:** break-even was guided for FY27, delivered in FY26 (company FCF ₹89 cr). Kept. [Q4 call]
- **New businesses:** Q4 call — ₹130–160 cr investment in FY27, Local to exit FY27 at a "north of ₹200 cr" run-rate. Q1 letter — Local ARR ₹100 cr+ at June-end, ~₹140 cr now, "on track" for ₹200 cr+. On the Q1 call the CEO said the target was ₹250 cr and ARR ₹150 cr and cited a ₹160–175 cr investment budget — **the call overstated the written figures**. Ahmedabad (first Local city) to break even in Q3 FY27. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Letter Q1FY27]
- **Q1 miss explained:** management estimates ₹30–35 cr of one-offs (one-month lag on diesel pass-through, minimum-wage hikes in Haryana, Karnataka, UP and Punjab, buffer staffing for elections/weather); says no change to FY27 or long-term margin targets. [Q1 call; Letter Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex intensity falling: 7.8% of revenue (FY23) → 4.7% (FY26) → 3.1% (Q1 FY27). Capital commitments ₹137 cr at March 2026 (₹62 cr a year earlier). [Q4 call; Letter Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 38]
- Spend is now mainly PTL/heavy-parcel capacity, mega-gateway automation (AGVs/AMRs; first 6,000-pallet ASRS commissioned in Q1), and 5,000+ electric cargo vehicles planned in FY27 with OEM partners. [Q4 call; Letter Q1FY27]
- Invested capital in transport ~21.5% of revenue; CFO sees 2–3 points of improvement. [Q4 call]
- Ecom Express acquired July 2025 for ₹1,369 cr; consolidated goodwill ₹2,384 cr at March 2026, of which ₹1,042 cr from Ecom. [AR FY26 Directors' report; auditor's report]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash ₹4,677 cr at June 2026 (₹4,555 cr at March). Borrowings ₹1,463 cr on Screener are mainly lease liabilities; no bank term loans taken in FY26. [Letter Q1FY27; Screener; AR FY26 CARO 3(ix)]
- Net working capital 9 days (Q1 FY27), 11 days (FY26) vs 38 days three years ago; driven by faster billing, prepaid customers, client selection and exiting quick-commerce mother-warehousing. Screener debtor days 48 (FY26) vs 58 (FY25). [Letter Q1FY27; Q4 call; Screener]
- Cash from operations FY26 ₹911 cr; Screener FCF ₹506 cr (company's own FCF ₹89 cr deducts lease payments and integration costs). [Screener; Q4 call]
- ESOP charge (non-cash) ₹139 cr in FY27, ₹94 cr FY28; 1.59 crore performance ESOPs unlock at share prices of ₹800 / ₹1,000 / ₹1,200. AGM (22 Sep 2026) approved 9 lakh ESOP-2021 options for the CEO and two directors. [IP Q1FY27 p.9; News: Sahi, Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- Scale: ~1 billion parcels and 2 million t PTL in FY26; after the Ecom Express deal, three listed express players remain (Delhivery, Blue Dart, Shadowfax). CEO: rival Xpressbees has no structural advantage. [Q4 call]
- Share gains: Q1 volume +5.2% QoQ in a usually weak quarter; CEO says share gained both vs other 3PLs and vs marketplaces' in-house arms ("flight to quality"). Long-tail D2C/SME growth 40–45% a year, where Delhivery has "disproportionate share", especially heavier parcels. [Q1 call]
- Pricing: PTL yields up organically; fuel pass-through clauses cover 97–98% of freight customers; minimum-wage hikes being passed on. CEO: "we don't see significant pricing pressure in the market any longer." [Q1 call]
- PTL: gross margin went from 14% to 28% in ~2.5 years while tonnage rose ~1.8x. [Q4 call]
- Tech: Delhivery Maps (built on 2+ billion shipments, 1 billion daily GPS pings), SmartNDR (500+ clients), AI in claims and billing. [Letter Q1FY27]
- Capital-light edge: capex <5% of revenue and NWC ~10 days are hard for sub-scale peers to match. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin fragility:** Q1 adjusted EBITDA halved QoQ (₹151 → ₹76 cr). Express service margin 15.6% is below the 16–18% band management called "firmly" achieved a quarter earlier. Wage and fuel inflation are structural (labour codes, gig-worker laws). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Earnings quality:** FY26 statutory PAT ₹153 cr vs other income ₹340 cr — the core business still loses money after tax at the statutory level before treasury income. The company's headline "PAT ₹347 cr" and "EBITDA ₹156 cr" exclude integration costs and use non-standard definitions. [Screener; AR FY26]
- **Customer concentration:** largest customer 19.23% of net sales in FY26 (17.71% in FY25); CEO's comfort threshold is 35%. [AR FY26 financial-risk note; Q4 call]
- **Captive competition:** Amazon opening its logistics to third parties; Meesho's Valmo and Flipkart's Ekart. CEO dismisses Amazon's move as "an old product in a new wrapper". [Q4 call]
- **Management exit:** co-founder and COO (earlier CFO) Ajith Pai left on 15 Sep 2026; Vani Venkatesh made Deputy CEO; CEO shifting time to technology and engineering. Board fully reconstituted (Neelam Dhawan chair; Romesh Sobti left). [News: Business Standard, 8-Aug-26; Q4 call]
- **Auditor (Deloitte Haskins & Sells):** unmodified opinion. Key audit matters: Ecom Express purchase-price allocation (goodwill ₹1,042 cr) and goodwill impairment testing (₹2,384 cr consolidated; standalone ₹1,326 cr allocated to the PTL cash-generating unit). CARO remarks: parent — clause (i)(c) (title deeds of properties acquired from FedEx in 2021 not transferred), (vii)(a) (₹0.8 cr professional tax unpaid FY23–FY26), (vii)(b) (disputed dues). Subsidiaries Delhivery Freight Services and Ecom Express flagged for cash losses (xvii). Audit trail not maintained in one subsidiary for April–October 2025. [AR FY26 auditor's reports]
- **Disputed dues vs contingent liabilities:** CARO lists disputed GST of ~₹108 cr and income tax ~₹69 cr + ₹40 cr not deposited, yet the consolidated contingent-liability note shows only income tax ₹38.3 cr and GST ₹0.7 cr. The gap suggests some demands are judged remote; worth watching. [AR FY26 CARO; note 38]
- **Related parties:** purchases from associate Falcon Autotech (40.92% owned, sortation equipment) ~₹25 cr in FY26 (~₹63 cr FY25); KMP remuneration ₹18.3 cr (₹9.3 cr FY25); employee loans ₹140.7 cr granted in the year (₹3.4 cr outstanding); KMP repaid ₹47.7 cr of loans. [AR FY26 related-party note; CARO iii — table columns partly garbled in PDF, treat as indicative]
- **New businesses:** NBFC licence received July 2026 for fleet-owner lending "asset-light" via partners — credit risk if it drifts onto the balance sheet. [Letter Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Quick commerce:** Delhivery avoids dark stores and last-mile QC delivery; if QC takes share from marketplace e-commerce, parcel growth could slow. [Q1 call]

## My read
Delhivery is winning volume — 55% parcel growth and a Q1 that grew sequentially — and the balance sheet (₹4,677 cr cash, ~10 days NWC, capex ~3–5% of sales) is strong. But profit has not caught up: statutory profit is still smaller than treasury income, and Q1 adjusted EBITDA halved as wage and fuel costs hit. The price needs the long-promised climb to ~10% transport adjusted EBITDA and a fall in corporate overheads from ~9% to ~7% of revenue; neither has shown up yet. Q2 and Q3 FY27 should show whether pass-throughs restore Express to 16–18% and PTL to ~15%. The view gets worse if margins stay near Q1 levels into the festive quarter, if the top client's share keeps rising, or if more senior leaders leave.
