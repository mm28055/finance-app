# Acutaas Chemicals (formerly Ami Organics) — deep research, Sep 2026

Screener code ACUTAAS (consolidated). Price ₹3,272, market cap ₹26,792 cr, ROCE 31.6%, ROE 24% [Screener, 28 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Two reported segments. **Advanced pharma intermediates** (key starting materials and late intermediates for generic APIs and for new patented drugs, incl. a CDMO — contract development and manufacturing — business anchored by one innovator contract) was ₹292.7 cr in Q1 FY27, +76.5% YoY, about 89% of sales. **Specialty chemicals** (parabens/methyl salicylate "commodity" fine chemicals, BFC semiconductor photoresist chemicals, and new battery electrolyte additives) was ₹37.0 cr, −10.6% YoY [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Segment EBITDA margin: pharma ~36%, specialty ~24% in Q1 FY27; in Q4 FY26 pharma ~44%, specialty ~29% [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- FY26 mix: pharma 87%, specialty 13%; management expects pharma to fall to ~80% within three years as battery and semiconductor lines grow [Q1 call].
- Portfolio: 500+ intermediates across 17+ therapy areas, ~600 customers, 55+ countries; claims 50–90% global share in key intermediates [AR FY26 MD&A p.86–88]. CARE says no single product is above 10–15% of sales (as at FY25) [CARE Dec-25] — but the CDMO anchor molecule has grown fast since, and management itself admits concentration "moved toward it" [Q1 call].
- Anchor CDMO customer: supplies an intermediate for darolutamide (Bayer's Nubeqa, supplied via Orion/Fermion); a 10-year supply contract with the first CDMO customer [Q4 call]. Four more CDMO products validated in FY26, each worth ₹50–100 cr a year at peak [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 p.86].
- Geography: sales outside India ₹1,021 cr of ₹1,329 cr product sales in FY26 (77%), up from 74% in FY25 [AR FY26 note 41; CARE Dec-25]. Negligible US exposure, so US tariffs are not a direct issue [CARE Dec-25].
- Plants: Sachin (Surat) 83% utilised in Q1 FY27 (75% in Q4); Ankleshwar 23% (31% in Q4); Jhagadia 55% (50%) [Q1 call; Q4 call]. Ankleshwar expected to fill by FY28; Sachin is "hitting the capacity utilization roof" [Q1 call]. BFC (Baba Fine Chemicals) at Greater Noida; Indichem (75% JV) in Gongju, South Korea [AR FY26 p.86; News: ScanX, 28 Aug 2026].
- Battery: 2,000 MT each of vinylene carbonate (VC) and fluoroethylene carbonate (FEC) at Jhagadia, fully contracted for three years with several customers in North America, Korea and elsewhere; a third additive under a signed contract (capex done by end-Q2 FY27); a fourth product in final contract talks [Q1 call].

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / margin / net profit: Jun-25 207 / 51 / 25% / 44; Sep-25 306 / 95 / 31% / 72; Dec-25 393 / 151 / 38% / 106; Mar-26 433 / 184 / 42% / 134; Jun-26 330 / 113 / 34% / 75 [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 sales 717, OP 128 (18%), PAT 49 (after a one-off loss); FY25 1,007, 232 (23%), 160; FY26 1,339, 480 (36%), 356. TTM sales 1,462, PAT 387 [Screener].
- Three-year sales CAGR 30%, profit CAGR 62% [Screener]. Margin more than doubled in two years on mix (CDMO) and exit from low-margin intermediates [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27: gross margin 57.9% (+466 bp YoY); EBITDA ₹113.1 cr (34.3%, +973 bp); PAT ₹74.9 cr (+70%). Other income fell to ₹1.8 cr from ₹15.9 cr a year ago (forex gain of ₹0.1 cr vs ~₹10 cr in Q4) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Employee cost ₹39.6 cr in Q1 (vs ₹25.1 cr) — includes a one-time bonus; guided ~₹150 cr for FY27 [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Seasonality: Q1 is always the weakest; H1 ~40% and H2 ~60% of the year [Q4 call]. Q1 FY27 was −24% QoQ, in line with that pattern.
- Unit economics: management will not split CDMO from base intermediates, nor give battery revenue; says investors can compute battery potential from 4,000 MT × market price [Q1 call]. Not disclosed — I do not estimate it.

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (30 Apr 2026): FY27 revenue +25%; EBITDA margin "similar" to FY26 (35.9%); ₹1,000 cr CDMO revenue by FY28 "remains correct"; Indichem complete in H2 CY2026; electrolyte phase 2 (third product) by Q1 FY27; pilot plant delayed to Q2 FY27; FY27 capex ~₹50 cr spill-over + ~₹40 cr maintenance; separate R&D centre ("10x" capacity) and land capex to be announced [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (24 Jul 2026): 25% growth and similar margin reiterated; Q1 margin (34.3%) not to be extrapolated; ₹1,000 cr CDMO target — "confident to not only achieve this target but to beat it"; battery plant trial runs done and commercial supply started; Indichem capex to finish by end-Q2 FY27, revenue from FY28, 3–4 years to fill; phase 2 slipped from "Q1 FY27" to "end of Q2 FY27"; maintenance capex now ₹40–45 cr [Q1 call; official transcript].
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: battery commercialisation — delivered; Indichem — delivered early (plant inaugurated 28 Aug 2026, built in 11 months, ~₹200 cr / KRW 30 bn) [News: ScanX, 28 Aug 2026]; phase 2 electrolyte — slipped one quarter; pilot plant — Q2 FY27 as re-guided; revenue growth — Q1 +59% YoY, well ahead of the 25% pace, but the H1/H2 pattern means the full-year number matters more. Guidance kept, not raised.
- CARE's Dec-25 note shows earlier slippage too: electrolyte project cost raised to ~₹220 cr from ~₹177 cr and timeline moved from H1 FY26 to H2 FY26 (extended monsoon) [CARE Dec-25].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹195 cr (mainly Jhagadia battery plant and Sachin pilot plant) plus ₹190 cr invested in Indichem [Q4 call]. CWIP rose to ₹332 cr at Mar-26 from ₹130 cr [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 capex ₹56 cr (₹15 cr Indichem, ₹41 cr India) [Q1 call].
- FY27 plan ~₹90–95 cr plus un-announced R&D centre and land [Q1 call]. Management says existing infrastructure supports growth through FY28 [Q4 call].
- Payback: Indichem expected at ~1x asset turn (≈₹200 cr revenue at full use) with margins like BFC's (above group average); slow start, full in 3–4 years [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Battery plant full in three years [Q1 call].
- Government support: ECMS (electronics component scheme) approval from MeitY for up to 25% incentive on ₹119.12 cr eligible investment at Jhagadia, benefits to FY31 [News: ScanX, 18 Aug 2026].
- Capital commitments fell to ₹39 cr at Mar-26 from ₹80 cr [AR FY26 note 39].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash ₹314 cr at 30 Jun 2026 (₹198 cr at Mar-26) [Q1 call; Q4 call]. Borrowings only ₹36 cr at Mar-26 [Screener]. CARE: gearing 0.01x, interest cover 120x in H1 FY26, nil term debt [CARE Dec-25].
- Cash from operations ₹292 cr in FY26 (84% of operating profit), but free cash flow −₹36 cr after capex and the Korea JV; FCF has been negative every year since FY21 [Screener].
- Working-capital days: 99 in Q1 FY27 vs 91 in Q4 (by management's measure; 120 for FY26 vs 114) [Q1 call; Q4 call]. Screener FY26: debtor 99 days, inventory 149, payables 99, cash conversion cycle 149 days (from 121) — inventory is climbing, partly deliberate buffer stock against Gulf supply disruption [Screener; Q1 call].
- Growth has been funded partly by equity: preferential allotment/QIP in FY25 (equity capital 37 → 41; promoter stake 39.9% → 32.7%) [Screener; CARE Dec-25].
- Dividend: ₹2.50/share final (ex-date 17 Sep 2026); payout 6% of FY26 profit [News: ScanX; Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Share: claims 50–90% global share in key intermediates [AR FY26 p.88]; primary supplier on the darolutamide chain with a 10-year contract [Q4 call].
- Pricing/mix: gross margin 57.9% vs 53.2% a year ago; FY26 EBITDA margin 35.9% vs 23.5% in FY25 — driven by mix, not one-off pricing [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 p.85].
- Approvals: Sachin and Ankleshwar passed Japan PMDA GMP inspections without critical or major observations; Sachin has two USFDA EIRs [AR FY26 p.86; CARE Dec-25].
- Switching costs: CDMO intermediates are locked into customers' regulatory filings; four new products wait only for customers' regulatory approvals [Q4 call].
- Battery: claims to be the pioneering non-Chinese maker of electrolyte additives at scale; capacity sold out for three years [AR FY26 p.86; Q1 call]. Only "India's only manufacturer of electronic-grade photoresist chemicals" via BFC [AR FY26 p.86].
- Cost: 90%+ backward integration to basic chemicals; 59% of raw materials sourced in India; flow chemistry; 16 MW solar [AR FY26 p.88; CARE Dec-25].

## Risks and red flags
- **Concentration** on one CDMO molecule (darolutamide chain). Management does not split CDMO revenue; the ₹1,000 cr FY28 target depends on Bayer/Orion demand plus four new products that still await customers' regulatory approvals [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Auditor**: unmodified opinion; key audit matter only on finished-goods inventory valuation (overhead allocation); audit trail operated throughout [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report].
- **Contingent liabilities** small: ₹17.25 cr (income tax ₹17.0 cr, GST ₹0.2 cr) [AR FY26 note 39].
- **MSME payments**: ₹75.3 cr paid to micro/small suppliers beyond the legal due date in FY26 (₹56.6 cr in FY25), with ₹1.23 cr interest — a small but rising process lapse [AR FY26 note 40].
- **Related parties**: modest — KMP pay ₹17.5 cr; raw-material purchases ₹1.6 cr from a KMP-relative firm [AR FY26 note 42].
- **Goodwill**: ₹104 cr, of which ~₹48 cr added in FY26 because the Korean partner received 25% of Indichem while Acutaas paid ₹190 cr — goodwill on a pre-revenue venture [Q4 call].
- **Subsidiary dilution**: 10% of the electrolyte subsidiary given to a business partner (ARZ) as agreed earlier [Q1 call].
- **Governance signal**: at the 24 Sep 2026 AGM shareholders **rejected the ESOP scheme** (72.63% in favour vs 75% needed) [News: ScanX, Sep 2026].
- **Execution**: two electrolyte timelines slipped (FY26 cost overrun ₹177→₹220 cr; phase 2 by one quarter); pilot plant slipped [CARE Dec-25; Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Working capital** rising (CCC 121 → 149 days) and FCF negative for six years [Screener].
- **Macro**: Gulf conflict/Strait of Hormuz closure raised feedstock and freight costs [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Promoter holding 32.7% — low for a mid-cap; no pledge disclosed [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "future-proofing" (10x R&D centre, land, phasing out commodity chemicals) to delivery (battery supply started, Indichem ahead of time, AI-driven memory-chip demand).
- Numbers: net cash up from ₹198 cr to ₹314 cr; working capital improved to 99 days from 120 (FY) / 91 (Q4) — the definitions differ between calls; specialty margin fell from 29% to 24% as commodity products were cut; pharma margin from 44% to 36% on Q1 seasonality.
- New: CDMO target language upgraded to "beat it"; battery "constrained by our plant capacity" not demand; fourth battery product near contract; Apixaban intermediate "started picking up"; Caplyta (lumateperone) generic intermediate qualified, awaiting launch.
- Slipped: phase 2 electrolyte from Q1 to Q2 FY27.

## My read
The business is clearly improving: margin moved from 18% (FY24) to 36% (FY26) on mix, the balance sheet is net cash, and each promised new engine (battery supply, Korea plant) arrived on or near time in the last two quarters. The near-term number is safe — 25% growth needs only about ₹1,675 cr in FY27, and Q1's ₹330 cr fits the usual 40/60 half-year split. The price, however, already assumes the ₹1,000 cr CDMO target is met and the battery and Korea engines fill on schedule; the main swing factor is one molecule's end-demand. What would change my view: a quarter where CDMO growth stalls or the four new products slip past FY28, or battery contracts being repriced; positively, disclosure of CDMO revenue or a signed fourth battery contract with capex. The ESOP rejection and rising MSME late payments are minor but worth tracking.
