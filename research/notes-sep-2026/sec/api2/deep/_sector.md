# Indian API (bulk drug) industry — sector research, 28 Sep 2026

Scope: China dependence, PLI for KSMs, pricing, US/EU regulation, the shift to CDMO; outlook FY27–FY29. Note: the web-search quota ran out mid-research, so US FDA inspection statistics and the current legal status of the US BIOSECURE Act could not be verified from a primary source; those points rely on company calls and one market-research house and are flagged.

## Market size and growth
- India API market ~US$15.3bn in 2026, forecast US$22.2bn by 2031 (7.7% CAGR 2026–31); ~US$14.2bn in 2025. [Mordor Intelligence, India API market report, 2026]
- Global API market ~US$256bn (2025) → ~US$376bn by 2034 (4.2% CAGR); India ~8% of the global API market by value and supplies ~20% of world generic volumes. [IMARC via IOL Annual Report FY26 MD&A; IOL AR FY26]
- ICRA's API sample (10 firms incl. **Aarti Drugs** and **NGL Fine-Chem**): industry CAGR 7–8% CY2023–CY2029 after ~8% CY2017–23; OPM seen at 12–14%. (Report dated Aug 2024 — older than 12 months, used for the long-run baseline only.) [ICRA, Indian API Industry, Aug 2024]
- Wider pharma (formulations + APIs): revenue +11–13% in FY27 vs 8% in FY26; exports +14–16% in rupee terms; domestic market +9–11%; volume growth recovering to 4–5% from ~2% in each of the past two years. Formulations are ~83% of pharma exports — APIs ~17%. 57% of exports go to regulated markets. [CRISIL Ratings/Crisil Intelligence, 25 Sep 2026 via The Week]
- Pharma exports US$31.1bn in FY26. [Pharmexcil data via Business Today, 28 Jun 2026]

## Demand drivers
- Steady growth of generic formulations (chronic therapies), patent expiries, and global buyers' "China-plus-one" diversification. [CRISIL Sep-26; Mordor 2026]
- Contract manufacturing: CDMOs/CMOs are the fastest-growing end-use (8.4% CAGR to 2031) while pharma companies still buy ~76% of APIs directly; CRDMOs projected from US$3–3.5bn (2025) to US$22–25bn by 2035; Mordor claims request-for-quotation volumes at leading Indian CDMOs jumped 50% in 2024 as buyers shifted from China. [Mordor 2026 — forecaster's claim, not independently verified]
- Company evidence of the CDMO shift in this group: **IOL** built a 1,500 mn-tablet EU-GMP formulation CDMO plant and a customer-funded chemical tolling unit (Sep 2026); **SMS** is building peptides and CDMO capability (updates due late 2026); **Solara** has CRAMS of only ₹10–12cr a year and parked a carve-out; **Shree Ganesh** repositioning as a CRAMS/speciality partner but FY26 CRAMS revenue "not significant". [IOL Sep-26 call; SMS Q4 call; Solara Q4 call; SGRL Q4 call]

## China dependence
- China supplied 43.45% of India's pharma imports by value in 2023-24 (US$3.6bn); for specific inputs far higher — 77% of penicillin G imports and 94.1% of 6-APA in FY24; industry estimates put 70–80% of APIs used in India as imported, mostly from China; paracetamol, ibuprofen, clavulanic acid, penicillin G, amoxicillin rely 50–80% on imported intermediates. [Business Today, 28 Jun 2026, citing Pharmexcil]
- India imported APIs worth ~₹37,700cr in FY2024, ~35% of demand. [Mordor 2026] ICRA: India imports ~35% of API/intermediate needs, 65–70% of it from China; 80–100% for some essential medicines; KSMs mostly from China even when the API is made in India. [ICRA Aug 2024]
- Company-level: **IOL** — metformin's DCDA "depend[s] on China… most companies are dependent on the same source"; **Aarti** — DCDA still imported, but Sayakha now makes the methylamine intermediate ("we are now the only ones"); **SMS** — ~₹150cr spent to make intermediates in-house, cutting China reliance; **Solara** — buys IBB for ibuprofen from third parties. [IOL Q1 call; Aarti Q4 call; SMS Q2 call; Solara Q4 call]

## Policy
- **PLI for bulk drugs** (outlay ₹6,940cr; 41 targeted products): by June 2026, ₹5,210.7cr invested; 39 projects for 28 APIs/KSMs commissioned; cumulative sales ₹3,792cr incl. exports ₹560cr; 5,127 jobs. Plants include penicillin G 15,000 MTPA, clavulanic acid 400 MTPA, para-amino-phenol (paracetamol KSM) 13,500 MTPA, atorvastatin 206 MTPA. [Department of Pharmaceuticals via Millennium Post, 25 Sep 2026]
- Progress a year earlier (Aug 2025): ₹4,709cr invested vs ₹3,938.5cr committed; 48 projects / 33 products approved for 32 firms; capacity for 26 products; sales ₹1,962cr, imports avoided ₹1,483cr. Sales nearly doubled in ten months, but remain small against ~₹37,700cr of API imports. [PIB, Rajya Sabha reply, 19 Aug 2025]
- Wider pharma PLI (₹15,000cr): ₹38,543cr invested vs ₹17,275cr committed; cumulative API/KSM/DI sales ₹62,966cr incl. ₹38,868cr exports. [PIB 19 Aug 2025]
- **Trade defence:** after China cut prices of 41 targeted APIs/KSMs by 40–50% (clavulanate −40%, penicillin G −50%), hurting the largest PLI plants (Himachal, Telangana) [Tribune, 12 Sep 2025], DGFT set a **minimum import price** for one year from 29 Jan 2026: penicillin G potassium ₹2,216/kg, amoxicillin trihydrate ₹2,733/kg, 6-APA ₹3,405/kg (EOU/SEZ/advance-licence imports exempt). [Medical Dialogues, 30 Jan 2026]
- Anti-dumping on salicylic acid (Aarti's application) delayed ~1 year — government asked for a longer injury period. [Aarti Q1 FY27 call]
- Other schemes: PRIP research scheme ₹5,000cr; Biopharma SHAKTI ₹10,000cr over five years (Budget 2026-27); bulk drug parks under development. [PIB Aug 2025; IOL AR FY26 MD&A]

## Cost drivers and pricing (cycle position)
- 2022–2025: Chinese overcapacity drove API price erosion ("larger price erosions" in 2024 and 2025 — NGL); ICRA's sample margin fell to 11–13%. [NGL Q4 call; ICRA Aug 2024]
- Mar–Apr 2026: the West Asia/US–Iran war spiked solvents, ammonia and API prices — metformin +15–20% over pre-war (Aarti), paracetamol and ethyl acetate up sharply (IOL); Aarti's Q1 FY27 had +16–17% price and only +3.5% volume; high prices cut Indian demand for price-controlled old antibiotics. [Aarti Q1 call; IOL Q1 call]
- Jul–Sep 2026: prices easing but above pre-war — "paracetamol… softened"; ibuprofen "hovering around the same level" (IOL, 11 Sep); NGL warns Q1 margins included inventory gains that will reverse; Chinese rivals held prices in March–April, then raised them. [IOL Sep call; NGL IP Q1FY27; NGL Q4 call]
- CRISIL expects cost inflation (raw materials, energy, freight) to compress sector operating margins by 150–200 bps in FY27 despite 11–13% revenue growth. [CRISIL Sep-26]
- Where we are: **early recovery after a 3-year downcycle, now in a temporary, war-driven price spike that is fading**. Volume growth, not price, will decide FY28.

## US/EU regulation
- Regulated-market approvals are the main moat: IOL 14 US DMFs / 21 CEPs; Aarti's Tarapur API plant re-approved by USFDA "after a long wait of 10 years", 9 CEPs; SMS plants approved by USFDA, PMDA, EU, ANVISA; NGL 3 CEPs granted and 3 pending (EU reviews taking ~18 months), US audit only after a customer files. [company calls/decks]
- Approval lags lengthen growth timelines: customers' own registrations stretched "easily six months" after the war; "more than one year" for pharma (SGRL). [SGRL Q4 call]
- US tariffs on pharma: an "intent has been announced, but there is no legal implementing framework" (Solara, Jul 2026); CRISIL lists potential US tariffs as a risk. [Solara Q1 call; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Europe accepting CDSCO inspections via EMA reliance routes and buyers accelerating vendor qualification (Mordor claim). [Mordor 2026]

## Competitive structure and where profit sits (this group, Screener FY26)
| Tier | Companies | FY26 revenue | FY26 OPM | What earns the margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commodity integrated APIs | IOL Chem, Aarti Drugs | ₹2,319cr; ₹2,565cr | 12%; 12% | Scale + backward integration; chemicals side of IOL earns 3–6% EBIT |
| Mid-size niche APIs | SMS Pharma, NGL Fine-Chem | ₹887cr; ₹501cr | 19%; 16% | Integrated ibuprofen/ARV (SMS); veterinary niche (NGL) |
| Speciality/complex | Solara (base business), Shree Ganesh | Solara base ₹1,041cr pro-forma; SGRL ₹109cr | base ~24% EBITDA (group 14%); 32% | Complex chemistry, polymer APIs, multi-step intermediates |
- Ibuprofen is a clear example of profit concentration: fully integrated **IOL** earns ~15% pharma EBIT and is adding 6,000 MTPA; **SMS** (integrated intermediates) adds 300 t/month; **Solara** (buys IBB) loses ₹10–15cr a quarter and is exiting. Integration, not scale alone, decides who profits. [IOL IP Q1FY27; SMS Q4 call; Solara Q1 call]

## Outlook FY27–FY29 (forecasters named)
- **Mordor Intelligence:** India API market 7.7% CAGR 2026–31; CDMO/CMO end-use 8.4% CAGR.
- **ICRA** (long-run baseline, Aug 2024): 7–8% CAGR to CY2029; OPM 12–14%.
- **CRISIL** (FY27): pharma revenue +11–13%, exports +14–16%, margins −150 to −200 bps.
- **Government/DoP:** PLI capacity for 28 of 41 targeted products commissioned; sales run-rate rising, but imports still dominate.
- My synthesis: plain APIs likely grow ~7–9% a year in FY27–FY29 with margins mean-reverting from the war spike; companies with (a) captive intermediates, (b) new regulated-market approvals turning into sales, or (c) real contract-manufacturing revenue should grow faster. The main swing factors are Chinese pricing after the war premium fades, US tariff design, and how quickly EU/US approvals convert — all of which have slipped in company timelines this year.
