# Alivus Life Sciences (formerly Glenmark Life Sciences) — deep research, Sep 2026

Screener code ALIVUS — **standalone** page (the consolidated page is empty after FY21; the company has no material subsidiaries). Price ₹1,366, market cap ₹16,766 cr, ROCE 23.8%, ROE 17.9% [Screener, 28 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Pure API (bulk drug) maker: 176 molecules, ~900 customers, 617 DMF/CEP filings at Jun-26; one reportable segment [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 note 28].
- Two management splits: **GPL vs non-GPL** — sales to former parent Glenmark Pharmaceuticals vs everyone else; and **generic API vs CDMO**. Non-GPL rose from 59% of sales in FY22 to 71% in FY26 [Q4 call]. In Q1 FY27 GPL fell 52.6% YoY (inventory rationalisation) to ~11% of sales; non-GPL +26.5% (≈20% in constant currency: volume +21–22%, price −1–2%) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- GPL is still the largest customer: ₹742 cr in FY26 (29% of revenue) vs ₹781 cr FY25 [AR FY26 note 28]; supply contract with GPL runs to FY29 [India Ratings Sep-25].
- CDMO ~6–7% of sales (~₹160–170 cr a year), five commercial projects, seven new enquiries, two new contracts targeted "early H2 FY27" [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Therapy: CVS + CNS 58% of Q1 FY27 sales; chronic 67% of FY26 [Q1 call; Q4 call]. Regulated markets 83% of Q1 FY27 revenue [IP Q1FY27]. FY26 revenue: India ₹1,164 cr, outside India ₹1,388 cr [AR FY26 note 28] (India figure includes sales to GPL).
- Capacity: reactor volume from 1,198 KL (FY24) to a planned 2,690 KL by FY28 [Q4 call]; utilisation 90–95% in FY25 [India Ratings Sep-25]. Brownfield: Dahej +160 KL, Ankleshwar +110 KL (on time); greenfield Solapur Phase 1 (370 KL backward-integration block + 120 KL API) [Q1 call; Q4 call]. Ankleshwar is 65–70% of revenue; Dahej is full [Q4 call].
- High-potency API (HPAPI) pipeline: 29 products (13 validated) addressing an $82 bn market; revenue from FY28 as patents expire from late CY2027 [Q1 call].

## Key numbers (Screener standalone, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / margin / net profit: Jun-25 602 / 172 / 29% / 122; Sep-25 588 / 179 / 30% / 130; Dec-25 673 / 231 / 34% / 150; Mar-26 689 / 215 / 31% / 163; Jun-26 640 / 212 / 33% / 160 [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 sales 2,283, OP 675 (30%), PAT 471; FY25 2,387, 684 (29%), 486; FY26 2,552, 798 (31%), 564. TTM PAT 603 [Screener].
- Company-reported EBITDA includes other income (forex): Q1 FY27 ₹234 cr (36.6%) vs Screener OP ₹212 cr (33%); FY26 ₹858 cr (33.6%) vs Screener ₹798 cr (31%) [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; Screener]. FY26 forex gain ~₹31 cr [Q4 call].
- Gross margin 60.2% in Q1 FY27 (+510 bp YoY) on launches, mix, efficiency [Q1 call].
- Growth is slow: 3- and 5-year sales CAGR 6% [Screener]. Price erosion in base (non-GPL, non-CDMO) business ~5.5% in FY26; overall ~4.5% [Q4 call].
- Q4 FY26 absorbed a ₹20 cr fire loss at Dahej (intermediates block) in other expenses [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 (15 May 2026): EBITDA margin 30–32% "going forward"; FY27 capex ~₹540 cr; Solapur Phase 1 operational in Q2 FY27; two CDMO contracts moved from "next six months" to "early H2 FY27"; HPAPI revenue FY28 [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 (31 Jul 2026): **new revenue guidance 10–12% for FY27**, H2-weighted; margin 30–32% kept (could reach 34%, "the war situation" makes them cautious); GPL flattish for the year; Solapur slipped to "by Diwali"/early Q3 FY27 (labour slowdown); capex ₹540 cr kept [Q1 call].
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: margin — beat (36.6% company basis); Solapur — slipped one quarter; CDMO deals — "on track" but not yet signed; Solapur Phase 2 pushed out about a year [Q1 call].
- Arithmetic: 10–12% growth means FY27 revenue ~₹2,805–2,860 cr; Q1 ₹640 cr leaves ~₹2,165–2,220 cr for nine months (~₹720–740 cr a quarter, above any quarter so far) — a real H2 step-up is needed.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹306 cr; FY25–26 total ₹472 cr; Q1 FY27 ₹85 cr; FY27 plan ~₹540 cr, all from internal accruals [Q4 call; Q1 call]. CWIP ₹284 cr at Mar-26 (₹102 cr FY25) [Screener].
- Capital commitments ₹106 cr at Mar-26 vs ₹33 cr [AR FY26 note 29].
- Solapur starts at 40–50% utilisation, 60–70% the next year; ROW-only until a regulator inspects (target within a year of start); 370 KL is backward integration and "should not be factored" into sales models [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Asset turn ~2.2x, will dip below 2x temporarily [Q4 call].
- Taloja R&D centre under construction; R&D 3.6–3.7% of sales, to settle ~4% [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and short-term investments ₹880 cr at Jun-26 (₹782 cr Mar-26); debt-free apart from ₹57 cr borrowings on Screener [Q1 call; Q4 call; Screener].
- CFO ₹565 cr FY26 (93% of OP); FCF ₹259 cr [Screener]. Q1 FY27 FCF ₹90 cr [Q1 call].
- Working capital is heavy: debtors 153 days, inventory 246, payables 132, cash conversion cycle 267 days in FY26 (235 in FY24) [Screener]. Receivables rose because of extended credit terms to GPL under the master supply agreement [India Ratings Sep-25]. Management will build more inventory to protect supply [Q1 call].
- Dividend ₹5/share final for FY26 (payout 11%) [AR FY26; Screener]. Before Nirma's 2024 takeover much cash was paid up to Glenmark (payout 110% in FY23) [Screener; Q4 call].
- Rating: IND AA/Stable (upgraded Sep 2025); fund-based limits unused [India Ratings Sep-25].

## Moat evidence
- Regulatory: three USFDA-approved sites; latest inspections VAI at Ankleshwar and NAI (no action) at Dahej [Q1 call; India Ratings Sep-25].
- Niche portfolio: 45% of DMFs filed FY23–25 had fewer than five competitors [India Ratings Sep-25]; top-10 products 45% of revenue [India Ratings Sep-25].
- Pricing power in the war shock: customers accepted freight and raw-material pass-through; boilers moved off gas to briquettes [Q4 call].
- Margin leadership: ~30% average EBITDA margin FY19–25, above peers [India Ratings Sep-25]; gross margin now ~60% [Q1 call].
- Limits: generic API contracts are short (1–2 years); annual price erosion 4.5–5.5% must be out-run by volume [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration**: GPL 29% of FY26 revenue and lumpy (−52.6% in Q1 FY27); longer credit terms to GPL [AR FY26 note 28; India Ratings Sep-25].
- **Execution**: Solapur delayed twice (Q2 → early Q3 FY27; Phase 2 a year later); the plant cannot serve regulated markets until inspected [Q1 call].
- **Growth ceiling**: base business erodes 4.5–5.5% a year in price; HPAPI revenue only from FY28 [Q4 call].
- **Accounting presentation**: headline EBITDA includes forex/other income (≈2–3 margin points) [IP Q1FY27].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; only key audit matter is revenue recognition (variable consideration) [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Contingent liability small: ₹4.8 cr excise dispute on amiodarone at CESTAT [AR FY26 note 29].
- Related parties: ₹15.6 cr CSR paid to Nirma Education and Research Foundation (promoter trust); KMP pay ₹7.2 cr; small sales to Aculife (Nirma group) ₹0.6 cr [AR FY26 note 25]. Possible future upstreaming of cash to Nirma is a monitorable [India Ratings Sep-25].
- Promoter Nirma holds 74.87%; free float small (FII 5.5%, DII 7.2%) [Screener].
- Dahej flash fire in Q4 FY26 (₹20 cr loss) [Q4 call; AR FY26 BRSR].

## What changed between the two calls
- Revenue guidance introduced (10–12%) where Q4 gave none; margin guidance unchanged despite a record quarter.
- GPL swung from a strong Q4 (≈35% of sales) to ≈11% in Q1; non-GPL took over growth.
- Raw-material inflation moved from solvents to key starting materials; price reviews with customers now "on a more regular basis".
- Solapur slipped again; CDMO deals still "early H2".

## Best management quotes
- "We could even go up to 34%. The issue is this war situation..." — on margin [Q1 call]
- "Growth will not be pursued at the cost of margins." [Q4 call]
- "Solapur will be up and running by Diwali and we are going to trigger an inspection." [Q1 call]

## My read
A high-quality, cash-rich API maker whose margins have genuinely stepped up under Nirma (reported EBITDA margin 28% in FY24 to ~34% in FY26), but whose revenue growth has been only ~6% a year. The next leg — Solapur, brownfield capacity, HPAPI launches from FY28 and CDMO wins — is real but back-ended, and FY27 needs a sharp H2 recovery in GPL orders to hit 10–12%. The stock needs high-single-digit to low-teens growth, which fits if the capacity arrives and is inspected on time. What would change the view: signed CDMO contracts and a regulated-market inspection at Solapur (positive); GPL staying weak in H2 or margins falling back below 30% (negative).
