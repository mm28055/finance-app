# Blue Jet Healthcare — deep research, Sep 2026

Screener code BLUEJET — **standalone** page (consolidated page shows only FY20–21). Price ₹571, market cap ₹10,801 cr, ROCE 26.1%, ROE 19.4% [Screener, 28 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Three product lines: **contrast-media intermediates** (building blocks for iodinated X-ray/CT and gadolinium MRI dyes), **pharma intermediates & API (PI/API)** — mainly an intermediate for bempedoic acid (Esperion's cholesterol drug) supplied under a CDMO arrangement — and **high-intensity sweeteners** (saccharin) [CARE Dec-25; Q4 call].
- FY26: contrast media ₹495 cr (+23%), PI/API ₹298 cr (−35%), sweeteners ₹131 cr (−2%); revenue ₹947 cr (−8%) [Q4 call, official].
- Q4 FY26 PI/API collapsed to **₹2.4 cr** (₹196 cr a year earlier) as the bempedoic customer destocked; contrast media hit ₹193 cr [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 (revenue from operations ex other operating income): sweeteners ₹39.7 cr (+14% YoY), contrast media ₹115.9 cr (+19% YoY, −40% QoQ), PI/API ₹121.0 cr (−43% YoY, back from ₹2.4 cr), others ₹15.2 cr [IP Q1FY27 slide 9].
- **Customer concentration**: two customers were 40.6% and 30.5% of FY26 product sales (41.4% and 34.1% in FY25); two customers are 84% of receivables [AR FY26 notes 35, credit-risk note]. Top-5 customers ~79% of FY25 revenue; bempedoic intermediate ~45% of FY25 income [CARE Dec-25].
- Geography FY26: Norway ₹383 cr, France ₹335 cr, India ₹95 cr, rest of world ₹129 cr — ~87–90% exports [AR FY26 note 35; CARE Dec-25].
- Capacity: contrast media plants at ~70% utilisation; Unit 2 (Ambernath) scaled up; Unit 3 Mahad backward-integration block (~₹250 cr) for a key contrast-media raw material; Vizag greenfield (102.48 acres bought for ₹43.5 cr) [Q1 call; AR FY26 highlights].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 (25 May 2026): contrast media "double-digit growth" in FY27 (customer forecast mid-single-digit for the flagship, plus launches); PI/API to "normalize and grow over the peak" of FY25; FY27 capex ~₹400 cr (Vizag, Mahad completion, Ambernath); Mahad production in H2 FY27; Vizag ~₹1,000 cr over ~3 years; 2 of ~20 RFPs to commercialise in FY27; 2 "lateral entry" blockbuster opportunities needing dedicated Vizag blocks (24–30 months) [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 (3 Aug 2026): PI/API run-rate sustainable for FY27 ("very good visibility" for 3–4 quarters); Mahad slightly **ahead** of schedule (later this calendar year); Vizag FY27 spend ~₹250 cr, company capex "similar" to ₹400 cr; QIP done; Vizag commercialisation only end-FY29 to FY30, full ramp FY31–32 [Q1 call, official].
- Delivery vs Q4: PI/API recovery — delivered; contrast media — dipped QoQ on transit timing; Mahad — ahead; R&D centre — on track. No formal revenue guidance.

## Moat evidence
- Long relationships (4–26 years) with the top contrast-media makers; 20 commercial contrast-media molecules [CARE Dec-25].
- Primary supplier on the bempedoic acid chain; order book "robust" for US, Europe, Japan [Q1 call].
- Backward integration at Mahad for "strategic independence" on a key raw material [Q4 call].
- ~70% of energy from renewables [Q1 call].
- Weakness: sweeteners face import price pressure [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Extreme concentration**: two customers ~71% of product sales; the bempedoic line went from ~₹196 cr to ₹2.4 cr in a single quarter [AR FY26 note 35; Q4 call].
- **Income-tax dispute**: demand of ₹193 cr for AY2020-21 to 2023-24 (disallowed depreciation on goodwill); ₹128.7 cr already provided, ₹16.5 cr paid under protest; interest of ₹64.6 cr carried as contingent liability [AR FY26 note 32].
- **Revenue timing**: revenue booked on arrival — transit delays swing quarters by ₹20–30 cr; auditor's key audit matter is revenue recognition/cut-off [Q1 call; AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Execution risk**: Vizag ₹1,000 cr is larger than the whole current gross block and only earns from FY30 [Q1 call].
- **Raw-material pass-through lag**: gross margin −3 pts in Q1 [Q1 call].
- Auditor opinion unmodified [AR FY26].
- Related parties: director pay (Shiven Arora ₹5.0 cr, Akshay Arora ₹3.6 cr); promoter-controlled entities (Blue Circle group) listed [AR FY26 note 36] — no large trading flows found.
- A key contrast-media customer received a US FDA warning letter at a formulation plant; management sees no offtake impact so far [Q4 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- PI/API went from ₹2.4 cr (Q4) to ₹121 cr (Q1); contrast media reversed from a record ₹193 cr to ₹116 cr.
- New: QIP ₹800 cr; Vizag timeline made explicit (FY30 revenue); Mahad ahead of plan; 4 NCE programmes in chronic therapy "entering execution".
- Raw-material inflation emerged as a margin headwind.

## My read
A very profitable niche intermediates maker (30–37% margins, ~26% ROCE) whose revenue swings violently with two customers' ordering — FY26 fell 8% and TTM sales are down 28%. The near term looks better (PI/API back, Mahad soon), but the big growth bet (Vizag, ₹1,000 cr) earns nothing before FY30, and the ₹800 cr QIP diluted holders by ~8%. The price needs sustained mid-teens growth; that requires the bempedoic chain to keep growing and at least one lateral entry or NCE to scale. What would change my view: a named new long-term contract (positive); another destocking or a lost contrast-media contract (negative); an adverse ruling on the ₹193 cr tax demand.
