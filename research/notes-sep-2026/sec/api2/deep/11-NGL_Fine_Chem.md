# NGL Fine-Chem (NGLFINE) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Note: NGL held no Q1 FY27 call (presentation only, 17 Aug 2026). The two latest calls are Q4 FY26 (25 May 2026) and Q2 FY26 (Nov 2025).

## Business and segments
- India's leading veterinary (animal-health) API maker: ~45 APIs (up from ~20 three years ago), mostly for farm animals (dewormers, anti-parasitics), plus companion-animal products such as fluralaner (commercial) and afoxolaner (technology ready, patent expires in ~2–2.5 years). Plants at Tarapur and Navi Mumbai; wholly owned subsidiary Macrotech Polychem (intermediates; a "material subsidiary" in FY26). [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- Mix Q1 FY27: animal API 95%, human API 3%, intermediates 1%, formulations ~0%. [IP Q1FY27 slide 6]
- Concentration is rising: top 10 products 68% of Q1 FY27 sales (63% in Q1 FY26); top 10 customers 38% (29% in Q4 FY26); top 3 customers 18%. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Regulated markets: 3 European CEPs granted, 3 under review (expected H2 CY2026), 2 more being filed; 5 ASMF/DMS registered, 4 under review; US: 5 VMFs filed since H2 CY2025, 6 more planned in 2026. US FDA audit only when a customer files NGL as a source — "can be anytime this year or it may be even next year". Europe sales to start in FY27, US in FY28. [Q4 call]
- Competition: ~20 Indian and ~40 Chinese makers; "no reduction in the competitive" intensity. [Q4 call; stored profile]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly revenue (₹cr): Jun-25 104, Sep-25 120, Dec-25 128, Mar-26 149, Jun-26 139. Operating profit 11 / 17 / 22 / 21 / 23; OPM 11% / 14% / 18% / 14% / 17%. Net profit 9 / 10 / 16 / 13 / 18. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 company figures: revenue ₹139.2cr (+34% YoY, −7% QoQ), EBITDA ₹23.3cr (16.7%, +620 bps YoY), PAT ₹18.4cr (+99%). Management: part of the margin came from **inventory gains** on price rises and a **₹2.5cr forex gain** (vs ₹4.5cr forex loss in Q4); "we expect margins to moderate over the coming quarters". [IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹339cr → FY25 ₹368cr → FY26 ₹501cr (+36%); operating profit 54 / 35 / 80; OPM 16% / 10% / 16%; net profit 41 / 21 / 48. FY21 OPM was 31% — margins remain far below the post-COVID peak. ROCE 20% / 10% / 19%. [Screener]
- Other income is large relative to profit (₹17cr TTM vs ₹57cr net profit) — includes treasury and forex MTM. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- Volume-led growth: FY26 growth "primarily driven by higher volumes"; 47 new customer registrations in Q4 (counted per country). [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q2 FY26 call (Nov 2025):** EU revenue up to ~₹30cr in FY26; Phase II completion Q4 FY26, commissioning Q1 FY27; new plant EBITDA break-even in FY28; margin 15–18%; debt peak "worst case max ₹95cr, best ~₹75cr"; new-plant opex ₹1.2–1.5cr/month; extra depreciation ₹6–8cr. [Q2 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (May 2026):** Phase II commissioning slipped to early Q2 FY27 (gas and labour shortages), commercial production H2 FY27 kept; capex raised to ₹210cr (from ₹160cr), ₹182.75cr spent; FY27 maintenance capex ₹15–20cr; margin target 15–18% ("very close"); regulated business 3–5 points higher margin; Europe revenue in FY27, US FY28; Phase II peak revenue ₹350–400cr over 3–4 years; ₹150cr/quarter run-rate to "stabilise" in 1–2 quarters. [Q4 call]
- **Delivery:** EU ~₹30cr for FY26 not visible (management now says Europe starts "in the current year"); debt reached ₹123cr at March 2026 vs "max ₹95cr" promised in Nov 2025; Q1 FY27 revenue ₹139cr (below the ₹150cr run-rate); margin 16.7% (inside 15–18%, flattered by one-offs). **Verdict: mixed — margin met, revenue run-rate and regulated-market timelines slipped.** [Q2 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; Screener]

## Capex and capacity
- Phase I (validation clean room + intermediates plant) running; Phase I plus outsourcing (15–20% of production outsourced) support ~₹150cr/quarter. [Q4 call; Q2 call]
- Phase II greenfield at Tarapur: five clean rooms; cost ₹210cr (₹160cr originally — ~₹20cr automation/digitisation, rest metal-cost inflation; the extra spend "may not result in additional sales"); commissioning in Q2 FY27 "during the current quarter" per Aug-26 deck; commercial H2 FY27; full use in 3–4 years; peak revenue ₹350–400cr (~1.7–1.9x asset turn). Initially for existing products/ROW and Europe until US approval. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Capital WIP ₹95cr at March 2026. [Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹123cr at March 2026 (₹34cr FY24; ₹78cr FY25); investments ₹39cr; net debt ~₹80cr. [Screener]
- Ratings: CRISIL BBB+ (outlook revised Negative → Stable, Mar 2026), A2; ICRA BBB+ (Stable), A2. [AR FY26 Directors' report]
- Cash from operations ₹43cr (FY26, 73% of operating profit); free cash flow −₹41cr (FY25), −₹43cr (FY26) because of capex. [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 95, inventory days 106, payable days 97; cash conversion cycle 104 days (FY26, up from 81). [Screener]
- Dividend payout ~2%; management has "not thought about" dividend policy. [Screener; Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- Niche position: one of few Indian veterinary API specialists; early mover in fluralaner generic API (price erosion has "settled down"); afoxolaner ready ahead of patent expiry. [Q4 call]
- Regulated approvals are the future moat — once in a US customer's ANADA, switching is costly — but none has earned revenue yet. Top-5 global animal-health companies still not customers ("some companies do not want to change their supply chain so easily"). [Q4 call]
- Pricing: only partial pass-through of 2026 cost spike; Chinese rivals held prices in March–April before raising. [Q4 call]
- Management does not expect a return to 20%+ margins "in the next two, three years" given Chinese and Indian overcapacity. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **SEBI settlement:** the company and promoters paid ₹54.4 lakh and ₹12.6 lakh to settle a show-cause notice for incorrect shareholding-pattern disclosures from December 2002 to June 2019 and SAST lapses; BSE/NSE fines for late related-party reporting (FY24); company did not inform exchanges of an independent director's cessation. [AR FY26 Corporate Governance report]
- **Promoter pay and related parties:** MD Rahul Nachane and WTD/CFO Rajesh Lawande each drew ~₹1.2cr salary plus ₹1.62cr profit commission in FY26 (commission up 2.6x); VP Ahaan Nachane (promoter family) ₹0.47cr — ~₹6cr total ≈ 12% of FY26 PAT; rent and professional fees paid to promoter-linked Nupur Remedies and to the promoters. [AR FY26 note 41]
- **Subsidiary dealings:** purchases from Macrotech Polychem ₹21.4cr and processing ₹13.7cr; loans to it (interest ₹0.6cr). [AR FY26 note 41]
- Auditor: key audit matters = revenue recognition, inventory valuation (complex overhead absorption) and expected credit loss on receivables. Contingent liabilities tiny (₹0.18cr PF claim). [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 40]
- Earnings quality: Q1 FY27 helped by inventory gains and forex reversal; other income volatile. [IP Q1FY27]
- Execution: capex overrun (+31%), Phase II delay, debt above guidance, rising customer concentration. [Q4 call; Screener]

## What changed between the two calls (Q2 FY26 Nov-25 → Q4 FY26 May-26; plus Q1 FY27 deck)
- Tone turned "the most bullish… over the past three, four years" (analyst) as revenue jumped 57% in Q4.
- Timelines slipped: commissioning Q1 → early Q2 FY27; EU revenue from FY26 → FY27; US → FY28.
- Capex ₹160cr → ₹210cr; debt above the ₹95cr cap.
- Q1 FY27 deck: first explicit warning that margins will moderate as price gains fade.

## My read
NGL is out of a three-year price slump: FY26 revenue +36% and margin back to ~16%, driven by volume and a doubled product list. The next leg — Phase II and US/EU sales — keeps slipping, cost 31% more than planned and was funded more by debt than promised, while management itself warns Q1's 16.7% margin will moderate. The price needs roughly 15–20% annual earnings growth, which requires Phase II to fill and first regulated sales in FY28. A US FDA inspection being triggered, three CEPs approved in late 2026 and ₹150cr+ quarters would support that; another delay, margin back toward 12–13%, or more governance lapses (disclosure fines, rising promoter commission) would weaken it.
