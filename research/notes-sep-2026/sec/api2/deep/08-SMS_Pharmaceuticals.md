# SMS Pharmaceuticals (SMSPHARMA) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Note: SMS held no Q1 FY27 earnings call (only a presentation, 1 Aug 2026). The two latest calls are Q4 FY26 (27 May 2026) and Q2 FY26 (11 Nov 2025); Q1 FY27 data comes from the presentation, Screener and CARE (Sep 2026).

## Business and segments
- Hyderabad-based API maker, 36 years old, 55+ APIs in 14 therapy areas, 140+ DMFs; two commercial plants (Unit II and Unit VII, Vizag) approved by USFDA, PMDA Japan, EU, ANVISA; Unit II also TGA. One reported segment (APIs). [AR FY26 MD&A; CARE Sep-26]
- Product mix is shifting: ARV (anti-HIV, e.g. tenofovir) 20.8% of revenue in FY25 → 28.4% FY26 → 31.5% Q1 FY27; anti-inflammatory (ibuprofen) 20.0% FY26 → 22.7% Q1 FY27; anti-diabetic (sitagliptin, gliptins/gliflozins) shrinking after the sitagliptin patent expiry. Ibuprofen alone "roughly around 20%" of revenue. [CARE Sep-26; Q4 call]
- Customers: top five ~53% of FY26 revenue and 50% of Q1 FY27; largest single customer 28% of FY26 revenue (across several products). Named clients: Lupin, Granules, Dr. Reddy's, Chemo. [CARE Sep-26; Q4 call]
- Geography: exports ~70% (Q4 call); annual report says international markets generate 85% of revenue and 88% comes from regulated markets. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Ibuprofen capacity 500 tonnes/month, ~80% used; FY26 run-rate ~350 t/month at H1 vs 450 t/month target. [Q4 call; Q2 call]
- Associate **VKT Pharma** (34.83% held; formulation company) and new subsidiary **SMS Peptides** (99.99%). JV with Spain's Chemo for first-to-market APIs (worked in anti-diabetics). [AR FY26; Q4 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly revenue (₹cr): Jun-25 196, Sep-25 242, Dec-25 210, Mar-26 238, Jun-26 207. Operating profit 39 / 48 / 44 / 40 / 41; OPM 20% / 20% / 21% / 17% / 20%. Net profit 20 / 25 / 23 / 33 / 21. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹207cr (+6% YoY, −13% QoQ), EBITDA ₹41cr (+4%), margin ~20%, PAT excluding associate ₹20cr (+8%); EPS ₹2.23 (−3%). [IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹709cr → FY25 ₹783cr → FY26 ₹887cr (+13%); operating profit 117 → 139 → 171; OPM 16% / 18% / 19%; net profit 50 → 69 → 102. FY26 PAT includes ₹14cr share of VKT profit (₹12cr of it in Q4); excluding VKT, PAT ₹88cr (+28%). [Screener; Q4 call]
- FY26 gross profit ₹303cr (34% margin); Q4 gross margin fell ~2 points QoQ on war-driven solvent costs. [Q4 call]
- ROCE 12% (FY24), 13% (FY25), 13% (FY26). [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Q2 FY26 call (Nov 2025):** FY26 ~20% revenue growth and 20% EBITDA margin; ₹280cr capex incl. ₹30cr greenfield land; ARV visibility for 3 quarters; ibuprofen to 450 t/month. [Q2 call]
- **Delivery for FY26:** revenue +13% (missed 20%), EBITDA margin 19–20% (met). [Screener; Q4 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (May 2026):** FY27 revenue +15% ("prudent", may exceed if Middle East logistics settle); EBITDA margin to improve on 20%, aim 22% (all-time high); FY28–FY29 growth "at least 20–25%"; high-value product share from ~47% to ~60%; 10 DMF/CEP filings in FY27 and 10 in FY28; five Chemo JV products to launch in FY27; peptides and CDMO updates "after the next 2 quarters"; VKT contribution "sustainable and strengthen into FY27". [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery:** revenue +6% (well below the 15% pace), margin 20% (flat, not 22%), 4 filings done (on track for 10). **Verdict: missed on growth, held on margin.** [IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- ₹280.78cr project: ibuprofen capacity 500 → 800 t/month, backward integration of ibuprofen intermediates, and a multi-API block (A2) for 4–5 new high-margin APIs; 87% brownfield, rest greenfield land. Funding: ₹180cr Exim Bank term loan + ₹100.78cr internal cash. [CARE Sep-26; AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- Spent ₹121cr (43%) by 30 June 2026 (₹35cr loan, ₹86cr own cash). Physical completion March 2027; **commercial operation July 2027** (CARE) — management said revenue "from FY28". Payback not given; Q2 call cited 1.5x net asset turnover as the model. [CARE Sep-26; Q4 call; Q2 call]
- Earlier ~₹150cr backward-integration spend over 12–18 months to Nov 2025 (intermediates made in-house, "reduces dependence… particularly from China"). [Q2 call]
- Peptides: ₹8cr invested in FY26; board approved loan of up to ₹50cr to SMS Peptides; commercial from FY29. R&D team raised to 200. [IP Q1FY27]
- Capital commitments ₹44cr at March 2026 (₹10cr a year earlier). [AR FY26 note 48]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹365cr at March 2026 (₹311cr FY25; ₹254cr FY23); free cash ~₹45cr. Gearing 0.46x; CARE projects debt ₹503cr and gearing 0.56x by March 2027; repayments ~₹43cr due in FY27. [Screener; CARE Sep-26]
- CARE revised outlook from Positive to Stable (Sep 2026) because the debt-funded capex "was not contemplated" at the prior review. Rating CARE A / A1; limits raised to ₹570cr. [CARE Sep-26]
- Cash from operations only ₹59cr in FY26 (49% of operating profit; Screener) — CARE cites ₹68.7cr; free cash flow negative for three years (−₹2cr FY24, −₹42cr FY25, −₹71cr FY26). [Screener; CARE Sep-26]
- Working capital heavy: inventory days 260 (Screener) / 161 (CARE); cash conversion cycle 260 days; operating cycle 178 days FY26, seen ~195 days in FY27. [Screener; CARE Sep-26]
- Dividend payout ~4%. Promoters converted warrants issued at ₹127 each (90 lakh warrants), lifting promoter holding from 64.67% to 68.07%. [Screener; AR FY26 Directors' report]

## Moat evidence
- Cost position via backward integration: management says without it "we wouldn't have been able to survive in the market" through the 2026 input spike; margin rose from 10% (FY23) to 19–20%. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Leading share in ibuprofen for Europe from the automated Vizag plant ("Asia's largest automated" ibuprofen facility, per company); first-to-market with Chemo in Europe in anti-diabetics. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q2 call]
- ARV share gains on "superior cost structure", tied to customers' tender wins — visibility only ~3 quarters. [Q4 call; Q2 call]
- Weak points: anti-diabetic price erosion after patent expiry shows first-mover gains fade; customer concentration high (top 5 ~50%). [Q2 call; CARE Sep-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Income-tax demand ₹45.6cr** (AY 2023-24), from GST-return turnover mismatches; admitted as a "high-pitched assessment"; appeal pending; not provided. About 6% of net worth. [AR FY26 note 47]
- **Related parties:** sales to associate VKT Pharma ₹54.7cr in FY26 (₹39.6cr FY25) — VKT's profit then flows back as ₹14cr share of profit; purchases of ~₹17cr from Haleos Labs (formerly SMS Lifesciences, promoter-linked); ~₹15cr purchases from other related firms (Raghavendra Engineering, Eswar Coal Movers, Fours Engineering). Executive director pay ₹12.9cr (FY25 ₹10.4cr) — ~13% of FY26 PAT. [AR FY26 note 45; Q4 call]
- **Quality of earnings:** ~14% of FY26 PAT and ~36% of Q4 PAT came from the associate, not from operations. Cash conversion 49%. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter = revenue recognition. Assets of ~₹1,168cr pledged as security for loans (normal bank charge, not promoter share pledge). [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 39]
- Execution: CARE's July 2027 COD is later than management's "FY27" framing; debt peaks before the new capacity earns.
- Customer concentration and tender-based ARV demand. [CARE Sep-26]

## What changed between the two calls (Q2 FY26 Nov-25 → Q4 FY26 May-26; plus Q1 FY27 presentation)
- Growth guidance cut from ~20% (FY26) to 15% (FY27), with the Middle East war as the reason; FY26 ended at 13%.
- Anti-diabetics went from "our largest therapeutic category… steady" (Nov) to a segment management "proactively" moved away from (May).
- New talking points in May: 22% margin ambition, 60% high-value mix, peptides and CDMO (details deferred two quarters), VKT's profit contribution.
- Q1 FY27 (no call): +6% growth only; the Board approved ₹50cr for peptides; CARE then flagged unplanned extra debt.

## My read
SMS has genuinely rebuilt its margin (10% in FY23 to ~20%) through backward integration, and the ARV and ibuprofen engines are working, but growth has slipped two years running against guidance (13% vs 20% in FY26; +6% in Q1 FY27 vs 15% for FY27). The stock prices in a step-up that only arrives when the ₹281cr project starts earning from mid-2027, while debt rises to ~₹500cr, cash conversion is weak (49%) and a slice of profit comes from a related associate that is also a customer. The price needs roughly 20%+ earnings growth for several years; that requires the new block and 800 t/month ibuprofen to fill quickly in FY28. A clean Q2–Q3 FY27 recovery toward 15% growth, a win on the ₹45.6cr tax demand and on-time July 2027 start would improve the view; another quarter of single-digit growth or further capex creep would weaken it.
