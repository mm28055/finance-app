# Shilpa Medicare — deep research, Sep 2026

Screener code SHILPAMED (consolidated). Price ₹1,013, market cap ₹19,821 cr, ROCE 10.9%, ROE 9.0% [Screener, 28 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Three verticals. **API** (oncology and non-oncology bulk drugs, peptides, polymers, payload/linkers, and "specialty CDMO") ₹260 cr in Q1 FY27 (+15% YoY) incl. captive sales to its own formulations; third-party API ~₹224 cr, of which specialty CDMO ~₹36 cr [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 slide 11]. **Formulations** (oncology injectables, oral films, transdermal patches, 505(b)(2) products — a US route for modified versions of existing drugs) ₹198 cr (+>100%; +112% ex-licensing). **Biologics** (biosimilars, CDMO, recombinant albumin) ₹52 cr (+42%) [Q1 call].
- Q1 FY27 formulation split: US ₹45 cr (from ₹4 cr a year ago), Europe ₹57 cr (+84%), India ₹24 cr (+179%, NorUDCA launch), RoW ₹43 cr, licensing/services ₹29 cr [IP Q1FY27 slide 15].
- FY26: API ₹985 cr (+16–17%, incl. captive), formulations ₹618 cr (+30%; ex-licensing +75%), biologics ₹149–150 cr (~+100%) [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]. API CDMO ~₹110 cr in FY26 [Q4 call, official]. Licensing income ₹120 cr in FY26 (₹190 cr in FY25), 7% of revenue [India Ratings Jul-26].
- Mix by geography FY26 (India Ratings): India 46%, EU 20%, emerging markets 21%, US 5%; regulated-market exports 26% [India Ratings Jul-26].
- One customer above 10% of revenue in FY26 (two in FY25) [AR FY26 consolidated note 38].
- Capacity: formulation plants at Jadcherla and Bengaluru; API at Raichur; biologics/albumin at Dharwad; >50% of formulation API needs made in-house [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]. Management would not give utilisation on the call ("connect with Monish") [Q1 call].
- Pipeline: NorUDCA (first-in-class fatty-liver drug, India launch Nov 2025, three marketing partners + own brand); Rotigotine patch (EU approved, US filed, launch FY28); Abraxane, enzalutamide, abiraterone US launches FY28; Aflibercept biosimilar India FY27; Nivolumab with Orion for Europe; recombinant albumin with Orion for EU (Phase 3); 25+ CDMO NCE programs, 3 late-stage for FY28; 20+ CDMO customers [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / margin / net profit: Jun-25 321 / 91 / 28% / 47; Sep-25 370 / 108 / 29% / 44; Dec-25 410 / 114 / 28% / 45; Mar-26 437 / 120 / 27% / 108; Jun-26 466 / 136 / 29% / 101 [Screener]. Company-reported "total revenue" is ₹469 cr for Q1 and EBITDA ₹139 cr (30%) [IP Q1FY27].
- Annual: FY24 sales 1,146, OP 239 (21%), PAT 32; FY25 1,281, 319 (25%), 78; FY26 1,535, 435 (28%), 243. TTM PAT 297 [Screener].
- Profit quality: Mar-26 PAT includes a ₹30 cr exceptional gain (sale of 31% of Sravathi) and ₹18 cr share of associate Maaia's profit (a write-back of prior losses); adjusted Q4 PAT ₹87 cr [Q4 call, official]. Jun-26 PAT ₹101 cr was lifted by a **negative tax rate** (deferred-tax reversal on moving to the new tax regime); operating PBT ₹92 cr; tax to normalise ~25% [Q1 call].
- Gross margin 71% in Q1 FY27 vs 76% a year ago (higher licensing then; raw-material inflation now, only partly passed on) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- 3-year sales CAGR 14%; 10-year 8%; ROE 5-year average 4% [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Company gives no revenue guidance [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Q4 FY26 (22 May 2026): API "steady growth" in FY27; formulations "good growth"; licensing income similar to FY26; 35% EBITDA margin "an ambitious target"; capex "no significant growth" over FY26's ₹361 cr; Unicycive (OLC) approval expected in FY27 with major revenue in FY28; albumin EU IMPD slipped to H1 FY27; Rotigotine EU launch FY27; Aflibercept launch FY27 [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 (5 Aug 2026): margins "in a similar range" (~30%), "conservative and overdeliver"; Unicycive got a **US FDA complete response letter (CRL)** and plans to refile in Q3 CY26; three CDMO NCEs commercial in FY28; peptide block commissioning end-FY27; "for at least the next 3 years, we don't need any significant capex" in biologics/albumin [Q1 call].
- Delivery vs Q4: record revenue and margin — delivered; Unicycive approval — missed (CRL, partner-driven); Rotigotine EU — the presentation says "gearing up for 1HFY27 launch", no launch confirmed on the call; US launch FY28 unchanged [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹361 cr (FY25 ₹225 cr), mostly API, CDMO, albumin; Q1 FY27 ₹114 cr [IP Q1FY27 slide 9; Q4 call].
- Gross block ₹2,328 cr at Jun-26 vs ₹1,587 cr in FY23 [IP Q1FY27]. CWIP ₹884 cr at Mar-26 (₹822 cr FY25) [Screener].
- Intangible assets under development (capitalised R&D/filings) about ₹450 cr at Mar-26, of which roughly ₹200 cr older than three years (my sum of the ageing table) [AR FY26 consolidated note 46]; India Ratings also cites ~₹4.5 bn [India Ratings Jul-26].
- Capital commitments jumped to ₹119 cr from ₹36 cr — more capex already contracted [AR FY26 consolidated note 31].
- Medium-term plan per India Ratings: ₹200 cr modernisation plus ₹50–75 cr maintenance a year, internally funded [India Ratings Jul-26].
- Payback: management says the "reinvesting is largely behind us"; biologics and NBE assets turned ROCE-positive in the last 1–1.5 years but still at a low level; adjusted ROCE excluding them 18.3% (TTM) vs group ~12.5% [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹660 cr at Mar-26 (₹588 cr FY25; ₹937 cr FY24) [Screener]. Net debt ₹613 cr (₹552 cr FY25) [Q4 call]. Net debt/EBITDA 1.3x (TTM) from 6.7x in FY23 [IP Q1FY27].
- Rating upgraded to IND AA-/Stable from A+ (Jul 2026). Working-capital lines ~91.5% utilised on average; cash ₹41 cr; repayments ₹60 cr a year FY27–29; free cash flow −₹20 cr in FY26 [India Ratings Jul-26].
- High-cost NCDs (12.5–13% from OCM Asia Credit) were mostly prepaid (₹75 cr in FY26) and the rest moved to bank loans; they were secured by a **pledge of 100% of SPLL shares** plus a parent guarantee [AR FY26 consolidated borrowings note; India Ratings Jul-26].
- Cash from operations ₹342 cr FY26 (93% of operating profit) but FCF negative every year for 12 years (−₹28 cr FY26) [Screener].
- Screener FY26: inventory 291 days, debtors 123 days, payables 113, cash conversion cycle 301 days (342 in FY25) — very heavy working capital [Screener]. India Ratings (sales basis) 176 days [India Ratings Jul-26].
- Share count doubled (equity ₹10 → ₹20 cr): a 1:1 bonus issue [Screener]. Dividend payout 5% [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Regulatory depth: 290 API DMFs (55 USFDA) [IP Q1FY27]; 109 US/EU formulation filings (85 approved) [India Ratings Jul-26].
- Differentiated products: NorUDCA claimed as first approval globally in its indication; EU approval for Rotigotine patch; first EU approval for multiple strengths of tadalafil oral film [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Integration: API → formulations (>50% captive), and biologics clone-to-fill-finish; partners include Orion (Nivolumab, albumin) [Q1 call].
- Pricing discipline: exited US azacitidine when price fell [Q4 call].
- Weak points: 10-year sales CAGR only 8% and ROCE below 12% for eight years — the moat has not yet shown up in returns [Screener].

## Risks and red flags
- **US FDA at Jadcherla (formulations)**: import alert since Feb 2021 (key products exempted); Nov 2025 inspection gave **eight Form 483 observations relating to data integrity**; awaiting re-inspection. Bengaluru got five procedural observations in May 2026 [India Ratings Jul-26]. The auditor's key audit matter on impairment of intangibles specifically cites the "USFDA warning letter situation" risk to approvals [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report].
- **Associate profit on unaudited accounts**: the ₹18.2 cr share of profit from one foreign associate (Maaia) was based on unaudited figures to Dec 2025 because its FY26 accounts were not finalised [AR FY26 auditor's "Other Matters"].
- Nine subsidiaries audited by other auditors had net losses of ₹9.8 cr on revenue of ₹26.7 cr [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Capitalised R&D** ~₹450 cr, ~₹200 cr aged over three years — write-off risk if products fail [AR FY26 note 46].
- **Partner dependency**: Unicycive CRL; NorUDCA and biosimilar launches through partners [Q1 call].
- **Earnings quality**: FY26 and Q1 FY27 profits boosted by exceptional gain, associate write-back and a tax reversal [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Contingent claims ₹33 cr; bank guarantees ₹3 cr [AR FY26 note 31].
- Promoter group fell from 44.2% to 40.1% in Dec 2025 quarter — due to reclassification of Suraj and Priya Inani from promoter group to public (postal ballot Oct–Nov 2025), not a market sale as far as found [Screener; AR FY26 corporate governance]. Pledge level not verified.
- MD remuneration ₹10.3 cr in FY26 (₹8.0 cr FY25) [AR FY26 related-party note].
- US tariff threat on generics: US sales ~₹45 cr a quarter; management says all complex products [Q1 call].

## My read
The business is improving: four straight record quarters, margin up from 21% (FY24) to ~29%, net leverage down to 1.3x and a rating upgrade. But reported profit flatters the core — exceptional gains, an associate write-back on unaudited figures and a tax reversal — and returns are still only ~11% ROCE with a 300-day cash cycle and ₹450 cr of capitalised R&D. The price assumes the FY28 wave (Abraxane, Rotigotine US, enzalutamide, three CDMO NCEs, biosimilars) lands on time, which depends on partners and on clearing the Jadcherla FDA issue. What would change my view: a clean FDA re-inspection and positive FCF would raise confidence; another CRL or impairment of capitalised R&D would cut it.
