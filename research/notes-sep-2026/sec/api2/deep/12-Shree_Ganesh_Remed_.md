# Shree Ganesh Remedies (SGRL, BSE 540737) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Note: No Q1 FY27 call was held (presentation only, 13 Aug 2026). The two latest calls are Q4 FY26 (15 May 2026) and Q2 FY26 (Nov 2025). BSE-only listing; NSE listing "planned" with no timeline. Screener's consolidated page has gaps (FY22–FY23 missing), so figures below use the standalone page (almost identical; one small US subsidiary).

## Business and segments
- Ankleshwar (Gujarat) maker of multi-step (4–12 step) intermediates using ~10 chemistries — core strengths halogenation/chlorination, Grignard and reduction. Two verticals: pharmaceutical intermediates (now "below 60–65%" of revenue; mostly older generic molecules sold in Europe and India) and speciality/fine chemicals incl. custom manufacturing (CRAMS) for agrochemical, pharma-innovator and Japanese electronics customers. Speciality ₹11.15cr in Q2 FY26 (₹22.64cr in H1). [AR FY26 MD&A; Q2 call]
- Explicitly not an API or formulation maker: "SGR is not going to be involved in any of the finished pharmaceutical products." Positions itself as a second source where "there is only one manufacturer from the Japan side and… China side". [Q4 call]
- Exports ₹63.1cr in FY26 (FY25 ₹63.7cr) ≈ 58% of revenue; Europe ~60% of revenue, US ~5% (Q2 call). Top 5 molecules ~60%, top 10 ~70–75% of sales. About 15–20% of raw materials imported. [AR FY26 Directors' report; Q2 call; Q4 call]
- Capacity: Unit 1 blocks, consolidated utilisation 60–70% (batch plant max 80–85%); Block 8 at ~50%; pilot plant (automation, flow chemistry) running; Block 7 (~₹34cr) due Q2 FY27; Dahej land with common utilities under construction. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]

## Guidance history
- **Q2 FY26 call (Nov 2025):** FY26 a "consolidation" year, revenue roughly flat; EBITDA 28–30% in FY26, 26–28% sustainable; Block 8 to 50–60% by year-end; Block 9 (later called Block 7) commercial H2 FY27; Dahej ~2 years away. [Q2 call]
- **FY26 delivery:** revenue flat (₹109cr, met), margin 32% (beat), Block 8 ~50% (met low end). [AR FY26; Q4 call]
- **Q4 FY26 call (May 2026):** FY27 "should be the year in which the groundwork… begins to translate into more visible business outcomes"; CRAMS pilots completed, commercial trials next, commercial production "in the later half of this year" but "no major impact in this year"; Block 7 commercial in Q2 FY27; sustainable margin 26–28%; base generic business "pretty much at the plateau"; Dahej to start after ~1 year, 16–20 months to build. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 (presentation):** a "challenging quarter"; European customers deferred orders; the company's marketing-and-manufacturing agreement with one EU distributor "reached its automatic termination owing to non-performance"; volumes to take "a couple of quarters" to restore; management "moderate[s] our earlier guidance of FY27 being a year of non-linear growth"; CRAMS in South-East Asia to contribute from H2 FY27. **Verdict: missed — guidance cut one quarter after being given.** [IP Q1FY27]

- Pilot plant commissioned FY26; Block 8 commissioned FY25–FY26. Dahej: common utilities under construction; plant (bulk speciality/commodity products) only after Ankleshwar is full. [Q4 call; Q2 call]

- Cash from operations ₹24cr (FY26, 82% of operating profit); free cash flow ₹7cr. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Chemistry depth and multi-step capability: "for some of the molecules which we practice in, there is no direct competition in India." Customer audits and approvals create switching costs once a molecule is registered. [Q4 call]
- High margins (32–36% OPM for three years) show niche pricing on the pharma side — but domestic generic intermediates face "more and more players", and the stored profile notes customers dual-source with China. [Q4 call; Screener]
- CRAMS contribution in FY26 was "no significant percentage"; project names and sizes withheld under NDAs — the moat is unproven in revenue. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Distributor termination:** the EU marketing and manufacturing agreement ended for non-performance — a key European channel lost; recovery "a couple of quarters". [IP Q1FY27]
- **Concentration:** top 10 molecules 70–75% of sales; Europe ~60%; CRAMS relies on a handful of projects whose timing depends on customers' own regulatory approvals ("easily six months is getting stretched"). [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- **Disclosure:** stopped naming or counting CRAMS projects; refuses quarter guidance; tiny, illiquid float (promoters 72.8%; ~7,800 shareholders; no institutional holders beyond 0.5% DII). [Q4 call; Screener]
- **Governance:** Gunjan Kothia (promoter, runs business development) resigned as whole-time director on 15 Sep 2025 but still leads calls; KMP pay ₹2.6cr (FY25 ₹3.2cr) ≈ 15% of FY26 PAT; small dealings with relatives' firms (Ganesh Corporation, Shree Ganesh FIBC) and a promoter-linked testing association. [AR FY26 note 39]
- Auditor reported **no key audit matters**; contingent liabilities ₹1.7cr (₹5.2cr FY25), mostly disputed tax. [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 31]
- Stock price: P/E ~56x on shrinking earnings.

## My read
SGRL is a small, high-margin chemistry shop whose revenue has not grown in four years, and Q1 FY27 was a clear break for the worse: revenue −42%, a key EU distributor lost, and management cut its FY27 growth talk three months after promising "visible outcomes". The CRAMS pipeline may be real, but it is undisclosed and gated by customers' approvals, so the timing keeps sliding. At a P/E in the mid-50s the price needs the CRAMS projects and Block 7 to roughly double revenue within 2–3 years; nothing in the filings yet shows that. I would reconsider on two quarters back at ₹30cr+ revenue, a named commercial CRAMS supply, and Block 7 running; further order deferrals or margin falling below the 26–28% band would confirm the downside.
