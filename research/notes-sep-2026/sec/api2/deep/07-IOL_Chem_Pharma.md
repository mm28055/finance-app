# IOL Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals (IOLCP) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

## Business and segments
- Two segments: **Pharmaceuticals** (APIs — ibuprofen plus paracetamol, metformin, clopidogrel, pantoprazole, fenofibrate, levetiracetam, lamotrigine, losartan, minoxidil) and **Chemicals** (ethyl acetate, acetic anhydride, iso-butyl benzene/IBB, acetyl chloride, MCA, new triacetin). One integrated site at Barnala, Punjab. [AR FY26 note 39; IP Q1FY27]
- FY26 segment revenue: pharma ₹1,396cr (FY25 ₹1,212cr), EBIT ₹172cr (12.3% margin; FY25 ₹126cr); chemicals net of inter-segment ₹923cr (FY25 ₹867cr), EBIT only ₹30cr (3.3%; FY25 ₹15cr). Pharma earns about 85% of segment profit on 60% of sales. [IP Q1FY27 slide 5]
- Q1 FY27: pharma revenue ₹469.5cr (+43% YoY from ₹329cr), EBIT ₹68.8cr (14.7%; Q1 FY26 ₹40.1cr, Q4 FY26 ₹62.2cr); chemicals ₹286.8cr (+29% from ₹222cr), EBIT ₹17.3cr (6.0%; Q1 FY26 ₹5.9cr). [IP Q1FY27 slide 5]
- Product concentration: ibuprofen ~34% of FY26 sales (FY25 35%); ibuprofen + ethyl acetate ~68% of sales. Non-ibuprofen APIs rose from 18% of pharma revenue in FY21 to 37% in FY26 and 43% in Q1 FY27 (36% in Q1 FY26), growing 67% YoY. [CARE Jul-26; IP Q1FY27 slide 10; Q1 call]
- Capacity: API 30,726 MTPA; chemicals 1,82,400 MTPA (ethyl acetate 1,20,000; acetic anhydride 32,000; IBB 12,000; triacetin 6,000). Ibuprofen 12,000 MTPA; paracetamol 10,800 MTPA (tripled from 3,600); metformin 7,200 MTPA; pantoprazole 276 MTPA; clopidogrel 240 MTPA. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A; CARE Jul-26]
- Utilisation: chemicals ~91% in FY26 (FY25 96%), pharma ~75% (FY25 63%). Ibuprofen, metformin, clopidogrel, fenofibrate run 85–95%; paracetamol ~55% of the enlarged capacity. [CARE Jul-26; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Geography: exports 28.5% of Q1 FY27 revenue (24.4% a year earlier); ~24% of FY26 sales, spread across Europe, LatAm, Asia, Middle East; 80+ countries. Top-10 customers ~19% of FY26 sales — low concentration. [Q1 call; CARE Jul-26]
- Regulatory base: 14 US DMFs, 21 CEPs (Europe); USFDA-inspected ibuprofen site; China NMPA approval for ibuprofen (Apr 2025) and clopidogrel (2026); ANVISA (Brazil) across all API units; new CEP for fenofibrate process II (25 Sep 2026) and EU-REACH registration for triacetin (23 Sep 2026). [IP Q1FY27; CARE Jul-26; BSE filings Sep-26]

- Quarterly revenue (₹cr): Jun-25 552, Sep-25 568, Dec-25 580, Mar-26 619, Jun-26 756. Operating profit 62 / 57 / 57 / 92 / 103; OPM 11% / 10% / 10% / 15% / 14%. Net profit 34 / 30 / 21 / 53 / 64. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 as reported by the company: revenue ₹756cr (+37%), EBITDA ₹111.7cr incl. other income (+61%), margin 14.6% (12.4%), PAT ₹64.5cr (+90%). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹2,133cr → FY25 ₹2,079cr → FY26 ₹2,319cr (+11.5%); operating profit 231 → 202 → 270; OPM 11% / 10% / 12%; net profit 134 → 101 → 138. TTM revenue ₹2,524cr, net profit ₹168cr. FY22 revenue was already ₹2,184cr — the business went sideways for four years before FY26. [Screener]
- Return on capital employed 12% (FY24), 9% (FY25), 11% (FY26) — modest for an A+ rated, cash-rich company. [Screener]
- Unit economics: management will not split price vs volume but said "the majority" of Q1 pharma growth was volume, with some better export realisation; a war-driven spike in ethyl acetate and paracetamol prices in March–April 2026 has since flattened or softened. [Q1 call; Sep call]

## Guidance history
- **Q3 call (Feb 2026):** FY27 revenue +10–15% and PAT +15–20% (as recalled by an analyst on the Q4 call). [Q4 call]
- **Q4 call (22 May 2026):** FY27 target around ₹2,700cr; "15% growth… may be 1–2% more or less"; EBITDA margin ~14–14.5%; medium-term "mid-teens to high-teens" growth; paracetamol utilisation 55% → 70–75% in FY27, full by FY28; non-ibuprofen regulated-market exports to reach ~25%. Greenfield on the ~100-acre new land: ₹1,200–1,400cr over 4–5 years at ₹200–250cr a year, from internal cash, first output in 4–8 quarters. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 call (13 Aug 2026):** raised the tone rather than the number — FY27 revenue growth 15–20% (≈20% said at one point), EBITDA margin 14–15%, exports 25–30%; "we may cross that number. We do not want to upgrade the guidance". New FY28 preview: revenue +15–20%, EBITDA margin 15–17%. Non-ibuprofen target 50–55% of pharma by FY29. [Q1 call]
- **Delivery vs Q4 promises:** Q1 revenue +37% and 14.6% margin both beat the Q4 bar (15%, 14–14.5%). Paracetamol still at ~55% (target 70–75% by March 2027 — not yet visible). Triacetin commissioned mid-May as promised. **Verdict: beating.** [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Special call (11 Sep 2026):** FY27 guidance kept at 15–20%. Announced ₹495cr of new projects (below) expected to add 25–30% to the top line once fully used, around FY29; company ROCE expected ~15% when all projects run. [Sep call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹160cr from internal cash. Routine capex ₹200–250cr a year, 60% growth / 40% efficiency and upkeep. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Ibuprofen expansion:** +6,000 MTPA to 18,000 MTPA, ~₹350cr, fully backward-integrated, commissioning Dec 2027 (Q3 FY28); ~25% utilisation in FY28, 80%+ in FY29. Asset turn 1.75–2x → peak revenue roughly ₹600–620cr, which management called "tentative". [Sep call]
- **CDMO formulation plant:** 1,500 million tablets a year (or direct-compression granules), ~₹110cr, EU-GMP certified, commercial from Q3 FY27, full use in 18–24 months; asset turn 1–1.25x → ~₹100–150cr revenue; anchor European customers asked IOL to supply bulk tablets made from its own APIs; 3–5 year contracts with escalation clauses expected. Margins "somewhat better" than API. [Sep call]
- **Specialty-chemical tolling plant:** dedicated facility for a "leading global chemical company" under a long-term tolling contract (customer-led). Size not disclosed. [Sep call]
- About 30% of the ₹495cr was already spent by September 2026; the rest over FY27–FY28 from internal cash. [Sep call]
- **Greenfield (separate, ~25–30 km away):** ₹1,200–1,400cr over 4–5 years; environmental clearance received, other approvals pending; management says "not delayed" but no output in FY27. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Sep call]
- Capital commitments outstanding ₹69cr at March 2026 (₹25cr a year earlier). [AR FY26 note 35]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹135cr at March 2026 (all working capital, no term debt); cash and liquid investments ~₹231cr per CARE (Screener shows ₹198cr cash + ₹21cr investments). Net cash roughly ₹85–95cr. Gearing 0.24x; interest cover 18.9x. [Screener; CARE Jul-26]
- Cash from operations: FY24 ₹290cr, FY25 ₹179cr, FY26 ₹214cr (93% of operating profit). Free cash flow ₹43cr in FY26 after ₹198cr investing outflow; negative in FY25 (−₹34cr). [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 95 (FY26, up from 79 in FY22), inventory 91 days, payables 118 days; cash conversion cycle 68 days. CARE: operating cycle 73 days, fund-based limits ~60% used. [Screener; CARE Jul-26]
- Dividend payout ~21% (FY26). No buybacks. [Screener]
- The ₹495cr programme plus ₹200–250cr/yr routine capex and a ₹1,200–1,400cr greenfield means cash outflow of roughly ₹450–550cr a year in FY27–FY28 against ₹220–300cr of operating cash — IOL's net cash will likely turn to modest net debt unless growth lifts cash flow sharply. Management says all internal accruals. [My estimate from Sep call + Screener]

## Moat evidence
- **Integration/cost:** one of the world's largest ibuprofen makers and among the largest IBB producers; makes IBB, acetyl chloride and MCA in-house, so ibuprofen input costs are "controlled by us to the maximum extent"; paracetamol also backward-integrated. ~80% of power from renewables (biomass). [CARE Jul-26; Sep call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Pricing:** contracts carry price-adjustment clauses; input spikes in March–June 2026 were passed on with a lag (one large customer's fixed price caused a Q1 gross-margin dip). [Sep call; Q1 call]
- **Approvals:** 14 DMFs, 21 CEPs, NMPA China for two APIs, ANVISA. For three products awaiting US approval, IOL depends on customers' ANDA approvals. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Limits:** metformin not integrated — relies on Chinese DCDA ("most companies are dependent on the same source"). Chemicals earn only 3–6% EBIT: no moat there. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- CARE: competitive advantage in ibuprofen "expected to sustain in the medium term". [CARE Jul-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Commodity pricing:** ibuprofen and ethyl acetate ~68% of sales; CARE flags post-COVID ibuprofen price collapse as proof of cyclicality. Margin fell to 10% in FY25. [CARE Jul-26]
- **Related parties:** FY26 purchases from promoter-linked NCVI Enterprises ₹40.0cr, Mayadevi Polycot ₹69.4cr and NM Merchantiles ₹22.5cr — ₹131.9cr in total, up 31% from ₹100.7cr; the same NCVI and Mayadevi are the entities buying IOL shares on market. Stated as arm's length. Managerial remuneration ₹21.75cr = ~16% of FY26 PAT (₹138cr) — high. NCVI gives a ₹27.65cr surety on customs bonds. [AR FY26 note 40]
- **Contingent liabilities** small: ₹5.7cr, including a ₹2.06cr customs order (duty + penalties) alleging wrong ASEAN-India FTA certificates of origin; company appealing. [AR FY26 note 35]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matters = revenue cut-off and inventory valuation (₹371cr inventory). CARO: no undisputed dues in arrears, no disputed statutory dues pending. [AR FY26 auditor's report and CARO]
- **Execution load:** three new projects plus a greenfield at once, new to CDMO formulations; ibuprofen capacity +50% into a market growing 3–4% a year (management's own figure) risks price pressure when it lands in FY28–FY29. [Sep call]
- **Disclosure:** refused to split price vs volume or regulated vs semi-regulated exports; FY28 margin talk was hedged ("cannot predict FY28"). [Q1 call]
- **Positive signal:** promoter group raised holding from 48.19% (Jun-24) to 62.28% (Jun-26) through open-market buying by NCVI and Mayadevi Polycot; no preferential issue in FY26. [Screener; News: Whalesbook/Scanx, Mar-26; AR FY26 CARO 3(x)]

## What changed between the two calls (Q1 Aug-26 → special call Sep-26; plus Q4 May-26)
- May → Aug: tone moved from "15% growth, 14% margin" to "15–20%, 14–15%, may cross it", plus the first FY28 view (15–20% growth, 15–17% margin).
- Aug → Sep: CDMO had been "working on it, will tell once proof of concept is ready" in August; by September a 1,500 mn-tablet EU-GMP plant already existed and ~30% of ₹495cr was spent — a big project disclosed only after it was largely built. Ibuprofen expansion (+50%) announced although in August management stressed diversification away from ibuprofen. Pricing: paracetamol "softened from peak"; ibuprofen prices "hovering at the same level"; management agreed margin expansion is likely.

## My read
IOL is finally growing again after four flat years: Q1 FY27 revenue +37% and pharma EBIT margin near 15% show that paracetamol, clopidogrel and pantoprazole are filling plants, and management is beating its own May guidance while refusing to raise it. The balance sheet (A+/A1+, net cash, no term debt) and integration give it staying power in a commodity business, but chemicals still earn thin 3–6% EBIT and ibuprofen plus ethyl acetate remain ~68% of sales. The price needs roughly 15–20% growth for three years with margins holding at 14–15%; the ₹495cr projects plus the greenfield could deliver that on paper (25–30% more revenue by FY29) but they add capacity into slow-growing commodity APIs and will absorb all free cash. I would turn more positive on proof that CDMO tablets ship in Q3–Q4 FY27 and that paracetamol reaches 70%+; more negative if ibuprofen prices fall as the new 6,000 MTPA approaches, or if related-party purchases keep rising faster than sales.
