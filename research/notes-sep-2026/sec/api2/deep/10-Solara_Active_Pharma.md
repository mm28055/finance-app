# Solara Active Pharma Sciences (SOLARA) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

## Business and segments
- Pure API maker (spun out of Strides/Shasun), ~69–70 commercial products; top 15–20 give ~75% of sales. Management reports two pro-forma blocks: the **base business** (catalogue generic APIs, polymer APIs, ibuprofen derivatives, small CRAMS) and the **commodity ibuprofen business**. Plants: Cuddalore, Mangalore, Ambernath (base, ~70% utilised), Pondicherry (ibuprofen), Vizag (built, USFDA-approved twice, mothballed since 2024), Mysore (intermediates). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- FY26 pro-forma: base revenue ₹1,041cr (+6%), gross margin ~55%, EBITDA ₹244.5cr (~24%, flat YoY); ibuprofen revenue ₹333.6cr (+9%), gross margin ~27%, EBITDA loss ₹52.7cr (−16%). [IP Q4FY26]
- Q4 FY26: base ₹307cr (+36% YoY), EBITDA ₹79.6cr (25.9%; Q4 FY25 was 30.4%); ibuprofen ₹84.9cr, EBITDA −₹17.9cr (−21%). [IP Q4FY26]
- Q1 FY27: base ₹307.7cr (+24% YoY; ~18–19% after ~₹17–18cr of cost pass-through), gross margin ₹158cr (51.3%; ~54.5% adjusted), EBITDA ₹72.2cr (+8%, ~23.5%); ibuprofen ₹76.5cr, EBITDA −₹8.7cr (−12%). Base revenue run-rate lifted from ~₹240–250cr/quarter (Q1–Q3 FY26) to ₹300cr+. [Q1 press release; Q1 call]
- Polymer APIs: "close to 60% plus" global share. CRAMS only ₹10–12cr a year. [Q4 call]
- Customers: sales to promoter-group company **Strides Pharma Science ₹329cr in FY26** (₹215cr FY25) — roughly a quarter of revenue. Markets ~75% regulated (stored profile; not re-verified in filings). [AR FY26 note 39]

- Interest (~₹95cr in FY26) and depreciation (~₹100cr) still absorb almost all operating profit. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026):** no formal outlook; base EBITDA "25%, plus or minus"; ~70% utilisation leaves ~30% headroom; ibuprofen strategic review (bankers appointed) to conclude in H1 FY27; polymer/CRAMS carve-out (Solyx) on hold; 4–5 DMF filings a year (first filed April 2026; none meaningful in prior 4 years; ~₹200cr R&D over 4–5 years produced nothing); debt to ~₹503cr by end-May 2026; net-debt-free by FY29; "10% growth" (mainly base). [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (23 Jul 2026):** net debt ₹479cr at 30 June (beat ₹503cr), down ₹135cr in the quarter (₹100cr rights final call + ₹35cr operating cash); base margin ~23.5% (slightly below 25% ±1); ibuprofen loss narrowed to ₹8.7cr; review "on track" for H1. **Verdict: delivering on debt, cautious on margin, review still pending.** [Q1 call]
- **New in Q1 call:** net debt ~₹440cr by March 2027 (1.7x annualised EBITDA); base growth "at least 10%"; gross margin range 50–55%; ibuprofen loss run-rate ₹10–15cr a quarter; "expecting the entire capital invested in the ibuprofen business to be recovered might be far-fetched" (₹700cr deployed). [Q1 call]
- As of 28 Sep 2026 no ibuprofen decision has been filed (H1 ends 30 Sep); management said it would address it with Q2 results. [Q1 call; web search 28 Sep 2026 found no filing]

## Capex and capacity
- No greenfield. FY27 capex committed ₹55–60cr: ~₹40cr debottlenecking (20–30% more capacity for high-margin products, payback "2–3 quarters"), ₹10–15cr maintenance. FY28–FY29 ₹40–50cr a year. [Q1 call]
- Vizag (built, idle) costs ~₹12–15cr a year to keep; its fate and Pondicherry's are tied to the ibuprofen review: sell to a better operator, or convert to multi-product. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- R&D ~₹25cr a year; new filings to earn from FY29–FY30. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹687cr at March 2026 (peak ₹1,037cr in FY22; ₹796cr FY25). Net debt ₹479cr at June 2026. Rights issue (partly-paid shares, SOLARAPP1) funded ~₹213cr of repayment across FY26–Q1 FY27. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Credit rating CRISIL BBB/Stable (outlook revised from Negative), A3+ short term — lowest rating in this group. [AR FY26 Directors' report]
- Cash from operations ₹213cr (FY25), ₹153cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹173cr and ₹101cr. [Screener]
- Working capital: inventory 170 days, debtors 117 days (up from 93), payables 146 days (FY26). [Screener]
- No dividend. Promoter holding 37.3% (Jun-24) → 42.0% after the rights issue. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Base business: ~54–55% gross margin and 24–26% EBITDA on complex, low-volume APIs; strong regulated-market compliance (Mangalore and Ambernath USFDA inspections cleared, per stored profile). Customers accepted most West Asia cost pass-through (~₹30cr in Q1) after Solara shared consumption norms. [Q1 call]
- Polymer APIs ~60%+ world share. [Q4 call]
- Ibuprofen: no moat — not backward integrated ("we source the IBB from third parties"), older process, rivals have newer technology; management: "Ibuprofen is fundamentally a commodity business." [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Base margin has slipped from 30.4% (Q4 FY25) and 62.7% gross to ~54% — pricing power is real but not rising. [IP Q4FY26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Income-tax demand ₹161.9cr** (AY 2019-20; goodwill/product-portfolio depreciation and R&D deduction disallowed), raised Feb 2026, under appeal; plus ₹342cr of carried-forward losses restricted for AY 2024-25 and similar disallowances for AY 2018-19, 2020-21, 2022-23, 2023-24. Demand ≈ 13% of net worth. [AR FY26 note 37]
- **Goodwill ₹364.9cr** (Human API business) — key audit matter; impairment depends on forecasts; plus a likely write-down on ~₹700cr ibuprofen capital. [AR FY26 auditor's report; Q1 call]
- **Related party / concentration:** ₹329cr sales to Strides (promoter group); loans from promoter entities Tenshi Pharmaceuticals and Chayadeep Properties. [AR FY26 note 39]
- **Management churn:** MD & CEO, CFO and Company Secretary all changed in 2025; executive director changed Jan 2026; a non-executive director resigned Nov 2025. [AR FY26 note 39]
- Exceptional ₹5.9cr labour-code charge in FY26; history of one-offs (FY24 loss ₹567cr). [AR FY26 note 35; Screener]
- Raw-material shortages caused intermittent line shutdowns in Q1 FY27. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls (Q4 May-26 → Q1 Jul-26)
- Tone from "turnaround momentum" to "resilience amid West Asia costs"; base gross margin dipped (51.3% reported) on pass-through.
- New numbers: net-debt target ₹440cr (Mar-27); base growth ≥10%; ibuprofen loss ₹10–15cr/qtr; capex ₹55–60cr.
- New admission: full recovery of ₹700cr ibuprofen capital "far-fetched" — signals a write-down.
- CRAMS/polymers carve-out and Vizag remain parked.

## My read
The base business is a decent 24–26% EBITDA franchise now running at ₹300cr a quarter, and the balance sheet has improved sharply (net debt ₹479cr vs >₹1,000cr peak), but the group still earns almost nothing after interest and depreciation, and the headline P/E is meaningless. The price already assumes the ibuprofen problem is solved cheaply; the likely outcome is a write-down of part of ₹700cr plus a ₹365cr goodwill test, and a ₹162cr tax demand hangs over equity. The stock needs the base business to compound ~15%+ with 25% margins and ibuprofen losses to stop. I would turn positive on a clean ibuprofen exit (sale, or a conversion with small cash cost) and two more quarters of ₹300cr+ base revenue; negative on a large impairment, a tax loss, or base margin slipping below ~22%.
