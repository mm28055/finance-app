# Supriya Lifescience — deep research, Sep 2026

Screener code SUPRIYA — **standalone** page (the consolidated page is now blank; the company reports standalone only). Price ₹985, market cap ₹7,929 cr, ROCE 25.2%, ROE 19.1% [Screener, 28 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Niche API maker: 40+ APIs, 120+ countries, 1,500+ customers; one site at Lote Parshuram (Ratnagiri, 932 KL/day reactor capacity) plus a new formulation/CDMO site at Ambernath [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Mar-26].
- Therapy mix: anaesthetics ~49% of FY25 sales (ketamine and inhalation anaesthetics), antihistamines 11% (chlorpheniramine), vitamins 11%, analgesics 8%, anti-asthma 7% [India Ratings Dec-25].
- Exports 81% of Q1 FY27 revenue (Asia 39%, Europe 35%, LatAm 20% of exports); FY26 exports 82%, Europe ~40% share; US only ~1–3% [Q1 call, official; Q4 call]. Top-10 customers 58% of FY25 revenue; customers include Sanofi India, GSK, Teva [India Ratings Dec-25].
- Backward integration: 72% of revenue fully integrated (76% in FY26) [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- New lines: cardiovascular advanced intermediate (launched Q3 FY26; contracts with 3–4 large users for ~300 MT over 2–3 years), ADHD API, liquid anaesthetic (sevoflurane-type) API and two inhalation formulations from Ambernath (launched Q2 FY27), DSM vitamin contract (₹30–35 cr in FY26, ~₹60 cr peak in FY27), contrast-media API (delayed two quarters to H2 FY27) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Capacity: ~76% utilised in FY26; F-block (+150–200 KL, ₹40–50 cr) over two years; Lote "close to exhausting" [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 (28 May 2026): ~20% annual revenue growth; EBITDA margin 33–35%; ₹1,000 cr revenue in FY27; growth non-linear because of an August shutdown of blocks A–D; Ambernath EU audit H2 FY27; contrast media H2 FY27; Patalganga Phase 1 ₹200 cr (groundbreaking FY27); working capital 170–180 days [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 (14 Aug 2026): ₹1,000 cr and 33–35% (later "32% to 35%") reiterated; shutdown replaced by phased maintenance; EU audit of Ambernath fixed for second half of November; liquid-anaesthetic CDMO term sheet "very close"; margins to stay 33–35% for 3–4 years [Q1 call, official].
- Delivery vs Q4: margin — **missed badly** (25% vs 33–35%); revenue +31% YoY but ~₹35 cr lost; contrast media — slipped two quarters (already slipped from Q4 FY26); EU audit — scheduled.
- Arithmetic: ₹1,000 cr needs ~₹810 cr in the last nine months, ~₹270 cr a quarter — equal to the record Q4 FY26 (₹277 cr) every quarter.

## Capex and capacity

- Patalganga (Isambe village) Phase 1 ~₹200 cr for API/advanced-intermediate blocks and utilities; MIDC requires 40% completion in ~2.5 years; Phase 2 "still larger" [Q1 call, official].

- Payback: Ambernath full effect in 3–4 years (regulated markets need the EU audit); Patalganga earns from ~FY29 [Q4 call; Q1 call].

- Regulatory: US FDA surprise inspection (Feb 2026, 7 days, 7 blocks) closed with VAI/one minor observation; EDQM, PMDA, COFEPRIS, ANVISA etc. [Q4 call; India Ratings Dec-25].

## Risks and red flags
- **Customs/NDPS case**: on 3 Jul 2026 the Customs Special Investigation and Intelligence branch began proceedings under the NDPS Act against the GM Sales & Marketing (Sreekant Sreedharan) over one psychotropic export; he was placed in judicial custody (still in custody at the Aug call); company calls it a two-day lapse in export authorisation, has a fresh CBN authorisation and booked the sale without provision [News: ScanX, Jul 2026; Q1 call, official].
- **Guidance risk**: FY27 needs record quarters from here [Q1 call].
- **Single site**: one water shortage stopped production; Lote is near full capacity until F-block/Patalganga [Q1 call].
- **Rising costs**: employee cost +77% YoY; power policy change; solvent inflation [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- **Related parties**: CSR of ~₹5.1 cr paid to the promoter's Satish Wagh Foundation; lease rent to promoter-linked Vaibhav Chemicals and job-work with Samaira Pharmaceuticals; key-management remuneration appears to be ~₹27.5 cr (₹26.7 cr FY25) — high relative to ₹209 cr PAT (table layout in the PDF is garbled; figure needs checking) [AR FY26 note 29].
- Auditor: no qualification or adverse remark; key audit matter revenue recognition; contingent liabilities small (₹0.8 cr tax + ₹8 cr bank guarantees) [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 32]. Exceptional item: ₹0.46 cr labour-code gratuity [AR FY26 note 38].
- Promoter holding 68.3%, unchanged for three years [Screener].

## My read
The franchise (niche anaesthetic/antihistamine APIs, 72%+ backward integrated, ~36% margins in FY25–26, net cash) is good and growing ~20% a year, but Q1 FY27 exposed operational fragility — one water shortage and a power-policy change cut margin by ten points — and a compliance lapse that put a senior manager in custody. The ₹1,000 cr FY27 target now requires record quarters every quarter, so a miss is more likely than not. The price needs ~20% growth for years; Ambernath (EU audit November), the CDMO contract and Patalganga are what would deliver it. What would change the view: margins back above 33% in Q2/Q3 and a clean EU audit (positive); any escalation of the NDPS case, a missed audit, or FY27 revenue well short of ₹1,000 cr (negative).
