# Aarti Drugs (AARTIDRUGS) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

## Business and segments
- One reported segment, but three businesses: APIs (77% of FY26 sales; 50+ molecules — antibiotics, antiprotozoals, antidiabetic metformin, anti-inflammatory, antifungal), formulations via 100% subsidiary Pinnacle Life Science (Baddi; FY26 ₹330.5cr, +16%), and specialty chemicals/intermediates (new Sayakha methylamines plant). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 standalone (APIs + spec chem): ₹627.6cr (+20%), 89% of group; domestic 68% (+25%), exports 32% (+12%). API therapy mix: antibiotics 35.0%, antiprotozoal 18.5%, antidiabetic 18.2%, anti-inflammatory 11.9%, antifungal 10.2%. Formulations ₹81.6cr (+8%), 74% exported. Specialty chemicals ~₹82cr in Q1 (+149%). [Q1 call]
- Group revenue by geography FY26: India ₹1,569cr, abroad ₹996cr (39%). Exports 38% of FY26 (35% FY25); within exports, regulated markets 73% (66%), mainly Latin America so far. [AR FY26 note 32; Q4 call]
- Customers spread thin: top 10 domestic clients ~25% of domestic sales (largest ~4%); top 10 export clients ~27% of export sales (largest ~7%). [IP Q1FY27]
- Capacity: API installed 48,204 MTPA (9 plants); intermediates 34,308 MTPA. Metformin 1,400 t/month at Sarigam, used "mid-80s" %. Sayakha methylamines plant ~65% utilised in Q1 FY27 (~3,500 t in the quarter; 29% Dec-25, 40%+ Mar-26). Salicylic acid plant (Tarapur, ~600 t/month) almost idle — 67 t in Q1. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Approvals: Tarapur API plant USFDA re-approved (after ~10 years); 9 European CEPs; EU-GMP on metformin plant; USFDA approval for the Baddi oncology formulation plant; first oncology product (bicalutamide) launched. [Q1 call; AR FY26]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly revenue (₹cr): Jun-25 591, Sep-25 653, Dec-25 602, Mar-26 720, Jun-26 703. Operating profit 74 / 84 / 55 / 96 / 98; OPM 13% / 13% / 9% / 13% / 14%. Net profit 54 / 45 / 41 / 55 / 50. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 reported: revenue ₹703.6cr (+19%), gross margin 39.3% (36.8%), EBITDA ₹96.9cr (+30%), margin 13.8% (+120 bps; includes ~₹2cr CWIP write-off), PAT ₹50.1cr vs ₹54.0cr (Q1 FY26 had a ₹15cr tax refund; underlying +29%). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Price vs volume: Q1 FY27 volume only +3.5%; price +16–17% YoY, driven by the West Asia war (metformin +15–20% vs pre-war). Prices have eased since March–April but stay above pre-war. [Q1 call]
- Annual: revenue FY23 ₹2,716cr → FY24 ₹2,529cr → FY25 ₹2,387cr → FY26 ₹2,565cr; operating profit 309 / 321 / 290 / 312; OPM 11% / 13% / 12% / 12%. Net profit 166 / 172 / 168 / 195 — FY26 boosted by an 8% effective tax rate (tax refunds). Revenue still below FY23 peak. [Screener]
- ROCE fell from 34% (FY21) to 15% (FY23–FY24), 13% (FY25), 12% (FY26) as ~₹600cr of greenfield capex waited for utilisation. [Screener; Q1 call]
- New projects lost ~₹18–20cr of EBITDA in FY26 (Sayakha ramp + salicylic acid). [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (18 May 2026):** FY27 EBITDA margin 13.5–14% (14–14.5% "had this war not been there"); volume growth 8–10% ("internal target 10–15%"); gross contribution +100 bps from backward integration; Sayakha 55–60% in June quarter, 70%+ within a year; salicylic derivatives plant ready late May–mid June; ₹300–400cr capex over 2–3 years (brownfield/quasi-greenfield); formulations to ₹1,000cr in 3–5 years; formulation run-rate ~₹90cr/quarter sustainable; anti-dumping duty on salicylic acid "in process". [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (3 Aug 2026):** EBITDA margin 13.8% (inside the band, near top); Sayakha 64–65% (beat 55–60%); derivatives plant commissioned, trial batches 5–10 t (on time); formulations ₹81.6cr (below the ~₹90cr run-rate); volume +3.5% (well below 8–10%). **Verdict: mixed — margin and project milestones met, volume missed; price did the work.** [Q1 call]
- **Q1 call updates:** "14% is quite doable… 15% should be very easy" once greenfield utilisation improves; 10–15% volume growth for the next two years ("10% should be doable" even if salicylic stalls); salicylic anti-dumping duty delayed ~1 year (government asked for a longer injury period). [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Phase 1 greenfield (~₹600cr, Sayakha methylamines + Tarapur salicylic acid) done; asset turn ~1.5x. About 50% of Sayakha output is captive for metformin — it lifts gross margin more than revenue; captive share of the key intermediate to rise from ~30–40% (June quarter) to 80–90% by December 2026; worth ~1 point of gross contribution at peak. [Q1 call]
- Phase 2 brownfield/quasi-greenfield ₹300–400cr over 2–3 years, asset turn "3x, 4x" because utilities already exist: [Q4 call; Q1 call]
  - Metformin 1,400 → ~2,200 t/month at Sarigam: +300 t/month general and a new ~500–550 t/month USFDA block, built in 10–12 months, then FDA inspection; US DMF already filed. US metformin supply realistically FY28+.
  - New quasi-greenfield USFDA API block beside the Tarapur E-22 plant: three ~10 t/month multipurpose lines, "slightly more than double" current USFDA capacity.
  - Baddi formulation brownfield doubling non-oncology oral-solid capacity, to be EU-approved.
- Capital WIP ₹214cr at March 2026 (co-gen boiler, brownfield, and a "major part" formulation R&D/oncology dossiers to be amortised on approval). [Q4 call; Screener]
- Capital commitments ~₹19cr (standalone+subsidiary) at March 2026. [AR FY26 note 30]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹577cr at March 2026 (FY25 ₹615cr): long-term ~₹328cr, short-term ~₹248cr; debt/equity "historically low". Net debt ≈ ₹568cr. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Ratings: CRISIL AA-/Stable and ICRA AA-/Stable (long term), A1+ short term. [AR FY26 Corporate Governance]
- Cash from operations ₹254cr in FY26 (80% of operating profit), ₹245cr FY25, ₹359cr FY24; free cash flow ₹80cr FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 118, inventory days 112, payable days 109; cash conversion cycle 121 days (FY26). [Screener]
- Dividend payout ~9% FY26; periodic buybacks in past years (equity capital fell from 93 to 91). [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Scale leader in several old molecules: company claims world's largest producer of nimesulide, ketoconazole, metronidazole benzoate, tinidazole and the fluoroquinolone group, and "one of the leading" metformin producers; 6,876 MT/month total capacity; metformin 1,400 t/month. [IP Q1FY27 slide 10]
- Backward integration: "for the indigenous intermediate, I believe we are now the only ones with a backward integration facility" (methylamines for metformin). DCDA is still imported from China/Europe. [Q4 call]
- Pricing: keeps "absolute gross profit per kilogram" constant when input prices jump, so margin % dips in spikes; won 16–17% price in Q1 FY27. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- "Competing with China was never an issue for our product line" — but salicylic acid is the exception, where Chinese makers cut prices "to drive the competition out". [Q1 call]
- Regulatory depth improving (USFDA plant, 9 CEPs) but "current sale doesn't reflect that profitability" yet. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Tax disputes:** income-tax demands in appeal ₹92.4cr; excise/customs/GST demands ₹58.3cr (+₹8.4cr in subsidiary) — ~₹159cr in all, ~10% of net worth; the auditor made tax receivables and these contingent liabilities its key audit matter. Bank guarantees/LCs ₹171cr. [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 30]
- **Earnings quality:** FY26 tax rate 7.6% (tax refunds) lifted PAT; Q1 FY26 had a ₹15cr refund. Operating profit barely above FY23. [AR FY26; Screener]
- **Price-led quarter:** Q1 growth was almost all price from a war-driven spike; management says selling prices will fall when raw materials fall; high API prices cut Indian demand for old antibiotics (price-controlled formulations). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Salicylic acid drag:** plant near-idle, anti-dumping duty pushed out ~1 year; Chinese effluent-cost advantage. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Leadership change:** Prakash M. Patil concluded his tenure as Chairman, MD & CEO; Rashesh C. Gogri and Adhish Patil (elevated to COO & CFO) lead. [AR FY26 Chairman's message; Q1 call]
- **Related parties:** routine purchases from Aarti group firms (Aarti Industries ~₹20cr, Aarti Pharmalabs ~₹11cr, Aarti Life Science ~₹10cr in FY26) and small interest to KMP lenders; directors' pay ~₹11cr. Not material. [AR FY26 note 33]

## What changed between the two calls (Q4 May-26 → Q1 Aug-26)
- Narrative moved from "recovering from the trough / formulations exports" to "war-driven price tailwinds and metformin scale-up".
- Margin language upgraded: from 13.5–14% for FY27 to "14% quite doable, 15% very easy". Volume target restated higher (10–15% for two years) even though Q1 volume was +3.5%.
- New: detailed metformin plan (2,200 t/month incl. 500–550 t/month USFDA block) and a second USFDA API block; specialty chemicals now a ~₹80cr/quarter line.
- Worse: salicylic anti-dumping duty delayed a year; formulations slowed to +8%.

## My read
Aarti Drugs is a large, low-cost maker of old APIs whose earnings have gone sideways for four years while it spent ~₹600cr on backward integration; FY26–Q1 FY27 show the first payback (Sayakha at 65%, gross margin 39%), but Q1's jump was mostly war-driven price with only 3.5% volume growth. The price needs roughly high-single to low-double digit earnings growth — achievable if Sayakha's captive supply lifts margin toward 14–15% and the cheaper brownfield metformin/USFDA blocks add volume in FY28. What would change the view: a clean fall-back in API prices without volume recovery (margin back to 12%), the ~₹159cr tax disputes going against the company, or the salicylic plant staying a loss-maker; on the upside, a USFDA inspection of the new metformin block and regulated-market API sales becoming visible in FY28.
