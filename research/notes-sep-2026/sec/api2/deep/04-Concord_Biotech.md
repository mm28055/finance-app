# Concord Biotech — deep research, Sep 2026

Screener code CONCORDBIO (consolidated). Price ₹1,454, market cap ₹15,207 cr, ROCE 17.1%, ROE 12.5% [Screener, 28 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Fermentation-based APIs (bulk drugs made by growing microbes: immunosuppressants such as tacrolimus, mycophenolate, cyclosporine, sirolimus; plus oncology, anti-infectives, anti-fungals) and formulations (critical care, nephrology injectables and tablets; softgels; US products via subsidiary Stellon Biotech, 75% owned) [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26].
- Q1 FY27: API ₹219 cr (+42%), formulations ₹39 cr (−23%); domestic +12%, exports +46% [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]. FY26: API ₹829 cr (−12%), formulations −13%; API:formulation ~80:20; domestic:export 53:47 [Q4 call].
- **Concentration**: immunosuppressants are 74% of API revenue and 58% of total revenue in FY26 [ICRA Sep-26]. Top-10 customers 37.6% of revenue in FY26 (48.2% in FY21) [AR FY26 p.~18].
- Capacity: 1,250 m³ fermentation across two API sites; utilisation Q1 FY27 — Unit 1 ~80%, Unit 3 (Limbasi) ~55%, Unit 2 (formulations) ~25%; injectable plant ~5% [Q1 call, official]. Formulation capacity 869 mn OSD units + 13 mn vials [IP Q1FY27].
- Management says current assets can support ~₹3,000 cr revenue (≈₹2,200 cr API + ₹600–700 cr formulations) over 5–6 years; injectables alone up to ~₹600 cr [Q1 call, official].
- CDMO (third-party IP only) 1–2% of revenue; one animal-health NDA product commercial at "a couple of million dollars"; target double-digit share over time [Q1 call].

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / margin / net profit: Jun-25 204 / 61 / 30% / 44; Sep-25 247 / 88 / 36% / 63; Dec-25 278 / 98 / 35% / 64; Mar-26 326 / 118 / 36% / 88; Jun-26 257 / 82 / 32% / 58 [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 sales 1,017, OP 432 (42%), PAT 308; FY25 1,200, 506 (42%), 372; FY26 1,055, 368 (35%), 259. TTM PAT 273 [Screener].
- FY26 was the first revenue decline (−12%): US destocking/tariff uncertainty, a CDSCO written-confirmation delay that blocked EU supplies for ~3 months, West Asia tender (~₹25 cr) on hold, US Veterans Affairs tender not finalised; volumes fell, prices flat [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 gross margin 78.9% (+100 bp); EBITDA ₹82 cr (32%); 37% excluding injectable plant and Stellon costs [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]. FY26 new-unit costs ~₹38–39 cr (injectables) + ~₹10 cr (Stellon) [Q4 call].
- Currency helped ~10% in Q1 FY27 vs a historical 3–4%; net EBITDA benefit 3–5% after input costs [Q1 call].
- Wallet-share gains in Q1 were "primarily driven by pricing" — i.e. Concord cut prices to win share on existing products [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 (1 Jun 2026): FY27 growth "slightly better" than the historical ~18%, visibility strong in H1; no EBITDA guidance but +1–1.5 pts from solar power and operating leverage on new units; capex ₹20–30 cr maintenance plus a small topical facility; inventory to normalise in H1 [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 (3 Aug 2026): growth "better than our historical growth rates" reiterated; 40% EBITDA margin pushed to FY28 (needs injectables and Stellon ramp-up); injectable third-party contracts in H2; emerging-market exports from injectables "by next year"; one CDMO deal likely this year [Q1 call].
- Delivery: Q1 revenue +26% — met; Stellon sales started — met; injectables — delayed "a couple of quarters" last year, now "on track" but at 5% utilisation [Q1 call].
- ICRA forecasts FY27 revenue growth of only 14–15% — below management's ">18%" language [ICRA Sep-26].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹65 cr (softgel line, plant changes for an innovator project); Q1 FY27 ₹9.5 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call]. CWIP ₹79 cr at Mar-26 [Screener].
- **Mismatch**: ICRA's rationale puts FY27 capex at ₹120–130 cr, against ₹20–30 cr "general capex" on the Q4 call — worth asking about [ICRA Sep-26; Q4 call].
- Next large API capex only when Unit 3 reaches 80–85% use; 160 acres at Unit 3, 20–25% used [Q1 call]. Adjacencies (peptides, veterinary) may need dedicated fermenters [Q1 call].
- Solar/renewable power operational; 1–1.5 pt margin benefit [Q4 call].
- Payback: the capacity is already paid for — growth now needs utilisation, not money. Fixed-asset turn has fallen (sales ₹1,055 cr on ~₹790 cr net block in FY26) [Screener].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Zero debt; cash and investments ₹442 cr at Jun-26 (₹414 cr Mar-26) [Q1 call; Q4 call]. Borrowings ₹2 cr [Screener].
- CFO ₹267 cr FY26 (100% of OP per Screener; 73% of EBITDA per company); FCF ₹172 cr [Screener; Q4 call].
- **Working capital ballooned**: inventory 485 days (286 in FY25), debtors 159 days, cash conversion cycle 493 days [Screener]. Management blames staggered customer buying and long fermentation cycles; expected to ease in H1 FY27 [Q4 call]. ICRA: working-capital intensity 63% of revenue (53% in FY25); credit terms 115–120 days [ICRA Sep-26].
- Receivables ₹465 cr at Mar-26, of which ~₹68 cr overdue by more than six months and ~₹40 cr by more than a year [AR FY26 trade-receivables ageing note].
- Dividend payout 30% [Screener].
- Rating: [ICRA]AA- (Stable)/A1+ reaffirmed 23 Sep 2026; liquidity "strong" [ICRA Sep-26].

## Moat evidence
- Scale in fermentation: 1,250 m³; claims leading global share in immunosuppressant APIs [AR FY26 p.15; ICRA Sep-26].
- Cost position lets it undercut rivals and still earn ~79% gross margin [Q1 call].
- Approvals: USFDA, EU-GMP, PMDA Japan, Korea MFDS, ANVISA (Limbasi inspected in Q1 FY27) [ICRA Sep-26; Q1 call]. New ANDAs: mycophenolate suspension, tofacitinib [Q1 call].
- New second-source wins: nystatin, fusidic acid (limited competition); two innovator customers added in FY26 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Integration API → injectable ("only integrated company in India") [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Product concentration** — immunosuppressants 58% of revenue [ICRA Sep-26].
- **Inventory and receivables** — 485 inventory days and ~₹68 cr of receivables overdue >6 months; revenue-recognition cut-off is the auditor's key audit matter [Screener; AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Pricing strategy** — growth in Q1 partly bought with lower prices on existing products [Q1 call].
- **New units losing money** — injectables (~₹38–39 cr cost in FY26) and Stellon; breakeven "next financial year" [Q4 call].
- **Regulatory/geopolitical** — CDSCO delay, West Asia tender, US tariffs (management says no customer concern) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Contingent liabilities small: ₹3.9 cr (excise, income tax) [AR FY26 note 34].
- Related parties: family remuneration and rent (Sudhir, Ankur, Megha Vaid); investment in Celliimune Biotech (cell/gene therapy) ₹0.66 cr; loans to Stellon — all small [AR FY26 note 39]. Auditor: unmodified opinion [AR FY26].
- Promoter holding 44.08% flat for 3 years; ~39% public [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from explaining a bad year to claiming broad-based recovery.
- Numbers: exports +46%; margin 32% (37% ex new units) vs FY26 35%.
- New: Stellon selling; ANVISA, Kenya and Uganda approvals; price-led share gains; 40% margin now an FY28 target.
- Formulations fell 23% (Middle East tender absence; buyers switching to API).

## Best management quotes
- "This has been purely a price-advantage game, based on which we have been able to gain wallet share." [Q1 call]
- "To reach 40%, we need the injectable facility and Stellon business to be fully ramped up." [Q1 call]
- "These issues are temporary in nature rather than structural." [Q4 call]

## My read
The core fermentation franchise is intact — ~79% gross margin, dominant immunosuppressant share, zero debt — and Q1 FY27 shows the FY26 dip was mostly timing. But growth is being bought partly with price cuts, working capital is at a record 490-day cycle, and the new injectable and US businesses still lose money. ICRA's 14–15% FY27 growth is more realistic than management's ">18%". The stock needs mid-to-high-teens growth for years; that requires the injectable plant to fill and new products (nystatin, fusidic acid, oncology) to scale. What would change the view: inventory days falling back toward ~300 and injectables passing ~30% utilisation (positive); another quarter of flat sales or price-led margin slippage (negative).
