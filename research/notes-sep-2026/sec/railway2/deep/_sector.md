# Indian railway equipment — sector research, Sep 2026

Covers wagons, passenger rolling stock (Vande Bharat, metro, EMU/MEMU), railway electronics (propulsion, Kavach, locomotive controls) and components (wheels, brakes). Company figures are from the six deep files in this folder; sector figures carry their own source tags.

## Market size and spending
- **Railway budget FY27:** record capex of ₹2,93,030 cr, of which ~₹2.78 lakh cr is budget allocation; ~₹1.20 lakh cr earmarked for safety works; 7 high-speed corridors (~4,000 km, ~₹16 lakh cr investment over time) and a new 2,052 km Dankuni–Surat freight corridor announced [PIB, 1-Feb-26].
- **Line items (FY27 BE vs FY26):** rolling stock ₹52,109 cr vs ₹50,008 cr (+4%); signalling & telecom ₹7,500 cr vs ₹6,500 cr; new lines ₹36,722 cr vs ₹30,632 cr; doubling ₹37,750 cr vs ₹29,026 cr; payments for leased assets ₹39,650 cr vs ₹28,157 cr [News: Angel One, 1-Feb-26; RailAnalysis, 1-Feb-26]. Headline capex grew only ~5% — spending is shifting to track and safety, not rolling stock.
- **Spending pace:** ₹1,14,973 cr (39% of the ₹2,93,030 cr grant) spent by July 2026 — on track, which supports supplier payments [Lok Sabha reply via NewKerala, 5-Aug-26].
- **Slower trend:** ICRA noted budgetary support grew only ~2% between FY2024 and FY2026 after a 130% rise in capital outlay over five years to ₹2.52 lakh cr (FY26 BE), and expected railway-supplier revenue growth to taper [ICRA via ThePrint, 15-Apr-25].
- **Wagons:** Railways and private buyers took 37,650 wagons in each of FY23 and FY24 and a record 41,929 in FY25 (first-phase order of 1,17,229 wagons placed 2022) [News: Construction World, 28-Jan-26]. Industry capacity ~60,000 a year vs output 35,000–40,000 [News: The Core, 26-Feb-26]. FY26 output fell sharply on the wheelset shortage — Titagarh 7,019, Texmaco 8,372, Jupiter 5,498 wagons (company files).
- **Passenger:** ~260 Vande Bharat sleeper trainsets planned — BEML 10, ICF 50, Kinet 120, Titagarh–BHEL 80 [News: Channel IAM, 4-Sep-26]. Metro network ~1,143 km in 29 cities with ~936 km under construction [Drishti IAS, 26-Mar-26]; ICICI Securities expects ~2,000 km by 2030 [News: BusinessToday, 15-Sep-26].
- **Kavach:** Kavach 4.0 commissioned on 2,633 route km (Delhi–Mumbai 1,423; Delhi–Howrah 1,210) by 31-Jul-26; work under way on 21,794 route km; 6,290 locomotives fitted, 7,190 more plus 1,200 EMU/MEMU rakes in the pipeline; ₹3,875 cr spent to June 2026 and only ₹2,066 cr allocated for FY27 [Lok Sabha reply via NewKerala, 5-Aug-26].
- **Propulsion:** ~1,600 electric locomotives built a year; ~15,000 in the fleet; 300–500 EMUs tendered a year; last CLW tender for 748 locomotive propulsion sets open [MV Q1 call]. Indian-made propulsion now exported to the US, Germany, France, Switzerland and Spain [PIB, 1-Feb-26].

## Demand drivers
- **Freight target vs reality:** Railways aim for 3,000 mt of freight by 2030 (from 1,617 mt in FY25) and a 45% modal share (from 27%) [News: Construction World, 28-Jan-26]. Actual loading: 1,670 mt in FY26 (+3.25%) [Indian Infrastructure, 6-Apr-26]; 419.08 mt in Apr–Jun 2026 vs 413.05 mt (+1.5%) [Indian Infrastructure, 6-Jul-26]. Ministry projection for FY27: 1,765 mt (+5.7%) [IBEF, 2-Apr-26]. Reaching 3,000 mt by 2030 would need ~15% a year — current growth is a fifth of that, which is why wagon ordering has stalled.
- **Wagon replacement arithmetic (management view):** ~4 lakh wagons, ~4% replacement plus GDP-linked growth → ~10% a year [Titagarh Q4 call]; Texmaco sees 25,000–30,000 a year for 5–7 years plus 10,000–14,000 retirements [Texmaco Q4 call].
- **Next wagon order:** National Rail Plan envisaged a second order of >2 lakh wagons to 2030 (40,000–46,000 a year FY26–FY30) [Construction World, 28-Jan-26]; a ₹40,000 cr, one-lakh-wagon tender (35,000–40,000 a year over 3–4 years) was in consultation in May 2026 [News: Swarajya, 26-May-26] and had not been floated by August [Titagarh, Jupiter, Texmaco Q1 calls].
- **Private and export wagons fill the gap:** Texmaco's FCD book is 96.4% private/export (incl. a ~₹4,100 cr South Africa order); Jupiter ~80% private; Titagarh added JSW (₹226 cr) and leasing (company files).
- **Passenger modernisation:** Vande Bharat sleeper, Amrit Bharat, Namo Bharat and metro expansion; ICICI Securities cites 800 Vande Bharat trainsets by 2030 and 4,500 by 2047 as government goals [News: BusinessToday, 15-Sep-26].
- **Safety spend:** Kavach, electronic interlocking, WILD detectors, OHE upgrades within the ~₹1.20 lakh cr safety envelope [PIB, 1-Feb-26].

## Policy
- **Localisation:** wheels (Railways' 20-year offtake from Titagarh–Ramkrishna JV; Jupiter–Lucchini Odisha plant), propulsion (developmental-source quota of 20% for new vendors — Hirect, MV), Kavach (RDSO developmental vendors — Concord's ProGOTA) (company files).
- **Leasing and private wagons:** Railways allowing PSU/private wagon leasing (Titagarh licence; Texmaco–Touax–Trinity JV) (company files).
- **Insourcing risk:** the Mumbai MRVC suburban-train tender was cancelled and moved to ICF, a Railways factory [Titagarh Q1 call].
- **Steel safeguard duty:** extended from January 2026, plus anti-dumping duty on Vietnamese HRC; finished-steel imports fell ~40% YoY in 9M FY2026 [ICRA Steel Outlook, Feb-26].

## Cost drivers
- **Steel:** domestic HRC fell to ~₹46,000/t in Dec-2025, rebounded to ~₹53,800/t by Feb-2026 on the safeguard duty; ICRA expects prices range-bound near import parity; steel demand +9–10% in FY2027 [ICRA Steel Outlook, Feb-26]. Railways wagon orders carry price-variation clauses; private and export orders are often fixed-price (CRISIL on Jupiter; Texmaco South Africa terms undisclosed) [CRISIL Apr-26].
- **Wheelsets:** the binding constraint in FY26 — SAIL Durgapur makes ~40,000 cast wheels; forged wheels for Vande Bharat/LHB/metro imported; metro wheel demand 50,000–80,000 a year within five years [The Core, 26-Feb-26]. New capacity: Ramkrishna–Titagarh 228,000 wheels (40,000 to Railways in FY27, 100,000+ in FY28); Jupiter Odisha 1 lakh wheelsets (interim March 2027, full March 2028) [The Core; Jupiter Q4 call].
- **Electronics and energy:** West Asia conflict raised electronics, LPG and consumable costs in Q4 FY26–Q1 FY27 (Titagarh, Jupiter, Texmaco, Hirect, MV calls).

## Competitive structure and share
- **Wagons (private):** Titagarh (12,000/yr capacity; 32% of Railways' wagon orders), Texmaco (15,400/yr incl. Nymwag JV; largest by capacity; ~40–45% of private demand), Jupiter; plus Railways' own units and smaller makers [CRISIL Mar-26; CARE Jul-26; Texmaco Q4 call].
- **Metro cars:** BEML, Alstom, Titagarh (claims to be the only Indian maker of both stainless-steel and aluminium cars) and others; Titagarh names Alstom and BEML as the main rivals [Titagarh Q1 and Q4 calls].
- **Vande Bharat sleeper:** BEML/ICF, Kinet Railway Solutions, Titagarh–BHEL [Channel IAM, 4-Sep-26].
- **Locomotive propulsion:** Siemens, Alstom, ABB, Medha, BHEL + new Indian entrants MV (~25–26% of last tender quantity) and Hirect (developmental source); Railways spread volumes across 7 winners in the last tender [MV Q1 call; Hirect Q1 call].
- **Kavach:** >15 developers; a handful RDSO-cleared as developmental vendors (Concord/ProGOTA among them) [Concord H1 call].

## Cycle position
- **Freight wagons: trough between cycles.** First-phase order executed; FY26 volumes fell 20–40% at the listed makers; order books shrinking except Texmaco (export); the next Railways order is the swing factor. Capacity (~60,000) is well above FY27 likely demand without the tender.
- **Passenger rolling stock: early up-cycle.** Vande Bharat sleeper and metro orders are in execution; first Titagarh Vande Bharat train due Q4 FY27; ramps have repeatedly slipped.
- **Electronics/safety: early and lumpy.** Kavach roll-out is behind schedule (2,633 km of ~21,800 km under way) with a small FY27 allocation; propulsion tenders shifting to new Indian vendors.

## Outlook FY27–FY29 (forecaster named)
- **Railways (Ministry):** freight loading 1,765 mt in FY27 (+5.7%) and 3,000 mt by 2030 [IBEF, 2-Apr-26; Construction World]. Q1 FY27 ran at +1.5%, below plan.
- **National Rail Plan (Ministry):** 40,000–46,000 wagons a year FY26–FY30 [Construction World, 28-Jan-26]; the pending ₹40,000 cr tender implies 35,000–40,000 a year over 3–4 years if placed [Swarajya, 26-May-26].
- **ICICI Securities (Sep-26):** Titagarh profit to grow ~52% a year FY26–FY29 on passenger ramp; metro network to ~2,000 km by 2030 [BusinessToday, 15-Sep-26].
- **ICRA (Feb-26):** steel demand +9–10% in FY2027 with HRC range-bound — input costs stable [ICRA Steel Outlook]. **ICRA (Apr-25):** railway-supplier revenue growth to taper with slowing budget support; margins ~12% [ThePrint].
- **CRISIL (Mar/Apr-26):** Titagarh coach capacity 300 → 850 a year; Jupiter capex ₹2,500 cr FY26–FY28, Odisha plant by end-CY2027 [CRISIL rationales].
- **Company guidance:** Titagarh ~200 coaches FY27; Jupiter ₹10,000 cr revenue by 2030; Texmaco 2x revenue by 2030; Hirect 30% standalone growth FY27 and US$1 bn by FY31; Concord 40–50% CAGR; MV ₹400 cr FY27 (company files).
- **My synthesis:** FY27 is a weak freight year for listed wagon makers unless the tender lands by Q3; FY28–FY29 depend on (a) the one-lakh-wagon order, (b) wheel self-sufficiency (Chennai and Odisha plants), (c) Vande Bharat sleeper ramp, (d) Kavach and propulsion tenders converting. Passenger and electronics should grow faster than freight, but from small bases and with working-capital strain.
