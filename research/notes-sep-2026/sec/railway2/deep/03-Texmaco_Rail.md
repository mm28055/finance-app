# Texmaco Rail & Engineering (TEXRAIL) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹126, market cap ₹5,113 cr, P/E 23.6, book value ₹58.4, ROE 7.3%, ROCE 11.2%, dividend yield 0.6% [Screener, 27-Sep-26]. Adventz (S.K. Poddar) group company; promoters hold 48.34% [Screener; CARE Jul-26].

## Business and segments
- Four reported segments: **Freight Car Division (FCD)** — wagons, bogies, castings (in-house foundry, 50,000 t a year), loco shells; **Infra-Rail & Green Energy** (the old Kalindee: track, signalling, hydro-mechanical, bridges); **Infra-Electrical** (the old Bright Power: overhead electrification, substations); **Real Estate** (new, to develop surplus land) [AR FY26 note 1.60; CARE Jul-26].
- FY26 standalone segment revenue / profit: FCD ₹3,413 cr / ₹285 cr (8.4%); Infra-Rail & Green ₹348 cr / loss ₹27 cr; Infra-Electrical ₹610 cr / ₹66 cr (10.8%) [AR FY26 note 1.60]. CARE: FCD profit fell from ₹389 cr in FY25 to ₹284 cr in FY26 [CARE Jul-26].
- Q1 FY27 standalone segments: FCD ₹518 cr, result ₹28.6 cr (5.5%) — vs ₹728 cr at 6.6% a year earlier and ₹905 cr at 8.6% in Q4; Infra-Rail & Green ₹60 cr at 1.4% (first profit in a while); Infra-Electrical ₹175 cr (+77% YoY) at 10.8% [Q1 FY27 results segment table; Q1 call].
- Capacity: 15,400 wagons a year including 2,400 at the Texmaco-Nymwag JV (commissioned April 2026) and 3,000 from the acquired Jindal Rail (now merged as Texmaco West) [CARE Jul-26]. FY26 output 8,372 wagons (~54% of capacity); Q1 FY27 1,054 (~27% annualised) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Customers: order book now 96.4% private and export in FCD (21% in FY25, 79% in FY26) [Q1 call]. Private market share ~40–45% [Q4 call].
- Order book: ₹6,951 cr (Mar-25) → ₹5,408 cr (Mar-26) → ₹9,923 cr (Jun-26) after a South African contract worth ~₹4,100 cr (2,200 wagons plus 15-year maintenance; 30 diesel locomotives to be priced later, with a partner) [CARE Jul-26; Q1 call]. Maintenance is ~30–35% of the South Africa value [Q1 call]. Pending wagons: more than 6,000 including South Africa [Q1 call]. Order-book split at 30-Jun-26: freight cars 62.3%, Infra-Rail & Green 18.2%, Infra-Electrical 9.9%, subsidiaries/JVs 9.6% (from the Q1 deck, via press; the deck itself could not be downloaded from the company site) [News: Multibagg, 5-Aug-26].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (consolidated, ₹ cr): Jun-25 911 → Sep-25 1,258 → Dec-25 1,042 → Mar-26 1,167 → Jun-26 757. Operating profit (excluding other income) 71 → 124 → 89 → 106 → 57; margin 8% → 10% → 9% → 9% → 8%. Net profit 29 → 64 → 42 → 58 → 50 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: sales −17% YoY and −35% QoQ; operating profit −20% YoY. Net profit +72% YoY only because of a tax **credit** — pre-tax profit ₹42 cr, tax −18% [Screener]. Company's "EBITDA ₹81 cr, 10.8%" includes other income (₹23 cr) [Q1 call; Screener].
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹3,503 cr, profit ₹113 cr; FY25 ₹5,107 cr, ₹249 cr; FY26 ₹4,377 cr (−14%), ₹194 cr. Operating margin 8% → 9% → 9% [Screener]. CARE's PBILDT margin 9.34% (FY25) → 8.92% (FY26) [CARE Jul-26].
- 3-year sales growth 25% a year; TTM sales −14% [Screener].
- Unit economics: FY26 FCD revenue ₹3,413 cr over 8,372 wagons ≈ ₹41 lakh a wagon including castings and components (derived) [AR FY26; Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- **FY27 growth:** Q4 call — "we will grow in the top line and bottom line both in this financial year"; explicitly rejected an analyst's suggestion that FY27 would be softer [Q4 call]. Q1 delivered sales −17% YoY. Q1 call: agreed to "at least 15% to 20% revenue growth in 1 or 2 years" and 1.2–3 points of EBITDA gain over 1–3 years [Q1 call]. **Behind in Q1; wording softened to "1 or 2 years".**
- **Volumes:** Q1 call — management refused to give a wagon target ("we do not want to value in terms of the quantity"). **Dropped as a metric.**
- **South Africa:** Q4 call — delivery "by financial year end 2028", bulk of revenue in FY28. Q1 call — ~50–60% of the wagon value in FY28, rest spread later; loco partner "in process". **Kept.**
- **Vision 2030 ("Texmaco 2.0"):** 2x revenue (from a ₹5,000–6,000 cr core) with "mid-teen" EBITDA — repeated on both calls.
- **Capex envelope:** ₹1,500–2,000 cr to 2030; ₹200 cr approved for defence [Q4 call]. South African plant ₹200–300 cr (press figure, not confirmed) [Q1 call].
- **Leasing:** Trinity Rail joins the Texmaco–Touax leasing JV; Texmaco's stake falls from 50% to 34%; aim to lift leasing share from 15% to 50%, with ~100 rakes (the three partners may invest ~₹1,800 cr over 3–5 years) [Q1 call].
- **Wagon tender:** Q4 — "can come any time"; need of 1.5–2 lakh wagons. Q1 — "we are all expecting". No date on either call.

## Capex and capacity
- Fixed assets ₹993 cr → ₹1,023 cr; CWIP ₹66 cr → ₹165 cr in FY26; FY26 cash capex ≈ ₹167 cr (CFO ₹363 cr less FCF ₹196 cr) [Screener].
- Nymwag JV plant (2,400 wagons, tank wagons) commissioned April 2026; Jindal Rail bought Sep-2024 for ~₹614 cr (+3,000 wagons) [CARE Jul-26].
- Diversification spend: defence (₹200 cr approved), Kavach/signalling, propulsion, wheels (plan not disclosed), metro interiors (51% of Saira Asia), AI services unit (Invariz.ai), real estate [Q4 call; CARE Jul-26].
- Asset turns are low: capacity 15,400 wagons vs 8,372 made in FY26.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹895 cr (Screener); CARE counts total debt ₹1,515 cr (₹1,537 cr a year earlier); gearing 0.65x (0.56x) because the ₹700 cr provision cut net worth; debt/PBILDT 3.88x (3.23x) [Screener; CARE Jul-26]. Management quotes net debt ₹444 cr and net debt/equity 0.18 [Q4 call].
- Cash flow: CFO +₹363 cr in FY26 after −₹47 cr in FY25; FCF +₹196 cr — the best in a decade [Screener]. Customer advances jumped from ₹52 cr to ₹213 cr [AR FY26 note 1.25].
- Working capital: debtor days 104, inventory days 95, payable days 66, cash cycle 133 days (117 in FY25) [Screener]. Debtors incl. retention ₹1,248 cr; **unbilled revenue ₹765 cr (₹679 cr)**, mostly in Infra-Rail & Green and Infra-Electrical; CARE says expected recoveries did not come and are now "medium term" [CARE Jul-26].
- Liquidity "adequate": ₹290 cr surplus, ~41% use of fund-based limits [CARE Jul-26]. CARE A/Stable/A1 reaffirmed 7-Jul-26 [CARE Jul-26].
- Dividend ₹0.75 a share recommended for FY26 [AR FY26 Directors' Report].

## Moat evidence
- Largest domestic wagon maker by capacity (15,400/yr) with in-house foundry [CARE Jul-26].
- Export credentials: supplies to 16 countries, AAR approval, multiple gauges; won what management calls the largest export rolling-stock order by an Indian company (South Africa) in competition with European and Chinese makers [Q4 call].
- Private-sector franchise: 96.4% of the FCD book is private/export; ~40–45% share of private wagon demand [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Partnerships: Wabtec (air brakes, condition monitoring), Nymwag (tank wagons), Touax + Trinity (leasing), Talleres/others [Q1 call; CARE Jul-26].
- Limits: FCD margin is thin (5.5–8.6% at segment level); Railways sets prices; the Infra-Rail business has destroyed value for years (FY25 and FY26 losses, rising unbilled revenue).

## Risks and red flags
- **Qualified audit opinion (standalone and consolidated), FY26:** the company created a **₹700 cr "provision for contingencies" by charging free reserves** (₹494 cr general reserve + ₹206 cr retained earnings) instead of the profit and loss account; the provision sits as a current liability [AR FY26 auditor's report para 2; note 1.37; note 1.26]. The board says the risks (geopolitics, supply chain, costs, currency) are not tied to any year or identifiable contract [AR FY26 note 1.37]. Management said it is "the maximum potential risk under the worst-case scenario" and that pending claims may allow "savings" out of it [Q4 call]. **Why it matters:** any future losses charged against this provision would bypass reported profit, and any write-back could inflate it — earnings quality needs watching quarter by quarter. Neither management nor analysts raised it on the Q1 call.
- Size: ₹700 cr is about 3.6x FY26 profit and ~30% of reported net worth before the charge (reserves fell from ₹2,757 cr to ₹2,333 cr) [Screener].
- Unbilled revenue ₹765 cr in legacy EPC contracts; plan to demerge Infra-Rail & Green; claims and arbitration pending [CARE Jul-26; Q4 call].
- Tax litigation ₹178.5 cr (key audit matter) [AR FY26 auditor's report KAM].
- Execution: Q1 wagon output down 52% QoQ; FCD margin down; supply-chain issues (bogies, consumables) blamed [Q1 call].
- South Africa: fixed-price terms not disclosed ("the East is very well covered"), locomotives need a partner, local plant needed — first large overseas execution [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Strategy sprawl: defence, AI services, real estate, Kavach, metro — in a company earning 7% ROE.
- Promoter group has "demonstrated fund support" via warrants and QIP in the last two years [CARE Jul-26].

## My read
Texmaco's order book looks transformed — ₹9,923 cr, almost all private and export — but the income statement is going the other way: Q1 sales fell 17%, freight-car margin fell to 5.5%, and profit was held up by a tax credit. The ₹700 cr reserve-funded provision, qualified by the auditor, is the single most important fact for an investor because it can absorb future losses without touching reported profit. At a P/E in the low 20s the stock is not priced for much, which is fair; the price needs South Africa to be executed at a profit in FY28 and the legacy EPC receivables (₹765 cr unbilled) to turn into cash. I would turn more positive on two quarters of 2,000+ wagons with FCD margin back above 8% and a clear statement of any use of the ₹700 cr provision; more negative if the provision starts being drawn down or South Africa slips.
