# Concord Control Systems (BSE SME: 543619 / CNCRD) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹2,278 (52-week range ₹1,469–3,070), market cap ₹2,365 cr, P/E 56.2, book value ₹202, ROE 25.6%, ROCE 31.2%, no dividend [Screener, 27-Sep-26]. SME-listed, reports half-yearly under Indian GAAP (AS), not Ind AS; shareholders approved migration to BSE Main Board and direct listing on NSE on 19-Sep-26 [Postal ballot result, 21-Sep-26].

## Business and segments
- Railway electronics for locomotives and coaches, sold mainly to Indian Railways production units (CLW, BLW, ICF) and zones: distributed-power wireless control (DPWCS, for "Super Anaconda" long freight trains), FSK wireless links, onboard remote monitoring, driver display units, brake interface units; locomotive propulsion; battery/zero-emission retrofits; overhead monitoring for Delhi Metro (German technology transfer); WILD wheel-impact detectors [IP H2FY26; H2 call; H1 call].
- Group structure: **Advanced Rail Controls** (Bengaluru; bought 2024, merged into Concord from 1-Apr-25 — now the main growth engine); **ProGOTA India** (Kavach 4.0; associate, 46.5% after +20.5% in 2025); **Concord Lab-to-Market Innovations** (WILD, IISc-linked; associate, 49%); **Fusion Electronics** (flex PCB/EMS; 80% subsidiary, bought 2025) [IP H2FY26 slide 33; AR FY26].
- Content: ₹70–80 lakh of electronics per locomotive today, rising with propulsion and Kavach [H1 call]. Management's addressable market: loco subsystems ~₹2,500 cr; Kavach ~₹40,000 cr to FY30; another product ~₹2,000 cr to FY30 [H1 call; IP H2FY26 slides 12–13].
- No segment reporting; management says locomotive business (DPWCS and propulsion subsystems) is "the larger contributor" [Official H2 transcript].
- Order book: ₹212.5 cr (Mar-25) → ₹313 cr (Sep-25) → **₹697 cr (Mar-26)**; FY26 orders received ₹695 cr, executed ₹210.5 cr. The deck notes the order book **includes orders of associates** — Kavach orders received total ₹258.27 cr, which sit mostly in ProGOTA (46.5%-owned, equity-accounted) [IP H2FY26 slides 12, 26; H1 call].

## Key numbers
- Half-yearly sales (consolidated, ₹ cr): H1 FY25 50 → H2 FY25 75 → H1 FY26 82 → H2 FY26 129. Operating profit 14 → 15 → 20 → 39; margin 28% → 20% → 25% → 30%. Net profit 8 → 14 → 16 → 25 [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹65 cr, profit ₹13 cr; FY25 ₹124 cr, ₹23 cr; FY26 ₹210 cr (+69%), ₹43 cr (+86%). Operating margin 26% → 23% → 29% [Screener]. Company: FY26 revenue ₹210.47 cr, EBITDA ₹62.1 cr (29.5%), PAT ₹42.7 cr [Official H2 transcript].
- 3-year sales growth 62% a year, profit 98% a year [Screener].
- H1 FY26: an analyst noted standalone revenue fell ~26% YoY while consolidated rose 64%; management said the old Concord business did ~₹26 cr and Advanced Rail drove the growth [H1 call].
- H1 FY27 results (Apr–Sep 2026) not yet published; there is no Q1 figure for an SME.

## Guidance history
- **Growth:** 40–50% revenue CAGR for 3–5 years; "5x FY25 revenue in 2–3 years" (i.e. ~₹620 cr by FY27–FY28) [H1 call, Nov-25]. H2 call kept 40–50% [Official H2 transcript]. FY26 delivered +69% — **beat**.
- **Margins:** guided 20–25% EBITDA (deck says 22–25%); delivered 29.5% in FY26 [Official H2 transcript; IP H2FY26 slide 21]. Kavach 25–30% EBITDA; Fusion 20–25%+ [H1 call; IP H2FY26].
- **Kavach:** H1 call (Nov-25) — field trial on 53 km Kacheguda–Mahbubnagar to start "in a few weeks"; big ICF (~2,500 trainsets) and loco (>6,000) tenders "finalized before this financial year ends". H2 call (May-26) — trial work "started"; trial completion and ISA (independent safety assessor) approval targeted "within this financial year" (FY27); 4,500-loco tender closed 14-Apr-26, "Railways has its own process". **Slipped roughly two quarters.**
- **Fusion Electronics:** H1 call — ₹200 cr revenue potential at ~20% EBITDA; H2 call — still a "turnaround", 300-day plan, FY27 "revenue-contributing"; deck now says ₹400 cr+ after doubling capacity at 25%+ EBITDA [IP H2FY26 slide 19]. No Fusion revenue disclosed yet. **Target raised while nothing delivered.**
- **Working capital:** H2 call — receivables high because of Q4 billing; "as of June" cycle "improved meaningfully"; FY27 to be "more disciplined" [Official H2 transcript]. Not yet verifiable.
- **Order conversion:** asked whether 70–75% of ₹697 cr converts in FY27, management would not commit; normal cycle 18–24 months [Official H2 transcript].

## Capex and capacity
- Fixed assets ₹23 cr → ₹43 cr; investments ₹7 cr → ₹24 cr in FY26 (associate stakes); goodwill of ~₹12.9 cr added on the Fusion business combination [Screener; AR FY26 fixed-asset schedule].
- Funding in FY26: preferential issue for cash ₹50.1 cr (Jan-26, 2.39 lakh shares at ₹2,100); share-swap issues for stakes (Jun-25 at ₹1,418; Jan-26 at ₹2,100); a bonus of 37.95 lakh shares in Oct-25 (about 3-for-5 on ~63 lakh shares, derived); borrowings rose from ~₹0 to ₹54 cr [AR FY26 Corporate Governance Report; Screener].
- Fusion: 2 lakh m² a year of flex-PCB capacity already installed (so little new capex claimed); acquisition price kept confidential "for 2 years" [H1 call; IP H2FY26 slide 19].
- 110–118 R&D engineers [H1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Operating cash flow: −₹7 cr (FY25), **−₹55 cr (FY26)**; free cash flow −₹63 cr in FY26. Profit of ₹43 cr did not turn into cash [Screener].
- Working capital (FY26): debtor days 210 (110), inventory days 313 (216), payable days 149, **cash cycle 375 days** (265) [Screener]. Trade receivables ₹121.3 cr vs ₹37.6 cr a year earlier — more than half of FY26 revenue [AR FY26 note 17].
- Receivables include ₹13.4 cr due from private companies in which a director is a director or member, and ₹0.85 cr from firms where a director is a partner [AR FY26 note 17].
- Borrowings ₹54 cr (nil a year earlier), including ~₹6.3 cr net borrowed from KMP [Screener; AR FY26 note 37].
- Guarantees given to banks/third parties ₹20.9 cr; claims not acknowledged ₹0.15 cr [AR FY26 notes on contingent liabilities].
- No credit rating rationale found for the company.

## Moat evidence
- Approvals: RDSO technical-prototype clearance for Kavach 4.0 (developmental source, eligible for 20% of tender quantities); DPWCS and remote monitoring in service on Indian Railways; first Kavach field-trial order ₹19.5 cr [H1 call].
- Integration claim: propulsion + wireless control + Kavach + diagnostics + in-house PCB; management argues few rivals have all four layers [Official H2 transcript; IP H2FY26 slide 23].
- Evidence of pricing power: 29.5% EBITDA margin in FY26 — high for railway electronics [Official H2 transcript].
- Limits: Kavach sits in an associate, not a subsidiary; Railways dictates prices; more than 15 firms developing Kavach [H1 call]; propulsion and Fusion are unproven.

## Risks and red flags
- **Cash conversion:** 375-day cash cycle, negative operating cash two years running, new debt — the "profit is unpaid" concern raised by an analyst on the H2 call remains unanswered by numbers [Screener; Official H2 transcript].
- **Related parties:** FY26 sales of ₹10.2 cr to associates/common-control entities (₹0.6 cr a year earlier), purchases ₹3.2 cr, investments ₹18.4 cr, interest income from them ₹0.6 cr; long list of KMP-linked entities (TankUp, Atlantic Trade, Vans Electroengineers, Concord Global Engineers etc.) [AR FY26 note 37]. Sales to related parties rising 16x alongside receivables from director-linked companies is the key thing to watch.
- **Order book quality:** ₹697 cr "includes orders from associates" — the Kavach part (~₹258 cr of orders received) will mostly show up as a share of associate profit, not revenue [IP H2FY26 slide 26].
- **Governance:** the joint MD is also the CFO; statutory auditor Seth & Associates paid only ₹4.18 lakh a year for the group; audit opinion unmodified, but SME accounts carry no key-audit-matter reporting [AR FY26 Corporate Governance Report; AR FY26 auditor's report]. Acquisition price of Fusion undisclosed.
- **Disclosure:** analysts complained orders are not announced consistently [H1 call]; promoter holding falling 73.5% → 65.6% (share issues) [Screener].
- Kavach execution: industry-wide delays; trial and ISA approval slipped.

## What changed between the two calls (H1 FY26, Nov-25 → H2 FY26, May-26)
- Tone: Nov-25 was about Kavach approval and "golden period"; May-26 was a "platform" pitch with more defensive answers on receivables and debt.
- Numbers: order book ₹313 cr → ₹697 cr; H2 revenue ₹129 cr vs H1 ₹82 cr; margin 25% → 30%; receivables ₹37.6 cr (Mar-25) → ₹121.3 cr (Mar-26); borrowings nil → ₹54 cr.
- New: Fusion target doubled to ₹400 cr; Main Board/NSE migration process; retrofit and hydrogen narrative. Dropped: specific Kavach tender timelines.

## My read
Concord has grown fast and profitably on paper — FY26 revenue +69% at a 29.5% EBITDA margin — driven by the Advanced Rail acquisition's locomotive wireless-control products. But none of that profit has turned into cash: operating cash flow was −₹55 cr, receivables tripled to more than half a year's sales, related-party sales jumped 16x, and debt appeared for the first time. At ~56x earnings the price assumes the ₹697 cr order book (part of it in an associate) converts smoothly and gets paid. I would want to see H1 FY27 operating cash flow turn positive, receivable days fall below 150, Kavach trial and ISA approval completed, and Fusion's first real revenue before trusting the growth; any rise in related-party receivables would be a clear negative.
