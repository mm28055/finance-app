# Hirect Ltd (formerly Hind Rectifiers, HIRECT) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹1,213 (52-week range ₹565–1,400), market cap ₹4,301 cr, P/E 110, book value ₹60.7 (price about 20x book), ROE 25.2%, ROCE 18.8% [Screener, 27-Sep-26]. 1:1 bonus issue (record date 27-Mar-26) doubled the share count [AR FY26].

## Business and segments
- Railway power electronics: traction transformers for locomotives (largest market share, up to 7,775 kVA), auxiliary and hotel-load converters, battery chargers, HVAC, locomotive panels, traction motors, TCMS/driver displays, and now indigenous locomotive propulsion; plus industrial rectifiers and ESP transformers [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Content per platform: ~₹5–5.5 cr per locomotive (propulsion ₹1.7–1.8 cr of that; traction transformer ₹1.5–1.6 cr), ~₹15 cr per MEMU trainset, ~₹60 cr per Vande Metro trainset [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 standalone revenue mix: railway transformers the largest block (deck pie; ~66% as far as the chart can be read); order book mix July 2026 ₹739.8 cr, anchored by railway transformers with railway electronics and electromechanical products next [IP Q1FY27 slide 23].
- Subsidiaries: **Elventive France** (EMS, printed electronics, robotics; bought 1-Oct-25 from BeLink Solutions' assets for €1 m plus ~€2 m capital; runs at €0.7–0.9 m revenue a month, loss-making), Hirect Global Holdings (Dubai), Hirect FZ-LLC, Coincade Studios (AI software, formed Apr-25) [Q4 call; AR FY26 Board's Report].
- Capacity: traction transformers raised 25% to ~75 a month at Satpur; locomotive propulsion ~120 sets a year at Sinnar; copper conductor plant 350 t a month (internal need ~220 t) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Customers: Indian Railways production units (80–90% of revenue), BHEL; first US orders (traction-motor assemblies; IGBT converters for mining — prototypes) [CRISIL Sep-25; Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (consolidated, ₹ cr): Jun-25 214.8 → Sep-25 227.2 → Dec-25 277.4 → Mar-26 279.8 → Jun-26 258.5. Operating profit 24.2 → 25.9 → 25.6 → 8.4 → 13.2; margin 11.3% → 11.4% → 9.2% → 3.0% → 5.1%. Net profit 12.8 → 14.7 → 12.7 → −1.6 → 6.5 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 consolidated: revenue +20.3% YoY; EBITDA ₹13.2 cr, **down 45.4% YoY**, margin 5.1%; PBT before exceptional ₹8.1 cr; PAT after minorities ₹9.4 cr including a ₹3.5 cr gain on sale of the Dehradun plant [Official Q1 transcript; IP Q1FY27 slide 27].
- Q1 FY27 standalone (the Indian business): revenue ₹236.4 cr (+10.1% YoY, −10.5% QoQ); gross margin 25.5% (26.2%); EBITDA ₹25.1 cr (+3.2%), margin 10.6%; PAT ₹15.1 cr [Official Q1 transcript; IP Q1FY27 slide 26].
- Elventive's drag: consolidated employee cost ₹40.8 cr vs ₹18.9 cr a year earlier; consolidated minus standalone revenue ≈ ₹22 cr a quarter [IP Q1FY27 slide 27].
- Annual (consolidated): FY24 sales ₹518 cr, profit ₹13 cr; FY25 ₹655 cr, ₹37 cr; FY26 ₹999 cr (+52%), ₹39 cr. Operating margin 9% → 11% → 9%. TTM sales ₹1,043 cr, profit ₹32 cr [Screener]. FY26 standalone: revenue ₹949 cr (+44.8%), EBITDA ₹102.5 cr (10.8%), PAT ₹57.7 cr, ROE 30.3%, ROCE 24.2% [Official Q4 transcript].

## Guidance history
- **FY27 standalone growth 30%:** set earlier, repeated on Q4 call and "intact" on Q1 call. Q1 standalone grew 10.1%. To hit ₹1,234 cr (949 × 1.3) the next three quarters must average ~₹333 cr — 41% above Q1's run-rate. **Kept in words; at risk in numbers.**
- **Elventive break-even:** Q4 call — 6 to 8 quarters to PBT break-even (also "about 6 quarters"). Q1 call — impact to continue "3 to 5 quarters"; consolidated margins back toward ~10.8% in "3 to 4 quarters". Measured from Oct-25 the two statements are roughly consistent; **kept**.
- **Propulsion:** Q4 call — type tests done, 50,000 km field trials "underway immediately", results "from Q3 onwards", eligible for 20% development quantities in June–July tenders. Q1 call — only 9,000–10,000 km done (floods), tenders delayed, finalisation expected end-Q2; now says staying a developmental source is "more advantageous". **Slipped.**
- **Orders:** Q4 call — "L1 positions in most of the tenders"; Q1 call — intake "flattish", refused to quantify delayed orders or give an FY27 closing order book. Order book ₹845.5 cr (Mar-26) → ₹739.8 cr (Jul-26). **Behind.**
- **Capex:** FY26 ~₹70 cr; FY27 plan ~₹50 cr (transformers, propulsion testing, copper) [Q4 call].
- **Long term:** 10x revenue to US$1 billion in five years (by FY31) at mid-to-late-teens EBITDA, via railways, adjacent industries (defence, mining, power, data centres) and acquisitions; no detailed plan published yet [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Fixed assets ₹107 cr → ₹196 cr in FY26 (includes Elventive assets); cash capex ~₹98 cr (CFO ₹86 cr, FCF −₹12 cr) [Screener]. Capital commitments ₹10.3 cr (₹25.2 cr) [AR FY26 note 47].
- Funding: ₹100 cr preferential issue to Tata Small Cap Fund at ₹920.50 (10.86 lakh shares) approved May-26 — for +20% transformer capacity, 3x copper conductor capacity, test systems, R&D and working capital [AR FY26 note on events after the balance sheet; Q4 call]. Promoter-group warrants: 2 lakh warrants at ₹1,368.22 (~₹27.4 cr) to Elventive Tech Pvt Ltd; 25% received, unused at 31-Mar-26 [AR FY26 Board's Report].
- Copper conductor plant saved ~2% of material cost in Q1 [Official Q1 transcript].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹245 cr at Mar-26 (₹164 cr a year earlier) vs equity ~₹209 cr — debt/equity ~1.2x before the Tata money [Screener]. CRISIL: gearing 1.1x at Mar-25, bank lines ~81% used, cash ₹10 cr [CRISIL Sep-25].
- FY26 CFO ₹86 cr (best yet) but FCF −₹12 cr after capex [Screener].
- Working capital (FY26): debtor days 90 (61), inventory days 74 (92), payable days 59, cash cycle 105 days [Screener]. Propulsion orders are paid "after delivery" [Official Q1 transcript].
- Dividend ₹1.40 a share (70% of face value) for FY26 [AR FY26].
- CRISIL BBB+/Stable/A2 (upgraded Sep-25). Downgrade trigger: operating margin below 8.5–9% — consolidated margin has been 3.0% and 5.1% in the last two quarters, though standalone is ~10.6% [CRISIL Sep-25].

## Moat evidence
- Largest share in locomotive traction transformers; won L1 in a transformer tender helped by in-house copper [Q4 call].
- Only developmental source for Indian locomotive propulsion; one other Indian full-capability source; the rest are multinationals; developmental sources get 20% of tender quantity [Official Q1 transcript].
- First MEMU trainset development order (MCF, ~₹60 cr for 4 trainsets) and first Vande Metro/Namo Bharat development order (RCF, ~₹60 cr) — combined ~₹120 cr [IP Q1FY27 slide 22].
- 190–200 engineers, 42–50 products in development; indigenous brake system for 6,000 HP locomotives [AR FY26; Q4 call].
- Limits: 80–90% dependence on Indian Railways; price-variation clauses exist but margins still fell with West Asia input costs [CRISIL Sep-25; Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Valuation vs earnings:** P/E 110 and ~20x book on TTM profit that fell 9%; consolidated margin has halved since the Elventive purchase [Screener].
- **Order book shrinking:** ₹1,000 cr (Jun-25) → ₹845.5 cr (Mar-26) → ₹739.8 cr (Jul-26) — about 8–9 months of sales [CRISIL Sep-25; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **Related parties:** a promoter-group company named **Elventive Tech Pvt Ltd** (same brand as the French subsidiary) holds preferential warrants and trades with the company — in H2 FY26 Hirect bought ~₹11.9 cr and sold ~₹6.8 cr with it, and bought ~₹16.7 cr from Force Motion Technology LLP, another KMP-influenced entity [RPT H2 FY26 filing; AR FY26]. Not illegal, but the overlap in names and dealings needs clear disclosure.
- Diversification risk: loss-making French EMS business, AI subsidiary, US prototypes, $1 bn target far beyond a ~₹1,000 cr company's current trajectory.
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter is revenue recognition timing; no emphasis of matter [AR FY26 auditor's report p.84]. Contingent liabilities small: GST ₹1.0 cr, bank guarantees ₹11.4 cr, LCs ₹5.8 cr [AR FY26 note 46].
- Management churn at the top: Global CEO Douglas Bailey on the Q4 call; Anand Chidambaram named Global CEO by Q1 [Official Q4 and Q1 transcripts].
- Promoter stake 43.92% → 42.58% in July 2026 (dilution from the Tata placement) [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 was the $1 bn vision; Q1 was defending a flat order intake and consolidated margins.
- Numbers: consolidated EBITDA margin 3.0% → 5.1%; standalone revenue ₹264 cr → ₹236 cr; order book ₹845.5 cr → ₹739.8 cr; field trial 0 → 9–10k km.
- New: MEMU and Vande Metro development orders; US orders; Global CEO change; Dehradun plant sale; "developmental source is more advantageous" argument. Elventive break-even language shifted from "6–8 quarters" to "3–5 quarters" (same end date roughly).

## My read
The Indian business is good — 10–11% EBITDA margins, a dominant transformer franchise, and genuine proof points in propulsion and trainsets — but the group numbers have gone backwards since the French acquisition, the order book has fallen for three quarters, and Q1 standalone growth (10%) is far from the promised 30%. At P/E ~110 the price is paying for the $1 bn vision, not for the business as it is. I would want to see the delayed propulsion/transformer tenders converted (order book back above ₹1,000 cr), consolidated margin back near 9–10%, and cleaner separation from promoter-linked "Elventive Tech" before getting comfortable; a Q2 standalone quarter above ₹300 cr would be the first sign the 30% target is real.
