# Kanpur Plastipack — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code KANPRPLA (consolidated; includes 76%-owned Valex Ventures UK and JV Essekan). Price ₹257, market cap ₹633 cr, P/E 14.0, book value ₹110, ROCE 17.0%, ROE 14.9% [Screener, 28-Sep-26].

## Business and segments
- Kanpur-based maker of polypropylene (PP) industrial packaging, ~70% exported to 40+ countries: FIBCs (big "jumbo" bulk bags), woven fabric, small bags, multifilament yarn, UV masterbatch; 4 plants; 80–85% repeat business [IP Q1FY27 slides 5, 8].
- Q1 FY27 revenue mix: FIBC 52%, fabric 20%, small bags 12%, multifilament yarn 8%, others 8% [Q1 call]. CRISIL puts FIBC at 55–60% of revenue and top-10 customers at ~35% [CRISIL Jul-26].
- End markets (FY26): food & agriculture 52%, industrial packaging 23%, construction 12%, automotive 7%, mining 6% [Q4 call].
- Exports Q1 FY27: Europe ~60%, South America ~20%, North America ~16%, rest ~4% [Q1 call]. FY26: Europe 56.5%, South America 21.8%, North America 16.9% [Q4 call].
- FIBC capacity 18,000 t a year at Unit 2, ~83% used in FY26 (15,000 t made, 14,351 t sold); quarterly FIBC output Q3 FY26 3,800 t → Q4 3,500 t → Q1 FY27 3,000 t (a "damaged number" from polymer and labour disruption) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Exited the loss-making CPP film division in Q1 FY26 (shown as discontinued); that building now houses the non-woven project [AR FY26 Board's report].
- New lines: Essekan (50:50 marketing JV with Essegomma S.p.A., Italy) for premium Taslan PP yarn — sales began Q1 FY27; non-woven needle-punch fabrics (two machines, ~10,000 t) from Q3 FY27; Valex Ventures, a UK FIBC distributor (GBP 2 mn revenue), bought Nov 2025 [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26].
- Polymer trading is opportunistic: ₹10.38 cr trading profit in FY26 (₹7.55 cr of it in Q4), ₹2.93 cr in Q1 FY27 [Q1 call].

- Quarterly sales: Jun-25 180 → Sep-25 165 → Dec-25 192 → Mar-26 180 → Jun-26 204 (+13%). Operating profit 13 → 15 → 15 → 20 → 17; OPM 7% → 9% → 8% → 11% → 8% [Screener].
- Net profit: 6 → 8 → 11 → 15 → 12 [Screener].
- Company (standalone) Q1 FY27: total income ₹207.49 cr (+13.9%), EBITDA ₹22.19 cr (10.69% vs 7.66%), PAT ₹12.14 cr (+112%), EPS ₹4.96. Consolidated: income ₹208.9 cr, EBITDA ₹21.7 cr (10.4%), PAT ₹11.7 cr [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 slide 15]. Company EBITDA includes other income such as trading and forex gains.
- Manufacturing-only EBITDA margin (ex trading) 10.5% Q2 FY26 → 11.5% Q3 → 12.2% Q4 → 12.6% Q1 FY27 [IP Q1FY27 slide 16].
- Annual: sales FY24 497 → FY25 563 → FY26 718; operating profit 20 → 43 → 65; OPM 4.0% → 8% → 9%; net profit 1 → 11 → 39; ROCE 5% → 11% → 17% [Screener].
- Unit economics: Q1 FY27 average selling price +31% vs Q4, raw material +18%; wage revision added ~₹3.5 cr a quarter to staff costs; PP rose from ~$1,000 to ~$1,700/t in March, management's "new normal" $1,200–1,350/t [Q1 call; Q4 call]. FIBC margin is earned per stitching minute, not per kg [Q4 call].
- DFIA export-incentive licences worth ₹7.03 cr were **not booked** at Mar-26 because PP import duty was suspended (contingent asset); ₹3.65 cr of earlier DFIA income was reversed in Q4 FY26. Normal run-rate ~₹3–3.5 cr a quarter [AR FY26 note 33; Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (4 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth ~10–15% (10% in another answer); margin ~11% "will sustain", manufacturing margin ~12%; non-woven ₹20–25 cr in FY27 (first machine Sep, second Dec) and ₹100–125 cr in FY28 at 15–16% EBITDA; Essekan ₹20–25 cr in FY27; FIBC Unit 3 building done by May, ~1,800 t extra in FY27, run-rate 2,400 t by year-end, 6,000 t (~₹130 cr, ~₹40 cr incremental) over 5 years [Q4 call].
- Q1 call (29 Jul 2026): **new long-range targets** — ₹900–950 cr revenue in FY28 and ₹1,000–1,050 cr in FY29 (at current polymer prices); margin "continue at the levels we are currently at"; Essekan **cut** to ~₹10 cr in FY27 (then ₹25 cr, ₹50 cr, ₹100 cr in four years); non-woven revenue "by Q3 FY27"; FIBC 5,000 t a quarter by Q1 FY28 [Q1 call].
- Delivery vs Q4: margin — **kept** (10.7% vs ~11%); FIBC volume — **missed** (3,000 t vs 3,500 t); Unit 3 building slipped from May to mid-September 2026; non-woven "September" became "Q3 FY27"; Essekan revenue guide halved [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP slide 12].
- CRISIL upgrade trigger: 20% revenue growth with 10–11% operating margin; downgrade if margin falls below 6% [CRISIL Jul-26].

## Capex and capacity
- **CRISIL: ₹108 cr capex planned**, ₹40 cr through a term loan and the rest from internal accruals, mainly for the non-woven expansion [CRISIL Jul-26]. Management confirmed a new ₹40 cr term loan this year; long-term debt to rise from ₹34 cr to ~₹67–68 cr by March 2027 [Q1 call].
- Non-woven: two needle-punch lines in the old CPP building; machines arriving from June 2026; target IRR 15–20% and ROCE above 20%; working capital 30–40 days of added sales [AR FY26 Board's report; Q1 call]. Project cost not stated in the documents read (stored profile says ₹55 cr — unverified).
- FIBC Unit 3: +6,000 t over five years (1,200 t a year); building floors 1–2 due 15 Sep 2026; also an automated roll store (Sep 2026) and a new owned warehouse [IP Q1FY27 slide 12].
- Essekan needs little capex: ~₹3 cr of yarn equipment installed in FY26; capacity supports ~₹30 cr sales; KPL makes the yarn and sells to the JV at a 20% markdown [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Capital advances rose from ₹1.46 cr to ₹13.75 cr at Mar-26 for these projects [Q4 call]. Screener CWIP ₹9 cr at Mar-26 [Screener].
- Renewable power now >60% of energy, saving ~₹3–5 cr a year [Q1 call; IP slide 13].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹112 cr at Mar-26 (₹224 cr Mar-24; ₹145 cr Mar-25) after the CPP sale and two warrant issues [Screener; AR FY26]. Net debt ₹112 cr at Mar-26 (₹78 cr short-term, ₹23.8 cr GECL, ₹9 cr term loans) and ₹132 cr at Jun-26 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Working capital ~₹98 cr, rising ₹5–10 cr by year-end; FY26 inventory 86 days, debtors 48 days, payables 18 days → cash cycle 116 days (down from 155) [Q1 call; Screener].
- Cash from operations ₹51 cr (FY26), free cash flow ₹80 cr (helped by CPP asset sale) [Screener].
- Equity raised: 7.52 lakh shares on warrant conversion (May 2025); 10.12 lakh warrants at ₹130 (Aug 2025), all converted by 13 Aug 2026 — last 93,500 to promoter Shashank Agarwal and Raghushree Earning Solutions LLP [AR FY26; BSE filing 13-Aug-26]. Promoter holding rose from 66.7% to 68.15% [Screener].
- CRISIL BBB+/Stable/A2 reaffirmed 1 Jul 2026; bank lines raised to ₹305 cr from ₹225 cr; gearing 0.43x, interest cover 5.75x, net worth ₹268 cr [CRISIL Jul-26].
- Term loans are personally guaranteed by the CMD and Deputy MD [AR FY26 note 15.5].
- Dividend payout ~7% of FY26 profit [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- 30+ years of FIBC exports; many customers of 20+ years; no single market share above 10–15% in any European country [Q4 call].
- Food-grade cleanroom FIBCs, BRCGS A+ certification, UN-certified bags; Essekan yarn has GRS (recycled) and Oeko-Tex certificates that "very few competitors" hold [IP slides 6, 9; Q1 call].
- Price pass-through with a lag of 2 weeks to a quarter [Q1 call].
- Limits: India supplies ~60% of Europe's FIBC imports, so competition is mainly other Indian makers; Asia is price-driven and low-margin; Japan entry stalled when Chinese PP was cheaper [Q4 call]. Margins were 4–5% as recently as FY23–FY24 [Screener].

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party acquisition.** The 76% stake in Valex Ventures (UK) was bought **from the CMD, Manoj Agarwal**, for ₹8.02 cr, paid with 3.34 lakh new shares issued to him, at a price above book value. The auditor made it a key audit matter, noting post-acquisition performance fell short of acquisition projections, yet no impairment was booked. Management calls Valex's revenue and profit "timid" [AR FY26 auditor's report KAM 2, CARO (xv), Board's report; Q1 call].
- Auditor's other key audit matters: CPP shown as discontinued (restated comparatives) and DFIA income not recognised (₹7.03 cr contingent asset) [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Earnings quality: trading profits (₹7.55 cr in Q4 FY26 alone) and forex gains swing reported EBITDA; DFIA income depends on customs policy [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Concentration: Europe 45–60% of exports; top-10 customers ~35% of revenue [CRISIL Jul-26; Q1 call].
- Ocean freight up from ~$2,000 to ~$5,000 per container in two months; order visibility only ~4 weeks; minimum-wage rise ₹3.5 cr a quarter [Q1 call].
- Execution: three new businesses at once (non-woven, Essekan, Valex) with timelines already slipping [Q4 and Q1 calls].
- ₹25 lakh provision on a ₹15 cr Food Corporation of India order (shortage/quality claims) [Q1 call].
- Contingent liabilities are small: bank counter-guarantees ₹7.9 cr, DGFT undertakings ₹0.2 cr [AR FY26 note 33].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was defensive (PP up 65%, DFIA reversal, 3–4 week order cycles); Q1 celebrated the first ₹200 cr quarter and gave FY28–FY29 revenue targets for the first time.
- New cost pressures appeared (freight, wages); raw-material volatility faded (±10% vs ±25%).
- Essekan FY27 revenue guide dropped from ₹20–25 cr to ~₹10 cr; non-woven start slipped a quarter; FIBC volume fell.

## My read
Kanpur Plastipack has turned around well — ROCE up from 5% to 17%, debt halved, manufacturing margin up four quarters in a row to 12.6% — and the stock at ~14x earnings is not expensive. But the next leg depends on three new ventures that are all slipping (non-woven, Essekan, Valex), plus a ₹108 cr capex plan that will add debt, while the core FIBC volumes fell in Q1 and freight and wages are rising. The Valex deal, bought from the CMD above book with shares, is a governance mark against an otherwise simple business. The view improves if FIBC output recovers to 3,500–3,800 t a quarter and non-woven sales start in Q3 FY27; it worsens if freight stays near $5,000 and margins slip below 9%.
