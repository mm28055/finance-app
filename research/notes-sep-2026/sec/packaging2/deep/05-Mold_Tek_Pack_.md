# Mold-Tek Packaging Ltd — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code: MOLDTKPAC. **Use the standalone page** — the consolidated page is stale (last data FY22, when a subsidiary existed). Standalone: price ₹670, market cap ₹2,226 cr, ROCE 13.3%, ROE 10.8%, dividend yield 0.75% [Screener standalone, 28 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- India's leading maker of injection-moulded rigid plastic containers with in-mould labels (IML: the label is fused into the pail during moulding); 10 plants, ~66,900–67,000 tonnes a year capacity, 185+ moulding machines; builds its own moulds and robots [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- IML share: 75.8% of Q1 FY27 volume, 77.8% of value [Q1 call].
- FY26 segment volumes: Paints 20,689 t (+14.4%), revenue ₹388 cr (+13.5%); Food & FMCG 13,070 t (+22.2%); Lubricants 8,015 t (−13.0%); Pharma revenue ₹35 cr (from ₹11 cr; volume +209%); total 42,629 t (+11.4%) [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Q1 FY27 segments (volume t / revenue): Paints 6,199 t (+10.8%) / ₹139 cr vs ₹105 cr; Lubes ~1,663 t (−17%) / ₹46 cr; Q-Pack (square edible-oil packs) ~1,985 t (+2%) / ₹38 cr; Food 1,980 t (+26%) / ₹66 cr vs ₹48 cr; Pharma 263 t (+39%) / ₹10.5 cr vs ₹7.4 cr [IP Q1FY27 p.11; Q1 call].
- Mix: paints ~46% of value, ~50% of volume; food ~24% (28–29% with Q-Pack); pharma ~2% of volume, ~3.5% of sales [Q1 call].
- Customers: Asian Paints, Aditya Birla (Grasim's Birla Opus) — ABG volumes +60% in FY26 and now a top-10 customer — plus Castrol, Shell, HUL, Mondelez; Berger and Nerolac increased orders during the March 2026 supply crisis [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Utilisation: ~72% in Q1 FY27 (12,089 t × 4 on ~67,000 t); dedicated ABG plants (12,000 t at Panipat, Cheyyar, Mahad) ~65% [my arithmetic; Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (11 May 2026): FY27 volume +10–13%, value +13–15%, revenue >₹1,000 cr; EBITDA/kg ₹42.5–43; EBITDA ≥₹210 cr (+20%); pharma ₹50–55 cr; capex ₹80–85 cr, no new debt; ROCE 13.5–14% FY27, ~15% FY28; pharma land plant by end-FY27 [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 delivery: EBITDA/kg ₹46.7 (beat); EBITDA +19% (on track for 20%); volume only +6.25% (below 10–13%) because lubes fell 17% on base-oil shortages; pharma +41% value (on track); revenue +25% (inflated by pass-through) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 call (27 Jul 2026): EBITDA/kg guidance **raised** to ₹44–45; volume "10% is possible" (kept low end); paint volume 10–15%; EBITDA growth 18–20% "for rest of the year"; capex ~₹90 cr (₹25–30 cr pharma, rest balancing/replacement) — slightly above the ₹80–85 cr given in May [Q1 call].
- Longer term: paints to fall to ~40% of mix in 3–4 years; pharma 40–50% CAGR; food/FMCG 18–20% CAGR; capacity +10–12% a year [Q1 call].
- Track record: a ₹1,200 cr FY28 revenue goal was mentioned by an analyst as "past guidance"; management only said "I hope so" [Q4 call].

## Capex and capacity
- ~₹500 cr invested over four years (pharma, food/FMCG, moulding capacity, printing), mostly greenfield for ABG plants (Panipat, Cheyyar, Mahad) and pharma [AR FY26 MD&A]. Capex ~₹140 cr a year for two years, ~₹120–135 cr in FY26 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- FY26 capitalisation ₹135 cr (auditor's key audit matter) [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Capital commitments ₹22 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 32].
- FY27 plan ~₹90 cr: Panipat thin-wall doubled to 8 machines (Aug 2026; ~2,000–2,500 t food/Q-Pack); ophthalmic packaging in a 25,000 sq ft building at Sultanpur (Hyderabad), commercial moulds in 5–6 months, entry early CY27; pharma capacity from ~1,500 t to 2,500 t; Q1 capex ₹20–22 cr [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- New land (~2.5 acres) reserved for medical devices (dosing pens incl. semaglutide pens): minimum plant ₹25–30 cr, 1 mn pens a month to start, 1 year if an IP partner (China/Korea talks from Sept 2026), 2–3 years if in-house [Q1 call].
- Longer shots: semiconductor trays (EBITDA/kg "₹150–200", guess), Vibe patented closures (pilot moulds ready; "a couple of crores" in Q4 FY27), Swiggy restaurant packaging tie-up [Q1 call; stored profile].
- Hyderabad consolidation (5 units → 2) done by Jan–Feb 2026; idle land parcels may be sold "in a year or 2"; Sandila (UP) lease and building transferred for an ₹0.8 cr gain [Q1 call; AR FY26 note 29].
- Asset turns: FY26 sales ₹887 cr on net fixed assets ₹623 cr ≈ 1.4x (vs ~2.4x in FY22) [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Pioneer of IML in India; in-house mould and robot making; metallic IML for Asian Paints that is "very complex to handle" [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Pass-through proved in a shock: polymer ₹105 → ₹160/kg in March 2026, fully passed on; some clients shortened repricing from quarterly to monthly or fortnightly [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Share gains in stress: clients "coming back in herds" (Berger, Nerolac) during supply scares [Q4 call].
- Weak spots: lost BPCL (₹14–15 cr sales) to L1 tendering; Q-Pack customers switch back to tins when polymer spikes; paints/Q-Pack EBITDA only ₹30–35/kg [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Regulatory: 30% recycled plastic mandatory in paint/lube packs (40% from April 2027) — Mold-Tek retooled screws in Q4 FY26 [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Returns:** ROCE fell from 22% to 12–13% after ₹500 cr capex; PAT still below FY23; management targets only ~15% by FY28 [Screener; Q4 call].
- **Customer concentration:** heavy dependence on Asian Paints and ABG (paints ~half of volume); Asian Paints' volume was negative until Q3 FY26 [Q4 call].
- **Working capital and FCF:** inventory days up from 83 to 148 in three years [Screener].
- **Related parties:** purchases from promoter-controlled firms — Friends Packaging ₹4.4 cr, Capricorn Industries ₹4.5 cr, Dynamic Metal ₹2.7 cr — and transport from Sri Kanaka Durga Mini Transport ₹1.0 cr; several family members employed as VPs [AR FY26 note 33]. KMP pay: CMD ~₹3.9 cr [AR FY26 note 33].
- **Diversification risk:** medical devices and semiconductor trays are early and depend on finding IP partners abroad [Q1 call].
- **Disclosure slip:** the FY26 MD&A's HR paragraph says "our journey of growth at Cosmo" — copied text from another company [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Auditor: unmodified; key audit matters revenue recognition and capitalisation of ₹135 cr; CARO clean, bank stock statements agree with books; disputed tax small [AR FY26 auditor's report, CARO].
- Contingent liabilities ₹6.5 cr (income tax ₹5.6 cr) [AR FY26 note 31].
- An investor on the Q4 call said exchanges stopped accepting Mold-Tek shares as margin collateral; management did not know why [Q4 call]. No promoter pledge is disclosed [Screener].

## My read
The business is improving on profit per kilo (₹37.6 → ₹46.7 in five quarters) thanks to the Hyderabad consolidation and a richer pharma/food mix, and paints demand has turned positive with Asian Paints adopting more IML. The weak link is capital efficiency: four years of heavy capex have left ROCE at 12–13%, free cash flow near zero and inventory days high, so earnings must grow ~20% a year just to pull returns back to mid-teens. Volume growth (6% in Q1) is below the 10–13% guide. I would turn more positive if EBITDA/kg holds ≥₹44 through H2 while volumes reach ~10% and FCF turns clearly positive; more negative if lubes and Q-Pack keep shrinking or medical-device spending starts before an IP partner is signed.
