# AGI Greenpac Ltd — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code: AGI (consolidated from FY25; standalone before). Price ₹803, market cap ₹5,197 cr, ROCE 19.5%, ROE 15.7%, dividend yield 0.87% [Screener, 28 Sep 2026].

Important context: the stored file holds the Q3 FY26 (Jan 2026) and Q2 FY26 (Oct 2025) calls. AGI held **no public earnings call** for Q4 FY26 or Q1 FY27; it published presentations and did one-on-one/conference meetings instead (Mumbai, 14–20 Aug 2026) [News: scanx.trade, 1 Aug 2026; Screener concall list]. So the latest management commentary on volumes and capex is eight months old — a disclosure gap in itself.

## Business and segments
- India's second-largest container-glass maker by capacity and now the largest by revenue; ~17–18% share of India's organised glass-packaging market [CARE Apr-26].
- Capacity: 2,100 TPD glass (Bhongir container 1,100 TPD, Hyderabad container 800 TPD, Bhongir specialty 200 TPD), rising to 2,600 TPD by March 2027; caps & closures 1,154 mn pieces a year; PET/plastic ~12,000 tonnes a year [IP Q1FY27 p.7].
- FY26 revenue mix: glass containers 91%, others (closures, PET) 9%; within glass, alcoholic beverages 75%, food & beverages 18%, pharma 7% [IP Q4FY26 p.24]. Value-added/premium products ~25% of FY26 revenue [IP Q1FY27 p.15]. Specialty glass is ~10% of glass revenue [Q2 call].
- Utilisation: container glass ~95% [Q3 call; AR FY26 MD&A]; specialty glass ~85% per the Q3 call but ~75% per the annual report and CARE [AR FY26 MD&A; CARE Apr-26].
- Customers: United Breweries, SABMiller (AB InBev), United Spirits, Coca-Cola; top 10 customers ~64% of sales [CARE Apr-26]; two customers = 27.3% of FY26 revenue (three customers = 40.6% in FY25) [AR FY26 note 47].
- Exports only 5–7% of sales; aspiration 10–15% overall and 40% in specialty [Q2 call].
- Segments reported: Packaging products ₹2,644 cr FY26; Investment property ₹21.6 cr (rent from promoter company Hindware Ltd) [AR FY26 note 47, note 53].

## Key numbers (Screener, ₹ cr)
- Quarters (consolidated): Jun-25 sales 688 / operating profit 142 (21%) / net profit 89; Sep-25 602 / 150 (25%) / 76; Dec-25 634 / 150 (24%) / 71; Mar-26 742 / 153 (21%) / 115; Jun-26 785 / 175 (22%) / 99 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: revenue +14.2%, EBITDA (excl. other income) +23.1% to ₹175 cr, margin 22.3%; PAT +11.8% to ₹99 cr; EBIT only +3.2% [IP Q1FY27 p.19]. Chairman blamed West Asia energy and raw-material costs for "temporarily weighing on margins" [IP Q1FY27 p.24].
- FY26 (company): revenue ₹2,665 cr (+5.4%), EBITDA excl. other income ₹595 cr (−3.1%), margin 22.3% vs 24.3%; PAT ₹352 cr (+9.1%) helped by other income of ₹90 cr and lower interest [IP Q4FY26 p.20; Screener].
- Q4 FY26 PAT ₹115 cr was flattered by ₹53 cr other income; operating margin was only 20.6% [Screener; IP Q4FY26 p.20].
- Years (standalone history): FY23 sales 2,281 / OP 462 (20%) / PAT 262; FY24 2,418 / 561 (23%) / 251; FY25 2,529 / 614 (24%) / 322; FY26 2,665 / 595 (22%) / 352 [Screener standalone and consolidated].
- Revenue CAGR FY21–26 16% (company chart), but FY24–26 only ~5% a year: the business has been capacity-bound [IP Q1FY27 p.21; Screener].
- Unit economics: guided EBITDA per tonne ₹9,500–10,500 (excl. other income) [Q3 call official]. Container glass realisation fell ~₹450/t QoQ and ₹1,200/t YoY in Q3 FY26 (raw-material pass-through), specialty realisation rose ~₹6,800/t YoY [Q3 call]. 9M FY26 volume growth: container ~8%, specialty >22% [Q3 call].

## Guidance history
- Q2 FY26 (Oct 2025): revenue growth 8–10% in FY26 and FY27; EBITDA margin 24–25% for 12–18 months, "1–2% higher in 24 months"; specialty margin +4–5 points in 18 months; FY28 growth from Gwalior [Q2 call].
- Q3 FY26 (Jan 2026): 9M revenue only +5.4%; FY26 now "7–9% on volume", value growth lower; FY27 volume +8–9% (container +3–4%, specialty +7–10%); FY28 growth 15–17%; margin 24–25% kept [Q3 call].
- Delivery: FY26 revenue +5.4% and EBITDA margin 22.3% — **missed** both the 8–10% growth and 24–25% margin [IP Q4FY26 p.20]. Q1 FY27 revenue +14.2% is strong but margin 22.3% still below guidance [IP Q1FY27 p.19].
- No updated guidance since January 2026 (no calls held) [disclosure gap]. CARE expects margins to "normalise at 22–24%" as rival HNG's supply returns [CARE Apr-26].

## Capex and capacity
- **Debottlenecking (done):** container glass 1,850 → 1,900 TPD and specialty 154 → 200 TPD, ~₹47 cr, commissioned by March 2026 [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q4FY26 p.25].
- **Gwalior (Madhya Pradesh) greenfield:** 500 TPD container glass, ~₹700 cr, commissioning March 2027 (CARE says COD December 2026); +25% glass capacity, aimed at North India [Q3 call; CARE Apr-26; IP Q1FY27 p.14].
- **Aluminium cans (Hathras, UP):** 1.6 bn cans/year (phase 1 of 950 mn cans per the Q2 call), board-approved capex ₹1,000 cr (CARE: ₹900–1,000 cr; Q2 call: ₹850 cr phase 1); ground-breaking at Hathras (34 acres) in early May 2026, initial output 1.3 bn cans; commercial operations "by June 2027" (alcircle) or "by October 2027" (Packaging South Asia) [AR FY26 secretarial report; CARE Apr-26; Q2 call; News: alcircle, 6 May 2026; News: Packaging South Asia, 2 Apr 2026].
- Spend profile: ~₹220 cr capex in 9M FY26; ₹1,100–1,200 cr in FY27; rest in FY28. Total programme ~₹1,900–2,000 cr to March 2028 [Q3 call official; Q2 call].
- Capital commitments jumped to ₹292 cr at Mar-26 from ₹35 cr [AR FY26 note 52].
- Payback: glass breaks even at 65–70% utilisation, cans at 60–65% [Q2 call]. At ~₹2,000 cr of new capital against a business doing ₹595 cr EBITDA, new assets need roughly ₹400 cr+ EBITDA to hold the ~20% ROCE [my arithmetic].
- Growth maths: Gwalior adds 25% tonnage (FY28 growth 15–17% guided); cans are a new ₹-scale line — at, say, ₹6–7 per can, 1.3–1.6 bn cans would be ₹800–1,100 cr revenue at full load [my estimate, not company guidance].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹241 cr at Mar-26 (₹553 cr Mar-25); all ECBs prepaid Dec 2025; net debt/EBITDA 0.11x at FY26 (company uses EBITDA incl. other income ₹690 cr) [Screener; IP Q4FY26 p.18; Q3 call].
- Net debt was ₹389 cr at Dec-25 because of unsold inventory from weather-hit beer demand [Q3 call].
- Cash from operations ₹572 cr FY26 (₹429 cr FY25); free cash flow ₹182 cr (₹181 cr) [Screener].
- Working capital: inventory days 215 (193 in FY25), debtor days 54, payable days 81, cash conversion cycle 188 days [Screener]. Inventory is structurally high (glass makers stock for the beer season), but rising.
- Funding plan: debt + accruals; peak debt ₹1,000–1,400 cr (~2x EBITDA) without equity; enabling resolution for up to ₹1,500 cr QIP passed Nov 2025, not used after the share price fell ~30% [Q2 call; Q3 call; AR FY26 secretarial report].
- Dividend payout ~13% of profit [Screener].
- Ratings: CARE AA-/Stable, A1+ reaffirmed 8 Apr 2026; CARE sees net leverage below 1.5x and GCA ₹500–600 cr in FY27–28 [CARE Apr-26].

## Moat evidence
- Scale and cost: ~95% utilisation for years; claims "best in Asia" container-glass profitability; 40%+ cullet (recycled glass) use lowers energy [Q2 call; IP Q1FY27 p.14].
- Pricing: ~60% of customers on raw-material pass-through formulas (lag 1 quarter to 1 year), rest market-priced [CARE Apr-26; Q2 call].
- Industry structure helped: main rival HNG (largest installed capacity) produced less during its insolvency, tightening supply [CARE Apr-26]. AGI lost its bid for HNG — Supreme Court rejected its resolution plan (29 Jan 2025) and dismissed review (16 May 2025) [AR FY26 Board's report]. HNG's return under new owners is the main competitive threat.
- Switching costs: long customer qualification cycles, custom moulds [IP Q1FY27 p.13].
- Margin trend (company): EBITDA margin 21% FY21 → 24% FY25 → 22% FY26 [IP Q1FY27 p.21].

## Risks and red flags
- **Guidance misses:** FY26 growth and margin both below guidance; Q3 volume shortfall blamed on weather two quarters running [Q2 call; Q3 call; IP Q4FY26].
- **No earnings calls** for two quarters while spending ~₹2,000 cr [Screener concall list; News: scanx.trade].
- **Capex scale:** ~₹2,000 cr programme is ~0.75x current gross block; a new product (cans) against established players (Ball, Can-Pack) [Q2 call; my inference].
- **Other-income dependence:** ₹90 cr FY26 other income = ~19% of PBT [Screener].
- **Customer concentration:** two customers 27% of revenue; top 10 ~64% [AR FY26 note 47; CARE Apr-26].
- **Related parties (Somany/Hindware group):** rent received from Hindware ₹21.6 cr; sales to Hindware ₹23.4 cr; management services paid to Hindware ₹19.6 cr in FY25 (nil FY26); KMP pay up 57% to ₹27.4 cr [AR FY26 note 53]. Promoter holds 60.4%, no pledge disclosed [Screener].
- **Regulatory:** SEBI penalty ₹5 lakh for LODR violation (order Apr 2024), appealed at SAT, deposited under protest [AR FY26 secretarial report].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter revenue recognition (rebates/discounts) [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Contingent liabilities ~₹100 cr, mostly tax, including ₹50 cr entry-tax demands [AR FY26 note 51].
- Input costs: fuel/gas 20–30% of costs; West Asia conflict raised energy prices in Q1 FY27 [CARE Apr-26; IP Q1FY27 p.24].
- Board churn: director G.L. Sultania resigned 28 Apr 2026; new non-independent director R.B. Kabra and independent director S.K. Roongta joined [IP Q1FY27 p.28].

## My read
The core glass business is excellent but capacity-bound: FY24–26 revenue grew only ~5% a year and margins slipped from 24% to 22% as input costs and a weaker beer season bit. The real story is FY28: Gwalior (+25% tonnage) and cans could lift revenue 40–60% over three years, but only if both ramp on time and margins hold while debt rises to ~2x EBITDA. The price does not need much growth today, so the risk is execution rather than valuation. I would turn more cautious if Gwalior slips past mid-2027, if margins stay near 22% once HNG's capacity returns, or if management keeps avoiding public calls.
