# TCPL Packaging Ltd — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code: TCPLPACK (consolidated). Price ₹4,107, market cap ₹3,737 cr, ROCE 17.7%, ROE 15.7%, dividend yield 0.61% [Screener, 28 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- One of India's largest organised makers of paperboard folding cartons (FMCG, food, tobacco, pharma, liquor), plus flexible packaging (laminates, cork-tipping paper, sleeves), rigid boxes and gravure cylinders; 10 plants (four in Silvassa, two in Haridwar, Goa, Guwahati, Chennai) [IP Q1FY27 p.16; CRISIL May-26].
- Only one reported segment (packaging) [AR FY26 MD&A]. Management gives rough splits only: folding cartons "more than half" of business; flexible packaging "about 20%", slightly up in FY26 [Q4 call]. The domestic/export split is no longer disclosed despite analyst requests [Q4 call; disclosure gap].
- Customers: top 5 ~40% of sales, no single customer above 15% [CRISIL May-26]. Names cited: Marico, Abbott [Q4 call].
- Subsidiaries (FY26): TCPL Middle East FZE (UAE) turnover ₹392 cr, PAT ₹4.9 cr; Creative Offset Printers (rigid/electronics boxes, Noida) turnover ₹54 cr, loss ₹4.4 cr; Accura Technik (gravure cylinders, Silvassa, 1,000 cylinders/month, commissioned Nov 2025) turnover ₹2.3 cr, small loss [AR FY26 AOC-1; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Utilisation: flexible packaging "almost fully utilized"; folding cartons "more than 70%" overall; Chennai greenfield ">50%" in June and "getting there" toward 70% in August [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarters: Jun-25 sales 425 / operating profit 73 (17%) / net profit 22; Sep-25 460 / 69 (15%) / 29; Dec-25 471 / 81 (17%) / 25; Mar-26 454 / 69 (15%) / 22; Jun-26 493 / 86 (17%) / 40 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 (company): total income ₹494.9 cr (+15.9%), EBITDA incl. other income ₹87.9 cr (+17.3%), margin 17.8%; PAT ₹40.0 cr (+79%) because the base quarter carried a ₹10.6 cr euro-loan mark-to-market charge in finance costs [IP Q1FY27 p.4, 9, 11].
- Growth quality: domestic "good double-digit", exports "not bad"; volume "good high single-digit" with slightly more from price [Q1 call].
- Years: FY24 sales 1,541 / OP 252 (16%) / PAT 99; FY25 1,770 / 294 (17%) / 143; FY26 1,810 / 292 (16%) / 98 [Screener]. FY26 PAT fell 32% on higher interest (₹79 cr incl. ₹18 cr ECB mark-to-market), a ₹13.5 cr labour-code exceptional charge and a 31% tax rate [Screener; Q4 call; AR FY26 notes].
- FY26 revenue growth only 2.3% (exports hit by Gulf shipping disruption); EBITDA margin 17.3% vs 17.2% [IP Q1FY27 p.9; AR FY26 MD&A].
- 5-year sales CAGR 15%, 3-year 7%; 5-year profit CAGR 26%, 3-year 3% [Screener].
- Unit economics not disclosed (no volumes, no per-tonne figures) [disclosure gap].

## Guidance history
- TCPL gives almost no numeric guidance. Q4 FY26 (3 Jun 2026): FY27 capex ~₹100 cr, "similar to last year"; margins "okay" if raw-material inflation "stops here"; expected good domestic year, export recovery; Chennai and cylinder plant to ramp "over the next one to two quarters" [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 delivery: record revenue, margin up 21 bps to 17.8% despite pass-through lag; exports grew; Chennai nearer 70% [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]. Promises kept.
- Q1 FY27 call (12 Aug 2026): no FY27 growth/margin guidance ("margins typically follow the top line"); capex now ₹100–150 cr, the increase being ₹30–40 cr of separator land, not core packaging [Q1 call].
- New long-term targets: separator phase 1 revenue ₹150–200 cr; at 500 mn sq m "₹1,100–1,300 cr" [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Core: FY27 ~₹100 cr, including a 4th flexible-packaging line (+30% flexible capacity, ₹50–60 cr, running by Jan–Feb 2027). Management expects the 4th line's return on assets to beat the 3rd and 2nd lines [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Past three years capex ran ~₹150 cr a year (Chennai greenfield, Silvassa cylinder plant) [Q4 call]. Gross block ₹1,327 cr, capital employed ₹1,294 cr at Mar-26 [IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- Folding-carton expansion is prepared (buildings enlarged, Chennai space for 2–3 lines) and can be ordered on "one quarter's notice" [Q1 call].
- **Battery separator films (new):** ~₹125 cr over 18 months via a new 100% subsidiary; phase 1 coating/conversion of 70 mn sq m a year (enough for 6–8 GWh of cells); commercial production targeted Q4 FY28 (Jan–Feb 2028); FY29 mostly customer qualification; long-term 500 mn sq m (~50 GWh) over 5–7 years with backward integration into base film (capex "more than 8x") [IP Q1FY27 p.8; Q1 call].
- Technology developed in-house "from various sources", no licensor; no Indian lithium-ion separator maker yet; no battery-materials PLI scheme exists yet [Q1 call].
- Returns claimed: phase 1 "good double-digit margin" and returns above TCPL's ~20% ROCE hurdle; asset turn ~1.2–1.6x on ₹125 cr if ₹150–200 cr revenue is reached [Q1 call; my arithmetic].
- EPCG export obligation outstanding ₹201 cr (duty-free machine imports must be matched by exports) [AR FY26 note, contingent liabilities].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹554.7 cr at Mar-26; net debt/equity 0.77x; net debt/EBITDA 1.75x; cash ₹20 cr [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.12]. Borrowings ₹634 cr (₹651 cr Mar-25) [Screener].
- Euro-denominated term loan (ECB) caused MTM charges: ₹18 cr in FY26 finance costs, ₹10.6 cr in Q1 FY26 [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.9].
- Cash from operations ₹271 cr FY26 (₹133 cr FY25); free cash flow ₹121 cr (−₹24 cr FY25) [Screener].
- Working capital: debtor days 93 (95 FY25, 73 FY23), inventory days 85, payable days 80, cash conversion cycle 98 days [Screener]. Company-stated net working capital 96 days [IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- Dividend ₹25/share for FY26 (₹30 for FY25), 23% payout, 26th straight year [Q4 call; AR FY26 Board's report].
- Ratings: CRISIL A+/Stable, A1 reaffirmed 21 May 2026, bank limits raised to ₹550 cr; fund-based utilisation ~67% [CRISIL May-26]. CRISIL expected FY26 revenue growth of ~5% (actual 2.3%).

## Moat evidence
- Scale and breadth among organised carton makers; long relationships with MNC FMCG/pharma; supplier awards (Marico, Abbott) [CRISIL May-26; Q4 call].
- Share gains: domestic volumes grew "ahead of underlying consumer market" in FY26 and Q1 FY27 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Pass-through is by negotiation, customer by customer, with a lag of "more than a quarter"; "drip-by-drip" board price rises are hardest to pass [Q4 call; Q1 call]. This is a cost-plus model with limited pricing power (CRISIL cites intense competition).
- Vertical integration: in-house gravure cylinders, inks (via related Accura Inks), recyclable mono-material PE film line (demand weak in India, used for exports) [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Margins steady at 16–17% for five years — stable, not rising [Screener].

## Risks and red flags
- **Separator venture:** new technology, no licensor, 1-year qualification, Chinese competition with capacity "in the billions of square metres"; ₹125 cr is ~17% of net worth [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- **Leverage and interest:** net debt 1.75x EBITDA; interest ₹79 cr in FY26 absorbed ~27% of EBITDA; unhedged euro-loan MTM swings [Screener; Q4 call].
- **Earnings volatility below EBITDA:** FY26 PAT −32% while EBITDA was flat; tax rate 31% on deferred tax from capex [Screener; Q4 call].
- **Disclosure:** export/domestic split withdrawn; no segment data [Q4 call].
- **Related parties:** raw-material purchases from TCPL Halma ₹53 cr and services from Accura Reprotech ₹4.3 cr (promoter-influenced entities); KMP remuneration ₹17.8 cr [AR FY26 note 38].
- **Subsidiary losses:** Creative Offset lost ₹4.4 cr on ₹54 cr sales; "long slog", only EBITDA break-even [AR FY26 AOC-1; Q4 call].
- Contingent liabilities modest: income tax ₹11.3 cr, GST ₹2.7 cr, excise ₹0.8 cr, bank guarantees/LCs ₹13.8 cr [AR FY26 contingent liabilities note].
- Auditor: unmodified; key audit matter inventory valuation across plants [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report].
- Governance transition: Executive Chairman K.K. Kanoria resigned 9 Feb 2026; Saket Kanoria became CMD; next generation (Akshay, Vidur) run calls [AR FY26 note 38]. Promoter holding steady at 55.7%, no pledge shown [Screener].
- End-market: tobacco carton exposure to cigarette tax changes; paperboard minimum import price may lift board costs [Q4 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (Gulf freight, "hope this is where it ends" on inflation) to expansionary (flexible line, separator venture) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Numbers: revenue growth jumped from +9% (Q4 total income) to +16%; PAT +79% on a low base [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- New: battery separator entry; capex range widened to ₹100–150 cr; Chennai utilisation rose from ">50%" to near 70% [Q1 call].
- Dropped: ECB MTM and Middle East shipping discussion [Q1 call].

## Best management quotes
- "The battery business... is very unforgiving." — Saket Kanoria [Q1 call]
- "When there is a steady drip-by-drip increase, then it is more difficult because it is harder to pass through." — Akshay Kanoria [Q4 call]
- "We want more capex and more pressure on the growth." — Akshay Kanoria [Q4 call]

## My read
The core carton-plus-flexibles business is improving again after a flat FY26: domestic share gains, Chennai ramping and a fourth flexible line should support low-to-mid-teens revenue growth with steady 17–18% margins. Profit growth depends as much on lower interest and normal tax as on operations, because debt is meaningful. The separator project is a real option but will not earn before FY29 and carries technology and pricing risk against Chinese suppliers. I would turn more positive if Chennai passes ~80% utilisation and net debt/EBITDA falls below 1.5x; more negative if separator spending grows beyond ₹125 cr before any customer qualification.
