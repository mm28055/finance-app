# EPL Ltd — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code: EPL (consolidated). Price ₹238, market cap ₹7,627 cr, ROCE 17.8%, ROE 15.7%, dividend yield 1.05% [Screener, 28 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- World's largest maker of laminated plastic tubes (toothpaste, creams, cosmetics, pharma ointments); 21 plants in 11 countries, 9 bn+ tubes a year, 6,000+ staff [IP Q1FY27 p.30; AR FY26 MD&A p.130].
- Category split of tube revenue, Q1 FY27: Oral Care 46%, Beauty & Cosmetics (B&C) 40%, Pharma 9%, Others 5%; "Personal Care & Beyond" (everything non-oral) 54%, up from 43% in FY19 [IP Q1FY27 p.31, p.12].
- Share: ~20% of the ~42 bn-tube global laminated-tube market; ~35% of global oral-care tubes ("one in three"); only ~10% in B&C/pharma and ~8% in food/home [CARE Apr-26]. Management put its B&C share at 8% and wants 16% "in the next few years" [Q1 call].
- Four regions (Q1 FY27 revenue, EBITDA margin): AMESA incl. India ₹437 cr, 18.5%; EAP (China, Philippines, Thailand) ₹360 cr, 20.2%; Americas ₹379 cr, 18.1%; Europe ₹321 cr, 13.1% (EBIT margin only 4.8%, down from 10.4%) [IP Q1FY27 p.27–28].
- FY26 regional trend: AMESA revenue +5.1% (margin 18.7%); EAP +15.7% (margin 22.1%); Americas +21.1%, EBITDA +37.1% (margin 20.5%); Europe +10.4% but EBITDA −3.2% (margin 14.5%) [AR FY26 MD&A p.132].
- FY26 category growth: B&C ~30%, Oral Care only ~3.4% [AR FY26 MD&A p.131–132]. Q1 FY27: B&C +23.6%, Oral +23.9%, Personal Care & Beyond +25.1% [IP Q1FY27 p.11, 13, 14].
- Thailand plant commissioned Nov 2025 for ~US$5 m; still in customer validation, "not much volume" as of May 2026 [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]. Brazil (3 years old) is the template: started with one customer, now several [Q4 call].
- Capacity utilisation is not disclosed [disclosure gap].

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarters: Jun-25 sales 1,108 / operating profit 227 (20%) / net profit 101; Sep-25 1,206 / 252 (21%) / 106; Dec-25 1,149 / 230 (20%) / 83; Mar-26 1,300 / 256 (20%) / 103; Jun-26 1,388 / 261 (19%) / 101 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: revenue +25.3% (underlying +20% after stripping out cost pass-through pricing), EBITDA +15.2%, reported margin 18.8% vs 19.6% underlying, PAT −1.4% because tax rate rose to 22% from 14%; PBT +10% [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.7; Screener].
- Depreciation is the drag: ₹90 cr (Jun-25) → ₹109 cr (Jun-26), +21%, faster than EBITDA. Interest flat at ₹28–29 cr a quarter [Screener].
- Years: FY24 sales 3,916 / OP 715 (18%) / PAT 210; FY25 4,213 / 837 (20%) / 364; FY26 4,763 / 965 (20%) / 394. TTM 5,043 / 999 / 393 [Screener]. FY26 PAT excluding exceptionals ₹417 cr (+15%) [AR FY26 MD&A p.131].
- Longer view: 10-year sales CAGR 8%, 5-year 9%; 5-year profit CAGR 10%; 10-year ROE 14% [Screener]. This is a steady high-single-digit grower that is now running faster.
- Unit economics: volume growth is not disclosed ("will not make too much sense" due to mix) [Q1 call]. Revenue per tube therefore cannot be tracked [disclosure gap].
- FY26 exceptional items ₹28 cr: merger costs ₹15.6 cr, China factory closure ₹6.7 cr, labour-code gratuity ₹5.9 cr [AR FY26 note 41].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (14 May 2026): long-run revenue growth "low double digits, 11–13%", EBITDA growth "slightly ahead" of revenue; ~20% EBITDA margin; would "recover all the additional cost" of the Middle East crisis with no lag; Thailand volumes "in the next couple of quarters"; FY27 capex "slightly elevated"; tax rate 18–22% [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +25.3% (underlying +20%) — well ahead; full cost pass-through achieved (underlying margin 19.6%, inside the ~20% band); tax at 22% (top of range) [Q1 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (11 Aug 2026): guidance **raised** to "high teens" revenue growth "for the next few quarters", 20% underlying margin kept; PAT to grow double digits for FY27; FY27 tax 20–22%; Europe margin back to "mid-teens" in coming quarters [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.11]. Analyst asked if EBITDA would also grow high-teens; management said "Yes" [Q1 call].
- Caveat: part of the "raise" is inflation pass-through (about 5 points of Q1 growth was price), so real volume/mix guidance is closer to mid-teens [Q1 call; my inference].
- Margin history shows the risk: after the post-COVID cost shock EBITDA margin fell to ~16% (FY23) and took two years to recover [IP Q1FY27 p.9; Q4 call].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex "₹480 cr plus" vs depreciation ₹385 cr [Q4 call]; the segment note shows ₹498 cr: AMESA ₹206 cr, Americas ₹133 cr, Europe ₹84 cr, EAP ₹76 cr [AR FY26 note 60].
- Q1 FY27 capex ₹108 cr vs ₹86 cr a year earlier [IP Q1FY27 p.8]. Spend is on extrusion lines, printing lines, tooling, metallisation and a B&C innovation centre; described as "ahead of the curve" [IP Q1FY27 p.5; Q1 call].
- Capital commitments outstanding ₹51 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 37]. CARE expects ~₹400 cr routine capex funded by accruals, cash and a ₹150 cr term loan taken in Mar-26 [CARE Apr-26].
- Asset turns: FY26 sales ₹4,763 cr on net fixed assets ₹2,397 cr ≈ 2.0x [Screener]. No payback figures are given [disclosure gap].
- Europe capex (new footprint, Poland centralisation) has not yet earned its return: Europe EBIT fell 44% in Q1 FY27 [IP Q1FY27 p.28].
- **Indovida merger (the big growth lever):** EPL absorbs Indovida India (Indorama Ventures' rigid PET preforms/bottles/closures business) by share swap of 286 EPL shares per 10,000 Indovida shares; transaction value ~₹6,256 cr (~12.5x EV/EBITDA). Indorama will own 51.8%, Blackstone 16.6%, public 31.6% [IVL deck Mar-26; CARE Apr-26]. Combined revenue ~US$1 bn, ~75% from emerging markets [AR FY26 MD&A p.133].
- Indovida CY25 EBITDA ~US$94 m (≈₹813 cr) [IVL deck Mar-26]; management said Indovida's latest quarter EBITDA was ₹383 cr, revenue +25%, margin 27%, and its PAT is "in line with EPL's PAT", making the deal "strongly EPS-accretive" [Q1 call — figure not verified in any filing I read].
- Approvals: CCI cleared 26 May 2026; 8 of 10 foreign anti-trust approvals received; exchanges, NCLT and shareholder votes pending; completion targeted Q4 FY27 [IP Q1FY27 p.25].
- My arithmetic: Blackstone's ~26.4% becoming 16.6% implies the share count rises ~59% (from ~32 cr to ~51 cr shares). If Indovida's profit really matches EPL's, EPS rises ~25% on day one [derived from IVL deck + Screener shareholding].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹581 cr at Jun-26 (0.58x EBITDA) vs ₹502 cr at Mar-26 (0.52x) and ₹390 cr at Jun-25 [IP Q1FY27 p.7; AR FY26 MD&A p.135].
- Working capital rose by roughly ₹180 cr in Q1 alone (analyst estimate; CFO confirmed it is inventory: higher prices plus safety stock) [Q1 call].
- Screener FY26: inventory days 177 (151 in FY25, 144 in FY24), debtor days 67, payable days 147, working-capital days 42 [Screener]. Inventory days have risen every year since FY21 (117).
- Cash from operations ₹723 cr in FY26 vs ₹795 cr in FY25; free cash flow ₹250 cr vs ₹439 cr; CFO/operating profit 86% [Screener].
- Borrowings ₹962 cr at Mar-26 (₹802 cr Mar-25); debt/equity 0.25; DSCR 3.98 [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A p.135].
- Dividend: no final dividend for FY26 because neither merging company may pay until completion; dividend payout fell to 21% of profit from 44% [Q4 call; Screener].
- Ratings: CARE AA+/A1+ on "Rating Watch with Developing Implications" (merger) [CARE Apr-26]; India Ratings IND AA+ (Stable) [AR FY26 MD&A p.135].

## Moat evidence
- Scale leader: ~35% global oral-care tube share and ~20% of all laminated tubes [CARE Apr-26].
- Pass-through: >50% of business on contracts with "landed cost plus power" pass-through; Q1 FY27 recovered 100% of polymer, freight and currency inflation within the quarter [Q4 call; Q1 call]. This is a clear improvement on FY23, when margin fell to ~16%.
- Wallet-share gains in a crisis: Oral Care +24% in Q1 as competitors struggled for supply; 80+ new B&C customers [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.5].
- Sustainability as a switching cost: sustainable (recyclable Platina) tubes 44% of mix in Q1 FY27 vs 21% in FY24; EcoVadis Platinum (top 1%), CDP A list [IP Q1FY27 p.17].
- Returns: ROCE ~18–19% through a cost shock [IP Q1FY27 p.7].
- Weak spots: B&C share is only ~8–10%, and competition includes Berry, Albéa, Hoffmann Neopac, Huhtamaki and unorganised players [CARE Apr-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Control change:** Blackstone sold 24.9% to Indorama at ₹240 a share (~₹1,908 cr) in May 2025; Indorama becomes 51.8% promoter post-merger [CARE Apr-26]. Minority holders will depend on a Thai conglomerate's capital allocation; deal priced at ~12.5x EBITDA versus ~9x for the May-2025 stake [IVL deck Mar-26].
- **Leadership change:** MD Anand Kripalu retired 31 Dec 2025; Hemant Bakshi MD & CEO from 1 Jan 2026 [AR FY26 note 43]. Two-CEO costs hit AMESA margins in Q4 FY26 [Q4 call].
- **Europe:** repeated footprint changes (UK/Germany/Poland), 13.1% Q1 EBITDA margin, 4.8% EBIT margin; management has called out "operational challenges" for three quarters [IP Q1FY27 p.28; Q1 call].
- **Capex running ahead of depreciation** and depreciation growing faster than EBITDA; EBIT margin fell from 12.4% to 10.9% in Q1 [IP Q1FY27 p.7].
- **Working capital and FCF:** inventory days 177 and FCF down 43% in FY26 [Screener].
- Tax rate swings (6%–25% by quarter) make PAT noisy [Screener].
- China: a factory closed in FY26 (₹6.7 cr cost), echoing earlier closures after customer relocations [AR FY26 note 41].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter is only revenue recognition; 15 foreign subsidiaries (₹3,645 cr revenue) audited by other auditors; one subsidiary and one associate unaudited but immaterial [AR FY26 auditor's report paras 15–16]. CARO: clean; bank stock statements agree with books; only a ₹2 lakh PF dispute (2013-14) [AR FY26 CARO].
- Contingent liabilities small: disputed indirect tax ₹39 cr, direct tax ₹25 cr, bank guarantees ₹7.7 cr [AR FY26 note 37]. Assets pledged for borrowings ₹1,368 cr (normal bank security, not promoter share pledges) [AR FY26 note 38].
- Customer concentration acknowledged as a risk but not quantified [AR FY26 MD&A p.136; disclosure gap].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defence (supply security, "cover till mid-July") to offence (share gains, guidance raised) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Growth guidance went from 11–13% "for a longish period" to "high teens for the next few quarters" in 90 days [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New issues: working capital up ~₹180 cr, net debt up, Europe EBIT down 44%, Americas margin dip from front-end investments [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Merger moved from "filed" to CCI-approved; management began quoting Indovida's own results to sell the deal [Q1 call].

## Best management quotes
- "We are raising our growth guidance to high teens for next few quarters while holding on to our margin range of underlying EBITDA of 20%." — Hemant Bakshi [Q1 call]
- "Our model right now is a landed cost plus power pass-through model." — management [Q4 call]
- "Currently, our market share is 8%. We believe this can increase to 16%." (B&C) — management [Q1 call]

## My read
The core tube business is clearly improving: five straight double-digit growth quarters, a pass-through model that finally worked in a cost shock, and B&C now the bigger half. But profit is lagging sales because capex, depreciation and working capital are all rising faster than EBITDA, and Europe remains a sore. On its own EPL looks like a ~12–15% earnings grower; the merger could lift EPS roughly a quarter on completion if Indovida's profits are as claimed, which is the real swing factor. I would change the view if Europe margins are not back near mid-teens by Q3 FY27, if inventory days do not ease once polymer prices settle, or if NCLT/shareholder approval slips past FY27.
