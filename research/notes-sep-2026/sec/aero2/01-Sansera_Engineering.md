## PROFILE (Q1 FY27)
# Sansera Engineering

## Executive Summary
**Sansera Engineering** is a specialized precision engineering powerhouse transitioning from a pure-play automotive component manufacturer to a diversified industrial technology supplier. The company solves the problem of high-precision, critical-tolerance manufacturing for complex geometries—specifically connecting rods, crankshafts, and rocker arms where failure is not an option. Historically a **Category Leader** in the Indian two-wheeler and passenger vehicle connecting rod market, Sansera is aggressively repositioning itself as a **Challenger** in the global Aerospace, Defense, and Semiconductor (ADS) sectors. With ADS revenues growing more than 3x year-over-year in Q1 FY27 and the non-auto segment now contributing nearly 21% of total sales, Sansera is shedding its "ICE-dependent" valuation discount to capture higher-margin, sticky industrial applications.

## The Business Model

### Revenue Drivers
*   **What they sell:** Precision forged and machined components. Core legacy products include connecting rods, rocker arms, and crankshafts. The expanding portfolio includes landing gear components, structural parts for aerospace, precision components for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, and a nascent entry into sheet metal manufacturing for defense.
*   **Who pays them:** Global and Domestic OEMs (e.g., Maruti Suzuki, Honda, Bajaj), Tier-1 Auto Suppliers, and increasingly, Aerospace Primes and Semiconductor Equipment manufacturers.
*   **How they make money:**
    *   **Automotive:** High-volume, long-term supply agreements with sticky wallet share.
    *   **Non-Auto (ADS):** Program-lifecycle contracts (often 5-10 years) with high switching costs.
    *   **Engineering:** Monetization of tooling and design capabilities via JVs (e.g., Niktek).

### Segment & Geographic Mix
Sansera is currently undergoing a structural mix shift. While domestic Auto remains the cash cow, the growth engine has shifted aggressively to Exports and Non-Auto.

| Segment | Trend | Commentary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Auto ICE (Domestic)** | High Growth | +20.8% YoY in Q1 FY27. Unprecedented momentum across 2W and PVs is driving capacity expansions (crank assemblies and con-rods) at the Pantnagar and Manesar facilities. |
| **Auto ICE (Exports)** | Stopgap Recovery | Customers are increasingly sourcing direct from India as a stopgap measure, given Sansera has deferred its US manufacturing facility timeline amid tariff uncertainties. |
| **ADS (Aero, Def, Semi)** | **Hyper-Growth** | **>3x YoY growth** in Q1 FY27. The standout engine of growth. The non-auto segment overall surged 129.9% YoY. |
| **Tech-Agnostic / xEV** | High Growth | +22.2% YoY. Aluminum forging and hybrid components gaining traction, supported by dedicated capacity augmentation at Bangalore Plant 2. |

### Competitive Moat (or Lack Thereof)
*   **Moat Status: Widening.**
*   **Evidence:** Sansera’s moat is built on **process complexity** and **switching costs**. In Automotive, they hold dominant market share in connecting rods, making displacing them risky for OEMs. In Aerospace/Semicon, the moat is rapidly deepening via "qualification barriers" and backward integration. The commissioning of an in-house surface treatment facility (targeting NADCAP validation) pushes Sansera into a technological tier where domestic auto-ancillaries simply cannot compete, drastically reducing external dependencies.
*   **The Bear Case:** The moat is threatened by the secular decline of the Internal Combustion Engine (ICE). If Sansera cannot scale its non-auto/EV revenue faster than the eventual ICE terminal value decline, their installed capacity for steel forging could become a stranded asset.

## Growth Vectors

*   **The ADS Pivot & Dedicated Scaling:** The primary growth vector is the **Aerospace, Defense, and Semiconductor (ADS)** division. The narrative has shifted from transition to aggressive structural scale-up. A cumulative 5-year unexecuted order book of **INR 57.5 billion** provides immense visibility through FY31, supported by peak annual revenue from non-ADS new business of INR 18.5 billion. Dedicated ADS capacities are being built to support a staggering ₹3,500 Cr in revenue potential by FY31.
*   **Capacity Expansion:**
    *   **Aerospace Hangar:** A new 80,000 sq. ft. facility for ADS is coming online in 1-2 months, followed by plans for a further 100,000 sq. ft. build-to-suit facility within 10 months.
    *   **ICE Augmentation:** Plant 6 (Pantnagar) and Plant 4 (Manesar) are expanding highly automated forging and machining capabilities for crankshafts and con-rods to capitalize on domestic ICE consolidation, tracking for Q3 FY27 commissioning.
*   **Defense as a Standalone Vertical:** Sansera is physically segregating operations by relocating its defense business to a dedicated facility to capture domestic and European export orders, aiming for a ₹500 Cr revenue potential over the next 5 years.
*   **Semiconductors & AI Upcycle:** Leveraging the global AI hardware boom, Sansera secured a landmark **$75M order** from a semiconductor equipment manufacturer (SEM) in Q1 FY27, locking in capacity that will peak in CY2029.

## Key Risks & Governance

*   **Regulatory & Operational Risks:**
    *   **Litigation & Tariffs:** Q1 FY27 included an exceptional hit of INR 16.9 crore due to a US litigation settlement, alongside an INR 12.6 crore opex provision for US import tariffs. 
    *   **Cost Inflation:** Mild aluminum cost inflation and higher labor costs require ongoing pass-through negotiations with customers, though forex gains have temporarily cushioned gross margins.
    *   **Export Volatility:** The business is heavily sensitive to US/EU trade policies. With the US plant construction delayed, reliance on direct Indian exports makes the ICE export business acutely vulnerable to any sudden retaliatory US tariffs.
*   **Execution Risk:** The explosive pivot to Semiconductor and Aerospace requires significantly tighter tolerances and quality controls than Automotive. A single quality failure in these sectors could result in immediate contract termination and reputational damage.
*   **Governance:** Management is supremely confident yet grounded, demonstrating excellent transparency regarding exceptional legal/tariff costs while operations outperformed. They have confidently upgraded FY27 revenue guidance to "high-teens to 20%" while maintaining a >19% EBITDA margin target.

## Investment Thesis

### The Bull Case
Own Sansera for the **valuation rerating**. The market still prices it largely as an Auto-ancillary player, but the flawless execution in Q1 FY27 confirms its reality as a critical global Tier-1 partner in complex engineering. With ADS scaling rapidly and margins expanding structurally to 19.2% (despite exceptional tariff hits and raw material inflation), the mix shift guarantees a highly defensive 20%+ margin profile. The unexecuted ADS order book of INR 57.5 billion—anchored by a massive $75M semiconductor win—provides ironclad visibility. Simultaneously, domestic ICE momentum and the new standalone Defense vertical ensure Sansera captures value across multiple upcycles, pacing aggressively ahead of its ₹8,000-₹9,000 Cr FY31 revenue vision.

### The Bear Case
Avoid if you believe the **ICE cliff** is imminent or if geopolitical trade wars accelerate. Despite rapid diversification, roughly 79% of revenue remains exposed to automotive platforms. If EV penetration in India accelerates faster than their "Tech Agnostic" pivot, core ICE assets face obsolescence. Furthermore, because management has downplayed the immediate timeline for a localized US manufacturing facility, the ICE export business remains dangerously exposed. Retaliatory US tariffs could cripple this highly profitable export channel before Sansera can establish a domestic American footprint.
