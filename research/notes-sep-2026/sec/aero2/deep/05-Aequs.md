# Aequs — deep research (Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Contract manufacturer listed Dec-2025 (IPO + pre-IPO raised ~₹814 cr). Two segments: Aerospace (aerostructures, actuation, interiors/cargo, now landing-gear and engine parts) and Consumer (consumer-electronics enclosures for an unnamed "one of the largest brands", toys for Mattel, cookware via 50:50 JV with Tramontina). [CARE Jul-26; Q1 call]
- Integrated Belagavi aerospace SEZ (forging, machining, surface treatment via JV with Magellan "API", assembly); consumer clusters at Hubballi and Koppal; small plants in Cholet (France) and Paris, Texas (US). 2.22 m sq ft; 438 CNC machines (220 aerospace, 218 consumer) plus 179 moulding machines; 4.78 m machining/moulding hours installed. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.4; Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹395.5 cr: Aerospace ₹322.2 cr (81%, +40% YoY), Consumer ₹73.4 cr (19%, +190%). FY26 revenue ₹1,230 cr: Aerospace ₹1,046 cr (+27%), Consumer ₹184 cr (+84%). Exports/USD-linked 93–94% incl. deemed exports. [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Customers: top customer 22.9% of FY26 revenue, second 19.1%, fourth 10.2%, third 5.9% — all aerospace (Airbus/Tier-1s); Hasbro stopped fresh POs in Q4 FY26. Aerospace utilisation 70% (India 78%); consumer utilisation 22% (23% in Q4). 5,740 aerospace parts qualified. [AR FY26 note 36; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.6]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales Q1 FY26→Q1 FY27: 256, 282, 326, 367, 396. Operating profit (ex other income): 27, 29, 29, 4, 15; OPM 11%, 10%, 9%, 1%, 4%. Net profit: 4, −21, −43, −54, −53. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: reported EBITDA incl. other income ₹21.5 cr (5%); "operational EBITDA" ₹14.8 cr vs ₹4.2 cr in Q4; PAT −₹53.2 cr; depreciation ₹45.3 cr; finance cost ₹18.9 cr (down from ₹35.8 cr after repaying ~₹253 cr of loans). Aerospace segment EBITDA ₹73.1 cr (23%); consumer EBITDA −₹36.1 cr (−₹47.3 cr in Q4). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: FY24 sales 965 / OP 129 / PAT −14; FY25 925 / 79 / −102; FY26 1,230 / 92 / −113. TTM sales ₹1,370 cr, PAT −₹170 cr. ROCE ~2%, ROE −10%. Company FY26 EBITDA incl. other income ₹154.5 cr (13%). [Screener; Q4 call]
- Segment returns: aerospace ROCE ~22%; consumer ROCE about −25% (Q1 FY27). Aerospace FY26 EBITDA 27% includes other income and excludes corporate costs; "operating" aerospace margin ~20%. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Order book: aerospace $889 m (Mar-26) → $1,004 m (Jun-26), 5–7 years; new Farnborough wins (two Tier-1 aerostructure LTAs; 15-year Safran contract for fully assembled Airbus A320 wheels) not yet in the book. [Q1 call; CARE Jul-26]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (May-26): FY27 revenue +45–50%; aerospace +25–30% at ~20% segment EBITDA; consumer +125–150%, utilisation 23% → 40–50% by year-end, consumer EBITDA break-even in Q4 FY27; "doubling of operational EBITDA"; consolidated PAT break-even by H1 FY28; capex ₹660 cr in FY27 (₹500 cr consumer, ₹160 cr aerospace). [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +55% (**ahead**); aerospace +40% at 23% (**beating**); consumer +190% but utilisation 22% (**flat/behind** the path to 40–50%); consumer loss narrowed 24% QoQ (**on track**); PAT loss ₹53 cr (**not yet improving** vs adjusted Q4 −₹63 cr). All guidance **kept**; capex total kept at ₹660 cr but mix may tilt to aerospace. [Q1 call]
- Longer term ("Vision 2031"): 4–6x revenue, 18–22% EBITDA, ~20% steady-state ROCE by FY31; consumer PAT break-even by FY30; 5-year capex $350–400 m (FY27–FY31); mix ~60% aerospace / 40% consumer in five years. [IP Q1FY27 p.5; Q1 call]
- Funding: said a ~$150 m raise would likely be needed but "not planning to do anything this year unless there is a need" (Jul-26) — then on 25-Sep-26 the board approved ₹650 cr promoter warrants (see Capex). [Q1 call; BSE filing 25-Sep-26]

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex ₹660 cr (Q1 spend ₹83 cr); consumer gross block ~₹830 cr plus ~₹500 cr more planned; consumer asset turn ~1.5x at maturity ("closer to 2029" for ~₹2,000 cr consumer revenue). Some consumer capex "will not happen if the utilization is not there". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- MoUs: Tamil Nadu ₹1,900 cr over 10 years for a Hosur aero-engine and landing-gear ecosystem (first machining plant H2 FY28; revenue from FY29); Karnataka ₹2,856 cr over 5 years for Belagavi and Hubballi. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New funding (25-Sep-26): preferential issue of up to 2.81 crore warrants at ₹231.55 to the promoter family trust (Mellwood Trustee / Melligeri Private Family Foundation) — ~₹650 cr, 50% (₹325 cr) upfront, balance by 31-Dec-2027; promoter group to rise from 59.09% to 60.73%; to fund Hosur, subsidiaries/JVs and act as the equity base for more term borrowing; board says it covers equity needs through FY28. EGM 22-Oct-26. Dilution ~4% at a price below the current market (₹246). [BSE filing and press release 25-Sep-26]
- Aerospace: adding machines continuously since Dec-25; comfortable up to ~75% utilisation before new capacity. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹1,106 cr (Mar-24) → ₹785 cr (Mar-25) → ₹701 cr (Mar-26); cash ₹301.5 cr at Mar-26; net debt/equity 0.23x (0.99x in FY25). Q1 FY27: cash ₹234 cr plus ₹53.7 cr other bank balances; ~₹253 cr of term loans repaid and ₹79 cr short-term debt cut in Q4. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Cash flow from operations: FY24 −₹19 cr, FY25 ₹26 cr, FY26 −₹99 cr; Q1 FY27 −₹41.4 cr. Free cash flow FY26 −₹440 cr. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Working capital: company net working-capital days 132 (FY25) → 151 (FY26) → 125 (Q1 FY27, annualised basis); Screener inventory 450 days, CCC 290 days (FY26). Raw-material lead times 52 weeks (titanium) to 65–75 weeks (some steels); stocking 125% of a quarter's needs because of West Asia shipping delays. 99% of raw material imported. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- Rating: CARE BBB− (Positive) reaffirmed Jul-26 — low investment grade; downgrade triggers: ATP (consumer-electronics) ramp delay, total debt/PBDIT >5x. [CARE Jul-26]

## Moat evidence
- Aerospace breadth: 5,740 qualified parts, 100–150 new parts added a month; Airbus Detailed Parts Award (2016–2023); single integrated SEZ lowers cycle time and logistics. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Value-chain climb: first fully assembled A320 wheel outside the customer's own plants, from India-sourced aerospace-qualified aluminium — "It is the first time a customer has done this outside of their own facilities." [Q1 call]
- Pricing: management keeps aerostructure margins moderate on purpose — "If we increase margins too much, our win rate globally might come down." Material costs passed through via long-term agreements. [Q4 call]
- Consumer moat unproven: Hasbro left; consumer is contract manufacturing without product IP. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Losses and dilution: consolidated losses every year (FY23–FY26) and TTM −₹170 cr; consumer segment burns ~₹36 cr EBITDA a quarter; now a ₹650 cr promoter warrant issue priced below market, soon after the IPO. [Screener; BSE filing 25-Sep-26]
- Standalone impairment: investments in subsidiaries ₹1,410 cr net of a cumulative impairment provision of ₹662.7 cr (70.6% of standalone assets) — a key audit matter, showing past value destruction in subsidiaries. [AR FY26 standalone auditor's report]
- Customer concentration: top two customers 42% of revenue. [AR FY26 note 36]
- Management change: CFO Dinesh Iyer left end-June 2026, months after IPO; VP Finance Harish Bang interim. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Controls: audit trail not enabled at application level 1-Apr to 9-Oct-2025 for certain tables across significant processes (auditor remark). [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Contingent liabilities small now: labour ₹2.0 cr, income tax ₹2.5 cr; a ₹78 cr FY18 tax demand was set aside by the Karnataka High Court and remanded; ₹4 cr of refunds adjusted under protest. Corporate guarantees given for loans of related entities (note 31B). Group restructuring: AeroStructures Manufacturing India, Aequs Engineered Plastics and Aequs Force Consumer Products being amalgamated into Aequs. [AR FY26 note 29; CARE Jul-26]
- Supply chain: 99% imported raw material; some Chinese equipment dependency flagged in the DRHP; West Asia shipping delays. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- May: defensive (Hasbro exit, Q4 margin collapse to ~1% operational, CFO exit). July: aerospace strength (order book >$1 bn, A320 wheels, two new Tier-1s), consumer loss narrowing, "operational EBITDA" emphasised. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Capex mix signalled to shift toward aerospace; fund-raise talk moved from "not this year" (Jul) to a ₹650 cr promoter warrant issue (Sep). [Q1 call; BSE filing 25-Sep-26]

## My read
- Aequs is two businesses: a good aerospace contract manufacturer (40% growth, ~20–23% margins, ~22% segment ROCE, $1 bn backlog) and a consumer arm that is still losing money at 22% utilisation. Consolidated profits depend entirely on consumer reaching break-even by Q4 FY27; Q1 showed a narrower loss but no utilisation gain. There is no P/E to anchor on (loss-making); at ~₹16,500 cr market value, about 12x TTM sales, the price assumes the Vision-2031 plan (4–6x revenue, 18–22% margin) largely works. Further equity needs (₹650 cr warrants now, more later) and a BBB− rating argue for caution. What would change my view: consumer utilisation above 40% by March 2027 with segment EBITDA at break-even, and consolidated operating cash flow turning positive.
