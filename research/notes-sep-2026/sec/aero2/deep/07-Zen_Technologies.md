# Zen Technologies — deep research (Q1 FY27)

Sources read: stored Q4 FY26 transcript (official BSE filing, 4 May 2026) and stored Q1 FY27 transcript (tijoristack copy) in full; the official Q1 FY27 transcript (company site, filed 31 Jul 2026); Q1 FY27 investor presentation (26 Jul 2026); FY26 annual report (MD&A, consolidated auditor's report incl. ## Business and segments
- Zen sells **combat training simulators** (tank, gunnery, infantry, naval, now first Air Force product) and **anti-drone systems (ADS)** — soft kill (jamming/spoofing, 70 MHz–12 GHz claimed) plus hard kill (remote weapon stations, L70/ZU-23 gun integration, new HyperStrike interceptor drone). It also earns **AMC (maintenance) revenue** that starts around year 3 of a simulator's 10-year life. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Reports one segment, "Defence and Homeland". No product split of revenue is disclosed; management gives only order-book splits. [AR FY26 note 41]
- Customers: Indian MoD dominates. **Three customers each >10% of revenue made up 56.94% of FY26 group revenue**; in FY25 a single customer was 60.96%. Standalone: three customers were 74.41%. [AR FY26 note 41]
- Geography: FY26 revenue ₹609.0 cr domestic, **₹78.7 cr overseas — down from ₹363.5 cr overseas in FY25**. The FY25 peak was export-led and that did not repeat. Order book at 30 Jun 2026: ₹1,153.8 cr domestic, ₹85.3 cr export. [AR FY26 note 41; IP Q1FY27 p.11]
- Group structure (shares held): Unistring/UTS electronic warfare 51%, ARI naval simulation 100% (remaining 24% bought in FY26 for ₹37.9 cr), Anawave naval training 76% (Nov 2025), TISA Aerospace drones (majority, Jun 2025), Vector Technics propulsion 51%, AI Turing (associate, ~45–49%), Bhairav Robotics (associate). [IP Q1FY27 p.3; AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL MA report Feb-26]
- Subsidiaries made ~₹260 cr of FY26's ₹688 cr revenue: **ARI ₹131 cr at ~30.3% PAT margin**, UTS the rest at ~26.5%, others ~₹10 cr. Management guided **UTS + ARI together ~₹365 cr in FY27**. [Q4 call]
- Minority interests are material: FY26 reported PAT ₹217.9 cr vs **₹193.45 cr attributable to Zen shareholders** (≈₹24.5 cr to minorities, mainly the 49% outside holders of UTS). [AR FY26 MD&A]
- Capacity: Vector Technics expanded propulsion capacity to **3 lakh units a year** (Jul 2026); HyperStrike line ~5,000/shift/month, scalable to 15,000. Main plant at Hardware Park, Hyderabad; 421 employees. [Press release 6 Jul 2026; Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (4 May 2026) promises:** (1) ~₹1,000 cr of the ₹1,336 cr book to be dispatched in FY27, mostly Q2–Q3; (2) "this first quarter should be a good quarter" for orders, "not later than second quarter"; (3) long-term **35% operational EBITDA and 25% PAT margins** "intact"; (4) **₹4,000 cr revenue across FY27+FY28** reiterated ("very comfortable"); (5) working capital back to 140–150 days long term; (6) UTS+ARI ₹365 cr FY27. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 delivery:** revenue ₹141.6 cr (−10.5% y/y, −20% q/q); operational EBITDA margin 27.3% vs 35% guide; Q1 orders only ₹44.6 cr (the ₹177.5 cr order came in July, i.e. inside the "Q2" window); working capital **257 days** vs 196 at March. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Q1 call guidance:** kept ₹4,000 cr for FY27–28 (simulators "about ₹2,000 cr" of it); FY27 full-year operational EBITDA margin "mid-30s"; **new target: exit FY27 with ~₹2,500 cr order book** (after executing the year). ₹700–800 cr of simulator orders "playing out", ADS tenders "at least a couple of thousand crores". US ambition: **$100 mn a year by the third year** via primes. Q4-call pipeline: ~₹1,500 cr bid + "another couple of thousand crores" single-vendor; split ~55:45 ADS:simulators. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Verdict: **kept but increasingly stretched**. With ~₹1,416 cr in hand and ~₹142 cr delivered in Q1, hitting ₹4,000 cr over two years needs roughly ₹3,000 cr of new orders won and largely executed inside ~7 quarters. Order promises have slipped repeatedly (Q4 call: "we told last year also that we are expecting some orders in the first quarter"). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: Zen designs and integrates, outsources non-critical manufacturing, assembles and tests in-house; it will keep this model even with the new arms licence. [Q4 call]
- FY26 gross block ₹239 cr, CWIP ₹15 cr; standalone capital commitments ₹31.0 cr for a new factory building (consolidated note shows ₹6.6 cr). No large capex plan announced. [Screener; AR FY26 notes 38/39 standalone]
- Growth spend goes into **acquisitions and R&D** instead: QIP (Aug 2024) raised ₹1,000 cr gross / ₹979.5 cr net — ₹410 cr earmarked for working capital, ₹350 cr for inorganic growth, ₹219.5 cr general purposes; monitoring agency reported no deviation to Dec 2025. [CRISIL MA report Feb-26]
- New lines: **Arms Manufacturing Licence (Apr 2026) for 12.7 mm, 23 mm, 30 mm, 40 mm cannons**; 30 mm programmable air-burst ammunition expected to be supplied "next year" (FY28); Vrishabh UGV launch in FY27; HyperStrike production to start in FY27. Management said arms/ammo won't be significant in the first two years. [Press release 17 Apr 2026; Q4 call]
- R&D is rising "structurally" (+₹3.3 cr q/q in Q4, +₹4.25 cr y/y in Q1). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and bank **₹1,308 cr (Mar-26) → ₹1,217 cr (Jun-26)**; debt ₹19 cr on Screener at Mar-26 (company calls itself debt-free). Cash is ~8% of market cap. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- FY26 cash from operations **₹245 cr** (CFO/op. profit 136%) and FCF ₹185 cr — a good year after weak FY24 (₹13 cr) and FY25 (₹31 cr). [Screener]
- Working capital days 196 (Mar-26) → **257 (Jun-26)** from supplier advances and inventory; debtor days 119 → 122. Screener FY26 inventory days 415. Long-term guide 140–150 days. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- Customer advances (contract liabilities) fell from ₹410 cr to ₹224 cr in FY26 — less customer money funding the build. [AR FY26 note 41 C]
- Dividend ₹1/share (payout ~5%). Promoter holding 48.51% (down from 57.45% in Sep-23 mainly through the QIP), unchanged for a year; no pledge disclosed. [AR FY26 AGM notice; Screener]
- Credit rating **CRISIL A/Positive, A1** (bank lines enhanced to ₹386 cr). [AR FY26 corp. governance; CRISIL]

## Moat evidence
- IDDM (indigenously designed, developed, manufactured) procurement preference; draft DAP 2026 emphasises IDDM, adds long-term bulk and low-cost fast-track routes. Management lobbied against equating licensed foreign tech with IDDM. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- In recent ADS tenders Zen was "the single vendor because others were disqualified technically". [Q1 call]
- Post-Operation Sindoor, government placed "almost ₹800 cr" of ADS orders on Zen; hard-kill orders ~₹40 cr received. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Switching cost: simulators run ~10 years with AMCs from year 3; AMC book ₹318 cr = 26% of the order book. The ₹177.5 cr July order was an upgrade/AI integration of Zen's own installed tank simulators. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Pricing/margin: gross margin ~73%; FY26 operational EBITDA 36%, three-year ROE 18% on Screener. ROCE fell to 16% in FY26 from 46% in FY24 because of the QIP cash. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Patents and IP: 60 mm mortar simulator patent (Dec 2025); CMMI Level 5. [Press releases]

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration** (3 customers = 57% of FY26 revenue) and lumpy MoD ordering; exports collapsed from ₹364 cr to ₹79 cr. [AR FY26 note 41]
- **Order slippage** — the "Q1 order" promise was only partly met; ADS tenders still depend on fast-track procurement. [Q1 call]
- **Working capital build** (257 days) ahead of H2 execution; if deliveries slip, cash is tied up. [Q1 call]
- **Earnings quality:** ~29% of FY26 pre-tax profit is treasury income; ~11% of profit goes to minorities (UTS 49% outsiders). [Screener; AR FY26]
- **Acquisition sprawl** — 11 subsidiaries plus associates/JV; eleven subsidiaries, an associate and a JV (₹488 cr of assets, ₹357 cr revenue before eliminations) are audited by other auditors. [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report, Other Matter]
- **Related parties:** advances to associate **AI Turing ₹15.2 cr** and **Bhairav Robotics ₹2.0 cr** as material suppliers; promoter commission ₹5.9 cr (Ashok) and ₹4.2 cr (Kishore) in FY26 (down from ₹10.6 cr/₹9.3 cr); several Atluri family members employed (VP, IR head, recruitment head); CSR ₹1.46 cr to Veer Sammaan Foundation where the company is founder trustee. Material RPT approval sought for UTS. [AR FY26 note 39; AGM notice]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matter = revenue recognition timing. CARO qualification only at subsidiary Anawave, clause (i)(c) (title deeds of property). [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Contingent liabilities** small: direct tax ₹10.1 cr, central excise ₹8.2 cr (both unchanged y/y). [AR FY26 note 38]
- **Management churn:** CFO Afzal Malkani left Nov 2025; Hari Haran Chalat joined Jan 2026; WTD M Ravi Kumar left Jun 2025; two new independent directors added Jul/Aug 2026. [AR FY26 note 39; AGM notice]
- Technology risk in ADS (fast-moving threats); a fire at a subsidiary in Q1. [Q1 call]

## My read
The franchise is real: ~73% gross margins, IDDM protection, an installed simulator base that throws off AMC and upgrade orders, and ₹1,217 cr of cash. But the business has shrunk for four straight quarters on a y/y basis, FY26 revenue fell 29%, and profit leans on treasury income. The ₹4,000 cr two-year target is now far from the ₹1,416 cr in hand and needs about ₹3,000 cr of new orders fast. The price needs both the order wave and H2 execution; the view improves if ₹700–800 cr of simulator orders and a large ADS order land by Q3 FY27 and working capital comes back under 200 days, and worsens if FY27 revenue ends near FY26.
