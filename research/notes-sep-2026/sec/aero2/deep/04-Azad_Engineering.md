# Azad Engineering — deep research (Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Hyderabad maker of turbine airfoils (blades and vanes) and other mission-critical rotating/stationary parts for gas, steam and nuclear turbines, aero engines, and oil & gas equipment. 1,700+ qualified parts, 45+ qualified processes; OEM qualification takes 30–48 months per part. CARE names only four global peers for similar parts (AECC Aero Science & Tech, Pietro Rosa, Wuxi and Azad). [IP Q1FY27 p.12, 15; CARE Jun-26]
- Two segments (standalone): Energy & Oil & Gas and Aerospace & Defence. FY26: Energy & O&G ₹481 cr (81.5%, +34%), A&D ₹102 cr (17.2%, +25%); oil & gas under ₹10 cr (Baker Hughes still qualifying). Q1 FY27: Energy & O&G ₹138.4 cr (81.2%, +26.7%), A&D ₹28.7 cr (16.8%, +24.7%). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.7; Q1 call official corrections]
- Exports ~88–93% of revenue (FY26 ~93%; Q1 FY27 88%). Energy customers: GE Vernova, Siemens Energy, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries (MHI), MAN, Arabelle (EDF nuclear), BHEL, Toshiba; aerospace: Honeywell, GE Aviation, Eaton, Rolls-Royce, Pratt & Whitney Canada, Boeing, Rafael, HAL; defence: GTRE/DRDO. [IP Q1FY27 p.9, 12; CARE Jun-26]
- Defence milestone: on 22-Jul-26 delivered India's first indigenous expendable turbojet engine (GTRE-designed, Azad manufactured and assembled) to DRDO/MoD; weapon-integration trials said to be "4 to 6 weeks away", volumes "2 digits or 4 digits, we don't know". [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.5]
- Capacity: ~20,000 sq m operating plus ~94,899 sq m under construction at Tuniki Bollaram (Azad Centre of Excellence): 8 customer-dedicated plants, 4 inaugurated — MHI (Mar-25), GE Vernova steam (Apr-25), Siemens Energy (Sep-25), Baker Hughes (Apr-26). Each plant ~₹150–180 cr revenue at full use → ~₹1,200 cr from the new park. [IP Q1FY27 p.8, 12; Q1 call]
- Subsidiaries (51% each): Azad VTC (coatings; NADCAP) and Azad Prime (JV with Leo Primecomp) — both small (FY26 turnover ~₹10.4 cr and ~₹8.3 cr). [AR FY26 AOC-1, note 5]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales Q1 FY26→Q1 FY27: 137, 146, 159, 162, 173. Operating profit: 49, 53, 62, 61, 64. OPM 36%, 36%, 39%, 38%, 37%. Net profit: 29, 33, 35, 37, 35. Steady ~25–27% YoY growth, no acceleration yet. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 standalone: revenue ₹170.5 cr (+26.8%), EBITDA ₹64.1 cr (37.6%), PAT ₹36.4 cr (+21.2%); consolidated revenue ₹172.6 cr, EBITDA ₹64.4 cr, PAT ₹35.2 cr. Employee cost ~₹42 cr vs ~₹29 cr a year ago (analyst's figures, not disputed; headcount +42%, hired ahead of H2). Other income fell to ₹4 cr from ₹17 cr in Q4 (forex). Finance cost ₹10.1 cr vs ₹5.6 cr. [IP Q1FY27 p.10; Q1 call]
- Annual: FY24 sales 341 / OP 117 / PAT 59; FY25 457 / 161 / 87; FY26 603 / 225 / 134. FY26 revenue +32%, EBITDA margin ~37%, but PAT helped by ₹46 cr other income (forex + treasury). 3-yr sales CAGR 34%. ROCE only 12% (FY26) and ROE 9% because of QIP cash and unused plant. TTM sales ₹638 cr, PAT ₹139 cr. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Order book ~₹6,500 cr (≈11x FY26 revenue): energy ₹3,966 cr, aerospace ₹1,834 cr, rest others (CARE); management: >$400 m energy, ~$200 m A&D, ~$100 m oil & gas; to be consumed "more or less over the next 5 years". Largest contracts: GE Vernova $112 m, Siemens $90 m, MHI $83 m (5 yrs) + 8-year single-source hot-section nozzle-vane LTCPA, Baker Hughes/Nuovo Pignone $53.5 m, Arabelle $40 m, Honeywell $16 m. [CARE Jun-26; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.9]

## Guidance history
- Standing guidance (Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27): revenue growth "25% plus" a year, called a "baseline" based only on already-qualified parts; EBITDA margin 32–35% (consistently beaten at 36–39%). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Q4 promises vs Q1 delivery: turbojet engine "weeks" away → **delivered** 22-Jul; infrastructure "70–80% complete" → "80% stabilised", rest by mid-Q2/Q3 (**on track**); working capital "closer to 200 days" in H1, 160–170/180 in H2 (**kept**, relies on bill discounting cutting debtor days from ~170–180 to ~90); Rolls-Royce first qualification batch "in H2" → "within this quarter" (Q2) (**on track**); remaining 4 plants' civil work by FY27 end (**kept**). Q1 growth +26.8% = at guidance, not above. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Ramp timing: substantive revenue from new plants "from H2 FY27"; Rolls-Royce volume "from Q4 FY27 or early FY28". Asked about 35% growth over 3 years: "As we shift to the next level, we will change this guidance." Next-phase capex plan to be detailed "next quarter". [IP Q1FY27 p.7; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Portfolio aim: in five years energy 55–60%, rest A&D and oil & gas. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capitalisation ₹392 cr; CWIP + capital advances up ₹191 cr; Screener fixed assets ₹436 cr → ₹779 cr and CWIP ₹80 cr → ₹257 cr (Mar-26). Capital commitments ₹118 cr (vs ₹52 cr). [Q4 call; Screener; AR FY26 note 30]
- ~₹180–190 cr of the ₹700 cr QIP still to deploy for remaining plants; about ₹900 cr spent over two years. FY29-onward capex being "sketched"; Saudi Arabia facility with Baker Hughes (MoU) delayed — "not our most urgent priority". A third expansion (85,000 sq m) only after current plants fill. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New hot-section facility for MHI "7–8 months away", then qualification; same line can serve aero-engine hot sections. [Q1 call]
- Asset turns (mine): ~₹1,200 cr revenue potential from ~₹1,000–1,300 cr of gross/net block implies roughly 1x asset turn — capital-heavy vs peers like Sansera (2x in ADS). Returns rely on the ~37% margin.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash flow from operations: FY24 −₹7 cr, FY25 ₹54 cr, FY26 **−₹119 cr**; free cash flow FY26 −₹691 cr (capex + working capital). [Screener]
- Borrowings ₹263 cr (Mar-25) → ₹474 cr (Mar-26); gross ₹457 cr, net ₹272 cr after ₹184 cr treasury (incl. ₹160 cr unspent QIP). CARE: gearing 0.30x, TD/GCA 2.28x, interest cover 8.05x, TOL/TNW 0.40x (downgrade trigger >0.5x). [Screener; Q4 call; CARE Jun-26]
- Working capital: CARE cycle 344 days (287 in FY25) — inventory 262 days (209), receivables 163 days (157), payables 80. Screener shows inventory days 2,067 and cash conversion cycle 1,720 days for FY26 (computed on raw-material cost, which is low, so it overstates but the direction is clear). Receivables ₹309 cr; 96% of inventory under a year old; ~₹100 cr GST credit to be recovered by H1 FY28. [CARE Jun-26; Screener; Q4 call]
- Fix being used: non-recourse bill discounting (cost goes into finance cost) — improves reported cash flow, not the underlying cycle. No dividend. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Ratings: CARE A (Stable)/A2+ reaffirmed Jun-26; facilities enhanced to ₹336.6 cr long-term and ₹200 cr short-term. [CARE Jun-26]

## Moat evidence
- Qualification barrier: 30–48 months per part, 1,700+ parts qualified; "no other qualified domestic suppliers for such components" (CARE). [CARE Jun-26; IP Q1FY27 p.15]
- Single-source wins: MHI 8-year LTCPA for hot-section nozzle vanes — parts previously made in-house by MHI; management says only three players worldwide have "cracked" this complexity. [Q4 call]
- Dedicated plants create switching costs: "once a global marquee OEM integrates a dedicated, qualified facility… shifting that business carries a huge switching cost." [Q1 call]
- Cost position: margins rose from ~18–19% years ago to 36–39%; domestic mills Sunflag and Starwire qualified with GE/Siemens cut material and freight cost (material consumption ~5% of sales in Q1 vs ~12% a year ago). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.10]
- Customer concentration moderate: served to OEMs controlling ~75% of the gas-turbine market; no single-customer >10% disclosure found in the parts read. [IP Q1FY27 p.12]

## Risks and red flags
- Cash burn and working capital: two of the last three years negative CFO; inventory build tied to four new plants; if H2 ramp slips, debt keeps rising (CARE downgrade triggers: margin <30%, working-capital debt rising, TOL/TNW >0.5x). [Screener; CARE Jun-26]
- Contingent liabilities: customs duty demand ₹21.9 cr on EPCG/advance-authorisation export obligations (export obligation "largely met" but discharge certificates pending); direct tax ₹2.0 cr; GST ₹0.2 cr; corporate guarantee for Azad VTC ₹5.1 cr. [AR FY26 note 30]
- Subsidiary structures: 51:49 JVs with call/put options — for Azad Prime, Azad must buy Leo Primecomp's 49% at the higher of fair value or ₹22.6 cr on trigger events, and has paid a non-refundable ₹5.4 cr advance; ₹23.3 cr refundable advance to the Azad VTC partner; ₹19.6 cr intercorporate loans to the two subsidiaries at 10%. Azad Prime had negative reserves at Mar-26. Small, but an unusual use of cash for tiny units. [AR FY26 notes 5, 6, 31; AOC-1]
- Audit: unmodified; key audit matter revenue cut-off; audit trail not enabled at database level for one accounting software (direct data changes not logged). Title deeds of ₹27.2 cr of property pledged with banks. [AR FY26 auditor's report, CARO]
- Disclosure quality: segment growth misstated on the Q1 call (A&D "38.4%", energy "21.6%") and corrected in the official transcript to 24.7% and 26.7%; "PAT… 36.4%" read as a percentage. [Q1 call official]
- Promoter holding fell from 65.9% (Dec-24) to 55.8% (Jun-25) through share sales; flat since. [Screener]
- Execution: hiring 150–200 people a month into 90-day training; ERP/QMS upgrades and senior hires replacing incumbents — a lot of change at once. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: from "calibrated ramp-up/stabilisation" (May) to "propulsion system player" after the engine delivery (Aug). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Numbers unchanged: guidance still 25%+ and 32–35%; working-capital path unchanged; H2 ramp reiterated. [Q1 call]
- New: engine production volume could be large and quick ("a story of a few months"), but "we have nothing in writing". Saudi JV dropped from the conversation. Bill discounting became the working-capital plan. [Q1 call]

## My read
- Azad is a genuine high-barrier business with best-in-class margins (37–39%) and an order book of about eleven years of FY26 revenue, but reported growth has stayed at ~25–27% while it builds eight plants, so the step-up is still a promise for H2 FY27. The cash picture is the weak spot: FY26 operating cash flow was negative, debt nearly doubled, and working capital is being eased with bill discounting rather than faster turns. A trailing P/E around 127 (Screener) needs roughly 35–40% earnings growth for years; that requires the new plants to fill (₹1,200 cr potential) and the engine programme to turn into orders. What would change my view: H2 FY27 revenue growth above 35% with positive operating cash flow, or conversely another year of negative CFO and rising debt.
