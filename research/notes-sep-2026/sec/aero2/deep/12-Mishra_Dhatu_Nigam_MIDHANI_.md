## Business and segments
- Government-owned (74%) Miniratna defence PSU in Hyderabad making **superalloys (nickel/cobalt), titanium alloys, special steels (maraging, armour, high-strength)**, plus downstream forms: forgings on a 6,000-ton press, ring-rolled aero-engine rings, wire, investment castings (~₹10 cr), aerospace fasteners (₹40 cr plant), railway springs (60,000+/yr, RDSO-approved), Bhabha Kavach and "Abhed" body armour. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- **No segment reporting** — exempted by MCA as a defence producer. Only verbal splits: FY26 turnover space ₹93 cr, defence ₹301 cr, PSUs (HAL, BDL, BEL etc.) ₹603 cr, energy ₹2 cr, others ₹129 cr, exports ₹78 cr. [AR FY26 note on segments; Q4 call]
- Order book: ₹2,290 cr on 1 Apr 2026 per the Q4 call (defence 79%), **₹2,594 cr on 1 Apr 2026 per the annual report MD&A**, **₹2,329 cr on 1 Jul 2026** (defence 66%, space 21%, energy 9%, others 4%); titanium orders ~₹600–660 cr. The two April figures do not reconcile. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- Capacity: titanium melting ~700–750 t in FY26 after a new vacuum-arc remelting furnace, "capacity to produce much more"; overall plant not capacity-constrained — constraint is raw materials and processing. [Q4 call; official Q1 transcript]
- All orders are now won through **competitive tenders**, not nomination. [Q1 call]
- Certifications: NADCAP heat treatment (FY26), CEMILAC airworthiness for 10 aero grades, GE **S400** testing approval (13 Aug 2026), NAS 410 NDT staff (NADCAP-NDT targeted by FY27-end), AS9100 in process. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (3 Jun 2026):** FY27 revenue growth **15%** (target 20% if raw materials and energy allow); EBITDA margin **23–25%** (also "24–25%"); PAT growth ~20%; order intake ~₹1,500 cr in FY27; exports ≥₹100 cr; ₹1,000 cr capex over three years with Board approval "in a month or two"; Metal Bank operational "within another 4 months"; spring plant operational in 1–2 months; fasteners ₹20–30 cr a year; export OEM approvals "towards the end of FY27"; inventory (WIP and scrap) to fall 10–15%. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 call (17 Aug 2026):** FY27 growth "higher than last year" (i.e. >12.5%), explicitly **not 40%**; margin back to **20–21% from Q3** (Q2 still hit) — a cut from 23–25%; exports "~10% of turnover", ₹100–120 cr; capex ₹1,000 cr now "over 2–3 years", benefits "from the fourth year", **FY27 capex only ₹50–60 cr (mostly maintenance)**, proposal still with the Ministry/Board, no timeline; Metal Bank procurement clarity "by end of Q2"; Abhed jackets testing this quarter; aluminium JV (UADNL, >₹1,000 cr Nellore plant) being closed. [official Q1 transcript]
- **Track record vs Q4 promises:** revenue +40% (beats, off a low base); **margin missed** (15% on Screener basis vs 23–25% promised; guidance cut to 20–21%); **capex approval slipped** (from "1–2 months" to no timeline); **Metal Bank slipped** (from ~Oct to "end of Q2 clarity"); spring plant delivered (BEML sample order); S400 certification new. Verdict: **mixed — growth fine, margin and capex guidance cut**. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹150 cr investing outflow (Screener); CWIP down to ₹16 cr — little is under construction. Capital commitments ₹58 cr (₹29 cr a year earlier). [Screener; AR FY26 note 41]
- The ₹1,000 cr plan replaces 1980s-era downstream equipment (forging, bar rolling, wire drawing) with automated machines to improve yield and cycle time; management says it is not new capacity and gave no revenue or asset-turn estimate. Funding: internal accruals and term loans. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Metal powder plant: German BAFA dual-use export licence (preliminary) obtained; equipment "possibly by next year". [Q1 call]
- Aluminium JV UADNL (with NALCO; 60,000 tpa plan) found commercially non-viable; strike-off recommended — no further capital at risk beyond the small equity already invested. [AR FY26 directors' report 6.2; Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- Only Indian producer qualified for many defence, space and nuclear grades (single-crystal blade feedstock, isothermal forging of fighter-engine superalloy, PFBR steam-generator tubes, AMCA development grades); 40+ years; government ownership and support (CRISIL's main strength). [Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Jan-26]
- ~₹8,000 cr of titanium, superalloy and special-steel imports a year are within MIDHANI's catalogue (EXIM data) — the opportunity is certification, not capability. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Weak pricing power:** fixed-price contracts with no price-variation clause; freight and raw-material spikes absorbed; tender-based wins; margins down from ~30% (FY19–23) to ~20%. Foreign peers (ATI, Carpenter, VSMPO-AVISMA, Haynes) hold OEM approvals and scale — MIDHANI's own SWOT says export share gains "will be challenging". [Q1 call; Screener; AR FY26 MD&A SWOT]
- Raw materials 50–60% imported (CRISIL); titanium sponge from Eastern Europe, the former USSR and Kazakhstan; imported raw materials ₹255 cr in FY26. [CRISIL Jan-26; Q1 call; AR FY26 note 43]

## Risks and red flags
- **Governance:** the company had **no independent directors from 17 Apr 2026** (MoD directive ended three terms), so FY26 accounts were approved directly by the Board without an audit committee — the auditor added this as an "Other Matter" (non-compliance with SEBI LODR Reg. 17(1)) **after CAG's observation**, re-issuing both audit reports. Two independent directors were appointed effective 21 Aug 2026. [AR FY26 auditor's reports; News: ScanX, Aug-2026]
- Auditor's **emphasis of matter**: receivables, payables, advances and other balances are "subject to receipt of confirmation of balances/reconciliation". KAMs: revenue recognition, inventory valuation, raw-material consumption. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Contingent liabilities ₹449 cr** (₹291 cr a year earlier), incl. **claims not acknowledged as debt ₹310 cr** (₹203 cr) and bank guarantees ₹118 cr (₹66 cr) — large against ₹131 cr annual PAT; nature of the claims not detailed. [AR FY26 note 41]
- Title: conveyance deeds for **275 acres** of land (allotted by the undivided AP government, recorded as "Super Alloy Plant of DMRL") not yet in the company's name; 1.5 acres encroached. [AR FY26 CARO i(c)]
- Inventory > 1 year of sales; old WIP and scrap build-up; CRISIL flags working-capital intensity. [AR FY26; CRISIL Jan-26]
- Disclosure gaps: two different order-book figures for 1 Apr 2026 (₹2,290 cr vs ₹2,594 cr); two export figures (₹78 cr vs ₹85 cr); no segment or product margins. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Capex and new-product timelines routinely slip (powder plant licence, Metal Bank, capex approval, springs, fasteners). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## My read
MIDHANI's franchise is real — it is the national source for strategic superalloys and titanium, and certifications (NADCAP, S400, AS9100) could open export and private-OEM demand. But the numbers have not followed: revenue grew ~12% in FY26 with very lumpy quarters, margins have reset from ~30% to ~20% under competitive tendering and fixed-price contracts, inventory exceeds a year of sales, and the capex that might lift yield is not yet approved. The price needs ~15% growth with margins back above 20% and visible working-capital release; the view improves with capex approval, export OEM qualification and a large AMCA/engine production order, and worsens if Q2–Q3 margins stay near 15% or the ₹310 cr of disputed claims crystallise.
