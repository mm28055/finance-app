# Krishna Defence & Allied Industries (NSE: KRISHNADEF) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Inputs read: stored file (only one transcript — Q4 FY26, 22 May 2026, a Tijori copy — compared with the official NSE transcript); the previous call (H1 FY26, 17 Nov 2025, official NSE transcript); Q1 FY27 investor presentation (14 Aug 2026; no Q1 call transcript is listed); Annual Report FY26 (auditor's report, contingent liabilities, related parties, segment note, revenue reconciliation); CRISIL rationale (21 Sep 2026); Screener consolidated. The company moved from the NSE SME board to the main board in Dec 2025; FY26 is its first Ind AS year.

## Business and segments
- Makes naval and armoured-vehicle metallurgy that used to be imported from Russia: bulb bars (hull stiffeners for warships and, now, commercial ships), special-alloy welding wires/electrodes for surface ships and submarines, low-magnetic ballast bricks for submarines, armour-steel profiles for T-90 tank hulls (HVF/AVNL, range raised from 17 to 26 profiles), large castings, and a high-altitude space heater (DRDO technology). Legacy dairy equipment (milk cans, cooling tanks) is ~8–10% of revenue. [Q4 call; H1 call]
- FY26 product mix: bulb bars ~60%, welding wires ~15%, armour profiles ~15%, others ~10%; similar mix expected in FY27. H1 FY26 revenue was 92% defence. [Q4 call; H1 call]
- Customers: Mazagon Dock, Hindustan Shipyard, Cochin Shipyard, other shipyards, MoD, AVNL; "about 35–40%" of revenue from one shipyard, which rotates by year; five or six customers make up the business. Exports not allowed for the classified steels. [Q4 call; CRISIL Sep-26]
- One reportable segment (defence); dairy too small to report. [AR FY26 segment note]
- Plant: ~150,000 sq ft at Halol, Gujarat; bulb-bar capacity doubled to ~4,000–4,500 tonnes a year in April 2025; utilisation ~60% at Nov-25; adjoining 50,000 sq ft plot bought. Management says current infrastructure can support ₹400–500 cr revenue with the right mix. [Q4 call; H1 call]
- New ventures (all early): 51% JV Krishna Vabo Composites (composite fire-resistant naval doors/hatches, trial production started); 46.81% of Tarabadkar Solutions (smart DART-type naval ammunition, ₹25 cr iDEX/Navy grant, 2–3 years from revenue); "Jalkappi" autonomous underwater vehicle with DRDO/Navy and a Sea Bot ToT from NIO Goa (Chennai facility); aircraft arresting gear design win. Associates: Conceptia (20%, ship design; FY26 revenue ~₹80 cr, PAT ~₹5 cr) and Waveoptix (40%, RF-over-fibre modules; revenue ~₹22–23 cr, PAT ~₹5–5.5 cr). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- FY23 → FY24 → FY25 → FY26 revenue 64 → 106 → 190 → 245; operating margin 14% → 15% → 16% → 22%; net profit 5 → 10 → 22 → 41. TTM revenue 234, net profit 43. [Screener]
- Last five quarters (revenue / operating margin / net profit): Q1 FY26 68 / 19% / 9; Q2 48 / 19% / 9; Q3 64 / 22% / 10; Q4 65 / 25% / 13; Q1 FY27 58 / 26% / 11. [Screener]
- Company (standalone) Q1 FY27: revenue ₹57.6 cr (–15.2% YoY), EBITDA ₹15.2 cr (26.3%, +776 bps), net profit ₹11.7 cr (20.3%). FY26: revenue ₹244.8 cr (+29.1%), EBITDA ₹52.0 cr (21.3%), net profit ₹41.3 cr. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Margin drivers: richer mix (armour profiles and weld consumables carry higher margins than bulb bars), operating leverage on the doubled capacity. [Q4 call]
- Late-delivery charges and discounts cut contracted revenue by ₹9.97 cr in FY26 (₹5.25 cr in FY25) — about 4% of contracted value and rising. [AR FY26 revenue reconciliation; H1 call]
- Unit economics: not disclosed (no tonnage or price per tonne).

## Guidance history
- H1 FY26 call (Nov-25): "grow with our revenue in the range of 30%, 40%"; H2 order inflow "in the range of about close to 100 crores, to 150 crores"; ₹5–10 cr capex a year; main-board listing by December. [H1 call]
- Delivered FY26: revenue +29% (just under the 30% floor); main-board move done (Dec-25); margins beat (21.3% vs 16% in FY25). Order book fell from ~₹196 cr (Sep-25) to ₹103.4 cr (Mar-26) — so H2 inflow was ~₹30 cr, well short of the ₹100–150 cr promised. **Revenue roughly met; orders missed.** [H1 call; Q4 call; Screener]
- Q4 FY26 call (May-26): guidance trimmed to "30% plus" for "the next 3 years"; the ₹221 cr tender pipeline "will be realized in FY27", "much of the tender pipeline should fructify in the first half"; margins to rise "incremental[ly]"; the ₹33,000 cr next-generation corvette programme "will certainly come to us" for bulb bars. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue fell 15% YoY to ₹57.6 cr; margin rose to 26.3%; order book only ₹117 cr at 30 Jun, pipeline ₹289 cr. To reach +30% (~₹318 cr) the company needs ~₹87 cr a quarter for three quarters — well above its best quarter (₹68 cr). **Revenue behind; margin ahead.** [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- CRISIL cites orders of ₹150 cr at 31 May 2026 to be executed in 12 months — higher than the ₹117 cr in the presentation a month later; the two figures are not reconciled. [CRISIL Sep-26; IP Q1FY27]
- Management's explanation for the thin book: government attention went to emergency buying for "4–5 months" after regional security events; shipyards now order in smaller batches with 6-month (not 12-month) delivery. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Bulb-bar doubling was completed in April 2025; fixed investment in machinery ~₹22 cr plus ₹3.5 cr CWIP at Nov-25; ongoing capex ₹5–10 cr a year for automation. Capital commitments ₹10.7 cr at Mar-26 (net of ₹8.1 cr advances). [H1 call; AR FY26 note 38]
- FY26 investing outflow ₹92 cr is mostly the ~₹65 cr long-term fixed deposit, the Halol land and the Chennai AUV venture — not plant. [Q4 call; Screener]
- JV funding: Krishna's share of Vabo JV capex ~₹4–5 cr from internal accruals. [Q4 call]
- Payback: capacity is ~60% used; asset turn is high (FY26 revenue ₹245 cr on fixed assets ₹34 cr), so new capex is small relative to revenue. [H1 call; Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Near debt-free: borrowings ₹2 cr at Mar-26; gearing 0.01x; interest cover ~54x (CRISIL). Unencumbered cash and bank ₹88 cr at Mar-26; company says total cash "north of 100 crores" including the ₹76.9 cr long-term FD. [Screener; CRISIL Sep-26; Q4 call]
- Cash flow turned: operating cash flow +₹86 cr in FY26 after three negative years (–₹7 cr, –₹3 cr, –₹11 cr); free cash flow +₹66 cr. [Screener]
- The FY26 cash is partly a year-end cut-off: inventory fell from ₹72.4 cr to ₹16.3 cr because February–March raw material arrived in early April; management says normal inventory is ~₹40 cr. Screener inventory days 270 → 47; CRISIL gross current assets (net of cash) 183 → 89 days. Expect some reversal in FY27. [Q4 call; Screener; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Contingent items: bank guarantees ₹31.7 cr (₹47.3 cr a year earlier); corporate guarantee ₹5 cr for associate Waveoptix's bank loans (CRISIL treats it as debt); liquidated-damages claim ₹0.83 cr disputed. [AR FY26 note 38; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Dividend: payout 5% of FY26 profit. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Sole or first Indian source for several ex-Russian items; weld consumables were approved after certification by Russian specialists; naval-approved bulb-bar source; DNV, ABS, IRS (commercial shipping) and AS9100D (aerospace) approvals. [Q4 call]
- Customer confidence: HVF extended the armour-profile range from 17 to 26 profiles; design win on arresting gear; ToTs from DRDO labs and NIO. [Q4 call]
- Pricing: margins rose five points in FY26 with no sign of customer push-back; management says raw-material inflation impact "is not substantial". [Q4 call]
- Limits: tender-based, L1 (lowest-bid) procurement; a narrow product set tied to warship build rates; late-delivery penalties show customers enforce terms. [AR FY26; CRISIL Sep-26]

## Risks and red flags
- Order book cliff: book ₹103–117 cr against a ₹300 cr+ revenue ambition; Q1 FY27 revenue already fell. [IP Q1FY27]
- Customer concentration: one shipyard ~35–40% of revenue; five or six customers make the whole business. [Q4 call]
- Rising late-delivery charges (₹5.25 cr → ₹9.97 cr). [AR FY26; H1 call]
- Related-party dealings: the company bought two residential flats from MD Ankur Shah during FY26 (₹43.6 lakh and ₹40.1 lakh); purchases and rent with promoter entities Krish Industries and White Gold Technologies; loans given to associate Waveoptix (earlier loan of ₹4.75 cr converted into Waveoptix equity at ₹19,000 a share); equity allotted to the MD's wife, Preyal Shah (2,52,000 shares). Individually small but worth watching. [AR FY26 note 42]
- Management churn: whole-time director Sandeep Kadam died in March 2026; CFO Piyush Patel left 7 Apr 2026, replaced by Manish Shah from 11 Apr 2026. [AR FY26 note 42]
- Promoter holding down from 73.4% (Mar-23) to 59.9% (Jun-26) through placements and sales. [Screener]
- "Venture-style" bets (smart ammunition, AUVs, composites, associates) absorb cash and management time with 2–3 year horizons; none is in guidance. [Q4 call]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion, no key audit matters; audit trail operated. [AR FY26 auditor's report]

## What changed between the two calls (Nov-25 → May-26)
- Growth guidance narrowed from "30–40%" to "30% plus"; H2 order inflow came in far below the ₹100–150 cr promised; order book halved; new explanations offered (emergency-procurement pause, batch ordering). Margins surprised upwards (17.9% in H1 to 24.8% in Q4). New ventures added (Tarabadkar, Vabo JV, arresting gear, castings, Chennai AUV site). The Q1 FY27 presentation then showed revenue down 15% but record 26% margin and a bigger pipeline (₹289 cr).

## My read
Krishna Defence has built a small, highly profitable niche in ex-Russian naval and armour steels, with margins up from 14% to 26% in four years, almost no debt and, finally, positive cash flow. The weak spot is orders: the book has shrunk to about ₹110–120 cr, H2 FY26 order inflow missed the promise badly, and Q1 FY27 revenue fell 15%, so the "30% plus" target already looks stretched for FY27. At the current price the stock needs corvette and other shipbuilding orders to land in H2 FY27 and margins to hold near 25%. A ₹150 cr+ order book by December would restore confidence; another flat quarter plus rising late-delivery penalties would mean FY27 growth in single digits.
