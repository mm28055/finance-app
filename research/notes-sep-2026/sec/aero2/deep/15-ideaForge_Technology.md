# ideaForge Technology (NSE: IDEAFORGE) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Inputs read: stored file (Q4 FY26 transcript = official BSE copy; Q1 FY27 transcript = Tijori AI copy, compared with the official BSE transcript of 11 Aug 2026), Q1 FY27 investor presentation (10 Aug 2026), Annual Report FY26 (auditor's report and KAMs, intangibles under development, liquidated damages, contingent liabilities, segment note, subsidiary AOC-1, MD&A), India Ratings (Jun 2026) and CRISIL (27 May 2026) rationales, Screener consolidated, news on the July 2026 QIP.

## Business and segments
- India's largest maker of small military/paramilitary drones (UAVs): quadcopters (NETRA / Q6), fixed-wing VTOL hybrid (SWITCH), tactical ZOLT (now in series production and with a fuel-hybrid long-endurance version), and YETI, a heavy-lift high-altitude logistics drone (200 kg at 6,500 m target) in development. Also FLYGHT Cloud software (drone-as-a-service, AI analytics). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Customers: Indian Army and other armed forces (emergency and fast-track procurement, and field-commander "run-rate" buying), central/state police and paramilitary (homeland security), and survey/mapping (GIS) customers. Defence was 69% of FY26 revenue; Q1 FY27 revenue split 60% defence / 40% civil (70:30 in FY26). [Ind-Ra Jun-26; Q1 call]
- One reported segment; no audited defence/civil split; order-book split not disclosed ("heavier towards defence"). [AR FY26 note 36; Q1 call]
- International: US subsidiary ideaForge Technology Inc. (Austin) had revenue of only ~₹0.19 cr and a loss of ~₹9.5 cr in FY26; a US joint venture with First Breach ("First Forge Inc.") is still subject to closing. First US order (Lamar police, Texas) in Q4 FY26 was a pilot; US federal/defence sales need "Blue sUAS" certification, which is pending. [AR FY26 AOC-1 and Directors' report; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Capacity: runs one shift at Navi Mumbai; "can increase production to 2 or 3 shifts in the same space"; no factory capex planned. [Q1 call]
- Indigenous content "roughly about 60% to 65%"; thermal cameras and some electronics are imported and currently constrained. [Q1 call]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- FY24 → FY25 → FY26 revenue 314 → 161 → 226; operating profit 57 → –52 → 6; margin 18% → –32% → 2.6%; net profit 45 → –62 → –17. TTM revenue 282, net profit 4. [Screener]
- Last five quarters (revenue / operating margin / net profit): Q1 FY26 12.8 / –149% / –23.6; Q2 40.8 / –28% / –19.6; Q3 31.6 / –84% / –33.9; Q4 141.0 / 43.8% / 60.0; Q1 FY27 68.6 / 3.4% / –2.6. [Screener]
- Company-defined figures (EBITDA includes other income): Q4 FY26 gross margin 67.6%, EBITDA ₹74.2 cr (52.6%); Q1 FY27 gross margin 49.0%, EBITDA ₹4.3 cr (6.2%). FY26 gross profit ₹131 cr (58%), EBITDA ₹27 cr. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- The business is binary on volume: fixed costs (employees ~₹15 cr a quarter, other expenses ~₹14–19 cr) mean profit only appears in a big-delivery quarter. [IP Q1FY27]
- Depreciation and amortisation keeps rising as capitalised R&D is amortised: ₹21 cr (FY24) → ₹29 cr → ₹43 cr (FY26) → ₹14.2 cr in Q1 FY27 alone. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q3 FY26 call (Jan): would deliver a large part of the order book in Q4 and post positive FY26 EBITDA. Delivered: executed ~40% of the open book in Q4; FY26 company EBITDA ₹27 cr positive (Screener operating profit ₹6 cr). **Met.** [Q4 call; Screener]
- Q4 FY26 call (May 4): opening FY27 order book ~₹310 cr to be executed "within the first 3 quarters"; blended FY27 gross margin 50–55%; no revenue guidance; "the task is now to move from quarterly profitability to annual profitability". [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: executed 20%+ of the book (₹68.6 cr); gross margin 49% (below range, blamed on 60:40 mix); net loss ₹2.6 cr; remaining ₹256.8 cr still promised "by Q3". 50–55% blended margin **kept**. **Execution on track; margin and profit below the promise.** [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Order inflow: FY26 bookings ~₹530 cr (a record). Q1 FY27 inflow is not stated; from the presentation's order book (₹314.2 cr → ₹256.8 cr) and revenue (₹68.6 cr), new orders were only ~₹11 cr. Management declined to project FY27 inflows ("We are not presently projecting that"). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Timing of new orders: field-commander (revenue) buying expected in Q3–Q4; of the ₹20,000 cr fast-track drone programme only one RFP is out (for a ZOLT-class hybrid ISR drone, in which ideaForge bid); "at least a couple" of fast-track outcomes expected in FY27, others may slip to FY28. [Q1 call]
- Q4 call also claimed Zolt ₹72 cr order "execution is still pending"; Q1 presentation says ZOLT moved to series production. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- YETI: "early commercial explorations" from FY28; first technical demonstrator did a tethered hover in Q1 FY27. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- No plant capex planned; spending is on product development. The company capitalises development cost: additions to "product under development" were ₹81.6 cr in FY26 (₹76.5 cr in FY25), and ₹68.0 cr left the account (capitalised as finished intangibles or charged to profit); the balance under development reached ₹109.5 cr at Mar-26 (₹95.6 cr a year earlier), with ₹13 cr of it more than two years old. [AR FY26 note 6A]
- A further ₹56.8 cr of intangible projects is listed in the "time and cost over-run" completion schedule, to be completed within a year. [AR FY26 note 6A]
- Funding: QIP of 62.89 lakh shares at ₹795 (4.89% below floor price), ₹500 cr, July 2026 — about 14% dilution; plus a letter of intent for up to ₹151 cr low-cost government RDI-scheme debt for YETI, released against milestones. [News: Sahi, Jul 2026; IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Asset turn is low: FY26 revenue ₹226 cr on fixed assets + CWIP of ₹248 cr. [Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Before the QIP: cash, mutual funds and FDs ~₹128 cr at Mar-26 (over ₹100 cr unencumbered); bank-limit use 13% average; lease liabilities the only long-term debt (Screener borrowings ₹84 cr include leases and working-capital lines). Cash was ₹265 cr at FY24 before two loss years ate into it. [CRISIL May-26; Ind-Ra Jun-26; Screener]
- Operating cash flow –₹77 cr (FY25) and –₹63 cr (FY26); free cash flow –₹164 cr and –₹152 cr (including capitalised R&D). [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 204 and inventory days 536 in FY26 (Screener); CRISIL describes a normal cycle of 80–90 days receivables and 120–150 days inventory, with gross current assets of 236–560 days over three years. [Screener; CRISIL May-26]
- Liquidated damages (delivery-delay penalties): ₹3.8 cr deducted from FY25 revenue; ₹3.9 cr provision carried at Mar-26. [AR FY26 notes 22, revenue reconciliation]
- No dividends. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Technology ownership: in-house autopilot, EW-resilient (anti-jamming) communications, GNSS-denied navigation, payloads, ground-control software; 112 patents (61 granted, 51 pending). EW-resilient systems passed user acceptance and "country of concern" inspections in Q4 FY26. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Field record: over 1 million customer missions; six platforms DGCA type-certified; first Indian UAV firm with a NATO Stock Number. [IP Q1FY27]
- Pricing evidence: Q4 FY26 gross margin 67.6% on EW-resilient defence deliveries vs 49% on a heavier civil mix — defence specs carry pricing power; civil work does not. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Order evidence: record ~₹530 cr bookings in FY26 when the Army needed EW-resilient systems. [Q4 call]
- Limits: competitive field is widening (logistics drones from others; large groups such as Tata and Adani in drone tenders); management won't guess share ("case-by-case"). Combat drones will need partners because ideaForge does not intend to hold munitions licences. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Capitalised R&D flatters EBITDA:** ₹81.6 cr of FY26 development spend (employee cost, materials, testing, overheads) went to the balance sheet, versus FY26 operating profit of only ₹6 cr. The auditor made "capitalisation of product under development" a Key Audit Matter (risk that costs are capitalised where criteria aren't met). The other KAM is revenue recognition (risk of revenue booked early "to meet expectations"). Opinion unmodified. [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 6A]
- Order-book cliff: book falls to ~zero after Q3 FY27 unless fast-track or field orders arrive; Q1 inflow ~₹11 cr. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Supply chain: thermal-camera shortages and electronic-part delays due to conflicts; liquidated damages if deliveries slip. [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- Customer concentration on the Indian MoD and its buying cycle (FY25 revenue halved when elections and caution slowed orders). [Ind-Ra Jun-26]
- US subsidiary losses (~₹9.5 cr in FY26) with no revenue until certification. [AR FY26 AOC-1]
- Promoter holding diluted to 25.26% after the QIP (29% before). [Screener]
- Disclosure: rating agency Ind-Ra's note calls ~₹950 mn gross profit (68%) an FY26 figure — it is actually the Q4 figure; and the official Q4 transcript records FY26 revenue as "INR286.1 crores" — the audited figure is ₹226 cr. Use audited numbers only. [Ind-Ra Jun-26; Q4 call; Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 (May): triumphant — record quarter, EW acceptance, US first order, NATO training, move into combat drones, 50–55% margin, ₹310 cr book for three quarters.
- Q1 (Aug): quieter — revenue ₹68.6 cr, gross margin 49%, loss; US JV and certification "taking a little bit of time"; supply-chain pressure now admitted; central story became the ₹20,000 cr fast-track pipeline, ₹52,000 cr DAC approvals, doubled field-commander powers (DFPDS-2026), the ₹500 cr QIP and ₹151 cr YETI funding. No order-inflow projection.

## My read
ideaForge is a real technology leader in small military drones, and FY26 proved it can earn very high margins when a large EW-resilient defence order ships (Q4: ₹141 cr revenue, ₹60 cr profit). But the business is still lumpy and cash-hungry: three of the last five quarters were losses, cash flow has been negative for two years, and reported EBITDA is helped by capitalising ~₹80 cr a year of R&D. With the ₹500 cr QIP the balance sheet is safe, so the question is purely orders: the book runs out after Q3 FY27 and Q1 inflow was tiny. The stock needs fast-track and field-commander orders to land in H2 FY27; a large win (₹300 cr+) would re-rate it, while an empty Q4 would bring back FY25-style losses.
