# Data Patterns (India) — deep research (Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Designs and builds defence electronics in-house: radars (full radars incl. mechanics and cooling), electronic warfare (EW: radar warning receivers, ELINT/COMINT, jammer pods), avionics (glass cockpits, LCA Mk2 mission systems), BrahMos fire-control and seeker, automated test equipment (ATE), communication, small satellites; over 1,500 reusable "COTS" building blocks. [IP Q1FY27 p.3–4; CRISIL Sep-26]
- One reportable segment (defence electronics). Revenue by type (presentation chart; labels hard to read, so approximate): FY26 production roughly 53–58%, development roughly 37–43%, services ~4–5%; development share was high because of a large radar programme executed in Q2 FY26. Order book at Mar-26 was ~38% services (IMD radar AMC). [Q4 call] [AR FY26 note 38c; IP Q1FY27 p.11]
- Customers: MoD, DRDO, BrahMos Aerospace, BEL, HAL, ISRO, IMD; exports to UK/Europe (Transportable Precision Approach Radars to a European country; airborne antenna for a UK fighter programme). [CRISIL Sep-26; Q1 call]
- Geography: FY26 India ₹863 cr, exports ₹61.8 cr (6.7%) vs ₹106.7 cr (15%) in FY25 — exports fell. Export order book ₹53 cr (Mar-26) → ₹39 cr (Jun-26). [AR FY26 note 38c; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- People and plant: 1,112 engineers (82 hired in Q1 FY27); headcount 1,545 (Mar-25) → 1,599 (Mar-26); Chennai campus; building "9 floors" of new factory space. [IP Q1FY27 p.3; AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- Acquisition: 100% of ST Advanced Composites (STAC), Chennai, composite structures for radars, for ~₹10 cr (₹1.5 cr for shares + ₹8.5 cr loan to clear its liabilities); SPA executed 25-Aug-26. [IP Q1FY27 p.10; BSE filing 25-Aug-26]

## Key numbers (Screener standalone, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales Q1 FY26→Q1 FY27: 99, 307, 173, 345, 116. Operating profit: 32, 68, 81, 193, 31. OPM: 32%, 22%, 47%, 56%, 27%. Net profit: 26, 49, 58, 138, 22. Extreme lumpiness; Q4 was 37% of FY26 revenue vs 55% in FY25. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +17% YoY; gross margin 78.9%; EBITDA ₹31.4 cr (27%); PAT ₹22.1 cr (−13.5%). Employee cost ₹42.5 cr = 36.7% of sales; other expenses +64% YoY (facility repairs + ~₹2 cr provision on old receivables). [IP Q1FY27 p.13; Q1 call]
- Annual: FY24 sales 520 / OP 222 / PAT 182; FY25 708 / 275 / 222; FY26 925 / 374 / 271. FY26 revenue +31%, EBITDA margin 40%, gross margin 63% (61%). 3-yr sales CAGR 27%, profit CAGR 26%. ROCE 22% (FY26), ROE 15%. TTM sales ₹941 cr, PAT ₹268 cr. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Other income fell from ₹46 cr (FY25) to ₹25 cr (FY26) as QIP cash was spent. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]
- FY26 order inflow ₹1,121 cr (+216%), led by IMD radar development+service order ₹288 cr, ECIL EW ₹84 cr, HAL ₹77 cr. Q1 FY27 inflow ₹117 cr (−36% YoY). [IP Q1FY27 p.16]
- Unit economics: in-house IP gives 60–80% gross margins; low-margin strategic contract in Q2 FY26 (EBITDA 22%) was taken consciously to build full-radar capability. [Q4 call; Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (May-26): revenue growth 20–25% "over the short term", EBITDA 38–40%, stay net-cash; negotiated orders ~₹1,090–1,100 cr to be signed "in the next 1 to 2 months"; ₹1,900 cr of single-vendor repeat orders expected in FY27; cash conversion cycle to settle at 320–340 days. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (Jul-26): revenue 20–25% kept; EBITDA band widened to 35–40% (from 38–40%) — a soft **cut**; negotiated orders grew to ₹1,726 cr but conversion "took an additional 6 months" and got "another 2 months" extension; FY27 fresh inflow target raised to ~₹2,000 cr; presentation: "Strong Order Book in Pipeline at Rs 20–40bn over next 24 months". [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.7]
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: negotiated-order conversion **missed** in the promised window, then partly delivered after the quarter — by 20-Aug-26 ₹771 cr received, of which ₹746 cr came from the negotiated list (incl. ₹585.8 cr BEL radar-electronics order). Q1 revenue +17% is below the 20–25% annual pace; Q1 margin 27% below the band (management says judge annually). BrahMos seeker production order: expected in "4 to 5 months" in May; in July "before the end of this year", after flight trials. [BSE filing 20-Aug-26; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- FY26 track record: revenue +31% beat the 20–25% guidance; EBITDA 40% at top of band. [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- CRISIL expects ~30% revenue CAGR over the medium term and 35–40% margin in FY27. [CRISIL Sep-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Historically light: ~₹185 cr capex over five years; FY26 gross block addition ₹36.5 cr (₹31.2 cr physical infrastructure); CWIP ₹74 cr (Mar-26); capital commitments ₹36.6 cr (vs ₹6.8 cr). [IP Q1FY27 p.5; AR FY26 Board's report, note 36]
- Plan: presentation says ~₹150 cr over the next two years; on the call CMD said "₹200 crores plus" and CFO "₹150–200 crores minimum" — for clean rooms, integration halls, test/reliability lines, export production, and AI servers. Funded from accruals (CRISIL). [IP Q1FY27 p.5; Q1 call; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Product development: ₹135 cr+ invested in new products; ₹131.2 cr of QIP money used for product development; ₹71.6 cr capitalised in FY25 and now being amortised (depreciation rose to ₹23 cr from ₹14 cr). ₹37.65 cr of the March-2023 QIP (₹487.7 cr net) still unspent at Mar-26. [AR FY26 MD&A, Board's report]
- Capacity is not the constraint; management says customer acceptance and orders are. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free (borrowings ₹5 cr, leases). Cash, deposits and investments ₹517 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹543 cr (Mar-25); CRISIL "free cash" ₹384 cr. At Jun-26 the call said ₹530 cr, but the presentation shows ₹465.9 cr — an unexplained ₹64 cr gap between two company documents. [AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Sep-26; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.9]
- Cash flow from operations: FY24 ₹139 cr, FY25 −₹90 cr, FY26 ₹80 cr — only ~45% of FY26 operating profit. Three-year cumulative CFO ₹129 cr against ₹675 cr of PAT. [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 287; inventory days 294 (Screener) / 318 (company); company cash conversion cycle 365 days (428 in FY25); CRISIL gross current assets 424 days (616 in FY24). Receivables are paid only after site acceptance; include retention money. Current ratio 10.45. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Bank guarantees given ₹677.7 cr (₹528.3 cr in FY25) — sizeable vs net worth ₹1,736 cr; lien-marked deposits back some of them. [AR FY26 note 36]
- Dividend payout ~20% (₹7.90/share for FY25). [Screener; AR FY26]
- Ratings: CRISIL A+ outlook revised to **Positive** from Stable, A1 reaffirmed (7-Sep-26); ICRA also rates (Dec-25). [CRISIL Sep-26; Screener doc list]

## Moat evidence
- Pricing power: gross margin 63% for FY26 and 73–79% in the last two quarters, well above assembly-type defence suppliers. [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27 p.13]
- Full-system capability: designed and delivered complete radars (Ashwini LLTR, Arudhra and Dharashakti programme work per CRISIL), 9 Precision Approach Radars to Army/Navy, EW suites; claims to be "the first company in India to build the complete radar". [CRISIL Sep-26; IP Q1FY27 p.4; Q1 call]
- Switching costs: on BrahMos, "I do not think it is easy to change the supplier, redevelop the systems, and qualify them." Repeat single-vendor orders (₹1,900–2,000 cr pipeline) rely on products already developed and accepted. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- R&D intensity: ~1,100–1,200 engineers; writes off most R&D annually; building blocks re-used across products, which shortens delivery (claims 2–3 months for some items). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Limits: Hawk/fire-control radars for MiG-29/Su-30 have hardware but no software; DRDO and Astra Microwave (with BEL) are developing in parallel — "It is a competition." [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Order conversion and lumpiness: single customer base (MoD/DPSUs); promised negotiated orders slipped ~8 months; Q1 margin collapse shows operating leverage runs both ways with a growing fixed engineering payroll. [Q1 call]
- Cash conversion: CFO well below profit for three years; FY25 CFO negative. Working capital is the main capital sink rather than capex. [Screener]
- Disclosure inconsistency: Q1 cash ₹530 cr (call) vs ₹465.9 cr (presentation); capex plan ₹150 cr (presentation) vs ₹200 cr+ (call). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Audit: Deloitte Haskins & Sells, unmodified; key audit matter revenue recognition (control transfer/cut-off); audit-trail gap in a third-party payroll software (not enabled 1–2 Apr 2025; auditor unable to comment for Jan–Mar 2026). Small. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Contingent liabilities tiny: ₹6.9 cr (income tax, sales tax, service tax); disputed dues not deposited ~₹5 cr. Liquidated damages written off ₹2.9 cr in FY26 (₹2.3 cr FY25) — shows occasional delivery penalties. [AR FY26 note 36, CARO, P&L notes]
- Related parties: clean — promoters S. Rangarajan (22.8%) and Rekha Rangarajan (19.0%); KMP pay ₹4.3 cr; no material RPTs. Promoter holding flat at 42.41% for three years. [AR FY26 note 35; Screener]
- Board: independent director S V R Vadlamani completed tenure 9-Sep-26 and did not seek a second term ("other commitments"). [BSE filing 9-Sep-26]
- Exports shrank in FY26 despite talk of European demand. [AR FY26 note 38c]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: from confident ("contracts in the next 1 to 2 months") to asking investors to "be patient with the yearly growth model"; target to build an order book of at least 3 years' revenue. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Margin band widened to 35–40%; order pipeline language grew (₹1,726 cr negotiated + ₹2,000 cr fresh + "₹20–40 bn over 24 months"; addressable market "₹40,000–50,000 crores"). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- New: STAC composites acquisition; counter-drone sensor orders starting (active radar, passive RF, jamming); AI server spending; export team build-out. [Q1 call]
- Post-call: ₹746 cr of negotiated orders signed by 20-Aug-26; CRISIL outlook to Positive on 7-Sep-26. [BSE filing 20-Aug-26; CRISIL Sep-26]

## My read
- The business is improving in substance: FY26 grew 31% at 40% EBITDA, full-radar and EW programmes are maturing, and the negotiated orders started converting in August (₹746 cr), lifting the executable book well above one year of revenue. The weak points are the ones that never go away here — lumpy quarters, slow government payments and cash flow that trails profit by a wide margin. Trailing P/E of about 91 (Screener) needs roughly 30%+ earnings growth for many years; CRISIL's 30% revenue CAGR supports that only if margins hold near 38–40% and the ₹2,000 cr FY27 order target lands. What would change my view: order book below ₹2,000 cr by March 2027, FY27 revenue growth under 20%, or another year of CFO below half of profit.
