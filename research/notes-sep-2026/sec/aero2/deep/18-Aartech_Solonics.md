## Business and segments
- Bhopal-based electrical engineering company (incorporated 1982; plants at Mandideep, MP and Parwanoo, HP): control & relay panels (CRP) for utilities up to 220 kV; the BTS 2000 bus-transfer system (keeps power running during faults in power plants, refineries, process industries); fault-current limiter (with a US partner); defence products — Adaptive Alternative Power Module (APM) for the Army, cranking ultracapacitors, parts for an electromagnetic launch system (EMLS); plastic enclosures; trading. [Q4 call; IP Q4FY26]
- Q1 FY27 standalone revenue ₹7.28 cr split: BTS/system solutions ~₹3.46 cr, trading ~₹1.81 cr, projects (mainly defence) ~₹1.56 cr, CRP ~₹0.40 cr. [Q1 call]
- Geography: exports expected ~10% of FY27 revenue (BTS win in Indonesia against ABB; Qatar and Oman utilities; first CRP exports to Africa). [Q4 call]
- Margins: BTS 2000 gross margin ~35–40%, tapering to ~10–15% net for the company because CRP is price-competitive (priced "around 10% plus"). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Group: wholly owned Faradigm Ultracapacitors Pvt Ltd (new 15,000 sq ft plant at Mohasa-Babai, Narmadapuram) and AIC-Aartech Solonics (former incubator, now in-house product development); associates Enerqual Technology (30%, in dispute) and Epsilon Ten Ltd (UK, written off); a UK "onshore" entity. [AR FY26 Directors' report; Q4 call]
- Scale: ~150 employees incl. contract staff; DSIR-approved R&D centre; two patents pending. [Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- BTS 2000: long installed base (>10-year life), retrofit demand, 35–40% margin, won against ABB in Indonesia and against European rivals in the Gulf. [Q4 call]
- Defence: iDEX (DISC 5 and DISC 11) winner; APM trials completed at Northern, Western and Southern Commands; EMLS product approved by an R&D lab in Pune; rugged mil-grade supplies to the Air Force and Navy. [Q4 call; IP Q4FY26]
- CRP: no moat — "very, very tough competition ... very price-sensitive". [Q4 call]
- Domestic wins in FY26: IOCL, PSPCL, CSPTCL, HPSEBL, MPPTCL; entry into 132 kV substation automation. [IP Q4FY26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor's emphasis of matter (FY26):** a management dispute with 30% associate Enerqual Technology means the company gets no financial information; investment kept at cost without impairment testing (only ₹3 lakh, so immaterial in money, but it shows a failed partnership). [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 45.1]
- UK associate Epsilon Ten fully impaired (₹3.93 cr) in FY25 — a costly overseas venture. [AR FY26 notes 6, 30]
- Earnings quality: a large share of profit comes from treasury gains on listed small-cap/SME shares and funds; R&D is capitalised. [Screener; AR FY26 note 6; Q1 call]
- Related parties: promoter-director Anil Raje (non-executive) received ₹66.3 lakh of "technical consultancy" and ₹18 lakh rent for the head office in FY26; ₹20.3 lakh of "business consulting" paid to Aartech Solonics UK Ltd, an entity under management influence. Directors' pay: MD ₹34.9 lakh, CEO ₹35.4 lakh. [AR FY26 note 34]
- Family-run: MD Amit Raje and CEO Arati Nath are promoter-group members; key-person risk. [AR FY26]
- Tiny scale (₹41 cr revenue), lumpy Q3–Q4-heavy execution; negative operating cash flow. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Disclosure: no order-book number in Q1; segment data only given verbally; no investor presentation for Q1. Screener's standalone Q4 FY26 column repeats Q4 FY25 figures — a data-feed error, use consolidated. [Q1 call; Screener]

## My read
Aartech is a small, family-run electrical-engineering firm with one genuinely good product (BTS 2000) and several early defence and energy-storage bets; FY26 margins jumped mainly because FY25 carried a ₹3.9 cr write-off. The defence story is real but slow (18–24 month trials) and is only a small slice of revenue today, while the core CRP business has no pricing power. Profit quality is weak — cash flow is negative, R&D is capitalised, and treasury gains on listed shares drove most of Q1 FY27 profit. I would want to see a disclosed order book above ₹25 cr, positive operating cash flow and a first sizeable defence order (APM or ultracapacitors) before treating it as a defence growth stock.
