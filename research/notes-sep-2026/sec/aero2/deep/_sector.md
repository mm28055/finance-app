# Indian aerospace & defence — sector research (Sep 2026)

Sources read (last 12 months unless marked): MoD production/export data (Jun 2026, via Swarajya and IBEF); MP-IDSA analysis of the 2026-27 defence budget (Feb 2026); BharatShakti on FY26 DAC approvals (Sep 2026); PIB release on the 7 Sep 2026 DAC meeting (via GlobalSecurity); Jefferies India-defence note (via Business Today, 8 Sep 2026); Crisil Ratings study of 25+ private defence firms (via BW Businessworld, Sep 2025); IBEF defence-manufacturing page (updated Sep 2026); Safran press release (26 Nov 2025); IADB on Airbus/Boeing India sourcing (22 Jul 2026); news on the AMCA engine and GE F404 deliveries; background on the post-Operation Sindoor emergency buying (May–Jun 2025, older than 12 months). Company facts come from the 18 deep files in this folder.

## Market size and growth
- **Defence production FY26: ₹1,77,995 cr (~₹1.78 lakh cr), +15.6%** on ₹1,54,071 cr in FY25; roughly 2x FY21 (₹84,643 cr). DPSUs and other PSUs made ~76%, the private sector ~24% (~₹42,000 cr). [MoD via Swarajya, 17 Jun 2026; IBEF Sep-26]
- **Defence exports FY26: ₹38,424 cr (~US$4.35 bn), +62.7%** on ₹23,622 cr; DPSUs 54.84% (their exports +151%), private 45.16%; 145 exporting firms (128), 80+ countries. [MoD via Swarajya; IBEF Sep-26]
- **Government target: ₹3 lakh cr production by FY29** — needs ~19% a year from FY26 vs 15.6% achieved in FY26. [IBEF Sep-26; my calculation]
- **Budget 2026-27: MoD ₹7,84,678 cr (+15.2%), ~2.0% of GDP (1.9%)**; capital outlay ₹2,19,306 cr (+~22%); **modernisation (capital acquisition) ₹1,85,467 cr (+24.6%)**, of which **₹1,39,100 cr (75%) earmarked for domestic industry** (40% in 2020-21) and ~25% of that for private firms (~₹34,700 cr). [MP-IDSA Feb-26; Jefferies via Business Today Sep-26]
- **DAC approvals (AoN, "acceptance of necessity" — the first formal step to buy): FY26 record ₹6.73 lakh cr across 55 proposals**; capital contracts signed in FY26 also a record — **₹2.28 lakh cr across 503 contracts** (search summary of MoD year-end data). FY27 so far includes ₹1.10 lakh cr on 7 Sep 2026 (ALH helicopters, CBRN vehicles, mine layers, trawl tanks, Arudhra radars, marine gas turbines, ground jammers) with **~98% from Indian industry**. [BharatShakti Sep-26; PIB via GlobalSecurity 7 Sep 2026]
- Private listed defence firms: Crisil Ratings expected **16–18% revenue growth in FY26** after a ~20% CAGR over FY22–FY25, **operating margins steady at 18–19%**, and a combined order book of **~₹55,000 cr by FY26-end** (₹40,000 cr FY24); ₹3,600 cr of equity raised FY22–FY25 on a ₹4,760 cr net-worth base, a third of it into working capital. [Crisil Ratings via BW Businessworld, Sep-25]
- Aerospace (civil): Airbus sources ~**US$1.5 bn** a year from India (US$0.5 bn in 2019) and targets **US$2 bn by 2030**; Boeing ~₹12,000 cr (~US$1.4 bn), >70% of it manufacturing; global backlog ~17,000 aircraft ≈ 12 years of production (IATA, cited by AXISCADES). [IADB 22 Jul 2026; AXISCADES AR FY26 MD&A]
- Market-research estimates quoted in company reports (low reliability, for scale only): India aviation-defence-space market US$19.8 bn (2025) → US$26.3 bn (2030), 5.8% CAGR (Mordor); global superalloys US$9.4 bn → US$23.1 bn by 2036 (8.5% CAGR). [MIDHANI AR FY26 MD&A]

## Demand drivers
- **Capital budget growth + domestic earmark:** capital acquisition +24.6% in FY27 with 75% reserved for Indian firms is the single largest driver; Jefferies sees **domestic defence capital spending growing ~16% a year through FY30** and total defence capex ~10% a year FY26–FY30, a **₹5.67 lakh cr (US$60 bn+) domestic opportunity over four years**. [MP-IDSA; Jefferies via Business Today Sep-26]
- **Post-Operation Sindoor (May 2025) re-stocking and new doctrine:** the government gave the services **emergency-procurement (EP-6) powers up to ~₹40,000 cr**, contracts within 40 days and delivery within a year, aimed at loitering munitions, kamikaze drones, counter-drone systems and long-range missiles (background, May–Jun 2025). Company evidence: Zen got "almost ₹800 cr" of anti-drone orders; ideaForge shipped a large EW-resilient drone order in Q4 FY26; a ₹20,000 cr fast-track drone programme is in RFP stage (one RFP out); field-commander buying powers doubled (DFPDS-2026). [Swarajya May-25; Zen, ideaForge, Avantel deep files]
- **Missiles, air defence and radars:** QRSAM, Akash-NG, Pinaka, MIGM mines, BrahMos-NG, Uttam AESA, Su-30 upgrade (Virupaksha radar, Angad EW) — the largest pipeline for electronics suppliers (Astra ₹8,000–9,000 cr intake target over 3–4 years; Apollo's QRSAM/Pinaka content). Big POs mostly land FY27–FY28 and execute FY28 onward. [Astra, Apollo, Data Patterns deep files]
- **Exports:** FY26 jump was DPSU-led (BrahMos, platforms); Jefferies expects exports to grow ~11% a year to **₹58,400 cr by FY30**. Private exporters in this sector are mainly civil-aerospace and energy component makers (Azad, Unimech, Aequs, Raymond aero, Rossell). [Jefferies via Business Today; deep files]
- **Civil aerospace sourcing shift to India:** Airbus to US$2 bn by 2030; **Safran to multiply India sourcing 5x and triple India revenue to >€3 bn by 2030**, with a €200 m LEAP-engine MRO shop (300 shop visits a year, operational 2026) and a >€40 m M88 MRO shop. Engine parts are the richest seam (LEAP content at Raymond/JK Maini, Azad, Unimech tooling). [Safran PR Nov-25; IADB Jul-26; deep files]

## Policy (subsidies, duties, rules)
- **Positive indigenisation lists:** five lists covering **5,012 DPSU items** notified by May 2026 (fifth list 346 items, ~₹1,048 cr import substitution); in total >500 platforms/systems and >5,000 sub-systems are on import-embargo lists. [IBEF Sep-26; Business Today Sep-26]
- **Procurement rules:** IDDM ("indigenously designed, developed and manufactured") gets top preference; draft DAP 2026 adds long-term bulk and low-cost fast-track routes; offsets are being replaced by "Make in India" local-content demands (AXISCADES had to re-shape OEM JV proposals). [Zen, AXISCADES deep files]
- **Competition instead of nomination:** even DPSU suppliers such as MIDHANI now win almost everything by tender — a margin headwind for incumbents. [MIDHANI deep file]
- **FDI** up to 74% automatic route; **iDEX**: ~676 start-ups/MSMEs, 551 design contracts; defence corridors in UP and Tamil Nadu (~₹10,000 cr each targeted). [IBEF Sep-26]
- **Drones:** import ban on finished drones (CBU/SKD/CKD) since Feb 2022; original PLI ₹120 cr; a larger "Drone Shakti"/PLI 2.0 was discussed in 2026 (not verified in an official document). [search summaries; low confidence]
- Budget absorption risk: MP-IDSA notes the MoD spent only ₹87,927 cr of a ₹1,72,700 cr capital BE in Apr–Dec 2024 — orders can be back-ended. [MP-IDSA Feb-26]

## Cost drivers
- **Imported alloys and energy:** 50–60% of MIDHANI's raw materials are imported; titanium prices rose ~90% during FY26 (AXISCADES); in Q1 FY27 the West Asia conflict doubled LPG prices and pushed nickel, molybdenum and tungsten up — MIDHANI lost ~₹18–19 cr of margin in one quarter; Raymond flagged carbide/HSS tooling and logistics. [MIDHANI, AXISCADES, Raymond deep files]
- **Fixed-price contracts:** defence contracts usually lack price-variation clauses; cost spikes are absorbed (MIDHANI, Rossell's Boeing programme). [MIDHANI, Rossell deep files]
- **Electronic components and single-source suppliers:** AXISCADES pushed ₹84 cr of revenue out of FY26 because a sole hardware supplier diverted output to war programmes. [AXISCADES deep file]
- **Engines:** GE F404 delays left ~30 finished Tejas Mk1A jets waiting for engines; GE promised 20 engines Jun–Dec 2026; a 113-engine F404 follow-on (deliveries 2027–2032) was signed; the F414 ToT deal remains unsigned on price. [news, 2025–26]
- **People and R&D:** in-house design firms spend 5–10% of sales on R&D (Astra ~5%, Apollo ~9.5%); Raymond expenses all aero development cost. [deep files]

## Competitive structure and share
- **DPSUs dominate platforms** (HAL, BEL, BDL, shipyards): ~76% of production. Private share of production rose to ~24%; within Jefferies' listed-company basket, private share rose from 9% (FY23) to 16% (FY26), forecast ~17.5% FY27 and ~17.6% FY28–29 — i.e. the private share gain slows. [MoD via Swarajya; Jefferies via Business Today]
- **Private tiers in this sector:** (1) design-led defence electronics with own IP — Data Patterns, Astra, Apollo, Zen, Avantel, Digilogic; (2) build-to-print precision/aero components for global OEMs — Azad, Unimech, Aequs, Raymond (JK Maini), Sansera ADS, Rossell, MTAR; (3) materials — MIDHANI (state), Krishna Defence; (4) drones/counter-drone — ideaForge, Zen; (5) transitioning engineering-services firms — AXISCADES.
- **Concentration is the norm:** Zen's top 3 customers ~57%; Rossell top 5 ~91%; Unimech top customer 44.5%; Raymond aero top 3 40–45%; MTAR's Bloom >70%; AXISCADES standalone top 2 = 73%. [deep files]
- Global competition in materials: ATI, Carpenter, VSMPO-AVISMA, Haynes hold OEM approvals and scale (MIDHANI's own SWOT). [MIDHANI AR FY26]

## Where profit sits (FY26, Screener basis from the deep files)
- **Highest margins: in-house-IP defence electronics and critical engine parts** — Data Patterns EBITDA ~40%, Azad ~37%, Zen ~36%, Unimech ~32%, Astra ~29%. These rely on qualification barriers and design ownership.
- **Middle:** Apollo ~24% (falling as explosives are added), Raymond aero ~21–25% vs auto 13–14%, MIDHANI ~20% (was ~30% before FY24), Krishna Defence ~22%, Avantel ~22% (down from 37%), MTAR ~19.5%, Digilogic ~20%.
- **Thin or negative:** build-to-print assemblies and consumer/aero contract manufacturing — Rossell ~13%, Aequs ~7.5% op. margin with losses; ideaForge ~3% in FY26 (lumpy); AXISCADES' retained business ~5–10% while it rebuilds.
- Pattern: **design ownership and critical-part qualification earn the profit; platform integration by DPSUs and build-to-print structures earn much less**; working capital (inventory and MoD receivables of 150–350 days) eats much of the cash. [deep files]

## Cycle position
- **Orders: late-early to mid upcycle** — record AoNs (₹6.73 lakh cr) and contracts (₹2.28 lakh cr) in FY26 and a 24.6% jump in the FY27 modernisation budget mean order books will stay high through FY28. [BharatShakti; MP-IDSA]
- **Execution: bottlenecked** — Q1 FY27 was weak for many firms (supply chain, West Asia costs, delayed POs): Data Patterns +17%, Apollo organic +17%, Zen and Avantel down, MIDHANI margin 15%. Guidance is increasingly back-ended to H2 FY27 and FY28. [deep files]
- **Valuations: high** — most listed names trade on P/Es that need 20–30% growth for years (see the live table in the note); the Jefferies forecast implies sector capital spend growth of ~16%, lower than many company guides. [Jefferies via Business Today; deep files]
- **Civil aerospace: strong demand, supply-constrained** — the 17,000-aircraft backlog and engine shortages favour qualified Indian engine-part suppliers, but ramp-ups depend on OEM build rates and titanium/nickel availability. [AXISCADES AR; Raymond deep file]

## Key risks for the sector
- PO timing (fast-track drone programme, QRSAM, MIGM, Su-30 upgrade) and MoD capital under-spend; receivables from MoD/DPSUs age (AXISCADES wrote off ₹9.6 cr in Q1). [MP-IDSA; deep files]
- Input shocks from West Asia (LPG, nickel, titanium, freight) on fixed-price contracts. [MIDHANI, Raymond deep files]
- Engine dependence (GE F404/F414 delays; AMCA engine partner — Safran–GTRE proposal sent for CCS approval in Aug 2026, no contract yet). [news 2026]
- Governance: many small/mid caps show customer concentration, promoter pledges (Apollo), promoter warrants priced far below market (Raymond ₹497 vs ~₹1,197), related-party dealings and repeated guidance slippage. [deep files]
