# MTAR Technologies — deep research (Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Hyderabad precision engineering firm (55 years old). Four reporting "sectors" (not Ind AS segments — one reportable segment): Clean Energy – fuel cells, hydel and others; Clean Energy – civil nuclear; Aerospace & Defence; Products & Others. [IP Q1FY27 p.7–10; AR FY26 note 40]
- Revenue mix FY26 (₹876 cr): fuel cells/hydel ₹615 cr (70%), products ₹134 cr (15%), aerospace & defence ₹104 cr (12%), nuclear ₹24 cr (3%). Q1 FY27 (₹361 cr): fuel cells/hydel ₹221 cr (61%), products ₹100 cr (28%), A&D ₹36 cr (10%), nuclear ₹3 cr (1%). [IP Q1FY27 p.7–10]
- Clean energy = hot boxes and other assemblies for Bloom Energy's solid-oxide fuel cells (Bloom supplies on-site power to US data centres). Bloom was ₹633 cr of ₹865 cr standalone FY26 revenue (73%); ICRA says ">70% of revenues" from one customer. [AR FY26 note 40c; ICRA Jul-26]
- Nuclear: fuel machining heads, coolant-channel assemblies, drive mechanisms for NPCIL reactors; supplied "a majority of the critical assemblies" for the PFBR core, which reached criticality in 2026; qualified for Calandria and end shields. [IP Q1FY27 p.16–17; Q4 call]
- Aerospace & defence: Vikas and cryogenic engine assemblies for ISRO; LCA Tejas actuators and structures; AMCA landing-gear test structure; MNC aerospace (Thales, GKN, IAI, Rafael, Elbit) moving from first articles to volume; NADCAP special-process facility (30+ processes); dedicated aerospace plant since Jan-25. [IP Q1FY27 p.5, 8, 19]
- New verticals: oil & gas (Weatherford whipstocks, SLB Class-A parts; greenfield plant due Q3 FY27, ₹400–500 cr potential in 3–4 years); data-centre infrastructure sheet-metal assemblies for SLB (₹45 cr first order, customer needs "eight such sets" a year). [IP Q1FY27 p.21–22; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Geography: exports 81% of Q1 FY27 revenue, 82% of FY26. Subsidiaries: Gee Pee Aerospace & Defence, Magnatar Aero Systems (100%). [IP Q1FY27 p.13; AR FY26]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales Q1 FY26→Q1 FY27: 157, 136, 278, 306, 361. Operating profit: 28, 17, 64, 62, 85. OPM: 18%, 13%, 23%, 20%, 24%. Net profit: 11, 4, 35, 44, 50. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +130% YoY to ₹360.7 cr; EBITDA ₹85.1 cr (23.6%); PAT ₹50.2 cr (+364%); gross margin 45.5% (vs 54.2% Q1 FY26, 44.2% Q4) — mix shift to lower-margin, high-volume fuel-cell work. [IP Q1FY27 p.11–12; Q1 call]
- Annual: FY24 sales 581 / OP 113 / PAT 56; FY25 676 / 121 / 53; FY26 876 / 171 / 94. FY26 EBITDA margin 19.5% vs guided 21% ±1% (missed on input and freight costs, more staff). 3-yr sales CAGR 15%, 3-yr profit CAGR −2% (FY23 PAT ₹103 cr was the old peak). TTM sales ₹1,080 cr, PAT ₹133 cr. ROCE 15% (FY26). [Screener; Q4 call]
- Interest cost rising: ₹16 cr in Q1 FY27 vs ₹6 cr a year earlier, as borrowings and non-fund limits grow. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Unit economics: data-centre assemblies "value addition of close to 70%"; LCA actuator orders ₹130–150 cr (value-add only, customer-supplied material); oil & gas potential $35–40 m over time. [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q3/earlier: FY27 revenue growth 50%. Q4 FY26 call (May-26): raised to **80% ±5%** with EBITDA ~24%; FY27 closing order book ~₹5,000 cr; capex ₹250–300 cr over FY27–FY28; debt/equity ~0.5; working capital ~172 days; H1 good, H2 better; FY30 revenue ₹5,000 cr "hopefully… we are on track", needing ₹500–700 cr capex. [Q4 call]
- FY26 track record: order book ₹2,592 cr vs ₹2,800 cr guided (**missed**, nuclear/defence orders slipped); EBITDA 19.5% vs 20–22% guided (**missed**); revenue +30%. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +130% and margin 23.6% — **on track/beating** the 80% / 24% ±100 bp guidance; order book ₹5,143 cr at June end (FY27 target hit in Q1) plus ~₹800 cr incremental by 30-Jul; working capital 59 days vs 172. Guidance kept at 80%/24% with "we are pretty confident to do better". Working-capital target changed to ~100 days (from 150–175). Capex raised to ~₹500 cr over FY27–FY28 (70% clean energy). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Longer term: products division could "cross ₹1,000 crores" and aerospace ₹600–700 cr in 3–4 years (from ~₹100–130 cr each). [Q1 call]
- ICRA (Jul-26) upgraded to A+ citing FY27 growth; notes May-26 orders of ~₹2,279 cr and ₹467 cr from international customer(s). [ICRA Jul-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Fuel cells: Phase 1 commissioned (FY26); Phase 2 in greenfield plant (shared with oil & gas) by Sep–Oct 2026 / Q3 FY27; Phase 3 "multi-fold" expansion at a new facility by March 2027 (numbers under NDA; earlier talk of 20,000 hot boxes). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.21]
- Capex spend: ~₹30–35 cr cash in Q1 FY27 (₹80 cr capitalised from CWIP); ₹500 cr planned over FY27 and H1 FY28; target asset turn "at least 4 to 5 times", later "at least 6 times". Capital commitments jumped to ₹160 cr (Mar-26) from ₹12 cr. [Q1 call; AR FY26 note 34]
- Funding: "combination of internal funding and debt"; ICRA assumed ₹250–300 cr funded by debt and accruals. [Q1 call; ICRA Jul-26]
- Aerospace: sub-units being added inside the dedicated aerospace facility for MNC volumes; "Outland test facility" (sic, official transcript) operational by October. Data-centre: dedicated facility being set up. [Q1 call]
- Payback maths (mine): ₹500 cr at 4–6x asset turn supports ₹2,000–3,000 cr of extra revenue; at ~22–24% EBITDA that is ₹450–700 cr of EBITDA — so payback is fast if volumes come, but relies on one customer.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹177 cr (Mar-25) → ₹377 cr (Mar-26) → ₹423.6 cr (Jun-26); investments ₹215 cr (Mar-26) and ₹379 cr (end-July); CFO says net debt only ₹20–30 cr. ICRA: gearing 0.46x, debt/OPBITDA 2.2x, interest cover 5.8x (FY26), free cash ₹218.5 cr at Mar-26; repayments ₹44.5 cr FY27, ₹60 cr FY28. [Screener; Q1 call; ICRA Jul-26]
- Cash flow from operations: FY24 ₹57 cr, FY25 ₹101 cr, FY26 ₹197 cr; FCF FY26 ₹64 cr — first meaningful positive year. Q1 FY27 CFO stated as ₹247.7 cr (driven by better payment terms/customer advances). [Screener; Q1 call]
- Working capital: company metric 172 days (FY26) → 59 days (Q1 FY27): receivables 82 days (140), inventory 145 (208), offset by current liabilities (customer advances) 168 days. Screener shows inventory 399 days, CCC 419 days for FY26 — on gross basis the business is still inventory-heavy; the improvement depends on advances. ICRA: NWC/OI 44% (62% in FY25). GST refunds ~₹70 cr a year targeted. [IP Q1FY27 p.14; Screener; ICRA Jul-26; Q1 call]
- Margin-money deposits back ₹149.9 cr of bank guarantees/LCs; bank loans secured on current assets. No dividend since FY22. [AR FY26 notes; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- 12–15-year relationship with Bloom; "we hold the majority of the share" of its hot-box demand; management not concerned about new vendors — "the learning curve is very steep". Bloom's recent ~₹3,200 cr order (of which ~₹800 cr incremental) shows volume commitment. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Nuclear: 35+ years with NPCIL (sole buyer; private participation not allowed); highest-ever nuclear order ₹504 cr for Kaiga 5&6; nuclear order book ~₹684 cr going to ~₹815 cr. [IP Q1FY27 p.7; Q1 call]
- Qualification barriers: NADCAP approvals covering 30+ processes ("available with only a select few companies in India"); first articles cleared for Thales, GKN, IAI; aerospace volumes "at least 10 to 15 times" first-article quantities. [IP Q1FY27 p.5; Q1 call]
- Pricing power limited: gross margin fell from 49.4% (FY25) to 47.7% (FY26) and 45.5% (Q1 FY27); management denied price renegotiation with Bloom. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- Customer concentration: one US customer ~73% of revenue and 67% of order book (clean energy ₹3,431 cr of ₹5,143 cr); exposed to US fuel-cell economics, US tariffs/policy and Bloom's own execution. [AR FY26 note 40c; IP Q1FY27 p.13]
- Promoter selling and pledging: promoter holding 36.4% (Jun-24) → 31.8% (Mar-25) → 30.4% (Mar-26) → 29.35% (Jun-26); FY26 AR shows sales by several promoter-group members; Sep-26 disclosures show further sales (Leelavathi Parvatha Reddy 20,000 shares on 16-Sep; K. Shalini 18,000 on 4-Sep) and a pledge of 32,000 shares by Kavitha Reddy Gangapatnam (17-Sep). [Screener; AR FY26 note 13; News: InvestyWise, Sep-26]
- Governance lapses (small): three designated persons traded without pre-clearance and did contra trades — ₹1.8 lakh profit disgorged to SEBI IPEF (Feb-26); one-day late RPT/XBRL filing fined ₹5,000 by NSE; books' electronic back-up not kept on servers in India Apr–Aug 2025 (auditor remark). [AR FY26 secretarial audit report, note 42]
- Audit: unmodified; only key audit matter revenue recognition (domestic ₹147.4 cr, export ₹717.9 cr). Contingent liabilities just ₹2.3 cr. Related-party dealings small (MD pay ₹3.1 cr; rent to promoter relative K. Shalini; internet charges to Nettlinx, an entity with common KMP). [AR FY26 auditor's report, notes 34, 38]
- Execution: tripling output in 12–18 months needs people, machines and training; nuclear revenue still negligible (₹3 cr in Q1) despite a ₹684 cr book — execution promised from H2. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Forex: >70% of revenue exported, 30–35% imported inputs; FY26 other income ~₹25 cr was mostly forex gains. [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- May: raised FY27 guidance to 80%; defensive on missed FY26 margin and order-book targets. July: "blowout" quarter, order book already above year-end target, working capital slashed, capex doubled to ₹500 cr. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New: dedicated data-centre facility; SLB as a customer; Phase 3 fuel-cell expansion dated to March 2027; products division talk of ₹1,000 cr+. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Risk shift: from balance sheet/working capital to execution capacity and concentration. [Q1 call]

## My read
- The business has clearly turned: revenue +130% in Q1, margin back to 24%, cash flow positive, and an order book near six times FY26 revenue. But this is one customer's capex cycle — 73% of revenue and two-thirds of the backlog is Bloom — and gross margins are drifting down as volume work grows. The trailing P/E of about 156 (Screener) needs roughly 40%+ earnings growth for years; FY27 may deliver far more than that, but the multiple leaves no room for a Bloom slowdown. Steady promoter selling and small pledges while the stock trebled are a caution flag. What would change my view: a second large customer (SLB data-centre, MNC aerospace) reaching ₹200 cr+ a year, nuclear execution showing up in H2 FY27, or gross margin stabilising above 45%.
