## Business and segments
- Until FY26 AXISCADES was mainly an **engineering-services** company (design hours sold to aerospace, heavy-engineering, auto and energy OEMs) with a **defence electronics** arm built around **Mistral Solutions** (58.68% owned; radar sub-systems, counter-drone, mission computers, land systems for MoD). Audited segments FY26: Technology Services & Solutions (TSS) ₹781 cr revenue, segment result ₹66 cr (8.5%); **Defence ₹378 cr (+24.7%), segment result ₹83 cr (+90.9%, 21.9% margin)**. [AR FY26 MD&A; note 42]
- Management's "domain view" FY26: Aerospace ₹388 cr (17.3% EBITDA margin, includes aerospace services now being sold), Defence ₹379 cr (22.9%), ESAI (electronics, semiconductor test, AI — now "XiDA", US-domiciled) ₹136 cr (26.1%); auto/energy/heavy engineering ~₹239 cr. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- **Portfolio reset in Q1 FY27:** (1) heavy engineering, auto and energy services sold to **Akkodis** for US$30.63 m (US$17.42 m at close, US$5.81 m after 12 months, US$7.40 m earn-out on EBITDA to 30 Jun 2027); (2) **aerospace engineering services** sold to Akkodis for US$206.3 m — US$77.70 m in tranche 1, US$76.02 m after 24 months and up to US$52.58 m contingent; after tranche 1 the unit is 51% Akkodis / 49% AXISCADES. Headline total **US$237 m (~₹2,256 cr at ₹95.2/$)**; the company calls ₹1,685 cr the "minimum consideration". [AR FY26 MD&A §3.2; IP Q1FY27 p.4]
- **Retained business Q1 FY27 (excluding add solutions GmbH): ₹180.7 cr** — Defence ₹125.0 cr (+111%), XiDA ₹49.5 cr (+62%, EBITDA ₹14.7 cr, 29.7%), Aerospace manufacturing ₹6.1 cr (EBITDA −₹5.4 to −₹6.2 cr). Discontinued operations ₹163.3 cr. [IP Q1FY27 pp.6–9]
- Geography: diversified across Europe, USA, Canada and Asia-Pacific in the services era; defence is India (MoD, DRDO, DPSUs) plus MBDA test benches (MICA, Meteor, CAMM, ASRAAM; ~5 benches a year for three years). [CARE Oct-25; Q1 call]
- Facilities: **DAL** (Devanahalli AeroLand, 165,000 sq ft, ~₹150 cr, operational — leased from associate Aero Electronics Pvt Ltd); **DAC** (Devanahalli Atmanirbhar Complex, 20 acres, phase 1 under construction, radar hangar in operation by Q1 FY28); **MAC** (Missile Atmanirbhar Complex, Hyderabad, 8 acres, ground broken July 2026, "indicative ₹600 cr" framework); **CAM** (Centre for Advanced Manufacturing, 240,000 sq ft on 20 acres, land allocation in process). [AR FY26 MD&A §3.4; IP Q1FY27 pp.12–13; related-party note 34]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (30 May 2026):** FY27 revenue "trending toward" **₹1,377 cr** (52% growth on a retained base of ₹903 cr); backed by ₹927 cr orders under execution (incl. the ₹142 cr carry-forward), ₹285 cr assured forecast visibility and ₹165 cr acquisition-linked visibility. Retained core EBITDA margin "already 20% plus", +150–200 bps a year, saturating at 25–27%. Deferred ₹142 cr to be recognised in Q1–Q2; ~₹120–140 cr cash released in H1 as land-systems units ship (20 ready, 25 in assembly, 85 in production). Engineering-services sale to close "July or August" with a ~₹175 cr gain. **Power 930**: ₹9,000 cr revenue or ₹960 cr PAT by FY30. No equity dilution. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 call / presentation (13 Aug 2026):** ₹1,377 cr "reaffirmed" but **restated as a "pro forma annualised" continuing-operations figure**, i.e. it now assumes acquisitions are counted for a full year; EBITDA **₹270 cr on the same pro forma basis**, PAT "broadly around" ₹135 cr. Defence +75% a year "over the next several years"; XiDA >100% in FY27; aerospace platform ₹375 cr+ annualised revenue and ₹84 cr EBITDA **by Q4 FY27**, ₹1,000 cr by FY29. Divestment gain raised to **₹1,255 cr** (both deals) in Q2–Q3. Assured forecast visibility **₹4,557 cr** (executable through FY30); pipeline "exceeding ₹24,000 cr". [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.14]
- **Track record vs Q4 promises:** deferred revenue — ₹60 cr+ of ₹142 cr recovered in Q1 (promised Q1–Q2; now "Q2 and Q3") → **slipped**. Engineering-services close — promised July/August → Phase 1 "by 31 Aug", Q3 FY27 per later reports → **slipped**. Margin — retained EBITDA 4.7% reported / 10% normalised vs "20% plus" → **missed**. Revenue guide kept in words but **softened in substance** by moving to a pro forma basis. Cash promise (₹120–140 cr released in H1) — Q1 cash rose only to ₹81 cr; not yet shown. Acquisitions (aero NBO, XiDA business transfer) — on schedule so far. Verdict: **guidance kept in form, weakened in substance; delivery behind**. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Stated plan (Q4 call): DAC ~₹1,200 cr, MAC ~₹300 cr (annual report says "indicative ₹600 cr"), DAL ₹100–150 cr already spent → **~₹1,600 cr organic**; acquisitions ~₹600 cr (one XiDA ₹50 cr, two aerospace ₹400–450 cr, a second XiDA ~₹150 cr). Total **₹2,100–2,250 cr**; divestment proceeds to cover ~80% post-tax, rest internal accruals and partnerships. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A §3.4]
- FY26 capex ~₹134 cr (11.6% of revenue, 3x the prior year); capital commitments jumped to **₹73.4 cr from ₹0.8 cr**. Q1 FY27 PP&E +₹40 cr. [AR FY26 MD&A supplementary ratios; note 36; Q1 call]
- Funding reality: near-term cash from the sales is **~₹192 cr in Phase 1 and ~₹718 cr net in Phase 2** (≈₹910 cr); the rest of the ₹2,256 cr is deferred 24 months or contingent. Bridge debt of ₹100–150 cr to be drawn until proceeds arrive; capex will be "timed" to inflows in FY27, FY28 and "the last and larger set" in FY29 — **no year-wise capex figure given**. OEM JV partners (the original plan was subsidiary-level equity from OEMs) are "early" with no timeline. [IP Q1FY27 p.14; Q1 call]
- Aerospace acquisition (NBO, AS9100D precision machining firm): FY27 annualised ₹180 cr revenue, ₹39 cr EBITDA (22%); second target in evaluation; XiDA business transfer (46% EBITDA margin in its first quarter) completing Q2. Payback depends on price paid — undisclosed. [IP Q1FY27 pp.11–12]

## Moat evidence
- Defence: sole-/limited-source design positions — Uttam radar antenna-beam controller, anti-tank missile and BrahMos on-board electronics (DcPP), 30 kW laser DEW technology transfer (technical selection), mobile mast (₹6.9 cr development order) and firing platform for a long-range missile, four Kusha modules, BrahMos-NG seeker prototype (revenue "from next financial year"). ~21 active design positions. [IP Q1FY27 p.10; Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Defence segment margin rose to 21.9% in FY26 from 14.3% — evidence of pricing on qualified content — but Q1 FY27 reported defence EBITDA was 2.1% (9.7% underlying). [AR FY26 note 42; IP Q1FY27 p.9]
- **Concentration is the flip side:** 73% of standalone revenue came from the top two customers (FY25). [CARE Oct-25]
- XiDA: new customers are "the world's largest semiconductor equipment maker" and "one of the largest AI/hyperscale companies" — acquired via a business transfer, not won organically. [IP Q1FY27 p.11]
- Assured forecast visibility is **customer-communicated programme requirements, not firm orders**; firm orders under execution were ₹927 cr at FY26 results. [IP Q1FY27 p.10; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Execution/funding gap:** ₹2,100–2,250 cr of plans against ~₹910 cr of near-term proceeds; rest depends on deferred/contingent tranches (FY28–FY29), bridge debt and unnamed OEM partners. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Guidance quality:** repeated moving of dates (divestment close; deferred revenue), a switch to "pro forma annualised" targets, and normalised figures that exclude recurring-looking items (receivable provisions, hedge losses). Power 930 needs ~10x revenue in four years. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Auditor findings (FY26):** unmodified opinion (S.R. Batliboi), but CARO flagged **serious delays in depositing provident fund** — ₹59.25 lakh for Jan–Sep 2025 paid only on 14 May 2026; fresh loans given to subsidiaries Explosoft and Epcogen to repay their overdue loans; audit trail not enabled at database level for one software; subsidiary auditors flagged CARO clauses ii(a), iii(c), ix(a) (ACAT, AAIPL) and vii(a). Eleven subsidiaries (₹422 cr assets, ₹226 cr revenue) audited by other auditors. Goodwill ₹150.5 cr is a key audit matter; ₹7.98 cr goodwill impaired in FY26. [AR FY26 auditor's reports, CARO; MD&A]
- **Related parties:** ₹6.00 cr "legal and professional charges" to Aerospace Resource Needs LLP (an entity in which a director or relative has significant influence) in FY26; DAL facility leased from associate Aero Electronics (₹2.8 cr deposit, ₹5.75 cr investment); advances to director-linked Microcon Machine Tech (₹0.31 cr) and BNC Motors (₹0.49 cr); chairman travel ₹1.27 cr (₹0.43 cr FY25). [AR FY26 note 34]
- **Contingent liabilities** ₹34.6 cr (tax) vs ₹67.8 cr; bank guarantees ₹73.4 cr (₹47.5 cr); service-tax dispute of ₹9.6 cr at the Supreme Court; GST ₹14.1 cr at Madras HC. [AR FY26 note 36; CARO vii(b)]
- **Leadership churn:** three CEO/chair changes since 2024 (Arun Krishnamurthi out Jan 2025; Alfonso Martinez CEO Jan–Aug 2025, now at add solutions); founder back as MD from Aug 2025. add solutions GmbH still loses money (Q1 PAT −₹6.7 cr). [AR FY26 note 34, MD&A §3.3; IP Q1FY27]
- Defence receivables from MoD can age (₹9.6 cr written down in Q1); supplier concentration caused the ₹84 cr slip. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Scope of the sale widened from engineering services (₹175 cr gain) to also aerospace services (gain now ₹1,255 cr), removing ~₹74 cr of aerospace EBITDA that must be bought back. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.14]
- Guidance changed basis to pro forma annualised; EBITDA ₹270 cr and PAT ~₹135 cr stated for the first time; defence +75% and XiDA +100% added. [Q1 call]
- Assured visibility jumped from ₹285 cr (Q4 definition) to ₹4,557 cr — a different, broader measure (opening ₹4,350 cr "as of FY26 results date"). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.10]
- Tone moved from explaining a miss ("Bahubali… two acts") to defending a reported loss as "expensive for one quarter, compounding for the next forty". [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## My read
AXISCADES is swapping a steady, cash-generative services business for a defence-electronics and manufacturing story that is real in parts (defence revenue doubled, qualified sole-source positions, a high-margin XiDA transfer) but not yet profitable on its own: the retained business made a loss in Q1 and the listed parent lost ₹83 cr before tax in FY26. Most of the headline ₹2,256 cr is deferred or contingent, the capex plan exceeds near-term cash, and targets have shifted to a pro forma basis. At a market value near ₹8,000 cr the price already assumes the ₹270 cr pro forma EBITDA becomes reported EBITDA in FY28 and that defence grows ~75% a year. The view improves if Phase 1 and 2 cash actually arrives by Q3, the aero acquisition closes at a sensible price, and retained EBITDA margin reaches the mid-teens on a reported basis; it worsens on further slips, more receivable write-downs, or new debt to bridge the capex.
