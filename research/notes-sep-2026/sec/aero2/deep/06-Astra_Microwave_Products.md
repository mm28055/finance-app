# Astra Microwave Products — deep research (Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Hyderabad RF/microwave defence electronics maker: components and in-house MMIC chips, radar sub-systems (active antenna array units, T/R modules), complete radars (tracking, weather, counter-drone), EW sub-systems, missile seekers/telemetry, space payload sub-systems, Doppler weather radars and wind profilers. One reportable segment. [AR FY26 note 33; Q4 call]
- Revenue mix FY26 (standalone ₹1,156 cr): radar ~60%, space & meteorology ~16%; by customer DRDO 28.2%, BEL & other DPSUs 22.7%, IMD 18.0%, exports (via ARC JV and Aelius) 11.9%, ISRO/SAC 6.4%, others ~13%. Three customers each >10% together ₹610.5 cr (53%). Direct exports only ₹2.2 cr; the rest is deemed exports to the JV. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 33]
- Group: Astra Rafael Comsys (ARC, JV with Rafael, software-defined radios; order book ₹836 cr); Astra Defence Technologies (subsidiary); Aelius Semiconductors (Singapore); Astra Space Technologies (ASTL, to receive the demerged space, meteorology and hydrology business). [Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Programmes: Uttam AESA radar (Tejas Mk1A; Astra is the development-cum-production partner, DCPP), Virupaksha radar and Angad EW suite for Su-30 upgrades, QRSAM, AMCA radar antenna (L1 in the "AAAU" tender), AEW&C, BrahMos seeker/telemetry, Tejas ASPJ sub-system. [Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Order book: standalone ₹2,141 cr (Mar-26) → ₹2,156 cr (Jun-26; production 65.7%, development 14.3%, export 8%, AMC/service 12%); consolidated ₹2,849 cr at June end; ~₹4,300 cr after the July Uttam order (CRISIL: ₹4,341 cr, ~84% defence). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales Q1 FY26→Q1 FY27: 200, 215, 260, 488, 177. Operating profit: 41, 48, 83, 161, 33. OPM: 20%, 22%, 32%, 33%, 19%. Net profit: 16, 24, 47, 106, 12. Heavy Q4 skew. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: consolidated revenue ₹177 cr (−11.5% YoY); standalone ₹176 cr (−10.7%), EBITDA ₹33 cr (18.8%). Management blamed "temporary delays in customer approvals and the closure of some last-stage technical issues". Order inflow ₹185 cr (as guided). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Annual: FY24 sales 909 / OP 192 / PAT 121; FY25 1,051 / 269 / 154; FY26 1,163 / 334 / 193. OPM 21% → 26% → 29%; CRISIL operating margin 29.6% (FY26) vs 25.1%. 3-yr sales CAGR 13%, 3-yr profit CAGR 40%. ROCE 20%, ROE 16%. TTM sales ₹1,140 cr, PAT ₹189 cr. [Screener; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Gross margin ~55% in FY26 (management: "probably as best as it gets" — factor slightly lower). Export (SDR RF via ARC) gross margin ~40–45% vs single digits in old build-to-print offset work. [Q4 call]
- R&D spend ₹57.8 cr in FY26 (₹55.7 cr revenue, ₹2.0 cr capital), ~5% of sales. [AR FY26 note 37]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (May-26): FY27 revenue ₹1,300–1,400 cr (15–20% growth; 40% R&D / 60% production), order inflow ₹1,600 cr+ (excl. Uttam); Uttam order "Q2 or Q3"; ARC FY27 sales ~₹600 cr (+50%) and order book visibility ₹1,200 cr; margins at current levels ("this is probably at the top end"); revenue to "nearly triple" in 4.5–5.5 years from five or six programmes; own-IP products before Diwali. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: Uttam order **delivered early** (₹2,205 cr incl. GST, ~₹1,870 cr ex-GST, late July); AMCA AAAU L1; order inflow ₹185 cr in line; revenue −11% YoY (**weak start**, blamed on approvals); ARC FY27 sales expectation **cut** to ₹360 cr (from "over ₹600 cr") though its order book rose to ₹836 cr. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 targets: FY27 revenue ₹1,350 cr ±25 (kept, narrowed); FY28 ₹1,600 cr ±50 (new); "completely different trajectory from FY29 onwards"; order intake ₹8,000–9,000 cr over the next 3–4 years (QRSAM ₹700–800 cr, ARC ₹500–750 cr, Su-30 Virupaksha + Angad ≥₹3,000 cr, Tejas EW ₹500–600 cr more); cumulative revenue "at least 6 to 7x our last year's annual turnover" over the next five years. EBITDA margin "similar to last year". Working capital: no big change expected in FY27. [Q1 call]
- Uttam execution: first 12 units by Sep-2027 (14 months), then ~25+ units a year; complete by FY32, possibly FY31. [Q1 call]
- CRISIL: revenue growth 15–18% near term; operating margin 28–30% medium term. [CRISIL Aug-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex light: ₹40–50 cr a year maintenance/augmentation per management; CRISIL assumes ₹75–80 cr a year. Fixed assets ₹229 cr → ₹267 cr (FY26). Capital commitments ₹44.5 cr. "There won't be any additional capex beyond what we are doing normally." [Q4 call; CRISIL Aug-26; Screener; AR FY26 note 34]
- Funding: ₹174 cr preferential warrants (20.14 lakh at ₹864, June-2025) — 25% (₹43.5 cr) received and used; balance ~₹130 cr due on conversion by 29-Dec-2026. Management: "we will not dilute equity for working capital" unless there is an accretive acquisition. [AR FY26 Corporate Governance report; CRISIL Aug-26; Q1 call]
- Execution capacity: invested in automated process-engineering workflows; SAP HANA migration done; new CTO, HR, quality, purchase and sales heads in 18 months. Main stated constraint is global component supply ("supply chains are stretched across the board"). [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash flow from operations: FY24 −₹182 cr, FY25 −₹90 cr, FY26 +₹387 cr (company: ₹370 cr) — driven by collecting long-held debtors; FCF FY26 +₹304 cr. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Borrowings ₹424 cr (Mar-25) → ₹288 cr (Mar-26); CRISIL: gearing 0.22x, interest cover 6.57x, unencumbered cash ₹190 cr, net cash accrual ₹216 cr. Q1 FY27 interest fell because overdraft use was minimal. [Screener; CRISIL Aug-26; Q1 call]
- Working capital still heavy: debtor days 273 → 216; inventory days ~394; CCC 537 days (Screener); CRISIL gross current assets ~429 days (>500). Trade receivables ₹708 cr with ₹14.8 cr expected-credit-loss provision (a key audit matter). Management expects working capital to tighten again in Q3–Q4. [Screener; CRISIL Aug-26; AR FY26 auditor's report; Q1 call]
- Dividend ₹2.40/share for FY26 (payout ~12%). [Q4 call; Screener]
- Ratings: CRISIL upgraded to A+ (Stable) from A (Positive) on 28-Aug-26; A1 reaffirmed; facilities ₹1,786.5 cr. [CRISIL Aug-26]

## Moat evidence
- Qualification and incumbency: DCPP roles on Uttam and Su-30 Angad; "the Uttam Radar belongs to Astra"; >45 Doppler weather radars supplied to IMD ("first Indian company" to make them). [Q1 call]
- Backward integration: in-house MMIC chip design since 2005; "sourcing most active devices from our in-house MMIC division"; ₹45 cr BEL order for MMIC chipsets in Q1. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Pricing/margins: gross margin ~55%, operating margin ~29–30% (FY26), rising from 12% in FY21–22 after leaving low-value build-to-print offset work. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Competition exists: BEL was L2 on AMCA AAAU; DRDO and Data Patterns are developing competing fire-control radar hardware. [Q1 call; Data Patterns Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Lumpiness and approvals: Q1 revenue fell 11%; FY26 Q4 alone was 42% of annual revenue. [Screener]
- Customer concentration: three customers 53% of revenue; almost entirely Government of India demand. [AR FY26 note 33]
- Contingent liabilities: corporate guarantees ₹43 cr for Astra Defence Technologies and ₹80 cr for the ARC JV; income-tax penalty ₹0.9 cr under appeal. ARC FY26 profit hit by ~$2 m forex provisions; Astra's share of JV profit only ~₹8 cr in FY26. [AR FY26 note 34; Q4 call]
- Audit: unmodified; key audit matter ECL on receivables; audit trail not maintained for direct database changes (auditor remark). [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Management transition: MD S.G. Reddy's "last interaction" as MD (moving to lead the demerged space company); M.V. Reddy and Atim Kabra take over. Promoter holding only 6.54% — a professionally run, widely held company. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Demerger of space/meteorology/hydrology (~14% of FY26 revenue per CRISIL; management wants the new entity to do ₹300 cr+ at 18–20% PBT in year one) — CRISIL dates it "from fiscal 2028"; management says operating independently from 1-Apr-2027. [CRISIL Aug-26; Q1 call]
- Disclosure slips: press release said 10–15% growth while presentation said 15–20% (twice); call cited "₹1,057 crores" as last year's revenue although FY26 was ₹1,156–1,163 cr; call's Q1 revenue "₹182 crores" vs ₹176–177 cr in the results. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## What changed between the two calls
- Uttam order landed (doubling the book); tone shifted from margins/cash flow to execution and supply-chain risk. [Q1 call]
- New FY28 revenue figure (₹1,600 cr); order-intake roadmap ₹8,000–9,000 cr over 3–4 years; ARC FY27 sales cut to ₹360 cr; MD change and space demerger date firmed. [Q1 call]

## My read
- Astra has genuinely moved up from component supplier to programme partner, and the Uttam order gives multi-year visibility; FY26 also showed it can turn profits into cash when payments come in. But near-term growth is modest by the company's own numbers (₹1,350 cr in FY27, ₹1,600 cr in FY28, i.e. ~16–18% a year), the real step-up is pushed to FY29+, and Q1 started with a decline. A trailing P/E near 81 (Screener) needs roughly 25–30% earnings growth for years — more than FY27–FY28 guidance implies, so the price leans on the FY29+ ramp (Uttam, QRSAM, Su-30). What would change my view: QRSAM and Su-30 production orders arriving on time and FY27 revenue at or above ₹1,350 cr with CFO positive again; or conversely another year of negative operating cash flow.
