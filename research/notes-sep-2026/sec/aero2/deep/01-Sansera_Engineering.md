# Sansera Engineering — deep research (Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Precision forged and machined parts. Legacy core: connecting rods, crankshaft assemblies, rocker arms, gear shifter forks for two-wheelers (2W), cars (PV) and trucks (CV); ICRA calls it among the largest suppliers of con-rods and rocker arms for 2W/light vehicles in India and the largest supplier of 2W gear shifter forks. [ICRA Jan-26]
- Three reporting buckets (Q1 FY27 sales): Auto ICE ₹627.5 cr (65% of sales, +20.8% YoY); Auto tech-agnostic + xEV ₹131.6 cr (+22.2%); Non-auto ₹199.8 cr (20.8% of sales, +129.9%). Within non-auto, ADS (aerospace, defence, semiconductor equipment) was ₹145.4 cr, more than 3x YoY. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.4–5]
- FY26 mix: Auto ICE 70% (83% in FY21), tech-agnostic/xEV 14% (5%), non-auto 16% (12%). By end market FY26: 2W 35.4%, PV 17.5%, CV 12.1%, ADS 14.2% (Q1 FY27 column), tech-agnostic 8.6%, xEV 5.1%. Long-term aim: 60% ICE / 40% non-ICE. [IP Q1FY27 p.12; Q4 call]
- Geography Q1 FY27: India 60.1%, exports from India 32.5%, Sweden subsidiary 7.4%; exports to "other foreign countries" (mostly semiconductor equipment) rose more than 3x; USA exports nearly doubled; international business +71.4% YoY vs India +17.4%. [IP Q1FY27 p.4–5]
- ADS inside the business: FY26 ADS revenue ₹335.9 cr incl. scrap and tooling (product sales ₹315.5 cr, +155%). Aerospace grew 63.3% in FY26; semiconductor grew from near zero (mass production began end-Q2/Q3 FY26); defence small (small batches to ISRO, HAL; some exports to Israel). Customers: Boeing and Airbus supply chains (A350, 737), a large engine maker onboarded in FY26, one large semiconductor-equipment maker (SEM). [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.7]
- Capacity: 17 plants (16 India, 1 Sweden); ADS campus 140,000 sq ft (2/3 aerospace + semicon, 1/3 defence); existing ADS facility "relatively full". About 120 million components made in FY26; 30–40% volume increase expected in next couple of years. [ICRA Jan-26; IP Q1FY27 p.7; Q4 call]
- Associate: MMRFIC Technology (mm-wave radar sub-systems) — ₹20 cr invested 2023–24, ₹30 cr more approved Nov-25 (₹25 cr paid in FY26); stake expected 45–50% after CCPS conversion; Sansera has given a letter of comfort for MMRFIC's bank borrowing. JV: Nichidai Sansera (60:40 with Nichidai, Japan; ~₹50 cr FY27 contribution). [AR FY26 Directors' report, RPT note; Q4 call]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales Q1 FY26→Q1 FY27: 766, 825, 908, 999, 1,021. Operating profit: 131, 142, 162, 193, 196. OPM 17%→19%. Net profit: 63, 71, 69, 123, 87. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +33% YoY; EBITDA ₹196 cr (company: ₹196.1 cr, 19.2% margin, +48%) after absorbing a ₹12.6 cr provision for US import-tariff costs of the prior year; PAT ₹87.4 cr after a ₹16.9 cr exceptional US litigation settlement; adjusted PAT ₹100 cr. Excluding the tariff provision, margin ~20.4% (analyst's arithmetic, not contested). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.9]
- Annual: FY24 sales 2,811 / OP 482 / PAT 188; FY25 3,017 / 528 / 217; FY26 3,498 / 645 / 327. FY26 PAT +51% helped by lower interest (₹39 cr vs ₹72 cr after the ₹1,200 cr QIP) and ₹31 cr forex gain. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Growth: 3-yr sales CAGR 14%, 3-yr profit CAGR 33%; ROCE ~15% (FY26), ROE ~12%. TTM sales ₹3,753 cr, TTM PAT ₹351 cr. [Screener]
- Q4 FY26 PAT ₹123 cr was flattered by other income ₹26.6 cr (₹17.2 cr forex gain). [Q4 call]
- Unit economics: con-rod content per vehicle typically ₹5,000–10,000 per vehicle across products; ADS and export margins 25–30% EBITDA vs 17–18% for domestic auto; ADS incremental asset turn 2.0–2.2x vs auto 1.25–1.3x. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.8]
- Maruti: Sansera supplies "almost 75%" of Maruti's volumes of its components. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (May-26): "similar growth trajectory" in FY27 with H1 stronger than H2 in auto on base effect; ADS FY27 revenue ₹550–600 cr (kept from earlier); capex similar to FY26's ₹510 cr; ~₹250 cr more ADS capex to service the ₹4,460 cr backlog; FY31 vision ₹8,000–9,000 cr revenue ("order book visibility" ₹8,000–8,200 cr by end of decade, maybe ₹10,000 cr); margin "move toward 19%" from 18.1%. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +33%, margin 19.2% — ahead of the margin aim; ADS ₹145 cr in Q1 (run-rate ~₹580 cr, in line with guidance). [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 call: presentation says "high-teens" FY27 revenue growth; on Q&A the CEO raised it to "between high-teens to 20%", with ADS growth of 75–80% and non-ADS "mid-teens". Margin: would like to "achieve and perhaps slightly overachieve" the 19% aim, not giving 20%+. Guidance **raised** (revenue) and **kept** (margin). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.3]
- FY31: ₹8,000–9,000 cr "by the end of the decade, say FY31" — management corrected an analyst who said "2–4 years". ADS aspiration ₹1,300–1,400 cr by ~FY30 (analyst-quoted, not contradicted). Denied reaching ₹1,500 cr ADS revenue in FY28. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Track record: FY26 ADS product sales guidance (₹315 cr) met exactly; FY26 revenue +16%, margin 18.1% (from 17.1%). [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex: FY25 ₹591 cr, FY26 ₹510 cr, Q1 FY27 ₹134 cr; FY27 "similar" to FY26 (~₹500 cr). Q1 FY27 split: plant & machinery 72%; by use: auto PV/CV 45%, auto 2W 18%, non-auto 13%, tech-agnostic/xEV 12%, maintenance 7%, common 5%. [IP Q1FY27 p.8; Q4 call]
- ADS: new 80,000 sq ft hangar in existing campus (commissioning ~Sep–Oct 2026, lines then need customer validation — First Article Inspections); surface-treatment (special process) facility inaugurated, NADCAP approval targeted within Q2; defence moved to a dedicated 45,000 sq ft facility, entry into sheet metal; build-to-suit 100,000–110,000 sq ft facility ~10 months from Aug-26. Board approved buying ~10 acres near Bengaluru airport aerospace park. [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.8]
- Revenue capacity by FY31: existing + hangar ₹1,400–1,500 cr; defence ₹500 cr over 5 years; new facility ₹1,500 cr; total ~₹3,500 cr ADS capacity. Machine lead times 7–9 months (DMG Mori, Makino). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Auto: forging + machining additions at Pantnagar Plant 6 (2W crankshafts, outsourcing from two South-based 2W OEMs), Manesar Plant 4 (80,000 sq ft, PV/2W), Bengaluru Plant 2 multistorey (tech-agnostic/xEV); on stream from Q3 FY27, complete within a year. Auto forging expansions take 18–24 months. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.8]
- Funding: internal accruals plus low leverage; no fundraise planned "at this moment" but "good opportunities" being watched. ICRA expected ₹350–400 cr a year capex funded mainly from accruals. [Q1 call; ICRA Jan-26]
- Payback logic: ADS asset turn 2.0–2.25x on peak revenue at 25–30% EBITDA margin implies pre-tax return on new ADS capital of roughly 35–50% at peak (my arithmetic), but with a 12–24 month lag from machine order to peak revenue.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹591 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹407 cr (Mar-25); cash ₹397 cr (Q4 call) → net debt ~₹195 cr, about 0.3x EBITDA. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Cash flow from operations: FY24 ₹374 cr, FY25 ₹377 cr, FY26 ₹387 cr; free cash flow negative in FY25 (−₹219 cr) and FY26 (−₹123 cr) because of capex. CFO/EBITDA fell to 75% in FY26 (84% FY25). [Screener]
- Working capital: inventory days rose from 126 (FY24) to 169 (FY26); debtor days 65; payable days 136; cash conversion cycle 98 days. CFO said ADS growth built new working capital; semiconductor raw material has long lead times until localised. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Dividend payout ~8–9%; no buyback. QIP of ₹1,200 cr in Q3 FY25 used mainly to repay debt. [Screener; ICRA Jan-26]
- ICRA [ICRA]AA (Stable)/A1+ reaffirmed Jan-26; downgrade trigger total debt/OPBITDA >1.5x sustained; term-loan repayments ₹58.2 cr in FY27 and ₹38.9 cr in FY28. [ICRA Jan-26]

## Moat evidence
- Share: ~75% of Maruti's volumes of its supplied components; single-source status for several components; gaining wallet share with TVS, Yamaha, Suzuki; Bajaj revenue +29.9% in Q4 FY26. [Q1 call; Q4 call; ICRA Jan-26]
- Qualification barriers in ADS: approved on Boeing parts "in record time"; only Indian firm (per management) machining an aero-engine blisk — first full samples due ~Sep-26; machining single-crystal (monocrystalline) parts; parts up to 4 m with ~10 types of five-axis machining. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Backward integration: in-house forging, heat treatment, machine building and automation; new surface-treatment line removes an outside dependency and (once NADCAP-approved) shortens lead times. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Customer commitment: semiconductor customer "blocking machining capacity"; new ~$75 m-a-year SEM order (≈₹1,250 cr over 5 years) from an existing customer; Sansera says it has no exclusivity and is courting the second large SEM. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Pricing power is modest in auto: steel/aluminium pass-through negotiations "in process" and not yet in the P&L; Q1 gross margin gain was mix plus forex. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Litigation: American Axle (AAM) sued over shifter-fork failure; dismissal reversed by the US 4th Circuit; trial had been set for Oct-26 and company had provided only ₹2 cr. Q1 FY27 carried a ₹16.9 cr exceptional settlement "without admission of liability", said to be "substantially covered by insurance". The provision was far below the eventual settlement — a disclosure-quality point. [AR FY26 note 38; IP Q1FY27 p.9; Q1 call]
- Frauds: standalone — former employee forged invoices, ₹1.22 cr misappropriated Jun-23 to May-25 (₹0.79 cr recovered); subsidiary Fitwel Tools & Forgings — ₹9.12 cr fraudulent payments over FY20–FY25, ₹1.9 cr charged in FY26, ₹1.46 cr cash plus ₹2.98 cr property recovered. Both reported by the statutory auditors; small in money but show control gaps. [AR FY26 Board's report on auditor remarks]
- Audit: Deloitte Haskins & Sells, unmodified opinion; key audit matter revenue cut-off; audit trail not enabled 1 Apr 2023–20 May 2024 (earlier period, flagged). [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Contingent liabilities (standalone): indirect-tax ₹9.3 cr, income tax ₹3.7 cr, customer claims ₹9.4 cr, "other matters" ₹1.2 cr (KSPCB pollution demand on Plant 12); corporate guarantee/SBLC for Sansera Sweden up to ~₹70.8 cr (€6.5 m); capital commitments ₹211.9 cr. Disputed tax dues not deposited ~₹10 cr. None material vs net worth ₹3,087 cr. [AR FY26 note 38, CARO]
- Concentration: 2W 43% of revenue and top-5 customers >50% (H1 FY26); auto ICE still 65% of sales; semiconductor revenue from essentially one customer, tied to the AI capex cycle ("do not expect to be able to meet demand until FY29–FY30"). [ICRA Jan-26; Q1 call]
- US exposure: con-rod exports to US OEMs; US plant deferred because of tariff uncertainty; ₹12.6 cr tariff cost provision in Q1. [Q1 call]
- Related parties: routine — CSR to Sansera Foundation ₹5.5 cr; CMD Sekhar Vasan pay ₹5.5 cr; MMRFIC investment ₹25 cr in FY26 plus comfort letter. Promoter holding 29.2% (Jun-26) down from 30.1% (Mar-26) and 35% in 2023; the reason for the Jun-26 dip was not found in the documents read. [AR FY26 RPT note; Screener]
- Board churn: three new independents; two long-serving independents left Jul-26 on tenure completion. [Q4 call]

## My read
- The business is clearly improving: margins have stepped up from 17% to 19% in four quarters on mix, and ADS is scaling with the capacity and approvals to reach ~₹550–600 cr this year and several times that by FY31. Auto ICE, the cash engine, is itself growing ~20% on domestic demand and outsourcing, which reduces the "ICE cliff" worry for now. The weak spots are cash: free cash flow has been negative two years running and inventory days keep climbing, so the ADS build is being funded partly by debt. The stock trades on a trailing P/E around 78 (Screener), which needs roughly 25%+ earnings growth for years; that is plausible only if ADS reaches ₹1,300 cr+ by FY30 at 25–30% margins. What would change my view: a stall in semiconductor-equipment orders (single-customer risk), ADS FY27 missing ₹550 cr, or margins slipping back below 18%.
