# Apollo Micro Systems — deep research (Q1 FY27)

Sources read: stored Q4 FY26 (19 May 2026) and Q1 FY27 (8 Aug 2026) transcripts in full — both are the company's BSE filings; Q1 FY27 investor presentation (company IR API); FY26 annual report (MD&A, standalone auditor's report incl. key audit matters and CARO annexure, board's report on loans, secretarial audit, consolidated notes 34 contingent liabilities, 35 related parties, 36 segment, 37 group structure); CARE rating intimation (27 Aug 2026); Screener consolidated page (raw HTML, 27 Sep 2026); news on the preferential issue and Premier Explosives deal.

## Business and segments
- Apollo designs and builds **mission-critical defence electronics** — onboard computers, guidance and actuation systems for missiles, torpedo homing, fuzes, underwater mines — mostly as a DRDO development-cum-production partner (DcPP) and Tier-1 supplier to DPSUs (BDL, BEL, HAL). Participation claimed in 150+ indigenous programmes and 60+ DcPP programmes as a sub-system partner. [IP Q1FY27 p.2; Q4 call]
- It is trying to become a **weapons OEM**: naval mines (MIGM "Vighna" DcPP partner; moored and limpet mines), aerial bombs (nine variants, 1–25 kg), 500 kg smart-bomb kit (IAF Make-II sanction, Jul 2026), SAVIOR-ASW semi-submersible autonomous vessel (Navy Make-II sanction), rockets, counter-drone. [IP Q1FY27 pp.12–19; Q1 call]
- **Explosives**: 100% of **IDL Explosives** bought via 76%-owned Apollo Defence Industries (completed 15–17 Nov 2025) — an industrial (mining) explosives maker supplying Coal India; IDL revenue "a little less than ₹380 cr" in FY26. **Premier Explosives**: SPA signed 9 Jul 2026 for 41.33% (2.22 cr shares at ₹698 = **₹1,550 cr**), plus open offer for up to 26% (1.40 cr shares at ₹698, ~₹977 cr); CCI cleared 22 Sep 2026; consolidation targeted Q3 FY27. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call; News: Sahi, Sep-2026]
- One reportable segment ("electromechanical components and systems"); the consolidated note puts all ₹904 cr under "electronic components" and "domestic" even though IDL's explosives are included — **no explosives split is disclosed**. [AR FY26 note 36]
- Customers: no customer >10% of FY26 revenue (one customer was 20% in FY25). **Export revenue nil** (first export order received but not yet billed). [AR FY26 note 36; Q1 call]
- Group holdings: Ananya SIP RF Technologies 51%, Apollo Defence Industries 76%, Apollo Strategic Technologies 51% (step-down), IDL 100% of ADIPL (≈76% effective), Shauryastra Defence Systems 34% associate. [AR FY26 note 37; AGM notice]
- Capacity: two plants in Hyderabad (Mallapur, Adibatla); new **3.5 lakh sq ft Unit 3** at Hardware Park-II — phase 1 production started; full operation "before March '27". [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (19 May 2026):** "continue to grow at a similar rate" in FY27 and FY28 (FY26 was +61% consolidated); large-ticket orders "anytime in this financial year"; MIGM DAC approval "any moment"; promoter pledge to be cleared "this financial year" (FY27); an RF-company acquisition news "before end of this quarter"; IDL guidance from Q3. Admitted FY26 standalone growth of 36% missed the earlier 45–50% organic guide ("we couldn't meet that… 8%, 9%… got missed") because customer approvals were pending. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 call (8 Aug 2026):** **40–45% growth for FY27, "console as well as standalone"**; exports excluded; **no EBITDA guidance** ("we are not giving EBITDA guidance anytime"); order book to reach **₹3,500–4,000 cr by FY27-end** on one single order of ₹2,500–3,000 cr; MIGM DAC approved 3 Jul 2026, enquiry expected next month, **PO by Dec/Jan**, Apollo expects ~70% of a ~₹3,500 cr budget with 20% advance, 1,000 mines over 3 years; QRSAM (BDL ₹11,000–12,000 cr, ~₹1 cr+ Apollo content per missile, 1,000 missiles) and Akash-NG similar; Pinaka 2,000 rockets at ~₹85 lakh content each; execution of these from FY28. Pledge now "another 1 year" → **Q1 FY28**. 2–3 more acquisitions planned. [Q1 call]
- **Track record vs Q4 promises:** Q1 standalone growth 16.7% is well below the 40–45% guide (consolidated +88% is acquisition-led). MIGM DAC came (met), but PO slipped to Dec/Jan. Pledge target **pushed back a year**. RF acquisition was **dropped in favour of Premier** (not mentioned). Verdict: **guidance kept, delivery behind on organic growth**; back-ended to H2 and FY28. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Standalone CWIP **₹170.6 cr** at Mar-26 (civil works ₹147.2 cr; test tools and plant ₹23.4 cr); auditor flagged ₹75.1 cr of costs where capitalisation needed judgement (key audit matter). Consolidated fixed assets ₹477 cr + CWIP ₹196 cr (from ₹182 cr + ₹72 cr a year earlier). Capital commitments ₹21.3 cr for the Shamshabad factory. [AR FY26 KAM 2, note 34; Screener]
- FY26 investing outflow ₹357 cr (capex plus IDL purchase); FCF −₹357 cr. [Screener]
- IDL: licences for TNT/RDX; small initial TNT (500 t) and HMX (50 t) capacities; "very large expansion" for defence explosives with DPRs in preparation; new component plants planned; plan to be announced after Premier closes. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Funding: **preferential issue of ₹3,322.23 cr at ₹416.60** — 2.28 cr equity shares to 55 non-promoter investors plus 5.69 cr convertible warrants to 93 investors including the promoter group (EGM 4 Aug 2026). Use: ~₹2,500 cr acquisition (Premier), ~₹500 cr debt/working capital, rest general purposes. Warrants are normally paid 25% up front, so not all cash is in hand yet. [News: ScanX, Aug-2026; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Consolidated borrowings **₹543 cr** (Mar-26) vs ₹335 cr (Mar-25) and ₹208 cr (Mar-24); standalone ₹485 cr; standalone cash ₹20 cr. Interest ₹47 cr in FY26. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Operating cash flow negative: −₹130 cr FY26, −₹76 cr FY24, +₹10 cr FY25**; five-year cumulative FCF is deeply negative. [Screener]
- Screener FY26: debtor days 194, inventory days 478, working-capital days 256; company says its WC cycle fell from 626 days (FY21) to 359 days (FY26). Standalone inventory **₹760 cr = 50.5% of current assets and 33% of total assets** (key audit matter for obsolescence risk). [Screener; IP Q1FY27 p.2; AR FY26 KAM 3]
- Management: inventory will rise with scale; holding periods to improve as in-house test facilities replace outsourced testing. [Q4 call]
- Dividend payout 8% in FY26. Promoter holding **49.98%** (Jun-26) vs 55.12% (Mar-25) after issues; about 39% of promoter shares were pledged at Q4 (reduced in the June quarter, exact figure not given). [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Rating: **CARE A- (Rating Watch with Developing Implications) / A2+ (RWD)** on ₹1,530 cr long-term and ₹350 cr short-term lines, 26 Aug 2026 — the watch reflects the Premier acquisition. Earlier ratings were from Acuité. [CARE intimation 27 Aug 2026]

## Moat evidence
- Qualified content on DRDO missile programmes (QRSAM, Akash-NG, Kusha, Pinaka, BrahMos-NG etc.) — replacing a qualified sub-system supplier mid-programme is rare; the moat is qualification, not price. [IP Q1FY27 p.2; Q1 call]
- MIGM: DcPP production partner; claims to be the only Indian company with shallow, deep-water (moored) and limpet mine offerings; limpet mines under a government import embargo. [IP Q1FY27 pp.12–15]
- Lifetime DPIIT licence covering UAVs, INS, radars, torpedoes, mines, ATGMs, bombs, rockets, missiles, loitering munitions; ToT for laser DEW and electro-optical tracking. [Q4 call]
- Pricing power evidence is thin: consolidated OPM 20–31% range and falling with the explosives mix; standalone margins improved (EBITDA margin ~30% FY26). [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]

## Risks and red flags
- **Governance / related parties:** standalone gave **₹297.7 cr of loans to subsidiaries in FY26**, ₹315.6 cr outstanding (IDL ₹187.6 cr, ADIPL ₹124.3 cr), repayable on demand at 9.9% with repayment terms "not stipulated"; CARO notes "some inadequate documentation"; a ₹7 cr loan to an outside company has ₹52 lakh interest overdue (₹59 lakh provided). CARO also says the company raised share capital "to meet the obligations of its subsidiaries". RPTs are a key audit matter (loans, sale and leasing of properties to subsidiaries). [AR FY26 CARO iii, ix(e); board's report; KAM 4]
- **Goodwill ₹145.2 cr** on IDL (net assets acquired were negative), provisional; purchase-price allocation still open (key audit matter). [AR FY26 KAM 5]
- **Contingent claims jumped to ₹63.6 cr from ₹3.2 cr**; bank guarantees/counter-guarantees ₹48.3 cr. [AR FY26 note 34]
- Promoter-linked entity Apollo Food & Beverages took and repaid a ₹4.5 cr "advance for supplies". [AR FY26 note 35]
- **Promoter pledge** (~39%) deadline slipped from FY27 to Q1 FY28. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Compliance slips: NSE penalty ₹88,500 for not submitting the audit report with results; ROC compounding for late cost-audit filing; CSR spent ₹0.75 cr vs ₹1.05 cr obligation. MD&A contains arithmetic errors (interest "increased by 8.67%" from ₹37.1 cr to ₹34.1 cr). [AR FY26 secretarial audit; CARO xx; MD&A]
- **Negative cash generation** and rising debt while growth is increasingly acquisition-led; dilution from a ₹3,322 cr issue (~8 cr new shares/warrants on ~36 cr). [Screener; News]
- Integration risk: IDL still barely profitable (PAT-positive in Q1 on thin margins); Premier adds a listed explosives company of similar scale. Order timing risk: MIGM, QRSAM, Pinaka all still pending POs. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- From "Vision 2036"/global OEM narrative and an unnamed RF acquisition to a concrete ₹1,550 cr + open-offer Premier deal funded by a ₹3,322 cr equity raise. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New numbers: year-end order-book target ₹3,500–4,000 cr; MIGM budget ₹3,500 cr with 70% share expected; QRSAM/Akash-NG/Pinaka content per unit. [Q1 call]
- Guidance became explicit (40–45%) but EBITDA guidance was refused; pledge timeline slipped. [Q1 call]
- IDL moved from EBITDA-positive (Q4 FY26) to PAT-positive (Q1 FY27). [Q1 call]

## My read
Apollo's electronics franchise is real and profitable at the standalone level (PAT margin ~16–18%), and the order pipeline (MIGM, QRSAM, Akash-NG, Pinaka) could step it up from FY28. But the headline growth is now mostly bought: Q1 organic growth was 17% against a 40–45% guide, cash flow is negative, debt has more than doubled in two years, and the company is layering two explosives acquisitions on top. The price needs the big POs to land by Q4 FY27 and FY28 to show 40%+ growth with positive operating cash flow. The view improves on a signed MIGM/QRSAM order and the pledge actually falling to zero; it worsens if standalone growth stays below 25% or subsidiary loans keep growing.
