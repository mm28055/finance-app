# Digilogic Systems (BSE SME: 544684) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Inputs read: stored file (one transcript — the maiden FY26 earnings call of 1 Jun 2026, the official BSE copy); FY26 results presentation (Jun 2026) and IPO-time presentation (Jan 2026); Annual Report FY26 (MD&A, auditor's report, IPO-fund utilisation, related-party form AOC-2, AGM notice); Screener (standalone; the company reports half-yearly as an SME). There is no current credit rating (India Ratings' last action was Dec 2020). Listed on BSE SME on 28 Jan 2026 after a ₹81 cr IPO (₹69.66 cr fresh issue at ₹104). H1 FY27 results are not yet due.

## Business and segments
- Hyderabad-based maker of Test, Measurement and Simulation (TMS) systems for defence: automated test equipment (ATE), missile/aircraft checkout systems, measurement and deployable spectrum-monitoring systems, simulators and satellite-payload checkout. Mostly integrates imported US/European instruments with its own software. [FY26 call; IP FY26]
- FY26 revenue split: measurement equipment 49%, checkout systems 22%, ATE 17% (ATE + checkout was 44% in FY25); these three are 88% of revenue. [FY26 call; IP FY26]
- Customers: DRDO labs and defence PSUs (BEL, BDL); "it is 100% B2G" in substance, with ~15% billed through private primes. Exports are indirect — its testers travel with missiles that BDL exports (Philippines, Malaysia, Cambodia mentioned). [FY26 call]
- Model: compete at prototype stage (vs Data Patterns, Zen Technologies), then get "nominated" as supplier in Design-cum-Production Programmes (DCPP) — part of "five to six" / "four critical" DCPPs (the two executives gave different counts). Revenue ~30% prototype, ~70% production. Testers are ~4–6% of a missile programme's budget. [FY26 call]
- Capacity: current Hyderabad setup can support ~₹150–160 cr revenue. New 65,000 sq ft "Project Udaan" plant (electronics manufacturing, environmental stress screening, ATE/checkout production) — roughly four times current build capacity. New subsidiary Abhedhya Systems (RF/microwave subsystems for radar and EW) set up 23 May 2026; revenue from FY28. [FY26 call; AR FY26 MD&A; IP FY26]

## Guidance history
- IPO presentation (Jan 2026): FY26 "on track to achieve a 20 to 25% growth in Revenue, 30 to 35% growth in EBITDA ... 40 to 45% in PAT"; order book ~₹46 cr and bids of ~₹200 cr. [IP Jan-26]
- Delivered FY26: revenue +8.4%, EBITDA +16.5%, PAT +33.8%. **Missed revenue badly, two months after listing.** Reason given: import lead times for US/European hardware doubled from ~2 to ~4 months due to the Iran conflict; ~₹22 cr of orders spilled into April. [FY26 call]
- FY26 call (Jun 2026): FY27 revenue growth "25%–30%", EBITDA and PAT growth "45%–50%" (presentation); in the Q&A the analyst quoted "20%, 25%" and the CMD later said "25% to 30%". Order book ₹31 cr at 30 May (down from ₹46 cr); tenders quoted ~₹110 cr (down from ~₹200 cr); target "by September I should have purchase orders in hand close to INR100-plus crores". [IP FY26; FY26 call]
- The CMD said ₹200 cr of opportunities are "delayed by ... 6 to 8 months" due to changed government fund allocation, not lost. [FY26 call]
- Checkpoint: the "₹100 cr+ POs by end-September" promise falls due now; no order announcement confirming it was found. H1 FY27 results (Oct–Nov 2026) are the test. **Open.**
- Project Udaan: presentation timeline — building Apr 2026–May 2027, machinery erection Jun–Jul 2027, trial run Oct–Nov 2027, production Dec 2027; on the call the CMD said "by mid of 2028". Udaan revenue projection: ₹11–13 cr (FY29), ₹28–30 cr (FY30), ₹57–61 cr (FY31). [IP FY26; FY26 call]

## Moat evidence
- Nomination in DCPPs: once a tester is validated (MSQA and similar agencies), follow-on production orders come "on a nomination basis ... on a part number". Company says DRDO has certified it as a direct supply-chain member on some programmes. [FY26 call]
- Delivered four checkout systems in FY26 for critical programmes; order for 250 deployable spectrum-monitoring units from a defence PSU. [FY26 call]
- AS9100D and ISO 9001:2015; MoUs with Sperry and Eirad for aerospace supply chains. [AR FY26; FY26 call]
- Weaknesses: depends on imported western hardware (the FY26 miss proves it); small scale; prototype-stage competition with much larger Data Patterns and Zen. In-house single-board computer is still at functional-demo stage. [FY26 call]

## My read
Digilogic is a tiny, profitable niche supplier (₹77 cr revenue, 20% margin) whose best feature is sole-source nomination on missile and aircraft test systems once a programme enters production. Its first report card as a listed company was poor: it missed IPO-time growth guidance by a wide margin, the order book and bid pipeline shrank, and within months the board voted the founding family a large pay rise. The FY27 target of 25–30% growth needs ~₹100 cr of orders by September and a normal import cycle; H1 FY27 results will show whether that happened. Until orders and cash collection prove out, I would treat the growth story as unproven and watch governance closely.
