## Business and segments
- Designs and makes defence communication electronics: satellite-communication (SATCOM) terminals and hub/ground stations (S-, C-, Ku-, Ka-band), UHF/HF radios and software-defined radios (SDRs), wind-profiler radars, the Real-Time Train Information System (RTIS) for Indian Railways, fishing-boat satellite transponders for NSIL, plus build-to-print MSS units for Lockheed Martin and Boeing. [AGM 2026; CARE Apr-26]
- Customers: Indian Navy, Coast Guard, shipyards (Mazagon, Cochin, Goa, GRSE), BEL, DRDO, NSIL, IMD, Indian Railways (via L&T), Lockheed Martin, Boeing. Top 10 customers are ~93% of the order book. [CARE Apr-26]
- Reported as one segment ("Electronics & Communication"); no product or customer split is audited. Exports only 6.48% of turnover in FY26. [AR FY26 note 42; AR FY26 BRSR]
- Mix: in FY25 "70 to 80%" of revenue came from proprietary defence products with no competition; FY26 shifted to lower-margin civilian work (NSIL fishing transponders — 27,000 units plus 5,000 more). [AGM 2026]
- Plants: Visakhapatnam (production), E-City Hyderabad (~100,000 sq ft, mostly R&D, 180 R&D engineers), new Unit 3 at Kondaparva village near Vijayawada (9 acres, ~1,13,660 sq ft) for satellite ground stations, "operational by Q3 of FY 2026-27". [AGM 2026; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Subsidiary: Imeds Global Pvt Ltd (medical devices, Visakhapatnam) — FY26 turnover ₹1.6 cr, loss ₹7.5 cr. [AR FY26 AOC-1]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- FY24 → FY25 → FY26 revenue 224 → 249 → 223 (–10%); operating profit 82 → 92 → 48; margin 36% → 37% → 22%; net profit 53 → 56 → 15. TTM revenue 241, net profit 17. [Screener]
- Last five quarters (revenue / operating margin / net profit): Q1 FY26 51.9 / 20.1% / 3.2; Q2 55.4 / 20.4% / 4.3; Q3 51.7 / 24.2% / 2.7; Q4 63.8 / 21.3% / 4.8; Q1 FY27 70.4 / 24.8% / 5.4. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 revenue +36% YoY — the first real growth quarter in over a year. [Screener]
- Why profit fell in FY26 (CMD's bridge): R&D up ₹7 cr, depreciation up ₹7 cr (new facilities), product mix ~₹9 cr, factory overheads ~₹5 cr, low-margin civilian manufacturing ~₹5 cr, finance cost up ₹2.6 cr. Standalone PBT fell from ₹82.7 cr to ₹32.2 cr. Depreciation rose from ₹12 cr (FY25) to ₹20 cr (FY26) and ₹7.1 cr in Q1 FY27 alone. [AGM 2026; AR FY26 MD&A; Screener]
- Tax rate is high and erratic (39% FY26; 53% in Q3), because Imeds' loss gets no tax credit in consolidation. Standalone total comprehensive income ₹22.3 cr vs consolidated ₹14.8 cr — the gap is Imeds. [Screener; AR FY26 Directors' report]
- Unit economics: not disclosed; CMD refuses product margins ("not in the interest of the company"). SDR market "₹3,000 crores per annum minimum", target 20% share. [AGM 2026]

## Guidance history
- AGM 2025 (Jun-25): "in the next two years on the conservative side, don't expect any growth"; FY26 and FY27 would be "more stable", steep growth from FY28. Order book ₹210 cr then; promised ≥₹200 cr of new orders within nine months. [AGM 2025]
- Delivered by AGM 2026: revenue fell 10% in FY26 (consistent with the warning); orders far exceeded promise — CARE counts ₹861 cr order book on 25 Mar 2026 (₹192.7 cr on 31 Jul 2025). **Orders beat; revenue as warned.** [CARE Apr-26; Screener]
- CARE was told FY26 revenue ~₹230 cr, margin ~20.7%, PAT margin ~10% — delivered ₹223 cr, 22% operating margin, consolidated PAT margin ~7% (standalone ~10%). **Roughly met.** [CARE Apr-26; Screener]
- AGM 2026 (Jun-26) — guidance raised sharply: FY27 ~₹300 cr, then ₹375–400 cr (FY28) and ~₹500 cr (FY29), ~25% CAGR "three years ensured and five years very likely"; ₹1,000 cr turnover by 2030. Order book "about ₹1,000 crores" including railways RTIS (₹460 cr contract, of which ₹160 cr is 5-year maintenance), NSIL ground stations with Safran (9 stations over 18 months), IMD wind-profiler radars and a ~₹100 cr DEAL hub order at price-negotiation stage. No margin guidance: "I cannot give guidance on that". [AGM 2026]
- The annual report (same period) is more cautious: "order book of approximately ₹720 Crores to be executed across ... FY 2026-27 and FY 2027-28". So ₹1,000 cr includes orders not yet signed. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: ₹70.4 cr. To reach ₹300 cr, the next three quarters must average ~₹77 cr — above any quarter in the company's history (best: ₹77.4 cr in Q2 FY25). **Early — pace needs to lift in H2.** [Screener]
- CARE also expects "moderation in profitability margins in the next two years" because most new orders are commercial products with lower margins — this conflicts with the CMD's promise that EPS and ROCE "would only improve year on year". [CARE Apr-26; AGM 2026]
- Imeds: AGM 2025 said revenue "4-5 crores this year, afterwards it could be 30 crores". FY26 turnover ₹1.6 cr, loss ₹7.5 cr; licences still pending at AGM 2026. **Missed.** [AGM 2025; AR FY26 AOC-1]
- Vijayawada unit: AGM 2026 said inauguration "first week of September"; AR says operational by Q3 FY27. [AGM 2026; AR FY26]

## Capex and capacity
- Rights issue of 2,02,26,100 shares (~₹81 cr, allotted May 2025) funded Vijayawada, infrastructure and general purposes; CARE-monitored and "fully utilised". [AR FY26; CARE Apr-26; AGM 2026]
- Capex spend: investing outflow ₹57 cr (FY25) and ₹93 cr (FY26); fixed assets up from ₹48 cr (FY24) to ₹181 cr (FY26). Asset turn fell from ~4.7x to ~1.2x — returns now depend on filling the new plants. [Screener]
- AGM 2025 bridge: ~₹14 cr corporate office, ~₹30 cr E-City (₹22 cr fixed assets, ~₹8 cr product development), ₹10 cr Vijayawada at that time. [AGM 2025]
- Capacity: CMD says the same infrastructure "can make ₹1,000 crores ... even ₹1,500 crores" — no further large capex implied; payback not disclosed. [AGM 2026]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹34 cr at Mar-26 (₹26 cr Mar-25) against net worth ₹338 cr — gearing ~0.1x. No term debt; working-capital lines enhanced (CC ₹94 cr, corporate loan ₹25 cr; fund + non-fund limits ~₹250 cr sanctioned vs ₹350 cr board approval). [Screener; CARE Apr-26; AGM 2026]
- Cash flow: operating cash flow ₹65 cr (FY24), ₹50 cr (FY25), only ₹10 cr (FY26); free cash flow –₹83 cr in FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital is the weak spot: Screener inventory days 521 and debtor days 117 in FY26 (cash conversion cycle 627 days). Receivables rose from ₹71.1 cr (Mar-25) to ₹102.6 cr (Dec-25), including ~₹43 cr overdue from NSIL; ~₹15 cr older than six months. CMD: "nothing is going to be a bad debt". [Screener; CARE Apr-26; AGM 2026]
- Bank guarantees ₹60.8 cr at Mar-26 (₹39.4 cr) — rising with advances taken against 100% guarantees. [AR FY26 note 41]
- Dividend payout 35% of FY26 profit (token in value); ESOPs of up to 45 lakh shares proposed. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]

## Moat evidence
- Proprietary-article-certified (PAC) products: many Navy SATCOM terminals are sole-source because Avantel owns the IP; CMD says a rival would need "about 4 or 5 years" to copy. [AGM 2026]
- Positioning claimed: S-band satellite terminals No. 1; SDRs "top 3 for sure"; other SATCOM bands top 5. [AGM 2026]
- Wins: NSIL fishing-transponder programme — "out of the 3 companies, we are the only company that completed all the requirements in time", earning 5,000 extra units. DRDO ground-segment hub, ₹117.9 cr (29 Aug 2026, execution to Feb 2029). [AGM 2026; News: Trade Brains, Aug 2026]
- Limits of the moat: on competitive tenders Avantel "matched" L1 prices of rivals (fishing project), so pricing power exists only on proprietary products; semiconductor parts are all imported. [AGM 2026]
- Five iDEX projects (3 Army, 2 Navy) ~90% developed and entering trials — potential future sole-source orders. [AGM 2026]

## Risks and red flags
- Imeds drain: Avantel has put ₹40.1 cr of equity plus ₹3 cr of convertible debentures into Imeds (₹10.1 cr equity + ₹3 cr CCD in FY26 alone); Imeds' accumulated loss is ₹18.3 cr and FY26 loss ₹7.5 cr on ₹1.6 cr sales. Board is exploring a strategic investor taking majority control. [AR FY26 AOC-1, note 38; AGM 2026]
- Family control: CMD, his wife (Whole-time Director and CFO) and son (Whole-time Director, 8.47% holder) are all executives — the CFO is not independent of the promoter. [AR FY26]
- Related-party dealings: CMD lent the company ₹12 cr unsecured during FY26 (interest ₹48.6 lakh); commission on profits paid to promoter-directors; CSR of ₹1.21 cr and donations of ₹0.61 cr to Lakshmee Foundation (hospital in Vijayawada); rent and marketing services with Wiki Kids Pvt Ltd (promoter-linked). Small amounts, but a pattern. [AR FY26 note 38]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; one Key Audit Matter — valuation of trade receivables (mostly government dues, no provision made). CARE notes the risk is timing, not credit. [AR FY26 auditor's report; CARE Apr-26]
- Promoter holding fell from 40.1% (Sep-23) to 37.04% (Jun-26) through ESOP/rights dilution and sales. [Screener]
- Disclosure: no earnings calls, no presentation, no segment or margin data; the order book number differs across documents (₹720 cr AR, ₹861 cr CARE, ₹1,000 cr AGM). [AR FY26; CARE Apr-26; AGM 2026]
- Satellite ground-station growth depends on GEO orbit slots and frequency allocation, which the CMD says is "bothering us". [AGM 2026]

## What changed between the two calls (AGM 2025 → AGM 2026)
- Tone flipped from "don't expect any growth" for two years to "25% CAGR ... three years ensured". Order book went from ₹210 cr to ~₹1,000 cr (per CMD). FY26 revenue fell 10% and net profit fell ~73%, explained as deliberate R&D and depreciation. New themes: energy-sector diversification, bidding for ₹2,000–3,000 cr orders (needs higher turnover to qualify), Imeds sell-down. Receivable delays (NSIL) became an issue.

## My read
Avantel's FY26 was a genuine reset: revenue fell, margin dropped from 37% to 22% and profit fell to ₹15 cr, partly by choice (R&D, new plants) and partly because lower-margin civilian work replaced proprietary defence sales. The order book has since jumped several-fold and Q1 FY27 revenue rose 36%, so the ₹300 cr FY27 target is plausible but needs a record H2. The catch is margins: CARE expects them to stay moderate as new orders are commercial, while the stock price assumes a return to high profits. Watch: quarterly revenue crossing ₹80 cr, margin holding above 24%, receivables and inventory falling, and the Imeds sell-down — a clean exit would remove a ₹7–8 cr annual drag.
