# Rossell Techsys (NSE: ROSSTECH) — deep research, 28 Sep 2026

Inputs read: stored file (Q4 FY26 transcript = official BSE copy; Q3 FY26 transcript = Tijori summary, compared with the official BSE transcript), Q1 FY27 investor presentation (28 Jul 2026), Annual Report FY26 (auditor's reports, EoM, borrowings, related parties, contingent liabilities, segment note, MD&A), India Ratings rationale (Nov 2025), Screener consolidated page. No Q1 FY27 earnings-call transcript is listed on Screener/BSE as of 28 Sep 2026 — only the presentation — so "last two calls" below are Q3 FY26 (4 Feb 2026) and Q4 FY26 (12 May 2026), with Q1 FY27 delivery taken from the presentation and Screener.

## Business and segments
- Makes electrical wiring interconnect systems (EWIS — wire harnesses), electrical panel assemblies, electronic assemblies and automatic test equipment, mostly "build-to-print" (customer owns the design). Customers: Boeing, Lockheed Martin, Honeywell, BAE and other US/European OEMs; a US LEO-satellite customer (named on the Q4 call as Amazon); a global semiconductor-equipment maker; a second "leading global semiconductor OEM" onboarded in Q1 FY27 with execution from Q2 FY27. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Reported as one segment ("Engineering and Manufacturing in Aerospace and Defense") — no audited split between A&D, space and semiconductor. [AR FY26 note 1(y)]
- Split per management: A&D ~93% of FY25 revenue; space + semiconductor "approximately 20%" of FY26 on the Q4 call, but the Q3 call said FY26 would be "70% aerospace and defence and 30%" non-A&D — the two calls don't agree. Target for FY27 is 50:50. [Ind-Ra Nov-25; Q3 call; Q4 call]
- Geography: ~98% exports, ~80% North America; US subsidiary Rossell Techsys Inc. (RTI, Tempe, Arizona, ~4,100 sq ft) got qualification audits, an LoI from an international defence company and a satellite-communications development project in Q1 FY27. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Customer concentration: top five customers ~91% of FY25 revenue; Boeing ~41% FY25, ~40% 1H FY26 (down from ~90% in FY22). [Ind-Ra Nov-25; Q3 call]
- Capacity: owned Unit I in Devanahalli, Bengaluru (~240,000 sq ft, A&D); leased Unit IA (~210,000 sq ft) for space and semiconductor, "expected to become operational during H2 FY27". Plant ran ~1.5 shifts at Q3. Headcount 1,281 at Q1 FY27 (1,177 at Dec-25), with 2,000 planned by FY27 and 2,500 by FY29. [IP Q1FY27; Q3 call]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- FY24 → FY25 → FY26 revenue 217 → 260 → 485 (+87% in FY26); operating profit 32 → 36 → 63; margin 15% → 14% → 13%; net profit 11 → 8 → 22. TTM revenue 552, net profit 26. [Screener]
- Last five quarters (revenue / operating margin / net profit): Q1 FY26 87.2 / 12.6% / 3.3; Q2 125.9 / 12.5% / 5.7; Q3 129.9 / 13.3% / 5.4; Q4 142.1 / 11.3% / 7.5; Q1 FY27 154.5 / 14.4% / 7.1. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 per company: revenue ₹154.7 cr (+78% YoY), EBITDA ₹23.3 cr (+95%), PBT ₹9.6 cr (+139%), EPS ₹1.85. [IP Q1FY27]
- Interest is eating the growth: finance cost rose from ₹4.8 cr (Q1 FY26) to ₹9.8 cr (Q1 FY27) — about 44% of operating profit. FY26 interest ₹24 cr vs ₹17 cr in FY25. [Screener]
- Unit economics: raw materials are 50–60% of revenue, ~80% imported; gross margin swung 45–55% over FY22–FY25. Employee cost ~15.5% of revenue in FY26. Space and semiconductor have higher material content than defence. [Ind-Ra Nov-25; Q4 call; Q3 call]
- Management's "profit ~₹9.5 cr" for Q4 is PBT, not net profit (₹7.5 cr). [Q4 call; Screener]

## Guidance history
- FY26 margin: at the start of FY26 management guided 17–18% margins (quoted back to them on the Q3 call), and told India Ratings to expect 15.5–16.5% EBITDA margin for FY26. Delivered: 13% (Screener). **Missed.** [Q3 call; Ind-Ra Nov-25; Screener]
- FY26 revenue: Q3 call said Q4 would beat Q3 and the company would deliver ₹1,300 cr over FY26+FY27 combined. FY26 came in at ₹485 cr (Q4 ₹142 cr > Q3 ₹130 cr). **Delivered.** [Q3 call; Screener]
- FY27 revenue: Q4 call — "targeting the same kind of growth this year", i.e. ~80–90% (≈₹870–920 cr); ₹1,300 cr over two years implies ≈₹815 cr. Q1 FY27 grew 78%. **On track, slightly below the 87% run-rate.** [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- FY27 margin: "definitely going to show an increased margin this financial year"; long-run 17–22% on current competencies, 21–30% after the "phase two" move into electromechanical integrated systems in FY28–29. Q1 FY27 operating margin 14.4% vs 13% in FY26 — **improving but far from 17%**. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Space + semiconductor revenue to grow "300% to 400%" in FY27; up to US$200m semiconductor revenue in 3–5 years. [Q4 call]
- Loss-making Boeing programme: ~₹75 cr executed in FY26; ~₹70 cr left in FY27, "high in the first 2 quarters". Management had told Ind-Ra its share would not exceed 10% of revenue. This is one reason H1 FY27 margins stay below target. [Q4 call; Ind-Ra Nov-25]
- QIP of up to ₹300 cr: Q2 call said close by Dec 2025; Ind-Ra was told "by end-FY26"; Q3 call "sooner rather than later"; Q4 call no date (7–10% dilution); Q1 FY27 presentation still "proposed QIP of up to 300 Cr". **Slipped three times.** [Q3 call; Ind-Ra Nov-25; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- New leased facility: Q3 call "taking this building on lease on April 1, 2026"; Q1 FY27 presentation — "operational during H2 FY27". **Slipped ~2 quarters.** [Q3 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Inventory: 10 months (Mar-25) → 7.5 months (Dec-25) → 7.67 months (Mar-26); target 4 months within 18–24 months. [Q3 call; Q4 call]
- Commercial aerospace: RFPs "by the second quarter of this financial year" (Q2 FY27) — still to be confirmed. T-7 (Boeing trainer) programme with ~US$200m strategic agreements to ramp from late FY27. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Company told Ind-Ra it would spend ~₹35 cr capex in FY26 and ~₹67 cr in FY27, funded by debt plus internal accruals, with QIP money partly used for capex and debt repayment. Screener shows FY26 investing outflow of ₹23 cr — below plan, as the company leased rather than built. [Ind-Ra Nov-25; Screener]
- Contracted capital commitments are tiny: ₹8.1 lakh at Mar-26 vs ₹90.6 lakh a year earlier; contingent liabilities are only bank guarantees of ₹1.08 cr (₹1.50 cr prior year). So no large contracted capex yet. [AR FY26 note 30]
- The leased unit "is expected to deliver revenue levels comparable to our existing facility" — i.e., capacity roughly doubles without heavy capex; the real capital need is working capital. [Q4 call]
- Payback: not disclosed. Asset turns are high (FY26 revenue ₹485 cr on fixed assets ₹124 cr ≈ 3.9x), so returns depend on working capital, not plant. [Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings rose from ₹168 cr (FY24) to ₹240 cr (FY25) and ₹409 cr (FY26), all short-term, repayable-on-demand bank lines (cash credit, packing credit, demand loans). Equity + reserves only ₹155 cr — debt about 2.6x net worth; the standalone debt-equity ratio in the annual report is 2.84x (1.85x a year earlier). [Screener; AR FY26 notes 13, 35]
- Cash from operations –₹41 cr FY25 and –₹83 cr FY26; free cash flow –₹55 cr and –₹105 cr. Cash flow has been negative every year the business has grown. On the Q4 call the CFO would only say "in the coming years you should be able to see much improvisation". [Screener; Q4 call]
- Inventory: ₹290 cr at Dec-25, of which ₹257 cr raw material; Screener inventory days 566 (FY25) → 380 (FY26). Debtor days 88. [Q3 call; Screener]
- Liquidity: Ind-Ra noted 89.2% average use of fund-based limits (12 months to Sep-25), ₹4.8 cr cash at 1H FY26, net leverage 4.8x (6.7x FY25), interest cover 2.8x. A further ₹75 cr working-capital line was added in Q1 FY27. [Ind-Ra Nov-25; IP Q1FY27]
- Dividends: token (payout 5% of FY26 profit). No buyback. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Qualification barriers: AS9100D, NADCAP (40 processes), ITAR registration, AS9110 (MRO) and a DPL (defence production licence); CMMC 2.0 in progress, required for US defence work. Qualification takes 12–24 months per programme, so incumbents are sticky. [IP Q1FY27; Q3 call]
- Customer endorsement: Boeing "agreed to level load future schedules"; Lockheed "Outstanding Supplier"; Boeing supplier-of-the-year 2015 and 2019; BAE special recognition 2025. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Order book ₹715 cr (May-26) → ₹800 cr (30 Jun 2026) plus ~₹3,000 cr of "strategic agreements" (long-term supply frameworks, not firm orders). Orders won FY26 ~₹570 cr; Q1 FY27 ₹240 cr of new POs and ₹350 cr of bids. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Limits: build-to-print means little pricing power; long-term fixed-price contracts left the company holding raw-material inflation on one Boeing programme. New contracts carry equitable-price-adjustment clauses — "some customers agree to it, some don't". [Q4 call; Ind-Ra Nov-25]

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor's emphasis of matter (FY26, standalone and consolidated):** after the demerger, supply agreements with some customers are still in the name of Rossell India Ltd (the promoter's tea company) and those "supplies ... have been routed through the demerged entity". Opinion unmodified, but part of revenue legally still flows through a related entity 18 months after the demerger. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Related-party security:** the bank loans are secured partly by mortgage of the Dikom, Nagrijuli and Romai tea estates of Rossell India Ltd and hypothecation of Rossell India's movables. Title deeds of some transferred property are still in Rossell India's name. The related-party note lists only the US subsidiary and key managers — it does not show the Rossell India routing or security as transactions. A disclosure gap worth asking about. [AR FY26 notes 13, 34/35, PPE note]
- Boilerplate copied from the tea company: the credit-risk note still says exports go to "Boeing, Lockheed Martin, Taylors of Harrogate" (a UK tea buyer). A small but telling sign of careless reporting. [AR FY26 financial-risk note]
- Funding: growth needs working capital; QIP repeatedly delayed; interest cost doubled YoY in Q1. If the QIP slips again, bank limits (already ~89% used in 2025) are the only buffer. [Ind-Ra Nov-25; Screener]
- Concentration: top five ~91%; Boeing ~40%; ~80% US; ~80% of raw materials imported (currency risk). [Ind-Ra Nov-25; Q3 call]
- Executive pay: MD Rishab Mohan Gupta's salary ₹3.0 cr in FY26 (same as FY25), about 14% of FY26 net profit. [AR FY26 note 34]
- Institutional holding fell: DIIs 3.51% (Mar-26) → 0.78% (Jun-26); promoters steady at 74.8%. [Screener]
- Disclosure quality: segment split not audited; call answers on the space/semicon share conflict between calls; no Q1 FY27 call held/published by late September. [AR FY26; Q3/Q4 calls]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q3 (Feb): tone was "record quarter", QIP "underway", lease from 1 April, order book ">₹750 cr", strategic pipeline ">₹2,500 cr" over 3–5 years, FY26 mix 70:30.
- Q4 (May): order book ₹715 cr (lower), strategic agreements up to ~₹3,000 cr, mix restated as ~80:20, new themes — commercial aerospace (RFPs by Q2 FY27), MRO licence, "phase two" integrated systems from FY28–29, US$200m semiconductor ambition. Margin fell to 11.3% in Q4 against a promise of Q4 improvement made on the Q3 call; management again blamed first-article (qualification) costs. QIP described with no date.
- Q1 FY27 presentation (Jul): margin recovered to 14.4%, order book ₹800 cr, second semiconductor OEM onboarded, facility pushed to H2 FY27, QIP still pending.

## My read
Rossell is growing fast for real — revenue nearly doubled in FY26 and grew 78% in Q1 FY27 — and has earned sticky places on US defence, satellite and chip-equipment programmes. But the earnings and cash quality lag the story: margins have missed every guidance number for a year (17–18%, then 15.5–16.5%, delivered 13%), interest cost doubled, cash flow is deeply negative and debt is ~2.6x net worth, with loans still leaning on the promoter's tea estates. At today's price the stock needs both the growth and a margin jump to 17%+ plus a successful QIP. Proof points to watch: H2 FY27 margin above 16% once the loss-making Boeing batch ends, the QIP actually closing, and positive operating cash flow in FY27. A further QIP slip or margin stuck near 13–14% would make the valuation hard to defend.
