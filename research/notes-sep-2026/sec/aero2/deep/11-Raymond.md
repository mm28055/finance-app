# Raymond Ltd (engineering: JK Maini Global Aerospace + JK Maini Precision Technology) — deep research (Q1 FY27)

Sources read: stored Q4 FY26 (5 May 2026, BSE-filed official copy) and Q1 FY27 (7 Aug 2026) transcripts in full; official Q1 FY27 transcript (BSE); Q1 FY27 investor presentation (BSE, 7 Aug 2026); FY26 annual report (MD&A, consolidated auditor's report incl. emphasis of matter, key audit matter, audit-trail remarks and CARO 3(xxi) annexure, standalone CARO, note 29 contingent liabilities, note 32 related parties); Screener consolidated page (raw HTML, 28 Sep 2026); news on the promoter warrant issue and pledge release. No current credit-rating rationale was found after the demerger (last CARE update Jul 2025, pre-demerger scope).

## Business and segments
- After the demergers of Raymond Lifestyle (listed Sep 2024) and Raymond Realty (effective 1 May 2025, listed 1 Jul 2025), **Raymond Ltd is an engineering holding company** — it owns ~66.3% of the engineering business ("you own 2/3 of that EBITDA"). [IP Q1FY27 p.4; Q4 call]
- **Aerospace & Defence — JK Maini Global Aerospace (JKMGAL):** precision aero-engine parts (>75% of output), plus landing-gear, hydraulic, fuel and structural parts; 1,300+ parts developed, 350+ parts on the **LEAP** engine, 25+ customers, "preferred supplier to top 3 global aircraft-engine makers holding 88% share". Q1 FY27 revenue split: international 76% (Europe 51%, US 25%), domestic 24%. Materials: titanium, Inconel, stainless — **100% imported** for aero. [IP Q1FY27 pp.15–21; Q4 call]
- **Precision Technology & Auto Components — JK Maini Precision Technology (JKMPTL):** flexplates and ring gears (India share PV ~55%, CV ~45%; global ~25%), hybrid/EV transmission parts, and **Tools & Hardware (JK Files: ~65% India share in files)**; 61% international. Existing capacity utilisation **~85–90%**. [IP Q1FY27 pp.25–27]
- FY26 segments: Aerospace ₹392 cr revenue (+26%), EBITDA ₹88 cr (22.3%); Precision/Auto ₹1,667 cr (+10%), EBITDA ₹223 cr (13.4%, includes a one-off ₹13 cr land-sale gain). Group total income ₹2,312 cr, EBITDA ₹335 cr (14.5%, includes other income). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.4]
- **Q1 FY27 segments:** Aerospace ₹123 cr (+40%), EBITDA ₹26 cr (+25%), margin **21.2%** (23.7% a year ago); Precision/Auto ₹444 cr (+11.5%), EBITDA ₹61 cr (+45.5%), margin **13.8%** (10.6%). Aerospace is ~20% of revenue. [IP Q1FY27 p.12; Q1 call]
- New lines: mass production of precision defence components for "a leading defence OEM"; formal audit approval from an Indian defence/aerospace OEM; 135 part awards on "a flagship aircraft localisation programme" (100 in serial production); first **build-to-spec** (own-design) order; medical-components certification (Aug 2026); auto aftermarket launch in Q2 FY27; ex-BEL executive hired to lead defence. [IP Q1FY27 p.28; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (5 May 2026):** aerospace **25% a year** organically; existing plant can deliver next year's 25%, Andhra must deliver the one after; group growth and margins "constant" vs history; capex ₹930 cr over five years (₹500 cr aero, ₹430 cr auto), ~₹100 cr per company per year; funded by accruals and debt, "don't need parent capital"; equity only if an acquisition comes; Andhra commercial production **late CY2027 (Q4 FY28)**, real revenue FY29; ~250 new aero parts this year; net cash ₹68 cr. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 call (7 Aug 2026):** aerospace +25% commitment kept ("slightly ahead"); consolidated margin "same trend" (~15–16% on the company's definition); capex restated as **~₹1,000 cr** (₹510 cr aero, ₹430 cr auto); existing aero plant now expected to exceed **₹600 cr** revenue (earlier ~₹600 cr) after the "Parivartan" improvement drive; order book restated to **₹5,960 cr over 10 years** (5-year slice ₹2,765 cr vs ₹2,350 cr last quarter, +17%); RFQ pipeline ₹1,632 cr; net cash ₹129 cr. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Track record vs Q4 promises:** aerospace growth 40% vs 25% guide → **beating**; auto margin held at 13.8% (above the 12–13% "sustainable" band) → **delivering**; Andhra timeline unchanged → **on track**; "no equity unless inorganic" → a **₹331 cr promoter warrant issue** followed within weeks for acquisitions (consistent with the caveat, but no target named). Aero margin slipped to 21.2% on expensed R&D → **mixed**. Verdict: **delivering or beating on growth; guidance kept**. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- **~₹1,000 cr over five years** at Gudipalli, Andhra Pradesh (near Bengaluru airport): aero ~₹510 cr on ~45 acres (special processes, heat-treatment, assemblies, "factory-in-factory" for customers); auto ~₹430 cr on ~32 acres. Ground-breaking done; aero commercial production late 2027, customer approvals up to 6 months, meaningful revenue from FY29. A training/production facility is being set up early. [IP Q1FY27 p.27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Implied payback: at 1.8–2.2x asset turns and ~22–25% aero margin, ₹510 cr could support ~₹900–1,100 cr of aero revenue at maturity — management's own "25% a year" path. [Q1 call; my calculation]
- Machines are ordered continuously against part approvals (15+ machine suppliers; 6–8-month lead times). [Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- 22 years in aero-engine parts; moved N4 → N3 → N2 criticality over ~10 years (N1 rotating parts not yet); build-to-spec order is a first step beyond build-to-print. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Qualification barrier: new-supplier approval takes ~2 years; a second location for an approved supplier is much faster; "self-approval" status on simpler parts with some customers. [Q4 call]
- Share: flexplates/ring gears ~55% PV and ~45% CV India, ~25% global; files ~65% India — genuine share leadership in precision/tools. [IP Q1FY27 p.26]
- Concentration: aerospace top-3 customers **40–45%** of business. [Q1 call]
- Order book caveat: the ₹5,960 cr "10-year order book" is a schedule of **~₹500–645 cr a year (2027–2036)** — roughly one year of current aero revenue per year, covering only parts already in production at the existing plant, not a multi-year backlog of new work. [IP Q1FY27 p.18; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Minority leakage:** Raymond shareholders own ~2/3 of the engineering profit; P/E on the parent looks high because TTM PAT (₹64 cr) is small and includes minority share. [Q4 call; Screener]
- **Promoter warrants at ₹497** (vs ~₹1,197 now) transfer value to the promoter if an acquisition does not add proportionate value; purpose is generic. [News: ScanX, Jul 2026]
- **Cash conversion weak:** FY26 OCF ₹42 cr against ₹232 cr op. profit; capex ₹200 cr a year planned with ~₹1,000 cr gross debt in subsidiaries. [Screener; Q4 call]
- **Audit findings (FY26):** unmodified opinion (Chaturvedi & Shah) with an **emphasis of matter** on the realty-demerger accounting; audit trail not operated throughout the year for some software (holding company, five subsidiaries, one associate, one JV); **CARO clause (i) adverse remarks at JKMPTL and JKMGAL** (property/asset records) and clause (ix) at Everblue Apparel. [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report; Annexure III]
- **Contingent liabilities ~₹95 cr**, mostly legacy of divested cement/steel/denim divisions (stamp duty ₹29.6 cr, 50% reimbursable), GST ₹19 cr, sales tax ₹16 cr; a Thane tenancy-rights claim. [AR FY26 note 29]
- Management churn: Group CFO changed Dec 2025 (Amit Agarwal → Rakesh Tiwary). Promoter-family litigation history (not examined here). [AR FY26 governance report]
- Cost pressure: tooling (carbide/HSS), logistics, minimum wages; aero raw materials 100% imported. Auto exposure to US tariffs and European demand. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## My read
The engineering business is improving: aerospace grew 40% in Q1 with ~21% margins, the auto/tools arm lifted margins to ~14% on synergies, and share leadership in flexplates, ring gears and files is real. But the listed parent is a two-thirds holding company whose reported profits are small, cash conversion was poor in FY26, and ~₹1,000 cr of subsidiary debt sits under a "net cash" headline that depends on treasury investments. The ₹5,960 cr order book is a ten-year schedule, not a surge in orders. At ~₹8,000 cr market value the price needs aerospace to compound ~25% for years and the Andhra plant to ramp in FY29; the view improves with a sensible acquisition and OCF catching up with EBITDA, and worsens if aero margins stay near 20% or the warrants fund a low-return deal.
