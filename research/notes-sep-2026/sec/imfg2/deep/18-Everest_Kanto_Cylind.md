# Everest Kanto Cylinder (EKC) — deep research (Sep 2026)

Screener code EKC (consolidated). Price ₹110, market cap ₹1,237 cr, P/E 9.3, book value ₹125, 52-week range ₹90–155 [Screener, 28-Sep-26]. No Q1 FY27 earnings call was held (results deck only, 13-Aug-26); the last two calls are Q3 FY26 (Feb-26) and Q4 FY26 (3-Jun-26).

## Business and segments
- India's largest maker of high-pressure seamless steel gas cylinders: CNG cylinders for cars, buses and trucks, CNG cascades for city-gas companies, industrial/medical gas, fire, hydrogen, jumbo and Type-3/Type-4 composite cylinders. Capacity ~1.8 million cylinders a year across Tarapur, Kandla SEZ and new Mundra (India), Jebel Ali (Dubai), Pittsburgh (USA, via CP Industries) and a new Egypt plant. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Reported by geography. FY26 segment revenue (after inter-segment): India ~₹961 cr, USA ₹356 cr, UAE ~₹152 cr gross, others (Hungary etc.) ₹32 cr. FY26 segment result: India ₹119 cr (₹72 cr FY25), USA ₹47 cr (₹58 cr), UAE ₹1.8 cr, others −₹2 cr. [AR FY26 consolidated note 55]
- India drives profit: standalone FY26 revenue ₹966.7 cr, EBITDA ₹154.4 cr (16.0% margin vs 10.6%), PAT ₹81.2 cr. [Q4 call]
- Customers: Tata Motors, VE Commercial Vehicles, Bajaj Auto; city-gas firms Mahanagar Gas, HPCL, Adani Total Gas. Top five ~45% of revenue. [CARE Sep-25]
- Hungary operations divested (completed by Q1 FY27); Egypt JV now 97% owned. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note]
- Seamless steel tubes are ~50–55% of revenue and >80% imported; price changes pass through with ~3-month lag. [CARE Sep-25]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue: FY24 ₹1,223 cr → FY25 ₹1,499 cr → FY26 ₹1,471 cr (−2%); TTM ₹1,430 cr. 5-year sales CAGR 9%, 3-year 5%. FY22's ₹1,699 cr / 24% margin was a one-off peak. [Screener]
- Operating profit: FY24 ₹163 cr (13%) → FY25 ₹179 cr (12%) → FY26 ₹203 cr (14%). Net profit ₹98 cr → ₹98 cr → ₹147 cr. [Screener]
- FY26 net profit is flattered: ~₹30 cr deferred-tax asset recognised at the US subsidiary (Q4 tax rate −101%) and ₹12.6 cr exceptional gain (US employee-retention tax refund). Underlying PAT is closer to ~₹105 cr. [AR FY26 consolidated notes; Screener]
- Last five quarters (revenue / OPM / net profit): Q1 FY26 ₹387 cr / 16% / ₹52 cr (incl. ₹13 cr exceptional); Q2 ₹360 cr / 12% / ₹14 cr; Q3 ₹365 cr / 16% / ₹36 cr; Q4 ₹358 cr / 11% / ₹46 cr; Q1 FY27 ₹346 cr / 14% / ₹30 cr. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27: revenue −10% YoY, EBITDA ₹47 cr (−23%), margin 13.6% (−217 bp), PAT −42%; "impacted by weaker profitability in the overseas business"; US "subdued", UAE improving. Employee cost +20%. [IP Q1FY27]
- ROCE ~12% for four years; ROE 11%. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q3 FY26 call (Feb-26): sustainable EBITDA margin "15%–17%"; FY27 revenue growth "about 15%–20%"; Mundra's two remaining lines "by next quarter" (+~15% capacity) plus ₹30 cr extra Mundra capex; Egypt operational "by May 2026", ₹50–60 cr revenue in year one; US $5.5 mn capex to add ~₹100 cr revenue in FY27/FY28 at 20%+ margins; UAE to break even in FY27. [Q3 official transcript]
- Q4 FY26 call (Jun-26): no numbers repeated. Mundra "ramp-up… maybe 6 months down the line"; Egypt "operational by the end of this month", initial use ~40% of target rising to ~80%; UAE at ~50% utilisation, "hopefully this year should be a better year"; GST case resolution in "6 months to a year"; new CEO (Mr Gupta) awaited. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue −10% (vs +15–20% target), margin 13.6% (below 15–17%), US weaker (contradicting the "more consistent" US story). Guidance was effectively **dropped** in June and **missed** in Q1. [IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Heavy capex cycle: investing outflow ₹137 cr (FY25) and ₹200 cr (FY26); fixed assets up from ₹417 cr to ₹593 cr in FY26; CWIP still ₹162 cr at Mar-26 (India, US, Egypt). [Screener; Q4 call]
- Mundra greenfield: two lines operational per the annual report; ₹30 cr extra approved. Egypt: commissioned (per AR), funded partly with high-cost Egyptian debt at 1.5% over the central bank corridor rate, part switched to US-dollar debt. US: $5.5 mn for large-diameter and Type-4 cylinders, partly funded by a US government grant tied to a government contract. [AR FY26 MD&A and borrowing notes; CARE Sep-25; Q3 call]
- Idle assets: ₹7 cr of PPE and ₹12.4 cr of CWIP have been idle "for a considerable period due to demand contraction for certain products" (auditor's key audit matter). [AR FY26 auditor's report p.152]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings rose from ₹41 cr (Mar-24) to ₹143 cr (Mar-25) to ₹267 cr (Mar-26). Equity ₹1,399 cr, so gearing still low (~0.2x). CARE (Sep-25): A-/Stable, A2+; cash ₹77 cr + MF ₹61 cr at Jun-25. [Screener; CARE Sep-25]
- Free cash flow −₹63 cr (FY25) and −₹31 cr (FY26) due to capex; operating cash flow ₹142 cr in FY26 (90% of operating profit). [Screener]
- Working capital heavy: inventory 258 days, debtors 55 days, payables 26 days; cash cycle 288 days (FY26). CARE upgrade trigger needs cycle <150 days. [Screener; CARE Sep-25]
- Dividend ₹0.70 a share (payout ~5%). [Q4 call; Screener]
- The parent gave a letter of financial support to the US subsidiary CP Industries "to meet its day to day obligations" (till 31-May-26). [AR FY26 standalone note 48]

## Moat evidence
- Regulatory barriers: PESO testing and approvals, CCOE import permissions; four decades of OEM and city-gas relationships; claims #1 position in India. [CARE Sep-25; IP Q1FY27]
- Pricing power is weak and cyclical: margin swung from 16% (Q3) to 11% (Q4) to 14% (Q1) as mix moved between specialised orders (semiconductor, defence, city-gas cascades) and standard auto-CNG; management admitted specialised orders "are often project-based". [Screener; Q3 call]
- Demand support: CNG was ~22% of Indian passenger-vehicle sales in FY26, second-largest fuel. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **GST dispute ~₹351 cr**: GST, interest and penalty demand of ₹351.3 cr for FY2017-18 to FY2022-23, pending before CESTAT Ahmedabad (product HSN classification, an industry-wide issue); shown as contingent, not provided. This is ~28% of market cap and ~25% of net worth. CARE calls crystallisation "a key rating monitorable". Management expects clarity in 6–12 months. [AR FY26 CARO and note 52; CARE Sep-25; Q4 call]
- **Earnings quality**: FY26 PAT boosted by deferred-tax recognition and a one-off US refund.
- **Overseas drag**: UAE ~50% utilised, US order book flat at ~$75 mn for two quarters and weaker in Q1; Egypt new and on high-cost debt.
- **Management churn**: new CEO announced at Q4 without details; HR head removed "based on assessment of his performance" on 22-Sep-26. [Q4 call; BSE filing 23-Sep-26]
- US environmental exposure noted as a contingency (no known claims). [AR FY26 note 52]
- Promoter holding steady at 67.4%; no pledge disclosed. Auditor opinion unmodified; key audit matter on idle-asset impairment. [Screener; AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q3: confident — 16.2% margin called "sustainable" at 15–17%, 15–20% growth, several dated milestones.
- Q4: a five-page call with no guidance, delays on Mundra and Egypt, CEO change, GST case back in focus.
- After Q4: Q1 FY27 revenue fell 10% and no call was held — less disclosure at a weaker moment.

## My read
EKC is a cheap, cyclical cylinder maker whose India business improved in FY26, but the group has gone backwards since: revenue fell 10% in Q1 FY27, the US and Gulf units are weak, guidance was quietly withdrawn and capex has added ₹226 cr of debt in two years. The low multiple reflects a ₹351 cr GST demand hanging over a ₹1,237 cr company and FY26 profits flattered by tax credits. A clean GST outcome plus Mundra/Egypt lifting group revenue back to growth would re-rate it; an adverse GST ruling or another quarter of shrinking sales would not.
