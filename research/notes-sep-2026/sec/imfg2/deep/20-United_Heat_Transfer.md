# United Heat Transfer (UHTL) — deep research (Sep 2026)

NSE SME listed (IPO Oct-2024). Reports **half-yearly**; only one earnings call has been held (H2 FY26, 3-Jun-26) — there is no second transcript to compare. Price ₹200, market cap ₹379 cr, P/E 75.7, 52-week range ₹44–204 (up ~169% in a year) [Screener, 28-Sep-26].

## Business and segments
- Nashik-based maker of shell-and-tube and air-cooled heat exchangers, charge-air coolers, extended-surface exchangers, marine jacket-water coolers, moisture separators, pressure vessels and process skids. ASME U and R stamp, Canadian, Australian (DOSH) and European (CE-PED) certifications. Plants at Ambad (2007) and Talegaon/Tidegaon (2021, 13 acres, ~25–30% used). [H2 call; IP H2FY26]
- Revenue model: OEM (compressors etc.) 35%, auto/engine OEM 23% — standard and semi-custom scheduled supply — and projects (fully custom, 3–6 month execution) 42%. [IP H2FY26]
- Customers: Cummins, Kirloskar, Weichai, Greaves (engines); Ingersoll Rand, Atlas Copco, Burckhardt, Siemens Energy, Elgi, Sullair, Xylem, Triveni (OEMs); trial orders for Hitachi and Vertiv; registered with GAIL and NPCIL. Exports to France, Italy, Germany, US. 57 new customers in H2 FY26 (16 overseas). [H2 call; IP H2FY26]
- Capacity: management says the existing plants can generate ₹200–250 cr of revenue (FY26 revenue was ₹73 cr); a 50,000 sq ft shop extension (taller bays for air-cooled exchangers) is due by Q3 FY27, lifting capacity 25–30%. [H2 call; IP H2FY26]
- Listed peers named by management: Patel Airtemp, Loyal Equipments. [H2 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, standalone, half-yearly)
- Revenue: FY23 ₹69.6 cr → FY24 ₹60.0 cr → FY25 ₹66.5 cr → FY26 ₹72.7 cr. 3-year sales CAGR only 1%; 5-year 17%. [Screener]
- Operating profit: FY24 ₹10.0 cr (16.6%) → FY25 ₹11.4 cr (17.1%) → FY26 ₹10.5 cr (14.4%). Net profit ₹6.2 cr → ₹5.3 cr → ₹5.0 cr (falling). [Screener]
- Half-years (revenue / OPM / PAT): H2 FY24 ₹39.9 cr / 10% / ₹3.7 cr; H1 FY25 ₹28.4 cr / 22.8% / ₹3.1 cr; H2 FY25 ₹38.4 cr / 12.8% / ₹2.2 cr; H1 FY26 ₹21.4 cr / 12.0% / ₹0.8 cr; H2 FY26 ₹51.4 cr / 15.4% / ₹4.2 cr. [Screener]
- H2 FY26 was a record half (+34% YoY), but it followed a very weak H1; the full year grew only 9%. [Screener; IP H2FY26]
- ROCE 11.8%, ROE 8% (FY26). [Screener]

## Guidance history
- H2 FY26 call and deck (Jun-26): revenue growth 30–35% in FY27; margins to improve "if the external situation remains controlled" (no number); Vertiv CDU trial order ₹10–20 lakh, approval in 2–3 months, then ₹10–20 cr a year ("from the second half of this year or the next financial year"); first commercial Vertiv supply targeted by 30-Jun-26; shop extension in 5–6 months; delivery cycle to fall from 14–16 weeks to 8–10 weeks. [H2 call; IP H2FY26]
- No later result yet (H1 FY27 due Oct/Nov-26), so no delivery test is possible. The Hitachi order discussed after the IPO was stalled by US tariffs and only revived in 2026 — an example of slow conversion. [H2 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Fixed assets ₹35.9 cr and CWIP ₹4.3 cr (Mar-26); investing outflow ₹7.1 cr in FY26. [Screener]
- New preferential issue (approved at EGM 19-Sep-26): 29.68 lakh shares at ₹105.50 to 17 non-promoter investors (Intelliquity Ventures, Mittal Analytics, Kvasa Capital, individuals) = ₹31.3 cr: ₹23 cr capex within 15 months, ₹5 cr working capital, ₹3.3 cr general purposes. The issue price is about half the current share price (it follows the SEBI formula on past prices); it adds ~15.6% to the share count. [NSE filings 19-Sep-26 and 21-Sep-26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹18.9 cr (Mar-26), down from ₹36.9 cr (Mar-24) after the IPO; equity ₹63.5 cr. [Screener]
- Working capital is very heavy for the size: inventory 263 days, debtors 84 days, payables 63 days — cash cycle 284 days (FY26), up from 68 days in FY23. Company says ~182 days in FY26 vs ~256 days in FY25 (its own definition). [Screener; IP H2FY26]
- Operating cash flow ₹8.1 cr in FY26 (−₹6.8 cr in FY25); free cash flow ₹1.8 cr. [Screener]
- Rating: **CRISIL B/Stable "Issuer Not Cooperating"** (27-Feb-26) on ₹25 cr bank lines (Saraswat Co-op Bank, HDFC) — the company has not given CRISIL information; earlier CRISIL BB (2023). A lender-facing red flag for a listed company. [CRISIL Feb-26]
- No dividend. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Certifications (ASME U/R stamp for 10+ years, CE-PED, DOSH) and 15+ years of saltwater marine applications; in-house cutting, rolling, fin stamping, welding and anti-corrosion coating. [H2 call]
- Vertiv chose UHTL for CDU piping/nozzle components because other vendors could not do certain "specialised joints or welding" — a qualitative claim, not yet a repeat order. Not exclusive. [H2 call]
- Scale is tiny: order book ₹22.5 cr at 31-Mar-26, ₹34.2 cr at 28-May-26 (~₹36–37 cr on the call) versus ₹200 cr+ claimed capacity. Price escalation clauses only on scheduled supply (3–5% bands). [IP H2FY26; H2 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Valuation vs fundamentals**: stock up ~4.5x from its low while revenue grew 1% a year over three years and profit fell two years running; the P/E rests on the Vertiv/data-centre story.
- **Preferential issue at ~₹105** to financial investors while the stock trades ~₹200 — immediate paper gain for allottees; dilution ~15.6%.
- **Credit rating "not cooperating"** at CRISIL B.
- **Related parties**: sales to promoter-group Uniheat Research and Solutions were ₹6.8 cr in FY25 (~10% of revenue) and ₹0.6 cr in FY26; a ₹0.27 cr receivable from Uni-Spec Foods (promoter entity) unchanged for two years; promoter family members are paid employees; loans from family repaid in FY26. [AR FY26 note 35]
- Contingent liabilities: PF demand ₹1.7 cr; bank guarantees/LCs ₹6.6–9 cr. [AR FY26 note 34]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; "no key audit matters". Company secretary changed in Nov-25. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Key-man risk (two founder brothers); half-yearly reporting; SME liquidity.

## What changed between the two calls
- Only one call exists. Versus the H1 FY26 deck (Nov-25), the story shifted to data centres (Vertiv), nuclear (NPCIL registration) and exports; capacity expansion and the preferential raise followed.

## My read
UHTL is a small, certified, genuinely capable heat-exchanger shop, but its numbers have barely grown in three years and profit has slipped. The share price has run far ahead on a data-centre option (Vertiv) that is still a ₹10–20 lakh trial. A cheap preferential issue to outside investors, a CRISIL "non-cooperating" B rating and heavy working capital add governance and quality doubts. The H1 FY27 result must show the promised 30–35% growth and Vertiv repeat orders; without them the valuation has little support.
