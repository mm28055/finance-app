# Praj Industries — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code PRAJIND. Price ₹316, market cap ₹5,807 cr, P/E 115, book value ₹71.2, ROCE 6.1%, ROE 1.5%, dividend yield 1.14% [Screener, 28-Sep-26]. Calls read: Q4 FY26 (29 May 2026) and Q1 FY27 (14 Aug 2026), both checked against the official BSE transcripts.

## Business and segments
- India's leading ethanol-plant technology and engineering company (1,000+ plant references in 100+ countries), plus breweries, compressed biogas (CBG), sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) engineering, bio-isobutanol (Bio-IBA), lifecycle services, Praj HiPurity (ultra-pure water for pharma/semiconductors), ZLD/wastewater and **Praj GenX** — a large modular-fabrication yard at Mangalore for oil & gas, refining, LNG and now data-centre cooling modules. [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Revenue mix Q1 FY27: bioenergy 66%, engineering 22%, HiPurity 12%; exports 25%. FY26: 67/22/11, exports 36%. Non-ethanol share ~33% of FY26 revenue vs 20–25% earlier. [Q1 call; Q4 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Order backlog ₹4,305 cr (Mar-26: ~₹3,300 cr bioenergy, ~₹800 cr engineering/GenX, ~₹230 cr HiPurity) → ₹4,589 cr (Jun-26; 63% domestic; bioenergy 77%, engineering 18%, HiPurity 5%). Order intake by quarter (₹ cr): 795, 813, 914, 658 (Q4 FY26, a four-year low), 1,000 (Q1 FY27; 43% export). [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Aug-26; Q4 call]
- Unit economics: a 100 KLPD ethanol plant costs ~₹100–120 cr; Praj's scope 50–60% (≈30% if process only). New Brazil corn-to-ethanol order is ~800 KLPD. [Q1 call]
- Capacity: GenX fixed costs ~₹10 cr a month; 12–13 international customers had audited/approved the Mangalore facility by May 2026 (9 at end-Q3). [Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- Sales FY24 ₹3,466 cr → FY25 ₹3,228 cr → FY26 ₹3,168 cr (–2%); operating profit ₹372 cr → ₹314 cr → ₹155 cr; OPM 11% → 10% → 4.9%; net profit ₹283 cr → ₹219 cr → ₹24 cr (FY26 includes a ₹23.8 cr labour-code exceptional charge; FY25 had a land-sale gain). Three-year sales growth –4% a year; profit –56% a year. [Screener; AR FY26 note 44]
- Last five quarters, sales / operating profit / net profit (₹ cr): Jun-25 640/31/5; Sep-25 842/56/19; Dec-25 841/41/–12; Mar-26 845/23/12; Jun-26 716/30/12. OPM 4.9% → 7% → 4.9% → 2.8% → 4.2%. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (deck): consolidated operating income ₹716 cr (+11.8%), EBITDA ₹30 cr (4.19% vs 4.90%), PAT ₹11.6 cr (+119%). Standalone EBITDA ₹20 cr (3.8%) but standalone PAT ₹25 cr — i.e. subsidiaries (mainly GenX) lose money. The CFO quoted "EBITDA before exceptional items ₹21.05 cr" on the call, which does not match the deck's ₹30 cr. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Q1 bioenergy revenue ~₹474 cr; exports fell year-on-year and the export revenue that was executed came from lower-margin African projects. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Company gives **no numeric revenue/margin guidance** (repeated on both calls). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Q4 FY26 call (29 May): promised the first Bio-IBA order "in the current quarter", a data-centre cooling order for GenX in Q1 FY27, "some uptick from FY27 onwards", "the worst is over" on execution costs, order intake back to ₹800–900 cr a quarter; ~₹300 cr of inquiries deferred because of steel-price uncertainty on fixed-price contracts. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: Bio-IBA commercial demo-plant order **received** (Gevo development agreement followed on 16 Sep 2026); GenX data-centre framework **signed** — minimum ~US$50 mn (~₹500 cr) over 2.5 years, only partly booked; Brazil 800 KLPD order **received**; order intake ₹1,000 cr (**met** the ₹800–900 cr band); margins still ~4% (**not yet**). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; BSE press release 16-Sep-26]
- New in Q1: GenX EBITDA break-even targeted "during this year" (FY27); Bio-IBA demo plant complete by Dec 2026; export revenue share to rise from Q3 as new export orders enter execution. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- The big GenX Mangalore investment is done (fixed assets rose from ₹317 cr in FY23 to ₹560 cr in FY26); no major new capex announced. R&D spend FY26 ~₹65–66 cr (₹20 cr capex, ₹45–46 cr opex; ~100 scientists). [Screener; Q4 call]
- The parent has lent **₹291 cr** to Praj GenX, whose accumulated losses are **₹183 cr** — flagged by the auditor as a key audit matter (impairment risk). [AR FY26 standalone auditor's report]
- A 28 Mar 2025 fire at the R&D centre: ₹12.5 cr insurance claim, ₹6.5 cr advance received, ₹12.75 cr replacement capex. [AR FY26 note 45]
- Payback: GenX needs enough orders to cover ~₹120 cr a year of fixed cost; the US$50 mn data-centre deal alone is ~₹200 cr a year of revenue. [Q4 call; Q1 call; my calculation]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free apart from leases (borrowings ₹173 cr on Screener are mostly lease liabilities); cash ₹612 cr (Mar-26) and ₹616 cr (Jun-26); CRISIL AA/Stable reaffirmed Aug 2026; nil use of fund-based limits. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- Operating cash flow ₹199 cr (FY24), ₹43 cr (FY25), ₹200 cr (FY26); cash-conversion cycle –8 days in FY26 (customer advances fund the work). Slow domestic project execution is delaying collections. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Dividend ₹3.60/share for FY26 (~₹66 cr) — payout 278% of FY26 profit; paid from the cash pile. [Screener; Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- Technology: Praj has built all three 2G ethanol plants in India ("this technology is available only at Praj"); first commercial Bio-IBA demo plant in India; ICAO has approved the sugarcane-to-jet pathway, supporting Praj's alcohol-to-jet engineering work. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Installed base of 1,000+ plants feeds lifecycle services (higher margin). [Q4 call]
- Brazil 800 KLPD greenfield corn plant is a marquee reference in the world's second-largest ethanol market. [Q1 call]
- Weakness: in CBG "a fair number of players have entered"; margins "in line with the industry"; Praj's share of India's CBG/SAF investment has been small (an investor's criticism on the Q4 call). Fixed-price contracts expose it to steel inflation. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Policy dependence:** domestic greenfield 1G ethanol has stalled since E20 was met; revival needs E25/E30 mandates (BIS specs notified; timing unknown — management "not longer than 1 year"). [Q4 call]
- **GenX drag:** ₹183 cr accumulated losses, ₹291 cr parent loans, ~₹10 cr/month fixed cost; CRISIL lists slow scale-up of new initiatives as a key weakness. [AR FY26; Q4 call; CRISIL Aug-26]
- **Execution/collections:** customer funding problems on domestic ethanol projects extend timelines and site costs; "rubbing effect on the collection of our dues". [Q1 call]
- Auditor: MSKA & Associates LLP; KAMs on revenue recognition, impairment of trade receivables, and the GenX investment/loans. Unmodified opinion. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Contingent liabilities are small (income-tax interest demand, GST on intra-group corporate guarantees). [AR FY26 note 28]
- Management: new MD (Ashish Gaikwad, ex-Honeywell) from June 2025; CFO Sachin Raole elevated to Joint MD; investors on the Q4 call openly questioned delivery. [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: defensive — FY26 PAT down ~89%, order intake at a four-year low, orders deliberately deferred, "the worst is over".
- Q1: the promised orders arrived (GenX data-centre framework, Bio-IBA, Brazil, semiconductor UPW+ZLD), intake ₹1,000 cr, GOBARdhan CBG scheme (₹23,731 cr outlay, FY27–FY36) approved; but margins stayed ~4% and exports dipped.
- Stored transcripts vs official: Q1 FY27 is a **tidied copy** with one gap — order intake shown as "[inaudible] billion" where the official text says ₹10 billion. Q4 FY26 is a **tidied copy (figures match)**; it even corrects an official typo (FY25 PAT printed as ₹289 mn in the official text; stored ₹2,189 mn is right). [BSE transcripts]

## My read
Praj is a debt-free, cash-rich (~₹615 cr) technology leader in a trough: domestic ethanol capex has dried up after E20, GenX is burning ~₹120 cr a year of fixed cost, and margins have fallen from ~11% to ~4%, leaving FY26 profit at just ₹24 cr. Q1 FY27 brought real order wins that could lift utilisation and mix in H2, and policy optionality (E25/E30, CBG under GOBARdhan, Bio-IBA in diesel, SAF) is large. But the current price already assumes a sharp profit recovery, so I would want to see GenX reach EBITDA break-even by Q4 FY27, consolidated margins back above 7–8%, and a firm E25/E30 notification. Further GenX losses, more execution-cost overruns, or no blending mandate in 12 months would weaken the view.
