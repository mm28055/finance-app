# Standard Engineering Technology (formerly Standard Glass Lining) — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code SETL (NSE: SETL; BSE 544333). Price ₹403, market cap ₹8,031 cr, P/E 93.9, book value ₹39.5, ROCE 14.7%, ROE 10.7%, no dividend [Screener, 28-Sep-26]. Listed Jan 2025. Renamed from Standard Glass Lining Technology to Standard Engineering Technology Ltd (SETL) in FY26. Calls read: Q4 FY26 (15 May 2026) and Q1 FY27 (6 Aug 2026), checked against the official BSE transcripts.

## Business and segments
- Hyderabad maker of process equipment for pharma, CDMO (contract drug makers), speciality chemicals, food and biotech: glass-lined reactors, stainless-steel and nickel-alloy vessels, filters/dryers, heat exchangers — about 180 products, 63 litres to 200 kilolitres — plus turnkey "concept-to-commissioning" projects (engineering, piping, automation, installation, validation). It makes 70–80% of project equipment in-house. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Reported as **one operating segment** (glass-lined reactors and related equipment) in the FY26 standalone accounts; the company says it will change its segment reporting. Glass lining itself is only "still 30%" of revenue, per the MD. [AR FY26 standalone segment note; Q4 call (official)]
- End markets: order book about 50% CDMO, the rest pharma and chemicals. Exports 5.3% of Q4 FY26 revenue, 2% of Q1 FY27; FY26 exports ~6.5% (up from 3.7% in FY25). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Subsidiaries: S2 Engineering (100%), Standard Engineering Solutions (100%), Standard Projects (75%), CPK Engineers, Standard Scigenics (bioprocess) and Standard C2C Engineering (process/civil/HVAC engineering) (51% each), Standard Flora (51%), Standard Engineering Inc. USA (100%). Scigenics and C2C each did ~₹24 cr in FY26, targeting ~₹60 cr each in FY27. [AR FY26 Directors' report; Q4 call]
- New legs (FY27): (a) **GScale Energy** — AI data-centre power and cooling skids, power distribution units, prefabricated modules and design-and-build turnkey work; SETL buying 51% (33.55% done by Q1), ₹190 cr phase 1 (₹125 cr cash + ₹65 cr share swap) within a ~₹487–500 cr phased programme; a 4 lakh sq ft factory, operations from Nov 2026. (b) **GL Hakko (Japan)** — ₹71.5 cr for 19.19%, option to reach 51.07% in 2–3 years once GL Hakko revenue grows from ~₹200 cr to ~₹400 cr; brings conductive glass, low-leaching semiconductor-grade glass and glass-lined shell-and-tube heat exchangers (80% made in Japan, 20% in India). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; CRISIL Jul-26]
- Capacity: existing plants 70–80% utilised; with automation they can support ~₹2,000 cr revenue; a 36-acre greenfield campus (₹130 cr, 50% live from 1 Apr 2027, rest 1 Apr 2028) adds another ~₹2,000 cr — management's "₹4,000 cr manufacturing capability". [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (15 May): no numbers — FY27 revenue growth "better than" FY26's 26.7% and margins "going to improve"; quarterly revenue run-rate of ₹250–300 cr "possible" from FY27; the delayed US$3.5 mn export order to ship in Q1; Scigenics and C2C to reach ~₹60 cr each; capex ₹130 cr over two years (₹65 cr in FY27). [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue ₹248 cr (sales), at the bottom of the ₹250–300 cr run-rate; EBITDA margin 17.5% (up from 15.5% in Q4, below 19.1% in FY25); exports only 2% — **the US$3.5 mn order again missed** ("uncertainty… in the global markets"), now promised at 5–6% of Q2 revenue. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 guidance (**raised and made specific**): FY27 consolidated revenue ₹1,450 cr = core ₹1,200 cr (+40–50%) + GScale ₹250 cr from ~4 months; core EBITDA margin 17–18%; GScale 23–25%; ~20% return on the ~₹500 cr data-centre investment. The older 3–4 year target of ₹1,600 cr was superseded. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Pre-cast buildings for pharma blocks (a new subsidiary, promoted on the Q4 call) were not mentioned on Q1. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Working capital: Q4 said days "improved from 174 days to 150 days" (official text); Q1 the MD said "last year 320 days", to fall "below 200 days" by September. The two statements are inconsistent. [Q4 call (official); Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹40 cr (plant modernisation, robots, automated welding/polishing); FY27 another ₹35–40 cr for modernisation plus ₹65 cr for the greenfield; total planned ₹130 cr over 2–3 years, 5.5 lakh sq ft. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- GScale: ₹190 cr phase 1 of a ~₹487–500 cr programme; "100% self-funded, zero new debt". GL Hakko: ₹71.5 cr. Together these deploy roughly ₹560–570 cr, i.e. most of SETL's ~₹788 cr net worth (equity ₹199 cr + reserves ₹589 cr at Mar-26). [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Capital commitments ₹6.5 cr at Mar-26 (standalone). [AR FY26 standalone note 41]
- IPO proceeds (₹232 cr fresh issue incl. pre-IPO): ₹185.6 cr used by 31 Mar 2026; ₹130 cr repaid debt; ₹19 cr of S2 Engineering capex money and ₹25 cr of general-purpose money still unused; ICRA is the monitoring agency. [AR FY26 Directors' report]
- Payback: management expects ~20% ROCE on GScale; not yet demonstrated. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹129 cr (Mar-24) → ₹68 cr (Mar-25) → ₹83 cr (Mar-26); net-debt-free per the company (debt/equity 0.10, net debt/equity ~0.09 at FY26). Unencumbered cash >₹220 cr at Mar-26 (CRISIL); ₹105 cr cash cited in the Q1 deck after deal payments. [Screener; IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Jul-26]
- **Working capital is heavy:** Screener FY26 debtor days 120, inventory days 386, payable days 171, cash-conversion cycle 335 days (309 in FY25); working-capital days 226. Inventory ~₹430–438 cr against a ₹1,000 cr order book; the company nets customer advances off inventory in its own day counts, which is why its figures look better. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Operating cash flow –₹65 cr (FY24), ₹5 cr (FY25), ₹45 cr (FY26); free cash flow negative every year FY22–FY26 (–₹16 cr in FY26). [Screener]
- Customer advances typically 10–30% with the order. [Q4 call]
- Contingent items (standalone, Mar-26): income-tax reassessment ₹1.65 cr for AY2020-21 arising from a search on Hetero Group entities; bank guarantees ₹7.8 cr; LCs ₹16.6 cr; corporate guarantees for subsidiary loans ₹57 cr (₹25 cr a year earlier). Promoters have personally guaranteed group loans (₹110–270 cr lines listed). [AR FY26 standalone note 41; consol. note 34]
- CRISIL: A/Positive; "large working capital requirement and exposure to intense competition and cyclicality"; the GScale deal "will not have any material impact" on credit profile. [CRISIL Jul-26; IP Q1FY27]

## Moat evidence
- Scale: claims to be becoming "number one" in Indian glass lining; the Indian glass-lined market is only ~₹1,500 cr by the MD's own estimate — the reason for the diversification. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Technology tie-ups: GL Hakko glass-lined shell-and-tube heat exchangers — 200+ units ordered, 100 delivered; priced at ₹12–15 lakh versus ~₹30 lakh for alternatives; India market ~₹2,000 cr; "no one" else makes them in India. Conductive and semiconductor-grade glass kept secret by making it in Japan. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Customer ties: CDMO/pharma capex customers; order book up ~40% in one quarter. [Q1 call]
- Limits: CRISIL flags "intense competition"; management admits equipment-only sales face "tight margins"; exports repeatedly delayed; GScale depends on components from Schneider, ABB, Eaton — analysts questioned whether it is a manufacturer or an assembler. [CRISIL Jul-26; Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Strategy drift:** in 12 months the company moved from glass-lined reactors to turnkey pharma projects, pre-cast buildings, nuclear/oil & gas (by FY28), semiconductor glass and AI data centres. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Working-capital statements conflict** (150 vs 320 days) and free cash flow has never been positive since listing. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- **Related parties:** many promoter-family firms deal with the company — purchases from Asahi Glass Plant Inc., Reliability Engineering Industry, ATR Asahi Process Systems and Schematic Engineering; sales to Standard Equipment Leasing Services and Schematic; rent to S2 Engineering Services/Equipment and a director; services from Stylosoft LLP. Purchases from these firms rose sharply in FY26 (several entries of ₹10–15 cr each vs ₹1–2 cr in FY25; the note's layout prevents exact totals). Remuneration to KMP and relatives ₹2.8 cr. [AR FY26 consol. note 34]
- Tax: income-tax reassessment tied to the Hetero Group search; Hetero Infrastructure also appears in board/director profiles in the annual report (the exact relationship was not established). [AR FY26 note 41; AR FY26 corporate governance section]
- Auditor: MSK A & Associates LLP, unmodified opinion; key audit matter = revenue recognition; re-appointed for a second five-year term on 18 Sep 2026. [AR FY26 auditor's report; BSE 18-Sep-26]
- Promoter pledge: the stored profile mentions promoter pledging "for personal reasons" — **not verified** in the documents read. Promoter holding steady at 60.5%; DII holding fell from 2.2% to 0.2% over five quarters. [Screener]
- Management style: guidance was vague on Q4 and very aggressive on Q1; many questions answered with "we will guide soon". [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## My read
The core business is growing fast on India's CDMO and pharma capex boom (Q1 sales +43%, order book ~₹1,400 cr), but profit is growing more slowly (margin 15–17% vs 17–18% before listing) and cash conversion is poor — free cash flow has been negative every year, and management's own working-capital numbers contradict each other. The FY27 guide of ₹1,450 cr needs H2 revenue of roughly ₹950 cr, including ₹250 cr from a data-centre business whose factory only starts in November. At the current price the market is paying for that pivot to succeed; I would want to see GScale revenue and orders actually booked, working-capital days genuinely below 200, and positive free cash flow before believing it. Any slip in the H2 ramp, more acquisitions, or rising related-party purchases would weaken the case.
