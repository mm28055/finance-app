# The Anup Engineering — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Ahmedabad-based Lalbhai (Arvind) group company making custom static process equipment: shell-and-tube and helical-baffle heat exchangers, pressure vessels, columns/towers, reactors, silos, centrifuges; licences for Lummus Helixchanger, Brembana & Rolle EMbaffle and Lummus Novolen PP reactors; ASME U, U2, S and R stamps. Customers: Reliance, IOCL, GAIL, Numaligarh, Nayara, L&T, Toyo, Technimont, Air Products, ADNOC projects. [CARE Aug-26; IP Q1FY27]
- Plants: Ahmedabad/Odhav (FY26 revenue ₹540 cr), Kheda (₹239 cr; Phase 2 done, ~8,000 TPA, ₹400–450 cr revenue potential; Phase 3 three bays "future plan"), Mabel Engineers, Chennai (100%, bought June 2024 for ₹33 cr; ₹43 cr revenue). Group capacity "up to ₹1,200 cr" revenue; Vadodara design office. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 41]
- FY26 mix: oil & gas 39%, petrochem 32%, fertiliser 9%, hydrogen 8%, others 12%; heat exchangers 56%, vessels/reactors/columns 35%. Exports ~52% of FY26 revenue (62% in FY25) per CARE. Q1 FY27: heat exchangers 60%, vessels 27%; domestic 49%, exports 49% incl. deemed exports 11%; power & energy rose to 22% of revenue. [Q4 call; CARE Aug-26; IP Q1FY27]
- New verticals: first nuclear order (NPCIL Kaiga-5/6), thermal power heat exchangers (>₹150 cr of orders in FY27), air-cooled heat exchangers (German customer; ~15% margin, volume business), skids (~₹30 cr ADNOC order), two proprietary licensed products for export, and Technical Services (field repair/maintenance; ~10 jobs worth ~₹4.5 cr in FY26). [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

- FY26 margin hit from freight, liquidated damages for a delayed order, higher legal expenses and forex loss (CARE: 20.97% vs 22.69%); a ₹1.5 cr (net) exceptional charge for the new labour codes. [CARE Aug-26; AR FY26 note 24A]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (28 May 2026): **no FY27 guidance** — "let's wait for the condition to settle"; order book ₹769 cr, ~₹200–250 cr of it with raw material not yet bought (waiting for steel to cool); FY27 to be a year of "stabilizing … consolidation and risk protection"; ₹1,200 cr enquiry pipeline at ~20% conversion; Technical Services to ₹200 cr in 3 years at ~40% margin; walked away from a ~₹200 cr order on margin. [Q4 call official]
- Q1 FY27 call (6 Aug 2026): guidance **introduced** at +5–10% consolidated revenue and "about 15%" EBITDA ("I understand this guidance sounds conservative"); order intake ₹200–250 cr a quarter; Technical Services ₹25 cr this year → ₹100 cr next → ₹200 cr in year three at >30% margin. [Q1 call official]
- CARE (10 Aug 2026), citing management: FY27 margin ~16–18% and revenue growth ~7–10%; recovery from FY28. The guidance given on the call (15%) is below CARE's band and below CARE's downgrade trigger (PBILDT <18% sustained). [CARE Aug-26]
- Delivery: Q4 promise to be net-cash-positive by end-May was met (net cash +₹1 cr by August; cash ₹45 cr vs long-term debt ₹44 cr); order intake strong (₹538 cr April–6 Aug; ~₹315 cr in Q1, "highest ever"). But Q1 revenue ₹125 cr surprised investors — an analyst noted "we did not really get that sense that Q1 is going to be this bad" on the late-May call. To hit guidance, Q2–Q4 must deliver ~₹740–780 cr (~₹250 cr a quarter, above any past quarter's ₹232 cr) at ~16–17% margin. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; Screener]

## Capex and capacity
- Kheda Phase 2A: one bay commissioned Q2 FY26, open bay Jan 2026; Phase 3 (three bays) planned. CARE expects capex of ₹40–50 cr a year (₹25 cr maintenance) with one additional bay in FY28, funded internally. [IP Q1FY27; CARE Aug-26]
- FY26 fixed assets ₹398 cr (from ₹217 cr in FY23); CWIP down to ₹2 cr — the big Kheda spend is done. ROCE 21% FY26. [Screener]
- Capacity isn't the constraint (₹1,200 cr capacity vs ₹822 cr revenue); the chairman says "we are at capacity" for this year's plan because long-cycle orders cluster in H2. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Operating cash flow weak: −₹7 cr (FY25), +₹2 cr (FY26) against operating profit of ₹168 cr and ₹177 cr; free cash flow −₹51 cr both years (Screener). CARE states FY26 cash from operations ₹127 cr — inconsistent with Screener; I use Screener. [Screener; CARE Aug-26]
- Receivables ₹416 cr at March 2026 (₹284 cr a year earlier) incl. ₹197 cr unbilled revenue; debtor days 141 → 185; working-capital days 116 → 144. ~₹260 cr billed near year-end was collected in April–June. [AR FY26 note 7; Screener; Q4 call]
- Borrowings ₹110 cr at March 2026 (₹32 cr a year earlier); net cash −₹73 cr at March, +₹1 cr by August. Gearing 0.21x, interest cover 16x (FY26). Dividend ₹12/share (~22% payout). [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call; CARE Aug-26; AR FY26 Directors' report]
- Rating: CARE AA-/Stable and A1+ reaffirmed (10 Aug 2026), limits raised to ₹681 cr + ₹143 cr; liquidity "strong"; fund + non-fund limits ~65% used. Bank guarantees outstanding ₹284 cr (₹460 cr a year earlier). [CARE Aug-26; AR FY26 note 26]

## Moat evidence
- Deep approvals: EIL, PDIL, Technip, Linde, Saipem, Toyo, Air Products; licensed proprietary designs; 500 MT single-piece capability at Ahmedabad, 1,000 MT at Kheda; delivered first 200 MT single-piece equipment to a Middle East client. [CARE Aug-26; IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Historic margins 20–23% for four years — top of Indian fabricators — but CARE flags "aggressive pricing adopted to secure orders amid heightened competition" in the last six months and management admits competition from lower-tier players in legacy products ("as long as we continue to remain in the product segments … answer is yes"). [CARE Aug-26; Q1 call]
- No price-variation clauses: all contracts fixed price; steel bought from AM/NS and Jindal after 4–6 weeks of design approval, so exposure sits between bid and approval. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Order book ₹985 cr at 6 Aug 2026 (highest ever; ~₹240 cr for FY28): heat exchangers 57%, vessels 30%; domestic 61%, exports 39% (US ~₹50 cr, Africa/Nigeria ~₹60 cr, rest Middle East, mostly ADNOC); enquiry pipeline ₹1,100 cr. Chairman says recent orders were taken "very close to our historic margins". [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Customers pay freight beyond FOB India port (99% of contracts), limiting Hormuz logistics cost. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin reset**: FY27 guided ~15% vs 21% in FY26; ~₹200–250 cr of the book was priced before steel inflation and bought late; CARE downgrade trigger is PBILDT <18% sustained. [Q1 call; Q4 call; CARE Aug-26]
- **Back-ended year**: guidance needs record quarters in H2; any slippage in long-cycle milestones or ODC shipping through Hormuz (large equipment has no road alternative) hits the year. [Q1 call]
- **Cash conversion**: two years of near-zero operating cash flow; receivables and unbilled revenue high; the auditor's key audit matter is timing of revenue recognition and contract assets. Opinion unmodified. [Screener; AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Concentration**: ~71% of FY26 revenue from refining/petchem (CARE); a ₹39 cr order is "stuck" and excluded by CARE from the executable book. [CARE Aug-26]
- Governance: clean — related-party dealings with Arvind group companies are small (licence purchase ~₹1 cr, services); a ₹38.6 cr land parcel is still titled in the old name (Anveshan Heavy Engineering), pending with the revenue department; contract workers' charter of demands pending at labour court (not quantified); tax disputes ~₹2.4 cr. Promoter holding 40.9%, unchanged; DIIs up from 11% to 16.5% in two years. [AR FY26 notes 26, 31, CARO i(c); Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 cautious and guidance-free ("we would wait for the prices to normalize"); Q1 contrite but constructive — chairman: "this performance, this quarter is short of the mark, and you're not wrong" — with a record order book and firm (low) guidance. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Numbers: order book ₹769 cr → ₹985 cr; enquiry pipeline ₹1,200 cr → ₹1,100 cr; net cash −₹11 cr (late May) → +₹1 cr; margin expectations from "21% of EBITDA?" (analyst) to ~15%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New: thermal power orders >₹150 cr, air-cooled exchangers started, two licensed export products won; Technical Services given a first-year number (₹25 cr) with the ₹200 cr three-year goal kept, while its margin claim eased from ~40% (Q4) to "above 30%" (Q1). [Q1 call]

## My read
Anup is a high-quality fabricator whose FY27 is a reset year: Q1 profit was almost nil, guidance implies roughly flat-to-lower profit (≈15% margin on ₹860–900 cr), and the rest of the year must be the best nine months in its history. The positives are real — record ₹985 cr order book with new orders near historic margins, diversification into thermal/nuclear/air-cooled exchangers, AA- balance sheet and clean governance. The price needs FY28 margins back near 19–21% with revenue above ₹1,000 cr; that depends on H2 FY27 execution proving the book is profitable. Watch Q2 revenue (needs ~₹200 cr+ and margin back above 15%), quarterly intake of ₹200–250 cr, and whether cash conversion finally improves.
