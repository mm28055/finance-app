# Tega Industries — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code TEGA. Price ₹2,092, market cap ₹15,714 cr, book value ₹454, ROCE 8.1%, ROE 5.9%, dividend yield 0.10%. Screener shows **no P/E** because TTM consolidated profit is about –₹1 cr (Q1 FY27 loss of ₹108 cr after acquisition costs). [Screener, 28-Sep-26]. Calls read: Q4 FY26 (2 Jun 2026) and Q1 FY27 (13 Aug 2026), both checked against the official BSE transcripts. Q1 FY27 is the **first quarter with Molycop, and only one month of it (June 2026)**.

## Business and segments
- Legacy Tega: polymer/rubber/steel composite **mill liners** and other wear parts (screens, trommels, pulp lifters) for mineral-processing mills — a recurring consumable bought from miners' operating budgets — plus **equipment** (screens, crushers, pumps; Tega McNally) for Indian mining, power and aggregates. Copper and gold mines are >75% of legacy revenue; mostly exported (Latin America, Australia, Africa, North America). About 75% repeat customer orders. [Profile; CRISIL May-26]
- FY26 segment revenue: consumables ₹1,434 cr (flat vs ₹1,430 cr), equipment ₹269 cr (+25% vs ₹216 cr); segment result (EBITDA-level) consumables ₹197 cr (vs ₹315 cr — hit by ₹77.5 cr acquisition costs charged to it) and equipment ₹34 cr (vs ₹25 cr). [AR FY26 MD&A segment table]
- **Molycop** (acquired 1 Jun 2026): the world's leading maker of **grinding media** (forged and high-chrome steel balls consumed inside mills) and mill liners; volume 1.204 million tonnes in the 12 months to Jun-26 (1.223 mt a year earlier); >50% share "in our core regions" (South America largest, then North America, Australasia, Africa). About 85% of contracts are indexed to steel prices, so revenue swings with steel while profit per tonne is protected. Revenue US$1,539 mn in the year to Jun-25; EBITDA US$191 mn in the year to Jun-26 (US$172 mn prior year). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 revenue mix: consumables 23%, Molycop 75%, equipment 2%. [Q1 call]
- Market: global mill-liner market ~US$2.83 bn (2025), US$2.97 bn (2026), US$4.59 bn by 2034 (Fortune Business Insights, cited in AR). Management's blended gold/copper demand growth ~3% a year to FY30; copper demand +40% by 2040 (UNCTAD), needing ~US$250 bn and ~80 new mines. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- Capacity: new Chile plant (liners) soft commissioning Jan 2027, commercial output ~Mar 2027 subject to local permits. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (2 Jun): consumables ~15% long-term CAGR "maintained"; equipment ~25% growth in FY27 at 12–13% EBITDA; legacy blended EBITDA margin 21–22%; Molycop FY27 growth ~3% (Cobre Panama and Grasberg restarts pushed to FY28); one-time Q1 deal costs "close to $30 million"; leverage to be cut to 3x over 3–4 years. [Q4 call]
- History of misses: an analyst noted consumables grew 10% (FY24), 11% (FY25) and ~0% (FY26) against a standing 15% guide, and that February 2026 guidance of 8% for FY26 was missed (flat). Management kept "15% plus on a long-term basis". [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: consumables +36% (flattered by Q4 spill-over); legacy margin 22% (in line); equipment –44% (**missed** the 25% path); one-time costs ₹191 cr ≈ US$22 mn (below the ~US$30 mn flagged); Molycop June EBITDA +3% y/y, "directionally ahead of our expectations". [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 guidance: consolidated EBITDA margin ~15% for FY27 (new); Molycop 10-month volume +~5% and EBITDA +~4% (**raised** from ~3% growth); consumables 15% long-term **kept**; US$20 mn cost synergies over 2–2.5 years (new); cross-selling numbers in "a couple of quarters". [Q1 call]
- Chile: Q4 said commissioning "by early Q3" with revenue possibly from end-Q4 or next year; Q1 says soft commissioning Jan 2027, commercial output March 2027. **Slight slip.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Group total debt ₹11,200 cr at Jun-26, including ~₹2,600 cr of redeemable preference shares; Molycop net debt US$672.5 mn (₹6,366 cr) at 30 Jun, down from US$1.0 bn at 31 Mar after US$235 mn repaid at closing. [Q1 call]
- Interest: legacy Tega finance cost ₹110–120 cr for FY27; Molycop interest plus principal ~US$70 mn for the 10 months. Group Q1 finance cost was ₹117 cr for just one month of Molycop. [Q1 call; Screener]
- CRISIL (May-26) downgraded Tega from AA- to **A+/Stable** because of the deal; expects net debt/EBITDA to peak at 4.0–4.5x in FY27 and fall to 3.0–3.5x by FY28; net cash generation >₹900 cr a year vs ₹200–300 cr debt obligations; liquidity "strong" (cash ₹282 cr at Tega + ₹431 cr at Molycop). [CRISIL May-26]
- Legacy working capital: debtor days 97, inventory days 233, payable days 178, cash-conversion cycle 153 days (FY26; 212 in FY25). Management targets DSO 100–105 days. CRISIL notes receivables of 182–219 days (gross). [Screener; Q4 call; CRISIL May-26]
- Cash flow: operating cash flow ₹252 cr (FY24), ₹195 cr (FY25), ₹350 cr (FY26); FY26 investing outflow ₹991 cr and financing inflow ₹1,690 cr reflect deal funding. Legacy net debt/equity 0.09 at Mar-26. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Molycop working capital swings with steel-bar purchases (~80% of its cost of goods), funded by an asset-based loan. [Q1 call]
- Non-core land parcels being evaluated for sale to repay debt. Dividend payout 11% in FY26. [Q1 call; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Legacy gross margin 59–62% through raw-material and freight inflation; costs passed through with about a one-quarter lag. [Q1 call]
- Switching cost: liners are designed per mill; a failed liner stops a mill, so miners stick with proven suppliers; 75% repeat orders. [CRISIL May-26; Profile]
- Molycop: >50% share in core regions, local plants for short lead times; ~85% steel-indexed contracts protect margin per tonne; EBITDA rose 11% in its last year even as volume dipped 1.6%. [Q1 call]
- Trade protection: Canada's trade tribunal (CITT, 22 Sep 2026) found Chinese forged grinding media threatens injury; anti-dumping and countervailing duties will be collected — positive for Moly-Cop Canada (Kamloops). [BSE disclosure 23-Sep-26]
- Weakness: Chinese grinding-media competition in Latin America raised by analysts; management answered only qualitatively. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Leverage:** ~₹11,200 cr group debt vs a ₹15,700 cr market cap; 4.0–4.5x net debt/EBITDA peak (CRISIL); rating already cut two notches. A copper/gold downturn or mine outages (Grasberg, Cobre Panama) would bite. [CRISIL May-26; Q1 call]
- **Earnings quality/visibility:** only one month of Molycop disclosed; purchase-price allocation provisional; goodwill ~₹5,000 cr now on the books (plus ₹73 cr older Australian goodwill, a key audit matter). [Q1 call; AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Guidance credibility:** consumables have not hit the 15% target in any of FY24–FY26; Q1 equipment fell 44%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion (Walker Chandiok & Co LLP); KAM on goodwill impairment; 8 subsidiaries (assets ₹888 cr, revenue ₹671 cr) audited by other auditors; audit-trail remarks for some entities. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Contingent liabilities are small: disputed income tax ₹8.4 cr, service tax ₹0.1 cr, sales tax ₹0.4 cr, GST ₹0.6 cr (figures in ₹ mn in the note: 83.94, 1.30, 4.36, 5.61). [AR FY26 consol. note 45A]
- Promoter holding fell from 74.8% to 67.3% (Dec-25) through the ₹1,713 cr preferential issue; former holding company Nihal Fiscal Services ceased to be the parent on 28 Nov 2025. No promoter pledge reported. [Screener; AR FY26 note 46]
- CFO change: interim CFO (S.P. Ganguly) on the Q4 call; Ravi Joshi appointed CFO by Q1. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- FX: Molycop is US$-based (except Spain, Australia) and hedges; Indian investors bear rupee translation. [Q1 call]

## My read
Tega has turned from a debt-light, high-margin (~22%) Indian niche exporter into a leveraged global grinding-media group about four times its old size, with a ~13% margin business now 75% of revenue. The legacy liner franchise is healthy (62% gross margin, 22% EBITDA margin, record order book), but its growth record lags its own 15% target, and equipment is erratic. The equity case now rests on Molycop's cash flow paying down ~₹11,200 cr of debt: roughly ₹900 cr+ of annual net cash accrual (CRISIL) against interest of several hundred crore means EPS stays thin in FY27 and should jump only as debt falls and synergies land. I would want two full quarters of Molycop numbers, net debt falling on schedule toward 3.0–3.5x by FY28, and consumables delivering 15% without spill-over help; a copper price slump or a slip in deleveraging would change the view.
