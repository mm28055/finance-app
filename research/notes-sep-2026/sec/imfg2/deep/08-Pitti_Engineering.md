# Pitti Engineering — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- India's largest maker and exporter of electrical steel laminations (the stamped steel sheets stacked inside motors and generators), now moving up the chain into stator/rotor assemblies, machined castings, shafts and fully integrated stator-frame/rotor-shaft assemblies. Customers are motor and generator OEMs: Wabtec, Siemens (incl. Siemens Mobility), Progress Rail, Caterpillar, Cummins, Voith, ABB, CG, Nidec, Marathon. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Single reported segment ("engineering products of iron and steel"); the company gives an end-market split instead. Q1 FY27 revenue mix: traction motor and railway components 28% (FY26: 33%; Q1 FY26: 36%), power generation 15%, industrial and commercial 12%, mining/oil and gas 10% (from ~5–6%), special-purpose motors 9%, data centres 5% (FY26: 3%), renewables 3%, others 17%. [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 segment note]
- Exports: ₹531 cr in FY26 (₹371 cr FY23, ₹434 cr FY24, ₹500 cr FY25) — but export share has fallen from 34% (FY24) to 27% (FY26). In Q1 FY27 direct exports were flat (about ₹137–139 cr) while domestic grew 23%. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Six plants: three in Telangana (Kothur laminations; Macharam foundry; Nandigaon fabrication), Aurangabad (Maharashtra) laminations and machined components, Tumkur and Hoskote (Karnataka) from the two acquired companies. [IP Q1FY27]
- Capacity (Q1 FY27): sheet metal 1,08,000 t/yr (was 90,000), machining 7,56,000 hours (was 6,40,800), casting 18,600 t, raised to 24,600 t on 10 Aug 2026. Utilisation: sheet metal 73% (70%), machining 86% (82%), casting 72% (69%). Machining is the bottleneck — "not the order book". [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 volumes: total lamination and assemblies 19,240 t (+18.8% YoY per IP); high value-added assemblies 4,143 t (+37%); loose/low value-added laminations 12,920 t (+16%); castings and machined components 3,191 t (+4.2% YoY per IP table; call said 14.2% — the IP table's figures give 3,191 vs 3,061 = +4.2%). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- The two acquired subsidiaries — Pitti Industries (ex-Bagadia Chaitra, laminations; FY26 revenue ₹314 cr) and Dakshin Foundry (castings; FY26 revenue ₹67.65 cr) — were merged into the parent; the NCLT scheme became effective 23 Sep 2026 with appointed date 1 Apr 2026. [Q4 call; BSE filing 23 Sep 2026]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue: FY24 ₹1,244 cr → FY25 ₹1,705 cr (+37%, acquisitions) → FY26 ₹1,913 cr (+12%). Operating profit ₹181 → ₹271 → ₹316 cr; OPM 15% → 16% → 17%. Net profit ₹90 → ₹122 → ₹118 cr (FY26 down 3% because interest rose to ₹83 cr and depreciation to ₹105 cr, and tax rate rose to 30%). [Screener]
- Quarters (revenue / operating profit / net profit, ₹ cr): Q1 FY26 457/75/23; Q2 478/78/40 (includes ₹21 cr other income, mostly Maharashtra incentive); Q3 477/81/28; Q4 501/82/27; Q1 FY27 529/86/30. OPM stuck at 16–17% for five quarters. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 company figures: revenue ₹529.1 cr (+15.9%), adjusted EBITDA ₹88.7 cr (16.8%, vs 17.1%), reported EBITDA ₹86.4 cr, finance cost ₹22.6 cr, PBT ₹36.2 cr (flat YoY), tax only ₹6.7 cr (19%) due to a deferred-tax credit on right-of-use assets, PAT ₹29.5 cr; adjusted PAT (ESOP added back) ₹31.9 cr. Other income fell to ₹0.9 cr from ₹7.4 cr. [IP Q1FY27]
- Profit before tax has not grown: ₹37 cr in Q1 FY26, ₹36 cr in Q1 FY27 — all growth in operating profit went to depreciation (+₹2.7 cr) and interest (+₹2 cr). [Screener]
- Unit economics management gave: loose lamination sells at ~₹1.7–1.8 lakh/t with EBITDA ₹15,000–18,000/t; high value-added assemblies ₹2.2–2.3 lakh/t with EBITDA ₹30,000–60,000/t; raw castings EBITDA ₹30,000–35,000/t; machined castings ₹80,000–1,00,000/t. Steel is a full pass-through, so % margin falls when steel rises even if ₹/t is stable. [Q4 call]
- ROCE: 18% (FY22–23), 19% (FY24), 17% (FY25), 15% (FY26). ROE last year 13%. [Screener]
- Standalone (pre-merger parent) FY26: revenue ₹1,590 cr (+4.3%), EBITDA ₹282 cr (+14%), PAT ₹97.5 cr (−8.7%). So most FY26 growth came from the acquired subsidiaries. [AR FY26 MD&A]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (18 May 2026): FY27 targets 78,000 t laminations (vs 69,517 t in FY26) and 16,000 t castings/machined (vs 12,012 t), "roughly ₹2,300 cr of topline" at current commodity prices, "similar margins in percentage terms". Q1 would be "slightly slower", no sharp recovery. Net debt ~₹570 cr, to fall to ~₹470 cr with ₹125 cr working-capital release; FY28 net debt ~₹250 cr if no new lamination capex. Tax rate ~33%. [Q4 call, official transcript]
- Q1 FY27 call (11 Aug 2026): targets **raised** — laminations 82,000 t (from 78,000), castings 17,000 t (from 16,000); FY27 EBITDA "roughly ₹370-odd crores"; FY28 revenue ~₹2,500 cr at 90,000 t with 17–17.2% EBITDA margin, excluding any new lamination capex. Longer-term margin "upwards of 18%… closer to 18%–18.5%". Tax rate cut to ~25% (was 33%). [Q1 call, official transcript]
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: ₹150 cr capex commissioned in Q1 (promised by end-H1 FY27) — **delivered early**. Net debt ₹491 cr at June (vs ~₹570 cr in March) — **ahead of path**. Margin flat at 16.8% — as guided ("not a sharp recovery"). Direct exports flat — the ₹20 cr of stuck March shipments did not visibly lift Q1. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Check on the ₹370 cr EBITDA target: Q1 adjusted EBITDA ₹88.7 cr; the remaining three quarters need ~₹94 cr each (+~10% on Q1). Plausible if new capacity absorbs, but margins have not moved yet. [IP Q1FY27; own arithmetic]
- Maharashtra mega-project incentive: Q4 call said it would be taken from FY28; Q1 call said it may start in FY27 if the state allows a 9-year recovery window, else FY28. ~₹40 cr/yr was cited on ₹400 cr of eligible capex; Ind-Ra quotes ₹40–50 cr/yr for seven years from FY28. About ₹70 cr of past incentive still to be received in cash over 9–12 months. FY26 Q2 had ~₹20 cr of such income that will not recur in FY27. [Q1 call; Q4 call; Ind-Ra Apr-26]

## Capex and capacity
- ₹150 cr expansion (announced 2025): ₹100 cr spent by March 2026; commissioned in Q1 FY27; took sheet metal to 1,08,000 t and casting to 24,600 t. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- ₹290 cr greenfield foundry + machining next to the Macharam (Hyderabad) foundry, announced May 2026: +11,400 t casting (to ~36,000 t) and machining from 7.2 lakh to 10.8 lakh hours. ~30% land/civil, 70% plant. Only ₹110–120 cr is foundry; the rest is machining centres added in modules from FY28. Target asset turn 1.2x, EBITDA margin 25–28%, net working capital 90–120 days. ₹60 cr spent by Q1 FY27. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Timing inconsistency:** both calls say foundry commissioning in Q1 FY30; the Q1 FY27 presentation's "Our Journey" slide says "expected to commissioned by Q1FY29" and casting at 36,000 t "by Q1FY29". [IP Q1FY27 p.10; Q1 call]
- Funding: of the ₹290 cr plus ₹50 cr pending from the earlier plan, ~₹100 cr from own funds and "the remaining is going to be funded through net debt" (Q4 call). This contrasts with Ind-Ra's April 2026 understanding that capex will be internally funded with "no major debt-funded capex". [Q4 call; Ind-Ra Apr-26]
- Next leg (not board-approved): lamination headroom is only ~8,000 t at 80% efficiency after FY27, so modular lamination capex around Q3–Q4 FY28, then a wholly owned Bengaluru plant (~₹200 cr land/building + ~₹200 cr equipment) in FY28–FY29. With ₹290 cr + ₹400 cr, management sees ₹3,000–3,300 cr revenue "comfortably"; current capacity (ex-₹290 cr) supports ~₹2,500 cr. [Q1 call]
- Asset turns so far: fixed assets rose from ₹465 cr (FY24) to ₹1,153 cr (FY26) while revenue rose ₹1,244 → ₹1,913 cr — asset turn fell from ~2.7x to ~1.7x, which is why ROCE fell. [Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Screener borrowings ₹811 cr at March 2026 (includes leases), cash ~₹147 cr. Company: consolidated net debt ₹554.6 cr, debt/equity 0.74 at March 2026; net debt ₹491 cr at June 2026. Total borrowing ₹698 cr at ~7–7.5% cost. [Screener; AR FY26 Directors' report; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Ind-Ra: net adjusted leverage (net debt incl. leases and LC acceptances / EBITDA incl. subsidy) 2.4x at 9M FY26 (FY25 2.5x, FY24 3.0x); interest cover 4.3x; unencumbered cash only ₹11 cr at Dec 2025; fund-based limits ₹450 cr at 45% average use. Repayments ₹89 cr FY27, ₹81 cr FY28. Downgrade trigger: net adjusted leverage above 2.0x on a sustained basis or major debt-led capex. [Ind-Ra Apr-26]
- Cash flow from operations: ₹80 cr (FY24), ₹289 cr (FY25), ₹205 cr (FY26); free cash flow −₹129 cr, −₹58 cr, +₹2 cr. FY25 capex was funded by a ~₹360 cr QIP (promoter stake 59.3% → 53.6%). [Screener; Ind-Ra Apr-26]
- Working capital: debtor days 39 (FY26, from 55), inventory days 124, payable days 77 (from 118) — cash conversion cycle widened from 55 to 87 days in FY26. Inventory ~₹395 cr at March 2026 vs a ₹300 cr aim, built up to secure steel; management expects it to hold at ₹390–400 cr, with payables rising ~₹125 cr. Q1 call: further working-capital release only ₹20–30 cr. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Dividend payout 8% (FY26); yield 0.2%. No buyback. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Scale leader in a fragmented stamping industry; long-standing, often sole-source relationships (Wabtec multi-year deal worth ₹500 cr signed earlier; supply to Siemens Mobility, Progress Rail — the company says it covers most global rail OEMs except Japanese makers). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; Ind-Ra Apr-26]
- Customer concentration is falling: top-10 clients 51% of revenue in FY25 (65% FY24, 68% FY23). Orders are rolling quarterly schedules against annual forecasts, so no meaningful order book. [Ind-Ra Apr-26]
- Pricing: quarterly price-variation clauses with most customers pass steel and currency moves through (with a one-month stagger), so EBITDA per tonne is protected but % margin is not a clean measure. Extra LPG/energy cost in the West Asia disruption is being recovered from customers. Forex is fully hedged. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Ind-Ra Apr-26]
- Integration (lamination + casting + machining + assembly under one roof) is the real differentiator; mix shift is visible — high value-added assemblies +37% vs loose +16% in Q1. Data centre genset platforms won with Cummins, Marathon, Nidec; Caterpillar (direct and via its vendor) under development with revenue expected from Q3 FY27. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Limits: it does not make large wind castings (20–30 t pieces are beyond its equipment) and has chosen not to enter gears or forging for now. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Returns dilution:** ROCE down from 19% to 15%; management openly says the next capex arrives "before the operating leverage comes from the Capex done". Each new plant (₹290 cr, then ~₹400 cr) keeps depreciation and interest ahead of profit. [Q1 call; Screener]
- **Leverage vs rating trigger:** Ind-Ra's downgrade trigger is sustained net adjusted leverage above 2.0x; it was 2.4x at Dec 2025, and the company intends to debt-fund most of the ₹290 cr. The rating assumes no major debt-funded capex. [Ind-Ra Apr-26; Q4 call]
- **Earnings quality:** FY24–FY26 other income (₹48 cr, ₹39 cr, ₹40 cr) was a large slice of PBT, mostly state incentive; it largely drops out of FY27. Q1 FY27 PAT was flattered by a 19% tax rate (full year ~25%). "Adjusted" EBITDA/PAT add back ESOP cost (~₹10.3 cr a year). [Screener; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Exports stalling:** direct exports flat in Q1 and share down to 27% of revenue; the "indirect exports" story cannot be verified from disclosures. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Cyclical new demand:** management itself warns mining/oil and gas "can grow very fast and correct very fast", and AI data-centre spend "may not be sustainable indefinitely". Railway share falling (38% → 28% within about five quarters). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Governance/accounting: auditors (standalone and consolidated) report no key audit matters, no qualifications, no CARO adverse remarks (no defaults, bank returns agree with books, audit trail operated). Consolidated tax disputes not acknowledged as debt total under ~₹10 cr (excise/GST/income-tax appeals); capital commitments ~₹50 cr. Related-party dealings are modest: KMP remuneration ~₹9 cr, rent/lease ~₹3.5 cr paid largely to promoter entity Pitti Electrical Equipment. [AR FY26 auditor's report, CARO, notes 25.2, 25.8]
- Disclosure slip: presentation says the greenfield will be ready by Q1 FY29, calls say Q1 FY30; the Q1 call says casting volumes grew 14.2% while the presentation's own table implies 4.2%. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from explaining a weak Q4 (energy/LPG disruption, ₹20 cr stuck shipments, gross margin down 100 bp) to confidence: volume targets raised and an explicit ₹370 cr FY27 EBITDA figure given for the first time. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New numbers: FY28 revenue ~₹2,500 cr (Q4 call: FY27 ~₹2,300 cr); margins "upwards of 18%" longer-term (Q4 call said "similar margins"). Tax rate guidance cut from ~33% to ~25%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New issue: management acknowledged ROCE dilution and floated a ~₹400 cr Bengaluru plant — a bigger, earlier capex cycle than the Q4 call implied (Q4 said lamination capacity suffices till FY28). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Dropped: the Q4 net-debt path (₹250 cr by FY28 if no new capex) was not repeated; instead "debt numbers are going to be dynamic". [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## My read
The business is genuinely improving in mix — high value-added assemblies, machined castings, data-centre and mining work are growing much faster than loose laminations, and the customer list is broadening. But the profit line is not yet showing it: operating margin has sat at 16–17% for five quarters, PBT is flat year on year, and ROCE has slid from 19% to 15% as land, buildings and new plants run ahead of revenue. The price needs the ₹370 cr FY27 EBITDA and a clear step-up towards 18% margins in H2 FY27, plus the incentive income resuming in FY28. What would change my view positively: margin above 17.5% for two quarters and net debt falling while capex continues; negatively: a further debt-funded plant (Bengaluru) before the ₹150 cr and ₹290 cr projects earn their return, or an Ind-Ra outlook change because leverage stays above 2x.
