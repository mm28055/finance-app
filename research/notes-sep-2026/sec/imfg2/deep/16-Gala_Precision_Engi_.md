# Gala Precision Engineering — deep research (Sep 2026)

Screener code GALAPREC (consolidated). Price ₹1,149, market cap ₹1,472 cr, P/E 38.7, 52-week range ₹648–1,260 [Screener, 28-Sep-26]. Listed Sep-2024 (IPO).

## Business and segments
- Makes precision springs and special fasteners: **DSS** (disc and strip springs, Gallock wedge-lock washers), **SFS** (special fastening solutions — high-tensile studs, bolts, anchor bolts, nuts, mostly for wind turbines) and **CSS** (coil and spiral springs for autos, off-highway, railways). 800+ SKUs, 175 active customers in 25 countries; reported as one segment. [IP Aug-26; AR FY26 segment note]
- FY26 mix: DSS ~49%, SFS ~34% (₹108 cr, +64%), CSS ~17%. Q1 FY27: DSS 54% (+31% YoY), SFS 29% (₹22.3 cr), CSS 17% (₹12.5 cr). By end-market in Q1: industrial 41%, renewables 33%, mobility 26%. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Aug-26]
- Geography Q1 FY27: India 66%, Europe 25%, Americas 6%, rest 3%; exports 33.7%. FY26 exports ₹111.5 cr of ₹314.3 cr (35%). Management expects exports to stay 35–40%. [IP Aug-26; AR FY26 segment note; Q4 call]
- Customers include Vestas, Siemens Energy, GE Vernova, ABB, Schaeffler, John Deere. One customer was 16.0% of FY26 revenue (23.6% in FY25). Wind fasteners ~25–26% of Q1 sales. [IP Aug-26; AR FY26 note; Q1 call]
- Capacity: Wada (Palghar, Maharashtra) — DSS 225.5 million units at ~85% use, CSS 20.9 million units at ~70–75%, SFS 4.2 lakh units at ~75–78%. Chennai (SIPCOT Vallam-Vadagal) fastener plant 4,600 t a year, ~35–40% used in FY26; Phase 1 at 70–80% in Q1 FY27. [IP Aug-26; Q1 call]
- Claimed ~70% share of domestic renewable disc springs and ~15% of the domestic wind-turbine special-fastener market (company claims, Lattice industry report cited). [IP Aug-26]
- One subsidiary, Gala Springs LLP, and a small step-down subsidiary in Shanghai (loss ₹0.07 cr in FY26). [AR FY26 p.58, consolidation note]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue: FY24 ₹203 cr → FY25 ₹238 cr → FY26 ₹314 cr (+32%); TTM ₹327 cr. 3- and 5-year sales CAGR 24%. [Screener]
- Operating profit: FY24 ₹39 cr (19%) → FY25 ₹41 cr (17%) → FY26 ₹52 cr (17%). Net profit ₹22 cr → ₹27 cr → ₹35 cr. [Screener]
- Last five quarters (revenue / OPM / net profit): Q1 FY26 ₹63.1 cr / 15.3% / ₹6.5 cr; Q2 ₹71.4 cr / 15.6% / ₹8.4 cr; Q3 ₹85.3 cr / 17.1% / ₹8.3 cr; Q4 ₹94.6 cr / 17.6% / ₹12.2 cr; Q1 FY27 ₹75.4 cr / 16.3% / ₹8.2 cr. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 was +19.5% YoY but −20% QoQ; management says Q1 is normally 20–22% of the year and a ~₹3 cr dispatch was held back for non-payment ("otherwise my growth rate would have been 25%"). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- FY26 EBITDA margin 16.5% vs 17.2% in FY25 and 19.2% in FY24; management blamed a ₹3.23 cr forex loss (~1 point). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- ROCE 16% (FY26) vs 23% in FY24 — diluted by IPO cash and the Chennai build; ROE 13%. [Screener]
- Tax rate unusually low: 18% FY26, 15% in Q1 FY27 (R&D deduction, ESOP perquisites, accelerated depreciation; captive solar from Q3). [Screener; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (May-26): revenue growth 20–25% a year; EBITDA margin 17–19% "by FY27 end"; Chennai ~₹80 cr revenue in FY27, capacity ₹120 cr a year after Phase 2 (Phase 2 "completing by June end, July"); Chennai at ₹5 cr a month "in April, May, June"; Wada land "June end earliest or maybe July"; ~₹50 cr capex "for the current year"; inventory days held at ≤103 and cash flow/EBITDA improved ~10%; offshore wind ~10% of fastener sales in 2–3 years. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery (Aug-26): revenue +19.5% (guide kept at 20–25%, "achieving or crossing that"); EBITDA margin 16.3% (below the 17–19% band — call said 16.51%, deck and Screener say 16.3%); Chennai dispatch ~₹4 cr a month, not ₹5 cr; Phase 2 slipped — mezzanine done in Q2, full Phase 2 in Q3/Q4; Wada land only at MoU/due diligence, capex of ₹40–45 cr pushed "majority… next year" (FY28); working capital "similar level" pending a KPMG study. [Q1 call]
- Offshore-wind target was moved closer: "by end of this year… 10% level" of fastener sales (Q4 call said 2–3 years). [Q1 call vs Q4 call]
- Verdict: revenue and margin guidance **kept**, but three dated milestones (Chennai run-rate, Phase 2, Wada land) **slipped** by one to two quarters.

## Capex and capacity
- Chennai plant was funded from the IPO: ₹45.4 cr allotted and fully spent. Phase 2 adds ~₹5 cr a month of revenue capacity, taking Chennai to ~₹120 cr a year; after that, the Chennai site has no spare land (backup: move stud making to a leased shed). [IP Q1FY27 slide 12; Q4 call]
- IPO raised ₹121 cr (fresh issue); ~₹27.7 cr sat unspent in the monitoring account/FDs at Jun-26, mostly earmarked for Wada capex (₹29.7 cr object). [IP Q1FY27 slide 12]
- New Wada land: MoU for 10.15 acres next to the existing plant; ~50,000 sq ft shed for fasteners and disc springs; capex ₹40–45 cr, mostly FY28; 15 months (±3) from land to production; funded by internal accruals and bank debt. Rule of thumb from management: capex ≈ 35–40% of the added annual sales, i.e. asset turn ~2.5–3x. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- In-house hot-dip galvanising (HDG) plant at Chennai commissioned in Q1 FY27; one bolt customer required it before placing orders. 1.8 MW open-access solar ordered. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- FY26 cash capex on Screener only ₹11 cr investing outflow (net of FD movements), while fixed assets + CWIP rose ₹28 cr. Capital commitments ₹12.9 cr at Mar-26. [Screener; AR FY26 note]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹38 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹24 cr (Mar-25); interest ₹3 cr a year; plus unspent IPO money in deposits — effectively low or no net debt. CRISIL: BBB+/Positive, A2 (Dec-25), gearing ~0.1x. [Screener; CRISIL Dec-25]
- Cash conversion is the weak point: operating cash flow ₹10 cr in FY26 on ₹52 cr of operating profit (34%) and ₹3 cr in FY25 (24%); free cash flow −₹31 cr (FY25) and −₹28 cr (FY26). [Screener]
- Working capital: inventory 257 days, debtors 95 days, payables 57 days — cash cycle 294 days (Screener basis). Management's own figure: inventory ~103 days, overall ~180 days. The gap is definitional (Screener uses cost of goods). KPMG hired in July for a working-capital study; actions from Q3. [Screener ratios; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Current assets reported to banks differed from the books by up to ₹7.2 cr a quarter (valuation differences); disclosed and reconciled. [AR FY26 note on borrowings]
- Forex: forward cover cut from 70% to 40% of expected export receipts. [Q1 call]
- No dividend yet. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Switching costs: 12–24 months to qualify with an OEM (plant audit, samples, pilot orders), after which share rises — some customers went from 5–10% to 50–75% share; "in last 25 years, we never lost a customer" for quality or delivery. [Q1 call]
- Cost position: offers 10–20% savings vs European rivals while "maintaining our margins"; raw material is contractually passed through with a lag. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- New approvals: a European seatbelt-retractor spring source approval in July 2026 (replacing a German supplier, slow ramp); first bulk order from an Indian electrolyser maker; bolts M27–M72 approved by a global wind OEM. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Order book ₹110 cr firm at 1-Jul-26 vs ₹80–85 cr a year earlier (~+30–40%); new products are ~30–35% of the order book. [Q1 call]
- Margins across DSS/SFS/CSS are similar at 17–19% EBITDA per management — so no single product is a high-margin fortress. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Income-tax search**: the Income Tax Department began proceedings at the registered office and at promoter Kirit Gala's residence on 18-Aug-26; the company said on 24-Aug-26 the proceedings had concluded with "no material impact on the operations". Outcome (any demand) unknown. [BSE filing 24-Aug-26; News: Reuters/TradingView, Aug-26]
- **Patent suit**: Nord-Lock AB sued Gala over Gallock wedge-lock washers; claim ₹2 cr shown as contingent, but plaintiffs reserved the right to claim on all infringing sales after trial; still at pleadings stage. [AR FY26 contingent liabilities note; Q4 call]
- Contingent liabilities otherwise small: disputed income tax ₹3.3 cr, bank guarantees ₹1.0 cr, LCs ₹5.5 cr. [AR FY26 note]
- Customer concentration: top customer 16% of revenue; wind is ~a third of sales and cyclical. [AR FY26; IP Aug-26]
- Cash burn while growing; working capital nearly 300 days on Screener's basis.
- Execution slippage on three milestones in one quarter (above).
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter only on property, plant and equipment/capex. Related-party dealings small (director pay ₹3.2 cr; promoter's son employed as VP). Promoter holding drifted from 55.5% to 54.3%. [AR FY26 auditor's report p.91; RPT note p.194; Screener shareholding]
- EU CBAM (carbon border tax) may cancel the benefit of the India–EU FTA's zero duty from Jan-27, per management. [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- From "capacity and diversification" to "efficiency and working capital": KPMG appointment, dispatch held for payment, Chennai run-rate lower than stated in May.
- New wins announced (electrolyser order, retractor-spring approval, HDG-linked bolt customer); FTA/CBAM talk dropped.
- An income-tax search (after the Q1 call) is the biggest new issue; not discussed on either call.

## My read
Gala is a real niche compounder: sticky OEM approvals, a growing order book (₹110 cr, up ~30–40%), and a credible move from springs into much larger fastener markets. But it is growing on the balance sheet — two years of negative free cash flow, ~300-day working capital, and Q1 showed milestones slipping and a customer not paying on time. The August income-tax search adds a governance question the market will want answered. At ~39x trailing earnings the price assumes 20–25% growth with margins moving into 17–19% and cash conversion improving; the KPMG outcome in Q3 and any tax-search demand are the two things that would change the view.
