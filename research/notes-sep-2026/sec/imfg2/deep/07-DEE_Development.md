# DEE Development Engineers — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code DEEDEV. Price ₹666, market cap ₹5,012 cr, P/E 61.1, book value ₹129, ROCE 10.7%, ROE 9.3%, dividend yield 0.15% [Screener, 28-Sep-26]. Listed June 2024. Calls read: Q4 FY26 (22 May 2026) and Q1 FY27 (6 Aug 2026), both checked against the official BSE transcripts.

## Business and segments
- India's largest maker of **prefabricated process piping** by installed capacity (93,500 MTPA piping; 32,400 MTPA heavy fabrication): high-pressure/high-temperature pipe spools, induction bends and fittings for coal and gas power plants (boilers, HRSGs — heat-recovery steam generators behind gas turbines — and gas-turbine piping), oil & gas/refineries, chemicals and fertilisers; plus heavy fabrication (wind-turbine towers, structures) and a new seamless alloy-pipe plant (P91/P92 thick-wall pipes). Seven manufacturing facilities across India and Thailand, including Palwal (Haryana), Anjar (Gujarat, 30,000 MTPA fabrication added in FY26) and Thailand (Bangkok, job work, ~14,500 MT). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- **Non-core:** biomass power plants — Malwa Power (Muktsar, a wholly owned subsidiary) and a plant at Abohar selling to PSPCL (Punjab's utility) — and a new 72,000 MTPA biomass pellet plant (commissioned mid-Q1 FY27) for co-firing in thermal plants. Non-core revenue target ~₹80 cr in FY27. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; CARE Oct-25]
- Customers: GE, Nooter/Eriksen, Siemens (from next year), Toshiba, Doosan, L&T, BHEL, Thermax, BPCL, Dangote. Top-10 customers were 58% of FY25 income; top two orders were ~75% of the order book (Sept 2025). [CARE Oct-25; Q1 call]
- Mix: FY27 revenue ~65–70% power; exports historically >50% of revenue, ~50% this year. Order book ₹1,228 cr (Mar-25) → ₹1,940 cr (Mar-26) → **₹2,428 cr (Jun-26)**; Q1 intake ₹781 cr incl. a ₹386.8 cr BPCL piping order. HRSG work in the book: ~₹400 cr India, ~₹200 cr Thailand. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Capacity reservations: Nooter/Eriksen holds 60% of Thailand capacity; Thailand "100% booked for the coming 3 years"; GE LOI for 15–16 HRSG units not yet converted to a purchase order; Siemens MoM for gas-turbine piping of ~10 units next year, 15 the year after, 25–30 in year three (~€1–1.5 mn per unit). [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Key numbers
- Sales FY24 ₹789 cr → FY25 ₹827 cr → FY26 ₹1,142 cr (+38%); operating profit ₹109 cr → ₹129 cr → ₹196 cr; OPM 14% → 16% → 17%; net profit ₹26 cr → ₹44 cr → ₹77 cr. TTM sales ₹1,213 cr, profit ₹80 cr. Three-year sales growth 24% a year. [Screener]
- Last five quarters, sales / operating profit / net profit (₹ cr): Jun-25 224/36/13; Sep-25 270/44/18; Dec-25 287/48/19; Mar-26 362/64/28; Jun-26 294/50/16. OPM 16% → 16% → 17% → 18% → 17%. Interest rose from ₹11 cr to ₹17 cr a quarter. [Screener]
- Company: FY26 operating EBITDA ₹189.3 cr (16.7% vs 15.0%); core-business EBITDA (incl. other income) ₹210.5 cr (+64%), i.e. non-core lost ~₹20 cr. Q1 FY27 revenue ₹294.5 cr (+31.6%), operating EBITDA ₹49.7 cr (16.9%, +38.7%), PAT ₹16.1 cr (+22%); ~₹25 cr of oil & gas export dispatches slipped to Q2 because of the Middle East. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Unit economics: Thailand earns ~₹150 cr on ~8,700 t of job-work (~₹160/kg, no material), so its EBITDA margin is well above 20%; seamless pipes expected to earn ≥20% EBITDA. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

- Q4 FY26 call (22 May): FY27 revenue ≥₹1,500 cr ("committed"), consolidated EBITDA margin >19%, order inflow >₹2,000 cr (~60% power; the inflow target "definitely contingent on the BHEL orders"), working-capital days ~180 in FY27 then 150; capex ₹20–30 cr; ₹2,500 cr revenue by FY30; clean audit opinion "by Q3"; non-core revenue ~₹47.7 cr. [Q4 call (official)]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +31.6% (on track for ₹1,500 cr only with a strong H2 — Q1 was ~20% of it); EBITDA margin 16.9% (below the >19% full-year guide; management says Q1 is seasonally weakest); inflow ₹700–780 cr; working-capital days 263 (vs 276), still far from 180. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 guidance: **kept** ₹1,500 cr+ (₹1,150–1,250 cr piping) and >19% margin ("100%, without any doubt"); inflow ">₹2,000 cr"; ₹2,500 cr target **pulled forward** to FY29; non-core **raised** to ~₹80 cr; Anjar 60–65% utilised this year, ~100% by end-FY28 (elsewhere "presently ~50%"); nuclear JV partner by Q2; GE HRSG purchase order **delayed**; BHEL orders "slower than our expectations". [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY25–FY26 growth capex (Anjar expansion to 30,000 MTPA; seamless pipe plant; total project cost ~₹144 cr per CARE) is done; fixed assets rose from ₹422 cr to ₹654 cr in FY26. FY27 capex ₹20–30 cr, plus a possible nuclear-piping facility (not yet approved). [Screener; Q4 call; CARE Oct-25]
- Anjar peak revenue potential ~₹1,500 cr by FY28–29; group asset turn 1.39x (Q1 FY27). [Q1 call]
- **Preferential issue:** ₹300 cr at ₹502/share allotted 8 Jul 2026 to institutions and promoters; ~₹224.5 cr used to repay working-capital debt, rest for general purposes. Promoter holding 70.18% → 65.13%. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- AGM (23 Sep 2026) raised authorised capital from ₹85 cr to ₹95 cr and approved conversion of the consortium's (Bank of India-led) loans into equity **upon an event of default** — a standard lender clause, but it shows lenders' terms. [BSE AGM outcome 24-Sep-26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹512 cr (Mar-25) → ₹771 cr (Mar-26); net debt ₹733 cr (Mar-26) → ₹718 cr (Jun-26); target ≤₹400–425 cr by Mar-27 after the equity raise; "no plan for any new debt". [Screener; Q1 call]
- **Cash burn:** operating cash flow –₹118 cr (FY25) and ₹20 cr (FY26); free cash flow –₹282 cr and –₹165 cr. [Screener]
- Working capital: Screener FY26 debtor days 123, inventory days 439, payable days 75, cash-conversion cycle 486 days. Company (Q1 FY27): inventory 174 days (243 a year ago), debtors 123 (99), creditors 34 (66), net 263 days (276) — creditors kept low deliberately (MSME 45-day rule; cash purchases for discounts). Goal 180–200 days. [Screener; Q1 call]
- CARE (Oct 2025): A-/Stable, A2+; flags "substantial exposure towards wholly owned subsidiaries", working-capital intensity and customer concentration; positive trigger = operating cycle below 150 days. [CARE Oct-25]
- Dividend ₹1/share for FY26 (first since listing). [BSE AGM outcome]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and certification: largest Indian capacity; in India "practically no competition except for one or two players with significantly less capacity"; global rivals are Korean and Turkish fabricators. [Q4 call]
- Capacity reservations by global HRSG makers (Nooter/Eriksen 60% of Thailand; GE LOI; Siemens MoM) show scarce, qualified capacity amid the US gas-turbine boom. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Backward integration into P91/P92 seamless pipe (previously imported from China/Europe) — approvals being obtained; ₹68 cr independent L&T order and ₹80–90 cr of captive use in a ₹200 cr BHEL order. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Weakness: gross margin falling as more jobs include material (power) instead of job work; customer concentration. [Q1 call; CARE Oct-25]

## Risks and red flags
- **Qualified audit opinion (second year running):** S.R. Batliboi & Co. LLP (EY) could not assess impairment of ₹47.6 cr of assets at Malwa Power after its power-purchase agreement expired on 27 Apr 2025. Management promised a clean opinion "by Q3". [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report; Q4 call]
- **Emphasis of matter:** PSPCL's appeal at APTEL and DEE's writ in the Punjab & Haryana High Court over PSPCL's **retrospective downward tariff revision** from 1 Jan 2024 on the Abohar biomass plant; no provision made (sub judice). [AR FY26 auditor's reports]
- Tariff inconsistency: Q4 call said Malwa tariff revised to ₹5.22/kWh from ₹3.50 (deck: ₹5.224 with 5% escalation); the Q1 call cited ₹5.44/kWh. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Cash conversion is the key weakness: negative free cash flow two years running despite rising profit, funded by debt and then equity. [Screener]
- Concentration and delays: GE HRSG PO not placed; BHEL tendering slow; top two orders ~75% of book (Sept 2025). [Q1 call; CARE Oct-25]
- Contingent liabilities: income-tax, GST and customs demands (the note's layout does not allow exact totals; they appear to be in the tens of crores), plus export obligations of ₹202 cr under duty-free import licences (normal, must be fulfilled). [AR FY26 standalone note on contingent liabilities]
- Promoter family on the board (Bansal family directors re-appointed); promoter holding now 65%. [BSE AGM outcome; Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: capex complete, 1,940 cr book, >19% margin, 2,000 cr inflow; the auditor's qualification questioned; equity raise "on the drawing board".
- Q1: ₹300 cr equity raised and debt cut; book jumped to ₹2,428 cr on the BPCL order; ₹2,500 cr target pulled to FY29; nuclear-JV partner search; but GE PO delayed, BHEL slow, ₹25 cr dispatch slip and the power division's EBIT turned negative again.
- Stored transcripts vs official: both are **tidied copies (figures match)**. The official Q1 text says non-core revenue of ₹80 cr "for FY26" (a slip for FY27); the stored copy says FY27. [BSE transcripts]

## My read
DEE is riding two real cycles — the global gas-turbine/HRSG boom and India's thermal-power restart — with scarce qualified capacity, a record ₹2,428 cr order book and capex now complete, so revenue can compound and margins can widen as Anjar fills. The weak spots are cash (free cash flow –₹447 cr over FY25–FY26, inventory days in the hundreds), a second qualified audit opinion on the Malwa power subsidiary, and a tariff dispute with PSPCL. At the current price the market is paying for the ₹1,500 cr/19% FY27 guide and ₹2,500 cr by FY29; I would watch H1 margin (needs to move toward 19%), working-capital days falling toward 200, net debt reaching ~₹400–425 cr by March, conversion of the GE LOI, and removal of the audit qualification. A miss on any two of these would change the view.
