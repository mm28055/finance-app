# John Cockerill India (JCIL) — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

Note: JCIL reports on a calendar year (CY). "Q2 CY26" = April–June 2026 (the dashboard's "Q1 FY27"); "Q1 CY26" = January–March 2026 (the dashboard's "Q4 FY26").

## Business and segments
- Designs, manufactures and installs steel-processing lines: cold rolling mills, pickling lines, continuous galvanising and colour-coating lines, acid regeneration plants, plus "value services" (spares, revamps, roll coating). Plants at Taloja and Hedavali (Maharashtra). Single reported segment. [AR CY25 note 39; CARE Apr-26]
- Customers are large steelmakers: Tata Steel, JSW (incl. JSW JFE Electrical Steel), Jindal, ArcelorMittal (consolidated). Top five customers ≈ 80% of revenue. Exports ~28% of standalone CY25 revenue (Bangladesh, Spain, US, Mexico, Europe). [Q2 call; CARE Apr-26]
- **Group restructuring:** from 1 Jan 2026 JCIL bought 100% of John Cockerill Metals International SA (the parent's metals businesses in China, Germany and Belgium) from its Belgian parent, John Cockerill SA, for €29.6 m (~₹320 cr); €5 m cash by June 2026 and the rest over five years interest-free; the US metals business is to follow later (total deal up to €50 m, ~₹500 cr). On the Q2 call management said the parent agreed to take convertible preference shares instead of cash, lifting its stake back above 72%. [CARE Apr-26; Q1 call; Q2 call]
- Parent-owned technologies: JVD (jet vapour deposition zinc coating) is inside JCIL; **Volteron** (electrolytic iron/green-steel) IP "belongs to John Cockerill SA today and not John Cockerill India Limited" and is excluded from the consolidation; how JCIL benefits is "under evaluation". The hydrogen business also stays with the parent. [Q2 call; Q1 call; IP Q2CY26]
- Value services were about one-third of Q1's level in Q2 CY26; management aims for 30–35% of revenue in 3–5 years. New thermal-spray roll-coating facility at Taloja began operating in June 2026 (trial orders); Shanghai office opened; China assembly workshop planned for Q3 CY26. [Q2 call; Q1 call; IP Q2CY26]

- Order book: standalone ~₹2,262 cr and consolidated ~₹4,600 cr at June 2026 (₹1,300 cr standalone and ₹3,300 cr consolidated at March). Q2 intake ~₹1,200 cr, incl. ## Guidance history
- Q1 CY26 call (May 2026): consolidated EBITDA "around 3%" now, target "more than 10% over the next three years", and "in the middle of the path" (~5–6%) by the beginning of 2027; margins to improve "from next quarter onward". US consolidation "by the end of the year" or slightly later. Board was weighing a fund-raise. [Q1 call, official]
- Q2 CY26 delivery: **Missed** — consolidated EBITDA margin fell to −9.1% (from positive in Q1); management now blames project timing (new orders in early, cost-heavy phase), a smaller share of high-margin services, one-off integration costs and the new burden of group R&D and compliance costs. Promises H2 ramp-up. [Q2 call; IP Q2CY26]
- Long-term "North Star": ₹8,000 cr revenue by **CY30** (official transcript) via JVD, Volteron and acquisitions. For CY26 the earlier ~₹2,000 cr pro forma revenue guide: "we may not be at 2,000, but… should not be very far" if the US is included. First JVD order hoped for in 2026; order sizes cited €50–100 m. [Q2 call official]
- Taloja roll-coating facility: repeatedly delayed, finally operational June 2026. [Q2 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: "capex is limited"; main spend was the roll-coating facility (standalone CWIP ₹2.6 cr). The real capital outlay is the acquisition (~₹320 cr for China/Europe; US later), paid mostly by deferred consideration/preference shares rather than cash. [Q1 call; IP Q2CY26; CARE Apr-26]
- Intangible assets under development rose to ₹18 cr (consolidated) — capitalised R&D (JVD etc.). [IP Q2CY26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Standalone cash fell from ~₹227 cr (Dec 2025) to ~₹64 cr (June 2026) while standalone non-current investments of ₹257 cr (the acquisition) and ₹214 cr of other non-current financial liabilities (deferred consideration) appeared. Consolidated current borrowings ₹27 cr appeared in June 2026. [IP Q2CY26 balance sheets]
- Business runs on customer advances: contract liabilities ₹585 cr consolidated (June 2026). CARE: gross current assets over 400 days; average receivables 353 days, creditors 170 days (CY25); fund-based limits unused. [IP Q2CY26; CARE Apr-26]
- Cash flow from operations (standalone): CY24 −₹66 cr, CY25 +₹159 cr (advances). [Screener standalone]
- Dividend yield 0.1%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Global Tier-1 technology in downstream steel processing; competes with SMS, Danieli, Primetals. Long relationships with Tata and JSW and repeat orders; MoU with SAIL (no orders disclosed). [Q2 call; Q1 call; CARE Apr-26]
- JVD is a genuine differentiator (1 m t already produced at ArcelorMittal's Belgian plant; claimed 2x faster than hot-dip), but no commercial JVD order has been signed yet — the first is delayed by the customer's "investment process validation". [IP Q2CY26; Q2 call]
- Weakness: Indian market "quite competitive", so material margin is lower in India than abroad; fixed-price contracts expose it to input costs. [Q1 call; CARE Apr-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Loss-making at group level** after the restructuring; margin target already missed one quarter after being set. [Screener consolidated; Q1 call; Q2 call]
- **Related-party economics favour the parent**: JCIL pays the parent a brand fee of 0.6% of net sales plus referral/technical royalty of 3%/2%/1% on contract slabs (₹1.8 cr and ₹2.7 cr in CY25); it now bears shared group R&D/compliance costs; and the most valuable new technology (Volteron) stays with the parent. [AR CY25 note 34; Q2 call]
- **Contingent liabilities are large relative to net worth**: service-tax "works contract" demands (2010–2017) of ₹236 cr including penalty and interest, under appeal at CESTAT since 2017 (pre-deposits ~₹4.3 cr), vs standalone net worth ₹209 cr. [AR CY25 note 35]
- **Customer arbitration**: after CY25 year-end a customer served an arbitration notice claiming liquidated damages, extra cost and lost profit for an allegedly under-performing cold rolling mill; management relies on a provision made in an earlier year. (The stored profile calls this "Santander"; the annual report does not name the customer — unverified.) [AR CY25 note 35]
- CARE rating is modest — CARE BBB / A3+ on **Rating Watch with Developing Implications** — pending clarity on the acquired businesses' finances. [CARE Apr-26]
- Promoter sold ~4.6% (75% → 70.4%) in Q4 CY25; explained only as the parent's "priorities". Non-executive director Vivek Bhide resigned 15 Sep 2026. [Screener shareholding; Q2 call; BSE filing 10 Sep 2026]
- Audit: key audit matter on revenue recognition for long-term projects (₹300 cr of CY25 revenue on the input method, incl. liquidated-damages assessment); SAP audit trail not enabled at database level. [AR CY25 auditor's report]
- Disclosure: management could not quantify the one-off costs in Q2, and the Q1 CY26 EBITDA reported on the call (₹4.9 cr, 1.4%) differs from what the H1 table now implies (~₹8.4 cr). [Q2 call; Q1 call; IP Q2CY26]

## My read
JCIL has become the listed hub of a global steel-equipment business, and orders prove the demand: the consolidated book has grown to ~₹4,600 cr, about 3.5–4 times Q2's annualised revenue. But the combined business loses money, margin guidance given in May was missed within one quarter, and the structure tilts value towards the parent (brand and royalty fees, shared R&D costs, Volteron IP retained, promoter selling before the deal). Large contingent tax liabilities and a customer arbitration add risk on a thin standalone equity base. The price needs consolidated EBITDA to turn clearly positive in H2 CY26 and a first JVD order; a clear positive would be margins reaching ~5% by early 2027 as promised, while another loss-making half, a cash-hungry US acquisition, or an adverse CESTAT/arbitration outcome would make me more cautious.
