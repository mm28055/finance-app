# JNK India — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Thane-based designer and EPC supplier of process fired heaters, reformers (hydrogen/syngas) and cracking furnaces for refineries, petrochemicals and fertiliser plants, plus flares and incinerators, and small renewable EPC (hydrogen fuelling, solar). About 120 in-house thermal/mechanical engineers; fabrication largely outsourced to approved shops near sites; own 5,000 MT export fabrication yard in the Mundra SEZ (20,243 sq m). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Promoter JNK Global (Korea, formerly JNK Heaters; KOSDAQ-listed) holds 32.6%; total promoter group 67.8%. Export heater/reformer/cracker orders are won by JNK Global and subcontracted back-to-back to JNK India; domestic orders JNK India can bid alone if qualified; if JNK India books an export heater order in its own name it pays JNK Global up to 2% technical fee. The cooperation agreement cited in the DRHP (3-year term) is "not officially renewed" — management believes it continues automatically. [AR FY26 shareholding note; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Segments FY26 (consolidated): Combustion equipment ₹759.7 cr revenue, segment profit ₹139.2 cr; Process equipment (JNK Chemdist, from 1 Oct 2025) ₹58.8 cr, segment profit ₹2.5 cr. Product mix FY26: heating 72.7%, process plant 17.4%, special fabricated 3.1%, flares/incinerators/other 6.3%. Geography: domestic ₹734.6 cr, exports ₹83.6 cr (10%). [AR FY26 consolidated note 44; Q4 call]
- **Customer concentration**: one customer gave ₹248.7 cr (30% of FY26 revenue) — matching ₹247.9 cr of "sale of goods" to JNK Global's India project office (BPCL Bina refinery-petchem project, executed with JNK Global). [AR FY26 note 44E; related-party note]
- JNK Chemdist Technologies: 51% JV formed Aug 2025 (Sunil Dhole 23%, Tushar Wagh 23%, Milind Joshi 3%) that bought the Chemdist Process Solutions business and Chemdist Membrane IP for ₹41.6 cr (incl. ₹2.8 cr royalty to the founders over 5 years); goodwill ₹1.7 cr. Technology: ethanol → ethyl acetate with green hydrogen as by-product; first plant (Hydrogen Valley, Pune, ~₹50 cr) is the proving project. [AR FY26 note 50; Q4 call; Q1 call]

- Quarters (revenue / operating profit / net profit, ₹ cr): Q1 FY26 99/3.3/1.1; Q2 178/16.5/13.0; Q3 203/25.9/17.6; Q4 338/46.2/33.0; Q1 FY27 180/15.9/9.6. OPM 3% → 9% → 13% → 14% → 9%. [Screener]
- Company figures include other income in "total income" and "EBITDA": Q1 FY27 total income ₹186 cr (+80.6%), EBITDA ₹21.9 cr (11.8%), PAT ₹9.6 cr; standalone EBITDA 14% on ₹170 cr; Chemdist revenue ₹16.5 cr (8.8% of group) with a ~₹3.6 cr operating loss. FY26: total income ₹838 cr, EBITDA ₹111.3 cr (13.3%), PAT ₹64.8 cr. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Margin history: the FY25 collapse (10%) came from legacy low-margin orders and a switch from output to input (cost-incurred) revenue recognition; quarterly company EBITDA climbed 7% → 12% → 14% → 15% through FY26. Management says older 20%+ margins came from small ₹50–100 cr product orders; with ₹500 cr+ EPC orders "14% to 15% is more sustainable". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 gross margin 26.9% (23.5% a year earlier); employee cost ₹18.4 cr vs ₹13.2 cr. [IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (May 2026): FY27 revenue growth 25–30% ("around 25% to 30% for the next two years"), EBITDA 14–15%, order intake ₹1,300–1,500 cr at a 25–30% hit rate on a ₹4,000 cr pipeline, order book to "cross around ₹2,000 cr" by next March; Dangote Phase 2 fired heaters to be finalised in Q1 FY27 (June–July), reformers Q2–Q3; an export waste-gas package (₹200–250 cr) "a week or two" from closing; H1:H2 revenue ~40:60; capex ₹10–15 cr for a small domestic facility. [Q4 call official]
- Q1 FY27 call (Aug 2026): revenue growth "around 20% to 25% remains intact" and EBITDA "about 12% to 14%" — both **lower** than Q4, though management said "nothing has changed as far as our margin projection goes" and promised to recheck; pipeline raised to ₹6,000 cr (50:50 export/domestic; 60% heating, 40% non-heating); hit rate 20–25% on heating, ~10% on new sectors; Dangote refinery and fertiliser orders now "Q2, Q3"; Chemdist to be profitable for the full year and 10–15% of revenue; no debt raising for 4–6 quarters, but bank-guarantee limits need raising. [Q1 call official]
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: order book fell from ₹1,961 cr to ₹1,801 cr (net Q1 inflow roughly ₹20 cr against a ₹1,300–1,500 cr annual target); the export incinerator order for ADNOC's TA'ZIZ salt project (via CC7 Emirates, "large" ₹100–300 cr, received 8 June) was cancelled on 16 July because the licensor had not approved JNK; Dangote heaters not yet awarded; the Q1 presentation had arithmetic errors in standalone PAT rows, re-filed. Revenue itself was on track (+82% on Screener). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; News: ScanX, Jul 2026]
- Track record: FY26 was delivered (+72% revenue, margins rebuilt as promised through the year); FY25 badly missed (revenue flat, margin halved). [Screener; Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: fixed assets ₹48 cr, CWIP ₹8 cr (March 2026); capital commitments only ₹0.5 cr. Planned ₹10–15 cr small domestic facility for critical fabrication and storage. Management says capacity is really engineers and working capital, not plant. [Screener; AR FY26 note 35; Q4 call]
- IPO (April 2024, ₹415/share): ₹264.6 cr of fresh money earmarked for working capital, ₹264.4 cr used by March 2026 (small ₹0.3 cr delay carried into FY27). [AR FY26 Directors' report]
- FY26 investing outflow ₹162 cr on Screener mostly reflects deposits/investments and the Chemdist purchase (₹21.7 cr business-combination outflow). [Screener; AR FY26 cash flow]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Operating cash flow weak for three years: −₹10 cr (FY24), −₹65 cr (FY25), −₹2 cr (FY26) against operating profit of ₹99 cr, ₹47 cr, ₹92 cr; free cash flow −₹22, −₹74, −₹12 cr. [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 162 → 260 → 154; working-capital days 98 → 211 → 131 (FY24–FY26). Unbilled revenue (contract assets) ~₹200–221 cr at June 2026. CRISIL puts gross current assets at ~470 days. [Screener; Q1 call; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Borrowings ₹91 cr at March 2026 (₹19 cr a year earlier); CRISIL says cash and bank "over ₹200 cr", fund-based limits used <30%, interest cover 6.4x (FY26). Net cash roughly ₹110 cr. Dividend ₹0.30/share (3% payout). [Screener; CRISIL Sep-26; AR FY26]
- JNK Global posts bank guarantees and passes payments through on joint orders, easing JNK India's working capital; BPCL Bina payment terms "quite friendly". [Q1 call]
- Rating: CRISIL A-/Stable, outlook revised **up from Negative** on 23 Sep 2026; bank lines raised to ₹604 cr from ₹457 cr. Downgrade triggers: margins below 10%, order-book moderation, working-capital stress. [CRISIL Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- Licensor/EIL approvals for fired heaters, reformers and crackers are the entry barrier; JNK says it is licensor-approved for crackers (e.g., can bid Haldia). But the TA'ZIZ cancellation shows approval gaps outside its core — the licensor qualified JNK only for future projects. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Won and executed two cracking-furnace orders (~₹650 cr) directly without JNK Global, finishing in FY27; incumbent on Dangote Phase 1 (five heaters via JNK Global). Competition for Dangote Phase 2 now includes "a couple of other reputed European players" (Heurtey no longer bids EPC in Nigeria). Lost a clean aviation fuel bid on price. [Q4 call]
- Order book ₹1,961 cr at March 2026: 94% heating, 97.5% domestic, ~₹1,600 cr linked to JNK Global (mainly BPCL Bina) — so the moat today is really the JNK Global partnership plus Indian refinery capex. [Q4 call]
- Addressable share: heater/reformer/cracker/flare scope is ~6–7% of refinery/petchem project cost; new offshore (India TAM $300–500 m) and metals & minerals ($0.5–1 bn) targeted with 3–4 technology partners. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Pricing: no escalation clauses except some PSU contracts; material ordered within 1–2 months of award on fixed-price sub-vendor contracts. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Order intake and concentration**: flat-to-falling order book; 82% of the book tied to JNK Global and one project (BPCL Bina) giving 30% of FY26 revenue; big wins (Dangote, fertiliser reformers, Russia, Middle East) keep sliding by quarters. [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26]
- **Parent dependence and parent governance**: export heating orders flow through JNK Global; the cooperation agreement is past its 3-year term and "not officially renewed"; an activist investor is litigating against JNK Global's board (sub judice). [Q1 call]
- **Chemdist JV governance**: 32.38% of votes were cast **against** material related-party transactions with JNK Chemdist and between Chemdist and the founders' Chemdist Process Solutions (postal ballots, Nov 2025 / Feb 2026). JNK India gave a ₹25 cr corporate guarantee for the JV's bank lines jointly with the founders. The JV's CARO report is adverse on four clauses: ii(b) stock statements to banks vs books, vii(a) delayed statutory dues, ix(d) short-term funds used for long-term purposes, xvii cash losses. [AR FY26 corporate governance; note 7/8; consolidated CARO]
- **Accounting**: auditor's key audit matter is revenue recognition incl. contract assets under the input method (judgement heavy); ~₹200 cr unbilled revenue; Q1 FY27 presentation needed correction. Opinion unmodified. Contingent liabilities negligible (₹0.3 cr tax). [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 35; Q1 call]
- **Management/guidance credibility**: interim CFO at Q4; guidance quietly lowered at Q1 while denying a change. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Shareholding**: DIIs cut holdings from 18.5% (June 2024) to 10.9% (June 2026); FIIs 3.5% → 0.9%. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 "strong numbers since our listing" and imminent export wins; Q1 defensive on the cancellation ("more of an accident") and margins, with the story pivoting to diversification (40% non-heating in 3–5 years). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Numbers: growth 25–30% → 20–25%; EBITDA 14–15% → 12–14%; pipeline ₹4,000 cr → ₹6,000 cr (mostly new domestic non-heating bids at ~10% hit rate); order book ₹1,961 cr → ₹1,801 cr. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New issues: ADNOC/TA'ZIZ cancellation; JNK Global activist dispute; Iraq branch office; cooperation agreement renewal question; revised filing. [Q1 call]

## My read
JNK rebuilt margins through FY26 as promised and revenue visibility for FY27 is good because BPCL Bina and the cracker orders are executing, so ₹950–1,000 cr of revenue (+20–25%) looks achievable. The problem is what comes after: the order book is shrinking, one project and the promoter channel dominate it, the flagship export wins keep slipping, and management trimmed guidance without owning it. Cash conversion is still poor (three years of negative operating cash flow) and the Chemdist JV brings a governance overhang (one-third of votes against its related-party deals, adverse CARO remarks). The price needs FY27 order intake near ₹1,300 cr — a Dangote or fertiliser reformer award in Q2–Q3 — and margins holding at 12–14%; without that, FY28 growth would fall well short of 20%.
