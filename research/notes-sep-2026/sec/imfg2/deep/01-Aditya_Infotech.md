# Aditya Infotech (CP PLUS) — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code CPPLUS. Price ₹3,688, market cap ₹43,623 cr, P/E 91.4, book value ₹159, ROCE 28.6%, ROE 25.4%, dividend yield 0.04% [Screener, 28-Sep-26]. Listed 5 Aug 2025 (IPO 100.69x subscribed) [News: Business Standard, 1 Aug 2025]. Calls read: Q4 FY26 (28 May 2026) and Q1 FY27 (13 Aug 2026), both checked against the official BSE transcripts.

## Business and segments
- India's largest video-surveillance (CCTV) brand, CP PLUS: IP (network) and HD-analog cameras, DVR/NVR recorders, dash cams, body-worn cameras, routers, plus second brands Mexiview (entry level) and Eyra/Lexee (channels that used to sell Chinese brands). Sold through 800+ distributors in 500+ cities and system integrators; 141 co-funded "Brand Galaxy" stores. [AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Apr-26; Q4 call]
- Reported as a **single operating segment** ("security and surveillance") — no segment split. CP PLUS own brand was 86% of FY26 revenue and 87% in Q1 FY27; IP products 73% of CP PLUS in FY26, 79% in Q1 FY27. The rest (~13%) is third-party/traded products, being run down. [AR FY26 note 11268; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Customer mix: B2B-led; consumer purchases under 10% of business; government (GeM, tenders via integrators/PSUs) 15–20% of revenue. [Q4 call]
- Market share: 43.3% of the Indian video-surveillance market in FY26 vs 20.2% in FY25 (Frost & Sullivan), 45.4% as of Q3 FY26. About 30 certified brands (company says 40+ by August); global brands hold ~10%; most domestic rivals are one-tenth CP PLUS's size or less. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Capacity: Kadapa (Andhra Pradesh) plant ~2.5 million units a month, running at 85–90% utilisation; 42 final-assembly lines, 4 MI lines and 12 SMT lines by end-Aug 2026. Company calls it "the third-largest factory in the world" (unverified claim). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Supply chain: semiconductors (SoC, memory, sensors) are ~35% of the bill of materials, all from Taiwan-fabbed non-Chinese suppliers since Chinese chips are barred under the STQC rule; passives another 15–20% (Taiwan and China). Target: 40% domestic content once housings, cables and lenses are localised over the next two quarters. [Q1 call]
- Market context: Indian video-surveillance market ~US$4.4 bn in 2025, seen growing 16–17% a year; IP cameras growing ~25%. The STQC (government cyber-security testing) mandate applied to all CCTV sold from 9 Apr 2025, and old uncertified stock could not be sold after 1 Apr 2026. [AR FY26 MD&A (Frost & Sullivan/Mordor); Q1 call]

TTM sales ₹4,883 cr (+52%), TTM profit ₹477 cr. [Screener]

- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹1,402 cr (+89.5%), gross margin 30.8% (+810 bp from 22.7%), EBITDA ₹208 cr (14.8%), adjusted PAT ₹142 cr (10.1%). Company margins are computed on total income. [IP Q1FY27 p.5–6]
- Q4 FY26 margin spike (18%) was explained by low-cost inventory, a Q4 price rise and a richer project SKU mix; management called it "a one-off". [Q4 call]
- Unit economics: FY26 volume grew ~18–20% and the rest was price/mix. FY27 plan: units +25–30% and average selling price +~25% (partly price hikes, partly mix). Price hikes taken so far 10–20% by product; 15–20% by end-H1; ~25% for the full year. A 6–8% hike was taken in Jan 2026. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (28 May): raised FY27 guidance to revenue ₹6,000–6,500 cr (~+42–54%), EBITDA margin 14–15%, PAT margin 8.5–9.5%; 14–15% called "the new normal for FY27 and FY28" (earlier view had been 12–13%). Industry unit growth cut to 15–16% (from >20% expected) because of memory-driven price inflation. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue ₹1,402 cr, 22–23% of the guided range (company says Q1 is normally 18–20% of the year); EBITDA margin 14.8% (inside range); adjusted PAT margin 10.1% (above range). **Kept** — management "stand[s] by" 14–15% and will revisit after H1. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.7]
- Cable JV with Orient Cables (Rajasthan, ~1 lakh sq ft): Q4 said commercial output Q2–Q3 FY27; Q1 says "by the end of this financial year". **Slipped.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Housing/enclosure plant: Q4 said phase 1 in Q2 FY27, phase 2 in Q4 FY27 (30 million housings a year); Q1 says operational by Q3. **Slight slip.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Capacity: double existing capacity by FY28 (Kadapa expansion, then a Greater Noida cluster; Noida Sector 68 3 lakh sq ft facility by Q4 FY27). Kadapa land acquisition "in final stages" at Q1. **Kept.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Exports: Q4 said "some action in this year"; Q1 says significant numbers only in 18–24 months. **Pushed out.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New products: CP PLUS Pro (enterprise/government, from the Taiwan R&D centre) and home IoT by end-Q3/early-Q4 FY27; drones, machine vision and industrial robots still at study stage. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex ₹200–300 cr (raised from ~₹200 cr as guidance went up), funded by internal accruals "or some debt" for plant and machinery. FY28 plan not given. [Q4 call, official transcript]
- Components: housing plant (50 machines in-house now), lens line 5 lakh lenses/month scalable to 10 lakh, second Kadapa shed of 50,000 sq ft, HD-analog job work moved to EMS partners (5 lakh units/month). Backward-integration margin gain: "a couple of basis points" in FY27, main benefit in FY28; no number given. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Capital commitments outstanding ₹69 cr at Mar-26 (₹86 cr at Mar-25). CWIP ₹84 cr at Mar-26. [AR FY26 consol. note 50; Screener]
- **QIP, Sep 2026:** 43,26,507 shares allotted at ₹3,467 (4.97% discount to the ₹3,648 floor) = about ₹1,500 cr, closed 25 Sep 2026. Stated uses: repaying group debt, capex, possible acquisitions and general purposes. Dilution about 3.7% of shares. [BSE QIP outcome 25-Sep-26; News: Business Today, 23 Sep 2026]
- Payback: at FY26 fixed assets of ₹813 cr against ₹4,221 cr sales, asset turn is ~5x, so capacity additions are cheap relative to revenue — working capital, not plant, is the capital that matters. [Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹509 cr (Mar-25) → ₹256 cr (Mar-26), cut with IPO money; debt/equity 0.07 at Jun-26. The QIP should make the company net cash. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- **Cash conversion is poor:** operating cash flow ₹27 cr (FY25) and ₹13 cr (FY26) against operating profit of ₹248 cr and ₹566 cr; free cash flow –₹120 cr in FY26 and –₹187 cr in FY24. CFO/operating profit 22% in FY26. [Screener]
- Working-capital days (Screener, FY26): debtor 121, inventory 134, payables 194, cash-conversion cycle 61; working-capital days rose from 9 to 74. Company (annualised Q1): inventory 101, debtors 93, creditors 130, CCC 64 days (vs 70). Creditor days fell sharply because the company is paying suppliers in advance to lock in chips and memory; management expects this "in the short term" to continue. [Screener; IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- CRISIL (Apr-26) calls operations working-capital intensive, gross current assets 190–246 days historically; liquidity "strong", net cash accrual ₹275–400 cr a year vs debt repayments ₹21–35 cr. [CRISIL Apr-26]
- Forex: hedges 85–100% of payables/receivables weekly, currently >90%. [Q1 call]
- Dividend ₹1.64/share proposed for FY26 (payout ~5%). [AR FY26 note 51]

## Moat evidence
- Share jump from ~20–30% to 43–45% in two years as Chinese brands (Hikvision, Dahua) were shut out by STQC; CP PLUS had one of the largest certified portfolios early. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- Pricing power: passed on 10–20% price hikes by Q1 with "no major effect on consumption"; gross margin rose 810 bp year-on-year. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Scale in procurement: in the memory shortage (DDR3 capacity being moved to AI data-centre memory), management says "the big usually get bigger"; it keeps a 3–4 quarter rolling supply forecast with six non-Chinese SoC makers (Ambarella, Qualcomm, Novatek, Realtek and others) and three sensor makers (Sony, SmartSens, and a third). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Brand: the only CCTV brand with consumer recall (IPL title sponsor of Punjab Kings, film-star campaigns). CRISIL lists "strong brand" and "strong financial risk profile" as strengths. [Q4 call; CRISIL Apr-26]
- Partnerships: L&T Semiconductor to supply chips for 9 million IP cameras over three years; Qualcomm AI platform in trials. [Q4 call]
- Limits: 40+ STQC-certified brands now exist; MD concedes "it is not as if there is no competition". Moat rests on a government rule (STQC) that could be relaxed. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Regulatory dependence:** the share gain came from a rule that excludes Chinese components; an analyst noted the government recently re-admitted Chinese firms to transformers. Management: "at the moment we do not see any deviation from government policy". [Q1 call]
- **Cash flow vs profit:** two years of near-zero operating cash flow despite rising profit; profit quality depends on inventory and advances being recoverable. [Screener]
- **Memory/chip inflation:** costs "likely to continue rising until 2027"; price hikes lag costs; entry-level IP buyers may trade down to analog. [Q4 call]
- **Contingent liabilities (FY26 consolidated):** customs order of ₹30.9 cr (₹10.3 cr duty plus penalties) on concessional duty for imported 4G routers, appealed to CESTAT (₹6 cr paid under protest); total customs demands ₹39.4 cr; GST demands ₹35.0 cr; income-tax demand ₹19.0 cr from a 2019 survey (appeal largely won in Jan 2026, ₹3.6 cr of additions still contested at ITAT); bank guarantees ₹21 cr and AEO customs bonds ₹310 cr. [AR FY26 consol. note 50]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion (Walker Chandiok). Key audit matters: revenue recognition (rebates/discounts) and impairment of the ₹258 cr investment in and ₹8 cr loan to subsidiary AIL Dixon. Exception: audit-trail (edit log) not enabled at database level. CARO: statutory dues paid with "slight delays in few cases". [AR FY26 auditor's report, CARO]
- **Related parties:** sales to and electricity reimbursements from promoter-group ARK Infosolution; rent paid to promoters; donations and CSR routed through the promoter family's Seth Parmanand Khemka Charitable Trust (group CSR obligation ₹3.9 cr). Amounts are small relative to sales (the note's layout is hard to read, so exact figures are not quoted). [AR FY26 consol. note 46, 52]
- Promoter holding 76.9% (Dec-25) → 74.75% (Mar-26) → 74.71% (Jun-26), and the QIP lowers it further; must reach 75% public over time (minimum public shareholding rule). [Screener]
- Management depth: CHRO joined Sep-26, M&A head being hired; a new R&D centre in Taiwan; acquisitions flagged as a use of cash (execution risk). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]

## My read
The business has improved sharply: a government security rule handed CP PLUS roughly double its old market share, and it has used that to raise gross margin by about 8 points and pass on 15–20% price hikes without losing volume. The weak spot is cash: two years of almost no operating cash flow against ₹900 cr+ of cumulative operating profit, because inventory and supplier advances are soaking up money, and now a ₹1,500 cr QIP follows the IPO. At the current price the market is paying for sustained 30%+ growth and a durable moat; the guidance (₹6,000–6,500 cr, 14–15% margin) implies FY27 PAT of roughly ₹510–620 cr, so the stock needs several years of that. I would turn more positive on H1 operating cash flow turning clearly positive and on proof that share holds as 40+ certified rivals scale up; a relaxation of the STQC rule or a margin slide below 13% would change the view.
