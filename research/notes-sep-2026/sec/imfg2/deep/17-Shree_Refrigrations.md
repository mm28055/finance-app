# Shree Refrigerations — deep research (Sep 2026)

BSE SME listed (code 544458, listed 1-Aug-2025). Reports **half-yearly**, so there is no Q1 FY27 result. Price ₹408, market cap ₹1,459 cr, P/E 67.8, 52-week range ₹161–439 [Screener, 28-Sep-26].

## Business and segments
- Karad (Maharashtra) maker of naval air-conditioning and refrigeration: magnetic-bearing (oil-free) chiller "AC plants" 5–300 TR, refrigeration plants 1–35 TR, turnkey HVAC for warships and submarines (air handling units, NBC air-filtration units, fans), plus its own electrical control panels. Also sells spares, installation and annual maintenance. Legacy line: refrigeration test equipment for appliance makers. [Q4 call; IP Aug-26]
- It is the only Indian firm registered with all three naval directorates (Marine Engineering, Naval Architecture, Electrical Engineering) for chillers, turnkey HVAC and control panels; 5 of 6 standard navy AC-plant sizes and ~70% of HVAC items are type-tested. [Q1 conference; Q4 call]
- Claimed market share: ~64% of naval HVAC tenders; ~60% of magnetic-bearing chillers. Won all but three tenders it bid last year (withdrew from those); retrofits near 100% hit rate. [Q4 call; Q1 conference]
- Revenue mix FY26: equipment ~93%, spares and service ~7% (30% in FY25) — the main reason margins fell. [Q4 call]
- Order book ₹270.8 cr at 31-Mar-26 (1.8x FY26 revenue): HVAC ₹159.7 cr, AC plants ₹61.7 cr, refrigeration plants ₹18.0 cr, turnkey ₹14.3 cr, spares/services ₹12.3 cr, chillers/others ₹4.8 cr. CARE: ₹279.7 cr at 30-Jun-26, to be executed over three years. [IP Aug-26 slide 30; CARE Sep-26]
- Customers are shipyards: Garden Reach, Mazagon Dock, Goa Shipyard, Hindustan Shipyard (Fleet Support Ships — L1 in all three packages). Top three customers ~69% of FY26 revenue (73% in FY25). [CARE Sep-26; Q1 conference]
- 100% domestic. Wholly owned subsidiary Trezor Technologies; new subsidiary Aquilon Deep Chill (sub-zero industrial chillers for pharma/food/chemicals) formed in 2026. Data-centre chillers via a technology tie-up with Smardt (Canada); authorised Danfoss Turbocor service partner in India. [CARE Sep-26; Q1 conference; Q4 call]
- Capacity: new 50,000 sq ft Hanbarwadi/Amberwadi plant on 6 acres opened 28-Jun-26 (expandable to 1 lakh sq ft, 40–50% capex subsidy); total ~70,000 sq ft after closing a leased unit. Management says current plants can produce "400 crore-plus" of revenue. [Q1 conference; Q4 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, half-yearly)
- Revenue: FY23 ₹51 cr → FY24 ₹80 cr → FY25 ₹99 cr → FY26 ₹154 cr (+55.5%). [Screener]
- Operating profit: FY24 ₹25 cr (31%) → FY25 ₹27 cr (27%) → FY26 ₹33 cr (21%). Net profit ₹12 cr → ₹13 cr → ₹22 cr. [Screener]
- Half-years: H1 FY25 ₹51 cr / 30% / ₹8 cr; H2 FY25 ₹48 cr / 24% / ₹4 cr; H1 FY26 ₹50 cr / 11% / ₹2 cr; H2 FY26 ₹103 cr / 26% / ₹20 cr. [Screener]
- Company's own FY26 EBITDA ₹32.8 cr = 21.4% margin (deck); the CFO said "22%" on the call; CARE computes 19.4% (24.4% FY25). [IP Aug-26 slide 27; Q4 call; CARE Sep-26]
- FY26 tax rate only 14% (32% in FY25) and other income ₹2 cr from IPO cash lifted PAT growth (+65%) above operating profit growth (+22%). [Screener]
- ROCE fell to 14% (18% FY25, 25% FY24) as IPO money swelled capital. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- H2/FY26 call (26-May-26): revenue CAGR 40% for 3–5 years excluding data centres; EBITDA margin "20% to 24%" (official transcript); spares/service back to 15–20% of revenue; H1 FY27 "not as skewed"; Amberwadi plant commercial production from June; no major capex for "at least the next couple of years"; data-centre reference installation in FY27, revenue from FY28; FY31 target ₹1,000 cr revenue and ~₹120 cr PAT "we stand by"; ~₹1,000 cr of naval tenders expected in FY27. [Q4 call; official transcript]
- Bharat Connect conference (25-Sep-26, stored as "Q1 FY27"): 40% CAGR repeated; plant opened 28-Jun as promised; H1 FY27 "will not be as muted as last year" (no numbers); working capital to fall to 325–330 days in FY27 and ~250 days over 3–5 years; capacity to be outgrown "in another 1.5 years"; investment-grade rating "within the next 1 year"; data centres still no order. [Q1 conference]
- No H1 FY27 result yet (due Oct/Nov-26), so no delivery test is possible. The plant milestone was delivered on time. Note the ₹1,000 cr FY31 target implies ~45% a year — above the 40% guide; management said growth "is not always a linear mathematical calculation". [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Phase 1 of the new plant cost ~₹25 cr, mostly spent by May-26 (CWIP ₹18 cr at Mar-26); state subsidy covers 40–50% of eligible capex. [Q4 call; Screener; Q1 conference]
- Business is "capex-light, working-capital-heavy": management claims ~10x asset turns at ₹400 cr peak (design, testing and assembly add most value; heavy fabrication, laser cutting, painting now in-house). [Q1 conference]
- IPO (Jul-25): fresh issue net ₹94.5 cr — ₹70 cr for working capital and ₹24.5 cr general corporate purposes; fully used by Mar-26, no deviation. [AR FY26 note 30]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- FY26 operating cash flow turned positive (₹14 cr Screener; ₹7 cr CARE) after four negative years (FY25 −₹25 cr); free cash flow still −₹11 cr. [Screener; CARE Sep-26]
- Borrowings ₹37 cr (Mar-26); net worth ₹215 cr; gearing 0.22x; free cash and liquid investments ₹62 cr at Mar-26; interest cover 8.4x. [Screener; CARE Sep-26]
- Working capital: receivable days 251, inventory days 249, payables 123 — cash cycle 377 days (Screener); CARE operating cycle 296 days; management "570 → 370 days". Receivables are slow because invoices are raised only when equipment is installed in the ship. GST refunds pile up (buys at 18% GST, sells to navy yards at 5%). [Screener; CARE Sep-26; Q4 call]
- Bank guarantees outstanding ₹28.1 cr (₹9.7 cr a year earlier), incl. ₹15.6 cr advance guarantees. [AR FY26 note 29]
- Rating: CARE upgraded to BBB-/Stable, A3 on 15-Sep-26 (from BB+/A4+). Upgrade triggers: revenue above ₹200 cr with steady margins and operating cycle below 250 days. [CARE Sep-26]
- No dividend "in the near term". [AR FY26 note 36]

## Moat evidence
- Three-directorate registration plus type-tested product range; rivals must buy panels or one of the plants from others; shipyards are moving to turnkey HVAC packages that favour Shree. [Q4 call]
- Share: ~64% of naval HVAC; L1 in all three Fleet Support Ship packages. [Q4 call; Q1 conference]
- Aftermarket annuity: completed P-17A frigates and Scorpene submarines generate spares/AMC for decades; gifted ships abroad carry its equipment (spares become exports). [Q1 conference]
- Pricing power is limited: fixed-price 2–3 year contracts with no price-variation clause (CARE); management "factors in" inflation when bidding. [CARE Sep-26; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration**: top three customers ~69% of revenue (CARE). On the Sep-26 call management called an investor's "40% to one customer, 80% top five" statement "not a correct fact" — CARE's figure supports high concentration. [CARE Sep-26; Q1 conference]
- **Lumpy, back-ended revenue**: H1 FY26 margin 11% vs H2 26%; half-yearly reporting only; management resisted quarterly updates. [Screener; Q4 call]
- **Working capital**: still ~300–380 days; growth to ₹400 cr will need more bank lines. [Screener; CARE]
- **Margin**: equipment-heavy mix and fixed-price contracts in a metals upcycle; CARE flags no price-escalation clause. [CARE Sep-26]
- **Governance**: family-run (Rajashri R. Shende is a whole-time director and Rucha R. Shende was company secretary until Jan-26; CARE notes "the Shende family oversees the financials"); pre-IPO ESOP of 7.0 lakh options at ₹2 exercise price (fair value ₹28). Auditor opinion unmodified; emphasis of matter only on the IPO/listing and use of proceeds; key audit matters: inventory valuation and completeness of related-party disclosures. No litigation. [AR FY26 auditor's report, notes 35–36]
- **SME board**: low liquidity; cannot move to main board for ~2 years. [Q4 call]
- **Data-centre story** is so far only a partnership and a hoped-for reference site; no orders. [Q1 conference]

- New: Aquilon Deep Chill subsidiary; working-capital targets quantified (325–330 days FY27, 250 medium term); CARE upgrade; confident talk of needing a new plant within 18 months and of shipyards' dilemma between home and export orders.

## My read
Shree is a genuine niche leader riding naval shipbuilding, with registrations and type-testing that take years to copy, and a clean-looking balance sheet after the IPO. But it is small (₹154 cr revenue), lumpy, concentrated (three shipyards ~69%) and heavy on working capital, and it only reports twice a year. Margins fell from 31% to 21% in two years as spares shrank and costs rose. At ~68x trailing earnings the price needs the promised 40% growth and a margin recovery to 22–24% more or less on schedule; the H1 FY27 result (Oct/Nov) is the first real test. A large new naval order or a first data-centre order would support the view; a weak H1 or slower collections would hurt it badly.
