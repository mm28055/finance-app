# ICE Make Refrigeration — deep research (Sep 2026)

Screener code ICEMAKE (consolidated). Price ₹664, market cap ₹1,047 cr (pre-allotment share count), P/E ~88 on consolidated TTM, 52-week range ₹648–920 [Screener, 28-Sep-26]. Shares issued on 10-Sep-26 raise the share count by ~15%.

## Business and segments
- Ahmedabad-based maker of refrigeration equipment: modular cold rooms, continuous PUF/PIR insulated panels, commercial refrigeration (chest freezers, visi-coolers, uprights), transport (reefer) refrigeration, industrial and ammonia refrigeration systems and turnkey cold-storage projects. Subsidiaries: Bharat Refrigerations (Chennai, 100%, FY26 revenue ₹48 cr) and Icebest (East, 60%, ~₹3 cr). [Q4 call; AR FY26 directors' report; CARE Jan-26]
- Revenue mix FY26: cold rooms ~42%, traditional commercial 14%, continuous panels 14%, new commercial freezers 12%, ammonia + projects 9–9.5%, transport 6%, industrial 3%. Q1 FY27: cold rooms 43%, commercial freezers 19%, continuous panels 17%, traditional commercial 7%, transport 7%, ammonia + projects 5%, industrial 3%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Quick-commerce dark stores were ~13% of FY26 revenue (~₹78–79 cr). Western India 55–60% of revenue; 200+ dealers for the new product lines. About 70% direct sales, top clients 20–40% of revenue. [Q4 call; CARE Jan-26]
- Ticket sizes: cold rooms ₹3–5.5 lakh; commercial products ₹60–70k; continuous panels ₹30–50 lakh a site. [Q4 call]
- Capacity: traditional lines at 85–90% utilisation; the two new lines (panels, freezers) ~40–60% utilised; management says installed capacity after phase-1 capex can support "more than Rs 950 crores" of revenue. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Order book ₹222 cr (Aug-26): ammonia ₹87 cr, projects ₹45 cr, cold rooms ₹39 cr, traditional commercial ₹29 cr, continuous panels ₹11 cr, industrial ₹5 cr, transport ₹4 cr, freezers ₹2–3 cr. It was ₹237 cr in April. About ₹100 cr of it (projects) was booked at old prices. Plus ₹50–60 cr a month of short-cycle orders. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue: FY24 ₹378 cr → FY25 ₹480 cr → FY26 ₹668 cr (+39%); TTM ₹736 cr. 5-year sales CAGR 38%. [Screener]
- Operating profit: FY24 ₹41 cr (11%) → FY25 ₹43 cr (9%) → FY26 ₹45 cr (7%). Net profit ₹26 cr → ₹23 cr → ₹12 cr. Revenue has almost doubled in two years while profit has halved. [Screener]
- Last five quarters (revenue / OPM / net profit): Q1 FY26 ₹111.5 cr / 3.9% / −₹1.5 cr; Q2 ₹147.5 cr / 6.5% / ₹2.0 cr; Q3 ₹153.4 cr / 6.5% / ₹1.5 cr; Q4 ₹255.9 cr / 8.4% / ₹10.1 cr; Q1 FY27 ₹178.9 cr / 1.7% / −₹1.7 cr. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +60% YoY; EBITDA ₹3.1 cr (₹4.5 cr a year ago); PBT −₹2.2 cr. Interest ₹3.3 cr a quarter (₹0.5 cr two years ago). [Q1 call; Screener]
- **Accounting change**: depreciation fell from ₹4.49 cr (Q4) to ₹2.05 cr (Q1) because the company switched from written-down-value to straight-line depreciation in Q1 FY27. Without the change Q1's loss would have been ~₹2.4 cr larger before tax. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Seasonality: Q4 is usually 35–38% of the year; H1 40–45%. [Q4 call]
- ROCE fell from 36% (FY23) to 11–12% (FY26). [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q3 FY26 call: FY26 EBITDA 7.5–8% (actual 6.9%); Q2 FY26 call had said 8%. [Q4 call, as recalled by an analyst]
- Q4 FY26 call (Jun-26): FY27 revenue ₹830–850 cr (official transcript), FY28 ₹1,000 cr; FY27 EBITDA 8–8.5% ("internally… stretch beyond 8.5%"); debt "at its peak… will not go higher"; "no comment on equity raising"; ~₹4 cr one-offs in FY26 (wage code gratuity, BIS/energy-label). [Q4 official transcript]
- Q1 FY27 call (18-Aug-26): revenue guidance "continues to be the same"; EBITDA guidance **cut** to 6–6.5% for FY27; price increases of ~11% taken against ~15–18% cost inflation, ~6–6.5% not yet passed on; third price rise planned for H2. [Q1 call]
- Two months after saying "no comment on equity raising", the company announced a ₹180 cr preferential issue to Japan's Galileo Holdings (plus ₹10 cr from others). Track record: margin guidance missed three times in a row (FY26 8% → 7.5–8% → 6.9% actual; FY27 8–8.5% → 6–6.5%).

## Capex and capacity
- Phase-1 capex (new plant for continuous panels and commercial freezers, Bharat Refrigeration's new premises, corporate office, warehouse lease) drove fixed assets from ₹50 cr (Mar-24) to ₹140 cr (Mar-26), CWIP ₹25 cr; investing outflow ₹84 cr (FY25) and ₹39 cr (FY26). "More than we did in the previous 10 years." [Screener; Q4 call]
- Phase 2 funded by the preferential issue: ₹58 cr for capacity expansion/modernisation (5–6 months to deploy; benefit from late FY27/FY28), ₹40 cr debt repayment, ~₹38–40 cr for ICE Make's 40% share of a 60:40 JV with Galileo (commercial upright and table refrigerators; production targeted Feb-2028; margins a year later), corporate office, R&D/testing lab, ₹39 cr general corporate purposes, and possible acquisitions. [Q1 call]
- Allotment done: 23,67,573 shares at ₹802.51 (₹190 cr) on 10-Sep-26 to non-promoters; promoter holding fell from 74.2% to 64.5%. The issue price is ~21% above today's ₹664. [NSE filing 10-Sep-26; Screener shareholding]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹158 cr at Mar-26 (₹27 cr Mar-24). Operating cash flow −₹14 cr in FY26; free cash flow −₹52 cr (FY25) and −₹53 cr (FY26). [Screener]
- Working capital: debtors 85 days, inventory 106 days, payables 105 days — cash cycle 86 days (FY26), up from 62–69 days. [Screener]
- CARE (Jan-26): A-/Stable, A2+ (bank lines ₹187 cr); gearing 0.70x at Mar-25; negative trigger if PBILDT margin stays below 8% or working capital cycle goes beyond 90 days — both now breached or close. [CARE Jan-26]
- After the ₹190 cr equity and ₹40 cr repayment, net debt should fall sharply in H2 FY27 (not yet reported).
- Dividend ₹2.25 a share for FY26 (payout ~29%) despite halved profit — "to support investor confidence". [Q4 call; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Brand and service network in western India built over 30+ years; claims No. 2 in cold rooms. [Q4 call]
- But weak pricing power: peers raised prices three times, ICE Make twice; it chose to protect new dealers rather than margins. Traditional products are "somewhat like commodity products". [Q1 call]
- New-product traction is real: freezers + panels went from ~5% of sales (FY25) to ~26% (FY26) and 36% in Q1 FY27; GP margin ~14–16% on panels and 20–22% on freezers. [CARE Jan-26; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Galileo partnership brings Japanese design for a ₹1,200–1,500 cr product market with "only 1 good manufacturing player" in India (management estimate). [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin collapse and repeated guidance misses** (see above); 22–25% of purchases are imported (PUF chemicals, compressors), so the rupee matters (₹0.5 cr forex loss in Q1). [Q1 call]
- **Depreciation policy switch** (WDV → SLM) flatters reported profit from FY27; compare years with care.
- **Equity dilution** at a time management had said it would not comment on fundraising; ~15% more shares.
- **Execution load**: new plant utilisation, a JV that will not produce until Feb-2028, possible acquisitions, and higher fixed costs (other expenses ~₹30 cr a quarter vs ₹22 cr a year ago).
- Related parties: sales of ₹2.3 cr to promoter-influenced firms (e.g. Frizics Transport Refrigeration), rent and vehicle hire to KMP relatives, loans from KMP ₹0.2 cr. Small. [AR FY26 note 45]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion ("does not contain any qualification"); key audit matter is revenue recognition on customised solutions. Contingent liabilities are only bank guarantees (₹4.3 cr and ₹24.7 cr in the two years shown). [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 40]
- Subsidiaries "subdued" per CARE. [CARE Jan-26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "investments done, operating leverage next" to "war-driven cost inflation, margins in H2".
- EBITDA guidance cut by ~2 points; revenue guidance kept; order book slipped ₹237 cr → ₹222 cr.
- New: Galileo investment and JV, depreciation-policy change, and phase-2 capex funded by equity rather than debt.

## My read
ICE Make is growing fast (+39% in FY26, +60% in Q1) but has not yet shown it can earn money on that growth: margins have fallen from ~11% to under 2% in Q1 and management has cut its margin promise for the third time. The ₹190 cr Galileo money fixes the balance sheet and brings a credible partner, but it dilutes holders by ~15% and the JV is 18+ months from production. Even after a 28% fall from the high, the stock is priced for a margin recovery to 6–8% on ₹850 cr+ revenue; H2 FY27 margins after the third price increase are the test. Another miss, or cold-room demand slowing, would make the price hard to justify.
