# HLE Glascoat — deep research (28 Sep 2026)

Note: HLE's last two earnings calls on BSE are Q4 FY26 (25 May 2026) and Q2 FY26 (Nov 2025); no Q1 FY27 call transcript was filed. Q1 FY27 (June 2026) results are covered from Screener and ICRA's 19 Aug 2026 update.

## Business and segments
- Process-equipment maker for pharma/API, specialty chemicals, agrochemicals and now oil and gas and water: glass-lined reactors and storage (India + Thaletec Germany), filtration and drying equipment (agitated nutsche filter-dryers etc.), heat exchangers (Kinam), and glass-fused-to-steel tanks and architectural panels (Omeras/OmeraStore, Germany, bought Aug 2025). [ICRA Aug-26; Q4 call; IP Q4FY26]
- FY26 segment revenue: glass-lined products ₹676 cr (of which Thaletec ~₹345 cr, Omeras ₹89.5 cr for 7.5 months, India glass-lined "a little under ₹250 cr"), filtration and drying ₹474 cr (+51%), heat transfer ₹200 cr (+65%). Glass-lined segment EBIT fell ~17% (incl. ₹17.4 cr Omeras EBIT loss). [IP Q4FY26 segment slides; Q4 call]
- India glass-lined volumes are up 30–40% over four years but revenue is flat (~₹200–250 cr) because "the value per equipment has gone down" — domestic price pressure. Thaletec-designed products now ~20–24% of India glass-lined sales. Exports from India only 5–7% of India business. [Q4 call]
- Utilisation: India glass-lined ~75%; heat exchangers 70–75%; Thaletec ~60% (Q2 call). [Q4 call; Q2 call]
- Order book ₹681.6 cr at March 2026 (~10 months' visibility); ~₹750 cr at July 2026 (ICRA); Omeras ₹78–79 cr. [IP Q4FY26; ICRA Aug-26; Q4 call]
- Kinam (heat exchangers; 70% owned, remaining 30% minority) — the listed Kinam Enterprise amalgamated into HLE in Aug 2025. [IP Q4FY26; AR FY26 NCI note]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue: FY24 ₹968 cr → FY25 ₹1,028 cr → FY26 ₹1,353 cr (+32%, incl. Omeras and Kinam growth). Operating profit ₹114 → ₹136 → ₹142 cr; OPM 12% → 13% → 11%. Net profit ₹41 → ₹62 → ₹57 cr. [Screener]
- Quarters (revenue / operating profit / net profit, ₹ cr): Q1 FY26 284/37.6/17.9; Q2 351/38.2/14.0; Q3 327/21.1/4.6; Q4 392/42.8/20.2; Q1 FY27 301/20.9/2.1. Q1 FY27 operating margin 7.0% vs 13.2% a year earlier. [Screener; ICRA Aug-26]
- ICRA's explanation for Q1 FY27: "deferment in dispatches (especially in the heat transfer equipment business)", finished goods held back; revenue is booked on dispatch. [ICRA Aug-26]
- FY26 company EBITDA ₹148.5 cr (11.0%, incl. other income) after Omeras EBITDA loss ₹15.3 cr (PAT loss ₹15.6 cr), ₹2.1 cr labour-code charge and ₹4.6 cr acquisition costs; management's "adjusted" margin ex-Omeras ~13.5%. [Q4 call; IP Q4FY26]
- Unit economics (management): Omeras breaks even at ₹45–50 cr revenue a quarter (~₹200–225 cr a year); Thaletec FY26 revenue ~€34 m at ~11–11.5% EBIT. [Q4 call]
- ROCE fell from 42–43% (FY20–21) to 12–13% (FY24–26); ROE 11%. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q2 FY26 call (Nov 2025): H2 FY26 EBITDA margin ex-Omeras "roughly about 16%"; Omeras "breakeven or above" by end-FY26; glass-lined business double-digit EBIT by Q4. [Q2 call official]
- Delivered by Q4 FY26: **Missed** — company-adjusted FY26 margin ex-Omeras ~13.5%, Q4 reported 11.2%; Omeras "very close to breakeven" but not there (Q4 revenue ₹43–44 cr vs ₹45–50 cr breakeven); glass-lined adjusted EBIT ~11.5–12% in Q4 (met the double-digit promise on an adjusted basis). [Q4 call]
- Q4 FY26 call (May 2026): 16% EBITDA ex-Omeras "in the next 2 years"; consolidated 14–15%; revenue ₹2,000 cr in two years incl. Omeras ("if we do less than ₹2,000 crores, we will be disappointed"); heat transfer growth 15–20% a year for two years; Omeras revenue "way above ₹200 cr" in FY27 and double-digit margin within ~2 years. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 outcome: revenue +6% YoY and operating margin 7.0% — far below the path to 14–15%; the ₹2,000 cr (FY28) goal implies ~22% annual growth from FY26. [Screener; ICRA Aug-26]
- Track record: repeated promises — Q4 FY24 call set peak revenue of ₹450 cr for Thaletec and ₹300 cr for Kinam "without major capex"; Kinam's target was already pushed from FY25 to FY26. [Q2 call official]

## Capex and capacity
- Omeras India: ₹35 cr brownfield expansion at Silvassa for glass-fused-steel tanks (biogas, wastewater, ultrapure water); commercial production expected by Q4 FY27. [ICRA Aug-26; Q4 call]
- Heat exchangers: new capacity to be considered from FY28 depending on FY27; otherwise capex is modest — investing cash outflow ₹82–86 cr in FY26 (incl. Omeras acquisition). [Q4 call; Screener; IP Q4FY26]
- Omeras was bought for about ₹27 cr (analyst's figure on the call, unchallenged) with ~₹200 cr of annual revenue; management "rationalized certain nonviable business segments". [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹348 cr at March 2026 (₹379 cr FY25, ₹390 cr FY24); cash and equivalents ₹34 cr → net debt ~₹314 cr. [Screener; IP Q4FY26]
- Operating cash flow improved: ₹95 cr (FY24), ₹134 cr (FY25), ₹161 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹40 cr, ₹68 cr, ₹108 cr — debt is being paid down. [Screener; IP Q4FY26 cash flow]
- ICRA ratios (FY26): TOL/TNW ~1.2x; total debt/OPBDITA ~2.4x; interest cover ~3.9x (FY25 ~3.5x). ICRA is monitoring "the pace of order execution, recovery in profitability and debt reduction". Rating [ICRA]A (Stable)/A2+ (July 2026), with a material-event update in Aug 2026. [ICRA Aug-26]
- Working capital: inventory days 205, debtor days 73, payable days 85 (Screener FY26); company net working capital ~80 days. [Screener; IP Q4FY26]
- Dividend 55% of face value (₹1.10/share) for FY26; payout ~15%. [Q4 call; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Market leader in India for filtration/drying equipment and one of two large domestic glass-lined makers; Thaletec gives German technology and European/US reach (orders from the US for two years). [Q4 call; IP Q4FY26]
- Weak pricing power at home: India glass-lined output up 30–40% while revenue is flat — clear evidence of price erosion (Chinese and domestic competition). [Q4 call]
- Customer stickiness via regulated pharma processes and after-market; 1,500+ customers to cross-sell Omeras tanks. [IP Q4FY26]
- Gas-cost increases in Germany (Iran conflict) passed on "to a large extent". [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin and guidance credibility**: 16% margin promised for H2 FY26, delivered ~11%; Q1 FY27 margin 7%. PAT fell from ₹17.9 cr to ₹2.1 cr in Q1. [Q2 call; Q4 call; Screener]
- **Acquisition risk**: Omeras loss-making (₹15.6 cr PAT loss in 7.5 months) and needs >₹200 cr revenue to break even; Kinam's peak-revenue target slipped. [IP Q4FY26; Q4 call; Q2 call]
- **Related parties** (auditor flagged related-party transactions as a key audit matter): KMP remuneration ₹12.8 cr (≈23% of FY26 PAT); unsecured loans from KMP of ₹11.1 cr taken and ₹26.9 cr repaid in FY26 (interest ₹0.7 cr); ₹1 cr capital advance to KMP; sales of ₹7.8 cr to entities in which directors are interested (₹3.0 cr receivable). [AR FY26 consolidated note f; auditor's report KAM]
- Audit scope: two foreign subsidiaries and three step-down subsidiaries (revenue ₹438 cr, assets ₹408 cr) audited by other auditors; three foreign step-downs (incl. Omeras entities) prepared under local GAAP and reviewed by local auditors. Key audit matters: defined-benefit obligation (labour-code changes) and related-party transactions. Opinion unmodified. [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report]
- Contingent liabilities are small (disputed service tax ~₹0.7 cr). [AR FY26 note l]
- End-market softness: agrochemicals subdued; CDMO/agro customers deferring decisions since Feb 2026 due to geopolitics. [Q4 call]
- Promoter holding steady at ~65.6%. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q2 FY26 → Q4 FY26: margin target moved from "16% in H2 FY26" to "16% ex-Omeras in the next 2 years" — a two-year deferral. Omeras moved from "breakeven by Q4" to "very close to breakeven". A new ₹2,000 cr revenue ambition appeared. [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- Since Q4 FY26: Q1 FY27 revenue growth slowed to 6% and margins halved; order book rose to ~₹750 cr (ICRA). [Screener; ICRA Aug-26]

## My read
HLE has grown revenue through acquisitions and genuine demand in filtration/drying and heat exchangers, and cash flow and debt are improving. But profitability has gone the wrong way: the core India glass-lined business is losing price, Omeras is still loss-making, and the margin promises of late 2025 were missed and then pushed out two years — with Q1 FY27 at a 7% margin. The auditor flagging related-party dealings and the size of promoter remuneration relative to profit add to caution. The price needs margins back towards 13–15% and revenue growth of ~20% a year; I would turn more positive on two quarters of double-digit margins with Omeras at breakeven, and more negative if dispatch delays persist into Q2–Q3 FY27 or debt reduction stalls.
