# Emcure Pharmaceuticals — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code EMCURE. Price ₹1,951, market cap ₹37,005 cr, 52-week range ₹1,260–2,101 [Screener]. Listed July 2024.

## Business and segments
- Pune-based maker of branded generics for India and generics/branded products for Europe, Canada and emerging markets (EM); strong in women's health (iron: Orofer XT, Orofer FCM), cardiology, CNS, oncology, nephrology and HIV (ARVs). 13 plants in India incl. injectables, liposomal, biologics (Gennova) [CARE Aug-26].
- FY26 revenue ₹9,204 cr (+16.6%): India ₹4,027 cr (+10%); international ₹5,177 cr (+22%) — Europe ₹1,850 cr (+25.5%), EM/RoW ₹1,840 cr (+21.8%), Canada ₹1,487 cr (+18.7%) [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹2,580 cr (+22.8%): India ₹1,095 cr (+10.2%; 42%); Europe ₹537 cr (+32.8%); RoW ₹522 cr (+44.8%, ~65% of it ARV tenders); Canada ₹427 cr (+24.6%); international 58% of sales [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Currency helped: ~6–7 points of company growth and ~12–13 points of international growth in Q1 came from the weaker rupee [Q1 call].
- India: ~4,000 reps (about 40% in Zuventus); in-licensed brands ~15% of India sales (Sanofi cardiac + Amaryl/Cetapin, Roche nephrology, Novo Nordisk's Poviztra semaglutide); 32–35% of India sales from own R&D products; productivity up from ₹5.4 lakh to ₹7 lakh per rep a month in two years [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Capacity/utilisation: not disclosed.

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue / operating profit / OPM (₹ cr): Jun-25 2,101 / 417 / 20%; Sep-25 2,270 / 475 / 21%; Dec-25 2,363 / 493 / 21%; Mar-26 2,470 / 480 / 19%; Jun-26 2,580 / 532 / 21% [Screener]. Company EBITDA Q1 ₹508 cr (19.7%) [IP Q1FY27].
- Net profit (reported): Q1 FY27 ₹292 cr (+36%); Q4 FY26 ₹244 cr (adjusted ₹279 cr before a ₹43 cr Mantra earn-out charge) [Screener; Q4 call].
- Annual: sales FY24 ₹6,658 cr → FY25 ₹7,896 cr → FY26 ₹9,204 cr; operating profit ₹1,230 → 1,469 → 1,746 cr; OPM 18% / 19% / 19%; net profit ₹528 → 707 → 941 cr [Screener]. Adjusted FY26 PAT ₹1,008 cr (+41%) [Q4 call].
- Gross margin: 60.3% FY26; 58.4% in Q1 FY27 (61.8% a year earlier) as low-margin ARV tenders and Europe grew [IP Q1FY27].
- India growth Q1: in-licensed Sanofi OAD and Roche added the difference — organic ~6–7%; price ~4–5%, new launches 1–2% [Q1 call].
- R&D ₹384 cr in FY26 (4.2% of sales); Q1 3.5% [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- ROCE 24% (Screener); company 23.7% at June 2026 [Screener; IP Q1FY27].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (5 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth "low to mid-teens"; EBITDA margin +75–100 bps; gross margin ~60%; India "low double digits", above an 8–9% market; Europe mid-teens CAGR for two years; capex ₹400–425 cr; staff costs +10%; rupee assumed at 92/$ [Q4 call].
- FY26 record: beat initial guidance (16.6% growth); adjusted PAT +41% [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (6 Aug 2026): revenue "higher end" of low-to-mid-teens (raised in tone); EBITDA +70–100 bps (kept); gross margin cut to ~59% (from 60–61%) because of mix; India back in line with the market in Q2 and faster in H2; R&D 4–5% of sales; net debt to peak ~₹1,450 cr in Q2, net cash by end-FY28 [Q1 call].
- Delivered in Q1: revenue +22.8% (well ahead), margin +50 bps (below the 70–100 bps full-year promise), India +10.2% (in line; organic only ~6–7%) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Verdict: **kept**, with revenue leaning up and gross margin trimmed.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹434 cr (FY25 ₹376 cr); Q1 FY27 ₹117 cr; FY27 guide ₹400–425 cr [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Money is going more into buying control than into plants: Zuventus minority buyout (FY26), Manx portfolio (UK, ~₹110 cr licence transfer), Mantra (Canada) earn-out, Gennova 12.05% for ~₹232 cr (July 2026). Gennova sold its mRNA vaccine business for ~₹140 cr [CARE Aug-26; Q4 call].
- Pipeline: liposomal amphotericin B across Europe; bevacizumab biosimilar (eye) in India H1 FY27 pending approval; Gilead lenacapavir (HIV) licence, registration FY27; MSD once-monthly HIV pill licence (129 countries); ICMR anti-HPV candidate; ADCs early stage [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Payback not disclosed.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹488 cr (Mar-25) → ₹1,054 cr (Mar-26) → ₹1,103 cr (Jun-26); gross debt ₹1,291 cr, cash ₹189 cr at June; to rise by ₹450–500 cr for Mantra and Gennova payouts, peaking ~₹1,450 cr in Q2 FY27 [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]. Net debt/TTM EBITDA 0.6x [IP Q1FY27].
- Borrowings ₹1,521 cr at March 2026 (₹1,023 cr) [Screener]. CARE: total debt ₹1,519 cr, gearing 0.31x, interest cover 13.5x [CARE Aug-26].
- Operating cash flow ₹944 cr FY26 (₹852 cr FY25); CFO/EBITDA 75%; free cash flow ₹400 cr [Screener].
- Working capital: debtor days 102, inventory days 239, cash conversion cycle 161 days (145 in FY25) [Screener]. Working capital rose in Q1, "stabilised this month" [Q1 call].
- Dividend payout ~7% [Screener].
- Rating: CARE AA/Stable, A1+ reaffirmed 31 Aug 2026; downgrade if net debt/EBITDA reaches 1.5x or margin below 15% [CARE Aug-26].

## Moat evidence
- Partner of choice for multinationals in India (Sanofi, Roche, Novo Nordisk, MSD voluntary licence, Gilead voluntary licence) — no upfront payments for the in-licensing [Q4 call].
- Own chemistry: iron franchise (Orofer), Tenectase, S-metoprolol (Metpure-XL) at 86% share of its molecule; 11–12 chiral-pure compounds [AR FY26; Q1 call]. Two brand families above ₹200 cr [CARE Aug-26].
- International: 15+ approvals in Q1; first-generic position in liposomal amphotericin B in some markets [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26].
- Weakness: organic India growth ~6–7% in Q1 is below the market; market share "flattish" in FY26; low gross margin in ARV/B2B tender business [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **India execution**: Zuventus (acute, ~40% of reps) lost staff after the minority buyout and leadership change; Q4 FY26 India growth only 5.2% [Q4 call].
- **Governance/legal history**: CEO Satish Mehta and Heritage (former US unit) named in US generic price-fixing cases; state attorneys-general case settled (approved 2 Apr 2025), other settlements being finalised [AR FY26 note 45].
- Income-tax search (Dec 2020) on Emcure, Zuventus and Gennova; appeals continuing; tax contingent liabilities ₹170 cr plus interest ₹89 cr [AR FY26 note 44].
- Contingent liabilities total ₹259 cr (₹394 cr in FY25) [AR FY26 note 44]. Cebis (Vayam) arbitration claim unquantified [AR FY26 note 45].
- Auditor: unmodified; key audit matter is revenue recognition; audit trail maintained [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Leadership: long-time chairman Berjis Desai leaves at the AGM; Satish Mehta becomes chairman + MD + CEO (concentration of roles); son Samit Mehta named COO and Gennova CEO [Q1 call].
- Gross margin pressure from ARV/tender mix; Canada semaglutide is a second-wave filing; Quebec launch depends on Dr. Reddy's supply after its product issue [Q1 call].
- Promoter holding 77.8%, stable [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- India recovered from +5% (Q4) to +10% (Q1) and management declared Zuventus "no longer my concern" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Revenue confidence rose ("higher end"), but gross margin guidance fell to ~59% and margin expansion band narrowed to 70–100 bps [Q1 call].
- Debt now rising to fund buyouts; a net-cash date (end-FY28) was given for the first time [Q1 call].
- Board change: chairman exit, founder takes the chair, next generation (Samit Mehta) elevated [Q1 call].

## My read
Emcure is growing fast (Q1 +23%), but a large part is currency (~6–7 points) and low-margin ARV tenders, so profit quality is lower than the headline: gross margin fell 340 bps YoY and EBITDA margin rose only 50 bps. India, the high-margin half, is growing ~6–7% organically and must reach double digits in H2 as promised. Debt is rising to ~₹1,450 cr to buy out minorities, which simplifies the group but uses cash. The price needs steady mid-teens profit growth; proof would be India at 10%+ organically and margins moving towards 20%+ by H2, while another India miss or further gross-margin dilution would weaken the case.
