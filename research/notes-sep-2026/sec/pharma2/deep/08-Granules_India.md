# Granules India (NSE: GRANULES) — deep research, Sep 2026

## Business and segments
- Vertically integrated maker of high-volume generics: it makes the active ingredient (API), a half-way blend (PFI, "pharmaceutical formulation intermediate") and the finished tablet (FD), mainly for paracetamol, metformin, ibuprofen and guaifenesin, plus a growing set of US "complex generics" (ADHD/controlled substances, modified-release), and since FY26 a Swiss peptide contract-manufacturing (CDMO) arm, Senn Chemicals AG / Ascelis Peptides. [IP Q4FY26 slides 6–10; AR FY26]
- Q1 FY27 revenue split (₹ cr): finished dosages 1,093 (74%), API 194 (13%), PFI 130 (9%), peptide CDMO 60 (4%). By region: North America 1,056 (72%), Europe 244 (16%), rest of world 177 (12%). Y/y growth: FD +22%, API +19%, PFI +9%, CDMO +106%; North America +13%, Europe +51%, ROW +56%. [IP Q1FY27 slide 8]
- Complex generics rose to 50% of finished-dosage revenue in Q1 FY27 from 39% a year earlier (43% for FY26 vs 31% FY25). US arm GPI ranks 27th among US generic companies by sales (74th in FY21) and 4th in controlled substances (IQVIA). Rank #1 in 10 of 35 US products and top-3 in 18. [IP Q1FY27 slides 4, 10; Q1 call]
- Exports were ₹5,144 cr of FY26 revenue of ₹5,366 cr (96%); no single customer is 10% of revenue. One reported segment. [AR FY26 consolidated note 34]
- The top five legacy molecules were still about 62% of FY25 sales (86% in FY15). [IndRa Jan-26]
- Capacity: new Genome Valley (GLS) plant adds ~10 bn doses a year (~40% more formulation capacity) — utilisation "very low" now, expected to cross 50–60% by end-FY27; GPI Virginia at ~70% utilisation (low-volume, high-value), small expansion by end-2028. [Q1 call; IP Q4FY26 slide 10]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly revenue: Jun-25 1,210 → Sep-25 1,297 → Dec-25 1,388 → Mar-26 1,471 → Jun-26 1,477. EBITDA 247 → 278 → 308 → 352 → 339; OPM 20% → 21% → 22% → 24% → 23%. Net profit 113 → 131 → 150 → 202 → 180. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +22% y/y (flat q/q), EBITDA +37%, PAT +60% (₹180 cr; the base quarter had a ₹25.9 cr exceptional charge). Gross margin 65.6% (+74 bps y/y, –11 bps q/q); R&D ₹88 cr (6.0% of sales, +30%). Peptide CDMO lost ₹12.4 cr at EBITDA level vs +₹1.5 cr in Q4. [IP Q1FY27 slide 7]
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹4,506 cr → FY25 ₹4,482 cr → FY26 ₹5,366 cr (+20%); OPM 19% → 21% → 22%; PAT ₹405 cr → ₹502 cr → ₹595 cr. Gross margin 50.0% (FY22) → 55.1% → 61.5% (FY25) → 65.0% (FY26). 3-yr sales CAGR only 6%, TTM +25%. [Screener; IP Q4FY26 slide 9]
- FY26 EBITDA includes a ₹44.5 cr loss in the peptide business; Gagillapur remediation cost ~₹50 cr in FY26, now under $1 mn a quarter. [IP Q4FY26 slide 4; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- ROCE: Screener 16% (FY26); company definition 18.0% for Q1 FY27 (17.6% FY26). [Screener; IP Q1FY27 slide 6]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (29 Apr 2026): margin guidance withdrawn — "taking a step back and not committing to a specific number" because of West Asia raw-material, packing and freight inflation; capex ~₹600 cr in FY27; net debt flat to slightly up; NWC about 33% of sales; peptide CDMO PAT-positive in FY27; remediation costs "substantially lower". [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: EBITDA margin 22.9%; net debt fell to ₹101 cr (0.07x EBITDA) instead of rising; NWC 29% of sales; capex ₹89 cr; peptide CDMO made a loss in the quarter (target is full-year). [IP Q1FY27 slides 6–7, 11]
- Q1 FY27 guidance: 22–23% EBITDA margin "expected to continue" (reinstated); capex kept at ₹600 cr; R&D 5.5–6% of sales; growth "will continue" (no number); peptide CDMO: annual revenue at least 4x Q1 (≈CHF 20 mn) and PAT-positive for FY27; new medium-term marker — $50 mn revenue at 30%+ EBITDA margin "midway through the third year" (about FY29–30), and "five times revenue in five years". [Q1 call]
- Launch pipeline: 9 US products waiting only for Gagillapur clearance (market ~$11 bn), 9 more waiting on patents, 5 others; 25 ANDAs under approval with ~$40 bn brand+generic market; second sole first-to-file (Sodium Oxybate ER, brand ~$267 mn). No new controlled-substance launch in FY27; 1–2 from FY28. Oncology (Vizag) first own launch FY28–FY29; 9–13 oncology products in development. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 slide 4]
- Track record: management admitted on Q4 that earlier Gagillapur re-inspection timelines "skipped three times"; it now gives no date. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex: FY25 ₹570 cr, FY26 ₹555 cr (₹2,311 cr over FY22–26, mainly Genome Valley, MUPS block and US packing), Q1 FY27 ₹89 cr, FY27 guide ₹600 cr (new API facility, IT/digital quality, US distribution centre/warehouse). Capital commitments outstanding ₹318 cr at Mar-26. [IP Q4FY26 slide 10; Q1 call; AR FY26 note 26]
- Peptides: Zurich upgrades (solid-phase reactors, purification columns, lyophilisation) arriving over coming months; India peptide intermediates plant ~₹100 cr, then India peptide API plant ~₹200 cr (land earmarked at Visakhapatnam). [Q1 call]
- DCDA (dicyandiamide) plant at Vizag: pilot nearly done; commercial plant ~₹200 cr, but Chinese price cuts are a problem. [Q4 call]
- Quality digitalisation capex ~₹26 cr spent in the last year and ₹75 cr planned over two years. [IP Q1FY27 slide 17]
- Asset turns: fixed assets rose from ₹2,096 cr (FY24) to ₹3,250 cr (FY26) while ROCE stayed ~15–16%; Genome Valley and peptides are the "ramp-up" assets that should lift returns. [Screener; IP Q1FY27 slide 11]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash from operations: FY24 ₹439 cr, FY25 ₹867 cr, FY26 ₹793 cr; free cash flow ₹61 cr / ₹297 cr / ₹239 cr. Q1 FY27 CFO ₹387 cr (vs ₹100 cr in Q4) as receivables fell. [Screener; IP Q1FY27 slide 11]
- Net debt: ₹706 cr (FY25) → ₹402 cr (FY26, 0.34x EBITDA) → ₹101 cr (Jun-26, 0.07x). Screener borrowings ₹1,512 cr at Mar-26 include ₹174 cr of leases. [IP Q1FY27 slide 11; AR FY26 note 9]
- Equity raised: ₹666 cr in FY26, including 25% (₹366 cr) upfront on 2.5 cr promoter warrants at ₹585 (total ₹1,463 cr). On 16 Sep 2026, 2.498 cr warrants were converted on payment of the remaining 75% (~₹1,096 cr); share count rose ~10% to 27.28 cr. Mrs Uma Devi Chigurupati's stake went to 12.51%. The company is now effectively net cash. [AR FY26 note 9; BSE filing 16 Sep 2026; SAST filing Sep 2026]
- Working capital: company NWC/sales 29% (Q1 FY27) vs 30–33% guide. Screener inventory days rose from 235 (FY24) to 326 (FY26); debtor days fell from 80 to 62; cash conversion cycle 256 days. [IP Q1FY27 slide 6; Screener]
- Dividend payout only ~7%; management "discussing internally" raising it. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Credit rating: India Ratings IND AA-/Positive/IND A1+ (Jan 2026) on ₹1,415 cr bank lines; forecasts "low-to-mid teens revenue growth over FY26–FY28" and EBITDA margin above 20%; resolution of the Gagillapur warning letter is the key trigger. [IndRa Jan-26]

## Moat evidence
- Cost/scale: among top-five global makers in its legacy molecules, backward-integrated to API and PFI; "API self-sufficiency ... provides a cost advantage". [IndRa Jan-26; IP Q4FY26 slide 9]
- Mix upgrade shows in gross margin: 50.0% (FY22) → 65.0% (FY26); held at 65.6% in Q1 FY27 despite input inflation (helped by opening US inventory). [IP Q4FY26 slide 9; IP Q1FY27 slide 7]
- ADHD/controlled substances: needs DEA quotas and consistent low-dose manufacturing; Granules says it gets the quota it needs and competitors go out of stock. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- First-to-file position: sole FTF on Amphetamine ER tablet and Sodium Oxybate ER suspension. [IP Q1FY27 slide 15]
- Weak spot: pricing power in legacy molecules is limited — management "held back some supply" in Europe because it could not pass on cost increases. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Gagillapur warning letter** (Feb 2025, after Aug–Sep 2024 inspection; OAI): remediation "essentially complete", FDA met in January 2026, but no re-inspection date after 19 months; 9 launches blocked. The annual report notes revenue was hit by the production slowdown and higher consultancy and air-freight costs. [AR FY26 note 22; Q1 call]
- **Promoter-related dealings:** ₹47.2 cr of packaging bought from Product Armor Packaging (promoter-influenced) in FY26 (₹41.5 cr in FY25); rent ₹7.6 cr to the Chigurupati Family Trust (₹2.0 cr in FY25); CMD's pay ₹28.4 cr (₹23.7 cr), about 5% of PAT; three other family members ₹2.8 cr each. [AR FY26 note 31B]
- **Dilution:** the promoter warrant conversion raised the share count ~10%; per-share growth will lag profit growth in FY27. [BSE filing 16 Sep 2026]
- **Peptide CDMO lumpiness:** ₹12.4 cr EBITDA loss in Q1 after one positive quarter; no order book disclosed. [IP Q1FY27 slide 7; Q4 call]
- **Litigation:** RMRx LLC suing Granules Consumer Health for breach of contract (New Jersey; depositions under way); size not disclosed. Tax contingent liabilities are tiny (₹0.9 cr). [AR FY26 note 26]
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; only key audit matter is US revenue recognition/chargeback and rebate accruals; one tiny subsidiary audited by others. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Inventory build:** inventory days up ~90 in two years (Screener); management calls it support for growth and launches.

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 was cautious ("year of recalibration", no margin guidance, net debt may rise); Q1 was confident ("innovation-led ... platform"), 22–23% margin reinstated and net debt almost gone.
- New in Q1: quantified peptide targets ($50 mn at 30%+), Genome Valley 50–60% utilisation by year-end, oncology first launch FY28–29, GPI inspection closed (VAI).
- Unchanged: Gagillapur still waiting; capex ₹600 cr; peptide PAT-positive target.

## My read
The business is improving in quality: gross margin up 15 points in four years, complex generics half of finished dosages, cash flow strong and the balance sheet now net cash after the promoter's ₹1,460 cr warrant money. But revenue growth over three years is only ~6% a year; the recent 20%+ pace depends on complex generics, Europe and peptides, and the biggest unlock (9 launches) still waits on an FDA visit it cannot schedule. The stock at a mid-30s P/E needs roughly mid-teens earnings growth; with 10% dilution, Gagillapur clearance in FY27 is close to necessary. A clean re-inspection would lift the view; another quarter of peptide losses or margin slipping below 22% would weaken it.
