# Pharma (formulations) — sector research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Scope: Indian formulations makers — India branded market, US generics, emerging-market exports, CDMO. Focus topics: India market growth, US pricing and tariffs, FDA inspections, GLP-1/semaglutide generics, NLEM price control, chronic vs acute, EM exports. Figures are from the sources tagged; forecasts name the forecaster.

## Market size and growth — India (IPM)
- The Indian pharma market (IPM) was about ₹2,64,900 cr on a moving-annual-total basis at June 2026, growing ~11%; June 2026 alone grew 16% to ₹23,060 cr [News: ICICI Direct, Jul 2026, citing IQVIA].
- Pharmarack: IPM grew 8.1% in calendar 2025; its January 2026 "realistic" forecast for 2026 was 7.8–8.1% — actual 2026 has run well ahead (six straight months of double-digit growth to May 2026) [BioSpectrum, 9 Jan 2026; ICICI Direct].
- Growth is price- and mix-led, not volume-led: April 2026 MAT value growth 10.3% with volume growth only 0.8% [BioSpectrum, Apr 2026, citing Pharmarack].
- Goldman Sachs: IPM grew ~11.6% in Q1 FY27, with price up ~5.8% YoY [IndiaMedToday, 10 Jul 2026].
- Company example of the split (Pharmarack MAT): industry volume ~1%, new products ~3%, price ~5%; Jagsonpal volume 2%, new products 3%, price 6.5–7% [Jagsonpal Q4 FY26 call].

## Chronic vs acute
- June 2026: chronic therapies +20% vs acute +13%; cardiac and anti-diabetic +20% each, VMN +19%, gynaecology/pain/neurology +16%, respiratory +14%, anti-infectives +13%, GI and derma +12% [ICICI Direct, Jul 2026 (IQVIA)].
- Pharmarack's 2026 view: chronic lifestyle segments lead (cardiac 11–12%, anti-diabetic 10–11%, oncology 12–13%); mature acute categories (GI, anti-infectives, pain) only 4–7% [BioSpectrum, 9 Jan 2026].
- Acute-heavy companies swing with monsoon and season (e.g. Indoco Q1 FY27 anti-infective/respiratory drop on a late monsoon) [Indoco Q1 FY27 call].

## GLP-1 / semaglutide generics
- Novo Nordisk's core Indian semaglutide patent expired in March 2026; day-one launches on 21 Mar 2026 by Dr Reddy's, Zydus, Alkem, Sun Pharma and Glenmark among others [Pearce IP, 21 Mar 2026].
- Launch prices ranged from ~₹325–440 a week (Glenmark vials) to ₹4,200 a month (Dr Reddy's); Alkem from ₹1,800 a month [Pearce IP, 21 Mar 2026].
- Market estimate quoted: about $1 bn over the next two years [Pearce IP, 21 Mar 2026]. (A Nomura estimate of ₹1,600 cr today growing to ₹12,000 cr in five years was reported by Business Standard but that page could not be opened — treat as unverified.)
- Pharmarack expects the GLP-1 segment to grow 60–70% in 2026 as branded generics widen access [BioSpectrum, 9 Jan 2026]; GLP-1s are a named driver of chronic outperformance and of Ind-Ra's FY27 view [ICICI Direct; Pharmabiz, Feb 2026].
- For the small caps in this group, GLP-1 is not a direct driver (none has a launched semaglutide), but it raises the chronic/diabetes growth bar they are compared against.

## Price control (NLEM/DPCO)
- NPPA allowed scheduled (NLEM) formulations a WPI-linked MRP rise of only 0.64–0.65% from 1 Apr 2026 (≈900–935 formulations); non-scheduled drugs may rise up to 10% a year [Outlook India, Mar 2026].
- Carboplatin and cisplatin ceilings were raised 50% for six months from 12 Jun 2026 under DPCO para 19 (cisplatin ₹7.26→₹10.29; carboplatin ₹60.49→₹90.74) after platinum costs spiked and supply dried up [BioSpectrum, Jun 2026]. Beta Drugs had stopped Platin supply for half of FY26 and restarted after the revision [Beta Q4 FY26 call; 17 Sep 2026 meet].
- Companies with portfolios mostly outside NLEM keep pricing power (Jagsonpal ~92% outside NLEM) [Jagsonpal Q4 FY26 call].

## US generics — pricing, tariffs, FDA
- Tariffs: the US Section 232 proclamation of 2 Apr 2026 put a 100% tariff on patented drugs (15% for EU/Japan/Korea/Switzerland; zero for firms signing MFN pricing + onshoring deals); **generics, biosimilars and their inputs are exempt for now**, with a Commerce review due within one year (≈April 2027) [India Briefing, Apr 2026].
- Ind-Ra also cites "zero import duty on pharma exports to the US under interim trade deal" as an FY27 support [Pharmabiz, Feb 2026].
- US revenue trend: ICRA expects US growth to fall to 3–5% in FY26 (from ~10% in FY25) and lower still in FY27, driving its sample-set growth down to 6–8% [ICRA Mar-2026 update; IBEF, 19 Sep 2025, citing ICRA: US 9.9% in FY25 → 3–5% FY26; Europe 10–12%; domestic 8–10%]. Brokers expected ~5% YoY US revenue decline for several large players in Q1 FY27 as gRevlimid fell away [Whalesbook, 21 Jul 2026; IndiaMedToday, 10 Jul 2026].
- Pricing: Goldman Sachs' price-erosion tracker shows a "relatively benign" environment, with erosion expected to normalise to mid-to-high single digits; injectables saw some monthly increase in erosion [IndiaMedToday, 10 Jul 2026].
- FDA inspections: OAI (official action indicated) outcomes at Indian sites fell to 3 in FY26 from 7 in FY25; FDA drug-site inspections in India 212 in 2025 vs 284 in 2024; unannounced foreign inspections expanded from May 2025; VAI outcomes rose [TradingView/Moneycontrol, 2026]. Warning letters still land — five in Q1 2026 cited data falsification at Indian firms/labs [Business Standard/qualityvigilance search results; not opened in full].
- Relevance here: only Indoco has material US exposure (sterile Goa site under OAI/warning letter awaiting re-inspection) [Indoco deep file].

## Exports and emerging markets
- India's pharma exports: US $9.46 bn = 30.42% of FY26 exports (implying ~$31 bn total); Brazil $0.92 bn, South Africa $0.75 bn, Nigeria $0.73 bn [IBEF, 2026].
- Reported but not opened: total FY26 exports a record $31.12 bn (+2.1%), US down ~10%, growth offset by LatAm, Africa and Europe; industry expects 6–9% export growth in FY27 [search results: ANI/Business Standard, May–Sep 2026].
- Crisil: exports to grow 14–16% in rupee terms in FY27; formulations are 83% of exports (57% to regulated markets); growth "broadening beyond the US" — complex generics/biosimilars in Europe, branded generics across Asia, Africa, LatAm [India Business Trade, Sep 2026, citing Crisil Ratings].
- Small-cap EM exposure in this group: Sakar (Africa/LatAm/APAC + EU oncology), Beta (LatAm/SE Asia/CIS tenders), Fredun (Africa/CIS), Sai (Australia/NZ via Noumed, Philippines) [company deep files].

## Cost drivers
- West Asia conflict (2026) lifted solvent, raw-material and freight costs and forced air freight for some exporters; ICRA notes West Asia is only ~2% of sales and ~7% of imports but prolonged disruption would hurt margins [ICRA Mar-2026 update; Sai Q1 FY27 call; Indoco Q1 FY27 call].
- Crisil expects sector operating margins to fall 150–200 bp to 21–21.5% in FY27 on raw material, energy and freight inflation [India Business Trade, Sep 2026 (Crisil)]. Goldman expected ~185 bp YoY margin decline in Q1 FY27 [IndiaMedToday, Jul 2026].
- Platinum price spike hit oncology (platin) makers until the NPPA revision [BioSpectrum, Jun 2026].

## Competitive structure and where profit sits
- Domestic branded prescriptions carry the best margins (gross margins 60–80%; e.g. Jagsonpal 65%, Corona ~82%); CDMO/contract manufacturing and hospital supply earn thinner margins (Beta CDMO EBITDA 17–18% vs branded 35–36%; hospital channel "price comes first") [Jagsonpal Q1 FY27 call; Beta Q4 FY26 call; Corona deep file].
- Crisil's broad 190-company set is expected at ~21–21.5% operating margin in FY27 [Crisil]; ICRA (Sep 2025) said Indian firms are lifting R&D to 6–7% of revenue for complex/specialty products [IBEF, 19 Sep 2025, citing ICRA].
- Market is fragmented: small companies rank #33 (Indoco, 0.49% share), #88 (Jagsonpal) — scale players (Sun, Torrent, Cipla, Mankind etc.) grow faster in chronic [Indoco IP; Jagsonpal IP; ICICI Direct].
- Consolidation continues via brand/portfolio acquisitions (Torrent–JB Chemicals; small caps buying brand portfolios: Jagsonpal–Aequitas/Group Pharma wellness, Beta–Nivian, Sai–Noumed) [Whalesbook, Jul 2026; company filings].

## Cycle position
- Domestic: mid-cycle upswing — IPM growth re-accelerated from ~8% (2025) to ~11–12% (H1 2026), led by chronic and price [Pharmarack; IQVIA via ICICI Direct].
- US generics: down-cycle for large players (gRevlimid cliff, erosion), but tariffs on generics avoided so far [ICRA; India Briefing].
- Margins: peak-ish in FY26, now compressing on input costs [Crisil; Goldman].

## Outlook FY27–FY29 (forecaster named)
- **Crisil Ratings (Sep 2026):** FY27 sector revenue +11–13% (vs ~8% FY26); domestic +9–11% (price 5–6%, volume recovering to 4–5%); exports +14–16%; operating margin 21–21.5% (−150–200 bp); credit profiles stable [India Business Trade/Whalesbook, Sep 2026].
- **ICRA (Mar 2026):** 25-company sample revenue +9–11% FY26, +6–8% FY27 (US slowdown); domestic +8–10% in both years [ICRA research summary, 31 Mar 2026].
- **India Ratings (Feb 2026):** neutral outlook FY27; revenue ~+10%; IPM +9% (price hikes 4–5% plus launches) [Pharmabiz, Feb 2026].
- **Pharmarack (Jan 2026):** IPM 2026 +7.8–8.1% (realistic case) — already being beaten [BioSpectrum, Jan 2026].
- **FY28–FY29:** no agency has published numbers. My working assumption, extrapolating the above: IPM 9–10% a year (price 4–6%, volume 2–4%, launches 2–3%); US generics flat to low single digit for Indian exporters unless the April 2027 Section 232 review extends tariffs to generics; EM exports high single to low double digit. Key swing factors: the generics-tariff review, GLP-1 price competition, and whether input costs ease.
