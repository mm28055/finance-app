# Jagsonpal Pharmaceuticals — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code JAGSNPHARM (standalone; the consolidated page is empty because Aequitas became a subsidiary only in July 2026). Price ₹242, market cap ₹1,598 cr, 52-week range ₹155–265, P/E 34, book value ₹41.2, ROCE 23%, ROE 17%, dividend yield 1.65% [Screener]. Controlled since Q4 FY22 by Infinity Holdings, a Mauritius-based private-equity fund (with Aresko entities); promoter group 69.1% after a buyback in which promoters did not participate [ICRA Jan-25; AR FY26 note 36; Screener].

## Business and segments
- Delhi-based branded-generics company selling in India only; asset-light — all products are made by third-party contract manufacturers (its own plants were sold/closed in FY17–FY22) [ICRA Jan-25; AR FY26 BRSR].
- Therapy mix (approx.): gynaecology ~50%, orthopaedics ~25%, dermatology 10–15%, plus paediatrics/general; brands include Maintane (~₹46 cr per Pharmarack: injections ₹34 cr, tablets ₹13 cr), Indocap, Endoreg, Lycored, Metadec, Divatrone, Eukroma, KTC [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Top 10 brands ~58–60% of sales; 9 of the top 10 rank in the top 5 of their molecule; 14 brands in top 5 and 5 ranked #1; IPM rank #88 (up 4 places) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Reach: 1,000+ medical reps, 1,200+ distributors, 4,000+ towns; ~92% of the portfolio outside NLEM price control, so up to ~9.9–10% annual price increases are allowed [AR FY26; Q4 call].
- Acquisitions: Yash Pharma brands (India/Bhutan, dermatology and paediatrics) in May 2024 for over ₹90 cr; 85% of Aequitas Healthcare (hospital-channel brands, FY26 revenue ₹53 cr, EBITDA ~₹0.5 cr) for ₹20.8 cr (EV ~₹25 cr), completed July 2026; business transfer agreement (23 Sep 2026) to buy Group Pharmaceuticals' wellness portfolio (FY26 turnover ₹24.6 cr) for ₹23.7 cr upfront plus up to ₹23 cr linked to FY28 sales (cap ₹46.7 cr), closing by 1 Nov 2026 [Q1 call; AR FY26; BSE filing 23 Sep 2026].
- Aequitas: 49 institutional reps, 1,000+ corporate hospitals (Max, Manipal, Medanta, Cloudnine, Rainbow, Aster DM); top 10 products >50% of sales; ~50 hospitals give most of revenue; no government tenders [Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Jun-25 76 → Sep-25 74 → Dec-25 73 → Mar-26 64 → Jun-26 82. Operating margin 19% → 22% → 22% → 17% → 21%. Net profit 11 → 13 → 11 → 9 → 13 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹82.2 cr (+8.8%), gross margin 65.2%, "operating EBITDA" ₹19.1 cr (23.2%, +241 bp) — note this is before ESOP cost (₹1.8 cr in Q1); PAT ₹13.2 cr (+22%, 16% margin) [IP Q1FY27].
- Secondary market growth (Pharmarack) was 18.9% vs IPM 11.6% in Q1, but primary sales grew only 9% — management is "looking into" the gap [Q1 call].
- Annual (Screener): sales FY22 ₹218 cr → FY23 ₹237 cr → FY24 ₹209 cr → FY25 ₹269 cr → FY26 ₹287 cr; operating margin 12% → 14% → 11% → 19% → 20%; net profit ₹19 cr → ₹27 cr → ₹22 cr → ₹55 cr (incl. ~₹26 cr one-off other income in Dec-24) → ₹43 cr. 5-year sales CAGR 9%, profit CAGR 21% [Screener].
- FY26 (company): revenue +7% to ₹287 cr; operating EBITDA ~₹61 cr (21%); profit from operations +19% to ~₹45 cr; exceptional item: new labour-code charge in Q3 [Q4 call].
- Growth mix (Pharmarack, MAT): JPL volume +2%, new products +3%, price +6.5–7% vs industry volume ~1%, new products 3%, price ~5% [Q4 call].
- Management: FY22–FY26 EBITDA and PAT up 2.5x, free cash flow up 8x; ₹250 cr+ operating cash generated over four years [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Standing guidance (Q3 and Q4 FY26 calls): grow at 1.5x the Indian pharma market (IPM) — "12% to 15% right now"; earlier framing 12–14% top line plus 100–150 bp EBITDA margin gain [Q4 call].
- Q4 FY26 call (28 Apr 2026): kept 1.5x IPM; 9–10 launches in FY27; ₹40 cr buyback at ₹250 (no promoter participation); 200% dividend (₹4/share incl. 75% special, ~₹26 cr) [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (30 Jul 2026): guidance kept; Aequitas to reach ~₹100 cr revenue and ₹10 cr+ EBITDA within ~2.5 years (FY28–29); PCPM (sales per rep per month) target above ₹2.5 lakh [Q1 call].
- Delivery: Q4 FY26 revenue +10% ("double digit" as promised). Q1 FY27 primary sales +9% — **below** the 12–15% implied by 1.5x IPM, though secondary sales ran at 18.9%. Margin guide **beaten** (+241 bp). Buyback and Aequitas **done** as promised [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Verdict: guidance **kept**; Q1 **mixed** (revenue short, margin ahead).

## Capex and capacity
- No manufacturing capex — outsourced model. Capital goes to brand acquisitions: Yash Pharma (>₹90 cr, FY25; fixed/intangible assets rose from ₹9 cr to ₹94 cr), Aequitas (₹20.8 cr), Group Pharma wellness (₹23.7–46.7 cr) [Screener; Q1 call; BSE filing 23 Sep 2026].
- Management says it can fund acquisitions of up to ~₹400 cr from cash plus ~₹200 cr of debt capacity [Q4 call].
- Payback evidence: Yash brands (Eukroma, Pru) now among growth drivers; Aequitas at ₹0.5 cr EBITDA must reach ₹10 cr to justify its price [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash ₹190 cr+ at Mar-26; ₹170 cr at Jun-26 after the ₹40 cr buyback and ₹20.8 cr Aequitas payment. Borrowings ~₹8 cr (lease-type) [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener].
- Working capital very lean: debtor days 22, inventory days 51, cash conversion cycle 33 days; working-capital days 11 (FY26) vs 52 (FY24) [Screener].
- Operating cash flow ₹47 cr in FY26 (101% of operating profit); free cash flow ₹47 cr [Screener].
- Shareholder returns: dividend payout 62% (FY26); ₹40 cr buyback (3.67x subscribed, ~40% premium to market) [Screener; Q1 call].
- Aequitas will run a longer working-capital cycle (hospital credit) and is kept as a separate subsidiary for that reason [Q1 call].
- Rating: none current — ICRA's BBB(Positive)/A2 withdrawn at the company's request in Jan 2025 (no debt to rate) [ICRA Jan-25].

## Moat evidence
- Prescription franchise in gynaecology: brand leadership (5 brands #1 in molecule; 14 in top 5); power brands grew 19% vs market 16% [Q1 call].
- Pricing power: ~92% of portfolio outside NLEM; price growth 6.5–7% vs market ~5% [Q4 call].
- Capital-light: gross margin 64–65%, ROCE 23% with no plants [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- Weak points: volume growth only ~2%; reliance on a few brands (Maintane is a top-3 brand and a large share of recent growth); rank #88 — a small player [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Risks and red flags
- **PE-controlled promoter**: Infinity Holdings (with Aresko) has held control since 2022; a future stake sale could change management or strategy [ICRA Jan-25; AR FY26 note 36].
- Primary vs secondary gap (9% vs 18.9%) and a C&FA distribution transition flagged in the risk section — watch for channel-inventory swings [Q1 call; AR FY26 risk management].
- "Operating EBITDA" in company material excludes ESOP cost (₹1.8 cr in Q1 FY27; MD Manish Gupta's FY26 pay ₹3.54 cr incl. ESOP perquisites; 1,55,000 shares allotted to him on exercise) — use Screener's margin (21%) for comparisons [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 corporate governance].
- Acquisition risk: moving into lower-margin hospital and wellness channels; Aequitas margins are thin ("in hospital settings… it is always the price that comes first") [Q1 call].
- Contingent liabilities (FY26): customs duty ₹10.2 cr, VAT ₹5.8 cr, income tax ₹4.8 cr (new), GST ₹1.6 cr — about ₹22 cr in total vs ₹16 cr a year earlier [AR FY26 note 38, ₹ million].
- Outsourced manufacturing: quality depends on contract manufacturers; NLEM/price-control and generic-prescription pushes named as structural risks [AR FY26 BRSR and risk section].
- Board change: independent director Pallavi Dinodia Gupta stepped down (personal reasons); Anil Kumar Matai joined [Q1 call].
- Auditor opinion unmodified; Key Audit Matter on revenue recognition only [AR FY26 auditor's report].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: "back on growth after two sluggish quarters", cash-return focus. Q1: acquisitions take centre stage (Aequitas done; Group Pharma wellness followed in September) [Q4 call; Q1 call; BSE filing 23 Sep 2026].
- New numbers: Aequitas ₹100 cr / ₹10 cr EBITDA target; PCPM >₹2.5 lakh [Q1 call].
- Revenue growth slowed from Q4's +10% to Q1's +9% while margins jumped [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Tone on concentration: management listed more brands (Indocap, Endoreg, Pru, Eukroma) as growth drivers beyond Maintane [Q1 call].

## Best management quotes
- "we continue to maintain our guidance that our objective will be to breach this 1.5x" (of industry growth) [Q1 call].
- "pharmaceutical industry for growth is not capital intensive. It is brand intensive." [Q4 call]
- "in hospital settings, it is more than the brand, it is always the price that comes first" [Q1 call].

## My read
Jagsonpal is a clean, cash-rich, asset-light brand business that has lifted margins from ~11% to ~21% and pays out most of its profit. Growth is the question: primary sales are still single-digit and the 1.5x-market promise has not yet shown in reported revenue, so the company is buying growth in lower-margin channels (hospital, wellness). At about 34x earnings the price needs sustained low-to-mid-teens growth with margins held above 20%. Two quarters of double-digit primary growth and Aequitas reaching ₹5 cr+ EBITDA would support it; another single-digit quarter or margin dilution from the new businesses would argue for caution.
