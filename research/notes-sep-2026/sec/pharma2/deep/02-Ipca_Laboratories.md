# Ipca Laboratories — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code IPCALAB. Price ₹1,971, market cap ₹50,000 cr, 52-week range ₹1,252–2,039 [Screener]. Ipca does not publish an investor presentation; the calls, press release, annual report and the India Ratings rationale are the main disclosures.

## Business and segments
- Fully integrated maker of APIs (active ingredients) and finished medicines: 350+ formulations, 80+ APIs, 15 API and 11 formulation plants, sales in 100+ countries [Ind-Ra Jul-26].
- FY26 revenue mix: domestic formulations ~40%, export formulations 22%, subsidiaries (mainly Unichem) 24%, APIs 14% [Ind-Ra Jul-26]. Consolidated revenue ₹9,646 cr; India ₹4,387 cr (45%), outside India ₹5,260 cr (55%), of which US ₹2,140 cr [AR FY26 note 36].
- FY26 by line: domestic ₹3,817 cr (+10%); export formulations ₹2,083 cr (+9%), of which branded ROW ₹664 cr (+14%), generics ₹1,149 cr (+17%), institutional tenders ₹270 cr (−24%); API ₹1,396 cr (+10%); US (Ipca + Unichem) ₹1,567 cr (+14%) [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27: domestic ₹1,082 cr (+13%); exports ₹603 cr (+34%): branded ₹143 cr (+16%), generics ₹340 cr (+27%; EU/UK ₹137 cr from ₹83 cr), institutional ₹112 cr (+107%, of which ~₹40 cr slipped from March); API ₹424 cr (+30%); consolidated ₹2,788 cr (+21%) [Q1 call].
- India: rank 16, market share 2.08–2.09%; six brands in the top 300; Zerodol ~₹1,300 cr with 8% share of pain management; chronic 33% of domestic sales; ~7,000 reps [Q1 call; Ind-Ra Jul-26]. Q1 chronic growth 17.2% vs market 15.2%; acute 8.9% vs 7.5%; anti-malarials −24% and now ~1% of sales [Q1 call].
- Unichem (bought Aug 2023): Q1 US +27% (mostly Ipca products sold through Unichem; Unichem's own portfolio +9%), Europe +3%, Brazil +52% and now profitable, API ₹58 cr from ₹33–34 cr [Q1 call]. Unichem FY26 EBITDA margin fell from 12% to about 8% [Q4 call].
- Capacity/utilisation not disclosed; API capacity at Ratlam is "running out" [Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue / operating profit / OPM (₹ cr): Jun-25 2,309 / 416 / 18%; Sep-25 2,556 / 545 / 21%; Dec-25 2,392 / 533 / 22%; Mar-26 2,388 / 484 / 20%; Jun-26 2,788 / 670 / 24% [Screener]. Company-reported Q1 EBITDA ₹638 cr (22.88%) vs ₹425 cr (18.39%) [Q1 call].
- Q1 net profit ₹424 cr vs ₹233 cr (+82%) [Screener].
- Annual: sales FY24 ₹7,662 cr → FY25 ₹8,897 cr → FY26 ₹9,599 cr; operating profit ₹1,324 → 1,731 → 1,990 cr; OPM 17% → 19% → 21%; net profit ₹523 → 785 → 1,184 cr [Screener].
- Standalone EBITDA margin FY26 25.18% (22.66%); Q1 FY27 26% [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Unit economics of Q1 margin gain (consolidated +449 bps): material cost −1.35 pts of sales, staff cost −1.95 pts, other costs −1.19 pts — i.e. mostly operating leverage [Q1 call].
- Currency: about 5 of the 21 points of Q1 growth came from a ~11% weaker rupee [Q1 call].
- India growth split Q1: volume ~5%, price 4.5–5%, new launches ~2% [Q1 call]. Price rises of 5–7% a year on non-controlled products [Q4 call].
- ROCE 17%, ROE 16% [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (1 Jun 2026): FY27 consolidated revenue growth 12–13% (India 12–13%, CIS 10–11%, generics 12–13%); consolidated EBITDA margin ~22–22.3% (from 20.7%); Unichem growth ~10% with 12–13% EBITDA margin; material costs to rise 10–12% if oil stays high; price hikes of 6–7% to offset; 6–8 US launches [Q4 call].
- FY26 track record: guided 20% consolidated margin, delivered 20.72% [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (14 Aug 2026): revenue growth **raised to 14–16%**; consolidated EBITDA margin **raised to 23%**; Unichem guidance kept ("let us see how it performs for some more quarters"); institutional business back to single-digit growth, ₹260–300 cr a year; US growth of 15–17% "possible"; FY27 capex ₹700–800 cr [Q1 call].
- Longer term: standalone margin from ~26% to ~30%; consolidated 25–26% in 2–3 years as subsidiaries improve [Q1 call].
- Delivered in Q1: revenue +21%, EBITDA margin 22.9% — both ahead of the Q4 promises. But ~₹40 cr of tender sales shifted from Q4 into Q1 and 5 points of growth were currency, so part of the beat is timing and FX [Q1 call].
- Verdict: **raised**.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹652 cr (FY25 ₹776 cr) for North Carolina (Pisgah Labs formulation plant), Dewas, Wardha and Pithampur [Ind-Ra Jul-26]. FY27 capex ₹700–800 cr, funded from internal cash [Q1 call; Ind-Ra Jul-26].
- Projects: Pithampur plant for controlled/extended-release products for US and Europe; new API plants at Dewas and Wardha because Ratlam is near full; continuous-process conversion; ~₹100 cr extra in FY27 for biotech pilot plants and R&D [Q1 call].
- Pisgah Labs (US) formulation plant to be ready in Q4 FY27; meaningful revenue only in FY28 [Q4 call].
- Biosimilars: 7 candidates; Phase 3 waivers from EU and US regulators (only PK/PD studies needed); clinical work from FY28; revenue from FY29–FY30; one large product targeted near patent expiry [Q1 call].
- Payback not disclosed. Ind-Ra's positive trigger is "better utilisation of expanded capacities" — i.e. the capex has not yet earned its return [Ind-Ra Jul-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹809 cr at March 2026 (₹1,363 cr a year earlier); Ind-Ra: debt ₹809 cr, unencumbered cash ₹1,198 cr, net debt/EBITDA −0.2x (net cash), interest cover 26x [Screener; Ind-Ra Jul-26].
- By Q1 debt ~₹193 cr long-term, zero working-capital debt; ~$50 mn dollar loans prepaid before March; ₹70 cr to remain at year-end [Q1 call].
- Operating cash flow FY26 ₹1,142 cr (FY25 ₹1,321 cr); CFO/EBITDA 78%; free cash flow ₹486 cr [Screener]. Ind-Ra FCF ₹393 cr [Ind-Ra Jul-26].
- Working capital heavy: inventory days 353, debtor days 77, cash conversion cycle 308 days [Screener]. Unichem inventory rose because ~40% of US shipments moved by air a year earlier, now 4–5%; sea freight lengthens transit [Q4 call].
- Dividend: ₹52 cr in FY26 (₹101 cr in FY25) [Ind-Ra Jul-26].
- Rating: IND AA+/Stable and IND A1+ affirmed (6 Jul 2026, corrected 13 Aug 2026); downgrade trigger net leverage above 1x [Ind-Ra Jul-26].

## Moat evidence
- Backward integration: own APIs for its own formulations; material cost only ~25% of sales, so input inflation is absorbed [Q4 call; Ind-Ra Jul-26].
- Pricing power: "we are increasing prices everywhere"; no dollar-price renegotiations after the rupee fell [Q1 call]. Domestic price growth 4.5–5% in Q1 [Q1 call].
- Leadership in pain (Zerodol 8% share of a ₹16,000 cr market), anti-malaria and urology [Ind-Ra Jul-26].
- Gross margin FY26 70.7%, up 182 bps [Ind-Ra Jul-26].
- Limits: domestic chronic share only 33%; many brands under price control where "you can't increase even a paisa" [Ind-Ra Jul-26; Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Krebs Biochemicals (associate, 49.65%)**: Ipca impaired ₹118.9 cr of its exposure in FY26 (₹11.1 cr in FY25). The auditor's CARO report lists ₹54.06 cr of loans and interest to Krebs unpaid, some for more than 730 days; ₹48 cr overdue beyond 90 days. Ipca also has a ₹11 cr deposit pledged for Krebs's bank loan [AR FY26 CARO 3(iii); note 32; note 38].
- **Lyka Labs (associate)**: revenue flat and EBITDA negative after field-force expansion; shareholders complained on the call about poor disclosure [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Unichem: EU fine for Niche Generics paid — €19.55 mn (₹204 cr) including ₹58 cr interest booked as exceptional in FY26; Ireland plant closed; Jogeshwari (Mumbai) land sold to Lodha for ₹279 cr (₹17.65 cr gain) [AR FY26 note 32].
- Auditor: unmodified; key audit matters are inventory valuation, impairment of plant (PPE + CWIP ₹4,942 cr, 40% of assets) and impairment of investments/loans to subsidiaries. Audit trail for direct database changes kept only three days due to system limits [AR FY26 auditor's reports].
- Contingent liabilities small: ₹249 cr total [AR FY26 note 38].
- Freight: rates up 50–80%; South America containers $3,000 → $9,000–10,000; US reefers $7,000 → $12,500–13,000 [Q1 call].
- Institutional (Global Fund type) tenders fell 24% in FY26 on donor funding cuts [Q4 call].
- Price control on part of the domestic portfolio [Q4 call; Ind-Ra Jul-26].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (raw material +10–12%, freight +25%, Unichem margins down to 8%) to confident (guidance raised on both revenue and margin) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New numbers: 14–16% growth, 23% margin, 25–26% medium-term margin, ₹700–800 cr capex, Phase 3 waivers for biosimilars [Q1 call].
- Unichem swung from problem to contributor in the US, but its own guidance was not raised [Q1 call].
- Oddity: in the same Q1 call the MD first answered "Yes" that 12–13% guidance stays, then raised it to 14–16% a few minutes later [Q1 call].

## My read
Ipca is improving: margins have risen for three years (17% → 21% OPM) and Q1 showed real operating leverage, with guidance raised on both revenue (14–16%) and margin (23%). Part of Q1 was flattering (₹40 cr of tender timing and 5 points of currency), so the full-year beat will be smaller than Q1 suggests. The price already assumes the margin path to 25–26%; it needs Unichem to keep recovering and the new US/Europe capacity (Pithampur, Pisgah) to fill. The Krebs write-downs and unpaid loans are a capital-allocation blot, though small next to a ₹50,000 cr company. A second quarter of 23%+ margin without tender or FX help would confirm the view; a fall back to 20–21% or more associate write-offs would weaken it.
