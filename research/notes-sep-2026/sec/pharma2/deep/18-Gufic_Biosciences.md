# Gufic Biosciences — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code GUFICBIO (consolidated). Price ₹424, market cap ₹4,249 cr, 52-week range ₹268–468, trailing P/E 58, ROCE 12.3%, ROE 10.1%, dividend yield 0.02% [Screener]. Promoters (Choksi family) 72.50%, no pledge disclosed [Screener; AR FY26 note 17].

## Business and segments
- Gufic is one of India's largest makers of freeze-dried (lyophilised) injections. Revenue mix (FY26, management): domestic brands 48–50%, exports 20–22%, contract manufacturing for other drug makers (CMO) 18–19%, APIs/tenders/others the rest [Q4 call].
- Domestic divisions: critical care (hospital anti-infectives and antifungals), Sparsh (hospitals/nursing homes, dual-chamber IV bags), women's health/IVF (Ferticare — leader in recurrent implantation failure; Zenova), botulinum toxin (Synnox/Zarbot; No. 2 in India with about 23% share; 3.5–4% of company revenue), and nutraceutical/Ayurveda [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 pp.6–10].
- CMO clients include Glenmark, Lupin, Abbott and Zydus; top five customers 31% of FY26 revenue; top five products over 14% [CRISIL Sep-26]. 70+ CMO partners; 20+ Indian pharma companies have audited or use Indore [IP Q1FY27 pp.5, 19].
- Plants: Navsari Unit I (toxin, API) and Unit II (lyophilised 18 m vials a year, EU-GMP Hungary), Belgaum (herbal), and the new Indore plant (lyophilised 5 m vials a month, liquid 6 m, ampoules 10 m; four lines, six lyophilisers) [IP Q1FY27 pp.4, 20; Q1 call].
- Indore utilisation: about 18% → 22–25% (FY26) → 30–35% in Q1 FY27; target 40–45% by March 2027. Indore reached EBITDA breakeven in Q4 FY26 [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- GLP-1: contract manufacturing of semaglutide for Hetero (approval May 2026; ramp from Q2, real traction Q3); ~70% of capacity aimed at export filings in 22+ countries; Gufic will not sell its own brand [Q1 call].
- International: moving from distributor-owned licences to Gufic-owned marketing authorisations (Gufic Ireland, Gufic UK), with its own teams in Africa, the Philippines and Mexico; approvals in 8 countries in Q1 FY27; CHAI tie-up for liposomal amphotericin B giving access to 109 public-health markets (WHO pre-qualification needed first) [IP Q1FY27 p.11–12; Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- Annual (Screener consolidated): sales FY24 ₹807 cr → FY25 ₹820 cr → FY26 ₹944 cr; OPM 19% → 17% → 16%; net profit ₹86 cr → ₹70 cr → ₹64 cr. TTM sales ₹981 cr, profit ₹73 cr [Screener]. Company: FY26 revenue ₹940.5 cr, EBITDA ₹152.9 cr (16.26%), PAT ₹63.2 cr [IP Q1FY27 p.17].
- Quarterly (Screener consolidated): Jun-25 ₹224 cr (OPM 15%), Sep-25 ₹237 cr (16%), Dec-25 ₹231 cr (14%), Mar-26 ₹252 cr (19%), Jun-26 ₹261 cr (18%). Net profit ₹13 cr, ₹17 cr, ₹12 cr, ₹22 cr, ₹22 cr [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹260.8 cr (+14.9%), EBITDA ₹47.2 cr (18.09% vs 14.6%), PBT ₹30.1 cr, PAT ₹22.46 cr (+86%) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.17].
- Why margins fell in FY25–FY26: Indore's costs, interest and depreciation were capitalised until about Q3 FY25, then hit the P&L while the plant ran at low utilisation; interest rose from ₹17 cr (FY24) to ₹37 cr (FY26) and depreciation from ₹17 cr to ₹31 cr [Q1 call; Screener].
- FY26 top line lost about ₹22 cr from moving the critical-care division off direct hospital billing (receivables had reached 140–150+ days) back to distributors [Q4 call].
- Unit economics: current Indore mix averages ₹80–100 a vial (e.g. pantoprazole ₹60–80); management aims for ₹300–400 and later ~₹500 a vial with depot, liposomal and complex products [Q1 call]. Indore expected margin 31–32% at scale vs Navsari 18–18.5% [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (1 Jun 2026): revenue growth 15% a year; EBITDA margin about 18% in FY27, then +0.5–1% a year, above 20% by 2030; gross margin +0.5–1% a year; gross debt about ₹400 cr to stay flat (working capital needs); ~₹20 cr a year replacement capex and no greenfield capex for two years; EU-GMP certificate for Indore "soon"; filler launch in Q3/Q4 FY27; US FDA operations 2028–2029 as a pure CMO [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (17 Aug 2026): growth widened to "15% to 20%… our bare minimum"; FY27 revenue about ₹1,100 cr; peak revenue from existing plants ₹1,600–1,800 cr without new capex; 40–45% Indore utilisation by year-end; EU-GMP now "in the next month or 2" after compliance queries were answered; filler launch December or January [Q1 call].
- Delivery: Q1 margin 18.09% met the ~18% promise; revenue +14.9% in line with 15%; EU-GMP certificate **slipped** (audit was in Dec-2025, certificate "expected soon" since Q4; the presentation says "corrective action responses submitted") [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- Verdict: guidance **kept/slightly raised** (15% → 15–20%); regulatory timeline **delayed**.

## Capex and capacity
- Indore greenfield: about ₹360 cr (ICRA) / "300-odd crores" (management), commercial production from January 2025 (first batch Oct 2024) [ICRA Nov-25; Q1 call; CRISIL Sep-26].
- Peak revenue from current assets: Navsari ~₹800–950 cr, Indore ₹800–1,200 cr depending on mix — management's total ₹1,600–1,800 cr [Q1 call]. At today's ~₹940 cr, this implies room for roughly 70–90% more revenue without big capex.
- New Indore lines (depot/microspheres, liposomal) are being added "within the existing capex"; GLP-1 readiness cost ₹30–35 cr over two years [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Replacement capex about ₹20 cr a year; no greenfield for two years. Future regulated-market toxin capacity would need a partner [Q4 call].
- R&D 8–12% of revenue a year (management claim); 17 clinical programmes; small investment in Australian immuno-oncology company Selvax [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.15].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹405 cr at Mar-2026 (₹336 cr a year earlier); unencumbered cash ₹64 cr; gearing 0.58x; interest cover 4.2x (FY26), 5.0x (Q1 FY27) [Screener; CRISIL Sep-26]. Management calls ₹400 cr "the peak" [Q4 call].
- Cash flow weak: operating cash flow ₹123 cr (FY25) but only ₹45 cr in FY26 (CFO/operating profit 38%); free cash flow ₹14 cr [Screener].
- Working capital heavy: Screener debtor days 115, inventory 281, payables 172, cash conversion cycle 224 days (FY26). CRISIL: gross current assets 280 days (receivables 120, inventory 149), extra stock bought on Middle East disruption; bank limits 75% used on average [Screener; CRISIL Sep-26].
- Collection terms: domestic 30–45 days, CMO 90–120 days [Q4 call].
- Dividend negligible (payout ~2%) [Screener].
- Rating: CRISIL A-/Stable/A2+ reaffirmed 8 Sep 2026; bank limits enhanced to ₹516 cr from ₹425 cr; ICRA A- (Stable) in Nov 2025 [CRISIL Sep-26; ICRA Nov-25].
- Equity: ₹99.99 cr preferential allotment in FY24 (Motilal Oswal fund) used partly to prepay term loans [ICRA Nov-25; Q4 call].

## Moat evidence
- Scale in lyophilisation: "one of the largest" capacities in India; Indore batch sizes 100,000+ vials vs industry average ~40,000 (2.5x) — lower per-vial cost for exports [IP Q1FY27 p.5].
- Approvals: 203 state FDA product approvals at Indore; 40 tech transfers done, 27 more; Navsari holds EU-GMP (Hungary), ANVISA, TGA, Health Canada etc. [IP Q1FY27 pp.4–5, 20].
- Own-strain botulinum toxin — No. 2 in India with ~23% share against Allergan; filler in-licensed from Revanesse/Prollenium (No. 2 in US) to complete the aesthetic bag; Indian filler market about ₹200 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- First-in-India launches: aztreonam-avibactam (monobactam combination) after patent expiry; dual-chamber bags [IP Q1FY27 p.6].
- Weakness: contract manufacturing is price-competitive ("tomorrow they find a manufacturer who is cheaper than me, they can easily replace me") — hence the push to own licences [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **EU-GMP delay** at Indore: audit December 2025; corrective actions submitted; certificate still awaited in September 2026. Export margin expansion depends on it [IP Q1FY27 p.12; Q1 call].
- **Working capital and cash conversion**: 224-day cash cycle; CFO only 30% of EBITDA in FY26; debt rose ₹69 cr in FY26 [Screener].
- **Related parties**: rent of ₹4.2 cr a year to promoter company Gufic Private Ltd and ₹0.48 cr to Gufic Chem; ₹3.5 cr security deposit with Gufic Private; the Navsari land used as loan security is owned by Gufic Private, which also gives a corporate guarantee (up to ₹36.4 cr) — normal in size but tie the plant to a promoter entity [AR FY26 notes 19, 39].
- **Bank relationship investment**: ₹6.5 cr put into Saraswat Co-operative Bank shares (bank exposure ~₹250 cr), justified by a ~15% dividend [Q4 call].
- **Governance nit**: exchange penalties (₹1.72 lakh NSE; ₹8,000 BSE) for appointing a director over 75 without a special resolution; NSE rejected the waiver on 23 Sep 2026 [BSE filing 24 Sep 2026].
- **Auditor**: unmodified opinion; key audit matter revenue recognition (rebates, returns, timing); audit trail maintained in SAP [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Contingent liabilities small: ₹2.1 cr sales tax/excise disputes, ₹9.6 cr LCs and guarantees [AR FY26 note 44].
- **Partner dependence**: GLP-1 revenue depends entirely on Hetero's selling; Gufic's role "purely as a CMO" [Q1 call].
- **Disclosure gaps**: no divisional revenue or margin tables despite analyst requests [Q4 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was about finishing the reset (hospital billing clean-up, Indore breakeven, 30% utilisation) and a ₹400 cr debt ceiling. Q1 moved to scaling: GLP-1 production, depot/liposomal lines at Indore, own field teams abroad, CHAI deal, and a peak-revenue figure of ₹1,600–1,800 cr.
- Growth wording rose from "15%" to "15–20%… bare minimum"; EU-GMP timing slipped again.

## My read
The business is improving: Indore's fixed costs are now absorbed, the margin is back to about 18% and profit nearly doubled in Q1. The catch is cash — a 224-day cash cycle and weak cash conversion mean profit growth has not yet turned into lower debt. At about 58 times trailing profit, the price needs roughly 20%+ earnings growth for several years, which requires the EU-GMP certificate, Indore utilisation above 40% and margins moving towards 20%. What would change the view: EU-GMP approval and a visible fall in working-capital days (positive), or another certificate delay and rising debt (negative).
