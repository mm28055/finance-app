# Ajanta Pharma — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code AJANTPHARM. Price ₹3,548, market cap ₹44,333 cr, 52-week range ₹2,330–3,797 [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Specialty branded-generics company: branded generics in India, Asia and Africa (~2/3 of sales), US generics, and anti-malarial tenders in Africa. 7 plants (2 USFDA-approved), one captive API plant; 850+ scientists; 600+ products; 6,050+ reps globally [CARE Aug-26; IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 revenue by business (₹ cr, YoY): India 509 (+24%, incl. trade generics 48); Asia branded 255 (−16%); Africa branded 295 (+30%); US generics 487 (+57%); Africa institutional 49 (+71%). Shares: India 32%, US 30%, Africa 18%, Asia 16%, institution 4% [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- FY26 (₹ cr): India 1,654 (+14%); Asia 1,175 (−1%); Africa 861 (+15%); US 1,557 (+49%); institution 160 (+9%); total revenue 5,453 (+17%) [Q4 call].
- India: rank 24 in IPM (up from 26); top 5 in its covered market; India branded mix cardiology 37%, ophthalmology 29%, dermatology 22%, pain 10%, new therapies (gynaec, nephro) 2%; ~3,750 India reps [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- US: 51 ANDAs commercialised, 17 awaiting approval, 5 tentative; market share "20% and above" in most of its products [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Capacity/utilisation not disclosed; Pithampur being expanded for emerging markets [Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue / operating profit / OPM (₹ cr): Jun-25 1,303 / 351 / 27%; Sep-25 1,354 / 328 / 24%; Dec-25 1,375 / 382 / 28%; Mar-26 1,422 / 333 / 23%; Jun-26 1,626 / 424 / 26% [Screener]. Company "adjusted EBITDA" (excluding forex loss) Q1 ₹454 cr, 28% [Q1 call].
- Net profit: Q1 FY27 ₹334 cr (+31% YoY); FY24 ₹816 cr → FY25 ₹920 cr → FY26 ₹1,056 cr [Screener].
- Annual sales FY24 ₹4,209 cr → FY25 ₹4,648 cr → FY26 ₹5,453 cr; operating profit ₹1,172 → 1,269 → 1,500 cr; OPM 28% / 27% / 28% [Screener].
- Hedging distorts reported margin: FY26 mark-to-market hedge loss ₹103 cr in other expenses vs forex gain ₹97 cr in other income; Q1 loss ₹31 cr vs gain ₹50 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Guidance is given excluding the forex loss.
- Gross margin 80% in Q1, 78% in FY26 [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Personnel cost Q1 ₹381 cr (+26% YoY); other expenses ₹493 cr (+32%) [Q1 call].
- India growth FY26: 13.1% (IQVIA) vs IPM 10% — volume 3.6%, price 4.8%, new launches 4.7% [Q4 call]. Q1 volume 4.7% vs IPM 3.4% [Q1 call].
- ROCE 35% (Screener FY26); company says 37% at June 2026 [Screener; Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (5 May 2026): FY27 revenue "high teens" (16–18%); EBITDA margin 27% ±1% (ex-forex); gross margin 77% ±1%; India mid-teens; Asia "high double-digit"; Africa high double-digit; US mid-single digit; 4–5 US launches in H2; capex ~₹400 cr (₹150 cr maintenance, ₹250 cr expansion); tax 26–26.5% [Q4 call].
- FY26 record vs earlier guidance: US guided mid-to-high teens, delivered 49%; Asia repeatedly guided to recover and fell 1% [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (30 Jul 2026): revenue high teens kept; EBITDA 27% ±1% kept; gross margin raised to 78% ±1%; India mid-teens kept; US mid-single digit kept ("upper end"); Asia now "mid-teens to high-teens" (softened); capex ₹400 cr with split changed to ₹100 cr maintenance + ₹300 cr Pithampur; tax ~26%; 5–7 US filings [Q1 call].
- Delivered in Q1: revenue +25% (ahead), adjusted margin 28% (top of band), India +24% (ahead), US +57% (far ahead), Asia −16% (missed a promised recovery for the second quarter running) [Q1 call].
- Verdict: **kept**, with a small raise on gross margin and a soft cut on Asia.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹330 cr (in line); FY27 ~₹400 cr; Q1 spend ₹83 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- ₹300 cr for Pithampur expansion on spare land at the existing site, aimed at emerging markets; funded from cash [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Asset turns high: fixed assets ₹1,867 cr against sales ₹5,453 cr (~2.9x) [Screener]. Payback not disclosed.
- R&D ~5% of sales (₹252 cr FY26); 53 launches in FY26 across markets [Q4 call; CARE Aug-26].
- New markets (Middle East, Latin America, more of Asia) are 3–5 years away; semaglutide (with Biocon) filings outside India started FY27, meaningful sales in 3–4 years [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹260 cr at March 2026 (₹47 cr a year earlier) — short-term working-capital loans taken by the US subsidiary; no term loans; gearing 0.06x; interest cover 99x [Screener; CARE Aug-26].
- Operating cash flow fell to ₹529 cr in FY26 from ₹1,157 cr; CFO/EBITDA 59%; free cash flow ₹169 cr (₹840 cr in FY25) [Screener].
- Cause: receivables rose to 125 days from 94 as the company switched from factoring to working-capital loans for the bigger US business; management calls 125 days "a new normal" [Q4 call]. Screener debtor days 124 [Screener].
- Cash and equivalents ~₹665 cr at March 2026 [CARE Aug-26].
- Payout: interim dividend ₹32/share (₹400 cr) declared in Q1 FY27; FY26 payout 33% (FY24 79%) [Q1 call; Screener].
- Rating: CARE AA+/Stable, A1+ reaffirmed 14 Aug 2026; CARE expects 10–12% revenue growth and margins above 25% [CARE Aug-26].

## Moat evidence
- India: grew 15% (MAT June 2026) vs IPM 11%; volume growth 40% above IPM; new-launch growth 75% above; ophthalmology growing 1.5x its market [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Leading brands: MET XL, Cinod (cardiology), Melacare, Pacroma (derma), Bimat, Nepaflam, Soft Drops (ophthalmology), Feburic (pain) [CARE Aug-26].
- US: dominant share (20%+) in most products; 8 launches in 15 months drove 49% FY26 growth [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Clean regulatory record at plants per CARE; but a Form 483 with 5 observations at Paithan in early 2026 [CARE Aug-26; Q4 call].
- Pricing: cannot pass on freight and raw material rises — "It is absorbed by us" [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Income tax search (August 2025)**: auditor's **emphasis of matter** in FY26 accounts — search at company premises, notice for the block period 1 Apr 2019 to 17 Nov 2025; block return filed 6 Apr 2026; impact "not ascertainable". Also disclosed in Q1 FY27 results notes. Not mentioned on either call or in the stored analysis [AR FY26 auditor's report; IP Q1FY27 results note 6].
- **Promoter pledge**: ~22.5% of promoter shares pledged (14.3% of company) as of 27 Jul 2026, by the two brothers not in management, for their own businesses; pledges are being rolled (e.g. Aayush Agrawal Trust released RBL pledge 19 Aug and created a new one with 360 One Prime on 21 Aug 2026) [CARE Aug-26; Q4 call; BSE SAST 27 Aug 2026].
- **Promoter holding fell** from 66.25% (Mar 2026) to 63.49% (Jun 2026) per Screener; I could not find the reason in documents read — needs checking (sale or pledge invocation) [Screener].
- US concentration and normalisation: management expects price erosion and share loss; FY27 US growth only mid-single digit after 49% [Q1 call].
- Asia branded fell 16% in Q1 on Middle East shipping disruption; recovery promised three times [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- USFDA Form 483 at Paithan (5 observations) [Q4 call].
- Auditor: key audit matter on revenue recognition (US chargebacks, rebates) [AR FY26].
- Contingent liabilities negligible (~₹8 cr standalone) [AR FY26 note 52].
- US tariff threat: generics exempt until April 2027; new study with August 2028 timeline [Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Numbers came in well ahead (revenue +25%, US +57%, India +24%) but guidance was only nudged (gross margin), which looks conservative [Q1 call].
- Asia recovery slipped again and guidance for it softened from "high double-digit" to "mid-teens to high-teens" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New: ₹400 cr interim dividend; warning that cheap inventory is used up and raw material costs may hit from Q2 [Q1 call].
- Capex split shifted towards expansion (₹250 → ₹300 cr) [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## My read
Ajanta is improving: FY26 profit grew 15% and Q1 FY27 31%, India is clearly beating the market on volume, and returns stay above 30%. The weak spots are cash conversion (59% of EBITDA in FY26 as US receivables ballooned), the lumpy US business that management itself expects to slow sharply, and Asia's repeated misses. Governance items need watching rather than panic: an income-tax search with an open block assessment, and rising pledges by non-executive promoter brothers. The price needs mid-to-high-teens earnings growth to hold; a clean tax outcome, Asia recovery in Q2 and US holding above ₹450 cr a quarter would support it, while a large tax demand or a pledge-driven sale would change the view.
