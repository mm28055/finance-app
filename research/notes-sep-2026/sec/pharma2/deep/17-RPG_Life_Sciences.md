# RPG Life Sciences — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code RPGLIFE (consolidated from FY26; standalone history before). Price ₹3,051, market cap ₹5,045 cr, 52-week range ₹1,731–3,130, trailing P/E 43, ROCE 25.7%, ROE 19.3%, dividend yield 0.79% [Screener]. Part of the RPG Group (Goenka family, promoters 72.95%) [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Three businesses (FY26): domestic branded formulations ₹483.5 cr (about 69% of sales), international formulations ₹123.4 cr (−6.7% on customer destocking), and APIs ₹95.1 cr (+5.3% despite a fire) [AR FY26 Directors' Report; Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 split: domestic ₹132.5 cr (+14.8%, 68.3% of sales), international ₹35.0 cr (+7.7%, 18.0%), API ₹26.6 cr (+35.6%, 13.7%) [IP Q1FY27 p.19].
- Domestic: "textbook" brands taught in medical colleges — Azoran and Mofetyl (immunosuppressants), Naprosyn (pain), Aldactone, Lomotil, Serenace, Norpace; reaches 1 lakh doctors including 36,000 specialists. About 30% of products are under price control (DPCO) [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.5].
- Therapy growth FY26: nephrology +55%, rheumatology +40%, oncology +23%, pain +19%; Naprosyn +15.6% (Q4 +24%); biosimilar antibodies (MAbs) +12% in Q4 [Q4 call].
- Rank in Indian pharma market improved from 62 (Mar-25) to 50 (Jun-26); among the top 5 fastest-growing companies in Q1 FY27 [IP Q1FY27 p.21].
- Plants: Ankleshwar (formulations) and Navi Mumbai (API, three blocks; one damaged by fire in early 2025). About 25–30% of production is outsourced to contract makers [IP Q1FY27 p.7; Q4 call].
- New structure (Jul–Sep 2026): API business moved into subsidiary RPG Active Pharma (RPGAP), 60% RPG / 40% PE firm InvAscent (₹243.33 cr primary investment; up to ₹700 cr committed in total). RPGAP bought Actis Generics (Vizag, API/intermediates, ~₹80 cr; revenue fell from ₹70 cr to ₹48 cr on intermediate price erosion) and the API business of Raghava Life Sciences (Hyderabad, 300 KL, EU-GMP, 29 molecules, ~₹135 cr; revenue potential ~₹200 cr at full use). Capacity rises from 110 KL to about 505 KL [IP Sep-26 transaction update; ICRA Sep-26; Sep-26 call].

## Key numbers
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹582 cr → FY25 ₹653 cr → FY26 ₹708 cr; OPM 22% → 25% → 21%; net profit ₹88 cr → ₹183 cr → ₹115 cr. TTM sales ₹734 cr, profit ₹120 cr [Screener].
- FY25 profit was inflated by a ₹98.9 cr gain on selling surplus Navi Mumbai land (net of a ₹16.3 cr fire loss). FY26 exceptional items net +₹3.9 cr: +₹24.75 cr fire insurance, −₹11.69 cr new labour codes, −₹9.16 cr intangible write-off; plus ₹7.91 cr loss-of-profit insurance in Q4 other income [AR FY26 note 35]. Underlying profit therefore grew in FY26 even though reported PAT fell.
- Company "EBITDA" is higher than Screener: FY26 ₹172.7 cr (24.4%) per company vs Screener operating profit ₹148 cr (21%); Q4 FY26 ₹45.2 cr (25.6%) vs Screener ₹33 cr (19%); Q1 FY27 ₹48.0 cr (24.5%) vs Screener ₹43 cr (22%). ICRA's OPBDIT margin is 21.8% FY26 and 22.1% Q1 FY27, closer to Screener [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27; ICRA Sep-26; Screener].
- Quarterly (Screener): Jun-25 ₹169 cr (OPM 21%), Sep-25 ₹182 cr (21%), Dec-25 ₹180 cr (22%), Mar-26 ₹177 cr (19%), Jun-26 ₹196 cr (22%). Net profit ₹26 cr, ₹37 cr, ₹22 cr, ₹30 cr, ₹31 cr [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: revenue +15.8%, PAT ₹30.8 cr (+17.0%) [IP Q1FY27 p.18].
- Growth mix FY26 (PharmaTrac): volume +7.0% vs market +0.7%; price +2.6% vs +5.4%; new products +4.1% vs +2.5%; total 13.7% vs 8.6% (1.6x market). Q4: 18.2% vs 10.1% [Q4 call].
- Productivity: ₹6.5 lakh per rep per month (₹5.7 lakh a year earlier); specialty reps ₹16.7 lakh [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (30 Apr 2026): no numeric company guidance. Historical run-rate given: domestic ~12%, API 9–10%, international ~9% a year for four years; aim to keep beating the market on volume and new launches; API margins "consistent around 30%"; Walmart Canada OTC Naprosyn launch by Sep–Oct 2026; 5 CDMO projects (3 in FY27, 2 in FY28); US FDA filings deliberately avoided for now; open to acquisitions "of prudent value" [Q4 call official].
- Sep 2026 acquisition call (3 Sep 2026; not a results call): API platform to grow 25%+ a year (one answer said 20% over 4–5 years) and reach ₹1,000 cr in 5–6 years through acquisitions and organic growth; API margins mid-20s, with "pressure in the initial period"; Raghava to cross ~₹200 cr revenue in about 2 years; domestic formulations to grow 1.5x the market (16% growth "this year"); ₹700 cr to be deployed within 12–18 months; US FDA approvals now "part of the larger strategy" [Sep-26 call, stored AI copy].
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: M&A promise **delivered** (Actis closed 24 Sep 2026; Raghava agreed 2 Sep 2026). API recovery **delivered** (+35.6% in Q1). Domestic beat-the-market **delivered** (+14.8%). Walmart OTC launch — **not updated**; no evidence of launch yet. There was no Q1 FY27 results call; only a presentation [Screener concall list; IP Q1FY27].
- Verdict: guidance **raised** for API (from ~10% to 25%+ a year, now partly inorganic); formulations target **kept**.

## Capex and capacity
- Organic capex over FY22–FY26 about ₹185 cr (plant upgrades, EU/TGA/PMDA approvals); capital commitments ₹14.1 cr at Mar-2026 [IP Q1FY27 p.24; AR FY26 note 26].
- Acquisitions so far ₹215 cr (₹80 cr Actis + ₹135 cr Raghava), funded by InvAscent money inside RPGAP — "does not entail any incremental debt" [ICRA Sep-26]. Remaining up to ~₹460 cr of the ₹700 cr to be spent over 12–18 months, shared 60:40 by RPG and InvAscent — so RPG itself may need to put in money for further deals [Sep-26 call].
- Raghava needs only small investments to raise utilisation; Actis runs near full capacity and will use Raghava's spare capacity [Sep-26 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and investments about ₹275 cr at Mar-2026 (₹266 cr a year earlier); borrowings ₹20 cr (ICRA-rated term loan ₹30 cr) [Q4 call; Screener; ICRA Sep-26]. After the deals consolidated cash "₹200 crores plus" [Sep-26 call].
- Operating cash flow fell: ₹94 cr (FY24) → ₹78 cr (FY25) → ₹56 cr (FY26); CFO/operating profit 63% in FY26. Working capital days rose from 45 to 64; inventory 179 days, debtors 60 days [Screener]. Management blames strategic buying, the larger API share (longer credit) and GST inverted duty [Q4 call].
- Dividend ₹24 per share for FY26 (300% of face value); payout 34% [AR FY26 note 36; Screener].
- Rating [ICRA]A+ (Stable)/A1, reaffirmed; debt/OPBDIT 0.1x, interest cover 88x (FY26) [ICRA Sep-26].
- Minority interest: from Q2 FY27 40% of RPGAP's profit belongs to InvAscent [Sep-26 call].

## Moat evidence
- Share gains: rank 62 → 50 in 15 months; volume growth 9.8% vs market 1.1% in Q4 FY26 [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Brand stickiness: legacy "textbook" brands with decades of prescriber habit; immunosuppressant leadership (Azathioprine is also the flagship export) [IP Q1FY27 p.14].
- Pricing power is limited: price growth 2.6% vs market 5.4%, 30% of portfolio under price control [Q4 call].
- API niche: "high-value, low-volume" molecules, "fairly insulated from price commoditization and Chinese competition"; historic API margin about 30% [IP Sep-26; Q4 call]. Actis's 32% revenue drop shows the acquired assets are less protected.
- Group backing: RPG Group ($5.2 bn revenue); brand licence fee paid to RPG Enterprises (₹1.31 cr in FY26), benchmarked against other groups [AR FY26 note 33].

## Risks and red flags
- **Strategy shift into acquired API assets**: a previously organic, asset-light story now owns two acquired plants with integration risk; Actis revenue fell 32% before purchase; margins guided to dip at first [Sep-26 call].
- **Minority leakage and dilution**: 40% of API profits go to the PE partner; an analyst noted a rights issue in RPGAP at a lower valuation (~₹210 cr) shortly before InvAscent's ~₹600 cr valuation — management said the two were unconnected [Sep-26 call, AI copy — unverified].
- **Single-site concentration** (before acquisitions): the Jan-2025 fire idled the API block for most of FY26 [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 35].
- **Cash conversion** weakening (CFO/OP 63%, working capital days up) [Screener].
- **Auditor**: unmodified opinion; key audit matter was revenue recognition; audit trail not enabled for certain direct database changes in some software [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Contingent liabilities are small (bank guarantees ₹4.8 cr; small tax claims) [AR FY26 note 25].
- **Related parties**: small — services from CEAT (₹1.49 cr) and RPG Enterprises (₹1.31 cr) [AR FY26 note 33]. No promoter pledge shown.
- **Exchange query**: exchange sought clarification on volume movement on 24 Sep 2026; company said no undisclosed event [Screener announcements].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 FY26 (April) was about domestic outperformance, API recovery after the fire, Walmart Canada OTC and avoiding US FDA costs. The September call was entirely about the new API platform: PE partner, two acquisitions, 4x capacity, ₹1,000 cr API ambition and eventual US FDA approvals — a clear change in capital allocation.
- Topics dropped: RPGServ digital platform, Naprosyn line extensions, Walmart launch, CDMO projects.

## My read
The core formulation business is improving steadily — above-market volume growth, rising productivity and a rank now inside the top 50 — and its margins after stripping one-offs are about 21–22% on Screener's basis. The new API platform adds growth but also integration risk, lower-quality acquired revenue and a 40% minority. At about 43 times trailing profit the price already assumes mid-to-high teens growth; it needs domestic growth to stay near 15% and the API platform to hit its targets without margin loss. What would change the view: clean integration with API margins back in the mid-20s by FY28 (positive), or further cash-hungry deals funded by RPG and weaker cash conversion (negative).
