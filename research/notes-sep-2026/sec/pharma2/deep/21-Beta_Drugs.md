# Beta Drugs — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code BETA (consolidated). Price ₹2,099, market cap ₹2,327 cr, 52-week range ₹990–2,808, P/E 51, book value ₹241, ROCE 19%, ROE 19%, no dividend [Screener]. Moved from NSE SME to the main board; began quarterly reporting in FY26 [Q4 call]. Promoters (Batra family) 59.1% (Jun-26) vs 64.6% (Mar-26) — diluted by CCD conversion and the Nivian share swap, not by selling; no pledge disclosed [Screener; AR FY26 directors' report].

## Business and segments
- Oncology (cancer) medicines maker from Baddi (Himachal) and Dera Bassi/Nandpur (Punjab): Beta Drugs formulation plant (ANVISA, INVIMA, COFEPRIS approvals), Adley Formulations (domestic) and Adley Lab (API). New intermediates (KSM) plant bought for about ₹9 cr, total cost about ₹27 cr, starting FY27 [Q4 call; CRISIL Jan-26; IP Q1FY27].
- FY26 segments (company): branded oncology ₹123 cr (+20%), CDMO/contract manufacturing ₹149 cr (+1%), exports ₹71 cr (−11%), API ₹25 cr (+23%), dermatology ₹16.6 cr (+35%) [Q4 call].
- FY26 segment EBITDA margins: branded 35–36%, CDMO 17–18%, exports 20–21%, API 21%; derma gross margin 57–58% [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 (₹ cr): branded ₹48.1 incl. Nivian ₹15.1 (branded oncology ₹33.0, +47%); CMO ₹47.4 (+41%); exports ₹17.2 (vs ₹32.3 in Q1 FY26); API ₹8.3; derma ₹4.8 [IP Q1FY27].
- Nivian Lifesciences (IVF/fertility brands): 66.09% bought for ₹69.4 cr (EV ₹105 cr) — ₹20.8 cr cash plus 2,83,668 new shares at ₹1,712.49; closed April 2026. FY26 revenue ₹45–46 cr, EBITDA 18–19%, PAT 9–10% [CRISIL Jan-26; AR FY26 directors' report; Q4 call].
- Reach: products in 85%+ of India's corporate and government cancer hospitals; ~65–70 oncology reps, 30–40 IVF, 50+ derma; exports to 25–45 countries (company numbers vary); top 5 export countries about 30% of exports [IP Q1FY27; 17 Sep CXO meet; Q4 call].
- Capacity use (IP): Beta formulation plant 67%, Adley Labs API 79%, Adley Formulations 70%; oral lines 40–45% used, liquid injectables 60–65%, lyophilised ~85% (one lyophiliser added, one ordered) [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; 17 Sep CXO meet].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Jun-25 100 → Sep-25 103 → Dec-25 87 → Mar-26 94 → Jun-26 126. Operating margin 20% → 21% → 19% → 19% → 22%. Net profit 12 → 12 → 9 → 9 → 16 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹125.5 cr (+25%), EBITDA ₹27.4 cr (21.8% incl. other income), PAT ₹16.5 cr (+41%); gross margin 56.6% vs 50.4%. Organic growth excluding Nivian only about 10% (₹110 cr vs ₹100 cr) because exports halved [IP Q1FY27; 17 Sep CXO meet].
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹296 cr → FY25 ₹362 cr → FY26 ₹385 cr (+6%); operating margin 20% → 21% → 20%; net profit ₹36 cr → ₹42 cr → ₹41 cr. Company's own FY26 revenue is ₹396 cr (includes other operating items) [Screener; Q4 call].
- FY26 profit was dragged by ₹9.36 cr of interest on ₹117 cr of compulsorily convertible debentures (CCDs, 0.65% a month); management says PAT margin would have been about 12.5% without it [Q4 call; CRISIL Jan-26].
- Other income ₹11 cr in FY26, of which ₹8.5–9 cr is interest on fixed deposits; EBITDA margin excluding all other income was 19.7% [Q4 call].
- ROCE fell from 36% (FY22) to 19% (FY26) as cash from the CCD issue sat idle and growth slowed [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Before FY26: management had guided FY26 revenue of ₹400–420 cr (cut mid-year) and FY27 ₹530 cr with 24% EBITDA; exports ₹200–250 cr by FY28 [Q4 call, as quoted by analysts].
- Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026): FY26 delivered ₹385 cr — **missed** the cut guide, blamed on export tenders slipping four months (₹20–25 cr) and stopping Platin supplies after price caps. FY27: 20–25% growth (≈₹530 cr incl. Nivian), EBITDA 23–24%, exports +50% to ₹105–110 cr, H1 exports +30%, Q1 exports ₹20–22 cr, EU-GMP audit "first week of September", branded oncology +20–25% a year, CDMO +5–6%, derma +30–40%, Nivian +30%; Vision 2030 ₹850–900 cr [Q4 call].
- Q1 delivery (IP, 27 Jul 2026): revenue +25% on target, margin 21.8% below the 23–24% aim; exports ₹17.2 cr missed the ₹20–22 cr guide and are −47% YoY, so H1 +30% now needs Q2 exports of about ₹39 cr [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- 17 Sep 2026 CXO meet: FY27 guidance kept at 20–25% (~₹530 cr), organic ~20%; "on track" for ₹100 cr exports; CDMO guide raised to 7–10%; EU audit now "this month or in the next 2–3 months" (slipped); Vision FY30 ₹900–1,000 cr, 22–25% CAGR [17 Sep CXO meet].
- Verdict: headline guidance **kept**; export and EU-GMP timelines **slipped again**; FY26 guidance was **missed**.

## Capex and capacity
- Investing outflow ₹36 cr (FY25) and ₹51 cr (FY26); FY26 additions include ₹25.9 cr of land (for an R&D/corporate office plan now dropped — land to be sold), ₹22 cr plant and machinery, ₹3.1 cr intangibles [Screener; AR FY26 note 28.15; Q4 call].
- Next two years: capex under ₹25 cr in total (lyophilisers, intermediates plant) [Q4 call].
- Asset base can support ₹700–800 cr (Q4 call) or ₹800–900 cr (CFO, 17 Sep) of revenue without big spending, versus ₹385 cr today [Q4 call; 17 Sep CXO meet].
- Intermediates plant: expected to add 0.5–1.5 points of EBITDA margin once running; main aim is DMF control and less China dependence [17 Sep CXO meet; Q4 call].
- Biosimilars being evaluated, not in the Vision 2030 plan or capex [17 Sep CXO meet].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Screener borrowings ₹148 cr (Mar-26) are mostly the ₹117 cr CCDs; 42,337 converted in FY26 and 6,65,314 on 25 May 2026, so debt should now be small. CFO expects FY27 bank borrowing ~₹7–10 cr working capital plus ~₹20 cr term loans [Screener; AR FY26; Q4 call].
- Cash about ₹122.5 cr (Sep 2025); roughly ₹100 cr in May 2026 before the ₹20.8 cr Nivian cash payment [CRISIL Jan-26; Q4 call].
- Working capital is heavy and worsening: receivable days 113, inventory days 159, cash conversion cycle 100 days (FY26) vs 53 in FY22 [Screener].
- Cash conversion weak: operating cash flow ₹37 cr = 49% of operating profit; free cash flow −₹16 cr (FY26) and −₹1 cr (FY25) [Screener].
- No dividend [Screener].
- Rating: CRISIL BBB+/Positive (Jan 2026) on ₹8 cr of bank lines. On 25 Sep 2026 CRISIL said it is "awaiting adequate information" from the company to carry out its review and flagged information-availability risk. CRISIL also notes Brickwork classed Beta as non-cooperative in June 2020 [CRISIL RU 25-Sep-26; CRISIL Jan-26].

## Moat evidence
- Niche oncology branding: branded oncology grew 25% (FY25) and 20% (FY26); Megestrol NDDS brand reached ~₹12 cr and expanded its market; 24 molecules readied for first-to-launch after patent expiry; 180+ oncology products [17 Sep CXO meet; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Switching costs: oncologists rarely switch a trusted cytotoxic brand ("Oncology does not offer second chances"); near-zero field-force attrition [17 Sep CXO meet].
- Backward integration: ~80% of APIs in-house, intermediates next; PIC/S audit cleared at API plant [17 Sep CXO meet; Q4 call].
- CDMO: supplies most Indian pharma firms; top 10 clients about 30% of CDMO; no long-term contracts [17 Sep CXO meet].
- Pricing power is limited where price control bites: Platin (carboplatin/cisplatin) supply was stopped for half of FY26 until NPPA raised ceiling prices; resumed ~Jul 2026 [Q4 call; 17 Sep CXO meet].

## Risks and red flags
- **Rating agency information gap**: CRISIL (25 Sep 2026) cannot complete its review for want of information; earlier non-cooperation with Brickwork (2020). A governance/disclosure warning sign [CRISIL RU 25-Sep-26; CRISIL Jan-26].
- Export concentration and tender lumpiness: top 5 countries ~30% of exports; a single slipped tender cost ₹20–25 cr in FY26; Q1 FY27 exports again weak [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Repeated slippage: FY26 guidance missed; EU-GMP audit moved from "April" to "first week of September" to "next 2–3 months" [Q4 call; 17 Sep CXO meet].
- Diversification into derma and IVF (acquired at ~2.3x sales for 66%) while the core export engine is still unproven; minority interest in Nivian 33.9% [CRISIL Jan-26; Q4 call].
- Capital allocation: ₹25.9 cr land bought then shelved [AR FY26 note 28.15; Q4 call].
- Related parties: building rent paid to promoter company B.T. Associates; promoters Rahul Batra ~₹1.51 cr salary (Varun Batra similar); purchases from subsidiary Adley Formulations ~₹26 cr [AR FY26 note 28.28 — table layout partly unreadable].
- Working capital stretch and negative FCF two years running [Screener].
- Price control (NLEM/NPPA) on key cytotoxics [Q4 call].
- Contingent liabilities small: bank guarantees ₹2.3 cr (consolidated); auditor opinion unmodified, no KAM or emphasis of matter found [AR FY26 notes 28.1/28.18; consolidated auditor's report].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 call was apologetic ("the growth expected last year was not at all encouraging"); Q1 printed +25% (Nivian-aided) and the Sep meet was confident [Q4 call; 17 Sep CXO meet].
- CDMO guide moved up from 5–6% to 7–10% after a +41% quarter [Q4 call; 17 Sep CXO meet].
- Export target softened from "₹105–110 cr" to "on track for ₹100 cr" [Q4 call; 17 Sep CXO meet].
- EU audit timing slipped again; Vision 2030 raised from ₹850–900 cr to ₹900–1,000 cr [Q4 call; 17 Sep CXO meet].
- Platins restarted after NPPA price revision [17 Sep CXO meet].

## Best management quotes
- "we accept that the growth expected last year was not at all encouraging for the entire company" [Q4 call].
- "We are on track to have a growth of more than 50% in exports in FY27" [Q4 call].
- "Oncology does not offer second chances" (on doctor switching) [17 Sep CXO meet, stored copy].

## My read
The core — branded oncology growing ~20% at 35% margins and a cheap, integrated manufacturing base — is good and Q1 margins improved. But the story has become a promise-and-slip cycle: FY26 guidance missed, exports halved in Q1, the EU audit keeps moving, and CRISIL now says it lacks information. At about 51x earnings the price needs the full 20–25% growth for several years with margins rising to 23–24%. I would get more comfortable if H1 exports hit ~₹55 cr, the EU-GMP audit happens and CRISIL completes its review; another export miss or a rating move to "issuer not cooperating" would make me negative.
