# Sai Parenterals — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code SAIPARENT (consolidated). Price ₹504, market cap ₹2,226 cr, 52-week range ₹400–706, P/E 107, ROCE 5%, ROE 5%, no dividend [Screener]. Listed on BSE/NSE in early 2026 through an IPO with ₹285 cr primary raise; first earnings call as a listed company was 27 May 2026 [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]. Promoters (Karusala family) 51.2%; FIIs cut from 5.7% to 4.0% and DIIs from 12.9% to 8.5% in the June quarter [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Hyderabad-based formulations maker (injectables, oral dosage, cephalosporins) with 5 plants in India (Units 1–4 at Jeedimetla etc., Revat Laboratories) and 1 in Australia; installed capacity 1,160 m units [IP Q1FY27].
- Two businesses by revenue (FY26 net revenue): CDMO products & services 63% (contract development and manufacturing under long-term agreements, 356% CAGR FY23–26 from a tiny base) and branded generic formulations 37% (15.6% CAGR) [IP Q1FY27].
- Exports 63% of FY26 revenue (3% in FY23); Australia 76% of exports, Philippines the other big market (USD 11 m, 4-year anti-TB contract) [IP Q1FY27].
- Noumed Pharmaceuticals (Australia/NZ): 74.64% bought for AUD 22 m, consolidated from 12 Nov 2025. Holds TGA registrations (451+ dossiers) and supplies private-label OTC medicines to pharmacy chains — Wesfarmers Health (Priceline) and EBOS (TerryWhite Chemmart, Pharmacy Choice) — plus iNova brands; 5 exclusive long-term contracts covering 526 SKUs; products currently made by Indian contract manufacturers [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Contracts: EBOS OTC deal renewed 1 Jul 2026 at AUD 202 m over 7.5 years (~AUD 27 m a year; ~₹1,300 cr) per management; separately a 3-year AUD 30 m (~₹204 cr) renewal with another leading chain announced 25 Aug 2026 [Q1 call; Press release 25 Aug 2026].
- Pipeline: 67 dossiers under development; 88 dossier approvals in FY26 [Q4 call].
- Utilisation not disclosed by plant.

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Jun-25 33.4 → Dec-25 96.2 → Mar-26 197.9 → Jun-26 178.7 (Sep-25 not shown on Screener). Operating margin 14.2% → −3.7% → 13.2% → 13.2%. Net profit 1.4 → −6.7 → 13.2 → 7.9 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 (company): total revenue incl. other income ₹182 cr (Q4: ₹201 cr); gross margin 41.8% (Q4 38.1%); EBITDA ₹27 cr (14.9%); PAT ₹8 cr (4.3%). Year-on-year comparisons are meaningless because Noumed was not in Q1 FY26 [Q1 call].
- Standalone Q1 FY27: revenue ₹56 cr (+175%), EBITDA ~₹17 cr (29%), PAT ₹9 cr [Q1 call].
- Annual (Screener): sales FY23 ₹97 cr → FY24 ₹154 cr → FY25 ₹163 cr → FY26 ₹381 cr; operating margin 19% → 21% → 24% → 10%; net profit ₹4 cr → ₹8 cr → ₹14 cr → ₹14 cr. FY26 profit included a tax credit (deferred tax asset plus move to the 22% regime) [Screener; Q4 call].
- FY26 (company): consolidated revenue ₹381 cr (+133%), EBITDA ₹47 cr (+18%); standalone revenue ₹162 cr (+30%), EBITDA ₹33 cr (21%), PAT ₹17 cr [Q4 call].
- Margin reality: the acquired Noumed business earns far less than Sai standalone — consolidated EBITDA ~14–15% vs standalone 21–29% [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (27 May 2026): FY27 revenue ₹750 cr with ~17% EBITDA; H1:H2 roughly 40:60–45:55; ₹440 cr own-funded capex (India upgrades ₹111 cr, R&D centre ₹15 cr, Adelaide AUD 53 m / ₹311 cr) all to complete in FY27 and contribute from FY28; FY27 peak debt year; debt/equity ≤0.6x [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (12 Aug 2026): ₹750 cr and ~17% kept ("sticking my neck only to Rs. 750 crores"); no FY28 guidance until later; Q1 said to be ~24% of the year, ahead of a 45:55 split [Q1 call].
- Delivery: Q1 revenue ₹182 cr on track; EBITDA 14.9% well short of the 17% full-year aim (air freight and delayed Indian supply into Australia). To hit ₹127 cr EBITDA, the next three quarters need ~18% margins on ~₹190 cr a quarter [Q1 call; arithmetic].
- Plan changed: the India EU-GMP upgrade (₹83.83 cr of IPO money) was dropped after Telangana's HILTP policy barred upgrades inside the Outer Ring Road; money redirected to 60% of Saicriti Pharma (a greenfield critical-care injectable plant under construction, total cost ~₹215–217 cr, 154.66 m units, target April 2027) and ₹15 cr for 60% of Prathyak Laboratories (R&D, 65–67 staff, 150 SKUs in development). Needs shareholder approval — passed at the 10 Sep 2026 AGM ("IPO proceeds variation") [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; Screener announcement list 10 Sep 2026].
- Verdict: headline guidance **kept**; margin **behind**; capex plan **changed** within six months of the IPO.

## Capex and capacity
- Group capex programme ₹571 cr, of which own-funded ₹440 cr (IPO proceeds plus Noumed's AUD 20 m Australian government grant) [IP Q1FY27].
- Adelaide (Australia) plant: AUD 53 m (~₹311 cr), AUD ~40 m spent by May 2026; physical completion Jan 2027, TGA inspection by 31 Mar 2027, Phase 1 production April 2027. Meant to bring Noumed's outsourced production in-house and cut its 9–10 months of inventory to 5–6 months [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Saicriti (60%): company puts in ₹83.83 cr; partner (Critigen/Questus) ~₹56 cr; rest project debt at the subsidiary. Saicriti's own ~₹52 cr domestic critical-care sales to be made there [Q1 call].
- US subsidiary (via Singapore) set up to "evaluate" US entry — preliminary [Q1 call].
- FY26 investing outflow ₹274 cr; CWIP ₹212 cr at Mar-26; fixed assets ₹364 cr [Screener].
- No revenue from the new assets expected in FY27; payback rests on FY28–FY29 [Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt ₹319 cr (Mar-26: ₹90 cr long-term, ₹229 cr short-term) → ₹310 cr (Jun-26); cash ₹184 cr at Jun-26. Screener shows borrowings ₹469 cr (likely includes lease liabilities) [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener].
- Equity jumped from ₹93 cr to ₹491 cr in FY26 (IPO and acquisition-related issuance) [Screener].
- Receivable days 173, inventory days 259, cash conversion cycle 208 days (FY26); Noumed must hold 9–10 months of stock because of 60–90 day shipping from India [Screener; Q1 call].
- Operating cash flow ₹93 cr FY26, but free cash flow −₹184 cr; FY23–FY24 operating cash flow was negative [Screener].
- Loans are secured by a pledge of the company's shares in Noumed Pharmaceuticals and promoters' personal guarantees [AR FY26 borrowings note].
- Goodwill ₹124.6 cr and intangibles ₹47.9 cr on consolidation — a Key Audit Matter (impairment testing) [AR FY26 consolidated balance sheet and auditor's report].
- Management says incremental revenue needs working capital of ~25–30% of the added sales [Q4 call].
- No credit rating rationale found on Screener's document list.

## Moat evidence
- IP and contracts in Australia: Noumed owns the TGA registrations while pharmacy chains own the brands; contracts typically 5+ years with volume forecasts; the EBOS 7.5-year renewal and AUD 30 m renewal show customer stickiness [Q1 call; Press release 25 Aug 2026].
- Over 50% of consolidated revenue is contracted (Noumed plus CDMO) [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Regulatory approvals: GMP, WHO-GMP, TGA and PIC/S at Indian sites; no EU-GMP or US FDA injectable site yet [IP Q1FY27].
- Pricing: contracts pass through cost increases with a 90–120 day lag [Q1 call].
- Weak points: small branded domestic business; institutional (government) share fell from 61% to 19% of sales as CDMO grew [IP Q1FY27].

## Risks and red flags
- **Valuation vs returns**: P/E above 100 with ROCE 5% — the price assumes FY28 transformation that depends on three unbuilt or unproven assets (Adelaide, Saicriti, Prathyak) [Screener].
- **Plan churn after IPO**: IPO objects varied within months; money now goes into a 60%-owned project company rather than the company's own plant, with project debt outside the listed entity's direct control [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Management says Saicriti and its owners are not related parties [Q1 call].
- Related-party dealings: MD Anil Kumar Karusala lent the company ₹24.7 cr and was repaid ₹27.8 cr in FY26; rent and security deposits to promoter family members; loan flows with Revat Laboratories (₹33.7 cr taken/₹35.1 cr repaid) [AR FY26 consolidated note 39 — amounts in ₹ million, layout partly garbled].
- Auditor's KAM text states Noumed was acquired "on April 1, 2025", while management and the call say consolidation from 12 Nov 2025 — a disclosure inconsistency worth checking [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report; Q1 call].
- Concentration: Australia ~76% of exports; two pharmacy groups dominate Noumed; supply chain from India exposed to shipping disruptions (Q1 air-freight hit) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Telangana relocation rule: Jeedimetla units (red/orange category) may have to move outside the ORR within ~3 years [Q1 call].
- Pledge of Noumed shares to lenders [AR FY26].
- Institutional selling in the June quarter [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- The India EU-GMP upgrade was replaced by the Saicriti JV and Prathyak R&D acquisition; capacity target up 47% (154.66 m vs 105 m units), timing one month later (April 2027) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- EBOS 7.5-year AUD 202 m renewal announced; US subsidiary added [Q1 call].
- Debt edged down ₹319 cr → ₹310 cr after repaying ₹50 cr from IPO money [Q1 call].
- Margin commentary shifted to air-freight costs and a lag in price pass-through [Q1 call].

## My read
Sai has bought itself a bigger, contracted Australian business and is spending heavily to make it vertically integrated, but today it earns a 5% return on capital and trades above 100x earnings. The FY27 guide (₹750 cr, 17% EBITDA) looks reachable on revenue but not yet on margin, and the post-IPO switch to a 60%-owned greenfield plant adds execution and governance questions. The price needs FY28 to show Adelaide and Saicriti running and consolidated margins near 20%. A clean TGA licence by March 2027 and 17%+ margins in H2 would support the story; slippage on either, or goodwill impairment at Noumed, would undermine it.
