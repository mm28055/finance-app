# Corona Remedies — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code CORONA (consolidated). Price ₹2,118, market cap ₹12,952 cr, 52-week range ₹1,337–2,292. Listed 15 Dec 2025 through a pure offer-for-sale IPO — the company received no new money [Screener; AR FY26 note 53].

## Business and segments
- Corona sells branded prescription medicines in India through doctors and pharmacies: women's health (27.5% of FY26 domestic sales), cardio-diabetes (25.4%), pain (11.4%), urology and others. About 96–97% of revenue is from India; chronic and sub-chronic therapies are 72% (FY26) and 73.4% (Q1 FY27) [AR FY26 MD&A p.50; IP Q1FY27 p.4].
- Rank in the Indian pharma market (IPM) 26th (MAT Jun-26), up from 32nd three years earlier; 5th in women's health and pain, 9th in urology, 20th in cardio-diabetes [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.10].
- Brands: 79 brands, 32–33 "engine brands" giving about 72% of domestic sales; two brands above ₹100 cr and 40+ above ₹10 cr (MAT Jun-26). Top 5 brands about 35% of revenue, top 10 about 50% [IP Q1FY27 p.10–11; AR FY26 p.49; Q1 call].
- Field force about 3,111 medical reps after adding about 450 in Q4 FY26; productivity (sales per rep per month) ₹4.11 lakh vs ₹3.62 lakh a year earlier [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Manufacturing: Bhayla (Gujarat, EU-GMP, ~1.25 bn tablets/capsules incl. 400 m added Dec 2025), Solan (Himachal, ~403 m units), and a new EU-GMP hormone plant at Bhayla (194 m tablets/capsules, 1.5 m ointments/gels) commercialised 30 Jun 2026. About 60–64% of sales are made in-house, the rest outsourced [IP Q1FY27 p.12; AR FY26 p.48; Q1 call].
- Associate: 31.15% of La Chandra Pharmalab, a hormone API maker that supplies 60–65% of Corona's hormone API under a right of first refusal [AR FY26; Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr), Screener: Jun-25 347 → Sep-25 361 → Dec-25 342 → Mar-26 353 → Jun-26 422. Operating margin 20% → 22% → 24% → 18% → 22%. Net profit 46 → 52 → 41 → 45 → 60 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: sales +21.9% (India +22.7%; organic ex-Wokadine +21.4%; exports flat); EBITDA ₹93.1 cr (+33.5%); PAT ₹60.1 cr (+30.1%); gross margin 81.6% [IP Q1FY27 p.6; Q1 call].
- Growth mix (MAT Jun-26, PharmaTrac): volume +6.3% vs IPM +1.3%; price +8.7% vs +5.6%; new launches +3.4% vs +2.9% [Q1 call].
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹1,014 cr → FY25 ₹1,196 cr → FY26 ₹1,403 cr; operating margin 15% → 20% → 21%; net profit ₹90 cr → ₹149 cr → ₹185 cr. TTM sales ₹1,479 cr, profit ₹199 cr. ROCE 33%, ROE 30% [Screener].
- FY26 profit carried a one-time ₹19.1 cr (₹14.3 cr after tax) charge for the new labour codes; adjusted PAT ₹199 cr, +33% [AR FY26 p.53; Q4 call].
- Q4 FY26 margin dipped to 17.6% because of about ₹14 cr of new-division and rep costs plus ₹1.6 cr of R&D [Q4 call].
- Advertising and sales promotion about 19% of sales (10% doctor consulting agreements, 5% promotion, 4% conferences); R&D spend ₹25.1 cr in FY26 vs ₹11.8 cr [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 51].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (12 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth 15%+ organic, 25% growth on acquired brands, PAT growth 20%+; gross margin to stay around 80%; no new reps in FY27; hormone plant to start in Q1 or Q2 FY27 [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (3 Aug 2026): unchanged — 15% organic plus 1.5–2% from acquisitions, about 17% revenue growth, and 20% PAT growth; management warned against annualising Q1 and flagged a possible ~100 bp gross-margin hit from costlier raw materials bought after the Middle East disruption [Q1 call].
- Delivery: Q1 revenue +21.9% and PAT +30.1% beat the guided 17% and 20%. Hormone plant delivered on time (30 Jun). Wokadine sales fell year on year in its first quarter during supply-chain transfer; the 25% growth promise for it has not yet shown [Q1 call].
- Verdict: guidance **kept** (not raised despite a beat). Record: FY26 revenue +17% and adjusted PAT +33% beat the 15%/20% guide [Q4 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Hormone plant capex about ₹130 cr; asset turn below 1x in FY27, rising to 2–3x over three years; depreciation about ₹6.5 cr a year (20-year life). Exports from it (EU, UK, rest of world) only from FY28–FY29 after dossiers (ready Nov–Dec 2026) and 12–18 months of registration [Q1 call].
- Acquisitions: Wokadine (povidone-iodine, from Dr Reddy's) for ₹97 cr plus GST on 31 Mar 2026, about ₹20 cr sales, amortised over 10 years; seven Bayer Zydus brands (IVF, cardio) for about ₹7 cr, which brought about ₹7 cr of sales in Q1 [Q1 call].
- Fixed assets rose from ₹370 cr to ₹554 cr in FY26; capital work-in-progress ₹143 cr at March 2026 [Screener]. Screener flags that the company "might be capitalizing the interest cost".
- CARE expects "no major debt-funded capex in pipeline" [CARE Feb-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹165 cr at March 2026 (₹86 cr a year earlier). Management says ₹142.9 cr is an overdraft against fixed deposits used to pay for Wokadine on the last day of the year, plus a ₹16 cr Myoril loan to be repaid in FY27; the official transcript records net cash as "INR 15 crores" [Q4 call; Screener].
- Cash from operations FY26 ₹229 cr (78% of EBITDA); free cash flow only ₹56 cr after capex and brand buys [Screener; Q4 call].
- Working capital is light: debtor days 38, inventory 175 days, payable 206 days, cash conversion cycle 7 days [Screener].
- Dividend ₹10 a share for FY26 (100% of face value), payout 33% [Q4 call; Screener].
- Rating: CARE A+ (Stable)/A1, reaffirmed 9 Feb 2026 on ₹95 cr of limits; gearing 0.09x at Sep 2025; interest cover 37.8x (H1 FY26) [CARE Feb-26].

## Moat evidence
- Price power: 92% of the portfolio is outside the national essential-medicines price list, so it can raise prices up to 10% a year; price-led growth 8.7% vs market 5.6% [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Share gains: fastest-growing of India's top 30 drug companies; rank up from 32nd to 26th in three years; revenue growth about twice the market for four straight quarters [Q1 call; AR FY26 p.51].
- Brand-building record: acquired brands grow well after purchase (Thyrocab 16.8% CAGR since 2018; GSK brands 36.5% CAGR since 2017) [AR FY26 p.50].
- Hormone niche: captive hormone API (La Chandra makes about 65% of India's progesterone) plus an EU-GMP hormone plant [Q1 call].
- Limits: productivity per rep (₹4.1 lakh a month) is below large peers, so employee and promotion costs are high; 96% dependence on India [Q1 call; CARE Feb-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Valuation risk**: the stock trades at about 61 times trailing profit (Screener) — any slowdown from 20% growth would hurt.
- **Investor exit**: Sepia Investments, Sage Investment Trust and Anchor Partners sold 2.63 m shares (about 4% of equity) on 10 Sep 2026; the exchange sought a clarification on volume the same day [BSE filings 10 and 19 Sep 2026, via Screener].
- **Input costs**: management flagged a ~100 bp gross-margin hit from API inflation and freight after the Middle East disruption [Q1 call].
- **Marketing spend**: 10% of sales goes on consulting agreements with key opinion leaders; management says it follows the UCPMP marketing code [Q4 call].
- **Auditor**: Deloitte Haskins & Sells (FRN 117366W/W-100018); unmodified opinion, no key audit matters on the standalone accounts; audit trail operated with no tampering noted [AR FY26 p.174–176].
- **Related parties** are small: KMP pay about ₹10.4 cr, rent to promoter relatives about ₹2.4 cr, purchases from La Chandra a few crore [AR FY26 note 40]. Contingent liabilities only ₹2.0 cr (tax) [AR FY26 note 50].
- **Execution**: Wokadine sales fell in Q1; semaglutide (two brands, made by partner MSM) is a crowded market where Corona aims only for a top-10 place [Q1 call; Q4 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was defensive on margin (17.6%) and busy with new divisions; Q1 showed the payoff (22.0% margin, +21.9% sales).
- New caution on raw-material inflation (~100 bp gross margin) in Q1.
- Guidance language became more precise (15% organic + 1.5–2% inorganic = ~17%) but was not raised.
- Hormone plant moved from "Q1 or Q2 FY27" to commercialised; exports from it pushed to FY28–29.

## Best management quotes
- "We continue to target 15% organic revenue growth and about 1.5% to 2% inorganic… and 20% PAT growth." — Nirav Mehta, MD [Q1 call]
- "100 basis point here and there we may get the hit." — on raw materials [Q1 call]
- "We don't have any single cash credit facility from any banks." — Bhavin Bhagat, CFO [Q4 call]

## My read
Corona is a clean, fast-growing Indian branded-generic company: 17–22% revenue growth, margins moving from 15% to 22% in three years, negligible debt, low working capital and no audit issues. The Q1 beat came from volume, not just price, which is the healthier kind of growth. The price already assumes this continues for years — at about 61 times trailing earnings the stock needs roughly 20% profit growth sustained; management's own guide is exactly that. Watch margins as costlier raw materials flow through in Q2–Q3, Wokadine's recovery, and further stake sales by the pre-IPO investors.
