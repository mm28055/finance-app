# Fredun Pharmaceuticals — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code FREDUN (consolidated history only from FY25; standalone from FY22 — figures below use consolidated where available, standalone for longer history). Price ₹1,357, market cap ₹2,244 cr, 52-week range ₹427–1,600 (bonus-adjusted), P/E 51, book value ₹170, ROCE 21%, ROE 16%, dividend yield 0.02% [Screener]. Promoters (Medhora family) 44.6% (Jul-26) vs 48.9% (Jun-25) — diluted by preferential equity/warrants, not sales; bank loans carry promoter personal guarantees [Screener; AR FY26 note 17.4].

## Business and segments
- Mumbai-based company with a cluster of plants at Palghar (Maharashtra) plus about 43 outsourced/loan-licence locations; about 80% of products made in-house. 2,100 SKUs across 23–27 therapy classes; ~1,100 registrations; exports to 46–52 countries (Africa, CIS, South Asia, LatAm) [Q4 call; Q1 call; Infomerics Mar-26; AR FY26 MD&A].
- One reportable segment (pharmaceuticals) in the accounts; the split below is management's on the calls [AR FY26 directors' report].
- "Vintage" business (FY26, approximate, Q4 call): direct exports ₹110–120 cr; institutional/indirect exports ₹120–130 cr; tolling (contract manufacturing) ~₹24 cr; domestic third-party branding ₹45–50 cr; Fredun DC ₹25–30 cr; Fredun GX (domestic generics, 19 states) ₹100–110 cr. Grows 15–20% a year [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- "New age" consumer health (FY26): pet care (Freossi, functional foods, grooming, diagnostics) ₹42–43 cr; mobility aids (BraceOn, DigiOn, NebOn) ₹29–30 cr; nutraceuticals ₹26 cr; mass cosmetics ₹20–22 cr; dermaceutics ~₹12 cr. Grows 35–50% a year; gross margins 40–55% (dermaceutics 70–90%) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Subsidiaries: Fredun Retail → One Pet Stop (acquired Mar 2025) and Wagr Retail (pet e-commerce, soft launch 15 Jun 2026; acquired for nil cash by issuing equity in the combined entity). 26% stake in Goodman Vetcare announced 22 Sep 2026 [AR FY26; Infomerics Mar-26; Q1 call; Screener announcement list, 22 Sep 2026].
- New lines FY27: hormone therapies (doctor channel), anti-ageing/NAD+ ("DAULCEL"), cat food, Mobilitics (physiotherapy) [Q4 call].
- Order book: ₹320–330 cr in hand (6–7 months) at Q4 FY26 [Q4 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Jun-25 120 → Sep-25 145 → Dec-25 161 → Mar-26 213 → Jun-26 228. Operating margin 14% → 15% → 16% → 14% → 14%. Net profit 7 → 9 → 10 → 11 → 13 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 (company, standalone): total income ₹228.25 cr (+90%), EBITDA margin 14.36%, PAT ₹13.17 cr (+95%), PAT margin 5.8% [Q1 call].
- Q1 was lifted by pre-booked orders: in Q4, with input prices rising, Fredun booked extra customer orders at old prices using its stock — management says later quarters will be "around the same level" as Q1 [Q1 call].
- Annual (Screener standalone): sales FY22 ₹222 cr → FY23 ₹275 cr → FY24 ₹348 cr → FY25 ₹454 cr → FY26 ₹635 cr; operating margin 6% → 10% → 11% → 12% → 14%; net profit ₹6 cr → ₹11 cr → ₹16 cr → ₹21 cr → ₹33 cr. TTM sales ₹748 cr, profit ₹44 cr [Screener].
- FY26 (company): total income ₹639 cr (+40%), EBITDA ₹94.8 cr (14.8%), PAT ₹33.2 cr (+60%) [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Interest eats much of the margin: FY26 interest ₹39 cr vs operating profit ₹91 cr; PAT margin only ~5–6% [Screener].
- Management claims 19 years of growth, 32% CAGR, no down year [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- History: FY26 roadmap ₹570 cr → delivered ₹635 cr (beat) [Q1 call].
- Q4 FY26 call (10 Jun 2026): FY27 top-line growth 25–30% (≈₹800 cr); PAT margin to reach 10–12% "in the next few years", with a "sudden spike" in profitability after 7–8 quarters; mobility ₹100 cr run-rate in 2–2.5 years; new-age share of revenue to reach ~50% [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (13 Aug 2026): ~₹800 cr target "in line… hopefully cross"; blended 30–35% growth for 3 years; GX 25–35% a year; pet care 40–50% a year; EBIT margin 12–13% "within the next 12 quarters"; inventory to fall from 135–140 days to ~120 days in 4 quarters; capex ₹30–40 cr this year and next [Q1 call].
- Delivery: Q1 +90% YoY far above the run-rate needed, but partly pull-forward; margin flat at ~14% [Q1 call; Screener].
- Verdict: guidance **kept** (not raised) despite a strong Q1; long-term margin promises are vague on timing.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 investing outflow ₹74 cr (vs ₹6–9 cr a year earlier); fixed assets ₹53 cr → ₹118 cr [Screener].
- FY27–FY28: ₹30–45 cr a year — 12–13 more packing lines by Sep 2026, a new plant wing, functional-food and wet-food facilities; aim to be among the top 3–4 single-location plants by end-2028 [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Auditor flagged capitalisation of PPE/CWIP as a Key Audit Matter (judgement on what is capital vs revenue) [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Funding: equity. ₹153.26 cr preferential issue approved Oct 2025 (6,52,360 shares + 5,73,600 warrants at ₹1,250): ₹99.5 cr received, ₹53.8 cr due on warrant conversion (conversions under way Sep 2026) [Infomerics Mar-26; Screener announcement list, Sep 2026].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹222 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹167 cr (Mar-25); cash rose after the equity raise (net cash flow +₹60 cr in FY26). Management: debt/equity 0.8; "almost 50% of our working capital as cash on hand" [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Cash generation is the weak spot: standalone operating cash flow −₹2 cr, −₹14 cr, ₹0, −₹21 cr, +₹16 cr in FY22–FY26; free cash flow negative every year (−₹57 cr in FY26). Growth has been funded by debt and equity, not by profits [Screener].
- Inventory days 219 (FY26) vs 267 (FY25); Infomerics put own inventory at 189 days in FY25; cash conversion cycle 201 days [Screener; Infomerics Mar-26].
- Working-capital loans at 9–11.5%; fund-based limits ~82% used (12 months to Dec 2025) [AR FY26 note 17.2; Infomerics Mar-26].
- Rating: Infomerics upgraded to IVR BBB+/Stable/A2 (31 Mar 2026) on ₹156 cr of bank lines, citing growth and the equity raise; weakness: elongated working capital cycle [Infomerics Mar-26].
- Dividend token (~1–2% payout) [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Registrations are the export moat: ~1,100 registered products, 1,000+ more in the pipeline; strategy of owning or co-owning registrations [Infomerics Mar-26; Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Licensing/validation depth: management claims it would take a rival 15–20 years to replicate its approvals even with ₹400–500 cr [Q4 call].
- Distribution: chemist-channel reach in Maharashtra used to push mobility and GX products; pet-care built through breeders, groomers and vets [Q4 call].
- Pricing: passed on most of the 2026 raw-material spike; buyers absorb 1–2% [Q4 call].
- No evidence of market-share leadership; many small categories against bigger consumer and pet brands.

## Risks and red flags
- **Chronic negative cash flow** despite reported profits — five years of negative FCF; profits sit in inventory and receivables [Screener].
- **Related-party trade**: sales to promoter-group UAE entities Innovus Healthcare LLC and Fredna FZE (roughly ₹26 cr in FY26 vs ~₹65 cr in FY25 per the RPT table), rent to promoter company Fredna Enterprises (~₹1.7 cr), interest paid on director loans (~₹2.4 cr to Fredun Medhora per table; layout partly garbled) [AR FY26 related-party note].
- AGM FY26 sought approval (Sec 185) to lend to or guarantee loans of bodies corporate in which directors are interested [AR FY26 AGM notice item 10].
- MD is also CFO — no independent finance head [AR FY26 related-party note].
- Unsecured non-interest-bearing loans to subsidiaries (~₹5.6 cr to Fredun Retail in FY26) repayable on demand with no terms [AR FY26 note 12.1].
- Sprawl: pharma exports, generics, pet food, wheelchairs, cosmetics, hormones, NAD+ and an e-commerce site — execution risk and cash drain (Wagr launch slipped 3–4 months) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Dilution: promoter stake down ~4 points in a year; more warrant conversions due [Screener].
- Secretarial audit exception: unpaid dividends for FY2015-16 to FY2017-18 not yet transferred to IEPF [AR FY26 secretarial audit].
- Auditor opinion unmodified; KAMs on revenue recognition and capitalisation; audit trail operated; no disputed statutory dues [AR FY26 auditor's report; CARO].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 set 25–30% FY27 growth; Q1 talked of 30–35% blended growth for 3 years but kept ~₹800 cr for FY27 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Margin target moved from "10–12% PAT in a few years" to "12–13% EBIT within 12 quarters" — similar, still no firm date [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New disclosure that Q1 was helped by orders pre-booked at old prices [Q1 call].
- Wagr moved from "launching" to soft-launched; pet-care acquisitions hinted ("some small, some big") — Goodman Vetcare followed in September [Q1 call; Screener announcement list, 22 Sep 2026].
- Interest cost fell QoQ (₹14 cr → ₹9 cr) after the rating upgrade and equity inflow [Screener; Q1 call].

## My read
Fredun is growing fast (sales nearly tripled in four years) and margins have crept up from 6% to 14%, but it has never turned that growth into cash, and a large part of profit goes to interest. The equity raise and rating upgrade have eased the balance sheet, yet the business is spreading into many unrelated consumer lines and has meaningful dealings with promoter-group entities. At ~51x earnings the price needs 25–30% growth for years and a real step-up in margins and cash conversion. Positive operating cash flow above ₹40 cr in FY27 and falling inventory days would change my view; another year of negative FCF or rising related-party sales would make me cautious.
