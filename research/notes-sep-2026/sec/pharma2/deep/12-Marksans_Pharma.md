# Marksans Pharma — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code MARKSANS (consolidated). Price ₹337, market cap ₹15,269 cr, 52-week range ₹155–351 (4% below the high); promoter holding 43.87% (Jun 2026) [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Marksans makes cheap, high-volume oral medicines — mostly over-the-counter (OTC) painkillers, cough-cold, digestive and allergy pills and soft-gel capsules — in Goa, and sells them through its own front-end companies abroad: Time-Cap Labs (US store brands for large retail chains), Bell Sons and Relonchem (UK), Nova (Australia/NZ, 60% owned) and Access Healthcare (UAE). About 80% of FY26 revenue was OTC and 20% prescription (Rx); US revenue is ~85–95% OTC; UK ~55% Rx / 45% OTC; the new European businesses are mostly Rx [IP Q1FY27 p.20; Q1 call; Q4 call].
- FY26 revenue by region: US & North America ₹1,533 cr (+24%), UK & Europe ₹1,015 cr (FY25 ₹1,030 cr — slightly down on UK price erosion), Australia & NZ ₹303 cr (+20%), rest of world ₹99 cr. Total ₹2,951 cr [AR FY26 consolidated note 45; Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹841 cr (+35.6%): North America ₹377 cr (+15.1%), UK & Europe ₹356 cr (+74.7%, of which QliniQ ₹44 cr; UK ex-QliniQ +53.1%), ANZ ₹88 cr (+53.7%), RoW ₹20 cr (−36.8%). Organic growth excluding QliniQ was 28.5% [IP Q1FY27 p.6–8; Q1 call].
- Europe build-out in 2026: bought QliniQ B.V. (Netherlands, Rx generics; CY2025 revenue €9.35 m, profit €1.01 m) for €7.5 m, effective 1 Apr 2026; bought ABCnow GmbH (Germany, wholesale; CY2025 turnover ~€0.23 m) for ~€0.89 m, closed 20 Jul 2026; set up Marksans Pharma (Europe) in Ireland and Marksans Pharma GmbH [NSE filings 1 Jun, 16 Jun, 8 Jul, 20 Jul 2026; IP Q1FY27 p.9].
- Capacity: 26 bn units a year across Goa Unit-1 (8.4 bn), the ex-Teva Goa Unit-2 (8 bn, scalable), Farmingdale US (6 bn) and Southport UK. Overall use ~50–55% (13–14 bn units); the ex-Teva plant was "slightly below 50%" in May 2026 [IP Q1FY27 p.22; Q4 call].
- Customer concentration: two customers each above 10% of revenue, together ₹679 cr (23% of FY26 revenue) [AR FY26 consolidated note 45C].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Jun-25 620 → Sep-25 720 → Dec-25 754 → Mar-26 856 → Jun-26 841. Operating margin: 16% → 20% → 21% → 23% → 25%. Net profit: 58 → 99 → 114 → 149 → 159 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: gross margin 59.1% (57.8% a year ago, 54.4% in Q4) helped by cheap raw material held in stock and a weak rupee; EBITDA ₹213 cr (+113%), margin 25.3%; PAT ₹159 cr (+174%), tax 22.8% [IP Q1FY27 p.6–7].
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹2,177 cr → FY25 ₹2,623 cr → FY26 ₹2,951 cr; operating margin 21% → 20% → 20%; net profit ₹315 cr → ₹383 cr → ₹420 cr. TTM sales ₹3,172 cr, TTM profit ₹521 cr. Other income is material: ₹82 cr in FY26, ₹102 cr TTM (interest on ~₹1,000 cr cash). ROCE 19% [Screener].
- FY26 (company): gross margin 56.7%, EBITDA ₹601 cr (20.4%), R&D ₹89 cr (3%); 112 SKUs launched in the US, 51 products in the US pipeline; UK 18 approvals, 30 filings, 24 awaiting approval, 200+ filings planned over four years [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (27 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth "conservatively… between 15% to 20%"; EBITDA margin "the same" ~20–21% despite 20–30% raw-material inflation from the West Asia war; ₹4,000 cr revenue "within the next 2 years" and doubling revenue in 3–5 years; "2027 will see some M&A transactions" (one target in due diligence); Europe revenue "maybe second half"; Canada approvals late FY27; ANZ to US$100 m within 3 years [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 call (13 Aug 2026): revenue guide kept at 15–20% ("we will stick with that plan because of the volatility"); margin now "21–22% is achievable" by year-end; sustainable gross margin 55–56% (vs 59% in Q1) as cheap inventory runs down; QliniQ ₹150–175 cr revenue in FY27 (€14–15 m, ~40% above last year), Europe overall ~₹180 cr; Europe ~₹1,000 cr within about five years, mostly through acquisitions; US target raised from US$200 m to US$300 m, then US$400 m over ~5 years; ₹4,000 cr within 2 years reaffirmed; Q2 revenue above Q1, Q3 the strongest [Q1 call, official].
- Delivery: Q1 revenue +35.6% (28.5% organic) beat the 15–20% guide; margin 25.3% beat 20–21%; Europe arrived in Q1, ahead of the "second half" promised in May; the M&A promise was met with two small deals [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Verdict: revenue guidance **kept** (despite a big beat); margin guidance **raised** (20–21% → 21–22%); long-term targets **raised** (US$300 m US; Europe ₹1,000 cr).

## Capex and capacity
- Capex FY26 ₹131 cr (net), "primarily for the new facility and ongoing maintenance… major capex cycle now complete"; Q1 FY27 net capex ₹33 cr; capital commitments ₹53.9 cr at March 2026 [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 note 46].
- The ex-Teva Goa plant (bought 2022) is the growth platform; CARE says it produced over ₹300 cr of revenue in FY25. Some in-plant capex is planned for new dosage forms [CARE Oct-25; Q4 call].
- Next step: management says reaching the 3–5 year plan "may need additional manufacturing capabilities, probably another unit in India", possibly outside Goa; "nothing concrete" [Q1 call].
- Backward integration into API is shelved; a CRAMS (contract manufacturing) model is being explored but "not actively" [Q1 call].
- Acquisition sizes so far are small (QliniQ about 7–8× last year's profit); management warns that an Indian deal "at a higher valuation" could absorb ₹200–400 cr [NSE 1 Jun 2026; Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash ₹1,058 cr and net cash ₹1,031 cr at June 2026 (₹990 cr cash at March 2026), after paying for QliniQ. The only bank debt is a ₹29.6 cr US working-capital line at Time-Cap; Screener "borrowings" of ₹343 cr are mostly lease liabilities (~₹314 cr) [IP Q1FY27 p.8; AR FY26 notes 21, 24, 25; Screener].
- Cash flow: FY26 operating cash ₹458 cr, free cash ₹328–329 cr (Screener CFO/operating profit 99% vs 62% in FY25); Q1 FY27 operating cash ₹185 cr, free cash ₹152 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener].
- Working capital: company cycle 132 days in Q1 (159 days a year ago; 138 days for FY26). Stock was deliberately built in FY25–FY26 against tariff and war risk — inventory ₹972 cr and receivables ₹645 cr at March 2026; Screener inventory days 278, cash conversion cycle 261 days (different basis) [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A; Screener].
- Dividend ₹0.90 a share (payout ~10%); management rejects index investing or buybacks and keeps cash for acquisitions [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener].
- Ratings: India Ratings IND AA-/A1+, outlook revised to **Positive** from Stable (9 Jun 2026); CARE AA-/Stable (upgraded Oct 2025) — CARE's downgrade triggers are revenue below ₹2,000 cr or margin below 18% [NSE 9 Jun 2026; CARE Oct-25].

## Moat evidence
- Store-brand position with top US retailers: US revenue grew from ₹635 cr (FY22) to ₹1,533 cr (FY26), ~2.6×; management says it is "the preferred choice of partners" and grows by adding items per customer [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Cost position: low-cost Goa manufacturing at scale (26 bn units), with regulated approvals (USFDA, MHRA, TGA, Health Canada, PMDA) [IP Q1FY27 p.19, p.22; CARE Oct-25].
- Pricing: Rx price erosion "in single digits"; OTC contracts are typically 2-year fixed-price, which delays pass-through of cost increases (in COVID the company re-priced without invoking force majeure) [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Front-end ownership in each market (licences, 175+ UK marketing authorisations, 100+ in ANZ, 230+ in RoW) is the barrier to entry, not intellectual property [IP Q1FY27 p.14–17].
- Weakness: price-taker in commodity molecules; UK revenue fell in FY26 on price erosion; one therapy (pain) is the largest segment [AR FY26 Directors' Report; CARE Oct-25].

## Risks and red flags
- **Peak margin.** Q1's 59% gross margin used cheap inventory; management itself guides 55–56% ahead, with raw materials up 20–30% [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Customer concentration.** Two customers = 23% of revenue [AR FY26 note 45C].
- **Acquisition risk.** ~₹1,000 cr cash earmarked for European and possibly Indian deals; each small deal so far, but management signals larger ones [Q1 call].
- **Auditor coverage.** M S K A & Associates LLP (partner Nitin Tiwari) gave an unmodified opinion; key audit matter is revenue recognition. It did not audit 12 subsidiaries that carry ₹2,817 cr of revenue (~95% of the group) — those rely on other auditors. Database-level audit trail was not enabled for one accounting software [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report].
- **Related parties.** Managerial pay for Mark Saldanha ₹13.3 cr and Sandra Saldanha ₹7.7 cr in FY26 (~5% of profit together); rent of ₹1.07 cr paid to Mark Saldanha; ESOP grants to the CFO and a director [AR FY26 note 44].
- **Contingent liabilities**: none reported; no loans against pledged shares (CARO) [AR FY26 notes 40, 46; CARO].
- **Tariff/geopolitics.** Talk of US pharma tariffs; West Asia shipping disruption hit RoW (−37% in Q1). US plant (Farmingdale) partly mitigates [Q1 call; CARE Oct-25].
- **Minority in ANZ.** 40% of Nova is owned by local management; no call option to buy it [Q4 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (raw-material inflation, "hope this war comes to an end") to expansive (Europe, US$300 m, "next leap").
- Europe went from "second half" to contributing ₹44 cr in Q1 and a ₹1,000 cr five-year ambition.
- Margin guidance raised to 21–22%; revenue guide unchanged.
- New: two acquisitions closed; India Ratings outlook turned Positive; a possible new Indian plant.

## My read
The business is clearly improving: four straight quarters of rising margin, 28% organic growth in Q1, strong cash generation and ₹1,000 cr of net cash, with the US, UK and Australia all growing at once for the first time in two years. Part of Q1's 25% margin is a one-off inventory gain, so a fair base is ~21–22% EBITDA and 15–20% growth, which the stock largely prices in after doubling from its low. The next leg depends on capital allocation — whether the cash buys European front-ends at sensible prices and whether a new plant is needed before 2028. The view improves if ₹4,000 cr revenue arrives by FY28 at 21%+ margin; it worsens if a large acquisition is expensive or US store-brand pricing turns.
