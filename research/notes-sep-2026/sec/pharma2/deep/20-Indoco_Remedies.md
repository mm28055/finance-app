# Indoco Remedies — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code INDOCO (consolidated). Price ₹254, market cap ₹2,349 cr, 52-week range ₹162–317, book value ₹102, ROCE 1%, ROE −9%; P/E not meaningful (TTM profit ₹3 cr, and only because of a one-off gain) [Screener]. Promoters (Kare/Panandikar family) 58.9%, no pledge [Screener; AR FY26 corporate governance].

## Business and segments
- Mumbai-based maker of branded medicines for India plus exports of finished doses and APIs. FY26 consolidated revenue (company) ₹1,793 cr, +9.2%; domestic formulations incl. OTC ₹937 cr [IP Q1FY27 p.2].
- Q1 FY27 standalone mix: domestic formulations 50%, international formulations 35–36%, API 13%, CRO/analytics 2% [IP Q1FY27].
- India: rank 33 in IPM with 0.49% share (IQVIA MAT Jun-26), 20th by prescriptions, 3,500+ reps reaching 2.42 lakh doctors. Mostly acute (29th in acute market): anti-infectives, respiratory, GI, stomatology (dental), vitamins, urology. Top 5 brands ₹550 cr (42% of domestic); Cyclopam ₹196 cr, Febrex Plus ₹118 cr, Oxipod ~₹78 cr, Cital ₹67 cr [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Ophthalmic division (India + agreed African territories, FY25 revenue ₹47.8 cr) sold to Sunways for ₹110 cr (announced 4 May 2026; completed in May per Q1 call) [News: ICICI Direct, May 2026; Q4 call; Q1 call].
- International (FY26 ₹645 cr, +29%): Europe 46%, emerging markets 32%, Americas 20%. Q1 FY27: US ₹45.9 cr (+62%), Europe ₹65 cr (+2.5%), emerging ₹31.7 cr (−31%), SA/Aus/NZ ₹2.4 cr [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- API: FY26 ₹145.6 cr (+12%); Q1 FY27 ₹52 cr (+42%); 66% exported; 21 US DMFs, 11 CEPs [IP Q1FY27].
- Subsidiaries: Warren Remedies (OTC toothpaste Sensodent/Kidodent + new API plant, Aurangabad), FPP Holding / Florida Pharmaceutical Products (US front end), Clarity Pharma (UK). A new subsidiary, Warren Lifesciences, was set up on 18 Sep 2026 [Q4 call; Screener announcements].
- 10 manufacturing plants: Goa I–III (Goa II/III sterile ophthalmic and injectables), Baddi I–III, Waluj; APIs at Rabale, Patalganga, Warren [IP Q1FY27].
- Utilisation (single-shift basis): Goa Plant I 70–80%, Baddi I 70–80%, Baddi III 70–75% [Q1 call].
- About 6,000 employees; about 900 fewer in operations after the Master Manufacturing Plan (MMP) [Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Jun-25 440 → Sep-25 485 → Dec-25 445 → Mar-26 476 → Jun-26 468. Operating margin 4.0% → 9% → 7% → 10% → 9%. Net profit −36 → −9 → −29 → −24 → +65 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 profit is **not** operating: other income was ₹100 cr, mostly the ophthalmic sale gain. Without it pre-tax profit was about −₹22 cr (PBT ₹78 cr less ₹100 cr) [Screener; Q1 call].
- Q1 FY27 (company): consolidated revenue ₹466 cr (+8.2%), EBITDA ₹41 cr (8.8%) vs ₹49.7 cr (10.9%) in Q4; standalone EBITDA margin 10.3%. COGS rose about 2 points from war-driven raw-material costs [Q1 call].
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹1,817 cr → FY25 ₹1,665 cr → FY26 ₹1,845 cr; operating margin 13% → 6% → 8% (21% in FY22); net profit ₹97 cr → −₹78 cr → −₹99 cr. Interest cost ₹38 cr → ₹66 cr → ₹122 cr; depreciation ₹92 cr → ₹114 cr → ₹126 cr [Screener].
- The core problem in one line: FY26 operating profit ₹142 cr did not cover interest plus depreciation (₹248 cr) [Screener; Q1 call].
- Q4 FY26 interest ₹46 cr included about ₹24 cr of exchange loss on a €10 m ECB loan [Q4 call].
- 5-year sales CAGR 8%; stock price −10% a year over 5 years [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (7 May 2026): repay about ₹140 cr of debt a year for three years and try to prepay more; no major capex for two years; margin benefit in FY27 from MMP; Europe and US liquid-oral launches; India to grow in line with the market; FPP losses to reduce; supplier dues to be settled "within a week" [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (28 Jul 2026): double-digit EBITDA margin aimed for FY27; India to grow high single to double digits; export business to double in 2–3 years ("directly in line"); overall 12–15% sales CAGR; capex ₹40–50 cr (maintenance only); other expenses ₹110–120 cr a quarter; repay ₹110 cr more this year (July–March) plus ₹150 cr next year; COGS pressure to last into Q2/Q3 [Q1 call].
- Delivery: Q1 margin 8.8% fell short of Q4's 10.9% and of the double-digit aim; debt fell only from ₹964 cr to ₹930 cr (about ₹34 cr); Europe and emerging markets fell short (said to be timing). Supplier dues were "much of it settled" [Q1 call].
- Debt repayment: the stored note calls ₹110 cr a "walk-back" from ₹140 cr. The official transcript shows ₹110 cr is the remaining July–March schedule after about ₹30 cr repaid in Q1 — roughly the same ₹140 cr total. Guidance **kept**, not cut [Q1 call].
- USFDA re-inspection of the sterile Goa site: expected "in the next couple of months" on each call for over six months; still not held [Q1 call].
- Verdict: guidance **kept**, but Q1 delivery **missed** on margin and exports.

## Capex and capacity
- The big spend is done: investing outflow ₹433 cr (FY24) and ₹339 cr (FY25) funded mostly by debt — plant upgrades at Goa, Waluj, Baddi (MMP), new sterile lines at Goa, and Warren Remedies' toothpaste and API plants [Screener; ICRA Sep-25].
- FY26 investing outflow fell to ₹102 cr; CWIP dropped from ₹321 cr to ₹90 cr as projects were capitalised (fixed assets ₹997 cr → ₹1,193 cr) [Screener].
- FY27 capex ₹40–50 cr, maintenance only; no major capex for two years [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Capitalised R&D is large: ₹44 cr of development cost added to "intangible assets under development" in FY26 (balance ₹61.7 cr vs ₹44.4 cr), ₹24 cr moved to intangibles, ₹2.8 cr written off — a Key Audit Matter [AR FY26 standalone auditor's report].
- Payback is poor so far: fixed assets up by about ₹640 cr since FY22 while operating profit fell from ₹328 cr to ₹142 cr [Screener].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt ₹930 cr at June 2026 (long-term ₹600 cr, short-term ₹325 cr), down from ₹964 cr in March; ICRA put it at ₹672 cr in Mar-24 and ₹994 cr in Mar-25. Average cost about 9% [Q1 call; ICRA Sep-25].
- Screener borrowings ₹1,090 cr at Mar-26 (includes leases/other items) vs ₹994 cr at Mar-25 [Screener].
- Debt/EBITDA was 10x in FY25 (ICRA); on FY26 EBITDA of ₹142 cr it is still about 6.5–7x [ICRA Sep-25; Screener].
- Operating cash flow ₹192 cr in FY26 (139% of operating profit) helped by stretching suppliers: trade payables rose from ₹208 cr to ₹309 cr, with MSME dues up from ₹56 cr to ₹90 cr. Management admitted suppliers were not paid on time [AR FY26 consolidated note 27; Q4 call].
- Receivable days 97 (FY26) vs 77; inventory days 273; cash conversion cycle 162 days [Screener].
- Land parcel (₹23 cr book value) held for sale, expected to close by September 2026 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Ophthalmic proceeds ₹110 cr: partly used for debt, partly "lying as investment" [Q1 call].
- Dividend token (yield 0.08%) [Screener].
- Rating: ICRA A (Negative)/A2+ on ₹1,019.65 cr of limits (Sep 2025) — negative outlook due to weak margins and high debt; interest cover 1.5x in FY25 and 0.7x in Q1 FY26 [ICRA Sep-25].

## Moat evidence
- Strong prescription franchise: 20th by prescriptions (overtook Pfizer), Rexidin-M Forte gel the most dentist-prescribed oral-care brand, 4 brands in the top 20 of stomatology; Cyclopam available at 3.5 lakh chemists [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Brand pricing is weak evidence: top 5 brands grew only 13% in four years (₹487 cr → ₹550 cr) [IP Q1FY27].
- Regulatory assets: 30 ANDA approvals (55 at various stages), 21 US DMFs, 11 CEPs, EU-GMP approvals at Goa and Baddi (MHRA inspected Goa Plant I in Sep 2026 with no critical or major observations) [IP Q1FY27; Screener announcement list, 10 Sep 2026].
- Europe contract manufacturing is sticky (long-standing partners, ₹250 cr+ order book) but pricing volatile; management says it will not sell when UK/EU prices do not pay [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Cost position was the weak spot and is improving: 26% fewer batches for the same sales, ~900 fewer operations staff [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Going-concern doubt at a subsidiary**: the auditor's Emphasis of Matter says FPP Holding LLC lost ₹23.7 cr in FY26 and has negative net worth of ₹42.3 cr — "material uncertainty" about going concern; no impairment was booked on the investment [AR FY26 standalone auditor's report].
- US sterile plant: the Goa sterile facility has been under an OAI/warning-letter status; only two of four lines cleared; new US sterile approvals are held until re-inspection. MD: "I'm soon running out of patience" [ICRA Sep-25; Q1 call].
- Leverage: interest cover below 1x in parts of FY26; a €10 m ECB adds FX losses to finance cost [ICRA Sep-25; Q4 call].
- Supplier stretch: payables up 48% in FY26; MSME dues ₹90 cr [AR FY26 note 27].
- Capitalised R&D of ₹44 cr in a loss year flatters reported results [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Sales-return provision ₹24.4 cr (KAM); ₹10 cr bad-debt provision in FY26 incl. a bankrupt customer [AR FY26; Q4 call].
- Contingent liabilities (consolidated): GST ₹32.6 cr, excise/service tax ₹6.6 cr, income tax ₹4.8 cr, bank guarantees ₹12.8 cr, plus an FMRAI wage case pending since 2012 [AR FY26 consolidated note 55].
- Warren Remedies still loss-making (EBITDA about −₹6 cr in Q1 incl. OTC advertising; API unsold until regulated approvals, "one more year") [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Acute-heavy India portfolio swings with monsoon and season [Q1 call].
- No promoter pledge; auditor opinion unmodified; no loan default [AR FY26].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was upbeat: first positive EBITDA quarter in six, exports +95% on channel fill. Q1 swung back: emerging markets −31% and Europe flat, blamed on timing [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Liquidity worry eased after the ₹110 cr sale; supplier issue "much of it settled" [Q1 call].
- Cost story arrived in the numbers: employee and other expenses fell; management now guides other expenses ₹110–120 cr a quarter [Q1 call].
- USFDA tone moved from "second sites help" to open impatience and hints of "design around that plant" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Margin guidance moved from implicit "maintain EBITDA" to "double-digit aim" [Q1 call].

## Best management quotes
- "we have been waiting for a U.S. audit for almost more than 6 months now, completely ready" [Q1 call].
- "we have in excess of INR250 crores of orders in hand for execution" [Q1 call].
- "we have not paid suppliers on time given some of the cash flow situation we had" [Q4 call].

## My read
Indoco is a decent Indian brand business buried under a failed expansion: ₹640 cr of added fixed assets, debt near ₹930 cr and operating profit that still does not cover interest plus depreciation. Cost cuts are real and the ophthalmic sale bought time, but Q1's reported profit is a one-off gain and core margin went backwards. The stock at 2.5x book prices in a recovery; it needs EBITDA margin back above 12% and debt below ₹800 cr within a year. A clean USFDA re-inspection of the Goa sterile site would change the view; another year of delay, or an impairment on FPP, would make it worse.
