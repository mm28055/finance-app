# Alembic Pharmaceuticals — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code APLLTD (consolidated). Price ₹864, market cap ₹16,978 cr, 52-week range ₹635–998; promoter holding 69.87% (Jun 2026), controlling company Nirayu Pvt Ltd [Screener, 27 Sep 2026; AR FY26 note 28(8)].

## Business and segments
- Alembic is a Vadodara-based generics maker with four businesses. FY26 revenue: India branded ₹2,458 cr (+5%), US generics ₹2,206 cr (+13%), rest-of-world (RoW) generics ₹1,494 cr (+20%), API (bulk drugs) ₹1,187 cr (+5%) — total ₹7,345 cr [AR FY26 Financial Capital p.92; Screener].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹2,150 cr (+26%): India ₹642 cr (+7%), US ₹778 cr (+49%), ex-US ₹383 cr (+17%), API ₹346 cr (+33%). US is now 36% of sales, India 30% [IP Q1FY27 p.4].
- India: ranked 21st in the Indian market with 1.1–1.2% share (1.4% a year ago), 5,500+ reps, 13% of portfolio under price control. Excluding animal health, India grew only ~2% in FY26. Animal health (~22% of India sales) grew 24% in Q1; acute ~37%; specialty (gynae, gastro, ophthal, cardio) ~60% of India revenue [IP Q1FY27 p.5, p.7; CARE Jun-26; Q1 call].
- US: 279 ANDA filings, 244 approvals (224 final, 20 tentative), 185 products launched; 7 launches and 10 approvals in Q1; 15+ launches planned for the rest of FY27 [IP Q1FY27 p.8; Q1 call].
- US branded (new, Feb 2026): Alembic Therapeutics bought Utility Therapeutics, owner of Pivya (a UTI antibiotic), and has added Nuvessa and one more small women's-health product; product comes from a contract maker, so no capex — only field force and marketing [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.8; CARE Jun-26].
- Plants: 9 manufacturing sites. Newer US-focused plants F2 (Panelav oncology oral/injectable) and F3 (Karkhadi injectables/ophthalmic) are filling up: F3's ophthalmic line is full and being expanded; other F3 lines run at ~40% and ~60%. The Sikkim plant is being exited (assets held for sale) [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 26].
- Customer concentration: one customer contributed ₹853 cr (12% of FY26 revenue) [AR FY26 consolidated note 7].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Jun-25 1,711 → Sep-25 1,910 → Dec-25 1,876 → Mar-26 1,848 → Jun-26 2,150. Operating margin: 16% → 17% → 16% → 12% → 15%. Net profit: 154 → 184 → 132 → 202 → 172 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: company EBITDA ₹348 cr (16%, +21%); EBITDA before R&D ₹523 cr (~24%); R&D ₹186 cr (9% of sales); PBT ₹223 cr (+17%); PAT ₹173 cr (+12%) with tax up to ~22% after moving to the new regime. Depreciation jumped to ₹98 cr (₹74 cr a year ago) as the Pivya intangible is amortised over ~7–8 years [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.3; Screener].
- Q4 FY26 profit ₹202 cr was flattered by a ₹101 cr one-time tax credit and hit by a ₹24 cr exceptional charge; Q4 operating margin was only 12% (R&D 11% of sales, Pivya launch costs) [Q4 call; Screener].
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹6,229 cr → FY25 ₹6,672 cr → FY26 ₹7,345 cr; operating margin 15% → 15% → 15%; net profit ₹616 cr → ₹582 cr → ₹671 cr. TTM sales ₹7,784 cr, TTM profit ₹690 cr. ROCE 13%; it was 22–38% before the FY19–FY23 plant build-out [Screener].
- FY26 R&D ₹712 cr (~10% of sales); EBITDA before R&D ₹1,846 cr (25%), after R&D ~16%; gross margin ~73% [AR FY26 p.93; Q4 call].
- Q1 US growth of 49% in rupees is ~37–38% in constant currency; ex-bosutinib the US grew "over 20%, closer to 25%" [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth "low double digit"; US generics +10–15% (in rupees), RoW 15%+, API ~10%, India "closer to market growth"; R&D ₹750–800 cr (~9% of sales); capex ₹300–350 cr; Pivya drag 100–150 bps on margin, offset by core operating leverage; no margin number, but "over a 2–3 year period we must go back up to 20% EBITDA" [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 call (4 Aug 2026): US raised to "mid to high teens"; company growth raised from low double digit to "closer to the mid-teen range". Margin: core business moving to high teens, less ~150 bps Pivya dilution — i.e. roughly flat to slightly better than FY26's ~16%. Pivya to break even around Q4 FY27 and contribute from FY28. Debt to return at least to March levels and towards ~1× EBITDA [Q1 call, official].
- Delivery: Q1 revenue +26% beat every segment guide except India (+7%, still below market). The beat leaned on bosutinib — 180-day exclusivity on two strengths, launched June 2026, exclusivity "till November", after which competition would be "quite drastic" [Q1 call].
- Verdict: revenue guidance **raised**; margin guidance **kept**; India **still missing** (hoped-for "market growth" has not come).

## Capex and capacity
- Capex FY26 ₹432 cr ("maintenance, replacement and debottlenecking"); FY27 guide ₹300–350 cr; CARE expects ₹300–400 cr a year. Capital commitments ₹191.5 cr at March 2026 [AR FY26 p.94 and note 27(2); Q4 call; CARE Jun-26].
- The heavy build-out (F2, F3, F4 injectables/oncology/derma, Indore India plant) is done — CWIP fell from ₹2,304 cr (FY22) to ₹683 cr (FY26) — and management says "in terms of capex, we are broadly done" [Screener; Q4 call].
- Peptides: API capacity built inside an existing plant (amount not disclosed); 5–6 peptide products, 2 filed. An NCE-1 peptide filing drove Q4's R&D spike [Q4 call].
- New geographies: 45% of a Canadian JV (with Difgen Holdings 45% and Mr. Sukhad Juneja; June 2026); a Saudi JV; new subsidiaries in Thailand, Philippines, Germany and Dubai (Aug 2026) [NSE filings 6 Jun and 7 Sep 2026; IP Q1FY27 p.9; Q1 call].
- Payback: the payoff is operating leverage in F2/F3 — the reason ROCE is 13% today; management targets margin back to ~20% over 2–3 years [Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹1,430 cr at March 2026 (₹1,258 cr a year earlier), net debt ₹1,164 cr; gross debt rose to ~₹1,600 cr in June 2026 because receivables grew with sales [Screener; AR FY26 p.93; Q1 call].
- FY26 cash from operations ₹783 cr (FY25 only ₹88 cr), free cash flow ₹290 cr; CFO/operating profit 79% [Screener].
- Working capital is heavy: net working capital ₹1,812 cr (AR) or ~₹3,000 cr (Q4 call, different basis); inventory ₹2,511 cr at March 2026 (an audit key matter); Screener inventory days 462, cash conversion cycle 381 days. CARE says inventory build for launches pushed the operating cycle out and caused "higher-than-envisaged reliance on working capital borrowings" [AR FY26 auditor's report; Screener; CARE Jun-26].
- Dividend ₹12 a share for FY26 (payout ~35%) [AR FY26 p.94; Screener].
- Ratings: CRISIL AA+/Stable on bank lines enhanced from ₹800 cr to ₹1,100 cr (30 Jun 2026); CARE A1+ on ₹1,100 cr commercial paper (11 Jun 2026). CARE: gearing 0.25×, debt/EBITDA 1.22×, current ratio 1.75×; downgrade if EBITDA margin stays below 15% or gearing exceeds 0.5× [CRISIL letter Jun-26; CARE Jun-26].

## Moat evidence
- Pipeline depth: 244 US approvals and a steady flow of first-to-file (FTF) wins — e.g. sole first filer on binimetinib 45 mg (US$259 m market), bosutinib exclusivity, and US distribution of Natco's olaparib (US$1.4 bn market, Para IV litigation ongoing) [NSE press releases 19 Jun and 18 Jul 2026; Q1 call].
- Vertical integration: captive API for key products; API sold at "premium prices… we don't compete at the bottom" [CARE Jun-26; Q4 call].
- India brands: 5 brands above ₹100 cr; Azithral, Althrocin and others rank #1 in their molecule; 137.6 mn prescriptions (17th by volume) [IP Q1FY27 p.5–6].
- Clean US compliance record: EIRs in place for all operating plants (F3 EIR May 2026); CARE notes "EIR in place for all its operational manufacturing facilities" [NSE 14 May 2026; CARE Jun-26].
- Weakness: US generics are a price-taker business (top three distributors buy >90%); India share has fallen from 1.4% to ~1.2% in a year [CARE Jun-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **India franchise erosion.** Human-health India grew ~2% in FY26; market share lost; senior sales and strategy people left (AVP Sales & Marketing, 4 May 2026; SVP Strategy, 14 May 2026); a new head (Ramesh Juneja) joined in mid-2026 and says turnaround will take "a couple of quarters" [CARE Jun-26; NSE filings 4 and 14 May 2026; Q1 call].
- **Bosutinib cliff.** Q1's US beat includes one month of exclusive bosutinib; exclusivity ends around November 2026 [Q1 call].
- **US branded is a new skill.** Pivya drag >150 bps in Q1; break-even only around Q4 FY27 [Q1 call].
- **Regulatory.** USFDA issued a warning letter (10 Jul 2026) to the clinical investigator for a bioequivalence study run at Alembic's Vadodara BE facility — informed-consent issue, "not data integrity"; since then USFDA closed the March 2025 inspection with an EIR, and an unannounced inspection of the same facility (31 Aug–10 Sep 2026) ended with no Form 483 observations [NSE filings 13 Jul, 11 and 16 Sep 2026].
- **Auditor (KKC & Associates).** Unmodified opinion; key audit matter is existence and valuation of ₹2,511 cr of inventory; no audit-trail exceptions reported [AR FY26 auditor's reports].
- **Contingent liabilities** modest: group ₹100 cr (letters of credit/guarantees ₹38.6 cr, a ₹20.9 cr indirect-tax dispute, a rooftop-solar dispute with MGVCL); standalone guarantees ₹348 cr (₹103 cr a year ago), mostly for subsidiaries [AR FY26 standalone note 27(2), consolidated note].
- **Related parties / pay.** KMP remuneration ₹61 cr in FY26 (~9% of profit), mostly the Amin family; ₹63 cr purchases from Shreno Publications and ₹31.5 cr of services and ₹12.4 cr rent from Alembic Ltd (promoter-group companies) [AR FY26 note 28(8)].
- **Exceptional items FY26**: ₹48.6 cr labour-code gratuity charge; ₹18.4 cr write-down on the Sikkim plant held for sale [AR FY26 note 26].
- Company Secretary changed (Manisha Saraf resigned Aug 2026) [NSE 10 and 20 Aug 2026].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from cautious ("not assuming any easing in pricing pressure") to confident ("strong start… broad-based growth").
- Growth guidance up (low double digit → mid-teens); US up (10–15% → mid-high teens).
- New drivers: bosutinib exclusivity, 7 launches, API +33%. New issue: gross debt up ~₹240 cr in a quarter on receivables.
- India still weak; leadership changed.

## My read
Alembic is finally getting paid for its ₹2,000+ cr plant build-out: US volumes and new launches are filling F2/F3, and Q1 revenue grew 26% with every export business ahead of plan. But margins are stuck at ~16% because R&D (₹750–800 cr) and the US branded launch absorb the leverage, and part of Q1 came from a six-month bosutinib window. The India business — historically the cash engine — is losing share and management. The stock needs profit growth in the low-to-mid teens for several years; that is plausible only if post-R&D margin moves from 16% towards 20% as promised. The view improves if India returns to market growth and Pivya breaks even by Q4; it worsens if US growth falls back after November or debt keeps rising with receivables.
