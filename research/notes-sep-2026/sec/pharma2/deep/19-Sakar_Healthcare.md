# Sakar Healthcare — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code SAKAR (consolidated). Price ₹1,136, market cap ₹2,527 cr, 52-week range ₹346–1,230, P/E 70, book value ₹146, ROCE 12.4%, ROE 10%, no dividend [Screener]. Promoter Sanjay Shah (CMD) and family hold 52.5% (Jun-26), down from 53.6% in Mar-25; no pledge disclosed [Screener; AR FY26].

## Business and segments
- Ahmedabad-based maker of finished medicines (tablets, liquids, injectables, dry-powder injections, lyophilised vials) and, since 2022, cytotoxic (cancer) APIs. One reportable segment in the accounts — the company does not publish audited segment data, so the oncology/non-oncology split comes only from calls [AR FY26 note 33; MD&A p.53].
- Two plants: Changodar (non-oncology: CDMO for Zydus, Emcure, Glenmark, Cipla, Ipca, Abbott; own-brand exports of generics to Africa, APAC, LatAm, CIS; 270+ marketing authorisations) and Bavla (EU-GMP oncology plant with oral solids, injectables, lyophilised, oral liquids and an API block) [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- FY26 split: oncology 38% of revenue (about ₹95 cr), non-oncology 62% (about ₹156 cr); in FY25 oncology was 21% (ICRA put FY25 oncology sales at about ₹36 cr) [Q4 call; ICRA Sep-25].
- Q4 FY26: oncology ₹31.46 cr, non-oncology ₹39.64 cr; oncology exports only ₹0.3 cr. Q1 FY27: oncology about ₹33 cr, of which exports ₹6.5 cr and domestic (CMO for Indian pharma + API sales) about ₹26.5 cr; non-oncology about ₹40 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Oncology customers: Accord-Intas, Torrent (UK and Heumann in Germany), Emcure (Tillomed UK), Glenmark, Zydus, plus 15+ regional Indian firms. The Zydus deal (GCC and emerging markets) was the 40th anti-cancer product agreement [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Regulatory pipeline (Q1 FY27): 261 oncology dossiers shared, 178 submitted, 16 marketing authorisations (MAs) received; 26 EU MA filings incl. 15 in Sakar's own name; 33 site variations (18 molecules) filed in EU/UK, 7 approved; 55 oncology molecules developed, 32 dossiers ready; 21 APIs developed, 16 with written confirmation, 2 with CEP (European API certificate), 5 CEP applications [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Bavla oncology utilisation about 29% (Q4 FY26); management targets 50–55% in two years and says the plant can support ₹800–1,000 cr revenue at optimal use in 4–5 years with no major new capex [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- 435 employees at 31 Mar 2026 [AR FY26 MD&A p.54].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly sales (₹ cr): Jun-25 53 → Sep-25 58 → Dec-25 70 → Mar-26 71 → Jun-26 73. Operating margin 24% → 20% → 26% → 37% → 29%. Net profit 5 → 5 → 10 → 11 → 10 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 (company, ₹ lakh): revenue 7,297 (+38%), EBITDA 2,125 (+67%, margin 29%), PAT 1,028 (+120%) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Q4 FY26 37% margin was flattered by inventory: management said better inventory planning and stocking lowered costs; gross margin jumped 10–11 points on flat oncology sales. Management now points to about 30% as the sustainable level [Q4 call].
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹153 cr → FY25 ₹178 cr → FY26 ₹251 cr (+41%); operating margin 25% → 28% → 27%; net profit ₹12 cr → ₹18 cr → ₹30 cr. TTM sales ₹272 cr, profit ₹36 cr. 3-year sales CAGR 24%, profit CAGR 34% [Screener].
- Returns are still low for the capital employed: ROCE 7% (FY24) → 9% → 12% (FY26); 3-year average ROE 8% [Screener].
- Tax rate stepping up to about 25% in FY27 (Q4 FY26 already 39%, Q1 29%), which will slow profit growth relative to EBITDA [Q4 call; Screener].
- Unit economics: management says oncology earns about 2x the margin of general formulations; CMO oncology EBITDA about 30%, integrated-API export products 35%+; non-oncology about 25% [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth "similar" to FY26's 42% (an analyst framed it as ₹380 cr; 40% growth on ₹251 cr is about ₹350 cr); oncology to roughly double to about ₹200 cr; tech-transfer supplies to start Q1 FY27 with 5 projects commercialising; Accord portfolio worth ₹50–100 cr "depending on commercialisation"; first milestone ₹500 cr total revenue in two years (60% oncology, of which 65% exports); oncology margins 25–30% [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (29 Jul 2026): oncology FY27 target ₹200 cr kept (about 30% exports, 40% domestic CMO, 25% API); oncology exports to rise about 10x from the ₹6.5 cr quarterly level (≈₹60–70 cr for the year); FY28 oncology ₹280–300 cr; MAs to double from 16 this year and exceed 100 next year; management agreed with an analyst that ₹350 cr total needs a ₹90 cr quarterly run rate "by end of Q2 or beginning of Q3"; non-oncology to grow 7–8% [Q1 call].
- Delivery: Q1 revenue ₹73 cr was flat on Q4 (₹71 cr) — the third straight quarter at ₹70–73 cr. Of the ₹200 cr oncology aim, Q1 delivered ₹33 cr (a ₹132 cr annual pace). Three of five Accord site-variation products were dispatched; first own MA supplies (Bulgaria) were delayed by serialisation and logistics set-up [Q1 call].
- A Q4 promise that Q4 oncology would reach about ₹38–40 cr (made on the Q3 call, per an analyst) was missed at ₹31 cr [Q4 call].
- Verdict: guidance **kept** in words, but delivery is **behind** and back-ended into H2 FY27. The earlier "₹30 cr cut" reading in the stored analysis is not supported — the ₹380 cr figure came from an analyst; management spoke of ~40% growth, which is about ₹350 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Heavy capex already done: debt-funded oncology plant built FY22–FY24 — formulations started Oct 2021, API Jul 2022, injectables Apr 2023 (delayed from Dec 2022) [ICRA Sep-25].
- Screener cash flow: investing outflow ₹90 cr (FY22), ₹74 cr (FY23), ₹52 cr (FY24), ₹31 cr (FY25), ₹28 cr (FY26). Gross block additions FY26 about ₹35.6 cr, plus ₹5.5 cr of capitalised product development (dossiers, licence fees, R&D staff) [Screener; AR FY26 note 3/3(a)].
- Management says no major capex needed for 3–4 years; ICRA expected ₹18–20 cr a year of maintenance/other capex [Q1 call; ICRA Sep-25].
- Payback depends on utilisation: ₹800–1,000 cr peak revenue on a fixed-asset base of about ₹326 cr (FY26 net block) would be asset turns of 2.5–3x versus about 0.8x today [Screener; Q1 call].
- Equity-funded: ₹178.6 cr raised FY17–FY25 via IPO and preferential issues (incl. FY24) — shareholders have been diluted to fund the plant [ICRA Sep-25].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹71 cr (Mar-26) vs ₹86 cr (Mar-25) and ₹119 cr (Mar-23); cash negligible. Net debt about ₹70 cr ≈ 1x FY26 EBITDA [Screener].
- Interest coverage 9.1x FY26 vs 6.5x FY25; company-stated debt-equity 0.14x (Screener's borrowings imply about 0.22x) [AR FY26 MD&A p.54; Screener].
- Operating cash flow ₹49 cr FY26 (77% of operating profit); free cash flow ₹21 cr, first meaningful positive year after −₹62 cr, −₹49 cr, −₹35 cr in FY22–FY24 [Screener].
- Working capital is heavy: inventory days 207 (FY26) vs 69 in FY22; cash conversion cycle 127 days (163 in FY25). Inventory was ₹70 cr at Mar-26 and is a Key Audit Matter [Screener; AR FY26 auditor's report].
- No dividend ever paid; profits retained [Screener].
- Rating: ICRA BBB (Positive)/A3+ on ₹118 cr of bank limits, outlook raised to Positive in Sep 2025 on the expected oncology scale-up; liquidity "adequate" [ICRA Sep-25].

## Moat evidence
- Regulatory lock-in: once a partner files Sakar's plant into its EU/UK dossier (site variation), switching takes 12–24 months of paperwork. 7 site variations approved, 33 filed [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- EU-GMP cytotoxic plant with in-house API is rare among small Indian firms; cytotoxic handling needs containment. 21 APIs developed, 2 with CEP; target 7 CEPs within 2–3 quarters, so a third of products are backward-integrated [Q1 call].
- Blue-chip partners (Accord-Intas, Torrent, Zydus, Glenmark, Emcure) validate quality. Torrent won German tenders for Letrozole and Exemestane supplied from Sakar [Q1 call].
- Pricing: contracts let Sakar pass on large input-cost jumps to partners [Q4 call].
- Limits: non-oncology is a commodity business growing 7–8% with price erosion; ICRA calls Sakar a mid-sized player with limited pricing flexibility [Q1 call; ICRA Sep-25].

## Risks and red flags
- Execution/timing: oncology export sales rest on MAs and partner tenders Sakar does not control; export off-take can come "in one go" (tenders), so quarters will be lumpy. Earlier timelines have slipped (injectables delayed, FY25 registrations delayed, Q4 oncology below plan) [Q4 call; ICRA Sep-25].
- Valuation: the stock is up 219% in a year and trades near 7.8x book with ROCE still 12% — the price already assumes the ₹200 cr oncology plan lands [Screener].
- Inventory: 207 inventory days and an auditor Key Audit Matter on existence and valuation of ₹70 cr of stock [AR FY26 auditor's report; Screener].
- Capitalised development: a second Key Audit Matter covers product-development spend capitalised as intangibles (₹5.5 cr in FY26, ₹4.1 cr in FY25) — modest but it flatters reported profit [AR FY26 note 3(a)].
- Promoter pay jumped: Sanjay Shah ₹2.40 cr (FY25 ₹0.72 cr), Aarsh Shah ₹1.80 cr (₹0.60 cr), Rita Shah ₹0.18 cr — about ₹4.4 cr, roughly 14% of FY26 PAT [AR FY26 note 37].
- Tax disputes: ₹2.82 cr + ₹0.09 cr income-tax demands for AY 2020-21 under appeal; company reports no contingent liability [AR FY26 CARO (vii)(b); note 32].
- No segment reporting: oncology figures are unaudited call numbers [AR FY26 note 33].
- Auditor opinion unqualified, no emphasis of matter; no loan default, no fraud reported [AR FY26 auditor's report; CARO].
- Promoter holding drifted down from 53.6% to 52.5% over five quarters [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone went from "setup ready for takeoff" (Q4) to more careful: Q1 stressed serialisation/logistics teething problems, MAs outside the company's control, and H2-weighted exports [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New numbers: FY28 oncology ₹280–300 cr (new), export ₹60–70 cr FY27 (new), FY27 oncology mix 30/40/25 (new), MA target 100+ by FY28 [Q1 call].
- Margin message shifted from "maintain the 37% trend" (Q4) to "about 30% by year-end, 35%+ on integrated products" (Q1) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Pipeline grew: dossiers submitted 125 → 178, MAs 12 → 16, site variations approved 4–5 → 7 [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- API CEP names differ between calls (Q4: Gefitinib and Capecitabine; Q1: Gefitinib and Cytarabine) — a disclosure inconsistency [official transcripts].

## Best management quotes
- "we believe it can support revenues of INR800 crores to INR1,000 crores at optimal utilization" (Bavla plant) [Q1 call].
- "we are on track for this INR90 crores quarterly run rate" [Q1 call].
- "Off-take for an entire year might happen in one go" (on tender-driven oncology exports) [Q4 call, stored copy].

## My read
The plant is built, the approvals are arriving and the balance sheet has healed (FCF positive, debt about 1x EBITDA), so the business is improving. But revenue has sat at ₹70–73 cr for three quarters and the FY27 plan now needs about ₹90 cr a quarter from Q3 — nearly all of it from exports that were ₹6.5 cr in Q1. At a P/E near 70 the price needs the ₹200 cr oncology and ₹350 cr total targets to land and FY28 to show another big step. I would turn more positive if Q2/Q3 sales cross ₹85–90 cr with margins near 30%; I would turn negative if exports stay below ₹15 cr a quarter by Q3 or inventory keeps climbing.
