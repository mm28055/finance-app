# Strides Pharma Science — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code STAR. Price ₹1,185, market cap ₹10,922 cr, 52-week range ₹769–1,261, P/E 18.8 [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Bengaluru-based maker of niche oral generics (soft gels, liquids, modified-release), plus controlled substances and nasal sprays from its US plant at Chestnut Ridge, New York. 7 plants, 4 USFDA-approved; sells in ~100 countries; 220+ ANDAs filed, 208 approved [CARE Jul-26].
- FY26 revenue ₹4,859 cr (+6.4%): US $284 mn (FY25 $291 mn); ex-US (excl. donor-funded "access" markets) $254 mn (FY25 $221 mn) [Q4 call; CARE Jul-26]. By region: Americas ~54%, Europe ~22%, Australia ~11%, Africa ~12% [CARE Jul-26].
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹1,265 cr (+13% YoY): US ₹628 cr ($68 mn); ex-US ₹588 cr ($63 mn, +17% YoY, but down from $70 mn in Q4) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- US: 72 commercial products; top-3 position in 37 products = ~70–75% of US sales; controlled substances ~5% of US revenue; top 10 products ~30% of group sales; nine low-margin products exited in FY26 [Q1 call; Q4 call; CARE Jul-26].
- Ex-US: B2C front-ends in UK, Nordics, South Africa, Francophone Africa, Kenya; B2B partners in Europe and Australia; regulated-market (ORM) revenue up from $31 mn a quarter (Q1 FY24) to $52 mn (Q4 FY26) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Capacity: "enough capacity to cover the next 2 years of volumes"; no greenfield plans [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue / operating profit / OPM (₹ cr): Jun-25 1,120 / 218 / 19%; Sep-25 1,221 / 231 / 19%; Dec-25 1,195 / 236 / 20%; Mar-26 1,323 / 240 / 18%; Jun-26 1,265 / 228 / 18% [Screener].
- Net profit: Jun-25 ₹106 cr; Sep-25 ₹132 cr; Dec-25 ₹208 cr (incl. property sale gain); Mar-26 ₹129 cr; Jun-26 ₹165 cr (incl. ₹53 cr after-tax gain on selling a majority of Pivot Path, its captive tech centre) [Screener; Q1 call]. Operational PAT Q1 ₹123 cr (+8%) [Q1 call].
- Annual: sales FY24 ₹3,890 cr → FY25 ₹4,565 cr → FY26 ₹4,859 cr; operating profit ₹528 → 803 → 925 cr; OPM 14% → 18% → 19%; net profit −₹94 cr → ₹3,598 cr (one-off OneSource demerger gain) → ₹575 cr [Screener]. Operational PAT FY26 ₹518 cr (+50%) [Q4 call].
- Gross margin Q1 60.9% (+60 bps); freight 6.2% of sales vs 5.3% a year ago; ₹13.1 cr of extra war-related freight/operating costs in Q1, ₹20 cr in Q4 [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- ROCE 15.3% TTM (company) / 18% FY26 (Screener) [Q1 call; Screener].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (18 May 2026): US "aspiration" $375–400 mn in FY28; H2 FY27 much stronger; sequential growth from Q4 to Q1 ("Yes, of course"); EBITDA margin 18–20%, long-term 20%+; gross margin 58–60%; capex + intangibles ~₹300 cr a year; R&D $20–25 mn+ a year; Sandoz Africa brands to close Oct–Mar (H2 FY27); controlled-substance quota uplift expected; nasal sprays FY28+ [Q4 call].
- Earlier: the $400 mn US target dates back 2–3 years (before the softgel demerger) [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (31 Jul 2026): US aspiration now stated as **$375 mn** (the "$400 mn" upper end dropped); first two quarters "very soft"; ~10 launches by March; first nasal spray approval Q3–Q4; Sandoz close in Q2; DEA June quota cycle result not yet out; net-debt-neutral in 2–3 years (counting ₹320 cr OneSource stake) [Q1 call].
- Delivered vs Q4 promises: sequential growth **missed** (revenue ₹1,323 cr → ₹1,265 cr; ex-US $70 mn → $63 mn); margin in range (18.2%); controlled-substance quota not yet received; second nasal spray filed (met) [Q1 call; Screener].
- Verdict: **kept on margin, trimmed on the US target, missed on the sequential-growth promise**.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 investment ₹418 cr: ₹236 cr tangible (nasal-spray capability at Chestnut Ridge, ex-US capacity, UK office) + ₹182 cr intangibles (product rights, SAP upgrade) [Q4 call].
- FY27–FY28: ~₹250–300 cr a year including maintenance, R&D and hard capex; no greenfield [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- $30 mn spent over 24 months on IP purchases and partnered R&D; benefits from H2 FY27 [Q4 call].
- Sandoz Sub-Saharan Africa brands bought for $12 mn; roughly doubles branded Africa portfolio [CARE Jul-26; Q4 call].
- ~One-third of US sales made at Chestnut Ridge (US plant) — a hedge against proposed US generic tariffs from August 2028 [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹1,437 cr (Mar-26) → ₹1,425 cr (Jun-26); net debt/EBITDA 1.52x (1.9x a year earlier); rupee fall added ~₹112 cr to debt in FY26; OneSource stake worth ~₹320–337 cr on top [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Borrowings ₹1,758 cr at March 2026 (₹3,030 cr in FY23) [Screener]. Weighted cost of debt ~7.6% [Q1 call].
- Operating cash flow FY26 ₹703 cr (76% of EBITDA); free cash flow ₹291 cr; Q1 FY27 operating cash only ₹109 cr (47% of EBITDA) as inventory rose 24 days [Q4 call; Screener; Q1 call].
- Cash-to-cash cycle 123 days (Q1) [Q1 call]; Screener inventory days 305, CCC 157 days [Screener].
- Dividend ₹5/share for FY26 [Q4 call].
- Rating: **CARE upgraded to A+/Stable (8 Jul 2026)** from A/Positive; CARE expects ~10% FY27 growth and 19–21% margins, net debt/EBITDA ~1.5x [CARE Jul-26].

## Moat evidence
- Niche US portfolio: top-3 in 37 products; launches only when a competitor exits or supply is disrupted — keeps gross margin at 58–61% [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Onshore US plant for controlled substances, nasal sprays, films — licences and DEA quotas are real barriers [Q1 call].
- Ex-US B2B partnerships (Australia, Europe) and branded Africa, where Strides says it grows faster than the market [Q4 call].
- Limits: US revenue flat for two years ($291 mn → $284 mn) despite launches; price competition in recent launches [CARE Jul-26; Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter pledge**: 27.27% of promoter shares pledged at March 2026 (down from 51.98%), on a promoter holding of only 27.91% — CARE calls it "relatively high" [CARE Jul-26].
- **USFDA inspection at Bengaluru (May 2026)** with observations; response filed; outcome awaited (Aug–Sep) [Q1 call].
- US target at risk: needs ~$90 mn more US revenue in 8 quarters from a flat base; DEA quota not granted in June cycle [Q1 call].
- Recall legacy: ranitidine (NDMA) and losartan (azide) recall and settlement costs still booked as exceptional items; FY26 exceptional items ₹32 cr net [AR FY26 note 35].
- Auditor: unmodified; key audit matters on goodwill/intangible impairment (non-current assets ₹3,314 cr) and US gross-to-net accruals (chargebacks, rebates, Medicaid) [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Contingent liabilities small: tax disputes ~₹83–85 cr and ~₹35 cr [AR FY26 note 42].
- Related-party history: OneSource (formerly Stelis, promoter-linked biologics/CDMO) spun out; Strides keeps a ~₹320 cr stake and related-party trade continues [AR FY26 related-party note; Q1 call].
- Pivot Path sale valued the unit at ~₹100–125 cr, below one times its ₹144 cr revenue (questioned on the call) [Q1 call].

## My read
Strides is a genuine turnaround — OPM up from 14% to 19% in two years, debt down by ~₹1,150 cr over four years, rating upgraded — and at ~19 times earnings it is priced far below the sector. The weak link is the US: revenue has not grown for two years, the $400 mn goal quietly became $375 mn, and Q1 broke the promise of sequential growth. The price needs only modest growth, so the key watch items are the Bengaluru USFDA outcome, the December DEA quota, the Sandoz close and whether ex-US recovers the lost ~$7 mn. A clean FDA result plus H2 US launches would re-rate it; a warning letter or another flat US half would keep it cheap for a reason.
