# Zydus Lifesciences — deep research (as of 28 Sep 2026)

Screener code ZYDUSLIFE. Price ₹1,204, market cap ₹1,20,089 cr, 52-week range ₹836–1,209 (at the high) [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Diversified pharma group with three reported segments. FY26 segment revenue: Pharmaceuticals ₹22,412 cr (FY25 ₹20,542 cr), Consumer Products ₹3,954 cr (₹2,698 cr), Medical Technologies ₹782 cr (new, from the Amplitude Surgical buy). Total ₹27,148 cr [AR FY26 note 44].
- Segment profit FY26: Pharma ₹7,049 cr (FY25 ₹5,923 cr); Consumer ₹267 cr, **down** from ₹347 cr even though revenue rose 47% (Comfort Click acquisition costs and amortisation); MedTech a **loss of ₹178 cr** (FY25 loss ₹24 cr). All the group's profit growth came from pharma [AR FY26 note 44].
- Business-line revenue FY26: North America formulations ₹11,682 cr (43.7% of sales), India formulations ₹6,574 cr (24.6%), Consumer Wellness ₹3,913 cr (14.6%), International markets ₹3,070 cr (11.5%), MedTech 2.9%, others (API, alliances) 2.6% [IP Aug-26].
- Q1 FY27: North America ₹3,100 cr (+5% QoQ), India branded +20% YoY, International ₹970 cr (+34% YoY), Consumer Wellness ₹1,430 cr (+67% YoY; Comfort Click like-for-like +25%, domestic +5%), MedTech ₹280 cr [Q1 call].
- Market position: #3 generic company in the US by prescriptions; 513 ANDAs filed, 443 approved; #1 in oncology in India; chronic + sub-chronic share of India sales 54.2% (up 360 bps over four years); 78 brands above ₹25 cr (56 four years ago); 7,000+ medical reps [IP Aug-26].
- Branded products (India, EM, US specialty, wellness, MedTech) were over 55% of group revenue in Q1; target: more than two-thirds over the medium term [Q1 call].
- Capacity: 44 manufacturing sites; oral solids 39 bn pills a year, injectables 343 mn units, vaccines 27.6 mn doses. Utilisation not disclosed [IP Aug-26].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue / operating profit (EBITDA) / margin (₹ cr): Jun-25 6,574 / 2,088 / 32%; Sep-25 6,123 / 2,016 / 33%; Dec-25 6,864 / 1,816 / 26%; Mar-26 7,587 / 2,554 / 34%; Jun-26 8,017 / 1,929 / 24% [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 read in plain words: revenue +22% YoY but operating profit **fell 8%** YoY and reported net profit (before minority interest) fell 35% to ₹990 cr (₹1,521 cr a year earlier). Depreciation more than doubled to ₹555 cr (from ₹238 cr) and interest rose to ₹156 cr (from ₹85 cr), both from acquisitions and the capitalised Mirabegron licence fee [Screener; Q1 call].
- Annual: sales FY24 ₹19,547 cr → FY25 ₹23,242 cr → FY26 ₹27,148 cr; operating profit ₹5,384 → 7,058 → 8,475 cr; OPM 28% → 30% → 31%; net profit ₹3,973 → 4,673 → 5,124 cr [Screener]. Adjusted PAT FY26 ₹5,456 cr, +15% [Q4 call].
- FY26 margin was lifted by one-offs: Revlimid (limited-volume cancer generic), semi-exclusive Mirabegron, and forex gains. CRISIL says the Q1 FY27 margin drop to 24.1% was "mainly due to reduction in forex gain and Revlimid erosion" [CRISIL Sep-26].
- US base: management put the US run-rate at "$300 plus to 310 million" a quarter and expects some erosion from Mirabegron competition [Q4 call]. US branded/specialty is 10% of US sales (about $60 mn rare-disease Sentynl + $60–70 mn 505(b)(2)) before Assertio [Q1 call].
- India oncology crossed ₹800 cr; vaccines target ₹300–400 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- ROCE 21% FY26 (24% FY25) [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (May 2026): FY27 consolidated revenue "high teens growth"; US single-digit growth; India to beat the market by 200–400 bps; International to keep ~40% momentum; Consumer double digit; EBITDA margin "in excess of 24%" with R&D at 8% of sales; capex ~₹1,500 cr; Saroglitazar US spend ~$70 mn in FY27 [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 call (Aug 2026): revenue guidance restated as "strong double-digit growth" — softer wording than "high teens"; India lifted to beat the market by 300–500 bps (mid-teens growth); US single digit; margin "24%" (the "+" dropped in the CEO's answer); capex raised to ₹1,500–1,600 cr; other expenses excluding R&D to run at ₹1,900–2,000 cr a quarter [Q1 call].
- Delivered in Q1: revenue +22% (ahead), margin 24.1% (on guidance), India +20% (ahead), International +34% (ahead) [Q1 call].
- Longer term: margin to rise to 28–30% "as we move closer to the 5-year period" as branded mix passes two-thirds [Q1 call]. Saroglitazar US launch in April 2027 (FY28); first two years are investment years; peak sales $200–300 mn conservative, $400 mn+ optimistic [Q1 call]. US biosimilars scale-up in calendar 2029 [Q1 call]. Rolvedon (Assertio) $15–20 mn a quarter from Q2 [Q1 call].
- Verdict: **kept** on margin, **raised** for India and capex, **softened** in wording on group revenue.

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex ₹1,500–1,600 cr (raised from ~₹1,500 cr): new SEZ-3 site; expansions at Moraiya, Goa, Baddi and SEZ Units 2–3; a larger formulations R&D centre; a CAR-T facility; a vaccine drug-substance plant; Zylidac (US biologics plant bought from Agenus); a one-off land purchase for wellness [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Screener cash flow implies FY26 capex of about ₹2,650 cr (operating cash ₹2,117 cr minus free cash flow −₹530 cr), up from ~₹1,640 cr in FY25; CWIP ₹3,698 cr at March 2026 [Screener].
- Payback not stated. Zylidac needs ~3 years to be well utilised; BOT-BAL supply to Agenus only $10–15 mn revenue [Q4 call]. MedTech is a 3–4-year platform build [Q4 call].
- Acquisitions in 12–18 months: Comfort Click (UK VMS), Amplitude Surgical (France, orthopaedics), Zylidac, LiqMeds, Assertio (~$166 mn, $23.50/share cash; closed Q1 FY27) [AR FY26 note 55; Q1 call]. Assertio purchase-price allocation still open; mostly to intangibles, so amortisation will rise further from Q2 [Q1 call].
- R&D: 8.4% of FY26 sales (₹2,273 cr); split ~50% generics/value-added, 40%+ NCE, biologics and vaccines [IP Aug-26; Q4 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings jumped to ₹12,496 cr at March 2026 from ₹3,213 cr a year earlier; investments ₹7,540 cr [Screener]. CRISIL: adjusted debt ₹12,515 cr, cash ₹7,465 cr, net debt ₹5,050 cr, net debt/EBITDA 0.6x, interest cover 22.5x [CRISIL Sep-26].
- Net debt/EBITDA 0.5x at March 2026, 0.7x at June 2026 after Assertio [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Management is "comfortable around one-times" and wants more bolt-on specialty deals [Q4 call].
- Cash flow weakened sharply: operating cash flow FY26 ₹2,117 cr vs ₹6,781 cr in FY25; CFO/EBITDA 44%; free cash flow −₹530 cr [Screener]. Causes given: $45 mn Mirabegron settlement, $75 mn prepaid licence, acquisition working capital [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 30].
- Working capital: inventory days 282 (227 in FY25), debtor days 73, cash conversion cycle 212 days (160) [Screener]. CRISIL: gross current assets 188 days, "stretched working capital cycle" listed as a weakness [CRISIL Sep-26].
- Shareholder returns: ₹1,100 cr buyback approved 19 May 2026 at ₹1,150/share (9.57 mn shares); earlier buybacks 2022 (₹750 cr) and 2024 (₹600 cr); dividend payout only 2% in FY26 [AR FY26; Screener].
- Rating: CRISIL AAA/Stable reaffirmed 9 Sep 2026; CRISIL expects net debt/EBITDA below 0.5x by March 2027 and margins of 24–25% [CRISIL Sep-26].

## Moat evidence
- India: grew 20% in Q1 against a market growing mid-teens or less; chronic MAT growth 15.8% vs 12% for the market; beats the market in cardiology, diabetology, oncology, nephrology [Q1 call; IP Aug-26]. Pricing: management says prices in India "generally deflate"; growth is volume-led [Q4 call].
- Own innovation: Saroglitazar and Desidustat (Lipaglyn + Bilypsa ₹481 cr combined) growing 30–45%; world-first Nivolumab biosimilar (Tishtha) at about a quarter of the innovator's price; 16 biosimilars approved in India [Q1 call; IP Aug-26; Q4 call].
- Semaglutide: first to market with a reusable pen; co-marketed with Lupin and Torrent; the three together hold a "very meaningful" share; Zydus own brand #3–4 [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- US: #3 by prescriptions, 6% TRx growth vs 2% for the market (4-yr CAGR); Indocyanine Green launched with 180-day exclusivity [IP Aug-26; Q1 call].
- Saroglitazar NDA for PBC (a rare liver disease) has USFDA priority review; competitors' PBC drugs have raised guidance, which management reads as a bigger market [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **USFDA warning letter at Baddi (dated 2 June 2026)** for testing of purified talc that did not meet USP standards, issued after a records request, not an inspection. The company says supplies are not affected. It was **not mentioned on the Q1 call** and is missing from the stored analysis; CRISIL calls its remediation "monitorable" [News: Business Standard, 3 Jun 2026; CRISIL Sep-26].
- Margin fall is real: Q1 EBITDA down 8% YoY and PAT down ~35%; H2 FY27 adds Saroglitazar launch costs and full Assertio amortisation [Screener; Q1 call].
- Integration load: five acquisitions across the US, UK and France at once; MedTech lost ₹178 cr in FY26 and consumer profit fell [AR FY26 note 44].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter = US legal proceedings (price-fixing multi-district litigation; Zydus is a defendant in five bellwether cases with trials 2026–2028; opioid cases settled for $17.5 mn) [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 30].
- Audit trail: in the Takeda joint venture, the audit trail was not enabled for some changes by users with privileged access [AR FY26 CARO/report on other legal matters].
- Contingent liabilities small: claims ₹39 cr, DPCO (drug price control) demands ₹272 cr, indirect tax ₹266 cr, income tax ₹220 cr [AR FY26 note 30].
- Exceptional items FY26 ₹517 cr: $45 mn Mirabegron settlement (₹398 cr), new labour codes ₹85 cr, Comfort Click deal costs ₹25 cr [AR FY26 note 48].
- Cash conversion fell to 44% of EBITDA; inventory at 282 days [Screener].
- Promoter holding stable at 75.0%; no pledge noted [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "record margins" (Q4 EBITDA 33.7%) to "investment year" (24.1%). The group-revenue promise softened from "high teens" to "strong double-digit" even though Q1 grew 22% [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- India outperformance target raised (200–400 → 300–500 bps). Capex nudged up. A 28–30% long-term margin ambition appeared for the first time [Q1 call].
- New: Saroglitazar priority review; Assertio closed; Nufymco (first US biosimilar) launched; $200–400 mn Saro peak-sales range; Rolvedon $15–20 mn a quarter [Q1 call].
- Dropped: Revlimid and forex talk; nothing said about the Baddi warning letter [Q1 call].

## My read
Zydus is improving as a franchise — India, international markets and US specialty are all growing faster than the old generic base — but FY27 earnings will not grow: Q1 operating profit fell 8% and net profit ~35% as Revlimid and forex gains fell away and acquisition amortisation arrived. The price, at a 52-week high, is paying for the FY28–FY30 story (Saroglitazar launch, US biosimilars in 2029, margin back to 28–30%), none of which is yet in the numbers. Watch three things: whether cash conversion recovers from 44%, the Baddi warning letter closing, and whether H2 FY27 margin holds near 24% with Saro costs. A Saroglitazar approval on time (priority review) would support the view; a slip in approval or a second warning letter would weaken it.
