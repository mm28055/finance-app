## Moat evidence
- Integration story: owning discovery (Housing.com), lead generation (Analytica), CRM (Sell.do, 1,000+ developers, 7% churn) and transactions (PropTiger) is unusual in India; cross-sell still "single digit" % of revenue, 22% of distribution clients use more than one product. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Sell.do shows SaaS stickiness (licences +41% YoY, 7% gross churn). Analytica lead volumes +77%. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Housing.com brand: ~₹2,200cr invested by past owners, ~₹1,000cr on brand; but listing portals in India "have historically not been profitable" and the leader (99acres) is entrenched. [Q1 call]
- Rental: 81% occupancy and 4.3 customer rating, but supply is shrinking and exposed to IT-hiring slowdowns in Pune/Bengaluru. [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- **Profit quality**: "structurally profitable" rests on a continuing PBT of ₹2–4cr a quarter; Q1 headline profit is a one-off building gain. [Q1 call]
- **Housing.com integration**: ~₹200cr burn vs Aurum's small profit; breakeven promises (4–6 quarters) rely on cost cuts at a larger, loss-making, formerly foreign-managed business. [Q1 call]
- **Guidance reliability**: M&A stance reversed within a quarter; SM-REIT dropped from the Q1 script; ₹1,000cr ARR date keeps moving. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Dilution**: two share-swap deals to REA and promoter warrants — equity base up ~37% fully diluted (7.64 cr → 10.49 cr shares). [BSE filing 11 Sep 2026]
- **Related parties**: FY26 borrowed ₹16cr from the promoter company and repaid; ₹18.9cr investment and ₹7.2cr loan outstanding to Integrow Asset Management (a related party); IT services sold to promoter entities; one-day delay in filing half-yearly related-party disclosures (BSE waiver granted). [AR FY26 consolidated RPT note; secretarial report]
- **Auditor**: unmodified opinion; emphasis of matter only on a subsidiary merger with no financial impact. Rental segment still loss-making for FY26. [AR FY26]
- **Shareholding**: promoter 47.9% (no pledge shown); FII 0.4%; 55,000 shareholders, falling. [Screener]

## My read
The legacy businesses have genuinely improved — distribution is profitable and growing fast, rentals are near breakeven, and bank debt is gone — but "profitable" is a stretch: continuing PBT is about ₹2–4cr a quarter, and Q1's big profit was a building sale. Housing.com transforms the story into a turnaround bet: it doubles revenue but brings ~₹200cr a year of losses, paid for with ~20% dilution. The stock (~₹222) is priced on revenue, not earnings, so what matters is whether Housing.com's burn falls quickly after closing and whether PropTiger/Analytica cross-sell shows up in numbers. A clear path to Housing breakeven by FY28 would support the view; slipping timelines, further dilution or rental supply shrinking further would undermine it.
