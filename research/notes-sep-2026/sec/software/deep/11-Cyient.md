# Cyient — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. Figures in ₹ crore (cr) unless stated; $ = US dollars. "DET" = Digital, Engineering & Technology, the core engineering-services segment (excludes semiconductors and the listed manufacturing arm, Cyient DLM). "cc" = constant currency.

## Business and segments
- Cyient is an engineering-services firm for asset-heavy industries (aircraft, rail, telecom networks, utilities, energy plants, mines). The group reports four segments: DET (core services), Cyient DLM (design-led electronics manufacturing, 52.1% owned and separately listed), Cyient Semiconductors (fabless chips, ~98–100% owned) and Others. [IP Q1FY27; Investor Day Aug-26]
- FY26 revenue by segment: DET $657.6M (−0.7% cc), Semiconductors $25.7M, DLM $142.5M. With TAO Digital and Kinetic added, management calls it a "~$1 Bn" revenue base. [Q4 call; Investor Day Aug-26]
- DET verticals, Q1 FY27: Transportation & Mobility (aerospace + rail + auto) $72.7M (~45% of DET, +14.8% YoY cc); Networks & Infrastructure (telecom connectivity + utilities) $53.1M (~33%, +2.5% YoY cc); Strategic Units (mining, energy, healthcare) $36.1M (~22%, −26.2% YoY cc). [IP Q1FY27]
- Geography (DET, Q1 FY27): Americas 52.6%, Europe/Middle East/Africa 24.8%, Asia-Pacific incl. India 22.5%. Americas share is rising (50.9% a year ago). [IP Q1FY27]
- Billing currency (DET): USD 54.0%, EUR 15.5%, GBP 5.2%, AUD 11.1%, others 14.2%. The average rupee was ₹94.71/$ in Q1 FY27 vs ₹85.57 a year earlier — a large tailwind to rupee revenue. [IP Q1FY27]
- Semiconductors: three lines — design services, custom ASIC turnkey (chips designed and sourced for clients at mature nodes, mainly TSMC 180nm), and own-IP power chips (ASSPs). Kinetic Technologies (low-voltage power chips; ~250 products, ~100 patents) bought for ~$85M, closed 8 Apr 2026, 74–83% stake. Q1 FY27 semis revenue $17.9M, of which Kinetic $10.4M and organic $7.5M (+5% QoQ, fifth straight quarter above 5%). [Q1 call; Semis call May-26; Investor Day Aug-26]
- TAO Digital Solutions (US data/AI engineering, 3,500+ staff): enterprise value $218M = $130M upfront + two earnouts over two years tied to EBITDA; CY2025 revenue ~$80M at >20% EBITDA margin; mainly debt-funded. CCI cleared 25 Aug 2026; completed 4 Sep 2026. Management expects $40–50M contribution in FY27 (part year). [News: ScanX, 30 May 2026; Q1 call; CRISIL Jun-26; News: MarketScreener, Sep 2026]
- Capacity/utilisation: DET headcount 14,175 (Q4: 14,236; year ago 13,623); technical staff 13,151. Utilisation is not disclosed. [IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue: FY24 7,147 → FY25 7,360 → FY26 7,268 (−1%); TTM 7,632. Operating margin (OPM) fell every year: 18% → 15% → 12%. Net profit 703 → 648 → 463. [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 1,712 / 13% / 157; Sep-25 1,781 / 12% / 143; Dec-25 1,848 / 13% / 97; Mar-26 1,927 / 12% / 66; Jun-26 2,076 / 13% / 109. Q1 FY27 profit was down 31% YoY despite +21% sales; the sales jump is mostly Kinetic plus a weak rupee. [Screener]
- Other income swung from ₹70cr (Jun-25) to −₹45cr (Mar-26) and ₹7cr (Jun-26) — forex hedge losses and the ₹71cr Project Astro diligence charge hit Q4. [Screener; Q4 call]
- DET Q1 FY27: revenue $162.5M (−0.5% QoQ cc, −0.9% YoY cc) = ₹1,540cr (+10.6% YoY in rupees). Normalised EBIT 13.2% (+79 bps QoQ, +114 bps YoY); normalised PAT ₹141cr (−13.5% YoY; year-ago had one-off forex gains). Gross margin 32.7% under a new, restated definition. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Group Q1 FY27: $219M (+9.1% YoY cc), ₹2,076cr; normalised EBIT 9.7%; normalised PAT ₹114cr, reported ₹104cr. Semis EBIT margin −21.0% (Kinetic −17.7%). [IP Q1FY27]
- Unit economics: DET revenue per employee ≈ $162.5M × 4 ÷ 14,175 ≈ $46k a year — typical of Indian ER&D (engineering R&D) mid-caps. Some clients accepted price increases in FY26. [IP Q1FY27, derived; Q4 call]
- Return on capital has halved: ROCE 22% (FY24) → 17% → 12% (FY26); ROE 8.6%. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (23 Apr 2026): "aspiring for mid- to high single-digit organic growth" in FY27; 15% DET EBIT margin by Q4 FY27 reaffirmed; semis break-even "late FY27/early FY28". [Q4 call; Semis call May-26]
- Q1 FY27 call (23 Jul 2026): "there was no guidance… we said we were aiming towards a mid-single" digit growth, and "that will be challenged given the mathematics". 15% EBIT pushed to H1 FY28; semis break-even pushed to FY28. Effective tax 27–28% for the year. [Q1 call]
- Investor Day (25 Aug 2026): ~15% DET EBIT is "a structural run-rate ambition, not a guided figure… internal planning currently assumes it is reached around FY28". Capital policy: payout up to 50% of PAT, debt up to 1.0x leverage, capex 1.0–1.5% of revenue, acquisitions must clear >15% ROIC with a "kill switch at >30% timeline slip". [Investor Day Aug-26]
- Verdict: growth guidance **cut** (mid-to-high single digit → mid-single, unlikely), margin target **delayed** two to three quarters. Delivery on capital allocation (buyback, semis fund-raise) **met**. Energy turnaround promised "next 2–3 quarters" at both calls — **not yet delivered** (Strategic Units −8.2% QoQ in Q1 after −12.4% in Q4). [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- DET capex is light: ₹25cr in Q1 FY27 (₹17cr Q4, ₹18cr year ago), raised by an IT refresh. Target 1.0–1.5% of revenue. [IP Q1FY27; Investor Day Aug-26]
- The real capital calls are acquisitions: Kinetic ~$85M (debt in a Singapore entity, <3% cost), TAO $130M upfront plus earnouts (US-dollar debt at SOFR + spread), and high-voltage chip R&D that will "consume cash for the next four, five quarters". [Semis call May-26; Q1 call]
- Cyient Semiconductors raised ₹300cr from EAAA (₹200cr debt + ₹100cr equity) at a $500M post-money valuation (≈6–7x revenue per its CFO); Cyient has committed up to $100M in total, ~$30M invested. [Semis call May-26; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- DET net cash ₹1,644cr at 30 Jun 2026 (cash and treasury ₹1,655cr, debt ₹12cr). Group gross cash ₹1,909cr. The ₹720cr buyback was paid after 30 June, and TAO's $130M (~₹1,230cr) upfront is mostly debt-funded, so the group moves to a modest net-debt position in Q2. An analyst estimated total debt near $250M after TAO; management did not dispute it. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Semis debt: ~$80M foreign (Kinetic) + ₹200cr India = ~$100M on the semis balance sheet. [Semis call May-26]
- Screener borrowings ₹431cr (Mar-26) vs ₹788cr (Mar-24). Debtor days 66 (FY26), working-capital days up to 68 from 43 in FY24. CFO steady at ₹726cr / ₹790cr / ₹787cr (FY24–26). [Screener]
- DET free cash flow: ₹114cr in Q1 (80.5% of normalised PAT), ₹225cr in Q4 (163%); five-year average >114% of PAT. Some receivables are factored without recourse. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; Investor Day Aug-26; AR FY26 note 36]
- Returns: ₹16 dividend for FY26 plus a ₹720cr tender buyback at ₹1,125 (6.4M shares, 5.76% of capital); promoters did not tender, lifting promoter holding to 24.70%. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- CRISIL AA/Stable reaffirmed Jun-26: net worth ₹6,100cr+, gearing 0.07x, interest cover ~18x; downgrade trigger is margins sustained below 12% or heavy debt-funded M&A. [CRISIL Jun-26]

## Moat evidence
- Deep, long-tenure OEM relationships: selected as one of three strategic suppliers in a global rail OEM's vendor consolidation; won a supplier consolidation at a mid-size airframe maker; multi-year MRB engineering deal with a US aerospace firm; several aero-engine OEM engagements. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Pricing: "Some of our existing customers have also backed us on price increases this year." [Q4 call]
- Client depth: 4 clients above $20M, 16 above $10M, 27 above $5M; top-5 clients 35.1% of DET, top-10 48.2%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Order intake $168.2M in Q1 (+5.3% YoY); new-business intake +64% YoY; 5 large deals in two quarters; large-deal pipeline >$300M (highest in 12 quarters). Converted orders are ~75% consumed within 9 months. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Weakness: the energy decline was one large project ending, and a larger peer keeps winning in the same vertical — evidence the moat in plant engineering is thin. [Q1 call]
- CRISIL flags modest scale as limiting ability to bid for large, complex deals. [CRISIL Jun-26]

## Risks and red flags
- Organic growth stalled: DET −0.7% cc in FY26 and −0.9% YoY cc in Q1 FY27; the Strategic Units cluster fell 12.2% in FY26 and 26.2% YoY in Q1. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Rising client concentration: top-5 share up from 30.6% to 35.1% in a year; $1M+ clients fell from 99 to 94. [IP Q1FY27]
- AI risk: management itself paused the "Project Astro" acquisition after ₹71cr of diligence costs, citing "how rapidly AI has evolved". Pivot now is to "lifecycle engineering" and an agentic-AI MRO platform. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- M&A and leverage: two acquisitions in six months (Kinetic, TAO ≈ $300M+ combined) take the group from net cash to net debt; TAO earnouts are contingent payments; Kinetic amortisation ~$3M a quarter. Goodwill was ₹1,972cr at Mar-26 before both deals (a key audit matter). [Q1 call; AR FY26 KAM; CRISIL Jun-26]
- Semiconductor losses: EBIT −21% of revenue in Q1; break-even slipped from "late FY27" to FY28. [IP Q1FY27; Semis call May-26; Q1 call]
- Macro: West Asia war delaying discretionary programs; management flagged risk to airline flying hours hitting aerospace aftermarket work. [Q1 call]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matters are goodwill and investment valuation, fixed-price revenue recognition, receivables and inventories. Audit trail operated all year. Contingent liabilities ₹82cr (mainly ₹55cr service tax, ₹15cr GST) — small. No pledge of promoter shares found. [AR FY26]
- Management churn: CFO changed Apr 2026 (Prabhakar Atla → COO; Shrinivas Kulkarni CFO); CEO Sukamal Banerjee ~18 months in; new Chief Growth Officer Feb 2026. [Q4 call]
- Tax rate up to 29.2% in Q1 (27–28% guided). [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (Q4: revenue miss "not structural", Astro paused, buyback) to "offense" (Q1: TAO deal, MRO platform, semis fund-raise closed).
- Numbers: DET revenue fell again (−2.4% cc in Q4, −0.5% in Q1) but EBIT rose from 12.4% to 13.2%. Networks & Infrastructure recovered (+0.3% QoQ); Strategic Units worsened.
- Growth aspiration trimmed; 15% EBIT delayed to H1 FY28; semis break-even to FY28.
- New risk: aerospace flying hours; delayed restarts of already-won connectivity programs; renewals of project work slipped.

## My read
The core services business is not growing: DET has been flat to down in constant currency for five quarters, and margins are being held up by cost cuts and a much weaker rupee rather than volume. Management is buying growth (Kinetic, TAO) and building a loss-making chip unit, which shifts Cyient from a net-cash services company to a leveraged mix of services, chips and manufacturing — more optionality, but lower visibility. At the current price the market pays for a recovery to high-single-digit growth and ~15% EBIT by FY28; that needs the energy cluster to stop falling and TAO to integrate cleanly. What would change the view: two quarters of positive DET cc growth with Strategic Units flat, or the opposite — a TAO earnout dispute or another cut to the margin timeline.
