# Intellect Design Arena — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. Note: the company reports "total income" (operating revenue **plus other income**) and EBITDA on that base; Screener shows operating revenue and operating profit excluding other income.

## Business and segments
- Banking and insurance software products: **eMACH.ai** (composable platform for corporate/transaction banking — iGTB; core/consumer banking and lending — iGCB; wealth, custody, capital markets and insurance) and **Purple Fabric** (enterprise AI platform, carved out as a separate unit). 500+ clients in ~60 countries. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Business units by size: Wholesale/transaction banking the oldest and largest; Consumer Banking "crossed ₹1,000 Crore" in FY26; the wealth/capital-markets/insurance unit "crossed ₹500 Crore". Unit margins not disclosed; management says iGTB runs well above the group and will approach Oracle Flexcube-type margins in 2–3 years. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Revenue types, Q1 FY27: license-linked revenue ₹457cr (+17% YoY) = license ₹166cr (+28%), platform/SaaS ₹141cr (+12%), AMC (annual maintenance) ₹150cr (+12%); balance is implementation services. LTM: license ₹554cr, platform ₹595cr (roughly double a year ago), AMC ₹585cr. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Geography: North America is now "one of our most important growth markets", nearing ~$100M a year after the Central 1 (Canada credit-union digital banking) asset takeover in Mar 2025; also Europe, Middle East, India, APAC and Africa. CRISIL: >50% of revenue from BFSI. [Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Jun-26; AR FY26]
- Scale: ~6,000+ staff (analyst figure, not disputed), including a 1,200-person research team; revenue per employee ~₹50 lakh vs ₹1–2.5cr at global peers. [Q1 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue FY24 2,506 → FY25 2,500 → FY26 3,038 (+22%); OPM 22% → 21% → 19%; net profit 323 → 334 → 343 — profit has been flat for five years (₹350cr in FY22). [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 702 / 20% / 94; Sep-25 758 / 20% / 102; Dec-25 731 / 14% / 27; Mar-26 847 / 22% / 120; Jun-26 845 / 20% / 102. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (company): total income ₹872cr (+19% YoY); EBITDA ₹194cr (+10%); PBT ₹135cr (+7%); PAT ₹102cr (+8%); gross margin ₹494cr (~57% of total income); collections ₹763cr (+30%). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- FY26 (company): EBITDA ₹703cr (+16%); "non-GAAP" EBITDA excluding stock compensation ₹756cr; license-linked ₹1,667cr (+34%); platform ₹580cr (+141%). [Q4 call]
- Costs: quarterly cost base rose from ~₹550cr to ₹677cr (Q1) after Purple Fabric investment (~₹72cr in Q4) and Central 1 (near-zero margin). Management: "we will not come back to a 550 crore cost base". [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- R&D: ₹160cr capitalised and ₹235cr expensed in FY26; FY27 R&D budget ₹180–200cr. Capital work-in-progress (products under development) ₹298cr. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- Unit economics: at a ₹700cr cost base, each extra ₹100cr of revenue drops largely to EBITDA — "If 900 crores… and my cost… 700 crores, it will be 250 crores EBITDA". [Q1 call]

## Deal wins / pipeline
- Q1 FY27: 19 strategic wins, 16 go-lives; 61 wins on a 12-month basis; pipeline ₹13,012cr; 101 "Destiny" deals (>₹20cr each: 29 above ₹50cr, 34 at ₹30–50cr, 38 at ₹20–30cr); 7 Destiny deals won, 4 lost in the quarter. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Discrepancy: the Chief Revenue Officer said the pipeline grew "15% year on year"; the presentation shows **+6% YoY**. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Wins: a large Mexican bank (CBX corporate banking), six Canadian credit unions, a top-5 European bank's asset-servicing arm, UAE and Saudi transaction-banking renewals, three Indian custody deals, Diamond Trust Bank (East Africa) lending. ~80–85% of business comes from existing customers. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- AI: FY26 AI (Purple Fabric) revenue ~₹160cr, order book >$50M, on ₹92cr of investment. [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (May 2026): no formal guidance; "designing for 20%" growth but "whether we achieve 15%, 14%, or 12%… time will tell"; EBITDA margin band 20–25% over time; milestone of holding ₹800cr+ quarterly for 2–3 quarters, then ₹900cr; long-standing target **₹4,000cr revenue / ₹1,000cr EBITDA by FY28**. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: total income ₹872cr (held above ₹800cr — **met**), but down from ₹884cr in Q4; EBITDA margin 22.2% of total income vs 25% in Q4 (**softer**). Platform revenue fell QoQ (₹141cr vs higher Q4, blamed on year-end usage true-ups). [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 call: CMD agreed analysts "can assume" EBITDA margin improves in FY27 vs FY26; "every three quarters, 100 crores" of added quarterly revenue; next milestone ₹900cr a quarter; ₹4,000cr/₹1,000cr by FY28 reaffirmed. [Q1 call]
- Reality check: ₹4,000cr in FY28 needs ~15% a year from FY26's ₹3,038cr (feasible); ₹1,000cr EBITDA needs 25% margins vs 22–23% now (stretch). Verdict: targets **kept**; delivery **mixed**.

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is mostly capitalised product development (~₹160cr a year) plus modest physical capex; net block ~₹450cr per CMD. Capital commitments ₹12cr at Mar-26. [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 35]
- Payback claim: "for each ₹100 Crore we invest in R&D, we generate ₹1000 Crore over the next 10 years" — management assertion, not verifiable. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and equivalents ₹1,269cr at 30 Jun 2026 (+30% YoY); borrowings ₹159cr (FY26), mostly leases; effectively net cash ~₹1,100cr. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Working capital is the weak spot: DSO 124 days (excluding contractually-not-due amounts) vs 126 at FY26 and 137 a year earlier; CRISIL puts the gross current-asset cycle at 262 days (up from 226). Large unbilled revenue on license/implementation contracts. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; CRISIL Jun-26]
- CFO ₹510cr in FY26 (₹431cr FY25) ≈ 1.5x net profit, but ~₹270cr a year goes into capitalised R&D and capex. [Screener]
- Dividend FY26: ₹4 final + ₹3 special = ₹7 (payout ~28%); yield ~0.6%. [Q4 call; Screener]
- CRISIL A+ reaffirmed, outlook raised to **Positive** (Jun 2026); unencumbered cash ₹1,257cr; downgrade trigger is margins below 15%. [CRISIL Jun-26]

## Moat evidence
- Ranked #1 in the IBSi Global Sales League Table 2026 across 8 banking-technology categories; Gartner Magic Quadrant leader (payment hubs), Forrester notable vendor (AI platforms). [IP Q1FY27]
- High switching costs: core/transaction banking platforms run for a decade-plus; AMC revenue ₹585cr a year is sticky. [IP Q1FY27]
- IP: 528 patents cited for Purple Fabric; ~2 million R&D hours a year. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Weakness: scale is modest vs Temenos/Oracle/FIS; CRISIL notes "intense competition in the BFSI vertical"; some large banks now try to build in-house with LLM coding tools — CMD: "few banks will succeed, but they will be spending more money". [CRISIL Jun-26; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Profit has not grown: ₹350cr (FY22) → ₹343cr (FY26) despite revenue up 62%, as reinvestment absorbs gains; stock down ~49% from its 52-week high. [Screener]
- Revenue lumpiness and accounting judgement: auditor's key audit matters are revenue on license/implementation contracts (multiple performance obligations, percentage-of-completion) and capitalisation of product development — both areas where profit can be moved between periods. Unmodified opinion; audit trail operated all year. [AR FY26]
- Receivables: 124–126 days DSO plus contractually-not-due unbilled amounts; any bank-client dispute or delayed go-live ties up cash. [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Jun-26]
- Disclosure: management declined quarterly unit margins and pushes investors to 12-month numbers; pipeline growth quoted inconsistently (15% vs 6%). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- AI disruption of product development and of client IT budgets — the CMD himself said a 60% cut in development effort "is a major risk for the industry". [Q1 call]
- Contingent liabilities: only tax proceedings, amounts not material; bank guarantees ₹13cr (fully cash-backed). Promoter holding 29.6% (slowly declining). No pledge disclosed. [AR FY26 note 35; Screener]
- Governance/people: Chief Assurance and Governance Officer retired 31 May 2026; four senior hires in Q1. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: record quarter (₹884cr total income, 25% EBITDA), triumphant tone, ₹4,000/₹1,000cr target restated.
- Q1: small sequential dip; platform revenue down QoQ; CMD combative ("your questions are on the lag indicators"); new emphasis on internal AI (a 78-project pilot showing ~60% lower development effort, "SDLC to AI-DLC").
- New risk named: banks building in-house with LLM tools. New positive: Middle East wins recovered after the West Asia war; collections +30%.

## My read
Intellect's products are winning — license-linked revenue grew 34% in FY26 and platform revenue doubled — but a much larger cost base (Purple Fabric, Central 1, R&D) means profit has stood still for five years and working capital stays heavy. The stock has halved, so the price now needs only mid-teens growth with a modest margin recovery; if the cost base really stays near ₹700cr a quarter while income climbs to ₹900cr+, EBITDA and profit could rise 25–30%. What would change the view: two quarters above ₹900cr total income with EBITDA ≥ 24% (positive), or DSO rising back above 135 days / another cost step-up without revenue (negative).
