# CE Info Systems (MapmyIndia) — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. "Map-led" = maps, navigation, APIs, GIS/digital-twin software; "IoT-led" = vehicle trackers/dash-cams plus the fleet software sold with them.
Transcript check: the stored Q1 FY27 transcript is the official BSE filing. The stored Q4 FY26 transcript is AI-generated (Tijori); it was checked against the official BSE transcript (call 20 May 2026, filed 25 May 2026).

## Business and segments
- Product split Q1 FY27: Map-led ₹98.7cr (71%), EBITDA ₹50.7cr (51.4%); IoT-led ₹41.1cr (29%), EBITDA ₹5.4cr (13.1%). A year earlier: Map-led ₹98.2cr at 54.8%, IoT-led ₹23.4cr at 8.7%. [IP Q1FY27]
- FY26 product split: Map-led ₹315.5cr (66.5%), EBITDA ₹149.5cr (47.4%); IoT-led ₹158.6cr (+35%), EBITDA ₹26.0cr (16%, from 14%); SaaS was 66% of IoT revenue (54% in FY25); 1.8 lakh new IoT devices sold or rented in FY26. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- Hardware vs services Q1 FY27: hardware ₹23.1cr (all IoT; ₹7.1cr a year ago), services ₹116.6cr. IoT services (the recurring part) ₹18.0cr vs ₹16.3cr — only +10% YoY while IoT hardware tripled. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- New customer segments from Q1 FY27 (restated for 9 quarters): Automotive ₹58.8cr (42%, +29% YoY), Enterprise ₹64.2cr (46%, +6%), Government ₹16.7cr (12%, +11%). FY26: Auto ₹189.8cr, Enterprise ₹174.4cr, Government ₹109.9cr; FY25: ₹181.7cr / ₹178.2cr / ₹103.4cr. [IP Q1FY27]
- Revenue model: map/software/platform subscriptions and licences (MaaS/SaaS/PaaS), device sale or device-as-a-service, and solutions/system integration; priced per vehicle, per API call, per user or per solution. No ARR, customer count or churn is disclosed. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Group: Mappls DT (100%, government/defence/digital twin), Gtropy (~96%, IoT/telematics), Kogo Tech Labs (40.2% associate, AI), PT Terra Link Technologies (40% JV, Southeast Asia). International revenue and JV losses "not material". [AR FY26; Q1 call]
- India-only in practice; supplies Maruti, Hyundai and Mahindra ("We are the supplier there"); powers Amazon Now's quick-commerce app. [Q1 call]

## Key numbers
- Screener (consolidated) annual: revenue FY24 ₹379cr → FY25 ₹463cr → FY26 ₹474cr (+2%); OPM 41% → 38% → 36%; net profit ₹134cr → ₹148cr → ₹134cr (−9%); other income ₹52cr a year (about a third of pre-tax profit). [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 122/54/45%/46; Sep-25 114/26/23%/19; Dec-25 94/25/26%/19; Mar-26 145/64/44%/51; Jun-26 140/55/40%/50. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹139.7cr (+14.9%), EBITDA ₹56.1cr (+0.4%; margin 40.2% vs 45.9%), PAT ₹49.7cr (+8.6%). [IP Q1FY27]
- The ₹4cr government write-off: charged in other expenses (hits EBITDA ~2.9 pts consolidated, ~4 pts on Map-led), while a ₹3.2cr back-to-back payable no longer due went to other income — net P&L hit ₹0.8cr. Management said EBITDA would have been "43% plus" without it. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Margin also fell from mix: hardware at 16.5% of revenue (5.8% a year ago). [IP Q1FY27, derived]
- Five-year CAGR since IPO (per chairman): revenue 24%, EBITDA 19%, PAT 11%. Screener 3-year profit CAGR 2%. [Q4 call; Screener]

## Order book (product company equivalent of TCV)
- Open order book ₹1,754.4cr at Mar-26 (+17%); new orders ₹785.4cr (+24%); billed ₹531.0cr. FY25: opening ₹1,372.5cr, new ₹633.5cr, billed ₹504.6cr. Of the book, fixed-price contracts ₹492.2cr; rest is volume-based (depends on cars built/APIs used). [AR FY26 MD&A]
- The book **includes 18% GST**, so it is ~₹1,487cr in revenue terms; management says it covers "3–4 years" — i.e. ~₹370–500cr a year, roughly today's revenue, not 25% growth. [Q4 call; AR FY26; derived]
- Conversion: ~18% of the opening book became revenue in FY26 (~₹270cr) plus ~₹200cr from in-year orders; analysts noted conversion had slid from 21–22% (FY22) to ~13% (FY25). [Q4 call]
- Government open book "significantly more than ₹200cr"; FY26 government billing and collections each crossed ₹100cr. [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- Q1 wins: 2-wheeler OEM renewal, export-maps programme for an existing car OEM, AI battlefield-awareness platform (defence), state PSU GIS platform, bus-booking video telematics, NBFC quick-commerce, solar company. No values given; order book not updated quarterly. [IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (20 May 2026): no formal guidance, but the chairman said "there is no reason not to believe that we cannot grow at 25% in the FY27", adding that "actual versus potential can vary"; ₹1,000cr revenue roadmap (set in 2023 for FY28) "has not changed", only timing; IoT margin to rise above FY26's 16%; government to see "significant growth"; a ~₹20cr auto IoT order and a large state emergency-response order pushed "to Q1 and Q2". Also claimed zero government bad debts. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +14.9% — **behind** the 25% hint; EBITDA 40.2% — **ahead** of the standing "35% plus for the whole year" target (reiterated on the Q1 call); IoT margin 13.1% — **below** FY26's 16% (though up YoY); Government +11% but only ₹16.7cr (seasonally weak Q1); Map-led flat YoY — the pushed-out orders did **not** show clearly; first government write-off (₹4cr) **broke** the "no write-off" claim. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- The ₹1,000cr target and the 25% number were not repeated on the Q1 call; Rohan Verma declined quantitative targets ("I'm not going to talk about quantitative target"). Treat both as effectively dropped. [Q1 call]
- Reporting changed from A&M / C&E to Automotive / Enterprise / Government, with 9 quarters restated — this is fuller disclosure, not less (contrary to the stored analysis). [IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capital allocation ~₹120cr "for organic growth": IoT devices on rent (capitalised, depreciated over 3 years), government working capital, and IP creation. Depreciation rose from ~₹4–5cr to ₹8–9cr a quarter. [Q4 call; AR FY26; Screener]
- Small investments: IwayPlus (₹2cr, 6.06%, indoor navigation), Prashanth Advanced Survey LLP (₹2cr, 20%, HD maps/LiDAR). [AR FY26]
- IoT is a "hardware first, SaaS later" model: device revenue now at low margin, subscription later — so capex and inventory rise before recurring revenue. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and financial investments ₹745cr at Jun-26 (₹685cr Mar-26); borrowings ₹7cr — effectively debt-free; about 15% of market value is cash. No credit rating (no debt instruments). [IP Q1FY27; Screener; AR FY26]
- Working capital worsening: debtor days 76 (FY23) → 101 → 105 → 136 (FY26); receivables ₹176cr vs ₹133cr; inventory days 119; working-capital days −1 (FY23) → 93 (FY26). Management says "majority, but not a large majority" of receivables are government. [Screener; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Cash from operations: FY24 ₹76cr, FY25 ₹117cr, FY26 ₹93cr — only ~0.7× FY26 net profit; free cash flow ₹59cr. [Screener]
- Bank guarantees ₹35.5cr (₹29.3cr FY25), backed by fixed deposits. [AR FY26 note 38]
- Dividend FY26 ₹3.50/share (payout ~14%); no buyback; priority is organic growth. [Q4 call; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- 30 years of proprietary Indian map data; AI used 5+ years to update maps. Supplier to India's three largest car makers; Automotive revenue grew from ₹26cr (Q1 FY25) to ₹59cr (Q1 FY27). [Q1 call]
- Map-led EBITDA margin 47–55% shows pricing power in the core data/API business. [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27]
- Embedded in customer apps and car dashboards — switching means re-integrating maps and APIs; but Enterprise grew only 6% and the core (non-IoT, non-government) enterprise API business was estimated by an analyst at ~₹80–85cr for 3–4 years, which management did not rebut with numbers. [Q4 call]
- Strategic holders: PhonePe 13.7%, Zenrin (Japan's map maker) 3.4%. [IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- Growth stall: FY26 revenue +2% and profit −9%; two very weak quarters (Q2–Q3 FY26 OPM 23–26%). A large auto OEM (named Hyundai on the Q4 call) chose not to fit the technology in some vehicles from H2 FY26. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Mix shift to IoT hardware and government projects lowers margin and raises working capital; first government write-off in Q1 FY27; management now "calibrated" on government deals after seeing "bad receivables" at peers. [Q1 call]
- Accounting presentation: the ₹4cr write-off was partly offset through other income, flattering PAT relative to EBITDA; other income (₹58cr TTM) is a large share of profit. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Contingent: new GST demand ₹8.05cr on input tax credit (FY26); company contesting, no provision. [AR FY26 note 38]
- Auditor: key audit matter on revenue including receivables and contract assets (estimates on fixed-price/multi-element contracts). No qualification seen. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Family-run: Rakesh Verma (chairman/MD), Rashmi Verma (whole-time director, CTO & CHRO), son Rohan Verma made Joint MD from 1 Jul 2026 at ₹1.5cr a year. Small related-party lease to Rashmi Verma (≤₹8.4 lakh). [AR FY26]
- Disclosure: no ARR, customer count, churn, top-customer share or order-book split by segment; management declined to break down order book or pricing ("competitive reasons"). [Q1 call]
- FIIs cut holdings from 4.44% (Mar-25) to 1.52% (Jun-26); stock is ~56% below its 52-week high. [Screener]

## My read
The core map business is excellent (≈50% EBITDA margins, embedded with top carmakers) but has barely grown for two years, and the recent growth comes from low-margin IoT hardware and slow-paying government projects that are stretching receivables to 136 days. The order book sounds large but, once GST and the 3–4 year span are stripped out, it roughly covers current revenue, not the 25% growth once hinted at; management has quietly stopped repeating the ₹1,000cr target. The price, after a ~56% fall, needs mid-teens growth with margins held above 35% — achievable only if Automotive keeps compounding and IoT subscription revenue (not just devices) starts growing. Upgrades: IoT services growing >25%, receivable days falling, a large government order converting; downgrades: another write-off, OPM below 35%, or FY27 growth under 10%.
