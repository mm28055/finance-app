# Sonata Software — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated; $ = US dollars; "cc" = constant currency; "GC" = gross contribution (the domestic resale business's gross profit).

## Business and segments
- Two very different businesses in one company. **International IT services** (~27% of FY26 revenue but ~two-thirds of profit): Microsoft-stack modernisation, cloud, data, Dynamics ERP/CRM, now repositioned as "engineering the AI enterprise". **Domestic products & platforms** (Sonata Information Technology Ltd, 100% subsidiary): resale of Microsoft, AWS and other cloud/software licences in India plus managed services — ~73% of revenue at ~3% gross contribution. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Ind-Ra 2024]
- International FY26: $328.4M (−2.1% YoY), ₹2,948cr; EBITDA before other income/forex 18.4% (17.0% in FY25); PAT ₹292.7cr. Domestic FY26: revenue ₹7,772cr (+5.9%), GC ₹288.7cr (−3.5%), PAT ₹171.7cr (−3.6%). [Q4 call]
- International verticals, Q1 FY27: TMT 34% (36% in Q4), Retail/Manufacturing/Distribution 32%, BFSI 16% (13%), Healthcare & Life Sciences 15%, emerging 3%. Service mix: Cloud 62% (53%), Dynamics 21%, Data 11%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Microsoft is central on both sides: 30+ year partnership, "Copilot agents and platform engineering Depth Partner" (one of ~40 firms invited globally); domestic resale dominated by Microsoft licences. The largest international client is a TMT account (not named on the call); leadership there was replaced in Q1 to revive growth. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Delivery: 6,293 staff (6,283 in Q4); utilisation 88.5% (91.8% in Q4 — a peak); onsite/offshore 30:70 (32:68); attrition 13%. [Q1 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue FY24 8,613 → FY25 10,157 → FY26 10,701; OPM 8% → 7% → 7%; net profit 308 → 425 → 464. [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 2,965 / 5% / 109; Sep-25 2,119 / 8% / 120; Dec-25 3,081 / 6% / 104; Mar-26 2,536 / 8% / 130; Jun-26 3,279 / 5% / 108. Swings in sales are domestic licence-renewal timing (Q1 and Q3 heavy). [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 International: $82.0M (+0.1% QoQ cc, +2.1% YoY cc; −0.5% QoQ reported); ₹777cr (+11% YoY); EBITDA 15.4% vs 20.2% in Q4 (−480 bps); PAT ₹62.2cr incl. ₹6.8cr forex loss (Q4: ₹84.2cr incl. ₹21.7cr forex gain). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Margin bridge (International, Q1): ~2 pts from a one-time compensation benefit in Q4 not repeating; utilisation hit from a delayed large-deal ramp-up; AI talent/advisory investment; ~0.5 pt cross-currency loss; partly offset by higher offshore mix. [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 Domestic: revenue ₹2,506cr (+42% QoQ seasonal, +10.2% YoY); GC ₹78.5cr (+4.2% QoQ, +14.5% YoY); PAT ₹45.9cr. [Q1 call]
- Consolidated Q1 FY27 PAT ₹108.6cr (−17% QoQ, −1% YoY); forex swing cost ₹35.4cr vs Q4. Tax rate ~21% this quarter due to a one-off US state R&D credit; normal ~25%. [Q1 call]
- Unit economics: International revenue per employee ≈ $82M × 4 ÷ ~6,000 ≈ $55k a year; Domestic earns ~3% on resale. [Q1 call, derived]
- Returns high because the resale business needs almost no capital: ROCE 30.7%, ROE 28.2%; reported International ROCE only 14.9% (goodwill from Quant). [Screener; Q1 call]

## Deal wins / TCV and clients
- International order book $97.4M in Q1 (book-to-bill 1.18x vs 1.16x in Q4); AI order book $21.4M (18.2% of total, up from $16.9M); AI-led pipeline $340M (+21% QoQ); 21% of engagements now output-based. [Q1 call]
- Wins: a multi-year retail/cloud modernisation deal with a global beverage brand (800+ locations, 11 countries); a multi-year digital-wallet modernisation for a global payments company (ramp-up delayed, now completed). Only one "key deal" in Q1; 7 new logos. [Q1 call]
- Client concentration: top-10 51% of International revenue (54% in Q4) — management attributes the drop to faster growth in clients 11–20, but declined to show absolute top-10 revenue; $5M+ clients 12, $3–5M 9, $10M+ run-rate 8; $1M+ count has been falling for several quarters. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Domestic: one customer above 10% of the subsidiary's revenue (₹990cr in FY26; two customers, ₹1,778cr, in FY25) — this is the large account partly lost to Microsoft direct billing. [AR FY26, SITL notes]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (11 May 2026): CEO Samir Dhir left after declining to extend his contract; Rajsekhar Datta Roy (30+ years at Sonata) became CEO of International. CFO: International EBITDA "endeavour will be to maintain EBITDA at a similar level" (FY26 18.4%, Q4 20.2%); utilisation may ease from peak. Domestic: "regular growth during this financial year", no further direct-billing hit expected. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: International EBITDA 15.4% — **missed** "similar level" by 3–5 pts; revenue flat (**weak**). Domestic returned to double-digit YoY GC growth (**met**, earlier than feared) after retaining renewals "in the probable list for possible direct billing". [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 call: no margin range ("I do not want that will become a guidance"); "positive traction every quarter" in EBITDA from Q2; top-5 clients "stability… growth back… in a couple of more quarters"; domestic growth momentum to continue. [Q1 call]
- Verdict: International margin guidance **dropped to qualitative**; revenue outlook "gradual improvement over the medium-to-long term" — effectively **cut** vs earlier growth ambitions.

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is small (IT services and resale); main capital use has been acquisitions (Quant Systems, US, bought 2023 with $75M of US-subsidiary term debt and contingent consideration of ~₹1,000cr paid over FY25–FY26). Goodwill ₹1,268cr at Mar-26. [Ind-Ra 2024; AR FY26 KAM]
- Capacity is not a constraint: utilisation 88.5% leaves room; investment now goes into ~100 "forward-deployed engineers", a Chief AI Officer (Hari Rebala) and the "Workbench" agentic delivery platform. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Gross cash ~₹567cr; borrowings ~₹500cr → net cash ~₹65cr (third straight quarter net positive, ~₹30cr in Q4). Screener borrowings ₹723cr at Mar-26 (incl. leases). [Q1 call; Screener]
- CFO ₹538cr in FY26 (₹644cr FY25, ₹281cr FY24); negative working-capital days (−16) because domestic resale collects from clients before paying Microsoft and others. DSO: International 60 days, Domestic 65 days (seasonal, 47 in Q4). [Screener; Q1 call]
- Parent guarantees: to domestic subsidiary suppliers up to $40M, and to banks for the North America subsidiary's loans ($42.5M + $35M). [AR FY26 contingent-liability note]
- Dividends: payout 47% in FY26 (final ₹4.15/share); interim 125% declared in Q1; yield ~2.8%. [Screener; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Credit rating: Ind-Ra IND AA-/Stable (last rationale read dates from 2024; noted strengths in business profile, weaknesses in working-capital swings and "moderate geographical and customer concentration"). [Ind-Ra 2024]

## Moat evidence
- Deep Microsoft relationship (Copilot Depth Partner, Fabric featured partner) gives deal flow and a domestic licensing franchise with 30-year relationships. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Domestic stickiness: after Microsoft moved some large Indian customers to direct billing, Sonata won back "two to three customers who were in discussion regarding direct billing" and SMB/corporate-segment business grew ~82% YoY. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Weakness: International growth has been negative-to-flat for two years ($328M in FY26 vs ~$335M in FY25); competes against larger Microsoft partners; pricing power not evident. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Partner dependence**: Microsoft can (and did) take large Indian customers direct — a structural threat to the domestic profit pool (~37% of FY26 PAT). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Largest-client exposure** in TMT has shrunk for ~6 quarters; revival depends on new leadership and AI work. [Q1 call]
- **AI deflation**: legacy app-development/testing work at tech clients is exposed; management pivots to output-based pricing (21% of engagements), which shifts delivery risk onto Sonata — the Q1 margin miss came partly from a delayed ramp and paperwork on such a deal. [Q1 call]
- **Leadership churn**: CEO exit in May 2026; new CEO, new Chief AI Officer, new head for largest account. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Tax disputes**: consolidated contingent liabilities include ~₹365cr of disputed income-tax demands (old section 10A and inter-company charge disallowances, 2001–2011, several with favourable tribunal orders) plus ~₹25cr service tax — ~19% of net worth. Auditor unmodified; key audit matters: goodwill impairment and fixed-price revenue recognition. [AR FY26]
- **Disclosure**: management declined to give absolute top-10 revenue or a margin range. [Q1 call]
- Forex: cross-currency and hedge swings moved PAT by ₹35cr between Q4 and Q1. [Q1 call]
- Promoter holding 28.2% (flat); FIIs cut from 15% (Sep-23) to 9.2%. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: defensive — CEO exit, "continuity", domestic direct-billing damage, International margins at a peak 20.2%.
- Q1: offensive AI narrative (six value pools, nine micro-verticals, Workbench platform, Chief AI Officer) but International EBITDA fell to 15.4%, utilisation dropped 3.3 pts, and management stopped giving a margin number. Domestic recovered faster than expected.
- New issues: delayed large-deal ramp; forex losses; declining $1M+ client count questioned.

## My read
The International business — the part that earns the valuation — is stuck: dollar revenue has been flat to down for two years, the largest client is shrinking, and Q1 margins fell sharply just after management promised stability. The domestic resale arm is steadier than feared but is structurally hostage to Microsoft's channel policy. At a P/E in the mid-teens with a ~2.8% yield the price assumes little growth; it needs International to return to mid-single-digit dollar growth and ~18% EBITDA to re-rate. What would change the view: two quarters of International EBITDA back above 18% with top-5 clients growing (positive), or another Microsoft direct-billing move in India or a further drop in the top TMT client (negative).
