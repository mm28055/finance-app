## Business and segments
- Indore-based mid-size IT services firm (~1,800 people) doing AI-led custom software engineering, Salesforce and ServiceNow implementation, and product engineering for large enterprises; focus verticals BFSI and storage/virtualisation. Acquired Philosophie Group (US, 2019) and Eternus Solutions (Pune Salesforce partner, 2021). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.2–5]
- Geography Q1 FY27 (by client HQ): US 50%, Europe 38% (mainly Germany), UAE 7%, India/rest 5%. Q4 FY26: US 53%, Europe 35%, Middle East 7%, India/APAC 4%. Europe was 0% in 2016. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Clients: 50 large enterprises, 18 of them Fortune 500; 32 with over $1bn revenue; top 5 clients ~40% of revenue, top 10 ~57%; no single client above 10% of FY26 consolidated revenue; 24 new clients in FY26, but only ~7% of revenue came from new clients; 94% of clients return each year; average tenure 9+ years. [Q4 call; AR FY26 consolidated notes; IP Q1FY27 p.11]
- Receivables concentration: two customers each owed over 10% of receivables at Mar-26; three customers were ~66% of receivables. [AR FY26 credit-risk note]
- Headcount 1,790 at Mar-26 (+~200 in Q4), +45 net in Q1 FY27; 1,562 permanent staff in the standalone company; employee turnover ~14% in FY26 (BRSR; up from ~12% in FY25). No utilisation figure disclosed. [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 Board's report and BRSR]
- AI: 43% of revenue from "AI-augmented" development (Mar-26), target 100% of the team within 12 months; registered Claude Partner Network partner, target 100+ certified engineers by year-end; accelerators InsaneSDD, Expona, EWSH, RAI, RDE. Management says clients are not asking for AI discounts. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.19]

## Guidance history
- Policy: no revenue guidance. Standing targets: 24% "EBITDA" (company definition) and 14% PAT margin; ambition to double revenue every three years (organic + acquisitions). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Q4 FY26 call: FY26 "EBITDA" 25–26% and PAT 16% — "don't be upset if we go back to 24%" as the company invests; 43% → 100% AI-capable delivery in 12 months; promoters may sell or pledge up to 3% until Oct-26 (~0.5% sold in Q4). [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: company "EBITDA" 22.6% — **below** the 24% target (blamed on AI and US sales investment with a 9–12 month payback lag); PAT margin 14% — **met** despite the tax catch-up; revenue +33% YoY / +7.6% QoQ — momentum **kept**. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.14]
- Doubling every three years implies ~26% a year; FY26 grew 30% in rupees but only 24% in dollars. [Screener; Q4 call; derived]

## Moat evidence
- Client stickiness: 94% repeat, 9+ year average tenure, 18 Fortune 500 clients; Salesforce and ServiceNow "elite" partner status and a ServiceNow APAC partner award. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.11]
- Weak pricing evidence: margins fell from 24% (FY22) to 14% (FY24) before recovering, showing limited protection in a downturn; much of FY26's margin gain came alongside a weaker rupee. [Screener; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Promoter selling: promoters cut holding from 74.4% (Dec-25) to 72.8% (Mar-26), including ~0.5% sold and a gift of shares to a charitable trust (to avoid capital-gains tax, no lock-in), with a window open to sell or pledge up to 3% until Oct-26. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Liquidity presentation: receivables counted inside "cash and cash equivalents" on slides. [IP Q1FY27 p.4; Q4 call]
- Currency: unhedged; a rupee rebound would cut reported growth and margins. [Q4 call]
- Concentration: top 10 = 57% of revenue; three customers = 66% of receivables. [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- Auditor: key audit matter on impairment testing of investments in subsidiaries (InfoBeans Inc., USA; Eternus/InfoBeans CloudTech); no qualification or emphasis of matter found. [AR FY26 standalone auditor's report]
- Adviser to the board: Phaneesh Murthy (also an ex-director of Ceinsys) since 2024. [IP Q1FY27 p.3]

## My read
InfoBeans has clearly broken out of its FY23–FY25 stagnation: revenue up ~30% and net profit more than doubled in FY26, with clean cash conversion and no debt. But roughly a third of the rupee growth is currency, margins are being deliberately stepped down to fund AI and US sales, and the promoters are selling. At the current price the stock needs only about 11% a year of earnings growth (formula: (P/E × 0.1726)^0.1 − 1), which looks achievable if dollar growth stays near 20%. What would change the view: dollar growth falling below ~12%, or the margin failing to return toward 24% (company basis) by H2 FY27.
