# Tanla Platforms — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. CPaaS = communications platform as a service (sending SMS/WhatsApp/RCS messages for businesses); OTT = WhatsApp, RCS and similar app channels; GP = gross profit.
Transcript check: the input file holds the Q4 FY26 call (AI-generated tijoristack copy) and the Q2 FY26 call (official BSE filing); it has **no Q1 FY27 transcript**, and none was found on BSE via Screener or on Tanla's IR page. Q1 FY27 facts below come from the official Q1 FY27 investor update (22 Jul 2026) and results release. The AI Q4 copy was checked against the official BSE transcript (filed 1 May 2026).

## Business and segments
- India's largest CPaaS company (internal estimate ~30–35% share): routes bank alerts, OTPs, UPI/e-commerce notifications and marketing messages over SMS, WhatsApp, RCS and voice for 3,200+ customers, including 8 of the top 10 banks. [IP Q1FY27]
- Two segments, Q1 FY27: **Enterprise Communications** ₹1,123cr (91.6% of revenue, +18.4% YoY, gross margin ~20%); **Digital Platforms** ₹103cr (8.4%, +12.2% YoY, flat QoQ, gross margin ~98.5%) — Wisely.ai (anti-spam for telcos, live with Indosat for 100M+ Indonesian users), Wisely ATP (anti-phishing for banks: three banks incl. Bandhan Bank and Bank of Baroda), Trubloq (TRAI DLT blockchain registry) and RCS MaaP. Platforms still make ~31% of gross profit. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call official]
- Channel mix: SMS ~60% of 9MFY26 revenue (68% in FY25); OTT ~31–32% of revenue, a ~₹1,000cr run-rate, >80–90% of it WhatsApp. [CARE Apr-26; Q4 call official]
- Platform pricing: ATP and Wisely.ai are billed per subscriber per month on 5-year-plus contracts, not per message blocked. [Q4 call official]
- ~1,100 employees; FY26 spend on talent ₹457cr. [IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Annual: revenue FY24 3,928 → FY25 4,028 → FY26 4,418 (+10%); OPM 19% → 17% → 16%; net profit 548 → 507 → 509. Margins have fallen from 22% in FY22. TTM revenue 4,603, profit 533. [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 1,041 / 16% / 118; Sep-25 1,078 / 16% / 125; Dec-25 1,121 / 17% / 131; Mar-26 1,178 / 16% / 134; Jun-26 1,226 / 16% / 142 — five straight quarters of sequential growth. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue +17.8% YoY, GP ₹326cr (+25.1%, margin 26.6% vs 25.0%), EBITDA ₹201cr (+22.7%), PAT ₹142cr (+20.1%), EPS ₹10.77. Sequential GP margin fell 40 bps on customer mix. Indirect costs ~10% of revenue. [IP Q1FY27]
- Growth source: of the ₹186cr YoY revenue gain, ₹120cr came from existing customers and ₹66cr from customers won in the last 12 months; Q1 new customers (71) added only ₹3.1cr. [IP Q1FY27]
- Returns: ROCE 26% (59% in FY22), ROE 21%. [Screener]

## Deals, customers, ARR and churn
- No ARR or churn disclosure. Proxies: 353 customers above ₹1cr annualised revenue (326 a year ago); 21 above ₹50cr (17); the ₹10–50cr cohort grew 44% YoY. 13 of last year's top-20 customers remain top-20 and the rest are in the top 30; 50%+ of revenue comes from 50 of the top-100 customers retained over five years. [IP Q1FY27]
- Concentration: top ~250 customers give 75–80% of enterprise revenue; a long tail of 50–60k firms gives the rest. Real-money gaming (Dream11 etc.) was lost to regulation in FY26. [Q4 call official; Q2 FY26 call]
- Wins cited: Reserve Bank of India and Bank of Baroda (new), a large e-commerce client raised Tanla's wallet share above 50%; Meta and Google "partner of the year". [Q4 call official]

## Guidance history
- Q2 FY26 call (Oct 2025): FY26 internal "aspiration" of ₹800cr EBITDA cut to "minimum of ₹750cr" (gaming ban, lower WhatsApp incentives); 20% EBITDA CAGR aspiration kept but called steep. **Outcome: FY26 EBITDA ₹723.7cr (company) / ₹726cr (Screener) — missed even the cut number.** [Q2 FY26 call; IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Q4 FY26 call (27 Apr 2026): no numeric guidance; revenue growth "definitely more than 10% a year" vs industry 8–10%; margins "range bound", hold or improve; capex ₹100–150cr a year; platform growth to accelerate; "one gigantic platform" to launch within about a month; ValueFirst International transfer — "we will close this quarter... Let me commit that", adding ₹150–170cr revenue at ~22% gross margin. [Q4 call official]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +17.8% — **beat**; EBITDA margin 16.4% vs 16.3% in Q4 — **held**; platform revenue flat QoQ — **not yet accelerating**; ValueFirst — **missed** (SPA via a new route signed 28 Jul, completion now "by Q2 FY27"); new platform — **no launch found** in the Q1 results release. [IP Q1FY27; BSE filing 28 Jul 2026; Tanla newsroom Jul-26]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex ₹100–150cr a year, mostly capitalised platform development (internally developed platforms ₹175cr on the books; intangibles under development ₹46cr) and data centres; Q1 FY27 net capex ₹30cr. Depreciation rose from ₹85cr (FY24) to ₹122cr (FY26). [Q4 call official; IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- ValueFirst Middle East (VF FZC, with UAE, Indonesia and 70%-owned Saudi units): enterprise value ₹148.5cr = ₹12cr cash + ₹136.5cr of its liabilities assumed; FY26 turnover ₹181.6cr; not a related party. Originally part of the 2022 ValueFirst deal, stuck ~3 years awaiting RBI clarification of "old matters". [BSE filing 28 Jul 2026; Q4 call official]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and liquid investments ₹1,197cr at June 2026 (after ₹80cr dividend) ≈ 18% of the ₹6,562cr market value; debt-free apart from ~₹50cr leases. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Cash conversion good: CFO/operating profit 94% → 106% → 102% (FY24–FY26); FY26 FCF ₹477cr; Q1 FY27 CFO ₹156cr, FCF ₹126cr (89% of PAT). DSO 79 days (76 in Q4). [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- Shareholder returns: dividend payout ~31%; buyback in FY26 (FY26 financing outflow ₹375cr); ₹1,340cr returned over eight years. Promoter stake rose from 44.1% to 46.2% as the buyback shrank the share count. [Screener; IP Q1FY27; Q4 call official]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and embeddedness: ~30–35% share, 10k+ APIs deployed; top-100 customers stay for years. CARE cites "stable operations with high customer retention". [IP Q1FY27; CARE Apr-26]
- Platform IP: 8 granted patents, 19 applications; Gartner "Visionary" in CPaaS 2026. [AR FY26; Tanla newsroom Jul-26]
- Limits: enterprise business earns only ~20% gross margin because telcos and Meta set input prices; CARE: margins "constrained due to high dependence on telecom operators and OTT vendors, limiting its pricing power". Meta is shifting WhatsApp from promotional to utility messages (~10x cheaper), which lowers realisation. [CARE Apr-26; Q4 call official]

## Risks and red flags
- **Input-price and regulatory dependence** on telcos, TRAI and Meta; regulated messages are only a single-digit share of volume, but SMS pricing keeps drifting down. [Q4 call official; CARE Apr-26]
- **Platform segment not scaling**: ₹395cr in FY26 (single-digit growth), ₹103cr in Q1 (flat QoQ). CARE's upgrade trigger is platforms above 25% of revenue — they are 8.4%. [Q4 call official; IP Q1FY27; CARE Apr-26]
- **Contingent liabilities** up to ₹63.6cr (₹16.8cr a year ago), mainly a ₹46.9cr income-tax demand (31 Mar 2026) for not withholding tax on the 2018–19 Karix acquisition payment; the seller has indemnified Tanla. Bank guarantees ₹189cr and a ₹200cr parent guarantee for a subsidiary. [AR FY26 contingent liabilities note]
- **Governance**: secretarial auditor noted designated persons traded in breach of insider-trading rules (contra trades, pledging), reported to exchanges. Auditor opinion unmodified; key audit matters are revenue recognition (high-volume IT billing, unbilled revenue, credit notes) and goodwill impairment. [AR FY26]
- **Execution credibility**: ₹800cr → ₹750cr → ₹724cr EBITDA; ValueFirst "commit" missed again; promised platform launch not visible; ₹12cr FX mark-to-market loss in Q4 on an unhedged payable. [Q2 FY26 call; Q4 call official; IP Q1FY27]
- **Investor flight**: FIIs cut from 15.5% (Dec 2023) to 7.6%; DIIs negligible (0.4%). [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q2 FY26 → Q4 FY26: tone moved from explaining a missed EBITDA target (gaming, WhatsApp incentives) to "back on growth", but still no numbers. New issues in Q4: FX loss, repeated ValueFirst delay, platform growth only single digit.
- Q4 FY26 → Q1 FY27 (from the investor update): revenue growth accelerated to 17.8% and EBITDA grew faster than revenue for the first time in a while; platform revenue stalled QoQ; ValueFirst moved to a signed SPA.

## My read
The core messaging business is growing again (18% in Q1, five quarters of sequential growth) and throws off cash, but it is a low-margin toll road whose prices are set by telcos and Meta, and the high-margin platform business that is supposed to lift margins is still only 8% of revenue and not accelerating. Management has a habit of over-promising (EBITDA aspirations, ValueFirst timing, a platform launch). With ~18% of market value in cash and profit growing ~20%, the stock needs only about 10% a year earnings growth; it would re-rate if platforms grow 25%+ with new telco wins, and de-rate if SMS pricing or WhatsApp realisation cuts push margins below 15%.
