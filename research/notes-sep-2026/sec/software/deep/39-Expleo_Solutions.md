# Expleo Solutions — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated (company reports in ₹ million; 10 mn = ₹1cr). Expleo holds earnings calls only twice a year (Q2 and Q4), so the "last two calls" are **Q4 FY26 (14 May 2026)** and **Q2 FY26 (14 Nov 2025)**. There was no Q1 FY27 call, only a deck (14 Aug 2026). The stored Q4 transcript is AI-generated (tijoristack) and the stored Q2 is a raw machine transcription with garbled names ("Funny Sangrila" = Phani Tangirala, "Ralph Galison" = Ralph Gillessen). I checked both against the official company transcripts; the figures and statements match.

## Business and segments
- Chennai-based listed arm (71.05% owned) of France's Expleo Group (Assystem is the ultimate holding entity), doing software testing/quality assurance ("digital assurance") and digital engineering for banks and insurers, plus aerospace & defence engineering (test-bench design and assembly) and a shrinking automotive practice. [AR FY26 Board's report; Q4 call]
- Industry mix Q1 FY27: BFSI 82%, aerospace ~9.7%, automotive ~8%, transportation 0.3% (Q1 FY26: 80.8% / 8.2% / 10.7%). FY26: BFSI 81% (+12.8%), aerospace 10% (+7.8%), automotive 9% (−22%). [IP Q1FY27 p.7; AR FY26 Board's report]
- Geography FY26 (revenue from operations): Europe 40% (37% in FY25), UK 14%, US 14% (13%); the rest is India, Middle East and Asia. Offshore/onsite 75/25 (65/35 in FY25). [AR FY26 Board's report]
- Group dependence: revenue from Expleo Group companies was 35% of FY26 revenue (34% in FY25); management said ~30–33% on the Q4 call. [AR FY26 Board's report; Q4 call]
- Clients: 213 active; top 5 = 34%, top 10 = 51% of Q1 FY27 revenue; clients billing over $1mn a year fell from 17 (Q1 FY26) to 14 (Q4) to 11 (Q1 FY27), while $0.5–1mn clients rose to 33. [IP Q1FY27 p.8]
- People: 3,683 delivery staff (Q1 FY27), 3,968 total at Mar-26 (3,948 a year earlier), so revenue grew with flat headcount. **Attrition 25% in FY26 (24% in FY25) per the annual report**, although the CEO told the Q4 call it was "moderating… at around 15%". [IP Q1FY27 p.8; AR FY26 Board's report; Q4 call]
- AI: 15% of FY26 revenue "AI-influenced", target 60% by moving all top-20 accounts onto the expleo.ai platform; 70% of staff "AI-qualified" (target 95–100% by Sep–Oct 2026); AI cuts effort by 40–60% and "the current market does not allow us to retain those gains". Digital revenue 49.6% of Q1 FY27 revenue. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.7]

## Guidance history
- Q2 FY26 call (Nov-25): FY26 "high single-digit" growth; FY27 "healthy double-digit"; two acquisition targets in due diligence, decision "in Q1 of next year"; dividend to depend on the M&A outcome. [Q2 call]
- Q4 FY26 call (May-26): FY26 delivered +8.1% in rupees (met the high-single-digit promise only because of currency); FY27 "sustainable double-digit growth"; EBITDA excluding other income 15.5–16%; nine targets screened, three in due diligence, "very likely to close something in the upcoming quarter"; **no FY26 dividend because M&A has priority** (Chairman). [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery (deck, Aug-26): revenue +12.3% YoY — **met** double digits in rupees; adjusted EBITDA 14.5% — **below** the 15.5–16% range; **no acquisition announced**. Then on 28 Jul 2026 the board **recommended a final dividend of ₹110 per share** (~₹171cr, about 45% of the ₹376cr cash and ~138% of FY26 profit) — a reversal of the May stance. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 Board's report; derived]
- Chronic pattern: M&A has been "in due diligence" since at least Nov-25 with the decision date moving from January to Q1 FY27 to "upcoming quarter". [Q2 call; Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex tiny: ₹3.1cr added to gross block in FY26; new Bengaluru aerospace & defence facility with a lab "at least 4 times larger" for test-bench assembly inaugurated May-26; Coimbatore centre closed and merged into Chennai. [AR FY26 Board's report; Q4 call]
- New GIFT City subsidiary incorporated 14 Jun 2026. [AR FY26 Board's report]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash ₹375.7cr at Mar-26 (₹229cr a year earlier); net cash ₹352.8cr at Jun-26. Borrowings ₹8cr (leases). FY26 CFO ₹119cr, FCF ₹117cr (95% of operating profit). [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27 p.4; Screener]
- **Loans to the parent:** overseas subsidiaries have lent Expleo Services SASU (France) ₹97.5cr (USD 5.5mn, SGD 2mn, GBP 2.5mn) at ~9.5–10%; ₹115cr a year earlier. Shareholders approved RPT limits including loans of up to ₹280cr by subsidiaries to group companies. [AR FY26 Board's report; Q4 call]
- Debtor days rose to 108 (Q1 FY27) from 104 (Q4) and 91 (Q1 FY26); Screener FY26 debtor days 76. [IP Q1FY27 p.8; Screener]
- Dividend history: a ₹50/share payout appears in the ten-year table and FY25 financing outflow was ₹87cr (the Q2 call refers to "the dividend payout, which we have done last year"); no dividend was declared with the May-26 results; ₹110 recommended on 28 Jul 2026 (subject to AGM). [AR FY26 ten-year table; Screener; Q2 call; AR FY26 Board's report]

## Moat evidence
- Deep, long bank relationships (some 25 years), group-sourced work (~35% of revenue) and a niche in payments testing (simulators for Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex, UPI) ahead of 2028 compliance changes. [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- But no pricing power on AI: management says clients take all AI savings; the number of $1mn+ clients is falling; constant-currency revenue has been flat for a year. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.8]

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party dependence:** 35% of revenue from the parent group; ₹97.5cr lent to the parent; a new management fee of ₹24.1cr paid to Expleo Services SASU entities in FY26 (nil in FY25). Parent revenue ~€1.4bn at ~10% EBITDA margin, with a new group CEO and weak European automotive. [AR FY26 RPT notes; Q4 call]
- **Disclosure conflict on attrition:** 25% in the annual report vs "around 15%" on the call. [AR FY26; Q4 call]
- **Capital allocation U-turns:** "no dividend, M&A first" (May) → ₹110/share dividend (July) with no deal. Positive for cash return but shows the M&A plan was not firm. [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- AI deflation: 40–60% effort reduction passed on to clients; growth depends on winning scope and new logos. [Q4 call]
- Currency: growth in rupees has come almost entirely from a weaker rupee against the euro and dollar. [Q4 call]
- Contingent liabilities ~₹78.5cr (mainly VAT and income-tax disputes). Auditor: revenue recognition (fixed-price contracts) is the key audit matter; no qualification or emphasis of matter. [AR FY26 Note 37; auditor's report]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q2 FY26 (Nov-25): margin jump to 17.1% on efficiency and forex; attrition "gone really up"; two M&A targets in due diligence; FY27 "healthy double-digit". [Q2 call]
- Q4 FY26 (May-26): tone more cautious on AI deflation ("we must start cannibalising our own revenue"); nine targets, three in due diligence; Egypt named as a new target market; margin guidance 15.5–16%; new Bengaluru A&D lab; dividend withheld for M&A. [Q4 call]
- After the Q4 call: Q1 FY27 margin 14.5% (below range), no acquisition, ₹110 dividend recommended. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]

## My read
Expleo is a cash-rich, low-growth testing house: constant-currency revenue was flat in FY26, reported growth comes from the weaker rupee, and AI is shrinking ticket sizes faster than the company can add scope. Margins (14.5–15.5%) are thin for a debt-free Indian IT firm, partly because a third of revenue comes from the parent at group-set prices, and the parent now also takes a management fee and borrows ₹97cr from the subsidiaries. The ₹110 dividend (~12% of the share price) makes the stock look cheap, but after it the cash pile halves and the underlying business needs only about 5% a year of earnings growth to justify the price (formula: (P/E × 0.1726)^0.1 − 1). What would change the view: a genuine constant-currency growth rebound above 8%, or an acquisition that fixes the US gap; a cut in group revenue or more cash moving to the parent would worsen it.
