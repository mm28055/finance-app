# Birlasoft — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated; $ = US dollars; cc = constant currency; TCV = total contract value of deals signed; LTM = last twelve months.
Transcript check: the stored Q4 FY26 transcript is the official BSE filing. The stored Q1 FY27 transcript is an AI-generated copy (tijoristack) and contains wrong numbers — every Q1 figure below was checked against the official BSE transcript filed 4 Aug 2026.

## Business and segments
- Mid-sized IT services company of the CKA Birla group (promoter 40.4%). Sells ERP implementation and support (SAP, Oracle, JD Edwards), digital/data/AI work and infrastructure management to mid-market and Fortune-500 clients, mostly in the US. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- Q1 FY27 revenue $145.2M / ₹1,379cr. Verticals: Manufacturing 37.6%, BFSI (payments, asset management, P&C insurance — not core banking) 25.3%, Energy & Utilities 17.5%, Lifesciences & Services 19.6%. Service lines: Digital & Data 56.7%, ERP 31.7%, Infra 11.6%. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Geography: Americas 82.9% (86.3% a year earlier), rest of world 17.1%. CARE put the US at ~86.5% of FY25 revenue. [IP Q1FY27; CARE Sep-25]
- Contract mix: time & material 49.5% vs fixed price/fixed monthly 50.5% — fixed-price share has **fallen** from 61.1% a year ago, the opposite of the "outcome-based pivot" story. Offshore effort 60.0% (51.2% a year ago). [IP Q1FY27]
- People: 11,057 staff (11,834 a year ago), technical 10,043, sales & support 1,014 (1,093 a year ago); utilisation 81.7%; LTM attrition 11.7% (13.0% in Q4). Revenue per employee ≈ $52k a year. [IP Q1FY27, derived]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Annual: revenue FY24 5,278 → FY25 5,375 → FY26 5,310; OPM 16% → 13% → 16%; net profit 624 → 517 → 518. FY26 in dollars $597.5M, −6% ($) and −6.5% cc — one of the worst declines in mid-tier IT. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 1,285 / 12% / 106; Sep-25 1,329 / 16% / 116; Dec-25 1,348 / 18% / 120; Mar-26 1,349 / 18% / 176; Jun-26 1,379 / 16% / 161. TTM sales 5,404, TTM profit 573. [Screener]
- Rupee growth hides dollar decline: Q1 FY27 revenue was −0.1% QoQ and −3.6% YoY in dollars (+0.3% QoQ cc) but +7.4% YoY in rupees because the average rate moved from ₹85.3 to ₹95.0/$. [IP Q1FY27]
- Margin: EBITDA 16.1% in Q1 FY27 vs 18.5% in Q4 (which had ~340 bps of one-offs: ~170 bps forex, ~170 bps reversal of bonus/leave provisions and lower stock-comp after three exits) and 12.4% a year ago. [Q4 call; Q1 call official]
- Tax: FY26 ETR 34.4% due to a one-time US federal tax provision; Q1 FY27 ETR 29.4%. FY26 also carried a ₹40.7cr Labour Code exceptional charge; "normalised" FY26 PAT ₹659.5cr. [AR FY26; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Returns: ROCE 21% (30% in FY24), ROE 14.1% — depressed by the cash pile. [Screener]

## Deal wins / TCV and clients
- TCV: Q1 FY26 $141M; Q3 FY26 $202M; Q4 FY26 $208M; Q1 FY27 $169M (+20% YoY). FY26 total $658M, of which $410M came in H2. Book-to-bill on Q1 ≈ 1.16x. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- **Net-new TCV is weak**: $76M (Q1 FY26) → $41M (Q4) → $57M (Q1 FY27). Management's "net new is up about $15M" compares with Q4, not with last year — YoY net new is down ~25%. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call official]
- Q1 wins: strategic AI partner for a US financial-services firm (plus renewal/expansion of run work); multi-year core modernisation for a European banking group; GenAI pharmacovigilance for a large pharma/MedTech firm; GenAI claims work for a P&C insurer. Deal tenures 18–24 months; "fiercely contested", larger firms now bid for mid-size deals. [Q1 call official; IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Concentration rising: top-5 clients 43.4% of LTM revenue (37.8% a year ago), top-10 56.2%, top-20 68.6%; top 36 accounts ≈ 80% of revenue. Active clients 213 (247 a year ago); $1M+ clients 72 (77); $5M+ 23; $10M+ 10 (12). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call official]
- Management says the tail cut is "done"; revenue shrinkage in Q1 was entirely outside the top 36 accounts; top 5/10/20 all grew. [Q1 call official]
- Verticals Q1 FY27: BFSI +5.1% QoQ, LSS +2.3% ($ terms); Manufacturing and E&U declined. Management expects Manufacturing/E&U weakness "for one more quarter", turnaround from Q3. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call official]

## AI impact
- Management says AI deals require committing productivity savings up front, so revenue deflates "in the immediate and the medium term"; the TCV-to-revenue gap should close over "3, 4 quarters". [Q4 call; Q1 call official]
- Over a quarter of code is now generated with agentic AI tools; own platforms Cogito (agent orchestration, shown to 15+ clients), Limits (testing), Sigma (AIOps). [Q1 call official]
- ERP business (31.7% of revenue) "consistently not performed"; CEO acknowledges Palantir-style claims of compressed SAP effort but says "system of records is not going away". New ERP head is a 25-year SAP veteran. [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- No revenue guidance, ever. Q4 FY26 call (6 May 2026) promises: (1) steady-state EBITDA margin "15% plus", with erosion from 18.5% expected from sales investment; (2) sales team up 30–40% YoY by mid-FY27 ("40% more sellers" by end-Q2); (3) ETR back to historical levels in FY27; (4) FY27 order book "substantially better" than FY26; (5) MedTech client issue and Manufacturing "bad news" behind. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: margin 16.1% — **beat**; ETR 29.4% — **met**; cc growth +0.3% QoQ after six quarters of flat/decline — **small positive**; TCV $169M (+20% YoY) — **on track** for "H1 better than last H1"; sales & support headcount fell 1,025 → 1,014 — **missed so far** (management says frontline sellers rose while support was cut, but gave no seller count); Manufacturing did not recover — **missed** the Q4 "bad news behind us" line; LSS recovered — **met**. [Q1 call official; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 guidance: wage hike from 1 July costs 170–200 bps, about half of it to show in Q2 margins, recovered via productivity; FY27 margin "at or about 15% or slightly better"; ETR 29–30%; "endeavour" of no negative quarters; H2 better than H1. [Q1 call official]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: depreciation ~₹80cr a year, fixed assets ₹834cr (includes leased offices). Capex is small (FY26 FCF ₹441cr on CFO ₹481cr). No capacity constraint; utilisation 81.7% leaves headroom. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- The real "capex" is people: 30–40% more sellers, new vertical/service-line leaders, and AI platform spend — all run through the P&L. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and equivalents ₹2,878.6cr ($304.1M) at June 2026, up 9% QoQ and 26% YoY — about 38% of the ₹7,664cr market value. Borrowings ₹141cr (mostly leases). Net cash ≈ ₹2,740cr. [Q1 call official; IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Cash generation is weakening despite higher profit: CFO 718 → 588 → 481 (FY24–FY26); CFO/operating profit 89% in FY26. Debtor days 72 → 67 → 83. Consolidated unbilled revenue rose to ₹312cr from ₹189cr (+65%) and receivables to ₹901cr from ₹791cr. Management blamed delayed documentation at two clients, since collected: DSO 62 → 55 days in Q1, collections $163.1M, OCF/EBITDA 108% (the stored AI transcript says 180%). [Screener; AR FY26 note on credit risk; Q1 call official]
- Dividend FY26 ₹6.50/share (payout ~35%, about ₹180cr); no buyback. Analysts on both calls pressed for a capital-allocation policy; management only said "the Board will take the right call". Any acquisition would be "capability-based", and not before three or four quarters of organic growth. [Q4 call; Q1 call official; Screener]
- Hedge book shrank to $43M (from $73M a year ago) plus £3.3M and €3.5M — less protection if the rupee strengthens. [IP Q1FY27]

## Moat evidence
- Weak-to-moderate. Strengths: long ERP/JDE relationships, top-5 clients still growing, renewals "not being lost", analyst recognitions (ISG "Leader" in digital engineering, 2026). [Q1 call official; IP Q1FY27]
- Against: revenue fell 6.5% cc in FY26 while peers grew; $1M+ client count falling; limited pricing power (CARE cites "limited pricing flexibility given its moderate scale"); AI deals force price cuts up front. [Q4 call; CARE Sep-25]
- Cost position is good: EBITDA margin rose ~370 bps YoY via tail-account exits (~200 bps of FY26 growth given up), offshore shift and SG&A cuts. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- **Client concentration rising** (top-5 43.4% LTM) and an unnamed MedTech client's "operational issue" hit Q4 revenue. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- **Management churn**: COO Selva stepped down (replaced by Vikram Puranik); three senior exits in Q3 FY26; earlier leadership hires "have not delivered" (CEO); Chief Strategy Officer Ankur Vaish, designated senior management in June 2026, resigned in Sept 2026. [Q4 call; News: ScanX, Jun/Sep 2026]
- **Contingent liabilities** (standalone) rose to ₹184cr from ₹120cr: income-tax matters ₹105.5cr (from ₹41.1cr, including transfer-pricing and 10A disputes), GST/service tax ₹68.1cr (incl. ₹47cr Business Auxiliary Services case at the Supreme Court), bank guarantees ₹6.5cr. Small against cash. [AR FY26 contingent liabilities note]
- **Auditor**: unmodified opinion; key audit matter on revenue recognition — percentage-of-completion on fixed-price contracts and unbilled revenue estimates; auditor checked ageing of unbilled revenue. No emphasis of matter found. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Related parties**: standalone receivables (incl. unbilled) from subsidiaries acting as agents ₹565cr (nil a year earlier) — an intra-group contracting change; eliminated in consolidation but worth watching. RPTs stated at arm's length. [AR FY26 standalone notes]
- **Governance signals**: 6.75% vote against re-appointing promoter CK Birla at the July 2026 AGM (institutional dissent); ₹1.23cr fine for a four-day delay in filing an NCLT order (Aug 2026). [News: ScanX, Jul/Aug 2026]
- **Disclosure gap**: no revenue guidance, no pipeline number, no seller count. [Q4 call; Q1 call official]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "we are at the bottom of the pit" (Q4) to "momentum is building" (Q1). Q4 blamed macro, a MedTech client and previous hires; Q1 dropped the blame.
- Numbers: first positive cc quarter after six flat/negative ones; margin 16.1% without one-offs (not 15.1% as the AI transcript says); DSO back to 55.
- New issues: wage-hike hit in Q2; analysts openly frustrated over cash hoard and missing seller additions; Manufacturing "structural issue ... a little bit of a yoyo".

## My read
The business has stopped shrinking and margins are genuinely better (16% without one-offs vs 12% a year ago), but growth is not yet back: dollar revenue is still down 3.6% YoY, net-new deal wins are below last year, sellers have not been added and concentration is rising. The share price already assumes very little — about 38% of market value is cash, so the operating business is valued at roughly 8 times trailing profit. The stock needs only low single-digit dollar growth plus a sensible use of cash (buyback or higher payout) to re-rate. Two or three quarters of positive cc growth with net-new TCV above $70M would improve the view; another Manufacturing/ERP decline, margin below 15% after the wage hike, or a large low-quality acquisition would weaken it.
