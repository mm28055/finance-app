# Zensar Technologies — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated; $ = US dollars; "cc" = constant currency; TCV = total contract value of deals signed.

## Business and segments
- Mid-size IT services firm of the RPG Group (promoter holding 49.0%), serving ~177 active clients from 30+ locations with 11,342 staff. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Service lines, Q1 FY27: Digital Application Services 77.4% of revenue (application services 27.9%, enterprise SaaS apps 12.1%, products & platforms incl. marketing services 26.6%, data engineering & analytics 10.8%); Cloud, Infrastructure & Security 22.6%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Verticals, Q1 FY27 (share / QoQ cc / YoY cc): Banking & Financial Services 48.8% / +8.3% / +14.7%; Manufacturing & Consumer 24.9% / −2.3% / −3.4%; Telecom, Media & Technology (TMT) 16.2% / −9.1% / −28.0%; Healthcare & Life Sciences 10.0% / −3.8% / −8.7%. BFS was 41.5% a year earlier — the company is becoming a BFS firm by default. [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Sep-26]
- Geography, Q1 FY27: US 66.2% (−3.7% YoY cc), Europe 21.5% (+3.0%), South Africa 12.3% (−0.8%). [IP Q1FY27]
- Delivery mix: onsite 46.5% of revenue, offshore 53.5% — high onsite share for an Indian IT firm, which raises cost and visa exposure. New nearshore centre in Belgrade, Serbia (Q4 FY26). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Capacity: utilisation (excluding trainees) 85.1% in Q1 (84.3% in Q4) — near the practical ceiling; gross hiring 1,451 in Q1, headcount +5.2% QoQ, largely for the mega deal. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue FY24 4,902 → FY25 5,281 → FY26 5,687 (+7.7% in rupees; +3.1% in dollars; +1.7% cc). OPM 18% → 15% → 16%. Net profit 665 → 650 → 775 (FY26 +19%). [Screener; Q4 call; ICRA Sep-26]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 1,385 / 15% / 182; Sep-25 1,421 / 15% / 182; Dec-25 1,431 / 17% / 200; Mar-26 1,450 / 16% / 211; Jun-26 1,508 / 15% / 184. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 in dollars: revenue $159.5M (+1.1% QoQ cc, −2.0% YoY cc); EBITDA 14.6% (−150 bps QoQ); gross margin 29.7% (32.6% in Q4); PAT margin 12.2% (−220 bps). The EBITDA fall was ~2 pts from mega-deal pre-staffing and ~1.5 pts from travel/visa/training, cushioned by +0.8 pt forex and a **one-off +1.3 pt reversal of management bonuses** — underlying margin was nearer 13.3%. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- FY26: revenue $643.7M; PAT $87.2M (+13.6%). [Q4 call]
- Unit economics: revenue per employee ≈ $159.5M × 4 ÷ 11,342 ≈ $56k a year; revenue per $10M+ client rising as the $10M+ count jumped from 15 to 19 in one quarter. [IP Q1FY27, derived]
- Returns: ROCE 23% (FY26), ROE 17.3%; debtor days 59; working-capital days 20. [Screener]

## Deal wins / TCV
- FY26 TCV $912.7M, including a record $401.8M in Q4 driven by a $210M, 5.5-year framework deal with a large financial-services client for AI-led automation (signed Feb 2026, ~$38M a year at full run-rate, ~6% of revenue). Excluding it, Q4 TCV was ~$192–206M. [ICRA Sep-26; Q4 call; News: ScanX, Feb 2026]
- Q1 FY27 TCV $149.2M — a multi-quarter low, though new-business share was "at an all-time high". Management wants book-to-bill of 0.9–1.1x of projected revenue (~$180–200M a quarter is "reasonable"). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Large deals (>$25M TCV) are 20–30% of the pipeline; "no mega deals" in the current pipeline. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (24 Apr 2026): no revenue guidance ("FY27 performance will be contingent on… the mega deal"); promised Q1 would not be a decline quarter; margin "mid-teens… we would like to continue with that overall guidance" (the 14–16% EBITDA band) with pressure in Q1–Q2 from transition costs; tax rate near statutory ±0.5%; HLS expected flat for FY27; expected mega-deal revenue "full-fledged from Q3". [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: grew +1.1% QoQ cc (**met** "no decline"); EBITDA 14.6% (inside band, but only thanks to a bonus reversal); HLS fell 3.8% QoQ (**missed** the flat hope); TMT fell 9.1% (as warned). [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (30 Jul 2026): margins in Q2 and Q3 "in the similar range as… Q1", recovery "from Q3 onwards" — the pressure window stretched from H1 to Q3. "Q2 will be better than Q1" on revenue; no full-year number. Management "less bullish" on Europe and Manufacturing/Consumer. [Q1 call]
- Verdict: revenue guidance none; margin band **kept but stretched**; timeline for recovery **pushed** one quarter.

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is small: capital commitments ₹12cr at Mar-26 (₹4cr a year earlier). Screener CFI −₹224cr in FY26 is mostly treasury investment movements. [AR FY26; Screener]
- Main use of capital ahead is M&A: "actively looking at scaled assets… north of $200 million… or maybe $150 million at least… looking at 2 or 3 assets". A $150–200M revenue buy would add 25–30% to revenue and likely consume most of the cash. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and investments $317.5M (≈ ₹2,900–3,000cr, ~30% of market value); borrowings only ₹79cr (lease-type). [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Sep-26; Screener]
- CFO ₹771cr in FY26 (₹565cr FY25, ₹642cr FY24) — ~100% of net profit; Q1 FY27 OCF/EBITDA ~70%. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Receivable days (billed) 75 in Q1 vs 78 at FY26 end; clients are "stretching" payment terms, 74–79 days seen as acceptable. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Dividend FY26: ₹2.40 interim + ₹12.60 final = ₹15 (payout ~44%); yield ~3.5% at ₹435. [Q4 call; AR FY26 AGM notice; Screener]
- ICRA AA+ (Stable)/A1+ reaffirmed 1 Sep 2026 on "robust capital structure… strong liquidity", RPG parentage. [ICRA Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- Won the $210M mega deal by displacing a Tier-1 incumbent (analyst framing, not denied) — evidence of competitive delivery in BFS. [Q4 call]
- Client satisfaction score up 470 bps in FY26 to top-3 in the industry; voluntary attrition 9.6% (sixth straight quarter below 10%) — low attrition is a real cost advantage. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- AI delivery claims: agent-based software development at BFS clients with "25+% productivity uplift with 44% of the code being AI generated"; 85% of staff AI-certified. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Weak points: "the competitive intensity is going up tremendously… almost everywhere, we are encountering a Tier 1"; AI spend is being carved out of existing budgets, "creating pressure on renewals and pricing". Consolidated out of one or two HLS clients. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Client concentration moderate and falling: top-5 24.7% (27.2% a year ago), top-10 38.9%, top-20 56.6%. [IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- Revenue shrinking in 3 of 4 verticals; TMT down 28% YoY, and the largest TMT client is budgeted for "continuous decline" because it is in-sourcing and cutting costs. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Heavy BFS and US dependence (48.8% and 66.2%) and one mega deal carrying FY27 growth — execution slip on the transition would hit both revenue and margin. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- AI deflation: management says limited annuity exposure shields it, but 77% of revenue is application services, the area most exposed to AI-driven price cuts. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Earnings quality: Q1 margin flattered by a 1.3-pt bonus reversal. [Q1 call]
- M&A risk: a scaled acquisition of $150–200M revenue would be ~3x larger than anything done before and could dilute returns. [Q1 call]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter is revenue recognition (fixed-price estimates, time-and-material approvals). Contingent liabilities small (~₹36cr in total at Mar-26, mostly indirect-tax disputes, bank guarantees and minor claims). Shareholders asked to approve ₹750cr of FY27 transactions with the South African step-down subsidiary — intra-group, arm's-length. No pledges found. [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 tone: record order book, "we don't see a degrowth quarter in Q1", Europe and South Africa "back". Q1 tone: delivered small growth but "less bullish" on Europe and MCS; margin pressure extended to Q3; TCV dropped to $149M.
- New: explicit hunt for a $150–200M revenue acquisition; ZenseAI.AgentMesh launch; biotech clients hurt by adverse FDA decisions.
- Positive: $10M+ clients up from 15 to 19; top TMT client "not too much to go down from there".

- "At least for Q2 and Q3, we would expect the margins to be in the similar range as what we are seeing in Q1." — Pulkit Bhandari [Q1 call]

## My read
Zensar is a well-run, cash-rich, low-attrition firm whose organic growth has stalled at 1–3% in dollars for three years, and whose FY27 hinges on one BFS mega deal while TMT and healthcare shrink. The share price (down ~47% from its high, P/E ~12, ~30% of market value in cash) already prices in low growth; it needs only mid-single-digit dollar growth with a return to 15–16% EBITDA to look cheap. What would change the view: the mega deal reaching full run-rate in Q3–Q4 with margins recovering (positive), or an expensive acquisition that burns the cash cushion, or a further 10–20% fall in the top TMT account (negative).
