# Xtranet Technologies — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. Listed on NSE/BSE on 30 Jul 2026 (IPO at ₹127, all fresh issue, ₹166.8cr), so there is **only one earnings call** (Q1 FY27, 24 Aug 2026) — no Q4 FY26 call exists. The stored Q1 transcript was checked against the official BSE copy and matches (not AI-generated).

## Business and segments
- Bhopal-based system integrator (founded 2002) that builds and runs data centres, disaster-recovery sites, network and security operations centres (NOC/SOC), implements ERP, and runs two own platforms: **XtraTrust** (a MeitY-licensed Certifying Authority issuing digital signature certificates and e-sign) and **Synergy** (low-code workflow platform). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.4]
- Segment mix Q1 FY27: data centre infrastructure & IT operations 48% (₹24.2cr), enterprise applications 26% (₹13.1cr), proprietary platforms 14% (₹7.1cr), digital services 12% (₹6.1cr). FY26 segment revenue: data centre ₹148cr, enterprise apps ₹121cr, digital services ₹58cr, platforms ₹38cr. [IP Q1FY27 p.11–14]
- Segment growth FY24→FY26: data centre ₹87cr → ₹105cr → ₹148cr; enterprise apps ₹111cr → ₹97cr → ₹121cr; digital services ₹13cr → ₹44cr → ₹58cr; platforms ₹23cr → ₹29cr → ₹38cr. [IP Q1FY27 p.11–14]
- Services vs products: services ~65–68% of Q1 FY27 revenue vs ~45–46% in Q1 FY26; FY26 was ~60% services / 40% products. Product (hardware resale) EBITDA margin 6–8%; services 20–22%. Three years ago the mix was ~80% product. [Q1 call]
- Customers: government/PSU ~47% of FY26 revenue; two customers each above 10% of revenue together gave ₹81.4cr (22%) in FY26. Almost entirely domestic: "rest of world" revenue only ₹0.46cr in FY26. [IP Q1FY27 p.17; AR FY26 consolidated segment note]
- XtraTrust: ~8.5 lakh subscribers on annual/biennial renewals, 10,000+ channel partners; about 11 private licensed CAs in India, and management names **eMudhra** as its main competitor. [Q1 call]
- People: 504 permanent + 370 contractual staff (group, Q1 FY27); standalone company had 243 permanent + 368 contractual at Mar-26. Actuarial attrition assumption 5%. No utilisation or voluntary attrition disclosed. [IP Q1FY27 p.2; AR FY26 MD&A and Note on gratuity]
- Group: subsidiaries Xtranet BPO (90.24%), Xtratrust Digisign (95%), Xtrasynergy Solutions; 50% associate Extranet Technology Solutions LLC, Dubai (FY26 profit ₹0.25cr vs ₹1.5cr in FY25). [AR FY26 Board's report; Note 39]

- Revenue FY23 ₹223cr → FY24 ₹233cr → FY25 ₹276cr → FY26 ₹365cr (+32%). Operating margin 6% → 9% → 17% → 17%. Net profit ₹6cr → ₹11cr → ₹30cr → ₹41cr. [Screener]
- Quarters available: Jun-25 revenue ₹45.7cr / OPM 12.0% / net profit ₹3.4cr; Mar-26 ₹161.0cr / 21.9% / ₹24.2cr; Jun-26 ₹50.5cr / 20.6% / ₹6.0cr. Sep-25 and Dec-25 were not published (pre-listing); by subtraction they totalled ~₹158cr. [Screener; derived]
- **Heavy Q4 skew:** Q4 FY26 alone was 44% of FY26 revenue and ~59% of FY26 profit. A Q1 of ₹50cr says little about the full year; project go-lives cluster at year-end. [Screener; derived]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹50.5cr (+10.5% YoY); EBITDA ₹10.4cr (+89%, margin 20.59%, +855bp); PAT ₹6.0cr (+76.5%); diluted EPS ₹1.55. [IP Q1FY27 p.23–25]
- Returns FY26: ROE 34.8%, ROCE 32.8% (Screener); 3-year ROE 38%. Returns are high partly because equity was small before the IPO. [Screener]
- Unit economics: FY26 revenue per permanent group employee ≈ ₹365cr ÷ 504 ≈ ₹72 lakh — high for IT services because ~40% of revenue is resold hardware. [derived]

## Guidance history
- No Q4 FY26 call (company listed Jul-26), so there is no prior promise to test. The IPO documents and CRISIL (Mar-26) expected FY26 revenue of ₹350–355cr; the company delivered ₹365cr — slightly **ahead**. [CRISIL Mar-26; Screener]
- Q1 FY27 call guidance: FY27 revenue "500 plus crores" (+35–40%); 35–40% revenue CAGR for three years; data centre segment to grow 35–40% a year and stay ~50–55% of revenue; platforms + digital services to reach 30%+; EBITDA margin to improve 50–75bp over FY26's ~17%; tax ~25%. [Q1 call]
- Q1 delivery vs that guidance: revenue +10.5% — well below the 35–40% run-rate; margin 20.6% — ahead. To reach ₹500cr, Q2–Q4 must deliver ~₹450cr, about 42% more than the same three quarters of FY26 (~₹319cr). This depends on converting the pipeline. [Screener; derived]
- Order support: order book ₹373cr (55 projects) at Jun-26, ~55% (≈₹205cr) executable in FY27; Q1 order inflow ~₹60cr; active bid pipeline ~₹1,200cr, 40–45% at advanced stage, "hopefully" ~30% closed within one quarter. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.15]
- Order book is only ~1.0x FY26 revenue; ₹205cr executable + ₹50cr booked in Q1 leaves ~₹245cr of FY27 revenue still to be won and executed within the year. [derived]

## Capex and capacity
- IPO use of proceeds: ₹102cr working capital, ₹20.2cr debt repayment, ₹8.5cr IT systems capex, ₹18.6cr general purposes. [Chittorgarh IPO page]
- FY26 investing outflow ₹68cr (fixed assets rose ₹24cr → ₹60cr, CWIP ₹25cr) — partly XtraTrust data-centre capacity, which management is expanding "in the next one quarter". Free cash flow was negative every year FY23–FY26 (FY26 −₹22cr). [Screener; Q1 call]
- Project economics: data-centre contracts are ~50–60% capex billing over 6–18 months, then 40–50% O&M billed straight-line over 3–7 years; internal IRR hurdle 17–18%. ~50% of the order book is recurring. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹86cr at Mar-26 (₹39cr at Mar-25); CRISIL BBB-/Stable on ₹100cr HDFC Bank lines (₹80cr cash credit); limit utilisation averaged 89% in the 12 months to Feb-26 and exceeded 95% in some months; unencumbered cash only ₹1.1cr at Mar-25. The IPO (₹167cr in Jul-26) should leave the company net cash of roughly ₹60–80cr after repaying ₹20cr — an estimate, not disclosed. [Screener; CRISIL Mar-26; derived]
- Debtor days 114 in FY26 (215 FY25, 157 FY24, 267 FY23); inventory days 128 (190 in FY25); CRISIL gross current-asset days 328 at Mar-25. Standalone inventory rose to ₹77.7cr at Mar-26 from ₹40.8cr — management deliberately stocked hardware before prices rose "3x-4x". [Screener; CRISIL Mar-26; AR FY26 Note 12; Q1 call]
- **Receivables ageing (standalone, Mar-26):** of ₹111cr, ₹33.6cr (30%) is more than three years past due, yet only ₹0.4cr is provided for. CRISIL counted ₹71.5cr of debtors older than six months (₹17cr recovered in Mar-26). [AR FY26 Note 13; CRISIL Mar-26]
- Cash conversion is weak: CFO/operating profit 53% in FY26 (26% FY25, 5% FY24). Payment terms: government capex billing 120–150 days, O&M 45–60 days. No dividend. [Screener; Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- XtraTrust is one of ~11 private licensed CAs — a genuine regulatory barrier; 8.5 lakh renewing subscribers and 10,000+ partners give a small annuity base (platform segment ₹38cr FY26). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.14]
- Data-centre work: 50+ data-centre / command-centre projects and 175 projects in FY24–FY26; O&M tails of 3–7 years create some switching cost. [Q1 call]
- Weaknesses of the moat: 55% of the order book is "indirect" (as a sub-contractor to prime bidders); the single largest order is ₹155cr (42% of the order book) from **Dynacons Systems & Solutions**, itself a system integrator. Government work is won on L1 (lowest-bid) tenders. [IP Q1FY27 p.15; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor emphasis (not a qualification):** the company did not book ₹2.69cr of interest it owes to MSME suppliers for late payment, saying it will book it only when it recovers a ₹25.8cr MSME-interest claim it has filed against one of its own debtors. The auditor "draws attention" but did not modify its opinion. [AR FY26 standalone auditor's report; Board's report]
- **Old receivables:** ₹33.6cr outstanding over three years with almost no provision — a write-off risk equal to ~80% of FY26 profit. [AR FY26 Note 13; derived]
- **Filing lapses:** the company never filed consolidated financial statements with the Registrar for FY2015-16 to FY2022-23, drawing an aggregate penalty of ₹13.08 lakh on the company plus the directors (orders Mar-26). Separate penalties (₹10 lakh on the company, ₹5 lakh each on two directors) for using March-2023 private-placement money before filing the allotment return; Section 185 (loans to directors) compliances also flagged. [AR FY26 secretarial audit report]
- Related parties: promoters/directors and the CFO lend small unsecured loans to the company (CFO interest ₹0.12cr a year); advances to promoter-directors appear in balances; loans of ~₹16cr to subsidiaries. Individually small, but governance habits of a formerly private company. [AR FY26 Note 40]
- Contingent liabilities ₹54.8cr at Mar-26 (₹24.5cr a year earlier), mostly bank guarantees (₹38.2cr), plus a ₹1.5cr GST appeal and a ₹1.13cr MSME claim against the company. [AR FY26 Note 36]
- Hardware inflation: management said hardware prices have risen sharply and supply is tight; contracts are fixed-price and escalation clauses are only now being added — a margin risk on new orders. [Q1 call]
- Concentration: 2 customers = 22% of FY26 revenue; top order = 42% of backlog; promoters hold 62.6% post-IPO (Screener still shows the pre-IPO 83.63%). [AR FY26; Chittorgarh; Screener]
- Stock is ~2.4x the IPO price two months after listing, pricing in the 35–40% growth guidance before any of it is delivered. [Screener; derived]

## My read
Xtranet has genuinely improved: margin went from 6% (FY23) to 17% (FY26) as services rose to ~60% of revenue, and the IPO fixes a stretched balance sheet (bank lines ~90% used). But it is still a small, lumpy, government-dependent system integrator — 44% of FY26 revenue came in Q4, the backlog is only one year of revenue, and 42% of it sits with one prime contractor. The ₹500cr FY27 target needs ~42% growth in the remaining quarters after a 10.5% Q1, and ₹34cr of 3-year-old receivables with almost no provision plus years of missed regulatory filings argue for a governance discount. At ~2.4x the IPO price the stock already assumes the guidance is met; I would want to see H1 revenue of at least ₹200cr and falling old receivables before trusting it.
