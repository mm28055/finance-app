# Unicommerce eSolutions (Unicomm. eSolutions) — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. The stored Q1 FY27 transcript is the official BSE filing (call 14 Aug 2026). The stored Q4 FY26 transcript is AI-generated (tijoristack; it misnames the CEO "Kapil Machida"); all Q4 statements below are checked against the official BSE transcript (call 28 Apr 2026). No credit rating exists (debt-free). "Adjusted EBITDA" = EBITDA before ESOP cost, which the company reports; Screener OPM is after ESOP cost.

## Business and segments
- E-commerce operations software for brands, retailers and 3PLs. Three platforms: **Uniware** (order/warehouse management SaaS; the core), **Shipway** (courier aggregation and shipping automation; transaction-priced, pays freight so gross margin ~20%) and **ConvertWay** (WhatsApp/voice marketing, "Catalyst AI"). Shipway and ConvertWay sit in subsidiary Shipway Technology Pvt Ltd, acquired in FY25. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Share: Uniware handled "approximately 25–30% of all dropship volumes" in India in FY25 (1Lattice report, Nov 2025). Uniware has 1,228 enterprise clients (979 a year ago); average realisation ~₹1 lakh a month per enterprise client, "broadly similar" for years. [IP Q1FY27 slides 3, 23; Q1 call]
- Mix: Uniware standalone gross margin ~80%, Shipway ~20%. Shipway revenue has run at roughly ₹20cr a quarter (analyst figure, not contested by management) — so Shipway+ConvertWay is ~40% of consolidated revenue, not the 25–30% the stored profile says. Shipway overlap with Uniware clients ~10%+. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Geography: international (7 countries; Dubai, Philippines, Malaysia largest) rose to 6–7% of revenue in FY26 and turned profitable. [Q4 call]
- Promoter: AceVector (Snapdeal parent) group, 36.0% (AceVector itself 25.9%); FIIs 0.02%, public 61.6%. [Screener; AR FY26 Board's report]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue FY24 104 → FY25 135 → FY26 204 (+51.6%, mostly the full-year Shipway consolidation); OPM 14% → 20% → 17%; PAT 13 → 18 → 20. TTM revenue 211, PAT 21; ROCE 21%, ROE 15.5%. [Screener]
- Company-reported FY26: adjusted EBITDA ₹43.9cr (21.5%); Uniware standalone adjusted EBITDA margin 25% → 37.5%; PAT ₹20.5cr. [Q4 call]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / PAT): Jun-25 44.9 / 18.7% / 3.9; Sep-25 51.4 / 17.8% / 5.8; Dec-25 56.4 / 19.4% / 7.4; Mar-26 51.6 / 13.4% / 3.4; Jun-26 51.4 / 10.6% / 4.7. OPM has fallen for two quarters as planned spending rose. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +14.3% YoY; Uniware +12.8% (over 15% excluding a former top-10 client that stopped multichannel selling in Q3 FY26); Shipway +16.8%. Adjusted EBITDA ₹8.1cr (15.8%) vs ₹9.5cr (21.1%); PAT up 20% only because of a tax benefit (PBT fell to ₹4.6cr from ₹5.2cr). Uniware standalone adjusted EBITDA ₹11.0cr (35.3%) vs ₹9.1cr (32.9%), so all the margin loss is Shipway/ConvertWay investment. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Standalone Uniware growth had slowed from 40–50% (FY22–23) to ~5.5% in FY26 before recovering to double digits from Q4 FY26. [Q4 call]
- ESOP cost ~₹2.5cr in Q1 (12–15% of staff cost); expected ₹2.5–4cr a quarter over the next year. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (28 Apr): Uniware "double-digit growth" (no number); Shipway double-digit and faster; lower adjusted EBITDA and PAT "over the next 2 quarters" from investment; "confident of delivering higher full year operational profitability in FY '27 compared to FY '26"; evaluating a Unicommerce–Shipway merger. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: Uniware +12.8% — **delivered** double-digit; Shipway +16.8% — **delivered**; adjusted EBITDA −14.5% YoY — **in line** with the warned dip; 115 enterprise adds vs 88 — **beat**. [Q1 call]
- Q1 targets: Uniware 15%+ YoY from Q4 FY27; Shipway 20%+ from Q4 FY27; Shipway adjusted-EBITDA breakeven from Q3 FY27, then run near breakeven (profits reinvested); Uniware margin to stay near 35–40%. The FY27 "higher full-year profitability than FY26" promise was **not repeated** on the Q1 call (only "improving profitability in H2"), and the merger was **not mentioned**. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: FY26 FCF ₹45cr on CFO ₹47cr; no capital commitments at Mar-26. The "investment" is opex — sales and marketing, AI product teams, mid/senior hires, AI tools — "a few crores", front-loaded in H1 FY27. New modules (UniReco reconciliation ~7% attach, UniCapture video evidence 3–4%, UniBot) have 18–24-month gestation. [Screener; AR FY26 note 28; Q1 call]
- M&A: evaluating small start-ups (pre-purchase journey, analytics, loyalty); "exploratory stage"; to be funded from cash, no fund-raise planned. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and investments ₹92.6cr at Jun-26 (₹81.3cr Mar-26; ₹53.8cr Jun-25). Borrowings ₹9cr are lease liabilities only. No dividend. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- Debtor days 20 (FY26) vs 37 (FY25) and 47 (FY24); CFO/PAT ~2.3x in FY26. [Screener]
- Goodwill ₹117.2cr from Shipway = 43% of total assets; auditor tested it for impairment as a key audit matter. [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report]

## Moat evidence
- Market leader in OMS/WMS ("7 out of 10 of their peers would be using Unicommerce"); ~350 integrations; clients up to 1,000 users; NRR "100% plus" excluding the one top-10 exit; churn is mostly long-tail brands shutting down, "rarely" a switch to a competitor. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Pricing power only partial: price-escalation clauses added for new clients since FY25; not yet applied to the old top-10 clients, whose growth (~3.5% in FY26) is "largely transaction growth". Top-10 share of Uniware revenue fell to ~12% (15.2% a year ago). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 slide 23]
- Shipway has no moat: courier aggregation "is not very sticky"; single-digit share of a ~₹4,000cr market led by a larger rival that listed in Aug 2026. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Volume-linked revenue tied to Indian e-commerce GMV; one top-10 client exit cut growth by ~2–3 points. [Q1 call]
- Shipway investment may not earn back — a challenger in a low-switching-cost market against a better-funded listed leader; revenue flat around ₹20cr/quarter for 4–5 quarters. [Q1 call]
- Related parties: select staff moved to parent AceVector under a shared-services deal; subcontracting charges jumped 303% to ₹11.6cr in FY26; also rent and recruitment payments to AceVector. Auditor fees for AceVector's IPO billed to AceVector. [AR FY26 MD&A p.58; RPT disclosure]
- Governance changes: Chairman Manoj Kohli resigned (effective 4 Jul 2026), replaced by Ullas Kamath; compliance officer changed 13 Aug 2026. [AR FY26 Annexure]
- Auditor Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP: unmodified opinion; KAM = goodwill impairment (DCF assumptions). No pending litigation or contingent liabilities. [AR FY26]
- ESOP dilution and cost running at ~5% of revenue. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- From vague "double-digit" to specific exit-rate targets (Uniware 15%+, Shipway 20%+ from Q4 FY27) and a Shipway breakeven date (Q3 FY27).
- The FY27 full-year profit-growth promise and the Shipway merger plan dropped out of the script.
- New: competitor listing, analysts pressing on Shipway stagnation and on ESOP cost; the CEO defended the moat against "vibe-coded" AI replacements.

## My read
The core Uniware business is improving — growth back to 12–15% and standalone margin at 35% — and the balance sheet is clean with ₹93cr of cash and no debt. The consolidated numbers are being diluted by Shipway, a low-margin, low-moat courier aggregator in which management is now spending ahead of revenue. At ~₹942cr market cap the stock needs roughly mid-teens profit growth for years, which depends on H2 FY27 margins recovering and Shipway reaching breakeven in Q3. Proof of Uniware 15%+ and Shipway 20%+ by Q4 would support the view; another quarter of flat Shipway revenue or quiet dropping of the FY27 profit-growth promise would weaken it.
