## Business and segments

Customers are TV networks and content owners, distributors (OTT, FAST and connected-TV (CTV) platforms such as Samsung TV Plus and Roku), and advertisers. It serves more than 400 content providers, 451 distributors and 80+ advertisers. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- **Capex is tiny** because infrastructure is rented from public cloud: fixed assets ₹94 cr, and FY26 free cash flow of ₹38 cr against adjusted operating cash flow of ₹60 cr. **But the group has committed to buy ₹2,170 cr of cloud infrastructure services as at 31 Mar 2026, up from ₹21.8 cr a year earlier.** That is about 1.4 times FY26 revenue. It is a large take-or-pay-style commitment and a new fixed-cost risk if volumes fall short. [AR FY26 note 39(b); Q4 call]
- **FinOps savings:** about $282,000 a month of cloud cost savings in Q1, which were reinvested in AI. [Q1 call]
- **M&A:** 33 targets evaluated since January, 23 passed and 10 under evaluation; no deal is imminent. Argoid Analytics (US) was bought in Nov 2024. [Q1 call; AR FY26 note 40]

## Moat evidence
- **Retention and account growth:** NRR of 122–127% for three years; $1 mn+ customers rose from 28 to 35. About 26 of the 31 points of growth come from existing customers. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Mission-critical track record:** all 104 FIFA World Cup 2026 matches for key customers, 100% on-air availability for a US broadcaster across 325 events, plus the Super Bowl, Olympics and Grammys. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Two-sided network:** 400+ content owners across 451 distributors; each new channel adds value to platforms and vice versa. Platforms are "looking to outsource rather than in-source" ad insertion. [Q1 call]
- **Pricing power is limited:** Amagi cut price for a large customer in FY26. The CFO says that customer is still "tracking at more than 30% run rate growth", so volume outweighs price. Gross margin has fallen from about 69% to 67–68%. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Market headroom:** TAM is about $17 bn, and only about 10% of playout has moved to cloud. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags

- **₹2,170 cr cloud commitment:** a sharp rise in fixed obligations that is not discussed on the calls. [AR FY26 note 39]
- **Gross-margin drift:** 69.5% in Q1 FY26 fell to about 66.8% in Q4 FY26 and 67.3% in Q1 FY27. Causes are price renegotiations, AI compute costs and migration "double bubble" costs. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Earnings quality:**
  - Adjusted EBITDA excludes ESOP cost, about 6–7% of revenue.
  - Reported operating margin is only about 7%.
  - Screener OPM for FY26 was 3.4% against adjusted 10.3%.
  - About 11 points of Q1 growth came from the weaker rupee.

  [Screener; Q1 call]

  - GST demands of ₹11.0 cr (up from ₹5.6 cr).

## My read
The business is improving fast. It has grown about 30% for three years, adjusted EBITDA went from 2% to 11.5% in about 18 months, and there is ₹1,616 cr of net cash. The underlying economics are still thin, though:
- the reported operating margin is about 7% once ESOP is counted;
- gross margin is slipping;
- one customer is 17% of revenue;
- a ₹2,170 cr cloud commitment now sits off the balance sheet.

At about 125 times trailing earnings and roughly 8 times sales, the price needs 25–30% dollar growth for several years and margins toward 20%+.

What would change my view:
- **Upside:** gross margin back at 69%+, AI products producing disclosed revenue, and ESOP falling below 5% of revenue.
- **Downside:** further price cuts at the large customer, a CTV ad slowdown, or cloud commitments running ahead of revenue.
