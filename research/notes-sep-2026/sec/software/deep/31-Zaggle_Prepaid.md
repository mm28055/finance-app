# Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. "Adj. EBITDA" = company's figure after ESOP cost and including its share of associate Mobileware's EBITDA; Screener's operating profit differs. Transcripts checked against the official BSE filings (the stored transcripts are AI-generated and contain wrong figures — see Stored-data errors).

## Business and segments
- Spend-management platform for Indian companies: employee benefits/tax wallets and expense cards (**Save**), accounts payable, branch and petty-cash payments (**Zoyer**, incl. the BROME module), channel-partner rewards (**Propel**), fleet fuel cards, and a retail credit-card-on-UPI app (**Zagg.money**, ex-Rio.money). Cards are issued with 19 partner banks on Visa/Mastercard/RuPay; Zaggle takes no balance-sheet credit risk. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Three revenue lines, Q1 FY27 consolidated: Propel points ₹251cr (59%), program fees ₹160cr (38%), SaaS fees ₹12.5cr (3%). Program fees = Zaggle's share of interchange and network incentives; about 66% of it is paid back to users as cashback/incentives. Propel is booked gross — on a net basis (after cost of vouchers) Q1 revenue was ₹190cr, not ₹423cr. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.8]
- Net-revenue lens: FY26 net revenue ₹857cr vs gross ₹1,908cr; adj. EBITDA is 18.3% of net revenue in Q1 FY27 (20.4% a year earlier). [IP Q1FY27 p.8]
- Customers 4,065 (3,559 a year ago, +14%); users 4.02 mn (+19%); revenue per customer ₹0.96 mn (+3%); ~450 employees; >50 mn cards issued since inception. [IP Q1FY27 p.7, p.26]
- Program-fee GTV split (standalone Q1): Zoyer 43%, Propel 33%, Save 24% — Zoyer, which had zero revenue at IPO (Sep 2023), is now the largest spend engine. [IP Q1FY27 p.9; Q4 call]
- Subsidiaries/associates, Q1 FY27: GreenEdge (golf/rewards programmes for ICICI, Amex, NPCI) revenue ₹44cr, EBITDA ₹4.3cr; Mobileware/"86400" (UPI switch for banks, associate) revenue ₹22cr, EBITDA ₹8.8cr (40% margin); Zagg.money EBITDA −₹2.5cr; TaxSpanner revenue ₹0.8cr, breakeven. [IP Q1FY27 p.11; Q1 call]
- Geography: India only. An ADGM (Abu Dhabi) subsidiary is being set up in Q2 FY27; US launch pushed from Jun-2026 to "by financial year-end". [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue FY24 776 → FY25 1,304 → FY26 1,908 (+46%); OPM 9% → 9% → 10%; net profit 44 → 88 → 139. TTM revenue 1,999, TTM PAT 130. [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 332 / 9% / 26; Sep-25 432 / 10% / 35; Dec-25 526 / 10% / 37; Mar-26 618 / 9% / 41; Jun-26 423 / 7% / 18. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +27.5% YoY but PAT −33% YoY (₹17.5cr vs ₹26.1cr); adj. EBITDA ₹34.7cr (8.2%) vs ₹33.4cr (10.1%); depreciation ₹13cr vs ₹7cr as capitalised software is amortised. Standalone revenue ₹390cr (+18%). [IP Q1FY27 p.10; Q1 call; Screener]
- Segment trend Q1 FY27 YoY: Propel +42.5%, program fees +10% (lowest in three years — management says "by design"), SaaS +18%. [IP Q1FY27 p.7; Q1 call]
- Seasonality: Q1 is ~18–23% of the year; ~60% of revenue comes in H2. [IP Q1FY27 p.6; Q1 call]
- Unit economics: cashback/incentives were 66.3% of program fees in Q1 (65.7% a year ago, ~69% in Q4). Standalone Propel margin ~5% (consolidated 7.1% helped by GreenEdge and overriding commissions); Q4 FY26 Propel margin had fallen to 4% from 10%. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Returns: ROCE 14%, ROE 10% — low because of ₹1,390cr of equity, much of it QIP cash. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (13 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth ~25–30% standalone and ~40% consolidated; EBITDA guidance withheld until Dice integration; 14–15% EBITDA margin "over the next 5 years"; GreenEdge +40–50%; OCF "just about minus ₹6 crores" standalone, to turn positive "in the coming quarters"; Propel margin back to ~5.5%; cashback to ~50% over 4–5 years; Zagg.money 5-year goal ₹500cr revenue / ₹65cr EBITDA. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: consolidated +28%, standalone +18% — both **behind** guidance, but management kept 40% and 25% ("not moving away from that guidance as yet"). Note the standalone target was quietly restated from "25–30%" to "about 25%". Margin fell to 8.2%; no OCF number was given (deferred to the Q2 call). The 14–15% margin target became "over 5 to 7 years". [Q1 call]
- Hitting 40% for FY27 needs ~₹2,670cr; after ₹423cr in Q1, the remaining three quarters must grow ~43% YoY (vs ₹1,576cr last year). Plausible only if Propel stays strong and Dice/program fees reaccelerate. [Screener; derived]
- Dice: FY26 revenue ~₹12cr; FY27 expected ₹15–16cr at 90%+ gross margin; revenue from 1 Jul 2026, full run-rate from Q3 after 85+ contracts are novated. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is software development: FY26 capitalised development/"tech spend" ~₹107cr (capitalised dev cost rose from ₹30cr in H1 to ₹56cr in H2). Other intangibles plus intangibles under development rose from ₹104cr (Mar-25) to ₹190cr (Mar-26). [Q4 call; AR FY26 consolidated balance sheet]
- Free cash flow FY26 −₹159cr (CFO −₹51cr); FCF negative every year FY23–FY26 (−32, −129, −48, −159). [Screener]
- Policy change: from Q1 FY27 Zaggle is moderating capitalisation "in close consultation with our advisors"; ~₹6cr of one product's cost was expensed fully in Q1; "we expense more than we capitalize over a period of time". This lowers reported margin but improves quality of earnings. [Q1 call]
- Acquisitions: Dice asset purchase ₹68cr ex-GST (down from ₹123cr share deal); ₹8cr in Unobanc (moneyHOP, AD Cat-II forex licence); Rivpe (Zagg.money) acquired Mar-26; GreenEdge subsidiary from Sep-25; EffiaSoft deal dropped. Capital commitments ₹67.9cr at Mar-26 (Dice). [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26 note 30]
- QIP: ₹594.8cr raised Dec-2024 at ₹523.20/share; by Mar-26 only ₹121.5cr of the ₹375cr acquisitions bucket and ₹10.7cr of the ₹140cr general-purpose bucket had been used. Preferential warrants at ₹567 (25% paid, ₹15cr) incl. ₹5cr from promoter entity RAN Ventures. [AR FY26 corporate governance report]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Mar-26: cash ₹106cr + other bank balances ₹440cr (₹653cr a year earlier); borrowings ₹45cr short-term (working capital) plus leases — net cash ~₹500cr. CARE cited cash and liquid investments over ₹508cr at Dec-25. [AR FY26 balance sheet; CARE Mar-26]
- Trade receivables ₹361cr (Mar-25 ₹215cr); debtor days 69 (60 in FY25); working-capital days ~100. Other current assets (prepaid card float, voucher stock, advances) ₹292cr vs ₹174cr. Cash is absorbed by growth: the float and receivables grow faster than revenue. [AR FY26; Screener]
- OCF: FY24 −83, FY25 +20, FY26 −51 (consolidated); standalone "−₹6cr" in FY26 vs "−₹33–34cr" at Sep-25. No Q1 FY27 number disclosed. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Levers stated in Q1: moving card volumes to banks that settle faster (re-carding takes time), cutting cash-heavy Propel "redemption model" customers, trimming cashback. Management: turning OCF positive "would take about 16 to 18 months". [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.12]
- No dividend; no buyback. CARE A-/Stable (₹100cr bank lines, reaffirmed Mar-26); gearing 0.01x (Mar-25). [Screener; CARE Mar-26]

## Moat evidence
- Scale: "#1 prepaid card issuer in the country" (company claim), 4,065 corporates, 4 mn users, >50 mn cards issued. Churn "below 1.5% in FY25" per CARE. [IP Q1FY27 p.26; CARE Mar-26]
- Switching costs: products sit on top of ERPs and HRMS; Zoyer case studies automate 3/4-way PO-invoice-GRN matching and TDS/GST rules for a large metals client; a retail chain rolling out 17,000 more stores after 2,000. 73% of Q1's new users came from existing clients (wallet upsell after the new tax rules). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.15, p.19]
- Policy tailwinds: new income-tax rules extend meal/gift wallets to the new regime (meal ₹50 → ₹200, gift ₹5,000 → ₹15,000), lifting per-user loads; proposed MDR on high-value UPI merchant payments would add interchange on Zoyer BROME and Mobileware. [IP Q1FY27 p.14–15]
- Contracts: 5-year HPCL DT-Plus aggregator deal (slab commission on fuel spends); 5-year PNB co-brand credit card; AU SFB co-brand; Bandhan AMC Zoyer contract (Sep-26). Many recent wins are 1–3 year contracts. [IP Q1FY27 p.16–21; News: BSE filing via search, 18 Sep 2026]
- Weakness: pricing power is limited — cashback is 66% of program fees and the SaaS fee is only ~3% of revenue; much of the economics is interchange that banks and RBI can reshape. CARE flags a possible interchange cap on prepaid cards like the one on debit cards. [Q1 call; CARE Mar-26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Accounting quality**: heavy capitalisation of development cost (intangibles + IAUD ₹190cr, up 83% in a year) flattered FY26 margins; the Q1 policy reversal cuts margins for "15–18 months". Management said the split will be agreed "in conjunction with our auditors". [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- **Cash conversion**: four straight years of negative FCF (cumulative ~−₹368cr FY23–FY26) against ₹394cr of cumulative net profit; OCF not disclosed for Q1. [Screener]
- **CFO exit**: CFO Aditya Kumar Grandhi left; Rajesh Tummalaganti interim CFO from 4 Apr 2026; no permanent CFO named by the Q1 call. [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 31]
- **Tax dispute**: income-tax contingent liability jumped to ₹8.1cr (₹1.5cr in FY25); total contingent liabilities ₹14.1cr (service tax ₹2.5cr, GST ₹3.5cr). The CIT(A) dismissed Zaggle's appeal against the AY 2024-25 assessment on 8 Sep 2026; company will go to ITAT (amount not stated in the filing). [AR FY26 note 30; BSE filing 9 Sep 2026]
- **Auditor**: MSKA & Associates — unmodified opinion; only key audit matter is revenue recognition (gross Propel voucher accounting). No emphasis of matter found. Audit trail operated all year. [AR FY26 auditor's reports]
- **Related parties** are small: professional fees to Prismberry/eYantra (KMP-linked) ₹0.3cr; salary to the chairman's daughter ₹0.1cr; ₹4.2cr loan to subsidiary TaxSpanner. [AR FY26 note 31; standalone note]
- **Acquisition risk**: five deals in two years (TaxSpanner, Mobileware, GreenEdge, Rivpe, Dice) plus Unobanc; CARE lists acquisition-led growth as a key weakness. Chairman said subsidiaries' "valuations have shot up by 5x, 6x" — unverifiable. [CARE Mar-26; Q1 call]
- **Ownership**: FIIs fell from 8.6% (Sep-25) to 2.3% (Jun-26) and DIIs from 13.3% (Mar-25) to 5.4%; promoters steady at 44.3%, no pledge shown. Stock ₹179 vs QIP price ₹523 and warrant price ₹567 — the 75% warrant balance is unlikely to be paid in. [Screener; AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "strongest year ever" (Q4) to "transformation through consolidation" (Q1): cash flow and capitalisation now come before growth.
- Numbers: margin 9.7% → 8.2%; program-fee growth slowed to 10%; standalone growth 18% vs 25–30% guided; OCF figure dropped from the script; 14–15% margin target stretched from 5 to 5–7 years.
- New: capitalisation policy review, Unobanc investment, HPCL and PNB deals, investor day with "Zaggle Brain" AI showcase planned, ADGM subsidiary. Middle-East plans reframed despite regional conflict.
- Positive: Zagg.money run-rate rose from 36–40k to ~84k cards a year; cashback ratio fell from ~69% to 66.3%; Propel standalone margin recovered to ~5% from 4%.

## My read
The franchise is real — 4,000+ corporates, low churn, rising users and a Zoyer product that is becoming the biggest spend engine — but the earnings have been lower-quality than the headline 46% growth suggested: margins leaned on capitalised development, and growth consumed cash every year. FY27 is a reset year: expect reported margins of 8–9% and growth nearer 25–30% than 40% unless H2 accelerates sharply. At ~₹179 the stock prices in little growth, so the test is simple — positive operating cash flow by H2 FY27 and Dice/program fees reaccelerating in Q2–Q3. A permanent CFO, a clear capitalisation policy and a stable cashback ratio would raise confidence; another quarter of undisclosed OCF or a prepaid interchange cap would lower it.
