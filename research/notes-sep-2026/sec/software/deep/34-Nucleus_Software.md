# Nucleus Software Exports — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. Stored transcripts are AI-generated (tijoristack); figures below are checked against the official BSE transcripts. Nucleus gives no revenue or margin guidance and publishes no investor presentation — the calls read out the numbers.

## Business and segments
- Banking software product company: **FinnOne Neo** (loan origination, lending, collections for banks and NBFCs) and **FinnAxia** (transaction banking — cash, liquidity, payments). "More than 200 banks and financial institutions across 50 countries". Latest release FinnOne Neo 9.0 with embedded AI went GA in Q1 FY27. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- Revenue model (product vs services): "Products" = licence fees + customisation/implementation + post-production maintenance (AMC); FY26 product revenue ₹740.6cr (85%), projects & services ₹135.5cr (15%). Q1 FY27 products ₹176.6cr (83.9%). AMC contracts are typically 5 years with annual escalation; CFO: "I will not call it as a subscription model". SaaS/subscription is only "a part" of revenue; share not disclosed. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Geography Q1 FY27: India 56%, Middle East 12%, South(-east) Asia 11%, Europe 5%, others 16%. FY26: ~42% of revenue from overseas. New Vietnam subsidiary (Feb 2026); one US auto-finance client won in FY26; Japan clients mostly use home-grown systems. [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- Customers: top-5 clients 28.1% of Q1 revenue (29.3% in Q4, 27% in Q3); no client above 10% in FY26. New logos: 7 in FY26 vs 3 in FY25; "almost 10" in the last 15 months; 3 added in Q1 FY27, but "some of the logos have left". Bajaj Finance moved away after investing in a competitor. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26 segment note]
- People: headcount 2,059 (Jun-26) vs 2,028 (Mar-26); quarterly attrition 3% (2% in Q4). Revenue per employee ≈ ₹43 lakh a year. [Q1 call; derived]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue FY24 826 → FY25 832 → FY26 876 (+5%); OPM 27% → 20% → 14%; net profit 192 → 163 → 117. In dollars FY26 revenue $99.6 mn vs $98.5 mn (+1%). [Screener; Q4 call]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 218 / 16% / 35; Sep-25 214 / 11% / 26; Dec-25 220 / 15% / 21; Mar-26 225 / 16% / 35; Jun-26 210 / 4% / 24. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹210.4cr — **down 6.4% QoQ and 3.4% YoY** (the call script said "growth"; Screener confirms declines); EBITDA ₹7.6cr (3.6%) vs ₹34.6cr (15.4%) in Q4. Cost of delivery 76.2% of revenue (70.0% in Q4); sales & marketing 10.0% (7.1%); G&A 10.1% (7.5%). [Q1 call; Screener]
- Q1 PAT ₹23.9cr (−32% YoY) was propped up by treasury income ₹18.9cr (mark-to-market gains on debt funds as rates fell) and a one-off ~₹9.8cr exceptional gain from cutting gratuity (₹6.99cr) and leave (₹2.83cr) liabilities after a pay-structure change. Without these, core profit was only a few crore. [Q1 call]
- FY26 cost jump: delivery cost ₹622.9cr (+10%), sales & marketing ₹61.9cr (+71%), G&A ₹67.1cr (+12%); R&D expensed ₹55.8cr standalone (₹33.3cr in FY25). New labour codes also raised staff costs. [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 2.35]
- Returns: ROCE 17%, ROE 13% (32% and 25%+ in FY24) — diluted by ~₹978cr of cash. [Screener]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: FY26 fixed-asset additions included ₹11.8cr on building/plant in Q4 alone; capital commitments ₹4.7cr. FY26 FCF ₹89cr (CFO ₹117cr). [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 2.33; Screener]
- Real investment is opex: +40–50 sales staff over two years, new Chief Business Officer, Chief Customer Success Officer (Q1), Chief Marketing Officer (Sep 2026), AI up-skilling, R&D up ~68%. [Q4 call; Q1 call; BSE filing 21 Sep 2026]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and investments ₹977.9cr at Jun-26 (Mar-26: ₹972.4cr = current accounts ₹80cr, mutual funds ₹597cr, FDs ₹263cr, tax-free bonds ₹32cr) — about 55% of market cap. No debt. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Receivables jumped to ₹174.7cr from ₹121.1cr; DSO 63 days vs 42 days. Working-capital days −45 (customers pay in advance; deferred revenue). [Q1 call; Screener]
- Currency: forward cover only $2 mn at ₹94.69/$ (Jun-26); ~42% of revenue overseas in USD/other currencies. [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- Shareholder returns: FY26 dividend payout 28%, yield ~1.8%; RSU Scheme 2026 — employee trust to buy up to 1,14,187 shares from the market by 30 Sep 2026 (cash used for staff stock, not a buyback). Share capital fell from ₹27cr to ₹26cr in FY25 with ₹126cr financing outflow (buyback plus dividend). [Screener; BSE filing 17 Aug 2026]
- No credit rating needed (no borrowings).

## Moat evidence
- Installed base: 200+ institutions, relationships like HDFC Bank (~30 years) and Hatton National Bank (5 years on FinnAxia); core lending systems are very hard to replace ("two decades" old FinnOne installs still running). [AR FY26; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Pricing: AMC renewals "at a significantly better price" in Q1; but CFO admits competition limits price increases — "we are maintaining the price segment". An investor noted the FY23–FY25 price correction that lifted margins to 25–27% has been given back. [Q1 call]
- Order momentum: order book doubled in a year to ₹1,244cr, mostly FinnOne Neo, mainly India plus Middle East and the US. [Q1 call]
- Weak points: Bajaj Finance left for a competitor it invested in; an analyst asked whether HDFC Bank also uses that competitor (management declined to comment). Migration of old FinnOne clients to Neo has been slow. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin collapse** with no stated recovery path; costs rising faster than revenue for two years (EBITDA ₹220cr FY24 → ₹124cr FY26 → ₹7.6cr in Q1). [Screener; Q1 call]
- **Earnings quality**: Q1 profit relied on treasury MTM gains and a one-off liability reversal booked as exceptional. [Q1 call]
- **Revenue recognition judgement**: auditor's key audit matter on percentage-of-completion for fixed-price contracts. Unmodified opinion; contingent liabilities small (income tax ₹4.0cr, GST ₹0.6cr). [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 2.33]
- **Client churn to competitors**: Bajaj Finance loss; top-5 still 28%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Governance/capital allocation**: promoters hold 73.6%; ~₹978cr idle cash earning MTM-sensitive returns; RSU grants while profits fall; FII holding down to 3.3%. Stock at 52-week low area (₹680 vs high ₹1,065). [Screener; Q1 call]
- **Disclosure**: no investor presentation, no ARR, customer count trend, licence/AMC/SaaS split or order-book burn schedule; the Q1 script mis-described declines as growth. [Q1 call]

- Numbers: order book ₹1,044cr → ₹1,244cr; receivables ₹121cr → ₹175cr; DSO 42 → 63 days; S&M 7.1% → 10.0% of revenue.
- New: Neo 9.0 GA with AI; Chief Customer Success Officer; RSU scheme; gratuity restructuring. Dropped: the Q4 claim of no significant client losses.

## My read
The order book says demand for FinnOne Neo is back — it doubled in a year — but none of it is in the P&L yet, while costs (sales hires, R&D, wage codes, RSUs) have been raised ahead of revenue, taking the margin from 27% (FY24) to 4% (Q1 FY27). Much of the order book is multi-year AMC, so the revenue ramp will be gradual; FY27 could see low single-digit growth with margins recovering only partially. The stock is cushioned by cash worth ~55% of market cap, so the downside is valuation-limited, but the upside needs proof: sequential revenue growth from Q2, EBITDA margin back above 15%, and receivables normalising. A second big-client loss, or continued cost growth without revenue, would argue for a value trap.
