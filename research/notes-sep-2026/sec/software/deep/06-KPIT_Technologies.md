## Business and segments
- KPIT is a pure-play automotive software engineering and integration company. It sells embedded software, integration and validation to car, truck and off-highway makers. FY26 revenue was $724.8 mn (FY25: $691.4 mn), or ₹6,455 cr. [AR FY26 p.21; Screener]
- Verticals, FY26: passenger cars 79.0%, commercial vehicles 18.1%, others about 3%. In Q1 FY27, passenger cars were $138.1 mn (-2.4% QoQ, -5.1% YoY) and commercial vehicles $34.0 mn (-12.2% QoQ, +29.1% YoY). The CV fall came after a one-time large licence sale in Q4. [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27 p.25; Q1 call]
- Practices, Q1 FY27: Feature Development & Integration $103.0 mn (-3.9% QoQ). Architecture & Middleware $32.1 mn (-0.6%). Cloud-based Connected Services $41.7 mn (-8.3% QoQ, +15.4% YoY). FY26 split: 59.8% / 17.9% / 22.3%. [IP Q1FY27 p.25; AR FY26 p.21]
- Geography, FY26: Europe 48.0%, US 27.4%, Asia 24.6%. In Q1 FY27, Europe was $87.8 mn (+1.2% QoQ, +11.4% YoY) and the US $53.9 mn (+10.5% QoQ, +4.0% YoY). JKC (Japan/Korea/China) was $28.0 mn (-25.0% QoQ, -29.7% YoY). SIMA (South-East Asia, India, Middle East, Africa) was $7.0 mn. On the Q1 call, Sachin Tikekar said "more than 50% comes from Europe". **US revenue share is about 30%**, Europe about 50%. [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27 p.25; Q1 call]
- Currency mix, Q1 FY27: EUR 37.9%, USD 31.5%, JPY 14.4%, GBP 9.5%, INR 3.4%, others 3.3%. Hedges outstanding: USD 58.75 mn, EUR 48.90 mn, GBP 15.95 mn, JPY 6,125 mn. Unlike most Indian IT firms, KPIT is mainly exposed to the euro and yen, not the dollar. [IP Q1FY27 p.27]
- Client concentration: strategic (T25) clients were 86.6% of FY26 revenue and 87.3% of Q1 FY27. BMW is about 12% of revenue (Business Today, citing the June disclosure and analysts). Honda cancelled its new platform programmes in April 2026. The Q1 fall came from "the top 2 clients". [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27; News: Business Today, 1 Jul 2026; Q4 meet; Q1 call]
- Contract type: fixed price was 67.7% of Q1 FY27 revenue (62.5% a year earlier) and time & material 32.3%. Management targets more than 75% outcome-based/fixed price and is converting four of the largest clients. [IP Q1FY27 p.26; Q4 meet]
- People: headcount was 12,303 at the end of Q1 FY27 (Q4 FY26: 12,545; Q1 FY26: 12,520), with 11,457 in development. Revenue per development employee was $60,901 (Q4: $61,716; Q1 FY26: $63,177). Quarterly attrition is not disclosed. The BRSR shows permanent-employee turnover of 26% in FY26, against 20% in FY25 and 24% in FY24 (this looks like the standalone company). **Utilisation is not disclosed.** [IP Q1FY27 p.28; AR FY26 BRSR q.22]
- Products and solutions (S&P) were about 15% of FY26 revenue, roughly $110 mn. This includes N-Dream/AirConsole (in-car gaming, licensed per vehicle), i-Dart (after-sales), Technica (validation), Cymotive (cybersecurity) and Caresoft (benchmarking and cost teardown). All sit on the "Beacon" AI platform, which has a Microsoft go-to-market tie-up. S&P made up 21% of the pipeline at Q4. [Q4 meet; IP Q1FY27 p.11,14]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 (Analyst Meet, 7 May 2026):**
  - FY27 set in "a very positive environment".
  - Two largest SDV (software-defined vehicle) programmes ending, but revenue "largely compensated by growth in newly acquired accounts". Without the ramp-down, FY27 would have had 4-5% sequential growth.
  - S&P revenue +30% YoY.
  - FY27 EBITDA 20.5-21.2%.
  - Medium term: "annualized sustainable double-digit growth", S&P to about 50% of revenue in three years ($550-600 mn of a $950 mn-1 bn total), and EBITDA of 22-24% by about FY29.

  [Q4 meet p.16-17, Q&A]
- **30 June 2026 business update (a cut):**
  - Q1 USD revenue -1% YoY, about -5% QoQ.
  - EBITDA and net margin down by more than revenue.
  - Q2 revenue "in the similar range as Q1".
  - Shares fell 16.7% on the day and about 25% over the week.

  [News: Business Today, 1 Jul 2026; Q1 call]
- **Q1 FY27 delivered:** revenue slightly better than the June warning (-0.6% USD YoY). EBITDA of 17.2% was far below the 20.5-21.2% FY27 guide. Deal wins were strong at $257 mn TCV (Q4: $349 mn). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **New stance (Q1 call):**
  - H2 better than H1, with a return to growth "by Q4". Q2 flat.
  - Margins "improve incrementally, not significantly until our revenues go back to growing ways". Q4 margin may be "flattish".
  - The FY27 EBITDA band is effectively dropped.
  - The 22-24% FY29 aspiration is kept ("we will be in a position to get where we have mentioned").
  - Wage hikes delayed for the first time: junior grades soon, senior grades later.

  **Verdict: FY27 guidance cut; medium-term target kept but now unsupported by near-term numbers.** [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- The business is asset-light: capex was ₹28.8 cr in Q1 FY27. Big spending goes into acquisitions. FY26 investing outflow was ₹1,188 cr (FY25: ₹567 cr), mainly Caresoft, completed on 15 Aug 2025. N-Dream became a subsidiary from 6 Oct 2025 (it had been an associate). [IP Q1FY27 p.8; Screener; AR FY26 Form AOC-1]
- Goodwill on consolidation rose from ₹1,173 cr (Mar-25) to ₹2,798 cr (Mar-26), now 39% of total assets of ₹7,256 cr. Management says it has made about $400 mn of strategic investments and acquisitions (Technica, PathPartner, Qorix, Helm.ai, Caresoft, N-Dream, Cymotive). [AR FY26 p.21; Q4 meet]
- Tech investment is over 5% of revenue (up from about 3%), before the acquisitions. [Q4 meet]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash was ₹901.7 cr at Q1 FY27 end (Q4: ₹964.7 cr): gross cash ₹1,314 cr less acquisition debt ₹412 cr (Q4: ₹462 cr). Screener shows borrowings of ₹838 cr at Mar-26, including lease liabilities. [IP Q1FY27 p.8; Screener]
- DSO was 51 days at Q1 FY27 (Q4 FY26: 47 days). Working capital absorbed ₹215.5 cr in the quarter. [IP Q1FY27 p.8; Q4 meet]
- Cash conversion is strong. CFO was ₹1,002 cr / ₹1,390 cr / ₹1,195 cr in FY24/25/26, against FY26 Screener operating profit of about ₹1,221 cr (about 98% conversion). [Screener]
- Dividend payout was 32% in FY26, including a final dividend of ₹5.25 per share. There is no buyback. [Screener; Q4 meet]
- Contingent liabilities are small: bank guarantees ₹15.9 cr, customs bond ₹2.3 cr and minor tax appeals. There are corporate guarantees for subsidiaries of ₹54.5 cr (GmbH) and ₹28.4 cr (US holding), with no subsidiary borrowings drawn. [AR FY26 note 54.2]

## Moat evidence
- KPIT claims about a 10-11% share of the wallet of its top-25 clients' outsourced software spend. The FY27 goal is to raise that wallet share by 20%. [Q4 meet]
- Switching costs: work is tied to multi-year vehicle programmes. Management says the Q1 hit was "about timing", not share loss, and that it is "doing pretty well" in OEM vendor consolidation. The June crash shows the flip side: when a client cancels the vehicle programme itself (Honda), revenue vanishes quickly. [Q1 call; Q4 meet]
- Pricing: management says it "continue[s] to hold some premium against the competition". Fixed price rose 5 points YoY to 67.7%, which lets it keep AI productivity gains. Revenue per employee is still falling. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Deal momentum: TCV of $349 mn in Q4 FY26 and $257 mn in Q1 FY27, plus 13 new clients last year. There are seven new off-highway OEMs, four truck/bus OEMs, new passenger-car OEMs in Korea, Europe, the US and Japan, and two Chinese OEM engagements. [Q4 meet; Q1 call]
- Product IP: AirConsole has more than 4 mn car-owner users and is licensed per vehicle, starting with Tata Motors on the Sierra.ev. Cymotive's cybersecurity runs in millions of VW Group vehicles. [IP Q1FY27 p.10; Q4 meet]

## Risks and red flags
- **Client and region concentration:** about 50% of revenue comes from Europe, BMW is about 12%, and the top-25 clients are 87%. European OEMs face "job cuts, profit warnings, pay cuts" (Patil). The Europe hit shows up in Q2, and both troubled accounts only "stabilize in Q3". [Q1 call; News: Business Today, 1 Jul 2026]
- **Guidance credibility:** the 7 May guide (EBITDA of 20.5-21.2%, positive FY27) was withdrawn within eight weeks. That was the first break in a 23-quarter run of sequential growth. [Q4 meet; News: Business Today; Q1 call]
- **Acquisition accounting and goodwill:** goodwill is ₹2,798 cr against equity of ₹3,541 cr. The auditor (BSR & Co.) made "accounting for business combination" a key audit matter, alongside fixed-price revenue recognition. The opinion has no qualification or emphasis of matter. The Q1 call mentioned "some provision because of the acquisitions" in other expenses. [AR FY26 auditor's report; Q1 call]
- **Loss-making JV:** Qorix GmbH (middleware, with ZF and Qualcomm) is equity-accounted. The loss share was ₹14.0 cr in Q1, and middleware programmes have been pushed out. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 meet]
- **Capitalisation of product development:** ₹12.2 cr of AI and software product spend was capitalised in Q1 FY27. This is small but new, and it flatters EBITDA. [IP Q1FY27 p.26]
- **People:** turnover was 26% in FY26 (BRSR). The wage hike is deferred, which risks attrition when demand returns. [AR FY26 BRSR; Q1 call]
- **AI cannibalisation:** management admitted "AI-led transformation led to near-term cannibalization in some areas". [Q4 meet]
- **Governance and promoters:** promoter holding is 38.59%, down 0.82 points in the June quarter (Screener). The data does not show why. Co-founder and Chairman Ravi Pandit died and ceased to be a director on 8 May 2026. No pledge is reported. [Screener; AR FY26; IP Q1FY27 p.3]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone went from "positive environment FY27… mid-term outlook remains strong" (May) to "we are not very comfortable where we are" (July).
- The FY27 margin guide was dropped. EBITDA fell from 20.6% to 17.2%. PAT fell from ₹163 cr to ₹116 cr.
- New issues: European OEM freezes (BMW, VW), and Honda's programme cancellation hitting harder (JKC -25% QoQ). The first-ever wage-hike deferral. Other expenses up on forex, acquisition provisions and European subcontracting.
- Unchanged: the S&P pivot, the 22-24% medium-term margin aim, the deal-win engine ($257 mn), and the diversification into trucks, off-highway, the US and India.

[Q4 meet; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## My read
The franchise is intact: $257 mn of wins in a bad quarter, net cash of about ₹900 cr, and cash conversion near 100%. But FY27 is a lost year. USD revenue is flat to down for three quarters, and margins are 3-4 points below the May guide. Growth depends on European OEMs that are themselves cutting costs. At about 23 times trailing earnings (Screener), the stock prices in a recovery to growth in FY28 with margins back near 20%. It does not need the 22-24% aspiration. What would change my view up: USD growth by Q4 FY27, rising revenue per employee, and S&P growing more than 30%. What would change it down: a Q2 below Q1, or another large-client programme cancellation or goodwill impairment.
