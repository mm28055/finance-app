# Ceinsys Tech (CS Tech Ai) — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. The stored Q4 FY26 transcript is the official BSE filing. The stored Q1 FY27 transcript is **AI-generated** (tijoristack); I checked it against the official BSE transcript (filed 18 Aug 2026). The substance and figures match; only names are garbled ("Phanish" for Phaneesh Murthy, "Allegro"/"Aligram" for Allygrow/Allygram, "Remote Mobility" for Emotiv Mobility).

## Business and segments
- Nagpur-based (Meghe group) provider of geospatial engineering (mapping, 3D/LiDAR data, GIS platforms, digital twins) and "technology solutions" (smart-water IoT/AMR meters, enterprise GIS, AI extraction), plus a mobility engineering division (auto design/simulation, from the 2022 Allygrow acquisition) and a US geospatial/telecom arm (Technology Associates Inc., bought from VTS in 2024). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.2–7]
- Segments FY26: Geospatial & Engineering Services ₹359cr (+76%), Technology Solutions ₹301cr (+41%). Q1 FY27: Geospatial ₹94cr (+30% YoY), Technology ₹63cr (−25%); segment EBIT Q1 FY27 Geospatial ₹36.3cr, Technology ₹6.8cr (vs ₹10.8cr Q1 FY26). Target: Technology Solutions above 51% of revenue. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.10]
- Run-rate business outside the order book (mobility + OEM products): ~₹130–150cr in FY26 (20–25% of revenue); ~₹50cr in Q1 FY27. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Customers: government ~70% of business on average (target below 50% in 2–3 years). Two customers above 10% gave ₹315cr = **48% of FY26 revenue** (one customer, ₹106cr, in FY25). CARE: water domain is over 90% of the order book and the top two orders ~50% of it. [Q4 call; AR FY26 Note 46.02; CARE Sep-26]
- People: 1,104 employees (FY26); management says revenue per employee rose from ~₹24 lakh (FY24) to ~₹55 lakh (FY26). No attrition or utilisation disclosed. [AR FY26 Board's report; Q4 call]
- Subsidiaries FY26 (AOC-1): Technology Associates Inc. (US) turnover ₹26.1cr, **loss ₹22.0cr**; Allygram (70% JV with Grammer AG, equity-accounted) turnover ₹46.2cr, profit ₹17.0cr. [AR FY26 AOC-1]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue FY24 ₹253cr → FY25 ₹418cr → FY26 ₹661cr (+58%); OPM 17% → 19% → 23%; net profit ₹35cr → ₹63cr → ₹133cr (FY26 tax only 15% because of an ₹8–9cr reversal of excess FY25 provision). [Screener; Q4 call]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 ₹157cr / 19% / ₹32cr; Sep-25 ₹163cr / 22% / ₹26cr; Dec-25 ₹170cr / 23% / ₹39cr; Mar-26 ₹171cr / 24% / ₹37cr; Jun-26 ₹158cr / 24% / ₹31cr. Revenue has been flat at ₹157–171cr for five quarters while margin climbed. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹157.8cr (+0.8% YoY, −7.6% QoQ); EBITDA ₹38.5cr (+27%, 24.4%, +505bp); PAT ₹31.0cr (−2.2%); diluted EPS ₹14.78 (−9.5%, dilution from warrant conversion). Other income ₹4.9cr and JV share ₹2.8cr = 25% of PBT. [IP Q1FY27 p.11]
- Returns: ROCE 27.7%, ROE 24.8% (Screener TTM); company FY26 ROE 15.3%, ROCE 19.8% on year-end equity (bloated by warrant money). [Screener; IP Q1FY27 p.13]
- Cash conversion is the problem: consolidated CFO ₹6cr (FY25) and ₹3cr (FY26) against operating profit of ₹80cr and ₹149cr; standalone CFO ~₹19cr on ~₹176cr EBITDA (analyst on Q1 call, not disputed). [Screener; Q1 call; CARE Sep-26]

## Guidance history
- Company policy is "no forward guidance" on revenue. Qualitative promises on the Q4 call (3 Jun 2026): three large orders where it is L1 (lowest bidder), to be won "any day"; order inflow in Q2 alone to exceed all of FY26's (~₹350cr), or by Q3 at the latest; US subsidiary revenue >₹20cr (2x+) and breakeven in FY27; unbilled revenue to be billed in Q1–Q2; tax rate 25%; growth "momentum will continue". [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery (14 Aug 2026): order inflow ₹143cr, order book up to ₹990cr (₹977cr at 31 Jul per CARE); one of the three L1 orders won (₹67cr MP urban-development LOI), two "in the offing"; revenue growth only 1% — **missed** the "growth will continue" message; margin 24.4% (stated as sustainable) — **met**; unbilled revenue not yet billed — now pushed to "next 2 quarters" and "before 31 March" — **slipped**. US arm: "substantial improvement" in Q1 but no numbers. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.9; CARE Sep-26]
- Track record of slippage on orders: on the Q4 call the MD admitted Q4 closures "got really postponed" and Q1 "again, it got slipped to Q2". FY26 order intake (~₹350cr) was about half of revenue executed. [Q4 call]
- Tax guided at ~25% for FY27–28 (Q1 FY27 actual 26%). [Q4 call; Screener]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: no capital commitments at Mar-26; fixed assets ₹85cr (includes windmills). IP development spend ₹12–15cr planned for FY27, expensed; ₹12cr of ~₹40cr US technology spend was capitalised. [AR FY26 Note 40.02; Q4 call]
- Capital raised: ~₹238cr preferential equity/warrants (₹100cr in Sep-24, ₹130cr in Mar-26) from promoters (Meghe family) and Rare CP Fund (US fund linked to strategic adviser Tarun Raisoni); meant ~70% for acquisitions. Two targets dropped over "business continuity" concerns; in May-26 the board **varied the objects** to allow JVs. [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 Board's report]
- New use: up to ₹25cr (₹5cr now, ₹20cr after due diligence) in a 50:50 JV with AI Fabrik (USA) for a "sovereign AI neo-cloud" (GPU-as-a-service for defence) — revenue not before FY28. This is a new business line outside geospatial. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.9]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash ₹248cr at Mar-26 (₹123cr a year earlier), mostly warrant money in bank deposits (₹285cr); borrowings ₹57cr; CARE gearing 0.09x, interest cover 23x. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A; Screener; CARE Sep-26]
- **Unbilled revenue** rose from ₹134cr (Mar-25, 32% of income) to ₹319cr (Mar-26, 48% of income) — auditor lists unbilled-revenue computation as a key audit matter. About ₹100cr of IoT/JJM debtors + unbilled is expected after a Maharashtra government order (GR) releasing Jal Jeevan Mission funds, "by end of third quarter". [CARE Sep-26; AR FY26 auditor's report; Q1 call]
- Debtors ₹153cr at Mar-26: ₹94cr under 90 days, ₹127cr under a year, ~₹27cr over a year. Screener debtor days 259; payable days 1,076 (back-to-back subcontractors paid only when the government pays). Net working-capital cycle 157 days (Q4) → 164 days (Q1). CARE collection period 200 days, gross current assets 294 days. [Q4 call; Screener; Q1 call; CARE Sep-26]
- CARE upgraded to BBB+/Stable, A3+ (16 Sep 2026); downgrade trigger: collection period including unbilled beyond 250 days. Fund-based limit use ~66%, non-fund (guarantees) ~95%. Bank guarantees ₹81cr. [CARE Sep-26; AR FY26 Note 40]
- Dividend small (Screener payout low); management rejected an investor's buyback request on the Q1 call. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- Domain know-how: 7mn+ engineering hours, 100k+ miles of electrical network and 35k+ miles of water network processed/designed; empanelments with state agencies; "zero client churn" on 100+ projects (company claim). [IP Q1FY27 p.2, 6]
- Margin rise from 17% to 24% over eight quarters, attributed to moving from data capture to enterprise solutions — evidence of some pricing/mix power, though all government work is tendered (L1 wins). [Q4 call; Screener]
- Weak points: order book water-heavy (>90%) and top-two-order-heavy (~50%); acquired US business is loss-making; much delivery is on third-party platforms (Esri, Bentley, Autodesk). [CARE Sep-26; AR FY26 AOC-1; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Management churn:** CFO Samir Sabharwal resigned (Sep-25); Whole-Time Director and Vice-Chairman Prashant Kamat (Nov-25); CEO-designate Surej Poyil, also US CEO (Dec-25); CMO Vishal Pawar (Dec-25); independent director Phaneesh Murthy (28 Apr 2026, "personal reasons"); Head-Mobility Anand Paranjape (Jun-26). CARE flags "frequent changes" in KMP. [AR FY26 Board's report; CARE Sep-26]
- **Cash conversion and unbilled revenue:** two years of near-zero operating cash flow while profit doubled; profit quality depends on the JJM/IoT money actually arriving. [Screener; CARE Sep-26]
- **Customer concentration:** 2 customers = 48% of FY26 revenue. [AR FY26 Note 46.02]
- **Capital allocation drift:** acquisition money idle for two years, objects varied, and now an AI-cloud JV in a new field. [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- Related parties: a material RPT approval (up to ₹50cr) among Ceinsys, Allygram and Grammer AG; Meghe-group entities and relatives appear in the RPT list (including guarantees to banks). No auditor qualification or emphasis of matter. [AR FY26 Board's report; Note on RPT]
- Growth has stalled: Q1 revenue flat YoY after 58% FY26 growth; stock down ~58% from its high. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Order book recovered (₹876cr → ₹990cr) and one L1 order converted; the "code of conduct" excuse was dropped. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New: the AI Fabrik sovereign-cloud JV; a Maharashtra GR on JJM funds used to explain unbilled revenue; investors openly questioned cash conversion, idle cash, and Phaneesh Murthy's exit. [Q1 call]
- Unbilled revenue timeline moved from "Q1 and Q2" (Q4 call) to "next 2 quarters… before 31 March" (Q1 call). Revenue went from "best-ever quarter" (Q4) to +1% YoY (Q1). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## My read
Reported profits have improved sharply (FY26 net profit ₹133cr, margin 23–24%), but the business has stopped growing for five quarters and almost none of the profit has turned into cash — unbilled revenue alone is ₹319cr, nearly half a year's revenue. The balance sheet is strong only because promoters and a US fund injected ~₹238cr, which management has not deployed and is now steering into an unrelated AI-cloud JV. With 48% of revenue from two customers, >90% of the backlog in water, and six senior exits in a year, the low valuation is not an obvious bargain. The view improves if Q2–Q3 show order inflow above ₹350cr, a large fall in unbilled revenue, and operating cash flow above ₹80cr in FY27; it worsens if unbilled revenue keeps rising.
