# Aurionpro Solutions — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr). Screener consolidated figures for all history. Calls read: Q4 FY26 (12 May 2026, stored copy is the official BSE transcript) and Q1 FY27 (stored copy AI-generated; checked against the official BSE transcript — figures match).

## Business and segments
- Two verticals. **Banking & Fintech** (56% of Q1 FY27 revenue): transaction-banking software iCashPro, trade finance (Fintra, acquired Apr 2025), lending (Integro/SmartLender, OmniFin), digital banking (AuroDigi), payments (Auropay), customer-communication (Interact), enterprise AI (Arya.ai, Lexsi Labs). **Technology Innovation Group (TIG)** (44%): smart-transit fare collection and platform screen doors, and data-centre design/engineering/programme management for hyperscalers. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- TIG mix: data centres ~one-third of TIG, expected ~40% of TIG by FY27 end; data-centre business growing 40–50% a year, guided to "50–100%". Data-centre margins run 4–5 points below company average but need little capital. [Q1 call]
- Revenue type (accounting segments): software services ₹933cr (66%) and equipment & product licences ₹478cr (34%) in FY26. Segment result on equipment/licences rose to ₹210cr from ₹116cr — margin ~44% vs ~29%, because Aurionpro now owns the full transit stack. [AR FY26 note 37]
- Geography Q1 FY27: India 61%, Asia-Pacific ex-India 23%, US & Europe 13%, MEA & others 2%, others 1%. MEA was a "double-digit percentage" of revenue in FY26 and "came down to zero" after the West Asia war. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Clients: 400+ across 30+ countries; 23 new logos in Q1 FY27 (record Q1); ~2 products per Indian bank on average, some banks use 5–6; 30+ NBFC clients. Largest-ever US order ($33M+, 3 years, a US digital-insurance payments platform). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- People: 2,281 permanent employees on the parent's rolls and 2,817+ resources group-wide (Mar-26); revenue per employee ₹46.7 lakh annualised in Q1. Attrition "lower than the industry average" (number not given). No hiring for now — AI coding tools are meant to supply capacity. [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹887cr → FY25 ₹1,173cr → FY26 ₹1,411cr (+20%); OPM 22% → 21% → 20%; net profit ₹143cr → ₹188cr → ₹212cr. TTM revenue ₹1,432cr, PAT ₹206cr. 5-yr profit CAGR 34%. [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / PAT): Jun-25 337 / 20% / 51; Sep-25 358 / 20% / 56; Dec-25 371 / 20% / 44; Mar-26 346 / 19% / 61; Jun-26 358 / 17% / 45. Revenue has been flat at ₹340–370cr for five quarters. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹358cr (+6.3% YoY, +3.6% QoQ); Banking ~₹200cr (+5%), TIG ₹157cr (+8.4%); EBITDA ₹61cr (17.2% vs 20.2% a year ago); PAT ₹45cr (−13.8% YoY). Q4 PAT of ₹61cr was flattered by ₹15cr other income and an 8% tax rate. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- R&D expensed at ~10.5–11% of revenue and expected to stay there this year; capitalised development is extra (intangibles + intangibles under development ~₹200cr, up from ₹20–30cr). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Returns falling: ROCE 22% (FY24) → 18% → 16%; Q1 annualised ROCE 12.8%, ROE 10.1%. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]

## Deal wins / order book
- Order book ₹1,650cr (Q3 FY26) → "exceeding ₹1,800cr" (Q4) → ₹1,950cr+ (Q1 FY27); 68–72% executable within 12 months. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Large deals: ₹350cr hyperscaler AI data-centre mandate (revenue slipped from Q4 to Q1, now mostly H2); MMRDA/Mumbai Metro and Delhi Metro transit wins; a large PSU transaction-banking mandate; Singapore bank for Integro lending; $33M US order. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Win rates "north of 50%" on built-out products (management claim). [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- FY26: planned ~30% growth and 20–21% EBITDA / 15–16% PAT margins; delivered 20.2% growth, 20.0% EBITDA, 15.0% PAT — margins at the bottom of range, growth well short. Q4 alone missed plan by "more than ₹100cr". [Q4 call]
- Q4 FY26 call (12 May 2026): no number; "grow ahead of the industry ... by a meaningful number"; "not planning on any significant margin dip"; ₹150–200cr development spend over the next four quarters; ₹200–250cr of extra working capital; data-centre deal revenue "a fairly large chunk within the year". [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (28 Jul 2026): no revenue or margin guidance for FY27; EBITDA fell to 17% despite the Q4 "no significant margin dip"; Q2 "pickup", "much more significant" Q3–Q4; M&A paused "for the next few quarters"; CEO admitted the H2-acceleration promise "last year ... was not true". **Missed** on margin; revenue promise **deferred** to H2; long-range 30% CAGR effectively **dropped**. [Q1 call]
- Long range: "Aurionpro 2030" slide — FY30 value potential $560M–$1.7B (5–15% of an $11.2B serviceable market), 85% ARR mix, >20% ROIC, >110% net revenue retention (aspirations, not guidance). [IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Net block of PPE + intangibles rose to ₹281cr from ₹222cr in FY26; fixed assets (incl. goodwill) ₹892cr vs ₹732cr. Building "software 2.0" AI-native versions of every product, tabular foundation models (Orion), transit back-office software, security stack. [AR FY26 MD&A; Screener; Q4 call]
- Acquisitions over 2–3 years: Arya.ai, OmniFin, InfraRisk (last 17.34% via share swap at ₹1,454), Fintra (Apr 2025), Fenixys (Europe). Now paused. [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26]
- Data-centre capacity (people) built ahead of revenue in Q1, which hurt gross margin. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹61cr (Mar-26); CRISIL: cash ~₹270cr, of which ₹146cr unencumbered; bank-limit use ~21%; gearing <0.1x. [Screener; CRISIL Apr-26]
- Operating cash flow fell to ₹56cr in FY26 (₹157cr FY25); free cash flow −₹114cr. Working-capital days 90 → 116 → 137; debtor days 98; trade receivables ₹377cr vs ₹306cr. Gross current assets 220–230 days; ~₹70cr of debtors are over 6 months old, mostly TIG. H1 cash flow is usually negative or near zero. [Screener; AR FY26; CRISIL Apr-26; Q1 call]
- Long-term loans and advances jumped to ₹129cr from ₹50cr. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- Dividend yield ~0.6%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- CRISIL upgraded to A/Stable and A1 (20 Apr 2026) on scale, >20% margin and ₹2,000cr+ order pipeline; facilities raised to ₹427cr. [CRISIL Apr-26]
- Gartner Market Guide listings for iCashPro and SmartLender; iCashPro handles 10 million transactions/hour. [IP Q1FY27]
- Full-stack transit IP (gates, fare software, screen doors, Mastercard-certified back office) lifts equipment/licence margin to ~44%. [AR FY26 note 37; Q4 call]
- Banking software is sticky: banks buy for 10–15 years; management says no shortening of contract terms due to AI. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Revenue stall and margin slide**: five flat quarters; OPM from 22% to 17%. [Screener]
- **Cash**: FCF negative (−₹114cr); receivables and advances up; CRISIL downgrade trigger is GCA above 260 days (now 220–230). [Screener; CRISIL Apr-26]
- **Capitalisation**: rising capitalised development (~₹200cr intangibles) flatters EBITDA; the auditors' key audit matter is revenue on fixed-price contracts by percentage of completion — both judgement-heavy. [Q4 call; AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Governance**: secretarial audit notes delayed AGM voting results and late board-meeting notice (fines ₹11,800 each per exchange), a late shareholder approval for an independent director, and contra-trades by a designated person (profits disgorged). Promoter holding only 26.9%, down 4.3 pts in 3 years. [AR FY26 secretarial audit; Screener]
- Contingent liabilities (tax, legal) ₹15cr — small. Discontinued operations loss ₹3.2cr. [AR FY26 notes 36, 38]
- MEA exposure and large-project timing (₹350cr data-centre deal) drive quarterly misses. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: "transient shock", margin to hold, H2 FY27 strong. Q1: MEA "we do not have a good handle on that", no guidance at all, M&A frozen, cash conversion the new priority, admits prior H2 promises failed.
- Order book grew from ₹1,800cr+ to ₹1,950cr+ but conversion slowed; data-centre growth talk rose from 40–50% to "50–100%".
- R&D framing moved from a "short-term spurt" (Q4) to "similar number" all year (Q1).

## My read
Demand looks real (order book up to ₹1,950cr, record logos, CRISIL upgrade) but conversion, margins and cash are all going the wrong way: revenue has stalled for five quarters, OPM is down five points, and free cash flow turned negative while capitalised spending climbed. The stock has halved from its high and the P/E is below 20, so the price needs only modest growth — roughly low-teens — if margins recover to ~20%. The view improves if Q2–Q3 show TIG revenue stepping up from the data-centre deal and operating cash flow turning positive; it worsens if EBITDA stays near 17% or receivables keep rising.
