# Onward Technologies — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. Both stored transcripts are the official BSE filings (Q4 FY26 call in May 2026; Q1 FY27 call filed 22 Jul 2026) — checked, no AI-generated text. Company "total revenue" includes other income; Screener sales = revenue from operations.

## Business and segments
- Mid-size engineering R&D (ER&D) services firm: mechanical engineering (~50% of revenue), embedded and digital/AI engineering (~50% "software"). 73 live clients (down from ~250 four years ago; aim ~50), all in the US and Europe; ~2,485–2,600 staff; Chennai digital/AI lab live; fourth Pune design centre opening Aug 2026. [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Verticals Q1 FY27: Industrial Equipment & Heavy Machinery (IEHM) 65%, Transportation & Mobility (auto + rail) 31%, Healthcare & Life Sciences 4%. Transportation shrank ~1% in FY26 after exiting Tier-1/Tier-2 suppliers; HCLS targeted to grow 20–50% a year (no $1 mn client yet). [IP Q1FY27 slide 18; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Geography Q1 FY27: USA ~47%, Europe ~40%, India ~13% (billing to global clients' Indian centres — the MD says "zero clients in India"); FY26 ~70% North America, ~30% Europe by client location. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Contract mix: time-and-material 84% / fixed price 16% (fixed price up from 11% a year ago); offshore 68% vs 72% a year ago (onsite rose, partly local subcontractors in US/Europe because staff could not travel during the Middle East war). [IP Q1FY27 slide 18; Q1 call]
- Concentration: top-25 clients ~87–88% of revenue; clients billing over $1 mn rose from 16 to 18 in Q1. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Promoters (Mehta family via JHM Enterprises etc.) 39.97% after the buyback; FIIs 9.3%. [Screener]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue FY22 307 → FY23 441 → FY24 472 → FY25 491 → FY26 544; OPM 7% → 6% → 11% → 9% → 13%; PAT 24 → 11 → 34 → 27 → 44. ROCE 23%, ROE 19.5%. [Screener]
- Company FY26: revenue ₹550.9cr (+10.5%), EBITDA ₹71.9cr (13.2% vs 9.1%), EBITDA +60.9%, PAT +72.3%; revenue per employee ~₹22 lakh (₹8–9 lakh four years ago); attrition 14.85%. [Q4 call; AR FY26 p.16]
- Quarters (Screener sales / OPM / PAT): Jun-25 133 / 13% / 12.7; Sep-25 139 / 14% / 11.9; Dec-25 135 / 15% / 10; Mar-26 137 / 11% / 9.5; Jun-26 149 / 12% / 11.2. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue from operations ₹149.4cr (+12.2% YoY, +9.0% QoQ); EBITDA ₹18.4cr (12.3%), **+7.2% YoY** and +20% QoQ; margin −60 bps YoY; PAT ₹11.2cr, **−12.3% YoY** (+16.9% QoQ). Subcontracting cost ₹34cr vs ₹25cr a year ago. [IP Q1FY27 slide 17; Q1 call]
- Note: FY26 constant-currency growth was not disclosed; the MD deferred the question to the IR agency. Rupee weakness flattered reported growth. [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (May 2026): "sustained double-digit revenue and EBITDA growth" in FY27 (no numbers); ~95–98% of FY27 revenue from existing clients; capex ~₹25cr over two years for labs; Chennai AI lab "in the next couple of months"; buyback "actively considering". An investor alleged a 10–12% FY26 growth figure was shared at a broker conference; the MD denied it. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +12.2% — **met** double-digit; EBITDA +7.2% YoY — **below** the double-digit promise for this quarter (MD frames it annually); PAT −12% YoY — **missed**; buyback of ₹18cr at ₹328/share (2.41% of equity, promoters did not tender) — **delivered**; Chennai lab — **delivered**. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; News: Chittorgarh buyback page]
- Q1 call: guidance kept ("double-digit revenue growth and double-digit EBITDA margin"— note the wording drifted from EBITDA *growth* to EBITDA *margin*); FY27 ACV order book "has crossed last year's revenue"; ₹33cr ODC win with a North American power-management client (billing from Q2, full run-rate Q3, ~40 engineers to start; client wants to grow "5x"). A further ₹44.46cr three-year digital-services renewal was announced in Sep 2026. [Q1 call; Screener announcements]
- Long-term aspiration: revenue per employee ₹30–40 lakh, mid-to-high-teens EBITDA margin; no timeline. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex light: ~₹25cr over FY27–FY28 for client labs (more if a large deal needs it); capital commitments ₹3.3cr at Mar-26 (₹7.9cr a year earlier). FY26 FCF ₹46cr on CFO ₹65cr. Headcount has stayed ~2,500 for four years while revenue grew ~15% CAGR. [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 26; Screener]
- New Pune centre (Aug 2026) and Chennai expansion; ODC build-out cost ~$3–3.5 mn per analyst estimate (not confirmed). [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash ₹127.3cr at Mar-26 (company); Screener borrowings ₹40cr are mainly leases. Buyback used ₹18cr in Q1 FY27. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- DSO 75 days in Q1 FY27 (75 in Q4, 71 a year ago); Screener debtor days 81 (FY26). [IP Q1FY27 slide 17; Screener]
- Dividend ₹8/share for FY26 (11th straight year; payout ~41%); yield ~2.8%. [Q4 call; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Long, sticky client relationships: two or three of the top five have been clients for ~10 years; engagements typically 5–8 years under MSAs; "we have not lost any customers in the last few years". [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Wallet share rising: top-25 share 80% (at ₹250cr revenue) → 88%; $1 mn+ clients 11 (FY22) → 18; revenue per client up ~3.6x since pruning. Still "0.5% to 1%" of each client's outsourcing budget. [Q1 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- No pricing-power evidence; the model is hours-based T&M. MD admits Onward is "2 or 3 years late" in software-defined vehicles. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Concentration: top-25 = ~87% of revenue; IEHM 65% — exposed to North American industrial/agri capex cycles. [IP Q1FY27]
- Auto/Europe weakness: transportation vertical fell 1% in FY26; European OEM cancellations noted. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- AI deflation: MD said in May "we are not using AI tools today to deliver client-based projects which are billable" and customers "have already removed" AI-addressable work from outsourcing budgets — a risk to hours-based billing. [Q4 call]
- Litigation: a former employee's suit in Cook County, Illinois led to a temporary freeze of $12.6 lakh (₹11.3cr) of US-subsidiary bank balances (since lifted); contingent liability $4.2 lakh (₹3.9cr). Tax notices judged remote and not disclosed as contingent liabilities. [AR FY26 note 26]
- Related parties: rent ₹0.5cr a year to Desai & Diwanji (KMP-controlled). Auditor B S R & Co. LLP: unmodified opinion, key audit matter revenue recognition; audit-trail not verified at database level for some software. [AR FY26 RPT note; auditor's report]
- Disclosure: no constant-currency or ACV figures; reluctance to quantify guidance. [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defending against AI (May) to a data-centre/power-engineering growth story (July); "ACV above last year's revenue" is new.
- Margin mix worsened: offshore share fell and fixed-price share rose, contrary to the MD's statement that T&M increased; subcontracting jumped because of travel limits.
- Buyback done; ICRA ratings on file date from 2023 (no current rationale found).

## My read
The business is steadily improving: revenue growth back above 10%, margin up from ~9% to ~12–13%, net cash of ~₹127cr and a dividend plus buyback. But Q1 EBITDA grew only 7% and PAT fell YoY, the offshore mix slipped, and the power/data-centre ODC is a starting order, not yet revenue. At ~₹640cr market cap (about 20% of it in net cash) the price needs only high-single-digit profit growth, which the order book appears to cover. Proof of margin expansion in Q2–Q3 as the ODC bills and offshore share recovers would support the view; a slowdown in US industrial capex or a lost top-5 client would change it.
