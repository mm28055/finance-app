# R Systems International — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr); $ = US dollars. R Systems keeps a **calendar financial year**: the dashboard's "Q1 FY27" (Apr–Jun 2026) is the company's **Q2 CY26**, and "Q4 FY26" (Jan–Mar 2026) is **Q1 CY26**. Calls read: Q1 CY26 (7 May 2026; stored copy is the official BSE transcript) and Q2 CY26 (5 Aug 2026; stored copy AI-generated, checked against the official BSE transcript — dollar figures and names are wrong in it). Management quotes "adjusted EBITDA" (before RSU/ESOP costs and one-offs), which runs ~2 points above Screener's operating margin.

## Business and segments
- Mid-sized digital product-engineering firm: builds and modernises software for tech/SaaS companies (ISVs), plus data, AI, cloud and agentic "business operations". Owned 51.85% by Blackstone. AI studio branded EXIQO (on its OptimaAI platform). [Q1 CY26 call; CRISIL Jul-26]
- Verticals (CY25): Tech, Internet, Platforms & Services (TIPS) ~39% (42% in CY24), plus Healthcare, Telecom/Media/Entertainment, Manufacturing & Logistics and BFSI. [AR CY25]
- Geography: Americas 71.5% (Q2 CY26; 69.3% in Q1), APAC 15.3%, Europe 9.7%, MEA 3.5% (from the Novigo deal). CY25: Americas 72.7%. US exposure is the main concentration. [Q2 CY26 call; AR CY25]
- Clients: top client 6.0%, top 10 24.4% (Q2 CY26); 250+ customers. [Q2 CY26 call; CRISIL Jul-26]
- Work type: >90% discretionary project work; fixed-price ~15–16% of revenue (10% in 2024), rest time-and-materials; AI and AI-enabled services ~29% of revenue; "Data, AI and Cloud" >50%. [Q1 CY26 call; Q2 CY26 call]
- People: ~5,400 employees; utilisation 80.5–81% (peak ~84% before AI bench build); attrition "approximately 11%" per the CEO — but the annual report shows **voluntary attrition 20.5% in CY25** (15.6% CY24, 13.7% CY23). Headcount flat YoY while revenue grew. [Q1 CY26 call; Q2 CY26 call; AR CY25 BRSR]
- Acquisitions: Velotio (2023; goodwill ₹266cr) and Novigo Solutions (13 Nov 2025, ₹400cr, funded by ₹275cr NCDs). Novigo's revenue, pitched at ~$32M a year, was **restated to ~$21–22M** after aligning to gross-to-net and fixed-price policies. [AR CY25; Q1 CY26 call; CRISIL Jul-26]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Annual (Dec years): revenue CY23 ₹1,685cr → CY24 ₹1,742cr → CY25 ₹1,958cr (+12%); OPM 15% → 15% → 16%; net profit ₹140cr → ₹131cr → ₹186cr. TTM revenue ₹2,230cr, PAT ₹193cr. 3-yr sales CAGR only 9%. [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / PAT): Jun-25 462 / 15% / 76 (incl. ₹47cr other income); Sep-25 499 / 14% / 35; Dec-25 555 / 17% / 36; Mar-26 575 / 18% / 65; Jun-26 602 / 18% / 56. [Screener]
- Q2 CY26 (company): revenue ₹601.7cr / $63.6M (+30.2% in ₹, +17.7% in $, +1.2% QoQ in $); adjusted EBITDA ₹120.7cr (20.1%); adjusted PAT ₹62.9cr (10.5%); reported PAT ₹55.6cr; gross margin 39.2% (36.0% in Q1). [Q2 CY26 call (official); IP Q2CY26]
- **Currency did most of the lifting**: H1 CY26 adjusted-EBITDA bridge shows +₹55.2cr from rupee depreciation and only +₹24.7cr from operations; in Q2 alone, rupee +₹9.8cr, operations −₹4.7cr. [IP Q2CY26]
- Organic growth: Q1 CY26 organic revenue was "flat"; YoY growth is flattered by Novigo (consolidated from mid-Nov 2025) and a weaker rupee. [Q1 CY26 call]
- Q1 CY26 PAT was lifted by ₹18.0cr from switching to hedge accounting (loss moved to OCI); Q2 CY26 took a ₹9cr realised forward-contract loss. [IP Q2CY26; Q2 CY26 call]
- ROCE 19.5% (TTM), 19% CY25 (31% in CY22); ROE 24%. [Screener]

## Deal wins / TCV
- TTM ACV bookings (excl. renewals): $76.5M (Q4 CY25) → $82.5M (Q1 CY26) → $82.9M (Q2 CY26) — flat last quarter. No TCV or book-to-bill disclosed. [Q1 CY26 call; Q2 CY26 call]
- Q2 CY26 wins: global telecom analytics; India GCC for a US small-business lender; AI quality engineering for a global insurer; Dynamics 365 CRM for an exchange group; AI modernisation of an ad-tech core platform. Q1: research-firm API platform, life-insurance platform modernisation, a hyperscaler. [Q1 and Q2 CY26 calls]
- AI impact: management says AI shrinks the size of a given job (e.g., a $1.5–2M migration becomes ~$1M) but widens the market; no deflation because work is project-based, not multi-year annuity. [Q1 CY26 call; Q2 CY26 call]

## Guidance history
- No revenue guidance ever. Margin: "+17%" range in Q1 CY26 call; Q2 CY26: "stay in that 18 to 19 percentage adjusted EBITDA on a sustainable basis" — a **raise** (not "18.5%" as the AI transcript says). Delivered 20.1% two quarters running, but mostly on currency. [Q1 CY26 call; Q2 CY26 call (official)]
- Q1 CY26 call promised SG&A to return to ~$11M+ a quarter (from $10M) as sales investment continued — delivered: SG&A rose ₹23.8cr to ₹115.3cr in Q2. [Q1 CY26 call; Q2 CY26 call]
- Constant-currency growth of 3–4% a quarter is the aspiration; "only a matter of time", no date. [Q2 CY26 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex light (capital commitments ₹1cr); spending goes to sales (new Bay Area CRO Farooq Ahmad), branding and the AI bench. [AR CY25; Q1 CY26 call]
- Intangible amortisation from acquisitions ₹10.6cr a quarter (not tax-deductible, lifting the tax rate to ~31%). [Q2 CY26 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹410cr (Dec-25) incl. ₹275cr NCDs and ₹95cr leases; cash surplus ₹314cr; interest cover 25x; interest cost ~₹9.5cr a quarter. CRISIL AA-/Stable reaffirmed 7 Jul 2026. [Screener; CRISIL Jul-26]
- DSO: billed 55–56 days, billed + unbilled ~75 days (Q2 CY26). Forward cover $43.3M at ₹93.27. [Q2 CY26 call]
- Cash conversion historically ~100% of operating profit (CFO ₹220cr CY24). High payout: interim dividends (₹8/share second interim, Oct 2026 record date); yield ~3.4%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Everest Group "Leader" in mid-market software product engineering; HFS "Horizon 2 GCC accelerator"; helped 8–9 mid-market firms set up India GCCs. [Q1 CY26 call; Q2 CY26 call]
- Blackstone portfolio gives introductions but "I have to win a deal based on merit". [Q1 CY26 call]
- Revenue per employee rising: +18% $ growth YoY with flat headcount. [Q2 CY26 call]
- Weak points: low client concentration means no single anchor; mid-market clients may build GCCs or use AI-native rivals (Blackstone itself backs an Anthropic-linked services venture). [Q1 CY26 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Currency dependence**: most of the margin gain since 2025 is rupee depreciation; hedge-accounting change shifted ₹18cr of losses to OCI in Q1. [IP Q2CY26]
- **Organic growth muted**: organic flat in Q1 CY26; ACV flat in Q2; 3-yr sales CAGR 9%. [Q1 CY26 call; Screener]
- **Acquisition quality**: Novigo bought for ₹400cr, revenue restated from ~$32M to ~$21–22M; it is a key audit matter (business combination) along with Velotio goodwill impairment testing (₹266cr). [AR CY25; Q1 CY26 call]
- **Attrition disclosure gap**: 11% quoted on the call vs 20.5% voluntary attrition in the BRSR. [Q1 CY26 call; AR CY25]
- Minor compliance: ₹5,000 fines per exchange for a 17-minute-late related-party filing; earlier penalty rectified 2023. [AR CY25]
- Leverage rose (debt ₹410cr vs ₹73cr); Blackstone exit (any stake sale) is an overhang; CRISIL downgrade trigger is a change in Blackstone support. [Screener; CRISIL Jul-26]
- AI-native competition from frontier-model-backed services firms. [Q1 CY26 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q1 CY26: margin guide "+17%", utilisation deliberately cut to ~80.5% for AI bench, SG&A low on provision reversal, organic flat, ACV up to $82.5M, AI studio EXIQO launched.
- Q2 CY26: margin guide raised to 18–19%; utilisation 80.5–81%; SG&A up $2M; ACV flat at $82.9M; realised hedge loss; GCC and agentic operations emphasised; wage hike absorbed.

## Best management quotes
- "A fairly large portion also comes from forex ... which we cannot take for granted." — CEO Nitesh Bansal, on margins [Q2 CY26 call]
- "Organically, this quarter was flat." [Q1 CY26 call]
- "Approximately 29% of our revenue today comes from AI and AI-enabled services." [Q1 CY26 call]

## My read
Reported growth (30% in rupees) overstates the business: in dollars it is ~18% with Novigo included, organic growth was flat in Q1 and ACV stalled in Q2, while two-thirds of the margin gain came from the weak rupee. Still, the stock has halved from its high, trades at a P/E in the low teens with a ~3% yield and a Blackstone-backed balance sheet, so it needs only mid-single-digit earnings growth — that looks achievable even if the rupee stops helping. The view improves if constant-currency growth reaches 3–4% a quarter and ACV breaks above $85M; it worsens if margin falls below 18% once currency gains fade or Novigo disappoints further.
