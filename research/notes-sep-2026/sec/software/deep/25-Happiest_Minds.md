## MATERIAL EVENT — merger into ITC Infotech (announced 31 Aug 2026)
- Promoter Ashok Soota sells 22.1% (of his 44.2%) to ITC Infotech (100% owned by ITC Ltd) for ~₹1,330cr in two tranches: ~11% at ₹390/share after CCI approval (expected Q3 FY27), ~11% at ₹400 after shareholder approval (Q1 FY28). Stated reason: fund his not-for-profit SKAN and his venture Happiest Health. [Sep-26 call; CARE Sep-26]
- Then Happiest Minds merges into ITC Infotech: holders get **25 ITC Infotech shares for every 81 Happiest Minds shares**. Valuers PwC and Grant Thornton (fairness opinion ICICI Securities) put Happiest Minds at ₹405/share (₹6,167cr equity; 15.1× FY26 EV/EBITDA) and ITC Infotech at ₹1,312/share (₹11,920cr; 13.6×). [IP merger Aug-26; Sep-26 call]
- Post-merger: ITC ~73.4%, Soota ~7.6% (no longer promoter), other Happiest Minds public ~19.0%. Combined entity to be listed on BSE/NSE, expected Q2–Q3 FY28; whole process ~15 months (CCI, shareholders, NCLT). **No open offer** — the 22.1% purchase stays under the takeover-code trigger. [Sep-26 call; CARE Sep-26]
- Pro-forma FY26: revenue ₹7,033cr (ITC Infotech ₹4,718cr), 19,000+ staff, 800+ customers; ITC Infotech adj. EBITDA margin ~18.3%, combined ~18.1%; target US$1bn revenue by FY28 (Happiest Minds' own target had been FY31); combined geography ~38% Americas / 31% Europe / 31% rest; largest vertical becomes CPG & retail (28%). Large-client overlap reported as nil. [Sep-26 call; IP merger Aug-26]
- Share price ₹310 vs ₹405 implied swap value (~23% gap) and ₹390–400 cash paid to the promoter — the market is discounting delay, the unlisted status of ITC Infotech until FY28, and approval risk. CARE placed its AA- rating on "Watch with Positive Implications" (10 Sep 2026). [Screener; CARE Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- Sticky mid-market base: 94% repeat business; clients >5 years give ~41–42% of revenue. [IP Q1FY27; CARE Sep-26]
- Niche platforms (Arttha digital banking, EduWeave, Insurance-in-a-Box, SecAIGenie) give some IP revenue, but licence deals have proved lumpy and slip. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Minority-shareholder terms:** promoter gets ₹390–400 cash; public gets unlisted ITC Infotech paper (listing ~FY28) at a swap based on an unlisted company's valuation; no open offer. ITC Infotech also does work for ITC group companies (related party, "not very significant" per management). [Sep-26 call]
- **Deal risk:** 15-month path needs CCI, shareholder and NCLT approvals; management retention not contracted ("no specific contract"). [Sep-26 call]
- **Growth risk:** Q1 cc growth of 6.7% YoY vs 12.5% guide; Arttha slippage for three quarters. [Q1 call]
- **Balance-sheet optics:** gross cash and gross debt both inflated by borrowing against own deposits; interest cost is material. [AR FY26 note 19]
- **Auditor:** unmodified; key audit matter on fixed-price revenue (percentage-of-completion). Contingent liabilities small (income tax ₹5.2cr, GST ₹8.7cr). [AR FY26]
- **Promoter selling:** Soota had already done bulk-deal sales before the ITC deal; DIIs cut from 10.6% (Mar-25) to 5.7% (Jun-26). [Sep-26 call; Screener]

## My read
As a standalone business Happiest Minds was drifting: cc growth ~7%, a 12.5% guide that needed a sharp second-half pickup, lumpy platform deals that kept slipping, and margins stuck at ~17.5%. The ITC Infotech merger changes the question entirely — the shares now behave like a long-dated swap into an unlisted ITC subsidiary valued by the same advisers, with the promoter taking cash at ₹390–400 while public holders get paper. At ₹310 the stock trades ~23% below the ₹405 implied swap value; that gap is the reward for 15 months of approval risk and for accepting ITC Infotech's valuation. What would change the view: a disclosed ITC Infotech related-party share and client concentration, any change to the swap ratio, CCI or shareholder objections, or a clean listing timetable.
