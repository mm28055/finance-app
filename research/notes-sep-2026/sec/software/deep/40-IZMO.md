# IZMO — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. IZMO holds calls twice a year, so the last two are **Q4 FY26 (2 Jun 2026)** and **Q2 FY26 (17 Nov 2025)**. Q1 FY27 had only a business update and deck (24 Aug 2026). Both stored transcripts are the official BSE filings (company cover letters) — genuine.

## Business and segments
- Bengaluru-based, promoter-family-run (Soni family, 34.8%) company with two platforms: (1) automotive "digital intelligence" — izmocars (dealer websites/CRM, e.g. Stellantis' European aftersales network), izmostudio/izmostock (world's largest automotive CGI/photo library, clients Hertz, Avis, Europcar) and FrogData (US dealer analytics); (2) izmo Microsystems — advanced semiconductor packaging (System-in-Package, RF/microwave, 3D stacked die, flip-chip) and silicon-photonics packaging, mainly for Indian defence. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Division revenue (management reporting; izmostudio not shown separately, ~₹24cr a quarter by difference): izmocars ₹16.0cr / ₹15.6cr / ₹28.8cr / ₹16.9cr in Q2 FY26–Q1 FY27; FrogData ₹15.9cr / ₹15.4cr / ₹15.3cr / ₹15.1cr (flat, slightly falling); izmo Microsystems ₹3.8cr / ₹3.7cr / ₹9.2cr / ₹9.2cr. [IP Q1FY27]
- Geography: exports 93.2% of FY26 revenue and 85.8% of Q1 FY27; domestic (mostly izmo Microsystems) ₹19.4cr in FY26 and ₹9.3cr in Q1 FY27. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 segment note]
- Customers: 3,000+ dealers in 22–28 countries; 170 new clients in Q1 FY27 (117 US, 53 Europe/UK); gross revenue retention 98.5% (Q1 deck) — the MD quoted "over 93%" on the Q4 call. izmo Microsystems' top sector is Indian defence. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- izmo Microsystems capacity: current plant ~₹150cr of annual revenue (MD in Jun-26; he said ₹80–100cr in Nov-25); planned expansion to ~₹1,000–1,200cr of revenue capacity near the new Bengaluru airport aerospace park. Separately, a photonics fab proposal under the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) with 50% capex subsidy "if approved", 2–3 years away. [Q4 call; Q2 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Planned ₹125cr capex for the packaging expansion (machines from Europe, ~9-month lead time), funded by a still-pending equity-cum-debt raise; total investment in Microsystems to date was "not more than INR20 crores" (Nov-25). CWIP ₹5cr at Mar-26. [Q4 call; Q2 call; Screener]
- Company capitalises software and content development: intangible assets ₹276cr (56% of total assets), mostly "technical know-how" (gross ₹417cr); ~₹23cr added in FY26 against only ~₹14.5cr amortisation (implying a very long ~28-year average life). Capitalised spend is ~40% of FY26 operating profit, which flatters EBITDA. [AR FY26 Note 5; AR FY26 MD&A; derived]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹17cr on Screener (₹3.6cr per the MD&A); net worth ₹409cr. FY26 CFO ₹20cr, FCF −₹13cr; FCF has been ≤ ₹2cr every year FY22–FY26 despite reported profits — the gap is capitalised development and receivables. [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Receivables ₹129cr at Mar-26 (₹99cr a year earlier); debtor days 165 (161 in FY25, 69 in FY23); working-capital days 142. The MD says rental/OEM clients pay in 4–5 months. Auditor key audit matter: receivables over six months and unconfirmed balances. [Screener; Q4 call; AR FY26 auditor's report]
- No dividend. Last equity fundraise was in 2007 (MD). [Screener; Q2 call]

## Moat evidence
- Automotive image library built over 20+ years and embedded OEM programmes (Stellantis Europe, Ford preferred vendor for 2,200+ dealers, FordDirect marketplace listing); retention 93–98.5%. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Microsystems: claims to be the only Indian company doing 3D/flip-chip and silicon-photonics packaging; packaging partner for the IIT Madras (CPPICS) photonics programme; DSIR-recognised R&D; qualified defence supplier with repeat orders. Production-stage photonics modules (quantum random-number generator, QKD module, thin-film lithium niobate modulator) shown at SEMICON India, Sep-26; optical transceivers only in 2027. [Q4 call; BSE release 15-Sep-26]
- FrogData, called "high growth" in the stored profile, has been flat at ~₹15cr a quarter for a year. [IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- **Unaudited subsidiaries (auditor):** the consolidated opinion is given "subject to" the fact that **none of the foreign subsidiaries — ₹229.7cr of turnover (~81% of group revenue) and ₹200cr of net worth — are audited by any auditor**; their accounts are "solely certified by management" and only reviewed. This contradicts the MD's statement on the Q4 call that the UK, France and Belgium units are audited (KPMG). The AGM notice nonetheless told members there were no qualifications. [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report; Q4 call; AGM outcome 25-Sep-26]
- **Emphasis of matter:** the company "is responding to inquiries from Indian regulatory authorities"; scope and outcome uncertain (Notes 39 & 41). [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report]
- Transfer-pricing and income-tax disputes of ~₹17.7cr (AY 2009-10 to 2017-18), some at the Karnataka High Court — relevant because most profit is booked in the foreign subsidiaries. [AR FY26 Note 39]
- Aggressive capitalisation of development costs and near-zero free cash flow (see above). [AR FY26; Screener]
- Family board: MD Sanjay Soni, his mother Shashi Soni (Chairperson, WTD) and wife Kiran Soni (WTD and CFO). Remuneration is modest (₹3–4 lakh a month). [AR FY26 AGM notice]
- Funding and execution: the Microsystems plan needs ₹150cr that has been "30–45 days" away since June (and "end of this month" since November); dilution size unknown. [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- Changing numbers on calls: capacity (₹80–100cr vs ₹150cr), order book (₹60cr vs ₹40cr), retention (93% vs 98.5%). [Q2 call; Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## My read
Underneath the semiconductor story, IZMO is a ~₹240cr-a-year automotive software and imagery business growing low-to-mid teens, with 25% operating margins that are helped by capitalising ~₹23cr a year of development and that have never produced meaningful free cash flow. izmo Microsystems is real (defence repeat orders, SEMICON-stage photonics products) but small (₹9cr a quarter), behind its own targets, and dependent on a fundraise that keeps slipping. The biggest issue is audit quality: four-fifths of group revenue sits in foreign subsidiaries that no auditor audits, plus a regulatory-inquiry emphasis of matter. At the current price the stock needs about 16% a year of earnings growth (formula: (P/E × 0.1726)^0.1 − 1); I would not rely on that until the subsidiaries are audited, the raise is closed and Microsystems reaches ~₹12cr a quarter.
