# Network People Services Technologies (NPST) — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr). Screener consolidated figures used for all history. Note: the company's own "revenue" and "EBITDA" headlines include other income (interest on the ₹300cr raised), so they run ahead of Screener's operating figures. The two calls read are the Q4 FY26 earnings call (29 May 2026, official BSE transcript) and the Q1 FY27 earnings call (12 Aug 2026, official BSE transcript); the stored "General 29 Jun 2026" transcript is a Valorem CXO-meet, not an earnings call.

## Business and segments
- Payment-software supplier to Indian banks (UPI switches, IMPS, BBPS, mobile banking "Super App", CBDC) — a "Technology Service Provider" (TSP). Three verticals: **TSP** (licence + SaaS), **Payments Platform-as-a-Service (PPaaS)** (merchant acquiring, QR/soundbox, paid per transaction), **RegTech** (AI merchant-risk engine RIDP, dispute resolution; pay-per-use). 20+ banks, 100+ customers, ~90 million daily transactions. [IP Q1FY27]
- Mix swung violently: PPaaS was ~85% of FY25 revenue at 30–35% margin; after the sponsor-bank problems of FY24 and price collapse (from 40–50 paise to ~3 paise a transaction), NPST cut it to ~5% and TSP became ~90% of FY26 revenue at 10–20% margin. [CXO meet 29 Jun 2026; Q4 call]
- "Bank-in-a-Box" SaaS for cooperative/small banks launched Feb 2025-26; 12–15 tenants live, pipeline 50 banks, target 200+ tenants by FY29; SaaS margins start negative, 15–18% expected this year, up to 40% at scale. No churn yet. ARPU not disclosed. [CXO meet; Q4 call]
- International: Dubai subsidiary NPST Global Solutions LLC set up Apr 2025; now 10–12% of consolidated revenue, mostly TSP. Q1 won an LOI/order for a global telecom operator's Super App; RegTech order from a large PSU bank. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26]
- Management target mix (Q4 deck slide 17, as read by analysts): international ~25% FY27, ~40% FY28, ~50% FY29; TSP share to fall from 90–95% to 30–40% by FY29. On the Q1 call the JMD said "around 30%" international in 2 years and the CMD said "around 50% ... or maybe more" — inconsistent. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Single operating segment in the accounts — no audited split of verticals or geography. [AR FY26 notes]
- Subsidiaries: SSK Citizen Services, Timepay Digital Infotech (B2C app, stalled pending MDR), NPST Global Solutions LLC; none material. Parent lends float/loans to the first two. [AR FY26]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹128cr → FY25 ₹173cr → FY26 ₹195cr (+12.5%); OPM 34% → 35% → 26%; net profit ₹27cr → ₹45cr → ₹41cr (−9%). TTM revenue ₹218cr, PAT ₹45cr. [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / PAT): Jun-25 34 / 29% / 7; Sep-25 47 / 29% / 10; Dec-25 53 / 27% / 12; Mar-26 62 / 21% / 12; Jun-26 56 / 25% / 11. Q1 FY27 sales +66% YoY but −9% QoQ. [Screener]
- Company-basis Q1 FY27: revenue from operations ₹56.48cr, other income ₹4.94cr, "total income" ₹61.42cr; EBITDA incl. other income ₹18.79cr (30.6%); PAT ₹11.05cr (18%); EPS ₹5.26. Other income was ~26% of "EBITDA". [IP Q1FY27]
- Cost lines Q1: project expenses ₹12.66cr, employee ₹10.12cr (up only 7% YoY), other expenses ₹19.85cr (of which ~₹17cr is hardware purchase for turnkey projects, "a grouping issue"). Depreciation jumped to ₹4.09cr from ₹1.43cr as capitalised products amortise. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- FY26 company-basis: total income ₹209.4cr, EBITDA incl. other income ₹65.1cr (31.1% vs 37.4%), PAT ₹40.8cr; Q4 total income ₹68.46cr, EBITDA ₹19.26cr. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- Unit economics: UPI PPaaS price fell to ~3 paise per transaction; RegTech engine trained on ~650–700 million transactions and 3 lakh merchants, claims 98% accuracy. [CXO meet; Q1 call]
- Returns fell after the equity raise: ROCE 19.7%, ROE 15%; book value ₹211/share. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (29 May 2026): ~70% revenue CAGR for 3 years, FY29 revenue ₹850–900cr; FY27 "anywhere around 70%", ₹340–350cr "anything around that range"; ~40% of FY27 international business "in our kitty"; full-year FY27 EBITDA back to "the same level what you have seen last to last year" (FY25 ~37%); PAT margin back to ~25% "by this financial year"; debtor days ~180 then reducing; growth to be lumpy, heavy in Q3–Q4. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (12 Aug 2026): growth guide softened to **60–70%** (₹320–340cr "around that"); FY27 EBITDA "around 30%" (company basis) — lower than the implied ~37% of the Q4 call; 35%+ EBITDA in two years; 60–70% CAGR for 2–3 years, organic only. Management insisted "no change in guidance". [Q1 call]
- Delivery vs promise: Q1 sales ₹56cr vs the ~₹85–90cr a quarter needed for the rest of the year to hit ₹320cr (analyst calculated ₹270–280cr in 9 months). Q1 PAT margin 18% vs 25% promised for the year. **Behind** on revenue; margin target quietly **cut** from ~37% to ~30%; growth range **trimmed** from 70% to 60–70%. [Q1 call; Screener]
- M&A: Q4 said a couple of deals were dropped; Q1 said three targets short-listed, deployment "over the next 2 quarters", but "Q2 is early". Only ~10–15% of the ₹300cr used after 8+ months. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Product development is capitalised: fixed assets rose from ₹13cr (FY25) to ₹55cr (FY26); intangibles under development commitment ₹4.7cr. This is why employee cost barely rises while "investment" goes on. [Screener; AR FY26 note 35]
- AI programme: aims for 30% efficiency in support, 50% faster development, revenue per employee +300% in 3 years. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- ₹300cr preferential issue to Tata Mutual Fund (14.465 lakh shares at ₹2,074, allotted 5 Sep 2025); ₹277.86cr unutilised at 31 Mar 2026, parked in FDs. [AR FY26]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash ~₹290cr: other bank balances ₹267cr + cash, borrowings ₹14cr (Mar-26). [AR FY26; Screener]
- Operating cash flow FY26 **−₹45cr** (FY25 +₹29cr); free cash flow −₹93cr. [Screener]
- Trade receivables ₹103.7cr at Mar-26 vs ₹31.8cr a year earlier; ₹65cr of it 0–6 months overdue, ₹3cr beyond 6 months. Debtor days 195 (67 in FY25). Management blames longer TSP credit terms taken on to replace PPaaS revenue. [AR FY26 note 9; Screener; Q4 call]
- Dividends are token: ₹4.09cr paid in FY26 (~₹2/share, ~0.1% yield). [AR FY26 Directors' Report; Screener]
- Contingent liability: bank guarantees ~₹15cr. [AR FY26 note 35]

## Moat evidence
- Claims ~7–8% of India's UPI volume runs on its platforms and ~35 million users on one PSU bank app. [CXO meet 29 Jun 2026]
- Switching costs are real in bank core-payment integrations, but pricing power is not: PPaaS pricing collapsed to ~3 paise and the domestic TSP business earns only 10–20%. [CXO meet; Q4 call]
- Competitors named: Mindgate, Olive, Sarvatra (TSP); large private banks build in-house, so NPST avoids them. [CXO meet]
- RegTech first-mover claim ("we do not have any competition at all right now") — unverified; only one large PSU order disclosed. [Q1 call]
- Certifications: PCI-DSS, ISO 27001, CERT-In. [CXO meet]

## Risks and red flags
- **Headline inflation**: company "revenue" includes other income (₹4.9cr in Q1, ~₹14cr in FY26); on Screener's basis Q1 OPM was 25%, not 30.6%. [IP Q1FY27; Screener]
- **Working capital**: receivables tripled, CFO negative; growth is being bought with credit. [AR FY26; Screener]
- **Regulatory**: NSE and BSE each fined ₹50,000 (Sep 2025) for issuing preferential shares without prior in-principle approval. [AR FY26 Directors' Report]
- **Use of proceeds**: CARE Ratings (monitoring agency) flagged a "technical deviation" of ₹3.18cr spent on rent and salaries under "general corporate purposes" (Q3 FY26); Board later defined GCP by circular resolution. [AR FY26]
- **Audit**: unmodified opinion; key audit matter is revenue recognition on fixed-price contracts by percentage of completion — judgement-heavy and relevant given milestone-driven lumpy revenue. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **MDR dependence**: PPaaS revival and TimePay depend on a UPI merchant-fee regime that is not yet notified; management gives no numbers. [Q1 call]
- Promoter holding down 7 pts in 3 years (now 60.5%) — mainly dilution from the preference issue. [Screener]
- Guidance credibility: the company has changed the FY27 margin and growth wording within one quarter while saying nothing changed.

## What changed between the two calls
- Growth language slid from "70%" / ₹340–350cr to "60–70%" / ₹320–340cr; EBITDA target from FY25-level (~37%) to "around 30%".
- Q4 call: debtor days and negative cash flow dominated; Q1 call: MDR on UPI dominated (a Parliament bill), receivables not discussed.
- International moved from "40% in kitty" to actual revenue (10–12% of Q1), plus one telecom Super App order and "two more deals in the pipeline".
- Acquisition talk moved from "dropped deals" to "three short-listed".

## Best management quotes
- "You should definitely be looking at yearly numbers that is coming out." — CMD Deepak Thakur [Q1 call]
- "The result versus the effort is not great." — on PPaaS [Q4 call]
- "Almost 40% is in our kitty right now at the beginning of the year." — on FY27 international [Q4 call]

## My read
The business is in transition, not yet improving: operating margin has fallen from 35% to ~25%, receivables have tripled and FY26 operating cash flow was negative, while the ₹300cr raise mostly sits in deposits propping up reported profit. Hitting even ₹320cr in FY27 needs roughly ₹88cr a quarter for the next three quarters versus ₹56cr in Q1 — a big H2 back-load resting on international milestones. At a P/E in the 80s the price assumes the 60–70% CAGR happens; a miss would hurt badly. The view would turn positive on two consecutive quarters of ₹75cr+ sales with positive operating cash flow and falling debtor days, or on a notified UPI MDR regime that revives PPaaS.
