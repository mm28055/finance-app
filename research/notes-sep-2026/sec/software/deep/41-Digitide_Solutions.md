# Digitide Solutions — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated. The stored Q1 FY27 transcript is AI-generated (tijoristack) and has errors (CEO's name, logo count, headcount); every call figure below is checked against the official BSE transcript (28 Jul 2026). The stored Q4 FY26 transcript is the official BSE filing (call 19 May 2026). Digitide was demerged from Quess Corp (effective 11 Jun 2025 listing); FY25 is a 13-month period on Screener, so there is no clean FY24.

## Business and segments
- India's largest India-to-India BPM (call centres, collections with ~15,000 field staff across 95% of pin codes, back office, payroll/HR outsourcing) plus a "Tech & Digital" (T&D) arm (insurance IT platforms, cloud/AI services, payroll SaaS). 300+ customers, ~54,000 staff, 40+ locations in 5 countries. Owns 73.39% of listed **Alldigi Tech** (international CLM and payroll; FY26 revenue ₹599cr, EBITDA ₹162cr). Promoter: Ajit Isaac group, 57.33%. [IP Q1FY27 slides 6–7; Screener]
- Segments Q1 FY27: BPM ₹537.7cr (69.4%, −0.2% YoY, −2.4% QoQ); T&D ₹237.4cr (30.6%, +20.3% YoY, −4.7% QoQ). Segment EBITDA before corporate cost: BPM ₹72.8cr (13.5%, vs 16.3% in Q4); T&D ₹18.9cr (8.0%, vs 12.1% in Q4); total ₹91.7cr (11.8%, vs 15.0%). [IP Q1FY27 slide 22]
- Geography: International ₹295.6cr (38.1%, +10.2% YoY) vs Domestic 61.9%. Verticals Q1: BFSI 51.3%, healthcare 17.3% (12.5% a year ago), rest manufacturing/energy, fast-growth tech, others. US healthcare revenue "crossed about $25 million" in FY26, targeted at 12–15% of revenue by FY31. [IP Q1FY27 slide 24; Q4 call]
- Concentration: top-10 clients 37.4% of Q1 revenue (35.4% in Q4, 35.6% a year ago) — concentration rose, reversing the FY26 claim of falling dependence. [IP Q1FY27 slide 24; AR FY26 p.~6]
- Headcount fell from 56.3k (Q1 FY26) to ~53.5k (Q1 FY27); management expects further decline "over the next couple of quarters" as AI is overlaid on delivery. AI-led new revenue ~₹15cr in Q1 (~2%); 5.7 mn bot interactions at 80–85% containment. [IP Q1FY27 slide 23; Q1 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- FY25 (13 months) revenue ₹3,269cr, OPM 15%, PAT ₹138cr; FY26 revenue ₹3,080cr (+7.1% on a comparable 12-month basis per company; constant-currency +5.2%), OPM 11%, PAT ₹6cr (adjusted ₹70cr after ₹65cr exceptional items: demerger costs and labour-code past-service cost). TTM revenue ₹3,120cr, PAT −₹1cr; ROCE 10.3%, ROE −0.7%. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / PAT): Jun-25 736 / 11.2% / 9.7; Sep-25 764 / 11.1% / 2.9; Dec-25 780 / 11.2% / −2.1; Mar-26 800 / 11.0% / −5.0; Jun-26 775 / 9.9% / 2.9. Margin was ~15% before the demerger (Jun-24 15.2%, Dec-24 15.1%). [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 bridge: EBITDA ₹76.9cr, down ₹11cr QoQ, of which ₹9.9cr was a Q4 one-off (short-term rent capitalised as leases) and ~₹10cr was new labour-code and state minimum-wage cost from 1 April. Depreciation ₹55cr (₹36cr is lease right-of-use); EBIT ₹22cr; finance cost ₹15cr (₹11cr lease interest). [Q1 call; Screener]
- Below-EBITDA drag is structural: annual cash lease outflow ₹175–180cr in FY27 (₹168cr in FY26) sits under depreciation and interest, so ~11% EBITDA leaves only ~3% EBIT. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Productivity: revenue per FTE ₹5.84 lakh in FY26 (+11.4%). [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (19 May 2026, CEO Gurmeet Chahal): FY27 "accelerated double-digit revenue growth", quantified as "early to mid-teens"; EBITDA margin +100 bps by exit of FY27; at least one acquisition this year ("six potential targets, two at an advanced stage"); OCF ~70% of EBITDA; $1 bn (₹8,400cr) revenue by FY31 incl. ₹1,700cr inorganic. TCV ₹620cr in Q4, ₹2,355cr in FY26. [Q4 call]
- Chahal resigned effective 31 May 2026 — twelve days after that call; **Sameer Ahluwalia** (ex-Alvarez & Marsal, Firstsource, HCL Tech) took over on 1 Jun 2026. [AR FY26 AGM notice item 5]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue −3.1% QoQ — **missed**; TCV ₹205cr vs ₹600cr+ in each of the prior two quarters — **missed**; EBITDA margin 9.9% vs 11.0% — **missed**; PAT back to +₹2.9cr — small positive. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Guidance reset on the Q1 call: revenue growth guidance **dropped** ("measure us on profitability growth rather than the revenue itself"; percentages promised "in subsequent quarters"); CFO: "on track with our 200-bps margin expansion in this fiscal" — a bigger number than Q4's 100 bps, but from a lower base, and unexplained. $1 bn FY31 downgraded to a "North Star". Book-to-bill conversion 11–13%; some client work deferred to Q2/Q3. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- Scale in Indian BPM (largest India-to-India BPM position, ~15,000 collections staff), Tier-2/3 delivery cost base, and 7-year "Great Place to Work" status. ~70% of revenue is annuity. Everest Group "Major Contender" ratings in P&C insurance BPS and multi-country payroll. [IP Q1FY27 slides 6, 9, 11]
- Pricing power is weak: the ~₹10cr/quarter wage-code hit is being negotiated with clients rather than passed through; management is walking away from "margin-dilutive" India BPM accounts. [Q1 call]
- Platforms (SmartPay payroll, DigiCollect, insurance platform) and hyperscaler partnerships (AWS, Azure, GCP; ~15% of pipeline hyperscaler-led) give some switching cost in T&D, but T&D margin (8%) is below mid-tier IT peers. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- **Leadership churn:** CEO exit 12 days after an upbeat call; Chief Revenue & Growth Officer Saket Bhatnagar resigned 21 Sep 2026; CFO Suraj Prasad moves to strategy from 1 Oct 2026 (Harigovind Krishnasamy, ex-Matrimony.com CFO, takes over). Five new senior appointments on 8 Sep incl. COO Ruchi Ahluwalia. [AR FY26; BSE filings 8 & 22 Sep 2026; News: TipRanks/Business Upturn, Sep 2026]
- **Group structure:** listed subsidiary Alldigi holds much of the profitable international business; investors on both calls noted that Digitide ex-Alldigi loses money; "not all of the corporate expenses are cross-charged". No timetable for a merger. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Labour-cost regulation:** new labour codes and 1 April state minimum-wage revisions; ₹16cr past-service exceptional in Q4 FY26. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Auditor (Deloitte Haskins & Sells):** unmodified opinion; key audit matter = revenue recognition (effort- and unit-based contracts, unbilled revenue). Audit-trail (edit-log) not enabled for payroll and procurement software and for one accounting package for Apr–Dec 2025. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Contingent liabilities (standalone) ₹12.9cr: PF damages/interest ₹2.9cr, PF on allowances ₹8.7cr (Bombay HC stay), Pune professional tax ₹1.4cr. Corporate guarantee of ₹118.8cr for MFXchange expired in FY26. Pass-through invoicing with Quess for un-novated contracts not treated as related-party. [AR FY26 notes 34–35 and RPT note]
- AI deflation of BPM seat revenue; BFSI is 51% of revenue and cost-focused. [IP Q1FY27]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone flipped from "Consistent Measurable Progression … FY27 a clear inflection point" to "Q1 was clearly below our expectations" and "we will not chase top line for its own sake". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Numbers: TCV ₹620cr → ₹205cr; revenue growth guidance withdrawn; margin target moved from +100 bps exit-FY27 to +200 bps for the year; top-10 concentration up; DSO up 7 days; segment margins fell in both BPM and T&D.
- New: portfolio "treatment — fix, divest or exit" of low-margin work; four-move plan; AI funnel ₹100–150cr; possible separate AI business unit.

## My read
The business is not improving yet: margins have slid from ~15% pre-demerger to 9.9%, revenue fell sequentially, bookings collapsed, and three of the top four executives who presented the FY27 plan in May have left or changed roles. The new CEO's restructuring (exit low-margin India BPM, reprice for the wage code, push West/digital) is sensible but will shrink revenue before it lifts margin, and leases absorb most of the EBITDA, so profit is thin. At a ₹1,172cr market cap (~0.4x sales, ~1.4x book) the price already assumes little; what it needs is proof of the 200 bps margin gain in Q2–Q3 and TCV back above ~₹500cr a quarter. A clean Alldigi merger, a repricing win on wages or a margin-accretive acquisition would improve the view; further senior exits or another guidance cut would worsen it.
