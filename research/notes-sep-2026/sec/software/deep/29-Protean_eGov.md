# Protean eGov Technologies — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr). Screener consolidated figures for history. Both stored transcripts (Q4 FY26, May 2026; Q1 FY27, Aug 2026) are AI-generated; both were checked against the official BSE transcripts. The Q1 copy names the wrong CEO (see Stored-data errors). Important: the company's "EBITDA" **includes other income** (treasury yield on ~₹800cr), so its margins run far above Screener's operating margins.

## Business and segments
- Formerly NSDL e-Governance; runs India's digital public-infrastructure rails: PAN issuance and tax-information network (for the Income Tax Department), the Central Recordkeeping Agency for NPS/APY/UPS pensions (for PFRDA), identity services (Aadhaar authentication, e-KYC, e-Sign, online PAN verification), and new RFP-led projects. No promoter; widely held (DIIs 21%). [AR FY26; Screener]
- Q1 FY27 revenue split (from presentation table): Tax ~₹100cr (40%, flat YoY), CRA ₹82cr (33%, +7–8%), Identity ₹27cr (11%, +16%), New initiatives ₹42cr (17%, vs ₹11cr, +276%). FY26: Tax ₹498cr (+17.5%), CRA ₹304cr (+7.5%), Identity ₹92cr, New ₹123cr (~3x; 10% of revenue vs 4%). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Market position: PAN share 62% in Q1 (59% in FY26; 64% cumulative), issued >1 crore PANs in Q1; CRA 97% share, took 95% of new subscribers (3.9 million in Q1), 1,000+ new corporates in Q1 (record). Only company offering all four foundational identity services. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- New initiatives: 190 UIDAI Aadhaar Seva Kendras (102 live across 25 states per Q1 deck; full rollout by Sep–Oct 2026, "bid estimate" ~₹200cr a year at full run-rate); CKYC registry rebuild for CERSAI; Bima Sugam insurance platform (~₹100cr over ~72 months incl. support); ONDC; AgriStack; Ethiopia digital-agriculture mandate; NSDL Payments Bank 4.95% stake (₹30.2cr). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Pricing: CRA moved to AUM-linked fees for private-sector pensions (short-term margin dip, long-term positive); tax processing charges ~35–38% of revenue. [Q4 call]
- People: 839 permanent employees (Mar-26), plus ~2,000 entry-level staff being added for Aadhaar centres. [AR FY26; Q4 call]
- Customer concentration: effectively the Government of India (ITD, PFRDA, UIDAI, CERSAI, IRDAI-backed Bima Sugam) — not quantified in the AR. Single reported segment.

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (May 2026): no numeric FY27 guidance; "endeavour to improve our margins significantly over the next 2 to 3 years"; new businesses to be ~25% of revenue in 2–3 years; order book "more than ₹1,500cr"; all 190 ASKs live by Sep–Oct 2026, full revenue by Feb–Mar 2027; CFO warned of a timing mismatch (costs before revenue). [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (12 Aug 2026, new MD & CEO Ajay Rajan's first): no numeric guidance; margins to "stabilize from Q2 or Q3"; no other one-time costs expected except ASK; "margins improving substantially ... over the next 2-3 years"; M&A in BFSI tech being evaluated; order book not mentioned. [Q1 call]
- Delivery vs promise: revenue growth kept up (+19%) and new-business share hit 17% — ahead of the 25%-in-3-years path; but profitability **missed** badly (PAT −75%), more than the "timing mismatch" implied. ASK rollout **on track**. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Fixed assets ₹86cr (FY24) → ₹167cr (FY26) plus CWIP ₹19cr: sovereign cloud, ASK fit-outs, platforms; depreciation now ~₹70cr a year run-rate. [Screener]
- ASK is a managed-operations contract billed monthly on volumes; working capital light apart from furniture/office set-up; government paying on time. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and marketable securities >₹850cr (Mar-26), >₹800cr (Jun-26); "zero debt" (Screener's ₹87cr borrowings are presumably lease liabilities). [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- CFO FY26 ₹78cr (FY25 ₹193cr); FCF ₹12cr. Debtor days 75 (63 in FY25). [Screener]
- Dividend ₹10/share for FY26 (~₹44cr, ~40% payout); yield ~1.45%. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Contingent liabilities: income-tax matters ₹38.9cr, other claims ~₹2cr. [AR FY26 note 34]

## Moat evidence
- Regulatory franchises: CRA 97% share; PAN 62% with share gains even as industry PAN issuance fell 12% in Q1 (Aadhaar no longer accepted as date-of-birth proof after a Supreme Court ruling). [Q1 call]
- Scale: 58.3 crore PANs issued to date; 9+ crore pension accounts; cumulative 1,827 crore online PAN verifications. [IP Q1FY27]
- Risk to moat: PAN 2.0 (new ITD system) "still some distance away"; management says assisted distribution will stay relevant but has no contract clarity. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin quality**: over a third of FY26 "EBITDA" (₹68cr of ₹188cr) was other income; core OPM 12% (FY26) vs 18% (FY22) and 5% in Q1 FY27. ASK is "not EBITDA-accretive" per the CFO. [Screener; Q1 call]
- **Leadership churn**: MD & CEO Suresh Sethi resigned effective 1 Apr 2026; director Jayesh Sule (Jul 2025) and senior managers Bertram Dsouza (Dec 2025), Metesh Singh Bhati (Dec 2025) and Dattaram Mhadgut (Jun 2026) left; Ajay Rajan (ex-Deutsche Bank, Yes Bank) joined 1 Jun 2026; V Easwaran became whole-time director Dec 2025. [AR FY26; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Policy dependence**: PAN 2.0, PFRDA fee changes, government RFP timing. [Q4 call]
- **Audit**: unqualified; key audit matter is revenue recognition (fraud-risk presumption). [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Capital allocation**: large idle cash now earmarked for BFSI acquisitions — integration risk under a brand-new CEO. [Q1 call]
- Small loss-making subsidiary (Account Aggregator, −₹1.4cr). [AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- CEO changed (interim WTD V Easwaran → Ajay Rajan); strategy reframed as "DPI 2.0", "DPI-in-a-box", solution-led selling and international partnerships.
- Order-book figure (₹1,500cr+) dropped from the narrative; M&A became an explicit priority; portfolio review signalled (Cloud and Account Aggregator "small ... sharpening the commercial model").
- The warned "timing mismatch" turned into a 75% PAT drop.

## My read
Protean owns durable government franchises (PAN, pensions) and its revenue is growing again, helped by new projects, but profitability has not followed: core operating margin has fallen from 18% to low teens, and the headline EBITDA leans on interest income. The stock trades on a P/E in the 30s, which needs roughly high-teens earnings growth — plausible only if Aadhaar centres, CKYC and Bima Sugam turn profitable in H2 FY27 and the new CEO lifts margins as promised. The view improves on Q2–Q3 core operating margin back above 12% and a clear PAN 2.0 role; it worsens on a pricey acquisition or more leadership exits.
