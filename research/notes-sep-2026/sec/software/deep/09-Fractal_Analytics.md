# Fractal Analytics (FRACTAL) — deep research, Phase A1

**Snapshot.** Screener code FRACTAL. Price ₹750 (25 Sep 2026), market cap about ₹13,000 cr. The stock is 33% below its 52-week high of ₹1,120 and close to its low of ₹732. It listed in FY26 (IPO); FY26 was its first full year as a listed company. Shareholding: promoters 16.97%, FIIs 40.5%, DIIs 13.3%. [Screener; Q4 call]

**Transcript check.** Both stored transcripts are the company's official filings, not AI versions: Q1 FY27 (call on 24 Jul 2026, filed 27 Jul) and Q4 FY26 (call on 12 May 2026). One labelling slip: in the official Q1 transcript, client-size bands are called "INR 20 million" etc. The fact sheet shows they are **US$** bands.

## Business and segments
- **What it sells.** Fractal is a pure-play enterprise AI and analytics company. It does AI-led transformation (redesigning workflows), AI foundations (data, ontology and agent layers) and AI for work and workforce. All three are sold on its Cogentiq agentic platform. It targets "Must-Win Clients": companies with $10 bn+ revenue, $20 bn+ market cap or 30 mn+ customers. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Segments.**
  - Fractal.ai (services and platforms): Q1 FY27 revenue ₹894 cr, +20.6% YoY. Adjusted segment margin 18.8%.
  - Fractal Alpha (Asper.ai, a revenue-growth SaaS for consumer-goods firms, plus Analytics Vidhya): revenue ₹24 cr, +19.6%. Adjusted EBITDA loss ₹14 cr (-58% of revenue). The loss widened because a product team (iqigai) was moved into this segment.
  - Associate Qure.ai (31.5% share, medical imaging AI): revenue ₹24 cr (+160%). Fractal's share of its loss was ₹23 cr.

  [Fact sheet Q1'27; Q1 call]
- **Verticals, Q1 FY27 (Fractal.ai).**

  | Vertical | Share of revenue | Growth YoY |
  |---|---|---|
  | Consumer goods & retail (CPGR) | 37.8% | +19% |
  | Healthcare & life sciences (HLS) | 23.5% | +69% |
  | Technology, media & telecom (TMT) | 18.0% | -22% |
  | Banking & finance (BFSI) | 13.0% | +36% |
  | Others | 7.7% | n/a |

  Growth excluding TMT was 37% (prepared remarks), or 35% (in Q&A). [Fact sheet Q1'27; Q1 call]
- **Geography, Q1 FY27 (Fractal.ai).** Americas 67.4% (+24% YoY), Europe 21.4% (+25%), APAC & others 11.2% (-2%). For FY26: Americas 67.3%, Europe & UK 19.9%, APAC 12.8%. **The US/Americas share is about 67%.** [Fact sheet Q1'27; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Client concentration.** Top 10 clients were 51.8% of revenue (55.9% a year ago) and the top 20 were 70.6%. Clients above $20 mn: 5 (6 at Q4; one TMT client slipped below). Clients above $1 mn: 58 (54 a year ago). There are 112 Must-Win Clients, making up 85.3% of revenue. [Fact sheet Q1'27; Q1 call]
- **Pricing model.** 42% of revenue is output, outcome or licence-based; 58% is input-based (time and materials). Licences alone are about 3% of revenue. The target is 60% non-input revenue "in the next two to three years". [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **People.** 6,029 employees at Q1 FY27 end (5,868 at Q4; 5,298 a year earlier). Trailing-twelve-month attrition was 15.6% (15.2% at Q4; 17.3% a year earlier). Revenue per billable employee in FY26 was ₹75 lakh ($85,000), +5% in rupees. **Utilisation is not disclosed.** [Fact sheet Q1'27; Q4 call]
- **Deal wins.** Fractal discloses no TCV (total contract value). Q1 highlights:
  - "one of the largest single programs in Fractal's history", an AI-foundations and data-modernisation deal for a large healthcare player;
  - a pilot MoU with Mumbai's municipal corporation (BMC) for Vaidya.ai / Cogentiq Health;
  - more than 10 early Cogentiq client wins;
  - five of the largest deals came through partners (Databricks, OpenAI, Anthropic).

  At Q4: a top-5 US life-sciences company as strategic partner. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 (12 May 2026).**
  - No numeric guidance; "our historical revenue growth rates are a good indication" (FY26: 19% in rupees, 13% CC).
  - TMT "client-specific growth issues to have worked themselves out through this year".
  - 60% output/outcome/licence revenue in 2–3 years.
  - Keep expanding margins while maximising growth.
  - R&D can rise toward 10% of revenue, but only funded by gross-margin gains.

  [Q4 call]
- **Q1 FY27 (24 Jul 2026).**
  - CC growth of 9% is below the historical 13% and TMT fell further (-22%). Srikanth Velamakanni: "we have certainly underachieved".
  - Management now expects "healthy sequential growth in TMT" in Q2 and no longer blames specific clients.
  - Outcome-based share is 42%, so the 60% target is kept.
  - Adjusted EBITDA margin improved YoY (+189 bps), as promised.
  - Merit increases took effect on 1 June (normally 1 April). The full-P&L impact is about 120–130 bps, and Q2 carries three months of it.
  - **Verdict: growth slipped; margin promise kept; no formal guidance to cut.** [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- The business is light on assets. Fixed assets were ₹765 cr at Mar-26, including right-of-use and capitalised R&D intangibles; there were no capital commitments at Mar-26 (₹25 cr a year earlier). [Screener; AR FY26 note on commitments]
- The main "capex" is capitalised R&D, about ₹20 cr a quarter, plus acquisitions. The auditor made the **capitalisation of internally generated intangibles** a key audit matter, along with fixed-price revenue (percentage of completion) and goodwill impairment. [AR FY26 auditor's report]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Cash.** At Mar-26, cash, mutual funds and deposits were ₹2,052 cr, including ₹957 cr of IPO proceeds. At Jun-26 the figure was ₹1,639 cr, after long-term debt of ₹286 cr was repaid in April 2026. The company is now debt-free apart from leases; Screener's borrowings of ₹418 cr at Mar-26 include leases and the pre-repayment loan. [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Operating cash flow.** CFO was ₹160 cr (FY24), ₹397 cr (FY25) and ₹409 cr (FY26). That is about 70% of adjusted EBITDA, which the CFO called "representative of our steady state". Q1 FY27 CFO was -₹103 cr, because the prior year's variable pay is paid in Q1. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Receivables (DSO).** 71 days in Q1 FY27, of which about 54 days is billed receivables. It was 72 days in FY26, 74 in FY25 and 86 in FY24. Screener debtor days: 89 / 77 / 79. [Q1 call; Q4 call; Screener]
- **Dividends and buybacks.** None yet. Currency hedging uses forward cover for cash-flow hedges only. [Q1 call]

## Moat evidence
- **Retention and satisfaction.** Net revenue retention was 117.3% in Q1 (108.4% a year ago). NPS was 77 (81 at Q4). Q1 client churn was 0%. New clients added only 3 points of growth, so growth comes mostly from existing accounts. [Fact sheet Q1'27; Q1 call]
- **Account depth.** Largest relationships run $2 mn to $100 mn a year per client, and the number of $1 mn+ clients rose from 53 to 59 in FY26. [Q4 call]
- **Intellectual property.**
  - Asper ARR (annual recurring revenue) was $9 mn (+59% YoY), used by about 20 consumer-goods clients.
  - Vaidya 2.0 was the first model to score above 50 on OpenAI's HealthBench Hard.
  - PiEvolve ranks near the top of OpenAI's MLE-bench.

  These show product depth, but product revenue is still small (licences about 3%). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Pricing.** Gross margin rose 218 bps in FY26 from the shift to output-based contracts, price rises and productivity (before currency and merit effects). [Q4 call]
- **Limits.** Management concedes the market is "becoming more competitive" and that AI "compression" shrinks the effort per project. Ad-hoc analysis and dashboards are "going to go to zero". [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **TMT decline.** TMT fell 1% in FY26, 19% in Q4 and 22% in Q1. The causes: one APAC client moved work into a joint venture, a software client restructured, and revenue at a "MAG 7" client was delayed by data issues. Big-tech clients are shifting spend from operating costs to AI capex. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **CFO exit.** CFO Ashwath Bhat left after the Q1 call "for personal reasons", about five months after the IPO. No successor had been named at the call. [Q1 call]
- **Concentration.** The top 10 clients are 51.8% of revenue and the top 20 are 70.6%. [Fact sheet Q1'27]
- **Associate losses.** Fractal's share of Qure.ai losses runs at about ₹23 cr a quarter. That is roughly a quarter of profit before the associate share (₹95.7 cr in Q1). [Q1 call]
- **Earnings quality.**
  - Adjusted EBITDA excludes ESOP costs, retention bonuses and exceptional items.
  - FY26 profit included a ₹53.4 cr deferred-tax asset and the Q3 tax credit.
  - Some R&D is capitalised.
  - FY26 exceptional items: ₹11 cr for the new labour codes.

  [AR FY26 note 26.1; MD&A]
- **Tax compounding.** In FY23 the parent company and all its directors received a show-cause notice over prosecution for late deposit of ₹40.5 cr of TDS (tax deducted at source) in FY20; the TDS was later paid with interest. The case was settled by paying ₹2.6 cr of compounding charges in FY26, with no admission of guilt. It is closed but is a past governance lapse. [AR FY26 note to other expenses]
- **Contingent liabilities.** Small: ₹9.7 cr, mainly tax and GST appeals. The audit opinion is unmodified. [AR FY26 note; auditor's report]
- **Currency.** About 11 points of Q1 rupee growth came from the weaker rupee. A rupee rebound would hit both growth and margin. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- **Tone.** Q4's "the era of enterprise AI is here" (with the 100x opportunity framing) gave way in Q1 to "our quarter one is not as great as we would like it to be", with execution named as the gap.
- **New.**
  - A reorganised go-to-market under a new Chief Commercial Officer (AIT/AIF/AIW).
  - Partner-led deals.
  - The CFO's exit.
  - A later wage cycle.
  - A decision to stop citing client-specific issues.
  - A promise to report Cogentiq revenue from next quarter.
- **Numbers.** CC growth 10% (Q4) → 9% (Q1); TMT -19% → -22%; adjusted EBITDA 22.1% → 16.8% (Q1 is seasonally low); NRR 111.7% → 117.3%.

## My read
The underlying franchise is strong: 117% net revenue retention, 0% churn, rising $1 mn+ client counts, and margins up 1.9 points YoY. But dollar/CC growth has slowed to about 9%. Much of the rupee growth and margin gain is the weaker currency. TMT is still shrinking, and the CFO has left. At about 39 times trailing earnings (Screener), with profit flattered by tax items and diluted by Qure.ai losses, the price needs a return to mid-teens CC growth and steady adjusted EBITDA near 18%. I would turn more positive on TMT returning to sequential growth in Q2, Cogentiq revenue being disclosed and growing, and a credible new CFO. I would turn more negative if CC growth stays in single digits or the rupee strengthens.

## Stored-data errors
- **Metrics block.** Q1 FY27 (912.5 / 120 / 13.2% / 72) and Q4 FY26 (886 / 173 / 19.5% / 116) match Screener. They use Screener's operating profit, which is not the company's adjusted EBITDA (16.8% and 22.1%). The stored analysis mixes the two; for example, it says adjusted EBITDA is "roughly 17%" while the block says 13.2%.
- **Stored profile.** It says "Net Promoter Score of 77 … zero churn among its existing client base". That is correct for Q1 only; Q4 NPS was 81.
- **Stored profile, missing context.**
  - It omits that CC growth was only 9%, and that a 273 bps currency benefit explains all of the gross-margin gain.
  - It omits the Q4 one-off grant and variable-pay benefit of 147 bps.
  - It says "debt-free", which is correct only after April 2026; Mar-26 borrowings were ₹286 cr.
- **Official Q1 transcript.** It labels client bands as "INR 20 million"; the fact sheet shows US$20 mn (and similar). The stored analysis does not repeat the error.
- **Stored analysis.** It says management "maintained their stance of not providing explicit numerical … guidance". That is correct. At Q4, though, management tied expectations to historical growth rates, which Q1 then missed (9% CC against 13%). That miss should be recorded as a shortfall.
