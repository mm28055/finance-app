# Saksoft — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated; $ = US dollars. Stored transcripts are AI-generated (tijoristack) and contain wrong figures; everything below is checked against the official BSE transcripts.

## Business and segments
- Mid-size IT services firm (Chennai) doing application development, legacy modernisation, testing, managed services, Salesforce and ServiceNow work, for clients with $100 mn–$3 bn revenue ("sweet spot" $750 mn–$1 bn). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.4]
- Verticals Q1 FY27: emerging verticals (hi-tech, telecom, media) 45%, banking & financial services 30%, transport & logistics 16%, digital commerce 9%. FY26: 47/31/14/8. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Geography Q1 FY27: Americas 52%, Europe 27%, APAC & others 21% (FY26: 50/29/21). Target: US to at least 65% "in the next two or three years". [Q1 call]
- Delivery: onsite/offshore 43%/57% (FY26 44/56); outcome-based billing only "about 10%", the rest time-and-material. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Headcount 2,434 (2,223 technical, 211 support) at Jun-26 vs 2,494 at Mar-26 and 2,277 technical — down ~4.5% in FY26 and falling again; utilisation (ex-trainees) 83%; voluntary attrition 13.4% in FY26 (10.8% in FY25). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.9; AR FY26 BRSR]
- Acquisitions: Augmento Labs (Jun-24, being merged — NCLT sanctioned 16 Sep 2026), Ceptes (Salesforce, Oct-24), Zetechno (ServiceNow, Jan-25) for ₹164.5cr in total. [CARE Sep-25; BSE filing 23 Sep 2026]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue FY24 762 → FY25 883 → FY26 1,007 (+14%); OPM 18% → 17% → 19%; net profit 96 → 109 → 133. [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 249 / 18% / 32; Sep-25 258 / 20% / 36; Dec-25 251 / 18% / 29; Mar-26 249 / 18% / 36; Jun-26 249 / 18% / 29. Revenue has been flat for four quarters. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹248.6cr (−0.2% YoY, −0.1% QoQ); EBITDA ₹45.4cr (18.26%); PAT ₹29.3cr (11.78%, −9.4% YoY) — lower other income (₹1.8cr vs ₹7.4cr in Q4) and a 31% tax rate. [IP Q1FY27 p.10; Screener]
- Organic growth is weak: CARE says acquisitions added ~₹115cr in FY25 and ~₹52cr in Q1 FY26 alone; management says ~3 points of FY26's 14% growth was currency. Organic constant-currency growth has therefore been close to zero for about a year. [CARE Sep-25; Q4 call]
- Revenue per technical employee ≈ ₹248.6cr × 4 ÷ 2,223 ≈ ₹45 lakh a year (~$52k). Employees plus subcontractors are ~77% of costs. [Q1 call; derived]
- Returns: ROCE 25%, ROE 20%; ROCE 24–28% for a decade. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (26 May 2026): "We are shooting for 30% growth… I'll put my money more on" 14–15%; 14–15% "should be the bare minimum"; EBITDA 17–18% with "a little bit of a dip" from AI investment and price cuts at renewal; pipeline $25 mn (vs ~$5 mn six months earlier); decision-making expected to "pick up" in Q1–Q2. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (10 Aug 2026): management cited FY27 revenue guidance of ₹1,200–1,250cr (≈19–24% growth; the CFO called it "about 25%") and said it would "still hold" it, but "if we need to restate it, we will do that at the end of second quarter"; Q2 "muted", growth hoped for in H2. Pipeline $28 mn. Note: the ₹1,200–1,250cr figure does not appear on the Q4 call — the Q4 call gave 30% "shooting" and 14–15% "minimum". [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: revenue flat YoY and QoQ — **missed** the 14–15% "bare minimum" run-rate; decision-making **did not** pick up; EBITDA 18.26% — **met**. To reach even ₹1,150cr (+14%), the remaining three quarters must average ~₹300cr, 21% above Q1. [Screener; derived]
- Long term: "Vision 2030" $500 mn revenue (~4x FY26 in dollars), to come mostly from wallet share in the top 20 accounts; M&A capped at ~₹100cr of target revenue, funded from internal cash. [Q4 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex is minimal (fixed assets ex-goodwill small; FY26 FCF ₹167cr on CFO ₹171cr). "No significant capex" needed for Vision 2030. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Cash outflow goes to acquisitions: FY26 investing outflow ₹172cr included settling earn-outs — contingent consideration of ₹39.5cr at Mar-25 was nil at Mar-26, and CARE counted ₹118cr of deferred earn-outs at Mar-25. Q1 call: founders of acquired firms "made their earnout and exited". [Screener; AR FY26 note 11.2; CARE Sep-25; Q1 call]
- Goodwill ₹518cr = 48% of total assets (auditor's key audit matter on impairment testing). [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash and bank ~₹223cr at Mar-26 (company). Borrowings ₹44cr (Screener, incl. leases); US-subsidiary term loan ~₹34cr taken in FY25 for acquisitions. CARE gearing 0.12x (Mar-25). [Q4 call; Screener; CARE Sep-25]
- DSO 70 days FY26 (80 in FY25); working-capital days 9. CFO/EBITDA 117% in FY26 — cash conversion is excellent. [Screener; IP Q1FY27 p.14]
- Dividend ₹1.00/share for FY26 (100% of face value), payout ~10%. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Currency: ~79% of revenue in USD/EUR/GBP; rupee weakness added ~3 points to FY26 growth; CEO: "we would love the rupee to keep depreciating". Forward contracts used (derivative liability in AR). [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- CARE A/Stable, A1 (Sep-25) — rating sensitivities: upgrade above ₹1,000cr scale with 20%+ margin and client diversification; downgrade if margin <12% or debt-funded M&A pushes gearing >0.5x. [CARE Sep-25]

## Moat evidence
- Client stickiness: top-20 clients ~69–70% of revenue for three years; 95–98% of growth comes from existing clients; CARE notes "history of repeat and regular business". [IP Q1FY27 p.9; Q4 call; CARE Sep-25]
- Clients: 16 above $1 mn a year (15 in FY24), 10 in the $0.5–1 mn band — a narrow base. [IP Q1FY27 p.9]
- Pricing power is weak: "no increase in pricing" in FY26; customers expect lower renewal prices because of AI productivity; Saksoft offers 25–30% cost cuts to win vendor-consolidation deals. [Q4 call]
- Positioning: CEO argues AI "levelled the playing field", getting Saksoft into RFPs it was never invited to, but concedes large players now compete for small deals too. GCCs (captive centres) a threat mainly for very large clients. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- AI impact: engineering 30–50% more efficient, managed services 20–30%; headcount falling while revenue stays flat; testing work could shrink 40–50%. AI revenue not tracked separately ("every project has AI"). [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Concentration**: top-5 clients 39%, top-10 56%, top-20 69% of revenue in Q1 FY27; two big clients deferred spending from Q3 FY26 and one "is still struggling". CEO has "no strategy to reduce" concentration. [IP Q1FY27 p.9; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Growth quality**: headline growth since FY24 was mostly acquired and currency-aided; flat revenue in a year when the rupee weakened implies falling constant-currency revenue. [CARE Sep-25; Screener]
- **Goodwill**: ₹518cr (48% of assets) from serial acquisitions; if acquired units stall, impairment risk rises. [AR FY26]
- **Guidance credibility**: FY27 target retained despite a flat Q1 and a Q2 guided "muted"; management openly flagged a possible cut after Q2. [Q1 call]
- **Audit coverage**: fifteen subsidiaries (₹861cr of revenue, ~85% of the group) audited by other auditors; group auditor relies on their reports. Unmodified opinion; no emphasis of matter found. Contingent liabilities small (income-tax ₹6.7cr). [AR FY26 auditor's report, notes]
- **Other**: a US customer "had gone bust" in FY25 (bad-debt provision); attrition rose to 13.4%; promoters hold 66.7% (no pledge shown); FII only 2.2%; stock fell from ₹206.7 (30 Jun) to ~₹142. [Q4 call; AR FY26; Screener; IP Q1FY27 p.15]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: "pipeline strongest ever", decision-making expected to pick up in Q1–Q2, 14–15% "bare minimum". Q1: "nothing is normalizing", Q2 muted, guidance kept only provisionally.
- New on Q1: deliberate exit from "tail accounts" and headcount-based deals; top-10 share down from 58% to 56% with revenue flat; new Chief Growth Officer for Europe (joined ~July) and a BU head for emerging verticals, funded by exits of earn-out founders.
- Pipeline $25 mn → $28 mn; one new logistics/airline client supplying Amazon.
- Commerce vertical named the weakest; emerging, BFS and logistics to drive growth.

## My read
Saksoft is a well-run, cash-rich, high-return small IT firm, but the growth engine has stalled: four flat quarters, organic constant-currency growth near zero once acquisitions and the rupee are stripped out, and falling headcount. Margins (~18%) and cash conversion (>100%) are holding, which supports the dividend and bolt-on M&A. The FY27 ₹1,200–1,250cr target looks unrealistic; a cut after Q2 is likely, and even 14–15% needs a sharp H2 rebound. At ~₹142 the price asks for little growth, so the stock mainly needs revenue to turn up — watch Q2/Q3 sequential growth, top-5 client trends, and whether the $28 mn pipeline converts. A goodwill write-down or loss of a top client would change the view for the worse.
