# RateGain Travel Technologies — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated; $ = US dollars. "Adjusted" figures add back the deferred payouts to the Sojern team (see below).

## Business and segments
- Software-as-a-service for travel and hospitality, now run as an "AI-powered operating system for travel revenue": three lines — **DaaS** (rate/price intelligence for airlines, OTAs, car rental, hotels), **Distribution** (channel manager/connectivity linking hotels to OTAs and GDSs), and **MarTech** (performance marketing for hotels and destinations via Sojern, plus social/email/CRM). Reported as one segment. [Q1 call; AR FY26 segment note]
- **Sojern acquisition** (completed Nov 2025): $250M total consideration (1.5x CY24 gross revenue) = $125M from balance-sheet cash (incl. QIP proceeds) + $125M SOFR-linked term loan, plus performance/time-linked deferred payouts over three years. Accounted consideration ₹2,222.7cr; group goodwill ₹1,581cr, all in the MarTech unit. Adara (bought 2023) was merged into the Sojern brand. [AR FY26 p.10–11; AR FY26 auditor's report KAM]
- Mix after Sojern: MarTech "over 81% of revenue"; Q1 FY27 YoY growth DaaS +22.7%, Distribution +3.1%, MarTech +341% (organic MarTech ~18.2%). Combined organic growth 17.5% YoY in rupees. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Customers 14,158; top-10 customers ~17.6% of revenue; revenue is mainly subscription and hybrid, with a transactional/outcome-based share growing with MarTech. North America is now the dominant geography (~two-thirds of revenue on the Q1 slide). [IP Q1FY27]
- Scale: 1,261 employees (+95% YoY with Sojern); revenue per employee ~₹2.5cr; attrition 14.0%. Utilisation is not a relevant/disclosed metric. [IP Q1FY27]
- Customer references: 33 of top 40 hotel chains, 7 of top 10 airlines (DaaS), 4 of top 5 car rental firms, all leading cruise lines. [IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Revenue FY24 957 → FY25 1,077 → FY26 1,824 (+69%); OPM 20% → 22% → 19%; net profit 145 → 209 → 194 (down in FY26 because of acquisition amortisation, interest and deal costs). [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 273 / 18% / 47; Sep-25 295 / 18% / 51; Dec-25 540 / 16% / 26 (first Sojern quarter); Mar-26 716 / 21% / 70; Jun-26 785 / 22% / 95. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹785cr (+187.6% YoY, +9.7% QoQ); adjusted EBITDA ₹193.4cr (24.6%); reported EBITDA ₹171.5cr (21.9%); adjusted PAT ₹116.8cr; reported PAT ₹94.9cr (12.1%). The quarter had a one-off $2.5M FIFA World Cup uplift and a ~$1.5M Middle East drag (regional revenue down from ~$970k to ~$425k a month). [Q1 call]
- Below EBITDA: acquisition amortisation ₹33.8cr a quarter (₹6.8cr a year ago); finance cost ₹16.5cr; other income collapsed to ₹3.1cr from ₹20.7cr because the cash went into Sojern. [Q1 call]
- Unit economics: gross margin ~70% (MarTech carries media/ad-spend cost, so lower than the ~75% pre-Sojern); gross revenue retention ~95% and net revenue retention ~100% in DaaS+Distribution; LTV/CAC 10.7x vs 14.5x a year ago. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Return ratios fell: ROCE 13.9%, ROE 11.9% (FY25 ROCE 14%, FY24 17%) as the balance sheet doubled. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (21 May 2026): FY27 revenue ₹3,000–3,100cr (+65–70%), organic 12–15% in rupees (10–12% in dollars, called "conservative"); EBITDA ₹650–700cr = 21.5–22.5% excluding earn-out cost; FCF/EBITDA >75%; debt-free by end-FY28; cost synergies raised to $15M annualised (from $12M). [Q4 call — stored AI transcript; official copy is a scanned image, key numbers confirmed by the Q1 official call]
- Q1 FY27 call (6 Aug 2026): revenue guided to the **upper end, ~₹3,100cr** ("that's the number we should definitely beat"); adjusted EBITDA margin **raised 100 bps to 22.5–23.5%**; organic exit-rate at "higher end of that 15% to 20% range"; medium-term aspiration 15–20% growth. [Q1 call]
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: organic growth 17.5% vs 12–15% guided (**beat**, partly FIFA); adjusted margin 24.6% vs 21.5–22.5% (**beat**); FCF conversion 78.8% vs >75% (**met**); debt repaid 38% vs 25.2% a quarter earlier (**on track**). Customer migration to the unified platform: "substantially complete". Guidance **raised**. [Q1 call]
- Deferred consideration add-back guided at ₹20–22cr a quarter (₹80–90cr a year) until Q3 FY29; Q1 was ₹21.9cr. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: capex is mostly capitalised software; the big capital item was Sojern ($250M). Management is not ruling out further M&A once debt is repaid (question asked on Q1 call; no commitment). [AR FY26; Q1 call]
- Payback on Sojern: at $15M annual cost synergies and ~19–20% Sojern EBITDA margin target, the business contributes the bulk of FY27's ~₹700cr adjusted EBITDA; payback depends on sustaining MarTech growth. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹615.4cr at 30 Jun 2026 (cash ₹255.6cr); acquisition loan repaid $47.5M of $125M (38%), outstanding $77.5M; target net-debt-free by FY28. Screener borrowings ₹949cr at Mar-26 (₹16cr a year earlier). [Q1 call; Screener]
- FCF ₹135.2cr in Q1 (78.8% of adjusted EBITDA), ₹230cr in FY26. CFO ₹234cr in FY26 vs ₹120cr in FY25. [Q1 call; Q4 call; Screener]
- Debtor days rose to 94 (FY26) from 72 (FY25) — MarTech billing with agencies/destinations runs longer. [Screener]
- No dividend; the standalone company has given a ₹1,291cr financial guarantee for the subsidiary's acquisition loan. [Screener; AR FY26 note 33]

## Moat evidence
- Scale in travel-intent data: 320+ data partners, "world's largest single source of travel intent data" after combining Adara and Sojern; audience grew ~14.5% YoY. [Q1 call]
- Pricing power: after merging two former competitors, "the need to discount is much lesser now… we can hold our price"; a weaker No.2/No.3 destination-marketing rival "approached us to buy them out"; negotiating higher outcome-based fees with an Indian regional hotel chain. [Q1 call]
- Cross-sell: APMEA new-customer wins +200% YoY; Southeast Asia airlines — "now the dominant provider"; renewed a multi-year deal with a leading global OTA. [Q1 call]
- Switching costs: channel-manager and connectivity are embedded in hotel operations; GRR ~95%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Caution: Distribution grew only 3.1%; "new contract wins" KPI (₹141cr) counts mainly new logos and management admits it under-represents business — disclosure is weak. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Adjusted numbers exclude real costs**: the Sojern team earn-out ($14M, tied to revenue/EBITDA targets) and cash-backstopped stock appreciation rights ($15M) — ~$29M (₹80–90cr a year) — are excluded from "adjusted" EBITDA/PAT. FY26 charged ₹9.3cr (earn-out) and ₹11.6cr (ESARs). Investors should value on reported profit. [AR FY26 note 40; Q1 call]
- Goodwill ₹1,581cr (≈75% of net worth ₹2,114cr) in MarTech; auditor (Deloitte Haskins & Sells) made business-combination accounting and goodwill impairment key audit matters; unmodified opinion. [AR FY26; Q1 call]
- Leverage and FX: dollar SOFR loan; rupee strength would reduce rupee revenue (most revenue is in dollars). [AR FY26]
- Concentration in MarTech ad spend — cyclical, tied to hotel and destination marketing budgets; Middle East destination budgets paused by the West Asia conflict. [Q1 call]
- Contingent liabilities: indirect-tax demand ₹62cr (unchanged from FY25) plus the ₹1,291cr loan guarantee (intra-group). No promoter pledge recorded. [AR FY26; Screener]
- Promoter holding 48.78%; FIIs only 5.75%. [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- From "integration" (Q4: cost synergies delivered, $15M run-rate, platform migration on track) to "monetisation" (Q1: pricing power, APMEA wins, new AI products — Agentic ARI, RateIQ, ChatGPT ads, Uno Viva voice agent).
- Numbers: margin guidance raised 1 pt; revenue guidance moved to top of range; debt repaid 25% → 38%; net debt ₹722cr → ₹615cr.
- New issues: FIFA one-off to reverse in Q2; Middle East revenue halved; management declined to split Sojern vs organic MarTech growth.

## My read
The business is improving: Sojern has been integrated fast, synergies beat plan, organic growth is back in the high teens, cash conversion is strong and debt is being paid down ahead of schedule. The catch is quality of reporting — "adjusted" profit excludes ~₹85cr a year of real payouts, amortisation eats ~₹135cr a year, and goodwill is three-quarters of equity. At the current price the stock needs roughly 18–20% earnings growth for several years, which is plausible if MarTech pricing power holds and Distribution returns to double digits. What would change the view: Q2–Q3 organic growth falling below 12% once FIFA drops out (negative), or a clean beat of ₹3,100cr with reported (not adjusted) EBITDA above 21% (positive).
