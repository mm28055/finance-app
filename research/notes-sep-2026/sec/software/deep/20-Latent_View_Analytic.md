# Latent View Analytics — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr) unless stated; $ = US dollars. CPG = consumer packaged goods; FDE = forward-deployed engineer (engineers placed at the client to build AI tools on site).
Transcript check: the stored Q1 FY27 transcript is the official BSE filing (call of 3 Aug 2026). The stored Q4 FY26 transcript is an AI-generated tijoristack copy; it was checked against the official BSE transcript (call of 18 May 2026) and has errors (see Stored-data errors).

## Business and segments
- Pure-play data analytics and AI services firm: data engineering (big Databricks and Snowflake practice), data science/AI and business analytics for US Fortune-500 clients; mainly marketing, customer, fraud/risk and increasingly supply-chain analytics. Owns ~80–90% of Decision Point (CPG revenue-management analytics, consolidated from 1 Jul 2024) and holds a $3M SAFE note in Healtheon AI (healthcare revenue-cycle agents, April 2026). [Q1 call; Q4 call official; AR FY26]
- Vertical mix Q1 FY27: Technology 58% (+4% QoQ), Financial Services 20% (+24% QoQ), Retail & CPG 15% (−32% QoQ), Industrials & others 7% (+10%). Geography: North America 90%, Europe & UK 2%, rest of world 8%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Contract mix: fixed-bid 18–20%, time & material ~80%, of which 65–68% is managed services (capacity contracted, not timesheets). Decision Point does most of the fixed-fee, fixed-scope work. [Q4 call official; Q1 call]
- People: ~1,744 full-time staff (Q4 FY26; 1,622 six quarters earlier), 1,800+ total; 1,451 in APAC, 243 in North America, 28 in LATAM. Utilisation rose from ~85% to ~89% in Q4 FY26 partly on one-off projects; attrition is shown only as a chart. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call official]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Annual: revenue FY24 641 → FY25 848 → FY26 1,060 (+25%); OPM 21% → 23% → 22%; net profit 159 → 173 → 202 (+16%). In dollars: $77.7M → $100.3M → $119.8M (+19.4%; organic ~18.2%). TTM revenue 1,111, profit 199. [Screener; IP Q1FY27; Q4 call official]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / net profit): Jun-25 236 / 21% / 51; Sep-25 258 / 22% / 46; Dec-25 278 / 22% / 51; Mar-26 289 / 23% / 55; Jun-26 287 / 20% / 47. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹286.9cr (+21.6% YoY, −0.6% QoQ); **$30.3M, −3.5% QoQ** (guided −1.5% to −2%), +9.9% YoY; adjusted EBITDA ₹58.6cr, 20.4% (24.1% in Q4, 22.2% a year ago); reported PAT ₹47.1cr, **−6.9% YoY**. Margin bridge Q4→Q1: wage hike −2.7 pts (≈8% raise for ~70% of staff), lower revenue −1.9 pts, lower travel +1.0 pt. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Other income is large**: ₹74–76cr a year from treasury (~28% of FY26 PBT); Q1 FY27 ₹21cr of ₹65cr PBT. Profit growth over 3 years is only 8% a year (Screener) despite 25% sales growth. [Screener]
- ROCE 16%, ROE 11.9% — depressed by a large IPO cash pile. [Screener]

## Deal wins, clients and concentration
- **Very concentrated**: top-5 clients 57% of LTM revenue (+1 pt), top-10 72% (+3 pts), top-20 86%; 96 clients served in the LTM. 69% of revenue from clients of 5+ years. [IP Q1FY27]
- The largest tech client insourced work after a leadership change and cost cuts, removing ~$7–9M of annual revenue; Q1 FY27 was the first full quarter of the hit (≈$0.5M in the quarter). Management reports new deals in the same account ($3M+, $1–1.5M). [Q1 call; Q4 call official]
- A financial-services client won in Feb 2025 reached a ~$10M run-rate and demanded one-time volume discounts in Q1 FY27. BFSI revenue was ~$18M in FY26 (+110% YoY per the presentation). [Q1 call; Q4 call official; IP Q1FY27]
- CPG: one-off Decision Point projects of about $0.8–0.85M ended in Q4 without follow-on; $2M of extensions and a $26M pipeline cited; Q2 CPG growth "back to 25% plus" QoQ promised. Tech pipeline $15M+. 7 new logos in Q1 (incl. an AI data-centre operator, an asset manager and a fintech). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Databricks practice grew from $11–12M to $17M+ in FY26; targeted to grow 60%+. [Q4 call official]

## AI impact
- 35% of Q1 revenue is "primary AI" (AI tools delivered to the client), up from 28% in FY26; ~80% of work now uses AI in some form. Productivity gains on completed projects range 30–80%, and in managed-services contracts "a lot of the productivity benefits... accrue to the client". [Q1 call]
- Response: FDE model (more onsite, lower margin for now), an "agent foundry" platform to price agents rather than people, a new Chief Technology & AI Officer organisation, 200+ staff in Anthropic certification; goal to raise fixed-price/outcome contracts above 20%. AI-led projects earn 55–58% gross margin vs 50.8% company average. [Q1 call; Q4 call official]
- Clients prefer "walled garden" AI (open models, RAG, semantic layers) — management argues this raises the need for integrators. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (18 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth with "high visibility" of **12–13%** in USD; aspiration of organic growth "similar to what we delivered this year, which is 18% to 20%" (visibility usually rises 8–10 pts through the year); EBITDA 21–22% (planned at ₹92/$); tech +5–8%, consumer +18–22%, BFSI at least +40%; $200M revenue target for FY28/FY29. [Q4 call official]
- Q1 FY27 (3 Aug 2026): Q1 dollar revenue −3.5% vs −1.5 to −2% guided — **missed**; 12% full-year kept (implies ~7% sequential growth in each of the next three quarters); the 18–20% aspiration was **dropped**; EBITDA target **cut** to 20–21%; BFSI hit by discounts — **behind** the 40% plan; CPG down 32% QoQ — **missed**. $200M now needs M&A: a letter of intent is signed with a healthcare/life-sciences + data-engineering target. Against the 26–27% a year needed for $200M, FY26 delivered 19–20%. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light; depreciation ₹41–43cr a year (mostly acquisition intangibles). FY26 FCF ₹142cr on CFO ₹165cr. Capacity is people; headcount has been flat at ~1,730–1,745 for four quarters. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- Capital use is M&A and strategic bets: Decision Point, Healtheon ($3M), a pending LOI deal. [AR FY26; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Treasury investments ₹917cr at March 2026 (plus bank balances) — at least 18% of the ₹5,048cr market value; borrowings ₹33cr (leases). No dividend ever paid. [Screener]
- Debtor days 64 → 80 → 80 (FY24–FY26), up because Decision Point's CPG clients pay in 90–120 days; CFO/operating profit 93% (FY25) and 101% (FY26). [Screener; Q4 call official]
- **Decision Point purchase dispute**: the remaining 20% was due to be bought by 30 Apr 2026, but the sellers claim ₹220.3cr against the company's estimate of at most ₹70.3cr (redemption liability ₹67.7cr booked). Not resolved. [AR FY26 note 30b]

## Moat evidence
- Long relationships with large tech and payments clients (69% of revenue from 5+ year clients); niche in front-end analytics (marketing mix, personalisation, fraud); partner status with Databricks, Snowflake, Anthropic, OpenAI; Forrester "Strong Performer" (marketing measurement, Q1 2026), ISG Leader (retail-CPG analytics 2025). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- But wallet share at large clients is only 2–5% among 20–30 vendors, the top client could cut $7–9M on one executive's decision, and a big BFSI client extracted discounts — pricing power is limited. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor emphasis of matter** on the Decision Point dispute (₹220cr claimed vs ≤₹70cr provided); auditor states the matter "may have an adverse effect on the functioning of the Group". Also an audit-trail (Rule 11(g)) exception at Decision Point's books; key audit matters: goodwill carrying value, revenue recognition, a new ERP migration. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Client concentration** (top-5 57%) and insourcing risk at tech clients spending heavily on AI infrastructure. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Leadership change**: CEO Rajan Sethuraman moved to "Strategic Advisor to CEO"; Sonal Ramrakhiani (ex-Wipro Engineering Americas, 24 years at Tata) joined as CEO days before the Q1 call; two independent directors' terms ended in Jun/Jul 2026. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Earnings quality**: ~28% of PBT from treasury income; transaction-related expenses in each quarter through Q1 FY27 (said to end now). [Screener; Q1 call]
- **Governance/ownership**: promoters (Venkat Viswanathan, Pramadwathi Jandhyala) 65.1%; institutions own <6%; no dividend despite ₹900cr+ treasury. Contingent liabilities tiny (₹0.05cr income tax). [Screener; AR FY26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 was confident ("crossed ₹1,000cr", tech bleeding "bottomed", BFSI 40%+); Q1 was explanatory — CPG one-offs, BFSI discounts, full insourcing hit — with a brand-new CEO saying she is "listening, learning".
- Numbers: dollar revenue fell 3.5% QoQ, EBITDA margin fell ~3.7 pts, PAT fell YoY for the first time in a while; EBITDA guide cut by 1 pt; the 18–20% aspiration disappeared; M&A now needed for $200M.
- New issues: Decision Point dispute (in the annual report), FDE margin drag, LOI for an acquisition.

## Best management quotes
- "We have a reasonable level of confidence in terms of high visibility pipeline... to deliver about 12% to 13% of growth." — Rajan Sethuraman, then CEO [Q4 call official]
- "In reality, the shrinkage was a little more than anticipated." — Rajan Venkatesan, CFO [Q1 call]
- "Whatever we are able to do through organic, the gap will look to fill through inorganic opportunities." — Rajan Sethuraman [Q1 call]

## My read
The business is still growing in rupees, but the dollar engine has stalled (Q1 −3.5% QoQ) because of heavy dependence on a few tech clients, lumpy CPG projects and AI-driven pricing pressure; margins are resetting from 23% to ~20%, and a quarter of profit is treasury income. At about half its peak price the stock needs roughly 10–12% a year earnings growth, which requires Q2–Q4 to deliver the ~7% sequential growth embedded in the 12% guide. A clean Q2 bounce, a sensible acquisition and settlement of the Decision Point claim near the company's number would improve the view; another dollar decline, loss of a top-5 client or a large payout to Decision Point sellers would weaken it.
