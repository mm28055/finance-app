# Route Mobile — deep research (Phase A1)

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. ₹ crore (cr). Screener consolidated figures for history. Calls read: Q4 FY26 (8 May 2026, strategy update; stored copy is the official BSE transcript) and Q1 FY27 (24 Jul 2026; stored copy AI-generated — checked against the official BSE transcript and it contains material errors, listed below).

## Business and segments
- Cloud communications (CPaaS) provider: sends bank OTPs, alerts and marketing messages for enterprises by SMS, WhatsApp, RCS, voice and email, through 280+ direct mobile-operator connections; 3,100+ enterprise clients; 174.9 billion billable transactions in FY26, 45.8 billion in Q1 FY27. [Q4 call; AR FY26; IP Q1FY27 via Investing.com]
- Owned ~75% by Belgium's Proximus group since 2024 (promoter 74.85%); sister companies BICS (carrier network) and Telesign (US identity/verification) are both channels and customers. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Revenue mix: new (non-SMS) products ₹94.5cr in Q1 (~8% of revenue, +14% YoY, +11% QoQ); ~₹350cr in FY26 (+11%; 43% CAGR FY22–26). International long-distance SMS (ILD — traffic from global firms into India) is "one-fourth to one-third" of revenue at lower percentage margin. Firewall revenue (blocking grey-route SMS for operators, 30–70% margins) sits inside SMS and is not disclosed. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 presentation: by geography India 36%, Europe 16%, Asia ex-India 13%; verticals include financial services 15%, digital natives 12%, telecom operators 10%. [IP Q1FY27 via Investing.com]
- Client concentration: top 10 clients 43% of revenue, top 50 clients 75%; 77 clients above $1M annualised; net revenue retention 98%. [IP Q1FY27 via Investing.com; Q1 call]
- Related party: sales of messaging services to Proximus-group fellow subsidiaries were the largest related-party line, ~₹659cr in FY26 vs ₹460cr in FY25 (≈15% of revenue; the table layout suggests mainly Telesign), priced at cost plus an agreed mark-up. [AR FY26 note 39; Q4 call]
- People: 783 employees in Q1 (502 overseas, 281 India); tech/support 346, sales/marketing 229 (sales headcount fell by double digits — management could not explain on the call). Parent-company permanent staff 497. Headcount to "hover around" 800. [IP Q1FY27 via Investing.com; Q1 call; AR FY26]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹4,023cr → FY25 ₹4,576cr → FY26 ₹4,408cr (−3.7%); OPM 13% → 12% → 12%; net profit ₹389cr → ₹334cr → ₹257cr (after ₹136cr exceptional write-offs). TTM revenue ₹4,509cr. [Screener]
- Quarters (sales / OPM / PAT): Jun-25 1,051 / 9% / 59; Sep-25 1,119 / 12% / −19 (exceptional write-off); Dec-25 1,107 / 13% / 103; Mar-26 1,131 / 12% / 114; Jun-26 1,152 / 9% / 69. [Screener]
- Company basis Q1 FY27: revenue ₹1,151.5cr (+9.6% YoY, +1.8% QoQ); gross profit ₹240.4cr, gross margin 20.9% (23.3% Q4, 21.4% Q1 FY26); adjusted EBITDA ₹108.9cr, 9.5%; adjusted PAT ₹68.6cr (+16.6% YoY, −40.1% QoQ). [Q1 call (official)]
- FY26 company basis: gross profit ₹1,007cr (22.9% vs 20.8%), adjusted EBITDA ₹526cr (11.9%), adjusted PAT ₹376cr. [Q4 call]
- Unit economics: revenue per billable transaction fell from ~29 paise to ~25 paise in FY26 as high-price ILD traffic was replaced by cheaper domestic traffic; WhatsApp prices were also cut during the year. [Q4 call]
- ROCE 17.4%, ROE 12.6%; trades near book value (₹440/share). [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (8 May 2026): first guidance in over a year — FY27 revenue growth "mid to high single digits", EBITDA margin "around 12%", strong EBITDA-to-cash conversion; regular dividend raised 50% to ₹16.5/share a year (paid quarterly). Claro firewall deal described as "now live". [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (24 Jul 2026): revenue +9.6% (on track); EBITDA 9.5% (**missed** the 12% run-rate); management "will hold" guidance update and said "we should not materially deviate" from 12%; volumes expected to rise 10–15% in the next couple of quarters; cash conversion 75–100% of EBITDA for the year. Claro now "behind on the deployment ... should have been live by now" — contradicts the Q4 call. [Q1 call (official)]
- Net: revenue guidance **kept**, margin guidance **at risk** (needs ~12.8% for the rest of the year to average 12%), dividend **kept** (₹4 interim in Q1). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 via Investing.com]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: organic capex "not a meaningful number", roughly flat; depreciation ~₹24cr a quarter (mostly acquired intangibles). [Q4 call; Screener]
- M&A: Heltar Technologies (AI chatbot/omnichannel platform) bought by slump sale, signed 13 Jul 2026, funded from internal cash, value not disclosed ("not material"). More small capability deals possible. [Q1 call]
- Goodwill ₹544.5cr from past acquisitions (Masivian, 365Squared, MR Messaging, Sarv, etc.) — auditors' key audit matter. [AR FY26 auditor's report]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash ~₹1,400cr at Mar-26; ₹1,345cr net cash at Jun-26 (≈46% of market cap); borrowings ₹42cr. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 via Investing.com; Screener]
- Cash from operations FY26 ₹581cr (>100% of EBITDA); FY25 ₹602cr; FCF FY26 ₹555cr. Debtor days 75. Q1 collections were late (India and UAE customers reconciling). [Screener; Q1 call]
- Cash yield under 3–4% — analysts complained; management promised a treasury review. Payout ~30% of profit before the dividend increase. [Q4 call]
- Uses "a meaningful amount of cash on vendor prepayments" — the same practice that produced the FY26 write-offs. [Q4 call; AR FY26]

## Moat evidence
- Direct operator connections (280+ own, 450+ with BICS) give delivery and cost advantages aggregators lack. [Q4 call]
- Gross margin rose from 19.3% (Q4 FY25) to 23.3% (Q4 FY26) as low-margin ILD left — then fell back to 20.9% in Q1 FY27 when one Indian bank's traffic was disrupted. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- No pricing power in WhatsApp/RCS: management refuses to chase price cuts, and peers' non-SMS share is 30–35% versus Route's ~8%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Firewall contracts (fixed + variable, exclusive termination of international traffic in select Claro markets) are the stickiest, highest-margin business. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Vendor-prepayment write-offs**: ₹135.9cr exceptional loss in FY26 — ₹108.0cr after a Singapore arbitration award (18 Nov 2025) went against a subsidiary on minimum-commitment prepayments, and ₹27.9cr to a vendor that "ceased its business". Both are emphasis-of-matter paragraphs in the auditor's report. Earlier years had a ₹50cr impairment and other provisions. Q1 FY27 had another "provision for an advance". [AR FY26 note 33.4; auditor's report; Q1 call]
- **Cyber incident** at Colombian subsidiary Masivian (late Q1 FY27); investigation ongoing, customers and regulator informed, impact not quantified; Masivian's CEO listed only "up to June 30, 2026". [Q1 call; AR FY26]
- **Concentration**: top 10 clients 43% of revenue; one Indian bank's upgrade turned the India segment loss-making in Q1. [IP Q1FY27 via Investing.com; Q1 call]
- **Structural**: ILD SMS in secular decline; OTT channels priced aggressively; Meta policy changes. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Parent**: Proximus is heavily indebted (analyst claim) and controls 75%; related-party sales ~15% of revenue; management says the Route board decides capital allocation independently. [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- Statements inconsistent between calls (Claro "live" vs "delayed").

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "the reset is largely behind us" (Q4) to "clear-eyed about the margin softness" (Q1).
- Gross margin swung from a 23.3% high to 20.9%; the 12% EBITDA guide moved from firm to "should not materially deviate".
- New issues: Masivian cyber incident, Indian bank disruption, Heltar acquisition, provision for an advance; Claro slipped.
- Cash-use debate continued; answer still "acquisitions first" plus the higher dividend.

## Best management quotes
- "The reset is largely behind us, and the business that has emerged from it is structurally healthier." — CEO Tushar Agnihotri [Q4 call]
- "It should have been live by now, but due to reasons beyond our control, there has been a delay." — on Claro [Q1 call]
- "We will revert to that cash conversion level for the year." — CFO Raj Gill [Q1 call]

## My read
The business is steady but not improving: revenue is back to ~10% growth on volume, yet gross margin has given back its FY26 gains and the 12% EBITDA target now needs a clean rest of year. The balance sheet is the real support — ~₹1,345cr net cash, ~₹550cr a year of free cash flow and a 2.4% yield — so the P/E in single digits prices in little growth; the price needs only low single-digit profit growth. Two things keep it from being a bargain: repeated write-offs of vendor prepayments (₹136cr in FY26 plus a fresh provision in Q1) and a controlling parent that decides how the cash is used. The view improves if gross margin returns above 22% in Q2–Q3 and cash is returned via buyback or special dividend; it worsens on another write-off or a sizeable Masivian loss.
