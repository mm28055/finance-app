## Business and segments
- Black Box is a digital-infrastructure integrator. It designs, cables, builds and maintains data-centre fit-outs, fibre and structured cabling, enterprise networks (LAN, WAN, Wi-Fi, private LTE), workplace audio-visual and unified communications, and managed security. It works in 35 countries for about 300 strategic accounts, including more than 120 Fortune 500 clients. [IP Q1FY27 p.25-26; Q1 call]
- **Business lines (FY26):** Global Solutions Integration about 84%, Technology Product Solutions (TPS: KVM switches, AV, IoT hardware) about 14%, other services about 2%. TPS is about $90 mn today, with a target of $200 mn by FY30, and gross margin of about 40%. It is still loss-making at EBIT: an analyst said EBIT losses rose with revenue, and management expects TPS to grow about 20% in FY27. [IP Q1FY27 p.26; CMD; Q1 call]
- **Data centre (DC):** about 17% of FY26 revenue, expected to reach about 30% in FY27. DC was about 25% of the order book at the CMD. [IP Q1FY27 p.4; CMD]
- **Geography, Q1 FY27:**

  | Region | Share of revenue |
  |---|---|
  | North America | 69% |
  | Europe | 9% |
  | APAC | 8% |
  | India | 6% |
  | Latin America | 6% |
  | Middle East & Africa | 2% |

  **US revenue share is about 69%.** Currency risk is mainly translation: plans are built at ₹90–95/$, and a weaker rupee lifts reported revenue. [IP Q1FY27 p.7; CMD]
- **Client concentration (Q1 FY27):** top 10 clients 51% of revenue, top 20 58%, top 50 68%, top 200 83%. Ten clients billed more than ₹100 cr each in FY26. Top-10 client tenure averages over 20 years. The largest financial-services client has passed $1 bn in lifetime revenue and spends about $100 mn a year. [IP Q1FY27 p.7; CMD]
- **Clients by type:** four of the top six hyperscalers and four of the top five US banks are clients. A new US hyperscaler was added in Q1 with a $131 mn (₹1,240 cr) order, which is 100% services. [CMD; Q1 call]
- **People:** about 4,000 staff, of whom about 2,000 are in the US and about 1,200 in India (including a 600-person Bengaluru GCC). The plan is to reach about 7,000, including 2,100 hires in the US DC business "in just 1" year, and to grow the GCC to 1,000. **Utilisation is not disclosed.** Attrition "has come down significantly", but no quarterly figure is given. [CMD; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **CMD (1 Jun 2026):**
  - FY30 revenue aspiration of ₹18,000 cr ($2 bn): about ₹12,000 cr organic (about 17% CAGR from ₹6,322 cr) plus about ₹6,000 cr from acquisitions.
  - EBITDA margin of 10% or more. Sanjeev Verma called 10% "our short-term goal to get into FY27".
  - Buy 2–5%-margin businesses at 6–8x EBITDA and lift them to 9–10% within 90–120 days. Pay 70% upfront and 30% as earn-out.
  - Debt/equity could rise to 1:1. [CMD]
- **Q1 FY27 call (13 Aug 2026), official figures:**

  | Item | FY27 guidance | Growth |
  |---|---|---|
  | Revenue | ₹7,800–8,000 cr | +23–27% |
  | EBITDA | ₹725–750 cr | +27–32% |
  | EBITDA margin | 9.3–9.4% | +30–40 bps |
  | PAT | ₹300–325 cr | +38–50% |
  | Bookings | $1.3–1.4 bn | +32–45% |
  | Year-end backlog | $1.3–1.4 bn | +65–75% |

  Management also said operating cash flow to EBITDA "to improve meaningfully over FY26". [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.13]
- **Margin target softened.** The CMD's 10% "into FY27" became a 9.3–9.4% full-year guide, with Q4 "in that range or more" and 10% "at scale". The reason given is upfront training and hiring for gigawatt-scale sites. [CMD; Q1 call]
- **Delivery against promises:** Q1 met the CMD tone. Revenue was a record, and backlog rose from about $800 mn to $949 mn. Q1 alone is 22% of the low end of revenue guidance, which leaves **H2 needing roughly 30–40% YoY growth**; an analyst put this to management. The new hyperscaler's ground work starts only "from November". [Q1 call]
- **Tax:** 10–15% in FY27–FY28 (tax losses carried forward), then about 20%. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- The business is asset-light: FY26 capex was about ₹72 cr (CFO ₹84 cr less free cash flow ₹12 cr, per Screener). PP&E is only ₹128 cr and right-of-use assets ₹283 cr. [Screener; IP Q1FY27 p.22]
- "Capacity" here means people. The plan adds about 3,000 staff (4,000 → 7,000) and 1,500 certifications. Hiring and training are expensed, which is why the FY27 margin guide sits below 10%. [CMD; Q1 call]
- **Acquisitions:**
  - 2S Inovações Tecnológicas (Brazil, a Cisco partner) closed 13 May 2026, effective 1 May. It brings about $50 mn a year in revenue, about ₹500 cr annualised in FY27.
  - Fujisoft (Abu Dhabi) was consolidated from 1 Jan 2026.
  - The plan is to buy about $200–250 mn of revenue a year, funded by internal cash and some debt. [AR FY26 Board's report; IP Q1FY27 p.11; CMD]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Balance sheet at Mar-26:**
  - Borrowings ₹827 cr (₹797 cr long-term, ₹30 cr short-term) plus lease liabilities ₹326 cr.
  - Cash ₹540 cr, so **net debt excluding leases is about ₹287 cr**.
  - Equity ₹1,287 cr, lifted by the preferential warrant issue. [IP Q1FY27 p.22; Screener]
- **Preferential issue:** ₹386 cr raised through warrants at ₹417 issued to promoter and non-promoter investors — 25% in FY25 and 75% by end-March 2026. The CMD said promoters invested about ₹425 cr across two rounds. [IP Q1FY27 p.20; AR FY26 note on share capital; CMD]
- **Cash conversion is weak:**

  | Year | Operating cash flow (₹ cr) | Company EBITDA (₹ cr) |
  |---|---|---|
  | FY24 | 129 | 428 |
  | FY25 | -88 | 531 |
  | FY26 | 84 | 570 |

  Working capital absorbed ₹551 cr in FY25 and ₹342 cr in FY26. [Screener; IP Q1FY27 p.23]
- **Receivables:**
  - Trade receivables doubled from ₹567 cr (Mar-25) to ₹1,153 cr (Mar-26).
  - Debtor days: 22 → 35 → 67 over FY24–FY26 (Screener).
  - CFO Deepak Bansal explained that 62% of Q4 revenue was billed in March (normally about 55% of a quarter's billing falls in its last month). He says normal collection is 45–75 days and expects 60–75 days, against "90 plus" at year-end. [Screener; CMD]
  - **Receivables are also sold (securitised).** The consolidated notes show a ₹354 cr "receivable against securitisation of trade receivables", and finance costs include "trade receivable securitisation charges". Reported debtor days therefore understate the true credit given to customers. [AR FY26 consolidated notes]
- **Other working-capital lines:** inventory ₹323 cr (Mar-26); payables ₹790 cr, up from ₹556 cr. Screener working-capital days: 2 → 33 → 54. [IP Q1FY27 p.22; Screener]
- **Dividend:** payout about 8%; the final dividend record date is 28 Aug 2026. No buyback. [Screener; AR FY26]
- **Credit rating:** CRISIL BBB+ / A2. The outlook was revised to **Positive** from Stable on 17 Jun 2026. [AR FY26 corporate governance report]

## Moat evidence
- **Scale of execution:** Black Box says it has executed "close to a gigawatt" of DC work and is "the only India origin" firm running gigawatt-scale DC programmes. Win rate on large deals "improved by over 2x". Management says hyperscalers pick on capability, not on price. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.4]
- **Sticky relationships:** top-10 tenure above 20 years; one bank relationship has lasted 27 years with $1 bn in lifetime revenue. About 10–15% of completed projects turn into annuity (recurring) contracts. [IP Q1FY27 p.7; CMD]
- **Contract terms:** large DC scopes are cost-plus, with "margins and costs… discussed upfront" and change orders covered. Smaller projects are fixed-price. This limits overrun risk but also caps margin. [CMD]
- **Weak side:** the moat is people and relationships, not IP. Gross margin is about 30%, and EBITDA has never been above 9.7% in a quarter. Management admitted "you can't be the only one playing". [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- **Customer concentration and the AI capex cycle:** top 10 clients are 51% of revenue and North America is 69%. DC is heading to about 30% of revenue. Growth guidance depends on hyperscaler timing ("assuming… no significant customer-led delays"). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- **Exceptional items every year:**

  | Year | Exceptional charge (₹ cr) |
  |---|---|
  | FY21 | 32 |
  | FY22 | 22 |
  | FY23 | 52 |
  | FY24 | 40 |
  | FY25 | 66 |
  | FY26 | 63 |
  | Q1 FY27 | 19 |

  The FY26 charge was severance at US unit BBX Inc. ₹47.3 cr, legal settlement ₹10.1 cr and Labour Code ₹5.6 cr. Adjusted profit is flattered by treating these recurring costs as one-offs. Screener nets them into "other income", which is why it shows negative other income (-₹15 cr in Q1) while the CFO said other income was +₹4 cr. [IP Q1FY27 p.8, p.21; AR FY26 note 32; Q1 call]
- **Auditor's emphasis of matter (consolidated, FY26):** delays beyond FEMA (foreign exchange law) timelines covering ₹29.37 cr of import payments, ₹6.73 cr of export proceeds and ₹3.02 cr of other receipts. Condonation applications are pending, and no penalty has been provided. The opinion is otherwise unmodified; auditor is MSKA & Associates. [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 47]
- **Receivable securitisation and swings in working capital** (see above). Operating cash flow has been negative or thin in two of the last three years.
- **Related parties:** Essar group entities buy small amounts of services, around ₹1–9 cr each (Essar Projects ₹9.0 cr in FY26). Dues include ₹6.8 cr from Essar Constructions and ₹6.6 cr from EPC Constructions. These are small but persistent. [AR FY26 related-party note]
- **Contingent liabilities are small:** about ₹25 cr of disputed tax, GST and vendor claims. [AR FY26 note 39]
- **Acquisition risk:** the plan needs about ₹6,000 cr of acquired revenue at 6–8x EBITDA, and gearing could rise to 1:1 debt/equity. Goodwill rose to ₹381 cr at Mar-26. [CMD; IP Q1FY27 p.22]
- **Low tax rate is temporary:** 9% in FY26 and 10–15% guided, before about 20% from FY29. [Screener; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- **Tone:** from "transformation is behind us" (CMD) to "the beginning of the scaling phase" (Q1). The narrative was unchanged; the Q1 numbers added evidence.
- **New:** a US hyperscaler ($131 mn order); backlog $949 mn (versus about $800 mn at the CMD); supply of cable and fibre "improved"; first explicit FY27 guidance; the 2S acquisition closed.
- **Softer:** the margin goal slid from 10% "into FY27" to 9.3–9.4%. The CFO said a large part of the backlog flows into FY28, not FY27.
- **Unchanged:** the FY30 $2 bn aspiration, the 10% margin "at scale", and the focus on about 300 accounts.

## My read
The business is improving in quality. The margin has doubled since FY23, the backlog is up 88% in 15 months, and a fourth hyperscaler has been won. But it remains a roughly 9%-margin integrator. It has thin cash conversion, recurring "exceptional" charges, receivables that are partly sold, and a FEMA compliance lapse. Revenue was flat from FY23 to FY26, so FY27 is the first real test of growth. At about 50 times trailing earnings (Screener), the price needs the FY27 PAT guide of ₹300–325 cr (+38–50%) and continued 25%-plus growth into FY28. My view would improve with operating cash flow above 60% of EBITDA and no exceptional charge for a full year. It would worsen if the H2 ramp slips (the new hyperscaler only starts in November) or if hyperscaler capex pauses.
