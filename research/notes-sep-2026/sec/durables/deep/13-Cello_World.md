# Cello World — deep research (Phase A1)

Researched 27 Sep 2026. Figures from Screener consolidated page (raw HTML read) unless tagged. Price ₹338, market cap ₹7,634 cr, 52-week range ₹315–674 [Screener, 27-Sep-26].
Note on definitions: company "EBITDA" includes other income (Q1 FY27 ₹117.1 cr = Screener operating profit ₹99 cr + other income ₹18 cr). Screener OPM is the cleaner operating measure.

## Business and segments
- Three verticals (single reportable segment "Consumer Products" in the accounts): **consumerware** — plastic houseware, opalware, glassware, insulated steel bottles, small kitchen appliances; **writing instruments** — Unomax and the re-acquired Cello pen brand; **moulded furniture and allied** (from the merged Wim Plast). [AR FY26 note 39; IP Q4FY26]
- FY26 revenue ₹2,324 cr: consumerware 69.1% (~₹1,606 cr, +10%), writing instruments 15.9% (~₹368 cr), furniture 15.0% (~₹349 cr). FY26 gross margins: consumerware 50.9%, writing 53.6%, furniture 40.2%. [IP Q4FY26; Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 mix: consumerware 63.6% (GM 55.0%), writing 21.2% (GM 53.8%, revenue +52%), furniture 15.2% (~₹80 cr, GM 39.5%). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Channels (Q1 FY27): general trade 71.1%, online (e-commerce + quick commerce) 16.3% (10.4% a year ago), exports 7.3%, modern trade 5.3%. FY26 exports ₹159 cr, down 10%. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 39.2]
- Manufacturing: 14–15 plants (Daman, Haridwar, Baddi, Chennai, Kolkata, Falna in Rajasthan); 77% of FY26 revenue made in-house. [IP Q1FY27; IP Q4FY26]
- Capacity/utilisation: opalware 80–85% (no room to expand at the current site); glassware (~20,000 tonnes a year, Falna) ~60%; new steel-bottle plant 8 lines, not yet at efficient output. [Q1 call; IP Q4FY26]
- Wim Plast merged in (NCLT order 14-May-2026, effective 27-May-2026, appointed date 1-Apr-2025); 46.5 lakh + 16.8 lakh new Cello shares to Wim Plast minority holders. [AR FY26 consolidated notes; BSE filing 17-Sep-26]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (₹ cr, Screener): Q1 FY26 revenue 529 / operating profit 109 / OPM 21% / PAT 81; Q2 FY26 587 / 128 / 22% / 91; Q3 FY26 554 / 106 / 19% / 69; Q4 FY26 654 / 129 / 20% / 90; **Q1 FY27 527 / 99 / 19% / 73**. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 vs Q1 FY26: revenue flat (−0.4%), gross margin 52.4% vs 54.0%, company EBITDA ₹117.1 cr (−7%), PAT ₹73.4 cr (−9%). [IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: FY24 revenue ₹2,000 cr, operating profit ₹509 cr (25%), PAT ₹356 cr; FY25 ₹2,136 cr, ₹510 cr (24%), ₹365 cr; FY26 ₹2,324 cr (+9%), ₹471 cr (20%), ₹332 cr. TTM ₹2,321 cr / ₹461 cr / ₹324 cr. [Screener]
- Margin slide explained by management: glassware grew revenue at break-even; steel bottles lost 5–6 margin points when imports stopped and OEM supply cost more; Cello pens inherited a "very poor P&L". [Q4 call]
- ROCE (Screener) fell from 48% (FY23) to 17% (FY26), partly because of the ₹737 cr QIP cash still on the books. [Screener; IP Q1FY27 "Our Journey"]
- Unit economics: price rises of 7–20% across products (average ~12–13% in consumerware); polymer inflation 12–20%; gas cost ~80% higher than March. Steel bottles were sold across ~150 SKUs at peak; now 20–25, rising to 50–55 over two quarters. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (29 May 2026):** FY27 revenue growth 10–12%; EBITDA margin up 2–2.5 points "majorly because of steelware and glassware"; writing instruments ₹500 cr+ in FY27 (from ₹368 cr); capex ~₹100 cr (mostly maintenance, ~₹30 cr steel and building); debtor days down 10–15; steel lines at full production "from July onwards"; path back to ~26% margins. Q1 flagged as "challenging". [Q4 call, official]
- **Q1 delivery (10 Aug 2026):** revenue flat, company EBITDA margin 22.2% (down from 23.9%); steel still at 20–25 SKUs; glassware still ~60%. Writing instruments +52% was the bright spot. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Q1 guidance:** withdrew numbers — "too early… I will be in a better position to give you guidance next quarter". Guidance effectively **dropped** (10–12% growth and +2–2.5 pt margin not repeated). No more price increases planned; channel inventory "has dropped", secondary sales better than primary. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹219 cr (steel-bottle lines, select consumerware, writing instruments, maintenance). FY27 plan ~₹100 cr. [Q4 call]
- Two more steel lines to be ordered, commissioning "early next year" (early 2027). [Q1 call]
- Peak revenue potential stated: glass plant ₹250–275 cr on Q1 call (₹300 cr and 28–30% EBITDA margin said on Q4 call); steel plant with 8 lines ~₹300 cr. Glassware needs "another 10–15%" utilisation to reach healthy profit; glass sales +30–35% year on year in Q1 and expected to be "a lot more" than ₹150 cr in FY27. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Opalware expansion would need a greenfield plant in Rajasthan; deferred because new competitors added capacity. [Q4 call]
- Payback not disclosed. Capital commitments ₹45 cr at March 2026. [AR FY26 note 38]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Essentially debt-free (debt-equity 0.01). Cash ₹126 cr plus current investments ~₹535 cr at March 2026 (Screener investments ₹620 cr). [AR FY26 consolidated balance sheet; Screener]
- Cash from operations ₹255 cr in FY26 (₹262 cr FY25); free cash flow only ₹37 cr after ₹219 cr capex. CFO has run at 68–78% of operating profit for three years. [IP Q4FY26; Screener]
- Working capital is the weak spot: debtor days 118, inventory days 191, payable days 54 — cash conversion cycle 255 days (Screener). Company-defined working capital ~178 days. Inventory ₹833 cr vs ₹700 cr a year earlier. [Screener; IP Q4FY26; AR FY26]
- Dividend payout ~10% (yield 0.44%); no buyback — cash kept for acquisitions in adjacent categories. [Screener; Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- Broadest material range among Indian consumerware brands (plastic, glass, opal, steel); the only domestic player with in-house glassware manufacturing. [IP Q1FY27]
- Pricing power partly shown: 12–13% average consumerware price rise took gross margin up sequentially (52.4% vs 49.8% FY26), but volumes fell and management says no further increases are possible because Chinese imports have not risen. [Q1 call]
- Distribution: 5,000+ distributors, 1.8 lakh+ retailers; online share rising fast. [IP Q4FY26]
- Re-acquired the Cello trademark for stationery (deed of assignment, 2025), giving a two-brand pen business. [AR FY26 Chairman's letter/timeline]
- Weakness: glassware exposed to Chinese dumping; steel bottles depended on Chinese imports until the new plant; opalware facing new capacity. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Guidance withdrawn within ~70 days** of being given (10–12% growth, +2–2.5 pt margin). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Working capital heavy:** 255-day cash cycle; receivables partly from government tenders; target to cut debtor days 10–15 not yet visible. [Screener; Q4 call]
- **Contingent liabilities rose:** disputed GST/sales tax ₹33.1 cr (from ₹2.2 cr), civil (weights and measures) ₹3.7 cr, income tax ₹1.8 cr. Auditor's opinion unqualified; key audit matter is inventory existence across many sites. [AR FY26 note 38; auditor's report]
- **Energy and polymer costs:** gas up ~80% since March hits glass/opal; polymers up 12–20%. [Q1 call]
- **Promoter holding** fell from 75.0% to 73.5% in the Sep-2026 quarter, consistent with dilution from new shares issued to Wim Plast minorities; an earlier media report of a promoter stake sale drew a "nothing as of the present" response. Promoters can hold at most 75% under listing rules, so further sales remain possible. [Screener shareholding; Q1 call]
- Related-party dealings are mostly with wholly owned subsidiaries (purchases ₹607 cr in FY26 at standalone level) — eliminated in consolidation; no large promoter-entity transactions spotted. [AR FY26 note 39]
- FII holding fell from 5.5% to 2.3% over the year. [Screener shareholding]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: "FY27 significantly better year", numeric guidance, steel at full production from July. Q1: no guidance, steel still ramping, demand "subdued", price rises "absorbed" but volumes down.
- New in Q1: channel destocking, 80% gas inflation, glass peak revenue cut from ₹300 cr to ₹250–275 cr, statement that no more price rises are possible.
- Unchanged: writing instruments strong; no buyback; acquisitions sought.

## Best management quotes
- "It is too early. I would not like to provide guidance at this point." — Gaurav Rathod [Q1 call]
- "We cannot take any more price increases because many of the products coming from China have not seen price increases." — Gaurav Rathod [Q1 call]
- Steel and glass scale-up should return "about 2%, 2.5% of margins", with Cello pens adding another point. — Gaurav Rathod [Q4 call]

## My read
The balance sheet is strong and the new plants (steel, glass) plus the Cello pen brand give real growth levers, but execution has slipped for three quarters and guidance was pulled almost as soon as it was given. Margins have fallen from 25% to about 20% and cash conversion is poor because of very long receivable and inventory cycles. The stock at ~₹338 (half its high) prices in little growth; it needs steel SKUs to reach 50–55, glass utilisation to move above 70% and pens to hit ₹500 cr. A second quarter of flat revenue in Q2 (festive season) would suggest the problem is demand and competition, not just supply.
