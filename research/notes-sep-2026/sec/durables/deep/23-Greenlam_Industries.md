# Greenlam Industries (NSE: GREENLAM) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Figures are consolidated from Screener unless tagged. Company "EBITDA" is before forex gains/losses; Screener "operating profit" is close to it.

## Business and segments
- India's largest laminate maker and exporter (120+ countries), now a three-segment wood-panel company: (1) laminates & allied (HPL, compacts, clads, melamine-faced chipboard sold under Greenlam), (2) plywood & allied (plywood, decorative veneer, engineered floors and doors, under Mikasa), (3) panel & allied (chipboard/particle board from Naidupeta, Andhra Pradesh) [AR FY26 note 43; Q4 call].
- FY26 segment revenue: laminates ₹2,433 cr (EBITDA margin 15.9%, ≈₹390 cr), plywood & allied ₹400 cr (EBITDA loss ₹29.5 cr), chipboard ₹213 cr (EBITDA loss ₹23 cr) [Q4 call]. FY26 segment results (EBIT basis): laminates +₹476 cr, plywood −₹28.7 cr, panels −₹86.0 cr [AR FY26 note 43].
- Q1 FY27: laminates ₹596 cr (+7%, margin 13.9%), plywood & allied ₹106 cr (+20%, loss ₹5 cr), chipboard ₹94.6 cr (+205%, EBITDA +₹3.4 cr — first profit); floors and doors ~₹29 cr revenue, ₹1.2 cr loss [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Geography: exports ₹1,336 cr in FY26 = ~44% of sales (46% in FY25); Middle East only ~6–7% of the business [CARE Sep-26; Q1 call].
- Capacity and use (Q1 FY27): laminates 24.52 mn sheets/yr at 80% (FY26 86%); plywood 18.9 mn sq m at 39%; chipboard 2,92,380 cu m at 61% (FY26 39%); veneer 29%, floors 12%, doors 27% (FY26) [IP Q1FY27; CARE Sep-26].
- Distribution: 10 regional distribution centres, 21 branches, 40,000+ distributors/dealers/retailers in India; 4 international distribution centres, 16 overseas offices [CARE Sep-26].

## Key numbers (Screener, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly revenue / operating profit (OPM) / net profit: Jun-25 674 / 44 (7%) / −16; Sep-25 808 / 104 (13%) / 32; Dec-25 706 / 68 (10%) / −1; Mar-26 858 / 108 (13%) / 41; Jun-26 797 / 80 (10%) / 21 [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 2,306 / 296 (13%) / 138; FY25 2,569 / 275 (11%) / 68; FY26 3,046 / 325 (11%) / 56; TTM 3,169 / 361 / 93 [Screener].
- The new plants crushed profit: interest rose from ₹44 cr (FY24) to ₹96 cr (FY26) and depreciation from ₹87 cr to ₹142 cr, so FY26 profit was 60% below FY24 despite 32% more revenue [Screener].
- ROCE 12% (FY24) → 7% (FY25) → 8% (FY26); ROE ~5% [Screener].
- Unit economics Q1 FY27: laminate realisation ₹1,240/sheet (+13.6%) on sales of 4.62 mn sheets (−6.4%); plywood ₹276/sq m (+3.8%) on 1.66 mn sq m (+19%); chipboard ₹22,764/cu m (+14.6%) on 41,418 cu m (+167%) [IP Q1FY27].
- Gross margin 52.9% in Q1 (53.1% a year ago); EBITDA before forex ₹81 cr (+48%), margin 10.2% (8.1%) [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- ~₹27 cr of export shipments slipped from Q1 into Q2 because of container/vessel shortages [IP Q1FY27].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (4 Jun 2026): FY27 revenue growth "18% kind of"; laminates +10–12%; laminate margin "16%, 17%"; both chipboard and plywood to reach EBITDA break-even in FY27; plywood not pan-India until FY28; no new capacity besides two laminate lines; net debt to fall "close to around ₹50 cr" in FY27 [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 call (10 Aug 2026): 18% kept; laminates 10–12% kept (Q1 was 7%); laminate margin now "around 15%, 16%" (slightly lower); chipboard break-even **delivered** a year early in Q1 at 61% utilisation, ~70% average utilisation expected for FY27 and 18–20% margin at full use by FY29; plywood break-even softened to "the exit quarter" (not full year), ~50% utilisation for the year; FY27 capex ₹125–135 cr (₹70 cr for laminate lines); debt to fall ~₹100 cr (raised from ~₹50 cr), then "upwards of ₹150 cr" in FY28 per repayment schedule [Q1 call, official].
- Scorecard: revenue +18% in Q1 = on target; chipboard beat; plywood slower than hoped; laminate volumes missed (−6%) with shipping blamed.

## Capex and capacity
- The FY23–FY25 build-out is finished: laminate (fourth plant, Naidupeta) plus chipboard project cost ₹1,150 cr (revised from ₹1,050 cr); chipboard alone ₹875 cr (from ₹775 cr) after capacity was raised to 2,92,380 cu m, production started Jan 2025 (late) [CARE Sep-26].
- Now: two new laminate press lines at Naidupeta, ~₹70 cr, +20 lakh sheets/yr (~+8%), commercial production Q4 FY27, internal accruals; equipment is domestic and ordered [CARE Sep-26; Q1 call].
- FY27 total capex ₹125–135 cr = ₹70 cr laminate lines + ~₹40 cr routine + ₹10–20 cr tail payments on past projects [Q1 call]. Capital commitments ₹17 cr at Mar-26 (₹41 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 note 40].
- Chipboard headroom: management will watch "3–5 quarters" before deciding on more capacity; current capacity seen as enough to FY29 [Q1 call].
- Asset turns: FY26 revenue ₹3,046 cr on ₹1,709 cr fixed assets ≈ 1.8x (down from ~4x before the capex cycle) [Screener]. Chipboard segment assets ₹829 cr earned an EBIT loss of ₹86 cr in FY26 [AR FY26 note 43] — payback depends on reaching 70–90% utilisation.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹934 cr at Jun-26 (total debt ₹1,012 cr, cash and liquid investments ₹78.5 cr); ₹941 cr at Mar-26 (debt ₹1,056 cr, cash ₹115 cr) [IP Q1FY27]. Screener borrowings (incl. leases) ₹1,160 cr at Mar-26.
- Term-debt repayments ₹168 cr due in FY27 vs expected cash accruals ~₹250 cr; fund-based working-capital use ~53% [CARE Sep-26]. Total debt/EBITDA 3.81x (4.42x in FY25) [CARE Sep-26].
- Cash flow turned: CFO ₹194 cr (FY24), ₹210 cr (FY25), ₹292 cr (FY26); free cash flow −₹440 cr → −₹56 cr → +₹204 cr [Screener].
- Working capital: net working capital 56 days in Q1 FY27 (59 a year ago), 51 days at Mar-26; Screener inventory days ~197, debtor days ~26, payable days ~131 [IP Q1FY27; Screener].
- Dividend payout ~15–18% of profit (FY26 18%) [Screener].
- Rating: CARE AA-/A1+ reaffirmed 7 Sep 2026, outlook raised from Negative to Stable on chipboard profitability and lower leverage; CARE expects ~12% average revenue growth over three years [CARE Sep-26]. ICRA also rates (May 2026, not read).

## Moat evidence
- Scale and reach: largest laminate exporter; own warehouses in Italy and Germany with teams in five European countries; gaining share from European local producers where "organic growth is not happening" [Q4 call; CARE Sep-26].
- Pricing: passed on a net 7–8% price increase in Q1 (two hikes, then cuts in June and July); chemicals are ~one-third of raw-material cost; exports took 4–5% plus rupee depreciation [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Laminate profitability: FY26 laminate EBITDA margin 15.9% (+210 bp), Q4 17.2% [Q4 call]; value-added products ~50% of laminates [Q1 call].
- Chipboard: organised players gaining; Greenlam's HMR (high-moisture-resistant) grade was new to the market; realisations up 15% in a year [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Weak spots: plywood realisations near the top of the market already ("may not go up in any significant manner"); plywood still regional (South, parts of West/East) [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Contingent liabilities jumped**: consolidated indirect-tax disputes ₹70.3 cr at Mar-26 vs ₹2.8 cr a year earlier; the parent's own figure is only ₹7.0 cr, so ~₹63 cr sits in subsidiaries and is not explained in the notes. Direct-tax disputes ₹5.7 cr; letters of credit ₹39.8 cr [AR FY26 note 40, consolidated and standalone].
- Related parties (Mittal family group): purchases from Greenpanel Industries ≈ ₹74 cr a year (FY26 ₹74.1 cr, FY25 ₹72.7 cr); commission (ORC charges) ≈ ₹5.8 cr to Seema Realcon (a company where the whole-time director's brother is a director); MD & CEO pay ≈ ₹9.9 cr (≈ ₹8.3 cr) — table layout partly garbled [AR FY26 note 44].
- Auditor S S Kothari Mehta & Co. LLP: unmodified opinion, key audit matter revenue recognition (₹2,376 cr standalone sales), audit trail preserved; no CARO qualifications in group companies [AR FY26 Auditor's reports].
- Leverage and returns: ROCE 8% with ~₹930 cr net debt; profits remain thin until plywood and chipboard scale [Screener; IP].
- Disclosure: the Q1 presentation restated Q4 FY26 laminate volume to 5.33 mn sheets and realisation to ₹1,202 (the Q4 call said 5.15 mn and ₹1,243); an analyst asked, and management took it offline [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Export logistics (containers, Red Sea/West Asia) and chemical price swings (+30–80% in some chemicals) [Q1 call].
- Institutional selling: DIIs cut holding from 13.96% to 10.89% in the June 2026 quarter; promoters steady at 50.98% [Screener].
- Plywood break-even target drifted from "FY27" to "the exit quarter" of FY27 [calls].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from celebrating ₹3,000 cr revenue to explaining shipping delays and price volatility.
- Debt reduction target doubled (~₹50 cr → ~₹100 cr for FY27).
- Chipboard became profitable (a year of losses ended); plywood break-even redefined as a single exit quarter.
- Laminate margin language slipped from "16%, 17%" to "15%, 16%".
- New disclosures: ₹27 cr export deferral; floors and doors revenue ₹29 cr with ₹1.2 cr loss; domestic laminate volume −7.5%.

## My read
The business is past the worst: the capex is done, chipboard turned profitable a year early, free cash flow turned positive (₹204 cr in FY26) and CARE lifted its outlook. But earnings are still depressed — TTM profit ₹93 cr on ₹3,169 cr revenue, ROCE 8% — so the stock (~₹5,720 cr) is priced on recovery, needing profit to roughly double or triple within three years. That is plausible if chipboard reaches ~70% utilisation, plywood breaks even and interest falls with ~₹100–150 cr a year of debt repayment. I would watch the unexplained ₹63 cr subsidiary tax dispute, laminate volumes (down 6% in Q1) and whether plywood's "exit-quarter" break-even actually happens.
