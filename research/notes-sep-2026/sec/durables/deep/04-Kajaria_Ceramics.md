# Kajaria Ceramics — deep research (Phase A1, written 27 Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- India's largest tile maker. Two reported segments in FY26: **Tiles** ₹4,280 cr revenue (FY25 ₹4,172 cr), segment result ₹651 cr (15.2%, vs ₹448 cr / 10.7%); **Others** (Kerovit bathware/sanitaryware/faucets, adhesives) ₹550 cr (FY25 ₹463 cr), result ₹44 cr (8.1%, vs ₹13 cr). [AR FY26, note 41]
- Q1 FY27 revenue split: tiles ₹1,162 cr (+18%), bathware ₹122 cr (+33%), adhesives ₹45 cr (from ₹25 cr). [Q1 call, official]
- Tile capacity 82.70 million sq m (MSM) excl. 5.10 MSM Nepal JV: Gailpur (Rajasthan) 35.95, Morbi group plants ~18 (Kajaria Vitrified 8.9, Kajaria Surfaces 6.0, Kajaria Infinity ~3), Sikandrabad (UP) 8.80, Srikalahasti (AP) 8.80, Malootana 6.50, Balanagar (Telangana, South Asian Ceramics) 4.75. [IP Q1FY27]
- Volumes: FY26 sales 118.5 MSM (+3.3% on 114.7), own production 84.6 MSM; the rest bought from JVs and Morbi outsourcing partners (~27% outsourced). Q1 FY27 sales 28.8 MSM (+6%); own production 20.9 MSM (−2.5%, Morbi plants shut until mid-April). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Channel: ~1,800 dealers (450 exclusive), 100 more planned in FY27 (50 exclusive); retail ~70% of revenue, projects ~30%; "breakthrough" with two very large builders. Regional mix North 35%, South 30%, East 20%, West 15%. Exports under 1%. [Q1 call; ICRA Jan-26]
- Adhesives: 9,000 t/month at Gailpur plus a new 9,000 t/month plant at Erode (TN) from May 2026. Bathware plants at Morbi (sanitaryware 7.5 lakh + 4.5 lakh pcs) and Gailpur (faucets 1.6 million pcs). [IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit: Jun-25 ₹1,103 / ₹187 / 17% / ₹110 cr; Sep-25 ₹1,186 / ₹213 / 18% / ₹134 cr; Dec-25 ₹1,168 / ₹200 / 17% / ₹86 cr; Mar-26 ₹1,373 / ₹263 / 19% / ₹157 cr; Jun-26 ₹1,328 / ₹260 / 20% / ₹171 cr. [Screener]
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹4,474 cr, OPM 16%, PAT ₹432 cr; FY25 ₹4,635 cr, 14%, ₹300 cr; FY26 ₹4,830 cr (+4%), 18%, ₹487 cr (+62%). TTM sales ₹5,056 cr, PAT ₹548 cr. [Screener]
- The FY26 profit jump came from cost cuts ("Operation Manthan"), dealer unification and closing the loss-making plywood arm, not volume: EBITDA margin 12.76% → 17.84%. [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +20% on only 6% volume — realisation up ~11–14% after gas-driven price rises; EBITDA 19.6% (16.72% a year ago); PAT to owners ₹169 cr (+55%). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Unit economics: FY26 revenue ~₹408 per sq m sold (₹4,830 cr / 118.5 MSM); own-plant price rise ~10–13%, while Morbi-sourced product rose 40–45% (sold at the same percentage margin). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- ROCE 23% FY26 (17% FY25); ROE 18%. [Screener]
- Q4 FY26 set up the turn: volume +11% (Jan–Feb +8–9% before the Morbi shutdown), revenue +12% to ₹1,373 cr, EBITDA 19.19% vs 10.01% a year earlier, PAT ₹136 cr vs ₹43 cr; own production fell 7% as Morbi plants shut in March, and finished stock fell by ~1.5 MSM. [Q4 call]
- Fuel costs: Q4 FY26 gas ₹55.5/scm North, ₹49.6 South, ₹46.6 West; April 2026 ₹62.5 North, ~₹81 South, ~₹79 West; Q1 blended ~₹71 (North ~₹64, South ₹72–73, West ~₹85). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Cost base: FY26 staff cost ~₹530 cr (~₹130 cr a quarter) and other operating costs ~₹480 cr, after cuts; FY26 ad spend ₹92–100 cr, to rise 40–50% in FY27. [Q4 call]
- Industry context: Morbi exports running ~₹1,000 cr a month in Q1 FY27 vs ~₹16,000 cr in FY26 (war-hit freight), which keeps more Morbi supply at home once gas normalises. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (30 Apr 2026): **refused volume guidance** ("guidance… didn't work out… we perform and prove"); EBITDA margin 18–19%; ad spend to rise 40–50% in FY27; promoters to take no salary again. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivered: 6% volume (soft April — Morbi shutdown to 15 April, pre-price-rise stocking in March, labour shortages), EBITDA 19.6% (above range). [Q1 call]
- Q1 call (31 Jul 2026): **reinstated** volume guidance — "double-digit" volume growth for the next nine months; ~130 MSM FY27 sales (≈ +10%); EBITDA 18–19% and "₹1,000 crore-plus" absolute EBITDA; bathware value growth 35–40% but "difficult" profits this year. [Q1 call]
- Management at a sales meet reportedly showed a ₹6,000 cr revenue figure (analyst's reference; not confirmed as guidance). [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Two brownfield GVT (glazed vitrified tile) lines for large formats: **Srikalahasti** 10 MSM, ~₹210 cr (approved 30 Apr 2026, due March 2027; needs a new 8.5 lakh sq ft shed costing ₹80–90 cr); **Gailpur** 11 MSM, ~₹165 cr (approved 31 Jul 2026, due April 2027; shed extension ₹40–50 cr). Total ~₹375 cr for ~21–22 MSM (+26% capacity). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- FY27 capex "roughly ₹400 cr" including maintenance; plus ~₹12 cr renewable acquisition and ₹50 cr to buy the 15% Kerovit stake from the PE investor. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Cost per tonne of capacity has roughly halved: "a 5 million sq m plant used to cost ₹150 cr"; new lines use 340 m kilns (vs 200 m). Management says the Gailpur line (₹165 cr) should yield ₹400–450 cr revenue — asset turn >2x — at better margins than existing plants. [Q1 call]
- Strategic reversal: from targeting ~40% outsourcing to more own capacity; FY27 still needs ~40 MSM outsourced; Morbi dependence to fall "drastically" from Q1 FY28. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash: presentation chart shows net cash of ~₹985 cr at Mar-26 and ~₹793 cr at Jun-26 after a **₹296.7 cr buyback** (21.5 lakh shares at ₹1,380, completed). Borrowings ₹229 cr (Mar-26). ICRA: gearing 0.1x, interest cover 36.5x (H1 FY26); [ICRA]AA (Stable)/A1+. [IP Q1FY27; Screener; ICRA Jan-26]
- Cash flow strong: operating cash ₹664 cr FY26, free cash flow ₹563 cr (₹305 cr FY25). [Screener]
- Working capital cycle 65 days (Dec-25) → 51 (Mar-26) → 46 (Jun-26); FY26 inventory days 105 (126 in FY25), debtor days 47. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- Dividend payout ~46% FY26; buyback adds to returns. Promoters hold 47.7%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Multi-location fuel advantage: North plants buy GAIL gas (~₹64/scm in Q1) plus ~30% cheap biofuel (~₹20/scm) in spray dryers; Morbi players rely only on GSPC (spot ~₹85/scm, from ~₹48 before the war). Q1 blended fuel ~₹71/scm. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Price gap versus Morbi unbranded tiles narrowed from ~40% to below 20%, shifting "undecided" buyers to brands. [Q1 call]
- Scale and brand: largest capacity, ~1,800 dealers, 24–25 experience centres; ICRA calls it the largest player with better pass-through ability than smaller peers. [Q1 call; ICRA Jan-26]
- Weakness admitted: "Kajaria missed out" on large slabs — the new lines are meant to fix this. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Kerovit Global fraud**: ~₹20 cr siphoned via forged authorisations by the step-down subsidiary's CFO; police/EOW complaints; EY forensic audit; ₹19.8 cr "embezzlement" plus ₹4.8 cr loan impairment in FY26 exceptional items (total exceptional ₹44 cr incl. labour-code charges). The subsidiary's CARO report is **qualified under clause xi(a)** (fraud). [AR FY26 chairman's letter, note 63, auditor's report para 17]
- Audit-trail exception: accounting software's audit trail not enabled at database level (reported under Rule 11(g)). Consolidated audit otherwise unmodified; key audit matter is revenue recognition/rebates. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Gas dependence: if gas prices fall, Morbi can cut prices again and the 20% gap widens; FY27 volumes rely on outsourcing from Morbi. [Q1 call]
- Execution: two large lines to be commissioned in Mar–Apr 2027; if demand cools, new depreciation lands on thin utilisation. [Q1 call]
- Bathware still loss-making/low margin (Others segment 8% result margin includes adhesives); new CBO hired April 2026. [AR FY26 note 41; Q1 call]
- Contingent liabilities small (tax/duty claims and a standby letter of credit for the Nepal JV loan, each under ₹30 cr); capital commitments ₹130 cr. [AR FY26 consolidated notes]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 cautious ("no guidance"), Q1 confident (double-digit volume, ₹1,000 cr+ EBITDA).
- Capex: one project (Srikalahasti) became two (plus Gailpur), ~₹375 cr in total.
- Numbers: price rises 12–16% (Q4 view) settled at ~10–14% on own products; EBITDA margin 19.19% (Q4) → 19.60% (Q1); working capital 51 → 46 days.
- New: projects/builder channel; outsourcing target reversed.

- "We are looking at INR1,000 crores plus EBITDA overall." — Vice Chairman [Q1 call]

## My read
Kajaria has moved from a two-year slump to a clear upswing: cost resets lifted margins by five points, and the Morbi gas shock has handed branded players pricing power and share. Much of Q1's 20% revenue growth is price, not volume, and part of that advantage disappears if gas prices normalise. The price needs double-digit volume growth to hold into FY28 as the new 21 MSM comes on, with margins staying near 18%; watch Q2–Q3 volume growth against the "double-digit" promise, Morbi gas prices, and bathware losses. The Kerovit fraud was small but shows weak subsidiary controls.
