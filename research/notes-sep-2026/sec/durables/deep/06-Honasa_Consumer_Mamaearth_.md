# Honasa Consumer (Mamaearth) — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged. Honasa reports two revenue lines: "reported" (Screener) and "like-for-like" (LFL), which adds back a Flipkart settlement change that nets logistics cost out of revenue (₹87 cr in FY26, ₹29 cr in Q1 FY27; no profit effect) [AR FY26 Board's report; IP Q1FY27 p.9]. Company margins quoted on LFL revenue are therefore ~0.5 pt lower than Screener's.

## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** beauty and personal care (BPC) under a "house of brands": Mamaearth (core, naturals), The Derma Co (actives), Aqualogica (Gen Z hydration), Dr. Sheth's, BBlunt (salon hair care + salons), Staze (colour cosmetics), Reginald Men/Molecular (men's skincare, via 95% of BTM Ventures bought Jan 2026), and FIKN (fine fragrance, launched Q1 FY27) [IP Q1FY27 p.14, p.20; AR FY26 Board's report].
- **Single reported segment** (BPC, India-dominated); no segment revenue split is disclosed; brand splits are given only as annual run-rates (ARR) [AR FY26 note on segment information].
- **Brand scale:** Mamaearth is the largest brand ("2,000+ Cr" by FY31 is a target) — no current revenue figure disclosed; The Derma Co reached ₹1,000 cr NSV ARR in Q1 FY27 (₹750 cr+ at Q4) with "teens" EBITDA; BTM Ventures ₹150 cr+ ARR (₹70 cr+ revenue in the 12 months to Oct-25 at ~25% EBITDA when bought); six brands above ₹100 cr ARR [IP Q1FY27 p.15–16; Q4 call; AR FY26].
- **Channel mix:** online 68% / offline 32% of FY26 revenue (FY25: 72.4/27.6) [AR FY26 key-metrics page]. Offline reach ~3 lakh FMCG outlets (NielsenIQ), ~1.2 lakh billed directly; direct distributors now ~80% of GT billing vs ~25% two years ago; distributor stock ~30 days [IP Q1FY27 p.17; AR FY26 MD&A].
- **Focus categories:** face cleansers, shampoo, sunscreen, moisturiser, serums, lipstick, baby — 85%+ of Q1 FY27 revenue (+450 bps YoY) [IP Q1FY27 p.11].
- **Manufacturing:** none — outsourced to contract makers ("does not operate any manufacturing facilities") [AR FY26 BRSR]. A small R&D lab/facility came with the Cosmogenesis asset purchase (FY25) [AR FY26 standalone PPE note].

## Guidance history
- **Investor Day (May 2026) targets for FY31:** revenue ₹5,500 cr+ (core brands ₹3,750 cr, young brands ₹1,500 cr, new horizons — nutraceuticals, fragrance, oral care — ₹250 cr); EBITDA margin +500 bps to ~15%; Mamaearth ₹2,000 cr+; The Derma Co ₹1,500 cr+; two more ₹500 cr+ brands; "fastest FMCG company to ₹5,000 cr" [IP Q4FY26/Investor Day pp.28–31].
- **Q4 call (May 2026):** high-teens revenue CAGR for five years; +100 bps EBITDA a year; Mamaearth double-digit CAGR; price increases already taken, "we do not expect further price increases"; Derma Co to ₹1,000 cr [Q4 call].
- **Q1 call (Aug 2026) — raised for FY27:** FY27 will be "better than that CAGR" (above high-teens), Mamaearth above its double-digit plan, and FY27 EBITDA margin +150–200 bps vs FY26 (FY26 LFL ~9.3%, so ~11–11.3%). Q2 will see gross-margin pressure from packaging/crude inflation, offset by late-Q1 price hikes; "growth first" if reinvestment is needed [Q1 call].
- **Delivery vs promise:** Derma Co ₹1,000 cr ARR — delivered in Q1. Mamaearth "double-digit" — beat (high-teens). Q4 said no further price rises needed; by Q1 packaging inflation required "calibrated" late-Q1 hikes — minor slip. Nutraceutical acquisition (Fluence, announced Jun-26) — **terminated 25 Aug 2026** for unmet closing conditions [News: ScanX, 25 Aug 2026].

## Capex and capacity
- **Asset-light:** outsourced manufacturing; capital commitments only ₹1 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 37]. Fixed assets rose from ₹302 cr to ₹489 cr in FY26, largely goodwill/brand from the BTM deal [Screener; AR FY26 note 41].
- **Capital goes into brands and acquisitions:** BTM Ventures 95% for ₹198 cr (Jan 2026), remaining 5% to be bought after 12 months at a pre-agreed valuation (liability ~₹11 cr); Couch Commerce (Fang Oral) 25% for ₹10 cr, with a right to go to 51% on revenue milestones; Fluence Pharma 58% at ₹135 cr enterprise value — approved 23 Jun 2026, cancelled 25 Aug 2026 [AR FY26 notes; News: ScanX, 25 Aug 2026; StartupTalky].
- **New subsidiaries:** Honasa Health (nutraceuticals, incorporated 7 Jul 2026, ₹1 lakh capital) [AR FY26 Board's report].
- **IPO money:** ₹182 cr for advertising, ₹20.6 cr for exclusive brand outlets, ₹26 cr for BBlunt salons, ₹122 cr general/acquisitions from the Nov-2023 fresh issue [AR FY26 note 47].
- **Growth engine is A&P, not plant:** 4,000+ influencer collaborations in FY26; A&P ~32% of revenue [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **No bank debt;** ₹135 cr of "borrowings" are lease liabilities [Screener; AR FY26]. Large treasury (investments ₹272 cr non-current plus deposits in other assets) produces ~₹75–80 cr other income a year [Screener].
- **Cash conversion:** CFO ₹141 cr in FY26 (85% of operating profit), FCF ₹134 cr; FY25 CFO ₹102 cr; FY24 ₹235 cr [Screener]. Q1 FY27 cash generated ₹83 cr [Q1 call].
- **Working capital:** negative — cash conversion cycle −71 days in FY26 (debtor 30, inventory 92, payables 193 days); company measure −14 working-capital days of sales [Screener; AR FY26].
- **Dividend:** maiden ₹3/share (~₹98 cr, ~51% of standalone PAT) for FY26; new policy of 25–75% payout adopted 13 Aug 2026 [AR FY26 Board's report].
- **Promoter buying:** Varun Alagh bought ₹50 cr of shares at ₹270 in a Dec-2025 block deal (from Fireside Ventures); promoter group 35.5% [News: Angel One / Entrackr, Dec 2025; Screener shareholding].

## Moat evidence
- **Share gains (NielsenIQ, all-India urban, Jun-26):** face cleanser value share 6.9% (+138 bps), share among handlers 11.2%; shampoo 2.5% (+93 bps), handlers 4.8%. In MT, +360 bps in face cleansers and +166 bps in shampoo [IP Q1FY27 p.13, p.17].
- **Brand health:** Kantar Brand Power for Mamaearth at a three-year high; Mamaearth ranked #1 online and #3 offline in face cleansers; Derma Co called the largest actives brand in India (Euromonitor) [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call].
- **Pricing power — modest:** calibrated price rises in Q1 matched competition; relative price index kept "at the premium we want" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Switching costs — low:** BPC consumers switch easily; defence is speed of launches, content and search share. Six blind-test wins in FY26 [AR FY26 MD&A].
- **Cost position:** negative working capital and no plants mean growth needs little capital; but gross margin ~70% is typical for premium BPC and A&P is heavy.

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matters are revenue recognition (discounts/returns provisions) and goodwill/brand impairment [AR FY26 auditor's report]. **Audit-trail lapse:** one price-master application ran without an edit log all year, and three systems (distribution, price master, point of sale) do not log privileged-user changes [AR FY26 auditor's report, Rule 11(g)].
- **Contingent liability — Dubai distributor dispute:** RSM General Trading claimed AED 45 m (₹100 cr); Dubai court once ordered AED 25.07 m (₹58 cr); after retrial experts cut it to AED 1.75 m (₹4.3 cr); Indian arbitral award (14 May 2026) found Honasa's termination valid and awarded Honasa AED 7.25 m (₹19 cr); a Dubai Cassation Court hearing was set for 17 Jun 2026 (outcome not found). Claims not acknowledged as debts: ₹34 cr (₹28 cr in FY25) [AR FY26 note 37].
- **Revenue-quality history:** the Q2 FY25 GT inventory correction (operating loss ₹31 cr) is the main credibility scar; management now tracks every distributor's stock (<30 days) [Screener; Q1 call].
- **Goodwill build-up** from BBlunt and BTM; impairment tested, no impairment [AR FY26].
- **Deal execution:** the Fluence cancellation two months after announcement shows diligence gaps or overreach in the nutraceutical push [News: ScanX, 25 Aug 2026].
- **Input costs:** crude-linked packaging inflation hits Q2 FY27; hikes taken [Q1 call].
- **Disclosure gaps:** no brand-wise revenue or channel split (quick-commerce vs marketplaces declined); ESOP one-off in Q1 flatters reported margin [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.8].
- **Related parties:** transactions are with subsidiaries and KMP pay; nothing unusual found [AR FY26 note 34].

## My read
The business is clearly improving: operating margin went from ~5% in FY25 to 15% in Q1 FY27, growth is volume-led (30.5%), and share gains show in NielsenIQ data, not just company claims. Capital needs are tiny, so growth converts to cash and dividends; the real "capex" is A&P and small acquisitions. The Q1 margin was flattered by summer mix and a ~150 bps ESOP reversal, so the clean run-rate is ~12%, and FY27 guidance implies ~11%. At a Screener P/E of ~60, the price needs earnings to grow roughly 26% a year for a decade; the FY31 plan (₹5,500 cr revenue at ~15% EBITDA, ~₹825 cr EBITDA vs ₹231 cr in FY26) would deliver that only if executed in full. The view would weaken on another channel-inventory correction, share loss in face wash/shampoo, or more aborted deals; it would strengthen if Mamaearth sustains double-digit growth offline and FIKN/nutraceuticals scale without margin drag.
