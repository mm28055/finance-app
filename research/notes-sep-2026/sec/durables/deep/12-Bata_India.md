# Bata India — deep research (Phase A1)

Researched 27 Sep 2026. Figures from Screener consolidated page (raw HTML read) unless tagged. Price ₹630, market cap ₹8,095 cr, 52-week range ₹605–1,238 [Screener, 27-Sep-26].

## Business and segments
- India's largest footwear retailer: Bata (core), Hush Puppies (licensed from Wolverine World Wide), Power (sports), Floatz, North Star (sneakers), Comfit. 50.16% owned by Bata (BN) BV, part of the Swiss-based Bata Shoe Organisation. Single reportable segment. [AR FY26 note on segments; Screener shareholding; ICRA Feb-25]
- Channel mix (FY26, management estimate): company-owned stores (COCO) about 65%; franchise "close to double digit"; e-commerce 8–13% (answers varied on the call); multi-brand distribution 10–12%. [Q4 call]
- Network (June 2026): 2,000+ exclusive stores — about 1,250 COCO (incl. ~125 Hush Puppies) and 750 franchise; ~370 shop-in-shops; distribution in 1,678 towns via 17,000+ multi-brand outlets; 1,050+ stores fulfil online orders. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Mix by price and brand: products under ₹1,000 MRP are 35–40% of sales and have stopped declining after GST cuts; Hush Puppies is 18–20% of turnover and the fastest-growing brand. [Q4 call]
- Manufacturing: in-house plants (Batanagar, Bataganj, Peenya, Hosur listed by ICRA) are being shrunk via VRS and plant closures; sourcing partners cut from ~120 to "close to 60", target ~30 (15 core + 15 satellite) over 3–5 years. [ICRA Feb-25; Q1 call; AR FY26 note 39]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (₹ cr): Q1 FY26 revenue 942 / EBITDA 199 / OPM 21% / PAT 52; Q2 FY26 801 / 145 / 18% / 14; Q3 FY26 945 / 212 / 22% / 66; Q4 FY26 828 / 151 / 18% / 2; **Q1 FY27 979 / 204 / 21% / 64**. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +3.9% (volume +2.3%, rest price/mix); gross margin ₹536 cr, +130 bps (about +230 bps before the franchise-mix drag of ~100 bps); PBT before exceptional items 9.3% of sales (+135 bps); "adjusted" PBT ₹90.7 cr vs ₹74.5 cr (+22%) after stripping VRS, forex loss on licence fees and one-time ERP cost. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Annual: FY24 revenue ₹3,479 cr, EBITDA ₹797 cr (23%), PAT ₹263 cr; FY25 ₹3,489 cr, ₹751 cr (22%), ₹331 cr (includes ₹134 cr land-sale gain); FY26 ₹3,516 cr, ₹726 cr (21%), ₹134 cr. TTM ₹3,553 cr, ₹712 cr, ₹146 cr. [Screener; AR FY26 note 39]
- The core problem: revenue has been flat for four years (₹3,452 cr in FY23 to ₹3,516 cr in FY26) while depreciation (mostly store leases) rose from ₹295 cr to ₹420 cr, so PBT fell from ₹430 cr to ₹182 cr. [Screener]
- FY26 exceptional and one-off items: VRS ₹42.4 cr, labour-code gratuity ₹6.7 cr, non-cash forex loss ₹22.4 cr on the dollar Hush Puppies licence liability; total one-offs ₹71.4 cr. [AR FY26 Board's report; note 39]
- Unit economics: inventory down 37% vs Q1 FY24; stock turns 2.54 (from 1.88 two years ago); full-price sales 88% (from 73% in Q1 FY25); lines per store at 68% of two years ago. Franchise partners earn 18–24% ROI; franchise like-for-like growth "high single digit" for four quarters. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Policy: no numeric revenue or margin guidance on either call. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Q4 call (3 Jun 2026) promises:** ZBM (zero-based merchandising, a store range-reset programme) to 800–850 stores by end-Q1; franchise "very close to 1,000" within ~12 months; raw-material inflation 5–6% to be offset by pricing ("neutral"); product reimagination to show over 12 months. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 delivery (13 Aug 2026):** ZBM at 775 stores (slightly short of 800–850, but covering ~80% of COCO revenue); franchise 750 (up from 722 in March); price increases taken, landing in stores from September because of 140–150 days of inventory; adjusted PBT +22%. Revenue growth slowed to 3.9% from ~5% in Q3/Q4. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- New medium-term statements (Q1): supply-chain consolidation to add "about 200 basis points" of margin over 3–5 years (0.2–0.3% a year from consolidation alone); advertising to run at 3–3.5% of sales vs ~2.5% a year earlier; new product range fully in stores by Jan–Mar 2027. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex only ₹55.4 cr (FY25 ₹337.6 cr, mostly the ₹257.8 cr Hush Puppies licence recognised as an intangible). Funded internally; no bank borrowings. [AR FY26 Board's report; AR FY26 note 40]
- Growth is asset-light: franchise stores are "bought-out" (franchisee pays rent and capex); Bata pays lease rent only on COCO stores. ~600 more franchise trade areas identified; long-run store ambition 3,000. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Manufacturing is being shrunk, not expanded (VRS at two units in FY26). Management wants India to be a sourcing/export hub for the Bata group, but no export numbers were given. [AR FY26 note 39; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Future growth drivers named: reimagined product funnel (30% fewer kits, 25% fewer styles), North Star sneaker relaunch, Hush Puppies store expansion, e-commerce (+13% in Q1; bata.com +25%), KRO/MBO expansion (KRO outlets 3,472, target 4,000+ by Q2 FY27). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and bank balances ₹707.6 cr plus small mutual-fund holdings at March 2026 (vs ₹815 cr a year earlier); no bank debt. Screener "borrowings" of ₹1,387 cr are lease liabilities (and the licence liability). [AR FY26 balance sheet; Screener]
- CFO ₹595 cr, free cash flow ₹530 cr in FY26 (FY25: ₹738 cr / ₹670 cr). [Screener]
- Inventory days fell to 164 from 227 (FY24); debtor days rose to 19 from 8 as franchise, B2B e-commerce and distribution (credit channels) grew — receivables up ~65% in FY26; management says no quality issue. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Dividend: ₹9/share proposed for FY26 (₹115.7 cr, about 86% payout), versus ₹19 for FY25 (₹10 interim + ₹9 final). Yield on ₹9 at ₹630 ≈ 1.4%. [AR FY26 note 26; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Largest footwear retail network in India (first brand to pass 2,000 exclusive stores). [Q1 call]
- Brand: consideration score 66 (from 60 in 2024); Google rating 4.85; NPS 88. [IP Q1FY27]
- Execution evidence: full-price sales 73% → 88% in two years, inventory −37% with availability +12%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Weakness: four years of flat revenue, lost relevance with younger buyers (sneakers), and margins that depend on premium licensed brands (Hush Puppies). Price rises of 5–6% have not yet been tested on shelves. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **CEO change:** MD & CEO Gunjan Shah leaves on 30 Sep 2026 at the end of his five-year term; Sanjay Rao (ex-Nike Retail France/Benelux, ex-Inditex, ex-Guess) joined as MD & CEO from 24 Aug 2026. The stock rose 18% intraday to ₹803 on the announcement (18 Jun 2026) but is now ₹630. Chief Strategy Officer Badri Beriwal resigned (effective 14 Sep 2026); several internal SMP reshuffles effective 1 Oct 2026. [News: Upstox, 18-Jun-26; BSE filings 9, 21, 23 Sep 2026]
- **Payments to the Bata group rising:** technical collaboration fees to Global Footwear Services ₹73.6 cr in FY26 (+31% from ₹56.1 cr) plus brand royalty to Bata Brands ₹11.6 cr and service fees ₹6.5 cr — about ₹92 cr, roughly 2.6% of sales, at a time when profit fell. [AR FY26 note on related parties]
- **Currency:** the Hush Puppies minimum royalty is a dollar liability (₹257.8 cr at inception); rupee weakness caused a ₹22.4 cr non-cash loss in FY26 and more in Q1 FY27. [AR FY26 notes 25, 40; IP Q1FY27]
- **Rating non-cooperation:** ICRA moved its rating from [ICRA]AA+ to [ICRA]BB+ "Issuer Not Cooperating" (Jul 2024–Jan 2025) and withdrew it in Feb 2025 at the company's request (the facilities were closed). Not a credit concern (no debt), but a disclosure lapse. [ICRA Feb-25]
- Contingent liabilities small: ₹19.0 cr (FY25 ₹23.8 cr). Auditor's opinion unqualified; key audit matter is revenue recognition across many stores. [AR FY26 note 29; auditor's report]
- Input costs: 5–6% blended raw-material inflation (imported synthetics, crude-linked); state minimum-wage hikes are "structural". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Competition from global and D2C brands at the premium end and unorganised players below ₹1,000. [AR FY26 MD&A]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 was "second consecutive quarter of accelerating growth"; Q1 growth slowed to 3.9% but profit quality improved (adjusted PBT +22%).
- New in Q1: explicit 200 bps supply-chain margin target, higher ad-spend baseline (3–3.5%), Project Elevate (ZBM 2.0), franchise ROI disclosure.
- Leadership: Q1 was the outgoing CEO's last call; analysts wished him well. The strategy owner is changing just as the product reset lands (Jan–Mar 2027).

## Best management quotes
- "I think only 10% of the work that we have put in, in investments have at least manifested to consumers." — Gunjan Shah [Q4 call]
- "All these initiatives combined… should give us about 200 basis points, thereabouts." — Bata management on supply-chain consolidation [Q1 call]
- Franchise partner ROI of "18% to 24% is almost like an underlying floor for a successful partner." — Gunjan Shah [Q1 call]

## My read
The back-end clean-up is real — inventory, full-price sales and franchise economics are all better — but revenue has not grown for four years and reported profit has fallen because lease depreciation, group fees and one-offs keep rising. The next two quarters test whether 5–6% price rises stick and whether the reimagined range (Jan–Mar 2027) restarts volume growth. A new CEO from Nike/Zara adds both upside and a strategy-reset risk. The price needs revenue growth to return to high single digits with operating leverage; another flat year would undermine the thesis.
