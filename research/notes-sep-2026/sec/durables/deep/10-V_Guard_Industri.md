# V-Guard Industries — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged. Kochi-based, promoter (Chittilappilly family) holds 53.2% [Screener].

**Transcript check (official BSE transcripts read):**
- **Q1 FY27 call (30 Jul 2026): materially wrong.** Stored copy says consolidated PAT "140 crores, up 89%" — official: **₹130 crores, up 76%** (presentation: ₹130.25 cr, +76.4%); stored EBITDA growth "64.5%" — official **54.5%**; stored cause of inflation "Russia-Ukraine conflict" — official **West Asia** war. The stored profile and Q1 analysis repeat the wrong ₹140 cr / 89%. Other figures (₹1,810 cr, +23.5%, South +36.7%, non-South +12%, 36.9% gross margin, 10.5% margin, ₹670 cr net cash, 14% price / 9% volume, 5–18% price range, ₹150–170 cr capex, >15% FY27 growth, 9–10% margin, ~65% in-house, 2.2% → 2.5% ad spend, electronics 18–18.5% margin kept) match.
- **Q4 FY26 call (12 May 2026): matches official** (stored text is the company's BSE filing).

## Business and segments
- **Four segments (Q1 FY27 revenue / segment result / margin):** Electronics (stabilisers, UPS/inverters, batteries, solar inverters) ₹658 cr / ₹131 cr / 19.8%; Electricals (house wires, pumps, switchgear, modular switches, lighting) ₹670 cr / ₹71 cr / 10.5%; Consumer Durables (fans, water heaters, kitchen appliances, air coolers) ₹417 cr / ₹15 cr / 3.6% (loss of ₹7 cr a year ago); Sunflame (kitchen brand bought 2022) ₹66 cr / ₹2.6 cr / 4.0% [IP Q1FY27 p.10].
- **FY26:** Electronics ₹1,640 cr (+8.6%, 27.5% of revenue); Electricals ₹2,461 cr (+13.4%, 41.2%); Consumer Durables ₹1,616 cr; Sunflame ₹250 cr with margin 5.3% (2.5% in FY25) [AR FY26 MD's message].
- **Market shares of organised market:** stabilisers 40–45% (leader); DUPS & batteries 5–7%; house wires 8–9%; switchgear ~5%; pumps 9–12%; water heaters 14–16%; fans 5–7%; solar water heaters 15–17%; mixer grinders 5–7% [IP Q1FY27 p.13–14]. Management: top-3 in water heaters, top-4 in domestic wires [Q1 call].
- **Geography:** non-South 48% of FY26 revenue (5% in FY08); Q1 FY27 South 51.5% after a 36.7% jump; Kerala ~15–16% [IP Q1FY27 p.11; Q4 call].
- **Manufacturing:** ~65% of revenue from in-house products; plants in Tamil Nadu, Uttarakhand, Himachal, Telangana, Haryana, Sikkim, Gujarat; average factory utilisation 59% in FY26 [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27 p.15].
- **Distribution:** 35 branches, ~1 lakh channel partners; ~35% of the debtor book under channel financing [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- **New bets:** solar inverters/rooftop (B2C, ~3 years old, "has not reached ₹500 crore"), battery energy storage launch due in 2–3 months, lighting entry, Gegadyne Energy (30.35% associate, battery tech) [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 note 39].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr, revenue / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 1,466 / 124 / 8% / 74; Sep-25 1,341 / 109 / 8% / 65; Dec-25 1,404 / 123 / 9% / 57 (₹22 cr Labour Code charge); Mar-26 1,755 / 171 / 10% / 112; Jun-26 1,811 / 191 / 11% / 130 [Screener].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹4,857 cr, op. profit ₹428 cr (9%), PAT ₹258 cr; FY25 ₹5,578 cr / ₹514 cr (9%) / ₹314 cr; FY26 ₹5,966 cr / ₹527 cr (9%) / ₹308 cr; TTM ₹6,310 cr / ₹594 cr / ₹365 cr [Screener]. 5-year sales CAGR ~17% (FY21–26, partly Sunflame acquisition); OPM stuck at 8–11% for a decade [Screener].
- **FY26:** revenue +7%, almost all in H2; EBITDA margin 8.8% (9.2% FY25); H2 margin ~9.5% [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27:** revenue +23.5% = ~14% price + ~9% volume; gross margin 36.9% (flat); ad spend 2.2% of revenue (3.0%); employee cost 9.1% (9.6%); other expenses 17.2% (18.9%); EBITDA ₹191 cr (+54.5%), 10.5%; PAT ₹130 cr (+76.4%) [IP Q1FY27 p.5, p.8].
- **Unit economics:** normal year = 10–12% volume + 2–3% price; this year a 12–14% portfolio price rise in four months — "unheard of"; wires volume "minimal" [Q1 call].
- **Returns:** ROCE 18.4% (Screener); TTM ROE 14.5%, core ROCE ex-cash 22.9% [IP Q1FY27 p.9].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (May 2026):** long-run 15% growth (10–12% volume + 1–3% price); FY27 price growth "could be higher"; margin: "ideally… 10%" but would wait "a couple of quarters" before committing given West Asia inflation; ~75% of needed price rises taken, the rest in May–June; lighting launch "early next quarter" [Q4 call].
- **Q1 call (Jul 2026):** FY27 growth "definitely… more than 15%"; 9–10% EBITDA margin maintained; 80–85% of pricing done; capex ₹150–170 cr a year for two years (vs analysts' ₹200–250 cr assumption); electronics segment margin 18–18.5% kept; ad spend 2.5% for FY27; Sunflame to regain pre-acquisition profitability in 3–5 years and combined kitchen business to reach "four digits" (₹1,000 cr+) [Q1 call].
- **Delivery vs Q4 promise:** margin 10.5% — beat the cautious Q4 stance; growth 23.5% — beat 15%; price pass-through completed as promised. Lighting launch still pending.
- **Credit agency view:** ICRA upgraded to AA+ (Stable) in Apr 2026 on scale, non-South diversification and in-housing; it had expected only 4–5% FY26 growth [ICRA Apr-26].

## Capex and capacity
- **FY26 capex ₹173.7 cr** for in-house manufacturing, capacity, NPD and IT; gross block ₹2,010 cr [AR FY26 MD&A].
- **Projects:** Hyderabad battery plant expansion (started May 2025); new fans factory in Hyderabad, commercial production in FY27; Innovation Campus (Kochi) opened Jan 2026 [AR FY26 MD's message].
- **Guidance:** ₹150–170 cr a year for FY27–28; management says it spent ₹300–400 cr over eight years building plants and the "extended investment phase" in costs may last 2–3 more years (renewables, lighting, innovation) [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Utilisation:** 59% average in FY26, so volume can grow without big new plants [AR FY26].
- **Gegadyne:** ₹25 cr more invested in FY26 (stake 30.35%); earlier ₹6.6 cr secured loan plus up to ₹4 cr interim loan, fully repaid by Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 39; Board's report].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Net cash** ₹670 cr at 30 Jun 2026 (cash ₹576 cr + current investments ₹102 cr − debt ₹8 cr) vs ₹231 cr at Mar-26 and ₹155 cr a year earlier; Screener borrowings ₹165 cr at Mar-26 include leases [IP Q1FY27 p.9; Screener].
- **Why the jump:** Q1 CFO ₹474 cr (₹134 cr a year ago) because creditor days rose to 91 from 71 — copper bought domestically on credit instead of imported against advances/LCs (mix 95:5 vs 70–80:20–30); management expects payables to normalise [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- **FY26:** CFO ₹459 cr (107% of operating profit), FCF ₹279 cr; cash conversion cycle 60 days (70 in FY25); debtor days 33 [Screener].
- **Dividend:** ₹1.50/share for FY26 (payout ~21%) [Q4 call; Screener].
- **Rating:** [ICRA]AA+ (Stable)/A1+ (Apr 2026) [ICRA Apr-26].

## Moat evidence
- **Stabiliser dominance:** 40–45% of the organised market; electronics segment margin ~19–20% [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Apr-26].
- **Pricing power in the South:** 36.7% South growth despite 12–14% price rises; in the non-South V-Guard raised prices ahead of rivals and lost some air-cooler share [Q1 call].
- **In-house manufacturing:** ~65% of revenue; supports a 36–37% gross margin, higher than peers per analysts, though EBITDA margin has not risen [Q1 call].
- **Weak spots:** fans 5–7% share; wires a commodity — "if you start a price war, you won't be able to end it" [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- **BLDC:** ~40% of ceiling-fan sales [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Wires competition:** a large new entrant launching around the festive season plus lighting companies entering; wires need ~₹1,000 cr scale to make money [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Weather and geography:** seasonal products (fans, coolers, stabilisers) swung FY26 to +7% growth; non-South (48% of sales) is weaker and more price-sensitive [Q4 call; ICRA Apr-26].
- **Kitchen/Sunflame:** acquired 2022; revenue ₹250 cr in FY26, margin 5.3%; merger into V-Guard approved in principle Jul 2025 (not yet done); recovery to pre-acquisition margins will take 3–5 years [AR FY26; Q1 call].
- **Auditor:** unmodified; key audit matter is revenue recognition (trade discounts and volume rebates); audit trail operated throughout the year [AR FY26 auditor's report & CARO].
- **Contingent liabilities (standalone):** ₹83.4 cr (₹69.1 cr) — direct tax ₹65.3 cr, indirect tax ₹17.5 cr [AR FY26 note on contingent liabilities] — small.
- **Related parties:** loans to associate Gegadyne (repaid) and equity top-ups; small [AR FY26].
- **Promoter holding** drifted from 55.6% (Sep-23) to 53.2% (Jun-26), including a ~1-point drop in the Sep-25 quarter (reason not verified) [Screener].
- **Payables-driven cash:** Q1 cash flow is flattered by temporarily high creditors.

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: cautious — would not commit to 10% margin, "wait a couple of quarters"; Q1: delivered 10.5% and said margins "should be okay for the full year" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Q4: 75% of price rises landed; Q1: 80–85% done, some commodities easing [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New in Q1: capex guided down to ₹150–170 cr; battery storage launch; kitchen four-digit ambition; wires competition framed as "lose-lose" price war to avoid.
- Q4 said solar business below ₹500 cr; Q1 described BESS as "the next big thing" with ₹1.5–2 lakh per home [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## My read
V-Guard is a steady, cash-generative mid-sized electricals company whose Q1 FY27 was exceptional (South summer plus a once-in-a-decade price surge), not a new normal: management's own long-run plan is ~15% growth at 9–10% EBITDA margin. Capex is tapering to ₹150–170 cr a year with plants at ~59% utilisation, so free cash should improve; the ₹670 cr net cash is partly a timing effect of higher payables. At a Screener P/E of ~35 the price needs earnings to grow about 20% a year for a decade, above the 15% revenue/flat-margin plan unless margins break above 10% or solar/BESS scales quickly. Signals to watch: whether 10%+ margins hold in Q2–Q3 as prices normalise, wires share after new entrants launch, and Sunflame margin progress.
