# Relaxo Footwears — deep research (Phase A1)

Researched 27 Sep 2026. Figures from Screener standalone page (Relaxo has no consolidated accounts; raw HTML read) unless tagged. Price ₹297, market cap ₹7,393 cr, 52-week range ₹236–473 [Screener, 27-Sep-26].
Calls: Relaxo held earnings calls for Q2 FY26 (14 Nov 2025) and Q4 FY26 (29 May 2026) only; no call was held for Q3 FY26 or Q1 FY27 (presentation only). The "last two transcripts" are therefore Q4 FY26 and Q2 FY26.

## Business and segments
- India's largest non-leather footwear maker by volume: rubber hawai slippers, EVA/PU slippers and sandals, sports and casual shoes. Brands: Sparx (~40% of sales), Flite (~35%), Relaxo/Bahamas (~25%) per the official Q4 transcript. Single segment. [Q4 call, official; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Mix: open footwear ~80%, closed ~20% (unchanged for two years); men 70%, women 25%, kids 5%; more than 98% of the range priced below ₹2,500. [Q2 call; Q4 call]
- Channels (FY26 presentation): general trade ~74%, new channels (e-commerce/quick commerce/LFR) ~12%, own retail ~9%, exports ~5%. ~630 distributors, 70,000+ retailers, 429 EBOs (June 2026), exports to ~37 countries. [IP Q1FY27]
- Manufacturing: 9 plants "spread across three cities" (ICRA; Bahadurgarh, Haryana is one, per AR note 32); capacity ~10.5 lakh pairs/day (~30 crore pairs a year per ICRA), **recalibrated down to 9.1 lakh pairs/day** by the board on 13-Aug-2026 while two plants are modernised for fashion products. [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Nov-25]
- Utilisation (our estimate): FY26 sales of 17.5 crore pairs against ~30 crore capacity ≈ 58%; still only ~65% on the reduced 9.1 lakh/day base. [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Nov-25]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (₹ cr, Screener): Q1 FY26 revenue 654 / EBITDA 99 / OPM 15% / PAT 49; Q2 FY26 629 / 81 / 13% / 36; Q3 FY26 668 / 69 / 10% / 27; Q4 FY26 751 / 124 / 17% / 68; **Q1 FY27 705 / 108 / 15% / 55**. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +7.7% but pairs sold fell to 4.2 crore from 4.3 crore; realisation per pair up ~10% to ₹166 from ₹151 — growth was price, not volume. EBITDA margin 15.4% vs 15.2%; PAT +12%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: FY24 revenue ₹2,914 cr, EBITDA ₹409 cr (14%), PAT ₹200 cr; FY25 ₹2,790 cr, ₹385 cr (14%), ₹170 cr; FY26 ₹2,702 cr (−3%), ₹377 cr (14%), ₹179 cr. TTM ₹2,753 cr / ₹383 cr / ₹185 cr. [Screener]
- Long view: revenue has not grown since FY22 (₹2,653 cr); PAT is 39% below the FY21 peak of ₹292 cr; OPM was 21% in FY21. Volumes FY25 17.8 crore pairs, FY26 17.5 crore. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- Management blames the Jan-2022 GST rise (5% → 12%) for "90%" of the slump; GST 2.0 (Sep-2025) cut it back to 5% for footwear under ₹2,500. [Q2 call]
- Cost push (Q4 call): blended cost of goods +15–20% (materials, e.g. one input from 120 to 240–250; Haryana wages +25–30%, other states +10–15%); blended price rises 15–18% taken in 2–3 steps. [Q4 call, official]

## Guidance history
- **Q2 FY26 call (Nov 2025):** Q3 flat or −3–4%; some growth in Q4; FY27 "definitely… much better", a return to ~10% growth "just a matter of time"; capex ₹100–150 cr a year, no capacity expansion for 18 months. [Q2 call]
- Delivery: Q3 FY26 revenue flat (₹668 cr vs ₹667 cr) — **met**; Q4 +8.1% — **met**. [Screener]
- **Q4 FY26 call (29 May 2026):** volume growth "at least 4–5%" a year; FY27 operating margin "1% plus" above FY26's 13.8%; 100 new EBOs in FY27 (30–40% in H1, most by December); capex ₹180–200 cr; ad spend 4–5% of sales. Price rises "cannot be reversed". [Q4 call, official]
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (no call; presentation 13 Aug 2026):** volume −2% (4.2 vs 4.3 crore pairs) — **behind** the 4–5% aim; EBITDA margin 15.4% for Q1 (seasonally strong quarter) — on track so far; EBOs 429 vs 420 in May — only ~9 net additions against 30–40 planned by September — **behind**. [IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹139.4 cr (FY25 ₹61.6 cr), internally funded; FY27 plan ₹180–200 cr: moulds, a new head office, machinery replacement, and ~₹30–35 cr for 100 EBOs (₹30–35 lakh per store). No capacity addition — capacity is being reduced/modernised. Capital commitments ₹34.5 cr at March 2026. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call; AR FY26 note 32]
- Plant modernisation (RFL-I and II) is meant to add "fashion-forward" production; payback not disclosed. [IP Q1FY27]
- Growth levers named: Sparx range extended from ₹999–1,800 up to ₹2,800; athleisure; women and kids; 100 new-format EBOs in West and later South; quick commerce (Blinkit, Zepto). [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- No term debt; Screener "borrowings" ₹233 cr are lease liabilities. Treasury: investments ₹436 cr on Screener (March 2026); ICRA noted cash and equivalents above ₹400 cr and ~₹135 cr undrawn limits at Sep-2025. Rated **[ICRA]AA (Stable) / [ICRA]A1+**, reaffirmed 28-Nov-2025. [Screener; ICRA Nov-25]
- CFO ₹348 cr, free cash flow ₹213 cr in FY26 (FY25 ₹406 cr / ₹291 cr). [Screener]
- Working capital: inventory days 184, debtor days 40, payable days 87, cash cycle 138 days (FY26). Q1 FY27 P&L shows inventories rising by ~₹99 cr in the quarter (change-in-inventory line) — the company is carrying more stock despite saying it is buying cautiously. Inverted GST duty (5% output vs 18% on some inputs) blocks credit on services and capital goods. [Screener; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Dividend ₹3.50/share for FY26 (~49% payout; yield ~1.2%). [AR FY26 MD&A; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and cost: in-house manufacturing of ~17.5 crore pairs a year at ~₹153 average realisation — cheaper than most branded peers and able to compete with the unorganised sector once the tax gap closed. [IP Q1FY27; Q2 call]
- Distribution: 70,000+ retailers; trade terms of ~30% retailer margin and ~9% distributor discount. [Q2 call]
- Pricing power partial: 15–18% rises taken in FY27 Q1, but Q1 volumes fell 2%; in FY22 a 20%+ rise hit volumes hard. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- ICRA: "intense competition… constrains its pricing power"; unorganised sector is ~65% of the Indian market. [ICRA Nov-25; AR FY26 MD&A]

## Risks and red flags
- **Volume stagnation:** four years of flat revenue and falling pairs; FY27 growth so far is price-led. [Screener; IP Q1FY27]
- **Cost inflation that may not reverse:** Haryana wage increases (30%+) are structural; if raw materials fall, competitors may cut prices while Relaxo's wage base stays higher. [Q4 call]
- **Capacity recalibration** from 10.5 to 9.1 lakh pairs/day — an admission of long-idle capacity; watch for impairments or write-offs of old lines. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Disclosure:** no earnings call for Q3 FY26 or Q1 FY27; investors got only a presentation for the quarter in which 15–18% price rises were tested. [Screener concall list; IP Q1FY27]
- **Family on payroll / related parties:** several promoter-family members are paid executives (Co-CEOs Gaurav and Ritesh Dua, EVPs Nitin and Rahul Dua; reappointments from 1-Oct-2026 at ₹14–20 lakh/month scales); lease rent to promoter parties ~₹7.6 cr; the related-party table also shows a "purchase of property" line totalling ₹17.5 cr in FY26 (counterparty not clearly legible in the printed table — to verify). No material RPT as defined. [AR FY26 note 41; AGM notice FY26]
- Contingent liabilities ₹124.9 cr (mostly GST/sales-tax ₹55.9 cr, income tax ₹58.2 cr, Haryana entry-tax interest ₹18.7 cr) vs net worth ~₹2,206 cr. Auditor's report unqualified; key audit matters: revenue rebates/discounts and litigation provisions. [AR FY26 note 32; auditor's report]
- Unspent CSR obligation ₹72.6 cr (down from ₹160.8 cr) shown under commitments. [AR FY26 note 32]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q2 (Nov 2025): defensive — destocking before/after GST cut, Q1/Q2 revenue −12%/−8%; hope for ~10% growth next year.
- Q4 (May 2026): recovery (+8.1%), best margin in years (16.5%), but new cost shock (war-linked materials, wages) and 15–18% price rises; target reset to 4–5% volume growth and +1 pt margin — a lower, more realistic bar than "10% plus".
- Management line-up changed: Sushil Batra (ex-CFO) now Executive Director; Amit Roy is CFO; Gaurav and Ritesh Dua are Co-CEOs. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Best management quotes
- "Since January '22, when the government implemented this new taxes of 5% to 12%, that became actually one of the main causes, 90% cause is that only." — Ramesh Kumar Dua [Q2 call]
- "The price hike cannot be reversed." — Relaxo management on wage-led cost increases [Q4 call]
- "We want to grow at least 4% to 5% in volume" with profitability. — Gaurav Kumaar Dua [Q4 call]

## My read
The GST cut removed the main excuse for the slump and Q4 FY26 showed the business can still earn 16%+ margins in season, but Q1 FY27 growth was entirely price, with volume down, and the promised EBO roll-out has barely started. The company is cash-rich and cheap to run, yet revenue has not grown in four years and management has lowered its ambition from "10% plus" to 4–5% volume. The stock needs evidence that volumes can grow after the 15–18% price rises and that the Sparx premium range and new EBOs lift realisation; another quarter of falling pairs would suggest share loss to unorganised and D2C players.
