# Somany Ceramics — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹618, market cap ₹2,535 cr (Screener consolidated, 27 Sep 2026).

## Business and segments
- India's No. 2 branded tile maker after Kajaria; also sanitaryware, bath fittings and (since July 2025) construction chemicals. One reported segment (building products); no segment margins disclosed. [AR FY26 consolidated note 48]
- Q1 FY27 consolidated sales ₹744 cr (company figure): own-made tiles ₹239 cr, JV tiles ₹209 cr, outsourced tiles ₹184 cr, bathware ₹76 cr, others ₹35 cr. Bathware is ~10% of sales; management says its margin is "about 1% better" than tiles. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Sourcing model: tile capacity ~80 msm (million square metres) a year including dedicated outsourcing; Q1 production 10.99 msm, of which own plants 6.21 and JVs 4.78. Sales mix by source: own 34%, JVs 34%, others (outsourced) 32%. CRISIL puts own+group capacity at ~57 msm with access to 75 msm; own plants made ~33% of FY26 output. [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Jun-26]
- Plants: own tiles at Kassar/Bahadurgarh, Haryana (25 msm) and Kadi, Gujarat (11.15 msm); JV tile plants at Morbi (12.04 msm) and Tirupati (8.40 msm); sanitaryware 0.48 mn pcs (Morbi JV); faucets 1.30 mn pcs (Derabassi); construction-chemical plants at Bahadurgarh and Kadi. Capacity use Q1 FY27: tiles 80% overall, 83% standalone (72% a year earlier); sanitaryware 77%; faucets 95%. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Customers/geography: 3,060 active dealers, 530 showrooms, ~14,000 touch points; North India 46% of FY26 revenue, South 25%, East 16%; institutional/project sales ~20%. Only 25% of tile dealers also sell Somany sanitaryware — the cross-sell headroom. [CRISIL Jun-26; Q4 call]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / net profit: Jun-25 604 / 48 / 7; Sep-25 685 / 54 / 12; Dec-25 682 / 64 / 17; Mar-26 818 / 92 / 37; Jun-26 750 / 86 / 34. Operating margin 8% → 8% → 9% → 11% → 12%. [Screener]
- Annual: FY24 sales 2,605, OP 253 (10%), PAT 99; FY25 2,659, 222 (8%), 58; FY26 2,790, 258 (9%), 74. TTM sales 2,935, PAT 101. Ten-year sales CAGR only 5%, three-year 4%; ROE 10–11% over 3–10 years; FY26 ROCE 13%. [Screener]
- Company view of Q1 FY27: sales +24% (price up 16–18%, tile volume only +2.8% to 16.46 msm), EBITDA ₹86 cr at 11.6% (8.0% a year earlier), PAT ₹34 cr vs ₹7 cr. Own-plant tile volume +22% (5.46 → 6.67 msm), JV +7%, outsourced –18% (Morbi shutdown in April). [IP Q1FY27]
- Unit economics: FY26 realisation up ₹6/sqm; the war-driven cost rise was ~₹7/sqm (gas ₹5.5–6, other inputs ₹1–1.5), fully passed on in retail and ~85–90% in projects. Gas cost: blended ~₹68/scm in Q1 FY27 (South/Morbi mid-70s, North ₹68–69) vs ₹45–51 in FY26. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Standalone FY26: revenue ₹2,640 cr (+2.8%), EBITDA 7.0%, PAT ₹99 cr; standalone is net cash (₹115 cr at June 2026). [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026): FY27 EBITDA margin +150 bps or more from 9.3% (i.e. ≥10.8%); revenue growth 20–25% "if prices remain at current levels"; tile volume "decent single digit"; sanitaryware and adhesives "healthy double digits"; FY27 capex contained at ₹70–80 cr (routine plus balancing equipment); Somany Max loss "under ₹10 cr or maybe breakeven"; buyback ruled out for 6–8 months because of the pending subsidiary merger. [Q4 call, official]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +24% (met), EBITDA margin 11.6% (beat), tile volume +3% (missed "decent single digit" — blamed on April Morbi outage), JVs swung from ₹10 cr loss to ₹3 cr profit, Somany Max loss ~₹1 cr vs ₹7 cr. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 changes: margin floor raised to "11-plus" with a 12%+ target and Q4 FY27 uplift from debottlenecking; volume guidance trimmed to "mid-single digit" (management: industry promises of high-single-digit volume "never has been delivered"); JV swing ">₹30 cr" for FY27 (FY26 JV loss ₹24–25 cr). [Q1 call]
- Verdict: margin guidance **raised**, volume guidance **cut**, capex guidance **raised sharply** (₹70–80 cr → ₹275 cr to end-FY27). [Q4 call vs Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- New South plant: 9+ msm glazed vitrified tile (GVT) plant near Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, ~₹220 cr, operational end-Q3/start-Q4 FY28; revenue potential ~₹350 cr — about 1.6x asset turn. Land already owned. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Structure is unclear: the board (13 July 2026) approved up to ₹58.8 cr for a **49% stake** in Siravit Ceramics Pvt Ltd for this plant, while the MD on the call called it a "60-40" JV whose loan carries "no corporate guarantee from Somany". At 49% it may be an associate (not consolidated). Same board meeting: ₹15 cr modernisation at Sudha Somany Ceramics and a ₹2 cr 50% JV in Nepal (construction chemicals). [News: Whalesbook/Sahi, Jul 2026; Q1 call]
- Total outlay to end-FY27 ~₹275 cr (₹220 cr plant + balancing equipment in tile, sanitaryware and bathware plants), 60–70% from internal accruals (MD gave both 60% and 65–70% in the same call). [Q1 call]
- Debottlenecking: 4–5 msm (also stated as 3–4 msm) at Bahadurgarh, Gujarat, Morbi and South, ready mid-Q3 to Q4 FY27, value-added mix. Max revenue from current own+JV capacity ~₹3,700 cr at today's prices (TTM ₹2,935 cr ≈ 79%), rising partly with debottlenecking. [Q1 call]
- Past capex: ₹30 cr press at Somany Max to lift use; Max (large slabs, ~85% subsidiary, commercial production Jan 2024) lost ₹27 cr in FY25 and ₹21 cr in FY26. Gross block (consolidated) up 8.2% in FY26 to ₹1,569 cr. [Q4 call; CRISIL Jun-26; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Construction chemicals: bought 51% of Dura Build Care (Bahadurgarh) for ₹10.3 cr on 15 July 2025; it made ₹7.4 cr revenue and a ₹0.4 cr loss in FY26. New large North capacity went live July 2026. [AR FY26 note 55; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- FY26 operating cash flow ₹247 cr, free cash flow ₹197 cr; FY25 ₹141 cr / ₹61 cr. Cash conversion cycle 52 → 44 days; debtor days 51 → 39; inventory days 103 → 97. [Screener]
- Consolidated net debt ₹105 cr at Mar-26 and ₹107 cr at Jun-26 (net debt/equity 0.12); Mar-24 was ₹257 cr. Standalone net cash ₹115–123 cr. Working capital 12 days consolidated at Jun-26 (from 17). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- CRISIL: free cash ₹125 cr at March 2026; bank-limit use averaged just 7%; cash accruals ₹180–210 cr a year vs term-debt repayment ₹35–45 cr; interest cover 5.4x. Rated AA-/Stable, A1+ (reaffirmed 22 Jun 2026). [CRISIL Jun-26]
- Dividend payout ~30% for FY26. Promoters bought shares before the Q4 trading window; no buyback until the amalgamation of three wholly-owned subsidiaries (approved Nov 2025, appointed date 1 Apr 2025) clears. [Screener; Q4 call; AR FY26 note 54]

## Moat evidence
- Brand and reach: AA- rating anchored on brand and distribution; 200 net dealer additions in FY26 to ~3,100 showrooms. [CRISIL Jun-26; Q4 call]
- Pricing: 16–17% tile hike held in Q1 FY27; but Morbi raised 30–35%, so Somany's premium over unorganised players **narrowed** — management agreed "that's right". Pricing is pass-through: "I'm not earning anything from the pricing." [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Cost position is weaker than the leader: Kajaria runs ~18–19% margins against Somany's ~11%; the MD declined to explain the gap on the call. Somany is fully dependent on Morbi for polished vitrified tiles (PVT), which cost it 2–2.5 points of volume in Q1. [Q1 call]
- Asset-light sourcing (two-thirds of volume from JVs/outsourcers) keeps capital needs low but hands margin to partners; JV losses of ₹24–25 cr in FY26 show the downside. [Q1 call; CRISIL Jun-26]
- Industry structure helped: 10–17% of Morbi capacity is not expected to restart after the gas shock, and all Morbi units now buy the same metered gas instead of cheaper propane/LPG "in cash" — a real, if possibly temporary, levelling. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Auditor opinion is clean (Singhi & Co.); key audit matters are receivables and inventory valuation. But the audit trail (edit log) was not enabled at database level for certain SAP tables and another software — a control gap. [AR FY26 auditor's reports]
- Contingent liabilities (consolidated) ~₹38 cr: GST/excise/service tax ₹22.3 cr, income tax ₹8.1 cr, claims ₹5.6 cr, GPCB pollution penalty ₹0.56 cr, plus undetermined labour disputes. [AR FY26 consolidated note 37]
- Related parties: standalone buys much of its product from group JVs/subsidiaries (FY26 purchases about ₹112 cr from Vintage Tiles and about ₹235 cr from Sudha Somany — the PDF table layout is garbled, so treat as approximate) and lends to them: inter-corporate deposits of ₹36.5 cr to loss-making Somany Max at March 2026 (₹29 cr a year earlier), ₹5 cr each to three others. [AR FY26 standalone notes 37, 41] Promoter pay: Abhishek Somany ₹6.6 cr (incl. ₹1.5 cr commission, up from ₹0.04 cr), Shreekant Somany ₹5.2 cr — together ~12% of standalone PAT; three family members in paid roles. [AR FY26 standalone note 41]
- Governance of structure: 12 consolidated entities plus associates; the new South plant's stake (49% per filing vs "60-40" on call) and debt are unclear. [CRISIL Jun-26; News: Whalesbook, Jul 2026; Q1 call]
- Gas: 25–30% of production cost; prices were still edging up in July–August 2026. A long-term contract was to cut South gas by ₹17–18/scm from June — not confirmed on the Q1 call. A gas price fall means price cuts. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Morbi exports down 50–60% from peak; management dismisses domestic dumping risk "at these gas prices". [Q1 call]
- Institutional holding falling: DIIs 23.6% (Mar-25) → 19.4% (Jun-26). [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "somebody's loss is somebody's gain" (Morbi disruption) to internal efficiency (own plants at 100%, JVs profitable). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Capex jumped from ₹70–80 cr "routine" to a ₹275 cr programme including the South greenfield plant. Note: the Q4 closing line about "no new plants for 18–24 months" referred to the **industry**, not Somany ("unless otherwise, it's already on route"), so this is a plan change, not a broken promise. [Q4 call official; Q1 call]
- Volume ambition cut from "decent single digit" to "mid-single digit"; margin ambition raised to 11%+/12%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## My read
The business is improving for identifiable reasons — own plants full, JV losses gone, debt down to ~₹105 cr — and two quarters above 11% margin is the best run in years. But volume growth is stuck at low-to-mid single digits, the premium over Morbi has narrowed, and margins still sit far below Kajaria's, so the gains rest on utilisation and on a gas shock that levelled the field. At about 23 times trailing earnings the price needs the 11–12% margin to hold after Morbi normalises and gas eases, plus the ₹275 cr capex to earn its keep; ROE has never sustainably beaten 11%. The view improves if H2 FY27 shows mid-single-digit volume with margins ≥11% and the Tirupati plant is funded without balance-sheet strain; it worsens if gas falls and price cuts squeeze margins back toward 9%.
