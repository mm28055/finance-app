# Wakefit Innovations (NSE: WAKEFIT) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Figures are standalone from Screener (no subsidiaries; the Screener "consolidated" page is empty). Three EBITDA measures matter: Screener operating profit ≈ company "reported EBITDA" (excludes other income but adds back store rent under Ind AS 116); company "operating EBITDA" deducts rent, ESOP and non-operating items and is the real cash margin. Listed 15 Dec 2025 at ₹195 (IPO: ₹377 cr fresh issue + ₹912 cr offer for sale).

## Business and segments
- D2C home brand: mattresses (65.9% of Q1 FY27 revenue; 61.4% of FY26), furniture (28.0%; 29.3%), furnishings & décor (6.3%; 9.3%). One reported segment ("Home Furnishing") [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26 note 42].
- Channels Q1 FY27: own channels (website + company-owned stores) 72.3% (+20.5% YoY), external (marketplaces + multi-brand outlets) 27.7% (+7.6%); online 52.7% / offline 47.3% [Q1 call].
- Network 30 Jun 2026: 165 company-owned stores (COCO) in 100 cities (139 at Mar-26; 27 added in Q1 vs 42 in all of FY26); ~2,250 multi-brand outlets in 701 cities on cash-and-carry terms; ~105 larger "mega" stores with furniture, ~60 "mini" bedding-first stores [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Manufacturing in-house (foam/mattresses; Hosur furniture plant); polyol and TDI are ~60–65% of mattress raw-material cost [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- Market position: largest D2C home & furnishings player and top-3 organised mattress brand (Redseer, company-cited); management estimates ~10% of the organised mattress market and aims to add 4–5 points in 3–5 years; <1% of the combined mattress, furniture and home market [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call].

- Operating EBITDA (after rent): FY26 ₹112.3 cr (7.5%); Q1 FY27 ₹36.8 cr (9.1%) vs ₹24.6 cr (7.1%). Rent add-back ₹21 cr a quarter (₹80.4 cr in FY26) [IP Q1FY27 EBITDA walk].
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹404.9 cr (+16.6%), gross margin 57.1% (55.8%), reported EBITDA ₹56.4 cr (13.9%), PBT ₹36.3 cr (+85%), PAT ₹23.4 cr after a ₹7.3 cr deferred-tax charge; PAT excluding deferred tax ₹30.7 cr [IP Q1FY27].

- Unit data: mattress growth two-thirds volume, one-third price; ~2 products per order; 36.7% of revenue from repeat customers; 100-day-trial returns below 10%; A&P 7.6% of revenue [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- ROCE 11% (FY26, first positive year); 3-year revenue CAGR ~22% (FY23 ₹813 cr → FY26 ₹1,489 cr) [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (22 May 2026): aspire to "at least about 20%" growth; if pushed, EBITDA "might be impacted by 1 percentage point, 2 percentage points"; A&P 7–8% of sales; ">80 stores net addition" in FY27, all regular format; first "Jumbo" (100,000 sq ft+) store in "early FY '28… April, May of next year", second later in FY28, breakeven ~18–24 months; FY27 capex ₹120–140 cr; ESOP cost ~₹12 cr; price increases of 7–8% in March and 7–8% in April (~15%) [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 call (7 Aug 2026): growth 16.6% (mattress +27.3%); "on track" for ~80 COCO stores; capex **cut** to ₹100–120 cr (80% retail/Jumbo, 20% automation); first Jumbo store now "June-July" 2027, second "August-September" 2027 (both Bangalore) — about two months later; operating EBITDA aspiration ~7.5% for FY27 (Q1 9.1%); gross margin in Q2 ~100 bp below Q1, H1 impact 100–120 bp; furniture back to "mid-teens to late-teens" growth, 25–30% only after Jumbo stores; ESOP ₹10–12 cr; rent ₹80–90 cr [Q1 call, official].
- Scorecard: growth slightly below the 20% aspiration but mattress strong; margins better than feared; store rollout on pace; Jumbo **slipped**; capex **lowered**.

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex ₹100–120 cr, mostly the Bangalore Jumbo stores (excavation done, civil work under way for #1; site for #2 under negotiation/approvals) and ~80 regular stores [Q1 call]. Capital commitments only ₹4.7 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 40].
- Regular stores are asset-light: ~₹4–5 lakh of display inventory each, fulfilment central; payback ~10–11 months (mini stores 2–3 months longer than mega stores); a town's total sales rise ~2.7–3x after a store opens [Q1 call].
- IPO fresh-issue use (₹377 cr): ₹161.5 cr for rent on existing COCO stores, ₹30.8 cr capex for 117 new stores, ₹15.4 cr machinery, ₹108.4 cr marketing, ₹33.1 cr general purposes; only a small part used by Mar-26 (~₹4.5 cr per the Q4 call); CARE is monitoring agency, no deviation reported [AR FY26 Directors' report; Q4 call].
- No manufacturing capacity expansion needed; automation only [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- "Investable cash" ~₹958 cr at Mar-26 (pre-IPO ~₹450 cr + ~₹400 cr net IPO proceeds + accruals) — about 20% of market value; no bank debt; Screener "borrowings" ₹272 cr are lease liabilities [Q4 call; Screener].
- CFO ₹81 cr (FY24), ₹76 cr (FY25), ₹245 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹52 cr → ₹25 cr → ₹215 cr [Screener]. Under Ind AS 116 store rent (~₹80 cr) sits in financing cash flow, so rent-adjusted FCF is lower (~₹135 cr, my estimate).
- Working capital light: debtor days ~1, inventory ~109, payables ~108, cash-conversion cycle ~2 days (FY26) [Screener].
- No dividend [Screener]. No bank-loan credit rating disclosed.

## Moat evidence
- Scale in D2C: largest online mattress seller; top-rated on the two biggest marketplaces (Redseer via IP) [IP Q1FY27].
- Supply advantage in the 2026 chemical shock: spot polyol/TDI rose 70–160% but Wakefit's procurement cost rose ~30–40%; unorganised rivals faced shortages [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Pricing: took ~15% (Q4 call) across March–April; volume still ~two-thirds of Q1 growth [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Note the Q1 call described the hikes as "two increases of about 5% each" — inconsistent with the Q4 call.
- Gross margin 57% and omnichannel flywheel (repeat 36.7%, multi-category baskets) [IP Q1FY27].
- Limits: management concedes low entry barriers; ₹100–300 cr D2C rivals have raised ad spend and matched prices in the last 6–9 months; one analyst's data showed industry profit pools shrinking over ten years [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **CFO churn**: Anil Arya (Jul 2023–Feb 2025), Navesh Gupta (13 May 2025–31 Dec 2025, left two weeks after listing), Parul Gupta from 10 Feb 2026 [AR FY26 note 39].
- **Auditor (B S R & Co. LLP)**: unmodified opinion; key audit matter revenue recognition; audit-trail exceptions — the revenue software had no edit log enabled, and for the third-party general-ledger software the auditor cannot confirm database-level logging [AR FY26 Auditor's report p.104–106].
- **Earnings quality**: TTM profit includes a ₹98 cr one-time deferred-tax credit; 43% of Q1 PBT was treasury income; reported EBITDA adds back ~₹80 cr of rent. Underlying operating EBITDA is 7.5–9% [IP Q1FY27].
- Disclosure slips: the official Q4 transcript and the Q1 presentation footnote call the ₹98.1 cr a deferred-tax "charge", though it was income [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]; price-hike sizes differ between calls.
- IPO money earmarked largely for store rent (₹161.5 cr) and marketing (₹108.4 cr) — i.e. to fund operating costs [AR FY26].
- Shareholders: stock ₹141 vs ₹195 IPO price (−28%); FIIs cut from 19.5% (Dec-25) to 14.5% (Jun-26); pre-IPO investors (e.g. Peak XV ~13.7% post-IPO) are potential sellers [Screener; News: Outlook Business/upstox, Dec 2025].
- Contingent liabilities small: GST disputes ₹6.4 cr (input-credit claims) [AR FY26 note 40].
- Competition and raw materials (crude-linked foam chemicals) [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- From bracing for a 15% price shock and demand hit (May) to reporting strong mattress volumes and better margins (August).
- Store expansion restarted (27 in Q1 vs ~2 net in Q4 FY26).
- Capex trimmed; Jumbo timetable pushed ~2 months; margin aspiration anchored to ~7.5% operating EBITDA.
- External marketplace channel returned to growth (+7.6%) after two quarters of 15–20% declines.

## My read
Wakefit is a genuinely improving business — 17% growth, first profitable year, gross margin 57% and a fast, asset-light store rollout — but its true profitability is thin: operating EBITDA after rent was ₹112 cr (7.5%) in FY26 and much of pre-tax profit now comes from interest on IPO cash. At ~₹4,690 cr market value (≈₹3,700 cr net of ~₹958 cr cash), the headline P/E of ~24 is flattered by the ₹98 cr tax credit; on underlying profit (~₹30 cr a quarter excluding deferred tax) it is closer to 40x, so the price needs ~20% growth with margin expansion for many years. I would turn more positive if stores keep paying back within a year and operating EBITDA holds above 8% through H2; negative if the Jumbo format slips again, growth falls to the mid-teens, or pre-IPO investors keep selling.
