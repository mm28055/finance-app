# Jyothy Labs (JYOTHYLAB) — deep research, Phase A1

Numbers note: Jyothy sold its only subsidiary (Jyothy Kallol Bangladesh) in March 2025, so Screener's consolidated page stops at Mar-25. All figures below are from the **standalone** Screener page, which is now the whole company.

Transcript check: the stored file holds the Q1 FY27 earnings call (12 Aug 2026) and a special "Pril and Fa update" call (18 Jun 2026). The actual previous earnings call (Q4 FY26, 4 May 2026) is **not stored**; I read the official copy from BSE.
- **Q1 FY27 stored copy: materially wrong.** (1) Employee cost "7.4% of revenue" — official says **11.4%** (vs 11.5%). (2) In the gross-margin answer it says "we took approximately a 6% price increase" — official says "**some 3% price increase**" (plus 3–4% efficiencies and mix). (3) The July launch is called "Margo Neem Sticks" — official and presentation say **Maxo incense sticks** (a mosquito-repellent agarbatti, not a Margo product); the stored profile repeats this error. (4) It inserts "{? 5% ?}" reported growth incl. Pril — the official question trails off with no figure. Also note: the official transcript itself says A&P was "10.8%" a year ago; the presentation says **7.8%** (₹58.9 cr), so trust the presentation.
- **18 Jun 2026 Pril/Fa update call: matches official** — the stored text is the company's own filed transcript (cover letter dated 22 Jun 2026).

## Business and segments
- Home and personal care FMCG: fabric care (Ujala fabric whitener, Henko, Mr. White, Morelight, Ujala detergents, liquids), home care (Exo dishwash, Maxo mosquito repellents, incense/dhoop, cleaners) and personal care (Margo neem soap, Neem toothpaste). Sold through 4 mn outlets, 1.4 mn direct; 23 plants; ~10,000 channel partners. [IP Q1FY27]
- Leadership: Ujala #1 in fabric whitener for four decades; Exo #2 in dishwash by value; Maxo coils #2 by volume. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A]
- FY26 revenue ₹2,944 cr (old five-segment view): fabric care ₹1,346 cr (segment result ₹268 cr, ~20%), dishwash ₹959 cr (₹151 cr, ~16%), personal care ₹321 cr (₹25 cr), household insecticides ₹196 cr (–₹5 cr), others ₹122 cr (~nil). [IP Q1FY27 segment table]
- New three-segment view from Q1 FY27 (revenue / segment result, ₹ cr, as read from the presentation table): Fabric care 399 / 38 (Q1 FY26: 349 / 67); Home care 280 / 8 (308 / 46); Personal care 94 / 13 (94 / 11). Home-care profit nearly vanished and fabric-care margin halved to ~9.5%. Mix now fabric 52%, home 36%, personal 12%. [IP Q1FY27]
- **Pril (dishwash liquid) and Fa (deodorant/soap) exited on 31 May 2026** after Henkel declined to renew fixed-term licences. Pril was "about 7%, 8%" of revenue (analyst figure ₹225–240 cr, confirmed "yes" by the CFO). Henko and Mr. White are held on perpetual, royalty-free licences; Margo, Neem, Tuhina and Chek are owned outright. [Jun-26 call]
- Channels: modern trade, e-commerce and quick commerce grew 26% in FY26 and 25–30% in Q1 FY27; general trade was weak in urban markets. [AR FY26; Q1 call]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener standalone, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 751 / op. profit 124 (17%) / PAT 97; Sep-25 736 / 118 (16%) / 88; Dec-25 740 / 111 (15%) / 81; Mar-26 717 / 97 (13%) / 68; **Jun-26 773 / 65 (8%) / 48**. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +3.0% reported; excluding Pril and Fa +8.1% in value and +5.3% in volume. Gross margin 38.5% vs 48.0%; A&P cut to 6.5% of sales (₹50.6 cr) from 7.8%; EBITDA ₹64.7 cr (8.4%) vs ₹124.2 cr (16.5%); PAT ₹47.6 cr (–51%). [IP Q1FY27]
- Management's bridge: raw-material impact ~15–16% of sales, offset by ~3% price and 3–4% efficiencies/mix, net ~9–9.5 points of margin lost. ~90% of the business (fabric + home care) is crude-linked; LABSA was up 60–65% from January and HDPE/PP packaging 50–55% by early May. [Q1 call official; Q4 call]
- Q4 FY26: revenue ₹717 cr (+7.7%, volume +10.8%); gross margin 45.2% (–400 bp); EBITDA 13.5%. [IP Q4FY26]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹2,754 cr → FY25 ₹2,844 cr → FY26 ₹2,944 cr; OPM 17% → 18% → 15%; PAT ₹370 cr → ₹371 cr → ₹333 cr. TTM sales ₹2,966 cr, PAT ₹284 cr (–23%). ROCE 44% → 37% → 29%. [Screener]
- FY26 detail: volume +6%, value +3.5%; fabric care +8% value/+9.5% volume; dishwash –1.3% value despite +6% volume (price cuts, extra grammage); HI losses cut from ~₹25 cr to ~₹5 cr. [Q4 call]
- Unit economics: value growth has lagged volume for six quarters because competitors cut MRPs and gave extra grammage; in liquids, price per wash is now below powder. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (4 May 2026): refused margin guidance for FY27 ("maybe … in a couple of quarters"); expected Q1 margin pressure; ~4% price increase taken in March; HI profitability target (end-FY27) possibly earlier; capex FY27 "similar" to FY26; tax rate 25–26%; "growth to be prioritised over margins". Said it would invest behind **both** Exo and Pril. [Q4 call; IP Q4FY26]
- Q1 delivery: margin fell to 8.4% (worse than the 13.5% Q4 exit), price increase realised only ~3% of the 4–4.5% taken; HI hurt by weather; no acquisition. **Missed** on margin; volume held (+5.3% ex-Pril). [Q1 call]
- Q1 call (12 Aug 2026): FY27 revenue growth **double-digit excluding Pril and Fa**; high-single-digit volume growth all year; margins "under pressure" through FY27 with H2 "substantially better" than H1; Q2 margin "not going to be materially lower" than Q1; raw-material relief "more meaningfully from October". Goal to return to "historical margin levels" (no date). [Q1 call]
- So guidance moved from "no guidance" to a **soft cut**: margin recovery pushed to H2 and beyond, and reported growth will be well below double digits because Pril (~7–8% of sales) is gone. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Capex ₹67 cr in FY26 (capacity expansion and upgradation, automation); FY27 guided "similar". Capital commitments only ₹3.4 cr at Mar-26 (₹12.3 cr at Mar-25). CWIP ₹10 cr. Net block ₹504 cr on ₹2,944 cr sales ≈ 5.8x asset turn. [AR FY26; AR FY26 note 33; Q4 call; Screener]
- Capacity is not a constraint: plants are multi-product and Pril's liquid capacity can be redeployed to Exo liquid; "no material stranded manufacturing exposure". [Jun-26 call]
- Growth spending is mostly brand and distribution, not plant: 50,000–100,000 new direct outlets a year; new launches (Exo bio-enzyme liquid, Maxo incense sticks, Ujala Young & Fresh, Dr. Wool, Crisp & Shine Intense). [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- The big capital decision is M&A: ~₹850 cr cash, "active dialogues with a couple of" targets in May; by August still none. Looked at TTK's consumer business (4 divisions; 2 not aligned, 2 overlapping) and passed; TTK Healthcare instead sold Good Home and Eva (FY26 revenue ₹148 cr) to Wipro Consumer Care for ₹256 cr (~1.7x sales) — a useful benchmark for what ₹850 cr can buy. Management hints at personal-care/beauty and less crude-dependent products. [Q4 call; Q1 call; News: Daily Pioneer, Jul 2026]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt-free; cash ~₹1,000 cr at Mar-26, ~₹850 cr in Aug 2026. The fall is roughly the size of the FY26 dividend (₹3.5/share ≈ ₹128 cr) — the company did not explain it. Screener "borrowings" ₹52 cr are lease liabilities (₹51.6 cr). [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 note 32; Screener]
- Operating cash flow ₹458 cr (FY24) → ₹312 cr (FY25) → ₹412 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹444 cr → ₹254 cr → ₹346 cr; CFO/operating profit 105%. [Screener]
- Working capital: net working capital 15 days (–4 days); debtor days 32, inventory 80, payables 74 (FY26). Much of general trade is sold on **advance payment**; distributor stock kept at 15–20 days. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]
- Dividend payout 39% (FY26). CARE AA/Stable on ₹250 cr bank facilities, reaffirmed 24 Sep 2026. [Screener; CARE rating disclosure Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- Real but narrow: Ujala whitener (category leader 40+ years), Exo bar (#2 in dishwash), Maxo coils (#2). Company says it held or gained share across categories in FY26, including liquids. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- **Pricing power is weak in this cycle**: Jyothy realised ~3% price in Q1 versus ~5% at HUL and GCPL (analyst figures); management says it matched competitors SKU-for-SKU because rivals cut MRPs and added grammage. [Q1 call]
- Distribution: direct reach up ~1 lakh outlets to 1.4 mn in FY26; advance-payment terms in GT show channel respect. [IP Q4FY26; Q1 call]
- Weak points: premium tier thin after Pril (Henko is the only clear premium brand); home care depends on crude-linked LAB and plastics; big rivals (HUL) can outspend. A larger player is reportedly entering dishwash in the South. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Henkel dispute**: Jyothy has started arbitration at the Singapore International Arbitration Centre over exit terms (brand valuation/consideration, transfer of the business). No claim amount disclosed; timing unknown. Any award is upside; legal costs and distraction are the downside. [Jun-26 call]
- **Disclosure concern**: on 4 May management said it would invest behind both Exo and Pril, yet by its own account talks with Henkel had run "for several months" and the licence ended 31 May; the non-renewal was only explained on 18 June. [Q4 call; Jun-26 call]
- **Goodwill**: ₹100.4 cr of goodwill from the 2011 Henkel India acquisition is tested as one unit; no impairment at Mar-26, but the loss of Pril weakens that unit. [AR FY26 note 3D]
- Commodity exposure: ~90% of sales crude-linked; last crude cycle took margins from 16–17% to 10–11% (analyst's reminder, not disputed). [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Tax disputes: CARO lists roughly ₹71 cr of demands under appeal (excise ~₹43 cr, sales tax/VAT ~₹27 cr); contingent liabilities shown are only ~₹10 cr, so the rest is either provided for or judged remote. Debtors written off ₹24.5 cr in FY26 (₹26.4 cr FY25) — worth watching given low receivables. [AR FY26 CARO; AR FY26 note 33; AR FY26 other expenses note]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; only key audit matter is revenue recognition. No emphasis of matter found. Two properties (₹6.9 cr) still await title transfer from an old acquisition. [AR FY26 auditor's report; CARO]
- Related parties: small — KMP pay, commission (₹5.1 cr payable at Mar-26 vs ₹0.6 cr), dividends and rent to promoter-linked entities; no loans or guarantees. Promoter holding flat at 62.9%; no pledge seen. [AR FY26 note 31; Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- May: margin pressure "near term", Pril still in the plan, cash ₹1,000 cr, M&A "active dialogues". August: margin collapsed to 8.4%, Pril gone and in arbitration, cash ₹850 cr, M&A still pending. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New defensiveness: management rejected the "commodity business" label, blamed rivals for commoditising categories, and promised higher-margin and non-crude products. [Q1 call]
- New segment reporting (fabric / home / personal) from Q1 FY27, which hides the dishwash and HI splits previously shown. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Consistent: volume growth priority, advance-payment trade terms, strong modern-channel growth.

## My read
The business is not improving right now: reported growth is ~3%, margins have halved, and the loss of Pril removes ~7–8% of sales and the only premium dishwash liquid. The core franchises (Ujala, Exo bar, Henko liquids) are still gaining volume, the balance sheet is clean with ~₹850 cr cash, and a crude reversal would lift margins quickly, as in past cycles. The price needs margin recovery to ~14–15% by FY28 plus Pril revenue replaced by Exo liquid and new launches; without that, earnings power is well below FY25's ₹371 cr. The view improves with a sensible acquisition in personal care, a favourable arbitration award, or two quarters of rising gross margin; it worsens if crude stays above ~$90 or HUL's dishwash push forces more price cuts.
