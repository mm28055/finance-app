# Campus Activewear (NSE: CAMPUS) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Figures are standalone from Screener (Campus's Screener "consolidated" page stops at FY22, so its headline P/E 47.2 and ROCE 31.8% there are stale). Company "EBITDA" includes other income; Screener "operating profit" excludes it. Both are labelled.

## Business and segments
- India's largest sports-and-athleisure (S&A) footwear brand by volume and value; sells running/walking/casual shoes, sneakers, school shoes, slippers, sandals and floaters, mostly at ₹500–1,500 realisations; FY26 average selling price (ASP) ₹683 a pair on 26.0 mn pairs [AR FY26 MD&A p.76].
- Single reported segment (footwear). New lines: athleisure apparel (pilot since Jan 2026) and "Elan by Campus" neo-casual shoes at ₹1,899–2,599, launched Jun 2026 and sold in ~100–110 own stores plus Amazon/Myntra/brand.com [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Channel mix Q1 FY27: trade distribution 53.9%, D2C online 32.9%, D2C offline (EBOs, franchise, large-format) 13.2%; D2C total 46.1% vs 44.4% a year ago. FY26: trade 52.1%, online 36.8%, offline 11.0% [IP Q1FY27 slide 13].
- Price bands Q1 FY27: above ₹1,500 = 53.8% of revenue (52.7% in Q1 FY26); ₹1,050–1,499 = 24.6% (28.0%); below ₹1,050 = 21.6% (19.3%) [IP Q1FY27 slide 30].
- Men : women/kids revenue mix 78:22 in Q4 FY26 (81:19 a year earlier); women's share of FY26 revenue ~15.8% [Q4 call; AR FY26 p.78].
- Reach: 31,000+ retailers, 850+ districts, 260+ distributors, 305+ EBOs, 2,350+ large-format counters [IP Q1FY27].
- Capacity: annual assembly capacity 31.1 mn pairs at 30 Jun 2026 (30.7 mn in FY26); final assembly 100% in-house; uppers 37.2% in-house, soles 49.6% in-house; ~90% domestic raw material [IP Q1FY27 slide 12; AR FY26 p.78]. FY26 volume of 26.0 mn pairs implies ~84% utilisation of assembly capacity. Capacity fell from ~36 mn pairs two years ago because 4–5 mn pairs of old "DIP" moulding capacity was shut [Q1 call].

## Key numbers (Screener, standalone, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly revenue / operating profit (OPM) / net profit: Jun-25 343 / 49 (14%) / 22; Sep-25 387 / 50 (13%) / 20; Dec-25 589 / 110 (19%) / 64; Mar-26 456 / 82 (18%) / 44; Jun-26 385 / 55 (14%) / 26 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 as reported by the company: revenue ₹385.2 cr (+12.2%), volume 5.71 mn pairs (+11.7%), ASP ₹674 (+0.4%), EBITDA ₹62.7 cr (15.9% of total income, flat YoY), PAT ₹26.1 cr (+17.7%) [IP Q1FY27 slide 18].
- Annual: FY24 revenue 1,448 / OP 211 (15%) / PAT 89; FY25 1,593 / 244 (15%) / 121; FY26 1,774 / 292 (16%) / 150; TTM 1,816 / 297 / 154 [Screener]. Company EBITDA FY26 ₹314.7 cr (17.5%) vs ₹258.2 cr (16.1%) [AR FY26 p.76].
- Growth is slower than the story: 3-year sales CAGR only 6% (FY23 ₹1,484 cr → FY26 ₹1,774 cr); 3-year profit CAGR 9% [Screener]. FY24 revenue fell 2%.
- Unit economics FY26: volume +4.2% (24.9 → 26.0 mn pairs), ASP +6.9% (₹639 → ₹683) [AR FY26 p.76]. Q1 FY27 ASP was held down by two items: marketplace fee netting (−2.5%) and school-shoe mix (−2%); "underlying" core ASP up ~5% after an ~8% MRP increase on 1 Apr 2026 [Q1 call].
- Returns: ROCE 17% (FY24), 20% (FY25), 21% (FY26); ROE 18% FY26 [Screener]. Company-defined ROCE 22.4% FY26, 22.8% TTM [IP Q1FY27].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (25 May 2026): no formal guidance but "endeavour to stay within" the 17–19% EBITDA margin band for FY27; 60–70 "or about 80" new EBOs (40% company-owned, 60% franchise); FY27 capex below FY26; full BIS compliance by 31 Jul 2026; raw-material costs "have seen the peak" [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 call (6 Aug 2026): 17–19% margin reaffirmed ("100% confident"); FY27 revenue growth "mid-double-digit" = ASP +6–7% from Q2 plus high-single-digit volume; sneaker volume +~30% (after ~100%+ in FY26); EBO openings raised to "80 to 120… about 90 to 100" [Q1 call].
- Delivery vs promise: Q1 margin 15.9% (company) / 14% (Screener) — seasonally the weak quarter and flat YoY, so the 17–19% band needs a strong H2 (FY26 H2 ran at ~19%). Volume beat (+11.7%) but ~40% of volume growth came from school shoes, which management says will normalise [Q1 call]. 18 EBOs opened in Q1, the most in 6–8 quarters. Guidance: **kept** on margin, **raised** on stores.
- FY26 delivered what FY26 promised: revenue +11.4%, EBITDA margin +145 bp to 17.5% [AR FY26 p.76].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 cash capex ₹90.3 cr (FY25 ₹59.2 cr) [AR FY26 cash-flow statement], plus the Pantnagar (Uttarakhand) site taken on a 71-year state lease, whose ~₹65.4 cr payment sits in "principal payment of lease liabilities" (₹99.1 cr in FY26) rather than capex [Q4 call; AR FY26 p.179]. True FY26 investment ≈ ₹155 cr.
- Pantnagar started commercial production of premium uppers in Jan 2026; Paonta Sahib (HP) upper unit stabilised. Combined output ~2 lakh uppers/month, "likely to double by end of FY27" [Q4 call; AR FY26 p.78].
- Sneaker plan: Pantnagar up to 6 lakh pairs/month after two phases, Haridwar II ~2 lakh — target 8–9 lakh pairs/month of sneakers, phased over ~3 years [Q4 call].
- FY27: capital commitments only ₹5.8 cr at Mar-26 (₹4.5 cr a year earlier); CWIP ₹11 cr; capex limited to maintenance, moulds, 30–35 company-owned stores and IT [AR FY26 note 39; Screener; Q4 call]. Q1 carried ~₹2.5 cr extra depreciation and ~₹2.5 cr indirect cost from the new plants [Q1 call].
- Payback: none disclosed. Asset turns: FY26 revenue ₹1,774 cr on ₹516 cr fixed assets (incl. leases) ≈ 3.4x [Screener].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Bank debt only ₹36.3 cr (working-capital loans from HDFC and CTBC, 6.65–8.49%) at Mar-26, nil a year earlier; lease liabilities ₹199.9 cr; cash ₹4.0 cr plus bank deposits ₹127.8 cr → net cash ≈ ₹95 cr excluding leases [AR FY26 notes 14, 15, 21]. Screener "borrowings" of ₹236 cr include leases.
- Cash conversion weakened: CFO ₹264 cr (FY24), ₹235 cr (FY25), ₹135 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹220 cr → ₹176 cr → ₹46 cr; CFO/operating profit 65% in FY26 [Screener].
- Inventory days 218 (FY24), 192 (FY25), 207 (FY26); cash-conversion cycle 143 → 118 → 140 days; working-capital days 96 → 89 → 109 [Screener]. Net inventory rose to ₹499.8 cr at Jun-26 from ₹414.6 cr at Mar-26, a deliberate pre-festive build ("highest ever Q1 production", July a record month) [IP Q1FY27 slide 23; Q1 call].
- The shift of 158 franchise stores to sale-or-return (SOR) puts store inventory on Campus's books until sold — structurally more working capital [Q1 call].
- Dividend: payout 25% (FY25), 31% (FY26); FY26 final dividend 30% of ₹5 face value [Screener; AR FY26 Directors' report].
- CRISIL A+ (outlook revised to Positive) long term and CRISIL A1 short term, Oct 2025, on ₹303 cr facilities; bank-limit utilisation ~4%; gross current assets 165 days; expected cash accrual ₹220–260 cr a year [CRISIL Oct-25].

## Moat evidence
- Brand leadership: ~17% share of the scaled S&A footwear market, No.1 S&A brand (FY25) [Screener key points, company-cited]. Management estimates the industry grew 7–8% in FY26 vs Campus 12% [Q4 call].
- Pricing power: ~8% across-the-board MRP increase on 1 Apr 2026 with volume still up 11.7%; distributor meet in May (after the hike) produced "record orders"; smaller and unorganised rivals faced working-capital and gas-supply constraints [Q1 call].
- Integrated, domestic supply chain: 60–90-day manufacturing lead time vs industry 90–120 days (Technopak); management says replicating it would cost ~₹2,000 cr [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]. Largely BIS-compliant, which hurt importers.
- Brand health FY26: spontaneous awareness 96% → 98%, consideration 63% → 66% [AR FY26 p.78].
- Online strength: D2C online ~37% of FY26 revenue; brand.com grew >100% in Q1; company-owned stores ~20% same-store growth [Q1 call].
- Limits: gross margin ~53–56% but EBITDA only 16–18%, and CRISIL notes limited ability to pass sharp raw-material rises to price-sensitive buyers [CRISIL Oct-25].

## Risks and red flags
- **CFO exit**: Sanjay Chhabra resigned (announced 12 Jun 2026, effective 7 Jul 2026, "personal reasons"); Q1 call was fronted by a finance controller; Rakesh Thakur appointed CFO from 17 Aug 2026 [News: Business Standard, 15 Jun 2026; News: Tijori Alerts, Aug 2026].
- **Auditor remark (BSR & Co)**: clean opinion, but a modification on audit trail — the new principal accounting software (from 4 Apr 2025) had no SOC report, so the auditor cannot say whether the database-level edit log was enabled, cannot comment on tampering from 1 Apr to 31 Dec 2025, and says the audit trail "has not been preserved" as required [AR FY26 Auditor's report p.169–170]. Key audit matter: revenue recognition (₹1,758.8 cr sale of goods).
- Contingent liabilities small: GST ₹3.14 cr; the ₹52.57 cr income-tax demand shown at Mar-25 is no longer listed at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 39]. Disputed GST dues of ~₹3 cr across 2017–25 at first appellate stage [CARO annexure].
- Related parties modest: rent ~₹1 cr a year to Kabeer Textiles (KMP-influenced), relatives' salaries; KMP short-term pay ₹13.97 cr (₹12.58 cr) [AR FY26 note 42]. ~100 exclusive job-workers are all third parties [Q4 call].
- Working capital and SOR model could tie up cash if festive sell-through disappoints; FY26 FCF only ₹46 cr [Screener].
- Mixed messages: the annual report says Campus will "consolidate the unit economics of its retail network before resuming EBO expansion" and that industry demand "has so far been slower to broaden", while the Q1 call raised store targets to 90–100 [AR FY26 MD&A p.81; Q1 call].
- Input costs: EVA and PU are crude-linked; statutory minimum wages added ~₹5 cr a quarter to other expenses; open-footwear output was hit by gas (LPG/CNG) rationing [Q1 call].
- Competition from global brands and D2C start-ups in the ₹1,500–3,000 band; counterfeits [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Promoter holding steady at 72.1%; DIIs trimmed from 11.8% to 11.4% over the year [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defending margins (raw-material spike, "war" disruption, BIS) in May to planning for festive demand in August.
- New topics: Walmart-owned platforms (Flipkart/Myntra) netting fees from revenue (−2.5% to growth), franchise switch to SOR (−2 to −2.5%), school shoes +~50% YoY, Elan launch, gas-supply constraint on sandals, floods in Gujarat/Maharashtra "tapering" demand.
- Store guidance up (60–80 → 90–100); margin band kept; new explicit FY27 revenue aim of mid-teens growth.
- CFO absent (resigned); finance controller Neeraj Gupta answered cost questions.

## My read
The business is improving slowly rather than sharply: FY26 profit grew 24% on 11% revenue growth, but the 3-year revenue CAGR is only 6% and free cash flow collapsed to ₹46 cr as inventory and the Pantnagar lease absorbed cash. The 8% price increase appears to have stuck, and if raw materials ease as management expects, H2 FY27 margins could beat FY26, which is the core of the bull case. At ₹6,419 cr market value on ₹154 cr TTM profit, the price needs roughly mid-teens earnings growth for years; mid-teens revenue growth plus a 17–19% margin could deliver that, but the record could not until FY26. I would turn more positive if Q2–Q3 show ASP growth of 6%+ with inventory falling back towards ₹415 cr; I would turn negative if festive sell-through is weak and SOR inventory piles up, or if the new CFO restates anything.
