# EPACK Durable — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹180, market cap ₹1,736 cr, trailing earnings negative (Screener consolidated, 27 Sep 2026). Stock is near its 52-week low (₹175) and down ~50% in a year.

## Business and segments
- Contract maker (ODM/OEM — designs and builds products that other brands sell) of room air conditioners (RACs), small and large domestic appliances (SDA/LDA: induction cooktops, air fryers, mixers, washing machines, coolers) and components (heat exchangers, copper tubing, PCBAs, cross-flow fans, moulding). One reported segment. [AR FY26 note 50; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹886 cr: RAC ₹622 cr (+43.8%), SDA & LDA ₹131 cr (+68.9%), components ₹85 cr (–23.1%), others ₹47 cr (+15.1%). Non-RAC was 45% of FY26 revenue (20% in FY23), ~30% in the RAC-heavy Q1. [IP Q1FY27]
- Customers: 72+ customers, 19 product categories (Voltas, Havells, Blue Star, Godrej, Whirlpool, Haier and others). Top-2 customer share fell from 72% (FY23) to 38% (Q1 FY27). The single largest customer bought ₹566 cr in FY26 (₹853 cr in FY25) — still ~30% of standalone revenue. [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Jan-26; AR FY26 note 50]
- Plants: Dehradun, Bhiwadi, Sri City (own), Sri City EMTPL (wholly-owned subsidiary dedicated to Hisense) and Bhiwadi EPAVO (50% JV for BLDC motors). Installed capacity: split AC indoor units 1.60 mn, outdoor 2.05 mn, window ACs 0.62 mn, washing machines 0.65 mn, induction stoves 2.0 mn. [IP Q1FY27]
- Utilisation: FY26 Dehradun/Bhiwadi ~55%, Sri City <25%; Q1 FY27 Dehradun/Bhiwadi >85%, Sri City ~50%; FY27 target "more than 60%" across the three. [Q1 call, official]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / net profit: Jun-25 662 / 54 / 23; Sep-25 213 / –1 / –22; Dec-25 428 / 29 / 3; Mar-26 591 / 24 / 0; Jun-26 886 / 53 / 12. Q2 is structurally loss-making (RAC off-season). [Screener]
- Annual: FY24 sales 1,420, OP 115 (8%), PAT 35; FY25 2,171, 155 (7%), 55; FY26 1,894 (–12.7%), 107 (6%), 3. TTM sales 2,118, PAT –8. Interest FY26 ₹61 cr and depreciation ₹54 cr exceed what the operating profit can carry. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue +33.8%, EBITDA ₹55 cr (+0.7%), margin 6.21% vs 8.24%; but Q1 FY26 had ₹13.3 cr of PLI (production-linked incentive) income — stripped out, margin rose only ~15 bps. PAT ₹11.8 cr (–48.5%) on depreciation +30.7%, finance cost +27% (₹15.9 → ₹20.2 cr) and a wider JV loss (₹2.3 cr). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Unit economics: RAC growth = ~30% volume + ~14% price (commodity pass-through); underlying EBITDA ex-PLI ~6.5%; material margin historically 13–14%; SDA/LDA gross margin ~1.5–2 points above AC. Forex loss of ₹6–7 cr hit Q1. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- FY26 one-offs: ₹32.42 cr PLI accrued in 9M reversed in Q4 (FY26 eligibility ₹56.5 cr not booked because the incremental-sales condition was likely missed); ₹21.78 cr Rajasthan RIPS incentive booked in Q4 (₹16.82 cr for earlier periods). [Q4 call, official]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (21 May 2026): "normalized EBITDA of around 7%" with incentives on top; outgrow the ~15% industry in FY27; "doubling revenue in the medium term"; ₹5,000 cr revenue "in the next two to three years" (i.e. FY28–29) reaffirmed; remaining capex ₹170–200 cr over 9–12 months; debt to stay ~₹700 cr; front-load washing machines for Hisense from mid-Q2; 50–100 bps short-term margin pressure possible. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +33.8% (beat), RAC +43.8% vs industry ~20% expected (beat), margin 6.2% (missed ~7%), finance cost up (miss), front-load washing machine production slipped to end-September/October. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 changes: margin said to normalise only "next year onwards" after PLI discounts passed to brands (~1% of sales) are clawed back by March 2027; seasonal Q2/Q3 losses to be fixed over "4 to 6 quarters"; remaining FY27 capex now ₹60–70 cr (plus ₹40–45 cr CWIP) — lower than Q4's ₹170–200 cr; ₹5,000 cr target "largely on track" (needs ~38% a year from FY26). [Q1 call]
- Verdict: growth guidance **kept** and beaten in Q1; margin target **deferred** to FY28; capex **cut**. [Q4 call vs Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Programme ₹470 cr (Q1 deck): ₹297 cr spent in FY26, ₹10 cr in Q1 FY27 — Dehradun ₹20 cr, Bhiwadi ₹125 cr (₹84 cr spent), EDL Sri City ₹100 cr (₹80 cr), EMTPL Sri City (Hisense) ₹225 cr (₹128 cr). ICRA describes it as "debt-funded". [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Jan-26]
- Hisense: all plant investment is EPACK's (technology and tools from Hisense). AC production began end-March 2026; Q1 FY27 ~35,000 ACs / ~₹65 cr; Jan–Jun ~60,000 ACs / ~₹120 cr; 5-year cumulative target ₹8,000 cr (~₹1,600 cr a year average). Front-load washing machines pilot now, mass production end-Sep/Oct 2026. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Andhra Pradesh MoU: ₹1,085 cr investment over 5 years (from Jan 2024), 35 acres allotted, state subsidy ~50% of capex over 10 years (per official transcript) — longer-term, not immediate. [Q1 call]
- Asset turns: net fixed asset turn fell from 3.2x (FY25) to 2.4x (FY26); target ~4x. Payback not stated. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- EPAVO JV (BLDC motors, Bhiwadi): ~₹100 cr invested, 3 mn motors/yr from Jan 2026, losses doubled in FY26, EPACK has lent it ₹23.8 cr; breakeven promised from FY28. [Q4 call; AR FY26 RPT note]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- FY26 operating cash flow –₹140 cr; free cash flow –₹455 cr. Inventory ballooned to ₹837 cr at March 2026 (compressor pre-buying before BIS certification lapsed, plus unsold season stock); inventory days 190, cash conversion cycle 96 days (59 in FY25). [Screener; Q4 call]
- Gross debt ₹707 cr, net debt ₹691 cr at March 2026 (₹356 cr a year earlier); net debt/equity 0.72x; ROCE 5.2%, ROE 0.4%. CFO: debt ~₹700 cr incl. ₹200 cr term loan, to stay there near term. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27: working capital "50 to 60 days" depending on season; channel AC inventory 3.5–4 mn units, the leanest in years. [Q1 call]
- Guarantees given jumped to ₹176.7 cr (₹26.2 cr a year earlier); the company books guarantee income from its Hisense subsidiary EMTPL, so this is largely off-balance-sheet support for EMTPL debt (the standalone note does not itemise it). No dividend. [AR FY26 contingent-liabilities note]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and integration: claims highest RAC backward integration at one location in India; RAC grew 43.8% in Q1 while "a large peer de-grew". [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]
- Customer stickiness is modest: ICRA notes large OEMs increasingly build in-house and contract manufacturing "limits pricing flexibility"; margins ~6–7% for a decade. [ICRA Jan-26]
- The Hisense tie-up is the biggest differentiator — a multi-category dedicated plant for a fast-growing brand — but concentrates new growth on one partner. [Q4 call]
- Pricing: quarterly pass-through contracts with a lag; forex not fully covered. Half of PLI benefit (~1% of sales) was handed to brands — evidence of weak bargaining power. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Qualified audit opinion (Deloitte Haskins & Sells) on standalone and consolidated FY26 accounts**: ₹19.61 cr receivable from one customer is disputed; no provision made; auditor cannot judge recoverability. The company has filed a criminal complaint with the Economic Offences Wing. The amount is ~6x FY26 PAT (₹3.3 cr). [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 49]
- **Material weakness in internal financial controls** over recoverability of disputed receivables. Audit trail not enabled at database level. [AR FY26 Annexure on IFC; auditor's report]
- **Secretarial audit qualifications**: no Statement of Deviation filed after shareholders approved changing IPO-proceeds use (Aug 2025); entries missing from the insider-trading digital database; risk policy not reviewed. [AR FY26 Board's report]
- **Income-tax demand of ₹29.0 cr** for AY2023-24 (appeal filed); only ₹4.76 cr shown as contingent. Other contingent: income tax ₹5.3 cr, GST ₹2.2 cr. [AR FY26 contingent-liabilities note]
- **CRISIL "issuer not cooperating"** rating cut to BB/Stable (26 Jun 2026) after EPACK stopped sharing information; ICRA rates it A (Stable)/A2+ (Jan 2026) with total debt/OPBDIT 6.4x in H1 FY26. [CRISIL Jun-26; ICRA Jan-26]
- **PLI uncertainty**: FY26 ₹56.5 cr and FY27 ~₹60 cr eligibility unbooked pending government clarity; any recovery is upside, but the Q4 reversal shows earlier accruals were aggressive. [Q4 call]
- **Management churn**: group CFO Narayan Lodha left 30 Apr 2026; four company-secretary changes since 2023. Promoter holding slipped from 48.1% to 46.4%. [AR FY26 RPT note; Screener]
- Loans to group entities at March 2026: EMTPL (Hisense subsidiary) ₹22.3 cr (peak ₹28 cr), EPAVO JV ₹23.8 cr; EPACK also earned ₹3.7 cr of corporate-guarantee income from EMTPL, so the jump in guarantees largely backs the Hisense subsidiary's borrowing. [AR FY26 RPT note, Schedule V loans table]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: from defensive (PLI reversal, BEE transition, inventory glut, qualified audit) to offensive (record revenue, share gains, Hisense scaling). The disputed receivable was raised on the Q1 call but went unanswered (line trouble). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New: forex loss, margin reset to FY28, lower remaining capex, AP ₹1,085 cr MoU, 3 new product categories. Dropped: PLI accrual (now zero). [Q1 call]

## My read
Revenue is recovering fast and diversification is real (non-RAC 45% of FY26, top-2 customers down to 38%), and Hisense could transform scale. But the business earns ~6% EBITDA on a balance sheet now carrying ~₹690 cr net debt, cash flow was deeply negative in FY26, returns are near zero, and the auditor qualification, material-weakness finding, secretarial lapses and CRISIL non-cooperation are governance warnings that should not be waved away. With trailing earnings negative, the price needs the FY28 margin normalisation (~7%+), PLI clarity and Q2/Q3 losses to shrink; any valuation is really a bet on the ₹5,000 cr target. The view improves on a Q2 FY27 near breakeven, inventory and debt falling, and resolution or provision of the ₹19.6 cr dispute; it worsens if the dispute is written off, Hisense volumes stall, or debt rises further.
