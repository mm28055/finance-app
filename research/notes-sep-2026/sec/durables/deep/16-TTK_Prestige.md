# TTK Prestige (TTKPRESTIG) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check: both stored transcripts were compared with the official ones (Q1 FY27 call, 28 Jul 2026, company website; Q4 FY26 call, 22 May 2026, BSE filing).
- **Q1 FY27 stored copy: materially wrong.** (1) It says of the ₹500 cr plan "we have spent only about 1 crore till now"; the official text says "only **one-third** of it till now. Balance INR300 crores-plus will be spent over the next two years." (2) It says "close to 20 or 30 crores in the last 14 months" of the ₹200 cr strategy opex; the official figure is "**INR120 crores, INR130 crores** in the last 13 months, 14 months" (matches the presentations: ₹29.8 cr FY25 + ₹82.6 cr FY26 + ₹12.4 cr Q1 FY27 ≈ ₹125 cr). (3) It drops management's only growth hint — "It would be better than single-digits" (growth over the next 12–18 months). (4) SKU count given as 400–500; official says 400–450. (5) Induction share "up from about 5% a year ago" is a guess at a garbled official answer ("5% to 8% to 10% to 12% now"). (6) Host named as "361 Capital"; it was 360 ONE Capital.
- **Q4 FY26 stored copy: materially wrong on one headline figure.** It says full-year "Operating EBITDA growth of 30%"; the official text and presentation say **12% (₹302.9 cr vs ₹270.2 cr, standalone)**. It also omits the answer that A&P spend is normally 5–6% of sales. Everything else (14.4% domestic growth, ₹81.7 cr Q4 EBITDA, ₹185 cr PAT, 85%+/75–80% utilisation, 13–14% margin target) matches.

## Business and segments
- India's leading pressure-cooker and cookware brand (Prestige), plus kitchen appliances (gas stoves, mixer grinders, induction cooktops, small appliances) and a value brand, Judge. Sells to Indian households through general trade, e-commerce/quick commerce, modern trade, 709 Prestige Xclusive stores and CSD (military canteens). [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A]
- FY26 standalone sales ₹2,772.7 cr: cookers ₹856.0 cr (30.9%, +8.7%), cookware ₹485.0 cr (17.5%, +12.4%), appliances ₹1,294.8 cr (46.7%, +9.9%), others ₹136.8 cr (4.9%, +3.3%). Kitchenware (cookers + cookware) is therefore ~48% of sales. [IP Q4FY26]
- Appliance detail FY26: gas stoves ₹344 cr, induction cooktops ₹321 cr (+18%), mixer grinders ₹231 cr, other kitchen/home appliances ₹399 cr. [AR FY26 MD&A table]
- Q4 FY26 appliances grew 20.1% (₹329 cr) on LPG-shortage-driven induction demand, while cookers grew 6.9% and cookware 4.1%. [IP Q4FY26]
- Domestic ₹2,704 cr (97.5%); exports only ₹68.3 cr (+2.6%). The "traditional channel" (GT, exclusive stores, e-com, modern trade) grew 11.6%; MFI-led rural sales and CSD dragged. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- Judge brand: ₹108 cr in FY26 (+59%); Q1 FY27 ₹34.6 cr (+89.9%). [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27]
- Share of sales from Prestige Xclusive: management said "12% to 15%" on the Q4 call but "18% to 20%" on the Q1 call — inconsistent. Induction cooktops ~8–10% of sales now. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Manufacturing: all kitchenware made in-house; appliances from exclusive contract partners. Kitchenware plants run at 85%+ utilisation, appliances 75–80%. New tri-ply cookware facility at Karjan (Gujarat). Some new products are imported from China. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Subsidiaries: Horwood Homewares (UK, 100%) — FY26 sales £14.0m, EBITDA –£0.9m; Q1 FY27 £2.6m sales, –£0.6m EBITDA. Ultrafresh Modular Solutions (India, 51%, modular kitchens) — FY26 ₹36.3 cr sales, EBITDA –₹7.6 cr; Q1 ₹10.1 cr, –₹0.2 cr. [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 609 / op. profit 40 (7%) / PAT 26; Sep-25 834 / 96 (12%) / 63; Dec-25 801 / 72 (9%) / 32; Mar-26 729 / 67 (9%) / 36; **Jun-26 814 / 82 (10%) / 59**. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: consolidated sales +33.6% YoY; standalone +34.2% (domestic +35.6%, exports down to ₹12.8 cr). Standalone operating EBITDA ₹88.8 cr (+74%); 13.1% before strategy expenses, 11.5% after (8.9% a year ago). Consolidated PAT ₹59.0 cr (+130%), helped by a ₹7.3 cr reversal of the labour-code provision. [IP Q1FY27]
- Of the 34% growth, only ~3% was price; the rest volume and mix. Cookware led, then appliances and cookers. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Annual (Screener consolidated): sales FY24 ₹2,678 cr → FY25 ₹2,715 cr → FY26 ₹2,974 cr; OPM 11% → 9% → 9%; PAT ₹225 cr → ₹108 cr → ₹157 cr. TTM sales ₹3,178 cr, PAT ₹190 cr. Three-year sales CAGR only 2%, profit CAGR –12%. ROCE 14% → 12% → 12%. [Screener]
- FY25 PAT was hit by a ₹71.4 cr consolidated impairment of the UK business; FY26 had ₹27.4 cr exceptional costs (Hosur VRS ₹10 cr, new labour code ₹16.9 cr). [IP Q4FY26]
- The strategy programme depresses reported margin: standalone EBITDA before strategy costs was 13.9% in FY26 (11.9% FY25) but 10.9% after. [IP Q4FY26]
- Unit economics: no volume or price per unit disclosed. Price increases of 5–8% were taken at the end of Q1 against ~8% average raw-material inflation (aluminium, steel, copper), so gross margin pressure should show in Q2. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- The company does not give numeric guidance. Standing aims: grow faster than GDP/market, gain share, return EBITDA margin to "13%, 14%" once the investment phase ends (about two more years from May 2026). [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Q4 call (May 2026) promises: price hikes "inevitable" against ~10% input inflation; induction demand "may continue for a couple of more quarters"; both appliances and kitchenware growing ~10%; capex in cookware and appliances over the next two years. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: price hikes of 5–8% taken (**kept**); induction demand continued and spread to all categories (**beat**); growth of 34% versus the ~10% run-rate (**beat**); exports still weak (**missed** again). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 call (Jul 2026) added: FY27 growth "better than last year", next 12–18 months "better than single-digits"; volume growth above inflation; value growth above volume over time; margin target restated as "13%, 13-plus". July demand "robust"; presentation says Q1 trends "largely continued in July 2026". [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Management itself warned Q1's pace is "not a sustainable consistent demand" and expects the industry to settle at a higher level than before. [Q1 call]
- CRISIL's November 2025 base case was far more modest: 4–5% revenue growth and ~11% margin (before strategy costs) over the medium term. Q4–Q1 results have already beaten that. [CRISIL Nov-25]

## Capex and capacity
- ₹500 cr three-year plan announced early 2025 (from Q4 FY25): ₹300 cr capex (automation, digitisation, factory upgrades, renewable energy, IT, extra lines) + ₹200 cr "business excellence" opex (external experts on innovation, go-to-market, sourcing, logistics). About one-third spent by July 2026; opex spend ~₹125 cr so far, so ~₹75 cr of opex and most of the ₹300 cr capex remain. [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Capex: ₹39 cr in FY25, ₹87 cr in FY26; FY27 estimated at ~₹100 cr (normal capex, logistics, capacity augmentation). CRISIL assumed ~₹180 cr a year. Capital commitments ₹46.7 cr at Mar-26 (₹34.4 cr a year earlier). CWIP ₹26 cr. [AR FY26 MD&A; AR FY26 note 34; CRISIL Nov-25; Screener]
- All funded from cash; no debt planned. With kitchenware at 85%+ utilisation, cookware (tri-ply) and appliance capacity additions are expected over the next two years. [Q4 call]
- Asset turns: FY26 sales ₹2,974 cr on net fixed assets ₹675 cr ≈ 4.4x (Screener, includes leased stores). Payback on the soft spend is not quantified; management points to gross-margin gains from sourcing and process work. [Screener; Q4 call]
- New product flow: 162 SKUs in FY26 (new products ~30% of FY26 sales), 26 in Q1 FY27, ~40 planned for Q2. New innovation centre in Bengaluru; Hosur centre being upgraded. [IP Q4FY26; IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Free cash of ~₹877 cr at Mar-26 and "over ₹870 cr" at Jun-26 (cash plus liquid investments, standalone). Standalone debt-free. Screener borrowings ₹174 cr consolidated are mostly lease liabilities (₹110 cr standalone) plus a ~₹44 cr UK term loan due FY28. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 note 35; CRISIL Nov-25; Screener]
- Operating cash flow ₹289 cr (FY24) → ₹157 cr (FY25) → ₹210 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹222 cr → ₹116 cr → ₹122 cr. CFO/operating profit 96% in FY26. [Screener]
- Working capital (FY26): debtor days 35, inventory days 146, payables 62, cash conversion cycle 119 days (125 in FY25). Inventory is the heavy item. [Screener]
- Shareholder returns: ₹250 cr buyback in FY25; dividend ₹7.50/share for FY26 (₹6.00 in FY25), payout 64% of consolidated profit. [CRISIL Nov-25; AR FY26 MD&A; Screener]
- Rating: CRISIL AA/Stable/A1+ reaffirmed Nov 2025; gearing ~0.03x, interest cover ~21x (FY25). Downgrade triggers include margin below 10–11% and cash reduction via buybacks. [CRISIL Nov-25]

## Moat evidence
- Category leader in pressure cookers; claims leadership in cookware, value-added gas stoves, induction cooktops and kettles, with "dominant market share in both value and volume" and share gains in most categories in FY26. Hawkins remains strong in inner-lid cookers. [AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Nov-25]
- Pricing power: 5–8% price increases taken with no visible volume loss; prices now back at pre-GST-cut levels; management calls the product "not that much price elastic" because it plays above the mass tier. [Q1 call]
- Distribution: 709 Prestige Xclusive stores in 337 towns, 568 service centres (487 a year ago); general trade grew after a three-year lag. [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Innovation: all cookers are induction-compatible; the industry has moved to 50–55% stainless-steel cookers; tri-ply, cast iron and ceramic cookware growing 20%+ at industry level. [Q4 call]
- Weak points: many D2C entrants compete on price in cookware and small appliances; e-commerce leads growth, which dilutes brand/shelf advantages. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]

## Risks and red flags
- **Demand spike may fade:** Q4–Q1 growth was triggered by an LPG shortage (West Asia conflict) that pushed induction buying and kitchen refurbishment; management itself calls the peak unsustainable. A pull-forward could mean weak comparisons from Q4 FY27. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Input costs:** ~8–10% raw-material inflation (aluminium, steel, copper), minimum-wage increases in several states and a weaker rupee (record ₹96.8/$ in May 2026). Price hikes took effect only at the end of Q1. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Related party:** TTK Prestige pays promoter firm **TT Krishnamachari & Co a logo licence fee of ₹14.2 cr** (FY26; ₹13.0 cr FY25) plus C&F charges (₹3.2 cr; ₹13.8 cr in FY25). Total expenses to promoter-controlled entities ₹17.6 cr (₹27.1 cr FY25). ₹11 cr of inter-corporate deposits lent to 51% subsidiary Ultrafresh (loss-making). ₹5 cr put into GramyaHaat CCDs. [AR FY26 note 36; AR FY26 MD&A]
- **Loss-making subsidiaries:** Horwood (UK) still loss-making after a ₹71 cr impairment in FY25; the auditor lists subsidiary impairment as a key audit matter. For the Ultrafresh investment (₹30 cr carrying value), headroom is only ₹3.19 cr — a small miss could trigger another write-down. The UK test avoided a further charge partly by raising assumptions: terminal growth 5.4% (2% last year) and 8.5% average sales growth (5.6%) for a business whose sales fell in FY26. [AR FY26 auditor's report; AR FY26 note 5]
- **Contingent liabilities (standalone):** guarantees ₹52.7 cr (₹50.3 cr for the UK subsidiary's £4m bank facility), indirect-tax claims ₹35.0 cr, customs ₹2.5 cr, direct tax ₹1.9 cr. CARO lists ₹36.3 cr of disputed dues not deposited, the largest a ₹23.2 cr GST dispute (FY2017–22) and ₹8.0 cr service tax. [AR FY26 note 34; AR FY26 CARO]
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matters are revenue recognition and subsidiary impairment; no emphasis of matter found. Audit trail enabled all year. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- The strategy opex is presented as "one-time", but it has run at ~₹80 cr a year for two years; investors should judge margins after, not before, these costs. [IP Q4FY26; Q1 call]
- Disclosure gaps: no capacity numbers, no volume data, inconsistent Xclusive share (12–15% vs 18–20%). Promoter holding steady at 70.5%; no pledge reported. [Q4 call; Q1 call; Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from cautious ("tempering on demand", "overhang of challenges for the next few quarters") to confident ("very robust quarter", market-share gains, July demand robust). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Growth driver broadened: Q4 was induction-led (appliances +20%, cookware +4%); in Q1 cookware led and growth spread across categories. [IP Q4FY26; IP Q1FY27]
- Input inflation cited as ~10% in May and ~8% in July; price hikes 5–8% now fully announced. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New issues: minimum-wage increases; 400–450 new SKUs cited as a share driver; a first hint on growth ("better than single-digits"). Exports and CSD still weak in both calls.

## My read
The business is clearly better than a year ago: after three flat years (sales CAGR 2%), sales grew 10% in FY26 and 34% in Q1 FY27, with share gains, a wider range and general trade recovering. But Q1 was inflated by a one-off LPG-shortage event and kitchen refurbishment, and the price increases only land in Q2, so margins face a test while ~₹75 cr of strategy opex and most of the ₹300 cr capex are still to come. The price needs sustained low-teens growth and a return towards a 13% margin after strategy costs by FY28 — plausible but unproven, because underlying margin was only ~11% after these costs in Q1. The view improves if growth stays in double digits through the festive quarter after the price increases, and if the soft spend visibly tapers; it worsens if Q3–Q4 growth falls back to mid-single digits against the high base, or if the UK/Ultrafresh losses need more support.
