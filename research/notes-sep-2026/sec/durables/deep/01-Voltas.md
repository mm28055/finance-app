# Voltas — deep research (Phase A1, written 27 Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Tata group (30.30% promoter holding) cooling and projects company in three segments. FY26 segment revenue: **Unitary Cooling Products (UCP)** ₹9,501 cr (FY25 ₹10,614 cr, −10.5%), **Electro-Mechanical Projects & Services (EMPS)** ₹4,053 cr (FY25 ₹4,157 cr), **Engineering Products & Services (EPS)** ₹599 cr (FY25 ₹569 cr). [AR FY26, note 49]
- FY26 segment profit (EBIT): UCP ₹305 cr (3.2% margin, vs ₹892 cr / 8.4% in FY25); EMPS ₹299 cr (7.4%, vs ₹169 cr / 4.1%); EPS ₹158 cr (26%). EMPS carried the group in FY26 while cooling profit fell by two-thirds. [AR FY26, note 49]
- Q1 FY27 segment split: UCP revenue ₹3,794 cr (+32% from ₹2,868 cr), result ₹202 cr (5.3%, vs ₹104 cr / 3.7%); EMPS ₹672 cr (−27% from ₹922 cr), result ₹38 cr (5.6%, vs ₹49 cr); EPS ₹159 cr (+17%), result ₹41 cr. [Q1 FY27 results filing]
- Room AC (RAC) is the core: 17.3% secondary market share for FY27 to June (15.9% for FY26), lead over no. 2 widened to 4 percentage points; 1 million RACs sold in 81 days; RAC volumes +45% (value ~+50%). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Manufacturing: Pantnagar ~1.4 million units and Chennai ~1.5 million units RAC capacity; Chennai built for 2 million with "small capex" to add the last 0.5 million. Split ACs are ~75:25 own-made vs outsourced; window ACs (~10% of Q1 volume) are fully outsourced. Both plants ran at "high utilisation" in Q1. [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26]
- **Voltbek** (49% JV with Arçelik/Beko) sells refrigerators and washing machines: FY26 turnover ₹2,510 cr; Voltas' total investment ₹935 cr (₹98 cr added in FY26). Share YTD June 2026: 9.4% washing machines, 7.4% refrigerators (vs 8.6% / 6.2% at the Q4 call); no. 2 in semi-automatic washers at 15.6%. [AR FY26 Board's report; Q1 call]
- EMPS order book: ₹6,345 cr at 30 Jun 2026 (₹6,200 cr at March, of which ₹4,500 cr domestic). Domestic projects sit in subsidiary UMPESL (FY26 turnover ₹2,958 cr, PBT ₹316 cr). [Q1 call; Q4 call; AR FY26]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit: Jun-25 ₹3,939 / ₹153 / 3.9% / ₹141 cr; Sep-25 ₹2,347 / ₹34 / 1.4% / ₹32 cr; Dec-25 ₹3,071 / ₹145 / 4.7% / ₹84 cr; Mar-26 ₹4,888 / ₹185 / 3.8% / ₹113 cr; Jun-26 ₹4,674 / ₹228 / 4.9% / ₹213 cr. [Screener]
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹12,481 cr, OPM 2.7%, PAT ₹248 cr; FY25 ₹15,413 cr, 6%, ₹834 cr; FY26 ₹14,244 cr (−8%), 3.6%, ₹370 cr (−56%). TTM sales ₹14,979 cr, PAT ₹442 cr. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 sales +18.7% YoY, net profit +51%, but the peak-season operating margin (4.9%) is still well below Q1 FY25's 8%. [Screener]
- Other income is large relative to profit: ₹212 cr in FY26 against PBT ₹557 cr; TTM ₹221 cr. Treasury income is holding up reported earnings. [Screener]
- Joint-venture losses (mostly Voltbek) cut PBT by ₹131 cr in FY26 (₹126 cr in FY25) and ₹37 cr in Q1 FY27 (₹26 cr a year ago). [AR FY26 note 49; Q1 results filing; Q1 call]
- Unit economics: 2.25 million RACs sold in FY26 against an industry of 14.3 million units primary (industry fell ~10–12%). Cost inflation this season 10–12% (7–8% from the new BEE energy table, 4–5% from commodities, rupee, freight, plastics); Voltas says it passed on all but "1–2 percentage points". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- ROCE 9% in FY26 (18% FY25); ROE 6% last year. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- No numeric company guidance. Q4 call (14 May 2026) promised: RAC industry +15–20% in FY27 on a weak base; commercial refrigeration (CR) +10%; commercial AC (CAC) +12–15%; UCP margin to "gradually" climb back toward FY25 levels (8.4% segment margin); no sharp margin dip in Q1. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivered: industry primary +20–22% volume (GfK secondary +15%) — above the range; Voltas RAC +44–45% — far above. CR **missed**: industry fell ~15% after 10–15% price rises. UCP margin 5.3% vs 3.7% a year ago and 5.0% in Q4 — improving but still ~3 points short of FY25. [Q1 call; Q1 results filing]
- Voltbek EBITDA break-even was "initially aspired" for FY27; now "pushed out by a few quarters" because of West Asia commodity spikes — **cut**. [Q1 call]
- EMPS: Q4 call said order book "will deliver robust profitability"; Q1 revenue fell 27% on Middle East order delays and cautious bidding. CFO: Q2 "may also not be very much better", recovery in Q3–Q4 — **deferred**. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Longer-term: UCP margin "upward of 7%" is the aspiration "over the next 8 quarters or so"; management repeatedly says market share first, "margin profile will take care of itself". [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- The big cycle is done: fixed assets rose from ₹548 cr (FY24) to ₹1,080 cr (FY26) and CWIP ran down from ₹368 cr to ₹22 cr as Chennai (India's largest RAC plant, 150 acres) and Waghodia (CAC, CR) came on stream. [Screener; AR FY26]
- CFO on FY27–28: "no material major capex", mainly maintenance; Chennai can go from 1.5 to 2 million units with small spend when demand justifies it. Much of the discretionary capex goes to commercial AC, which management calls "the next growth engine". [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- New: binding term sheet for a 50:50 **compressor JV with Atomberg** — about 2.8 million RAC compressors eventually; start below 1 million units ~18 months out, ramp to 1–2 million, then above 2.5 million. Capex not yet sized; split 50:50; spend mainly FY28–FY29. Aim is supply security under the compressor Quality Control Order (imports capped at 30% of FY25 volumes), not cost savings; Atomberg's motor covers "40–50% of the BOM". Subject to due diligence and definitive agreements. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Payback: management gave no return target for either Chennai or the JV. With FY26 ROCE at 9%, the new capacity has not yet earned its keep; Q1's high utilisation is the first sign it can. [Q1 call; Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Balance sheet (Mar-26): borrowings ₹992 cr vs investments ₹2,762 cr; net worth ~₹6,376 cr. Investments fell from ₹3,508 cr (FY24) as cash was absorbed by working capital and capex. ICRA (Feb 2025) cited >₹2,500 cr cash and liquid investments and ₹1,545 cr of sparingly used bank lines; rating [ICRA]AA+ (Stable)/A1+. [Screener; ICRA Feb-25]
- Cash flow is weak: operating cash flow −₹225 cr in FY25 and ₹71 cr in FY26; free cash flow −₹416 cr and −₹58 cr. [Screener]
- FY26 working capital stretched: inventory days 113 (83 in FY25), debtor days 78 (53), payable days 172 (119); cash conversion cycle 19 days. Management blamed pre-season stocking and supply-chain precaution; at Q1 it said the quarter ended with "strong liquidity" and channel stock ~4 weeks. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Dividend ₹4/share for FY26 (₹7 for FY25), ~₹132 cr, 39% payout. No buyback. [AR FY26 Board's report]

## Moat evidence
- Share: RAC secondary share 15.9% (FY26) → 17.3% (Q1 FY27), a 4-point lead; primary-share gap of 5.1 points over the next cluster claimed at Q4. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Pricing: passed through 10–12% cost inflation almost fully in a year when peers' UCP EBIT reportedly fell 3–4 points; Voltas' rose 1.6 points YoY. [Q1 call]
- Distribution: Tata brand trust plus a wide dealer network (the profile's "29,000 touchpoints" figure was not found in the documents read). Voltbek rides the same channel. [Q4 call]
- Cost position: localisation and import content below 30% (AR), 75% own manufacturing of split ACs, the only large player now building its own compressor supply. [AR FY26; Q1 call]
- Limits: ~60 brands compete in RAC; FY26 showed that a bad summer takes UCP margin from 8.4% to 3.2% — the moat protects share, not margin. [Q4 call; AR FY26]

## Risks and red flags
- **Qatar (Sidra) bank guarantees — resolved in Voltas' favour.** ₹433 cr (QAR 166.6 million) of guarantees shown as a contingent liability; Qatar Court of Appeal (4 May 2026) ordered them returned and awarded dues to the Kentz–Voltas consortium; guarantees cancelled in Q1; Court of Cassation dismissed the contractor's appeal on 30 Aug 2026. Auditor's FY26 **emphasis of matter** on this point should fall away; recovery of the award is still pending. [AR FY26 auditor's report & note 47; BSE filing 1 Sep 2026]
- Other claims not acknowledged as debt: ₹328 cr at Mar-26 (₹306 cr a year earlier), incl. GST ₹93 cr, sales tax ₹99 cr, contractual ₹87 cr. New GST show-cause notice (18 Sep 2026): ₹15.4 cr tax + ₹14.8 cr interest/penalty for FY23 input-credit; company says no material impact. [AR FY26 note 47; BSE filing 21 Sep 2026]
- Auditor: unqualified; key audit matters are percentage-of-completion revenue on ₹3,845 cr of project contracts and impairment testing of the ₹935 cr Voltbek investment and ₹337 cr in Voltas Netherlands. [AR FY26 auditor's reports]
- Voltbek remains loss-making (₹131 cr JV-share loss in FY26) with break-even now slipping; the AR itself says the business "continues to operate at sub-scale profitability". [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- Guarantees for subsidiaries/JV: limits fell to ₹759 cr (₹3,010 cr a year earlier), ₹209 cr used. [AR FY26 note 47]
- Earnings quality: other income is about a third of PBT; FY26 also had a ₹26 cr labour-code exceptional charge and ₹55 cr of H2 forex/treasury mark-to-market losses. [Screener; AR FY26 note 44; Q4 call]
- Weather and commodities: a cool summer (FY26) cut UCP profit by 66%; copper, rupee and freight remain volatile with the West Asia conflict. [AR FY26; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (FY26 profit −56%, "progressive recovery") to confident (share gain, 1 million units in 81 days, margin up while peers' fell).
- Numbers: UCP margin 5.0% (Q4) → 5.3% (Q1); RAC share 15.9% → 17.3%; Voltbek share 8.6%/6.2% → 9.4%/7.4%; order book ₹6,200 → ₹6,345 cr.
- New issues: the Atomberg compressor JV; Voltbek break-even pushed out; EMPS revenue drop and Middle East order delays; CR demand slump after price rises.
- Dropped: the GST cut (28%→18%) as an affordability cushion, and the forex mark-to-market drag.

## My read
The cooling franchise is clearly back: a 45% RAC volume jump, a record share lead and margins rising when rivals' fell show the Chennai plant and cost programme working. But the whole group earned only a 4.9% operating margin in its best quarter, cash flow has been negative or thin for two years, Voltbek keeps losing ₹130 cr a year, and other income props up a third of profit — so earnings power is still well below FY25. The price needs UCP margins back near 7–8% and Voltbek at break-even within about two years; watch Q2–Q3 UCP margins, the Voltbek loss line and EMPS order intake. A second hot summer with steady margins would support the stock; a margin stall near 5% would not.

## Stored-data errors
- Dashboard revenue lines are **total income**, not sales: Q1 FY27 ₹4,765 cr (Screener sales ₹4,674 cr); Q4 FY26 ₹4,930 cr (₹4,888 cr); Q2 FY26 ₹2,411.93 cr (₹2,347 cr).
- EBITDA recorded as 0 for Q2, Q3, Q4 FY26; Screener shows ₹34 cr, ₹145 cr, ₹185 cr (OPM 1.4%, 4.7%, 3.8%).
- Q4 FY26 "yoy=1.7" is total-income growth; sales growth was +2.5%.
- Stored analysis says Q4 UCP margin "plunged to 3.2%" and Q1 recovered "from Q4's dismal 3.2%". Wrong: 3.2% is the **FY26 full-year** UCP margin; Q4 FY26 UCP margin was 5.0% (₹174 cr on ₹3,493 cr), so Q1's 5.3% is a +30 bp QoQ gain.
- Stored profile says Voltas' "30-35%" import dependence; the call gives 30–35% as the **industry** figure; the AR says Voltas is below 30%.
- Profile's "29,000 consumer touchpoints" not found in the call, presentation or annual report read.
- Stored analysis says management "maintained the 5.1% lead"; 5.1 points was a primary-share gap (Q4 call); Q1's 4-point lead is on secondary share, so the two are not comparable.
