# Eureka Forbes — deep research (Phase A1)

Researched 27 Sep 2026. Figures from Screener consolidated page (raw HTML read) unless tagged. Price ₹371, market cap ₹7,175 cr, 52-week range ₹355–668 [Screener, 27-Sep-26].
Definitions: the company reports "adjusted EBITDA" (excludes ESOP cost and non-operating income); Screener operating profit is lower (e.g. Q1 FY27 ₹69 cr vs adjusted ₹73.9 cr).

## Business and segments
- India's leading water-purifier brand (Aquaguard) plus vacuum cleaners and robotic cleaners (Forbes), air purifiers and water softeners, and a large after-sales service business (AMCs — annual maintenance contracts — and filters). Single reportable segment. [AR FY26 notes; IP Q1FY27]
- FY26 standalone revenue split: sale of products ₹2,064 cr (+13.7%), sale of services ₹630 cr (+4.1%), other operating ₹16 cr — services ~23% of revenue. [AR FY26 note 24]
- Water purifier category penetration only ~7% of households; robotics now 65–66% of the company's vacuum-cleaner revenue; air purifiers grew 2.7x in FY26 on a low base. [Q1 call; Q4 call, official]
- Reach: service in 19,500+ pin codes, 8,000+ technicians, first-party data on ~15 million customers, ~2.5 million monthly active digital users. [CRISIL Sep-26; Q1 call]
- Ownership: Lunolux Ltd (Cyprus), 100% owned by Advent International funds, holds 62.56%. [AR FY26 note on share capital]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (₹ cr, Screener consolidated): Q1 FY26 revenue 608 / operating profit 61 / OPM 10% / PAT 39; Q2 FY26 772 / 98 / 13% / 63; Q3 FY26 645 / 69 / 11% / 10; Q4 FY26 684 / 86 / 13% / 51; **Q1 FY27 700 / 69 / 10% / 57**. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 (standalone): revenue ₹700.8 cr (+15.3%, highest growth "in the transformation journey"); gross margin 58.4% (−131 bps); adjusted EBITDA ₹73.9 cr, 10.5% (−46 bps); other expenses +21% on in-store A&SP; pre-exceptional PAT ₹40.9 cr (+6.1%); reported PAT ₹55.4 cr includes a one-time ₹19.5 cr gratuity reversal. [IP Q1FY27]
- Annual: FY24 revenue ₹2,189 cr, operating profit ₹199 cr (9%), PAT ₹96 cr; FY25 ₹2,437 cr, ₹266 cr (11%), ₹164 cr; FY26 ₹2,709 cr (+11.3%), ₹314 cr (12%), ₹163 cr (labour-code charge in Q3). TTM ₹2,802 cr / ₹321 cr / ₹181 cr. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Company adjusted EBITDA margin: 6.3% FY23 → 12.2% FY26 (three years of expansion); FY26 adjusted EBITDA ₹332 cr (+16.4%). [Q4 call, official]
- Unit economics: price rise of ~6–7% on products in April 2026 "landed well"; AMC prices raised 3–12%, which slowed AMC bookings. Filters: SKUs cut from ~65 to 5 universal filters sold through a new open distribution network. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- ROCE only ~6% (Screener) because ~₹4,967 cr of goodwill and brand intangibles from Advent's 2022 acquisition sit on the balance sheet. [Screener; AR FY26 auditor's report]

## Guidance history
- **Standing targets (Investor Day, reiterated):** FY30 revenue 2x FY25 (≈₹4,870 cr, ~15% a year) and EBITDA 3x; robotics revenue ₹1,000 cr+ by FY30. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Q4 call (20 May 2026):** FY27 — "step up" revenue growth over FY26's 11.3% and "at least hold" margins (12.2%); service revenue growth to step up as FY26 multi-year AMC bookings are recognised; price rise 6–7% already taken. [Q4 call, official]
- **Q1 delivery (13 Aug 2026):** revenue +15.3% — **delivered**; margin 10.5% vs 11.0% — management says Q1 is always front-loaded with A&SP (last year Q1 was also down ~46 bps but full year ended +55 bps) — **on track but unproven**; service growth similar to Q4 (not stepped up) and AMC bookings softer after price rises — **missed**. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- **Q1 outlook:** full-year margin "broadly in line with last year" (softened from "at least hold"); A&SP to grow faster than revenue; ESOP cost ₹25–26 cr for FY27; Q2 service growth similar to Q1; FY30 goals reaffirmed. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: FY26 capex ₹84 cr (FY25 ₹55 cr). No FY27 capex number given. [Q4 call]
- Robotics being localised: assembly from semi-knocked-down kits moving to an exclusive Dixon arrangement "over the next 3–4 months" (from May 2026) ahead of a BIS quality-control order due September 2026. [Q4 call, official]
- Capacity/utilisation not disclosed (mix of own plants and contract manufacturing).
- Growth levers: 4-year-filter-life purifiers launched ~July 2026 (Glow 4X ~₹22,000, Ritz Pro 4X ~₹25,000) — first in India; stainless-steel, hot/cold, IoT and under-counter ranges; D2C cross-selling (multi-category ownership <1% of base); air purifiers beyond North India; softeners (hard water in "90% of India"); genuine-filter campaigns. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Cash of ~₹425–443 cr kept partly for acquisitions ("inorganic growth opportunities as and when they arise"). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash ₹443 cr at March 2026 and ₹425 cr at June 2026; plus ~₹62 cr mutual funds; borrowings ₹29 cr (mainly leases). Rated **CRISIL AA/Stable** (upgraded from AA-/Positive on 8-Sep-2026). [Q4 call; Q1 call; CRISIL Sep-26; Screener]
- Free cash flow ₹237 cr in FY26 (148% of reported PAT, company definition); Screener shows CFO ₹250 cr, FCF ₹163 cr. [Q4 call; Screener]
- Working capital (Screener FY26): debtor days 31, inventory days 123 (up from 94), payable days 75. Contract liabilities (AMC income received in advance) rose to ₹204.6 cr from ₹150.9 cr — revenue booked into later years. [Screener; AR FY26 note on other liabilities]
- No dividend has been paid since listing. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Aquaguard is the category's leading brand ("market leaders in water purifiers and vacuum cleaners"); Q1 FY27 water-purifier growth "well ahead of the category" with share gains and double-digit volume growth. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Pricing: 6–7% product rise taken first; peers followed within ~30 days. [Q1 call]
- Switching costs/annuity: installed base serviced via AMCs, multi-year AMC mix rising to "elbow out grey-market operators". [Q4 call]
- Weakness: service growth only ~4% in FY26 vs products ~14%; consumers resisted AMC price rises; local plumbers and platforms (Urban Company) compete for service. [AR FY26 note 24; Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Promoter pledge:** Lunolux (Advent) has pledged **98.69 million shares = 51.0% of the company's equity (about 81% of its own 62.56% stake)** to lenders via Catalyst Trusteeship. The pledge grew from 64.95m shares (May 2024) with a top-up of 11.42m (July 2026) and a further 22.32m on 15-Sep-2026 when the holding-company loan was extended and increased. A falling share price could trigger further top-ups or, in stress, a forced sale/overhang. [BSE SAST filing by Catalyst Trusteeship, 17-Sep-26]
- **Goodwill/intangibles:** ₹2,056 cr goodwill + ₹2,911 cr indefinite-life brands = 74% of total assets; impairment testing is the auditor's key audit matter. A sustained growth miss could force a write-down (non-cash). Auditor's opinion unqualified. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Service engine stalling:** AMC bookings softened after 3–12% price increases; management now leans on filters, which is a behaviour-change bet that it says needs "patience". [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Margin pressure:** gross margin −131 bps on commodities and a weaker rupee; A&SP to grow faster than revenue; CRISIL's downgrade trigger is operating margin below 10% (Q1 adjusted was 10.5%). [IP Q1FY27; CRISIL Sep-26]
- Contingent liabilities ~₹80 cr (sales tax ₹26.6 cr, GST ₹21.8 cr, excise ₹14.4 cr, income tax ₹14.0 cr, others). [AR FY26 note 33A]
- Related-party dealings are only with subsidiaries (small); no fees to Advent/Lunolux seen in the note. [AR FY26 note 34]
- Share price: −44% from the 52-week high while the pledge was enlarged — the combination is the key watch-point.

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: confident — "highest ever" 13.2% margin, AMC bookings double-digit for four quarters, "at least hold margins".
- Q1: growth accelerated (15.3%) and share gains, but margins dipped, AMC bookings slowed after price rises, and margin language softened to "broadly in line".
- New: 4-year-filter purifiers, in-store promoter push in modern retail, ESOP cost guidance; after the call, the promoter pledge was increased (Sep-2026) and CRISIL upgraded the rating.

## My read
The product engine is clearly improving — 15% growth with double-digit volumes, share gains and new categories scaling — and the company is debt-free with strong cash generation. The weak links are services (the supposed high-margin annuity) and margins under cost and ad-spend pressure. The FY30 plan needs ~15% revenue growth and margin expansion every year; FY27 is already a "hold margins" year. The large and growing promoter pledge by Advent's holding company is the main non-operating risk; any sign of a block sale or pledge invocation would weigh on the stock regardless of results.
