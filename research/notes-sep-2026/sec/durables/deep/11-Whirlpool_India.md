# Whirlpool of India — deep research (Phase A1)

Researched 27 Sep 2026. Figures from Screener consolidated page (raw HTML read) unless tagged otherwise. Price ₹919, market cap ₹11,658 cr, 52-week range ₹706–1,438 [Screener, 27-Sep-26].

## Business and segments
- Makes and sells refrigerators (direct-cool and frost-free), washing machines (semi-automatic, top-load, front-load) and air conditioners, plus kitchen chimneys and hobs through 100%-owned **Elica PB Whirlpool** ("Elica"). Sold to Indian households mainly through multi-brand outlets (MBOs), large-format chains and e-commerce. [Q1 call; AR FY26 p.33]
- Three plants: Pune (frost-free refrigerators, washers), Faridabad (direct-cool refrigerators) and Puducherry. ACs are not made in-house; they are contract-manufactured to Whirlpool designs. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- Mix (not formally reported; single segment under Ind AS 108): refrigerators "more than 50%" of sales, washers "about 25–30%"; refrigerators plus ACs are "nearly two-thirds". [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 note 37]
- Elica is small: Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue ₹2,727 cr less standalone ₹2,582 cr implies about ₹145 cr, roughly 5% of group sales, but with a 16.0% PBT margin in FY26 (revenue +12% in FY26; +26% revenue, +22% PBT in Q1 FY27). [Q1 call; AR FY26 p.33]
- Share: #2 in MBO volume share for refrigerators + washers combined in March 2026; #1 in direct-cool refrigerators for several months; front-load washer share only ~5% (3% in weak regions, near 10% in the best); AC share "single digits". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Capacity: utilisation across products about **70–75%** a year; front-load washer lines not even on three shifts. [Q1 call]
- Ownership: Whirlpool Corporation (via Whirlpool Mauritius) cut its stake from 75% to 51% (Feb-2024) and to 39.76% (Nov-2025). [Screener shareholding; Rumour clarification 24-Sep-26]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (consolidated, ₹ cr): Q1 FY26 revenue 2,432 / operating profit (EBITDA) 211 / OPM 9% / PAT 146; Q2 FY26 1,647 / 58 / 3.5% / 42; Q3 FY26 1,774 / 91 / 5% / 27; Q4 FY26 2,181 / 121 / 5.5% / 80; **Q1 FY27 2,727 / 139 / 5.1% / 103**. [Screener]
- So Q1 FY27 revenue rose 12.1% but EBITDA fell 34% and PAT 29% year on year — a record top line with the weakest peak-season margin in years. [Screener; Q1 call]
- Standalone Q1 FY27: revenue ₹2,582 cr (+11.4%), EBITDA ₹120 cr (4.6%, down 377 bps), PBT ₹120 cr (4.6%, down ~320 bps). [Q1 call]
- Annual: FY24 revenue ₹6,830 cr, EBITDA ₹403 cr (6%), PAT ₹224 cr; FY25 ₹7,919 cr, ₹550 cr (7%), ₹363 cr; FY26 ₹8,034 cr (+1.4%), ₹481 cr (6%), ₹295 cr. TTM: ₹8,329 cr, ₹409 cr (4.9%), ₹252 cr. [Screener]
- FY26 PBT before exceptional items ₹425.6 cr (5.3%), down 12%; a one-time ₹38.8 cr labour-code (wage code) provision was booked. [AR FY26 p.33; Q4 call]
- Long-run context: OPM was 11–12% in FY18–FY20 and has been 6–7% since FY22. Stock price CAGR is −17% over five years. [Screener]
- Gross margin (standalone) 27.6% FY23 → 29.0% FY24 → 30.6% FY25 → 29.9% FY26; the FY26 dip was due to the low-margin AC mix; P4G cost savings added 220 bps over three years. [Q4 call]
- Unit economics are not disclosed. Growth drivers in Q1: industry volume mid-single digits (4–5%), share gain of ~30–35 bps, AC revenue +50%, front-load washer volume +80%, and April/May price rises. [Q1 call]

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (22 May 2026):** no numeric guidance; "FY27 will be challenging" (energy rules, e-waste, war). Long-run ambition: revenue growth at "early double digits" (raised from high single digits said two years ago); margins for the core business in the long run "high single-digit" — explicitly not a medium-term target. Promised large frost-free refrigerators in Q2 FY27. Said cash would go to growth, not buybacks or dividends. [Q4 call]
- **Q1 delivery (6 Aug 2026):** revenue +11–12% (ahead of the early-double-digit ambition); large four-door "Luxurion" refrigerators launched on the call day, shipping from Pune from July — **delivered on time**. Margins fell further (5.1% vs 5.5% in Q4). [Q1 call; Screener]
- **Q1 outlook:** Q2 (Jul–Sep) will be "a difficult quarter financially" because it is the weakest season and costs stay high; refused to guide revenue or say whether FY27 operating margin could match last year's ~6–6.5%. Guidance effectively **cut in tone**; the high-single-digit margin aim was not repeated. [Q1 call]
- Capital-use plan: a "complete blueprint" for capacity, new products, automation and possible acquisitions within 12 months (by roughly Aug-2027); unused cash "could be subject to other options". [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- CWIP rose to ₹203 cr at March 2026 from ₹49 cr; the CFO said ~₹200 cr in CWIP related to premium refrigerators. Total investment in the Pune four-door line: ₹245 cr. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Capex implied by Screener cash flow: about ₹261 cr in FY26 (CFO ₹298 cr less free cash flow ₹37 cr) vs about ₹119 cr in FY25. [Screener]
- Run-rate ("base ongoing") capex is "about 100 plus" crore a year, with more for specific projects; no FY27 budget disclosed ("budget is not a constraint"). [Q1 call, official transcript]
- No big capacity capex needed in the next 6–12 months given 70–75% utilisation. AC in-house manufacturing is under review; the company will build only if economics beat contract manufacturing. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Payback or asset-turn targets: not disclosed. Fixed-asset turns are roughly 4x (FY26 revenue ₹8,034 cr on ₹1,960 cr net block). [Screener]
- Future growth: the four-door range fills a gap above 500 litres; new top-load washer products promised "over the next 12 to 15 months"; target to take front-load and AC shares to double digits in 2–3 years; Elica to grow at "strong double-digit" rates. [Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Effectively debt-free: Screener "borrowings" of ₹359 cr are lease liabilities; CRISIL notes zero debt. [Screener; AR FY26 note 9; CRISIL Feb-26]
- Consolidated cash and bank deposits about ₹2,553 cr at March 2026 (cash equivalents ₹615 cr + other bank balances ₹1,938 cr) — about 22% of market cap. Standalone: ₹538 cr + ₹1,645 cr. The FY26 "investing outflow" of ₹2,029 cr is mostly cash moved from short to longer deposits, not spending. [AR FY26 consolidated & standalone notes; Screener]
- CFO fell to ₹298 cr in FY26 from ₹571 cr; free cash flow ₹37 cr vs ₹452 cr. Debtor days rose to 34 from 24; inventory days 103; payable days 128; cash conversion cycle +9 days vs −3. [Screener]
- Company definition of net working capital was negative in 5 of the last 8 quarters and back to negative at June 2026 after the March AC build-up was collected. [Q1 call]
- Dividend payout 22% in FY26 (yield 0.54%); no buyback — management wants to keep cash for growth. [Screener; Q4 call]

## Moat evidence
- #2 MBO volume share in refrigerators + washers (March 2026); #1 in direct-cool refrigerators; #2 in top-load and semi-automatic washers in Q4 FY26. [Q4 call; IP Q4FY26]
- Proprietary India-built features: "Dynamix" detergent dispenser now in about half of semi-automatic volume and driving triple-digit bps share gains; auto-defrost direct-cool; fastest convertible refrigerator. [Q1 call; IP Q4FY26]
- Negative working capital in the core businesses shows strong trade terms. [Q1 call]
- New 30-year exclusive brand licence (Whirlpool Properties) and technology licence (Whirlpool Corp) signed 16-Oct-2025 secure the brand and product pipeline. Brand royalty ₹70.2 cr and technical fee ₹29.4 cr in FY26 (about 1.2% of sales). [AR FY26 p.33; AR FY26 note 36]
- **Weakness:** no pricing power. Management said it cannot raise prices unless key rivals do; its 2022 unilateral price rise caused "a sharp decline in market share". Margins have halved from 11–12% (FY18–FY20). [Q1 call; Screener]

## Risks and red flags
- **Control change:** on 22-Sep-2026 Whirlpool Corp told the board that Whirlpool Mauritius intends to sell its shares "as part of a control transaction", being explored with more than one counterparty; the stock rose 20% on 23-Sep (₹721 → ₹865). A new owner would trigger an open offer but could also change strategy, royalty terms or the cash plan. [Rumour clarification 24-Sep-26, responding to a Moneycontrol/CNBC-Awaaz report of 23-Sep-26]
- **E-waste provision:** FY26 e-waste expense ₹143.6 cr (FY25 ₹80.6 cr) after CPCB raised minimum recycling prices (Sep-2024). ₹139 cr of this sits as a provision above the contracted rate, pending an industry challenge — a possible write-back if resolved, or a continuing cost if not. [AR FY26 note 35]
- **Energy-label changes:** January 2026 BEE upgrade for refrigerators and ACs is a permanent cost rise; the next is due about January 2029. The war-related cost hit in Q1 was about twice the energy-rule hit. [Q1 call]
- **Costs and currency:** crude $67 → $97 year on year; rupee ~88 → 95–96 per dollar; under 30% of inputs imported, more dollar-linked. [Q1 call]
- **Contingent liabilities:** ₹216.8 cr (tax and legal) — a key audit matter; in addition a ₹1,036 cr transfer-pricing (advertising) exposure is judged "remote" after a Supreme Court win and not disclosed as contingent. Auditor's opinion unqualified. [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 35]
- **Related parties:** sale of services to Whirlpool Corp about ₹237 cr in FY26 (cost-plus, contracted until March 2029); after that it is at the parent's discretion. [AR FY26 note 36; Q1 call]
- IT systems still run on transition service agreements with the parent — a stated transition risk. [AR FY26 MD&A]
- Parent's own weakness: S&P cut Whirlpool Corp to BB/Negative; CRISIL says no material impact on the Indian company, rated **CRISIL AA+/Stable, A1+**. [CRISIL Dec-25, Feb-26, May-26]
- ESOPs at face value (performance-vested) drew minority-shareholder criticism on the Q1 call. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "challenging but momentum" (Q4) to "perfect storm… black swan" (Q1).
- Revenue growth improved (7.4% → 11.4% standalone) and share rose, but margin fell again and a difficult Q2 was flagged.
- New: war-led supply shortages (LPG, oil-based inputs), rupee fall, labour-code costs in Noida/Haryana; 12-month capital blueprint including acquisitions; ESOP scheme.
- Dropped: the long-run high-single-digit margin aim was not repeated.

## Best management quotes
- "In this business, having volumes does not guarantee profits, but not having volumes almost certainly guarantees failure." — N. Eswar, MD [Q1 call]
- "I think there is going to be a difficult quarter financially in JAS." — N. Eswar [Q1 call]
- "To get to a high single-digit margin is probably what is likely" in refrigerators, washers and ACs over a long span. — N. Eswar [Q4 call]

## My read
The franchise is improving on share, products and working capital, but it is a price-taker: margins have halved from pre-2020 levels and every cost shock flows straight to profit. Near term the P&L is at a low (5% margin, weak Q2 ahead); a recovery needs the war costs and rupee to ease, the e-waste provision to reverse, or competitors to raise prices. About ₹2,550 cr of cash (over a fifth of the market cap) gives a floor and optionality. The biggest swing factor now is the parent's proposed control sale — the buyer's plans and the open-offer price will matter more than the next two quarters.
