# Orient Electric (ORIENTELEC) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹162, market cap ₹3,462 cr, 52-week range ₹149–218, Screener P/E 29.0, ROCE 19.9%, ROE 14.2% [Screener standalone, 27 Sep 2026; the company has no subsidiaries, so no consolidated page].

## Business and segments
- CK Birla group fan-and-lighting maker. Two segments: **Electrical Consumer Durables (ECD)** — ceiling, table/pedestal/wall (TPW) and exhaust fans, coolers, water heaters, room heaters, irons and small appliances; **Lighting & Switchgear (L&S)** — consumer LED lamps and luminaires, professional (street, facade) lighting, switches, MCBs and, since FY25, house wires [AR FY26; IP Q1FY27].
- Mix: ECD ~70%, L&S ~30% of FY25 sales [CARE Sep-25]. Q1 FY27: ECD ₹669 cr (+22.7%), EBIT ₹58 cr, 8.7% (6.8% a year ago, 11.4% in Q4); L&S ₹281 cr (+25.4%), EBIT ₹42 cr, 15.0% (17.4% a year ago, 14.1% in Q4) [IP Q1FY27].
- Plants: Faridabad (fans), Kolkata (fans), Noida (lighting & switchgear; being consolidated), Hyderabad (new greenfield, Industry 4.0, TPW and export fans) [AR FY26].
- Fans: BLDC ~25% of domestic ceiling-fan revenue in Q4, "close to about 27% to 30%" in Q1; premium mix ~35% in Q4 → ~36% in Q1; new products ~30% of fan revenue [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]. Consumers buy ~45% of fans between March and June [Q4 call].
- Channels: 4,000+ dealers, 1.25 lakh+ outlets [CARE Sep-25]; direct-to-market (DTM) distribution in 11 states [CARE Sep-25]; ~3,600 retailers added in Q1 [IP Q1FY27]; quick commerce ~10% of digital sales [Q4 call]; exports to 30+ countries, mainly Africa and SAARC [CARE Sep-25; Q1 call].
- Market: consumer-durables market seen growing ~11% a year to ₹3 lakh crore by FY29 (company-cited industry estimate); revised BEE star norms took effect 1 Jan 2026 and the entire ceiling-fan range has been re-rated [AR FY26 MD&A].

## Key numbers (Screener standalone)
- Annual revenue FY24 ₹2,812 cr → FY25 ₹3,094 cr → FY26 ₹3,326 cr; operating profit ₹145 cr (5%) → ₹204 cr (7%) → ₹229 cr (7%); net profit ₹75 cr → ₹83 cr → ₹96 cr [Screener]. EBITDA margin path 5.3% → 6.6% → 6.9% [Q1 call].
- Quarters (revenue / operating profit / margin / net profit): Jun-25 ₹769 cr / ₹46 cr / 6% / ₹18 cr; Sep-25 ₹703 cr / ₹38 cr / 5% / ₹12 cr; Dec-25 ₹906 cr / ₹68 cr / 7% / ₹26 cr; Mar-26 ₹948 cr / ₹77 cr / 8% / ₹40 cr; Jun-26 ₹950 cr / ₹67 cr / 7% / ₹31 cr [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 reported: revenue ₹949.8 cr (+23.5%); gross margin 29.8% (32.6% a year ago, 31.0% in Q4); EBITDA ₹66.6 cr, 7.0% (+102 bps YoY, –115 bps QoQ); exceptional loss ₹4.0 cr (Noida plant write-down); PAT ₹31.5 cr (+79.7%) [IP Q1FY27].
- Cost lines Q1: employees ₹84.5 cr (8.9% of sales vs 9.9%); marketing ~4.5% of sales vs 5.5% (IPL dropped) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Pricing: six fan price rises Dec–Jun totalling ~15–16%; appliances four rises ~10%; consumer lighting ~10%; switchgear "high double-digit"; wires repriced every ~15 days with copper [Q1 call]. Sanchay cost programme: ₹68 cr saved in FY26, ₹10 cr in Q1 [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Standing targets: gross margin back to 32–34%; EBITDA to double digits; first milestone ₹5,000 cr revenue needing ~14–15% CAGR (no date given) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Q4 call (8 May 2026): expected Q1 demand to improve on a hotter, longer summer; April price rise ~6% in fans, lighting and switchgear; working capital had risen to 30+ days from 23 because of inventory build [Q4 call]. On margins: "we are committed to our path to double-digit margins" — no timeline; an analyst reminded management of an earlier "6 to 8 quarters" goal, which was not repeated [Q4 call].
- Q1 delivery: demand did improve (+23.5%) (**delivered**); working capital back to 25 days (**delivered**); EBITDA margin 7.0%, lower than Q4's 8.2% and gross margin fell further to 29.8% (**missed** on margin direction) [IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 wording change: the pace to double-digit margin "will have one factor of these inflationary trends, which could delay it" — timeline **softened**, target kept [Q1 call]. Marketing to stay at 4.4–4.5% of sales [Q1 call].
- New Q1 targets: Europe TPW fan exports — "hopefully, before the next season, we should get some foothold" [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- The big project is done: Hyderabad greenfield plant commissioned in FY25, funded entirely from internal accruals; it held back ROCE while under-used [CARE Sep-25]. Now "seeing traction in the utilization" as TPW moves there; certifications for export markets obtained [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- No large new capex announced; spending is on new-product tooling, maintenance and plant automation to offset minimum-wage rises (Haryana +35%, UP +24%) [Q4 call; Q1 call]. FY26 investing outflow ₹73 cr [Screener]. "Nothing of that sort" on buybacks [Q4 call].
- Noida consolidation: two Noida units being merged; FY26 write-down ₹1.51 cr, Q1 FY27 ₹4.0 cr, both exceptional [AR FY26 note 48; IP Q1FY27].
- Growth engines for FY27–FY29: BLDC and premium fans; consumer luminaires (60% of lighting mix); switches/switchgear (new 6kA MCBs launched in Q2); house wires (>2x growth, North and East first, 45% of large fan dealers also sell wires); exports via Hyderabad; e-commerce/quick commerce [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash ₹133 cr at 30 Jun 2026; working-capital cycle 25 days [IP Q1FY27]. FY26: operating cash flow ₹110 cr, free cash flow ₹77 cr — cash conversion only 64% of operating profit [Screener].
- Supplier finance: trade payables include ₹139 cr of bank-paid "acceptances" and ₹26 cr of TReDS borrowings at 6.1–6.25% [AR FY26 notes 14, 17]. CARE's adjusted gearing including acceptances 0.37x (FY25) [CARE Sep-25].
- Debtor days 68, inventory days 68, payable days 96, cash conversion cycle 40 days in FY26 (31 in FY25) [Screener].
- Dividend yield 0.9% [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Fan brand and export leader: gained 30–40 bps fan share in FY26 per a third-party report [Q4 call]; "outperformed the industry" in Q1 [IP Q1FY27].
- Pricing: led the industry with six fan price rises; "most of the leading brands have not taken price increase in June. We were the only ones" [Q1 call]. Yet gross margin fell 277 bps YoY, so pricing did not fully cover costs [IP Q1FY27].
- Design/innovation: three Red Dot awards; Aero O2 (~₹16,000), Aerosilent (<50 dB, ~₹8,000), battery-backup fan [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]. In-house BLDC PCB design [Q1 call].
- Lighting: B2C lighting has beaten the market for 10–12 quarters; luminaires 60% of mix [Q1 call].
- Weakness: gross margin (~30%) well below its own 32–34% goal and behind larger peers; ROCE fell from 34–35% (FY21–22) to 14–20% [Screener].

## Risks and red flags
- Contingent liabilities ₹108 cr, including GST anti-evasion demands of ₹29.25 cr plus an equal penalty (Maharashtra, MP, AP) alleging goods bought at higher and sold at lower prices, and a ₹2.87 cr Vijayawada demand on the same ground; company has filed writs [AR FY26 note 33].
- Haryana Pollution Control Board demand over Faridabad discharge (50% paid under protest); summons to directors stayed by the High Court [AR FY26 note 33].
- Margin exposure to copper, aluminium, freight, rupee and wages; gross margin fell for three straight quarters [IP Q1FY27].
- Supplier-finance dependence (acceptances) flatters working capital [AR FY26 note 17].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matter revenue recognition (timing of delivery) [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Related parties small: sales to Orient Paper ₹0.13 cr; chairman's relative Avani Birla employed as President–Strategy [AR FY26 note 34; Board's report]. Promoters 38.3%; FII holding fell from 6.9% to 3.3% in a year while mutual funds rose to 31% [Screener; IP Q1FY27].
- Seasonality: weak summers (FY26 H1) hit both revenue and margin.

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: from "unprecedented situation", unseasonal rain and cautious dealers (Q4) to "constructive demand backdrop" and 23.5% growth (Q1).
- Margin: Q4's 8.2% EBITDA was not held; management now ties the double-digit path to inflation.
- New items: Europe export pitch, marketing shift from IPL to digital/airports, labour automation, Noida consolidation charge.
- Working capital normalised (30+ days → 25).

## My read
The business is improving: revenue growth has returned to the 20%s, EBITDA margin is up ~100 bps a year, and the balance sheet holds net cash with the Hyderabad capex behind it. But gross margin has slipped to ~30% and the double-digit EBITDA goal keeps receding, so the price needs sustained mid-teens growth plus at least 8–9% margins to be justified. A second strong quarter with gross margin back above 31% would strengthen the case; a GST demand crystallising or margins stuck near 7% would weaken it.
