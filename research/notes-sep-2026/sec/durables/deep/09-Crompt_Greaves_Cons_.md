## Business and segments
- **Segments (Q1 FY27):** Electrical Consumer Durables (ECD: fans, pumps incl. solar pumps, water heaters, air coolers, small kitchen appliances) revenue ₹1,754 cr, EBIT ₹237 cr (13.5%); Lighting (B2C + B2B) ₹269 cr, EBIT ₹32 cr (12.0%, −70 bps); Butterfly Gandhimathi (kitchen appliances, 75%-owned subsidiary) ₹214 cr, EBITDA ₹15 cr (7.0%), EBIT 4.2% [IP Q1FY27 pp.7–10].
- **FY26 mix:** ECD ~76%, lighting ~13%, Butterfly ~12% of H1 FY26 revenue [CRISIL Jan-26]; lighting revenue ~₹1,083 cr in FY26 [AR FY26 MD's message].
- **Market shares:** fans 26%, pumps 18%, LED 10%, geysers 10%, air coolers 6% [CRISIL Jan-26]; #1 in ceiling fans ("World's No.1 ceiling fan brand"), #1 residential pumps, #1 storage water heaters by volume in trade (from rank 4 in FY23), #3 in lighting [IP Q1FY27 p.3; CMD deck Aug-26].
- **New businesses:** solar rooftops (order book ~₹500 cr, mostly 38,000 PM Surya Ghar homes in Andhra Pradesh; B2C sales started), solar pumps (state tenders in Haryana, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, MP; from ₹20 cr three years ago to ₹200 cr, then "significant growth" in FY26), residential wires ("Crompton Amar", outsourced, 14 towns in TN/Karnataka) [Q1 call; Q4 call]. New super-premium line "Crompton Rion" [Q4 call].
- **Distribution:** 2,800+ distributors, 236,000 retailers; alternate channels 16% of revenue [CRISIL Jan-26; IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- **TAM:** ~₹75–80k cr core, ₹1.6–2.0 lakh cr with solar and wires [IP Q1FY27 p.4].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr, revenue / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 1,998 / 192 / 10% / 124; Sep-25 1,916 / 158 / 8% / 75; Dec-25 1,898 / 195 / 10% / 101; Mar-26 2,283 / 271 / 12% / −531 (Butterfly impairment); Jun-26 2,235 / 224 / 10% / 143 [Screener].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹7,313 cr, op. profit ₹714 cr (10%), PAT ₹442 cr; FY25 ₹7,864 cr / ₹901 cr (11%) / ₹564 cr; FY26 ₹8,096 cr / ₹827 cr (10%) / −₹231 cr; TTM ₹8,332 cr / ₹849 cr [Screener]. FY26 consolidated PBT before exceptional ₹677 cr vs ₹756 cr [AR FY26 Board's report].
- **FY26 exceptional items (standalone) ₹754.9 cr:** Butterfly investment and trademarks impaired ₹716.0 cr, Vadodara plant restructuring ₹20.4 cr, new Labour Code ₹18.5 cr [AR FY26 financial highlights].
- **H1 FY26 was weak:** revenue −3%, ECD −5% on early monsoon, OPM ~9% [CRISIL Jan-26]; EBIT margin recovered from 6.8% in H1 to ~10% in Q4 [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27:** revenue +11.8%, EBITDA +14.2%, EBIT +14.1% (8.0%), PBT +15.2% (8.6%); price rises "high single digit to low double digit" covering ~80% of inflation; ~₹200 cr primary sales lost to supply shortages (fans mainly) because Crompton carried no buffer stock into the war-driven shortage [IP Q1FY27 p.6; Q1 call].
- **Unit trend:** BLDC fans ~44–45% growth, highest-ever quarterly sales; SDA (small kitchen appliances) growth early-20s% in FY26, ~30% in Q4 [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- **Returns:** ROCE 18% FY26 (35–39% in FY19–21, 16% in FY23–24); ROE 10.6% [Screener]. The impairment lowers capital employed, flattering future ROCE [AR FY26 MD's message].

## Guidance history
- **Policy:** no numeric revenue/profit guidance on calls [Q1 call].
- **Q4 call (May 2026):** margins "should go back up" as war turbulence settles; solar portfolio ₹2,000 cr in 3–4 years; wires national rollout "will not be spread over the entire year"; solar rooftop order book to be executed "reasonably quickly" [Q4 call].
- **Q1 delivery:** margin +20 bps (kept); solar rooftop revenue "relatively modest" — recognition on installation pushed to Q2 (slipped); wires still 14 towns in two states (slow vs "not spread over the year"); ~₹200 cr sales loss not flagged in May [Q1 call].
- **Capital Markets Day (20 Aug 2026) — new long-term targets:** FY29: revenue ~13–14% CAGR, exit EBITDA margin 11–12%, new businesses ~14–15% of revenue, smart/connected mix ~15%; FY31: revenue 2x FY26, EBITDA margin 12%+, new businesses ~20%; ~35% of cash paid as dividend and/or buyback; maintenance capex ~₹120 cr a year excluding the greenfield plant; "~15% ambition" revenue CAGR for FY27–31 vs 6% in FY17–22 and 11% in FY23–26 [CMD deck Aug-26 p.~20].
- **Business-unit FY31 targets:** fans share +100 bps, NPD 20% of sales, premium 30%; water heaters +120 bps, air coolers +200 bps; pumps share +250 bps; mixer grinders +400 bps and SDA 3x in 5 years; Butterfly doubled in 5 years; B2C lighting share +250 bps, government-linked B2B exposure −500 bps [CMD deck Aug-26].

## Capex and capacity
- **Greenfield plant:** ~₹350 cr (CRISIL: ₹350–400 cr) over 2–3 years, next-generation fan manufacturing plus a large warehouse; funded by internal accruals [Q1 call; CRISIL Jan-26]. Location/timeline not disclosed.
- **Regular capex:** ~₹120 cr a year [CMD deck]; FY26 capital commitments ₹11.4 cr [AR FY26 note 27]. FY26 cash from investing −₹175 cr [Screener].
- **Capital-light record:** Baddi fan capacity raised 50% for ₹50 lakh; wires and solar installation outsourced (installation partner is paid a share of government receipts, so gross margin ≈ EBITDA margin) [Q1 call].
- **Returns logic:** management says solar and wires have "good" ROCE and EBITDA margins similar to the company average; rooftops gross margin is lower [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Debt-free:** final NCD tranche repaid; company "net cash positive"; Screener borrowings ₹199 cr are leases [AR FY26 Board's report; Screener]. Cash ₹575 cr at Sep-25 plus ₹320 cr unused bank lines; Crisil AA+/Stable/A1+ (Jan 2026) [CRISIL Jan-26]. Butterfly holds ~₹170 cr of cash [Q4 call].
- **Cash flow:** CFO ₹723 cr in FY26 (107% of operating profit), FCF ₹644 cr; ₹750 cr and ₹843 cr in FY25/FY24 [Screener].
- **Working capital:** cash conversion cycle −24 days FY26 (debtor 49, inventory 49, payables 122) [Screener]; the lean-inventory policy caused the Q1 stock-outs [Q1 call]. Solar B2G business does not run on negative working capital [Q1 call].
- **Dividend:** FY26 dividend "identical" to FY25 (FY25 payout 35% of profit) [Q4 call; Screener]; ~35% cash payout (dividend and/or buyback) policy from the CMD.

## Moat evidence
- **Share leadership with evidence:** 26% in fans, 18% in pumps; water heaters from #4 to #1 in trade FY23–26 (+200 bps); resi pumps +180 bps, agri +140 bps; fans +60 bps FY23–26 [CRISIL Jan-26; CMD deck].
- **Pricing power:** led the industry with two ECD hikes after the war started and 7–8% on fans in FY26; in Q1 FY27 passed ~80% of inflation while "we did not see the same kind of pricing actions from others" [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Regulatory tailwind:** BEE fan rating norms raise entry-level costs; management expects to "consolidate the industry at the entry segment" [Q1 call].
- **Scale/cost:** Project Unnati cost savings; ~₹100 cr a year on R&D; 57 new products in Q1 [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27 p.12].
- **Switching costs:** low for consumers; distribution breadth (236k retailers, 1,300+ service points) is the moat [CRISIL Jan-26; CMD deck].

## Risks and red flags
- **Butterfly acquisition value destroyed:** ₹716 cr impairment of investment and trademarks in FY26 (bought 2022); Butterfly EBIT margin only 4.2%; merger into Crompton "remains on the cards" [AR FY26; Q4 call]. Auditor's key audit matters: Butterfly impairment and goodwill impairment testing (goodwill ₹779 cr from the 2015 demerger) [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; audit trail operated throughout the year with no tampering [AR FY26 CARO].
- **Contingent liabilities (standalone, Mar-26):** income-tax ₹143.9 cr (₹113.8 cr), GST/VAT ₹222.6 cr (₹215.6 cr), excise/customs ₹11.3 cr, third-party claims ₹32.8 cr, EPR e-waste ₹83.8 cr (₹24.2 cr — higher CPCB floor prices under legal challenge); guarantees for Butterfly (₹249 cr) ended [AR FY26 note 27].
- **Lean-inventory risk:** ~₹200 cr sales lost in the peak fan season; management won't change the model [Q1 call].
- **Solar concentration:** rooftop book mostly one state-government programme (AP, SC/ST homes); revenue recognised only on installation; monsoon delays [Q1 call].
- **Management churn:** Sachin Phartiyal rejoined as Home Electricals head; new supply-chain head from Whirlpool; roles reshuffled (Rajat to sales) [Q1 call].
- **No promoter:** board/management-run; institutions own ~86% [Screener].
- **Lighting B2B margins** hit by fixed-price contracts in inflation [IP Q1FY27 p.9].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 tone was offence (Rion super-premium line, wires, rooftops); Q1 spent time defending the lean-inventory model after the ~₹200 cr loss [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Price hikes widened from 7–8% on fans (Q4) to high-single/low-double digit across categories (Q1) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New: brand-architecture refresh (launch 17–18 Aug), Capital Markets Day with FY29/FY31 targets, new executives [Q1 call].
- Solar rooftop revenue timing moved to Q2–Q3 FY27.

## My read
Crompton's core franchise is intact — leading shares, pricing leadership, strong cash generation and no debt — and margins are recovering from the weak H1 FY26 (10% in Q1 FY27). The big blemish is Butterfly (₹716 cr written off), and the new growth legs (rooftop solar, wires) are still small, government-linked or early. The FY31 plan (revenue doubling, EBITDA margin 12%+) implies ~15% revenue CAGR vs 11% delivered in FY23–26. At a Screener P/E of ~31 (on earnings before the impairment), the price needs roughly 18% a year earnings growth for a decade — achievable only if revenue compounds ~14–15% and margin rises from ~10% towards 12%. Watch Q2–Q3 solar installations, whether fan share holds after the Q1 stock-outs, and Butterfly margins.
