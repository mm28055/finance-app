# Sheela Foam (SFL) — deep research, Phase A1

Transcript check (official copies from BSE):
- **Q1 FY27 stored copy (call of 5 Aug 2026): matches official** — it is the company's filed text, page footers included.
- **Q4 FY26 stored copy (call of 15 May 2026): materially wrong on one headline figure.** It says Furlenco "clocked a PAT of close to **16 crores**" in FY26; the official text says "close to **INR60 crores**" (and later in the same call, "we have done INR60 crores PAT"). All other figures checked match (₹1,050 cr Q4 revenue, ₹121 cr core EBITDA, 11.5%, ₹3,821 cr FY26, ₹161 cr PAT, U2O +65% volume/+111% value, ₹125–150 cr capex, ₹140–145 cr depreciation, ~43% Furlenco stake, ₹250 cr synergy target).

## Business and segments
- India's largest maker of polyurethane (PU) foam and mattresses: brands Sleepwell and Kurlon (Kurlon Enterprise, bought Oct 2023, merged by NCLT order Sep 2025), plus foam for furniture, auto, footwear and other industries. Subsidiaries: Joyce Foam (Australia, ~40% share of flexible PU foam there), Interplasp (Spain), Staqo (IT/ERP software) and a Dubai trading arm; ~35% economic share (≈43% fully diluted) in Furlenco (House of Kieraya), a furniture rental/sales company. [Q4 call; Ind-Ra Sep-26; AR FY26 note on related parties]
- Q1 FY27 standalone (India) ₹761 cr: mattresses ₹369 cr (8.59 lakh units, +6% volume, +15% value); foam ₹365 cr (11,764 t, +4% volume, +26% value) — technical foam ₹163 cr, comfort foam ₹156 cr, furniture cushioning ₹46 cr; others ₹27 cr. Mattress realisation ≈ ₹4,300/unit vs ≈ ₹3,940 a year earlier. [IP Q1FY27; derived]
- Overseas Q1: Australia ₹120 cr (+31%, EBITDA 12.8%), Spain ₹133 cr (+54%, EBITDA 14.7%); Staqo ₹24 cr (EBITDA 29%). FY26: Australia ₹422 cr (10% margin), Spain ₹391 cr (10.4%), Staqo ₹70 cr, Furlenco ₹370 cr revenue and ~₹60 cr PAT (~₹27 cr of it deferred-tax credit). [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Home comfort (mattresses, pillows, cushions) is ~72% of FY26 revenue. Channels: ~3,500 EBOs, ~11,000 touchpoints, U2O ("unorganised to organised") network of ~9,900 dealers in 5,500+ towns; e-commerce grew 30% in Q1 (brand.com +69%). [Ind-Ra Sep-26; IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Market: India mattress market ~$2.3 bn (FY25) to ~$3.5 bn by 2030 (Research and Markets, cited in AR); ~85% still unorganised; SFL's share of the organised market "close to 20%" per management (Ind-Ra quotes ~30% of branded modern mattresses). [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call; Q1 call; Ind-Ra Sep-26]
- Plants: 12 in India after cutting Kurlon + Sleepwell units from 21; 5 in Australia, 1 in Spain. [Q1 call; Ind-Ra Sep-26]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 821 / op. profit 75 (9%) / PAT 7; Sep-25 875 / 87 (10%) / 10; Dec-25 1,074 / 114 (11%) / 53; Mar-26 1,050 / 117 (11%) / 92; **Jun-26 1,032 / 109 (11%) / 62**. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: consolidated revenue +26%, EBITDA +45% to ₹109 cr (10.6%); standalone revenue +20%, EBITDA +13% to ₹68 cr but margin down to 9.0% and gross margin down to 37.6% from 44.4% (raw-material swings of +40% to –20% within the quarter; more low-margin foam sold). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹2,982 cr → FY25 ₹3,439 cr → FY26 ₹3,821 cr; OPM 10% → 7% → 10%; PAT ₹184 cr → ₹90 cr → ₹161 cr. TTM sales ₹4,031 cr, PAT ₹217 cr. ROCE 8% → 4% → 6%; ROE ~5%. [Screener]
- **Accounting boost to profit**: from 1 Jan 2026 SFL switched depreciation from written-down value to straight line and doubled the assumed life of certain machinery from 20 to 40 years (engineer's assessment). This lifted FY26 PBT by ₹14.2 cr for one quarter alone; depreciation falls from ₹179 cr (FY26) to a guided ₹140–145 cr in FY27 — roughly ₹35–40 cr of extra annual pre-tax profit from the policy change, not from operations. Screener quarterly depreciation dropped from ₹47–50 cr to ₹34–36 cr. [AR FY26 note 3(a)(h); Q4 call; Screener]
- Company "core" EBITDA FY26 ₹414 cr (10.8%) vs Screener ₹395 cr (10%). Management's own ROCE excluding Kurlon intangibles ~18%. [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (May 2026): revenue growth "better than 15%" for FY27; EBITDA already "in an 11% to 12% bracket" and to move up; Q1–Q2 gross-margin impact from raw materials "not more than" 50–100 bp; last ₹40 cr of the ₹250 cr Kurlon synergy from new machines installed "by the mid or end of this quarter"; Furlenco ₹500 cr revenue in FY27 and IPO decision "in the next 3 to 4 months"; India debt (~₹300 cr) repaid in 1–1.5 years; capex ₹125–150 cr. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: revenue +26% (**beat**); EBITDA 10.6% (**below** the 11–12% bracket; standalone gross margin fell ~690 bp, far worse than the 50–100 bp flagged); standalone volumes +6% mattresses / +4% foam vs 12% / 18% in FY26 (**missed**; management wants ~10%); synergy machine still installing — full effect now **Q3** (delayed again, first promised earlier); no Furlenco IPO update. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 call (Aug 2026): growth guide **kept** (>15%); FY27 margin **11–12% kept** ("we never guided for 13%"); 15% EBITDA margin now a target "for the next year" (FY28); new target: Australia + Spain revenue >₹1,000 cr with ~₹120 cr EBITDA; ROCE 20–25% in three years; India debt cleared in "another 1 year". Volatility expected until October–November. [Q1 call]
- Outside view: Ind-Ra expects FY27 revenue ₹4,200–4,300 cr and EBITDA margin of only 10–11%, then 8–9% growth in FY28 — more cautious than management. [Ind-Ra Sep-26]

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex ₹125–150 cr (maintenance in India, Australia, Spain; debottlenecking after 15% volume growth; new COCO stores). Ind-Ra assumes ₹90–100 cr a year over FY27–FY28. CWIP fell to ₹32 cr at Mar-26 from ₹131 cr. [Q4 call; Ind-Ra Sep-26; Screener]
- Retail: 50 company-owned (COCO) stores planned at ~₹50 lakh each (₹27 lakh capex + ₹20–22 lakh working capital) ≈ ₹25 cr; ~42 operating by August. Furlenco shop-in-shops in 40–50 stores, aiming for 100. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Capacity is not the constraint: the Kurlon integration shut 9 of 21 plants; utilisation not disclosed. The heavy capital was the Kurlon purchase (Screener FY24 investing outflow ₹2,258 cr) and ~₹430 cr put into Furlenco — returns on these, not new plants, drive ROCE. [Q1 call; Q4 call; Screener]
- Asset turn: FY26 sales ₹3,821 cr on net block ₹3,198 cr ≈ 1.2x because the block includes ₹1,612 cr of goodwill and brand. [Screener; AR FY26 auditor's report]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹1,462 cr (Mar-25) → ₹908 cr (Mar-26) on Screener; Ind-Ra: gross debt ₹714 cr, adjusted debt incl. leases ₹908 cr, cash ₹170 cr, net debt/EBITDA 1.9x (3.3x in FY25), interest cover 4.1x. Debt ≈ ₹300 cr India + ₹350 cr overseas (self-liquidating). NCDs (₹181 cr left) due to be fully repaid by mid-FY27; FY27 repayments ₹437 cr. [Screener; Ind-Ra Sep-26; Q1 call]
- Operating cash flow ₹406 cr (FY24) → ₹247 cr (FY25) → ₹409 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹146 cr (FY25) → ₹368 cr (FY26). Investments fell ₹873 cr → ₹588 cr as treasury was used to repay debt. [Screener]
- Working capital: debtor days 42, inventory 69, payables 80 (FY26, Screener); Ind-Ra net working capital ~60 days. India inventory ~15–30 days; overseas carries more. [Screener; Ind-Ra Sep-26; Q1 call]
- First-ever dividend: 20% (₹1/share) for FY26, payout ~7%. Ratings: IND AA/Stable on bank loans and NCDs, affirmed 8 Sep 2026. [Q4 call; Screener; Ind-Ra Sep-26]

## Moat evidence
- Scale and brand: two of India's best-known mattress brands; ~20% of the organised market; ~40% of Australian flexible foam. [Q1 call; Ind-Ra Sep-26]
- Supply reliability as a moat: kept supplying foam through the Mar–Apr 2026 TDI/polyol shortage (GNFC shut; new Korean/Japanese/Chinese suppliers added) and won customers from smaller players who stopped. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Pricing: passed through the 25–35% raw-material rise in B2C and industrial foam; B2B foam slower. [Q4 call]
- U2O: 5–6 inch mattresses and price increases lifted U2O value +81% in Q1 on +19% volume — evidence of pricing headroom at the bottom end. [Q1 call]
- Weakness: Kurlon integration took two years longer and cost margins (FY25 OPM 7%); returns on the acquisition are still below cost of capital on reported numbers (ROCE 6%). [Screener; Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Earnings quality**: the depreciation change (WDV → SLM, machinery life 20 → 40 years) flatters FY27 profit by roughly ₹35–40 cr pre-tax versus the old policy; Furlenco's FY26 PAT included ~₹27 cr of deferred-tax credit; FY26 profit also had treasury mark-to-market swings. Compare EBITDA, not PAT, across years. [AR FY26 note 3(a)(h); Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Goodwill and brands ₹1,612 cr** (Kurlon, Joyce, Spain) — a key audit matter; Kurlon goodwill was raised by ₹30 cr to ₹406.8 cr after re-measuring the purchase consideration. Other key audit matter: revenue net of discounts/rebates (secondary-sale schemes). Opinion unmodified; no emphasis of matter found. [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report; AR FY26 note 5B]
- **Raw materials**: TDI and polyol are 65–70% of COGS; 10–15% imported. Gross margin swings are large (standalone 44.4% → 37.6% in a year). [Q1 call; Ind-Ra Sep-26]
- **Contingent liabilities (standalone)** ~₹56 cr: GST ₹34.8 cr, income tax ₹13.8 cr, sales tax ₹4.0 cr, excise ₹2.9 cr, entry tax ₹0.6 cr. [AR FY26 note 53]
- **Related parties**: buys ~₹20 cr a year of IT services from its own subsidiary Staqo; continuing investment in Furlenco, where SFL is the largest holder but not majority owner; corporate guarantees for Joyce and the Spanish subsidiary. Promoters 65.4% (down from 65.7% in Mar-26). [AR FY26 related-party note; Screener]
- Management churn: CEO (N. Mazumdar) left Mar-25; roles reshuffled Nov-25 (Rahul Gautam MD, Tushaar Gautam Vice Chairman, Rakesh Chahar Deputy MD); CHRO resigned 31 Aug 2026 (MD covering). Management says it is "open to" selling the overseas businesses. [AR FY26; BSE filing Sep-26; Q4 call]
- Promised timelines keep slipping: synergy machine (1.5 quarters late, now Q3), Furlenco IPO decision (no update). [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- May: celebratory (record EBITDA, first dividend, 11–12% "already"), raw-material impact said to be small. August: Q1 gross margin fell sharply, standalone volume slowed to 4–6%, and the 15% margin goal was pushed to FY28. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Offsetting positives: overseas margins doubled (partly cheap older inventory, which management says will fade in Q2), U2O and e-commerce still growing fast, consolidated EBITDA +45%.
- New: sofa beds under Sleepwell/Kurlon using Furlenco's design and manufacturing; COCO store rollout; new overseas target (>₹1,000 cr revenue, ~₹120 cr EBITDA).

## My read
The business is recovering from a hard Kurlon integration: revenue is growing 20%+, EBITDA has nearly doubled from FY25's trough, and debt is falling fast. But reported returns are still poor (ROCE ~6%), India volume growth slowed to mid-single digits in Q1, and part of the FY27 profit rise will come from the depreciation-policy change rather than the business. The price needs EBITDA margins moving from ~10.5% toward 12–13% by FY28 with mid-teens revenue growth; management's 15% target looks optimistic next to Ind-Ra's 10–11%. The view improves if festive-quarter volumes return to ~10% and gross margin recovers once TDI/polyol settle; it worsens if margins stay near 10% or goodwill on Kurlon comes under pressure.
