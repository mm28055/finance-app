# Rushil Decor (RUSHIL) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Figures from Screener consolidated page (FY25–FY26 only; a subsidiary was added recently) and Screener standalone page for longer history. Share price ₹16.1, market cap ₹472 cr, 52-week range ₹12.5–30.0; price is ~0.73x book value of ₹22.2 [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Maker of MDF boards and decorative laminates ("VIR" brand), plus small PVC/WPC boards [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26].
- Capacity: MDF 3.3 lakh CBM a year (Chikmagalur, Karnataka 90,000 CBM; Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh 2.4 lakh CBM, operating since 2021); laminates 3.49 mn sheets a year at three units around Mansa, Gujarat; Jumbo (large-format, thick) laminates 2.8 mn sheets a year (1 mm basis), both phases commissioned in FY26 at ~₹90 cr [AR FY26; Q1 call].
- Revenue mix FY26: MDF ~75%, laminates ~20%, PVC ~5%; MDF revenue ₹609 cr, laminates ₹211 cr [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 segments [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]:
  - MDF ₹145.6 cr (+17.2%), EBITDA ₹12.3 cr (8.4%), utilisation 66%; India MDF ₹144.7 cr (+32.9%) on volume 52,162 CBM (+16.1%) and realisation ₹27,736/CBM (+14.4%); MDF exports collapsed to 255 CBM (7,150 CBM a year earlier).
  - Laminates ₹73.6 cr (+65.3%), EBITDA ₹5.2 cr (7.0%), utilisation 51% incl. Jumbo; 8.8 lakh sheets (+27.9%) at ₹836/sheet (+29.3%); exports ₹48.5 cr (+67.9%).
  - Jumbo within laminates: ₹11 cr, 1.13 lakh sheets, EBITDA ₹2.3 cr (20.6%), utilisation 29% (on product-mix basis).
  - PVC ₹9.6 cr (+13.1%), EBITDA ₹0.8 cr.
- MDF channel: 35–40% of domestic volume to OEM furniture makers, rest via dealers; normal MDF export run-rate 4,000–5,000 CBM/month, mostly Middle East [Q1 call; Infomerics Jan-26]. Exports were 30–35% of total revenue before the war [Infomerics Jan-26].
- Value-added MDF (pre-lam, HDFWR etc.): 42% of FY26 volume and 54% of value, target 50%/60% in FY27 [Q4 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 179, Sep-25 236, Dec-25 217, Mar-26 231, Jun-26 229; operating margin -1.2%, 9.6%, 10.7%, 11.4%, 7.95%; net profit -14.1, 5.1, 5.2, 10.1, 2.0 [Screener consolidated].
- Annual (standalone): revenue FY22 ₹624 cr → FY23 838 → FY24 844 → FY25 891 → FY26 856; operating margin 12% → 18% → 14% → 12% → 8%; net profit ₹23 cr → 78 → 43 → 48 → 8 [Screener standalone]. Consolidated FY26: revenue ₹862 cr, EBITDA ₹80 cr (9.3%), PAT ₹6.4 cr [Q4 call; Screener].
- FY26 was hurt by a fire at the AP MDF plant in April 2025 (production lost in Q1 FY26), resin inflation and freight disruption; insurance claims partly offset [Q4 call; Infomerics Jan-26].
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹229 cr (+27.8%), gross margin 39% (48% in Q4), EBITDA ₹18.2 cr (7.9%), PBT ₹2.5 cr, PAT ₹2.0 cr [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]. The drop from Q4 came from 35–40% higher resin prices, a one-week April shutdown and the loss of MDF exports.
- Unit economics: MDF EBITDA ~₹2,300/CBM in Q1 (₹12.3 cr / ~52,400 CBM) vs ~₹3,700/CBM in Q4 (14% margin on ~₹26,000) (my calculation from IP figures).
- Price rises from 1 April 2026: MDF +15%, laminates +10%, "just to mitigate" chemical cost (resin +40% ≈ 10–11% of selling price) [Q4 call]. No further rise; "the market is not going to accept any further price hike" [Q1 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (3 June 2026): Q1 utilisation "in the range of Q4" (MDF 83%); FY27 MDF utilisation 85%+ ("targeting 90% across the year"); MDF margin 10–12% (from ~8% "current"); Jumbo utilisation 60–65% in FY27, 85% in FY28; laminate margin 10–12%; debt down ₹50 cr+ in FY27, no new debt [Q4 call].
- Q1 delivery (10 Aug 2026): MDF utilisation 66% — **missed** (blamed on the April shutdown, chemical prices and export freight); MDF margin 8.4% — **missed** (now "1 or 2 more quarters"); Jumbo 29% utilisation and the FY27 aim **cut** to 50–60% (≈₹75 cr revenue), 70–75% next year, peak ~₹140 cr at 90%; debt cut ₹18 cr in the quarter — **on track** [Q1 call].
- Earlier: a Jumbo question in June implied ~₹900 cr potential (28 lakh sheets × ₹4,000); management rejected that math and in August capped Jumbo at ~₹140 cr [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Verdict: operating targets cut or deferred; debt target kept.

## Capex and capacity
- Capex cycle finished: Jumbo laminates ₹90 cr (asset turn ~1.4–1.5x at full use, i.e. ~₹140 cr revenue) [Q1 call]. Capital commitments fell from ₹48 cr to ₹1.5 cr in FY26 [AR FY26 note 36].
- FY27 capex only ₹5–10 cr; no new debt for 1–2 years [Q1 call].
- Payback math: MDF at 85% utilisation could give ₹750–800 cr revenue (vs ₹609 cr FY26), laminates ₹200–250 cr plus Jumbo ₹140 cr → ~₹1,100 cr company revenue in 2–3 years if all plants run near 85% (analyst's arithmetic, broadly accepted by management) [Q1 call].
- Industry supply: management says MDF demand grows 15–20% a year and can absorb ~650–800 CBM/day of new capacity a year, but three new plants arrive in FY27, so oversupply continues [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹260 cr at March 2026 (₹272 cr a year earlier); net debt ₹254 cr, net debt/equity 0.39 [Screener; Q4 call]. Total debt ~₹260 cr incl. working capital at Q1; ₹55 cr scheduled repayment a year; ₹18 cr repaid in Q1 FY27; debt cleared "approximately Q2 FY29", debt-free in FY29 [Q1 call].
- Euro exposure: forex moved net debt by ~₹9 cr in FY26 [Q4 call].
- Operating cash flow ₹89 cr, free cash flow ₹22 cr in FY26; FY25 FCF ₹2 cr [Screener].
- Working capital is heavy: inventory ~204 days, receivables 83 days, payables 207 days (Screener basis); cash cycle 79 days [Screener].
- Dividend yield 0.31%; payout 22% of a small FY26 profit [Screener].
- Rating: Infomerics IVR A-/Stable, reaffirmed 2 Jan 2026 (downgrade triggers: interest cover below 2x, gearing above 1.5x) [Infomerics Jan-26]. Q1 FY27 interest cover (EBITDA ₹18.2 cr / interest ₹7.8 cr) was ~2.3x — close to the trigger (my calculation from Screener).

## Moat evidence
- Scale: one of India's larger MDF makers (3.3 lakh CBM), with a southern plant base near timber and a port at Vizag [AR FY26; Infomerics Jan-26].
- BIS: all Indian makers now hold BIS licences; only 8–10 foreign plants (Vietnam, Thailand) have one, and imports are not substantial [Q1 call].
- Laminates: long export franchise (59 countries), Jumbo gives entry to façade/countertop niches with 2–3-year contracts [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- Weakness: management says realisations are reported ex-freight (freight ~9%), but analysts note Rushil's MDF realisation has trailed listed peers by 12–16% for years and raw material cost is 4–6% higher (resin bought from the north) [Q1 call]. No pricing power: "we did not take any price hike after the 15% increase" [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Oversupply**: three new MDF plants in FY27; Greenpanel says Century, Action Tesa and smaller players are discounting and new Action Tesa/Greenply lines are coming [Q4 call; Greenpanel Q1 FY27 call].
- **Export dependence**: MDF exports went from ~7,000 CBM/quarter to 255 CBM; July recovered only to ~3,200 CBM [Q1 call].
- Resin: +35–40% since March 2026, only "very marginal" relief in July [Q1 call].
- Margins close to the rating agency's interest-cover trigger (~2.3x vs 2x) [Infomerics Jan-26; Screener].
- Auditor: clean opinion; key audit matter is revenue recognition (discounts, cut-off) [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Contingent liabilities small: bank guarantees ₹8.8 cr, LCs ₹0.5 cr; customs dispute closed [AR FY26 note 36].
- Related parties: promoter-family partnership Rushil International is a promoter shareholder with small transactions; one loan is secured against key-man insurance policies of the directors [AR FY26 notes].
- History of disruption: fire at the AP plant (April 2025) and repeated guidance misses.
- Institutional holding negligible (FII 1.1%, DII 0.06%); shareholder count doubled since FY24 as the price fell [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- June: confident — "Q1 utilisation in the range of Q4", MDF 85–90% utilisation for FY27, margins 10–12%, 15% price rise holding.
- August: defensive — utilisation 66%, margin 7.9%, exports nearly nil, Jumbo target lowered, "market is not going to accept any further price hike"; the only upbeat items were laminate growth (+65%) and debt repayment.

## My read
Rushil is a leveraged, sub-scale MDF maker in an oversupplied market, and FY27 has started badly: margins fell to 8% as resin costs rose 35–40% and Gulf exports vanished, and management has already cut its utilisation and margin targets. The good parts are real but small — laminates are growing fast and the ₹90 cr Jumbo line earns ~20% margins at low use — and debt is falling ₹50 cr+ a year with no new capex. At ₹16 the stock trades below book and prices in little; it needs MDF margin back above 10% and Jumbo near ₹75 cr this year to earn its cost of capital. The view improves if MDF exports resume and utilisation returns above 80%; it worsens if interest cover slips below 2x or new rival capacity forces price cuts.
