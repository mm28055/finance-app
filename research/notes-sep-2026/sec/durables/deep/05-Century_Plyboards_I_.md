# Century Plyboards (India) — deep research (Phase A1, written 27 Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Wood panels maker with five segments. FY26 (consolidated, company segment data): **Plywood** (incl. decorative veneer) revenue ₹2,895 cr (+16.5%), EBITDA excl. forex ₹444 cr (15.2%); **MDF** ₹1,284 cr (+25.6%), ₹163 cr (12.7%); **Laminates** ₹743 cr (+13.9%), ₹63 cr (8.5%); **Particle board** ₹196 cr (+37.5%), ₹2.3 cr (1.2%); **Logistics** (container freight station + Kolkata port terminal) ₹158 cr, ₹26 cr. [IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27: plywood volume 1,36,995 CBM (+28.8%), revenue ₹855 cr (+32.5%), EBITDA excl. forex ₹145 cr (**16.9%**); MDF volume 83,078 CBM (+23%), revenue ₹334 cr (+28.9%), EBITDA excl. forex ₹23.5 cr (**7.0%**, vs 14.3% a year ago and 11.3% in Q4); laminates ₹199 cr (+14.7%), 10.2%; particle board ₹86 cr (+159%), 3.7%; logistics ₹54 cr (+66%), 20.7%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Plywood market share ~9.5–10% of the whole industry (unorganised included), target 15% in five years; ICRA puts share of the organised segment at 30–32%. Industry growing 5–7%. [Q1 call; ICRA Jun-26]
- Capacities: plywood 4,06,000 CBM/yr (Mar-26), running ~99–100% in Q4; MDF ~5.3–5.4 lakh CBM/yr going to ~6 lakh after the Badvel (AP) expansion from 700 to 950 CBM/day in Q1; particle board 2,40,000 CBM (new Chennai line, ~₹550 cr); laminates 87.7 lakh sheets at 84% utilisation plus a compact-laminate unit at 47%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Distribution: 4,100+ dealers and 19,000 retailers. Plywood realisation ~₹59,179/CBM in Q1 (+6.3% YoY) after a 7% April price rise. [ICRA Jun-26; IP Q1FY27]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (25 May 2026): **no FY27 numbers** ("situation is so fluid"); MDF "high-teens EBITDA business in steady state"; 15% MDF price rise "barely covers" chemical costs, "early days to see how much sticks"; plywood capacity +30% in FY27 (20% brownfield + Hoshiarpur in October); long-term debt within 1x EBITDA. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivered: revenue +33.5% (well ahead of ICRA's 13–15% FY27 forecast); plywood margin 16.9% (above FY26's 15.2%); MDF margin **fell to 7.0%** after a large part of the 15% price rise was rolled back and the plant was shut for expansion — **missed**. Particle board 3.7% (still early). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 call (4 Aug 2026): still no numeric guidance; MDF "head towards 15% plus"; particle board ~15% "towards next year"; 20% ROCE hurdle for MDF and particle board; plywood 12–15% a year to reach 15% share. [Q1 call]
- Long-term: ₹12,000 cr revenue by FY31 (current assets support ₹7,500–8,000 cr; ~₹2,500 cr more capex needed at 1.5–2x asset turns). [Q1 call]
- Timelines moved: UP plywood plant from "capex start Feb–Mar 2027, ready Q1 FY29" (Q4) to "April 2028 if land arrives by year-end" (Q1) — same date, but land still not in hand; UP MDF "wait and watch"; Odisha still at MoU stage. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Big cycle done: fixed assets ₹1,069 cr (FY23) → ₹2,804 cr (FY26); CWIP fell from ₹730 cr to ₹188 cr as MDF (Badvel), particle board (Chennai) and laminate presses were capitalised. [Screener]
- FY27: Hoshiarpur greenfield plywood — 60,000 CBM/yr per the Q1 call and ICRA (the Q4 call said 48,000 CBM) — to start in Q3 FY27; Chennai plywood 8,000 → 10,000 → 12,500 CBM/month by Q3; third laminate press (~₹25 cr) commissioned end-July for exports. No large MDF/PB capex planned. [Q1 call; Q4 call; ICRA Jun-26]
- Capital commitments ₹130 cr at Mar-26 (₹88 cr a year earlier). [AR FY26 note 34]
- Next leg: UP plywood (~₹200 cr indicated) then possibly UP MDF and Odisha; plywood outsourcing (Sainik MR, ~8% of plywood revenue) to be brought in-house. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Debt: company total debt ₹1,531 cr (Mar-26) → ₹1,644 cr (Jun-26), of which short-term bank debt ₹812 cr; ICRA/Screener gross borrowings ₹1,765 cr at Mar-26 (incl. buyers' credit). Net worth ~₹2,630 cr. Debt/EBITDA ~2.5x; ICRA expects 2.2–2.3x by Mar-27 and 1.6–1.7x by Mar-28; [ICRA]AA (Stable)/A1+. [IP Q1FY27; ICRA Jun-26; Q4 call]
- Cash flow: operating cash −₹3 cr in FY25, ₹457 cr in FY26; free cash flow −₹593 cr (FY24), −₹666 cr (FY25), +₹47 cr (FY26). [Screener]
- Working capital: inventory days 143, debtor days 42 (FY26); company cash-conversion cycle 81 days (FY26) → 69 days (Q1); management says 33% growth needs more working-capital debt. [Screener; IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Dividend payout only ~8% (FY26) — cash is going to capex and debt. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Pricing power in plywood: passed a 7% rise in April and still grew volume 29%; plywood margin 16.9% — management calls it "highest by any player in the industry". [Q1 call]
- Brand trust: new "Total Cover" warranty reimburses full furniture cost (plus labour and transport) for 10 years on Club Prime and Architect ply; claim ratio 0.06%; ads with Aamir Khan and Rahul Dravid; brand/marketing spend 4–4.5% of sales. [Q1 call]
- Weak spots: MDF and particle board face "lumpy capacity additions" and competition (ICRA); MDF price rises were largely rolled back within a quarter. [ICRA Jun-26; Q1 call]
- Regulatory help: Quality Control Orders curb cheap panel imports. [stored profile; not verified in documents read]

## Risks and red flags
- MDF profitability: segment EBITDA fell 36% YoY in Q1 despite 23% volume growth; the "high-teens" steady-state claim is unproven. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Leverage and forex: ~₹600 cr foreign-currency loans create mark-to-market swings (₹57 cr loss in FY26); total debt still rising with working capital. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Auditor: unmodified; key audit matter is revenue cut-off. CARO remarks in subsidiary reports — about ten small group companies (e.g., Ara Suppliers, Adonis Vyaper, Century Infotech, Century MDF) flagged for cash losses (clause 3(xvii)); one subsidiary also qualified on bank stock statements, disputed dues and an auditor resignation. Holding company's own CARO report clean. [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report, para (xxi)]
- Contingent liabilities rose: income-tax claims ₹32 cr (₹0.2 cr a year earlier), excise/service tax ₹10.5 cr. [AR FY26 note 34]
- Related parties minor: small purchases from Star Cement and Century Led (under ₹1 cr), donations to Century Charitable Trust. Promoter family holds multiple board seats; MD Sanjay Agarwal and ED Nikita Bansal re-appointed for five years (AGM 16 Sep 2026). [AR FY26 note 39; BSE filing 16 Sep 2026]
- Non-core diversion: Century Ports (₹260–270 cr spent over three years) — management open to a strategic partner. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 cautious on guidance but bullish on MDF ("calm before the storm"); Q1 bullish on plywood and brand, defensive on MDF ("rolled back a large part of the price increase").
- Numbers: revenue growth 24.5% (Q4) → 33.5% (Q1); MDF margin 11.3% → 7.0%; plywood margin 16.1% → 16.9%; debt ₹1,531 → ₹1,644 cr.
- New: Total Cover warranty; Century Ports now EBITDA and cash positive (9,000+ containers in July); particle board margin target ~15% "towards next year".

## My read
The plywood franchise is excellent and gaining share at 16–17% margins, and revenue is growing fast as the ₹1,700 cr of new assets fill up. But the newer MDF and particle-board businesses earn low single-digit to low-teens margins, so group ROCE is only ~11–14% on a debt-funded balance sheet and profit is still below FY23. The price needs MDF back to mid-teens margins and particle board to ~15% by FY28 while debt stops rising; watch Q2–Q3 MDF margins, the Hoshiarpur start-up and net debt. If MDF stays near 7–10%, the stock is expensive for the returns it earns.
