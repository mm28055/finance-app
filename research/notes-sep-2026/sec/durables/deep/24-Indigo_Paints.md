# Indigo Paints (NSE: INDIGOPNTS) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Figures are consolidated (Indigo + 51%-owned Apple Chemie) from Screener unless tagged "standalone".

## Business and segments
- Decorative paints maker (Pune HQ; six plants including Kochi and Jodhpur) selling emulsions, enamels and wood coatings, primers/distempers, putty and cement paint through ~19,382 active dealers, 12,395 tinting machines and 55 depots in 28 states [IP Q1FY27].
- "Differentiated" category-creator products (floor coat, metallic and tile-coat emulsions, PU enamels etc.) are ~29–30% of revenue, up from ~25–26% at the 2021 IPO; management does not expect them to reach 35% [Q1 call].
- Market share only ~2.5% of Indian paints (MD's estimate); the market leader holds ~50% and more in premium emulsions [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 category growth (value / volume): primers & distempers +29.7% / +18.3%; putty & cement paint +21.2% / +13.7%; enamels & wood coatings +17.6% / ~+10%; emulsions +17.1% / ~+12.3% [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]. Category revenue shares are not disclosed.
- Subsidiary Apple Chemie (construction chemicals/waterproofing, sells to infra contractors such as L&T, Afcons): FY26 revenue ₹75.1 cr (+17.8%), Q1 FY27 ₹19.7 cr (+40%), gross margin normally ~40%; Indigo is raising its stake from 51% to 62% [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Capacity: water-based paints 1,72,000 KL/yr rising to 2,62,000 KL/yr with the new 90,000 KL Jodhpur plant; putty 2,76,000 t/yr; new 12,000 KL solvent plant at Jodhpur running since early 2026 [AR FY26 corporate overview; IP Q1FY27].

## Key numbers (Screener, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly revenue / operating profit (OPM) / net profit: Jun-25 309 / 44 (14%) / 26; Sep-25 312 / 47 (15%) / 25; Dec-25 359 / 68 (19%) / 37; Mar-26 425 / 96 (22%) / 59; Jun-26 370 / 62 (17%) / 42 [Screener].
- Annual: FY24 1,306 / 238 (18%) / 149; FY25 1,341 / 233 (17%) / 142; FY26 1,405 / 255 (18%) / 148; TTM 1,466 / 272 / 163 [Screener]. Revenue grew only 2.7% (FY25) and 4.8% (FY26) — two weak years.
- Q1 FY27 standalone: revenue ₹350.0 cr (+18.7%), gross margin 45.3% (slightly below a year ago), EBITDA ₹61.9 cr (+42%, margin 17.7% vs 14.8%), PAT ₹42.4 cr (+60.7%); A&P 4.3% of revenue vs 6.8% (IPL advertising skipped) [IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 profit was helped by treasury mark-to-market gains: other income ₹10 cr vs ₹6 cr a year ago (Q4 FY26 was ~₹0 after MTM losses) [Screener; Q1 call].
- FY26 standalone: revenue ₹1,330 cr (+4.1%), gross margin 46.9%, EBITDA ₹246.7 cr (18.5%); PAT ex-exceptional ₹149.8 cr [Q4 call].
- ROCE 23% (FY24), 19% (FY25), 19% (FY26); ROE ~14% [Screener].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (25 May 2026): new strategy — chase share, accept 2–2.5 pp lower gross margin; EBITDA margin "largely… unchanged"; asked if ~24% FY27 growth (2x an industry growing ~12% on price hikes) is achievable, MD said "Yes and yes"; Jodhpur water-based plant trial production "next month, that is in June 2026"; "no further major capex until FY '29"; Apple Chemie 30%+ growth; FY26 dividend ₹5/share (+43%) [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 call (14 Aug 2026): delivered 18.7% standalone / 19.7% consolidated — ahead of peers but short of a 24% pace; margin rose instead of falling because trade discounts were suspended industry-wide for the first ~45 days; MD: "we were not aggressive enough" and Q2 spending will rise; growth target softened to "10 percentage points higher than industry" if industry grows 15%, "2x" only if industry grows ~5%; EBITDA margin could move ±1 pp; Jodhpur trial production "second half of August"; no significant capex for ~3 years; two-pack PU wood-coatings range to launch Sep–Oct 2026 (company estimates a ₹9,000 cr segment) [Q1 call, official; IP Q1FY27].
- Scorecard: growth guidance **softened**; margin guidance (down) not yet visible; plant **missed** again (MD: "you are actually being kind to us" — it was due before June, delayed by a slow civil contractor). No exchange filing of the plant's commercial start appears in Screener's announcement list by 27 Sep 2026.

## Capex and capacity
- Jodhpur water-based plant (90,000 KL/yr, highly automated) replaces a 20-year-old manual legacy plant on the same site; opex should not rise much because staff move across [Q1 call]. Equipment arrived about a year before commissioning [Q1 call].
- CWIP ₹220 cr at Mar-26 (₹136 cr at Mar-25), almost all Jodhpur; fixed assets ₹590 cr [Screener]. The project cost is not separately disclosed in what I read.
- Cash capex ≈ investing outflow; investing cash flow −₹196 cr in FY26 (includes treasury investments) [Screener].
- After Jodhpur, management says no major capex until FY29 — so free cash flow should step up [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Payback: undisclosed. Incremental water-based capacity is +52% (1,72,000 → 2,62,000 KL/yr), far ahead of demand; the benefit is freight and lead time for North, East and Central India rather than volume [AR FY26; Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Effectively debt-free: borrowings ₹21 cr (mostly leases) vs investments ₹300 cr at Mar-26 [Screener]. Parent guarantees ₹18 cr of Apple Chemie's bank facilities [AR FY26 note 31].
- CFO ₹151 cr (FY24), ₹206 cr (FY25), ₹223 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹47 cr → ₹70 cr → ₹88 cr while Jodhpur was built [Screener].
- Working capital loosening: debtor days 62 → 66 → 74 (FY24–FY26); inventory days 91 → 77 → 87; cash-conversion cycle 31 → 55 → 55 days [Screener]. Aggressive trade schemes could push receivables higher.
- Dividend: ₹5/share for FY26 (₹3.5 in each of the prior two years), payout ~16% [Q4 call; Screener].
- No live credit rating of note (last CARE action Apr 2021 per Screener's list).

## Moat evidence
- Gross margin leadership: 45–49% vs listed-peer average ~41–43% for 13 straight quarters (company chart) [IP Q1FY27]. Reasons given: >90% of putty made in-house near raw materials, few plants close to inputs, 30% differentiated products with pricing power, and discipline on discounts [Q4 call].
- Pricing: announced its own price increases around 24–25 Mar 2026, before the market leader (8 Apr) [Q4 call].
- Distribution depth: tinting machines up ~1,100 in a year to 12,395 (committed counter space) vs dealers +~800 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Near-monopoly in some niches: Screener's key points cite ~80–90% share in some differentiated products (company-sourced) [Screener].
- Painter/contractor loyalty programmes (health cover for 30,000+ painter families) [Q1 call].
- Limits: the market leader wins when prices rise sharply because dealers stock it first; premium emulsions share is low [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Tax disputes**: contingent liabilities ₹29.2 cr (₹23.3 cr a year earlier), of which GST ₹27.4 cr; includes a ₹19.26 cr GST demand (with interest and penalty) on IPO-related expenses, under appeal; income-tax matters ₹1.6 cr; Kerala building tax ₹0.2 cr [AR FY26 note 31; AR FY26 BRSR; CARO annexure].
- **Auditor (Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP)**: unmodified opinion; key audit matter = revenue net of discounts/rebates (judgemental accruals — relevant now that trade spending is being raised); audit-trail exception — edit log not kept for changes by certain privileged users or for direct database changes [AR FY26 Auditor's report p.138–141].
- Related parties: MD Hemant Jalan pay ₹3.6 cr; CSR of ₹2.14 cr paid to the promoter-controlled Payal Jalan Charitable Trust (₹1.71 cr in FY25); small dealings with Halogen Chemicals (KMP-controlled associate) and Apple Chemie [AR FY26 note 28].
- Ownership shift: FIIs cut from 12.4% (Mar-25) to 5.7% (Jun-26) while DIIs rose from 16.7% to 25.3%; promoters steady at 53.9% [Screener].
- Disclosure timing: the Q1 call (14 Aug) transcript was filed only on 3 Sep 2026 [BSE filing].
- Strategy risk: buying share with trade discounts and influencer spend lowers net revenue per litre; if the industry leader and Birla Opus respond, Indigo's margin lead could shrink without the growth gap widening [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Plant execution: repeated Jodhpur slippage; automation benefits not yet visible [Q1 call].
- Earnings quality: treasury MTM swings (Q4 FY26 treasury income fell to ₹0.19 cr from ₹5.6 cr; Q1 FY27 had a gain) move PAT by several percent a quarter [Q4 call; Screener].
- Q2 is seasonally the weakest margin quarter, and spending is being stepped up [Q1 call].

## What changed between the two calls
- May: "Yes and yes" to ~24% growth with 2 pp gross-margin sacrifice. August: growth came in at ~19% with margins up; target reframed as a gap to industry.
- New CBO (Aishwarya Pratap Singh, ex-ITC) now driving dealer micro-segmentation and category-specific spend.
- New launches: two-pack PU wood coatings (Sep–Oct).
- Jodhpur plant slipped from June to late August; IPL advertising skipped (supply fears), money to be spent later.
- Apple Chemie margins squeezed by high-cost inventory; recovery expected by Q3; stake to rise to 62%.

## My read
The business is improving after two flat years: Q1 revenue +19%, EBITDA +40%, and volumes grew double-digit in every category, while the Jodhpur capex is almost finished and free cash flow should rise. But the stated strategy is to spend margin to buy share, so the Q1 margin jump is unlikely to repeat, and the 24% growth ambition has already been softened. At ~₹5,390 cr market value on ₹163 cr TTM profit, the price needs roughly mid-teens earnings growth for years — achievable only if growth stays well above the industry without a big margin give-back. I would turn more positive if Q2–Q3 show ~20% growth with gross margin above 45% and receivable days stable; negative if growth fades toward the industry while spending stays high.
