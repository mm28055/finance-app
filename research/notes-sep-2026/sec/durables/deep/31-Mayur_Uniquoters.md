# Mayur Uniquoters (MAYURUNIQ) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹730, market cap ₹3,170 cr, 52-week range ₹471–907, Screener P/E 15.3, ROCE 24.5%, ROE 18.0% [Screener consolidated, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- India's largest organised maker of PVC-coated synthetic leather ("artificial leather"): 48.6 million linear metres a year of PVC capacity plus 5.0 million of PU (polyurethane) capacity at Gwalior; 400+ variants for car seats and trims, footwear, furnishing and fashion; own knitted-fabric base cloth (backward integration) [AR FY26 MD&A; CARE Nov-25].
- Customers: Ford, Chrysler/Stellantis (USA), BMW and Mercedes (South Africa), Maruti, Hyundai, Mahindra, Toyota, Kia, MG, two-wheeler makers; footwear brands Bata, Relaxo, Paragon, VKC [AR FY26 MD&A]. OEM approvals take 2–3 years and suppliers are rarely switched mid-model [CARE Nov-25].
- One reported segment [AR FY26]. Q1 FY27 standalone sales mix (₹247.0 cr): export OEM ₹73.6 cr, export general ₹30.2 cr (exports ₹103.8 cr, 42%); domestic auto OEM ₹56.1 cr, auto replacement ₹36.5 cr, footwear ₹41.2 cr, furnishing ₹6.1 cr, others ₹3.4 cr (domestic ₹143.2 cr) [Q1 call]. Q4 FY26: export OEM ₹88 cr, export general ₹21 cr, domestic auto OEM ₹55 cr, replacement ₹41 cr, footwear ₹48 cr, furnishing ₹5 cr [Q4 call].
- FY26: standalone exports ₹386 cr (₹285 cr); auto OEM exports ₹290 cr (₹194 cr); total volume 31 million metres (+4.5%); value growth 15% (exports +35.5%, domestic +4%) [Q4 call]. PU sales only ₹27.1 cr for the year [Q4 call].
- Subsidiaries (all wholly owned): Mayur Uniquoters Corp (USA, just-in-time warehouse for US OEMs; FY26 turnover ₹176.9 cr, loss ₹1.5 cr), Mayur Uniquoters SA (₹93.3 cr, profit ₹4.5 cr), Futura Textiles (USA), UAB Futura Textiles Europe (Lithuania, loss ₹1.6 cr), Mayur Tecfab (India retail furnishing brand "Texture and Hues") [AR FY26 AOC-1].
- Capacity use: PVC 75–78% (2.7–2.8 million m/month against 3.5–3.7 million) [Q1 call].
- Market: global synthetic leather ~US$49 bn (2025) growing to ~US$104 bn by 2035; automotive fastest-growing application (~13% CAGR) (industry study cited in AR) [AR FY26 MD&A].

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (20 May 2026): value growth 8–10% domestic and 15–20% exports; exports 40–45% of sales in FY27; margin "25%-26%… we should be able to maintain that for sure", 25–30% possible; Q4's ~31% operating margin explained as export mix, efficiency and cost control, not forex; auto price increases to flow in Q1 via supplementary invoices; new domestic coating line "by the end of this calendar year… 1 month plus or minus"; global plant (~₹300 cr) under study [Q4 call].
- Q1 call (6 Aug 2026): overall top-line growth "10% to 12% for the next 3 years… anywhere between 10% to 15%"; US auto business up 60–70% (cumulative) over three years from existing customers (Ford, Chrysler); margin "25 plus 1% or 2%"; Q4 now called "abnormally high because of foreign exchange" [Q1 call].
- Delivery vs Q4: revenue growth 25% (**beat**); operating margin 22% (**missed** the 25–26% floor on an operating basis); no price increase taken from US OEMs — request emailed, "not pushed" (**not delivered**); domestic line slipped to "February, March 2027" (**delayed** ~2–3 months); global plant still not decided (**no progress**) [Q1 call].
- Blended growth guidance essentially **kept** (8–10% domestic + 15–20% export ≈ 10–13%); margin language **softened**.

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex ~₹50 cr: one more PVC coating line in the existing Jaipur premises, +5 lakh m/month (~15%), start Feb–Mar 2027; peak revenue ~₹120–150 cr at ~₹300/m [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Placing it in the existing plant instead of a new South India site cut cost from ₹150–200 cr to ~₹50 cr [Q4 call].
- Payback: ~₹50 cr for up to ₹150 cr of sales — asset turn about 3x, very quick payback at 20%+ margins (computed from call figures).
- Global plant (Mexico, US or elsewhere in NAFTA): ~₹250–300 cr over two years if approved; on hold because of US tariffs and the West Asia war; "we will do it outside India first" [Q1 call; Q4 call]. Rationale: OEMs want suppliers near them for de-risking [Q1 call].
- Total headroom: with the new line, utilisation drops to ~65–66%, giving 25–30% more volume without new plants; management sees ₹250–400 cr of extra revenue potential from existing sites depending on mix [Q1 call].
- PU plant (Gwalior): persistently under-used; auditor made its recoverability a key audit matter — carrying value ₹102.7 cr, operating losses in current and previous years; fair value assessed by an outside valuer [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Vendor approval from "a very big customer" but no orders [Q4 call]; "I cannot give you a very strong recommendation for PU plant at the moment" [Q1 call].
- New R&D centre planned [AR FY26 MD&A].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹8 cr; investments ₹285 cr at Mar-26 [Screener]; debt/equity 0.01, interest cover 247x [AR FY26 MD&A]. Effectively debt-free with ~₹300 cr treasury.
- Working capital is heavy: inventory days 170, debtor days 79, cash conversion cycle 197 days in FY26 (177 in FY25) [Screener]; US subsidiary must hold 8 weeks' stock plus goods in transit, longer since the war [Q4 call]. CARE flags the "elongated working capital cycle" [CARE Nov-25].
- FY26 operating cash flow ₹133 cr, free cash flow ₹110 cr (86% of operating profit) [Screener]. Dividend ₹5/share for FY25 paid in FY26 [AR FY26 Board's report].
- Rating CARE AA/Stable/A1+ [CARE Nov-25].

## Moat evidence
- Largest domestic organised capacity; "among the few Asian companies approved as a vendor" by Ford, Chrysler, Mercedes and BMW [CARE Nov-25; AR FY26].
- Switching costs: 2–3-year approvals and model-life supply [CARE Nov-25]. Never disrupted OEM supply through COVID, Ukraine and West Asia shipping crises [Q1 call].
- Pricing: export OEM prices fixed in dollars — rupee fall to ~94/US$ worked as a ~10% "natural hedge" [Q1 call]. Operating margins 20–24% for years vs a fragmented industry [Screener; CARE Nov-25].
- Weak spots: footwear (PVC and sole prices up 2–3x, domestic footwear shrank in Q1); PU faces Chinese competition [Q1 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **PU plant impairment risk** (₹102.7 cr carrying value, losses) [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Margin quality**: Q1 operating margin 22%; a large part of reported profit comes from other income (₹23 cr of ₹74 cr PBT) [Screener]. Management's explanation of Q4's 31% changed between calls (operational in May; "forex" in August) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Customer concentration in a few US programmes (Ford, Chrysler); US tariffs and freight (up 4x) [Q1 call].
- Key-man risk: CMD Suresh Poddar (five decades in the business) "had a slight infection… having a little difficulty in talking on con-call"; son Manav Poddar not involved in operations; ED Arun Bagaria (son-in-law) runs day-to-day [Q1 call; CARE Nov-25].
- Transfer pricing: parent sold ₹180.75 cr to the US subsidiary in FY26 at arm's-length (TNMM); the US unit made a small loss [AR FY26 AOC-2, AOC-1].
- Contingent liabilities small: ₹12.8 cr (GST ₹9.5 cr, income tax ₹2.1 cr, customs ₹1.2 cr), down from ₹22.5 cr [AR FY26 note 36].
- Promoters 58.8%, no pledge reported [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Growth stayed strong (25% vs 9% in Q4) but volume growth is only ~3%.
- Margin: from "exit margin 30%… we are expecting that it should be maintained" to "25 plus 1% or 2%" and "last quarter… abnormally high".
- New issues: 4x freight, US price-rise request parked, footwear slump, CMD's health.
- Unchanged: global plant undecided, PU unsold.

## My read
The export-OEM engine is real (auto exports +50% in FY26) and the balance sheet is cash-rich, so the business is improving; at a Screener P/E of about 15 the price asks only for modest growth. But volumes are barely growing, operating margins are closer to 22% than the 25–26% guided, and the PU plant may need a write-down. What would lift the view: the new line starting on time with US price rises accepted; what would cut it: a PU impairment, US programme losses, or tariffs hitting auto exports before a foreign plant exists.
