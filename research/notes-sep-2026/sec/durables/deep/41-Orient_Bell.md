# Orient Bell (ORIENTBELL) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Figures from Screener consolidated page unless tagged. Share price ₹408, market cap ₹601 cr, 52-week range ₹241–435 [Screener].

## Business and segments
- Pure-play branded tile company (ceramic wall/floor, polished and glazed vitrified tiles), plus a small tile-adhesive line ("Masterbond") [IP Q1FY27].
- Capacity 42.4 mn sq m (MSM) a year including associates: owned plants Sikandrabad UP 14.8 MSM, Hoskote Karnataka 6.6 MSM, Dora Gujarat 5.5 MSM; two Morbi associates (Proton Granito, Corial Ceramic) 15.5 MSM [IP Q1FY27].
- Blended utilisation ~60% in FY26, 64–65% in Q4 FY26, 73% in Q1 FY27; vitrified lines ~80%, ceramic higher [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Mix Q1 FY27: vitrified 60% of sales (58% a year earlier), glazed vitrified (GVT) 47% (40%); projects (orders ≥3,000 sq m) 18%, retail the rest (78% retail in FY26) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Only a low single-digit share of GVT is bought from Morbi; Dora supplies 15–20% of GVT, the rest from Sikandrabad [Q1 call].
- Distribution: 2,000+ business partners, ~381 dedicated "Orient Bell Tile Boutiques", 3,000+ dealers [IP Q1FY27; Crisil Jul-25]. No customer is 10% of sales [AR FY26 note 41].
- Advertising ~3.6–3.7% of revenue; year-round TV in five languages [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 143, Sep-25 165, Dec-25 169, Mar-26 215, Jun-26 204; operating margin 3.5%, 5.6%, 6.1%, 7.0%, 8.0%; net profit -0.4, 3.2, 3.4, 6.2, 8.3 [Screener].
- Annual: revenue FY23 ₹705 cr → FY24 674 → FY25 670 → FY26 691; operating margin 7% → 3.1% → 4.2% → 6%; net profit ₹22 cr → 1 → 3 → 12 [Screener]. Revenue has been flat for four years; FY26 volume +4.4%, revenue +3.1% [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27: volume 63 lakh sq m (+22.9%), average price +15.9%, revenue ₹203 cr (+42.8%), gross margin 39.7% (record), company EBITDA ₹17.6 cr (8.7%, +480 bp), PBT ₹11.2 cr vs a ₹0.6 cr loss [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]. Screener shows operating profit ₹16.3 cr (8.0%).
- Unit economics: revenue ~₹322 per sq m (₹203 cr / 63 lakh sq m), EBITDA ~₹28/sq m; like-for-like production cost down 2.3% y/y [IP Q1FY27; my calculation].
- Price rises: ~20% in March–April 2026, "18–19% vs pre-war" held in Q1, no cuts yet [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Operating leverage: ~60% of FY26's extra revenue flowed to profit [Q4 call].
- Q4 FY26 (before the Morbi shock fully hit): volume +7%, revenue +7.5%, company EBITDA ₹16.4 cr (+66%, margin +270 bp), PBT ₹8.4 cr vs ₹3.6 cr; FY26 EBITDA ₹42.5 cr (+38%), PBT ₹16.4 cr after a ₹1.3 cr labour-code charge; receivable days cut by 9 to 48 [Q4 call]. So margin recovery was under way before the war — the Morbi shutdown accelerated it rather than created it.
- Gas cost per SCM: Q3 FY26 ~₹44, Q4 ~₹45.5, Q1 FY27 ~₹60 — a ~33% jump that the ~19% price rise more than covered because gas is only part of cost [Q4 call; Q1 call; my inference].

## Guidance history
- Policy: no numeric revenue or margin guidance, repeated on both calls [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Q4 call (19 May 2026): margins to keep rising quarter on quarter ("trajectory is likely to continue"); FY27 "better than FY26"; no capacity capex needed in FY27; maintenance capex ~₹10 cr (±₹4–5 cr); price rises already cover gas costs [Q4 call].
- Q1 delivery: margin rose again (8.7% vs 7.7% in Q4 on company figures) — **delivered**; but capex view **changed**: ~₹10 cr to convert 1 MSM of ceramic capacity to GVT and ~₹15 cr in all over 4–5 months, and a larger expansion decision promised "over the next 3–4 months" [Q1 call].
- Crisil (July 2025) expected FY26 revenue growth of only 4–5% and margin 5–5.5%; actual 3.1% and ~6% — broadly in line [Crisil Jul-25; Screener].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ~₹5.5 cr; FY25 ~₹6.5 cr — very low, below depreciation of ~₹22 cr [AR FY26 cash flow; Screener].
- FY27: ~₹15 cr (ceramic-to-GVT line conversion, new digital printers and polishing machines), funded internally [Q1 call].
- Next phase: board evaluating a manufacturing expansion (location/product not disclosed) to use ~₹75 cr of cash; decision expected by about November–December 2026 [Q1 call].
- Headroom: at 73% utilisation of ~42 MSM, the company can add roughly a quarter more volume before new capacity is needed (my estimate).
- Hoskote (dry-process plant, 50% less water) and Dora were the last expansions (FY23–FY24) aimed at South and West; Q1 FY27 retail volume grew 37% in the South and 60% in the West [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash ₹47.7 cr at June 2026 (₹29.7 cr at March 2026); gross borrowings ₹26.3 cr; cash and liquid investments ₹74 cr [IP Q1FY27].
- Cash cycle 26 days (Mar-25) → 20 (Mar-26) → 18 (Jun-26); receivable days 48 → 43; inventory ~32 days [IP Q1FY27].
- Operating cash flow ₹48 cr, free cash flow ₹43 cr in FY26 [Screener].
- Dividend yield 0.25% (payout 12% in FY26) [Screener].
- Rated Crisil A-/Stable (downgraded from A/Negative in July 2025 on falling revenue and low margins) [Crisil Jul-25].

## Moat evidence
- Pricing: price gap to Morbi unbranded tiles narrowed from ~₹100 to ₹50–55 per unit (management's example) while the company held a ~19% increase [Q1 call].
- Demand generation: company-generated "sell-out" orders covered ~40% of dealers' purchases in Q1 vs 26% a year earlier; 2,000+ new projects/month in its tracking tool; 50,000 tile designs shown monthly on its visualiser [Q1 call].
- Geography: three factories outside Morbi gave supply security when Morbi shut from end-March to mid-May 2026; dealers are now adding non-Morbi suppliers [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Organised-vs-Morbi dynamics: Morbi was shut from end-March to mid-May 2026 (gas, then labour shortages); 500+ units reopened from 1 May, mostly GVT, with polishing/packing short of labour; Morbi design stocks ran down, which favoured branded players with inventory [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Weakness: scale is small versus Kajaria/Somany; four years of flat revenue before FY27 show share was not rising until the Morbi shock [Screener].

## Risks and red flags
- **Morbi snap-back**: Morbi exports normally run ₹1,500–1,600 cr a month; they fell to ₹500 cr in April and ₹1,000 cr in May 2026 as Gulf freight rose 5–6 times. If exports stay weak, surplus Morbi tiles return to the domestic market and prices fall [Q1 call].
- Gas: Sikandrabad price rose from ₹44–45 to ₹60–62 per SCM; supplied by GAIL on a formula partly linked to spot [Q1 call].
- Price rises usually "readjust" within 3–4 months once gas falls — so Q1 margins may not last [Q4 call].
- Related parties: ~₹69 cr of tiles bought from the two Morbi associates in FY26 (Proton ₹42.9 cr, Corial ₹26.1 cr), ~10% of revenue; rent ₹0.95 cr to promoter-linked Freesia Investment [AR FY26 note 42].
- Key management pay ~₹8.4 cr in FY26 against PAT of ₹12 cr [AR FY26 note 42].
- Contingent liabilities ~₹16 cr, mainly old sales-tax/GST disputes (₹7.8 cr) plus interest (₹7.1 cr) [AR FY26 note 38].
- Auditor: clean opinion; key audit matter is the accounting for dealer schemes and discounts [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Stock is thinly held by institutions (FII 0.2%, DII 0.03%) [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- May: tone "optimistic but cautious", worried about gas allocation cut to 50% ("which lines we should shut"), dealers holding back after a 20% price rise.
- August: confident — volume +23%, record gross margin, net cash up, Morbi normal again from 15 May; new capex and an expansion study announced.
- New issue raised in August: Morbi export slump could push supply back home.

## My read
Orient Bell is finally growing after four flat years, and Q1 FY27 shows how much operating leverage a debt-free, under-used tile maker has: revenue +43%, profit from a loss to ₹8 cr. But much of the jump came from a one-off — Morbi shut for six weeks — and from price rises that management itself expects to unwind within months of gas prices falling. At ₹408 the price already assumes the higher margin mostly sticks. The view improves if margins stay above 7% through Q2–Q3 as Morbi returns and the expansion plan uses cash sensibly; it worsens if Morbi's blocked exports turn into domestic discounting.
