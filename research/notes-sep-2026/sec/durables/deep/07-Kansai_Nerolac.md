# Kansai Nerolac Paints (KNPL) — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged. KNPL is 75% owned by Kansai Paint Co., Japan. It reports one segment ("paints") and does not publish a decorative/industrial revenue split; growth by business is given only in words on calls.

**Transcript check:** both stored transcripts are the text of the company's BSE filings (cover letter, page numbers included). Spot checks of every figure (₹601 cr capex, 66,000 KL, 10,000 MT resin, 1,700 dealers, 65,000 painters, "4% to 5%" pricing, "1.3-odd" contribution drop, ₹150–200 cr normal capex, 18% ROCE; Q4: 25 March hike, 70–75% utilisation, 250% dividend, 10%+ projects, 30.6% PBDIT growth, "low teens" price rise, crude "100") — **Q1 FY27 (3 Aug 2026): matches official. Q4 FY26 (6 May 2026): matches official.**

## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** decorative paints (interior/exterior emulsions, enamels, wood finishes, construction chemicals and waterproofing under "Perma", tile adhesives) and industrial coatings (automotive OEM for cars, two/three-wheelers, CVs, tractors; auto refinish; powder coatings; general-industrial and high-performance liquid coatings for infrastructure, railways, coil, appliances) [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27].
- **Position:** leader in automotive and powder coatings; third-largest decorative paint maker in India; "number two" brand by top-of-mind awareness; awareness "95 plus" [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call].
- **Mix:** no segment revenue. Management: industrial is "a sizable portion… may not be 50-50, maybe it is 45 this quarter" — Q1 is the decorative-heavy quarter. Decorative projects ~10%+ and new businesses (waterproofing, construction chemicals, wood finish) slightly above 10% of decorative [Q1 call; Q4 call]. CRISIL describes decorative as 70–75% of the Indian paint industry [CRISIL Aug-26].
- **Distribution:** 35,000+ dealers, 20,000+ tinting machines, 100+ depots; 1,700 dealers added in Q1 FY27; network grows 5–10% a year; 186 NXTGEN shops, 275 shop-in-shops, 385 Paint+ zones; painter programme 65,000 participants in Q1 [CRISIL Aug-26; Q1 call].
- **Subsidiaries:** Nerofix (construction chemicals; FY26 revenue ₹164 cr, loss ₹7.6 cr; being merged into KNPL, appointed date 1 Apr 2025); KNP Japan, Nepal (revenue ₹76 cr, PAT ₹6 cr); Kansai Nerolac Paints Bangladesh (revenue ₹91 cr, down from ₹143 cr; loss ₹27.6 cr); Sri Lanka 60% stake sold Dec 2025 for about ₹5 lakh [AR FY26 Board's report].
- **Capacity/utilisation:** powder and industrial liquid plants at ~70–75% utilisation [Q4 call].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr, revenue / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 2,162 / 303 / 14% / 216; Sep-25 1,954 / 215 / 11% / 133; Dec-25 1,982 / 240 / 12% / 117; Mar-26 1,954 / 216 / 11% / 110; Jun-26 2,374 / 328 / 14% / 228 [Screener].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹7,801 cr, op. profit ₹1,028 cr (13%), PAT ₹1,176 cr; FY25 ₹7,823 cr / ₹942 cr (12%) / ₹1,109 cr; FY26 ₹8,052 cr / ₹975 cr (12%) / ₹576 cr; TTM ₹8,263 cr / ₹1,000 cr / ₹589 cr [Screener]. FY24 and FY25 profits include one-off gains (other income ₹678 cr in Jun-23 and ₹671 cr in Dec-24 quarters) [Screener]; FY26 had exceptional losses (₹44.7 cr Wage Code gratuity, ₹10.2 cr Ghaziabad warehouse fire, ₹5.8 cr impairments — standalone) [AR FY26 Board's report].
- **Growth is slow:** 3-year sales CAGR 2%, 5-year 10%, 10-year 8%; 10-year profit CAGR 2% [Screener]. FY26 consolidated revenue +2.9%, PBDIT +3.4% [Q4 call].
- **Q1 FY27 (consolidated):** revenue +9.8%, material cost 65.0% of revenue vs 63.9% (gross margin down ~1.1 pt), PBDIT ₹328 cr (13.8% vs 14.0%), PBT +5.8% [IP Q1FY27 p.33]. Decorative value growth high single digit, volume low single digit (market leader: volume ~9%, value ~16%); economy grew mid-single digit, super-premium double digit; industrial double digit; auto refinish flat [Q1 call].
- **Pricing:** Q1 FY27 consolidated price increase ~5%; decorative hikes in FY27 so far "low teens" cumulative (four rounds: ~2% on 25 Mar, then 5–6% each on 10 Apr, 21 Apr, 11 May); industrial ~5% so far; Q2 to add ~3% decorative and 3–5% industrial [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Returns:** ROCE 12% (FY26), ROE 9% [Screener]; standalone return on net worth ex-exceptional 10.3% [AR FY26].

## Guidance history
- **Nov 2025 (Q2 FY26):** FY26 EBITDA margin "above 13%"; short-term 13–14%, long-term 15%; normal capex "over ₹200 crores" [News: ScanX, 6 Nov 2025]. **Outcome:** FY26 OPM 12.1% on Screener (standalone PBDIT ₹986 cr on ₹7,739 cr = 12.7%) — **missed**.
- **Q4 call (May 2026):** FY27 margin 13–14% "though our endeavor is always to go on higher end"; price increases would neutralise inflation with some timing lag; decorative to prioritise profitable mix over share [Q4 call].
- **Q1 call (Aug 2026):** margin guidance kept at 13–14% for FY27; medium-term "14% plus" in 2–3 years; aim to be #1 in non-auto industrial within ~2 years; decorative "maintain our position, if not improve" [Q1 call].
- **Q1 delivery vs Q4 promise:** margin held (13.8% vs 14.0%) while decorative volume lagged the leader; revenue growth re-accelerated to ~10%. Guidance kept, not raised.
- **Credit agency view is more cautious:** revenue growth 6–8% a year medium-term; operating margin 11–13% [CRISIL Aug-26].

## Capex and capacity
- **New programme ~₹600 cr (₹601 cr)** at Sayakha (Gujarat: automotive, powder, resin), Bawal (Haryana: automotive) and Hosur (Tamil Nadu: automotive) — adds 65,880 KL/yr paint+powder and 9,780 KL/yr resin [IP Q1FY27 p.29]. Split: ₹412 cr industrial paint capacity, ₹189 cr powder coating and resin at Sayakha; phased to FY2028–29; funded from internal accruals [News: Sahi, 4 Aug 2026; CRISIL Aug-26].
- **Timing and scale:** "over about 2 years, 2-plus years"; normal capex ₹150–200 cr a year continues, so annual capex ~₹400–450 cr for 2–2.5 years [Q1 call]. FY26 capex was "more or less normal" [Q4 call]; capital commitments ₹92.8 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 35].
- **Rationale:** industrial plants at 70–75% utilisation; industrial start-to-end lead time long, so capacity needed by ~FY29–30; resin plant is backward integration for automotive paint (KNPL says it is the only Indian maker of the full automotive resin range, CED to clear coat) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Payback/returns:** asset turns ~1x "similar" for industrial; management expects ROCE "slightly on the higher end towards 18%" because some infrastructure is front-loaded [Q1 call]. Screener ROCE is 12% today, so this is an aspiration.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **Net cash:** borrowings ₹303 cr (largely leases); cash and liquid investments "over ₹2,500 crore"; gearing 0.05x; interest cover 33x [Screener; CRISIL Aug-26]. Rated Crisil AAA/Stable and A1+, reaffirmed 21 Aug 2026 [CRISIL Aug-26].
- **Cash flow:** CFO ₹894 cr in FY26 (115% of operating profit), FCF ₹682 cr; FY25 CFO ₹672 cr [Screener].
- **Working capital:** cash conversion cycle 95 days in FY26 vs 109 in FY25 — inventory days cut from 133 to 119; debtor days rose to 67 [Screener]. Management: "special focus has been on reduction on inventory" [Q4 call].
- **Dividend:** ₹2.50/share (250%) for FY26, payout 51% of reported PAT; parent received ₹227 cr in dividends [Screener; AR FY26 note 37].

## Moat evidence
- **Automotive:** leader; gained share in FY26; Kansai Japan technology; supplier awards from Toyota Kirloskar, Maruti Suzuki, Suzuki Motorcycle, TAFE; growing in alloy wheels, underbody, seam sealers, EVs [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]. OEM approvals create switching costs.
- **Pricing power — weak in auto, fair in deco:** CRISIL cites "pricing pressure from auto OEMs" and limited pricing flexibility; industrial hikes lag 1–2 quarters (contracts carry an effective date, so some is recovered) [CRISIL Aug-26; Q1 call]. Operating margin has swung between 10% and 17% over a decade [CRISIL Aug-26].
- **Decorative share — slipping:** volume growth low single digit vs the leader's ~9% in Q1 FY27; management says it is deliberately cutting unprofitable economy volume [Q1 call].
- **Cost position:** raw materials are 55–60% of sales (TiO2, crude derivatives, pigments, resins) [CRISIL Aug-26]; royalty to Kansai Japan ₹32.7 cr (0.4% of sales) [AR FY26 note 37].

## Risks and red flags
- **Decorative competition:** Birla Opus and JSW keep "high competitive intensity"; no let-up in dealer schemes or painter incentives; freebies continue in some markets [Q1 call]. Structural risk of losing shelf space.
- **Industrial price lag:** gross contribution in industrial fell ~1.3 pts in Q1 because OEM price talks lag [Q1 call].
- **Input costs:** West Asia war, crude, rupee depreciation; management said crude at ~$100 is manageable with current prices [Q4 call].
- **Auditor:** unmodified opinion; key audit matter is estimation of variable consideration (rebates/discounts) [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- **Contingent liabilities (consolidated):** tax disputes ~₹45 cr (excise/service tax/GST credits ₹44.8 cr); corporate guarantee for the Bangladesh subsidiary's bank loan; PF Supreme Court issue judged immaterial [AR FY26 note 35].
- **Loss-making subsidiaries:** Bangladesh (loss ₹27.6 cr; revenue down 36%), Nerofix (loss ₹7.6 cr); Sri Lanka exited at a nominal price [AR FY26].
- **Related parties:** royalty ₹32.7 cr and small technical fees to the parent; sales to fellow Kansai subsidiaries ~₹7 cr [AR FY26 note 37] — modest.
- **Management churn:** new MD (Pravin Chaudhari from Apr 2025), new CFO (Yash Ahuja from Aug 2025), Japanese whole-time director left 31 Mar 2026 [AR FY26 note 37].
- **No promoter pledge** (MNC parent).

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: "very balanced growth", decorative mid-single digit, volume-value gap "not very significant". Q1: management admitted decorative volume is "low single digit", clearly behind the leader, and framed it as mix correction [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- New in Q1: the ₹601 cr capacity plan; target to be #1 in non-auto industrial in two years; employee value proposition to fight talent attrition [Q1 call].
- Inflation: Q4 feared a long West Asia disruption; Q1 saw "slight deflation" from the June peak, helping Q2 [Q1 call].
- Q1 FY27 benefited from low-cost inventory; Q2 will carry higher-cost stock, offset by further hikes [Q1 call].

## My read
KNPL is a solid, cash-rich, AAA-rated business whose industrial side is healthy and now getting fresh capacity, but whose decorative arm is losing volume share to newer, bigger-spending rivals. Q1 FY27 was the best quarter in two years (revenue +10%, margin ~14%), yet guidance was only kept, and CRISIL expects just 6–8% growth and 11–13% margins. At a Screener P/E of ~23 the price needs earnings to grow roughly 14–15% a year for a decade, well above the 2–7% the business has delivered over three to ten years; the gap can close only if the ₹600 cr capex earns the hoped-for ~18% ROCE and decorative volume stops lagging. Evidence that would change the view: decorative volume growth back in line with the leader, a sustained 14%+ margin through a cost upcycle, or a plan to use the ₹2,500 cr cash pile.
