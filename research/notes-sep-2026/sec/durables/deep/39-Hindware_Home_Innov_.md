# Hindware Home Innovation (HINDWAREAP) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Figures from Screener consolidated page unless tagged otherwise. Share price ₹174, market cap ₹1,453 cr, 52-week range ₹167–393; Screener shows no P/E because trailing profit is a loss (-₹6 cr) [Screener].

**Note on calls:** there was no Q1 FY27 earnings call; the company filed only a results presentation and a "business-wise update" on 12 Aug 2026. The two calls available are Q4 FY26 (20 May 2026) and Q3 FY26 (13 Feb 2026) [Screener documents; BSE].

## Business and segments
- Somany-group (promoter 52.74%) holding company with three businesses [Screener; CARE Apr-26]:
  - **Bathware** (sanitaryware, faucets, outsourced tiles, wellness) — FY26 revenue ₹1,520 cr (+10%), EBITDA ₹157 cr (10.3%) [Q4 call]. Plants: sanitaryware Bahadurgarh 1.75 mn pieces and Bibinagar 2.1 mn pieces; faucets Kaharani 3.7 mn pieces. Utilisation Q1 FY27: sanitaryware ~90%, faucets ~85% [IP Q1FY27; Update Q1FY27]. In-house share: sanitaryware ~70%, faucets ~50% [Update Q1FY27].
  - **Pipes & fittings** ("Truflo") — FY26 revenue ₹673 cr, EBITDA ₹31 cr, PBT loss ₹29 cr [Q4 call]. Capacity 78,500 t: Sangareddy/Isnapur 66,000 t and Roorkee 12,500 t (commercial output from 30 Jan 2026) [IP Q1FY27].
  - **Consumer appliances** (chimneys, hobs, cooktops, sinks; water heaters via Hintastica) — FY26 revenue ₹317 cr, EBITDA -₹12 cr [Q4 call official]. Asset-light: outsourced, ~75% sourced domestically [Update Q1FY27].
- Channel: bathware ~75% retail/general trade, ~25% institutional; Tier 2/3 ~70% of bathware sales; ~640 brand stores; bathware premium range ~40% of sales [Update Q1FY27; IP Q1FY27].
- Market position: No. 1 top-of-mind brand in sanitaryware (21% TOMA, company claim), second-largest in faucets, top-three in chimneys [IP Q1FY27; CARE Apr-26].
- Market size (management): sanitaryware ~₹7,000–8,000 cr, faucets ~₹13,000–14,000 cr [Q4 call].
- **Demerger**: consumer business to be demerged into HHIL Limited; bathware + pipes (Hindware Limited) to be merged back and listed; 1 share of each per HHIL share; appointed date 1 Apr 2025. Shareholders approved 7 Mar 2026 (with 57.06% of votes polled [News: Whalesbook, Mar-2026]); NCLT sanction still awaited on 12 Aug 2026, though in May management expected it within 2–3 months [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 531, Sep-25 676, Dec-25 640, Mar-26 663, Jun-26 625; operating margin 9.2%, 8.3%, 7.5%, 6.7%, 8.0%; net profit -29.1, 5.0, 3.6, -18.8, 4.4 [Screener].
- Annual: revenue FY23 ₹2,872 cr → FY24 2,800 → FY25 2,523 → FY26 2,510; operating margin 9% → 8% → 6% → 8%; net profit ₹58 cr → 28 → -68 → -39 [Screener]. Interest ₹70 cr and depreciation ₹117 cr in FY26 eat almost all operating profit of ₹199 cr [Screener].
- FY26 loss came from exceptional items: ₹44 cr for exiting fans, coolers, purifiers and furniture fittings (asset write-offs, bad debts, stock), ₹39 cr standalone write-down of the Hintastica investment (~₹34–35 cr goodwill at group level), ₹0.7 cr labour-code cost [AR FY26 note 58; Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 by business [IP Q1FY27]:
  - Bathware revenue ₹387 cr (+14% from ₹341 cr), EBITDA ₹46 cr (~12%, including other income), EBIT ₹30 cr.
  - Pipes revenue ₹153 cr (+29% from ₹119 cr), sales volume 10,221 t (+28%) against quarterly capacity ~19,700 t (~52% used); EBITDA ~3%; small PBT loss.
  - Consumer revenue ₹83 cr (+16%), EBITDA ~₹6 cr (~7%) versus a loss a year ago.
- Unit economics: pipes sell at roughly ₹150,000 per tonne (₹153 cr / 10,221 t) and earn ~₹4,500/t EBITDA at 3% — thin versus leaders (my calculation from IP figures).

## Guidance history
- Q3 call (Feb 2026): bathware growth "mid-teens"; bathware margin +3–4 points over 18–24 months; pipes 12–15% volume growth; consumer ₹90+ cr in Q4 and ₹100 cr/quarter "by the start of next year"; consumer ₹650–700 cr by FY31 [Q3 call].
- Q4 call (May 2026): bathware 15–20% growth in FY27 with 1–2 points margin gain each year ("teens within this year and next"); consumer 15–20% growth, EBITDA positive from Q1 FY27, 8–10% margin within FY27–28, ₹100 cr/quarter in Q1 or Q2; pipes 14–15% volume; group margin +1.5–2 points a year for two years; net debt ₹708 cr, ₹145–150 cr repaid in FY27, 30–40% lower in two years [Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 delivery vs promise [IP Q1FY27; Update Q1FY27]:
  - Bathware +14% — below the 15–20% just promised; the August update quietly **trimmed** FY27 to "mid-teen" growth, and the margin target to +2–4 points over 18–24 months from 11% in FY26.
  - Consumer turned EBITDA-positive (**met**) but revenue ₹83 cr vs the ₹100 cr run-rate (**missed** so far); new targets ₹500+ cr in two years, ₹700–750 cr by FY30/FY31 (the deck gives both years).
  - Pipes volume +28% (**beat** the 15%) but margin ~3% vs FY27 target 4–5% (now guided).
  - Consumer Q4 target of ₹90 cr was missed (₹80 cr) because of component shortages [Q4 call].
- Track record: margin targets have repeatedly slipped (bathware H2 FY26 13–14% promised in Q2, 10% delivered in Q3) [stored Q3 analysis; Q3 call].

## Capex and capacity
- The heavy capex is done: Roorkee pipes plant (12,500 t) started January 2026 after a state-approval delay; management said no further expansion is needed [Q3 call].
- FY27 capex: bathware ~₹50 cr (debottlenecking, brand stores), pipes ~₹15 cr, consumer ~₹5 cr — about ₹70 cr in all, against FY26 depreciation of ₹117 cr [Update Q1FY27; Screener].
- Roorkee should add ~₹200 cr of annual revenue once ramped (2–3 more quarters); North India pricing is aggressive, slowing the ramp [Q3 call; Update Q1FY27].
- Water-heater JV Hintastica sold its Telangana factory (~₹115 cr) in December 2025, repaid its debt, and became a wholly owned trading subsidiary in Q1 FY27 [Q3 call; Update Q1FY27].
- Asset turns are low: fixed assets ₹999 cr + CWIP ₹94 cr against revenue ₹2,510 cr; ROCE 7% in FY26 vs 22% in FY22 [Screener]. Bathware targets ROCE 16–18% in 18–24 months [Update Q1FY27].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹856 cr at March 2026 (₹1,046 cr FY24) [Screener]. Net bank debt: bathware ₹224 cr, pipes ₹427 cr, consumer ₹18 cr at June 2026 → ~₹669 cr, down from ₹708 cr in May [Update Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- CARE downgraded the rating to BBB+ (from A-) on 8 Apr 2026 citing weak profits, higher debt from capex and heavy FY27 repayments (moderate debt-service cover); still on watch pending the demerger [CARE Apr-26].
- Promoters put in ~₹150 cr of a ₹250 cr rights issue in December 2024 [CARE Apr-26].
- Cash flow is better than profit: operating cash flow ₹258 cr and free cash flow ₹133 cr in FY26 [Screener].
- Working capital days: bathware 95 → 87 (Q1 FY26 → Q1 FY27); pipes 86 → 87 with debtor days up 74 → 87; consumer 117 → 77 [Update Q1FY27].
- No dividend (0% yield) [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Brand: strong in sanitaryware; price rises passed through in 2026 — faucets +15% (Jan) and +3% (May), sanitaryware +6% (Feb) and +7% (Apr), ~20% faucet price rise over 12 months, "no major impact on sales" [Q4 call; Update Q1FY27].
- Share: bathware grew 10% in FY26, which management says beat the market; 1 lakh plumbers on its app [Q4 call].
- Premium mix ~40% with 2–3 points higher gross margin [Update Q1FY27].
- Pipes has no moat: price-taker on PVC resin, lost share in the North before Roorkee, top-30 distributors = ~58% of pipe sales [Q3 call; Update Q1FY27].
- Consumer appliances: gross margin 35–40% but crowded category; exited most products [Update Q1FY27].

## Risks and red flags
- **Auditor**: consolidated report carries a "material uncertainty related to going concern" paragraph for subsidiary Evok Homes (discontinued; net worth -₹5.6 cr; investment ₹8.4 cr fully written off); two companies were consolidated on management-certified unaudited figures (Other Matters) [AR FY26 consolidated auditor's report].
- Standalone contingent liabilities ~₹28–30 cr (tax demands and claims; "claims not acknowledged" jumped to ~₹23.5 cr) plus a ₹25 cr corporate guarantee for Hintastica's loans (₹69.5 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 notes 44, RPT].
- Credit downgrade and tight debt service in FY27 [CARE Apr-26].
- Demerger delay; the vote carried with 57.06% of polled shares — since promoters own 52.74%, most public votes cast appear to have been against (my inference) [News: Whalesbook].
- Commodity: PVC resin went ₹68 → ₹114/kg (Jan–Mar 2026) then ~₹80–85; brass +40% year on year; kiln gas costs up [Q4 call; Update Q1FY27].
- Repeated one-offs (goodwill impairment, category exits) make "adjusted" figures look better than reported ones.
- Institutional holding falling: DIIs 7.1% (Jun-24) → 3.7% (Jun-26) [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Q3 (Feb): upbeat — cleanup done, consumer at breakeven, Roorkee started, resin bottomed.
- Q4 (May): defensive — pipes lost March sales for lack of stock when resin spiked; tile vendors hit by fuel shortage (~₹10 cr lost sales); consumer back to -₹7 cr EBITDA; JV goodwill written off; but new, bigger targets (15–20% growth, 1.5–2 points margin a year).
- Q1 deck (Aug): targets trimmed to "mid-teen"; pipes volume strong but margin thin; consumer profitable.

## My read
Bathware is a decent, improving franchise (14% growth, 12% margin in Q1, working capital falling), but it sits inside a group burdened by a low-return pipes plant, a shrunken appliance arm and ~₹670 cr of net debt that swallows most operating profit in interest and depreciation. Reported profit has been negative for two years because of repeated write-offs; cash flow (₹133 cr FCF) is the better guide. At ₹174 and ~2x book, the price assumes the demerger unlocks value and pipes margins reach 5%+; that is plausible but management has missed most margin timelines. The view improves with NCLT sanction, two quarters of pipes EBITDA above 5% and net debt below ₹550 cr; it worsens if the rating slips further or bathware growth falls under 10%.
