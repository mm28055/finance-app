# Greenply Industries (GREENPLY) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹287, market cap ₹3,588 cr, 52-week range ₹176–334, Screener P/E 32.1, ROCE 14.3%, ROE 11.8% [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- India's second-largest branded plywood maker, now building a large MDF (medium-density fibreboard) business. Plywood brands: Green Club/Green Gold (premium); Ecotec, Bharosa, Jansathi, Wood Crest (mid/low, mostly outsourced) [CARE Jul-26]. Also veneers, PVC/WPC doors (plant started Apr 2026), and a 50:50 furniture-hardware JV with Samet [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 split: plywood & allied ₹526.6 cr (73% of revenue), core EBITDA 8.4%; MDF ₹195.7 cr (27%), core EBITDA 17.3% [IP Q1FY27].
- Plywood capacity 52.8 million sq m a year across Bamanbore (Gujarat), Sandila (UP), Kriparampur (WB) and Tizit (Nagaland), plus 13.5 msm under construction in Odisha [IP Q1FY27]. Q1 production 12.3 msm vs sales 19.4 msm — roughly a third of plywood sold is bought in from outside makers [IP Q1FY27]. Plywood utilisation ~96% in FY26 (87% in FY25) [CARE Jul-26]; Q1 FY27 fell to 92–93% (98–99% in Q4) [Q1 call].
- MDF: Vadodara plant 3,00,000 CBM a year; Line 2 of 2,10,000 CBM under construction [IP Q1FY27] (CARE describes the expansion as taking capacity to 6,10,000 CBM [CARE Jul-26] — the two documents differ). MDF effective utilisation 72% in FY26 (68% FY25) [CARE Jul-26]. Value-added products (pre-lam + HDHMR) above 50% of MDF revenue [Q4 call]. HDF flooring line started 20 Jul 2026 [Q1 call].
- Market: management puts its MDF share at ~7% now, 8–8.5% at full capacity; MDF industry growth 15–20% a year vs plywood 3–4% [Q4 call]. Industry MDF capacity 5.1 million CBM vs demand 3.7 million CBM in FY26 (utilisation 72%, up from 51% in FY24) [AR FY26 MD&A].
- Distribution: 3,000+ dealers, 1,100+ towns, 56 branches [CARE Jul-26].

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated)
- Annual revenue FY24 ₹2,180 cr → FY25 ₹2,488 cr → FY26 ₹2,739 cr; operating profit ₹185 cr (9%) → ₹204 cr (8%) → ₹242 cr (9%); net profit ₹70 cr → ₹92 cr → ₹90 cr [Screener]. Company "core EBITDA" FY26 ₹270.5 cr (9.9%) [Q4 call] — higher than Screener because it excludes JV losses and forex.
- Quarters (revenue / operating profit / net profit): Jun-25 ₹601 cr / ₹52 cr / ₹28 cr; Sep-25 ₹689 cr / ₹51 cr / ₹16 cr; Dec-25 ₹673 cr / ₹51 cr / ₹14 cr; Mar-26 ₹776 cr / ₹87 cr / ₹31 cr; Jun-26 ₹725 cr / ₹73 cr / ₹38 cr [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 as reported: revenue +20.7%; core EBITDA ₹78.3 cr, 10.8% (+50 bps YoY, –120 bps QoQ); PAT ₹37.6 cr (+32%) [IP Q1FY27].
- Unit economics: plywood realisation ₹265/sq m (+3.8% YoY), volume +13.8%; MDF realisation ₹33,525/CBM (+5.5% YoY), volume 57,805 CBM (+24.7%) [IP Q1FY27]. MDF EBITDA about ₹5,900 per CBM in Q1 (₹33.9 cr ÷ 57,805 CBM) [computed from IP Q1FY27].
- FY26 segments: plywood revenue ₹2,105.7 cr (+7.5%), volume +8.3%, EBITDA 8.8%; MDF ₹635.6 cr (+19.9%), EBITDA 13.4%; hardware JV revenue ₹44.3 cr, PAT loss ₹50.8 cr (Greenply share ₹25.4 cr) [official Q4 transcript].

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (30 Apr 2026): FY27 volume growth 10% plywood, 25–30% MDF; March-quarter consolidated margin (12%) is "the new norm" — "Absolutely right"; MDF 17% sustainable; peak net debt/equity 0.70–0.72x, back to 0.5–0.6x the following year; hardware JV break-even "the year after this, somewhere in the middle of the year" (mid-FY28); FY27 capex ~₹480–500 cr [Q4 call].
- Q1 delivery (24 Jul 2026): volumes on track (plywood +13.8%, MDF +24.7%); MDF margin held at 17.3% (**delivered**); consolidated margin fell to 10.8% and plywood to 8.4% (**missed** the "new norm") — blamed on election-time labour shortage and weak outsourcing: "we've lost sales in Q1" [Q1 call]. 10% plywood margin guidance kept, conditional on ₹600 cr+ quarterly plywood sales.
- Debt guidance **raised**: peak net debt ₹710–730 cr and 0.75x at 31 Mar 2027, below 0.7x six months later [Q1 call].
- Hardware JV break-even mid-FY28 **kept**; FY27–28 JV revenue ₹120–150 cr a year [Q1 call].
- MDF margin path: 16–17% sustainable, +1 point after Line 2 [Q1 call]. ContiRoll plywood process in all four plants by H1 FY27, P&L gains "quarter four onwards" [Q1 call].
- Longer term: MDF ROCE of 17–18% over any 5–7 year cycle; "if I start doing a 800 crores number in plywood for the quarter… 13%, 14%, 15%" margins [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- FY27 capex ~₹500 cr: ~₹300 cr in Greenply Speciality Panels (MDF Line 2), ~₹100 cr in Greenply Sandila, ~₹47 cr in the parent including loss funding for the Samet JV [Q1 call].
- MDF Line 2: ₹425 cr per management (₹300 cr FY27, ₹125 cr FY28) [Q4 call]; CARE puts it at ₹451 cr for 1,000 CBM/day, funded 28:72 equity:debt with a ₹325 cr term loan, commissioning Q2 FY28 [CARE Jul-26]. Cheaper per CBM than Line 1 — part-Chinese press, European fibre line [Q1 call].
- Odisha greenfield plywood: ~₹130–160 cr (₹130 cr debt-funded), commissioning Dec 2026; plus ~₹50 cr land development in Odisha [Q4 call; CARE Jul-26].
- Plywood ContiRoll technology: ₹45–50 cr across plants [Q4 call].
- Payback: flooring line peak revenue ₹75–80 cr (flooring sells at ₹60,000–70,000/CBM vs ₹24,000–25,000 for plain board); WPC plant peak ₹75–80 cr [Q1 call; Q4 call]. MDF ROCE was about 8% in FY26 (analyst figure, not disputed) and needs 100% utilisation to reach the 17–18% target [Q1 call].
- Hardware JV: ₹105 cr equity invested so far; Phase 2 capex to localise imported products due end-FY27/early FY28 [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]. Board also has an ₹115 cr commitment for a proposed investment in an entity [AR FY26 note 38].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹533 cr at 30 Jun 2026 (₹461 cr Mar-26), net debt/equity 0.57x; gross debt ₹554 cr (ply ₹180 cr, MDF ₹374 cr) [IP Q1FY27]. Borrowing cost ~7–7.25% term loans, ~7% working capital [Q1 call].
- FY26 operating cash flow ₹247 cr, free cash flow ₹91 cr [Screener]. CARE sees debt-service cover "close to unity" during the capex [CARE Jul-26]; unencumbered cash only ₹27.5 cr at Mar-26, but working-capital lines only ~21% used [CARE Jul-26].
- Working capital 42 days (58 days a year earlier); receivable days 54 in FY26 vs 47 in FY25 — management says OEM sales are LC-backed and "we are not satisfied with these numbers" [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call; Screener].
- Guarantees given for subsidiaries and the JV total ₹554.6 cr [AR FY26 note 38]. Dividend ₹0.50/share [Q4 call].

## Moat evidence
- Brand: premium plywood leader; BIS rules have removed cheap MDF imports and some premium imported plywood [Q4 call].
- Share gains: West Asia war made resin suppliers demand cash; many unorganised plywood makers shut or ran three days a week [Q4 call]. Price rises: MDF industry +15% list (Greenply netted 7–9% after schemes), plywood 3–5% [Q1 call].
- Cost position is weak in plywood: 8.5% average EBITDA over three years vs ~13.5–14% for Century (analyst comparison, accepted by management) because of smaller scale and shared overheads [Q1 call].
- Hardware: dealer landing price 8–10% better than Hettich, but loses on imports; BIS enforcement in hardware is poor [Q1 call].
- Rating CARE AA-/Stable/A1+ reaffirmed 9 Jul 2026 [CARE Jul-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Income-tax search** at company premises, 26 Feb–2 Mar 2026 (same raid covered Greenlam and Greenpanel); no order or demand yet; disclosed in the auditor-reviewed notes [AR FY26 note 38; official Q4 transcript].
- **CEO exit**: JMD & CEO Manoj Tulsian resigned (now adviser); no replacement planned "immediately"; HR, IT and sales heads also changed in six months [Q4 call]. Promoter family (Rajesh and Sanidhya Mittal) now runs the business directly.
- **International write-offs every year**: FY26 exceptional ₹15.2 cr on GMEL, Dubai (guarantee ₹9.5 cr, investment ₹2.7 cr, advances ₹3.0 cr); FY25 ₹6.6 cr on Greenply Holdings, Singapore; stake in GMEL cut from 49% to 19% and entity renamed Greenwud Panel [AR FY26 note 34; IP Q1FY27; CARE Jul-26].
- Hardware JV losing ~₹11.5 cr a quarter; auditor CARO report flags cash losses at the JV (clause xvii) and delays in statutory-dues deposits (clause vii(a)) at the parent and subsidiaries [AR FY26 consolidated CARO table; Q1 call].
- Contingent liabilities modest: indirect tax ₹26.9 cr, consumer cases ₹2.8 cr [AR FY26 note 38].
- Related parties: family-linked Greenlam and Greenpanel are listed related parties with small approved trades (e.g., sales to Greenlam up to ₹10 cr) [AR FY26 note 39; corporate governance report].
- Debt rising into an MDF industry that still has 28% spare capacity [AR FY26 MD&A]; chemical costs spiking again in July [Q1 call].
- Auditor opinion unmodified; key audit matter: revenue recognition [AR FY26 auditor's reports].

## What changed between the two calls
- From "new norm" 12% margin to 10.8% and an admission of lost sales.
- Debt peak lifted from 0.70–0.72x to 0.75x (₹710–730 cr).
- New talking points: ContiRoll technology, labour/election disruption, chemical inflation returning in July.
- Dropped: CEO exit and tax search not raised on the Q1 call.

- "We will hit peak debt… anything between 710 to 725, 730… debt equity that day we'll be at 0.75x." — Sanidhya Mittal [Q1 call]

## My read
Volumes are genuinely strong and MDF is now a solid 17% margin business, so the growth story is improving. But plywood margins depend on hitting ₹600 cr+ a quarter, debt is heading to 0.75x equity, and governance noise (tax search, CEO exit, repeated overseas write-offs) has not gone away. The price needs plywood back above 10% margin and Line 2 filled quickly after Q2 FY28 to justify the multiple. Positive trigger: two quarters of ₹600 cr+ plywood sales at 10%+; negative trigger: a tax demand, JV losses beyond mid-FY28, or MDF prices cracking as new industry capacity arrives.
