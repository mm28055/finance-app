# Carysil (CARYSIL) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹1,064, market cap ₹3,027 cr, 52-week range ₹732–1,281, Screener P/E 28.1, ROCE 18.0%, ROE 17.4% [Screener consolidated, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- India's leading maker of composite quartz ("granite") kitchen sinks, mostly exported; also stainless-steel sinks (subsidiary Carysilnox), kitchen appliances (hoods/hobs; chimneys assembled in-house), faucets, the Sternhagen bath brand, and stone/solid-surface fabrication in the UK (Carysil Products, Carysil Surfaces) and US (United Granite) [AR FY26; ICRA Mar-26].
- Installed capacity: quartz sinks 10 lakh a year (88% used in Q1 FY27), steel sinks 2.5 lakh (~94% on a weighted basis), appliances 0.5 lakh, faucets 0.5 lakh [IP Aug-26].
- Q1 FY27 revenue mix: quartz sinks 51%, steel sinks 12%, kitchen appliances 11.8%, surfaces 25% [Q1 call]. India-operations sales: domestic ₹56 cr (+39.8%), exports ₹111 cr (+10.6%) [Q1 call].
- Customers: IKEA, Lowe's, Home Depot (US & Canada, extended), Howdens (UK), Karran, Amazon USA (first order), Hafele (Australia/NZ, India), Kohler, Grohe [Q1 call; IP Aug-26]. Present in 55+ countries; 5,000+ Indian dealers [IP Aug-26].
- FY26 volumes: quartz 7.82 lakh units (+~20%), steel 1.67 lakh, appliances & others 71,000; faucets 36,000 (+44%) [IP Aug-26; AR FY26 chairman's letter]. Domestic revenue ₹178 cr in FY26, target ₹500 cr by FY29/FY30 [IP Aug-26].
- Q1 FY27 volumes: quartz 2.01 lakh (+6%), steel 49,400 (+16%), appliances 9,800 (+12%, 53% made in-house), faucets 12,500 (+43%, 67% in-house) [Q1 call].
- US subsidiary United Granite: revenue US$9.7 m (+15%), EBITDA ₹11.4 cr in FY26 vs a loss two years earlier [AR FY26]. UK subsidiaries: revenue down as the UK market contracted [AR FY26].

- Unit economics: India realisation +12% with volume +25% [Q1 call]; kitchen hoods/faucets ~50–60% gross margin [Q4 call]; quartz and steel ASPs up on premium ranges and the US discount rollback [Q1 call].
- Forex gain only ₹2.5 cr in Q4 (<1% of margin) [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (21 May 2026): growth 15–20% with 18–20% EBITDA margin; India ₹500 cr "within 5 years" (30–40% a year); quartz +2.5 lakh units "operational in quarter Q4-FY27"; steel +70,000 units to mature within 90 days; appliances +50,000 (phase 1) in FY28; FY27 capex ₹70–75 cr; surfaces growth 7–10%; India marketing ~10% of sales [official Q4 transcript].
- Q1 delivery (11 Aug 2026): margin 21.2% — above the band; steel at ~94% use (**delivered**); 90% of US tariff discounts rolled back (**delivered**); quartz growth only 6% because containers slipped into Q2 (**missed** on volume); quartz expansion still Q4 FY27 (**kept**) [Q1 call].
- Q1 guidance: revenue growth "15%" (bottom of the earlier 15–20% range — **narrowed/softened**) and margin at "the upper side of the 18% to 20%" (**raised**); new ambition to add ₹1,000 cr of revenue in five years at 15% a year; FY27 capex raised to ₹80–90 cr (**raised**) [Q1 call].
- Longer term: at least ₹50–60 cr capex a year to sustain 15% growth; UK builder projects and B2B India to show in Q2 [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹68 cr [official Q4 transcript]. FY27 ₹80–90 cr: ₹40–50 cr quartz (+2.5 lakh to 12.5 lakh units, March 2027), ₹20 cr steel (+1.5 lakh units, March 2027, new factory on adjacent land), ₹20 cr faucets and appliances [Q1 call]. Infrastructure is being built for 5 lakh quartz sinks, so later additions are cheaper [Q1 call].
- Appliances: new plant with infrastructure for 2 lakh units, ₹30–40 cr, phase 1 (50,000) in FY28; seeking a technical tie-up with an international firm [Q4 call].
- India CNC surface-fabrication unit by March 2027; Carysil Blue bathroom stores (first 10 by March 2027) [Q1 call].
- Payback: management expects the new quartz line to fill within a year ("less than 6 months" if things go well) given "the highest ever export order booking" [Q4 call; Q1 call]. ₹40–50 cr for 2.5 lakh sinks (~25% more quartz capacity) implies very high asset turns if filled (computed).
- Funding: internal accruals plus existing lines; no large debt-funded capex planned [ICRA Mar-26].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Gross debt ₹270 cr, cash ₹59 cr at 31 Mar 2026 (net debt ~₹211 cr) [official Q4 transcript]. QIP proceeds (FY25) used to cut short-term debt and fund capex [ICRA Mar-26]. ICRA expects total debt/operating profit below 1.5x and DSCR above 2x in FY26–FY27 [ICRA Mar-26].
- Working capital is the weak point: inventory days 319, debtor days 64, cash conversion cycle 257 days in FY26 [Screener]; ICRA cites high inventory from customer-specific SKUs and imported inputs [ICRA Mar-26].
- Operating cash flow ₹109 cr in FY26 but free cash flow –₹2 cr after capex; free cash flow has been near zero or negative in four of the last five years [Screener].
- Dividend ₹3/share proposed for FY26 (₹2.40 for FY25; yield ~0.3%) [AR FY26 AGM notice; Screener]. Rating [ICRA]A (Stable)/A2+ reaffirmed 26 Mar 2026 [ICRA Mar-26].

## Moat evidence
- One of few non-European makers of composite quartz sinks at scale; "sole partner for global brands like Howdens, Karran, and Lowe's"; rising share of IKEA's global quartz supply [IP Aug-26].
- Pricing power shown in 2025–26: absorbed US tariff discounts, then rolled 90% back without losing volume; Lowe's asked for a 50% share of US$3–4 m display costs, which will now not be discounted [Q1 call].
- Cost position: "most cost effective player… in the global sink space" (management claim); European competitors weakened [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Switching costs: joint display investments (US$5–6 m at Lowe's across 1,890 stores) [Q1 call].
- Weak spots: UK surfaces business shrinking; appliances and faucets are young, competitive categories [AR FY26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Cash conversion**: 257-day cash cycle and near-zero free cash flow despite 19–21% margins [Screener].
- Freight/containers: dispatches slipped from Q1 to Q2; factory "has to literally run now 7 days a week" [Q1 call].
- Customer and market concentration: exports ~2/3 of India-operations sales; US tariffs could return [Q1 call].
- Raw materials: MMA (resin) up ~25–35% in early 2026; ICRA flags gas-supply risk from the West Asia war [Q4 call; ICRA Mar-26].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matters include impairment of investments in subsidiaries (₹29.9 cr carrying value); small impairments in FY26 on the Turkish appliance venture and Carysil Ceramictech [AR FY26 auditor's report; RPT note].
- Related parties: CMD's daughter Rhea Parekh employed as VP International Marketing with a remuneration rise sought at the AGM; promoter entity Acrycol Minerals is a supplier; other RPTs small (Café Chico ₹0.01 cr) [AR FY26 AGM notice; RPT note]. New guarantees given for subsidiaries in FY26 were small (₹1.5 cr, Carysil FZ LLC; ₹14.2 cr in FY25) [AR FY26 standalone RPT note].
- Contingent liabilities negligible (₹0.23 cr) [AR FY26 note 33]. Promoters 41.3% [Screener].

## My read
The business is improving: margins are at a record 21%, steel is sold out, and the order book supports the March 2027 capacity additions. But growth in Q1 was only ~15% on Screener sales, quartz volume grew 6%, and heavy inventory keeps free cash flow near zero, so the price needs the new quartz and steel lines filled quickly and working capital to improve. What would change the view: quartz volume growth back to 15%+ in Q2 once containers clear (positive), or margins slipping below 19% as resin and freight costs bite, or a US tariff reset (negative).
