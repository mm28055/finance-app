# Eveready Industries India — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹322, market cap ₹2,341 cr (Screener consolidated, 27 Sep 2026). Promoter: Burman family (Dabur group), 43.6%.

## Business and segments
- India's largest dry-cell battery maker (brands Eveready, PowerCell), plus flashlights and lighting/electricals (LED bulbs, emergency bulbs, luminaires, mosquito rackets, wires, MCBs). One reported segment (consumer goods), but the MD&A gives segment figures. [AR FY26 note 32.3; AR FY26 MD&A]
- FY26 segments: batteries revenue ~₹972 cr (+9.3%), EBITDA ₹154.4 cr (15.9%); flashlights ₹179.7 cr (+3.1%), EBITDA ₹10.6 cr (5.9%, down from ₹12.4 cr); lighting & electricals ₹340.9 cr (+8%), EBITDA breakeven. Batteries earn ~90% of group EBITDA. [AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Oct-25]
- Q1 FY27: batteries ₹267.6 cr (64% of sales, +11.9%), flashlights ₹63.0 cr (–6.7%; rechargeables +20%), lighting ₹87.5 cr (+13.7%). Alkaline battery revenue ₹20.6 → ₹32.1 cr (+56%; ~48% volume), now 12% of battery revenue; carbon-zinc +8%. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Market: dry-cell market ~₹4,000 cr (~₹4,100 cr at MRP), growing ~5%; carbon-zinc ~85–90% and flat, alkaline ~₹550 cr growing 20–24%. Eveready holds ~58% of carbon-zinc and ~18% of alkaline (from ~3% 30 months ago; Duracell leads alkaline), >50% of the total. Nippo and Panasonic are the zinc challengers. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- Reach: ~4.5 mn retail touchpoints, ~0.6 mn directly covered; 1.3 bn+ batteries, 17 mn+ flashlights, 36 mn+ LED lights sold a year. Plants: Matia, Lucknow, Haridwar, Maddur, Kolkata and (from May 2026) Jammu; Noida closed. [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / net profit: Jun-25 374 / 54 / 30; Sep-25 387 / 49 / –8; Dec-25 367 / 33 / 7; Mar-26 327 / 28 / 142; Jun-26 408 / 61 / 37. OPM 14% → 13% → 9% → 9% → 15%. [Screener]
- Annual: FY24 sales 1,314, OP 141 (11%), PAT 67; FY25 1,345, 153 (11%), 82; FY26 1,455, 164 (11%), 172. TTM sales 1,489, PAT 178. Five-year sales CAGR only 3%; ROE 18–19%, ROCE 17%. [Screener]
- **FY26 profit is flattered by one-offs**: net exceptional gain ₹48.6 cr (Noida Plot B1 gain ₹105.2 cr less ₹32.3 cr workmen ex-gratia, ₹15 cr Real Touch arbitration settlement, ₹9.4 cr labour-code charge) and a tax *credit* from recognising ₹85.1 cr of old MAT credit as a deferred tax asset. Standalone PBT before exceptionals was ₹118 cr. Stripping these, underlying TTM profit is roughly half the reported ₹178 cr. [AR FY26 notes 30, 31]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹407.7 cr (+9.0%, 7th straight quarter of growth), EBITDA ₹61.5 cr (15.1% vs 15.0%), PAT ₹37.0 cr (+22%, helped by last year's ₹7.1 cr exceptional). No one-offs in Q1 per CFO. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Unit economics: zinc ~$3,500/t vs <$3,000 most of last year; rupee ~94–95/$; price increases taken in Q4 FY26–April 2026 held margins; A&P spend ~10% of sales. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (30 Apr 2026): FY27 EBITDA margin "hold around" FY26's 11.5%; A&P ~10% of sales; possible Q2 price increase; alkaline share 16% now, "exiting with 20%" by end of next year; Jammu commercial production "in a few weeks", ≥30% use by year-end, >100 mn units in year 1, operating breakeven from year 1, payback 5–6 years; more debt reduction; shift to 22% tax regime in FY27 subject to carried-forward losses. [Q4 call, official]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: margin 15.1% (well above 11.5%); Jammu commercial production 29 May (met); alkaline share 18% (on track); Noida Plot D2 sale still pending (slipped); J&K GST incentive approval still pending (no movement). [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 changes: margin "aspiration" is to sustain Q1 levels but no number; alkaline exit share raised to 25–30% within 2 years; "like-to-like debt free" in 4–5 quarters; wires/MCBs to double in FY27; long-term vision (and any M&A stance) deferred "1 or 2 quarters". [Q1 call]
- Verdict: margin guidance **beaten**, alkaline target **raised**, long-term guidance **still withheld** (promised since Q4). [Q4 call vs Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Jammu alkaline plant: ~₹200 cr total (CRISIL: ~₹180 cr), plant & machinery ₹90–95 cr; installed 456 mn cells/yr, peak practical output ~360 mn; also has carbon-zinc, flashlight and lighting lines. Funded by a sanctioned ₹150 cr term loan plus internal cash; CWIP was ₹176 cr at March 2026. Pre-operative costs were capitalised up to 29 May. [AR FY26 MD&A; CRISIL Oct-25; Screener; Q1 call]
- Economics: alkaline gross margin +~10 points once stable (imports replaced); alkaline is only 6–7% of company sales, so the group-level lift is ~0.6–0.7 point of margin at today's mix — larger as alkaline grows 50%+. Payback 5–6 years; white-label exports a later option. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Incentive: J&K NCSS scheme — GST refunds up to 3x eligible plant & machinery over 10 years; approval not yet received. [Q1 call]
- No further major capex beyond routine; renewable power via leasing, not ownership. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net debt ₹178 cr at March 2026 per company (₹201 cr including ₹22 cr leases), after ₹100+ cr repaid in FY26 and Jammu funded; CFO said ~₹165 cr at the Q1 call. Debt/equity 0.3x (0.7x a year earlier). [AR FY26 Board's report; AR FY26 note on net debt; Q1 call]
- Noida sale: Plot B1 done (proceeds ~₹116 cr); Plot B2 ~₹136 cr with ₹44 cr advance received, ~₹90 cr due on closing — first use is debt reduction. [Q4 call, official]
- FY26 operating cash flow ₹83 cr (after ₹44.8 cr of exceptional cash payouts); cash conversion cycle 69 days; inventory days 134. Dividend payout ~11%. [Screener; AR FY26 cash-flow statement]
- Rating: CRISIL A+/Stable, A1 (assigned Oct 2025) — gearing 0.7x, interest cover ~6x in FY25; CRISIL expected mid-single-digit revenue growth and an 11–12% EBITDA margin. [CRISIL Oct-25]

## Moat evidence
- Share: >50% of India's dry-cell market for decades; 58% of carbon-zinc; alkaline share from ~3% to ~18% in 30 months while Eveready still imported the product. [Q1 call; CRISIL Oct-25]
- Pricing power: passed zinc/EMD inflation through several price rounds and lifted Q1 margin to 15.1%; CEO warns another round may be needed. [Q1 call]
- Distribution: 4.5 mn outlets — "uniquely positioned in the general trade" for lighting too. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- Only operating alkaline plant in India (company claim) — a cost and supply advantage over importers as alkaline grows. [Q1 call]
- Weak spots: lighting is a crowded, loss-to-breakeven business; flashlights are mostly unbranded/Chinese (70% of market); BIS mandate is "on paper" and not yet enforced. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **CCI penalty ₹171.55 cr** (2018 cartel order, zinc-carbon batteries 2008–16), stayed by NCLAT with 10% deposited; shown as a contingent liability; auditor's **emphasis of matter**. Because Eveready applied under the lesser-penalty (leniency) rules, "a certain amount of penalty will be levied". Next hearing was due end-September 2026. [AR FY26 auditor's report; note 32.1; Q1 call]
- **Legacy promoter baggage**: ₹15 cr paid (July 2025) to settle Real Touch Finance's arbitration over old McLeod/Williamson group loans; ~₹500 cr of old inter-corporate deposit (ICD) provisions written off for tax, creating the losses that shield the Noida gain from tax. [AR FY26 note 30; Q4 call; CRISIL Oct-25]
- **Non-executive director pay**: the RPT note shows ₹2.1 cr commission a year (FY26 and FY25) to non-executive director Arjun Lamba. On both calls analysts questioned ~₹2 cr paid to a promoter-representative director who is now a whole-time director at another promoter company; management said the Board/NRC approved it and would not commit to stopping it. (The AR does not itself link the two.) [AR FY26 RPT note; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- CARO: the Q4 FY26 stock statement to banks had not been submitted when the audit was signed. [AR FY26 CARO annexure]
- Other contingents: indirect tax ₹19.8 cr, others ₹0.3 cr. EPR (battery recycling) cost is unquantified and under negotiation with regulators. [AR FY26 note 32.1; Q1 call]
- Commodity/currency: zinc and EMD up, rupee weak; flashlight demand hit by a late monsoon. [Q1 call]
- Earnings quality: reported P/E looks low only because of one-off gains and tax credits. [AR FY26 notes 30–31]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone from "hold 11.5%" defensiveness to confident premiumisation (15.1% margin, alkaline 18%). [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New: Jammu live, alkaline target raised, lighting breakeven declared, Q-commerce growth, innovation launches. Still pending: Plot B2 sale, Jammu GST incentive, long-term guidance, CCI outcome. [Q1 call]

## My read
The core is improving: a dominant, cash-generating battery franchise now adding a domestic alkaline plant just as alkaline grows 20%+, with debt heading toward zero after the Noida sale. But reported profits overstate the run-rate (land gain, MAT credit), revenue growth is still high-single-digit, and a 15% quarter came with price hikes that may be tested if zinc stays high. The price therefore needs margins to settle well above the 11.5% guided and alkaline to keep compounding; on underlying earnings it is not cheap. The view improves with the Plot B2 cash, J&K incentive approval and a benign CCI outcome; it worsens if the CCI penalty is upheld in size or margins fall back to 11%.
