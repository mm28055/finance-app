# Sirca Paints India — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹410, market cap ₹2,330 cr (Screener consolidated, 27 Sep 2026). Note: Sirca held **no Q1 FY27 earnings call**; Q1 results and a deck were filed on 28 July 2026. The two latest transcripts are the Q4 FY26 results call (15 May 2026) and an investor event hosted by Finportal ("ValuePortal", 22 Jan 2026) — the latter is not a results call.

## Business and segments
- Wood-coatings specialist: polyurethane (PU), acrylic, polyester and UV finishes under the licensed **Sirca** (Italy) brand, plus Oikos/Unico decorative textures and the acquired mass-market brands **Wembley** and **Welcome** (nitrocellulose lacquers, sanding sealer, melamine, thinners). Brand/technology licence with Sirca S.p.A. runs to 2041; royalty 0.75% of total reported revenue. One reported segment ("wood coating products"). [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 41]
- FY26 revenue ₹492 cr: core Sirca ~₹372 cr (Italian imports ~₹124 cr, made-in-India ~₹252 cr), Wembley ₹74 cr, Welcome ₹46.5 cr. Acrylic (fastest-growing premium line) ~₹120 cr. Exports only ~₹9 cr (Nepal, Sri Lanka). [Q4 call, official]
- Customers: ~70% retail (4,000+ dealers, sold on architect specification; 25,000+ contractors on the Sirca Parivaar app), ~30% OEM (900+ furniture/kitchen/door makers such as Godrej, Spacewood). ~80% of revenue from North India; 20 depots, 5 opened last year in South/West. [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call]
- Share: ~15% of India's premium PU/acrylic wood-coating segment; ~4–4.5% of the ~₹9,500 cr total wood-coating market; target 10% by 2030. [Jan-26 event, official]
- Capacity: core plant 16,000 → 17,750 t/yr (FY26), ~12,000 t used (~68%), single-shift full by end-FY27; core peak revenue ₹480–500 cr. New consolidated Wembley plant (~3x old Wembley capacity) can do ₹250–300 cr. [Q4 call; Jan-26 event]

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / net profit: Jun-25 114 / 23 / 14; Sep-25 131 / 27 / 18; Dec-25 113 / 23 / 15; Mar-26 134 / 26 / 18; Jun-26 130 / 24 / 16. OPM 20% → 21% → 20% → 19% → 19%. [Screener]
- Annual: FY24 sales 311, OP 69 (22%), PAT 51; FY25 372, 67 (18%), 49; FY26 492 (+32%, with Wembley/Welcome ~₹120 cr inorganic), 99 (20%), 65. TTM 508 / 100 / 67. Core Sirca revenue in FY26 (~₹372 cr) equalled the whole company's FY25 revenue (₹372 cr); since FY25 may include part-year acquired sales (goodwill was already on the books at 1 April 2025), organic growth looks modest but cannot be pinned down from disclosures. ROE 16%, ROCE 20%. [Screener; Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 (company): revenue ₹130.0 cr (+13.8%), EBITDA ₹24.2 cr (+6.5%), margin 18.62% (19.90%), PAT ₹16.2 cr (+14.1%). Deck blames crude-linked inputs, freight and an "industry-wide price" environment. [IP Q1FY27]
- Unit economics: localising 11 PU product sets cut average selling price ~20% (from ~₹550/l); of a ~20% cost saving, 12–15% went back to customers as price cuts and 4–5% into marketing — so gross margin rose but EBITDA did not. PU sells ~₹450–460/l vs melamine ~₹230–240/l (~10% more per sq ft). Wembley's gross margin is 10–15 points below Sirca's. [Q4 call; Jan-26 event]
- Price hikes: Sirca +5% (April 2026) and +5% (15 May 2026); Wembley/Welcome ~₹35/litre. [Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Jan 2026 event: FY26 growth guidance "25–40%" (official wording) "more or less in line"; ~25% a year for 2–3 years; EBITDA 19–21%; 10% total-market share by 2030. FY26 delivered +31.8% (inorganic-aided) and 20.1% margin. [Jan-26 event, official; Q4 call]
- Q4 FY26 call (15 May 2026): 25–30% revenue CAGR "as minimum"; EBITDA 19–21%; ₹1,000 cr revenue by FY29 (~27% a year needed); Wembley + Valentino +40% in FY27; exports 3–4% of revenue from Q1; acrylic/polyester/UV local production from Q1 FY27; inventory down ₹15–20 cr by June and debtors down as Wembley transition ends; FY27 capex only ₹5–6 cr. [Q4 call, official; AR FY26 MD&A repeats 25–30% and ₹1,000 cr by FY29]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +13.8% (**missed** 25–30% badly), margin 18.6% (**below** 19–21% band), local acrylic/polyester production started (met). No call to explain. Deck now says "target double-digit revenue growth with EBITDA" — a softer target than 25–30%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Verdict: growth guidance effectively **cut** (from 25–30% to "double-digit") without a call; margin band **missed** in Q1; ₹1,000 cr by FY29 now looks stretched. [Q4 call vs IP Q1FY27]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: FY26 capex ~₹13.5 cr (₹10 cr to consolidate Wembley/Welcome's 3–4 small units into one plant, ₹3.5 cr for +20% core capacity via premix reactors); FY27 capex ₹5–6 cr for acrylic. Management says current plants can support ~₹750–800 cr revenue (core ₹480–500 cr + Wembley ₹250–300 cr) — "more or less sorted" for ₹1,000 cr. [Q4 call; Jan-26 event]
- Balance-sheet fixed assets ₹174 cr at March 2026, but ₹100.75 cr of that is **goodwill** from the Wembley/Welcome purchase (FY25). Tangible asset turn is very high (>5x). [Screener; AR FY26 intangible-assets note]
- Funding: preferential issue of 19.84 lakh shares at ₹379.50 (₹75.3 cr) to non-promoter investors, allotted Sept 2025 (promoter stake 67.5% → 65.2%). Borrowings ₹32 cr; investments ₹57 cr. [AR FY26 Board's report; Screener]
- Payback on the Wembley deal is not yet visible: Wembley sales ₹74 cr in FY26 vs ~₹72 cr at acquisition. [Q4 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Working capital is the weak spot: cash conversion cycle 193 days, debtor days up from 92 to 115, inventory days 151 (six months of imported acrylic safety stock), working-capital days 152. FY26 operating cash flow ₹44 cr (67% of operating profit); free cash flow ₹17 cr; FY25 FCF –₹53 cr (acquisition). [Screener; Q4 call]
- Management blamed the Wembley transition (old owners collected first) and import safety stock; promised relief from June 2026. Q1 FY27 balance sheet not in the deck — unverified. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Net cash: borrowings ₹32 cr vs cash ~₹43 cr and investments ₹57 cr at March 2026 — net cash positive. Dividend ₹2/share (payout ~17%). [Screener; AR FY26 note 44; AR FY26 AGM notice]
- No credit rating listed on Screener; none found. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Specification selling: architects "specify like a doctor"; 100% of retail sale is "secondary" (pulled by specification). Premium segment faces "lesser challenge" from big paint makers; mass segment is where competition bites. [Q4 call]
- Italian licence to 2041 plus 20-year architect relationships; ~15% share of premium PU/acrylic. [Q4 call; Jan-26 event]
- But pricing power is limited: localisation savings were largely handed to customers as competitors localised too ("the competition has also followed the same activity"). [Q4 call]
- Structural tailwind: furniture BIS rules and factory-made furniture shift demand to PU/acrylic from melamine; OEM channel growing. [Q4 call]

## Risks and red flags
- **Growth slowdown and silence**: Q1 FY27 growth of 13.8% vs 25–30% guided, margin below band, and no earnings call held to explain it. [IP Q1FY27; Screener document list]
- **Working capital** at ~190+ days ties up most of the equity raised; debtor days rising while expanding into new regions. [Screener]
- **Wembley execution**: flat year one, old distributors pre-loaded ₹5–6 cr before hand-over, NC-cotton (Nitrex) shortage lost ₹4–5 cr of March sales; ₹100.75 cr goodwill (~21% of equity) is exposed if it stays flat. [Q4 call; AR FY26]
- Auditor's opinion unmodified; key audit matter is revenue recognition (cut-off risk around year-end, credit notes after year-end). IFC opinion unmodified. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- FY26 are the company's **first Ind AS financial statements** — comparisons with earlier years need care. [AR FY26 notes]
- Secretarial audit: still regularising FEMA filings (FC-TRS not filed for FY2008-09 and FY2009-10). [AR FY26 secretarial audit report]
- Related parties: KMP pay ₹3.1 cr plus ₹0.6 cr to relatives (daughter and son-in-law of the MD); promoter group includes BGB Italia. No contingent liabilities disclosed. [AR FY26 note 42]
- Licence dependence on Sirca S.p.A. (to 2041); 80% North India concentration; raw materials are crude-linked (solvents + resins ~95% of RM). [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two transcripts (Jan 2026 event → Q4 FY26 call)
- From integration ("teething problems mostly over") to expansion (Wembley Valentino exports to the Middle East, acrylic localisation, South/West push). [Jan-26 event; Q4 call]
- New issues at Q4: raw-material inflation and freight from the West Asia conflict, NC-cotton shortage, stretched working capital. Growth guidance narrowed from "25–40%" (FY26) to 25–30% CAGR. [Q4 call]
- After Q4 (deck only): growth target softened again to "double-digit". [IP Q1FY27]

## My read
Sirca is a capital-light niche leader with real specification-based demand and a net-cash balance sheet, and the shift from melamine to PU/acrylic is a genuine long-run tailwind. But FY26's 32% growth was mostly bought (Wembley/Welcome), organic core growth was thin, localisation gains were competed away, working capital is very heavy, and Q1 FY27 undershot guidance by half without a call. At roughly 35 times trailing earnings the price still assumes ~20%+ compounding; that needs Wembley to grow, the South/West push to work and receivables to come down. The view improves if H1 FY27 growth returns above 20% with margins back at 19–21% and a cash-flow improvement; it worsens if growth stays in the low teens.
