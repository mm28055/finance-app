# Bajaj Electricals (BAJAJELEC) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹319, market cap ₹3,681 cr, 52-week range ₹301–575; book value ₹138/share; TTM net profit –₹43 cr so no P/E [Screener, 27 Sep 2026].

## Business and segments
- Two reported segments. **Consumer Products (CP)**: fans, coolers, mixer-grinders, water heaters, irons, kitchen appliances under Bajaj, Morphy Richards (premium) and Nirlep (cookware). **Lighting Solutions (LS)**: consumer LED plus professional lighting projects (smart cities, stadiums, industrial), and since FY26 switchgear, solar and wires (booked inside LS) [AR FY26 note on segments; Q1 call].
- Mix: CP about 75% of revenue, LS 25% [CRISIL Apr-26]. FY26 revenue from contracts: CP ₹3,335 cr, LS ₹1,119 cr [AR FY26 note 41]. FY26 CP revenue –12.2%, LS +9.5%; LS full-year EBIT margin about 8.5%, a record [AR FY26 MD's letter; Q4 call].
- Q1 FY27 segments: CP revenue ₹820 cr (+1.7%), EBIT ₹32 cr, 3.9% (–1.7% a year ago); LS ₹269 cr (+4.4%), EBIT ₹18 cr, 6.7% (10.6%) [IP Q1FY27].
- Market position: No. 1 in mixer-grinders, No. 2 in water heaters [AR FY26 MD's letter]; top 2–3 in appliances, coolers, dry irons; top five in fans and lighting [CRISIL Apr-26].
- Channels: general trade about 55%, "alternate" (e-commerce, modern trade, institutions, exports) about 45%; e-commerce 15–18%, quick commerce 8–10% of e-commerce [Q1 call]. In Q1 GT fell ~2% while alternate channels grew ~11% and exports grew "high double digit" [IP Q1FY27]. Strongest in the East, weak in the South [Q1 call]. Network: 2,000+ channel partners, ~2 lakh outlets, 778 service centres [AR FY26].
- Morphy Richards brand and IP for India and South Asia bought from Glen Dimplex for ₹141.4 cr (plus ₹22.4 cr withholding tax and ₹4.2 cr stamp duty) on 16 Mar 2026, after 24 years as licensee [AR FY26 note 45].

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated)
- Annual revenue FY24 ₹4,639 cr → FY25 ₹4,826 cr → FY26 ₹4,461 cr; operating profit ₹260 cr (6%) → ₹307 cr (6%) → ₹138 cr (3.1%); net profit ₹131 cr → ₹133 cr → –₹91 cr [Screener]. Ten-year revenue CAGR 0%.
- Quarters (revenue / operating profit / margin / net profit): Jun-25 ₹1,065 cr / ₹33 cr / 3.1% / ₹1 cr; Sep-25 ₹1,107 cr / ₹57 cr / 5% / ₹10 cr; Dec-25 ₹1,051 cr / ₹8 cr / 0.8% / –₹34 cr; Mar-26 ₹1,240 cr / ₹40 cr / 3.2% / –₹68 cr; Jun-26 ₹1,089 cr / ₹77 cr / 7% / ₹48 cr [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 as reported: revenue +2.3%; gross margin 32.3%, +130 bps (CP +220 bps); EBIT ₹72 cr, 6.6% (2.5%) [IP Q1FY27, Q1 call]. **Quality check:** EBIT includes ₹26 cr other income; without it EBIT is about ₹46 cr (4.2%). PBT ₹66 cr includes a ~₹9 cr gain on selling property. Finance cost ₹16 cr includes ~₹10 cr interest on vendor financing [IP Q1FY27].
- FY26 exceptional charges ₹91 cr: labour-code gratuity ₹28.7 cr, Nirlep (Aurangabad) goodwill impairment ₹26.4 cr, moulds/dies impairment ₹29.3 cr, Nashik VRS ₹6.7 cr [AR FY26 note 44].
- Unit economics: cost inflation of 6–13% by category in Q1 (coolers highest), covered by a mix of price rises and savings [Q1 call]. CP price rises of 3–10% in Q4, another round in April and one announced for May [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (20 May 2026): grow "ahead of the market", which it put at mid-single digit; profit to reach industry average "between 6 to 8%, 9%"; LS EBIT "may be close to where we are" (~8.5%) as extra gross margin is reinvested; fix BLDC fan gap "in the next 12 months"; working capital to stay negative; "whatever we could find… we have taken care of" on exceptional items [Q4 call].
- Q1 call (6 Aug 2026): new numeric targets — revenue growth of 8–10% a year for two years (industry 6–7%); CP EBIT margin to "stabilize between six to 7%" for two years, then move toward 10% [Q1 call]. **Guidance made more specific and slightly raised.**
- Delivery vs Q4 promises: CP swung to +3.9% EBIT (delivered on the turnaround); LS margin fell to 6.7% from ~8.5% (**missed** — blamed on pre-war fixed-price professional lighting contracts and wires start-up; double digits promised "in a quarter or two"); fans lost more share (**missed**; recovery now promised in 2–3 quarters); stock correction described as "an ongoing journey" after Q4 said inventory was "much lower" except coolers (**softened**); no new exceptional charges — a ₹9 cr property gain instead (**kept**).
- Revenue growth delivered 2.3% in Q1, against the 8–10% annual ambition — needs acceleration from Q2.
- Future: a new Chief Growth Officer hired to build new businesses; cables "seriously" evaluated; "company of a different kind and a different size" in five years [Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Q4 call: capex "will be less than half" of past levels for FY27–FY28, mainly moulds and innovation [Q4 call]. CRISIL expects ₹30–60 cr a year funded from cash accruals [CRISIL Apr-26]. Open capital commitments fell to ₹2.9 cr from ₹15.5 cr [AR FY26 note 40].
- Past capex did not pay: fixed assets rose from ₹531 cr (FY21) to ₹908 cr (FY26) while revenue fell [Screener]; FY26 wrote down ₹29 cr of moulds and ₹26 cr of Nirlep goodwill [AR FY26 note 44].
- The main capital call is the brand: Morphy Richards instalments of ₹49 cr (paid), ₹49 cr by 31 Mar 2027 and ₹43.4 cr by 31 Mar 2028 [AR FY26 note 45].
- Growth engines for FY27–FY29: Morphy Richards premium appliances (double-digit growth in Q1), BLDC fans (industry BLDC share 30–35%, Bajaj "under-indexed"), wires/switchgear/solar inside LS ("progress in wires is better than what we were expecting"), exports (almost doubled in Q1), e-commerce and quick commerce [Q1 call]. No revenue disclosed for wires.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and treasury investments ₹884 cr at 30 Jun 2026, down from ₹934 cr at 31 Mar 2026 [IP Q1FY27, Q4 call]. Q1 operating cash flow was negative, blamed on withholding tax and GST on the Morphy deal [Q1 call].
- FY26 operating cash flow ₹619 cr (FY25 ₹347 cr); Q4 alone ~₹400 cr, half from inventory, half from receivables [Screener; Q4 call].
- Reported borrowings ₹159 cr (mostly leases); CRISIL calls it "nil debt" [Screener; CRISIL Apr-26]. **But the balance sheet carries ₹1,508 cr of "trade credits": ₹656 cr of receivables factored or channel-financed (still on the books, with a matching liability) and ₹853 cr of supplier credit including vendor financing on which Bajaj pays interest** [AR FY26 note 22.1]. Payable days rose to 237 from 195 in FY23 and the cash conversion cycle is –79 days [Screener]. The "negative working capital" is partly bank-financed.
- Debtor days 93, inventory days 64 [Screener]; inventory held at 50–60 days in Q1 [Q1 call]. Inventory provision ₹48 cr against ₹534 cr stock (standalone) [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Dividend ₹3/share (₹34.6 cr) held despite the loss [AR FY26 chairman's letter].

## Moat evidence
- Brand and reach: No. 1 mixer-grinders, No. 2 water heaters; share stable in most categories, gaining in irons and mixer-grinders [Q1 call; AR FY26].
- Pricing: gross margin up 130 bps in Q1 while absorbing 6–13% cost inflation [IP Q1FY27, Q1 call] — some pricing power in appliances.
- Weak spots: fans — "we know we are not competitive in fans" (BLDC gap) [Q4 call]; LED lighting is commoditised; GT channel shrinking (–2% in Q1) [IP Q1FY27]. Ten-year sales CAGR 0% and five-year ROE 7% [Screener] say the moat has not earned its cost of capital.
- Group support: part of the Bajaj group; Jamnalal Sons has lent in the past [CRISIL Apr-26]. Rating Crisil AA-/Stable; bank limits cut to ₹1,400 cr from ₹2,000 cr at the company's request [CRISIL Apr-26].

## Risks and red flags
- Hidden leverage: ₹1,508 cr of factoring and vendor-finance liabilities vs ₹884–934 cr cash [AR FY26 note 22.1; IP Q1FY27]. Vendor-finance interest ~₹10 cr a quarter [IP Q1FY27].
- Earnings quality: Q1 EBIT includes ₹26 cr other income and PBT a ₹9 cr property gain [IP Q1FY27]; FY26 had ₹91 cr exceptional charges and FY25 ₹60 cr [AR FY26 note 44].
- Contingent liabilities about ₹253 cr (GST/sales tax ₹185 cr, up from ₹148 cr; income tax ₹7.3 cr; claims ₹20 cr; E-waste EPR dispute ₹28.9 cr) plus ₹10 cr of bank guarantees still in place for the demerged EPC business (Bajel Projects) [AR FY26 note 40].
- Auditor: unmodified opinion; key audit matters were revenue timing on distributor sales, inventory ageing provisions and ₹190 cr goodwill (Starlite, Nirlep) impairment [AR FY26 auditor's report]. Revenue discounts rose to ₹424 cr (₹361 cr) [AR FY26 note 41].
- Management churn: new CFO Ashween Anand joined in 2026 after an acting CFO period; MD Sanjay Sachdeva in first full year; investor call of May 2026 was cancelled once for "technical reasons" [Q4 call].
- Competition: every player is entering wires, fans and appliances; Bajaj is a late entrant in wires and switchgear [Q1 call].
- Related parties: small (rent, services, CSR to group entities in lakhs) [AR FY26 RPT note]. Promoters 62.7% [IP Q1FY27]; no pledge reported.

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: from "modest performance" and apologies to "turnaround" and "very satisfying" margins.
- Numbers: Q4 gave only directional aims; Q1 gave 8–10% growth and 6–7% CP margin for two years.
- LS flipped from star (8.7% EBIT in Q4) to laggard (6.7%); CP flipped from loss to profit.
- New issues: PCB and gas shortages in fan production; legacy fixed-price lighting contracts; Morphy tax outflow turning Q1 cash flow negative.

## My read
The turnaround is real but thin: CP is back in profit and gross margins are up, yet reported 6.6% EBIT leans on other income and 2.3% growth is far from the 8–10% target. The balance sheet looks cash-rich but ₹1,508 cr of factoring and vendor finance means the true cushion is smaller than headlines suggest. At ₹3,681 cr market cap (about 0.8x sales) the price needs only a return to FY25 profits (~₹130 cr) to look reasonable; it needs sustained 8–10% growth to re-rate. What would change my view: two more quarters of CP margin at 4%+ with fans stabilising (positive), or LS margin failing to recover and trade credits rising (negative).
