# Greenpanel Industries (GREENPANEL) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Figures from Screener standalone page (Screener's consolidated page stops at FY24; the company now reports standalone only). Share price ₹163, market cap ₹1,994 cr, 52-week range ₹149–314 [Screener].

## Business and segments
- India's largest MDF (medium-density fibreboard) maker with a small plywood arm. MDF was 91.2% of FY26 revenue; plywood is under 10% [AR FY26 MD&A; CARE Aug-26].
- MDF capacity 8.91 lakh cubic metres (CBM) a year across Uttarakhand (Rudrapur) and Andhra Pradesh (Chittoor); the AP brownfield line (2.31 lakh CBM) was commissioned March 2025 at a cost of ₹613 cr, of which ~₹372 cr was debt [CARE Aug-26; IP Q1FY27].
- Utilisation is low: MDF 66% in FY25 → 56% in FY26 → 51% in Q1 FY27 (47% in Q1 FY26). Plywood capacity 9 mn sq m, utilisation 49% FY26, 56% Q1 FY27 [CARE Aug-26; IP Q1FY27].
- Channel: ~90% of sales through dealers; 1,750+ distributors/stockists and ~12,500 retailers; top-10 dealers only 17% of sales [CARE Aug-26]. Management puts retail at ~75–80% of MDF and OEMs (furniture factories) the rest [Q1 call].
- Exports were ~₹145 cr in FY26 (roughly a tenth of sales), 80–85% to the Middle East; they fell to zero in Q1 FY27 because container freight jumped from $400–500 to $5,500–6,000 after the West Asia war began in March 2026 [CARE Aug-26; Q1 call].
- Value-added MDF (pre-laminated, high-density water-resistant, flooring) was 43% of FY26 volume and 55% of value; pre-lam sells at up to a 50% premium to plain board [Q4 call].

## Key numbers
- Quarterly revenue (₹ cr): Jun-25 328, Sep-25 396, Dec-25 416, Mar-26 399, Jun-26 350; Screener operating margin -4.8%, 6.3%, 9.8%, 7.5%, 8.5%; net profit -34.6, -6.1, 10.2, 1.4, 1.2 [Screener].
- Annual: revenue FY23 ₹1,783 cr → FY24 1,567 → FY25 1,436 → FY26 1,539; operating margin 23% → 16% → 9% → 5%; net profit ₹230 cr → 135 → 72 → -29 [Screener]. FY26 Screener margin includes a ₹49 cr euro-loan forex loss; company "operating EBITDA" excluding it was ₹132.7 cr (8.8%) [Q4 call; CARE Aug-26].
- Q1 FY27: revenue ₹350 cr (+8.5%); domestic MDF volume +12% (retail ~+20%, OEM -14%), total MDF volume -2.3% (no exports); domestic MDF realisation ₹31,758/CBM, +7.4%; gross margin 52.7% (+570 bp); company operating EBITDA ₹33.5 cr (9.6%); MDF operating margin 10.3% vs 4.4%; plywood margin 2.7% (7.5% in Q4) [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call].
- The gross-margin jump had temporary help: price hikes later rolled back, low-cost inventory carried from Q4, and no low-price export/OEM sales. Timber-mix changes and efficiency saved 6–7% [Q1 call].
- Unit economics: at ₹31,758/CBM and ~10% MDF margin, profit is about ₹3,200 per CBM against ₹5,000–6,000 in FY22–23 when margins were 23–26% (my estimate from Screener margins and realisations).
- Other income no longer carries EPCG (export-promotion duty credit) income: ₹25 cr booked in FY26, nil in Q1 FY27 [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Guidance history
- Before FY26 the company had given volume guidance; it cut it mid-FY26 and met the revised version (FY26 MDF volume +12.9%, operating EBITDA 8.8%) [Q4 call].
- Q4 call (18 May 2026): no numbers for FY27. Promised to "grow with or better than the market", retain or increase market share, and "maintain or improve" the 8.8% margin; said the 15% MDF price hike (5% + 10%) "just about absorbed" cost inflation [Q4 call].
- Q1 delivery: margin 9.6% — above FY26's 8.8%, so the margin promise held for now. But the price hike was almost entirely rolled back by July after peers cut; domestic growth of 12% was below the industry's "mid-teens", so share was lost, as an ICICI Securities analyst pointed out (Century out-sold Greenpanel in India for two quarters) [Q1 call].
- Q1 call: still no numbers; new strategy is to price "at par" with Century instead of at a premium and chase volume; says double-digit margins are "not a very tough ask" if utilisation rises [Q1 call]. Verdict: guidance kept vague; pricing stance cut.

## Capex and capacity
- Big capex is over: FY25 AP line ₹613 cr. For FY27, only ₹20–30 cr maintenance capex; no MDF expansion for 18–24 months [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Plywood: may add machines in the existing plant to lift output 30–40% with a "very minimal" spend once current capacity is full (it is 56% used) [Q1 call].
- Payback on the ₹613 cr line has been poor so far: ROCE fell from 27% (FY23) to 4% (FY25) and about 0% (FY26) [Screener]. The line needs utilisation above ~70% to earn its cost of capital (my estimate).
- EPCG export obligation still outstanding: ₹154 cr (₹306 cr a year earlier). If exports stay shut, the time to meet it lengthens; failing it would mean repaying duty saved with interest [AR FY26 note 34].
- Industry supply keeps rising: ~4 lakh CBM of new lines (Action Tesa in the south, Greenply's second press in Madhya Pradesh) arrive through FY27, on top of a 40–45 lakh CBM industry base against ~26–30 lakh CBM demand [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Gross debt ₹317 cr at June 2026 (₹353 cr in March); net debt ₹141 cr (₹156 cr March; ₹233 cr June 2025). Cash and liquid investments ~₹176 cr plus ₹160 cr unused bank lines [IP Q1FY27; CARE Aug-26].
- EUR 29.4 mn of loans (~₹316 cr) are unhedged; the rupee's fall from 92 to 107.6 per euro caused a ₹49 cr loss in FY26 [CARE Aug-26].
- Repayments ~₹73 cr due in FY27 versus expected cash accruals of ₹120–130 cr (CARE). Net debt/EBITDA 1.55x (March 2026) [CARE Aug-26].
- Cash from operations FY26 ₹111 cr; free cash flow ₹73 cr (after -₹109 cr in FY25 and -₹210 cr in FY24) [Screener].
- Working capital: cash cycle 38 days FY26 → 42 days Q1 FY27; receivable days rose from 11 (FY25) to 21 (FY26) as March sales spiked and export receipts slowed [IP Q1FY27; AR FY26 ratios]. Screener: debtor 21, inventory 89, payable 55 days in FY26.
- Dividend yield 0.31%; a dividend was paid for FY26 despite the loss (Screener payout shows -21%). No buyback [Screener].

## Moat evidence
- Scale: largest MDF capacity in India and a known brand; CARE calls its market position "established" and its brand "strong" [CARE Aug-26].
- BIS certification (mandatory from February 2025) cut cheap imports and helped FY26 volume grow 13% [CARE Aug-26].
- But pricing power is weak: the 15% hike was rolled back within three months, and the MD calls MDF "a commodity-type business" where Greenpanel must follow peers' cuts [Q1 call]. CARE notes MDF's ceiling is its cost edge over low-end plywood [CARE Aug-26].
- Share: the company lost the No. 1 domestic volume spot to Century for two quarters (analyst statement not disputed by the MD) [Q1 call].
- Cost position: European (not Chinese) lines cost more per unit to build, but the company claims better efficiency; timber-species switching lowers cost; DSOs of ~21 days are best in the panel industry [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Tax searches.** Income Tax Department searched the registered office, plants and the promoter's home from 26 Feb to 2 Mar 2026; no demand received yet; impact "cannot presently be determined" [AR FY26 note 34(d)].
- **GST case.** DGGI (GST intelligence) searched offices and the Rudrapur plant in July 2025; the company voluntarily paid ₹4.8 cr for ineligible input-tax credit FY20–FY25, with interest and penalty [AR FY26 note 34(e)].
- Auditor (standalone): clean opinion; the only key audit matter is estimating discounts and rebates in revenue — relevant because discounting is now aggressive [AR FY26 auditor's report].
- Contingent liabilities small: ₹10.4 cr indirect-tax disputes [AR FY26 note 34].
- Related parties: small sales to Greenlam group firms (₹5.6 cr FY26) and rent to a promoter firm; no concern [AR FY26 note 36].
- Oversupply: CARE flags "capacity surpassing demand" and sub-70% industry utilisation [CARE Aug-26].
- Forex: unhedged euro loan; every ₹1 fall against the euro adds ~₹3 cr loss (my estimate from EUR 29.4 mn).
- CFO change in June 2025 and new sales head in March 2025 — a new team whose record is only a year old [AR FY26 note 36; Q4 call].
- Institutional exit: FIIs down from 10.7% (FY20) to 0.94% (June 2026) [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "optimistically cautious" with a 15% price hike in place (May) to defensive: hike rolled back, channel buying hand-to-mouth, exports nil, pricing now at par with peers (August).
- New admission of lost share; new plan to win OEMs back at lower prices.
- Chemicals: in May up 40–45%; by August corrected but still 4–5% above pre-war, and "another price hike may come" if they rise again.
- Positive: margin actually rose to 9.6%, gross debt fell ₹36 cr in one quarter, and CARE moved its outlook from Negative to Stable (20 Aug 2026).

## My read
The business stopped getting worse in FY26 — margin recovered from -4.8% to 8–10% and debt is falling — but the recovery is fragile because the MDF industry has far more capacity than demand and Greenpanel has just shown it cannot hold a price rise. The ₹613 cr AP line is only half used, so profits hinge almost entirely on utilisation; each 10 points of utilisation adds roughly ₹90 cr of revenue at high incremental margin. At ₹163 the stock sits near 1.5x book and prices in little recovery, but earnings power is unproven until exports restart and new rival lines are absorbed (FY28 at the earliest). The tax search is an unresolved governance overhang. The view improves if utilisation passes 65% with margins above 12%; it worsens on a tax demand or a second price war when new lines start.
