# Stylam Industries (NSE: STYLAMIND) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Figures from Screener (consolidated; the subsidiary is immaterial, so consolidated ≈ standalone) unless tagged otherwise.

## Business and segments
- Makes high-pressure decorative laminates (HPL), compact laminates, exterior cladding ("Fascia"), PU-lacquer-coated laminates, pre-lam boards, restroom cubicles/lockers and acrylic solid surfaces ("Granex"); one reported segment ("laminates and allied products"); no customer above 10% of revenue [AR FY26 note 46].
- Export-led: FY26 revenue ₹1,129 cr = exports ₹831 cr (74%) + domestic ₹298 cr (26%). Domestic has been stuck near ₹300 cr for four years (FY23 ₹314 cr, FY24 ₹303 cr, FY25 ₹294 cr, FY26 ₹298 cr) while exports rose from ₹638 cr to ₹831 cr [IP Q1FY27].
- Q1 FY27 (from the presentation's quarterly bars, which sum to Screener revenue): exports ≈ ₹247 cr (record; ≈ +20% YoY), domestic ≈ ₹79 cr (≈ flat YoY). Volume 3.17 mn sheets (domestic 1.10 mn, export 2.07 mn) vs 3.27 mn in Q4 FY26 [IP Q1FY27].
- Export geography: Europe strongest; US and Middle East "10% to 15% each"; sells to 80+ countries, largely FOB [Q1 call, official; Q4 call].
- Acrylic solid surfaces only ~₹15 cr revenue in FY26 on 3 lakh panels/year capacity — "completely empty right now" [Q4 call; Q1 call; IP Q1FY27].
- Capacity: Panchkula + Manak Tabra-1 ≈ 8.60 crore sq m a year (1 mm basis, ~80,000 t), ~90% utilised; the presentation cites ~74% laminate utilisation for FY26. New Manak Tabra-2 adds ≈ 5.25 crore sq m (~60,000 t), +61%; commercial production began 22 Sep 2026 [BSE filing 22 Sep 2026; IP Q1FY27].
- About 60% of raw materials (phenol, melamine, papers) are imported — a partial natural hedge for export receipts [CARE Mar-26].

- Quarterly revenue / operating profit (OPM) / net profit: Jun-25 283 / 53 (19%) / 28; Sep-25 292 / 57 (19%) / 37; Dec-25 271 / 56 (21%) / 46; Mar-26 283 / 56 (20%) / 38; Jun-26 326 / 69 (21%) / 48 [Screener].
- Q1 FY27: revenue +15% YoY, operating profit +30%, net profit +71% (the base quarter carried ₹7 cr of "interest", which includes exchange differences) [Screener; AR FY26 note 32].
- Annual: FY24 914 / 184 (20%) / 128; FY25 1,025 / 185 (18%) / 122; FY26 1,129 / 221 (20%) / 150; TTM 1,173 / 237 / 170 [Screener].
- 3-year sales CAGR only 6% (FY23 ₹952 cr → FY26 ₹1,129 cr) because the plant was full; 3-year profit CAGR 15% [Screener].
- ROCE 34% (FY24), 27% (FY25), 27% (FY26); ROE ~20% [Screener]. Gross margin ~49% in Q4 FY26 [Q4 call].
- Unit economics: management no longer counts sheets for mix reasons (a 12 mm compact ≈ 20 sheets of 0.7 mm); revenue per sheet sold ≈ ₹1,030 in Q1 FY27 (₹326 cr / 3.17 mn) — my calculation, not a disclosed figure [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Guidance history
- Plant timing has slipped repeatedly: November 2025 → March 2026 → "end of June, maximum middle of July" (Q4 call, 12 May) → "maximum by 1st September… hopefully 15th or 20th August" (Q1 call, 24 Jul) → actual start 22 Sep 2026 [Q4 call; Q1 call; BSE filing 22 Sep 2026]. Reasons given changed: Supreme-Court-linked environment clearance (Q4 call), then "our old family problem… we ourselves slowed down the project" and heavy rain (Q1 call).
- New-plant revenue: Q4 call — Jagdish Gupta "₹300–400 cr in the next 3 quarters"; Manit Gupta "₹250–300 cr" for FY27, ~80% utilisation / ₹600–700 cr in FY28, ₹900–1,000 cr at peak; 30–40% utilisation "from the second quarter". Q1 call — first-year utilisation "20% to 30% plus", revenue ₹250–300 cr kept; ₹600–700 cr for FY28 kept [Q4 call; Q1 call]. Guidance **trimmed** on utilisation, **kept** on rupees, and the plant is now three weeks later than the Q1 "maximum" date.
- Company FY27 revenue growth "20%, 25%" (rough), Q4 call; not repeated in Q1 [Q4 call].
- Margins: Q4 call — new plant "same pattern" as 22–24% asked by analyst; FY28 ~22% "hopefully"; Q1 call — "similar kind of margins 19%, 20% plus". Q1 delivered 21% (Screener) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Acrylic: ₹50–70 cr FY27 promised in Q4; Q1 call says capacity is "completely empty" — no progress shown [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Next capex: promised "next quarter" in May; deferred again in July until the new plant runs; direction is adjacent products "going along laminates" for the domestic market [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Domestic turnaround: "2–3 quarters" (May) → results "from Quarter 3" (July) [Q4 call; Q1 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Manak Tabra-2: ~₹300 cr, funded entirely from internal accruals; ₹226 cr spent by early 2026; management said ₹334 cr "at this moment" in May [BSE filing 22 Sep 2026; CARE Mar-26; Q4 call]. CWIP rose from ₹83 cr (Mar-25) to ₹221 cr (Mar-26) [Screener; AR FY26 note 4].
- Unfinished capital contracts ≈ ₹116 cr at Mar-26 (≈ ₹186 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 note 38; table layout partly garbled].
- Fixed costs: senior hiring for the new plant already in the P&L; only ~150 extra workers needed; depreciation starts from commissioning (roughly ₹15–20 cr a year extra is my estimate, not disclosed) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Asset turns: management's peak revenue of ₹900–1,000 cr on ~₹300 cr capex implies ~3x; at the FY27 target (₹250–300 cr) and ~20% margin the plant would earn ₹50–60 cr EBITDA in its first part-year — payback ~5 years at year-1 run-rate, ~2 years at peak (my arithmetic).
- Existing plant has "5–10% more" headroom through efficiency [Q1 call].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash ₹2.2 cr + bank balances ₹21.4 cr at Mar-26 vs short-term (export packing) borrowings ₹29.1 cr → roughly net-cash-neutral, despite "zero debt" branding [AR FY26 balance sheet, note 21]. Free cash was ~₹27 cr at Sep-25 [CARE Mar-26].
- CFO ₹109 cr (FY24), ₹107 cr (FY25), ₹177 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹95 cr → ₹0 → −₹2 cr as capex absorbed everything [Screener].
- Working capital: debtor days 64 → 73 → 67; inventory days 112 → 121 → 132; cash-conversion cycle 147 → 163 → 150 days (FY24–FY26) [Screener]. Receivables ₹208 cr and inventory ₹218 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26].
- No dividend for FY25 or FY26 (payout 0%) — cash kept for capex [Screener].
- Forex: ~two-thirds of revenue exported; forward cover at Mar-26 only USD 0.6 mn and EUR 0.6 mn sold (vs USD 2.35 mn / EUR 1.47 mn a year earlier); MTM loss ₹3.4 cr charged in FY26 [AR FY26 note 38; CARE Mar-26].
- Rating: CARE A+ / A1, on "Rating Watch with Developing Implications" since the Aica deal (Mar 2026), ₹100.6 cr facilities; working-capital limit utilisation ~13% [CARE Mar-26].

## Moat evidence
- Export franchise: record ₹247 cr export quarter; supplies white-label compact laminates to global brands (analyst cited Wilsonart); certifications (Greenguard Gold, NSF 35/51, E0.5, EPD) — "highest number… among laminate manufacturers in India" (company claim) [IP Q1FY27; Q4 call].
- Cost/scale: one of Asia's largest single-location laminate plants (80 acres); largest press formats in India (1,860 × 4,320 mm, 28-daylight press); world-first hot-coating line [IP Q1FY27].
- Margins above peers: OPM 20–21% vs CARE's expected 17–19% for the company; interest cover 47x in FY25 [Screener; CARE Mar-26].
- Pricing: domestic hikes of ~0.5–5% by product (Q1), 3–5% to customers in May; FX (euro ~₹112, dollar ~₹96) added "3–5%" profitability [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- Strategic validation: Aica Kogyo (Japan) bought 40% at ₹2,250/share (27.12% in Feb 2026 from the Manav Gupta branch, 2.75% via open offer, 10.13% on 17 Jun 2026 from the Jagdish Gupta branch) [CARE Mar-26; News: ScanX, Jun 2026].
- Weakness: domestic brand; dealers "aware, but not really enthused" (analyst check); domestic flat for four years [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27].

## Risks and red flags
- **Ownership and control**: Aica holds 40% and has an SHA put/call structure; the operating family (Jagdish and Manit Gupta) now holds only about 17% ("we already have a 17%", Q1 call). Screener shows "promoters" 56.86% because Aica is now counted in the promoter group. The family sold at ₹2,250; the stock is ₹3,369. Future control and minority-holder treatment depend on Aica [Q1 call, official; Screener; News: ScanX].
- Board churn: whole-time directors Manav Gupta and Sachin Bhatia and several family relatives resigned 13–17 Feb 2026; Aica nominee joined Feb 2026 [AR FY26 note 42; CARE Mar-26]. The same June 2026 filing covered increased managerial remuneration for Jagdish and Manit Gupta [News: ScanX, Jun 2026].
- Execution: four timeline misses on Manak Tabra-2 with shifting explanations; acrylic line idle; domestic turnaround still a promise [calls].
- Auditor (Mittal Goel & Associates, Chandigarh): unmodified opinion; key audit matter revenue recognition; audit trail operated all year with no tampering found [AR FY26 Auditor's report p.155–159]. Small auditor for a ₹5,700 cr company now 40%-owned by a Japanese listed group — watch for a change.
- Disputed income tax ≈ ₹15 cr (largest ₹12.4 cr for AY2018-19) and GST ≈ ₹0.2 cr [AR FY26 CARO annexure]. Export obligations under advance licences are large (≈ ₹1,064 cr, table partly garbled) — routine for an exporter but a penalty risk if exports fall short [AR FY26 note 38].
- Related parties: small — sales to Gourmet House (KMP-linked), reimbursements with Aica Vstyle (Malaysia); MD remuneration ≈ ₹3.1 cr [AR FY26 note 42].
- Concentration and macro: ~74% exports; US tariff at 10% (volatile); West Asia war raised phenol/melamine and freight; thin hedging [Q1 call; AR FY26].
- Liquidity thinner than the "debt-free" label: cash ~₹24 cr at Mar-26 after the capex [AR FY26].

## What changed between the two calls
- May: bullish ("₹300–400 cr in 3 quarters", new capex "next quarter", Aica technology "within 2, 3 months"). July: more conservative (20–30% first-year utilisation), capex deferred, Aica involvement limited to quarterly board meetings and possible tech transfer.
- Reason for delay switched from environment clearance to the family dispute and rain.
- New: record export quarter and 21% margin; domestic losses "significantly reduced" (not quantified); CFO answered little.
- US tariff: 10% held; plan unchanged.

## My read
The core export business is excellent and still improving — 21% margins, record exports, and a 61% capacity jump that finally started on 22 Sep 2026. But three years of flat revenue growth (6% CAGR) came from a full plant, and the next two years depend on filling the new line (₹250–300 cr in FY27, ₹600–700 cr in FY28) with guidance that has already slipped four times. The price (₹5,710 cr, ~34x trailing profit) needs roughly 15%+ earnings growth for a decade, which the new plant can deliver if it ramps as promised. The biggest unknown is governance: a family with ~17% runs a company in which Aica holds 40%, and I would want to see Aica's intentions, a quarterly new-plant revenue number and any domestic revival before paying up.
