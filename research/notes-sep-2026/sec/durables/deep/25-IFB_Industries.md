# IFB Industries (NSE: IFBIND) — deep research, 27 Sep 2026

Figures consolidated from Screener unless tagged. Note: Screener "sales" (e.g. ₹1,585 cr in Q1 FY27) includes other operating revenue; management quotes a narrower "revenue" (₹1,529 cr). Management "EBITDA/PBDIT" is its own measure. The Q1 FY27 presentation is image-only, so segment detail below comes from the calls and the annual report.

## Business and segments
- Two businesses: (1) Home Appliances (front- and top-load washing machines, dryers, dishwashers, microwaves, ACs, kitchen appliances; also sells refrigerators made by associate IFB Refrigeration) and (2) Engineering (fine-blanked auto parts, Kolkata and Bengaluru), plus small motor and steel divisions [AR FY26 note 33].
- FY26 segment revenue: Home Appliances ₹4,461 cr (FY25 ₹4,060 cr), Engineering ₹1,007 cr (₹900 cr), Motor ₹66 cr, Steel ₹185 cr; total ₹5,619 cr [AR FY26 note 33, table partly garbled].
- Gross-to-net: FY26 gross sale of products ₹7,105 cr less trade schemes and discounts ₹1,778 cr (25% of gross) = ₹5,327 cr; traded (bought-in) products ₹1,733 cr of gross sales [AR FY26 note 21]. An analyst called the ~₹1,800 cr of schemes "excessively high"; management said it will look for leakages [Q1 call].
- Market position: front-load washers ~23% share (No. 2; ~25.5–26% excluding the 12 kg+ segment it did not serve), top-load ~9%, ACs ~3–3.5% [Q4 call].
- Capacity: AC line ~75,000 units/month, ~85% used in peak month; debottlenecking rather than new shifts [Q1 call].
- Engineering EBITDA ~15% (target 17–18%) [Q4 call]. Implied Home Appliances EBITDA ≈ 4% (my estimate: company FY26 PBDIT ₹334 cr less ~15% of ₹1,007 cr).

## Key numbers (Screener, ₹ cr)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit (OPM) / net profit: Jun-25 1,338 / 62 (4.6%) / 26; Sep-25 1,370 / 98 (7%) / 51; Dec-25 1,413 / 74 (5%) / 24; Mar-26 1,498 / 79 (5%) / 43; Jun-26 1,585 / 89 (6%) / 43 [Screener].
- Company-stated Q1 FY27: revenue ₹1,529 cr (+16.65%), EBITDA ₹88.46 cr (5.79% vs 5.34%), PBT ₹51.54 cr, PAT ₹38.06 cr (+50%); Home Appliances revenue +18%, Engineering +17% [Q1 call].
- Annual: FY24 4,438 / 196 (4.4%) / 50; FY25 5,092 / 287 (6%) / 119; FY26 5,619 / 321 (6%) / 144; TTM 5,866 / 340 / 160 [Screener]. FY26 company-stated: revenue ₹5,476 cr (+10%), PBDIT ₹334 cr (6.1% vs 6.5%), PAT ₹133.3 cr after ₹14 cr labour-code charge [Q4 call].
- ROCE 6% (FY23) → 10% → 18% → 20% (FY26) [Screener].
- Unit data Q1 FY27: front-load volume +9–10%, top-load >20%, AC +6–8% (industry ~20%); refrigerators sold by IFB branches +14% in volume; gross margin −160 bp YoY on material costs [Q1 call].
- Commodity plus forex hit: ₹84 cr in FY26 (₹32 cr commodity, ₹52 cr forex), ₹49 cr more in April–May 2026 [Q4 call].

## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (June 2026; new MD & CEO Home Appliances Sandeep Joseph Abraham joined 8 Apr 2026; Chairman Bikramjit Nag "unwell" and absent): Home Appliances growth aspiration 20%+; cost-initiative savings ₹67 cr in FY26, ₹29 cr in Apr–May, "another ₹120 cr" in the next 10 months (~₹150 cr for FY27); AC share to "double digits… this year itself, but… a tough ask"; 13 kg and 14 kg front-loaders to launch in FY27; Engineering +20–25% a year for 2–3 years with 17–18% EBITDA target [Q4 call, official].
- Q1 FY27 call (14 Aug 2026): 18% Home Appliances growth "moving in that direction" of 20%; July "good"; cost savings ₹42 cr in Q1, FY27 target stated as ₹120–150 cr (versus ₹150–200 cr earlier suggested by the chairman, per an analyst); FY27 capex ~₹110 cr; AC share aspiration restated as "7–10%" with no date; PBDIT margin should be "early double digit" in the long run; commodity and forex still "not behind us" [Q1 call, official].
- Engineering: Tata Agratas EV battery-parts LOI (~₹150 cr) is on hold after turmoil at Tata; land bought in Gujarat; machinery not ordered; stamping plan of ~₹400 cr capex for ~₹1,000 cr of business will proceed only with customer LOIs (₹250 cr+ "close to closing") [Q1 call].
- Scorecard: growth near target; cost savings on/above plan; AC share goal **cut**; EV project **stalled**; margins flat.

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex ₹88.6 cr (Home Appliances ₹43 cr), about half on new platforms (13/14 kg washers) and ₹20–30 cr on washer and AC line expansion [Q4 call]. FY27 budget ~₹110 cr [Q1 call]. Capital commitments ₹56.7 cr at Mar-26 (₹13.1 cr a year earlier) [AR FY26 note 35].
- Engineering: new capacity added in both plants in the last 1–2 years; Gujarat land for EV/stamping; ~₹400 cr stamping capex (Gujarat, Gurgaon, Bengaluru) contingent on orders; target revenue ~₹2,000 cr over time from ~₹1,000–1,100 cr now [Q1 call].
- No new AC or washer capacity needed near term [Q1 call]. Payback figures not disclosed.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Borrowings ₹156 cr at Mar-26 (₹250 cr a year earlier); investments ₹313 cr; CRISIL cited cash and bank ~₹299 cr at Jun-25 → net cash [Screener; CRISIL Oct-25].
- CFO ₹351 cr (FY24), ₹118 cr (FY25), ₹299 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹286 cr → ₹12 cr → ₹190 cr — lumpy [Screener].
- Negative working capital: cash-conversion cycle −22 → −1 → −10 days; debtor days 28, inventory 68, payables 106 (FY26) [Screener].
- **No dividend** for five years despite profits and net cash [Screener].
- Rating: CRISIL AA-/Positive (outlook raised from Stable) on ₹500 cr NCDs and A1+ on ₹50 cr CP, Oct 2025; downgrade trigger if operating margin falls below 4–5%; upgrade if above 7% [CRISIL Oct-25].

## Moat evidence
- Front-load leadership (23% share, No. 2) and a large in-house service network; service business growing ~17% with double-digit PBT margin [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Distribution push: ~10,000 outlets identified as 80% of volumes, in-store promoters being added; SKU cuts (front-loaders 55–57 → 24–25 models; washers ~95–100 → ~half) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Weak pricing power, admitted: "we have not been able to pass on the commodity and forex impact to the market" [Q1 call]. OEM AC customers have walked away (OEM sales down ~5% in FY26) [Q4 call].
- Engineering: sticky OEM relationships; ~13% revenue CAGR over five years; 15% EBITDA [Q4 call].

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party flows are large**: FY26 purchases from associate IFB Refrigeration ₹235.7 cr (₹141.1 cr in FY25, +67%); services bought from promoter-group company IFB Appliances Ltd ₹145.3 cr (₹120.4 cr); sales to investor company IFB Automotive Pvt Ltd ₹80.5 cr; Travel Systems Ltd ₹13.6 cr; advances to the associate ₹61.8 cr (₹32.5 cr); KMP pay ₹53.9 cr across a long KMP list [AR FY26 note 37 — layout partly garbled].
- **Associate opacity**: management refuses to discuss IFB Refrigeration on calls ("That is a different company"); IFB's 41% share of its Q1 profit was ₹0.33 cr [Q1 call]. The auditor's key audit matter is the impairment test of the ₹97 cr investment in this associate (DCF-based, "significant level of management's judgement") [AR FY26 Auditor's report p.88].
- Auditor Price Waterhouse & Co Chartered Accountants LLP: unmodified opinion; database-level audit trail enabled only from 18 May 2025 [AR FY26].
- Contingent liabilities modest: ₹36.6 cr (GST ₹12.3 cr, excise ₹5.5 cr, income tax ₹5.8 cr, share of associate ₹10.2 cr); no provision for higher e-waste EPR floor prices, which the industry is contesting in the Delhi High Court [AR FY26 note 36].
- Leadership transition: new appliance CEO (April 2026); Engineering MD P H Narayanan left 31 Mar 2026; chairman unwell at the Q4 call [Q4 call; AR FY26 note 37].
- Governance/float: promoters 74.96%, FIIs 0.8% — thin institutional ownership; no dividends [Screener].
- Thin margins: 5–6% EBITDA leaves little room if commodity/forex pressure persists; CRISIL's downgrade trigger is 4–5% [CRISIL Oct-25].
- Disclosure inconsistencies: Q1 FY26 fine-blanking revenue shown as ₹176 cr this year vs ₹185 cr a year ago (analyst question, taken offline) [Q1 call].
- EV/stamping growth depends on a single uncertain customer (Tata Agratas) [Q1 call].

## My read
IFB is improving from a low base — ROCE up from 6% to 20% in three years, net cash, and 17% revenue growth in Q1 — but it remains a thin-margin (5–6%) appliance business with admitted weak pricing power. At ~₹4,965 cr (about 31x TTM profit, down 40% from the 52-week high), the price assumes margins rise toward management's "early double-digit" aspiration; each 1 pp of margin on ~₹5,900 cr revenue is ~₹60 cr of EBITDA, so a commodity/forex reversal would help sharply. The related-party web (₹236 cr of purchases from the associate, ₹145 cr of services from a promoter company, rising advances to the associate) and refusal to discuss the associate are the main reasons for caution. I would turn more positive on two quarters of 7%+ margin and a dividend; negative if the associate needs more support.
