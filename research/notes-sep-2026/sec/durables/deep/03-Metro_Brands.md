# Metro Brands — deep research (Phase A1, written 27 Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Single reported segment: retail of footwear, bags and accessories (Ind AS 108 – one segment). Company-owned, company-operated stores plus e-commerce. [AR FY26, note 28]
- Store network 1,041 stores in 222 cities at 30 Jun 2026 (1,032 at March; 9 net adds in Q1 – 13 opened, 4 closed). Mix: Metro 375, Mochi 289, Crocs 234, Walkway 106, FitFlop 14, New Era 10, Foot Locker 6, Fila 4, MetroActiv 3. [IP Q1FY27]
- FY26 openings: 147 opened, 23 closed, 124 net; ~40 of the openings were value-format Walkway stores (base grew from ~70 to 103). [AR FY26 CEO letter; Q1 call]
- Mix: own brands (Metro, Mochi, Walkway, Da Vinchi, J. Fontini) ~71% of store product sales in Q1 FY27 (73% in FY26); third-party brands (Crocs, FitFlop, Fila, Clarks) ~29%. Products priced above ₹3,000 are ~57% of sales (up from the 40s a few years ago). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Channels (standalone, Q1 FY27): in-store 86%, online 9%, omnichannel 4%, others 1%; e-commerce incl. omni 13.1% of revenue (13.7% a year earlier). [IP Q1FY27]
- Geography: East has been a steady 13–15% of the business (14% in FY26); presentation shows a rising share of stores in Tier II/III cities. [Q4 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Four verticals now run by separate leaders: core (Metro/Mochi), value (Walkway, Shoe Depot), strategic brands (Crocs, Clarks, FitFlop), sports (Fila, Foot Locker, MetroActiv, New Era). [Q1 call]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit: Jun-25 ₹628 / ₹194 / 31% / ₹99 cr; Sep-25 ₹651 / ₹171 / 26% / ₹69 cr; Dec-25 ₹811 / ₹265 / 33% / ₹130 cr; Mar-26 ₹773 / ₹238 / 31% / ₹118 cr; Jun-26 ₹720 / ₹215 / 30% / ₹95 cr. [Screener]
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹2,357 cr, OPM 30%, PAT ₹415 cr (flattered by a tax credit); FY25 ₹2,507 cr, 30%, ₹354 cr; FY26 ₹2,864 cr (+14%), 30%, ₹416 cr (+17%). TTM sales ₹2,956 cr, PAT ₹412 cr. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: revenue +14.7%, EBITDA +10.4% (margin 29.8% vs 31.0%), PAT **−3.6%** to ₹95 cr (margin 13.2% vs 15.7%); gross margin 59.5%. Standalone revenue +14.1%, EBITDA +9.1%. [IP Q1FY27]
- Reported margins include the lease accounting rule (Ind AS 116); pre-Ind AS FY26 EBITDA ~21% and PAT 15.5%; Q1 FY27 pre-Ind AS PAT margin 14.3% (16.8% a year earlier). [Q4 call official; IP Q1FY27]
- Depreciation ₹229 → ₹258 → ₹311 cr (FY24–FY26) and interest ₹79 → ₹90 → ₹111 cr, mostly lease-related, growing faster than sales as new stores open. [Screener]
- Unit economics: revenue per sq ft steady at ~₹4,350 (standalone, Q1) despite new stores; average realisation rose ~6% YoY in Q1 (mix-led), 3–4% expected for the year; FY26 average realisation ~₹1,675 per pair (from ~₹1,400 in FY22). [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- ROCE 20% (FY26); ROE 22%. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Standing guidance: revenue growth ~15% "give or take a couple of points", gross margin 55–57%, EBITDA ~30%, PAT 13–15% (CFO) — the CEO said "15% PAT for the full year". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Q4 call (21 May 2026): 15%+ long-run growth; ~50-store aggregate opportunity across Fila, Foot Locker, Clarks and MetroActiv in FY27 "depending on BIS"; e-commerce 12–15% of revenue; Fila "meaningful in 18 months". [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivered: revenue +14.7% (in range); EBITDA 29.8% (in range); PAT margin 13.2% (bottom of range); only 9 net stores (vs the usual "triple-digit" annual pace); e-commerce +9% only. CEO kept the "triple digit" store target and double-digit e-com growth (20–30% "healthy"). [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Sports: Fila acceleration now "towards the end of FY27" (slipped from the FY26 EBO plan); runway 300–500 Fila/Foot Locker/MetroActiv stores in 5–7 years; sports to reach 10–15% of Metro/Mochi sales. [Q1 call]
- Clarks: in ~300 own multi-brand stores (women's) and ~100 (men's); target 700 by year-end; Clarks stand-alone stores from Q3 FY27; runway 100–150 stores. [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27]
- Q2 FY27 warning: later Diwali shifts festive buying into Q3. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: products are made by 250+ outside vendors; capex is store fit-outs, IT and warehousing. Capex ₹139 cr in FY26 (₹87 cr FY25). [AR FY26 cash flow; CARE Apr-26]
- New ~2–2.5 lakh sq ft distribution centre opened March 2026, fully integrated by Q1. New POS system from June 2026 and SAP upgrade later in FY27. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Returns logic: Walkway to earn 25–30% ROCE vs 7–8% on treasury; new stores dilute margins in their first years. [Q1 call]
- Store payback not disclosed. The steady ₹/sq ft suggests new stores are not dragging productivity much yet. [IP Q1FY27]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Balance sheet (Mar-26): Screener "borrowings" ₹1,570 cr are almost entirely lease liabilities; investments ₹673 cr plus other treasury. CARE: "ample liquidity and low gearing"; rating CARE AA (Stable)/A1+ reaffirmed Apr 2026. [Screener; CARE Apr-26]
- Cash flow: operating cash ₹474 cr in FY26 (₹698 cr FY25), free cash flow ₹335 cr (₹611 cr). Cash conversion 69% of operating profit, lower than usual because inventory rose. [Screener]
- Inventory is the big item: inventory days 273 in FY26 (228 in FY25); cash conversion cycle 197 days. Management said part of it was deliberate forward-buying (~6 months of stock, orders 4–5 months out) ahead of crude-linked cost rises. [Screener; Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Dividend payout 40% in FY26 (155% in FY25 incl. a special dividend). Promoter holding steady at ~71.8%. [Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Pricing: gross margin ~59–60% held through a 10% input-cost scare by forward-buying; "nothing more than normal inflationary price hikes" of 3–5%. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Brand partner of choice: exclusive India rights for Crocs (234 stores from 7 in FY16), FitFlop, Foot Locker, New Era, Clarks (India and South Asia) and Fila (owned via acquisition; ₹40 cr goodwill). Clarks has moved production to India for Metro. [CARE Apr-26; AR FY26 note 48; Q1 call]
- Customer base: 20+ million loyalty members; premium share rising; conversion and new-customer counts rising per CEO. [IP Q1FY27; Q1 call]
- Margins: ~30% reported EBITDA held for five straight years (FY22–FY26) — an edge from company-run stores, a high own-brand share and low discounting. [Screener; Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Growth engines are stalled or slow: BIS (quality-certification) licence renewals for ASEAN factories are "erratic", holding back Foot Locker, MetroActiv and Fila; 1 of 3 MetroActiv stores is weak; some Walkway stores underperform. [Q1 call]
- Profit is lagging sales: FY26 PAT margin 14.5% vs 15.7% in Q1 FY26; Q1 FY27 PAT fell as marketing (~100 bp), talent, new-store occupancy and lower treasury income bit. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Inventory heavy (273 days); any fashion miss or demand slump means markdowns. [Screener]
- Related parties: showroom rent/concessions paid to Chairman Rafique Malik (₹1.8 cr) and Aziza Malik (₹2.6 cr), and retail-agency commission to Metro Shoes (₹2.5 cr) in FY26 — small, at arm's length per the Board. MD's re-appointment from Apr 2027 at up to ₹10 cr a year. [AR FY26 note 27; Board's report]
- Auditor: unmodified; key audit matter is revenue recognition at high-volume stores and discount accruals. Contingent liabilities only ~₹5 cr (small tax cases). [AR FY26 auditor's report, note 25]
- Wage-floor rises in some states; front-line pay currently above minimums. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 (20% standalone growth, 1,000-store milestone) was upbeat; Q1 was steady but defensive on margins and store count.
- Numbers: stores 1,032 → 1,041; e-com growth 53% → 9%; PAT margin 15.7% (Q4) → 13.2% (Q1).
- New: separate vertical heads (sports CBO, Metro/Mochi President); Clarks EBO timing firmed to Q3 FY27; reduced 3P e-commerce discounting as a policy.
- Dropped: crude-linked input-cost worry (neutralised by forward buying).

## My read
Metro is a high-quality, very profitable retailer growing steadily at ~14–15%, but the newer engines (sports, Walkway, Clarks stores) are each slower to scale than hoped and currently dilute profit. With PAT growing slower than sales for now and inventory at 273 days, earnings growth in FY27 may only match revenue growth at best. The price needs a return to mid-to-high-teens profit growth from FY28 as marketing and talent costs are lapped and Clarks/sports scale. Watch store additions in Q2–Q3, the PAT margin versus the 13–15% guide, and any easing of BIS approvals.
