# Cera Sanitaryware (CERA) — deep research, Phase A1

Numbers note: Cera sold its two 51%-owned LLPs (Packcart Packaging, Race Polymer Arts) on 29 Sep 2025 and now has no subsidiaries, so Screener's consolidated page stops at Jun-25. Figures below use the **standalone** Screener page. From Q1 FY27 Cera also nets turnover discounts off revenue (–2.5% to –3%); Screener's Mar-26 quarter (₹624 cr) is already on the new basis, while the Q4 call quoted ₹644 cr on the old basis.

Transcript check (official copies from BSE):
- **Q4 FY26 stored copy (call of 9 May 2026): matches official** — it is the company's own filed text, page footers included.
- **Q1 FY27 stored copy (call of 8 Aug 2026): tidied copy (figures match; gaps listed).** All headline figures match (₹486 cr revenue, ₹49.2 cr EBITDA, 10.1%, 14%/25% growth, 61%/96% utilisation, ₹943 cr cash, ₹43 cr capex, ₹85 cr brand budget, one-offs of 1.3/0.75/0.8/1.5 points). Gaps: (1) it has "we grew by something like {? 9.5% ?}" — official **19.5%**; (2) "revenues dropped by, you can say, 3%" — official says **margins** dropped ~3 points; (3) "we stopped operating one kiln from July onwards" — official says the second kiln **restarted in June**. None changes the key numbers.

## Business and segments
- Bathroom-products maker: sanitaryware (WCs, basins), faucetware (brass fittings, showers), tiles (fully outsourced) and wellness; brands Cera (core, mass/mid-premium), Cera Luxe, Senator (luxury, launched FY25) and Polipluz (entry level, rural/Tier-4). [Q4 call; IP Q4FY26]
- Q1 FY27 revenue mix: sanitaryware 47% (+14% YoY), faucetware 40% (+25%), tiles 11% (+22%), wellness 2% (–7%). Q4 FY26: 46% / 43% / 9% / 2%. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Channel: projects (developers, institutions) rose from ~30% of sales a few years ago to 39% in Q4 FY26 and 43% in Q1 FY27; management expects ~60:40 retail:project to hold. Premium 44% / mid 37% / entry 19% of Q1 sales; Tier-1 38%, Tier-2 24%, Tier-3 38%. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Plants: sanitaryware at Kadi (Gujarat) with two kilns; faucetware plant raised from 3 to 4 lakh pieces/month, made 4.3 lakh in March 2026, going to 5 lakh by Q4 FY27 for ~₹5 cr. Utilisation Q1: sanitaryware 61% (one kiln shut April–May), faucetware 96% (106% in Q4). Outsourcing in Q4: 60% of sanitaryware, 46% of faucetware — mostly from Morbi. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Industry: organised players ~60% of Indian sanitaryware; market projected at ~$1.34 bn by 2030 (company-cited industry estimates). [AR FY26 MD&A]

## Key numbers
- Quarterly (Screener standalone, ₹ cr): Jun-25 sales 407 / op. profit 53 (13%) / PAT 47; Sep-25 488 / 67 (14%) / 57; Dec-25 499 / 51 (10%) / 24; Mar-26 624 / 98 (16%) / 77; **Jun-26 486 / 49 (10%) / 45**. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27 growth split: sanitaryware +14% = volume 10% + price 2% + mix 2%; faucetware +25% = volume 18% + price 4% + mix 3%. EBITDA ₹49.2 cr (10.1%) vs ₹53.1 cr (13.1%); gross margin ~46% vs "51% typical". [Q1 call]
- Management's bridge of the Q1 margin (10.1%) to an "underlying" ~14.5%: worker wage settlement arrears ₹6.3 cr (1.3 pts), one-kiln under-absorption ₹3.7 cr (0.75 pt), clearing old low-price project orders ₹4 cr (0.8 pt), price increases not yet billed (1.5 pts). [Q1 call]
- Q4 FY26: revenue ₹644 cr (old basis, +11.4%), EBITDA ₹98 cr (15.2%) vs ₹106 cr (18.3%); PAT ₹77 cr vs ₹86 cr. [Q4 call]
- Annual (Screener): sales FY24 ₹1,871 cr → FY25 ₹1,915 cr → FY26 ₹2,050 cr; OPM 16% → 15% → 13%; PAT ₹239 cr → ₹246 cr → ₹204 cr. TTM PAT ₹203 cr (–17%). ROCE 25% → 22% → 19%. Three-year profit CAGR –1%. [Screener]
- Company-reported FY26: EBITDA margin 15.3% (17.9% FY25), ROCE 17.8% (21.4%), ROE 14.5% (18.3%). Q3 FY26 carried an ₹18 cr labour-code provision, ₹10 cr of which was written back in Q4. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q4 call]
- Unit economics: brass (≈60% of faucet cost) rose from ₹665/kg (Dec-25) to ~₹900/kg (Jul-26); gas cost ₹48.43/scm in Q1 vs ₹33.17 a year earlier (3.3% of revenue; 69% from GAIL at below-market rates). Price rises taken: ~12% sanitaryware, ~16% faucets (March–May 2026), in line with peers. [Q1 call; Q4 call]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (May 2026): FY27 revenue growth **18–20%** (sanitaryware ~12% = 7% volume + 5–6% price; faucets ~18% = 10–12% volume + 8% price; tiles and construction chemicals ~₹250 cr; Senator + Polipluz ₹70–80 cr vs ₹19 cr in FY26). Margin: "sustain EBITDA margins at around **14% to 15%**" if demand holds. Senator stores 40 → **60** in FY27; Polipluz 102 → 200 distributors, 1,120 → 2,000 dealers. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: revenue +19.5% (**on track**); volumes 10% sanitaryware and 18% faucets (**beat** the 7–8% and 10–12% guides); margin 10.1% (**missed**, though a wage-settlement hit was flagged in May). [Q1 call]
- Q1 call (Aug 2026): revenue guide **kept** at 18–20%; margin guide now **13.5–14%** for FY27, called "the margin that we were predicting earlier" — in fact a **cut** from 14–15%, partly offset by the smaller revenue base (13.5–14% of ₹97 ≈ 13.9–14.4% on the new basis). Q2 will still carry some project-price lag; gross margin back to ~51% "from Q3" if brass and gas hold. Senator store target stated as **35 → ~50** — quietly lower than the 60 promised in May. Senator/Polipluz ₹70–80 cr target "intact" but no quarterly numbers. No FY28 guidance; no date for a return to 16%. [Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- FY26 capex small: ₹14.5 cr per the Q4 call, ₹21.95 cr per the annual report (buildings and plant); gross block ₹616 cr. [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- FY27 plan ₹43 cr: ~₹25 cr routine, ~₹5 cr faucet debottlenecking to 5 lakh pieces/month (Q4 FY27), ~₹15 cr office space. Capital commitments at Mar-26 only ₹2.1 cr. [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 note 47]
- Greenfield sanitaryware plant: first phase ~₹150 cr (land already bought for ₹27 cr); **on hold**, to be reviewed at year-end FY27; 18 months to build once started. With both kilns running from June, utilisation is ~80%, so capacity could tighten in FY28–29 if 10% volume growth holds. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Internalising some Morbi-sourced sanitaryware SKUs needs only ₹2–3 cr. Asset turn: FY26 sales ₹2,050 cr on net block ₹372 cr ≈ 5.5x — very capital-light because ~half of volume is outsourced. [Q1 call; Screener]
- Brand spend is the real growth investment: ₹49 cr in FY26 → ~₹85 cr budgeted for FY27 (new ambassador Kriti Sanon). Senator/Polipluz cost ~₹15.5 cr salaries + ₹10–12 cr publicity in FY27; they lost ~₹8.5 cr in FY26. [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Cash and investments ₹853 cr (Mar-26) → ₹943 cr (Jun-26); borrowings only lease liabilities (₹47 cr on Screener; long-term debt ₹36.8 cr of lease liabilities). Debt/equity 0.03x; interest cover ~44x. [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A; Screener]
- Operating cash flow ₹228 cr (FY24) → ₹116 cr (FY25) → ₹212 cr (FY26); free cash flow ₹167 cr → ₹89 cr → ₹192 cr. [Screener]
- Working capital improving fast: company NWC 78 → 64 days (Mar-26) → 50 days (Jun-26) as inventory days fell 80 → 68 and receivables 38 → 30. Screener FY26: debtor days 49, inventory 139, payables 77. Finished-goods stock cut from ₹365 cr (Dec-25) to ₹303 cr (Mar-26). [Q1 call; Q4 call; Screener]
- Dividend ₹75/share for FY26 (payout 47%). CARE AA/Stable/A1+ reaffirmed 28 Aug 2026 on ₹100 cr of bank lines. [Q4 call; Screener; CARE disclosure Aug-26]

## Moat evidence
- Brand and reach: 272 dealer-owned Cera Style Galleries plus experience centres; 46 years of brand building; Cera MRPs "higher than what other brands have been". [IP Q4FY26; Q4 call]
- Cost position: GAIL gas at below-market rates and in-house kilns kept Cera supplying while gas-starved Morbi units shut in March–May 2026 — management says this won customers and let it clear inventory. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Pricing: 12–16% increases absorbed "well" by the market, in line with peers (16–18% faucets, 10–12% sanitaryware industry-wide). But FY26 margins fell on "elevated trade discounts", which shows pricing power is cyclical. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- Share: faucetware volumes +18% vs a guided 10–12%, suggesting share gains; no market-share figures disclosed.

## Risks and red flags
- **Margin credibility**: margin guidance slipped from 14–15% (May) to 13.5–14% (Aug) within one quarter, and every recent weak quarter (Q3 FY26 10.2%, Q1 FY27 10.1%) has been explained by one-offs. FY27 needs ~15% average in Q2–Q4 to reach the guide. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- **Brass and gas**: brass at ~₹900/kg; management said a move to ₹950–1,000 needs another price rise. Gas cost up 46% YoY. [Q1 call]
- **Project mix**: 43% of sales from projects at year-old prices; project pricing catches up only from Q3. [Q1 call]
- **Management change**: Mr. Baliga, head of Senator and Polipluz, resigned (leaving 30 Sep 2026); a national head takes over. Senator store target cut from 60 to ~50. [Q1 call; Q4 call]
- **Tax dispute**: claims not acknowledged as debt jumped to **₹29.1 cr** (Mar-26) from ₹2.2 cr, mainly a ₹28.7 cr income-tax demand for FY2019-20 at CIT (Appeals); GST disputes add ~₹2.6 cr. LCs/guarantees ₹7.6 cr. [AR FY26 note 47; AR FY26 CARO]
- **Auditor**: unmodified opinion; key audit matters are expected credit losses on ₹274 cr of receivables (₹15.4 cr provision) and revenue net of discounts/incentives/rebates — relevant because Cera just changed how it presents turnover discounts. No emphasis of matter found. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- **Related parties**: KMP remuneration ₹20.4 cr in FY26 (≈10% of PAT; ₹19.0 cr FY25); rent ₹1.3 cr to promoter entity Madhusudan Industries. LLPs sold for ₹18.7 cr with a ₹5.5 cr profit booked in other income. Promoters 54.4%, unchanged; no pledge seen. [AR FY26 notes 7.1(C), 40; Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- May: confident — margin back to 15.2%, 14–15% "sustainable", greenfield "to be seen in the coming quarters". August: defensive — 10.1% margin explained by four one-offs, guide trimmed to 13.5–14%, greenfield deferred to year-end review. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- New: accounting change (discounts netted from revenue), wage settlement (~₹1 cr/month extra, ₹18.3 cr total FY27 hit), Senator head's exit, ₹85 cr brand budget, DMS extended to retailer loyalty. [Q1 call]
- Better than expected: volumes (10%/18%), working capital (NWC 50 days), cash (+₹90 cr in a quarter).

## My read
Demand has clearly turned: two quarters of double-digit volume growth, retail recovering and Morbi disruption helping the organised players. But profitability is not yet following — FY26 PAT fell 17% and the Q1 margin was the lowest in years; the recovery case rests on price increases reaching project billing from Q3 and brass not rising further. The price needs FY27 revenue growth near 18% and margins back to ~14% in H2, then a return to mid-teens profit growth in FY28 on cheap growth capital (the plant is capital-light and cash is ₹943 cr). The view improves if Q2–Q3 margins print 13–15% and the greenfield is approved on real demand; it worsens if the 13.5–14% guide is cut again, brass crosses ₹1,000/kg, or the ₹28.7 cr tax demand is upheld.
