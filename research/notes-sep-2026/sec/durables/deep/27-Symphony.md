# Symphony (SYMPHONY) — deep research, Sep 2026

Price ₹589, market cap ₹4,043 cr, 52-week range ₹561–970 [Screener, 27 Sep 2026]. Reported TTM profit is negative after the Australia write-off, so Screener's P/E (1,039) and ROE (3%) are not meaningful.

## Business and segments
- World's largest air-cooler maker; asset-light — manufacturing is outsourced in India, the company keeps design, R&D, brand and distribution. Products: household and commercial coolers, large-space venti-cooling (LSV), tower/kitchen/personal fans and (since FY26) storage and instant water heaters [AR FY26 p.94].
- One reported operating segment ("Air Cooling and Other Appliances"). Geography FY26: India ₹684 cr (FY25 ₹1,065 cr), rest of world ₹447 cr (FY25 ₹510 cr). India PBIT before exceptionals ₹154 cr (FY25 ₹300 cr); rest-of-world PBIT ₹11 cr before a ₹215 cr exceptional charge [AR FY26 note 36].
- "Beyond India Summer Products" (BISP = LSV, fans, water heaters, exports and all overseas subsidiaries): ₹558 cr, 49% of FY26 consolidated revenue [Q4 call]; ₹560 cr, 48% of TTM to Jun-26 [Q1 call, IP Q1FY27]. Standalone BISP ₹179 cr, 23% of TTM, EBITDA "close to double digit" [Q1 call].
- India channels: modern trade (large-format, regional chains, e-commerce, D2C) is "in excess of 1/3rd" of India revenue, general trade 60–65%; margins "almost comparable" [Q1 call]. D2C has reached "scale parity with its two largest e-commerce platforms combined" [AR FY26 p.95].
- Subsidiaries (all wholly owned): IMPCO Mexico, GSK China (two entities), Climate Holdings/Climate Technologies Australia (CTPL), Bonaire USA (moved directly under Symphony India in May 2026), Symphony Brazil [AR FY26 Board's report].
- Q1 FY27 subsidiary figures: Bonaire USA revenue ₹36 cr (₹27 cr), EBITDA ₹18 cr (₹7 cr), PAT ₹17 cr; IMPCO ₹54 cr (₹66 cr), EBITDA ₹3 cr (₹7 cr); GSK China ₹34 cr (₹24 cr), EBITDA ₹6 cr (₹2 cr); CTPL ₹27 cr (₹31 cr), EBITDA –₹4 cr (–₹2 cr) [Q1 call].
- FY26 subsidiaries: IMPCO revenue ₹182 cr, EBITDA ₹21 cr, PAT ₹6 cr; GSK China ₹96 cr, EBITDA ₹8 cr; CTPL ₹182 cr, PAT –₹11 cr before exceptionals; US business about ₹45 cr (range ₹30–140 cr over five years); Brazil PAT –₹3 cr [Q4 call, official].
- Market: residential cooler market about ₹5,000 cr at primary level, which management expects to double in 5–7 years; BIS rules already in force and energy-rating norms expected soon, which should hurt unorganised metal-body makers [AR FY26 MD&A p.85].

## Key numbers (Screener consolidated)
- Annual: revenue FY24 ₹1,156 cr → FY25 ₹1,576 cr → FY26 ₹1,130 cr; operating profit ₹171 cr (15%) → ₹312 cr (20%) → ₹126 cr (11%); net profit ₹148 cr → ₹212 cr → –₹141 cr (after ₹259 cr consolidated impairment) [Screener].
- Quarters (revenue / operating profit / margin): Sep-25 ₹163 cr / ₹24 cr / 15%; Dec-25 ₹233 cr / ₹24 cr / 10%; Mar-26 ₹338 cr / ₹50 cr / 15%; Jun-26 ₹378 cr / ₹46 cr / 12%; net profit Mar-26 –₹218 cr, Jun-26 ₹40 cr [Screener].
- Q1 FY27 as reported: consolidated revenue ₹378 cr, +8% on ₹350 cr; EBITDA ₹48 cr (₹38 cr), margin 12.6% (10.7%); gross margin 49.8% (48.4%); PAT ₹40 cr (₹42 cr). Adjusted for a ₹5 cr one-off non-cash cost this year and ₹9 cr extra other income last year, EBITDA ₹53 cr vs ₹38 cr and PAT ₹43 cr vs ₹35 cr [Q1 call, IP Q1FY27].
- Standalone Q1 FY27: revenue ₹241 cr (₹229 cr), India revenue +15% "underpinned by volumes", EBITDA ₹30 cr (₹24 cr), margin 12.3% (10.3%), gross margin flat at 50.9%; exports fell [IP Q1FY27].
- FY26 standalone: revenue ₹765 cr (₹1,182 cr); PBT before exceptionals ₹164 cr (₹329 cr); PAT after exceptionals –₹166 cr; Q4 standalone EBITDA margin 17% vs about 27% a year earlier [Q4 call, official].
- Returns: standalone core capital employed ₹73 cr, ROCE 164%, RONW 22%; consolidated capital employed ₹206 cr (₹409 cr), ROCE 67%, RONW 18% (TTM, before exceptionals) [IP Q1FY27].
- Unit economics: a cooler costs about one-third of an entry AC and saves over 90% of electricity — payback 1–2 years [Q4 call]. Gross margin ~46–50% is the moat; EBITDA swings between 10% and 27% purely on operating leverage from summer volume.

## Guidance history
- No numeric revenue/margin guidance on either call; management declined to guess summer growth ("If you can give me… the precise weather") [Q4 call].
- Q4 promise on costs: "starting 1st July, we will be in a position to pass entire price increase" [Q4 call]. Q1 delivery: management now says it will pass on "some, but not all", and "in the short term, margins are likely to be impacted"; 7–10% hikes taken in non-household categories, none yet in household coolers [Q1 call]. **Guidance softened (cut).**
- Q4 promise: no further capital to Australia. Q1: kept — "our strategy is not to deploy any additional capital" [Q1 call]. **Kept.**
- Q4 promise: GSK China to repay the last ₹4 cr loan within six months. Q1: fully repaid, China debt-free [Q1 call]. **Delivered early.**
- Q4 promise: ₹52 cr (₹30 cr Bonaire USA equity + ₹23 cr IPR) to be remitted, proceeds to clear the guaranteed Australian working-capital loan. Q1: done; CTPL booked a ₹42 cr one-time gain eliminated on consolidation [Q1 call]. **Delivered.**
- Water heaters: Q4 said FY26 revenue "nothing to write home about", wider geography in FY27 [Q4 call]; Q1 says already in modern retail and GT in top cities [Q1 call]. No numbers given — **unmeasurable.**
- Modern trade +100% in Q1 flagged as a one-off from GT carrying old stock — "we shouldn't expect that going forward" [Q1 call].
- Longer term: Mexico summer 2027 expected to be "fairly robust" after two mild summers; US sales to rise further if the summer repeats [Q1 call].
- Buyback: "no share buyback as of now… at the right time, it can and will happen" [Q4 call].

## Capex and capacity
- Asset-light: no manufacturing capex plan; open capital commitments only ₹5.4 cr at Mar-26 [AR FY26 note 35]. Fixed assets fell from ₹322 cr to ₹171 cr, mainly the Australia write-down [Screener].
- The real "capex" was ₹165 cr sent to Australia in March 2026 to repay loans guaranteed by Symphony, plus about ₹52 cr for US equity and brand rights — about ₹225 cr in total to 30 Jun 2026 [Q4 call, Q1 call]. Cumulative Australia equity of ₹348 cr is now fully written off in the standalone books [Q4 call].
- Growth spending now goes through the P&L: A&P and launch costs for BISP (water heaters, tower fans) and D2C [Q4 call, AR FY26 p.94]. Capacity is not a constraint; outsourced vendors and plants in Mexico and China supply the US.
- Future growth levers: (1) India residential cooler market doubling in 5–7 years with share moving from unorganised to branded under BIS/energy-rating rules [AR FY26 p.85]; (2) BISP margin rising from high single digit toward cooler margins with scale — LSV already earns more than household coolers [Q4 call]; (3) US retail through Home Depot and Lowe's, where "Air Force" rebranded Indian SKUs drove +35% [Q1 call]; (4) zero Australia drag from FY27.

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Standalone treasury ₹345 cr at 30 Jun 2026 (₹363 cr a year earlier) after about ₹225 cr remitted to Australia; ₹287 cr at 31 Mar 2026 [Q1 call, Q4 call].
- Consolidated borrowings ₹173 cr at Mar-26 (₹142 cr Mar-25) [Screener] — these sat in Australia and should fall as the guaranteed working-capital loan is cleared with the ₹52 cr [Q4 call]. China and Mexico owe the parent nothing [Q1 call].
- FY26 cash flow was poor: operating cash flow –₹81 cr, free cash flow –₹96 cr (FY25 +₹237 cr) [Screener]. Inventory days jumped to 193 (129) and the cash conversion cycle to 162 days (81) after the failed summer of 2025 [Screener]. Management says both trade and company inventory were "completely normalized" by 30 Jun 2026 [Q1 call].
- Dividends: FY26 total ₹62 cr (final ₹5/share) despite the loss [Q4 call]; Q1 FY27 interim ₹1/share, ₹6.9 cr [IP Q1FY27]. Last buyback 2024.

## Moat evidence
- Category leader with "the majority share of voice and market share" in India; brand recall is the category name [AR FY26, chairman and strategy section]. Symphony prices as "mass premium" with the widest range, to fight cheaper entrants at every price point [Q4 call].
- Gross margin held at 46.4% in the worst summer in memory (Q4 FY26) and rose to 49.8% in Q1 FY27 despite polymer inflation [Q4 call, IP Q1FY27] — evidence of pricing power and value engineering.
- Capital-light: standalone ROCE 164% on ₹73 cr capital employed [IP Q1FY27].
- Weakness: the moat is against other cooler brands, not against weather — FY26 revenue fell 28% and operating profit 60% [Screener]. In water heaters its share is "relatively small" against entrenched players [AR FY26 p.94].

## Risks and red flags
- Weather: two bad summers in Mexico and one in India cut FY26 profit by more than half; results will stay lumpy.
- Cost inflation from the West Asia war: polymer and freight costs; management has walked back the "pass entire increase" promise [Q1 call]. Exports to the Middle East still disrupted [Q1 call].
- Capital allocation history: ₹348 cr lost on the 2018 Australia acquisition; the Board approved selling CTPL and IMPCO in April/July 2025, then reversed that decision on 28 Jan 2026 [AR FY26 Board's report]. CTPL still loses money (Q1 EBITDA –₹4 cr) and management rules out winding it up [Q4 call].
- Management churn: Executive Director and Group CEO Amit Kumar resigned effective 27 Mar 2026; no replacement named in the report [AR FY26 Board's report; News: Prysm, Mar 2026].
- Contingent liabilities rose to ₹25.9 cr (₹9.7 cr), driven by a central-excise demand of ₹22.0 cr (₹0.9 cr) [AR FY26 note 35]. Small versus treasury.
- Auditor: BSR & Co; unmodified opinion; key audit matters were Australia goodwill impairment and revenue timing (cut-off) [AR FY26 auditor's report]. FY25 comparatives restated — ₹21.6 cr of currency-translation losses had been booked straight to equity instead of other comprehensive income [AR FY26 note 14.4]. NSE fined ₹5,000 for late related-party disclosure (H1 FY26), later waived [AR FY26 secretarial audit].
- Disclosure quality: the MD&A says "IMPCO closed 2025 with net sales of $413 million… washing machines" — this cannot be IMPCO (FY26 revenue ₹182 cr) and looks like pasted text [AR FY26 p.95]. Revenue-timing risk was also visible in Q4 FY25 ("huge unbilled advances") [Q4 call].
- Related parties are clean: only ₹0.15 cr to a director-linked design firm plus KMP pay [AR FY26 note 37]. Promoter holding steady at 73.42%, no pledge reported; FII holding fell from 6.47% to 2.43% over the year [Screener].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from damage control (write-offs, "nothing more to lose") to a recovery story: Indian channel inventory cleared, India +15%, US and China strong [Q4 call → Q1 call].
- Cost stance hardened the other way: full pass-through promised in May became partial pass-through plus admitted margin pressure in August.
- New issue: freight and West Asia disruption now hitting export demand, not just shipments [Q1 call].
- Subsidiaries: US flipped from "intact" to a real profit engine (Q1 EBITDA ₹18 cr); Mexico worsened (EBITDA ₹3 cr vs ₹7 cr).

## Best management quotes
- "Entire equity investment totalling 348 crores have been impaired… nothing more to impair or… nothing more to lose." — Nrupesh Shah [Q4 call]
- "In the short term, margins are likely to be impacted." — Achal Bakeri [Q1 call]
- "As of 30th June '26… there is no inventory overhang either at a trade level or at a company level." — Nrupesh Shah [Q1 call]

## My read
The core Indian business is intact — gross margins near 50%, a debt-free standalone balance sheet with ₹345 cr treasury, and ROCE above 150% — and FY27 should look much better simply because channel stock is clean and Australia no longer drains cash. But growth is still hostage to the weather, BISP is only 23% of standalone sales at high-single-digit margins, and the cost pass-through promise has already been diluted. The price needs a normal summer 2027 plus steady BISP margin gains to get back to FY24–FY25 profit (₹150–210 cr). What would change my view: a second quarter of margin compression, any fresh money into Australia, or a clear CEO appointment and water-heater numbers that show real traction.
