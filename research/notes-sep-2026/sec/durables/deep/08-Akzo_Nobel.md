# JSW Dulux (formerly Akzo Nobel India) — deep research, Phase A1

Prepared 27 Sep 2026. **Name change:** Akzo Nobel India Ltd became **JSW Dulux Ltd** on 11 Mar 2026; Screener code is now JSWDULUX (the old AKZOINDIA page returns 404). JSW Paints bought 61.20% (open offer + off-market) in Dec 2025; AkzoNobel N.V. sold its remaining 13.56% on the exchanges by 17 Dec 2025 [AR FY26 Board's report; Screener shareholding]. Figures: Screener consolidated unless tagged. **Comparability warning:** the powder-coatings business and International Research Centre were sold to AkzoNobel on 1 Jul 2025, so reported revenue is not comparable; management quotes "like-to-like" (L2L) growth that strips out the carved-out business.

## Business and segments
- **What it sells:** decorative paints under Dulux (Velvet Touch, Weathershield, Promise), Sadolin wood care, waterproofing (Aquatech); industrial coatings — automotive and specialty coatings (ASC: vehicle refinish via Sikkens/Lesonal, auto plastics, specialty), marine and protective (International), coil and "ICO" [IP Q1FY27 p.7–8; AR FY26 MD&A].
- **Mix:** decorative ~60% of revenue; premium is ~45% of decorative (vs ~22% for the market); projects ~20% of decorative, retail 80% of which ~75% goes through distributors [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Share and rank:** #4 overall; ~5% share in decorative, ~7% in industrial paints [IP Q1FY27 p.7]. Targets: #2 overall with JSW Paints by 2030–31, #3 in decorative, #1 in industrial; ASC top-2 and marine/protective leadership by 2031 [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.8].
- **Reach:** presence in 5,000+ towns but "meaningful" presence in 3,400–3,500; target 4,500 towns this year; ~22,000 retailers, ~4,000 B2B customers; 1,387 employees [Q1 call; IP Q1FY27 p.7; ICRA Sep-26].
- **Capacity:** five plants (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Gwalior/Bhind, Mohali, Navi Mumbai), aggregate 305 million litres a year; utilisation ~55–60% in decorative, ~70–80% in industrial [IP Q1FY27 p.7; ICRA Sep-26; Q4 call].
- **Subsidiary:** ICI India Research & Technology Centre (99.99%), which held property later monetised [AR FY26].

## Key numbers
- **Quarterly (₹ cr, revenue / operating profit / OPM / net profit):** Jun-25 995 / 134 / 14% / 91; Sep-25 835 / 111 / 13% / 1,683 (includes ~₹1,882 cr other income, mainly gain on the powder-coatings sale); Dec-25 894 / 136 / 15% / 74; Mar-26 883 / 127 / 14% / 126; Jun-26 965 / 115 / 12% / 80 [Screener].
- **Annual:** FY24 revenue ₹3,962 cr, op. profit ₹633 cr (16%), PAT ₹427 cr; FY25 ₹4,069 cr / ₹642 cr (16%) / ₹430 cr; FY26 ₹3,599 cr / ₹509 cr (14%) / ₹1,974 cr (one-off sale gain); TTM ₹3,578 cr / ₹489 cr [Screener]. ICRA: FY26 revenue ex-carve-out ₹3,584 cr, "flattish"; OPBDIT margin 13.8% [ICRA Sep-26].
- **Slump-sale gain:** powder coatings sold for ₹2,073 cr and research centre for ₹70 cr to an AkzoNobel subsidiary; profit ₹1,892.5 cr [AR FY26 note on exceptional items].
- **Q1 FY27 — standalone vs consolidated:** revenue ₹965 cr (+18.8% L2L; −2.8% vs reported ₹993.1 cr); gross margin 37.4% vs 43.5% L2L; EBITDA ₹115.1 cr (11.9%) vs ₹100.4 cr (12.4%) L2L; **standalone PAT ₹135.5 cr includes a ₹55.9 cr dividend from its own subsidiary and ₹21.5 cr interest on a tax refund; consolidated PAT is ₹79.7 cr** (+18.7% L2L, −12.4% vs reported) [IP Q1FY27 p.13–15].
- **Volume and price:** volume +25% (~18–19% adjusted for price); Q4 FY26 volume +23% with L2L revenue +6.2% because of price cuts of the old 7–9% premiums in Sep–Oct 2025 [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Margin drivers Q1:** ~2 pts from buying high-priced raw material with only ~60 days of decorative inventory; ~2.5 pts from reclassifying painter promotions to net revenue (industry practice); ~160 new sales/R&D staff; underlying gross margin ~39.5–40% [Q1 call].
- **Returns:** ROCE 23% FY26 (42% in FY24–25 before the ₹1,152 cr brand purchase), ROE 19% [Screener].

## Guidance history
- **Q4 call (May 2026):** no FY27 numbers — "toughest question to answer"; margin compression possible "for a quarter"; volume–value gap to close by Aug–Sep 2026; long-term double-digit volume growth; ~3–4% more pricing needed industry-wide; SAP integration around Diwali; blended 8% share target; "strong number three" first [Q4 call].
- **Q1 call (Aug 2026):** FY27 EBITDA margin 13–15% "given crude prices"; double-digit volume and value growth "I continue to maintain"; Q2 "in a similar band"; the 160-person hiring is a one-time "correction"; Project Akshaya synergies are a 3-year road map; ambition upgraded to #2 overall by 2030–31 [Q1 call].
- **Delivery vs Q4 promise:** volume growth delivered (25%); L2L revenue growth jumped from 6.2% to 18.8% as the gap narrowed — ahead of promise. Margin fell to 11.9%, below the new 13–15% band — missed in Q1, with explanations. ICRA expects 14–16% operating margin for FY27 [ICRA Sep-26].
- **Earlier (Akzo era):** ~15% EBITDA; stored profile says a 14.5–15% band was "withdrawn in Q3" — not verified.

## Capex and capacity
- **FY26 capex ₹47 cr** — low [AR FY26 MD&A]. Capex under JSW: "going to be seriously investing in capex… capacity expansion and localization", including more capacity at the Bengaluru industrial plant; **no amount or timeline disclosed** [Q4 call; AR FY26 MD&A].
- **Brand purchase is the big capital item:** Dulux and related decorative IP for India, Bangladesh, Bhutan and Nepal bought from AkzoNobel for ₹1,152 cr (completed 1 Jul 2025), capitalised as an indefinite-life intangible; ends royalty payments on decorative sales [AR FY26 note on intangibles; AR FY26 BRSR].
- **Spare capacity first:** decorative plants at 55–60% leave room; cross-manufacturing started — JSW Paints products made at Gwalior and Mohali, JSW Dulux products at JSW Paints' Vijayanagar/Bengaluru plant [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Synergy money:** ₹2.4 cr realised in Q1 FY27 under Project Akshaya [IP Q1FY27 p.16].

## Cash, debt and working capital
- **No external debt;** borrowings ₹79 cr are leases [Screener; ICRA Sep-26]. Unencumbered cash ₹324 cr at Mar-26, unused working-capital lines; rated [ICRA]AA- (Stable), assigned 4 Sep 2026 [ICRA Sep-26].
- **Where the cash went in FY26:** ₹1,152 cr brand purchase (FCF −₹1,034 cr on Screener) and a special interim dividend of ₹156/share (~₹710 cr) from the slump-sale proceeds, plus ₹50 final — ₹206/share in total [Screener; AR FY26 Board's report].
- **Q1 FY27 inflow:** ₹108 cr income-tax refund (AY 2013-14) incl. ₹21.5 cr interest; ₹64.8 cr real-estate income in Q4 FY26 [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Working capital worsened:** CFO only ₹93 cr in FY26 (33% of operating profit) vs ₹312 cr in FY25; cash conversion cycle 29 days vs −3; debtor days 61 vs 52 [Screener]. Rebates/discounts reached ~25% of gross revenue (includes 4.5–4.7% distributor margin) [Q1 call].

## Moat evidence
- **Brand:** Dulux is a top-tier premium brand; premium ~45% of decorative mix vs market 22%; painters rate quality highly, though "our incentives were historically lower" [Q4 call].
- **Pricing:** old premiums of 7–9% over peers were cut; now ~2% premium on some premium brands and at par with Berger in mid-market; benchmarks prices to the market leader, not new entrants [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Share:** only 5% in decorative; regional strongholds (Bengal, Gujarat, Punjab, Delhi) with weighted share above 4.5% and 8–10% in some states [Q1 call].
- **Industrial:** Sikkens/International technology; wins with Porsche, MG (JSW group), Mahindra; marquee coil projects (Navi Mumbai airport) [Q1 call; Q4 call]. The international industrial brands remain AkzoNobel's; the listed company sells them under licence (royalty still paid to AkzoNobel entities per related-party note; amount not verified).
- **Group backing:** JSW group flexibility is a core rating strength [ICRA Sep-26].

## Risks and red flags
- **Related-party risk under new promoter:** combined operations with unlisted JSW Paints (cross-manufacturing, unified projects team, shared HR head); CFO says Big Four firms are hired so there are "no concerns… related to related-party transactions" [Q1 call]. A merger of JSW Paints into the listed company is widely expected but unannounced — terms would matter to minorities [News: Multibagg, 2026].
- **Auditor:** unmodified; key audit matters are revenue variable consideration and income-tax/indirect-tax litigation; **one whistle-blower complaint under investigation** at report date (CARO 3(xi)(c)); audit trail not kept for some privileged-user changes and database logs lack pre-change values [AR FY26 auditor's report & CARO].
- **Contingent liabilities:** tax disputes ₹47 cr (income tax ₹26.5 cr, sales tax ₹7.6 cr, excise/customs ₹10.2 cr, GST ₹2.7 cr) plus claims ₹6.7 cr [AR FY26 consolidated note 27].
- **Margin reset:** price cuts, 160 hires, promotions and raw-material inflation took EBITDA from ~16% (FY24–25) to 11.9% in Q1 FY27 [Screener; IP Q1FY27].
- **Integration:** SAP/ERP migration by end-2026 (around Diwali peak), exit from AkzoNobel transitional service agreements, HQ moved Gurugram→Mumbai [Q1 call; Q4 call].
- **Competition:** Birla Opus discounting continues; "very competitive for the next 1-2 years"; mass/economy growth "underwhelming" [Q1 call].
- **Reported profit quality:** FY26 PAT dominated by the one-off sale gain; Q1 FY27 standalone PAT inflated by an intra-group dividend.

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: "strong number three first"; Q1: ambition raised to #2 overall (with JSW Paints) by 2030–31 and #1 in industrial [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- Q4 had no margin guidance; Q1 gave 13–15% and explained the 11.9% print.
- New in Q1: Project Akshaya, 160 hires, direct/hybrid distribution in top 20 towns, painter lifecycle programme [Q1 call].
- L2L revenue growth accelerated from 6.2% to 18.8% as the price-cut base effect faded.

## My read
JSW Dulux is growing much faster than before (25% volume, 18.8% L2L revenue) because JSW cut Dulux's price premium and widened distribution, but it is buying that growth with lower margins (11.9% vs ~16% two years ago). The balance sheet is strong but no longer cash-rich: ₹1,152 cr went on the brand and ~₹940 cr on dividends, leaving ₹324 cr of cash. Screener's P/E of ~38 implies earnings growth of roughly 20–21% a year for a decade; TTM reported profit is distorted by the sale gain, and the underlying consolidated run-rate is ~₹80–125 cr a quarter. The case needs margins back at 13–15% while volume growth stays double-digit, plus fair terms if JSW Paints is merged in. It would weaken if margins stay near 12% after Q2, if the ERP switch disrupts the festive season, or if related-party dealings with JSW Paints grow opaque.
