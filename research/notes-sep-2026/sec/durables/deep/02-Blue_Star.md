# Blue Star — deep research (Phase A1, written 27 Sep 2026)

## Business and segments
- Three segments. FY26: **Segment 1 – Electro-Mechanical Projects (MEP contracting) & Commercial AC** ₹6,763 cr revenue (+12.8%), EBIT ₹502 cr (7.4%, vs 8.2% in FY25); **Segment 2 – Unitary Products** (room ACs, commercial refrigeration, air coolers) ₹5,332 cr (−5.1%), EBIT ₹435 cr (8.2%, vs 8.4%); **Segment 3 – Professional Electronics & Industrial Systems** ₹307 cr (−12%), EBIT ₹35 cr (11.4%). Segment 1 is ~55% of revenue. [Q4 call; CARE Jul-26]
- Q1 FY27: Segment 1 ₹1,625 cr (+15.1%), EBIT ₹111 cr (6.8%, vs 7.9%); Segment 2 ₹1,689 cr (+12.8%), EBIT ₹50 cr (2.9%, vs 5.8%); Segment 3 ₹63.6 cr (−9.7%), EBIT ₹9.5 cr (15.1%). [Q1 call, official transcript]
- Room AC share: 14.25% by value, ~11.25% by volume at FY26 end; target 15%. Q1 FY27: lost ~50 bp in April, regained 10 bp in May and 50 bp in June; ended Q1 "marginally lower than 14%" (−30 bp secondary, −65 bp primary). [Q4 call; Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Commercial AC leadership: 53% share in ducted systems, 48% in scroll chillers, ~19% in VRF and ~20% in screw chillers; commercial AC market ~₹5,500 cr (+9% in FY26). Commercial refrigeration share ~35% (CARE). [AR FY26 MD&A; CARE Jul-26]
- Data-centre MEP: ~30% share of the data-centre MEP market (₹3,500 cr, "to more than double in three years"); only 10–12% share in data-centre chillers where MNCs dominate. [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Plants: Himachal (two), Dadra, Wada, Ahmedabad, Sri City (subsidiary Blue Star Climatech, 10.5 lakh split-AC capacity per AR). MD at Q4: Himachal ~6.5 lakh units at 100%, Sri City ~9 lakh, built in 3-lakh modules and able to go to 12 lakh. [AR FY26 Board's report; Q4 call]
- Exports (US/Europe heat pumps as a contract design-manufacturer, plus Middle East): ~US$80–85 million in FY26, up ~40% from US$55–60 million; target an extra US$100 million a year from FY28, dependent on US tariffs. [Q1 call]
- Sold the loss-making MedTech business of subsidiary Blue Star E&E to Cyrix Healthcare for up to ₹40 cr (FY26 revenue ₹42 cr, net worth ~₹7 cr), effective 1 Sep 2026. [BSE filings 14 Aug & 2 Sep 2026]

## Key numbers (Screener, consolidated)
- Quarterly sales / operating profit / OPM / net profit: Jun-25 ₹2,982 / ₹199 / 7% / ₹121 cr; Sep-25 ₹2,422 / ₹182 / 8% / ₹99 cr; Dec-25 ₹2,925 / ₹220 / 8% / ₹81 cr; Mar-26 ₹4,072 / ₹326 / 8% / ₹227 cr; Jun-26 ₹3,378 / ₹175 / 5% / ₹103 cr. [Screener]
- Annual: FY24 sales ₹9,685 cr, OPM 7%, PAT ₹414 cr; FY25 ₹11,968 cr, 7%, ₹591 cr; FY26 ₹12,402 cr (+3.6%), 8%, ₹527 cr (−11%). TTM sales ₹12,798 cr, PAT ₹509 cr. [Screener]
- Q1 FY27: sales +13%, operating profit −12%, net profit −15%; PBT before exceptional items ₹125.6 cr (−23.7%). [Screener; Q1 call]
- Depreciation is climbing with new capacity: ₹98 cr (FY24) → ₹128 cr → ₹179 cr (FY26) → ₹57 cr in Q1 FY27 alone. Interest ₹72 cr in FY26 (₹49 cr FY25). [Screener; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Unit economics: industry needed ~13% price rise (5% energy-label, 8% commodities/rupee); Blue Star passed through only ~5% (3–5% by model). Q1 industry RAC volume +21%, value +25%; Blue Star volume +18%, value +21%. Commercial refrigeration fell ~15% (deep freezers, ice-cream OEMs). [Q1 call]
- ROCE 21% FY26 (26% in FY24–FY25); ROE 17%. [Screener]

## Guidance history
- Q4 call (7 May 2026): Segment 2 margin **8–8.5%** for FY27 ("as we see today"), Segment 1 **7–7.5%**; 13% price rise to be fully passed on by May–June; commercial AC growth 8–10%; data-centre MEP ~₹1,000 cr revenue now, ~₹3,000 cr in three years (~15% of revenue). [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivered: Segment 2 margin 2.9%; Segment 1 6.8%; only 5% of 13% passed. MD: "we have lost the first match". [Q1 call]
- Q1 call (7 Aug 2026) reset: Segment 2 **"over 6.5%"** for FY27 (7–7.5% aspiration, no visibility) — **cut** from 8–8.5%; Segment 1 **6.5–7%** — **cut** from 7–7.5% (management called it "remains the same"). Q2 "slight improvement", Q3 "much better", Q4 "defining". [Q1 call]
- Data-centre MEP **raised**: FY27 order inflow ~₹3,000 cr and revenue ₹1,350–1,400 cr (both figures said on the call); FY28 inflow ~₹4,500 cr, revenue ~₹2,100 cr; ~₹4,000 cr (~20% of revenue) by FY29. Segment 1 growth "8–10% may go up to 12% for a couple of years". [Q1 call]
- Long-term view on the industry: RAC volumes ~15 million → 30 million units by FY30 (18–20% CAGR); industry margin drifting down — "12% once, then 9.5–10%, then 8–8.5%, now 7.5–8%"; he still expects 7.5% for Blue Star's products business. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]

## Capex and capacity
- Annual spend ₹250–350 cr including maintenance, R&D intangibles and IT; Q1 FY27 ₹60–70 cr; FY27 plan ₹300–350 cr, funded internally. [Q4 call; Q1 call; CARE Jul-26]
- Fixed assets rose from ₹859 cr (FY23) to ₹1,697 cr (FY26) as Sri City came on stream; CWIP ₹137 cr. Capital commitments ₹191 cr at Mar-26. [Screener; AR FY26 note 38]
- Next step: an extra Sri City assembly line (3-lakh module, building already in place) — decision deferred from 2025 to **October 2026**. [Q4 call]
- Product capex shifts to cost-down: re-engineered entry-level RAC range, alternate components and some outsourcing by Q4 FY27; indigenised VRF cassette units at Sri City. No compressor making in-house. [Q1 call; AR FY26 MD&A]
- Payback: ROCE slid from 26% to 21% as the asset base doubled; the new capacity needs volume and better pricing to earn back. [Screener]

## Cash, debt and working capital
- Net cash (company definition): ₹640 cr (Mar-25) → ₹175.5 cr (Mar-26) → ₹900 cr (Jun-26, vs ₹371 cr a year earlier) as Q1 released working capital. [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- CARE's view at Mar-26: debt ~₹1,177 cr against cash and liquid investments ~₹833 cr (net debt ~₹344 cr on its definition), gearing 0.36x, interest cover 12.9x; ratings CARE AA+ (Stable)/A1+, commercial-paper limit raised to ₹4,202 cr. Screener borrowings ₹810 cr (₹381 cr FY25). [CARE Jul-26; Screener]
- FY26 cash flow weak: operating cash ₹154 cr (₹688 cr FY25), free cash flow −₹169 cr. Inventory days 131, debtor days 63, payable days 177 (209 in FY25); cash conversion cycle turned from −18 to +16 days. [Screener]
- Dividend ₹8.5/share for FY26 (₹9 for FY25), ~33% payout. [Q4 call; Screener]

## Moat evidence
- Commercial AC: dominant shares in ducted (53%) and scroll chillers (48%); preferred data-centre MEP contractor with ~30% share, clients "blocking our capacity" and asking for three-year contracts; data-centre jobs take 8–12 months, have metal-price escalation clauses and good payment terms. [AR FY26 MD&A; Q1 call]
- RAC brand: 14.25% value share on 11.25% volume share shows a price premium; but Q1 showed it cannot hold price when rivals with cheap old stock and OEM-made ACs "operate with 3.5% to 4% margin". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Service reach: 2,100+ service centres, 4,000+ channel partners, 8,800+ commercial refrigeration stores. [AR FY26 MD&A; IP Q1FY27; CARE Jul-26]
- Cost position weaker than Voltas on components: ~40% of RAC bill of materials imported (analyst's figure, not disputed); compressors bought in. [Q1 call]

## Risks and red flags
- Price war in room ACs: industry capacity "almost double the market size" thanks to PLI; MD warns the industry margin could settle at 7–7.5%. [Q1 call]
- Oman JV dispute: W.J. Towell claimed up to OMR 21.2 million (~₹462 cr) in ICC arbitration over Blue Star Oman; award (Jul 2026) dismissed all claims; WJT has sought annulment before the Dubai Court of Appeal (Sep 2026). Not in contingent liabilities as a claim. [BSE filing 15 Sep 2026]
- Contingent liabilities ₹179 cr at Mar-26 (₹202 cr a year earlier), mostly income tax ₹140 cr and GST ₹26 cr; uncertain tax positions ₹16.5 cr. [AR FY26 note 38]
- Auditor (Deloitte): unmodified opinion, no emphasis of matter; key audit matter is cost estimates on fixed-price contracts. [AR FY26 auditor's report]
- Working capital: inventory 131 days and debt-funded stocking in FY26; margin guidance depends on commodity and rupee moves the company cannot control. [Screener; CARE Jul-26]
- Export plan hostage to US tariffs; Middle East business muted by the West Asia conflict. [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone: Q4 was confident (Q4 Segment 2 margin 10.4%, "8–8.5% is possible"); Q1 was defensive ("lost the first match"), with margin guidance cut in both main segments.
- Strategy: from "we are not chasing market share" (Q4) to "we should not allow market share to deteriorate", funded by consumer-finance schemes and in-store promotion.
- Numbers: order book ₹6,923 → ₹7,764 cr; net cash ₹175.5 → ₹900 cr; data-centre revenue target lifted from ~₹3,000 cr to ~₹4,000 cr by FY29.
- New: portfolio re-engineering toward entry-level models ("90% has to be" lowest-cost entry products in hindsight); MedTech sale; Oman award.

## My read
Blue Star is two businesses heading in opposite directions: a strong, cash-generating projects and commercial-AC arm riding the data-centre boom, and a room-AC arm whose pricing power broke in Q1 FY27. The FY27 guide of >6.5% for Segment 2 needs Q2–Q4 margins near 8–9% after a 2.9% Q1, which is demanding in a lean Q2 with copper at records. The price needs both a return to ~7.5% product margins and the data-centre ramp to ₹2,000+ cr of revenue by FY28. Watch Q2–Q3 Segment 2 margins, data-centre order inflow against the ₹3,000 cr FY27 target, and the October decision on the Sri City line.
