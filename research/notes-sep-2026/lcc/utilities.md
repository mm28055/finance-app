######## 01-Va_Tech_Wabag.md
## Guidance history
- Medium-term outlook (set ~FY24, 3–5 years): revenue 15–20% CAGR; EBITDA 13–15%; ROCE >20%; ROE >15%; order book ≥3x revenue; O&M 20% of revenue; net cash positive. [IP Q1FY27 p.18]
- Q4 call (May 2026): kept all bands; "we are conservative"; Kuwait order expected in Q1, Hadda (KSA) in Q2; municipal HAM investment platform "another quarter" away. [Q4 call]
- Q1 delivery: revenue +20.8% (top of band) — **met**; Kuwait won — **met**; UAE entry (Ajman) — **met**; Hadda still "preferred EPC partner", not signed — **pending**; O&M 18% of revenue, growing 7% — **behind**; municipal platform — **no update (slipped)**; margin excluding forex 9% — **missed** on the underlying measure, met on the company's measure.
- Q1 call: CFO now says "fairer to expect a 13% to 14% kind of band in this year" — a soft trim from the 13–15% band; a new 5-year "Wabag 2.0" plan due at the May 2027 investor meet. [Q1 call]
- "Future energy" (solar, semiconductors, data centres, green H2): Q4 meet pitched a $4–6 bn opportunity; Q1 call said "decadal view", only 1–2 selective orders in 12–18 months. **Walked back.** [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from a visionary investor-day pitch ($75–100 bn market, $4–6 bn future energy) to defending margin quality: analysts pushed on forex, falling gross margins, higher provisions and slower O&M.
- Backlog cleaned: ₹600 cr of framework orders removed; backlog rose from ₹17,200 cr to ₹19,400 cr anyway.
- Margin guidance softened to 13–14% for FY27; the long-term plan deferred to May 2027.
- Geography: Kuwait and UAE entries delivered as promised; Hadda still pending.

######## 02-ION_Exchage.md
## Guidance history
- Q4 call (May 2026) promises vs Q1 (Aug 2026) delivery:
  - Roha resin plant at 25% utilisation in FY27 — Q1: "maintain that guidance", but "performance has been softer than we expected"; update promised after Q2. **At risk.**
  - Other (non-UP) large legacy project to close in FY27 — Q1: "a significant part" should progress by year-end; completion depends on client readiness. **Softened.**
  - UP Jal Jeevan project: Q4 said ~30% of scope pending, not finishing in FY27; Q1: unexecuted part is ~11% of order backlog (~₹270 cr), will spill over; execution throttled to fund release. **Unchanged (slow).**
  - GCC shipments (~₹60 cr deferred in Q4) — resumed in Q1. **Met.**
  - Consumer break-even in FY27 — Q1 loss ₹0.3 cr on +33% revenue. **On track.**
  - Chemicals margin recovery via price increases — Q1 chemicals EBIT still −52%; ~6 pts of margin hit from Roha, ~3–4 pts from absent forex gain, rest from input-cost lag. **Missed so far.**
- No numeric FY27 guidance: "traditional practice" is to give an outlook in H2. Management: return to double-digit margins "will take time". [Q4 call; Q1 call]
- Longer targets: resin capacity ~5x the pre-Roha base after Phase I (2x) + Phase II + debottlenecking; aim for 10% of global resin volumes; pharma-resin capacity 6x in 12 months; Roha asset turn "just under 2x" at full capacity in ~4 years; chemicals revenue +50% "over the next couple of years". [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: West Asia blamed; promises of Roha 25% utilisation and legacy closure this year. Q1: no excuse left for the chemicals miss beyond Roha and cost lag; management admitted Q1 was "disappointing" and that "we are not out of the woods".
- New five-segment disclosure: good for transparency, but it exposed that project EPC has been sliding for four years.
- New wins (Hyundai ₹500 cr+, Oman concession, Malawi $18.1 mn JV) point to a more selective, export/service-heavy order book.

######## 03-Enviro_Infra_Engine_.md
## Guidance history
- FY26: guided ~35% revenue growth and (on the Q3 call) ~₹230 cr PAT; delivered 7.5% and ₹188 cr. Management blames slow tender finalisation, a re-bid, and design approvals (MIDC ZLD, two Bangalore jobs). **Missed badly.** [Q4 call]
- Q4 call (May 2026) for FY27: revenue ₹2,000 cr (water ₹1,350 cr + renewables ₹650 cr); EBITDA 21–22% (cut from 22–24%); PAT "₹270–280 cr", ~14%; order inflow ≥₹2,500 cr; working capital back to ~90 days; FY28 revenue ₹2,500–2,600 cr "conservative". [Q4 call official]
- Q1 call (Aug 2026): revenue ₹2,000 cr **kept**; blended EBITDA now **19–20%** (21–22% applies only to water); PAT **₹260–270 cr** (13–14%) — a quiet ₹10 cr cut; employee cost to fall from 7% to 5–5.5% of sales; finance cost 3–3.5% of sales; growth "25–30%" a year after FY27 (was "35–40%" in May). [Q1 call official]
- Q1 delivery vs promise: revenue ₹359 cr = 18% of the ₹2,000 cr target (management: "20% ±1–2%"); ~80% of FY27 must come in Q2–Q4, and ~40% in Q4 alone. Order inflow ₹257 cr of ₹2,500 cr. Working capital "still bloated", cash-flow improvement now promised "by September". **Behind on cash; on track only if H2 is very heavy.**

## What changed between the two calls
- May: defensive about the FY26 miss, confident on ₹270–280 cr PAT. August: PAT range lowered to ₹260–270 cr and blended margin to 19–20% without being flagged as a cut; cash-flow normalisation pushed from "very soon" to "by September".
- New emphasis on HAM and on renewables being "not aggressive" in new orders; desalination and overseas bids still only talk.

######## 04-EMS_Limited.md
## Guidance history
- FY26: guided ~20% growth; on the Q3 call (Feb 2026) said Q4 would recover lost ground; CRISIL in April still assumed ₹950–1,000 cr. Actual ₹731 cr (−25%). **Missed badly.** [Q4 call; CRISIL Apr-26]
- Q4 call (May 2026) for FY27: revenue "somewhere about ₹1,000-odd crores"; order wins >₹1,500 cr; PAT margin ~15%, EBITDA ~21% "try to raise it to about 25%"; ₹50 cr of finished Dehradun work to be billed in Q1; West Bengal billing to restart "within 10 to 15 days"; pledge "zero by the end of next year". [Q4 call official]
- Q1 call (Aug 2026): revenue cut to **₹900–950 cr** ("at par with FY25"); Q2 to be only 30–35% above Q1, Q3–Q4 +50% each; West Bengal full run-rate only from Q3; margins "slightly lower than FY24" because competition is rising. [Q1 call official]
- Q1 delivery: revenue ₹157 cr (+30% QoQ but −34% YoY); order inflow ₹317 cr against ₹1,500 cr target; West Bengal restart slipped a quarter. To reach ₹900 cr the company needs ~₹745 cr in three quarters vs ₹493 cr in the last three. **Guidance cut; credibility weak.**

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4: apology and a list of external causes (election, SPARSH payment portal, bitumen shortage, Uttarakhand rains), plus sharp investor questions on pledges and the fund-raise. Q1: tone calmer, but FY27 revenue target cut from ~₹1,000 cr to ₹900–950 cr and pushed into H2; pledges not discussed.

######## 05-Antony_Waste_Hand.md
## Guidance history
- Q4 call (1 Jun 2026): 15–20% revenue CAGR for 5 years; EBITDA 20–22% "maintained"; Labour Code impact "just ₹5.2 cr"; "we don't expect a timing mismatch significantly" on escalations; PCMC WTE running at ~86% PLF after maintenance; ₹15 cr Bhiwandi arbitration award to be paid within 3 months; maiden dividend ₹0.50/share. [Q4 call official]
- Q1 delivery (11 Aug 2026): EBITDA 16.8% — **missed**; labour cost called "a surprise item"; escalation lag now expected to take ~3 quarters, historic margins by FY28 — **pushed out**; PCMC WTE shut after the 8 July landslide, restart first/second week of October, ₹2.5–3 cr/month fixed cost and ₹22–24 cr impairment in Q2 — **reversed**; Bhiwandi ₹15 cr "still pending" — **slipped**; C&D ramp — **met**; growth reframed as 6–9% from existing contracts + 10–15% from new wins — **kept in substance**. [Q1 call official]
- Vehicle scrapping / tyre recycling: promoted earlier, now "wait and watch"; still "evaluating". [Q4 call; Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- June: celebratory (25 years, first dividend, ₹18,000 cr order book, margins "held at ~22%"). August: nine deaths at PCMC, WTE shut, margins 16.8%, PAT near zero, and management conceding costs were a surprise. The order book and EPR/"diversification" talk faded; the message became "transitional quarter".
