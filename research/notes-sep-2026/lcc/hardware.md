######## 01-GNG_Electronics.md
## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (5 May 2026): FY27 revenue growth ~25%; PAT margin up "at least 50 bps" from 7.0%; implied EBITDA margin ~11.5% (from 10.6%); quarterly margins to be "slightly above" Q4's 9.8% from Q1 on; debtor days held at ~40–45; net debt ~₹300 cr "is the trajectory we expect"; no equity raise needed through FY28–29. [Q4 call]
- FY26 delivery vs its own guidance: management says it beat both the original FY26 guidance and the raise made on the Q3 call (FY26 revenue +34%, PAT +91%). [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (30 Jul 2026): **raised** FY27 revenue growth to ~30% (from 25%) and PAT-margin expansion to 75–100 bps (from 50 bps), i.e. PAT margin ~7.75–8.0%. [Q1 call]
- Q1 delivery vs Q4 promises: revenue +32% (ahead); EBITDA margin 12.8% (ahead of 11.5% implied); PAT margin 7.0% (flat on FY26's 7.0%, but Q1 is seasonally weak); **net debt missed** — ₹406 cr vs "~₹300 cr trajectory". [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- CARE's own base case (Apr-26) is more cautious: 18–20% revenue growth a year over the medium term, PBILDT margin above 10%. [CARE Apr-26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "component supercycle" to "trust as a margin lever" and new-buyer segments (students in India/Africa).
- Guidance raised (revenue 25% → 30%; PAT-margin expansion 50 → 75–100 bps).
- Gross margin jumped from 19.2% (Q4) to 24.6% (Q1), helped by seasonal mix (more US/Europe) and inventory gains.
- Net debt went the wrong way (from ~₹300–399 cr to ₹406 cr) despite the Q4 promise, and the two calls gave different March figures.
- New: Redington pact; ASP ceiling argument (30% of new price, could go to 35–50%); admission that corporates may extend laptop life, shrinking the disposal pool.

######## 02-Rashi_Peripheral.md
## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (14 May 2026): long-run 20% revenue CAGR "in the coming year as well"; run-rate business 15–20% plus projects only "subject to ROI"; PES prices to rise another ~20%; PES units may fall 5–10% but value grow 10–15%; PAT margin "range of 1.5 to 1.75" for the industry; working-capital days "around same range" (58); Dell to approach double-digit revenue share in FY27; consumer softness possible in H2. Margin expansion from semicon/enterprise is a 2–3 year story, not FY27. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +62% (far above 20%); PAT margin 2.05% (above the 1.5–1.75% range); working-capital days 56 (better); Dell ~5% of revenue, "above the target"; no large data-centre project taken. [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- Q1 FY27 call (5 Aug 2026): Q2 to show a "similar trend" to Q1's ~60% growth, with price increases running at about half Q1's speed; 20% CAGR floor restated for FY28; Restar JV target >US$100 mn revenue within three years; VDA to add ~5% of revenue. Guidance effectively **raised** for the near term (no formal FY27 number). [Q1 call]
- CRISIL (Sep-26) expects continued growth in FY27; upgrade needs growth above 25% sustained with margins held. [CRISIL Sep-26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Growth jumped from 51% (Q4) to 62% (Q1); Q2 guided similar.
- Talk of a ₹20,000–25,000 cr data-centre funnel (Q4) gave way to "neo-cloud" run-rate deals and a clear statement that big projects would stretch the balance sheet.
- Strategy moved from intent to deals: VDA bought, Restar JV signed.
- New cautions: "orange alert" on component supply; 50% shortage in entry-level laptops; channel inventory build-up.
- PAT margin moved above the 1.5–1.75% "industry range" management had described in May.

######## 03-Control_Print.md
## Guidance history
- Q4 FY26 call (21 May 2026): coding & marking "this year should also be reasonably good"; Markprint and Codeology to grow 15–20%; CP Italy loss to fall from ~€2–2.5 mn to ~€1.5 mn in FY27 and "could easily breakeven this year"; the ₹31.2 cr IP purchase from CP Italy to be the last infusion; Track & Trace a "major addition to sales" this year; Guwahati (UNNATI) plant to cut sachet-film cost ~40% (₹2.5 → ₹1.5 a pack) with ₹7.5 cr capital subsidy, 5% interest subvention and GST refunds. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: standalone revenue +4.2% (vs a 12-quarter record of ~10%+); consolidated +3.8%; EBITDA and PAT down; packaging still loss-making; UNNATI incentives suspended by government. [IP Q1FY27] [Screener] [Q1 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (24 Jul 2026): standalone coding & marking to grow 10–15% for FY27 (kept); packaging breakeven **pushed** to H1 FY28 ("I don't think it'll necessarily break even in the second half of this financial year"); told packaging team to "slow down on the sales" until machines are reliable; one price increase plus an Iran-war surcharge to recover "a couple of percent" of margin; Track & Trace pilots with three pharma customers — more in Q2/Q3. [Q1 call]
- Verdict: core guidance kept but Q1 far behind it; subsidiary guidance **cut**.

## What changed between the two calls
- Q4 was a spirited defence of the Italy bet as a deliberate IP-building choice; Q1 admitted the machines do not work well enough and slowed sales.
- Breakeven moved from "could easily breakeven this year" (FY27) to H1 FY28.
- UNNATI incentives — a central plank of the Q4 pitch — suspended.
- Core business: Q4 standalone revenue grew ~21% (₹138 cr vs ₹114 cr); Q1 only +4%.
- New: price increase plus surcharge; pharma QR draft could widen the Track & Trace market.

######## 04-TVS_Electronics.md
## Guidance history
- Company policy is no numeric guidance. [Q2 call] [Q4 call]
- Q2 FY26 call (Nov 2025): growth initiatives "started showing results" and the trend should continue; margins should improve as volumes rise on a fixed cost base. H2 FY26 did deliver: EBITDA margin rose from 3.7% (Q2) to 5.96% (Q4). [Q2 call] [Screener]
- Q4 FY26 call (25 May 2026): margin improvement is "structural rather than temporary"; "double-digit growth, obviously" for FY27; SMT utilisation (30–40%) to rise in FY27; EMS prototype orders to "fructify in FY27"; delayed PSG orders "may fructify in this year". [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 delivery: revenue +9.6% (just short of double-digit); margin **reversed** to –2.3% — the "structural" margin claim failed its first test. No call was held to explain it. [IP Q1FY27]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "turnaround quarter" (Q2 FY26, first profit in a year) to "structural" margin gains (Q4 FY26).
- Q4 added memory-price trouble: customers postponed PSG orders; inventory and receivables rose, pushing debt up.
- Revenue growth slowed from 22% (Q2) to 2% (Q4) as low-margin orders were dropped.
- Since then (no call): Q1 FY27 swung back to a loss.
