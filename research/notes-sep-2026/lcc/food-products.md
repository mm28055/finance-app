- **EBITDA margin target:** long-standing aim of 17–18% within "the next couple of years", excluding Comfort Click, restated on the Q4 FY26 call. Q1 FY27 reported 16.8% (down from 18.1%); management says like-for-like margin rose ~0.4 percentage points. **Kept; not yet met.** [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- **Comfort Click accretion:** at the deal, management said it would add to EPS within two years. Q4 said it was already EPS-accretive in Q4 FY26, and Q1 repeated this. **Delivered early.** [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Comfort Click margins:** Q4 said it was "in line or slightly exceeded" the ~14% EBITDA expected at acquisition; about 3 percentage points of reported growth was a currency (GBP/₹) benefit. [Q4 call]
- **Seasonal brands:** on the Q4 call, management expected recovery "from May onwards", and the chairman agreed that Q1 FY27 seasonal brands could grow on a weak base. Actual Q1: seasonal brands -12% (Glucon-D flat, Nycil sharply down because of rain in the East and North and high stock with retailers). **Missed.** Management now expects a "more positive note" for the rest of FY27. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- **Tax:** Q4 guided ~25% effective tax in FY27. Q1 came in at ~27% because UK thin-capitalisation rules disallowed some interest; about 12–15% of it will be paid in cash in FY27, rising to the full 25% in cash from FY28. **Slightly worse.** [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- **Interest cost:** the GBP bridge loan was refinanced into a cheaper EUR loan at the end of Q4. Q1 interest fell to ₹26 cr from ₹39 cr in Q4, and the Q1 run-rate should hold. **Better.** [Q1 call] [Screener]
- **Growth:** management avoids numbers — "double-digit" for international and for the non-seasonal portfolio over 3–4 years. RiteBite is growing at more than twice its pre-acquisition 25% CAGR, reaching about ₹244 cr in FY26 (figure put by an analyst, not disputed). [Q1 call] [Q4 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone on the seasonal portfolio moved from "recovery from May" (Q4) to "impacted, but second half of the quarter recovered" (Q1). Q1 showed a third straight poor summer quarter.
- Interest cost fell after the EUR refinancing. Tax came in slightly above guidance because of UK thin-cap rules.
- Comfort Click growth stayed high (like-for-like international +24.8% in Q1 vs +31.4% in Q4). New steps: a US D2C site, a Walmart listing, noon UAE and a physical UAE base.
- Organised-channel share jumped to 38% (Q1) from 30% (FY26 average).
- Chairman Sharvil Patel attended the Q4 call but not the Q1 call; the CEO and CFO ran Q1.
