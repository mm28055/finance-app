######## 01-Dr_Lal_PathLabs.md
## Guidance history
- **Q4 FY26 call (30 Apr 2026) set for FY27:** revenue growth "early to mid-teens", which the CEO defined as **13–15%**; EBITDA margin **27–28%** (extra margin to be reinvested); capex **₹100–120 cr**, including 1–2 radiology centres, 12–15 new labs and a precision lab; no new Sovaaka centres; no price increase "immediately on the cards", "a few quarters away"; patient-volume growth of 6–7% (as recalled on the Q1 call) [Q4 call; Q1 call].
- **Q1 FY27 delivery (24 Jul 2026):** revenue +19.1% (ahead of 13–15%), EBITDA margin 31.0% (ahead of 27–28%), patient volume +8.2% (ahead of 6–7%) [Q1 call]. Management **kept** the formal guidance until after Q2 but leaned up: "chances are we might be more towards mid-teens rather than early teens". The extra CGHS margin will flow through "for maybe one or two quarters more", but the "dominant idea" is to reinvest [Q1 call].
- **Capex raised:** "roughly about ₹140–150 crore" (from ₹100–120 cr). Radiology plans went from 1–2 to "maybe three to four centres" in FY27, including 1–2 pilots in Tier-2 towns in the North [Q1 call]. The official wording ("We touch roughly…") is loose, but the direction is up.
- **Price increase:** still deferred. It will be reconsidered only after H1 FY27. The company passed on the GST cut and has not raised list prices for three years [Q4 call; Q1 call]. CARE noted in April that management had "no plan to do so in the near term" in order to gain share [CARE Apr-26].
- International: no revenue target. The contribution will stay under 5% for several years, with focus on Africa, the Middle East, the CIS countries and South-East Asia [Q1 call].
- CARE's own forecast is 11–12% revenue growth a year and a 26–29% PBILDT margin in the medium term [CARE Apr-26].

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from "early to mid-teens, don't read one quarter" (Q4, with 16.6% growth and 8.2% volume) to "more towards mid-teens", with the formal upgrade held until after H1 (Q1, with 19.1% growth and 31% margin).
- New driver named: the CGHS/ECHS rate revision (2–3 points of growth, flowing to margin for 1–2 more quarters). This was not mentioned in April.
- Spending plans went up: capex from ₹100–120 cr to ₹140–150 cr, and radiology from 1–2 centres to 3–4.
- International became concrete: Ghana acquisition, Uzbekistan joint venture, Neuome stake. In April there was only the Dubai subsidiary.
- Unchanged: the 27–28% margin anchor, no price hike before H2, 12–15 labs, a reagent-cost watch.
