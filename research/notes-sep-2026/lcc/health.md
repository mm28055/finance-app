######## 01-Poly_Medicure.md
## Guidance history
- Q3 FY26 promise: H2 revenue ~20% above H1. It delivered ₹1,029 cr vs ₹847 cr (+21%). FY26 standalone EBITDA came in at 26.8% against a 25–27% guide. Delivered. [Q4 call]
- FY26 miss vs the start-of-year hope: management had talked of mid-teens growth. Consolidated growth was 12.3% including acquisitions, and standalone only 3.8%. [Q4 call]
- Q4 FY26 call (25 May 2026), FY27 guidance: consolidated revenue ₹2,300–2,400 cr (+23–28%); standalone ₹1,900–1,950 cr (domestic >20%, international >15%); standalone EBITDA margin 25–27%; consolidated 23–25%; capex ₹200–225 cr. It warned gross margin could slip 200–300 bps to ~66% from crude-linked inputs. Renal growth ~20% (cut from an earlier 30–35% hope because of Chinese imports). Citieffe to grow 10–12% in euros; PendraCare guidance "restricted". [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (10 Aug 2026): consolidated guidance **kept** at ₹2,300–2,400 cr. The standalone range **widened up** to ₹1,900–2,000 cr. Margin guides kept, with standalone expected at the top of 25–27%. Standalone gross margin to settle at 68–69%, consolidated at 71–72%. **Renal cut again** to 15–18% ("possibly close to 20%") after a −3.8% Q1. New Vision 2030 ("Polymed 3.0"): **double revenue by FY30** (~18% CAGR from ₹1,875 cr), about 80% organic, at similar margins. [Q1 call, official transcript]
- Q1 delivery vs Q4 promises: consolidated margin 24.1%, inside the guided band; standalone 28.0%, above it. The gross-margin squeeze did not happen, helped by price rises and inventory bought before the input spike. Revenue ₹525 cr is +30%, but reaching ₹2,300 cr needs ~₹592 cr a quarter for the rest of the year. The Middle East remains stuck. Renal missed. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- CRISIL's base case (Jun-26) is more cautious on margins: 20–22% revenue growth in FY27, operating margin moderating to 23–25%. [CRISIL Jun-26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (Q4: "year of deliberate transition", 200–300 bps gross-margin warning) to offensive (Q1: Polymed 3.0 and a doubling by FY30). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Margin fear disappeared once price rises stuck, but management still guides standalone gross margin back to 68–69%, so Q1's 71.5% should not be extrapolated. [Q1 call]
- Renal worsened: the Q4 guide was ~20% growth, Q1 came in at −3.8%, and the new guide is 15–18%. The fix now depends on an anti-dumping duty. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- New leadership hires: a CEO for India/APAC (ex-B. Braun, Translumina), a CEO for Brazil, and a head of renal (ex-Nipro, Fresenius, DaVita). [IP Q1FY27]
- The Middle East went from "logistics nightmare" to a 32% revenue decline. [Q4 call] [Q1 call]

######## 02-Tarsons_Products.md
## Guidance history
- Start of FY26: full commercial commissioning of Panchla by the start of FY27 (as quoted by an analyst on the Q1 call). [Q1 call]
- Q4 FY26 call (25 May 2026): whole capex programme to be "fully commissioned during the first half" of FY27. Depreciation to peak at ₹105–110 cr (standalone) in FY27. Maintenance capex ~₹20 cr. PAT to stay "single-digit" but no accounting losses. Gross margin "should not go below 65%". Debt to stay under 2x EBITDA, with the focus on deleveraging. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (11 Aug 2026): revenue growth guided at **15%+ for FY27** (Q1 +21%), with 20–22% possible if new products and exports work. Q1-level margins were expected for the rest of the year. Net debt to fall ₹40–50 cr in FY27 ("we can achieve ₹40 cr"). New products are expected to bring ₹65–70 cr in FY28, and Panchla plus Amta 20–25% of FY28 standalone revenue. Interest runs at ~₹20 cr a year and should fall in FY28. [Q1 call]
- Q1 delivery vs Q4 promises: the **commissioning timeline slipped again**. It had been "H1 FY27"; it is now cell culture lines "towards the end of Q2 FY27", remaining lines in Q2/Q3, and cell-culture revenue from H2 FY27. The **"no accounting losses"** promise was **missed** immediately: Q1 consolidated PAT was −₹1.4 cr. Standalone gross margin of 67.1% stayed above the 65% floor. Growth of +21% beat the 15% guide, but management admits "a significant shipment benefit" in exports. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- CARE (Apr-26) had expected production of most products by early April 2026, with Petri dishes "a few more months" later. [CARE Apr-26]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone moved from defensive (Q4: exports −13%, a 70% input-cost spike) to confident (Q1: +21% revenue, 15%+ FY27 guide, "industry position... strongest in 4–5 years"). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Input-cost pressure is still unresolved: prices eased briefly and then "started seeing input costs move up sharply" again in the fortnight before the call. [Q1 call]
- The commissioning date moved again, and the promise of no loss was broken within one quarter. [Q1 call] [Q4 call]
- New numbers: ₹65–70 cr from new products in FY28 and a peak revenue potential of ₹750–800 cr. [Q1 call]

######## 03-Laxmi_Denta.md
## Guidance history
- Q3 FY26 (per an analyst on the Q4 call): EBITDA margin 18–20%, gross margin 75–80% and PAT margin 13–15% by FY27–28. [Q4 call]
- Q4 FY26 call (22 May 2026): **declined to guide** ("calling out anything today... is not what we want to do"). Growth "similar to what we have done in the past" (24%, then 16.2%). Capex from IPO money as needed. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (12 Aug 2026): **aspiration reinstated**: revenue growth 15–20% and EBITDA margin 18–20%; margin to hold at 19.2% "or go higher". Domestic lab growth (12%) to speed up in Q2–Q4 as scanners mature. Scanner target of 800–1,000 kept. A letter of intent was signed to buy land in Palghar to merge its two rented domestic plants (current rent ~₹2 cr a year) into one owned site about 3x the size. [Q1 call]
- Q1 delivery vs Q4: margin 19.2% vs 18.3% (held and improved); revenue +13.9% (just below the 15–20% aspiration); international lab +37%; aligners recovered to +29% (from +11% for Bizdent in Q4). But the margin gain came partly from a 62% drop in scanner sales. If ~800 scanners are to be placed, the remaining quarters must be much heavier on scanners, which will dilute gross margin. [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]

## What changed between the two calls
- Moved from no guidance (Q4) to a restated 15–20% growth and 18–20% margin aspiration (Q1). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- New: US CEO hired (32 years in medtech, 11 in dental), a letter of intent for the Palghar land, three new support facilities in Indian cities, and AI-crown automation spending (adding to other expenses this year). [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- Aligners recovered from the Q3–Q4 price war (Bizdent +11% in Q4, +28% in Q1). [Q4 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- Scanners went from a growth tool (211 in Q4) to "minimal" in Q1, with the technology roadmap "under evaluation". [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]

######## 04-OSEL_Devices.md
## Guidance history
- H1 FY26 call (Nov 2025): a follow-up mobile order of 3 lakh units was in hand, with a 5 lakh-unit order targeted "by December". Hearing-aid retail push and JNPA plans were outlined. [H1 call]
- H2 FY26 call (2 Jun 2026): FY27 to "continue the growth momentum", with EBITDA and revenue both above FY26. The CEO agreed that **₹500 cr+ FY27 revenue** is possible ("If the math suggests so, we will be able to do that"). Export target ₹50 cr. EBITDA margin "similar or improve". CMS software to reach 50,000–100,000 screens in 2–3 years (300 today). Quarterly reporting promised "maybe from the third quarter". [H2 call]
- Delivery vs promises: FY26 +57% growth was delivered, but H2 was flat on H1 because of the March slippage. The 5 lakh-unit mobile order target from H1 is not confirmed as met; FY26 total was ~4.5 lakh phones. No H1 FY27 results are out yet (due around Nov 2026). [H1 call] [H2 call]
- CRISIL (Feb-26) expected an FY26 operating margin of 17–18% (delivered 18.2%) and gearing below 0.5x. [CRISIL Feb-26]

## What changed between the two calls
- The mobile business went from pilot orders (H1) to ~₹55 cr and smartphones (H2). [H1 call] [H2 call]
- The company moved into retail hearing aids through SFL, and into export/SEZ through the JNPA letter of intent. [H2 call]
- Governance questions sharpened: investors raised the cash-flow reclassification and the mutual-fund-backed borrowing. [H2 call]
- The growth ambition grew to ₹500 cr+ for FY27, versus ₹293 cr in FY26. [H2 call]

######## 05-Vasa_Denticity.md
## Guidance history
- Earlier guidance (FY25/early FY26): ₹500–600 cr of FY27 revenue (per investors on both calls) and "₹800–1,200 cr in 5 years" (a webinar ~1.5 years ago). Gross margin target was 30–33% over the medium term. [Q4 call] [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]
- Q4 FY26 call (5 Jun 2026): **dropped specific revenue guidance** ("Earlier calls or guidance showed our immaturity in handling capital markets"). The new "North Star" is doubling revenue every 3–4 years (~20–26% a year), with EBITDA margin back to mid-teens in 3–4 years. Inventory days to run at 120–150 near term and below 100 later. Operating cash flow to improve "this year". No acquisition: IDS Denmed was dropped. Own-brand stock-outs had peaked above 33% and were at 14.6%. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 call (12 Aug 2026) and deck: **gross margin target cut** from 30–33% to **27–30%** ("On review of achievable mix and FX"). Mid-teens EBITDA brought forward to "2–3 years". Growth in Q2 expected to match Q1. Stock-outs at 12.87%, with a target below 5% by December. The ₹800–1,200 cr five-year ambition is still called "achievable". [IP Q1FY27] [Q1 call]
- Q1 delivery vs Q4 promises: margin recovery started (gross margin +110 bps QoQ, EBITDA from 0.4% to 6.1%), costs fell, and stock-outs fell. But the gross margin is still ~9 points below a year ago and below the new band. Inventory rose another ₹12.67 cr in the quarter, and working capital is not disclosed until the half-year. [IP Q1FY27]

## What changed between the two calls
- Tone went from apology (Q4: "Last year was not as I promised") to cautious repair (Q1: "a direction, not a destination"). [Q4 call] [Q1 call]
- Numbers: EBITDA margin from 0.4% to 6.1%; gross margin from 23.7% to 24.8%; stock-outs from 14.6% to 12.87%; warehouses from 7 to 5. [Screener] [IP Q1FY27]
- New: an explicit gross-margin band (cut to 27–30%), the Insta-Dent rollout, and AI chat/voice for returns. Newly flagged risks: too little marketing spend ("it has a cost of its own at the top of the funnel") and governance departures. [Q1 call] [IP Q1FY27]

######## 06-Prevest_Denpro.md
## Guidance history
- H1 FY26 call (20 Nov 2025): "Definitely, we expect to cross 20% in the next half year" (H2 FY26), and domestic growth in double digits in H2. Revenue to cross ₹100 cr "within three years", growing ~15% a year at the same margin, with no big new capex needed. [H1 call]
- Delivery: **H2 FY26 growth was 11.97%, missing the >20% promise**; domestic H2 growth was 12.72% (the double-digit promise was met); Q4 was only +2.6%. [IP FY26] [Q4 call]
- Q4 FY26 call (29 Jun 2026; the original 2 Jun call was cancelled because the MD had a medical emergency): **no numerical FY27 guidance**. Plans are to grow the US, open the UAE, scale disinfectants and 3D printers, and launch 2–3 OraDoc products. Four new products (caries-removal agent, irrigant, self-etch resin, periodontal gel) are near market (TRL 7), and an indigenous 3D printer is targeted by 2028. [Q4 call]
- Q1 FY27 (results only, no call): sales +21% and PAT +28%, a return to the ~15–20% trend after the weak Q4. [Screener]
- The ₹100 cr in 3 years target needs ~12% a year from FY26's ₹72 cr, which looks achievable on current run-rates. [H1 call] [Screener]

## What changed between the two calls
- H1 call (Nov-2025): upbeat, with export +24%, US +43%, a >20% H2 promise and ₹100 cr in 3 years. Q4 call (Jun-2026): more cautious. Q4 grew only 2.6%, no guidance was given, and the MD was absent. [H1 call] [Q4 call]
- New: the UAE subsidiary was delayed by the war, OraDoc was hit by MoCRA, and figures for printer and resin growth appeared. [Q4 call]
- Margins held; the stored profile's claim that Q4 "margins expanded to nearly 41%" includes other income. On Screener's operating basis Q4 OPM was 36.5%. [Screener]
